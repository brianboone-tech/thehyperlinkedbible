## Theme Information

**Trajectory Summary:**
The "image of God" (imago Dei) is not merely a static attribute but a **dynamic priestly vocation**—humanity created to reflect God's glory, represent His rule, and relate to Him in worship. This is fundamentally a **priestly** role: priests stand between God and creation, mediating God's presence and blessing. The image was damaged (not destroyed) at the Fall, partially restored in Israel's priesthood, perfectly embodied in Christ the true Image, and progressively renewed in believers until final glorification.

**Theme Vocabulary:**
- **Hebrew Terms**: צֶלֶם (*tselem*, image), דְּמוּת (*demut*, likeness), כָּבַשׁ (*kavash*, subdue), רָדָה (*radah*, have dominion), נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה (*nephesh chayyah*, living soul)
- **Greek Terms**: εἰκών (*eikōn*, image), πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*, firstborn), πλήρωμα (*plērōma*, fullness), ψυχὴν ζῶσαν (*psychēn zōsan*, living soul), πνεῦμα ζῳοποιοῦν (*pneuma zōopoioun*, life-giving spirit), χοϊκός (*choikos*, earthy), ἐπουράνιος (*epouranios*, heavenly)
- **Key Concepts**: image of God, imago Dei, likeness, dominion, subdue, priestly vocation, royal priesthood, first Adam, last Adam, creation mandate, fill the earth, glory, crowned with glory, firstborn, representation, reflect, mediate, dwelling, tabernacle, temple, sacred space, new creation, restoration, conformity to Christ, transformation, resurrection body, heavenly man, earthy man
