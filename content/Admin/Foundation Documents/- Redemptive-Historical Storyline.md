# Redemptive-Historical Storyline

**The Unified Narrative of Scripture from Genesis 1-3 to Its Fulfillment in Christ**

---

## Introduction

This document traces the **redemptive-historical storyline** that begins in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1|Genesis 1-3]] and unfolds throughout the Old Testament, finding its climax and fulfillment in Jesus Christ. The storyline reveals how God progressively reestablishes his new-creational kingdom out of chaos over a sinful people by his word and Spirit through promise, covenant, and redemption, resulting in worldwide commission to the faithful to advance this kingdom—all unto his glory.

**Source Framework**: Based on G. K. Beale's analysis in [[Books and Articles/G. K Beale/The Unfolding of the Old Testament in the New Testament/02. The Redemptive-Historical Storyline of the Old Testament.md|The Redemptive-Historical Storyline of the Old Testament]]

## Part 1: The Foundation — Genesis 1-3

### The Adamic Commission (Genesis 1:26-28)

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Core Elements</span>:
1. **Blessed** by God
2. **Be fruitful and multiply**
3. **Fill the earth**
4. **Subdue** the earth
5. **Rule over** all the earth

**Key Passage**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]

"Then God said, 'Let Us make man in Our image, after Our likeness, to rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air, over the livestock, and over all the earth itself and every creature that crawls upon it.' So God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them. God blessed them and said to them, 'Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth and subdue it; rule over the fish of the sea and the birds of the air and every creature that crawls upon the earth.'"

### Adam as Image-Bearer: Priest-King in the First Temple

<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Functional Definition of the Divine Image</span>:
- **Kingship**: Subduing and ruling as God's representative
- **Priestly Service**: "Cultivate and guard" the garden-temple ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|Genesis 2:15]])
- **Reflecting God's Glory**: Spreading divine presence throughout creation
- **Obedience to God's Word**: Knowing and keeping God's command ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 16|Genesis 2:16-17]])

**Ontological Attributes**: Volitional, rational, moral capacities—righteousness, knowledge, holiness, justice, love, faithfulness, integrity

### The Design of Escalated Blessings

<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">If Adam had remained faithful, he would have experienced irreversible, escalated blessings</span>:

1. **Decisive Victory over Evil**: Judging and subduing the serpent ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 1|Genesis 3]])
2. **Worldwide Extension of God's Glory**: Filling the entire earth with God's glorious presence (cf. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 1|Psalm 8]])
3. **Eternal Life**: Decisive eating from the tree of life, securing irreversible life ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|Genesis 3:22]])
4. **Eschatological Rest**: Unending Sabbath rest after overcoming opposition
5. **Incorruptible Existence**: Transformation from corruptible to incorruptible (spirit, soul, and body)
6. **Secured Creation**: The earth itself would be secured forever
7. **Consummated Marriage**: Human marriage as shadow of God's relationship with his bridal people

### The Tree of Discerning Between Good and Evil

**The Judgment Tree**: The place where Adam should have executed judgment on the serpent ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 9|Genesis 2:9, 17]])

- "Discerning between good and evil" = Hebrew expression for judicial authority (cf. [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 9|1 Kings 3:9]]; [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 14#2 Samuel 14 . 17|2 Samuel 14:17]])
- Adam should have judged the serpent as evil and pronounced God's judgment
- Instead, the serpent ruled over Adam and Eve through deceptive words

### Adam's Mission Trip: From Garden to Earth

- **Starting Point**: Rule begins in the garden sanctuary
- **Scope**: "Rule over all the earth" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26, 28]])
- **Expansion**: Moving outward from Eden until worldwide dominion achieved
- **Goal**: Extend the boundaries of the Eden temple around the entire earth

### The Fall: Failure and Exile

**Adam's Threefold Failure**:
1. Failed to guard the garden from the unclean serpent
2. Failed to execute judgment on evil
3. Failed to obey God's word

**Eve's Priestly Failure**: Misquoted God's word ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 2|Genesis 3:2-3]]), minimizing privileges and judgment while maximizing prohibition

**Consequences**:
- Expelled from God's glorious presence ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 23|Genesis 3:23-24]])
- Excluded from eternal life
- Forfeited the escalated blessings
- Creation subjected to curse and decay

## Part 2: The Pattern Repeated — Cycles of New Creation and Failure

### The Redemptive-Historical Pattern

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Five-Part Cycle</span>:
1. **Chaos/Judgment**
2. **New Creation**
3. **Adamic Commission** (for divine glory)
4. **Sinful Fall**
5. **Exile/Judgment**

**For exhaustive cycle-by-cycle analysis**: See [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles.md]] - Detailed examination of each commission restatement from Genesis 4 through Malachi with complete Scripture references and theological development.

## Part 3: The Commission Passed On

### Noah: The Second Adam (Genesis 6-9)

**New Creation After the Flood**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 1|Genesis 8-9]]

**Commission Restated**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1, 7]]

"God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them, 'Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth... Be fruitful and multiply; populate the earth abundantly and multiply in it.'"

**Pattern**:
- <span style="color: #c0392b;">Chaos</span>: Flood judgment
- <span style="color: #1f618d;">New Creation</span>: Waters recede, dry land appears
- <span style="color: #16a085;">Commission</span>: Adamic mandate repeated
- <span style="color: #922b21;">Fall</span>: Noah's drunkenness, Ham's sin ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 20|Genesis 9:20-27]])
- <span style="color: #8e44ad;">Exile</span>: Babel dispersion ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 1|Genesis 11:1-9]])

---

### Abraham: The Third Adam (Genesis 12-25)

**Key Passage**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Genesis 12:2-3]]

"I will make you a great nation, and I will bless you, and make your name great; and so be a blessing; and I will bless those who bless you, and the one who curses you I will curse. And in you all the families of the earth will be blessed."

**Commission Echoes**:
- **Blessed**: "I will bless you" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Gen 12:2]])
- **Multiply**: "I will multiply you exceedingly" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 2|Gen 17:2, 6]])
- **Fill**: "Your seed as the stars/sand" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 17|Gen 22:17]])
- **Subdue/Rule**: "Possess the gate of enemies" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 17|Gen 22:17]])
- **Universal Scope**: "All nations blessed" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Gen 22:18]])

**New Element**: <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Promise Form</span> - God promises enablement to fulfill the commission

**Pattern**:
- Abraham called from chaos of Babel
- Enters promised land (new Eden)
- Commission to bless all nations
- Partial failures (Egypt, Hagar)
- Seed continues through Isaac

---

### Isaac and Jacob: Generational Transmission

**Isaac** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 26#Genesis 26 . 3|Genesis 26:3-4, 24]]):

"Sojourn in this land and I will be with you and bless you... I will multiply your descendants as the stars of heaven, and will give your descendants all these lands; and by your descendants all the nations of the earth shall be blessed."

**Jacob** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 3|Genesis 28:3-4, 13-14]]; [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 11|35:11-12]]):

"May God Almighty bless you and make you fruitful and multiply you, that you may become a company of peoples. May he also give you the blessing of Abraham, to you and to your descendants with you, that you may possess the land..."

**New Formula**: <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">"I am with you"</span> - Divine presence promised as enablement

---

### Israel: Corporate Adam (Exodus - Malachi)

#### Exodus: New Creation Through the Red Sea

**Pattern**:
- <span style="color: #c0392b;">Chaos</span>: Egyptian oppression and plagues
- <span style="color: #1f618d;">New Creation</span>: Exodus through Red Sea ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 21|Exodus 14-15]])
- <span style="color: #16a085;">Commission</span>: "Kingdom of priests" ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]])
- <span style="color: #922b21;">Fall</span>: Golden calf ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 1|Exodus 32]])
- <span style="color: #8e44ad;">Exile</span>: Wilderness wandering (first generation dies)

**Fulfillment Begins**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 47#Genesis 47 . 27|Genesis 47:27]]; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 1#Exodus 1 . 7|Exodus 1:7]]

"Israel lived in the land of Egypt, in Goshen, and they acquired property in it and were fruitful and became very numerous... But the sons of Israel were fruitful and increased greatly, and multiplied, and became exceedingly mighty, so that the land was filled with them."

**Commission Expanded**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]

"You shall be to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation."

<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Corporate Adamic Role</span>:
- Spread light of God's presence to dark world
- Mediators between God and nations
- Witness to God's glory
- Fill earth with God's image-bearers

#### Promised Land as New Eden

**Land = Garden of Eden** (explicitly compared):
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 51#Isaiah 51 . 3|Isaiah 51:3]]: "Her wilderness He will make like Eden, and her desert like the garden of the Lord"
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 35|Ezekiel 36:35]]: Land compared to garden of Eden
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 3|Joel 2:3]]: "Before them the land is like the garden of Eden"

**Temple = Garden Sanctuary**:
- God's glorious presence dwelling among them
- Priests guarding the holy place
- Cherubim imagery echoing Eden's guardians ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]])

#### Conquest: Subduing the Land

**Joshua** - Second exodus through Jordan ([[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 3#Joshua 3 . 1|Joshua 3-4]]):
- Possessing the land = fulfilling "subdue and rule"
- Partial success: "The Lord gave them rest on every side" ([[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 44|Joshua 21:44-45]])
- Incomplete: Enemy nations remain ([[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 23#Joshua 23 . 12|Joshua 23:12-16]])

**Repeated Command**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 13|Deuteronomy 7:13]]

"He will love you and bless you and multiply you; He will also bless the fruit of your womb... in the land which He swore to your forefathers to give you."

#### Judges: Cycle of Failure

**Repeated Pattern**:
1. Israel sins
2. Oppression by enemies (judgment)
3. Cry to God
4. Deliverance through judge
5. Return to sin

**Ruth**: Preserving the Davidic line through chaos

#### Kingship: Return to Genesis 1-3 Pattern

**Why Kingship?** - Adam was installed as king over creation

**Saul**: First king fails like first Adam

**David**: The Promised King

**Key Promise** - [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-19]]:

"When your days are complete and you rest with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who will come from your own body, and I will establish his kingdom. He will build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever."

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Davidic Covenant Elements</span>:
- Eternal throne ([[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 13|2 Sam 7:13, 16]])
- Son of God ([[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Sam 7:14]])
- Worldwide rule
- Temple builder
- Defeats all enemies

**Solomon**: Temple built, God's glory fills it ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-13]]) - yet incomplete fulfillment

#### Divided Kingdom and Exile

**Pattern Continues**:
- <span style="color: #c0392b;">Chaos</span>: Sin, idolatry, oppression
- <span style="color: #922b21;">Judgment</span>: Assyrian conquest (Northern Kingdom)
- <span style="color: #8e44ad;">Exile</span>: Babylonian exile (Southern Kingdom)

**Like Adam**: Israel expelled from garden-land, excluded from God's presence

**God's Presence Departs**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 22|11:22-23]]

## Part 4: The Prophetic Hope — Eschatological Adam

### The Promise of Future Fulfillment

Despite repeated failure, the prophets promise a future eschatological fulfillment of the Adamic commission.

#### Psalm 8: The Ideal Adam

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 1|Psalm 8:1-9]]

**Structure**:
- Opens and closes: "How majestic is Your name in all the earth" (vv. 1, 9)
- **Goal**: God's glory/splendor filling the entire earth
- **Means**: Humanity crowned with glory and majesty (v. 5)
- **Method**: Ruling over all creation (vv. 6-8)
- **Includes**: Making "the enemy and the revengeful cease" (v. 2)

<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">This psalm describes the eschatological Adam who will finally fulfill Genesis 1:26-28</span>

#### Psalm 72: The Eschatological King

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 1|Psalm 72]]

**Key Themes**:
- **Universal Rule**: "From sea to sea... to the ends of the earth" (v. 8)
- **Overcomes Opposition**: Crushes oppressor (v. 4), enemies lick the dust (v. 9)
- **Eternal Reign**: "As long as the sun endures" (vv. 5, 7, 17)
- **Blessing to Nations**: "All nations call him blessed" (v. 17 - echo of Gen 22:18)
- **Ultimate Goal**: "May the whole earth be filled with His glory" (v. 19)

#### Psalm 89: Forever Throne

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 19|Psalm 89:19-37]]

**Davidic King**:
- Rules over opposition (vv. 22-23)
- Throne lasts forever (vv. 27-29, 36-37)
- Sonship: "I will also make him My firstborn" (v. 27)
- Echoes 2 Samuel 7 covenant

#### Isaiah: New Creation and New Adam

**Eden Restored**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 51#Isaiah 51 . 2|Isaiah 51:2-3]]

"Then I blessed him [Abraham] and multiplied him... Her [Israel's] wilderness He will make like Eden, and her desert like the garden of the Lord."

**Fruitfulness**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 1|Isaiah 54:1-3]]

"The sons of the desolate one will be more numerous... Enlarge the place of your tent; stretch out the curtains of your dwellings... Lengthen your cords and strengthen your pegs. For you will spread abroad to the right and to the left. And your seed will possess the nations."

**Servant Songs**: The suffering servant who will restore Israel and be a light to the nations ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1-9]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 1|49:1-13]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 13|52:13-53:12]])

**New Heavens and New Earth**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17-25]]

#### Jeremiah: Future Multiplication

[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 3|Jeremiah 23:3]]:

"Then I Myself will gather the remnant of My flock out of all the countries where I have driven them and bring them back to their pasture, and they will be fruitful and multiply."

[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 3#Jeremiah 3 . 16|Jeremiah 3:16]]:

"It shall be in those days when you are multiplied and increased in the land..."

#### Ezekiel: Renewed Commission

[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 9|Ezekiel 36:9-12]]:

"You [the promised land] will be cultivated and sown. I will multiply men on you [the land]... I will multiply on you [the land] man [Israel] and beast; and they will increase and be fruitful... and possess you."

**New Heart**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]]

"I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you... I will put My Spirit within you and cause you to walk in My statutes."

#### Daniel 7: The Son of Man

[[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14, 27]]

**The One Like a Son of Man**:
- Comes with clouds of heaven
- Given eternal dominion over all peoples
- His kingdom never destroyed
- Replaces beastly kingdoms
- Saints possess the kingdom forever

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">This is the eschatological Adam who will rule eternally in an incorruptible kingdom</span>

#### Hosea: Countless as Sand

[[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 10|Hosea 1:10]]:

"Yet the number of the sons of Israel will be like the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured or numbered... 'You are the sons of the living God.'"

## Part 5: Summary — The Storyline Pointing to Christ

### The Unified Pattern

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Genesis 1-3 establishes the pattern</span>:
- Creation in God's image
- Commission to fill, subdue, rule
- Priestly service in God's presence
- Obedience to God's word
- Goal: Filling earth with God's glory
- Design: Escalated blessings leading to eternal, incorruptible existence
- Failure: Sin, judgment, exile

<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">OT repeats the pattern</span>:
- Noah (new creation after flood) → fails
- Abraham (blessed to bless nations) → partial
- Israel (corporate Adam, kingdom of priests) → fails
- David (promised eternal king) → incomplete

<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Prophets promise future fulfillment</span>:
- Eschatological Adam (Psalm 8)
- Eternal King (Psalm 72, 89)
- Suffering Servant (Isaiah 53)
- Son of Man (Daniel 7)
- New Creation (Isaiah 65)
- Worldwide blessing (all nations)

### Key Modifications After the Fall

1. **Promise Form**: God promises enablement (not just command)
2. **Divine Presence**: "I am with you" formula
3. **Spiritual Warfare**: Subduing now includes overcoming evil in human hearts
4. **Witness Mission**: Blessing nations = converting them to image-bearers
5. **New Heart**: Internal transformation necessary (Ezek 36:26-27)
6. **Suffering Path**: Victory through suffering (Isaiah 53)

### The Expectation Building

**Three Certainties Emerge**:
1. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">A Last Adam must come</span> - no mere human has succeeded
2. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">He will be an eternal, incorruptible King</span> - ruling forever in righteousness
3. <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">He will fulfill the Genesis 1:26-28 commission</span> - filling earth with God's glory

### The Christocentric Fulfillment

**Jesus Christ is**:
- <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The Last Adam</span> (Romans 5:12-21; 1 Cor 15:45-49)
- <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The True Israel</span> (succeeding where corporate Adam failed)
- <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">The Promised Seed</span> (Genesis 3:15; Galatians 3:16)
- <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The Eternal King</span> (2 Samuel 7; Luke 1:32-33)
- <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">The Son of Man</span> (Daniel 7; Mark 14:62)
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">The Suffering Servant</span> (Isaiah 53; Mark 10:45)
- <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">The Temple Builder</span> (Zechariah 6:12-13; John 2:19-21)
- <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">The Image of God</span> (2 Corinthians 4:4; Colossians 1:15)

**In Christ**:
- Genesis 1-3 finds its fulfillment
- The Adamic commission is accomplished
- The earth will be filled with God's glory
- New creation is inaugurated and will be consummated
- Eternal, incorruptible kingdom established
- All nations blessed
- God's presence dwells with his people forever

## Conclusion: The Story of Scripture

**Beginning** (Genesis 1-3):
- Adam as king-priest in first creation
- Commission to fill earth with God's glory
- Failure, sin, exile

**Middle** (Genesis 4 - Malachi):
- Commission passed on repeatedly
- Cycles of new creation and failure
- Promise of eschatological fulfillment

**Climax** (Christ):
- Last Adam succeeds
- Eternal kingdom established
- New creation inaugurated

**Consummation** (Revelation 21-22):
- New heavens and new earth
- God's glory fills all creation
- Eden restored and expanded to cosmic scale
- God dwells with his people forever
- No more curse, sin, death, or exile
- Eternal rest achieved

---

**The redemptive-historical storyline of Scripture is the story of God progressively reestablishing his new-creational kingdom out of chaos over a sinful people by his word and Spirit through promise, covenant, and redemption, resulting in worldwide commission to the faithful to advance this kingdom and judgment for the unfaithful—all fulfilled in Jesus Christ, the Last Adam, unto God's eternal glory.**
