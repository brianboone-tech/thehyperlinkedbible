# Meredith Kline - Theological Framework

**Reformed theologian (1922-2007)** | Professor at Westminster Theological Seminary, Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary, and Westminster Seminary California

---

## Overview

Meredith G. Kline stands as one of the most creative and profound biblical theologians of the 20th century Reformed tradition. His work integrates ancient Near Eastern treaty studies, biblical theology, systematic theology, and eschatology into a comprehensive framework centered on the Glory-Spirit theophany and covenant structure. Kline's theology is marked by several distinctive contributions: the **Intrusion** concept, **Glory-dimension metaphysics**, **typological ethics**, **temple cosmology**, and a radical re-envisioning of the relationship between creation, covenant, and eschatology.

---

## Core Theological Contributions

### 1. **The Intrusion and Eschatology**

**Central Thesis**: The Consummation intrudes into the age of Common Grace through the Covenant of Grace, particularly in the Old Testament Theocracy.

**Key Concepts**:

- **Common Grace**: The delay of final judgment, allowing time for redemptive history to unfold
- **The Intrusion**: The breaking through of Consummation realities into the present age before their appointed time
- **Typology as Intrusion**: Old Testament types are not mere pedagogical illustrations but anticipatory actualizations of the Consummation

**The "Perez Principle"** (Genesis 38:29): Like Perez breaking through the womb first despite being the second twin, the Consummation breaks through beforehand, making a breach into history. This becomes the paradigm for understanding how eschatological realities intrude into the present age.

**Eschatological Structure**:

**Covenant of Works** (Creation Covenant):
- Original eschatological pathway
- Pattern: probationary work → confirmed rest (Creator's own pattern)
- Eschatology precedes soteriology—creation itself was oriented toward consummation
- Adam's task: meritorious obedience leading to glorification and Sabbath rest
- **Never successfully opened** (door never used due to the Fall)

**The Fall**:
- Introduced delay but did not cancel the Consummation itself
- Made redemptive history necessary
- Shifted pathway from Works to Grace

**Covenant of Grace** (Redemptive Covenant):
- New pathway involving anticipatory intrusions of final realities
- Same eschatological goal (Sabbath rest, glorification)
- Different means: vicarious work by God-Man
- Progressive administrations with increasing intensity of Intrusion
- Old Testament Theocracy as concentrated Intrusion zone

**Common Grace**:
- Epitomizes and maintains the delay
- Provides historical field for Saving Grace to operate
- **Coterminous with the present age**—when Common Grace ends, history ends
- Characterized by restraint of final judgment
- Mixed state: wheat and tares together
- Positive contribution to eschatological program (not merely negative delay)

**The Tension**: Covenant of Grace and Common Grace are antithetical yet coexistent. Common Grace delays what Covenant of Grace anticipates. This explains the "already/not yet" character of redemptive history.

### 2. **Heaven as the Glory-Dimension (Doxa-Dimension)**

**Metaphysical Innovation**: Kline's most radical contribution to Christian metaphysics.

**Heaven is NOT**:
- A place outside the cosmos (spatial transcendence model)
- A separate part of the universe at distance from earth
- Remote from earthly reality
- "Up there" in any spatial sense

**Heaven IS**:
- **Coextensive with the visible cosmos** in its entirety
- Present right here, interpenetrating all space
- Distinguished by a **metadimension** (Glory/Spirit-dimension)
- The invisible dimension of the one cosmos
- What makes heaven "heavenly" is not location but **Glory-quality**

**The Two-Register Cosmology** (Genesis 1):

**Upper Register**:
- The invisible Glory-creation (heaven)
- The Spirit-dimension
- The archetypal temple

**Lower Register**:
- The visible earth
- Material dimension
- The anti-typical temple (creation modeled after heaven)

Both created simultaneously ("In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth"), interpenetrating as one cosmos with two dimensions.

**The Glory-Spirit as Structural Reality**:

The Glory-cloud is not merely a *sign* of God's presence but the very *structure* of heaven itself:

- **Heaven's Architecture**: The Glory-Presence itself constitutes the temple
- **"God is the temple"** (Revelation 21:22)—not God *has* a temple but God *is* the temple
- The epiphanic Glory forms both atmosphere and architecture
- Identified with the Spirit ("Spirit-dimension")
- Out of this Glory-dimensioned epiphanic stuff heaven is constructed

**Key Images of the Glory-Spirit**:

- **Royal Court**: Heaven as throne-palace, God enthroned amid heavenly council
- **Holy Temple**: Heaven as sanctuary (*qōdeš*, *miqdāš*)
- **Glory-Canopy**: Shekinah as structural/architectural element (Isaiah 4:5-6)
- **Smoke-Filled Space**: Glory as atmosphere filling the temple (Isaiah 6:4)
- **Cherubim-Chariot**: Glory-Spirit as mobile throne (Ezekiel 1)
- **Pillar of Cloud and Fire**: Glory as visible manifestation in history

**The Atmosphere of Heaven = The Secret of Immortality**:

- Spirit is the breath of life (Genesis 2:7; John 20:22)
- Heaven-dwellers breathe continually the Spirit-breath
- This perpetual inhalation of the Spirit of life **is** immortality
- Separation from this atmosphere = death
- Glorification = transformation to breathe Spirit permanently

**Implications**:

- Heaven is not "far away" but presently here, veiled
- The Parousia does not bring heaven *from* distant space but *unveils* the Glory-dimension already present
- Glorification is not transportation but transformation of perception/capability
- New heavens and earth = unveiling of the Glory-dimension to glorified humanity

### 3. **Typology and the Pattern of Heaven**

**The Archetypal-Antitype-Type Structure**:

Traditional typology: OT type → NT antitype

Kline's innovation: **Heavenly Archetype** → OT Antitype → NT/Consummation Type

**The Pattern**:

1. **Archetype**: The heavenly reality itself (the Glory-Spirit temple in heaven)
2. **Antitype**: Old Testament earthly copies looking *backward* to heaven
3. **Type**: These same copies pointing *forward* to Messianic fulfillment

**Three Temporal Directions**:

Every OT symbol simultaneously:
1. **Looks backward** (antitype to heavenly archetype)
2. **Functions presently** (sacramental aspect of current Intrusion)
3. **Points forward** (type to future Messianic/Consummation fulfillment)

**Earthly Replicas of Heaven**:

**The Tabernacle/Temple**:
- Miniaturized models of God's holy heaven
- Antitype of the Glory-cloud structure
- Flame-colored linen = Glory-fire
- Cherubim = heavenly council
- Multiple coverings = cloud layers
- Holy of Holies = heaven itself
- Architecture mirrors Glory-cloud structure (lintel/pillars = cloud canopy/pillars)

**The Promised Land**:
- Image of paradise character of heavenly inheritance
- Canaan = microcosm of restored creation
- Land flowing with milk and honey = Edenic abundance
- Conquest = typological final judgment
- Rest in the land = eschatological Sabbath rest

**Aaron's Priestly Vestments**:
- Replica of the tabernacle, which is replica of Glory-cloud
- Therefore Aaron's robes = replica of Glory-Spirit
- Flame-colored linen with gold
- For "glory and beauty" (same terms as Glory theophany)
- Ephod, breast-piece, turban = architectural elements of Glory-structure
- Investiture = image of God symbolism

**Prophetic Visions**:
- Heaven unveiled through supernaturally opened eyes
- Isaiah 6, Ezekiel 1, Daniel 7, Revelation 4 = glimpses of archetypal reality
- What prophets saw = the Glory-dimension made visible

**Core vs. Periphery Distinction**:

**Core**:
- Permanent spiritual reality
- The Glory-Spirit itself
- Ethical/spiritual dimensions
- Carries through all administrations

**Periphery**:
- Temporary typological forms
- External ceremonies
- Cultural/historical specifics
- Pass away at fulfillment

Example:
- Core = God's presence with His people
- Periphery = Specific tabernacle structure, Aaronic priesthood, animal sacrifices

**Characteristics of Valid Typology**:

1. **Historical Reality**: Both type and antitype are actual historical events/persons, not allegories
2. **Divine Design**: God intentionally structured earlier to prefigure later
3. **Correspondence**: Genuine analogical relationship in structure/function
4. **Escalation**: Antitype is greater than type
5. **Prospectiveness**: Type inherently points forward (though recognized retrospectively)
6. **Attribute of the Intrusion**: Typology belongs to Covenant of Grace, not Covenant of Works

**Intrusion Character of Typology**:

- Typology = the Intrusion in symbolic/sacramental form
- OT types bring Consummation realities into present history
- Not mere pedagogy but actual (though veiled) presence of future reality
- "Realized eschatology as core with typical surface"

### 4. **Creation, Cosmos, and the Image of God**

**Space and Time in Genesis Cosmology**:

**The Seven-Day Framework**:

The creation week is simultaneously:
1. **Historical narrative** of actual creation events
2. **Literary structure** (sabbatical framework)
3. **Prototypal paradigm** of the Day of the Lord
4. **Covenant document** (treaty structure implicit)

**The Two-Register Creation** (Genesis 1:1):
- "Heavens" (plural) = Glory-dimension, invisible
- "Earth" = material-dimension, visible
- Both created together as integrated cosmos
- Genesis 1:2 describes lower register (earth) pre-formation state
- Genesis 1:2 also reveals upper register (Spirit hovering)

**The Glory-Spirit in Creation** (Genesis 1:2):

"The Spirit of God hovering over the waters"

Kline's interpretation:
- This is the Glory-cloud theophany
- Same phenomenon as Sinai cloud, tabernacle Glory, Ezekiel's chariot-throne
- Spirit in oath-stance (compare Revelation 10:1-7)
- Covenant witness from the beginning
- Archetypal temple hovering over proto-temple earth

Functions:
1. **Paradigm**: Pattern for all subsequent creation
2. **Power**: Creative agent bringing order from chaos
3. **Presence**: Covenant witness sanctifying creation
4. **Promise**: Alpha-archetype of Omega-Sabbath goal

**Temple Cosmology**:

Creation itself is a cosmic temple:
- **Eden as Proto-Temple**: Microcosmic sanctuary within macrocosmic sanctuary
- **Guardianship by Cherubim**: Heavenly council members as sanctuary guardians
- **East-facing Orientation**: Direction of Glory-presence
- **Sacred Space**: Structured with increasing holiness toward center
- **God's Dwelling**: Creation designed as His residence

Progression:
- Cosmic temple (creation) → Eden (microcosmic temple) → Tabernacle → Temple → Christ (incarnate temple) → Church (corporate temple) → New Jerusalem (consummated cosmic-temple)

**Creation in the Image of the Glory-Spirit**:

Genesis 1:26-27 is the climax of creation, but what does "image of God" mean?

**The Divine Paradigm**: The Glory-Spirit itself
- Humanity created to replicate the Glory-likeness
- Not abstract divine attributes but specifically theophanic Glory-image

**Three Dimensions of the Image**:

1. **Ethical Glory**:
   - Holiness and righteousness
   - Moral likeness to God
   - Capacity for covenant faithfulness

2. **Functional/Judicial Glory**:
   - Royal dominion over creation
   - Priestly mediation
   - Judicial authority (knowledge of good and evil)
   - Exercising God-like sovereignty

3. **Physical/Formal Glory**:
   - Eschatological goal (not yet in Genesis 1)
   - Luminous transfiguration (Moses' face, Christ's transfiguration)
   - Ultimate: resurrection bodies radiating Glory
   - Consummation: humanity as living light-bearers

**Two Models of Image-Creation**:

**Priestly Model** (Investment):
- Image = being clothed with Glory-vestments
- Aaron's investiture as paradigm
- God "dresses" humanity in His likeness
- Nuptial imagery: bride adorned for husband
- Ezekiel 16: God spreading Glory-robe over Israel

**Prophetic Model** (Rapture):
- Image = being caught up into Glory-council
- Prophets transformed by encounter with Glory
- Moses' transfigured face
- Standing in divine council
- Eschatological: all believers as prophets (Joel 2, Acts 2)

Both models unite in Christ:
- Christ = ultimate Prophet-Priest
- Church clothed in Glory (priestly) and filled with Spirit (prophetic)
- Revelation 21-22: Bride-city wearing Glory-vestments, all God's servants as prophets

**Marriage Covenant and Image**:

- Creating humanity in God's image = covenant of marriage
- God as husband, humanity as bride
- Image-bearer = bride bearing husband's glory and name
- Genesis 2 marriage = parable of divine-human covenant
- Consummation = marriage supper of the Lamb

### 5. **The Covenant of Works and Probation**

**Creational Covenant Identity**:

Kline's defense of "Covenant of Works" terminology against modern critics:

Evidence that Genesis 1-3 is covenantal:

1. **Substance of berith present**: Commitment, sanctions, stipulations, blessings/curses
2. **Divine oath-stance**: Glory-Spirit in Genesis 1:2 = covenant witness
3. **Image-investiture**: Creating in God's image = covenant pledge (Ezekiel 16)
4. **Sabbath as covenant sign**: Celebrates Creator's covenant lordship
5. **Later biblical references**: Isaiah 24:5, Hosea 6:7, Jeremiah 33:20-25
6. **Two-Adams parallel**: Christ as second Adam under covenant of works

**The Probationary Structure**:

**Eschatology Built-In**:
- Creation itself oriented toward consummation ("eschatology precedes soteriology")
- Sabbath rest as goal from the beginning
- Not static perpetuation but dynamic movement toward glorification
- The in-created longing for consummated image-likeness

**The Works Principle**:

"Do this and live" - meritorious obedience required

Why "Works" not "Grace"?:
- Grace = response to demerit (covenant-breaking)
- Works = reward for merit (covenant-keeping)
- Adam had neither merit nor demerit initially
- Obedience would *earn* (merit) the reward
- This is justice, not grace

Defense against critics:
- **Objection**: Creatures cannot merit anything from God
- **Answer**: God defines justice by covenant terms; if He says obedience merits reward, it does
- **Objection**: Paternal love excludes works-principle
- **Answer**: Father-Son covenant (Christ's probation) proves otherwise
- **Objection**: Disproportionality between act and eternal reward
- **Answer**: This impugns God's justice; He defines proportionality

**The Two Trees**:

**Tree of Life**:
- Sacramental seal of eternal life
- Symbol of the Glory-Spirit (archetype)
- Not mere endless existence but glorified life
- Access reserved for post-probation confirmation
- Points to Christ as "the Life"
- Restoration in Revelation 22

**Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil**:
- The judgment/probation tree
- Knowledge = judicial discernment (not abstract information)
- God-like judicial authority signified
- Positive: pronounce judgment on evil (Satan)
- Negative: don't usurp autonomous judgment (eating)
- Crisis point: will Adam judge Satan or join him?

**The Probationary Assignment**:

Negative aspect:
- "You shall not eat..." (Genesis 2:17)
- Recognition of God's absolute authority
- Submission to God's interpretive word
- Resisting autonomous epistemology

Positive aspect:
- Exercise judicial authority against intruder
- Guard sanctuary holiness
- Pronounce covenant curses on covenant-breaker
- Perform "one act of righteousness" (Romans 5:18)

**Satanic Temptation as Necessary Component**:
- Probation required actual test
- Satan's presence not accident but divine arrangement
- Tested Adam's covenant loyalty at deepest level
- Would Adam fight LORD's battle?
- Judicial combat = the assigned meritorious work

**The Dual Sanctions**:

**Blessing Sanction**:
1. **Immediate**: Confirmation in righteousness (no longer able to sin)
2. **Semi-Eschatological Stage**: Completing cultural mandate in confirmed state
3. **Ultimate Consummation**:
   - Physical glorification
   - Entrance into cosmic Sabbath rest
   - Metapolis (glorified culture beyond earthly culture)
   - Unveiling of Glory-dimension
   - Full image-likeness realized

**Curse Sanction**:
- Death = reversal of image-likeness
- Loss of all glory (ethical, functional, physical)
- Frustration of eschatological hope
- Separation from Glory-breath = spiritual death
- Ultimate: "second death" (lake of fire)
- Not initially physical death (that comes later as mitigated curse)

**Federal Representation**:
- Adam as covenant head of humanity
- One man's act determines all
- Imputation of Adam's act to posterity
- Parallel to Christ as second Adam
- Romans 5:12-21 as exposition

### 6. **Common Grace and the Delay of Consummation**

**Definition**: Common Grace is the delay of final judgment, providing historical space for redemptive history.

**Central Thesis**: Common Grace and the Covenant of Grace are antithetical yet coexistent principles.

**Common Grace Characteristics**:

1. **Universal Scope**: Benefits all humanity, not just elect
2. **Temporal**: Coterminous with history—when it ends, history ends
3. **Restraining**: Holds back full judgment and full blessing
4. **Mixed State**: Wheat and tares, sheep and goats together
5. **Cultural**: Enables human culture, science, art, society
6. **Providential**: Maintains order of nature (Genesis 9)

**Relationship to Covenant of Grace**:

**Antithetical**:
- Common Grace delays what Covenant of Grace anticipates
- Common Grace principles opposed to Consummation ethics
- Love enemies (Common Grace) vs. perfect hatred of reprobate (Consummation)
- Mutual exclusivity: when one ends, the other arrives

**Yet Coexistent**:
- Common Grace provides historical field for Saving Grace
- Saving Grace operates within Common Grace period
- Common Grace makes positive contribution to eschatological program
- Elect gathered during Common Grace period

**The World That Then Was** (2 Peter 3:5-7):

Kline's cosmic periodization:

**First World** (Prediluvian):
- Genesis 1:1 to Noah's Flood
- Complete world-history cycle
- Creation → Culture → Judgment → Re-creation
- Paradigm for subsequent history

**Second World** (Present):
- Noah's Flood to Second Coming
- Common Grace explicitly covenanted (Genesis 9)
- Follows same pattern as first world
- Moves toward fiery judgment

**Third World** (Consummation):
- New heavens and new earth
- No more Common Grace
- Glorified state

**Genesis 9 Covenant**:
- Explicit Common Grace covenant
- Guarantees natural order
- Rainbow sign
- "While earth remains... seedtime and harvest..."
- Restrains both judgment and full blessing
- Makes history possible post-Flood

### 7. **Intrusion Ethics: The Ethical Problem of the Old Testament**

**The Central Problem**: How do we explain seemingly immoral commands in the Old Testament?

- Genocide of Canaanites
- Imprecatory Psalms
- Deception commanded (Rahab, Samuel)
- Sacrifice of Isaac
- Hosea's marriage to harlot

**Traditional Solutions** (all inadequate):

1. **Progressive Revelation**: OT ethics were "lower," now superseded (✗ implies God's character changed)
2. **Cultural Accommodation**: God accommodated fallen cultural standards (✗ makes God approve sin)
3. **Hyperbole**: Commands weren't literal (✗ dismisses plain text)
4. **Dispensationalism**: Different rules for different ages (✗ arbitrary divisions)

**Kline's Solution: Ethics of Intrusion vs. Ethics of Common Grace**

**Two Ethical Systems Operating Simultaneously**:

**Common Grace Ethics**:
- Govern present age
- Love your enemies
- Show mercy to all
- Patience with sinners
- Mixed community
- Second Table commands apply straightforwardly
- "Turn the other cheek"

**Consummation Ethics**:
- Govern final state
- Perfect hatred of incorrigible reprobate
- Great gulf fixed (Luke 16:26)
- No mercy for condemned
- Separated community (wheat/tares separated)
- Second Table commands transformed
- "Vengeance is mine, says the Lord"

**The Intrusion Principle**:

In Old Testament Theocracy, **Consummation ethics intruded** into the Common Grace age:
- Not evolution *from* lower ethics
- But intrusion *of* higher (eschatological) ethics
- Required *highest* faith, not lowest ethics
- Typological anticipation of final judgment

**The Two Tables of the Law**:

**First Table** (Worship of God):
- Commands: Worship God alone, no idols, honor God's name, Sabbath
- **Immutable in Application**: Same duties in all ages
- **Variable in Sanctions**: Different enforcement in different ages
  - OT Theocracy: Death penalty
  - NT Church Age: Church discipline
  - Consummation: Second death

**Second Table** (Relationships with Others):
- Commands: Honor parents, no murder, no adultery, no theft, no false witness, no coveting
- **Mutable in Application**: Because relationships themselves change
- Grounded in both permanent divine nature AND present covenantal conditions
- The identity of "neighbor" changes eschatologically

**Three Types of Ethical Changes**:

**Type 1: Pedagogical/Remedial**:
- Toleration of polygamy
- Divorce permissions
- Gradual ethical instruction
- Not Intrusion but accommodation to sin
- Temporary until NT clarification

**Type 2: Consummation Changes**:
- No marriage in heaven (Matthew 22:30)
- Great gulf fixed between saved/lost
- Transformation of relationships
- These are permanent changes at Consummation

**Type 3: Old Testament Intrusions**:
- Anticipatory application of Consummation ethics
- Typological/prophetic
- Time-bound to OT Theocracy
- Not normative for Church Age

### 8. **Intrusions of Judgment**

**Conquest of Canaan**:

**The Problem**: How can God command genocide?

**Kline's Answer**:
- Not justified by Common Grace ethics
- Intrusion of final judgment into history
- Canaanites = typological reprobate
- Israelites = hosts of Yahweh executing judgment
- "The meek shall inherit the earth" typologically enacted
- Required highest faith to trust God's word over conscience
- Prophetic preview of Consummation

**Key Principles**:
- This was *God's* act, Israel merely instrumental
- Not repeatable in Church Age (no Intrusion now)
- Typological, not normative
- Points to greater reality: final judgment

**Imprecatory Psalms**:

**The Problem**: How can righteous pray for enemies' destruction?

**Kline's Answer**:
- Not violations of "love your enemies"
- Anticipations of Consummation attitudes
- At final judgment, hatred of reprobate will be holy duty
- Psalmists speaking prophetically from Consummation perspective
- Intrusion of eschatological ethics

**Three Ethical Stances on Enemies**:

1. **Common Grace Age** (normative now):
   - Love enemies, pray for persecutors
   - Show mercy, seek conversion
   - Leave vengeance to God

2. **Intrusion** (OT Theocracy):
   - Imprecate God's judgment
   - Prophetic stance
   - Typological

3. **Consummation** (coming age):
   - Perfect, holy hatred of incorrigible wicked
   - Justice demands it
   - Love and holiness united

**Other Judgment Intrusions**:

**Rahab's Deception** (Joshua 2):
- Violation of 9th commandment?
- No—5th commandment Intrusion
- Allegiance to God's kingdom trumps allegiance to doomed earthly kingdom
- Eschatological loyalties override present-age loyalties
- Typological picture of choosing Christ over world

**Samuel Deceiving Saul** (1 Samuel 16:1-5):
- God commands Samuel to mislead Saul
- 9th commandment Intrusion
- God executing judgment on rejected king
- Truth-telling obligation to covenant-breaker modified
- Anticipates Consummation separation

**"Coveting" Canaanite Land**:
- 10th commandment concerns *wrongful* desire
- Desiring what God promises to give = not covetousness
- Intrusion: eschatological inheritance typologically actualized
- "Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth"

**Hermeneutical Principle**:

"It was only in union with the highest outreach of faith that there could be true compliance with the demands of [Intrusion] ethic."

- These acts required trusting God's word absolutely
- Not lower ethics but higher faith
- Obedience to covenant revelation over abstract principles
- Must hear Father's voice in specific revelation

### 9. **Intrusions of Salvation**

**Abraham's "Sacrifice" of Isaac**:

**The Problem**: God commands human sacrifice?

**Kline's Answer**:
- God sovereignly redefines Isaac's covenantal significance
- Not about ethics of human sacrifice per se
- Testing purely covenantal nature of obedience
- **Typological Intrusion of the Cross**
- Points forward to God sacrificing His own Son

**Key Insights**:
- Isaac = typological Christ
- Abraham = typological Father
- Ram substitute = inadequacy of animal sacrifice
- Tests whether Abraham fears God above all
- Requires hearing God's specific word, not abstract principles
- Ultimate obedience to covenant Lord

**Hosea's Marriage to Gomer**:

**The Problem**: God commands prophet to marry prostitute?

**Kline's Answer**:
- Contrary to Mosaic law provisions
- **Intrusion of Christ taking defiled Church as Bride**
- Prophetic embodiment of covenant love
- Typifies Church formed from sinners

**Significance**:
- Hosea = typological Christ
- Gomer = typological Church
- Marriage = covenant between God and sinful humanity
- Redemption of harlot = redemption of covenant-breakers
- Intrusion requires setting aside Levitical purity laws typologically

**Theocracy and Gentile Kings**:

**Cyrus, Darius Supporting Temple** (Ezra, Nehemiah):

**The Problem**: Pagan kings funding God's temple?

**Kline's Answer**:
- **Anticipation of Revelation 21:24**: "Kings bring their glory into New Jerusalem"
- Intrusion of Consummation pattern
- Not normative for NT Church-State relations
- Typological Theocracy receiving tribute

**Church Age Distinction**:
- NT Church ≠ OT Theocracy
- No political kingdom now
- "My kingdom is not of this world"
- Awaiting Consummation for full manifestation

### 10. **The Image of God: Priestly and Prophetic Models**

**Two Biblical Models for Understanding Image**:

### **Priestly Model** (Images of the Spirit, Chapter 2)

**Core Concept**: Image of God = being invested with Glory-vestments

**Paradigm**: Aaron's investiture with priestly garments

**The Tabernacle-Vestments Connection**:

1. Tabernacle = replica of Glory-Spirit
2. Aaron's robes = replica of Tabernacle
3. Therefore: Aaron's robes = replica of Glory-Spirit

**The Symbolic System**:

**Material**:
- Flame-colored linen (blue, purple, scarlet)
- Gold threads
- Same materials as Tabernacle inner curtains
- Radiating fiery glory-effect

**Structure**:
- Multi-layered (like cloud layers)
- Ephod = tent-like structure
- Shoulder pieces + turban = gate of heaven motif
- Breast-piece = Holy of Holies

**Significance**:
- "For glory and beauty" (Exodus 28:2)
- Same terminology as Glory theophany
- Purpose: make priest look like Glory-Spirit
- Investiture = clothing in God's image

**Image Theology**:

Creating humanity in God's image = God clothing humanity with His Glory-likeness

Supporting passages:
- **Ezekiel 16**: God spreading Glory-robe over Israel = covenant marriage
- **Psalm 104**: God clothed with light as garment
- **Revelation 21**: Bride adorned with Glory-vestments
- **2 Corinthians 5**: "Clothed with our dwelling from heaven"

**Redemptive Recapitulation**:

- Original: God creates Adam in His image
- Fall: Adam loses Glory-covering, becomes "naked"
- Fig leaves: inadequate self-covering
- Animal skins: God's provisional covering
- Aaron's robes: Symbolic restoration of Glory-image
- Christ: Perfect Glory-image incarnate
- Church: Being clothed with Christ = restored image
- Consummation: Complete Glory-transformation

**Marriage and Image**:

- Image-bearing = bride wearing husband's glory
- Creating in God's image = covenant marriage
- Genesis 2:23-24 as parable
- Woman derived from man, clothed with his glory
- Israel as God's bride, clothed with Glory (Ezekiel 16)
- Church as Christ's bride, clothed with Glory (Revelation 21)

### **Prophetic Model** (Images of the Spirit, Chapter 3)

**Core Concept**: Image of God = being transformed by rapture into Glory-council

**Paradigm**: Moses, Elijah, and OT prophets

**The Prophet's Experience**:

**Call**:
- Caught up "in the Spirit" into heavenly council
- Isaiah 6: Seeing God enthroned in Glory
- Ezekiel 1: Transported to Glory-chariot throne
- Daniel 7: Vision of Ancient of Days

**Transformation**:
- Encounter with Glory produces Glory-likeness
- Moses' transfigured face (Exodus 34)
- Reflecting Glory's radiance
- Ethically conformed to God's holiness
- Empowered with judicial authority

**Three Dimensions of Prophetic Image**:

1. **Ethical Glory**:
   - Profound sympathy with God's righteousness
   - Hatred of sin
   - Passion for God's glory
   - Holiness reflecting God's holiness

2. **Functional/Judicial Glory**:
   - Authority to pronounce judgment
   - Spokesman for divine council
   - "Thus says Yahweh"
   - Power over nature and nations
   - Judicial office

3. **Physical Glory** (proleptic):
   - Moses' shining face
   - Elijah's translation
   - Miraculous powers
   - Foretaste of resurrection glory

**The Prophetic Office**:

- Standing in divine council (Jeremiah 23:18, 22)

**The Works Principle Defended**:

**Objection 1**: "Creatures can't merit anything from God"

**Answer**:
- God defines justice by covenant terms
- If covenant says obedience merits reward, it does
- This is not creature pride but God-defined justice
- Refusing this impugns God's justice

**Objection 2**: "Paternal love excludes works-justice"

**Objection 3**: "Disproportionality: finite act can't earn infinite reward"

- This implies Adam's sin can't deserve infinite punishment
- Would make Hell unjust
- Would make Cross unjust (God punishing Son infinitely for finite sins)
- God's covenant terms *define* proportionality
- Refusing this embraces Roman Catholic "congruent merit" theology

**Objection 4**: "Unmerited favor present from beginning"

**Answer**:
- Confusion of categories
- God's creational goodness ≠ grace
- Grace = response to demerit (covenant-breaking)
- Works = reward for merit (covenant-keeping)
- Neither merit nor demerit before probation
- Unmerited ≠ Grace (grace requires demerit present)

**Positive Case for Works**:

1. **Justice as foundation**: "Justice and judgment are foundation of your throne" (Psalm 89:14)

## Interpretive Framework

### **Hermeneutical Keys**:

1. **Distinguish Core from Periphery**:
   - Core = permanent spiritual reality (God's presence, image-likeness, covenant relationship)
   - Periphery = temporary typological forms (ceremonial laws, sacrificial system, theocratic state)
   - Core passes through all administrations; periphery fulfilled and passes away

2. **Recognize the Intrusion**:
   - OT Theocracy = Consummation ethics intruding into Common Grace age
   - Not evolutionary development but prophetic anticipation
   - Requires highest faith, not lower ethics
   - Typological, not directly applicable to Church Age

3. **Eschatology Precedes Soteriology**:
   - Creation already oriented toward Sabbath consummation
   - Probation and eschatological reward built into original creation
   - Fall made redemption necessary but didn't create eschatology
   - Christ's work restores and completes original eschatological program

4. **Typology is Prospective**:
   - Not mere illustration or allegory
   - Divinely designed historical prefigurement
   - Actual events/persons structured to point forward
   - Recognized retrospectively but intended prospectively

5. **Three-Directional Typology**:
   - Backward: OT symbol as antitype of heavenly archetype
   - Present: Sacramental/symbolic function in current administration
   - Forward: Type pointing to Messianic/Consummation fulfillment

6. **Covenantal Epistemology**:
   - Must hear Father's voice in specific covenant revelation
   - Not autonomous reason determining ethics
   - Not abstract principles detached from covenant context
   - Obedience to God's word even when seemingly contradicting general principles

7. **Federal Representation**:
   - Understanding corporate solidarity and headship
   - Adam's act imputed to humanity
   - Christ's act imputed to elect
   - Key to understanding Paul's Adam-Christ parallel (Romans 5)

8. **Glory-Spirit as Hermeneutical Center**:
   - Genesis 1:2 Spirit = theophanic Glory
   - Image of God = likeness to Glory-Spirit
   - Heaven = Glory-dimension
   - Eschatology = unveiling of Glory
   - All theology radiates from Glory-Spirit revelation

### **Common Misunderstandings to Avoid**:

**Error**: Treating OT ethics as "lower" or "evolutionary," now superseded by "higher" NT ethics

**Truth**: OT Intrusion Ethics required *higher* faith, not lower standards. They were prophetic anticipations of Consummation ethics, requiring absolute trust in God's specific word even when contradicting Common Grace principles.

---

**Error**: Seeing OT/NT distinction as different moral standards or dispensations

**Truth**: Same fundamental moral law (Decalogue), but different *covenantal conditions*. Common Grace ethics govern Church Age; Intrusion ethics characterized OT Theocracy; Consummation ethics await final state. The law doesn't change, but its application context does.

---

**Error**: Attempting to apply OT Intrusion Ethics directly to NT Church (theonomy, Christian nationalism)

**Truth**: Those were typological phenomena specific to OT Theocracy. Church Age operates under Common Grace principles. We don't execute heretics, conquer nations for Christ, or establish political theocracy. We await Consummation when Christ Himself establishes His kingdom.

---

**Error**: Pantheistic identification of God with cosmos or Glory-dimension with divine essence

**Truth**: Glory = *manifestation* (epiphanic presence), not essence. God is ontologically distinct from creation. The Glory-Spirit is God present *in* creation, not God *as* creation. Heaven's structure is *constituted by* Glory-manifestation, but God transcends even this.

---

**Error**: Reading Genesis 1-3 as mere parable, myth, or non-historical narrative

**Truth**: Kline affirms historical reality of creation, Fall, and early chapters. His "literary framework" view concerns *literary structure* (sabbatical pattern, two-register presentation), not historical truth. Creation really happened; Genesis presents it with sabbatical theological structure.

---

**Error**: Treating typology as allegory (finding hidden meanings unrelated to text)

**Truth**: Typology deals with historical correspondences divinely designed. Both type and antitype are real historical events/persons. The prefigurement is intentional, not arbitrary. Allegory spiritualizes away history; typology honors history as divinely orchestrated.

---

**Error**: Confusing Common Grace with Saving Grace or treating them as continuous

**Truth**: They are antithetical principles. Common Grace delays judgment and maintains mixed community. Saving Grace anticipates Consummation and separates elect. Common Grace ends at Parousia; Saving Grace reaches consummation then. They coexist in tension during history.

---

**Error**: Denying Covenant of Works or works-principle in favor of universal "grace"

**Truth**: Works-principle is justice, not opposed to love. It operated in Adamic covenant and operates in Christ's probation. Grace is forensically defined as response to demerit. To deny works-principle undercuts gospel (no meritorious work of Christ to impute) and doctrine of justice.

---

**Error**: Seeing heaven as spatially distant realm believers go to after death

**Truth**: Heaven is the Glory-dimension coextensive with cosmos, presently invisible. At death, believers enter this dimension (present with the Lord). At Parousia, Glory-dimension unveiled to all. Consummation = transformation of perception, not transportation to distant location.

---

**Error**: Reducing image of God to abstract divine attributes (reason, morality, personality)

**Truth**: Image of God = concrete likeness to Glory-Spirit theophany. Ethical (holiness), functional (dominion), and ultimately physical (glorification) dimensions. Tied to covenant, marriage, and eschatological transformation. Specific to Glory-manifestation, not generic divine attributes.

1. **Heaven is Near**:
   - Not spatially distant but dimensionally distinct
   - Interpenetrating our world right now
   - Present with us though veiled
   - Closer than we think

2. **Glory-Transformation Coming**:
   - Parousia will unveil Glory-dimension
   - Our glorification = capacity to perceive/inhabit it
   - Not transportation but transformation
   - Heaven becomes visible, earth becomes heavenly

3. **Creation Groans for Manifestation**:
   - Romans 8: All creation awaiting revelation of God's sons
   - Cosmos itself will be glorified
   - Not destruction but transformation
   - New heavens and earth = present cosmos unveiled in Glory

4. **Consummation is Both Judgment and Glory**:
   - Day of wrath for impenitent
   - Day of glory for faithful
   - Same event, opposite experiences
   - Glory-fire consumes chaff, purifies gold

5. **Our Hope is Concrete**:
   - Bodily resurrection
   - Physical glorification
   - Real city (New Jerusalem)
   - Actual presence with God
   - Not ethereal spirituality but transformed physicality

### **For Worship and Sacraments**:

1. **Sacraments as Intrusions**:
   - Baptism: Re-creation, exodus, death-resurrection
   - Lord's Supper: Messianic banquet, communion with Glory
   - Present participation in future realities
   - Signs and seals of eschatological promises

2. **Sabbath Observance**:
   - Weekly pointer to eternal rest
   - Sign of covenant lordship
   - Reminder of creation's eschatological orientation
   - Anticipation of consummated Sabbath

3. **Glory in Worship**:
   - Worship = entering Glory-dimension proleptic
   - Corporate worship = foretaste of heavenly assembly
   - Sursum corda: "Lift up your hearts" to heavenly throne
   - Already participating in heavenly worship

### **For Cultural Engagement**:

1. **Value Culture as Provisional**:
   - Human culture serves necessary purpose
   - But external culture passes away at glorification
   - Glorified humanity transcends need for technology
   - Work with eternal, not ultimate, significance

2. **Cultivate with Eschatological Perspective**:
   - Cultural mandate still operative
   - Building anticipates Metapolis
   - Prototypal foreshadowing of antitypical fulfillment
   - Do all for God's glory

3. **Common Grace Enables Culture**:
   - Current age allows mixed culture
   - Believers and unbelievers work together
   - Common grace restrains total depravity
   - Enables provisional human flourishing

4. **No Triumphalism**:
   - Church doesn't bring kingdom through politics/culture
   - Christ brings kingdom at Parousia
   - We're pilgrims, not transformers
   - Salt and light, not conquerors

## Integration with Redemptive-Historical Cycles

Kline's framework provides theological foundation for understanding [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles]]:

**Creation → Probation → Fall → Redemption → Consummation Pattern**:

- **Creation**: Glory-Spirit as archetype, humanity as image
- **Probation**: Covenant of Works, eschatological goal
- **Fall**: Intrusion of death, loss of Glory-image
- **Redemption**: Covenant of Grace, Intrusions of Consummation
- **Consummation**: Full Glory-transformation, eternal Sabbath

Each cycle recapitulates this pattern:

- **Adamic Cycle**: Original creation covenant → Fall
- **Noahic Cycle**: Re-creation → Common Grace covenant
- **Abrahamic Cycle**: Covenant of Grace inaugurated
- **Mosaic Cycle**: Typological theocracy, concentrated Intrusions
- **Davidic Cycle**: Kingdom promises, Messianic anticipations
- **New Covenant**: Christ as second Adam, inaugurated eschatology
- **Consummation**: Full realization of original eschatological goal

Kline's concepts illuminate each stage:
- Glory-Spirit present throughout
- Covenant structure underlying all
- Typology prospectively pointing forward
- Intrusion principle explaining OT phenomena
- Eschatological orientation from beginning to end
