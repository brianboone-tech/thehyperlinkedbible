### A. OCCASIONAL/PROVIDENTIAL TYPES (3)

#### 1. **JACOB'S LADDER** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Existing Coverage**: NONE
**Mather's Treatment**: Sermon 8, extensive (4+ pages)
**Scriptural Foundation**:
- OT: Genesis 28:12-16 (Jacob's vision at Bethel)
- NT: John 1:51 (Jesus explicitly references it: "angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man")

**Why HIGH Priority**:
- Christ himself applies this type to his own person (John 1:51)
- Mather provides 5-point detailed exposition
- Clear typology of Christ as mediator between heaven and earth
- Represents the incarnation (foot on earth, top in heaven = two natures united)

**Trajectory Outline** (from Mather):
1. **OT Vision** - Genesis 28 (Jacob at Bethel)
2. **Prophetic Development** - Angels ministering to God's people through the covenant
3. **NT Fulfillment** - John 1:51 (Christ as the ladder)
4. **NT Superiority** - Christ is the only way to the Father (John 14:6)
5. **NT Application** - Access to God through Christ alone (Hebrews 10:19-22)
6. **Eschatological** - Face-to-face vision (1 Cor 13:12) vs. present vision in glass darkly

**Mather's Key Insights**:
- Ladder foot on earth = humanity; top in heaven = deity; one ladder = one person
- Angels ascending/descending = ministry of angels through Christ to saints
- God at top renewing covenant = God speaks peace through Christ alone
- Seen at Bethel (house of God) = Christ seen in the church

## METHODOLOGICAL OBSERVATIONS

### What Makes Mather's Work Valuable for TT Discovery?

1. **Detailed Ceremonial Exposition**: Mather excels at unpacking multi-stage ceremonial laws (leprosy, Nazarite, purifications) that modern interpreters often overlook

2. **Occasional/Providential Types**: Mather uniquely develops types from visions (Jacob's Ladder) and miraculous events (Burning Bush, Pool of Bethesda) that aren't permanent institutions

3. **Minor Characters**: Mather identifies types in "background" characters modern readers skip (Methuselah's prophetic name, Heber preserving the faith, Eliakim's key)

4. **NT Connections**: Mather always grounds typology in NT testimony (Rev 3:7 quoting Isa 22:22 for Eliakim; Matt 2:23 for Nazarite; Heb 6:18 for Cities of Refuge)

5. **Christological Focus**: Every type ultimately points to Christ (not just moral lessons). Mather maintains redemptive-historical focus throughout.

### Gaps in Existing 127 Tables (Based on Mather)

1. **Visions/Dreams**: No tables for Jacob's Ladder, Burning Bush (despite clear NT testimony)
2. **Reformer Kings**: Hezekiah and Josiah completely absent (despite extensive biblical treatment and prophetic prediction)
3. **Gentile Deliverers**: Cyrus absent (despite being called "anointed" and "shepherd" in Isaiah)
4. **Ceremonial Details**: Many multi-stage ceremonies compressed into single rows (leprosy cleansing is 7 stages, Nazarite has 3 rules + 2 defilement protocols)
5. **Rejected-Then-Exalted Types**: Jephthah pattern (rejected by brethren, then called to save) is shadow of Christ's rejection (John 1:11) and exaltation (Phil 2:9-11)
