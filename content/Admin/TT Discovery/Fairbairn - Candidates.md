## HIGH PRIORITY CANDIDATES (8)

These are personal types or major institutional types that Fairbairn devotes substantial discussion to and which do NOT have existing trajectory tables.

### 1. **Cain** (Antitypical/Negative Type)
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6 (extensive discussion)
- **Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type (negative)
- **Trajectory**: Represents the seed of the serpent, apostasy, false worship, worldly kingdom
- **Why HIGH**: Fairbairn contrasts Cain with Abel extensively. Represents the rejected line, false religion, Christless civilization. Balances Abel table.
- **NT Fulfillment**: Judas, antichrist spirit (1 John 3:12, Jude 11)
- **Notes**: This would be a NEGATIVE trajectory (showing what Christ is NOT, and what His rejecters become)

### 2. **Seth**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6
- **Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type
- **Trajectory**: Replacement seed after Abel's death, continuation of godly line
- **Why HIGH**: Critical link in messianic genealogy. Fairbairn emphasizes Seth as "appointed" seed
- **NT Fulfillment**: Christ as appointed/established Seed (Luke 3:38 genealogy)
- **Notes**: Connects Seed Promise trajectory to specific lineage

### 3. **Enoch**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6 (full section devoted to him)
- **Type Classification**: Forward-looking Providential Type
- **Trajectory**: Walk with God → translation without death → eschatological witness
- **Why HIGH**: One of only two pre-flood personal types Fairbairn treats extensively (with Noah). Translation prefigures resurrection/rapture.
- **NT Fulfillment**: Hebrews 11:5, Jude 14-15, those alive at Christ's return
- **Scriptural Support**: Explicitly called a type by NT (Jude references his prophecy)

### 4. **Shem** (and Ham/Japheth as contrast)
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6
- **Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type
- **Trajectory**: Blessed line through which Messiah comes / Gentile inclusion (Japheth) / Curse (Ham/Canaan)
- **Why HIGH**: Fairbairn devotes significant space to Noah's prophecy over his sons (Gen 9:25-27). Foundational for understanding blessing/curse trajectories and Gentile inclusion.
- **NT Fulfillment**: Christ from Shem's line; Gentiles "dwell in tents of Shem" (Eph 2-3)
- **Notes**: Could be combined table "Sons of Noah" or separate Shem trajectory

### 5. **Hannah**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book First, Chapter 5 (Prophetical Types section)
- **Type Classification**: Forward-looking Providential Type with prophecy
- **Trajectory**: Barren woman → miraculous son → prophetic song of Messiah's kingdom
- **Why HIGH**: Fairbairn treats her song (1 Sam 2:1-10) as first specimen of prophecy combined with type. Direct parallel to Mary's Magnificat.
- **NT Fulfillment**: Mary/Jesus (Luke 1:46-55 echoes Hannah's song)
- **Scriptural Support**: Luke 1 shows NT authors saw the connection

### 6. **Cyrus**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book First, Chapter 5 (Prophetical Types); multiple appendices
- **Type Classification**: Forward-looking Providential Type (explicitly prophesied)
- **Trajectory**: Gentile deliverer → destroyer of Babylon → restorer of temple/city
- **Why HIGH**: Explicitly named in prophecy 150 years before his birth (Isa 44:28, 45:1). Called "anointed" (messiah). Prefigures Christ as Gentile-includer and Satan-defeater.
- **NT Fulfillment**: Christ destroys spiritual Babylon, rebuilds temple (John 2:19-21, Rev 18)
- **Scriptural Support**: Isaiah explicitly types him

### 7. **Hezekiah**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Appendix A; Volume I, Book First, Chapter 5
- **Type Classification**: Forward-looking Providential Type
- **Trajectory**: Faithful king → deliverance from Assyria → healing/resurrection sign → psalms of deliverance
- **Why HIGH**: Fairbairn discusses how several Psalms (e.g., Psalm 118) are Hezekiah's but point beyond him to Messiah. Resurrection imagery (Isa 38).
- **NT Fulfillment**: Christ's resurrection and victory over Satan
- **Notes**: Several Davidic/royal Psalms originate with Hezekiah

### 8. **Saul** (Negative Type)
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6 (referenced); Volume I, Book First, Chapter 1
- **Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type (negative)
- **Trajectory**: People's choice vs. God's choice / Rejected king / Persecutor of David
- **Why HIGH**: Clear contrast type to David. Represents fleshly kingship, rejection of God's anointed, persecution of Messiah's type.
- **NT Fulfillment**: Jewish leaders rejecting Christ, Saul of Tarsus persecuting church (before conversion)
- **Notes**: Negative trajectory showing false vs. true kingship

## MEDIUM PRIORITY CANDIDATES (15)

These types have clear trajectories but are either covered partially in existing tables, are institutional aspects, or have less extensive Fairbairn treatment.

- **Notes**: Clear trajectory but brief treatment in Fairbairn

- **Type**: Direct Type (God commanded)
- **Trajectory**: Salvation through water/wood → Baptism/Church
- **NT Antitype**: 1 Peter 3:20-21 explicitly
- **Notes**: May overlap with Noah table, but ark itself deserves separate treatment

### 11. **Lamech** (Noah's father)
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6

### 12. **Bethel** (House of God)

- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential place
- **Trajectory**: Jacob's vision → ladder → temple → Christ as meeting place
- **NT Antitype**: John 1:51, Hebrews 10:19-22
- **Notes**: Could combine with Ladder/Jacob's Vision

### 14. **Hagar and Ishmael**

- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential (negative)

### 15. **Esau**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book Second, Chapter 6
- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential (negative)
- **Trajectory**: Profane person → despises birthright → rejected
- **NT Antitype**: Hebrews 12:16-17, Romans 9:13
- **Notes**: Contrasts with Jacob; represents those who despise spiritual inheritance

### 16. **Boaz**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume II, Book Third, Chapter 4
- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential
- **Trajectory**: Kinsman-redeemer → marries Gentile → restores inheritance
- **NT Antitype**: Christ as Redeemer (Ruth book typology)
- **Notes**: Strong candidate - book of Ruth is typological

### 17. **Ruth**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume II, Book Third, Chapter 4
- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential
- **Trajectory**: Gentile → gleaner → bride → Davidic line
- **NT Antitype**: Church/Gentile believers included in Messiah's line

### 18. **Miriam**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book First, Chapter 6
- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential
- **Trajectory**: Prophetess → song of deliverance → judgment for pride
- **Notes**: Brief mention; shows both positive (Exodus 15) and negative (Numbers 12) aspects

### 19. **Absalom**
- **Fairbairn Location**: Volume I, Book First, Chapter 6
- **Type**: Backward-looking Providential (negative)
- **Trajectory**: Beautiful son → rebellion against father → hanged on tree → mourned
- **NT Antitype**: Judas? Those who rebel against God's anointed
- **Notes**: Negative type; complex (David's grief mirrors Father's grief over lost)

### 20. **Priests and Levites (distinction)**

- **Type**: Direct Type
- **Trajectory**: Levites serve → Priests minister → Christ as both

### 21. **Ephod**

### 23. **Breastplate**

## TYPES FAIRBAIRN EXPLICITLY REJECTS

Fairbairn devotes significant space to CRITIQUING allegorical excess. He rejects:

- **John the Baptist as separate type** - Treated as prophetic forerunner, not type
- **Mary** - Mentioned only in context of incarnation theology, not as type
- **Peter/Paul** - NT figures, not OT types
- **Daniel's visions** - Prophecy, not type
- **Malachi/Zechariah/Haggai** - Prophets, not types themselves
- **Nehemiah/Ezra** - Historical figures without clear typological treatment

## METHODOLOGY NOTES

**Source Analysis**: Examined all 33 chapters of Fairbairn across 2 volumes plus 9 appendices.

**Filtering Criteria**:
- Removed types already in existing 127 tables
- Removed NT figures (not OT types)
- Removed general categories vs. specific types
- Removed rejected/allegorical interpretations Fairbairn critiques
- Prioritized based on extent of Fairbairn's treatment

**Fairbairn's Five Principles** (applied to all candidates):
1. Organize by Dispensations (Primeval/Patriarchal/Mosaic)
2. Trace Progressive Clarity
3. Always Show Escalation (antitype > type)
4. Distinguish Type Classification (Direct/Providential, Forward/Backward-looking)
5. End with Eschatological Consummation

**Type Classification Key**:
- **Direct Type**: Divinely commanded institution (e.g., sacrifices, feasts)
- **Providential Type**: Sovereignly arranged person/event (e.g., Adam, David, Exodus)
- **Forward-Looking**: Contains textual indicators pointing forward (expectational in original context)
- **Backward-Looking**: Recognized as typological only from NT vantage point (prefigurative)
