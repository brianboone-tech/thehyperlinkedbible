## HIGH Priority Candidates

### 1. Scapegoat (Removal of Sins)

**Found in**: Chapter 8 - Day of Atonement section

**Owen's Description**:
> "The scapegoat bearing sins into the wilderness showed how Christ removes our guilt"

**Why This is a GAP**:
- Existing table: "Day of Atonement (Christ's Atoning Sacrifice).md" covers the general ceremony
- The scapegoat is a SPECIFIC type within that ceremony with unique symbolism
- Similar to how we have both "Sacrificial System" AND individual sacrifice tables (Burnt Offering, Sin Offering, etc.)

**Proposed Trajectory Stages**:
1. **OT Type - Levitical Ceremony**: Leviticus 16:7-10, 20-22 - Two goats, one for sacrifice, one for removal
2. **OT Observance**: Annual removal of sins from the camp
3. **Prophetic Anticipation**: Isaiah 53:6 "The LORD has laid on him the iniquity of us all"
4. **NT Fulfillment - Sin Removed**: Hebrews 9:26 "put away sin by the sacrifice of himself"
5. **NT Superiority - Eternal Removal**: Hebrews 10:17 "Their sins and iniquities will I remember no more"
6. **NT Application**: 1 John 1:9 - Assurance of complete forgiveness
7. **Eschatological Consummation**: Revelation 21:4 - No more sin in New Jerusalem

**Type Classification**: DIRECT TYPE (divinely commanded), FORWARD-LOOKING (contains textual indicators)

**Priority Justification**: Owen explicitly mentions this as distinct from general atonement theme. It focuses on REMOVAL rather than PROPITIATION (covered by other sacrifices).

### 2. Visions of Christ's Glory / Theophanies

**Found in**: Chapter 8 (implied throughout discussion of OT representations)

**Owen's Description**:
Owen discusses "representations that were made of him and his glory under the Old Testament, before his actual incarnation" - this includes direct appearances and visions of divine glory.

**Why This is a GAP**:
- No existing table covers pre-incarnate appearances of Christ (Angel of the LORD, glory visions)
- Distinct from prophetic utterances (which predict future coming)
- These are PRESENT manifestations of the pre-incarnate Son

**Proposed Trajectory Stages**:
1. **OT Type - Early Theophanies**: Genesis 18 (Abraham), Genesis 32 (Jacob), Exodus 3 (Moses at burning bush)
2. **OT Development - Angel of the LORD**: Judges 13 (Manoah), Joshua 5:13-15 (Commander of LORD's army)
3. **OT Development - Glory Visions**: Isaiah 6:1-5 (throne vision - John 12:41 identifies as Christ)
4. **OT Development - Prophetic Visions**: Ezekiel 1 (Son of Man on throne), Daniel 7:13-14 (Son of Man)
5. **NT Fulfillment - Incarnation**: John 1:14 "We beheld his glory"
6. **NT Fulfillment - Transfiguration**: Matthew 17:1-8 (Peter, James, John see his glory)
7. **NT Fulfillment - Revelation**: Revelation 1:12-18 (John's vision of glorified Christ)
8. **Eschatological Consummation**: Revelation 21:23 "The glory of God did lighten it, and the Lamb is the light thereof"

**Type Classification**: PROVIDENTIAL TYPE (sovereignly arranged appearances), FORWARD-LOOKING (pointed to incarnation)

**Priority Justification**: This is a MAJOR category in biblical theology that spans the entire canon. Owen's discussion of "representations of Christ's glory" makes this central to his typological framework.

## MEDIUM Priority Candidates

### 3. Communication of Properties

**Found in**: Chapter 3 - "The mysterious Constitution of his Person"

**Owen's Description**:
> "From this personal union arises what divines call the communication of properties. The properties of both natures are attributed to the one person."

**Why This Might Warrant a Table**:
- This is more of a CHRISTOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE than a trajectory
- However, it has OT roots in passages that blend divine/human attributes
- Could trace how OT anticipated the God-man union

**Proposed Trajectory Stages**:
1. **OT Type - Prophetic Blending**: Isaiah 9:6 "Mighty God...Everlasting Father...child is born"
2. **OT Development**: Micah 5:2 "goings forth from everlasting" yet born in Bethlehem
3. **OT Development**: Psalm 110:1 "The LORD said unto my Lord"
4. **NT Fulfillment - Paradoxical Statements**: 1 Corinthians 2:8 "crucified the Lord of glory"
5. **NT Fulfillment**: Acts 20:28 "God purchased with his own blood"
6. **NT Application**: Understanding Christ's dual nature in one person
7. **Eschatological**: Revelation 5:12-13 (Lamb worshipped as God)

**Type Classification**: This is more of a HERMENEUTICAL PRINCIPLE than a type itself

**Priority Justification**: MEDIUM - Interesting but may be better handled in foundation documents rather than trajectory table. The principle explains HOW to read texts, rather than being a type itself.

### 4. Eternal Covenant (Covenant of Redemption)

**Found in**: Chapter 4 - "The eternal covenant between the Father and the Son"

**Owen's Description**:
> "Before the world was made, before sin entered, there was a covenant transaction between the persons of the Trinity concerning the salvation of the elect."

**Why This is a GAP**:
- Existing tables cover "Two Covenants (Law and Promise)" - but that's OLD vs. NEW covenant
- The ETERNAL COVENANT (pactum salutis) is the pre-temporal agreement between Father, Son, and Spirit
- This is distinct from the covenant of grace (which is with humanity)

**Proposed Trajectory Stages**:
1. **OT Type - Covenantal Language to Messiah**: Psalm 2:7-8 "Thou art my Son; today have I begotten thee. Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance"
2. **OT Development**: Psalm 110:4 "The LORD hath sworn, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever"
3. **OT Development**: Isaiah 42:1, 6 "mine elect...I will give thee for a covenant of the people"
4. **OT Development**: Zechariah 6:13 "the counsel of peace shall be between them both" (priest and king)
5. **NT Fulfillment - Son's Obedience**: John 17:4 "I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do"
6. **NT Fulfillment - Surety**: Hebrews 7:22 "Jesus made a surety of a better testament"
7. **NT Fulfillment - Spirit's Role**: Romans 8:11, Ephesians 1:13-14 (Spirit applies redemption)
8. **NT Application**: Believers' security grounded in eternal covenant
9. **Eschatological**: Revelation 5:9-10 (Lamb receives promised inheritance)

**Type Classification**: DIRECT TYPE (God's ordained plan), FORWARD-LOOKING (contains explicit covenant language)

**Priority Justification**: MEDIUM - This is significant Reformed theology but may overlap with existing covenant tables. Worth considering as ENHANCEMENT to existing covenant tables rather than standalone.

### 5. Mystical Union

**Found in**: Chapter 9 - "The intimate conjunction and union with his church"

**Owen's Description**:
> "This union is so close and real that Scripture describes it as one body, one spirit, and even as a marriage relationship."

**Why This Might Be a GAP**:
- Existing table: "Church as Israel (New Covenant People)" - focuses on corporate identity
- Existing table: "Marriage (Christ and His Bride)" - focuses on marriage metaphor
- "Mystical Union" emphasizes the SPIRITUAL UNION itself (distinct from metaphors)

**Proposed Trajectory Stages**:
1. **OT Type - Adam and Eve**: Genesis 2:24 "one flesh" (Ephesians 5:31-32 applies to Christ/Church)
2. **OT Development - Vine Imagery**: Psalm 80:8-16 (Israel as vine - but failed)
3. **OT Development - Marriage Covenant**: Ezekiel 16:8 "I...entered into a covenant with thee"
4. **NT Fulfillment - Vine and Branches**: John 15:5 "I am the vine, ye are the branches"
5. **NT Fulfillment - Body and Head**: Ephesians 1:22-23, 5:30 "members of his body"
6. **NT Fulfillment - One Spirit**: 1 Corinthians 6:17 "he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit"
7. **NT Application**: Romans 6:3-5 (union in death and resurrection)
8. **Eschatological**: Revelation 19:7-9, 21:2 (marriage supper, bride adorned)

**Type Classification**: PROVIDENTIAL TYPE (Adam/Eve union), FORWARD-LOOKING in some texts, BACKWARD-LOOKING in others

**Priority Justification**: MEDIUM - Significant theme but overlaps with existing "Marriage" and "Vine/Vineyard" tables. Could be addressed through ENHANCEMENTS to existing tables.

### 6. Recapitulation of All Things

**Found in**: Chapter 11 - "The recapitulation of all things in him"

**Owen's Description**:
> "God's eternal purpose to gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven and which are on earth." (Ephesians 1:10)

**Why This is a GAP**:
- Existing table: "New Creation (Cosmic Redemption)" - focuses on re-creation
- "Recapitulation" emphasizes Christ as HEAD gathering all things, not just renewal
- This is the UNIVERSAL HEADSHIP theme (Christ as cosmic summing-up)

**Proposed Trajectory Stages**:
1. **OT Type - Adam's Dominion**: Genesis 1:26-28 (given dominion over creation)
2. **OT Crisis - Dominion Lost**: Genesis 3:17-19 (creation cursed through sin)
3. **OT Development - Royal Dominion**: Psalm 8:4-6 "thou madest him to have dominion"
4. **OT Development - Universal Kingship**: Psalm 2:8 "I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance"
5. **NT Fulfillment - All Authority**: Matthew 28:18 "All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth"
6. **NT Fulfillment - Cosmic Headship**: Ephesians 1:10, 22 "gather together in one all things in Christ"
7. **NT Fulfillment - Preeminence**: Colossians 1:16-18 "by him all things...in him all things consist"
8. **NT Application**: Romans 8:19-22 (creation awaiting redemption)
9. **Eschatological Consummation**: 1 Corinthians 15:24-28 "that God may be all in all"

**Type Classification**: PROVIDENTIAL TYPE (Adam's headship), FORWARD-LOOKING (contains expectational language)

**Priority Justification**: MEDIUM - Important cosmic theme but significant overlap with "New Creation" table. Could be addressed as ENHANCEMENT to that table.

## Processing Notes

### Owen's Typological Methodology

Owen's approach in Chapter 8 follows clear patterns:

1. **Comprehensiveness**: He surveys ALL major OT categories (sacrifices, tabernacle, persons, prophecies, feasts)
2. **Escalation Principle**: Always shows how antitype SURPASSES type ("shadow vs. substance")
3. **Christocentric**: Every type points directly to Christ's person or work
4. **Pastoral Application**: Moves from typology to practical Christian living

### Comparison with Existing 127 Tables

**Strong Overlap Areas** (Owen's types already covered):
- Sacrificial system (all major sacrifices covered)
- Tabernacle furniture (all major pieces covered)
- Personal types (Adam, Melchizedek, Moses, David all covered)
- Feasts (Passover, Pentecost, Atonement, Tabernacles all covered)
- Prophetic texts (birth, ministry, suffering, resurrection themes covered)

**Weak Overlap Areas** (potential gaps):
- Theophanies/pre-incarnate appearances (NO dedicated table)
- Scapegoat as DISTINCT from general atonement (NOT separated out)
- Eternal covenant (Covenant of Redemption distinct from Covenant of Grace)
- Hypostatical union implications (more principle than type)
