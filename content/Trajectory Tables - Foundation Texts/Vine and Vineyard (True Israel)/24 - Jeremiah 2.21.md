### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- שֹׂרֵק (soreq, H8321) - choice vine (same root as Genesis 49:11)
- זֶרַע אֱמֶת (zera emet, H2233 + H571) - seed of truth/faithfulness
- סוּר (sur, H5493) - to turn aside, degenerate
- גֶּפֶן נָכְרִיָּה (gephen nokriyah, H1612 + H5237) - foreign/strange vine

**Context**: Part of Jeremiah's indictment against Judah for apostasy and covenant unfaithfulness. Chapter 2 contrasts Israel's early devotion with her current spiritual adultery.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Directly echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11]] ("choice vine" language)
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalm 80:8-16]] (vine brought from Egypt now devastated)
- Anticipates [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] (vineyard producing sour grapes instead of good)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11-12]] - choice vine imagery
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalm 80:8-16]] - vine from Egypt
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]]
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 15#Ezekiel 15 . 1|Ezekiel 15:1-8]]
  - [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 10#Hosea 10 . 1|Hosea 10:1]]
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1]] - Jesus as "True Vine" contrasts with Israel's degenerate vine
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 33|Matthew 21:33-44]]
**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-looking (the "choice vine from seed of truth" anticipates the True Vine who is Himself the Truth)

**Christological Connection**: God planted Israel as a "choice vine from the very best seed," but she became a "rotten, wild vine." This failure points to Christ, who is the True Vine and "the way, the truth, and the life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]). Unlike degenerate Israel, Jesus remains faithful and produces perfect fruit. Only in union with Him can branches bear the fruit God originally intended.
