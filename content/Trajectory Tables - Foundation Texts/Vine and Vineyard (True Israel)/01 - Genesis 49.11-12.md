### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- גֶּפֶן (gephen, H1612) - vine
- שֹׂרֵקָה (soreqah, H8321) - choice vine
- יַיִן (yayin, H3196) - wine
- עֵנָב (enab, H6025) - grapes

**Context**: Jacob's deathbed blessing of his twelve sons, particularly the blessing of Judah which contains messianic prophecy. The vine imagery appears in verses 11-12 as part of the promise that the scepter will not depart from Judah until Shiloh (the Messiah) comes.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Later referenced in [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalm 80:8-16]] (vine brought from Egypt)
- Picked up in prophetic vineyard songs ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]], 27:2-6)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]] echoes the "choice vine" language

**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-looking (contains textual indicators of future messianic fulfillment)

**Christological Connection**: The donkey tied to the vine and garments washed in wine point to messianic abundance and the royal-priestly work of Christ. Judah's line leads to David and ultimately to Jesus, the True Vine who provides the wine of the new covenant (His blood) and whose kingdom brings eschatological abundance. Where Israel failed as God's vine, Christ succeeds perfectly.
