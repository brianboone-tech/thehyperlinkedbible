### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 17|Romans 11:17-24]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἐλαία (elaia, G1636) - olive tree (related to vine imagery)
- κλάδος (klados, G2798) - branch
- ἐγκεντρίζω (enkentrizō, G1461) - to graft in
- ἐκκλάω (ekklaō, G1575) - to break off
- ῥίζα (rhiza, G4491) - root
- πιότης (piotēs, G4096) - richness, fatness

**Context**: Romans 9-11 addresses Israel's role in God's plan after Paul established justification by faith (chs. 1-8). Chapter 11 argues that Israel's hardening is partial and temporary, serving to bring Gentiles to faith, which will provoke Israel to jealousy and lead to their restoration.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Adapts vine/vineyard imagery to olive tree ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 11#Jeremiah 11 . 16|Jeremiah 11:16-17]]; [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 14#Hosea 14 . 6|Hosea 14:6]])
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 2|Isaiah 27:2-6]] (restoration hope) and [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 25|Romans 9:25-26]] (Hosea quotations)
- Presupposes [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1-8]] (union with True Vine/Israel)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 11#Jeremiah 11 . 16|Jeremiah 11:16-17]] - Israel as olive tree
  - [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 14#Hosea 14 . 6|Hosea 14:6]] - Israel will blossom like olive
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 21|Deuteronomy 32:21]] - provoking to jealousy
- **FROM OT**:
  - All vineyard texts point to this inclusion of Gentiles into true Israel
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1-8]] - abiding in True Vine
  - [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 11|Ephesians 2:11-22]] - Gentiles brought near
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 28|Galatians 3:28-29]] - Abraham's seed by faith
**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-looking (prophets anticipated Gentile inclusion and Israel's restoration)

**Christological Connection**: Gentiles are "grafted in" to share in the "nourishing root" (v. 17)—Christ Himself, the True Vine/Olive. Participation in God's people is not by ethnic descent but by faith-union with Jesus. Natural branches (ethnic Israel) were broken off due to unbelief but can be grafted back in (vv. 23-24). This demonstrates that Christ is the true Israel into whom all believers—Jew or Gentile—must be incorporated. The "root" that nourishes is Christ; the branches are all who abide in Him by faith.
