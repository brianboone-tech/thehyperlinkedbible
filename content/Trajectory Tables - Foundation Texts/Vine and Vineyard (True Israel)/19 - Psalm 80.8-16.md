### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalm 80:8-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- גֶּפֶן (gephen, H1612) - vine
- נָטַע (nata, H5193) - to plant
- בֶּן־אָדָם (ben-adam, H1121 + H120) - son of man

**Context**: A corporate lament psalm by Asaph pleading for restoration during a time of national crisis (likely the Assyrian or Babylonian period). The vine metaphor recalls the Exodus and God's planting of Israel in the land.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11-12]], 22 (vine imagery for God's people)
- Directly referenced in [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] (vineyard judgment song)
- Echoed in [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 15#Ezekiel 15 . 1|Ezekiel 15:1-8]] (worthless vine wood)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 17|Exodus 15:17]] - planted on the mountain of Your inheritance
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 32|Deuteronomy 32:32-33]] - degenerate vine
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]]
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 2|Isaiah 27:2-6]]
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]]
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 15#Ezekiel 15 . 1|Ezekiel 15:1-8]]
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1-8]] - Christ as True Vine
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 33|Matthew 21:33-44]] - vineyard parable
**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-looking (v. 17 contains textual indicators anticipating messianic figure)

**Christological Connection**: Verse 17 pleads, "Let Your hand be upon the man at Your right hand, the son of man You have raised up for Yourself." While immediately referring to Israel or the king, this language anticipates Christ, the Son of Man who sits at God's right hand ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]]; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 56|Acts 7:56]]). Jesus as the True Vine fulfills what Israel could not—bearing perfect fruit and mediating God's blessing to all nations.
