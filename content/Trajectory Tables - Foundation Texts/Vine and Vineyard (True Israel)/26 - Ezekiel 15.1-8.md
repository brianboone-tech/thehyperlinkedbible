### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 15#Ezekiel 15 . 1|Ezekiel 15:1-8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- גֶּפֶן (gephen, H1612) - vine
- עֵץ (ets, H6086) - wood/tree
- יָתֵד (yated, H3489) - peg/stake
- אֵשׁ (esh, H784) - fire

**Context**: Ezekiel's prophecy during the Babylonian exile (593-571 BC). Chapter 15 comes after the prophetic dramas and before the allegory of Jerusalem as an unfaithful wife (ch. 16). This parable exposes Jerusalem's worthlessness apart from bearing fruit.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Radicalizes [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] (vineyard judgment) by showing vine has NO value except fruit-bearing
- Extends [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]] (degenerate vine) to its logical conclusion—useless for anything
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 16|Psalm 80:16]] ("Your vine has been cut down and burned")

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalm 80:8-16]] - vine burned with fire
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] - vineyard made desolate
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 2|Isaiah 27:2-6]] - restoration hope
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 6|John 15:6]] - fruitless branches thrown into fire
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 43|Matthew 21:43]] - kingdom taken away
**Type Classification**: Providential, Backward-looking (recognized as typological primarily from NT vantage point)

**Christological Connection**: Vine wood is worthless for any purpose except burning—it cannot even be made into a peg. This exposes the fundamental problem: Israel apart from bearing fruit has no value. The answer is Jesus, the True Vine whose very essence is fruitfulness. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 6|John 15:6]] echoes this text: "If anyone does not remain in Me, he is like a branch that is thrown away and withers...thrown into the fire and burned." Only through abiding in Christ can branches fulfill their purpose.
