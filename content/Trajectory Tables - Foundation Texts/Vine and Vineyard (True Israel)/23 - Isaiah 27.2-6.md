### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 2|Isaiah 27:2-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כֶּרֶם־חֶמֶד (kerem-chemed, H3754 + H2531) - pleasant/delightful vineyard
- נָצַר (natsar, H5341) - to guard, watch over
- שָׁקָה (shaqah, H8248) - to give drink, water
- שֹׁרֶשׁ (shoresh, H8328) - to take root
- פָּרַח (parach, H6524) - to bud, blossom

**Context**: Isaiah's "Little Apocalypse" (chapters 24-27) concluding with eschatological restoration. After judgment on Leviathan (27:1), God announces the restoration of His vineyard. This reverses the judgment song of Isaiah 5:1-7.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Directly reverses [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] (from judgment vineyard to pleasant vineyard)
- Fulfills the hope implied in [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 14|Psalm 80:14-19]] (restore the vine)
- Answers [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]] and [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 15#Ezekiel 15 . 1|Ezekiel 15:1-8]] with promise of fruitfulness

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] - vineyard judgment—now reversed
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalm 80:8-16]] - restoration plea
- **FROM OT**:
  - Later prophetic restoration promises ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 1|Jeremiah 31:1-14]]
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 8|Ezekiel 36:8-12]]
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1-8]] - True Vine makes restoration possible
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 17|Romans 11:17-24]] - grafting into Israel); Revelation 21-22 (eschatological fruitfulness
**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-looking (textual indicators of eschatological fulfillment: "in that day," "days to come")

**Christological Connection**: God promises, "I, the LORD, am its keeper; I water it continually" (v. 3). This finds fulfillment in Christ, the True Vine whom the Father tends as vinedresser ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1]]). The promise "they will fill the whole world with fruit" (v. 6) is realized through the gospel spreading to all nations. Those united to Christ by faith become the pleasant vineyard God always intended—bearing fruit not through self-effort but through divine watering and tending.
