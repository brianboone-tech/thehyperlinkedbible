### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 1|Genesis 5:1-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5612|H5612]] סֵפֶר (sēp̄er) - "book/record" - the book of generations
- [[Lexicon/H8435|H8435]] תּוֹלְדוֹת (tôləḏôṯ) - "generations/account" - structural marker in Genesis
- [[Lexicon/H6754|H6754]] צֶלֶם (ṣelem) - "image" - God made man in His image
- [[Lexicon/H1823|H1823]] דְּמוּת (dəmûṯ) - "likeness" - Adam's likeness transmitted to Seth
- [[Lexicon/H3205|H3205]] יָלַד (yālaḏ) - "begat" - physical and spiritual succession

**Context**: Genesis 5 begins a new section with the formula "This is the book of the generations of Adam." After recounting God's creation of man in His likeness, the text notes that Adam begat Seth "in his own likeness, after his image." This language deliberately echoes Genesis 1:26-27, establishing Seth as the continuation of image-bearing humanity and the head of the messianic line.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-27]] - God created man in His image and likeness
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 25|Genesis 4:25]] - Seth appointed as seed in place of Abel
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 3|Genesis 5:3]] - Adam begets Seth in his own image and likeness
- [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1#1 Chronicles 1 . 1|1 Chronicles 1:1]] - Chronicles begins with this same genealogy

**Type Classification**: Providential (sovereignly arranged genealogy) and Forward-Looking (pointing toward Christ the true Image)

**Christological Connection**: Seth bearing Adam's image points to Christ, "the last Adam" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 45|1 Corinthians 15:45]]), who is "the image of the invisible God" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]). Where Adam's image was marred by sin and Seth received that marred image, Christ is the perfect image-bearer who restores what was lost. The genealogy from Adam to Seth to Christ traces the redemption of the image—from creation, through fall and transmission, to restoration in Christ who makes believers "conformed to the image of His Son" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]).
