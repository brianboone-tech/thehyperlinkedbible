### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 8|Genesis 6:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2580|H2580]] חֵן (ḥēn) - "grace/favor" - Noah found grace in God's eyes
- [[Lexicon/H4672|H4672]] מָצָא (māṣāʾ) - "found" - Noah found favor
- [[Lexicon/H6662|H6662]] צַדִּיק (ṣaddîq) - "righteous" - Noah was righteous
- [[Lexicon/H8549|H8549]] תָּמִים (tāmîm) - "blameless/complete" - Noah was blameless
- [[Lexicon/H1980|H1980]] הָלַךְ (hālaḵ) - "walked" - Noah walked with God
- [[Lexicon/H430|H430]] אֱלֹהִים (ʾĕlōhîm) - "God" - Noah walked with God

**Context**: As the corruption of humanity reaches its zenith (6:1-7), one man stands apart: "Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD." He alone is described as "righteous, blameless among his contemporaries"—and like Enoch before him (5:22, 24), he "walked with God." Through this one man of the Sethite line, humanity will be preserved through the flood.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 29|Genesis 5:29]] - Lamech names Noah, anticipating comfort/deliverance
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 22|Genesis 5:22]], 24 - Enoch "walked with God"—now Noah does the same
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 8|Genesis 6:8-9]] - Noah finds favor and walks with God
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 1|Genesis 7:1]] - God declares Noah righteous in his generation

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 3|Genesis 5:3-32]] - The Sethite line culminates in Noah
- **FROM OT**:
  - Through Noah, the seed continues to Abraham
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 7|Hebrews 11:7]] - By faith Noah built an ark
  - [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 5|2 Peter 2:5]] - Noah as "preacher of righteousness"
**Type Classification**: Providential (sovereignly preserved through judgment) and Forward-Looking (the salvation pattern anticipates Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Noah as the righteous one who "found grace" and brought his household through judgment typifies Christ, who is the truly righteous One through whom believers escape final judgment. As the world was saved through Noah entering the ark, so humanity is saved through Christ. Peter explicitly connects baptism to Noah's flood ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20-21]]): the waters that destroyed the wicked saved Noah, just as baptism into Christ's death brings salvation. The Sethite line, nearly extinguished, is preserved through one man—anticipating how all humanity is saved through one Man, Jesus Christ ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 17|Romans 5:17-19]]).
