### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1471|H1471]] גּוֹי (gôy) - "nation" - I will make you a great nation
- [[Lexicon/H1288|H1288]] בָּרַךְ (bāraḵ) - "bless" - used five times in three verses
- [[Lexicon/H8034|H8034]] שֵׁם (šēm) - "name" - I will make your name great
- [[Lexicon/H779|H779]] אָרַר (ʾārar) - "curse" - whoever curses you
- [[Lexicon/H4940|H4940]] מִשְׁפָּחָה (mišpāḥâ) - "families/clans" - all families of the earth blessed

**Context**: God calls Abram out of Ur to an unknown land with a sevenfold promise. The Sethite line, which had narrowed to Noah and then spread again through his three sons, now focuses on one man: Abram. Through him "all the families of the earth shall be blessed." This is the narrowing of the appointed seed that will eventually focus on one descendant: Christ (Galatians 3:16).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Genesis 5-11 traces the Sethite line from Adam through Noah to Shem to Terah to Abram
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Genesis 22:18]] repeats the blessing promise with explicit "seed" language
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 26#Genesis 26 . 4|Genesis 26:4]] and 28:14 renew the promise to Isaac and Jacob
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] adds royal dimension to the seed promise

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 25|Genesis 4:25-5]]:32 - The Sethite line that leads to Abram
- **FROM OT**: The blessing promise is renewed to Isaac, Jacob, and David
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 8|Galatians 3:8-16]] identifies this as the gospel preached beforehand; Christ is THE seed

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (explicitly promises future blessing through offspring) and Providential (Abraham sovereignly called)

**Christological Connection**: Paul interprets [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] christologically: "Scripture... preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, 'In you shall all the nations be blessed'" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 8|Galatians 3:8]]). The blessing to all nations comes through Abraham's singular Seed, Christ (3:16). Jesus is the ultimate "appointed seed" through whom the nations are blessed. The Sethite line, narrowed to Abraham, narrows further until it focuses on one Man who accomplishes what the line anticipated: blessing for all families of the earth.
