### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 23|Luke 3:23-38]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (huios) - "son" - repeated throughout the genealogy
- [[Lexicon/G4590|G4590]] Σήθ (Sēth) - "Seth" - the son of Adam
- [[Lexicon/G76|G76]] Ἀδάμ (Adam) - "Adam" - the son of God
- [[Lexicon/G2316|G2316]] θεός (theos) - "God" - Adam is called "son of God"
- [[Lexicon/G756|G756]] ἄρχω (archō) - "began" - Jesus began His ministry at about thirty

**Context**: Luke traces Jesus' genealogy backward from Joseph through David, Abraham, Noah, and Seth to "Adam, the son of God." Unlike Matthew's genealogy (which traces royal succession from Abraham forward), Luke shows Jesus' actual lineage through the Sethite line. The genealogy ends with creation itself: Adam, son of God—and implicitly, Jesus as the new Adam, the true Son of God.

- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 1|Genesis 5:1-32]] - The Sethite genealogy from Adam to Noah

- [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 23|Luke 3:23-38]] - The genealogy extended through David to Jesus

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment (the genealogy demonstrates Jesus as culmination of the appointed seed)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus is the goal of the Sethite line. Every link in the chain from Adam through Seth through Noah through Abraham through David points to Him. The genealogy's ending—"the son of Enosh, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God"—is profound. Adam was "son of God" by creation; Jesus is Son of God by nature. The Sethite line, which began as replacement for murdered Abel, culminates in Christ, who by His death and resurrection provides what Abel could not: eternal life for all who belong to Him.
