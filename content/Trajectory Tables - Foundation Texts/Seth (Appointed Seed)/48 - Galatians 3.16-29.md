### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16, 29]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1860|G1860]] ἐπαγγελία (epangelia) - "promise" - the promises spoken
- [[Lexicon/G4690|G4690]] σπέρμα (sperma) - "seed/offspring" - singular, not plural
- [[Lexicon/G5547|G5547]] Χριστός (Christos) - "Christ" - who IS the seed
- [[Lexicon/G2818|G2818]] κληρονόμος (klēronomos) - "heir" - heirs according to promise
- [[Lexicon/G907|G907]] βαπτίζω (baptizō) - "baptize" - baptized into Christ

**Context**: Paul argues that the Abrahamic promises find their fulfillment in Christ, not in Torah-keeping. He makes a grammatical argument: the promises were spoken to Abraham "and to his seed"—singular, not plural. That singular seed is Christ. All who belong to Christ through faith and baptism are "Abraham's seed and heirs according to the promise."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 7|Genesis 12:7]]; 22:18 - The Abrahamic seed promise
- **FROM OT**: The entire trajectory of the appointed seed
- **FROM NT**: Romans 4 on Abraham's faith; Galatians 3-4 on inheritance through Christ

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment (Paul explicitly identifies Christ as THE seed to whom promises were made)

**Christological Connection**: This text is the explicit christological interpretation of the entire seed trajectory. From Seth ("appointed seed") through Abraham ("in your seed all nations blessed") through David ("your seed after you"), the promises pointed to one person: Christ. Jesus is THE appointed seed—the one through whom blessing comes to all nations, the one whose kingdom endures forever, the one in whom believers find their identity as children of Abraham. The Sethite line that began with divine appointment of a replacement for Abel ends with divine appointment of the Savior of the world. All who are "in Christ" are Seth's spiritual descendants—the true "appointed seed."
