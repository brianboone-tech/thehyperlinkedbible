### [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zeraʿ) - "seed/offspring" - your seed after you
- [[Lexicon/H4467|H4467]] מַמְלָכָה (mamlāḵâ) - "kingdom" - I will establish his kingdom
- [[Lexicon/H1004|H1004]] בַּיִת (bayiṯ) - "house" - he shall build a house for my name
- [[Lexicon/H3678|H3678]] כִּסֵּא (kissēʾ) - "throne" - his throne forever
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (ʿôlām) - "forever/eternal" - repeated for kingdom and throne

**Context**: David desires to build a house (temple) for God; God responds by promising to build David a house (dynasty). The "seed" promise now receives royal dimensions: "I will raise up your seed after you... and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever." The Sethite line, already narrowed to Abraham and then to Judah (Genesis 49:10), now focuses on David's royal house.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 25|Genesis 4:25]] - Seth appointed as seed
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]] - The scepter belongs to Judah
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] - David's seed will reign eternally
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 26|Psalm 89:26-37]] - The Davidic covenant celebrated in worship
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6-7]] - The child who reigns on David's throne forever

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - ; 22:18 - The seed promise now includes royalty
- **FROM OT**:
  - Psalms 2, 89, 110, 132 develop the Davidic promise; Isaiah 9, 11 describe the coming King
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 1|Matthew 1:1]] - identifies Jesus as "son of David"
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]] - applies 2 Samuel 7 to Christ
**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (explicitly promises eternal royal seed) and Direct (covenant explicitly established by God)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus is "the son of David" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 1|Matthew 1:1]]) who fulfills this promise. The angel Gabriel applies 2 Samuel 7 to Jesus: "The Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David, and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and His kingdom will have no end" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]]). Peter interprets David's words as prophetic of Christ's resurrection and enthronement ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 30|Acts 2:30-31]]). The appointed seed, promised to Eve, entrusted to Seth, preserved through Noah, focused in Abraham, and crowned in David, finds its ultimate fulfillment in Jesus, the eternal King.
