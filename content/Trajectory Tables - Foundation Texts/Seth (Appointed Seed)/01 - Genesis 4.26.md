### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 26|Genesis 4:26]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H583|H583]] אֱנוֹשׁ (ʾĕnôš) - "Enosh" - meaning "man" (in his frailty/mortality)
- [[Lexicon/H2490|H2490]] חָלַל (ḥālal) - "began" - initiation of public worship
- [[Lexicon/H7121|H7121]] קָרָא (qārāʾ) - "to call/proclaim" - invoking God's name
- [[Lexicon/H8034|H8034]] שֵׁם (šēm) - "name" - the name of Yahweh
- [[Lexicon/H3068|H3068]] יְהוָה (YHWH) - "LORD" - the covenant name of God

**Context**: After noting Seth's birth and the birth of his son Enosh, Genesis records: "At that time men began to call upon the name of the LORD." This marks the establishment of public Yahweh-worship in the Sethite line. While Cain's descendants developed cities, music, and metallurgy (4:17-22), Seth's line cultivated covenant relationship with God.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 17|Genesis 4:17-22]] - Cain's line: technological and cultural achievement without God
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 26|Genesis 4:26]] - Seth's line: calling on Yahweh's name
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 8|Genesis 12:8]] - Abraham builds an altar and calls on the LORD's name
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 32|Joel 2:32]] - "Everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 3|Genesis 4:3-4]] - Abel's worship was accepted; now worship continues through Seth's line
- **FROM OT**: The pattern continues: Abraham (12:8), Isaac (26:25), Israel at Sinai
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 13|Romans 10:13]] and [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 21|Acts 2:21]] apply [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 32|Joel 2:32]] to salvation in Christ

**Type Classification**: Providential (sovereignly arranged distinction between lines) and Forward-Looking (anticipating those who call on Christ's name)

**Christological Connection**: Calling on the name of Yahweh anticipates calling on the name of Jesus. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 9|Romans 10:9-13]] applies [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 32|Joel 2:32]] ("everyone who calls on the name of the LORD will be saved") to faith in Christ. The same Lord who answered the Sethites' worship answers those who call on Jesus. The worship begun in Enosh's time reaches its consummation in the church, which "calls on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 2|1 Corinthians 1:2]]).
