### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5301-5400#H5002|נְאֻם יְהוָה]] (*nəʾum YHWH*) - "declaration of the LORD, oracle of the LORD"
- [[Lexicon/H0001-0100#H0113|לַאדֹנִי]] (*laʾdōnî*) - "to my Lord, to my master"
- [[Lexicon/H3301-3400#H3427|שֵׁב]] (*šēb*) - "sit" (imperative)
- [[Lexicon/H3101-3200#H3225|לִימִינִי]] (*lîmînî*) - "at my right hand, to my right"
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5704|עַד]] (*ʿad*) - "until"
- [[Lexicon/H7701-7800#H7896|אָשִׁית]] (*ʾāšît*) - "I make, I place"
- [[Lexicon/H0301-0400#H0341|אֹיְבֶיךָ]] (*ʾōyəbeykā*) - "your enemies"
- [[Lexicon/H1901-2000#H1916|הֲדֹם לְרַגְלֶיךָ]] (*hădōm ləragleyḵā*) - "footstool for your feet"

**Context**: The LORD (YHWH) speaks an oracle to David's Lord (Adonai), inviting Him to sit at His right hand until enemies become His footstool. This divine invitation to sit establishes the Messiah's royal authority and becomes the most-quoted OT verse in the NT, foundational to Christ's session theology. The seated position signals both completed work and sovereign authority.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]'s divine invitation to sit at God's right hand is the foundational text for Christ's session theology. Where Levitical priests stood daily because their work never ended ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]), YHWH invites the Messiah to sit, revealing finished work and established authority. Jesus claimed this text for Himself ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 44|Matthew 22:44]]), Peter proclaimed its fulfillment at Pentecost ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 34|Acts 2:34-35]]), and Hebrews builds its entire argument on Christ's seated priesthood versus standing Levitical priesthood. The trajectory moves from priests who could never sit (no chairs in temple, standing posture required) to Christ who sits at God's own right hand, from servants who stand to the Son who reigns, from work that continues endlessly to work completed and vindicated. Christ's session fulfills every dimension of [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]: (1) **Divine appointment**—"the LORD said" proves God initiated Christ's enthronement; (2) **Completed atonement**—sitting proves sacrificial work finished; (3) **Royal authority**—right hand position demonstrates supreme sovereignty; (4) **Ongoing reign**—session continues "until" all enemies subdued; (5) **Divine identity**—only God's equal sits at God's right hand. The seated Christ embodies the standing-sitting contrast: priests stood because atonement was never complete; Christ sits because atonement is finished forever.
