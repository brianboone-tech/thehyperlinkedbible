### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 42|Exodus 29:42]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5901-6000#H5930|עֹלַת]] (*ʿōlat*) - "burnt offering"
- [[Lexicon/H8501-8600#H8548|תָּמִיד]] (*tāmîd*) - "continually, regularly, perpetually"
- [[Lexicon/H1701-1800#H1755|לְדֹרֹתֵיכֶם]] (*lədōrōtêkem*) - "throughout your generations, for your generations"
- [[Lexicon/H6501-6600#H6607|פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד]] (*petaḥ ʾōhel môʿēd*) - "entrance of the tent of meeting"

**Context**: God establishes the continual burnt offering at the tent of meeting entrance, to be maintained "regularly" throughout Israel's generations. This perpetual offering requires continuous priestly attendance, revealing the old covenant's ongoing, unfinished nature—work that never ceases because atonement is never complete.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (God dwelling among Israel), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 6#Leviticus 6 . 13|Leviticus 6:13]] (fire continually burning), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 1|Numbers 18:1-7]] (priests standing to minister)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11-12]] (priests standing daily vs. Christ sitting), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 1|Hebrews 10:1-4]] (law a shadow, sacrifices never perfect), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 34|Romans 8:34]] (Christ at right hand interceding)

**Christological Connection**: The continual burnt offering's perpetual nature—"regularly... throughout your generations"—pointed to need for Christ's once-for-all sacrifice. Where priests stood continuously offering daily sacrifices, Christ offered once then sat, His seated posture at God's right hand revealing completed atonement that renders standing, repetitive ministry obsolete.
