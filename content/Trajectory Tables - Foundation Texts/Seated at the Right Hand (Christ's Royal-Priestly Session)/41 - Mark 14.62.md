### [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1473|Ἐγώ εἰμι]] (*Egō eimi*) - "I am"
- [[Lexicon/G3601-3700#G3700|ὄψεσθε]] (*opsesthe*) - "you will see" (future indicative)
- [[Lexicon/G5101-5200#G5207|τὸν υἱὸν τοῦ ἀνθρώπου]] (*ton hyion tou anthrōpou*) - "the Son of Man"
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2521|καθήμενον]] (*kathēmenon*) - "seated, sitting" (present participle)
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1188|ἐκ δεξιῶν τῆς δυνάμεως]] (*ek dexiōn tēs dynameōs*) - "at the right hand of Power"
- [[Lexicon/G2001-2100#G2064|ἐρχόμενον]] (*erchomenon*) - "coming" (present participle)
- [[Lexicon/G3401-3500#G3507|μετὰ τῶν νεφελῶν τοῦ οὐρανοῦ]] (*meta tōn nephelōn tou ouranou*) - "with the clouds of heaven"

**Context**: Before the Sanhedrin, when asked "Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?" Jesus responds with unambiguous "I am" (*Egō eimi*), explicitly claiming divine identity. He then declares they will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power and coming with clouds of heaven, combining Psalm 110:1 and Daniel 7:13. The high priest immediately tears his garments and declares blasphemy, recognizing Jesus claimed equality with God by announcing His session at God's right hand.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]] contains Jesus's most explicit claim to divine session at God's right hand. The unqualified "I am" (*Egō eimi*) directly affirms the high priest's question about messianic identity and divine sonship. The declaration "you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power" applies [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]'s royal session directly to Jesus, claiming He will occupy the position of supreme authority at God's right hand.

The phrase "seated at the right hand of Power" is revolutionary. Where Levitical priests, including the high priest conducting this trial, stood daily in earthly sanctuary offering repeated sacrifices ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]), Jesus claims He will sit at God's right hand in heavenly sanctuary. The contrast is stark: the standing high priest condemns the coming seated High Priest; the earthly priest officiating in temporary sanctuary rejects the heavenly Priest who will minister in eternal sanctuary; the representative of standing, ongoing, incomplete ministry condemns the One whose seated, finished, complete work will make the standing ministry obsolete.

The present participle "seated" (*kathēmenon*) indicates continuous state—Jesus will be permanently seated at God's right hand, not temporarily visiting. This seated position proves: (1) **Work completed**—sitting indicates finished atonement; (2) **Authority established**—right hand position signifies supreme power; (3) **Divine identity**—only God's equal sits at God's right hand; (4) **Priesthood superior**—seated posture contrasts with standing Levitical ministry. The high priest tears his garments because he recognizes Jesus claimed to sit where only God sits, sharing divine throne and divine authority.

The trajectory from [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 12#Mark 12 . 36|Mark 12:36]] to 14:62 shows Jesus's progression from teaching about [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] to claiming it for Himself. Where 12:36 proved from Scripture that Messiah is David's divine Lord, 14:62 declares "I am" that divine Lord who will sit at God's right hand. This claim costs Jesus His life—the Sanhedrin condemns Him to death—yet establishes the foundation for resurrection vindication and apostolic proclamation. Jesus's prophecy "you will see" finds fulfillment in His resurrection, ascension, and session, where He takes His place at the Father's right hand, accomplishing through His seated priesthood what the standing high priest and his predecessors could never achieve—eternal redemption through finished atonement.
