### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 69|Luke 22:69]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G0501-0600#G0575|ἀπὸ τοῦ νῦν]] (*apo tou nyn*) - "from now on, from this time forward"
- [[Lexicon/G2001-2100#G2071|ἔσται]] (*estai*) - "will be" (future indicative)
- [[Lexicon/G5101-5200#G5207|ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου]] (*ho hyios tou anthrōpou*) - "the Son of Man"
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2521|καθήμενος]] (*kathēmenos*) - "seated, sitting" (present participle)
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1188|ἐκ δεξιῶν]] (*ek dexiōn*) - "at the right hand"
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1411|τῆς δυνάμεως τοῦ θεοῦ]] (*tēs dynameōs tou theou*) - "of the power of God"

**Context**: Before the Sanhedrin, when asked "Are you the Son of God then?" Jesus responds with a declaration of future vindication: "From now on the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God." Luke's version emphasizes the immediacy ("from now on") and future reality ("will be") of Jesus's session, presenting His enthronement as beginning with His death-resurrection-ascension sequence. This claim leads directly to His condemnation, as the council recognizes He has claimed divine authority.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] (sit at my right hand), [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (Son of Man)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 64|Matthew 26:64]] (parallel), [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]] (parallel), [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 20#Luke 20 . 42|Luke 20:42]] (Jesus teaches on [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]), [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 55|Acts 7:55-56]] (Stephen sees this), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]] (sat down at right hand)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 69|Luke 22:69]] presents Jesus's trial claim to future session at God's right hand with unique emphasis on immediacy and certainty. The phrase "from now on" (*apo tou nyn*) marks the decisive turning point: though Jesus appears defeated before the Sanhedrin, His vindication and exaltation begin immediately through the death-resurrection-ascension sequence. What judges view as condemnation becomes pathway to coronation; apparent humiliation leads to exaltation; human rejection results in divine vindication.

The declaration "the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God" combines [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]]'s Son of Man imagery with [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]'s royal session. Jesus claims He is the Son of Man who receives authority from the Ancient of Days, and this authority manifests in His session at God's right hand. The future tense "will be" (*estai*) promises certain fulfillment—though not yet visible, Jesus's enthronement is guaranteed. The present participle "seated" (*kathēmenos*) indicates ongoing state—Jesus will continuously occupy this position, permanently established in authority.

The phrase "at the right hand of the power of God" places Jesus in the supreme position of authority. Where Levitical priests, including the high priests conducting this trial, stood daily in earthly sanctuary ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]), Jesus prophesies He will sit at God's right hand in heavenly sanctuary. The contrast is devastating: standing priests who condemn will witness the condemned One seated in the position of ultimate authority. The standing ministry, with its daily repeated sacrifices proving perpetual incompleteness, will be superseded by seated ministry of the One who offers Himself once for all.

Luke's emphasis on "from now on" reveals that session begins not at distant future but immediately. The sequence unfolds rapidly: condemnation ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 71|Luke 22:71]]) leads to crucifixion (23:33), death (23:46), resurrection (24:6), appearances (24:13-49), and ascension (24:51). Within days, Jesus moves from trial defendant to enthroned Lord. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]] confirms: "Being therefore exalted at the right hand of God... he has poured out this that you yourselves are seeing and hearing." Peter's Pentecost sermon interprets Jesus's session as immediate fulfillment of this prophecy.

The trajectory is from standing condemnation to seated vindication, from human judgment to divine exaltation, from apparent defeat to certain victory. Jesus's claim "from now on the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God" finds fulfillment in His resurrection and ascension, where He takes His place at the Father's right hand, exercising the priestly ministry and royal authority that standing Levitical priests could never achieve—making intercession ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 34|Romans 8:34]]), distributing gifts ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 8|Ephesians 4:8-11]]), and ruling until all enemies are subdued ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 25|1 Corinthians 15:25]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 12|Hebrews 10:12-13]]).
