### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7650|נִשְׁבַּע יְהוָה]] (*nišbaʿ YHWH*) - "the LORD has sworn"
- [[Lexicon/H3801-3900#H3808|וְלֹא יִנָּחֵם]] (*wəlōʾ yinnāḥēm*) - "and will not change His mind, and will not relent"
- [[Lexicon/H0001-0100#H0859|אַתָּה־כֹהֵן]] (*ʾattāh-kōhēn*) - "you are a priest"
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5769|לְעוֹלָם]] (*ləʿôlām*) - "forever, for eternity"
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5921|עַל־דִּבְרָתִי]] (*ʿal-diḇrātî*) - "according to the order of, after the manner of"
- [[Lexicon/H4401-4500#H4442|מַלְכִּי־צֶדֶק]] (*malkî-ṣeḏeq*) - "Melchizedek" (lit. "my king is righteousness" or "king of righteousness")

**Context**: The LORD swears an irrevocable oath that the One seated at His right hand (v. 1) is also a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek. This divine oath combines royal authority (seated at right hand) with priestly ministry (eternal priest), transcending the Levitical system by invoking Melchizedek's unique priesthood. The sworn oath's permanence ("will not change His mind") guarantees the Messiah's eternal priesthood, establishing a new covenant order that supersedes the temporary Levitical standing ministry.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 18|Genesis 14:18-20]] (Melchizedek, priest of God Most High), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] (sit at my right hand), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 23#Numbers 23 . 19|Numbers 23:19]] (God is not man that He should change His mind)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 13|Zechariah 6:13]] (priest on his throne)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 6|Hebrews 5:6]] (You are a priest forever), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 17|Hebrews 7:17]] (You are a priest forever), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 21|Hebrews 7:21]] (The Lord has sworn and will not change His mind), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 20|Hebrews 6:20]] (high priest forever according to order of Melchizedek)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] combines the royal session of verse 1 with eternal priestly ministry, creating the foundation for understanding Christ's royal-priestly office. Where verse 1 announces "sit at my right hand" (royal authority), verse 4 swears "you are a priest forever" (priestly ministry), uniting in the Messiah what the OT kept separate—kings didn't serve as priests (cf. Uzziah's judgment in [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 16|2 Chronicles 26:16-21]]), and priests didn't reign as kings. The Melchizedekian priesthood transcends this division, as Melchizedek was both "king of Salem" and "priest of God Most High" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 18|Genesis 14:18]]).

The divine oath's irrevocability—"the LORD has sworn and will not change His mind"—guarantees the permanence of this royal-priestly ministry. Where Levitical priests stood daily offering repeated sacrifices ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]) until death prevented their continuance ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 23|Hebrews 7:23]]), Christ sits at God's right hand as eternal priest who "always lives to make intercession" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]). The contrast is total: Levitical priesthood based on genealogy, Christ's on divine oath; Levitical priesthood temporary, Christ's eternal; Levitical priests standing, Christ seated; Levitical priests many, Christ one.

Hebrews develops the Christological significance extensively. Christ is "high priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 20|Hebrews 6:20]]), superior to Levitical priesthood because established by divine oath ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 20|Hebrews 7:20-21]]), permanent because He possesses indestructible life ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 16|Hebrews 7:16]]), effective because He "always lives to make intercession" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]). The seated position at God's right hand ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1]]; 10:12) proves His priesthood's superiority—Levitical priests stood because work was never done; Christ sits because work is finished.

The trajectory moves from **Levitical standing priests whose service ended at death** to **the Melchizedekian seated Priest whose priesthood is eternal**, from priesthood based on fleshly genealogy to priesthood established by divine oath, from priests who served temporarily in earthly sanctuary to the Priest who ministers permanently in heavenly sanctuary. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]'s divine oath guarantees what Levitical priesthood could never achieve: permanent atonement, eternal intercession, and complete redemption through the royal-priestly work of the One who sits at God's right hand forever.
