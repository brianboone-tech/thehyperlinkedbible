### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 12|Leviticus 16:12-15]]

**Context**: At the climax of the Day of Atonement ritual, the high priest enters the Most Holy Place behind the veil. He carries burning coals and incense to create a protective cloud over the mercy seat, then returns with the blood of the sacrificed bull and goat to sprinkle on and before the mercy seat. This annual entry demonstrates both the possibility of access (through blood) and its severe restriction (once yearly, one person, elaborate preparation, death threatened).

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 22|Exodus 25:22]] (I will meet you above the mercy seat), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 10|Exodus 30:10]] (Aaron shall make atonement once in the year with blood)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 46|Numbers 16:46-48]] (Aaron makes atonement with incense to stop plague)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 7|Hebrews 9:7]] (high priest enters once a year with blood), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]] (Christ entered once for all with his own blood), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]] (Christ entered heaven itself to appear before God on our behalf)

**Christological Connection**: The high priest's annual entry behind the veil with incense and blood profoundly anticipates Christ's perfect high priestly ministry. Aaron entered with a protective cloud of incense "so that he may not die" (v. 13), highlighting the danger of approaching God's holy presence—but Christ enters with no such fear because He is the sinless Son who perfectly satisfies the Father ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 26|Hebrews 7:26]]: "holy, innocent, unstained"). Aaron sprinkled the blood of bulls and goats on the mercy seat to make temporary atonement; Christ entered "once for all into the holy places... by means of his own blood, thus securing an eternal redemption" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]). The annual repetition demonstrated the insufficiency of animal blood—"it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 4|Hebrews 10:4]])—but Christ's once-for-all sacrifice "put away sin" permanently ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26]]). Aaron entered the earthly copy; Christ "entered... into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God on our behalf" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]]). Aaron's entry required elaborate preparation and protective measures; Christ's entry required only His perfect obedience and substitutionary death. The blood Aaron sprinkled externally on the mercy seat; Christ's blood cleanses our consciences internally ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]). Aaron's ministry pointed to its own insufficiency through repetition; Christ's ministry declares "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]])—eternal redemption secured, access to God opened, the veil torn.
