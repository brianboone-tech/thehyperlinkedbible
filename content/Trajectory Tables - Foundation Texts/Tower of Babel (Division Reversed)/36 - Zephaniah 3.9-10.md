### [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 9|Zephaniah 3:9-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2015|H2015]] הָפַךְ (*haphak*) - "to turn, change, transform"
- [[Lexicon/H8193|H8193]] שָׂפָה (*saphah*) - "lip, language, speech"
- [[Lexicon/H1305|H1305]] בָּרוּר (*barur*) - "pure, clear"
- [[Lexicon/H7121|H7121]] קָרָא (*qaraʾ*) - "to call, invoke"
- [[Lexicon/H8034|H8034]] שֵׁם (*shem*) - "name"
- [[Lexicon/H5647|H5647]] עָבַד (*ʿabad*) - "to serve, worship"
- [[Lexicon/H7926|H7926]] שְׁכֶם (*shekhem*) - "shoulder" (idiom: "shoulder to shoulder" = unified)
- [[Lexicon/H3568|H3568]] כּוּשׁ (*Kush*) - "Cush, Ethiopia"

**Context**: Zephaniah 3 moves from judgment (vv. 1-8) to restoration (vv. 9-20). After describing how God will "pour out upon [the nations] My indignation" (v. 8), verse 9 announces transformation: "For then I will restore pure lips to the peoples, that all may call upon the name of the LORD and serve Him shoulder to shoulder." This is explicit Babel-reversal language: Babel's confused lips (שָׂפָה, Gen 11:1, 7, 9) become "pure lips" (שָׂפָה בְרוּרָה). The scattered nations will serve God "shoulder to shoulder" (שְׁכֶם אֶחָד)—unified worship replacing divided rebellion.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The verbal link is unmistakable: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 1|Genesis 11:1]] has "one lip" (שָׂפָה אֶחָת); Zephaniah promises "pure lip" (שָׂפָה בְרוּרָה). The same word שָׂפָה carries the Babel connection.
- "Call upon the name of the LORD" reverses Babel's attempt to "make a name for ourselves" (Gen 11:4).
- "From beyond the rivers of Cush" (v. 10) represents the remotest nations—even those most distant from Jerusalem will worship.
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 18|Isaiah 19:18-25]] similarly prophesies Egypt and Assyria (Israel's oppressors) worshiping alongside Israel.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 11:1, 7, 9 (Babel's שָׂפָה confused), Genesis 12:3 (all families blessed)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 18|Isaiah 19:18-25]] (Egypt/Assyria worship), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 18|Isaiah 66:18-21]] (gathering all nations), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 86#Psalm 86 . 9|Psalm 86:9]] (all nations worship)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 1|Acts 2:1-11]] (pure proclamation in all languages), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]] (every tongue worships)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (explicit prophetic promise of Babel reversal); Direct (not typological but predictive)

**Christological Connection**: Zephaniah's prophecy finds fulfillment through Christ and His Spirit. (1) **Pure Lips at Pentecost**: When the Spirit came, the disciples spoke in tongues—pure proclamation understood by all nations ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 4|Acts 2:4-11]]). The confusion of Babel becomes clarity through the Spirit. (2) **Calling on the Name**: Peter quotes [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 32|Joel 2:32]]: "Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 21|Acts 2:21]]). Through Christ, Zephaniah's promise becomes reality—all peoples can call upon the LORD. (3) **Serving Shoulder to Shoulder**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14-18]] describes Jews and Gentiles unified in Christ as "one new humanity." The wall of hostility is destroyed; nations serve together. (4) **From Beyond Cush**: The Ethiopian eunuch ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 8#Acts 8 . 27|Acts 8:27-39]]) represents the remotest nations receiving the gospel. "From beyond the rivers of Cush" they come to worship. (5) **Every Tongue Confesses**: The ultimate fulfillment: "At the name of Jesus every knee should bow... and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord" (Phil 2:10-11). Babel's confused tongues become unified confession.
