### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9-10]]

**Context**: Revelation 7:9-10 provides a vision of the redeemed community in heaven. John sees "a multitude too large to count" (ὄχλος πολύς ὃν ἀριθμῆσαι αὐτὸν οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο)—fulfilling God's promise to Abraham of innumerable offspring (Gen 15:5). They come "from every nation and tribe and people and tongue" (ἐκ παντὸς ἔθνους καὶ φυλῶν καὶ λαῶν καὶ γλωσσῶν)—Babel's scattered nations reunited. Standing before the throne and the Lamb, they cry with unified voice: "Salvation to our God!" What Babel failed to achieve through pride—unified humanity reaching heaven—God accomplishes through grace.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The fourfold designation "every nation, tribe, people, and tongue" appears repeatedly in Revelation (5:9; 7:9; 10:11; 11:9; 13:7; 14:6; 17:15), always emphasizing universal scope.
- This fulfills [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]]'s promise that "all families of the earth" would be blessed through Abraham.
- The innumerable multitude echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 5|Genesis 15:5]] ("count the stars... so shall your offspring be") and [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 17|Genesis 22:17]] ("as the sand on the seashore").
- [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 9|Zephaniah 3:9]]'s promise of nations serving "shoulder to shoulder" finds ultimate fulfillment in this unified worship.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 11:1-9 (Babel's scattering), Genesis 12:3; 15:5; 22:17 (Abrahamic promises), Zephaniah 3:9 (pure lips/unified worship)
- **FROM REVELATION**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] (purchased from every tribe), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]] (nations bring glory)
- **NT PARALLELS**: Philippians 2:10-11 (every tongue confesses), Matthew 28:19 (make disciples of all nations)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (eschatological consummation of all Babel-reversal promises); Direct (explicit vision of fulfillment)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9-10]] presents the ultimate Christ-centered reversal of Babel. (1) **Before the Lamb**: The nations stand "before the Lamb" (v. 9). Christ's sacrifice is the means of their gathering. What Adam's sin divided and Babel's rebellion scattered, the Lamb's blood purchases and reunites. (2) **Salvation to Our God**: The unified cry "Salvation to our God!" (v. 10) is a direct counter to Babel's "let us make a name for ourselves" (Gen 11:4). At Babel, humanity sought self-glory; in heaven, redeemed humanity gives all glory to God. (3) **Purchased by Blood**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] explains how this happens: "You were slain, and with Your blood You purchased for God persons from every tribe and language and people and nation." The cross reverses Babel. (4) **One Voice, Many Tongues**: The multitude cries "in a loud voice" (φωνῇ μεγάλῃ)—one voice from many languages. Unity in content while preserving diversity. (5) **Tower vs. Throne**: Babel built a tower to reach heaven; God brings the nations to His throne through the Lamb. Human ascent failed; divine descent (incarnation) and ascent (exaltation) succeeds.
