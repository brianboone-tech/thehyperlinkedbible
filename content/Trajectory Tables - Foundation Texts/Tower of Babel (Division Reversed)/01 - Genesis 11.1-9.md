### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 1|Genesis 11:1-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8193|H8193]] שָׂפָה (*saphah*) - "lip, language, speech"
- [[Lexicon/H1697|H1697]] דָּבָר (*dabar*) - "word, speech, matter"
- [[Lexicon/H8152|H8152]] שִׁנְעָר (*Shinar*) - "Shinar, land of Babylon"
- [[Lexicon/H4026|H4026]] מִגְדָּל (*migdal*) - "tower"
- [[Lexicon/H8034|H8034]] שֵׁם (*shem*) - "name, reputation"
- [[Lexicon/H6327|H6327]] פּוּץ (*puts*) - "to scatter, disperse"
- [[Lexicon/H1101|H1101]] בָּלַל (*balal*) - "to confuse, mix"
- [[Lexicon/H894|H894]] בָּבֶל (*Babel*) - "Babylon, confusion"

**Context**: Genesis 11:1-9 narrates humanity's post-flood attempt at unified rebellion. The people settle in Shinar (Nimrod's kingdom, Gen 10:10) rather than filling the earth as commanded (Gen 9:1). They build a city and tower "to make a name for ourselves" (לְנַעֲשֶׂה־לָּנוּ שֵׁם)—self-glorification in defiance of God. The tower "reaching to the heavens" (v. 4) echoes the serpent's promise to "be like God" (Gen 3:5). God confuses their language (בָּלַל) and scatters them—hence "Babel" (בָּבֶל). This judgment also prevents worse rebellion ("nothing they devise will be beyond them," v. 6).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 10#Genesis 10 . 10|Genesis 10:10]] establishes Babel as Nimrod's first kingdom—connecting the Tower to the empire-building spirit.
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]] immediately follows with God's call to Abraham: blessing for "all families of the earth." The scattering will be reversed through Abraham's seed.
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 8|Deuteronomy 32:8]] references when God "divided mankind" and "set the boundaries of the peoples"—possibly alluding to Babel.
- [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 9|Zephaniah 3:9]] promises "pure lips to the peoples"—direct reversal of Babel's confused lips.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 10:10 (Nimrod's Babel), Genesis 9:1 (command to fill earth)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]] (blessing reversed the curse), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 8|Deuteronomy 32:8]] (nations divided), [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 9|Zephaniah 3:9]] (pure lips restored)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 1|Acts 2:1-11]] (Pentecost reversal), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]] (every tongue unified)

**Type Classification**: Backward-Looking (recognized as anti-type pattern from NT perspective); Providential (sovereignly arranged to set up redemptive reversal)

**Christological Connection**: Babel establishes the condition Christ reverses. (1) **Making a Name**: At Babel, humanity sought to "make a name" through self-exaltation. God has given Christ "the name above every name" (Phil 2:9)—true glory through humiliation. (2) **Scattering Reversed**: Christ came to "gather together in one the children of God who are scattered abroad" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 52|John 11:52]]). What Babel dispersed, Christ collects. (3) **Confused Lips Made Pure**: At Pentecost, the Spirit enables clear proclamation to all nations. Babel's confusion of tongues becomes the church's gift of tongues. (4) **Tower vs. Temple**: Babel's tower failed to reach heaven. Christ builds a temple of living stones (1 Pet 2:5) where God actually dwells. (5) **One Language in Christ**: The eschatological assembly (Rev 7:9) cries with unified voice: "Salvation to our God!" Babel's failed unity through pride becomes true unity through the Lamb.
