### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 27#Exodus 27 . 20|Exodus 27:20-21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8401-8500#H8081|שֶׁמֶן זַיִת זָךְ]] (*šemen zayit zāk*) - "pure olive oil, beaten olive oil"
- [[Lexicon/H3901-4000#H3974|לַמָּאוֹר]] (*lammāʾôr*) - "for the light, for illumination"
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5927|לְהַעֲלֹת]] (*ləhaʿălōt*) - "to cause to ascend, to set up, to trim"
- [[Lexicon/H5201-5300#H5216|נֵר תָּמִיד]] (*nêr tāmîd*) - "lamp continually, perpetual lamp"
- [[Lexicon/H6101-6200#H6153|מֵעֶרֶב עַד־בֹּקֶר]] (*mēʿereb ʿad-bōqer*) - "from evening until morning"

**Context**: God commands Israel to provide pure beaten olive oil for the lampstand that must burn continually in the tent of meeting. Aaron and his sons shall tend it from evening to morning before the LORD—a perpetual statute throughout Israel's generations. This establishes uninterrupted light in God's sanctuary, symbolizing His constant presence and requiring priestly vigilance.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 31|Exodus 25:31-40]] (golden lampstand construction), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 2|Leviticus 24:2-4]] (expanded lampstand regulations), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 7|Exodus 30:7-8]] (incense offered when tending lamps)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 3#1 Samuel 3 . 3|1 Samuel 3:3]] (lamp not yet gone out when God called Samuel), [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 11|2 Chronicles 13:11]] (lamps kept burning every evening), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 119#Psalm 119 . 105|Psalm 119:105]] (Your word is lamp to my feet)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]] (Christ as light of the world), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 14|Matthew 5:14-16]] (believers as light of the world), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 12|Revelation 1:12-20]] (seven golden lampstands as churches)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 27#Exodus 27 . 20|Exodus 27:20-21]]'s perpetual lamp prefigures Christ as unfailing Light who illuminates God's people continuously. As the golden lampstand required daily tending by mortal priests to maintain its light "from evening until morning," dependent on human provision of oil and faithful ministry, Christ's light shines eternally, requiring no human maintenance: "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]). The lampstand's light was sustained; Christ's light is self-sustaining. The trajectory reveals escalation: (1) **Source of light**—lampstand burned oil provided by people; Christ is the "true light, which gives light to everyone" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 9|John 1:9]]), self-originated, not dependent on external provision; (2) **Duration of light**—lampstand burned "from evening until morning," requiring daily relighting; Christ's light never dims, for "in him was life, and the life was the light of men" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 4|John 1:4]]); (3) **Priestly ministry**—mortal priests tended lamps vigilantly; Christ as eternal High Priest maintains His church's light ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 12|Revelation 1:12-13]], walking among lampstands); (4) **Location of light**—lampstand illuminated earthly tent; Christ illuminates heavenly sanctuary and will be eternal light of New Jerusalem, where "the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). The lampstand's witness function extends to believers who become "light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 14|Matthew 5:14]]) not by self-generated illumination but by reflecting Christ's light, as lampstand reflected God's presence. The pattern moves from **light maintained by human effort** to **Light sustained by divine nature**, from lamps requiring daily tending to the Lamp who "is the same yesterday and today and forever" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 8|Hebrews 13:8]]), from temporal symbol to eternal reality.
