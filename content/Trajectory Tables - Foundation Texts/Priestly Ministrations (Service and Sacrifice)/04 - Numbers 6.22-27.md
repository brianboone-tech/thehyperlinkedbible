### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 22|Numbers 6:22-27]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:

- [[Lexicon/H8001-8100#H8104|וְיִשְׁמְרֶךָ]] (*wəyišməreka*) - "and keep you, guard you"
- [[Lexicon/H0201-0300#H0215|יָאֵר]] (*yāʾēr*) - "may he cause to shine, illuminate"
- [[Lexicon/H6401-6500#H6440|פָּנָיו]] (*pānāyw*) - "his face"
- [[Lexicon/H5301-5400#H5375|יִשָּׂא]] (*yiśśāʾ*) - "may he lift up, elevate"
- [[Lexicon/H7701-7800#H7965|שָׁלוֹם]] (*šālôm*) - "peace, wholeness, well-being"
- [[Lexicon/H7701-7800#H7760|וְשָׂמוּ]] (*wəśāmû*) - "and they shall put, place"
- [[Lexicon/H8001-8100#H8034|שְׁמִי]] (*šəmî*) - "my name"

**Context**: God teaches Moses the priestly blessing that Aaron and his sons shall pronounce over Israel. This threefold benediction invokes God's name upon His people, mediating divine favor through priestly ministry. The blessing encompasses protection, divine favor, and peace—comprehensive covenant benefits flowing from God through His appointed mediators.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]] (Aaron appointed to priesthood), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 22|Leviticus 9:22-23]] (Aaron blesses people after first sacrifice), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 8|Deuteronomy 10:8]] (Levites chosen to bless in God's name)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 67#Psalm 67 . 1|Psalm 67:1]] (God be gracious and bless us), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 121#Psalm 121 . 7|Psalm 121:7-8]] (the LORD keep you from all evil), [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 2|Malachi 2:2]] (cursing blessings when priests fail)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 14|2 Corinthians 13:14]] (threefold apostolic benediction), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 3|Ephesians 1:3]] (blessed with every spiritual blessing in Christ), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 4|Revelation 1:4-5]] (grace and peace from Father, Spirit, and Son)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 22|Numbers 6:22-27]]'s priestly blessing prefigures Christ's superior mediatorial ministry, through whom all spiritual blessings flow. As Aaron's sons pronounced "the LORD bless you and keep you," mediating covenant benefits through authorized ministry, Christ "is able to save to the uttermost those who draw near to God through him" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]), mediating better promises through superior priesthood. The blessing's threefold structure finds fulfillment in Trinitarian blessing: "The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ and the love of God and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 14|2 Corinthians 13:14]]). Each clause escalates christologically: (1) **Protection**—"bless and keep" fulfilled in Christ's promise "I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 28|John 10:28]]); (2) **Divine favor**—"make his face shine and be gracious" fulfilled in "the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]]); (3) **Divine peace**—"lift up his countenance and give peace" fulfilled in "my peace I give to you" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 27|John 14:27]]) and "the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding" ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 4#Philippians 4 . 7|Philippians 4:7]]). The promise "they shall put my name upon the people of Israel, and I will bless them" (v. 27) finds ultimate fulfillment in believers who are "called by the name of the Lord" ([[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 2#James 2 . 7|James 2:7]]) and "blessed... with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 3|Ephesians 1:3]]). Christ places the Father's name on His people: "I made known to them your name, and I will continue to make it known" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 26|John 17:26]]). The trajectory moves from **provisional blessing mediated by mortal priests in temporal covenant** to **comprehensive blessing mediated by eternal Priest in everlasting covenant**, from blessing pronounced liturgically to blessing secured redemptively, from grace hoped for to grace experienced in Christ.
