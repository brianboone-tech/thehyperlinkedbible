### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26-28]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5547|πεφανέρωται]] (*pephanerōtai*) - "he has appeared, been manifested" (perfect passive)
- [[Lexicon/G0501-0600#G0530|ἅπαξ]] (*hapax*) - "once, once for all, one time"
- [[Lexicon/G4901-5000#G4930|συντέλεια τῶν αἰώνων]] (*synteleia tōn aiōnōn*) - "end of the ages, consummation of ages"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0114|ἀθέτησιν τῆς ἁμαρτίας]] (*athetēsin tēs hamartias*) - "putting away of sin, annulment of sin"
- [[Lexicon/G4201-4300#G4254|προσενεχθείς]] (*prossenechtheis*) - "having been offered" (aorist passive participle)
- [[Lexicon/G0301-0400#G0399|ἀνενεγκεῖν]] (*anenenkein*) - "to bear, carry up"
- [[Lexicon/G1301-1400#G1537|ἐκ δευτέρου]] (*ek deuterou*) - "a second time, for the second time"
- [[Lexicon/G5501-5600#G5565|χωρὶς ἁμαρτίας]] (*chōris hamartias*) - "apart from sin, without sin"

**Context**: Christ appeared once at the end of the ages to put away sin by His sacrifice. Unlike high priests who offered sacrifices repeatedly, Christ was offered once to bear the sins of many. He will appear a second time, not to deal with sin but to save those eagerly waiting for Him. This passage contrasts repeated priestly offerings with Christ's unrepeatable, sufficient sacrifice.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 1|Leviticus 16:1-34]] (Day of Atonement), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 38|Exodus 29:38-42]] (daily burnt offerings), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 40#Psalm 40 . 6|Psalm 40:6-8]] (sacrifice and offering you have not desired)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 27|Hebrews 7:27]] (no need to offer sacrifices daily), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10]] (sanctified through offering of body of Jesus once for all), [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 18|1 Peter 3:18]] (Christ suffered once for sins)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26-28]] demonstrates Christ's sacrifice's unrepeatable finality, contrasting with Levitical priesthood's endless repetitions. Where priests offered sacrifices "daily" ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 38|Exodus 29:38]]) and "annually" (Leviticus 16), Christ appeared "once for all at the end of the ages" (v. 26). The trajectory moves from **repeated insufficiency** to **unrepeatable sufficiency**. Christ's single offering "put away sin" (*athetēsin hamartias*)—not covering like Day of Atonement sacrifices but actual annulment, removal, abolition. Where Levitical sacrifices left "a reminder of sins every year" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 3|Hebrews 10:3]]), Christ's sacrifice removes consciousness of sins ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 2|Hebrews 10:2]]). The phrase "offered once to bear the sins of many" (v. 28) fulfills [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]]: "he bore the sin of many." Christ accomplished through single offering what countless sacrifices across fifteen centuries could not achieve. The second appearing "apart from sin" (*chōris hamartias*) proves atonement's completeness—when Christ returns, no sin-bearing needed; that work is eternally finished. The trajectory culminates in believers who "eagerly wait for him" (v. 28), confident that His finished work secures final salvation. From **provisional covering requiring annual repetition** to **permanent removal achieved once for all**, from sacrifices that maintained covenant to sacrifice that perfects it, from priests who stood daily offering to the Priest who sat having offered once, the trajectory declares: "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]).
