### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3037|λίθοι ζῶντες]] (*lithoi zōntes*) - "living stones"
- [[Lexicon/G3501-3600#G3618|οἰκοδομεῖσθε]] (*oikodomeisthe*) - "are being built up" (present passive)
- [[Lexicon/G3401-3500#G3624|οἶκος πνευματικός]] (*oikos pneumatikos*) - "spiritual house, spiritual temple"
- [[Lexicon/G2401-2500#G2406|ἱεράτευμα ἅγιον]] (*hierateuma hagion*) - "holy priesthood"
- [[Lexicon/G0301-0400#G0399|ἀνενέγκαι]] (*anenengkai*) - "to offer up"
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4152|πνευματικὰς θυσίας]] (*pneumatikas thysias*) - "spiritual sacrifices"
- [[Lexicon/G2101-2200#G2144|εὐπροσδέκτους]] (*euprosdektous*) - "acceptable, well-pleasing"

**Context**: Peter describes believers as living stones being built into a spiritual house to be a holy priesthood, offering spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ. This democratizes priesthood—what was restricted to Aaron's descendants now extends to all believers united to Christ, the living Stone. Yet acceptability still depends on Christ's mediation: sacrifices are acceptable "through Jesus Christ."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]] (kingdom of priests and holy nation), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]] (Aaron's exclusive priesthood), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 1#Leviticus 1 . 3|Leviticus 1:3-9]] (acceptable offerings)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 6|Revelation 1:6]] (made us a kingdom, priests to his God), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 10|Revelation 5:10]] (made them a kingdom and priests), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1]] (present bodies as living sacrifice)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]]'s proclamation of believers as holy priesthood offering spiritual sacrifices fulfills the trajectory from Aaronic exclusivity to new covenant inclusivity through Christ's mediatorial work. Aaron's appointment restricted priesthood to one family ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]]); Christ's accomplishment extends priesthood to all who are "in Him." The pattern progresses: (1) **OT promise**—"you shall be to me a kingdom of priests" ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]), anticipated but unrealized under old covenant; (2) **OT practice**—priesthood limited to Aaron's descendants, creating distinction between priests who minister and people who watch; (3) **NT fulfillment**—believers constitute "holy priesthood" through union with Christ. Yet this democratization maintains essential truth: access to God requires priestly mediation. Under old covenant, Aaronic priests mediated; under new covenant, Christ mediates, but His mediation extends to all believers, making them priests who "offer spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ." The sacrifices transform: where Levitical priests offered bulls and goats that "can never take away sins" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 4|Hebrews 10:4]]), believers offer "spiritual sacrifices"—bodies presented as living sacrifice ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1]]), praise as fruit of lips ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 15|Hebrews 13:15]]), good works and sharing ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 16|Hebrews 13:16]]), prayers ascending like incense ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 8|Revelation 5:8]]). The phrase "through Jesus Christ" is decisive—democratization doesn't eliminate Christocentric mediation, it extends it. Where Aaronic priests stood between God and Israel, Christ stands between God and church, yet His mediation grants all believers priestly access. The trajectory moves from **exclusive priesthood ministering to passive laity** to **inclusive priesthood where all minister**, from physical sacrifices offered by few to spiritual sacrifices offered by all, from priests who stood daily serving to priests who enter boldly "by the blood of Jesus" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19]]), from "kingdom of priests" as unfulfilled promise to royal priesthood as present reality in Christ.
