**Context**: Isaiah 2:2-4 prophesies: "It shall come to pass in the latter days that the mountain of the house of the LORD shall be established as the highest of the mountains, and shall be lifted up above the hills; and all the nations shall flow to it... He shall judge between the nations... they shall beat their swords into plowshares... nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war anymore." This magnificent vision shows Zion exalted, nations streaming to it, Torah going forth, universal peace established—the Messianic kingdom. The "latter days" reach their consummation when Christ reigns and all nations worship the God of Israel.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 2|Isaiah 2:2-4]]'s vision—"in the latter days the mountain of the house of the LORD shall be established... and all the nations shall flow to it"—prophesies Messianic kingdom with Zion exalted, Torah going forth, universal peace established. The "latter days" (*'aḥărît hayyāmîm*) connect to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 1|Genesis 49:1]]'s eschatological timeline. Christ inaugurates this fulfillment: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]] commands: "make disciples of all nations"—instead of nations coming to Jerusalem physically, gospel goes to nations spiritually. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 5|Acts 2:5-11]] shows Pentecost firstfruits: "devout men from every nation... each one was hearing them speak in his own language"—nations beginning to "flow" to Zion. The church becomes the locus where nations gather to worship—not geographic Jerusalem but spiritual Zion. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22]] states believers "have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem." The Torah going forth finds fulfillment in gospel proclamation—[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 3|Isaiah 2:3]] declares "out of Zion shall go forth the law, and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem." [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]] commissions: "you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth"—word going from Jerusalem to nations. The universal peace—"they shall beat their swords into plowshares... nation shall not lift up sword against nation"—awaits Christ's return and millennial reign. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11-16]] depicts Christ returning as conquering King to establish peace. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]] describes new Jerusalem: "the nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it... they will bring into it the glory and the honor of the nations." The trajectory shows: Isaiah prophesies nations flowing to Zion in "latter days" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 2|Isaiah 2:2-4]]) → Christ inaugurates through Great Commission ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]]) → Pentecost draws nations ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 5|Acts 2:5-11]]) → church age sees progressive fulfillment (nations entering kingdom) → Christ's return establishes millennial peace ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 1|Revelation 20:1-6]]) → new Jerusalem becomes eternal worship center ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]]). The "latter days" have begun ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 2|Hebrews 1:2]]) but await consummation—nations streaming to Christ now through gospel, ultimately in glorified state.
