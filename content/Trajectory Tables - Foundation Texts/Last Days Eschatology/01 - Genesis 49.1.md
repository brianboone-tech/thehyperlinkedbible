### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 1|Genesis 49:1]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H0622-0623#H0622|הֵאָסְפוּ]] (*hē'āsəp̄û*) - "gather yourselves" (Niphal imperative)
- [[Lexicon/H5046-5047#H5046|אַגִּידָה]] (*'aggîdâ*) - "I will tell, declare" (Hiphil imperfect cohortative)
- [[Lexicon/H7122-7123#H7122|יִקְרָא]] (*yiqrā'*) - "shall befall, happen" (Qal imperfect)
- [[Lexicon/H0319-0320#H0319|אַחֲרִית]] (*'aḥărît*) - "end, latter part, future"
- [[Lexicon/H3117-3118#H3117|הַיָּמִים]] (*hayyāmîm*) - "the days" (definite plural)

**Context**: Genesis 49:1 records Jacob gathering his sons: "Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you what shall befall you in the last days." This is the first biblical use of the phrase "last days" (*'aḥărît hayyāmîm*), establishing a pattern: patriarchs prophesy concerning distant eschatological future. Jacob's deathbed blessing includes Messianic prophecy (v. 10—Shiloh from Judah), showing "last days" encompasses not merely tribal futures but ultimate divine purposes. The phrase becomes technical eschatological terminology, developed throughout Scripture and reinterpreted in the NT as inaugurated at Christ's coming.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] (seed of woman will crush serpent's head), [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] (in you all families of the earth shall be blessed), [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 48#Genesis 48 . 21|Genesis 48:21]] (Joseph - God will be with you and will bring you again to the land)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 14|Numbers 24:14]] (what this people will do... in the latter days), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 30|Deuteronomy 4:30]] (when you are in tribulation... in the latter days), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 2|Isaiah 2:2]] (in the latter days the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]] (in the last days... **Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 1|Genesis 49:1]]'s introduction—"I will tell you what shall befall you in the last days"—establishes eschatological expectation that finds fulfillment in Christ. The phrase *'aḥărît hayyāmîm* becomes technical terminology throughout Scripture, initially referring to distant future but reinterpreted in the NT as inaugurated at Christ's coming. Jacob's blessing includes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]]'s Messianic prophecy: "The scepter shall not depart from Judah... until tribute comes to him; and to him shall be the obedience of the peoples." This points to Christ from Judah's line, receiving universal obedience. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 2|Matthew 1:2-3]] traces Jesus' genealogy through Judah. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 5|Revelation 5:5]] declares Christ "the Lion of the tribe of Judah." The "last days" thus encompass Messianic reign. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 16|Acts 2:16-17]] marks the hermeneutical pivot: Peter declares "this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel: 'And in the last days, says God, I will pour out my Spirit.'" The Spirit's outpouring at Pentecost inaugurates the "last days"—what OT patriarchs and prophets anticipated as distant future has arrived in Christ. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 1|Hebrews 1:1-2]] states: "God, who at various times and in various ways spoke in time past to the fathers by the prophets, has in these last days spoken to us by His Son." Christ's incarnation is the eschatological moment—God's final word. First Peter 1:20 declares Christ "was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you." The "last days" began at Christ's first coming, not merely His return. Believers live in the "already/not yet" tension—eschatological age inaugurated ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 2|Hebrews 1:2]]) but awaiting consummation (Revelation 19-22). The trajectory shows: patriarchs prophesy of "last days" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 1|Genesis 49:1]]) → prophets develop eschatological vision ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 2|Isaiah 2:2]]; [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 28|Daniel 2:28]]) → Christ inaugurates "last days" through incarnation, death, resurrection ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 2|Hebrews 1:2]]) → Spirit's outpouring marks "last days" arrival ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]]) → church age is "last days" ([[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 3#2 Timothy 3 . 1|2 Timothy 3:1]]; [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 5#James 5 . 3|James 5:3]]) → Christ's return consummates what His first coming began (Revelation 19-22). Jacob's deathbed prophecy initiates a theme culminating in Christ—the "last days" are now, though not yet fully consummated.
