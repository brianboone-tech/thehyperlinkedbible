### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 1|Revelation 20:1-3, 10]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G12|G12]] ἄβυσσος (abyssos) - "abyss/bottomless pit" - place of demonic confinement
- [[Lexicon/G1210|G1210]] δέω (deō) - "bind" - restraining
- [[Lexicon/G906|G906]] βάλλω (ballō) - "cast/throw" - forceful removal
- [[Lexicon/G3041|G3041]] λίμνη τοῦ πυρός (limnē tou pyros) - "lake of fire" - final judgment place

**Context**: Revelation 20 describes Satan's binding and ultimate fate. The imagery echoes the scapegoat's removal: Satan (the source of temptation to sin) is "cast" into the abyss, then finally into the lake of fire. What the scapegoat symbolized—sin's complete removal—finds cosmic fulfillment in evil's eternal banishment.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 21|Leviticus 16:21-22]] - Scapegoat sent into wilderness (associated with demonic realm)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 12|Isaiah 14:12-15]] - Satan cast down
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 16|Ezekiel 28:16-19]] - The fallen cherub expelled and destroyed

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] - Scapegoat sent to "azazel" (wilderness/demon)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 24#Isaiah 24 . 21|Isaiah 24:21-22]] - "The LORD will punish the powers in the heavens above"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|Matthew 25:41]] - "Eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Complete and eternal removal of sin and its author

**Christological Connection**: Christ's victory over sin (accomplished at the cross, symbolized by the scapegoat) culminates in Satan's eternal banishment. The trajectory completes: annual removal (scapegoat) → decisive removal (cross) → eternal removal (lake of fire). What was symbolized by a goat driven into the wilderness becomes reality when evil itself is cast into eternal fire. The new creation has "nothing impure" (21:27) because sin and its source are permanently removed.
