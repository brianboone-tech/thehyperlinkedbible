### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 28|Hebrews 9:28]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4374|G4374]] προσφέρω (prospherō) - "offered" - sacrificial presentation
- [[Lexicon/G539|G539]] ἅπαξ (hapax) - "once" - unrepeatable single occurrence
- [[Lexicon/G399|G399]] ἀναφέρω (anapherō) - "bear/carry up" - lifting and carrying (sacrificial term)
- [[Lexicon/G4183|G4183]] πολλοί (polloi) - "many" - the beneficiaries

**Context**: Hebrews 9 expounds Christ's superior priesthood and sacrifice. Verse 28 summarizes: Christ was offered once to bear the sins of many. The language directly echoes both Isaiah 53:12 ("bore the sin of many") and Leviticus 16 (scapegoat bearing sins). Christ's sin-bearing fulfills both prophecy and type.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] - Scapegoat carries (נָשָׂא) sins
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] - Servant "bore the sin of many"
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 1|Numbers 18:1]] - Priests "bear" responsibility for sanctuary

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] and [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] - The sin-bearing tradition
- **FROM OT**: The entire sacrificial system anticipates this bearing of sin
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 24|1 Peter 2:24]] - "He Himself bore our sins in His body on the tree"

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Explicit application of scapegoat/Suffering Servant to Christ

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 28|Hebrews 9:28]] explicitly presents Christ as fulfilling the scapegoat's sin-bearing function. "Offered once to bear sins" combines sacrificial offering (slain goat) with sin-carrying (scapegoat). Christ accomplishes what both goats together symbolized. His return will be "not to bear sin"—that work is complete. The scapegoat went out; Christ will return, His sin-bearing mission accomplished.
