### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1-3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2631|G2631]] κατάκριμα (katakrima) - "condemnation" - judicial sentence
- [[Lexicon/G3568|G3568]] νῦν (nyn) - "now" - present reality
- [[Lexicon/G4561|G4561]] σάρξ (sarx) - "flesh" - human nature under sin
- [[Lexicon/G2633|G2633]] κατακρίνω (katakrinō) - "condemn" - what God did to sin

**Context**: Romans 8:1 announces the glorious conclusion of Paul's argument: "There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus." This is the experiential result of Christ's sin-bearing. The scapegoat carried sins away; believers live in the freedom that results—no accusation, no guilt, no condemnation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] - Sins carried away, no longer present
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 32#Psalm 32 . 1|Psalm 32:1-2]] - "Blessed is the one whose sin the LORD does not count"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 50#Isaiah 50 . 8|Isaiah 50:8-9]] - "He who vindicates me is near. Who will contend with me?"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] - Sins removed from God's people
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 50#Isaiah 50 . 8|Isaiah 50:8-9]]'s vindication language anticipates Romans 8's courtroom imagery
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 33|Romans 8:33-34]] - "Who will bring any charge against those whom God has chosen?"

**Type Classification**: NT Application - Believers experience what the scapegoat symbolized

**Christological Connection**: The scapegoat carried sins to a remote place where they could not accuse Israel; Christ carried sins to the cross where they condemn no more. "No condemnation" is the believer's standing because the sin that would condemn has been removed—not merely pushed away but dealt with decisively. The scapegoat's journey into the wilderness becomes the believer's journey into freedom.
