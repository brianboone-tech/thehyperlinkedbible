### [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 18|Micah 7:18-19]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5375|H5375]] נָשָׂא (nasa) - "forgives/bears away" - the same word for the scapegoat carrying sins
- [[Lexicon/H5674|H5674]] עָבַר (abar) - "passes over" - God overlooking transgression
- [[Lexicon/H3533|H3533]] כָּבַשׁ (kabash) - "subdue/tread down" - conquering iniquities
- [[Lexicon/H7993|H7993]] שָׁלַךְ (shalakh) - "cast/hurl" - violent throwing into sea

**Context**: Micah's prophecy concludes with doxology celebrating God's incomparable mercy. The prophet develops the removal theme with vivid imagery: God "treads our iniquities underfoot" and "casts all our sins into the depths of the sea." This echoes and expands the scapegoat's wilderness journey.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] - Scapegoat carries sins into wilderness
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 103#Psalm 103 . 12|Psalm 103:12]] - Sins removed as far as east from west
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 38#Isaiah 38 . 17|Isaiah 38:17]] - God casts sins behind His back

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]] - The scapegoat paradigm of sin removal
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 4|Exodus 15:4-5]] - Pharaoh's army cast into the sea (judgment imagery)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 14|Colossians 2:14]] - God "having canceled the charge of our legal indebtedness"

**Type Classification**: Prophetic development of removal theme, expanding scapegoat typology

**Christological Connection**: Micah's imagery finds fulfillment in Christ's complete removal of sin. The sea depths represent irretrievable distance; Christ's atonement places sins where they can never resurface. "There is now no condemnation" (Rom 8:1) because sin has been cast where it cannot be found, accusing no more.
