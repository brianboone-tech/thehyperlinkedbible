### [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 3#2 Chronicles 3 . 1|2 Chronicles 3:1-2]]

**Context**: "Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to David his father, at the place that David had appointed, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite." The Chronicler explicitly identifies the temple site as Mount Moriah—the same mountain where Abraham offered Isaac (Genesis 22:2). This is crucial typological interpretation: the place of Abraham's near-sacrifice becomes the place of Israel's sacrificial worship.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- הַר הַמּוֹרִיָּה (*har haMoriyyah*) - "Mount Moriah"—connecting temple to Aqedah
- נִרְאָה לְדָוִיד (*nir'ah leDavid*) - "appeared to David"—divine theophany at this location
- גֹּרֶן אָרְנָן (*goren 'Ornan*) - "threshing floor of Ornan"—where David's altar stopped the plague

**Christological Connection**: The Chronicler's identification of the temple site as Mount Moriah is profound typological interpretation. Abraham nearly offered his son where generations later Israel would offer substitutionary sacrifices, and where (near that same mountain) Christ would be offered as the final sacrifice. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 14|Genesis 22:14]] says "On the mount of the LORD it shall be provided"—pointing beyond the ram to Christ. The trajectory: Abraham offers Isaac on Moriah (Genesis 22) → Temple stands on Moriah with daily sacrifices → Christ offered near Moriah as the sacrifice all others prefigured.
