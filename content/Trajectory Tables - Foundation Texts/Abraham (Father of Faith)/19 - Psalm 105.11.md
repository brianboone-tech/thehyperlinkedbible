### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 105#Psalms 105 . 11|Psalm 105:11]]

**Context**: This historical psalm celebrates God's covenant faithfulness: "To you I will give the land of Canaan as your portion for an inheritance." The psalm recounts God's dealings with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob (vv.9-15), emphasizing that God "remembers his covenant forever, the word that he commanded, for a thousand generations, the covenant that he made with Abraham" (vv.8-9). This demonstrates post-exilic Israel understanding their existence as fulfillment of Abrahamic promises.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- לְךָ אֶתֵּן אֶת־אֶרֶץ־כְּנָעַן (*lekha 'etten 'et-'erets-Kena'an*) - "to you I will give the land of Canaan"
- חֶבֶל נַחֲלַתְכֶם (*chevel nachalatkhem*) - "the portion of your inheritance"—measured allotment
- בְּרִיתוֹ לְעוֹלָם (*berito le'olam*) - "his covenant forever"—eternal duration

**Christological Connection**: Psalm 105 celebrates Israel's inheritance of Canaan as fulfillment of God's promise to Abraham. Yet this was always typological—pointing to greater fulfillment. Paul expands the promise: Abraham was not merely heir of Canaan but "of the world" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|Romans 4:13]]). Hebrews explains that the patriarchs looked beyond physical land to "a better country, that is, a heavenly one" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 16|Hebrews 11:16]]). The trajectory: land promised to Abraham → Israel inherits Canaan (partial fulfillment) → Christ inherits all things ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 2|Hebrews 1:2]]) → believers as co-heirs inherit the new creation ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 17|Romans 8:17]]; Revelation 21-22).
