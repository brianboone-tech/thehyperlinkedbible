### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 26|Isaiah 41:26]]

**Context**: God challenges the idols: "Who declared it from the beginning, that we might know, and beforehand, that we might say, 'He is right'? There was none who declared it, none who proclaimed, none who heard your words." God alone predicts and fulfills—contrasting His prophetic power with idols' silence. Jesus' statement "Before Abraham was, I am" (John 8:58) echoes this divine prerogative of existing before and declaring from the beginning.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מִי הִגִּיד מֵרֹאשׁ (*mi higgid mero'sh*) - "who declared from the beginning"—predictive revelation
- וְנֵדָעָה (*veneda'ah*) - "that we might know"—knowledge through fulfillment
- צַדִּיק (*tsaddiq*) - "right/righteous"—vindicated through prophecy-fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: Jesus' claim "Before Abraham was, I am" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 58|John 8:58]]) connects to [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 26|Isaiah 41:26]]'s theme of divine preexistence and prophetic authority. God alone declares "from the beginning" because God alone exists before the beginning. Jesus claimed this prerogative—He existed before Abraham and saw His day approaching. The trajectory: God declares future events to Abraham → God alone fulfills what He predicts → Jesus claims "I am" (divine identity) → Jesus existed before Abraham → Jesus is the one whom Abraham saw ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 56|John 8:56]]). This connects Abraham's faith to Christ's eternal existence.
