### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 8|Isaiah 41:8-10]]

**Context**: God addresses exilic Israel: "But you, Israel, my servant, Jacob, whom I have chosen, the offspring of Abraham, my friend; you whom I took from the ends of the earth... I said to you, 'You are my servant, I have chosen you and not cast you off'; fear not, for I am with you." The designation "offspring of Abraham, my friend" connects Israel's election to the Abrahamic covenant. God's faithfulness to Abraham guarantees His faithfulness to Abraham's descendants.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- עַבְדִּי (*'avdi*) - "my servant"—Israel as God's chosen instrument
- זֶרַע אַבְרָהָם אֹהֲבִי (*zera' 'Avraham 'ohavi*) - "offspring of Abraham my friend"—Abraham's special relationship with God
- בְּחַרְתִּיךָ (*bechartikhka*) - "I have chosen you"—divine election through Abraham
- אֶחֱזַקְתִּיךָ (*'echezaqtikhka*) - "I will strengthen you"—covenant faithfulness

**Christological Connection**: Isaiah identifies Israel as "offspring of Abraham, my friend" to assure exiles that God's covenant faithfulness remains. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 16|Hebrews 2:16]] applies this to Christ: "He helps the offspring of Abraham." Christ took on flesh to identify with and redeem Abraham's descendants—not merely physical Israel but all who share Abraham's faith ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 7|Galatians 3:7]], 29). The trajectory: God befriends Abraham → God remains faithful to Abraham's offspring → Christ becomes Abraham's offspring to help them → believers are Abraham's offspring through faith in Christ.
