### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16-18]]

**Context**: After Abraham demonstrates his faith by offering Isaac, God swears by Himself: "because you have done this and have not withheld your son, your only son, I will surely bless you, and I will surely multiply your offspring as the stars of heaven... and in your offspring shall all the nations of the earth be blessed, because you have obeyed my voice." This is the covenant oath—God's sworn promise that guarantees fulfillment. Hebrews 6:13-18 emphasizes the certainty this oath provides.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- בִּי נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי (*bi nishba'ti*) - "by myself I have sworn"—God swears by His own existence
- לֹא חָשַׂכְתָּ (*lo' chasakhta*) - "you have not withheld"—echoed in Romans 8:32 of God and Christ
- וְהִתְבָּרְכוּ בְזַרְעֲךָ (*vehitbarekhu vezar'akha*) - "shall be blessed in your seed"—universal blessing promise
- יַעַן אֲשֶׁר שָׁמַעְתָּ בְּקֹלִי (*ya'an 'asher shama'ta veqoli*) - "because you have obeyed my voice"

**Christological Connection**: God's oath after the Aqedah is the most solemn possible guarantee. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 13|Hebrews 6:13-18]] explains: God swore by Himself because there was no one greater, giving believers "two unchangeable things" (promise and oath) as absolute assurance. The phrase "did not withhold your son, your only son" is deliberately echoed in [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 32|Romans 8:32]]: "He who did not spare his own Son." Abraham's offering of Isaac typifies God's offering of Christ. The escalation: Abraham was stopped; God was not. Isaac was spared; Christ was not. The seed in whom all nations are blessed is Christ.
