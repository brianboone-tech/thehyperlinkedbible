### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 8|Galatians 3:8]]

**Context**: "And the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, 'In you shall all the nations be blessed.'" Paul makes the remarkable claim that Genesis 12:3 is the gospel preached in advance. Scripture itself "foresaw" Gentile justification by faith and announced it to Abraham. The Abrahamic covenant is not merely background but the gospel in seed form.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- προϊδοῦσα ἡ γραφή (*proidousa hē graphē*) - "the Scripture, foreseeing"—prophetic foresight
- δικαιοῖ τὰ ἔθνη (*dikaioi ta ethnē*) - "would justify the Gentiles"—Gentile justification
- προευηγγελίσατο (*proeuēngelisato*) - "preached the gospel beforehand"—gospel anticipated
- ἐν σοὶ ἐνευλογηθήσονται (*en soi eneulogēthēsontai*) - "in you shall be blessed"—blessing through Abraham

**Christological Connection**: Paul identifies [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] as the gospel. How is a blessing promise the gospel? Because the blessing comes through Abraham's seed—Christ ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]]). Justification by faith was always God's plan, announced to Abraham before law or circumcision existed. The trajectory: God announces blessing to nations through Abraham → this is the gospel preached beforehand → Christ comes as the seed → Gentiles justified by faith receive the promised blessing. Paul's argument demolishes any claim that law-keeping is necessary for justification—the gospel predates law by 430 years.
