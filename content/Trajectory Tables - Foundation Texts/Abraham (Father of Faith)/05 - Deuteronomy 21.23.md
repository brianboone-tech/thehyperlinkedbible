### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 23|Deuteronomy 21:23]]

**Context**: "A hanged man is cursed by God. You shall not leave his body on the tree overnight... for a hanged man is cursed by God." This law about executed criminals becomes foundational for understanding Christ's crucifixion. Paul quotes this in Galatians 3:13: "Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us—for it is written, 'Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree.'" Christ bore the Law's curse so that the Abrahamic blessing might come to the Gentiles.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- קִלְלַת אֱלֹהִים תָּלוּי (*qillelat 'Elohim taluy*) - "cursed by God is the hanged one"
- עֵץ (*'ets*) - "tree"—same word used for tree of knowledge (Genesis 2:17) and cross
- לֹא־תָלִין (*lo'-talin*) - "you shall not leave overnight"—corpse must be removed

**Christological Connection**: Paul's logic in [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13-14]] is crucial for the Abrahamic trajectory: Christ became a curse (by hanging on the tree) "so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles." The Law pronounced a curse on all who don't keep it perfectly ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 10|Galatians 3:10]]); Christ bore that curse on the cross, freeing believers from condemnation and opening the way for Gentiles to receive the Abrahamic blessing through faith. The trajectory: blessing promised to Abraham → Law brings curse for disobedience → Christ bears the curse → Abrahamic blessing flows to all nations through faith.
