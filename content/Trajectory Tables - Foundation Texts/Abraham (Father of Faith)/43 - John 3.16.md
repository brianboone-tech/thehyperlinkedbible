### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]]

**Context**: "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life." The phrase "gave his only Son" deliberately echoes Genesis 22:2 ("Take your son, your only son Isaac, whom you love"). The Greek μονογενής (monogenēs, "only, unique") corresponds to Hebrew יָחִיד (yachid, "only, beloved"). John presents the crucifixion as the ultimate Aqedah—the Father offering His Son.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἠγάπησεν (*ēgapēsen*) - "loved"—divine love motivating sacrifice
- τὸν μονογενῆ (*ton monogenē*) - "the only/unique [Son]"—echoes Genesis 22:2 יָחִיד
- ἔδωκεν (*edōken*) - "gave"—handed over in sacrifice
- πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων (*pas ho pisteuōn*) - "whoever believes"—faith as means of receiving

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]] is the gospel summary, and its Abrahamic background is unmistakable. The language deliberately evokes Genesis 22: Abraham was commanded to give his "only son" (יָחִיד); God gave His "only Son" (μονογενής). The escalation is dramatic: Abraham was stopped at the last moment; God was not. Isaac was spared; Jesus was not. A ram died for Isaac; Jesus died for the world. The trajectory: Abraham willing to give his son (Genesis 22) → God actually gives His Son ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]]) → whoever believes receives eternal life. Abraham's faith model is completed in believers who trust God's ultimate gift.
