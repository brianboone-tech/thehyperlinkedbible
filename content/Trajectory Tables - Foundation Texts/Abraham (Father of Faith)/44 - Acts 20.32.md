### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 20#Acts 20 . 32|Acts 20:32]]

**Context**: Paul's farewell to the Ephesian elders: "And now I commend you to God and to the word of his grace, which is able to build you up and to give you the inheritance among all those who are sanctified." The language of "inheritance" (κληρονομία, klēronomia) connects Gentile believers to the Abrahamic promises. Through Christ, Gentiles become fellow heirs with Jewish believers, inheriting what was promised to Abraham.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- τῷ λόγῳ τῆς χάριτος αὐτοῦ (*tō logō tēs charitos autou*) - "the word of his grace"—gospel message
- οἰκοδομῆσαι (*oikodomēsai*) - "to build up"—edification
- τὴν κληρονομίαν (*tēn klēronomian*) - "the inheritance"—Abrahamic promise language
- ἐν τοῖς ἡγιασμένοις (*en tois hēgiasmenois*) - "among those who are sanctified"—set apart people

**Christological Connection**: Paul's language of inheritance connects Gentile Ephesian believers to the Abrahamic promises. The inheritance promised to Abraham's seed (land, blessing) is now available to "all those who are sanctified"—not merely physical descendants but those made holy through faith in Christ. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 6|Ephesians 3:6]] states this explicitly: "Gentiles are fellow heirs... through the gospel." The trajectory: inheritance promised to Abraham's seed → Christ is the seed ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]]) → believers in Christ become co-heirs ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|Galatians 3:29]]) → inheritance includes new creation ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|Romans 4:13]]).
