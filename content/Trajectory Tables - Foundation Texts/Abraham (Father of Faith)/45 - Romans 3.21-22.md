### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 21|Romans 3:21-22]]

**Context**: "But now the righteousness of God has been manifested apart from the law, although the Law and the Prophets bear witness to it—the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ for all who believe." This introduces Paul's exposition of justification by faith, which he grounds in Abraham's example (Romans 4). The righteousness "apart from law" is the same righteousness credited to Abraham before the Law existed (Genesis 15:6).

**Greek Key Terms**:
- δικαιοσύνη θεοῦ (*dikaiosynē theou*) - "righteousness of God"—God's saving righteousness
- χωρὶς νόμου (*chōris nomou*) - "apart from law"—not through law-keeping
- πεφανέρωται (*pephanerōtai*) - "has been manifested"—now revealed in Christ
- διὰ πίστεως Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (*dia pisteōs Iēsou Christou*) - "through faith in Jesus Christ"

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 21|Romans 3:21-22]] establishes that the righteousness available in Christ is the same righteousness Abraham received—by faith, apart from works of law. Paul explicitly grounds this in Abraham (Romans 4): if Abraham was justified by works, he has something to boast about, but not before God (4:2). Instead, "Abraham believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness" (4:3, quoting [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]]). The trajectory: Abraham justified by faith before law existed → Law and Prophets witness to this righteousness → Christ manifests this righteousness → all who believe receive it.
