### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3-4]]

**Context**: God's covenant promise to Abraham includes the universal blessing: "in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed" (v.3). Abraham's response (v.4) is immediate obedience—"So Abram went, as the LORD had told him." This establishes the pattern: God's gracious promise calls forth faith that obeys. The blessing promise is foundational to redemptive history—Paul calls it "the gospel preached beforehand to Abraham" (Galatians 3:8).

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- וְנִבְרְכוּ בְךָ (*venivrekhu vekha*) - "shall be blessed in you"—Niphal/Hithpael, nations receive blessing through Abraham
- כֹּל מִשְׁפְּחֹת הָאֲדָמָה (*kol mishpechot ha'adamah*) - "all the families of the earth"—universal scope
- וַיֵּלֶךְ אַבְרָם (*vayelekh 'Avram*) - "and Abram went"—immediate obedient response

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] is the gospel in seed form. Paul identifies this promise as "the gospel preached beforehand to Abraham" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 8|Galatians 3:8]]), and argues that "the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand" through this verse. The blessing comes through Abraham's "seed" (singular)—Christ ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]]). Abraham's immediate obedience (v.4) demonstrates the response faith produces: he believed God's promise and acted accordingly. The trajectory: promise of blessing to all nations → Christ the seed brings blessing → Gentiles justified by faith receive the blessing.
