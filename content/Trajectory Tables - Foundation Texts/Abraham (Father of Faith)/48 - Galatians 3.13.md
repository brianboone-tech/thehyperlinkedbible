### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]]

**Context**: "Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us—for it is written, 'Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree.'" Paul quotes Deuteronomy 21:23 to explain how Christ removed the Law's curse. The purpose (v.14): "so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles." Christ bore the curse so the Abrahamic blessing could flow unimpeded to all nations.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἐξηγόρασεν (*exēgorasen*) - "redeemed/bought out"—purchase from slavery
- ἐκ τῆς κατάρας τοῦ νόμου (*ek tēs kataras tou nomou*) - "from the curse of the law"
- γενόμενος ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν κατάρα (*genomenos hyper hēmōn katara*) - "becoming a curse for us"—substitutionary
- ἐπικατάρατος (*epikataratos*) - "cursed"—quoting Deuteronomy 21:23

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13-14]] is the theological heart of the Abrahamic trajectory. The Law pronounces curse on all who fail to keep it perfectly (3:10). Christ became the curse (bearing judgment sinners deserve) so that the blessing of Abraham might flow to the Gentiles. The ram that died for Isaac (Genesis 22) prefigures this: a substitute dies so the son is spared, and blessing follows. The trajectory: blessing promised → curse blocks blessing (sin under Law) → Christ bears curse → blessing flows to nations through faith.
