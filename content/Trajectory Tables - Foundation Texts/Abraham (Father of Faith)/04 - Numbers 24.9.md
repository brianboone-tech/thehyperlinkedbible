### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 9|Numbers 24:9]]

**Context**: Balaam's third oracle blesses Israel using Abrahamic covenant language: "Blessed are those who bless you, and cursed are those who curse you." This directly echoes Genesis 12:3, demonstrating that God's promise to Abraham extends to his descendants. The royal-messianic context is significant: "He crouched, he lay down like a lion... who dares rouse him?" This lion imagery connects to Judah (Genesis 49:9) and ultimately to Christ, the Lion of Judah (Revelation 5:5).

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כָּרַע שָׁכַב כַּאֲרִי (*kara' shakhav ka'ari*) - "he crouched, he lay down like a lion"—royal power
- מְבָרְכֶיךָ בָרוּךְ (*mevarkekhayikha barukh*) - "those who bless you are blessed"—echoes Genesis 12:3
- וְאֹרְרֶיךָ אָרוּר (*ve'orerekha 'arur*) - "those who curse you are cursed"

**Christological Connection**: Balaam's oracle applies the Abrahamic blessing to Israel while introducing royal-messianic imagery. The lion who cannot be roused anticipates the Davidic king and ultimately Christ. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 17|Numbers 24:17]] makes this explicit: "A star shall come out of Jacob, and a scepter shall rise out of Israel." The trajectory: Abraham receives blessing promise ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]]) → Israel inherits the promise ([[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 9|Numbers 24:9]]) → Davidic king embodies the promise → Christ the Lion of Judah fulfills it completely. Those who bless Christ's people are blessed; those who curse them are cursed.
