### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 32|Romans 8:32]]

**Context**: "He who did not spare his own Son but gave him up for us all, how will he not also with him graciously give us all things?" Paul deliberately echoes Genesis 22:16 ("you have not withheld your son, your only son"). The verbal parallel is unmistakable: Abraham "did not withhold" Isaac; God "did not spare" His Son. This is explicit Aqedah typology—Abraham offering Isaac prefigures God offering Christ.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- τοῦ ἰδίου υἱοῦ οὐκ ἐφείσατο (*tou idiou huiou ouk epheisato*) - "did not spare his own Son"—echoes Genesis 22:16 LXX
- ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν πάντων παρέδωκεν (*hyper hēmōn pantōn paredōken*) - "gave him up for us all"—substitutionary
- τὰ πάντα ἡμῖν χαρίσεται (*ta panta hēmin charisetai*) - "graciously give us all things"—comprehensive blessing

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 32|Romans 8:32]] is the NT's clearest statement that God accomplished what He prevented Abraham from completing. Abraham was willing to offer his only son, and God stopped him at the last moment. But God did not stop Himself: "He did not spare his own Son." The escalation from type to antitype is dramatic: Abraham was tested; God was not (He gave willingly). Isaac was spared; Christ was not. A ram died for Isaac; Christ died for us. The trajectory: Abraham willing to give → God actually gave → if God gave His Son, He will give us all things (complete salvation).
