### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40-42]]

**Context**: After listing covenant curses for disobedience, God promises restoration: "But if they confess their iniquity... if then their uncircumcised heart is humbled and they make amends for their iniquity, then I will remember my covenant with Jacob, and I will remember my covenant with Isaac and my covenant with Abraham, and I will remember the land." Restoration depends on heart circumcision—the internal reality physical circumcision signified.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- וְהִתְוַדּוּ (*vehitvaddu*) - "and they confess"—acknowledgment of sin
- לְבָבָם הֶעָרֵל (*levavam he'arel*) - "their uncircumcised heart"—heart hardened against God
- יִכָּנַע (*yikkana'*) - "is humbled"—broken, submitted
- וְזָכַרְתִּי (*vezakharti*) - "and I will remember"—covenantal remembrance (not mere recollection)

**Christological Connection**: This passage reveals that physical circumcision always pointed to spiritual reality—heart circumcision. The prophets repeatedly called for this internal transformation ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 4#Jeremiah 4 . 4|Jeremiah 4:4]]), which the Mosaic covenant could command but not accomplish. Christ accomplishes what the Law could not: "the circumcision of Christ" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 11|Colossians 2:11]]) removes the sinful nature, not merely the foreskin. The trajectory: physical sign (Genesis 17) → call for heart circumcision (Leviticus, Deuteronomy, Prophets) → Christ performs spiritual circumcision → believers receive it by faith through union with Christ.
