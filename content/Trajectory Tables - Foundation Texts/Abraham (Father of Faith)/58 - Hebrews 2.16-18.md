### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 16|Hebrews 2:16-18]]

**Context**: "For surely it is not angels that he helps, but he helps the offspring of Abraham. Therefore he had to be made like his brothers in every respect, so that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest." Christ's incarnation is specifically for Abraham's offspring—not angels. He took on human nature to help those who share Abraham's faith, becoming the compassionate high priest who can sympathize with our weaknesses.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- σπέρματος Ἀβραὰμ ἐπιλαμβάνεται (*spermatos Abraam epilambanetai*) - "takes hold of/helps the offspring of Abraham"
- ὤφειλεν (*ōpheilen*) - "he had to/was obligated to"—necessity of incarnation
- κατὰ πάντα τοῖς ἀδελφοῖς ὁμοιωθῆναι (*kata panta tois adelphois homoiōthēnai*) - "to be made like his brothers in every respect"
- ἐλεήμων καὶ πιστὸς ἀρχιερεύς (*eleēmōn kai pistos archiereus*) - "merciful and faithful high priest"

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 16|Hebrews 2:16-18]] explains why the Son became human: to help Abraham's offspring. The incarnation was not for angels but for humans who are Abraham's spiritual descendants through faith. Christ became like His brothers (humanity) so He could become their high priest—compassionate because He suffered, faithful because He overcame. The trajectory: God promises to help Abraham's offspring ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 8|Isaiah 41:8-9]]) → Christ becomes Abraham's offspring (incarnation) → Christ helps Abraham's offspring (redemption) → believers as Abraham's offspring receive Christ's priestly help ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|Galatians 3:29]]).
