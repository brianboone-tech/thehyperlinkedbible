### [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 7|Titus 3:7]]

**Context**: "So that being justified by his grace we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life." This concise statement captures the Abrahamic trajectory: justification by grace leading to inheritance. Abraham was justified by faith (Genesis 15:6) and promised an inheritance (Genesis 15:7). Believers are justified by grace through faith and become heirs of eternal life—the ultimate inheritance.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- δικαιωθέντες (*dikaiōthentes*) - "having been justified"—declared righteous
- τῇ ἐκείνου χάριτι (*tē ekeinou chariti*) - "by his grace"—unmerited favor
- κληρονόμοι (*klēronomoi*) - "heirs"—inheritance language from Abraham
- κατ' ἐλπίδα ζωῆς αἰωνίου (*kat' elpida zōēs aiōniou*) - "according to the hope of eternal life"

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 7|Titus 3:7]] captures the entire Abrahamic trajectory in one verse. The pattern Abraham established—justified by faith, promised inheritance—is exactly the pattern for believers in Christ. The inheritance has been clarified: not merely Canaan but eternal life in the new creation. Paul makes this explicit in [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|Romans 4:13]]: the promise was "that he would be heir of the world." The trajectory: Abraham justified by faith → promised land inheritance → believers justified by grace → promised eternal life inheritance. Same pattern, same grace, same faith, escalated fulfillment.
