### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 72#Psalms 72 . 17|Psalm 72:17]]

**Context**: This royal psalm prays for the ideal Davidic king: "May his name endure forever, his fame continue as long as the sun! May people be blessed in him, all nations call him blessed!" The phrase "all nations be blessed in him" directly echoes the Abrahamic promise (Genesis 12:3; 22:18), explicitly applying it to the Davidic king. This is foundational OT-to-OT messianic interpretation: the blessing promised to Abraham's seed flows through David's greater son.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- יְהִי שְׁמוֹ לְעוֹלָם (*yehi shemo le'olam*) - "may his name endure forever"—eternal dynasty
- וְיִתְבָּרְכוּ בוֹ (*veyitbarekhu vo*) - "may they be blessed in him"—same construction as Genesis 12:3
- כָּל־גּוֹיִם (*kol-goyim*) - "all nations"—universal scope matching Abrahamic promise
- יְאַשְּׁרוּהוּ (*ye'ashsheruhu*) - "call him blessed"—nations acknowledge his blessing

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 17|Psalm 72:17]] is explicit OT-to-OT messianic interpretation of the Abrahamic covenant. The royal psalmist understands that God's promise of blessing to all nations through Abraham's seed finds fulfillment in the Davidic king. The trajectory is clear: Abraham receives promise ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]]) → promise confirmed to his seed ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Genesis 22:18]]) → Davidic king identified as that seed ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 17|Psalm 72:17]]) → Christ fulfills as both Abraham's seed and David's son ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 1|Matthew 1:1]]; [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]]). The nations are blessed not merely through Abraham but through his royal descendant—Christ.
