### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 1|Genesis 17:1-27]]

**Context**: God appears to 99-year-old Abraham, establishes the covenant of circumcision, changes his name from Abram to Abraham ("father of a multitude"), and promises Isaac through Sarah. Circumcision becomes the covenant sign (v.11), marking Abraham's descendants as the covenant people. Paul argues in Romans 4:9-12 that Abraham was justified by faith (Genesis 15:6) before circumcision (Genesis 17), proving circumcision is a sign of righteousness already received, not a means of obtaining it.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- אֲנִי אֵל שַׁדַּי (*'ani 'El Shaddai*) - "I am God Almighty"—divine self-revelation
- בְּרִית עוֹלָם (*berit 'olam*) - "everlasting covenant"—eternal duration
- אוֹת בְּרִית (*'ot berit*) - "sign of the covenant"—circumcision as visible mark
- אַבְרָהָם (*'Avraham*) - "father of a multitude"—name change signifying destiny

**Christological Connection**: The covenant of circumcision points forward to its spiritual fulfillment in Christ. Physical circumcision was always meant to signify heart circumcision ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 16|Deuteronomy 10:16]]; 30:6; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 4#Jeremiah 4 . 4|Jeremiah 4:4]]). Paul identifies Christ's death as the true circumcision: "In him also you were circumcised with a circumcision made without hands, by putting off the body of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 11|Colossians 2:11]]). The trajectory: physical sign (Genesis 17) → prophetic call for heart circumcision → Christ accomplishes spiritual circumcision → believers receive it by faith.
