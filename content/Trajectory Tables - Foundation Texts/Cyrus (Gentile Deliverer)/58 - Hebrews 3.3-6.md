### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 3|Hebrews 3:3-6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1391|G1391]] δόξα (doxa) - "glory" - Christ's surpassing honor
- [[Lexicon/G2680|G2680]] κατασκευάζω (kataskeuazō) - "build/construct" - house-builder
- [[Lexicon/G2324|G2324]] θεράπων (therapōn) - "servant" - Moses' position in God's house
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (huios) - "son" - Christ's position over God's house

**Context**: Hebrews 3 compares Christ to Moses, demonstrating Christ's superiority. While the comparison is explicitly with Moses, the principle applies to all OT figures who served God's redemptive purposes—including Cyrus. Christ is the builder and Son; all others were servants within the house He builds.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 12#Numbers 12 . 7|Numbers 12:7]] - Moses is "faithful in all My house"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 28|Isaiah 44:28]] - Cyrus will rebuild Jerusalem and temple
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 12|Zechariah 6:12-13]] - The Branch will build the LORD's temple

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Moses as faithful servant in God's house (Num 12:7)
- **FROM OT**: The "house" concept encompasses tabernacle, temple, and people
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]] - "You also, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house"

**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - Christ surpasses all OT figures who served God's purposes

**Christological Connection**: Cyrus was God's instrument to rebuild an earthly temple; Christ builds the eternal temple—His body, the Church. Cyrus served God's purpose unknowingly; Christ came to do the Father's will with full knowledge and devotion. Cyrus's work was temporary and preparatory; Christ's work is final and consummating. The relationship is not replacement but fulfillment—Cyrus's work anticipated what only Christ could complete.
