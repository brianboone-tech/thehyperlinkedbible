### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 18#Revelation 18 . 4|Revelation 18:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1831|G1831]] ἐξέρχομαι (exerchomai) - "come out" - command to depart
- [[Lexicon/G897|G897]] Βαβυλών (Babylōn) - "Babylon" - the world system opposed to God
- [[Lexicon/G2842|G2842]] κοινωνέω (koinōneō) - "share/participate" - joint participation in sin
- [[Lexicon/G4127|G4127]] πληγή (plēgē) - "plagues" - divine judgments

**Context**: Revelation 18 describes the fall of "Babylon the Great"—the world system in rebellion against God. The command "Come out of her, my people" echoes the exodus commands of Isaiah and Jeremiah, applying the deliverance pattern to the eschatological church. What Cyrus's decree accomplished physically, this heavenly voice commands spiritually.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 20|Isaiah 48:20]] - "Go out from Babylon! Flee from the Chaldeans!"
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 51#Jeremiah 51 . 6|Jeremiah 51:6]], 45 - "Flee from Babylon! Run for your lives!"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 11|Isaiah 52:11]] - "Depart, depart, go out from there! Touch no unclean thing!"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: The historical exodus from Babylon under Cyrus's decree
- **FROM OT**: Isaiah and Jeremiah's commands to flee Babylon
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 17|2 Corinthians 6:17]] - "Come out from them and be separate"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - The final exodus completes the trajectory

**Christological Connection**: Cyrus opened the way for return to Jerusalem; Christ opens the way to the New Jerusalem. The trajectory from Cyrus to Christ to consummation reveals God's consistent pattern: He delivers His people from bondage and brings them to His dwelling place. The Babylonian return was to an earthly, temporary Jerusalem; the final exodus is to the eternal city where "God's dwelling place is now among the people" (Rev 21:3). What Cyrus began, Christ accomplishes, and eternity consummates.
