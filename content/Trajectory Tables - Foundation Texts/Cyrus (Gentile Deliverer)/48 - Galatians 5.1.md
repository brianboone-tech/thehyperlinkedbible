### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 1|Galatians 5:1]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1657|G1657]] ἐλευθερία (eleutheria) - "freedom/liberty" - the state Christ grants
- [[Lexicon/G1659|G1659]] ἐλευθερόω (eleutheroō) - "set free" - Christ's liberating action
- [[Lexicon/G4739|G4739]] στήκω (stēkō) - "stand firm" - perseverance in freedom
- [[Lexicon/G2218|G2218]] ζυγός (zygos) - "yoke" - bondage to be avoided

**Context**: Galatians 5:1 summarizes Paul's argument against returning to law-based religion. Christ has accomplished freedom from the law's condemnation; believers must not re-enslave themselves. This applies the exodus pattern: as Cyrus freed captives who must not return to Babylon, Christ frees from sin's bondage—a freedom to be maintained.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 2|Exodus 20:2]] - "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 20|Isaiah 48:20]] - "Go out from Babylon! Flee from the Chaldeans!"
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 30#Jeremiah 30 . 8|Jeremiah 30:8]] - "I will break the yoke off their necks and tear off their chains"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - The exodus pattern of liberation from bondage
- **FROM OT**:
  - Isaiah's new exodus prophecies; the return from Babylon under Cyrus
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 18|Romans 6:18-22]] - "You have been set free from sin"
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 36|John 8:36]] - "If the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed"
**Type Classification**: NT Application - Believers live out the freedom Christ (the true Cyrus) has won

**Christological Connection**: Cyrus's decree freed bodies; Christ's gospel frees souls. Cyrus opened prison doors; Christ opens hearts. The pattern is identical: bondage → deliverer's intervention → freedom → responsibility to live as free people. But Christ's freedom is absolute and eternal: freedom from sin's guilt, power, and ultimately presence. Believers are the true returned exiles, called never to return to their former captivity.
