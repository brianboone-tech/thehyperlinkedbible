### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18-21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5548|G5548]] χρίω (chriō) - "anoint" - the basis for "Christ" (Christos)
- [[Lexicon/G2097|G2097]] εὐαγγελίζω (euangelizō) - "preach good news" - gospel proclamation
- [[Lexicon/G859|G859]] ἄφεσις (aphesis) - "release/forgiveness" - liberation from captivity
- [[Lexicon/G164|G164]] αἰχμάλωτος (aichmalōtos) - "captive" - prisoners of war/bondage

**Context**: Jesus reads from Isaiah 61 in the Nazareth synagogue and declares: "Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing." This programmatic statement at the beginning of His public ministry identifies Jesus as the ultimate "Anointed One"—the Messiah who surpasses all previous anointed figures, including Cyrus.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 45#Isaiah 45 . 1|Isaiah 45:1]] - Cyrus is mashiach (anointed) for political deliverance
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 1|Isaiah 61:1-2]] - The prophet/Servant is anointed for spiritual deliverance
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 2|Psalm 2:2]] - "against the LORD and against His Anointed One"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 1|Isaiah 61:1-2]] - Jesus claims this prophecy as His own
- **FROM OT**: The "anointed" title links to Cyrus (Isa 45:1), but Jesus fulfills the greater anointing of Isaiah 61
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 10#Acts 10 . 38|Acts 10:38]] - "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and power"

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Christ as the true Anointed One whom Cyrus typified

**Christological Connection**: Cyrus was called "anointed" for a task; Jesus IS the Anointed One by nature and office. Cyrus did not know God (Isa 45:4); Jesus is God incarnate. Cyrus freed bodies from Babylon; Jesus frees souls from sin. Cyrus rebuilt an earthly temple; Jesus raises the temple of His body ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19]]) and builds believers into a spiritual house. The political deliverance through Cyrus was a shadow; the spiritual deliverance through Christ is the substance.
