### [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 1|Ezra 1:1-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5782|H5782]] עוּר (ur) - "stirred" - the LORD awakened Cyrus's spirit
- [[Lexicon/H6485|H6485]] פָּקַד (paqad) - "appointed/charged" - divine commission
- [[Lexicon/H1129|H1129]] בָּנָה (banah) - "build" - the house/temple construction
- [[Lexicon/H7725|H7725]] שׁוּב (shuv) - "return" (implied) - the exiles go back

**Context**: Ezra 1 marks the end of the Babylonian exile and the beginning of the restoration period. The decree of Cyrus (538 BC) fulfills both Isaiah's specific prophecy about Cyrus and Jeremiah's prophecy of seventy years' duration. This historical moment serves as a hinge in redemptive history.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 28|Isaiah 44:28-45]]:7 (Cyrus named and commissioned)
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 11|Jeremiah 25:11]]; 29:10 (seventy years' exile concludes)
- Echoes Exodus language—a "new exodus" from bondage
- Anticipates Haggai and Zechariah's temple-rebuilding prophecies

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 28|Isaiah 44:28]] - "Let [Jerusalem's] foundation be laid"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 11|Jeremiah 25:11]]; 29:10 - "When seventy years are completed"
- **FROM NT**: The pattern of deliverance from bondage shapes NT soteriology (Rom 6; Col 1:13-14)

**Type Classification**: Historical fulfillment of prophetic type, serving as ground for greater messianic fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: Cyrus's decree begins the restoration of temple and people, but this restoration remains incomplete until Christ. Ezra, Nehemiah, and the post-exilic prophets all express dissatisfaction with the partial nature of the return. The greater exodus—from sin's bondage—and the greater temple—the body of Christ—await the true Anointed One whom Cyrus foreshadowed.
