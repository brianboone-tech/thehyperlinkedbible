### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 6|Ephesians 3:6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4862|G4862]] σύν (syn) - with, together (prefix indicating union)
- [[Lexicon/G2818|G2818]] κληρονόμος (kleronomos) - heir, inheritor
- [[Lexicon/G4954|G4954]] σύσσωμος (syssoma) - belonging to the same body
- [[Lexicon/G4830|G4830]] συμμέτοχος (symmetochos) - partaker together, co-participant
- [[Lexicon/G1860|G1860]] ἐπαγγελία (epangelia) - promise
- [[Lexicon/G1484|G1484]] ἔθνος (ethnos) - nation, Gentiles (plural ἔθνη, ethne)

**Context**:
Ephesians 3:1-13 explains the "mystery" hidden for ages but now revealed: Gentiles are full participants in the body of Christ. Paul describes himself as prisoner of Christ Jesus "on behalf of you Gentiles" (v. 1), having received grace to preach "the unsearchable riches of Christ" to the Gentiles (v. 8). Verse 6 is the crux: three compound words beginning with σύν (syn, "together with") emphasize Gentile equality with Jewish believers—fellow heirs, fellow members, fellow partakers.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] promised all families blessed in Abraham.
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 56#Isaiah 56 . 6|Isaiah 56:6-8]] welcomed foreigners who hold fast God's covenant, promising they would be brought to God's holy mountain and made joyful in his house of prayer "for all peoples."
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 11|Zechariah 2:11]]: "And many nations shall join themselves to the LORD in that day, and shall be my people."
- The OT anticipated Gentile inclusion but did not fully reveal the mechanism or extent. Paul calls this the "mystery"—not that Gentiles would be blessed (that was revealed) but that Gentiles would be equal members of the same body as Jews, with no distinction.

**Type Classification**:
- **Forward-Looking Type**: The OT texts anticipated Gentile blessing but did not fully disclose the equality Paul reveals. The promises were expectational, but the full reality remained veiled.
- **Providential Type**: God sovereignly planned from eternity to create one multinational body in Christ (Ephesians 1:4-5, 9-10), orchestrating history to accomplish and reveal it.

**Christological Connection**:
[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14-16]] explains how Christ accomplished Gentile inclusion: "For he himself is our peace, who has made us both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility by abolishing the law of commandments expressed in ordinances, that he might create in himself **one new man** in place of the two, so making peace, and might reconcile us both to God in one body through the cross, thereby killing the hostility." The cross is the mechanism. Christ's death:
1. Broke down the barrier (ceremonial law separating Jew and Gentile).
2. Created "one new man" (neither Jew nor Gentile as separate entities, but a new unified humanity in Christ).
3. Reconciled both to God in one body.

The trajectory: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] promises blessing to all families → Prophets anticipate nations worshiping at Zion → Christ dies and rises, breaking down the wall → Gospel proclaimed to Gentiles → Gentiles believe and are incorporated as equal members → Church becomes "fellow heirs, fellow body members, fellow partakers" → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]] consummates (great multitude from every nation). What was mystery is now manifest: "Christ in you, the hope of glory" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 27|Colossians 1:27]]) applies to Gentiles as much as to Jews. The συγ- (syn-) prefix words in [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 6|Ephesians 3:6]] are the linguistic embodiment of Christ's prayer in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 21|John 17:21]]: "that they may all be one, just as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be in us." The multinational Church is the visible demonstration of the mystery, the proof that God's promise to Abraham is fulfilled, and the preview of the eternal multinational multitude worshiping the Lamb.
