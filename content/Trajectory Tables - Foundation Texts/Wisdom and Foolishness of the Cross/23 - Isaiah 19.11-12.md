### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 11|Isaiah 19:11-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2450|H2450]] חָכָם (chakam) - wise, wise man
- [[Lexicon/H2451|H2451]] חָכְמָה (chokmah) - wisdom
- [[Lexicon/H191|H191]] אֱוִיל (ewil) - fool, foolish
- [[Lexicon/H3289|H3289]] יָעַץ (ya'ats) - to counsel, advise

**Context**:
Isaiah 19 is an oracle against Egypt, declaring judgment on the nation renowned for its wisdom. Egypt's "wise counselors" and Pharaoh's advisors—historically famous throughout the ancient world—are shown to give "senseless advice." Isaiah taunts: "Where are your wise men now? Let them tell you and reveal what the LORD of Hosts has planned against Egypt." This prophetic word exposes the futility of worldly wisdom when confronted with God's purposes.

- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 30|1 Kings 4:30]]: Solomon's wisdom "surpassed the wisdom of all the men of the east and all the wisdom of Egypt"

- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 25|Isaiah 44:25]]: God "makes diviners look foolish and turns the knowledge of the wise into nonsense"

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking Direct (contains expectational language pointing to God's pattern of confounding the wise)

**Christological Connection**:
[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 11|Isaiah 19:11-12]] establishes the pattern Paul applies to the cross. Just as Egypt's renowned wisdom proved foolish before God's judgment, so the "wisdom of this age" proves foolish before the cross ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 20|1 Corinthians 1:20]]). The wise counselors who could not discern God's plan for Egypt foreshadow the "rulers of this age" who "did not understand" God's hidden wisdom—"for if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 2#1 Corinthians 2 . 8|1 Corinthians 2:8]]). The cross is the ultimate demonstration that God makes foolish the wisdom of the world.
