### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 12|Numbers 19:12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2401-2500#H2398|חָטָא]] (*ḥāṭāʾ*) - "to purify oneself, make atonement"
- [[Lexicon/H3001-3100#H3117|יוֹם]] (*yôm*) - "day"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7992|שְׁלִישִׁי]] (*šəlîšî*) - "third"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7637|שְׁבִיעִי]] (*šəbîʿî*) - "seventh"
- [[Lexicon/H2901-3000#H2891|טָהֵר]] (*ṭāhêr*) - "to be clean, be pure"

**Context**: The cleansing from corpse-defilement required sprinkling with purification water on two specific days: the third day and the seventh day. Only after both sprinklings would the defiled person be clean. This dual sprinkling, especially the third-day requirement, prefigures Christ's third-day resurrection as the means of purification from death's defilement.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 1|Numbers 19:1-10]] (red heifer ritual), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 18|Numbers 19:18-19]] (sprinkling procedure)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 6#Hosea 6 . 2|Hosea 6:2]] (on third day he will raise us up), [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 4|Genesis 22:4]] (on third day Abraham saw the place)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 4|1 Corinthians 15:4]] (raised on third day according to Scriptures), [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 7|Luke 24:7]] (third day rise), [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 46|Luke 24:46]] (Christ should suffer and on third day rise)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 12|Numbers 19:12]]'s requirement for third-day sprinkling typologically prefigures Christ's third-day resurrection as God's provision for purification from death. Paul declares, "he was raised on the third day in accordance with the Scriptures" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 4|1 Corinthians 15:4]]). While no single Old Testament verse explicitly predicts third-day resurrection, the pattern appears throughout Scripture—Abraham on the third day sees Moriah ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 4|Genesis 22:4]]), Israel on the third day encounters God at Sinai ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 16|Exodus 19:16]]), Jonah on the third day emerges from the fish ([[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 1#Jonah 1 . 17|Jonah 1:17]]). Numbers 19's third-day sprinkling for purification from death-defilement adds to this pattern. Christ's resurrection on the third day fulfills what the typology foreshadowed—victory over death, purification from death's defilement, new life breaking through. Where the third-day sprinkling began the cleansing process, Christ's third-day resurrection accomplished death's defeat. The red heifer's water cleansed ceremonially; Christ's resurrection cleanses spiritually and eternally. Believers share in this third-day victory: "you were also raised with him through faith in the powerful working of God, who raised him from the dead" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 12|Colossians 2:12]]). The pattern continues—Christ's third-day resurrection (past), believers' present newness of life, and ultimate bodily resurrection (future) when "the last enemy... death" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 26|1 Corinthians 15:26]]) is finally destroyed.
