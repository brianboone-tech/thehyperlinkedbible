### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- καινὸν (*kainon*) - "new" - qualitatively new, not merely chronologically new (21:1, 5)
- σκηνὴ τοῦ θεοῦ (*skēnē tou theou*) - "tabernacle of God" - dwelling place, tent (21:3)
- σκηνώσει (*skēnōsei*) - "will dwell/tabernacle" - take up residence (21:3)
- ἐξαλείψει (*exaleipsei*) - "will wipe away" - completely remove (21:4)
- πρῶτα (*prōta*) - "former things" - old order passed away (21:4)

**Context**: Climax of Revelation; follows final judgment (20:11-15); introduces New Jerusalem descending (21:2, 9-22:5). Part of "all things new" vision (21:5). Brackets entire Scripture: Genesis 1-2 (original creation) → Revelation 21-22 (new creation).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]] - In the beginning God created heavens and earth
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-14]] - Eden with river of life
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]] - expelled from garden
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]] - He will swallow up death forever; wipe away tears
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] - I create new heavens and new earth
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - my dwelling place shall be with them
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18-20]] - Great Commission to all nations
  - [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 13|2 Peter 3:13]] - we are waiting for new heavens and new earth
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] - ransomed from every tribe, language, people, nation
**Christological Connection**: Christ accomplishes new creation through death/resurrection and consummates it at second coming. **Ultimate escalation of Garden Commission**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] commanded Adam to "fill the earth" with God's presence by multiplying image-bearers; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-4]] shows earth **filled** with God's presence eternally. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18-20]] (Great Commission: "make disciples of all nations") finds fulfillment in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] ("ransomed people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation") and 7:9 ("a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages"). **Trajectory complete**: From localized Eden → to frustrated commission (Fall) → to renewed commission (Abraham) → to Israel's land as Eden-prototype → to prophetic vision ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 9|Isaiah 11:9]]: "earth shall be full of knowledge of LORD") → to Christ inaugurating kingdom → to Great Commission advancing kingdom → to new creation where commission is **fulfilled**. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]: "the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them" - no longer localized to garden or temple but **cosmic dwelling**. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]] realized: "the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea." All curse removed ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]], reversing [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]]). Servants worship and "reign forever and ever" (22:5) - Adamic dominion restored and escalated. From Adam commissioned to extend small garden → to glorified multitude filling entire new creation with worship and dominion. Sacred space no longer expands **into** secular space; all reality **is** sacred space because "the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb" are its temple (21:22). Garden Commission consummated eternally.
