### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 6|Isaiah 11:6-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- זְאֵב (*ze'ev*) - "wolf" - predator reconciled with prey (11:6)
- נָמֵר (*namer*) - "leopard" - carnivore dwelling with domestic animals (11:6)
- הַר־קָדְשִׁי (*har-qodshi*) - "my holy mountain" - Zion as center of renewed creation (11:9)
- דֵּעָה אֶת־יְהוָה (*de'ah et-YHWH*) - "knowledge of the LORD" - intimate covenantal knowledge filling earth (11:9)
- נֵס (*nes*) - "signal, banner" - Messiah as rallying point for nations (11:10)

**Context**: Part of messianic prophecy beginning 11:1 ("shoot from stump of Jesse"). Follows judgment oracles against Assyria (10:5-34); precedes praise for salvation (12:1-6). Branch from Jesse will judge righteously (11:1-5), then restore creation to Edenic peace (11:6-9), gathering nations (11:10-16).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28-30]] - animals originally herbivorous, harmony in creation
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - enmity between seed of woman and serpent
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 2|Isaiah 2:2-4]] - nations flowing to mountain of LORD
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17-25]] - new heavens/earth, wolf/lamb together
  - [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]] - earth filled with knowledge of LORD's glory
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 19|Romans 8:19-22]] - creation's liberation from bondage
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 5|Revelation 5:5]] - Lion of Judah, Root of David
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-4]] - new creation, no more death
**Christological Connection**: Christ is Branch from Jesse who brings cosmic Eden-restoration. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] commissioned Adam to "fill earth" with God's presence; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 9|Isaiah 11:9]] prophesies earth **filled** "with knowledge of the LORD" - commission fulfilled through Messiah. Where Adam's sin introduced enmity ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]: serpent vs. seed; [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]]: humans vs. creation), Christ's redemption removes all enmity. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 10|Ephesians 1:10]]: God's plan is "to unite all things in [Christ], things in heaven and things on earth" - cosmic reconciliation. [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 20|Colossians 1:20]]: "through him to reconcile to himself all things, whether on earth or in heaven, making peace by the blood of his cross." **Literal or symbolic?** Both/and. Isaiah's imagery depicts **real cosmic renewal** ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21]]: creation liberated from bondage) using **evocative symbolism** (animals representing nations, [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 12|Psalm 22:12-13]]). Christ's first coming inaugurated kingdom ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 28|Matthew 12:28]]: "the kingdom of God has come upon you"); second coming consummates it. Revelation 21-22 shows fulfillment: new creation without curse (22:3), river of life (22:1), tree of life for healing nations (22:2). From localized Eden → to prophetic vision of global Eden → to Christ establishing kingdom → to new creation where "earth shall be full of knowledge of LORD" eternally. Garden Commission consummated.
