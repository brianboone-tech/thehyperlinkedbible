### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18-20]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἐδόθη (*edothē*) - "has been given" - divine passive indicating God's action
- ἐξουσία (*exousia*) - "authority" - right to rule, sovereign power
- μαθητεύσατε (*mathēteusate*) - "make disciples" - create followers/learners (aorist imperative)
- πάντα τὰ ἔθνη (*panta ta ethnē*) - "all nations" - universal scope, not limited to Israel
- συντελείας τοῦ αἰῶνος (*synteleias tou aiōnos*) - "end of the age" - consummation of present evil age

**Context**: Final words of risen Christ before ascension. Follows resurrection (28:1-10), appearance to eleven disciples in Galilee (28:16-17). Matthew's Gospel climaxes with this commission, framing entire narrative: begins with "Jesus Christ, son of David, son of Abraham" (1:1 - Jewish particularity); ends with "all nations" (28:19 - Gentile universality).

**Christological Connection**: Christ as last Adam commissions new humanity to fulfill original Eden-expansion mandate. **Escalation at every level**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] gave limited authority to Adam; [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18]] declares Christ has **all** authority in heaven and earth - unrestricted sovereignty. Adam was to fill earth biologically; Christ commands filling earth **spiritually** through disciple-making. Adam's commission was frustrated by Fall (Genesis 3); Christ's commission succeeds because He conquered sin/death through resurrection. Where Adam was expelled from Eden ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]]), Christ sends disciples out from Jerusalem ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]) to expand sacred space globally. **Profound difference**: Adam's commission was **implicit** (fill earth by multiplying); Great Commission is **explicit** (make disciples of all nations). Adam multiplied human image-bearers; Christ multiplies **renewed** image-bearers being "transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]). The trajectory shows Eden-expansion fulfilled through gospel: from Adamic physical multiplication → to Abrahamic promise of blessing to nations → to prophetic vision of earth filled with God's knowledge ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 9|Isaiah 11:9]]; [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]) → to Christ commissioning disciples → to Pentecost empowerment (Acts 2) → to global missions → to consummation when "the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea" ([[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]). [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]] previews result: "a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne" - Garden Commission fulfilled as redeemed humanity from all nations fills new creation with worship.
