### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 17|Matthew 4:17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- μετανοεῖτε (*metanoeite*) - "repent" - change of mind and heart, turning to God
- ἤγγικεν (*ēngiken*) - "has come near" - perfect tense indicating arrival with continuing presence
- βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν (*basileia tōn ouranōn*) - "kingdom of heaven" - God's reign breaking into world

**Context**: Transitional verse following Jesus's temptation (4:1-11) and beginning Galilean ministry. Echoes John Baptist's message (3:2: identical wording). Precedes calling disciples (4:18-22) and summary of ministry: teaching, proclaiming, healing (4:23-25).

  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 7|Isaiah 52:7]] - beautiful feet bringing good news

**Christological Connection**: Christ's ministry is **Eden-expansion** through kingdom proclamation. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] commissioned Adam to extend Eden's sacred space by filling earth; Christ announces God's kingdom (sacred space par excellence) is now **invading** fallen world. Where Adam failed to maintain Eden, Christ **establishes** kingdom that cannot be shaken ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 28|Hebrews 12:28]]). Jesus's healings and exorcisms demonstrate kingdom power reversing curse: blind see, lame walk, lepers cleansed, deaf hear, dead raised ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 5|Matthew 11:5]]) - creation being restored to Edenic order. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 9|Isaiah 11:9]] prophesied earth filled "with knowledge of LORD as waters cover sea"; Jesus's ministry begins this filling as kingdom expands from Galilee to Judea to Samaria to ends of earth ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]). **Kingdom = renewed Eden**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 19#Matthew 19 . 28|Matthew 19:28]] speaks of "new world" (παλιγγενεσία *palingenesia*, regeneration) when Son of Man sits on throne. Kingdom parables (Matthew 13) show kingdom growing from small beginnings (mustard seed, 13:31-32; leaven, 13:33) to fill whole earth - organic Eden-expansion. From localized garden → to kingdom breaking into world → to global kingdom filling cosmos. Christ's first coming inaugurates; Great Commission advances ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18-20]]: "make disciples of all nations"); second coming consummates when "the kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ, and he shall reign forever and ever" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 15|Revelation 11:15]]). Garden Commission fulfilled as kingdom fills new creation.
