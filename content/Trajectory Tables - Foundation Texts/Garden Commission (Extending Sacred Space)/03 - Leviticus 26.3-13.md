### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 3|Leviticus 26:3-13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- חֻקּוֹתַי (*chuqqotai*) - "my statutes" - covenant stipulations (26:3)
- פָּרָה וְרָבָה (*parah veravah*) - "be fruitful and multiply" - Genesis 1:28 language (26:9)
- הִתְהַלַּכְתִּי (*hithallakhti*) - "I will walk" - God's presence among them (26:12; cf. Genesis 3:8)
- עֹל (*ol*) - "yoke" - bars of bondage broken (26:13)
- קוֹמְמִיּוּת (*qomemiyyut*) - "upright, erect" - walking tall in freedom (26:13)

**Context**: Conclusion of Holiness Code (Leviticus 17-26). Chapter 26 presents covenant blessings (26:3-13) and curses (26:14-46), mirroring Deuteronomy 28. Follows Sabbath/Jubilee laws (25) and regulations for holiness (17-24). Sets choice before Israel: obedience → blessing; disobedience → curse.

**Christological Connection**: Christ fulfills covenant blessings perfectly, establishing ultimate Eden-land. Where Israel failed to maintain obedience and was exiled, Christ succeeds through perfect obedience. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 9|Leviticus 26:9]] - "I will make you fruitful and multiply you" → [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]] - "make disciples of all nations" (spiritual multiplication surpassing physical). [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 12|Leviticus 26:12]] - "I will walk among you" → [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word became flesh and dwelt among us (God not merely walking in land but incarnate in Person). [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 6|Leviticus 26:6]] - peace in land → [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14]] - Christ "is our peace" who reconciles Jew and Gentile. [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] - tabernacle presence → [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] - Christ's body is temple. Most profound: Leviticus blessings limited to **Israel's land**; Christ's blessings extend to **whole earth**. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|Romans 4:13]]: promise to Abraham "that he would be heir of the world." [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] consummates [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 12|Leviticus 26:12]]: "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God" - no longer localized to promised land but filling new creation. From Israel's land as Eden-prototype → to Christ establishing kingdom → to new heaven/earth as cosmic Eden where redeemed humanity walks with God in unbroken fellowship forever. Garden Commission to "fill the earth" fulfilled when God's glory-presence fills all reality.
