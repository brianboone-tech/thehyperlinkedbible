### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- לֶךְ־לְךָ (*lekh-lekha*) - "go for yourself" - emphatic call to leave homeland (12:1)
- בָּרַךְ (*barakh*) - "bless" - appears five times (12:2-3), creating blessing crescendo
- גּוֹי גָּדוֹל (*goy gadol*) - "great nation" - numerous descendants (12:2)
- בְּרָכָה (*berakhah*) - "blessing" - Abram himself becomes source of blessing (12:2)
- מִשְׁפָּחוֹת הָאֲדָמָה (*mishpachot ha'adamah*) - "families of the earth" - universal scope (12:3)

**Context**: Follows primeval history (Genesis 1-11): creation, Fall, flood, Babel dispersion. Marks transition from universal scope to particular family (Abraham) serving universal purpose. Abram introduced briefly (11:27-32); now receives divine call at age 75 (12:4).

**Christological Connection**: Christ fulfills Abrahamic covenant as promised "seed" through whom all nations are blessed. **Escalation**: Abraham's physical descendants (Israel) were to be light to nations ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 6|Isaiah 49:6]]), but largely failed. Christ, true Israel, succeeds - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]]: "make disciples of all nations." Where Abraham's blessing was biological fertility and land inheritance, Christ's blessing is spiritual regeneration and cosmic inheritance ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|Romans 4:13]]: "the promise to Abraham and his offspring that he would be heir of the world"). [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 8|Galatians 3:8]]: "the Scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, 'In you shall all the nations be blessed'" - Abrahamic covenant **is** gospel in seed form. **Ultimate escalation**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] promises blessing to "families of earth" (אֲדָמָה *adamah*); [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9-10]] shows fulfillment: Christ "ransomed people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation, and you have made them a kingdom and priests to our God." From one man called to leave homeland → to one Man (Christ) gathering nations → to multinational, glorified humanity filling new creation. Eden-expansion commission, frustrated at Fall and Babel, renewed through Abraham, fulfilled in Christ, consummated when "the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea" ([[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]).
