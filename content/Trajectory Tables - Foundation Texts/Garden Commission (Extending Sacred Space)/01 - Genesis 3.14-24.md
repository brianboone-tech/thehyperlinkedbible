### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 14|Genesis 3:14-24]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- אָרוּר (*arur*) - "cursed" - covenant curse formula (3:14, 17)
- קוֹץ וְדַרְדַּר (*qots vedardar*) - "thorns and thistles" - anti-Eden vegetation (3:18)
- עִצָּבוֹן (*itsavon*) - "pain, toil" - laborious sorrow (3:16 - childbearing; 3:17 - ground cultivation)
- כְּרוּבִים (*keruvim*) - "cherubim" - angelic guardians blocking Eden access (3:24)
- לַהַט הַחֶרֶב (*lahat hacherev*) - "flaming sword" - turning every direction, guarding tree of life (3:24)

**Context**: Divine judgments following interrogation (3:9-13). Serpent cursed first (3:14-15), then woman (3:16), then man and ground (3:17-19). Concludes with expulsion from garden (3:22-24). Structured as covenant lawsuit - accusation, judgment, sentence, execution.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - commission to fill earth, subdue, have dominion
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|Genesis 2:15]] - placed in Eden to work and keep it
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 11|Genesis 4:11-12]] - Cain further cursed from ground
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 29|Genesis 5:29]] - Lamech hopes Noah will bring relief from curse
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 13|Isaiah 55:13]] - instead of thorn, cypress; instead of brier, myrtle
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 20|Romans 8:20-22]] - creation subjected to futility, groaning
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]] - Christ became curse for us
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 8|Hebrews 6:8]] - land bearing thorns is worthless
**Christological Connection**: Christ reverses every aspect of Genesis 3 curse. **Escalation through reversal**: Serpent cursed to eat dust → Christ crushes serpent's head ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 16#Romans 16 . 20|Romans 16:20]]). Woman's painful childbearing → woman's seed (Christ) brings salvation ([[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 15|1 Timothy 2:15]]; [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]]). Ground cursed to produce thorns → Christ wears crown of thorns, bearing curse ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 29|Matthew 27:29]]; [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]]). Toilsome work "by sweat of face" → Christ's sweat like drops of blood in Gethsemane ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 44|Luke 22:44]]). Expulsion from Eden, cherubim guarding access → Christ tears temple veil, opens way to God ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 51|Matthew 27:51]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-20]]). Commission to fill earth frustrated → Great Commission renews Eden-expansion through gospel ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18-20]]). The trajectory shows curse as temporary obstacle, not permanent defeat. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 19|Romans 8:19-22]]: creation groans, awaiting redemption. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]: "No longer will there be anything accursed" - Genesis 3 curse finally removed in new creation. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 13|Isaiah 55:13]] prophesies: "Instead of the thorn shall come up the cypress; instead of the brier shall come up the myrtle" - Eden abundance restored. From frustrated commission → to curse-bearing Messiah → to renewed commission → to curse-free new creation where "earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea" ([[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]).
