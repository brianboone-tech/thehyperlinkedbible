### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3595|H3595]] כִּיּוֹר (*kiyyor*) - laver, basin
- [[Lexicon/H5178|H5178]] נְחֹשֶׁת (*nechoshet*) - bronze, copper
- [[Lexicon/H7364|H7364]] רָחַץ (*rachats*) - to wash, bathe
- [[Lexicon/H3027|H3027]] יָד (*yad*) - hand
- [[Lexicon/H7272|H7272]] רֶגֶל (*regel*) - foot
- [[Lexicon/H4191|H4191]] מוּת (*mut*) - to die

**Context**: Exodus 30:17-21: "The LORD said to Moses, 'You shall also make a laver (כִּיּוֹר) of bronze, with its stand of bronze, for washing. You shall put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and you shall put water in it, with which Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet... lest they die (וְלֹא יָמֻתוּ).'"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- First mention of the laver in tabernacle instructions
- Position: between altar and tent - after sacrifice, before service
- Death penalty for neglect shows approaching God unclean is fatal

**Connections**:
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 38#Exodus 38 . 8|Exodus 38:8]] - Laver made from mirrors
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 23|1 Kings 7:23-26]] - Solomon's bronze sea
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 13#John 13 . 5|John 13:5-10]] - Jesus washes disciples' feet
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]] - Washing of regeneration

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded institution)

**Christological Connection**: The laver required priests to be cleansed before service. Christ cleanses believers "once for all" (Heb 10:10) enabling us to draw near with "bodies washed with pure water" (Heb 10:22). The death penalty for neglect shows that unclean approach to God is fatal - a reality that makes Christ's cleansing work essential for our access to God.
