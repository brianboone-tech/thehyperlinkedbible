### [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4726|H4726]] מָקוֹר (*maqor*) - fountain, spring, source
- [[Lexicon/H6605|H6605]] פָּתַח (*pathach*) - to open
- [[Lexicon/H2403|H2403]] חַטָּאת (*chatta'th*) - sin, sin offering
- [[Lexicon/H5079|H5079]] נִדָּה (*niddah*) - impurity, uncleanness

**Context**: Zechariah 13:1: "On that day there shall be a fountain (מָקוֹר) opened for the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin (לְחַטַּאת) and uncleanness (וּלְנִדָּה)."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "On that day" = eschatological formula
- "Fountain opened" = permanent, flowing source
- Cleanses both "sin" (moral guilt) and "uncleanness" (ritual impurity)
- For both royalty (David) and commoners (Jerusalem)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Laver for priestly cleansing
- **TO**: Leviticus 15 - Laws of ritual uncleanness
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]] - "I will sprinkle clean water"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] - Blood and water from Christ's side
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 7|1 John 1:7]] - Blood cleanses from all sin

**Type Classification**: Prophetic Anticipation - OT type projected forward to eschatological fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: The "fountain opened" finds fulfillment in Christ's death. When the soldier pierced Jesus' side, "blood and water came out" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]). The opened fountain cleanses from both sin (blood/guilt) and uncleanness (water/defilement). What the laver provided for priests, the cross provides for all - a permanently opened source of cleansing for "the house of David" (messianic line) and all inhabitants of the New Jerusalem.
