### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4215|G4215]] ποταμός (*potamos*) - river
- [[Lexicon/G5204|G5204]] ὕδωρ (*hydor*) - water
- [[Lexicon/G2222|G2222]] ζωή (*zoe*) - life
- [[Lexicon/G2929|G2929]] κρυσταλλίζω (*krystallizo*) - to be clear as crystal
- [[Lexicon/G3586|G3586]] ξύλον (*xylon*) - tree, wood
- [[Lexicon/G2322|G2322]] θεραπεία (*therapeia*) - healing

**Context**: Revelation 22:1-2: "Then the angel showed me the river of the water of life (ποταμὸν ὕδατος ζωῆς), bright as crystal (λαμπρὸν ὡς κρύσταλλον), flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb... also, on either side of the river, the tree of life... and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Laver (finite basin) → River (infinite flow)
- Temple's sea → New Jerusalem's river
- Connects to [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] (river from temple)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Laver for cleansing
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 23|1 Kings 7:23-26]] - Solomon's bronze sea
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] - River from temple bringing life
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]] - Fountain opened
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - Rivers of living water (Spirit)

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Laver's function fulfilled in eternal river

**Christological Connection**: The bronze laver was a basin of limited water for repeated cleansing. The river of life flows eternally from "the throne of God and of the Lamb" - Christ as its inexhaustible source. "Bright as crystal" suggests complete purity - no need for cleansing because sin is gone. The healing leaves echo Ezekiel 47's vision. What the laver began (cleansing for service), the river completes (eternal life in God's presence). The Lamb on the throne is both the source and goal of all cleansing.
