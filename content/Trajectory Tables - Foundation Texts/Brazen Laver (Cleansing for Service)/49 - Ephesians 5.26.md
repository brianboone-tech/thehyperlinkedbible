### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G37|G37]] ἁγιάζω (*hagiazo*) - to sanctify, make holy
- [[Lexicon/G2511|G2511]] καθαρίζω (*katharizo*) - to cleanse, purify
- [[Lexicon/G3067|G3067]] λουτρόν (*loutron*) - washing, bath
- [[Lexicon/G5204|G5204]] ὕδωρ (*hydor*) - water
- [[Lexicon/G4487|G4487]] ῥῆμα (*rhema*) - word, saying

**Context**: Ephesians 5:25-26: "Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her, that he might sanctify her, having cleansed her (καθαρίσας) by the washing of water with the word (τῷ λουτρῷ τοῦ ὕδατος ἐν ῥήματι)."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Priests washed themselves at laver
- Christ washes His bride - superior agency
- Water + word unite in cleansing

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Priestly self-washing
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 38#Exodus 38 . 8|Exodus 38:8]] - Laver from mirrors (self-reflection)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 3|John 15:3]] - "You are already clean because of the word"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 23|James 1:23-25]] - Word as mirror

**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - Christ's washing surpasses priestly self-washing

**Christological Connection**: The laver required priests to wash themselves; Christ washes His bride. This is superior agency - not self-cleansing but cleansing by the Bridegroom. The union of "water" and "word" (ἐν ῥήματι) connects to the mirrors becoming laver (Exod 38:8) - the word (like a mirror, [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 23|James 1:23]]) reveals our need; the water (Spirit's application of Christ's work) cleanses. Christ's love initiates, His death accomplishes, and His word applies the cleansing.
