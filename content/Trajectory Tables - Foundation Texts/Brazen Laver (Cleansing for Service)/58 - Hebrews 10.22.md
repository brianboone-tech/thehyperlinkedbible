### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 22|Hebrews 10:22]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4334|G4334]] προσέρχομαι (*proserchomai*) - to approach, draw near
- [[Lexicon/G4472|G4472]] ῥαντίζω (*rhantizo*) - to sprinkle
- [[Lexicon/G2588|G2588]] καρδία (*kardia*) - heart
- [[Lexicon/G4893|G4893]] συνείδησις (*syneidesis*) - conscience
- [[Lexicon/G3068|G3068]] λούω (*louo*) - to wash, bathe
- [[Lexicon/G4983|G4983]] σῶμα (*soma*) - body

**Context**: Hebrews 10:22: "Let us draw near (προσερχώμεθα) with a true heart in full assurance of faith, with our hearts sprinkled clean (ῥεραντισμένοι τὰς καρδίας) from an evil conscience and our bodies washed (λελουσμένοι τὸ σῶμα) with pure water."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Priests washed hands and feet (external)
- Believers have hearts sprinkled and bodies washed (internal + external)
- Result: bold approach to God

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Laver washing before approach
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 4|Leviticus 16:4]] - High priest bathes before Day of Atonement
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 18|Numbers 19:18-19]] - Sprinkling for cleansing
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 13|Hebrews 9:13-14]] - Blood of Christ cleanses conscience

**Type Classification**: NT Application - Laver's function (enabling approach) now universally available

**Christological Connection**: The laver enabled priests to approach God safely. Hebrews applies this universally: all believers may "draw near" because of twofold cleansing - hearts (internal/conscience) and bodies (external/behavior). Christ's blood cleanses the conscience (Heb 9:14); baptism signifies bodily cleansing. What required priests to wash repeatedly at the laver, Christ accomplishes definitively. The result: confident, bold approach to the throne of grace.
