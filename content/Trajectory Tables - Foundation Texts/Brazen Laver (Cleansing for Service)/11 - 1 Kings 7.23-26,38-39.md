### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 23|1 Kings 7:23-26, 38-39]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3220|H3220]] יָם (*yam*) - sea
- [[Lexicon/H5178|H5178]] נְחֹשֶׁת (*nechoshet*) - bronze
- [[Lexicon/H1324|H1324]] בַּת (*bath*) - bath (liquid measure, ~6 gallons)
- [[Lexicon/H3595|H3595]] כִּיּוֹר (*kiyyor*) - laver, basin
- [[Lexicon/H4350|H4350]] מְכוֹנָה (*mekhonah*) - stand, base

**Context**: 1 Kings 7:23-26: "Then he made the sea of cast metal... It held two thousand baths (אַלְפַּיִם בַּת)." Verses 38-39: "He made ten basins of bronze... And he set the basins, five on the south side of the house, and five on the north side of the house."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Tabernacle's single laver becomes massive "sea" + ten basins
- "Sea" = cosmic symbolism (primordial waters subdued)
- Scale increase reflects temple's greater glory

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Original laver
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 38#Exodus 38 . 8|Exodus 38:8]] - Tabernacle laver construction
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 4#2 Chronicles 4 . 2|2 Chronicles 4:2-6]] - Parallel account; sea for priests, basins for offerings
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 4#Revelation 4 . 6|Revelation 4:6]] - Sea of glass before throne

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Temple furnishing with escalated significance

**Christological Connection**: Solomon's massive bronze sea (2,000 baths) vastly exceeds the tabernacle's single laver. This escalation points to Christ's inexhaustible cleansing. The laver was finite; the river of life in Revelation 22 is eternal. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 4#Revelation 4 . 6|Revelation 4:6]]'s "sea of glass" before God's throne may echo Solomon's sea - in the consummated temple, the sea is crystal clear because cleansing is complete.
