### [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]]; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 5|John 3:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3067|G3067]] λουτρόν (*loutron*) - washing, bath
- [[Lexicon/G3824|G3824]] παλιγγενεσία (*palingenesia*) - regeneration, new birth
- [[Lexicon/G342|G342]] ἀνακαίνωσις (*anakainosis*) - renewal
- [[Lexicon/G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα (*pneuma*) - spirit
- [[Lexicon/G5204|G5204]] ὕδωρ (*hydor*) - water
- [[Lexicon/G1080|G1080]] γεννάω (*gennao*) - to beget, give birth

**Context**: Titus 3:5: "He saved us... by the washing of regeneration (λουτροῦ παλιγγενεσίας) and renewal of the Holy Spirit (ἀνακαινώσεως πνεύματος ἁγίου)."

John 3:5: "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit (ἐξ ὕδατος καὶ πνεύματος), he cannot enter the kingdom of God."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] (water + Spirit + new heart)
- "Regeneration" (παλιγγενεσία) = cosmic renewal term
- Water and Spirit inseparable in new birth

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Laver for priestly cleansing
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] - Clean water + new Spirit promised
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]] - Washing of water with the word
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 21|1 Peter 3:21]] - Baptism now saves

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Laver's physical washing fulfilled in spiritual regeneration

**Christological Connection**: The laver provided external cleansing for service; regeneration provides internal transformation for kingdom entrance. "Washing of regeneration" (Titus) and "born of water and Spirit" (John) fulfill what Ezekiel promised. Christ accomplishes this through His atoning death and gift of the Spirit. What priests did daily at the laver, believers receive once in regeneration - with ongoing renewal by the Spirit.
