### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2236|H2236]] זָרַק (*zaraq*) - to sprinkle
- [[Lexicon/H4325|H4325]] מַיִם (*mayim*) - water
- [[Lexicon/H2889|H2889]] טָהוֹר (*tahor*) - clean, pure
- [[Lexicon/H2319|H2319]] חָדָשׁ (*chadash*) - new
- [[Lexicon/H3820|H3820]] לֵב (*lev*) - heart
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (*ruach*) - spirit

**Context**: Ezekiel 36:25-27: "I will sprinkle (וְזָרַקְתִּי) clean water (מַיִם טְהוֹרִים) on you, and you shall be clean from all your uncleannesses... And I will give you a new heart (לֵב חָדָשׁ), and a new spirit (רוּחַ חֲדָשָׁה) I will put within you... And I will put my Spirit within you."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Divine initiative: "I will sprinkle" (not human washing)
- New heart + new spirit + God's Spirit = triple transformation
- Links cleansing to regeneration and indwelling

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Priestly self-washing
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 18|Numbers 19:18-19]] - Sprinkling with hyssop
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]] - New covenant; law on hearts
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 5|John 3:5]] - Born of water and Spirit
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]] - Washing of regeneration and renewal of Spirit

**Type Classification**: Prophetic Anticipation - OT cleansing rituals transcended by divine action

**Christological Connection**: What priests did at the laver, God promises to do directly: "I will sprinkle clean water on you." This divine cleansing is inseparable from new heart (regeneration) and Spirit indwelling. Christ accomplishes all three: His blood cleanses ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 7|1 John 1:7]]), His Spirit regenerates ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 5|John 3:5-6]]), and He sends the Spirit to indwell ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 17|John 14:17]]). The laver required human effort; Ezekiel promises divine initiative fulfilled in Christ.
