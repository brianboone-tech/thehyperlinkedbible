### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 38#Exodus 38 . 8|Exodus 38:8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3595|H3595]] כִּיּוֹר (*kiyyor*) - laver, basin
- [[Lexicon/H4759|H4759]] מַרְאָה (*mar'ah*) - mirror, vision
- [[Lexicon/H6633|H6633]] צָבָא (*tsaba*) - to serve, minister
- [[Lexicon/H6607|H6607]] פֶּתַח (*pethach*) - entrance, door
- [[Lexicon/H168|H168]] אֹהֶל (*ohel*) - tent

**Context**: Exodus 38:8: "He made the laver of bronze and its stand of bronze, from the mirrors (מַרְאֹת) of the ministering women who ministered (הַצֹּבְאֹת אֲשֶׁר צָבְאוּ) at the entrance of the tent of meeting."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Unique detail: source material from women's mirrors
- Women already "ministering" at tabernacle entrance
- Vanity instruments transformed into cleansing vessels

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 17|Exodus 30:17-21]] - Laver instructions
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 22|1 Samuel 2:22]] - Women ministering at tabernacle
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 23|James 1:23-25]] - Word as mirror that transforms

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - God sovereignly arranges detail with typological significance

**Christological Connection**: Mirrors that once revealed outward appearance are transformed into instruments of cleansing. This anticipates James's teaching that the word of God is like a mirror ([[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 23|James 1:23-25]]) - not for self-admiration but for transformation. Looking into Christ (the perfect mirror of God's glory - 2 Cor 3:18) leads to cleansing and transformation "from one degree of glory to another."
