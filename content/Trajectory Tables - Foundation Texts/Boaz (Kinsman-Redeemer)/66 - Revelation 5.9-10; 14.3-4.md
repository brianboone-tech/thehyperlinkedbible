**Context**: Revelation 5:9-10 records the heavenly song to the Lamb: "You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, because You were slain, and with Your blood You purchased (ἠγόρασας) people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation. You have made them to be a kingdom and priests to serve our God, and they will reign on the earth." Revelation 14:3-4 describes the 144,000: "They sang a new song before the throne...No one could learn the song except the 144,000 who had been redeemed (ἠγορασμένοι) from the earth. These are the ones who have not defiled themselves...They were purchased (ἠγοράσθησαν) from among mankind as firstfruits to God and to the Lamb."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The "purchase" (ἀγοράζω) language echoes goel redemption—acquiring what belongs to another by paying the price.
- "From every tribe and language and people and nation" universalizes what Boaz did for one Moabitess. The kinsman-redeemer's scope expands to all nations.
- "Kingdom and priests" (v. 10) fulfills [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]—what Israel was called to be, the redeemed from all nations become.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Leviticus 25:25, 47-49 (goel purchase), Exodus 19:6 (kingdom of priests), Isaiah 43:1 (I have redeemed you), Psalm 74:2 (the tribe You have redeemed)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 9|Ruth 4:9-10]] (Boaz's public declaration of purchase), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 49#Psalm 49 . 7|Psalm 49:7-9]] (no man can redeem another)
- **PARALLEL**: 1 Corinthians 6:20; 7:23 (you were bought with a price), 2 Peter 2:1 (the Master who bought them), Galatians 3:13 (Christ redeemed us)

**Type Classification**: Direct (eschatological fulfillment of redemption); Forward-Looking from OT perspective (goel pattern reaches cosmic scope).

**Christological Connection**: Revelation portrays Christ as the ultimate Kinsman-Redeemer. (1) **"You Were Slain"**: Boaz paid money; Christ paid with His life. The Lamb's slaughter is the redemption price. (2) **"With Your Blood You Purchased"**: ἠγόρασας—the marketplace purchase verb. Christ bought people for God. The transaction is complete. (3) **"From Every Tribe"**: Boaz redeemed one Moabitess; Christ redeems from every ethnic group. The kinsman's local act becomes the Lamb's universal achievement. (4) **"Kingdom and Priests"**: Boaz's redemption produced David's royal line; Christ's redemption produces a "kingdom and priests"—royal priesthood from all nations. (5) **"They Will Reign on Earth"**: Boaz's act secured Ruth's inheritance in the land; Christ's redemption secures believers' inheritance in the new earth. (6) **"Redeemed from the Earth"**: Complete reversal—those who were slaves become rulers; those who were lost become "firstfruits." The kinsman-redeemer trajectory reaches its eschatological consummation in the Lamb's eternal victory.
