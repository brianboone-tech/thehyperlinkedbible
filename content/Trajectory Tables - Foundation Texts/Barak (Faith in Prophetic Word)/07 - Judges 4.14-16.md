### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 4#Judges 4 . 14|Judges 4:14-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6965|H6965]] קוּם (qûm) - "arise" - Arise, for this is the day
- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (nāṯan) - "give/deliver" - the LORD has delivered Sisera
- [[Lexicon/H3318|H3318]] יָצָא (yāṣāʾ) - "go out" - Has not the LORD gone before you?
- [[Lexicon/H2000|H2000]] הָמַם (hāmam) - "rout/confuse" - the LORD routed Sisera
- [[Lexicon/H2719|H2719]] חֶרֶב (ḥereḇ) - "sword" - with the edge of the sword

**Context**: At the decisive moment, Deborah delivers the command: "Arise, for this is the day that the LORD has delivered Sisera into your hand. Has not the LORD gone before you?" Barak descends Mount Tabor with his 10,000 men against 900 iron chariots. The LORD routed Sisera's entire army—"not a single man was left." The prophetic word is completely fulfilled. Faith's obedience brought the promised victory.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The rhetorical question "Has not the LORD gone before you?" echoes [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 30|Deuteronomy 1:30]]; 20:4
- Divine routing of enemies is pattern ([[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 10#Joshua 10 . 10|Joshua 10:10]]; [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 7#1 Samuel 7 . 10|1 Samuel 7:10]])
- Total victory ("not a single man was left") shows complete divine action

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 20#Deuteronomy 20 . 4|Deuteronomy 20:4]] - The LORD goes before to fight
  - [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 10#Joshua 10 . 10|Joshua 10:10-11]] - LORD routs enemies with hailstones
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 7#1 Samuel 7 . 10|1 Samuel 7:10]] - LORD thunders against Philistines
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 68#Psalm 68 . 1|Psalm 68:1]] - God's enemies scattered
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 33|Hebrews 11:33]] - "through faith conquered kingdoms"
**Type Classification**: Providential (victory through faith in God's word), Forward-Looking (pattern of faith conquering through God's power)

**Christological Connection**: God going before His people to fight anticipates Christ's definitive victory over sin and death. As the LORD routed Sisera at the cross, Christ "disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, triumphing over them" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]]). Believers now follow the victorious Christ who has already conquered. Faith descends from Mount Tabor because the Lord has already determined the outcome.
