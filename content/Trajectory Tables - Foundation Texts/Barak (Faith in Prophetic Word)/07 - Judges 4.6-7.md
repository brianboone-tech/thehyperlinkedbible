### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 4#Judges 4 . 6|Judges 4:6-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6680|H6680]] צָוָה (ṣāwâ) - "command" - the LORD is commanding you
- [[Lexicon/H4900|H4900]] מָשַׁךְ (māšaḵ) - "draw out" - I will draw out Sisera
- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (nāṯan) - "give/deliver" - I will deliver him into your hand
- [[Lexicon/H5158|H5158]] נַחַל (naḥal) - "river/wadi" - the River Kishon
- [[Lexicon/H2022|H2022]] הַר (har) - "mountain" - Mount Tabor

**Context**: Deborah summons Barak and delivers God's explicit command. The word is unambiguous: "Go and march to Mount Tabor... And I will draw out Sisera... and I will deliver him into your hand." God takes responsibility for the battle's outcome—He will draw out the enemy, He will deliver. Barak's role is simply to go and trust the promise. The prophetic word provides both command and assurance.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Divine commands through prophets become standard pattern (Moses, Samuel, Elijah, etc.)
- The promise "I will deliver him into your hand" echoes God's promises to Moses ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 12|Exodus 3:12]]), Joshua ([[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 5|Joshua 1:5-9]]), and later David ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 23#1 Samuel 23 . 4|1 Samuel 23:4]])
- River Kishon becomes the site of divine judgment (cf. [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 18#1 Kings 18 . 40|1 Kings 18:40]])

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 12|Exodus 3:12]] - Pattern of divine commission and promise
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 20#Deuteronomy 20 . 1|Deuteronomy 20:1-4]] - Holy war promises
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 23#1 Samuel 23 . 4|1 Samuel 23:4]] - Similar "I will deliver" pattern
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 18#1 Kings 18 . 40|1 Kings 18:40]] - Kishon as judgment site
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 32|Hebrews 11:32]] - Barak's response to this word is counted as faith
**Type Classification**: Providential (prophetic word sovereignly given), Forward-Looking (sets pattern of faith responding to divine promise)

**Christological Connection**: God's prophetic word through Deborah anticipates the ultimate Word made flesh. As Barak was called to trust God's promise delivered through a prophet, believers are called to trust God's promise in Christ. The "I will deliver" of [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 4#Judges 4 . 7|Judges 4:7]] finds its ultimate fulfillment in Christ's definitive deliverance from sin, death, and the evil one. Faith's content is always God's revealed promise.
