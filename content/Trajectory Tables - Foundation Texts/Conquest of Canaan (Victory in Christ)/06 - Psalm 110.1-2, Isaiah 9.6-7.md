### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalms 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1-2]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H113|H113]] אָדוֹן (*'adon*) - lord, master
- [[Lexicon/H3427|H3427]] יָשַׁב (*yashab*) - sit (at right hand)
- [[Lexicon/H1869|H1869]] הֲדֹם (*hadom*) - footstool
- [[Lexicon/H4951|H4951]] מִשְׂרָה (*misrah*) - government, dominion
- [[Lexicon/H7965|H7965]] שָׁלוֹם (*shalom*) - peace

**Context**: Psalm 110:1-2 presents the LORD (YHWH) inviting David's Lord (Adonai) to sit at His right hand "until I make your enemies your footstool." Isaiah 9:6-7 prophesies a child whose names include "Mighty God" and whose government and peace will have no end upon David's throne.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Escalation from Joshua's earthly conquest to Messiah's cosmic victory
- "Enemies as footstool" = complete subjugation (ancient Near Eastern imagery)
- David's throne → eternal throne of Messiah

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's conquest - earthly prototype of divine victory
- **TO**: Davidic covenant (2 Sam 7:12-16) - eternal throne promised
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] - Son of Man given dominion
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 34|Acts 2:34-35]] - Peter applies Psalm 110 to risen Christ
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 13|Hebrews 1:13]] - Christ seated at God's right hand

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking Prophecy (explicit messianic promise)

**Christological Connection**: Psalm 110 is the most quoted OT text in the NT because it reveals the Messiah's divine status (sitting at YHWH's right hand) and guaranteed victory (enemies as footstool). Joshua conquered earthly Canaanites; Messiah conquers all enemies including sin, Satan, and death. "His government and peace will have no end" - the partial, temporary conquest of Canaan points to Christ's eternal, complete victory.
