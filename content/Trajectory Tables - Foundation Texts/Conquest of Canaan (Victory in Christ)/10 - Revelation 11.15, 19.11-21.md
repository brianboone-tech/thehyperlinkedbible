### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 15|Revelation 11:15]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11-21]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G932|G932]] βασιλεία (*basileia*) - kingdom, reign
- [[Lexicon/G4170|G4170]] πόλεμος (*polemos*) - war, battle
- [[Lexicon/G3528|G3528]] νικάω (*nikao*) - to conquer
- [[Lexicon/G2537|G2537]] καινός (*kainos*) - new (qualitatively new)
- [[Lexicon/G2288|G2288]] θάνατος (*thanatos*) - death

**Context**: Revelation 11:15 announces: "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ, and he shall reign forever and ever." Revelation 19:11-21 depicts Christ as conquering King on a white horse who defeats all enemies. Revelation 20:10 records Satan's final defeat ("thrown into the lake of fire"). Revelation 21:1-4 describes the new creation where "death shall be no more."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Joshua's incomplete Canaan conquest → Christ's complete new creation
- Temporal rest from enemies → eternal rest with no enemies remaining
- Partial land inheritance → entire cosmos made new

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's conquest (Josh 11:23; 21:43-45) - land taken, rest given
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 14|Daniel 7:14]] - everlasting kingdom given to Son of Man
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - serpent's head crushed
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] - "new heavens and new earth"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 24|1 Corinthians 15:24-26]] - last enemy death destroyed

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - ultimate fulfillment of conquest typology

**Christological Connection**: The trajectory completes: Joshua's incomplete conquest of Canaan → Christ's "already" conquest through cross and resurrection → Christ's "not yet" conquest at His return → eternal reign in new creation with no enemies remaining. "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord" - what Joshua began, Christ will finish. Death itself is conquered (Rev 20:14; 21:4). The promised "rest" (Josh 21:44) becomes eternal Sabbath (Heb 4:9-10) in God's presence forever.
