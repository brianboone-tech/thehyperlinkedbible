### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 10#Joshua 10 . 11|Joshua 10:11-14]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H68|H68]] אֶבֶן (*'eben*) - stone
- [[Lexicon/H1259|H1259]] בָּרָד (*barad*) - hail(stones)
- [[Lexicon/H8121|H8121]] שֶׁמֶשׁ (*shemesh*) - sun
- [[Lexicon/H3394|H3394]] יָרֵחַ (*yareach*) - moon
- [[Lexicon/H1826|H1826]] דָּמַם (*damam*) - to stand still, be silent

**Context**: During battle against five Amorite kings at Gibeon, the LORD intervenes directly: "He threw down large stones from heaven on them" and more died from hailstones than from Israelite swords. Then Joshua commands the sun and moon to stand still - "and the sun stopped in the midst of heaven and did not hurry to set for about a whole day." The text emphasizes: "There has been no day like it before or since."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Divine warrior motif - YHWH fights directly for Israel
- Creation obeys Creator - cosmic signs confirm divine presence
- Anticipates eschatological "day of the LORD" with cosmic upheaval

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Exodus plagues - God's direct intervention (Exod 9:23-24 hail)
- **TO**: Creation - God commands cosmic bodies (Gen 1:14-18)
- **FROM OT**: Day of the LORD cosmic signs ([[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 30|Joel 2:30-31]]; Isa 13:10)
- **FROM NT**: Darkness at crucifixion (Matt 27:45)
- **FROM NT**: Revelation cosmic signs (Rev 6:12-14)

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (unprecedented divine intervention)

**Christological Connection**: The sun standing still demonstrates that the conquest is not merely Israel's war but the LORD's war - cosmic in scope. Christ's victory over sin, Satan, and death is similarly cosmic: darkness covered the land at His crucifixion (Matt 27:45), and His return will involve cosmic signs (Matt 24:29). Creation itself responds to God's redemptive purposes. "There has been no day like it" - except Calvary.
