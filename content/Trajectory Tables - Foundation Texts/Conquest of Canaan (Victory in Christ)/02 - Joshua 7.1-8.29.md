### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 7#Joshua 7 . 1|Joshua 7:1-8:29]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4603|H4603]] מָעַל (*ma'al*) - to act unfaithfully, trespass
- [[Lexicon/H2764|H2764]] חֵרֶם (*cherem*) - devoted thing, ban
- [[Lexicon/H2398|H2398]] חָטָא (*chata'*) - to sin, miss the mark
- [[Lexicon/H5916|H5916]] עָכָר (*'akar*) - to trouble, bring disaster (Achan's name wordplay)

**Context**: After Jericho's miraculous victory, Israel is surprisingly defeated at small Ai. The reason: Achan took devoted things (cherem) that belonged to the LORD. God tells Joshua "Israel has sinned" - one man's sin affects the whole community. After Achan's sin is exposed and judged, Israel conquers Ai on the second attempt.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Contrast with Jericho: obedience → victory; disobedience → defeat
- Community solidarity: one person's sin affects all (cf. 1 Cor 5:6)
- Confession and judgment restore relationship (cf. [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 9|1 John 1:9]])

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Jericho victory (Josh 6) - obedience brought success
- **TO**: Adam's sin affecting humanity (Rom 5:12)
- **FROM OT**: Israelite camp purification laws (Lev 4:13-21)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 . 6|1 Corinthians 5:6-7]] - "a little leaven leavens the whole lump"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 22|Ephesians 4:22-24]] - "put off the old self"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (negative example - consequence of sin)

**Christological Connection**: Achan's sin brought defeat; dealt-with sin brought restored victory. This pictures the believer's spiritual warfare: hidden sin gives the enemy foothold (Eph 4:27); confessed sin restores communion and victory. Christ bore the ultimate cherem judgment so believers are no longer under condemnation (Rom 8:1), but ongoing sin still hinders their walk and warfare.
