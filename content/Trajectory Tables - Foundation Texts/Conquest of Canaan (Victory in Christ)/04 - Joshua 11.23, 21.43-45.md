### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 11#Joshua 11 . 23|Joshua 11:23]]; [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3423|H3423]] יָרַשׁ (*yarash*) - to possess, inherit, dispossess
- [[Lexicon/H5117|H5117]] נוּחַ (*nuach*) - to rest
- [[Lexicon/H5159|H5159]] נַחֲלָה (*nachalah*) - inheritance
- [[Lexicon/H1697|H1697]] דָּבָר (*dabar*) - word, promise

**Context**: Joshua 11:23 summarizes the northern campaign: "So Joshua took the whole land... and the land had rest from war." Joshua 21:43-45 provides the theological summary of the entire conquest: "Thus the LORD gave to Israel all the land... Not one word of all the good promises that the LORD had made to the house of Israel had failed; all came to pass."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fulfillment of Abrahamic land promise (Gen 15:18-21)
- "Rest" language connects to creation Sabbath and eschatological rest
- Yet Judges reveals the rest was incomplete and temporary

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Abrahamic covenant (Gen 15:18-21) - land promised
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 10|Deuteronomy 12:10]] - rest anticipated
- **FROM OT**: Judges 1-2 - incomplete conquest revealed
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8-9]] - Joshua's rest was insufficient; Sabbath rest remains
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|Romans 4:13]] - Abraham heir of the world

**Type Classification**: Providential Type with typological significance (rest language)

**Christological Connection**: God kept every promise to Israel - "not one word failed." This grounds confidence in all His promises, including those fulfilled in Christ. Yet [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] notes that Joshua's rest was incomplete ("if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken of another day afterward"). The earthly Canaan rest was a type pointing to the true and eternal rest in Christ. God's faithfulness in Joshua guarantees His faithfulness in Christ.
