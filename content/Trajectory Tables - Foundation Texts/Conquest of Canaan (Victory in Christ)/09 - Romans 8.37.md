### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 37|Romans 8:37]]; [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 57|1 Corinthians 15:57]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 7|Revelation 2:7, 11, 17, 26]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 5|Revelation 3:5, 12, 21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5245|G5245]] ὑπερνικάω (*hupernikao*) - to more than conquer, overwhelmingly conquer
- [[Lexicon/G3528|G3528]] νικάω (*nikao*) - to conquer, overcome, be victorious
- [[Lexicon/G3534|G3534]] νῖκος (*nikos*) - victory
- [[Lexicon/G25|G25]] ἀγαπάω (*agapao*) - to love

**Context**: Romans 8:37 declares "in all these things we are more than conquerors through him who loved us." 1 Corinthians 15:57 proclaims "thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ." Revelation 2-3 repeatedly promises rewards "to the one who conquers" (νικάω) - the same root word.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Israel's conquest required fighting; believers' victory is given through Christ
- ὑπερνικάω (hyper + conquer) = super-conquer, overwhelmingly win
- Not just victory but MORE than victory

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's conquest - Israel had to fight
- **TO**: David's victories (2 Sam 8) - kingdom expanded through warfare
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 33|John 16:33]] - "I have overcome the world"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 4|1 John 5:4]] - "this is the victory that has overcome the world - our faith"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 17#Revelation 17 . 14|Revelation 17:14]] - "the Lamb will conquer them"

**Type Classification**: NT Application - believers participate in Christ's conquest

**Christological Connection**: The key phrase is "through him who loved us." Believers are conquerors not by their own strength but by union with Christ who has already won. Israel fought for victory; believers fight from victory already secured. "More than conquerors" - we don't just survive; we overwhelmingly triumph. The Revelation promises to "the one who conquers" show that conquering requires persevering faith, but the victory itself is Christ's gift to us.
