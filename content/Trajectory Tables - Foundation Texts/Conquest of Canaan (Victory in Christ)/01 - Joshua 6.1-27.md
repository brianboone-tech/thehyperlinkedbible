### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 6#Joshua 6 . 1|Joshua 6:1-27]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2764|H2764]] חֵרֶם (*cherem*) - devoted thing, ban (complete destruction)
- [[Lexicon/H7794|H7794]] שׁוֹפָר (*shophar*) - trumpet, ram's horn
- [[Lexicon/H2346|H2346]] חוֹמָה (*chomah*) - wall
- [[Lexicon/H5307|H5307]] נָפַל (*naphal*) - to fall

**Context**: Jericho is Israel's first major conquest after crossing the Jordan. God commands an unusual strategy: march around the city for seven days, then on the seventh day blow trumpets and shout. The walls fall down flat, and Israel takes the city. Everything is devoted to destruction (cherem) except Rahab's family and precious metals for the treasury.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Paradigmatic victory establishing pattern: God fights, Israel obeys
- Rahab's salvation (Josh 2) shows faith saving from judgment (Heb 11:31)
- Seven-day pattern echoes creation; conquest is new creation work

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Crossing the Jordan (Josh 3-4) - entry precedes conquest
- **TO**: Abraham promised the land (Gen 15:18-21)
- **FROM OT**: Achan's violation brings defeat at Ai (Josh 7)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 30|Hebrews 11:30]] - "By faith the walls of Jericho fell"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 31|Hebrews 11:31]] - Rahab saved by faith

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (sovereignly arranged historical pattern)

**Christological Connection**: Jericho's walls fell by divine power, not human military might. This establishes the conquest pattern: God commands → His people obey → God gives victory. Christ conquers spiritual enemies not through earthly weapons but through the cross - apparent weakness that is actually divine power (1 Cor 1:18). The "devoted things" (cherem) picture total judgment on sin; Christ bore that total judgment for believers.
