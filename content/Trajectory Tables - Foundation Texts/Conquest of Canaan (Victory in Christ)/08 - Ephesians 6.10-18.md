### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 10|Ephesians 6:10-18]]; [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 10#2 Corinthians 10 . 3|2 Corinthians 10:3-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3833|G3833]] πανοπλία (*panoplia*) - full armor
- [[Lexicon/G3823|G3823]] πάλη (*pale*) - wrestling, struggle
- [[Lexicon/G4752|G4752]] στρατεία (*strateia*) - warfare, military campaign
- [[Lexicon/G3794|G3794]] ὀχύρωμα (*ochuroma*) - stronghold, fortress
- [[Lexicon/G2507|G2507]] καθαιρέω (*kathaireo*) - to tear down, demolish

**Context**: Ephesians 6:10-18 commands believers to "put on the whole armor of God" because "we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against... spiritual forces of evil." 2 Corinthians 10:3-5 adds that "the weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh but have divine power to destroy strongholds" and "take every thought captive to obey Christ."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Israel fought Canaanites with swords; believers fight cosmic powers with spiritual weapons
- Joshua's armor → believer's spiritual armor (truth, righteousness, gospel, faith, salvation, word, prayer)
- Conquest language applied to spiritual warfare

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's physical warfare (Josh 6-12)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 17|Isaiah 59:17]] - God wearing armor
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 4|Isaiah 11:4-5]] - Messiah's righteousness and faithfulness
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 8|1 Thessalonians 5:8]] - armor of faith, love, hope
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 3|2 Timothy 2:3-4]] - soldier imagery

**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - spiritual warfare surpasses physical

**Christological Connection**: Israel conquered Canaan with swords; believers conquer spiritual enemies with spiritual weapons. The escalation: physical enemies → cosmic powers; human weapons → divine power; earthly land → heavenly places. Each piece of armor points to Christ: He is our truth, righteousness, gospel, faith, salvation, and the Word. We fight "in Christ" (Eph 6:10 "in the Lord"), wearing His armor, using His weapons.
