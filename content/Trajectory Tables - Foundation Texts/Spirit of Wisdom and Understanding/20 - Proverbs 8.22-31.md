### [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8#Proverbs 8 . 22|Proverbs 8:22-31]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- **qanah** (קָנָה) - created, possessed, acquired
- **me'olam** (מֵעוֹלָם) - from everlasting, from eternity
- **amon** (אָמוֹן) - master craftsman, architect, skilled workman
- **sha'ashu'im** (שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים) - delight, pleasure
- LXX: **ektisen** (ἔκτισεν) - created (from *ktizō*)
- LXX: **archē** (ἀρχή) - beginning, first principle
- LXX: **harmozousa** (ἁρμόζουσα) - one who arranges, fits together (master builder)

**Context**: Proverbs 8 is wisdom's great self-testimony, personifying wisdom as a woman who speaks in the first person. After calling people to listen (vv. 1-21), wisdom declares her origin and role in creation (vv. 22-31). This passage is the most explicit OT statement about wisdom's preexistence and activity before and during creation.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Spirit hovering over creation ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]]); all things made through God's word ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 33#Psalm 33 . 6|Psalm 33:6]])
- **FROM OT**:
  - Wisdom builds her house ([[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 9#Proverbs 9 . 1|Proverbs 9:1]]); God's wisdom in creation ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 104#Psalm 104 . 24|Psalm 104:24]])
- **FROM NT**:
  - All things created through Christ the Logos ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1-3]]); Christ the image of the invisible God, firstborn over all creation ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15-17]])
**OT Context**: This passage is the climax of Proverbs' wisdom theology. Wisdom is not merely a divine attribute or human virtue but a personal reality present with God before creation. The phrase "The LORD created/possessed me" (v. 22) is foundational—wisdom originates from God, not independently. Wisdom's presence "when He established the heavens" (v. 27) through "when He marked out the foundations of the earth" (v. 29) shows wisdom as integral to cosmic order.

**Jewish Backgrounds**: Jewish interpreters debated whether *qanah* means "created" or "possessed." The LXX's *ektisen* ("created") influenced later debates. Some rabbis identified wisdom with Torah—"the Torah preceded the creation of the world" (Genesis Rabbah 1:1). Others saw wisdom as God's blueprint for creation. Philo of Alexandria identified wisdom with the divine Logos. Early Christians saw this passage as prefiguring Christ.

**Text Form**: The passage uses temporal markers throughout: "before" (vv. 23, 24, 25, 26), "when" (vv. 27, 28, 29). This creates a timeline showing wisdom's priority over all created things. Verse 30's "I was **amon** at His side" is debated—**amon** can mean "master craftsman" (see Jeremiah 52:15) or "little child" (nursling). Most English translations prefer "master craftsman," linking wisdom to construction. The alternation between God's creative acts and wisdom's presence creates a rhythm showing wisdom as both observer and participant.

**Hermeneutical Use**: Church fathers (especially Athanasius and Augustine) used this passage to articulate the doctrine of Christ's eternal generation. Arians cited v. 22 ("created me") to argue Christ was created; Nicene fathers countered that *qanah* means "possessed" and that wisdom is eternally begotten, not made. The passage's personification of wisdom as active in creation prepared readers for the NT revelation that the Son is agent of creation.

**Theological Use**:
1. **Wisdom's Preexistence** - Wisdom exists "from everlasting" (v. 23), before creation
2. **Wisdom's Role in Creation** - Wisdom is God's "master craftsman" (v. 30), not passive observer
3. **Wisdom's Delight in Humanity** - Wisdom rejoices "in His whole world, delighting together in the sons of men" (v. 31)
4. **Wisdom Anchored in God's Character** - Wisdom is not abstract philosophy but flows from God's eternal nature

**Rhetorical Use**: Personified wisdom uses her resume to persuade readers to listen to her. The argument is: "I was with God from the beginning, active in creating the world you live in—therefore my counsel is trustworthy." The cosmic scope (heavens, deep, mountains, earth) contrasts with wisdom's personal interest in "sons of men," bridging transcendence and immanence.

**Christological Connection**: This passage is foundational for understanding Christ as eternal Wisdom:

1. **Christ as Preexistent Wisdom** - "The LORD created me as His first course" (v. 22) parallels "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1]]). "From everlasting I was established" (v. 23) echoes "Before Abraham was, I AM" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 58|John 8:58]]). Christ existed before all creation, eternally with the Father.

2. **Christ as Agent of Creation** - "I was a skilled craftsman at His side" (v. 30) finds fulfillment in "All things were made through Him, and without Him was not anything made that was made" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 3|John 1:3]]). Paul declares, "In Him all things were created, in heaven and on earth... all things were created through Him and for Him" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 16|Colossians 1:16]]). The craftsman metaphor (***amon***) points to Christ as the divine architect.

3. **Christ's Delight in Humanity** - "Delighting together in the sons of men" (v. 31) anticipates the incarnation. Wisdom's joy in humanity foreshadows Christ taking on flesh to dwell among us ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). Jesus came "to seek and to save the lost" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 19#Luke 19 . 10|Luke 19:10]]), fulfilling wisdom's pursuit of humanity.

4. **From Shadow to Substance** -
   - Proverbs: Wisdom **personified** in poetic metaphor
   - NT: Wisdom **incarnate** in historical person (Christ)
   - Proverbs: Wisdom as God's craftsman in creation
   - NT: Christ as Creator sustaining all things by His word ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]])

**Typology** (from PDF analysis): Bezalel (Exodus 31:3) and Hiram (1 Kings 7:14) are types of Christ, the ultimate architect building God's spiritual house (Hebrews 3:3-4). Proverbs 8:30 reveals the archetype—Wisdom as God's eternal master craftsman. Bezalel built a temporary tent; Christ builds an eternal temple.

**Longitudinal Theme**: Wisdom evolves through Scripture:
- **Exodus**: Wisdom enables skillful labor (craftsmanship)
- **Proverbs 2**: Wisdom provides moral discernment (ethics)
- **Proverbs 8**: Wisdom orders cosmic creation (cosmology)
- **NT**: Wisdom IS Christ Himself (Christology) - "Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God" (1 Corinthians 1:24)

**Escalation**: Wisdom personified → Wisdom incarnate; Wisdom as attribute → Wisdom as Person; Wisdom in creation → Wisdom as Creator; Wisdom delights in humanity → Wisdom becomes human to redeem humanity.
