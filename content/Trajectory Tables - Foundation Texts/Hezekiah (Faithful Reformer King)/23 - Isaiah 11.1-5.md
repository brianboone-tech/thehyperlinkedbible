### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1-5]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2415|H2415]] חֹטֶר (choter) - "shoot/branch" - new growth from apparent death
- [[Lexicon/H1503|H1503]] גֶּזַע (geza) - "stump" - the cut-down tree (Jesse's line)
- [[Lexicon/H5342|H5342]] נֵצֶר (netser) - "branch" - the messianic title (cf. "Nazarene")
- [[Lexicon/H6664|H6664]] צֶדֶק (tsedeq) - "righteousness" - the king's defining characteristic

**Context**: Isaiah prophesies during Hezekiah's reign, yet looks beyond any contemporary king to the ultimate Davidic ruler. The "stump of Jesse" imagery suggests the dynasty will be cut down (Babylon) before the Branch emerges. This ruler will judge perfectly, not by appearance but by truth.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 2|Isaiah 4:2]] - "The Branch of the LORD will be beautiful and glorious"
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5]] - "I will raise up to David a righteous Branch"
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 8|Zechariah 3:8]]; 6:12 - "My servant, the Branch... He will branch out and build the LORD's temple"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: 2 Samuel 7 - The Davidic covenant promise of an eternal throne
- **FROM OT**: The "Branch" becomes a messianic title throughout prophetic literature
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 23|Matthew 2:23]] - Jesus "would be called a Nazarene" (wordplay on netser)

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking prophecy pointing directly to Christ

**Christological Connection**: Hezekiah was the best of Judah's trusting kings, yet he failed (showing treasures to Babylon, 2 Kgs 20:12-19). Isaiah's prophecy looks beyond any contemporary king to One who would perfectly fulfill the Davidic ideal. Jesus—born in David's line after the stump was cut down (post-exilic period)—is the Branch. He judges with perfect righteousness, is empowered by the Spirit without measure, and rules with the rod of His mouth. What Hezekiah anticipated, Jesus fulfills.
