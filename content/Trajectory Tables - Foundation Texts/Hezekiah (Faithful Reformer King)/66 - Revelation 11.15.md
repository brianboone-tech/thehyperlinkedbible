### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 15|Revelation 11:15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G932|G932]] βασιλεία (basileia) - "kingdom" - sovereign rule
- [[Lexicon/G2889|G2889]] κόσμος (kosmos) - "world" - the entire created order
- [[Lexicon/G5547|G5547]] Χριστός (Christos) - "Christ/Anointed" - the Davidic king
- [[Lexicon/G165|G165]] αἰών (aiōn) - "forever" - eternal duration

**Context**: The seventh trumpet announces the consummation of God's kingdom. The declaration echoes Davidic promises: God and His Anointed (Christ) will reign forever. What faithful kings like Hezekiah could only approximate for a few decades, Christ accomplishes eternally.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 13|2 Samuel 7:13]], 16 - "I will establish the throne of His kingdom forever"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 6|Psalm 2:6-9]] - "I have installed My King on Zion... I will make the nations Your inheritance"
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 14|Daniel 7:14]] - "His dominion is an everlasting dominion that will not pass away"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: The Davidic covenant promise of eternal kingdom (2 Sam 7)
- **FROM OT**: All faithful Davidic kings (David, Solomon, Hezekiah, Josiah) anticipated this
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 9|Philippians 2:9-11]] - "Every knee should bow... every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - The Davidic promise fully realized

**Christological Connection**: Hezekiah reigned twenty-nine years over one nation (2 Kgs 18:2). Christ reigns forever over all creation. Hezekiah trusted God and was delivered; Christ IS God and delivers. Hezekiah's reforms were reversed by his son Manasseh; Christ's kingdom will never be overthrown. The trajectory from faithful Davidic kings to Christ shows escalation: partial → complete, temporal → eternal, national → universal, human → divine. What Hezekiah approximated, Christ consummates.
