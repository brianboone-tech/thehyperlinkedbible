### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 4|2 Kings 18:4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1116|H1116]] בָּמָה (bamah) - "high place" - illicit worship sites
- [[Lexicon/H4676|H4676]] מַצֵּבָה (matstsebah) - "sacred pillar" - pagan worship stones
- [[Lexicon/H842|H842]] אֲשֵׁרָה (asherah) - "Asherah pole" - Canaanite goddess symbol
- [[Lexicon/H5180|H5180]] נְחֻשְׁתָּן (Nechushtan) - "bronze thing" - what the serpent had become

**Context**: This verse summarizes Hezekiah's religious reforms. Most remarkably, he destroyed the bronze serpent Moses had made (Num 21:8-9)—a legitimate religious artifact that had become an idol. This demonstrates Hezekiah's commitment to pure worship even when it meant destroying venerable traditions.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 8|Numbers 21:8-9]] - Moses makes the bronze serpent at God's command
- 2 Chronicles 29-31 - Extended account of Hezekiah's temple restoration
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4-20]] - Josiah's similar reforms follow Hezekiah's pattern

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 8|Numbers 21:8-9]] - The original bronze serpent as God's provision
- **FROM OT**: The serpent becomes "Nehushtan"—a mere bronze thing, no longer a means of grace
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 14|John 3:14]] - Jesus claims the bronze serpent pointed to Him

**Type Classification**: Reform action pointing to Christ's authority over all tradition

**Christological Connection**: Hezekiah destroyed the bronze serpent; Jesus fulfilled what it meant. The serpent was lifted up for Israel's healing; Jesus was "lifted up" on the cross for the world's salvation ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 14|John 3:14-15]]). Hezekiah's act preserved the type's meaning by preventing its idolatrous distortion. Jesus' crucifixion realized the type's meaning by actually accomplishing what the serpent only signified—eternal life for all who look in faith.
