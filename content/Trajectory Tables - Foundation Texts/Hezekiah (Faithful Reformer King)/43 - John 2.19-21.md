### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3485|G3485]] ναός (naos) - "temple/sanctuary" - the inner sacred space
- [[Lexicon/G3089|G3089]] λύω (lyō) - "destroy/loose" - what the Jews will do
- [[Lexicon/G1453|G1453]] ἐγείρω (egeirō) - "raise up" - resurrection language
- [[Lexicon/G4983|G4983]] σῶμα (sōma) - "body" - what Jesus meant by "temple"

**Context**: After cleansing the temple, Jesus is challenged for a sign. His response—"Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up"—is misunderstood. John explains: "He was speaking about the temple of His body." This connects Jesus' resurrection to temple theology.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 29#2 Chronicles 29 . 3|2 Chronicles 29:3-11]] - Hezekiah cleanses and restores the temple
- [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 15|Ezra 6:15]] - The second temple rebuilt
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 12|Zechariah 6:12]] - "The man whose name is the Branch... will build the temple of the LORD"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Hezekiah's temple cleansing established the pattern Jesus follows (2 Chr 29)
- **FROM OT**:
  - Temple theology: God's presence dwelling among His people
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16-17]] - "You are God's temple"
  - [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21]] - "built together to become a dwelling in which God lives"
**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - Christ IS the temple Hezekiah cleansed

**Christological Connection**: Hezekiah cleansed the temple building; Jesus IS the temple. Hezekiah restored proper worship in the Jerusalem sanctuary; Jesus establishes worship "in Spirit and truth" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 24|John 4:24]]) centered on His own person. The trajectory moves from building to body: what Hezekiah preserved as sacred space, Christ embodies as sacred person. His resurrection "raises up" the true temple that can never be destroyed.
