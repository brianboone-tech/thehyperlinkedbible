### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 3|2 Kings 18:3-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3477|H3477]] יָשָׁר (yashar) - "right/upright" - moral rectitude before God
- [[Lexicon/H982|H982]] בָּטַח (batach) - "trust" - confident reliance on God
- [[Lexicon/H1692|H1692]] דָּבַק (dabaq) - "cling/remain faithful" - covenant loyalty
- [[Lexicon/H5493|H5493]] סוּר (sur) - "turn aside" (negated) - steadfastness

**Context**: The summary introduction to Hezekiah's reign provides an unparalleled assessment: "No king of Judah was like him, either before him or after him" (v. 5). This evaluation exceeds even David's, focusing specifically on trust. Hezekiah becomes the paradigm of the trusting king.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 14|1 Kings 3:14]] - Solomon's conditional promise: "if you walk in My ways"
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 25|2 Kings 23:25]] - Josiah receives similar praise for repentance
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 20|Deuteronomy 10:20]] - "Fear the LORD your God and serve Him. Cling to Him"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Deuteronomy's covenant call to trust and faithfulness
- **FROM OT**:
  - David set the standard (1 Kgs 15:11); Hezekiah matches and exceeds it in trust
- **FROM NT**:
  - Jesus embodies perfect trust (Heb 2:13 - "I will put my trust in Him")
**Type Classification**: Providential (Davidic covenant heir), Forward-looking (points to perfect trust in Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Hezekiah's trust was remarkable but not absolute—he later showed the Babylonian envoys his treasures (2 Kgs 20:12-18), a failure of faith. Jesus, by contrast, demonstrated perfect trust throughout His ministry, especially in Gethsemane: "Not my will, but yours be done" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 42|Luke 22:42]]). Where Hezekiah trusted and was delivered, Jesus trusted and was crucified—yet through death achieved the greater deliverance. He is the King whose trust never wavered.
