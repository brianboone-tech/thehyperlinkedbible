### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]]; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-38]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4720|H4720]] מִקְדָּשׁ (*miqdash*) - sanctuary, holy place
- [[Lexicon/H7931|H7931]] שָׁכַן (*shakan*) - to dwell, tabernacle
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kavod*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H4393|H4393]] מָלֵא (*male*) - to fill

**Context**: Exodus 25:8: "Let them make me a sanctuary (מִקְדָּשׁ), that I may dwell (שָׁכַנְתִּי) in their midst." Exodus 40:34-38: "Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled (מָלֵא) the tabernacle... the cloud of the LORD was on the tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- God's purpose: dwelling WITH His people, not just FOR them
- Pattern from heaven (25:40) - earthly copy of heavenly reality
- Glory-presence confirms acceptance of the structure
- Tabernacle → Temple → Christ → Church → New Creation

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Eden - God walking with humanity
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - glory fills Solomon's temple
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 4|Ezekiel 43:4-5]] - glory returns to new temple
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word "tabernacled" among us
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 22|Ephesians 2:22]] - church as dwelling place for God

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - explicitly commanded by God as His dwelling

**Christological Connection**: The tabernacle points to Christ who "tabernacled" (ἐσκήνωσεν, [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) among us. The glory that filled the tabernacle is the same glory seen in Christ. God's purpose from Sinai - "that I may dwell in their midst" - is ultimately fulfilled in Emmanuel, God with us, and then in His Spirit dwelling in believers.
