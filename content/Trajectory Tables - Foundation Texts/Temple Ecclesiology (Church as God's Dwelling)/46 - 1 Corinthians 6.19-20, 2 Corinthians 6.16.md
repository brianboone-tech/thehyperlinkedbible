### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19-20]]; [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3485|G3485]] ναός (*naos*) - temple
- [[Lexicon/G4983|G4983]] σῶμα (*soma*) - body
- [[Lexicon/G1392|G1392]] δοξάζω (*doxazo*) - to glorify
- [[Lexicon/G1704|G1704]] ἐμπεριπατέω (*emperipateo*) - to walk among

**Context**: 1 Corinthians 6:19-20: "Do you not know that your body is a temple (ναὸς) of the Holy Spirit within you... You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body." 2 Corinthians 6:16: "We are the temple of the living God; as God said, 'I will make my dwelling (ἐνοικήσω) among them and walk among (ἐμπεριπατήσω) them.'"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Corporate temple (1 Cor 3) → individual bodies as temples
- Walking among (Gen 3:8; Lev 26:12) → walking in believers
- Temple holiness demands personal holiness

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 8|Genesis 3:8]] - God walking in the garden
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 12|Leviticus 26:12]] - "I will walk among you"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - "my dwelling place shall be with them"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 9|Romans 8:9-11]] - Spirit dwells in you

**Type Classification**: NT Application - ethical implications of temple identity

**Christological Connection**: We glorify God in bodies "bought with a price" - Christ's redemptive work. Because of the cross, the Spirit now indwells believers. The "walking among" promised in Leviticus 26 is fulfilled as Christ dwells in us by His Spirit. Our bodies become sacred space because Christ has purchased them and the Spirit inhabits them.
