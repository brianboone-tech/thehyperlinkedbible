### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 26|Ezekiel 37:26-28]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4908|H4908]] מִשְׁכָּן (*mishkan*) - dwelling place, tabernacle
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (*olam*) - forever, eternal
- [[Lexicon/H4325|H4325]] מַיִם (*mayim*) - water
- [[Lexicon/H2421|H2421]] חָיָה (*chayah*) - to live (living waters)

**Context**: Ezekiel 37:26-28: "My dwelling place (מִשְׁכָּנִי) shall be with them, and I will be their God, and they shall be my people. Then the nations will know that I am the LORD who sanctifies Israel, when my sanctuary is in their midst forever." Ezekiel 47:1-12 describes water flowing from the temple threshold, deepening into a river that brings life wherever it goes.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- After glory departs (ch. 10-11), promise of greater return
- "Forever" - eschatological finality
- Life-giving river echoes Eden's rivers (Gen 2:10-14)
- Nations recognize God through Israel's temple

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10-14]] - river from Eden
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]] - living waters from Jerusalem
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - rivers of living water (Spirit)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] - river of life from throne

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - explicit eschatological promise

**Christological Connection**: Jesus offers "living water" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10]]; 7:37-39) - the Spirit flowing from those who believe in Him. He IS the temple from which life flows. The promise of God dwelling forever with His people is fulfilled in Christ and the Spirit indwelling believers. Revelation 22 shows the river of life flowing from "the throne of God and of the Lamb" - Ezekiel's vision finds ultimate fulfillment.
