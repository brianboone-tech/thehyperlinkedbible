### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3, 22]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4633|G4633]] σκηνή (*skene*) - tent, dwelling, tabernacle
- [[Lexicon/G3485|G3485]] ναός (*naos*) - temple
- [[Lexicon/G4215|G4215]] ποταμός (*potamos*) - river
- [[Lexicon/G2222|G2222]] ζωή (*zoe*) - life

**Context**: Revelation 21:3: "Behold, the dwelling place (σκηνὴ) of God is with man. He will dwell (σκηνώσει) with them, and they will be his people." Revelation 21:22: "I saw no temple (ναόν) in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb." Revelation 22:1-5: "The river of the water of life... flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb... the tree of life."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Eden's river → Ezekiel's temple river → New Jerusalem's river
- From limited temple to no-temple-needed (God is the temple)
- Creation's goal achieved: God dwelling with humanity forever

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10-14]] - rivers from Eden; tree of life
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - "that I may dwell in their midst"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] - healing river from temple
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]] - living waters from Jerusalem

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - ultimate fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: "The Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb" ARE the temple. Christ, who is the temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]), shares this identity with the Father. The river flows from "the throne of God and of the Lamb" - Ezekiel's life-giving waters find their source in the exalted Christ. The trajectory from garden to tabernacle to temple to Christ to church reaches its goal: God and the Lamb dwelling with redeemed humanity forever, with no barrier between.
