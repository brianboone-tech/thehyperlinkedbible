### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4637|G4637]] σκηνόω (*skenoo*) - to dwell, tabernacle
- [[Lexicon/G1391|G1391]] δόξα (*doxa*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/G3485|G3485]] ναός (*naos*) - temple (sanctuary proper)
- [[Lexicon/G4983|G4983]] σῶμα (*soma*) - body

**Context**: John 1:14: "The Word became flesh and dwelt (ἐσκήνωσεν, 'tabernacled') among us, and we have seen his glory (δόξαν), glory as of the only Son from the Father." John 2:19-21: Jesus said, "Destroy this temple (ναόν), and in three days I will raise it up... he was speaking about the temple (ναοῦ) of his body (σώματος)."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- σκηνόω directly recalls Hebrew שָׁכַן (tabernacle presence)
- Glory seen in Jesus = glory that filled tabernacle/temple
- Temple shifts from building to person

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]]; 40:34 - tabernacling presence and glory
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 4|Ezekiel 43:4-5]] - glory returns
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 19|Colossians 1:19]]; 2:9 - fullness of deity dwells in Christ
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]] - radiance of God's glory

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Christ as true temple

**Christological Connection**: This IS the Christological center of temple theology. All that tabernacle and temple pointed to - God dwelling with humanity, glory manifested, access provided - is fulfilled in Jesus Christ. His body is the temple; destroy it (crucifixion) and it rises (resurrection). In Christ we see God's glory; in Christ we access God's presence. The Word who was "with God" has now "tabernacled among us."
