### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16-17]]; [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 19|Ephesians 2:19-22]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3485|G3485]] ναός (*naos*) - temple (inner sanctuary)
- [[Lexicon/G3624|G3624]] οἶκος (*oikos*) - house
- [[Lexicon/G2310|G2310]] θεμέλιος (*themelios*) - foundation
- [[Lexicon/G204|G204]] ἀκρογωνιαῖος (*akrogoniaios*) - cornerstone

**Context**: 1 Corinthians 3:16-17: "Do you (plural) not know that you are God's temple (ναὸς θεοῦ) and that God's Spirit dwells in you? If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy him." Ephesians 2:19-22: "You are... members of the household of God, built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone... a holy temple in the Lord... a dwelling place for God by the Spirit."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Physical temple → spiritual temple (church community)
- Building imagery: foundation, cornerstone, stones growing
- Spirit dwelling in community, not just building

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - God dwelling in their midst
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 28#Isaiah 28 . 16|Isaiah 28:16]] - precious cornerstone in Zion
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - my sanctuary in their midst forever
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4-5]] - living stones; spiritual house

**Type Classification**: NT Expansion - church as temple of the Spirit

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the cornerstone - the church-temple is built on Him and derives its identity from Him. The foundation of "apostles and prophets" is the gospel of Christ they proclaimed. Believers are "in the Lord" - united to Christ, we become His temple. The Spirit who dwelt in the physical temple now dwells in the Christ-community.
