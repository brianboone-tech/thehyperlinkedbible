### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 22|Ezekiel 11:22-23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kavod*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H3318|H3318]] יָצָא (*yatsa*) - to go out, depart
- [[Lexicon/H5927|H5927]] עָלָה (*alah*) - to go up, ascend
- [[Lexicon/H4217|H4217]] מִזְרָח (*mizrach*) - east

**Context**: Ezekiel 10:18-19: "The glory of the LORD went out from the threshold of the house, and stood over the cherubim... and stood at the entrance of the east gate of the house of the LORD." Ezekiel 11:22-23: "The glory of the LORD went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that is on the east side of the city."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Glory that entered (Exod 40; 1 Kgs 8) now departs
- Movement: threshold → east gate → Mount of Olives → gone
- Judgment: temple without presence is just a building

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-38]]
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - glory entering
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 1|Ezekiel 43:1-5]] - glory returns (same eastward path reversed
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23#Matthew 23 . 38|Matthew 23:38]] - "Your house is left to you desolate"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 50|Luke 24:50-51]] - Jesus ascends from Mount of Olives
**Type Classification**: Crisis pointing to need for new temple

**Christological Connection**: The glory departed eastward over Mount of Olives; Jesus ascended from Mount of Olives ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 12|Acts 1:12]]). The glory returns in Christ - "we have seen his glory" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). Jesus' lament "your house is left to you desolate" (Matt 23:38) echoes Ezekiel's departure vision. Christ IS the returning glory, and His church becomes the new dwelling place.
