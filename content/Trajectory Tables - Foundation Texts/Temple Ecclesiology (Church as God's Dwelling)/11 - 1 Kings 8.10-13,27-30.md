### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-13, 27-30]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kavod*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H6051|H6051]] עָנָן (*anan*) - cloud
- [[Lexicon/H3557|H3557]] כּוּל (*kul*) - to contain
- [[Lexicon/H8085|H8085]] שָׁמַע (*shama*) - to hear

**Context**: At temple dedication, "the cloud filled the house of the LORD... the glory of the LORD filled the house" (8:10-11). Yet Solomon acknowledges: "Behold, heaven and the highest heaven cannot contain (יְכַלְכְּלוּךָ) you; how much less this house" (8:27). He prays God will "hear in heaven your dwelling place" (8:30).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Tabernacle glory → temple glory (same pattern)
- Tension: God truly dwells here yet transcends any building
- Temple as place of prayer and forgiveness

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-38]] - glory filling tabernacle
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 1|Isaiah 66:1-2]] - heaven is throne, earth footstool
- **FROM OT**: Ezekiel 10 - glory departs from temple
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 48|Acts 7:48-50]] - Stephen quotes Isaiah; God not in temples
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 19|Ephesians 3:19]] - filled with all the fullness of God

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Solomon's temple as commanded dwelling

**Christological Connection**: The temple's paradox (God dwells here yet cannot be contained) is resolved in Christ: the eternal Son "becomes flesh" - fully present in human form while remaining infinite God. Christ fulfills what the temple anticipated: the place where God is truly accessed for hearing and forgiveness. His body is the temple Solomon's building pointed toward.
