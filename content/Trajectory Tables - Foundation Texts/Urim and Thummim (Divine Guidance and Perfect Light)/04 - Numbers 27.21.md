### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 21|Numbers 27:21]]

- [[Lexicon/H3068|H3068]] יְהוָה (*YHWH*) - "the LORD"

**Context**:

Numbers 27 records the transition of leadership from Moses to Joshua. After the daughters of Zelophehad's case (vv. 1-11) and God's announcement that Moses will not enter Canaan (vv. 12-14), Moses requests that God appoint a successor "that the congregation of the LORD may not be as sheep which have no shepherd" (v. 17). God commands Moses to commission Joshua publicly.

Verse 21 establishes Joshua's dependence on priestly mediation: "He shall stand before Eleazar the priest, who shall inquire for him by the judgment of the Urim before the LORD. At his word they shall go out, and at his word they shall come in, both he and all the people of Israel with him, the whole congregation." This is the **first explicit mention of the Urim functioning as an oracle**. Unlike Moses, who spoke with God face to face, Joshua must seek divine guidance through the high priest and the Urim.

**OT-to-OT Development**:

Numbers 27:21 establishes the **constitutional principle**: even Israel's highest civil authority submits to divine revelation mediated through the priesthood. This develops through:
- **[[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 9#Joshua 9 . 14|Joshua 9:14]]** - Israel's failure to inquire results in covenant error (negative example)
- **[[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 14#1 Samuel 14 . 36|1 Samuel 14:36-42]]** - Saul attempts to use the oracle but God does not answer (divine silence shows covenant breach)
- **[[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 23#1 Samuel 23 . 2|1 Samuel 23:2]], 4, 9-12; 30:7-8** - David repeatedly inquires and receives clear answers (the oracle functioning properly under the true king)
- **[[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 2#Ezra 2 . 63|Ezra 2:63]]** - The post-exilic community awaits "a priest with Urim and Thummim" to resolve disputes (messianic expectation)

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded pattern) and Forward-Looking (the need for priestly inquiry points beyond itself to the coming High Priest who embodies perfect revelation).

**Christological Connection**:

[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 21|Numbers 27:21]] points to Christ through contrast and fulfillment:

1. **Contrast: Mediated vs. Direct Revelation** - Joshua required priestly intercession to know God's will. Christ **is** the revelation of God's will: "Whoever has seen me has seen the Father" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 9|John 14:9]]). The mediator becomes the Message.

2. **Fulfillment: Perfect Submission** - Joshua's dependence on divine guidance through the Urim foreshadows Christ's perfect obedience: "I have come down from heaven, not to do my own will but the will of him who sent me" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 38|John 6:38]]). But Christ's submission is not through external oracles—He knows the Father's will directly ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 19|John 5:19-20]]).

3. **Escalation: From Oracle to Incarnate Word** - Joshua sought guidance through stones in a breastplate. Believers seek guidance through the living Word: "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God" ([[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 5|James 1:5]]). The external oracle gives way to prayer in Jesus' name ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 13|John 14:13-14]]) and the Spirit's illumination ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 2#1 Corinthians 2 . 10|1 Corinthians 2:10-12]]).

The trajectory moves from **Moses (face-to-face revelation)** → **Joshua (priestly mediation via Urim)** → **Christ (incarnate revelation of the Father)** → **Church (Spirit's indwelling guidance)**. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 21|Numbers 27:21]] captures the middle stage, showing both the privilege of divine guidance and the limitation of external mediation—preparing for the One who says, "I am the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]).
