### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 30|Exodus 28:30]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H224|H224]] אוּרִים (*urim*) - "lights, illuminations" - from H215 אוֹר (*or*) meaning "to be or become light"
- [[Lexicon/H8550|H8550]] תֻּמִּים (*thummim*) - "perfections, completeness, integrity" - from H8552 תָּמַם (*tamam*) meaning "to be complete, perfect"
- [[Lexicon/H2833|H2833]] חֹשֶׁן (*choshen*) - "breastplate, breastpiece" - the priestly ornament containing the Urim and Thummim
- [[Lexicon/H4941|H4941]] מִשְׁפָּט (*mishpat*) - "judgment, decision, ordinance" - designating the breastplate's oracular function

**Context**:

Exodus 28 provides detailed instructions for the high priestly garments, with the Urim and Thummim as a crucial component. The chapter opens with God's command to consecrate Aaron and his sons for priestly service, describing garments "for glory and for beauty" (v. 2). The breastplate of judgment is described in verses 15-29, featuring twelve precious stones representing the twelve tribes of Israel, fastened to the ephod with gold chains and rings.

Verse 30 stands as the climax of this section: "And you shall put in the breastplate of judgment the Urim and the Thummim, and they shall be over Aaron's heart when he goes in before the LORD. So Aaron shall bear the judgment of the children of Israel over his heart before the LORD continually." Significantly, **no instructions are given for making the Urim and Thummim**—only for placing them in the breastplate. This suggests they were given directly by God to Moses, not crafted by human hands, underscoring their divine origin as the means of oracular inquiry.

**OT-to-OT Development**:

The Urim and Thummim establish the pattern that **divine guidance flows through priestly mediation**. This theme develops through:
- **[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 21|Numbers 27:21]]** - Joshua's leadership subordinated to priestly inquiry
- **1 Samuel 23, 30** - David's repeated consultations showing the oracle in active use
- **[[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 8|Deuteronomy 33:8]]** - Moses' blessing linking Urim/Thummim to Levi's teaching ministry
- **[[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 2#Ezra 2 . 63|Ezra 2:63]], [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 7#Nehemiah 7 . 65|Nehemiah 7:65]]** - Post-exilic absence creates eschatological longing

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (explicitly commanded by God) and Forward-Looking (the very names "Lights" and "Perfections" point beyond themselves to perfect revelation yet to come).

**Christological Connection**:

The Urim and Thummim point to Christ in three ways:

1. **Christ as Perfect Light** - The Urim ("lights") find fulfillment in Christ's declaration: "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]). What the stones symbolized—divine illumination of God's will—Christ **is** in His person.

2. **Christ as Perfect Truth** - The Thummim ("perfections/truth") converge in Christ's self-revelation: "I am the way, the truth, and the life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]). The integrity and completeness the Thummim represented are perfectly embodied in Him who is "full of grace and truth" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]).

3. **Christ as Mediatorial Oracle** - As the high priest bore the Urim and Thummim "over his heart" to mediate divine guidance, Christ, our great High Priest, carries His people on His heart ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]) and mediates perfect knowledge of the Father's will. The external oracle gives way to the incarnate Word.

Samuel Mather writes: "The true Urim and Thummim is in the Pectoral of Jesus Christ; all our Illuminations and Perfections are in him" (Types of the OT, [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 7|Leviticus 8:7-9]]). The trajectory moves from symbolic stones → absent stones → incarnate Light and Truth → Spirit's internalized guidance → eternal light in the New Jerusalem.
