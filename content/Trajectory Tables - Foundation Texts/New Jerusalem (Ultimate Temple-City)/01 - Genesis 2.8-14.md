### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-14]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- גַּן (*gan*) - "garden" - enclosed, cultivated space (2:8, 9, 10, 15)
- עֵדֶן (*eden*) - "Eden" - delight, pleasure; suggests sanctuary/paradise (2:8, 10, 15)
- נָהָר (*nahar*) - "river" - flowing water from Eden (2:10, 13, 14)
- זָהָב (*zahav*) - "gold" - precious metal in land of Havilah (2:11, 12)
- בְּדֹלַח (*bedolach*) - "bdellium" - aromatic resin or precious stone (2:12)
- שֹׁהַם (*shoham*) - "onyx stone" - precious gem (2:12)

**Context**: Second creation account with focus on humanity and Eden. Follows cosmic creation (Genesis 1:1-2:3); precedes woman's creation (2:18-25). Details localized garden within broader created order. Sets stage for Fall narrative (Genesis 3).

**Christological Connection**: Eden foreshadows New Jerusalem with Christ as center. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8]] - garden **localized** "in the east," limited geography; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-2]] - city encompasses **entire new creation**, heaven and earth united. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10]] - river flows **out from** Eden to water earth; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] - river of life flows from **throne of God and of the Lamb** through middle of city (22:2). [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 9|Genesis 2:9]] - tree of life **at center**; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]] - tree of life **on both sides** of river, bearing twelve fruits, leaves for healing nations. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 11|Genesis 2:11-12]] - gold and precious stones in land **outside** Eden; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 18|Revelation 21:18-21]] - entire city made of gold and precious stones. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 8|Genesis 3:8]] - God **walks** in garden; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - "dwelling place of God **is with** man... **God himself will be with them**" - permanent, unmediated presence. **Trajectory through Christ**: Eden lost ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 23|Genesis 3:23-24]]) → tabernacle/temple as Eden-replicas (Exodus 40; 1 Kings 8) → Christ as true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]: "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it") → church as living temple ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]) → New Jerusalem as cosmic garden-temple-city (Revelation 21-22). Christ is key at every stage: Word through whom creation occurred ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 3|John 1:3]], [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 16|Colossians 1:16]]); Immanuel dwelling among us ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 23|Matthew 1:23]]; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]); cornerstone of spiritual temple ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20-22]]); "the Lamb" whose throne is river's source ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]). From small garden meant to expand → to global gospel expansion → to New Jerusalem filling renewed cosmos. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-14]] is **seed**; Revelation 21-22 is **harvest**. What Adam failed to accomplish (extending Eden through obedience), Christ achieves (gathering nations into restored paradise through redemption).
