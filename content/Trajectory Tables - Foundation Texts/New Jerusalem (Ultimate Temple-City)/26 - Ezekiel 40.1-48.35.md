### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 40#Ezekiel 40 . 1|Ezekiel 40:1-48:35]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מַרְאוֹת (*mar'ot*) - "visions" - divine revelatory experiences (40:2)
- תַּבְנִית (*tavnit*) - "structure, pattern" - blueprint, design (43:10, 11)
- כְּבוֹד יְהוָה (*kevod YHWH*) - "glory of the LORD" - Shekinah presence (43:2, 4, 5; 44:4)
- נַחַל (*nachal*) - "river, stream" - water flowing from temple (47:5, 9, 12)
- יְהוָה שָׁמָּה (*YHWH shammah*) - "The LORD is there" - city's name (48:35)

**Context**: Final vision during Babylonian exile (573 BC, 25th year of exile, 14th year after Jerusalem's fall). Follows judgment oracles (chapters 1-32), restoration promises (33-39), concludes with temple-city vision (40-48). Parallels Moses on Sinai receiving tabernacle pattern (Exodus 25-40).

**Christological Connection**: Ezekiel's temple-city vision finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ and New Jerusalem. **Escalation**: Ezekiel 40-48 shows idealized temple-city; Revelation 21-22 shows **reality surpassing ideal**. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1]] - river flows from temple threshold; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] - river flows from "**throne of God and of the Lamb**" - Christ is source. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 12|Ezekiel 47:12]] - trees bearing fruit monthly, leaves for healing; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]] - "tree of life... yielding its fruit each month... leaves... for the healing of the nations" - nearly identical language, showing fulfillment. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 48#Ezekiel 48 . 35|Ezekiel 48:35]] - city named "**The LORD is There**"; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - "the dwelling place of God is with man. **He will dwell with them**" - permanent, universal presence. **Profound difference**: Ezekiel's vision includes temple (chapters 40-42), sacrifices (chapters 43-46); [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]] declares "**I saw no temple** in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb" - Christ **is** temple, making localized building obsolete. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 1|Hebrews 10:1]]: "the law has but a shadow of the good things to come instead of the true form of these realities" - Ezekiel's vision is exalted shadow; Christ is substance. **Trajectory**: Solomon's temple (1 Kings 8) → destroyed (2 Kings 25) → Ezekiel's vision of restored temple-city (Ezekiel 40-48) → Second Temple built (Ezra 6) → Christ as true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]) → church as living temple ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]) → New Jerusalem where "its temple is... the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]). From physical building → to vision of idealized building → to Christ embodying temple reality → to cosmic temple-city. What Ezekiel saw prophetically, John sees fulfilling in Christ. River bringing life (Ezekiel 47) fulfilled in Christ: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]] - "Whoever believes in me, as the Scripture has said, 'Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water'" (quoting Ezekiel). Consummation: river of life in New Jerusalem ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]]). From localized temple in restored Israel → to cosmic temple-city filling renewed creation.
