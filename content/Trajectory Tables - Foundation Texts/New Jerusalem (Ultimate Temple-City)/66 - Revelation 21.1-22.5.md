### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-22:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- καινὸν (*kainon*) - "new" - qualitatively new, fresh (21:1, 5)
- σκηνὴ τοῦ θεοῦ (*skēnē tou theou*) - "tabernacle/dwelling of God" (21:3)
- νύμφην (*nymphēn*) - "bride" - prepared for husband (21:2)
- κατάβασιν (*katabasin*) - "coming down, descending" (21:2, 10)
- ποταμὸν ὕδατος ζωῆς (*potamon hydatos zōēs*) - "river of water of life" (22:1)
- ξύλον ζωῆς (*xylon zōēs*) - "tree of life" (22:2)

**Context**: Climactic vision concluding Revelation and entire Scripture. Follows final judgment (20:11-15), presents eternal state. Parallels Genesis 1-3 (beginning) with Revelation 21-22 (consummation), showing God's purposes coming full circle. One of seven bowl angels shows John bride, New Jerusalem (21:9).
