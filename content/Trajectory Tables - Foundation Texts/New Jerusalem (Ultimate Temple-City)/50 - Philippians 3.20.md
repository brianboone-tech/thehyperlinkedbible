### [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 20|Philippians 3:20]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- πολίτευμα (*politeuma*) - "citizenship, commonwealth" - political belonging (3:20)
- οὐρανοῖς (*ouranois*) - "heavens" - divine realm (3:20)
- ὑπάρχει (*hyparchei*) - "exists, is" - present reality, not merely future hope (3:20)
- σωτῆρα (*sōtēra*) - "Savior" - deliverer, rescuer (3:20)
- ἀπεκδεχόμεθα (*apekdechometha*) - "we await eagerly" - expectant waiting (3:20)

**Context**: Warning against false teachers emphasizing circumcision/law-keeping (3:1-3), contrasted with Paul's credentials (3:4-6) counted as loss for Christ (3:7-11). Pressing toward resurrection (3:12-16), imitating Paul vs. enemies of cross (3:17-19). Part of prison epistle from Rome (1:7, 13; 4:22).

**Christological Connection**: Heavenly citizenship finds ultimate realization in New Jerusalem descending. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 20|Philippians 3:20]] - citizenship **in heaven** (location above); [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]] - New Jerusalem "**coming down out of heaven from God**" (heaven descending to earth, uniting both). Where Philippians speaks of believers ascending mentality ("our citizenship is in heaven"), Revelation shows God descending reality (city comes down) - God bridges gap. **Trajectory**: Abraham sought city "whose designer and builder is God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 10|Hebrews 11:10]]) → believers' citizenship in heaven ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 20|Philippians 3:20]]) → New Jerusalem descends ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]]). **From anticipation to realization**: Philippians - "we await" (future expectation); [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]] - "I saw" (John witnesses fulfillment). [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 21|Philippians 3:21]] - Christ "will transform our lowly body to be like his glorious body" - intermediate fulfillment: resurrection at second coming; ultimate fulfillment: glorified bodies dwelling in New Jerusalem where "they will see his face" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]]). **Citizenship consummated**: Presently, believers have **legal** citizenship in heaven (positional); at consummation, **actual** residence in New Jerusalem (experiential). [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 26|Galatians 4:26]]: "Jerusalem above... is our mother" - present spiritual relationship; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]]: New Jerusalem "**prepared as a bride adorned for her husband**" - future marital union. **From exile to homeland**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 13|Hebrews 11:13]] - "strangers and exiles on the earth"; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 14|Hebrews 13:14]] - "here we have no lasting city"; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - "**the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people**" - eternal homecoming. **Transformation**: [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 21|Philippians 3:21]] promises bodily transformation; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]] shows comprehensive transformation: "death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore, for the former things have passed away." From mortal bodies suited for earth → to glorified bodies suited for new creation. **Political implications**: Philippians' citizenship language subverts Roman empire; Revelation depicts New Jerusalem supplanting Babylon (false city, Revelation 18) - ultimate political reversal. True King (Lamb, [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22-23]]) rules true city forever. From waiting for city → to dwelling in city. From heavenly citizenship as abstract status → to New Jerusalem as concrete reality where "the nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24]]).
