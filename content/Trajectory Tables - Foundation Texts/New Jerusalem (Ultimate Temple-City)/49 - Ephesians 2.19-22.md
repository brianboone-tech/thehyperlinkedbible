### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 19|Ephesians 2:19-22]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- συμπολῖται (*sympolitai*) - "fellow citizens" - shared citizenship (2:19)
- οἰκεῖοι (*oikeioi*) - "members of household" - family belonging (2:19)
- ἐποικοδομηθέντες (*epoikodomēthentes*) - "built on" - constructed upon foundation (2:20)
- ἀκρογωνιαῖος (*akrogōniaios*) - "cornerstone" - foundational stone determining alignment (2:20)
- ναὸν (*naon*) - "temple" - sanctuary, holy dwelling (2:21)
- κατοικητήριον (*katoikētērion*) - "dwelling place" - permanent residence (2:22)

**Context**: Gentile inclusion section (Ephesians 2:11-22). Previously "strangers and aliens" (2:12, 19), now brought near through Christ's blood (2:13). Christ is "our peace" breaking down dividing wall (2:14-18). Follows broader argument about salvation by grace (2:1-10). Part of prison epistle (3:1; 4:1; 6:20).

**Connections**:

  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 27|1 Kings 8:27]] - will God indeed dwell on earth?
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]] - stone that builders rejected has become cornerstone
- **FROM OT**:

- **FROM NT**:

  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4-8]] - living stone, living stones built into spiritual house
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 14|Revelation 21:14]] - twelve foundations with names of twelve apostles of Lamb
**Christological Connection**: Church as living temple fulfills OT temple trajectory through union with Christ. **Escalation**: Solomon's temple = physical building, localized in Jerusalem; church = **global community**, present wherever believers gather. OT temple required pilgrimage to Jerusalem; NT temple is **portable** - Spirit indwells believers everywhere ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]]). OT temple excluded Gentiles beyond outer court; NT temple **incorporates** Gentiles as "fellow heirs, members of same body" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 6|Ephesians 3:6]]). **Christ as cornerstone** (2:20): [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 28#Isaiah 28 . 16|Isaiah 28:16]] promised God would lay cornerstone in Zion; Peter applies to Christ ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 6|1 Peter 2:6-7]]). Rejected stone ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]], crucifixion) becomes chief cornerstone (resurrection/exaltation). **Trajectory**: Tabernacle (Exodus 40) → Solomon's temple (1 Kings 8) → destroyed (586 BC) → Second Temple (Ezra 6) → Christ as living temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]) → **church as spiritual temple** ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]]) → New Jerusalem ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]). **Already/not yet**: Church is presently "being built" (2:22, ongoing) but not yet complete. Consummation: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 14|Revelation 21:14]] - New Jerusalem's wall has "**twelve foundations, and on them twelve names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb**" - apostolic foundation eternalized in eschatological city. **From foundation to fulfillment**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20]] - apostles/prophets are foundation in present age; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]] - "**I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb**" - building imagery transcended because entire reality sanctified. **Dwelling place** ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 22|Ephesians 2:22]]): presently Spirit dwells in church; ultimately "**the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them**" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]) - corporate temple consummated in cosmic dwelling. From living stones being assembled → to glorified temple-city where "the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). Christ is cornerstone now (determining church's alignment); Christ is temple then (filling New Jerusalem with presence). Church's temple-identity anticipates New Jerusalem's temple-reality.
