### [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 6|2 Samuel 5:6-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- יְבוּס (*yevus*) - "Jebus" - pre-Israelite name for Jerusalem (5:6, 8)
- צִיּוֹן (*tsiyyon*) - "Zion" - fortress/stronghold, becomes name for Jerusalem (5:7)
- מְצוּדָה (*metsudah*) - "stronghold, fortress" - defensible citadel (5:7, 9)
- עִיר דָּוִד (*ir David*) - "City of David" - David's capital (5:7, 9)
- מִלּוֹא (*millo*) - "Millo" - fortification or fill/terrace (5:9)

**Context**: David's consolidation of kingdom. Follows anointing as king over all Israel (5:1-5); precedes Philistine conflicts (5:17-25). Conquest of Jerusalem strategic - centrally located, neutral territory between northern and southern tribes. Becomes permanent capital uniting kingdom.

**Christological Connection**: David's conquest of Jerusalem foreshadows Christ establishing eternal city. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 7|2 Samuel 5:7]] - David captures **earthly** stronghold; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22]] - believers come to **heavenly** Jerusalem. David's Jerusalem was physical city in Israel; Christ's Jerusalem is **cosmic city** encompassing new creation ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-2]]). David brought ark to Jerusalem (2 Samuel 6), making it holy; Christ **is** ark (embodying God's presence), making everywhere He dwells holy. David built city "from Millo inward" (5:9); Christ builds church ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 16#Matthew 16 . 18|Matthew 16:18]]: "I will build my church"). David's Jerusalem repeatedly conquered and destroyed; Christ's New Jerusalem "coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]]) - **indestructible**, descending rather than built by human hands. **Trajectory**: Jebusite stronghold → City of David → temple added (Solomon) → destroyed and rebuilt → Jesus enters as King ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 1|Matthew 21:1-11]]) → destroyed again (AD 70) → ultimately replaced by New Jerusalem. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 48#Psalm 48 . 2|Psalm 48:2]]: "Mount Zion... city of the great King" - David ruled there; Christ rules from heavenly Zion ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22-24]]) and will rule from New Jerusalem eternally ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3-5]]). **From limited to universal**: David's city = capital of Israel only; Christ's city = dwelling for redeemed "from every tribe and language and people and nation" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]]). [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]]: "the nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it" - global scope surpassing David's kingdom. From conquerable fortress → to eternal city where "death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]]). What David established temporally, Christ establishes eternally.
