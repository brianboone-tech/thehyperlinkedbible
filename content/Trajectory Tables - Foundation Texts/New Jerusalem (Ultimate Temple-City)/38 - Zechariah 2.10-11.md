### [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 10|Zechariah 2:10-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- רָנִּי (*ranni*) - "sing, rejoice" - joyful exclamation (2:10)
- שָׁכַנְתִּי (*shakhanti*) - "I will dwell" - take up residence (2:10; root of "Shekinah")
- נִלְווּ (*nilvu*) - "join themselves" - attach, unite (2:11)
- גּוֹיִם רַבִּים (*goyim rabbim*) - "many nations" - Gentile multitude (2:11)
- עַם (*am*) - "people" - covenant people (2:11)

**Context**: Part of Zechariah's night visions (chapters 1-6) encouraging post-exilic community rebuilding temple (520-518 BC). Follows vision of man with measuring line (2:1-5) and call to flee Babylon (2:6-9). Precedes vision of Joshua the high priest (chapter 3).

**Christological Connection**: Zechariah's promise of God dwelling with multinational people fulfilled in Christ and New Jerusalem. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 10|Zechariah 2:10]] - "**I will dwell** in your midst" (future promise); [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word "**dwelt** among us" (accomplished fact); [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - "**He will dwell** with them" (eternal consummation). [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 11|Zechariah 2:11]] - "**many nations** shall join... shall be **my people**"; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] - Christ ransomed "from **every tribe and language and people and nation**"; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24]] - "**nations** will walk by its light" - universal fulfillment. **Trajectory of dwelling**: Promise in Zechariah → incarnation in Christ → indwelling Spirit in church → eternal presence in New Jerusalem. Christ is **hinge**: Zechariah prophesied God would dwell; Christ **is** God dwelling ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 23|Matthew 1:23]]: "Immanuel, God with us"). Where Zechariah saw future hope, NT declares: "**Behold, now is the favorable time; behold, now is the day of salvation**" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 2|2 Corinthians 6:2]]) - already/not yet. Presently, Christ dwells through Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 23|John 14:23]]: "we will come to him and make our home with him"); ultimately, unmediated presence ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]]: "they will see his face"). **Gentile inclusion**: Zechariah's "many nations... my people" (2:11) finds intermediate fulfillment in church ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14-18]]: Christ "has made us both one... that he might create in himself one new man") and final fulfillment in New Jerusalem encompassing "every tribe and language and people and nation" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]]). **From localized to universal**: Zechariah - God dwells "in midst of Jerusalem" (8:3); Revelation - New Jerusalem **descends**, uniting heaven and earth (21:2), God dwelling with glorified humanity throughout renewed cosmos (21:3). From temple in one city → to Christ dwelling with disciples → to Spirit indwelling church globally → to New Jerusalem filling creation. Consummation: dwelling so comprehensive that "**I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb**" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]) - entire reality sanctified by divine presence.
