### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3089|λύσατε]] (*lysate*) - "destroy, loose, tear down" (aorist active imperative)
- [[Lexicon/G3485|ναόν]] (*naon*) - "temple, sanctuary" (accusative)
- [[Lexicon/G1453|ἐγερῶ]] (*egerō*) - "I will raise up" (future active)
- [[Lexicon/G5140|τρεῖς]] (*treis*) - "three" (accusative)
- [[Lexicon/G2250|ἡμέραις]] (*hēmerais*) - "in days" (dative plural)
- [[Lexicon/G4983|σώματος]] (*sōmatos*) - "of body" (genitive)
- [[Lexicon/G0450|ἀναστῆναι]] (*anastēnai*) - "to rise, stand up again" (aorist active infinitive)
- [[Lexicon/G3498|νεκρῶν]] (*nekrōn*) - "from dead" (genitive plural)

**Context**: John 2:19-21 records Jesus' cryptic declaration: "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.' The Jews then said, 'It has taken forty-six years to build this temple, and will you raise it up in three days?' But he was speaking about the temple of his body." Christ transforms temple imagery—His resurrection body becomes the true temple, the meeting place between God and humanity. Where Israel accessed God through the Jerusalem temple, believers access God through Christ's glorified body. This radical shift from geographic location to incarnate Person revolutionizes worship and access to God's presence.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]'s declaration—"Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up... He was speaking about the temple of his body"—revolutionizes worship and access to God. Where [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 5|Deuteronomy 12:5]] commanded seeking "the place that the LORD your God will choose," Christ replaces geographic location with incarnate Person. His resurrection body becomes the true temple, the meeting place between God and humanity. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] declares: "the Word became flesh and dwelt [*eskēnōsen*, 'tabernacled'] among us"—Christ's body is God's dwelling. [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 9|Colossians 2:9]] states: "in him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily"—permanent localization exceeding the temple's temporary Shekinah glory. Where [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 11|1 Kings 8:11]] describes "the glory of the LORD" filling Solomon's temple, [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]] describes Christ as "the radiance of the glory of God"—inherent, not reflected, divine glory. The imperative "destroy" (*lysate*) predicts crucifixion—Jewish and Roman authorities will kill Him. The future "I will raise" (*egerō*) demonstrates Christ's resurrection power. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 11|Romans 8:11]] states: "the Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you"—but here Christ actively raises His own body, showing divine sovereignty. The "three days" timeframe proves true: crucified Friday, in tomb Saturday, raised Sunday—confirming Jesus' prophetic authority. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-20]] describes the access Christ provides: "we have confidence to enter the holy places by the blood of Jesus, by the new and living way that he opened for us through the curtain, that is, through his flesh." Christ's body is both curtain (barrier requiring sacrifice) and access point (means of approach). First Corinthians 3:16 extends this: "you are God's temple"—believers corporately become God's dwelling. The trajectory shows: tabernacle as God's dwelling ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]]) → temple as permanent dwelling ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 29|1 Kings 8:29]]) → Christ's body as true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]) → church as God's temple ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]) → individual believers as temples ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]]) → new creation where God and Lamb are temple ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]). The place transforms into Person, then expands to encompass all the redeemed.
