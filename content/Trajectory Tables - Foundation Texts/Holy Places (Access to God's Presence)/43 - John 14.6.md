**Context**: John 14:6 records Jesus' declaration: "I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me." This parallels Deuteronomy 12's exclusive worship location—God designates one place (then), one Person (now). Just as Israel couldn't approach God at unauthorized sites, humanity cannot access God except through Christ. The exclusivity of the central sanctuary prefigures the exclusivity of Christ as Mediator. Where the temple was the designated meeting place, Christ is the designated Person through whom alone we approach the Father. All other paths to God are rejected.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]'s declaration—"I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me"—parallels [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 5|Deuteronomy 12:5]]'s exclusive worship location with exclusive Person. Where God designated one geographic place (temple), He now designates one Person (Christ) through whom alone humanity approaches Him. The exclusivity that characterized Israel's worship—one altar, one temple, one high priest—characterizes Christian salvation: one mediator. First Timothy 2:5 declares: "there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus." [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 9#Job 9 . 32|Job 9:32-33]] lamented the absence of mediator: "There is no arbiter between us, who might lay his hand on us both"—bridging infinite God and finite humanity. Christ fulfills this role perfectly—fully God ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1]]) and fully man ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]), qualified to mediate. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 12|Acts 4:12]] states: "there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved"—absolute exclusivity. This offends modern pluralism but reflects biblical pattern: one ark saved from flood (Genesis 7), one bronze serpent healed snakebites ([[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 9|Numbers 21:9]]), one temple designated for worship, one sacrifice on the Day of Atonement. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-20]] describes access through Christ: "confidence to enter the holy places by the blood of Jesus, by the new and living way that he opened for us through the curtain, that is, through his flesh." Christ's body is both curtain (barrier requiring sacrifice) and way (means of access). The triple predicate—way, truth, life—shows comprehensiveness: Christ isn't merely path-shower but the path itself; not truth-teacher but Truth incarnate; not life-giver but Life inherent. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 25|John 11:25]] declares: "I am the resurrection and the life." [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 4|John 1:4]] states: "In him was life." The trajectory shows: one designated place for worship ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 5|Deuteronomy 12:5]]) → fulfilled in Jerusalem temple ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 29|1 Kings 8:29]]) → Christ's body replaces temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]) → Christ is exclusive way to Father ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]) → access through Christ's blood ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-20]]). The principle of divine designation remains—God chooses how to approach Him—but the place becomes Person, and that Person is the only way to the Father.
