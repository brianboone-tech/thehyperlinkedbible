### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20-22]]

**Context**: Paul describes the cosmic reconciliation accomplished in Christ, bringing Jew and Gentile together into one new humanity. He employs temple-building imagery: believers are "built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone, in whom the whole structure, being joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord. In him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit." This corporate temple imagery connects to rock typology—Christ the cornerstone/rock is foundation, and believers built upon Him become God's dwelling through the Spirit.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20-22]] culminates the rock/stone typology by presenting Christ as cornerstone of God's eschatological temple. The connection to rock-water typology appears through the temple-water link: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1]] envisions water flowing from the temple threshold, bringing life. Christ is both the temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19]]) and the Rock struck ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]), from whom living water flows. Jesus promised: "Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water... this he said about the Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]). [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 22|Ephesians 2:22]] reveals the Spirit's role: believers are "being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit" (*en pneumati*). The Rock struck at Calvary releases the Spirit at Pentecost; the Spirit then builds believers into God's temple. The progression fulfills redemptive history: OT tabernacle/temple housed God's presence but excluded Gentiles and restricted access; Christ the true temple provides unrestricted access to God for all who believe; the church as Christ's body becomes the locus of God's dwelling through the Spirit. The cornerstone function shows Christ's centrality—He is the reference point from which entire structure derives alignment. A building with faulty cornerstone collapses; the church built on Christ stands eternally. The "growing into a holy temple" combines organic and architectural imagery: temples are built, but this temple grows—it's alive because built on the living Stone ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4]]). The foundation is secure: Christ Himself, proclaimed by apostles and prophets. Believers are "fitted together" (*synarmologoumenē*)—each stone in proper place, the whole integrated. This corporate unity reflects Christ's work: "that he might create in himself one new man in place of the two" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 15|Ephesians 2:15]]). The Rock provides water (Spirit) that creates temple (church). What began as water from Horeb's rock, prophesied as river from temple, flows as Spirit from Christ, building living stones into God's eternal dwelling.
