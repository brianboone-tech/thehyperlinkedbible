### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 33|John 19:33-37]]

**Context**: At Jesus' crucifixion, the soldiers come to break the legs of the crucified to hasten death before Sabbath. Finding Jesus already dead, they don't break His legs, fulfilling Scripture ("Not one of his bones will be broken"). However, one soldier pierces Jesus' side with a spear to confirm death, "and at once there came out blood and water." John emphasizes eyewitness testimony to this event and explicitly cites Zechariah 12:10: "They will look on him whom they have pierced." The blood and water flowing from Christ's pierced side fulfill the rock-water typology—the Rock struck yields life-giving water.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 33|John 19:33-37]] provides the New Testament's clearest fulfillment of rock-water typology. The soldier's spear thrust into Christ's side fulfills [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]: "You shall strike the rock, and water shall come out of it." As Moses struck (*nakah*) the rock and water flowed, the soldier pierces (*nyssō*) Christ and "at once there came out blood and water." Paul's identification "the Rock was Christ" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]) enables recognizing this as fulfillment, not mere parallel. The striking/piercing is singular and decisive—Moses struck the rock once at Horeb (repeated striking at Kadesh violated the type); Christ is crucified once for all ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 28|Hebrews 9:28]]: "Christ... offered once to bear the sins of many"). The water flowing from the rock sustained physical life in wilderness; the water (representing the Spirit) flowing from Christ's side provides eternal life. Jesus promised at the Feast of Tabernacles: "Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]]), which John explains as "the Spirit, whom those who believed in him were to receive" (7:39). The prophecy's fulfillment begins at the cross—when Christ is "glorified" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]], referring to crucifixion/resurrection), the Spirit flows from His pierced side. The blood accompanying the water shows completeness: blood for atonement ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]: "the life of the flesh is in the blood... it is the blood that makes atonement"), water for cleansing ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]]: "I will sprinkle clean water on you"). Together they fulfill [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]]: "On that day there shall be a fountain opened for the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and uncleanness"—that fountain is Christ's pierced side. The rock struck at Horeb, prophesied as pierced in Zechariah, fulfilled at Calvary, flows eternally as "the water of life" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]).
