### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4-8]]

**Context**: Peter exhorts believers to "come to him, a living stone rejected by men but in the sight of God chosen and precious" (v. 4), then identifies believers as "living stones" being built into a spiritual house. He weaves together three OT stone prophecies: Isaiah 28:16 (cornerstone in Zion), Psalm 118:22 (rejected stone becomes cornerstone), and Isaiah 8:14 (stone of stumbling). Christ is simultaneously precious cornerstone for believers and stumbling stone for those who disobey. This stone imagery connects to rock typology—Christ the living Stone/Rock is the foundation from which believers derive life and upon which God builds His temple.

**Christological Connection**: First Peter 2:4-8 connects Christ-as-Stone to the broader rock typology. The "living stone" (*lithon zōnta*) designation links Christ's stone identity to His resurrection life—He is living Rock, not dead monument. This connects backward to wilderness provision: Israel drank from "the spiritual Rock that followed them, and the Rock was Christ" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]). That Rock was living because Christ, the eternal Word, provided through it. Peter's "living stone" advances the typology: Christ not only provided water from rock but becomes the foundation Rock upon which God builds His temple. The dual function—cornerstone for believers, stumbling stone for unbelievers—reflects the rock's OT dual role: struck for provision (blessing) or left unstruck (judgment—Israel dies of thirst). Those who come to Christ receive living water; those who reject Him stumble and perish. The corporate dimension—believers as "living stones" built into spiritual house—shows participation in Christ's life. As living water flowed from the struck Rock to Israel, now living Stone-life flows from Christ to believers who become "living stones." The progression advances from external rock providing external water (Exodus 17) to living Stone providing internal Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]) to believers themselves becoming stones in God's temple. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20-22]] parallels Peter's imagery: believers are "built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone, in whom the whole structure, being joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord." The Rock struck at Horeb, the Stone rejected by builders, the Cornerstone laid in Zion—all converge in Christ crucified and risen. From this living Stone flows the life that makes dead sinners into living stones, built together as dwelling place for God's Spirit. The rejected Stone becomes head of the corner ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]])—from lowest place (rejected, crucified) to highest (risen, exalted). The Rock provides eternally.
