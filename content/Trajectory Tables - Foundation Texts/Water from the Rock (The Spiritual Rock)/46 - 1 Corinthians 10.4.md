### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4095|ἔπινον]] (*epinon*) - "they drank" (imperfect, continuous action)
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4152|πνευματικῆς]] (*pneumatikēs*) - "spiritual" (genitive feminine)
- [[Lexicon/G0101-0200#G0190|ἀκολουθούσης]] (*akolouthousēs*) - "following, accompanying" (present participle)
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4073|πέτρας]] (*petras*) - "rock, cliff"
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5547|Χριστός]] (*Christos*) - "Christ, Messiah, Anointed One"

**Context**: Paul warns the Corinthian church against presumption by recalling Israel's wilderness failures. Though all experienced exodus blessings—cloud covering, sea crossing, manna eating, water drinking—most fell in the wilderness under God's judgment. Paul states explicitly: "all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual Rock that followed them, and the Rock was Christ." This identification establishes that Christ himself accompanied Israel in the wilderness, providing living water, making their rejection of Him doubly culpable and providing warning against presuming on grace.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]] (water from rock at Horeb), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 11|Numbers 20:11]] (water from rock at Kadesh), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 15|Deuteronomy 8:15]] (water from flinty rock)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 15|Psalm 78:15-16]] (split rocks in wilderness), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 41|Psalm 105:41]] (rock streams gushed), [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 15|Nehemiah 9:15]] (water from rock for thirst)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10-14]] (living water from Jesus), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] (drink from Christ, rivers of Spirit)

**Christological Connection**: First Corinthians 10:4 provides the NT's explicit hermeneutical key for rock-water typology: "the Rock was Christ." This isn't New Testament retrojection but recognition of Christ's eternal existence and pre-incarnate activity. The Word who "became flesh and dwelt among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) is the same Word who accompanied Israel as the Rock providing water. Paul's identification enables the typological connections: (1) The rock struck at Horeb ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]) prefigures Christ struck in crucifixion; (2) The water gushing from the rock prefigures living water flowing from Christ's pierced side ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]); (3) The rock's ability to satisfy physical thirst prefigures Christ satisfying spiritual thirst: "whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never be thirsty again" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 14|John 4:14]]); (4) The rock following Israel (Paul's *akolouthousēs*) prefigures Christ's promise: "I am with you always, to the end of the age" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 20|Matthew 28:20]]); (5) The single rock providing for entire nation prefigures the one Christ providing for all believers. The identification also explains Numbers 20's significance—Moses was told to speak to the rock, not strike it again, because striking represents Christ's once-for-all sacrifice. To strike repeatedly would be to "crucify the Son of God all over again" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 6|Hebrews 6:6]]). Christ as the Rock demonstrates His deity—Deuteronomy calls Yahweh "the Rock" repeatedly ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 4|Deuteronomy 32:4]], 15, 18, 30-31). [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 8|Isaiah 44:8]] declares "Is there a God besides me? There is no Rock; I know not any." For Paul to identify Christ as the Rock is to identify Him as Yahweh. The wilderness provision thus reveals trinitarian operation: the Father commands striking ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 5|Exodus 17:5-6]]), the Rock struck is Christ, the water flowing is the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]). What began as water from Horeb's rock flows ultimately from Christ the eternal Rock.
