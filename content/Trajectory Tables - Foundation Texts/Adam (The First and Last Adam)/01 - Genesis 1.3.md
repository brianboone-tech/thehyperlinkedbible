### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]]

**Context**: God's first creative word calls light into existence out of darkness, establishing the pattern of divine speech bringing order from chaos. This foundational act of creation is applied by Paul to spiritual regeneration in 2 Corinthians 4:6, where the God who said "Let light shine out of darkness" now shines in believers' hearts. The connection develops Adam typology by showing redemption as new creation—Christ as last Adam brings spiritual light to those in the darkness inherited from Adam's fall.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- וַיֹּאמֶר (*vayomer*) - "and He said" (divine speech as creative power)
- יְהִי (*yehi*) - "let there be" (jussive command bringing reality into existence)
- אוֹר (*or*) - "light" (the first created element, symbolizing life and revelation)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - First instance of creation by divine word; establishes light/darkness contrast pervading Scripture
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 104#Psalm 104 . 2|Psalm 104:2]] - describes God as wrapping Himself in light
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 2|Isaiah 9:2]] - promises light to those in darkness
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 4|John 1:4-5]] - identifies Christ as life and light shining in darkness
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]] - applies creation light to gospel illumination
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 5|1 John 1:5]] - declares God is light

**Christological Connection**: Paul's application of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]] to conversion in [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]] is profoundly christological: "For God, who said, 'Let light shine out of darkness,' has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." The parallel between original creation and new creation is central to Adam typology. Just as God's creative word called physical light from primordial darkness, so His regenerating work calls spiritual light into hearts darkened by Adam's fall. Christ as "the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]) is the agent of this new creation, reversing the spiritual darkness that Adam's sin brought upon humanity.
