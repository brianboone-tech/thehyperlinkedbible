### [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]

**Context**: Paul identifies Christ as "the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation," fulfilling what Genesis 1:26-27 established—humanity created in God's image. This is core Adam typology: the first Adam bore God's image but marred it through sin; Christ as last Adam is the perfect image-bearer who restores humanity to its intended glory. Christ embodies what Adam should have been.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- εἰκὼν τοῦ θεοῦ (*eikōn tou theou*) - "image of God" (perfect representation, echoing Genesis 1:26-27)
- ἀοράτου (*aoratou*) - "invisible" (Christ makes visible what cannot be seen)
- πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*) - "firstborn" (preeminence and sovereignty, not created being)
- πάσης κτίσεως (*pasēs ktiseōs*) - "of all creation" (Christ's supremacy over created order)

**Christological Connection**: Christ as "image of the invisible God" is the definitive statement of Adam typology's fulfillment. Adam was created in God's image ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-27]]) but that image was marred through sin. The effects appear immediately: Adam's son is born "in his own likeness, after his image" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 3|Genesis 5:3]])—no longer simply in God's image but in Adam's fallen image. Christ reverses this: He is not merely "in" God's image but "is" the image of God—perfect, unmarred, complete. Where Adam reflected God imperfectly and then defectively, Christ reflects God perfectly and eternally. Moreover, Christ as "firstborn of all creation" holds the position Adam forfeited—supreme authority over the created order. The trajectory from Adam's image-bearing to Christ's perfect image is the story of redemption: humanity created to image God → that image marred → that image restored and perfected in Christ → believers "being renewed in knowledge after the image of their Creator" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 10|Colossians 3:10]]).
