### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]]

**Context**: Genesis 1:1 establishes the foundational context for Adam typology by declaring God as Creator of all things. This verse is echoed in Isaiah's new creation prophecies (Isaiah 65:17; 66:22), establishing the pattern: original creation → fall through Adam → new creation through Christ as last Adam. The "heavens and earth" language becomes shorthand for the entire created order that Adam was given to rule and that Christ will ultimately restore.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- בְּרֵאשִׁית (*bereshit*) - "in the beginning" (marks the commencement of God's creative work and history)
- בָּרָא (*bara*) - "created" (divine creation ex nihilo, used only of God)
- שָׁמַיִם (*shamayim*) - "heavens" (the cosmic realm above)
- אֶרֶץ (*erets*) - "earth" (the domain given to Adam's dominion)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Foundation for all subsequent creation narrative including Adam's formation ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 7|Genesis 2:7]])
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] - ; 66:22 echo this language for new creation
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 3|Psalm 8:3]] - meditates on God's creative work
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1]] - "In the beginning was the Word") deliberately echoes this verse
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]] - applies creation light to new creation in Christ
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]] - announces new heavens and earth

**Christological Connection**: John's Gospel opens with deliberate echo of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]], identifying Christ as the eternal Word present "in the beginning" and the divine agent through whom "all things were made" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 3|John 1:3]]). This establishes Christ's superiority to Adam: the last Adam is not merely another creature but the Creator become man. Where Adam was formed from dust and placed in a garden, Christ existed before creation and made all things. The trajectory from original creation to new creation runs through Christ—He who created the first heavens and earth will create new heavens and earth ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]]), accomplishing what Adam forfeited through the fall.
