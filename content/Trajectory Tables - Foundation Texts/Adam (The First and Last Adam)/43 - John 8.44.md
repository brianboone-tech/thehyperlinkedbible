### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 44|John 8:44]]

**Context**: Jesus identifies the devil as "a murderer from the beginning" and "a liar and the father of lies," echoing the serpent's deception in Genesis 3:4 ("You will not surely die") and Cain's murder in Genesis 4. This connects to Adam typology by identifying the spiritual enemy behind Adam's fall and the ongoing conflict between the serpent's seed and the woman's seed (Genesis 3:15). Christ as last Adam comes to destroy the devil's works (1 John 3:8).

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἀνθρωποκτόνος (*anthrōpoktonos*) - "murderer/man-slayer" (from the beginning, through tempting Adam to death)
- ἀπ' ἀρχῆς (*ap' archēs*) - "from the beginning" (pointing to Genesis 3's deception)
- ψεύστης (*pseustēs*) - "liar" (the serpent's lie: "you will not surely die")
- πατὴρ αὐτοῦ (*patēr autou*) - "father of it/him" (originator of lies)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus' identification of the devil as "murderer from the beginning" reads Genesis 3 through the lens of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]'s protoevangelium. The serpent's lie ("you will not surely die") was itself murderous—it led to Adam's death and the death of all his posterity ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 12|Romans 5:12]]). The devil has been murdering humanity "from the beginning" through the sin he instigated. Christ as last Adam comes to reverse this: "The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil" ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 8|1 John 3:8]]). Where the first Adam believed the serpent's lie and brought death, the last Adam defeats the liar and brings life. The enmity promised in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] between the serpent and the woman's seed finds its climax in Christ's confrontation with "the father of lies"—and Christ's death and resurrection destroy the one who had the power of death ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14]]).
