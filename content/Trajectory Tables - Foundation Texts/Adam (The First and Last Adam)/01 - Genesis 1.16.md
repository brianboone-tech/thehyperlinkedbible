### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 16|Genesis 1:16]]

**Context**: God creates the sun, moon, and stars to govern day and night—the "greater light" and "lesser light" that rule the heavens. Psalm 8:3 directly references these heavenly lights as the starting point for David's meditation on humanity's dignity: "When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars, which you have set in place, what is man...?" This connection is central to Adam typology, contrasting the vastness of celestial creation with the surprising dignity God granted to humanity.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מָאוֹר (*maor*) - "light/luminary" (light-bearing body)
- גָּדוֹל (*gadol*) - "great/greater" (the sun as greater light)
- קָטָן (*qatan*) - "small/lesser" (the moon as lesser light)
- מֶמְשֶׁלֶת (*memsheleth*) - "dominion/rule" (the lights govern time periods)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Establishes the luminaries within created order that humans will rule over
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 3|Psalm 8:3]] - explicitly references these heavenly lights when meditating on human dignity
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 136#Psalm 136 . 7|Psalm 136:7-9]] - praises God for sun, moon, and stars
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 5|Hebrews 2:5-9]] - applies Psalm 8's meditation christologically to Jesus
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]] - declares the Lamb is the light of New Jerusalem, surpassing sun and moon

**Christological Connection**: The Psalm 8 connection is crucial for Adam typology. David gazes at the moon and stars ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 16|Genesis 1:16]]), marvels at their grandeur, and asks "What is man that you are mindful of him?" ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 4|Psalm 8:4]]). The answer—humanity crowned with glory and given dominion—reflects [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 6|Hebrews 2:6-9]] applies this directly to Christ: "We do not yet see everything in subjection to him. But we see him who for a little while was made lower than the angels, namely Jesus, crowned with glory and honor." The celestial lights that prompted David's meditation point ultimately to Christ as the true man who fulfills humanity's calling. Where the first Adam failed to exercise faithful dominion, the last Adam succeeds—and in the new creation, even the sun and moon give way to Christ's glory ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]).
