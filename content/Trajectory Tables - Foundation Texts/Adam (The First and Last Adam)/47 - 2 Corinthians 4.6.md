### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]]

**Context**: Paul adapts Genesis 1:3 ("Let light shine out of darkness") to describe spiritual illumination in conversion: "For God, who said, 'Let light shine out of darkness,' has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." This connects to Adam typology by showing redemption as new creation—Christ as last Adam brings light to those in darkness, reversing the spiritual darkness resulting from Adam's fall.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ὁ εἰπὼν ἐκ σκότους φῶς λάμψει (*ho eipon ek skotous phōs lampsei*) - "the one who said, 'Out of darkness light shall shine'" (echoing Genesis 1:3)
- ἔλαμψεν (*elampsen*) - "has shone" (aorist, decisive divine action in conversion)
- πρόσωπον (*prosōpon*) - "face" (glory of God visible in Christ's face)
- δόξα (*doxa*) - "glory" (what Adam lost, Christ reveals and restores)

**Christological Connection**: Paul's application of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]] to conversion is profoundly christological and central to Adam typology. The original creation began with God speaking light into darkness; the new creation begins with God shining light into darkened hearts. Adam's fall plunged humanity into spiritual darkness—"darkened in their understanding" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 18|Ephesians 4:18]]). Christ as last Adam brings light to those in this Adamic darkness. But the parallel runs deeper: the light God shines is "the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." Adam was created to reflect God's glory but forfeited it through sin ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 23|Romans 3:23]], "all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God"). Christ is the perfect image of God ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]), the radiance of God's glory ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). The new creation in Christ restores what Adam lost—believers are "being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]).
