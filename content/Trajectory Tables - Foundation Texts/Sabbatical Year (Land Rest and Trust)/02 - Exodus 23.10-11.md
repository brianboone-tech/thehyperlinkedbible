### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 10|Exodus 23:10-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8058|H8058]] שָׁמַט (*shamat*) - to release, let drop, let fall
- [[Lexicon/H5203|H5203]] נָטַשׁ (*natash*) - to leave, abandon, forsake
- [[Lexicon/H7676|H7676]] שַׁבָּת (*shabbat*) - sabbath, rest

**Context**: Exodus 23:10-11: "For six years you shall sow your land and gather in its yield, but the seventh year you shall let it rest (תִּשְׁמְטֶנָּה) and lie fallow (וּנְטַשְׁתָּהּ), that the poor of your people may eat; and what they leave the beasts of the field may eat."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This is the first mention of agricultural sabbath rest
- Expanded in [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 1|Leviticus 25:1-7]] with more detail
- Connected to Sabbath principle (Gen 2:2-3; Exod 20:8-11)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] - God rested on seventh day (pattern)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]] - Weekly Sabbath commandment
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 1|Leviticus 25:1-7]] - Detailed sabbatical legislation
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 34|Leviticus 26:34-35]] - Threatened judgment for violation
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 6#Matthew 6 . 25|Matthew 6:25-34]] - Trust in divine provision

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded institution) - Forward-looking in that it embeds trust in future divine provision

**Christological Connection**: The sabbatical year required Israel to trust God for provision before they could obey. This anticipates Christ's teaching: "Seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you" (Matt 6:33). The land's rest points to Christ who gives rest to all who come to Him (Matt 11:28-30).
