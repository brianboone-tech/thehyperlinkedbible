### [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 4#Philippians 4 . 19|Philippians 4:19]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 5|Hebrews 13:5-6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4137|G4137]] πληρόω (*pleroo*) - to fill, fulfill, complete
- [[Lexicon/G5532|G5532]] χρεία (*chreia*) - need, necessity
- [[Lexicon/G4149|G4149]] πλοῦτος (*ploutos*) - riches, wealth
- [[Lexicon/G714|G714]] ἀρκέω (*arkeo*) - to be sufficient, content
- [[Lexicon/G5366|G5366]] φιλάργυρος (*philarguros*) - loving money, avaricious

**Context**: Philippians 4:19: "And my God will supply (πληρώσει) every need (χρείαν) of yours according to his riches (πλοῦτος) in glory in Christ Jesus."

Hebrews 13:5-6: "Keep your life free from love of money, and be content (ἀρκούμενοι) with what you have, for he has said, 'I will never leave you nor forsake you.' So we can confidently say, 'The Lord is my helper; I will not fear; what can man do to me?'"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Hebrews quotes [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 6|Deuteronomy 31:6]], 8 and [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 5|Joshua 1:5]] (divine presence promise)
- Also quotes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 6|Psalm 118:6]] (confidence in God as helper)
- Sabbatical year trust becomes permanent spiritual disposition

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 20|Leviticus 25:20-22]] - God provides for obedient faith
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 6|Deuteronomy 31:6]], 8 - "I will not leave you or forsake you"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 5|Joshua 1:5]] - "I will not leave you or forsake you"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 6|Psalm 118:6]] - "The LORD is on my side; I will not fear"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 6#Matthew 6 . 25|Matthew 6:25-34]] - Do not be anxious about provision

**Type Classification**: NT Application - Sabbatical year trust internalized as ongoing spiritual disposition

**Christological Connection**: God's supply is "according to his riches in glory IN CHRIST JESUS." The sabbatical year provision pointed to Christ as the source of all blessing. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 5|Hebrews 13:5-6]] grounds contentment in Christ's promise never to leave or forsake His people. The sabbatical year required trust for one year; in Christ, believers live by continuous trust. "The Lord is my helper" fulfills what the sixth-year provision typified - God enables obedient faith.
