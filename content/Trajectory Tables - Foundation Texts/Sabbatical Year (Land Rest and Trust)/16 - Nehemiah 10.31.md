### [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 31|Nehemiah 10:31]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7676|H7676]] שַׁבָּת (*shabbat*) - sabbath
- [[Lexicon/H5203|H5203]] נָטַשׁ (*natash*) - to leave, let lie fallow
- [[Lexicon/H7651|H7651]] שֶׁבַע (*sheva*) - seven
- [[Lexicon/H4853|H4853]] מַשָּׁא (*massa*) - load, burden
- [[Lexicon/H3027|H3027]] יָד (*yad*) - hand (idiom for debt)

**Context**: Nehemiah 10:31: "And if the peoples of the land bring in goods or any grain on the Sabbath day to sell, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day. And we will forego the crops of the seventh year and the exaction of every debt."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Post-exilic covenant renewal includes sabbatical year commitment
- Community had learned from exile (2 Chr 36:21)
- Combines weekly Sabbath, annual sabbatical, and debt release

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 10|Exodus 23:10-11]] - Original sabbatical year command
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 1|Deuteronomy 15:1-2]] - Debt release legislation
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 21|2 Chronicles 36:21]] - Exile for violated sabbaths
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 5#Nehemiah 5 . 1|Nehemiah 5:1-11]] - Nehemiah's economic reforms
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 44|Acts 2:44-45]]; 4:32-35 - Early church economic sharing

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Renewed commitment to commanded institution after experiencing judgment for violation

**Christological Connection**: The post-exilic community's recommitment demonstrates the pattern of restoration after judgment. This anticipates the new covenant community's economic sharing in Acts 2 and 4. As Israel emerged from exile with renewed commitment to sabbatical principles, the church emerges from Christ's atoning work with transformed economic relationships. "There was not a needy person among them" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 34|Acts 4:34]]) echoes [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 4|Deuteronomy 15:4]]'s promise: "There will be no poor among you."
