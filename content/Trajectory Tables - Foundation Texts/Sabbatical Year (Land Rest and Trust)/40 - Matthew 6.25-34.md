### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 6#Matthew 6 . 25|Matthew 6:25-34]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3309|G3309]] μεριμνάω (*merimnao*) - to be anxious, worry
- [[Lexicon/G5315|G5315]] φαγεῖν (*phagein*) - to eat
- [[Lexicon/G2068|G2068]] ἐσθίω (*esthio*) - to eat
- [[Lexicon/G932|G932]] βασιλεία (*basileia*) - kingdom
- [[Lexicon/G1343|G1343]] δικαιοσύνη (*dikaiosyne*) - righteousness

**Context**: Matthew 6:25-26, 31-33: "Therefore I tell you, do not be anxious (μὴ μεριμνᾶτε) about your life, what you will eat (φάγητε)... Look at the birds of the air: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them... Therefore do not be anxious, saying, 'What shall we eat?'... But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Jesus echoes [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 20|Leviticus 25:20]]: "What shall we eat in the seventh year?"
- Same question, same divine answer: trust and provision
- Sabbatical year faith extended to daily discipleship

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 20|Leviticus 25:20-22]] - "What shall we eat?" with divine promise
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16#Exodus 16 . 4|Exodus 16:4-5]] - Daily manna provision
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 3|Deuteronomy 8:3]] - Man lives by every word from God
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 4#Philippians 4 . 6|Philippians 4:6-7]] - Be anxious for nothing
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 5#1 Peter 5 . 7|1 Peter 5:7]] - Cast all anxieties on him

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Christ teaches what the sabbatical year typified: radical trust in divine provision

**Christological Connection**: Jesus answers the sabbatical year question ("What shall we eat?") with Himself. As God promised supernatural provision in the sixth year, Jesus promises that the Father "knows you need" all these things. The sabbatical year required faith for one year in seven; Christ calls disciples to perpetual faith. "Seek first the kingdom" is the sabbatical year principle made permanent - trust God's provision, obey His commands, receive His blessing.
