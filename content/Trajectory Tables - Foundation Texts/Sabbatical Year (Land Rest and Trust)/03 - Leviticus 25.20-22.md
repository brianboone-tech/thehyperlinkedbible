### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 20|Leviticus 25:20-22]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1293|H1293]] בְּרָכָה (*berakah*) - blessing
- [[Lexicon/H6680|H6680]] צָוָה (*tsavah*) - to command, ordain
- [[Lexicon/H8393|H8393]] תְּבוּאָה (*tevu'ah*) - produce, yield

**Context**: Leviticus 25:20-22: "And if you say, 'What shall we eat in the seventh year, if we may not sow or gather in our crop?' I will command (וְצִוִּיתִי) my blessing (אֶת־בִּרְכָתִי) on you in the sixth year, so that it will produce a crop sufficient for three years. When you sow in the eighth year, you will be eating some of the old crop; you shall eat the old until the ninth year."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- God anticipates Israel's anxiety and answers before they ask
- Divine blessing is "commanded" - supernatural intervention guaranteed
- Three-year provision demonstrates abundance beyond natural expectation

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16#Exodus 16 . 4|Exodus 16:4-5]], 22-26 - Double manna on sixth day
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 1|Leviticus 25:1-7]] - Sabbatical year legislation
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 3|Deuteronomy 8:3]] - Man lives by every word from God's mouth
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 6#Matthew 6 . 25|Matthew 6:25-34]] - Do not be anxious about provision
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 4#Philippians 4 . 19|Philippians 4:19]] - God will supply every need

**Type Classification**: Direct Type with Promise - Forward-looking faith required; provision precedes obedience

**Christological Connection**: God's question "What shall we eat?" and answer anticipates Christ's teaching in [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 6#Matthew 6 . 31|Matthew 6:31-33]]. Just as the sixth-year blessing enabled sabbatical year faith, so Christ's provision enables kingdom living. The command "seek first the kingdom" is possible because "your heavenly Father knows that you need them all." Faith in Christ's provision produces the same radical trust the sabbatical year demanded.
