### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 19|Romans 8:19-23]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2937|G2937]] κτίσις (*ktisis*) - creation
- [[Lexicon/G603|G603]] ἀποκαραδοκία (*apokaradokia*) - eager expectation
- [[Lexicon/G1659|G1659]] ἐλευθερόω (*eleutheroo*) - to set free, liberate
- [[Lexicon/G5356|G5356]] φθορά (*phthora*) - corruption, decay
- [[Lexicon/G2671|G2671]] κατάρα (*katara*) - curse

**Context**: Romans 8:19-21: "For the creation waits with eager longing for the revealing of the sons of God... the creation itself will be set free (ἐλευθερωθήσεται) from its bondage to corruption (φθορᾶς) and obtain the freedom of the glory of the children of God."

Revelation 22:1-3: "Then the angel showed me the river of the water of life... on either side of the river, the tree of life... No longer will there be any curse (κατάθεμα)."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Land's sabbath rest (Lev 25) pointed to creation's ultimate liberation
- Exile judgment on land (Lev 26:34) reversed in new creation
- Curse of Genesis 3 finally removed

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]] - Ground cursed because of Adam
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 1|Leviticus 25:1-7]] - Land's sabbath rest
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 34|Leviticus 26:34-35]] - Land "enjoys" its sabbaths during exile
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17-25]] - New heavens and new earth
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 13|2 Peter 3:13]] - New heavens and new earth

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Ultimate fulfillment of what the sabbatical year typified

**Christological Connection**: The sabbatical year taught that land belongs to God and needs rest. This anticipates creation's "bondage to corruption" being replaced by "the freedom of the glory of the children of God." Christ's redemption extends to all creation (Col 1:20). The land's enforced sabbath during exile (Lev 26:34) pointed to creation's longing for liberation. In the new creation, there is "no longer any curse" - the sabbatical year's temporary rest becomes eternal. The tree of life yields fruit perpetually, and labor's curse is ended forever.
