### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 1|Leviticus 25:1-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7676|H7676]] שַׁבָּת (*shabbat*) - sabbath, rest
- [[Lexicon/H7677|H7677]] שַׁבָּתוֹן (*shabbaton*) - sabbath observance, complete rest
- [[Lexicon/H8141|H8141]] שָׁנָה (*shanah*) - year
- [[Lexicon/H2232|H2232]] זָרַע (*zara*) - to sow
- [[Lexicon/H2168|H2168]] זָמַר (*zamar*) - to prune

**Context**: Leviticus 25:1-7: "The LORD spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai, saying... When you come into the land that I give you, the land shall keep a sabbath to the LORD (שַׁבַּת לַיהוָה). For six years you shall sow your field... but in the seventh year there shall be a sabbath of solemn rest for the land (שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן), a sabbath to the LORD."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Expands [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 10|Exodus 23:10-11]] with comprehensive legislation
- "Sabbath to the LORD" language parallels weekly Sabbath (holy to the LORD)
- Links agricultural cycle to covenant relationship

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 10|Exodus 23:10-11]] - First mention of sabbatical year
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] - Sabbath pattern established at creation
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 20|Leviticus 25:20-22]] - Promise of provision
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 34|Leviticus 26:34-35]] - Judgment for violation
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-10]] - Sabbath rest remains for God's people

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded) - The land's sabbath is explicitly "to the LORD" (לַיהוָה), indicating sacred purpose

**Christological Connection**: The land keeping "sabbath to the LORD" personifies creation's participation in rest. This anticipates [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 19|Romans 8:19-22]] where creation "waits with eager longing" for liberation. Christ's redemption extends to all creation (Col 1:20), and the new creation will experience eternal sabbath rest where there is no more curse (Rev 22:3).
