### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 34|Leviticus 26:34-35, 43]]; [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 21|2 Chronicles 36:21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7521|H7521]] רָצָה (*ratsah*) - to be pleased with, accept, enjoy
- [[Lexicon/H7673|H7673]] שָׁבַת (*shavat*) - to cease, rest
- [[Lexicon/H8074|H8074]] שָׁמֵם (*shamem*) - to be desolate

**Context**: Leviticus 26:34-35: "Then the land shall enjoy (תִּרְצֶה) its sabbaths as long as it lies desolate, while you are in your enemies' land; then the land shall rest (תִּשְׁבַּת), and enjoy its sabbaths. As long as it lies desolate it shall have rest, the rest that it did not have on your sabbaths when you were dwelling in it."

2 Chronicles 36:21: "to fulfill the word of the LORD by the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had enjoyed (רָצְתָה) its sabbaths. All the days that it lay desolate it kept sabbath, to fulfill seventy years."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Leviticus threatens; 2 Chronicles records fulfillment
- 70-year exile = 70 missed sabbatical years (490 years of violation)
- The land itself "enjoys" what Israel denied it

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 1|Leviticus 25:1-7]] - Sabbatical year commanded
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 11|Jeremiah 25:11-12]]; 29:10 - Seventy years prophesied
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 9#Daniel 9 . 2|Daniel 9:2]] - Daniel reads Jeremiah's seventy years
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 19|Romans 8:19-22]] - Creation groans, awaiting liberation

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - The exile itself becomes typological (Daniel's "seventy weeks" builds on seventy years)

**Christological Connection**: The land's enforced rest demonstrates that God's creation ordinances cannot be indefinitely violated. This points to Christ's redemption of creation itself. As the land "enjoyed" rest during exile, so creation "waits with eager longing" for the revealing of the sons of God (Rom 8:19). Christ's redemption extends to all creation, ensuring the eternal sabbath rest that Israel's disobedience delayed.
