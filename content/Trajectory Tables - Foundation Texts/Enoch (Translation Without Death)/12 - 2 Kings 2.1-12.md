### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 2#2 Kings 2 . 1|2 Kings 2:1-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5927|H5927]] עָלָה (alah) - "went up" - ascended into heaven
- [[Lexicon/H5591|H5591]] סְעָרָה (se'arah) - "whirlwind/storm" - divine vehicle of translation
- [[Lexicon/H7393|H7393]] רֶכֶב (rekeb) - "chariot" - the chariot of fire
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (esh) - "fire" - divine presence/glory
- [[Lexicon/H3947|H3947]] לָקַח (laqach) - "took" - same word used for Enoch (Gen 5:24)

**Context**: Elijah's translation occurs at the climax of his prophetic ministry, after decades of contending against Baal worship in Israel. The narrative builds tension through the threefold journey (Gilgal → Bethel → Jericho → Jordan), with the sons of the prophets repeatedly confirming what Elisha knows: "the LORD will take your master today." Unlike Enoch's quiet removal, Elijah's departure is dramatic, public, and attended by heavenly fire.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 4#Malachi 4 . 5|Malachi 4:5]] prophesies Elijah's return before "the great and dreadful day of the LORD"
- The chariot/horses of fire connect to theophanies throughout Scripture (Ezek 1; Ps 68:17)
- The mantle transfer to Elisha parallels Moses → Joshua succession

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Enoch's translation (Gen 5:24) establishes the pattern; Elijah confirms it is repeatable
- **FROM OT**:
  - Moses' mysterious burial (Deut 34:5-6) and appearance at transfiguration
- **FROM NT**:
  - Jesus' transfiguration with Moses and Elijah (Matt 17:3); Christ's ascension ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 9|Acts 1:9-11]]); John the Baptist as "Elijah" (Matt 11:14)
**Type Classification**: Providential (sovereignly arranged), Forward-looking (explicit eschatological expectations in Malachi)

**Christological Connection**: Elijah's ascension in a chariot of fire anticipates Christ's ascension in glory. Owen observes the connection: as Elisha received a double portion of Elijah's spirit after witnessing the ascension, so the apostles received the Holy Spirit after witnessing Christ's ascension. The pattern of translation → Spirit-empowered succession is fulfilled in Christ → Church.
