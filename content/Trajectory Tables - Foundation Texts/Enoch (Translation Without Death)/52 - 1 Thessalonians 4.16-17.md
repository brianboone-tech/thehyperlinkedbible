### [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16-17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2597|G2597]] καταβαίνω (katabainō) - "will descend" - Christ's return from heaven
- [[Lexicon/G726|G726]] ἁρπάζω (harpazō) - "caught up" - snatched/seized forcefully (rapture)
- [[Lexicon/G3507|G3507]] νεφέλαι (nephelai) - "clouds" - divine glory/theophany
- [[Lexicon/G529|G529]] ἀπάντησις (apantēsis) - "to meet" - technical term for welcoming a dignitary
- [[Lexicon/G109|G109]] ἀήρ (aēr) - "air" - the atmospheric realm

**Context**: Paul addresses the Thessalonians' concern about believers who have died before Christ's return. He reveals a "mystery" (cf. 1 Cor 15:51): the dead in Christ will rise first, then living believers will be "caught up" together with them to meet the Lord. This represents the corporate fulfillment of what Enoch experienced individually.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Enoch's "taking" (Gen 5:24 - לָקַח) becomes corporate for believers
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 2|Daniel 12:2]] - resurrection of the dead
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 19|Isaiah 26:19]] - "Your dead shall live; their bodies shall rise"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Enoch (Gen 5:24) and Elijah (2 Kgs 2:11) as individual translations; this is the corporate application
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|Daniel 12:1-2]] - resurrection at the end
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 5|Zechariah 14:5]] - coming with holy ones
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 51|1 Corinthians 15:51-52]] - parallel teaching on transformation
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 3|John 14:3]] - "I will come again and receive you to Myself"
**Type Classification**: NT Application - What was exceptional for Enoch becomes normal for believers

**Christological Connection**: Owen's insight proves true: "Faith in God through Christ hath an efficacy in the procuring of such grace, mercy, and favor in particular, as it hath no ground in particular to believe." Enoch could not have anticipated that his unique experience would become the hope of millions. What God did for one man by sovereign choice, He will do for all who are in Christ. The "catching up" (rapture) extends Enoch's blessing to the entire body of Christ.
