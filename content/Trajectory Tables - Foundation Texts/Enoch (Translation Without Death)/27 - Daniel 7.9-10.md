### [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 9|Daniel 7:9-10]]

**Aramaic/Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6268|H6268]] עַתִּיק יוֹמִין (Attiq Yomin) - "Ancient of Days" - divine title
- [[Lexicon/H7240|H7240]] רִבּוֹ (ribbo) - "myriad/ten thousand" - countless multitude
- [[Lexicon/H6925|H6925]] קֳדָם (qodam) - "before" - in the presence of
- [[Lexicon/H8399|H8399]] תְּקוֹמֵם (teqomem) - "stood" - ministering before the throne

**Context**: Daniel's night vision portrays the divine throne room where the Ancient of Days sits in judgment. Four beasts representing world empires are judged, and dominion is given to "one like a son of man" (v. 13-14). The scene depicts the eschatological judgment attended by myriads of holy ones—the same imagery Enoch prophesied and Jude quotes.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 2|Deuteronomy 33:2]] - "The LORD came from Sinai... with myriads of holy ones"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 68#Psalm 68 . 17|Psalm 68:17]] - "The chariots of God are tens of thousands, thousands upon thousands"
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 5|Zechariah 14:5]] - "The LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with Him"
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 1#Job 1 . 6|Job 1:6]]; 2:1 - Heavenly council scenes with "sons of God"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Enoch's prophecy (preserved in Jude 14-15) draws from this prophetic stream
- **FROM OT**:
  - Moses' blessing (Deut 33:2) establishes the "coming with holy ones" motif
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 11|Revelation 5:11]] - "myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands"
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|Matthew 25:31]] - Son of Man coming in glory with angels
**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (explicitly prophetic and eschatological)

**Christological Connection**: The Ancient of Days scene anticipates Christ's coming in glory. The "Son of Man" who receives dominion (Dan 7:13-14) is the ascended Christ who will return with His holy ones. Enoch's translation pointed to this reality—the faithful will accompany the Lord when He comes to execute judgment, fulfilling what Enoch prophesied.
