### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 9|Acts 1:9-11]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1869|G1869]] ἐπαίρω (epairō) - "was lifted up" - passive elevation
- [[Lexicon/G3507|G3507]] νεφέλη (nephelē) - "cloud" - divine glory cloud/Shekinah
- [[Lexicon/G5274|G5274]] ὑπολαμβάνω (hypolambanō) - "received/took up" - caught away from sight
- [[Lexicon/G353|G353]] ἀναλαμβάνω (analambanō) - "taken up" (v. 11) - ascension
- [[Lexicon/G2064|G2064]] ἔρχομαι (erchomai) - "will come" - parousia promise

**Context**: Jesus' ascension occurs forty days after His resurrection, with the apostles as witnesses. The cloud receives Him—echoing OT theophany—and two angels announce His return "in the same way." This forms the hinge between Jesus' earthly ministry and the Church's mission, bookended by promise and hope.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The cloud motif connects to Sinai theophany (Exod 19:16), tabernacle glory (Exod 40:34), and Daniel's Son of Man vision (Dan 7:13)
- The "taken up" language echoes both Enoch (Gen 5:24 LXX: μετέθηκεν) and Elijah (2 Kgs 2:11 LXX)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Enoch's translation (Gen 5:24); Elijah's ascension (2 Kgs 2:11)
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] - Son of Man coming with clouds
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] - seated at right hand
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 8|Ephesians 4:8-10]] - ascended leading captivity captive
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 14|Hebrews 4:14]] - passed through heavens
**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Christ as the ultimate translated one

**Christological Connection**: Christ's ascension fulfills and transcends all OT translations. Owen notes: "Enoch was translated by faith; yet did not Enoch believe he should be translated, until he had a particular revelation of it." Christ, however, announced His own departure and return. Where Enoch was taken from death, Christ conquered death first. Where Elijah left a successor with doubled spirit, Christ sent the Holy Spirit Himself to indwell all believers.
