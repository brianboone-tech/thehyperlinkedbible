### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 7|Leviticus 4:7, 17-18, 25, 30]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1801-1900#H1818|H1818]] דָּם (dam) - blood
- [[Lexicon/H7101-7200#H7161|H7161]] קֶרֶן (qeren) - horn (plural קַרְנוֹת qarnot)
- [[Lexicon/H4501-4600#H4567|H4567]] מִזְבֵּחַ (mizbeach) - altar
- [[Lexicon/H5301-5400#H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (natan) - to give, put, place

**Context**: In the sin offering procedure, the priest places blood "on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense" (for priest/congregation) or "on the horns of the altar of burnt offering" (for ruler/individual). The horns—projections at the four corners—were places of power and appeal.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 27#Exodus 27 . 2|Exodus 27:2]] - Altar horns as part of the original design
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 1#1 Kings 1 . 50|1 Kings 1:50]] - Adonijah grasps altar horns for refuge
- [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 3#Amos 3 . 14|Amos 3:14]] - "The horns of the altar shall be cut off and fall to the ground"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 10|Genesis 4:10]] - Blood's voice now channeled through sacrificial system
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 9#Revelation 9 . 13|Revelation 9:13]] - "A voice from the four horns of the golden altar"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]] - Blood that "speaks better things"

**Type Classification**: **Direct** - The altar horns amplify blood's "voice" in the divine hearing

**Christological Connection**: Andrew Bonar observed that blood on the altar horns represented "the strong appeal to God made by atonement." The horns symbolized strength and power; blood placed there made its appeal with maximum force. This transforms Abel's blood-cry from ground-level accusation to altar-level intercession. The Levitical system doesn't silence blood's voice—it redirects it. Instead of crying for vengeance against sinners, sacrificial blood cries for mercy on behalf of sinners. Christ's blood, placed on the heavenly altar, makes the ultimate appeal.
