**Context**: Blood is applied to specific body parts: "You shall take part of the blood... and put it on the lobe of Aaron's right ear and on the lobes of his sons' right ears and on the thumbs of their right hands and on the great toes of their right feet... Then you shall take part of the blood... and part of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it on Aaron and his garments." The right ear (hearing God's word), right thumb (serving God), and right toe (walking in God's ways) receive blood—consecrating the entire person for holy service. The combination of blood and oil prefigures Christ's sacrifice and Spirit's work in redemption.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 20|Exodus 29:20-21]] describes blood applied to Aaron's right ear, right thumb, and right toe, then blood and oil sprinkled on his garments—total consecration through sacrifice and anointing. The ear sanctified for hearing God's word, the thumb for serving with hands, the toe for walking in obedience—the entire priest consecrated for ministry. [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 23|Leviticus 8:23-24]] records Moses applying this blood: visible testimony that even mediators needed atonement to serve. This prefigures Christ's perfect consecration through His own blood. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11-12]] declares Christ "entered once for all into the holy places, not by means of the blood of goats and calves but by means of his own blood, thus securing an eternal redemption." Where Aaron was consecrated through a ram's blood, Christ consecrated Himself through His own sacrifice. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 19|John 17:19]] records Jesus' prayer: "for their sake I consecrate myself, that they also may be sanctified in truth"—voluntary self-consecration to death accomplishing what Aaron's consecration symbolized. Christ's perfect obedience fulfilled what the blood application represented: ear (hearing Father's voice perfectly—"the Son can do nothing of his own accord, but only what he sees the Father doing," [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 19|John 5:19]]), hand (serving unto death—"he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross," [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 8|Philippians 2:8]]), foot (walking in God's ways flawlessly—"I always do the things that are pleasing to him," [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 29|John 8:29]]). Where Aaron's consecration was external, Christ's was internal; where Aaron's was partial, Christ's was complete; where Aaron's was through another's blood, Christ consecrated through His own. Believers share this consecration. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 22|Hebrews 10:22]] exhorts: "let us draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith, with our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience"—Christ's blood cleansing and consecrating for service. First Peter 1:2 describes believers "chosen... for obedience to Jesus Christ and for sprinkling with his blood." [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1]] calls for presenting bodies "as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God"—entire person (ears hearing Word, hands serving others, feet walking in righteousness) consecrated through Christ's blood. The trajectory shows: Aaron consecrated through ram's blood (external, temporary, repeated) → Christ consecrated through His own blood (internal, eternal, once-for-all) → believers consecrated through Christ's blood (participation, permanent, complete)—cleansed and empowered for priestly service to God.
