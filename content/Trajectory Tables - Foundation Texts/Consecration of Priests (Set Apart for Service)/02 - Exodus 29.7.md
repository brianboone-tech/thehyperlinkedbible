### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 7|Exodus 29:7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3947|וְלָקַחְתָּ]] (*wəlāqaḥtā*) - "and you shall take"
- [[Lexicon/H8081|שֶׁמֶן]] (*šemen*) - "oil"
- [[Lexicon/H4888|הַמִּשְׁחָה]] (*hammišḥâ*) - "the anointing" (from māšaḥ, to anoint)
- [[Lexicon/H3332|וְיָצַקְתָּ]] (*wəyāṣaqtā*) - "and you shall pour"
- [[Lexicon/H7218|רֹאשׁוֹ]] (*rōʾšô*) - "his head" (with suffix)
- [[Lexicon/H4886|וּמָשַׁחְתָּ]] (*ûmāšaḥtā*) - "and you shall anoint" (Qal perfect)

**Context**: After washing and robing, "You shall take the anointing oil and pour it on his head and anoint him." The oil symbolized the Holy Spirit's empowerment for ministry. Anointing consecrated Aaron for his mediatorial role—prophet (teaching), priest (interceding), and prefiguring the kingly office. The oil poured abundantly on the head (Psalm 133:2) signified lavish divine enabling. This prefigures Christ's anointing with the Spirit "without measure" (John 3:34) and believers receiving the Spirit's indwelling.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 7|Exodus 29:7]] commands pouring anointing oil on Aaron's head, prefiguring Christ's Spirit-anointing as ultimate High Priest. The oil symbolized the Holy Spirit's empowerment for ministry—Aaron needed divine enabling to serve as mediator between God and Israel. [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 12|Leviticus 8:12]] describes Moses pouring oil "on Aaron's head and anointed him to consecrate him." [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 133#Psalm 133 . 2|Psalm 133:2]] celebrates this anointing: "like the precious oil on the head, running down on the beard, on the beard of Aaron, running down on the collar of his robes"—abundant outpouring signifying lavish divine blessing. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 1|Isaiah 61:1]] prophesied the Messiah's anointing: "The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me, because the LORD has anointed me to bring good news to the poor." [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18]] records Jesus applying this to Himself: "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me." At His baptism, "the Holy Spirit descended on him in bodily form, like a dove" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 22|Luke 3:22]])—visible anointing authenticating His messianic identity. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 10#Acts 10 . 38|Acts 10:38]] declares "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power." Where Aaron received oil symbolizing Spirit, Christ received the Spirit Himself. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 34|John 3:34]] explains: God "gives the Spirit without measure"—infinite anointing for infinite ministry. The title "Christ" (Christos) means "Anointed One"—Jesus is THE Messiah, fulfilling all anointed offices: Prophet (teaching with authority), Priest (mediating through His blood), King (reigning over all). [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 9|Hebrews 1:9]] declares: "God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness beyond your companions"—Christ's anointing surpasses all others. Believers share this anointing. Second Corinthians 1:21-22 states: "it is God who... has anointed us... and given us his Spirit in our hearts." First [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 20|John 2:20]], 27 speaks of believers' "anointing from the Holy One"—the Spirit indwelling and teaching all who are in Christ. The trajectory shows: Aaron anointed with oil (symbol, temporary empowerment) → Christ anointed with Spirit (reality, infinite power) → believers anointed with Spirit (participation, permanent indwelling). What was external ritual becomes internal reality; what was symbolic becomes substantial; what empowered one high priest now indwells all God's royal priesthood.
