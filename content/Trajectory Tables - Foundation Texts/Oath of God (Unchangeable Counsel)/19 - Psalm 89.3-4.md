### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 3|Psalm 89:3-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1201-1300#H1285|H1285]] בְּרִית (bĕrît) - "covenant" (v. 3). God's sworn covenant with David.
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7650|H7650]] שָׁבַע (shābaʿ) - "to swear" (Niphal: "I have sworn," v. 3).
- [[Lexicon/H2201-2300#H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zeraʿ) - "seed, offspring" (v. 4). Davidic dynasty established forever.

**Context**: Psalm 89 is a Davidic covenant psalm that intensifies 2 Samuel 7 by explicitly adding the element of divine oath. While 2 Samuel 7 does not use oath language, Psalm 89 interprets it as oath-confirmed (vv. 3-4, 34-37, 49). This shows canonical development: later texts make explicit what was implicit.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 11|2 Samuel 7:11-16]]** - Nathan's oracle to David, promising an eternal dynasty ("Your house and kingdom will endure forever before Me," v. 16).
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 34|Psalm 89:34-37]]** - The oath is reiterated with cosmic imagery: "I will not violate My covenant or alter what My lips have uttered. Once for all I have sworn by My holiness—I will not lie to David" (vv. 34-35).
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132:11-12]]** - Another Davidic psalm emphasizing the oath: "The LORD swore to David an oath He will not revoke: 'One of your descendants I will place on your throne.'"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 11|2 Samuel 7:11-16]] - Davidic Covenant
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16-18]] - oath to Abraham—both oaths converge in Messiah
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 3|Isaiah 55:3]] - "I will make an everlasting covenant with you, the steadfast love promised to David"
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 20|Jeremiah 33:20-22]] - oath to David compared to covenant with day and night
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]] - angel to Mary: "The Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David... His kingdom will never end"
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 30|Acts 2:30]] - Peter: "David was a prophet and knew that God had promised him on oath that He would place one of his descendants on his throne"
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (explicit divine oath) and **Forward-Looking** (Psalm 89:36 "His offspring will endure forever" points beyond Solomon to Messiah).

**Christological Connection**: The oath to David finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ, "great David's greater Son," whose kingdom will have no end ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 33|Luke 1:33]]). Hebrews connects this Davidic oath with the Melchizedekian oath (Ps 110:4), showing Jesus as the royal-priestly king appointed by divine oath.
