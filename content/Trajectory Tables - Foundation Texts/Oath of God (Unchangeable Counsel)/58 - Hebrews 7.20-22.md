### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 20|Hebrews 7:20-22]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3601-3700#G3727|G3727]] ὅρκος (horkos) - "oath" (vv. 20-21). The distinguishing feature of Christ's priesthood.
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1450|G1450]] ἔγγυος (engyos) - "surety, guarantee" (v. 22). Only NT use; a legal term for one who pledges to fulfill obligations.
- [[Lexicon/G1201-1300#G1242|G1242]] διαθήκη (diathēkē) - "covenant" (v. 22). The New Covenant.

**Context**: Hebrews 7:20-22 is the climax of the author's argument for Christ's superiority over the Levitical priesthood. The key proof is the oath in Psalm 110:4: "The LORD has sworn and will not change His mind: 'You are a priest forever'" (quoted in Heb 7:21).

**NT-to-OT Development**:
- Levitical priests were appointed by genealogical descent (Exod 40:12-15; Num 3:10) without an oath.
- Christ was appointed by divine oath (Ps 110:4), demonstrating the superiority and permanence of His priesthood.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - oath appointing Melchizedekian priest
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 17|Hebrews 6:17-18]] - immutable counsel confirmed by oath
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6]] - "mediator of a better covenant"
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 15|Hebrews 9:15]] - "mediator of the new covenant"
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (explicit divine oath in Psalm 110:4) and **Forward-Looking** (the psalm addresses a future priest-king, fulfilled in Christ).

**Christological Connection**: Jesus is the ultimate fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]. The oath that appoints Him "priest forever" guarantees:
1. **Permanence**: His priesthood never ends (Heb 7:23-25).
2. **Efficacy**: He "always lives to intercede" for us (Heb 7:25).
3. **Security**: He is the "surety" of the New Covenant, guaranteeing its fulfillment.

The convergence of the Abrahamic oath (Heb 6:13-18) and the Melchizedekian oath (Heb 7:20-22) in Christ demonstrates that God's entire redemptive plan is oath-bound and therefore certain. The immutability of God's counsel is the believer's anchor (Heb 6:19).
