### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 35|Jeremiah 31:35-37]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2601-2700#H2706|H2706]] חֹק (ḥōq) - "statute, decree, fixed order" (v. 35-36). The unchangeable laws governing creation.
- [[Lexicon/H2201-2300#H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zeraʿ) - "seed, offspring" (v. 36-37). Israel's covenant identity.

**Context**: Jeremiah 31 contains the New Covenant promise (vv. 31-34). Verses 35-37 ground this covenant in God's cosmic decrees: "If this fixed order departs from before Me, declares the LORD, then shall the offspring of Israel cease from being a nation before Me forever" (v. 36). Similarly, Jeremiah 33:20-22 says if the covenant with day and night can be broken, only then can the covenant with David be broken.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Jeremiah uses creation ordinances (sun, moon, sea) as analogies for the certainty of God's covenant promises. Just as cosmic order is unchangeable, so is God's oath to Israel and David.
- This echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 22|Genesis 8:22]] (post-flood promise: "seedtime and harvest... will never cease") and anticipates the "new heavens and new earth" of [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]]; 66:22.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 22|Genesis 8:22]] - cosmic covenant after flood
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 34|Psalm 89:34-37]] - oath to David compared to cosmic stability
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 20|Jeremiah 33:20-26]] - covenant with day and night as metaphor for Davidic/Levitical covenant permanence
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 5|2 Peter 3:5-13]] - new heavens and new earth
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]] - fulfillment of cosmic renewal grounded in God's oath-bound promises
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (God's covenant decrees are explicit acts of self-binding) and **Forward-Looking** (points to New Covenant fulfillment in Christ).

**Christological Connection**: The New Covenant (Jer 31:31-34) and the cosmic stability oath (Jer 31:35-37; 33:20-22) converge in Christ. He is the mediator of the New Covenant (Heb 8:6-13, quoting Jer 31:31-34), and His resurrection inaugurates the "new creation" (2 Cor 5:17; Gal 6:15). The certainty of God's oath guarantees the eschatological new heavens and new earth (Rev 21:1-5).
