### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 5|Revelation 21:5-7]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4103|G4103]] πιστός (pistos) - "faithful, trustworthy" (v. 5). "These words are faithful and true."
- [[Lexicon/G101-200#G228|G228]] ἀληθινός (alēthinos) - "true, genuine" (v. 5). God's words are absolutely reliable.
- [[Lexicon/G2801-2900#G2816|G2816]] κληρονομέω (klēronomeō) - "to inherit" (v. 7). "The one who overcomes will inherit all things."

**Context**: Revelation 21 describes the New Jerusalem, the eternal state where God dwells with His people. Verse 5 emphasizes the certainty of this promise: "These words are faithful and true" (οἱ λόγοι οὗτοι πιστοὶ καὶ ἀληθινοί εἰσιν). This echoes Hebrews 6:18: "it is impossible for God to lie."

**NT-to-OT Development**:
- The promise to Abraham: "I will surely bless you" (Gen 22:17) finds ultimate fulfillment in the New Jerusalem where "there will no longer be any curse" (Rev 22:3).
- The promise to David: "I will establish your offspring forever" (Ps 89:4) culminates in the eternal reign of "the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David" (Rev 5:5).
- The New Covenant promise: "I will be their God, and they will be My people" (Jer 31:33) is consummated in Revelation 21:3: "God Himself will be with them as their God."

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (explicit divine declaration) and **Consummatory** (final fulfillment of all oath-bound promises).

**Christological Connection**: The Lamb who was slain (Rev 5:6-12) is the fulfillment of:
- The seed of Abraham through whom all nations are blessed (Gen 22:18; Rev 7:9, "a great multitude... from every nation")
- The Son of David who reigns forever (2 Sam 7:16; Rev 22:16, "I am the Root and Offspring of David")
- The Melchizedekian priest who intercedes eternally (Ps 110:4; Rev 1:6, "He has made us to be... priests to His God and Father")

All of God's oath-bound promises converge in Christ and reach their eschatological consummation in the New Jerusalem. The immutability of God's counsel (Heb 6:17), demonstrated through His self-sworn oaths, guarantees the eternal security and glory of all who are in Christ.
