### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 19|Hebrews 6:19-20]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G101-200#G45|G45]] ἄγκυρα (ankyra) - "anchor" (v. 19). Metaphor for stability and security.
- [[Lexicon/G4201-4300#G4268|G4268]] πρόδρομος (prodromos) - "forerunner" (v. 20). Only NT use of this term; refers to Jesus going ahead to prepare the way.
- [[Lexicon/G2601-2700#G2665|G2665]] καταπέτασμα (katapetasma) - "curtain, veil" (v. 19). The veil separating the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place.

**Context**: Hebrews 6:19-20 transitions from the oath to Abraham (vv. 13-18) to the oath appointing Christ as Melchizedekian priest (Psalm 110:4, quoted in Heb 7:21). The "anchor" metaphor connects God's immutable counsel (vv. 17-18) with Christ's heavenly priesthood (v. 20).

**NT-to-OT Development**:
- The "inner sanctuary behind the curtain" (ἐσώτερον τοῦ καταπετάσματος, v. 19) alludes to the Most Holy Place in the tabernacle/temple (Exod 26:33; Lev 16:2).
- On the Day of Atonement, only the high priest could enter "behind the veil" (Lev 16:12-15). Jesus, as our "forerunner," has entered once for all into the heavenly sanctuary (Heb 9:11-12, 24).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Leviticus 16 (Day of Atonement; high priest entering Most Holy Place)
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - oath appointing Melchizedekian priest
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11-14]] - Christ entered the greater tabernacle
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-22]] - we have confidence to enter the Most Holy Place by the blood of Jesus
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (Christ's entry into the heavenly sanctuary) and **Forward-Looking** (the earthly tabernacle always pointed forward to Christ's heavenly ministry).

**Christological Connection**: Christ is both the anchor and the one who casts the anchor. His entrance into the heavenly sanctuary, confirmed by God's oath (Ps 110:4; Heb 7:21), guarantees that our hope will not fail. The immutability of God's counsel (vv. 17-18) is actualized in Christ's eternal priesthood (v. 20).
