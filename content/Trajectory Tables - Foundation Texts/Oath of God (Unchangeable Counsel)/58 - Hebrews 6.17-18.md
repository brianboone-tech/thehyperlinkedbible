### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 17|Hebrews 6:17-18]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G101-200#G186|G186]] ἀμετάθετος (ametathetos) - "unchangeable, immutable" (v. 17-18). Used twice: "unchanging nature of His purpose" (v. 17) and "two unchangeable things" (v. 18).
- [[Lexicon/G1001-1100#G1012|G1012]] βουλή (boulē) - "purpose, counsel, will" (v. 17). God's deliberate, fixed purpose.
- [[Lexicon/G3601-3700#G3727|G3727]] ὅρκος (horkos) - "oath" (v. 17). God guaranteed His purpose with an oath.
- [[Lexicon/G3801-3900#G3874|G3874]] παράκλησις (paraklēsis) - "encouragement, consolation" (v. 18). "Strong consolation" (ἰσχυρὰν παράκλησιν).

**Context**: These verses are the theological climax of Hebrews 6:13-20. The author explains *why* God added an oath to His promise: "to make the unchanging nature of His purpose very clear to the heirs of the promise" (v. 17).

**NT-to-OT Development**:
- The "two unchangeable things" (δύο πραγμάτων ἀμεταθέτων, v. 18) are:
  1. God's **promise** (ἐπαγγελία) to Abraham
  2. God's **oath** (ὅρκος) confirming the promise
- Both are rooted in God's immutable nature, making it "impossible for God to lie" (ἀδύνατον ψεύσασθαι τὸν θεόν, v. 18).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16-18]] - promise + oath to Abraham
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 19|Hebrews 6:19-20]] - hope as anchor
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 20|Hebrews 7:20-22]] - oath makes Jesus surety of better covenant
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** and **Forward-Looking** (the oath to Abraham intentionally pointed forward to "heirs of promise" in all generations).

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the mediator of the oath-confirmed promises. The "hope set before us" (v. 18) is anchored in Jesus, who "entered the inner sanctuary behind the curtain" (v. 19) as our forerunner and eternal high priest (v. 20). The immutability of God's counsel, demonstrated in the Abrahamic oath, is the foundation for the perseverance of the saints.
