### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 13|Hebrews 6:13-15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3601-3700#G3660|G3660]] ὀμνύω (omnýō) - "to swear, take an oath" (v. 13). God swore by Himself (ὤμοσεν καθ' ἑαυτοῦ).
- [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3106|G3106]] μακροθυμέω (makrothymeō) - "to be patient, endure patiently" (v. 15). Abraham waited patiently.
- [[Lexicon/G1801-1900#G1843|G1843]] ἐπαγγελία (epangelia) - "promise" (v. 15). "Obtained the promise."

**Context**: Hebrews 6:13-20 expounds the theological significance of God's oath to Abraham (Genesis 22:16-18). The author is pastorally encouraging believers to "hold unswervingly to the hope we profess" (Heb 10:23) by grounding their hope in the immutability of God's counsel.

**NT-to-OT Development**:
- Hebrews interprets Genesis 22:16-18 through the lens of divine immutability. The key hermeneutical move is identifying *why* God swore by Himself: "since He had no one greater to swear by" (v. 13).
- This reveals God's self-sufficiency and the absolute reliability of His word.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16-18]] - oath to Abraham
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 17|Hebrews 6:17-18]] - immutable counsel
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 8|Hebrews 11:8-19]] - Abraham's faith exemplified
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** and **Forward-Looking** (the oath to Abraham points forward to fulfillment in Christ and application to all heirs of promise).

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the ultimate "seed" in whom the oath to Abraham is fulfilled (Gal 3:16). Believers are "heirs according to promise" (Gal 3:29) through union with Christ. The oath that secured Abraham's blessing now secures our salvation.
