### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 6#Exodus 6 . 8|Exodus 6:8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7650|H7650]] שָׁבַע (shābaʿ) - "to swear" (Hiphil: "I lifted up My hand," i.e., swore with raised hand, oath gesture).
- [[Lexicon/H5301-5400#H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (nātan) - "to give" (v. 8). "I will give it to you as a possession."

**Context**: Moses, discouraged by Pharaoh's hardening and Israel's unbelief, receives God's renewed promise grounded in the oath to the patriarchs. The Exodus is not a new plan but the fulfillment of the ancient oath (Exod 6:2-8).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 8|Deuteronomy 7:8]] explicitly states: "Because the LORD loved you and kept the oath He swore to your fathers, the LORD brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of slavery."
- The Exodus becomes the paradigmatic demonstration of God's oath-keeping fidelity.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16-18]] - ; 26:3-4 (oaths to Abraham and Isaac
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 8|Deuteronomy 7:8]] - ; 9:5 (oath as basis for Exodus and conquest
  - [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 15|Nehemiah 9:15]] - post-exilic appeal to God's oath
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 73|Luke 1:73]] - Zechariah: "the oath He swore to our father Abraham, to grant us... rescue from the hand of our enemies"
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 17|Acts 7:17]] - Stephen: "As the time drew near for God to fulfill His promise to Abraham..."
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** and **Forward-Looking** (Exodus foreshadows greater exodus through Christ; see Luke 9:31, Hebrews 3-4).

**Christological Connection**: The Exodus, grounded in God's oath, typifies the greater redemption accomplished by Christ. As God brought Israel out of Egypt "because of the oath," so He brings believers out of sin and death through Christ, the ultimate Passover Lamb (1 Cor 5:7).
