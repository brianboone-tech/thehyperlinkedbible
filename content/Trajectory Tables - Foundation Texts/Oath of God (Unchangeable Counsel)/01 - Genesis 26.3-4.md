### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 26#Genesis 26 . 3|Genesis 26:3-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7650|H7650]] שָׁבַע (shābaʿ) - "to swear, take an oath" (v. 3). Hiphil: "I will confirm/establish the oath."
- [[Lexicon/H2201-2300#H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zeraʿ) - "seed, offspring" (v. 4). Covenant succession through Isaac.

**Context**: During famine, Isaac is tempted to go down to Egypt (like Abraham), but God appears and commands him to stay in the land, promising, "I will confirm the oath I swore to your father Abraham" (Gen 26:3). This demonstrates the oath's intergenerational validity.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The oath made to Abraham (Gen 22:16-18) is now explicitly transferred to Isaac, establishing covenant succession.
- Later, the oath extends to Jacob (Gen 28:13-15; 35:11-12), creating a patriarchal chain rooted in divine oath.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16-18]] - original oath to Abraham
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 13|Genesis 28:13-15]] - ; 35:11-12 (oath renewed to Jacob
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 6#Exodus 6 . 8|Exodus 6:8]] - oath to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as basis for Exodus
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 3#Acts 3 . 25|Acts 3:25]] - Peter: "You are sons of the prophets and of the covenant God made with your fathers, saying to Abraham, 'Through your offspring all the families of the earth will be blessed'"
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** and **Forward-Looking** (covenant succession points forward to ultimate seed, Christ).

**Christological Connection**: The transfer of the oath from Abraham to Isaac prefigures the transfer from old covenant Israel to Christ, the true Son and true seed in whom all nations are blessed.
