### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 21|Galatians 4:21-31]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G238|G238]] ἀλληγορέω (allēgoreō) - to speak allegorically, interpret typologically
- [[Lexicon/G1242|G1242]] διαθήκη (diathēkē) - covenant
- [[Lexicon/G4561|G4561]] σάρξ (sarx) - flesh (κατὰ σάρκα, kata sarka - according to flesh)
- [[Lexicon/G1860|G1860]] ἐπαγγελία (epangelia) - promise (δι' ἐπαγγελίας, di' epangelias - through promise)
- [[Lexicon/G1658|G1658]] ἐλεύθερος (eleutheros) - free
- [[Lexicon/G1401|G1401]] δοῦλος (doulos) - slave
- [[Lexicon/G1559|G1559]] ἐκδιώκω (ekdiōkō) - to cast out, expel, persecute
- [[Lexicon/G2816|G2816]] κληρονομέω (klēronomeō) - to inherit

**Context**: This passage is the definitive NT exposition of the Hagar-Ishmael type. Paul addresses Galatians tempted to submit to circumcision and law-observance by showing that such reliance on "flesh" makes them spiritual descendants of Ishmael (slave woman's son), not Isaac (free woman's son). He explicitly states these things are "being spoken allegorically" (ἀλληγορούμενα, v. 24)—recognizing divinely intended typological significance.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Paul synthesizes Genesis 16-21 (Hagar-Ishmael narrative) with [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 1|Isaiah 54:1]] (barren woman rejoicing) to show the OT itself anticipated that the barren woman (Sarah/Church) would have more children than the fertile woman (Hagar/Judaism)
- The pattern of younger/weaker/unexpected chosen over elder/stronger/expected (Isaac over Ishmael) recurs throughout Scripture

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Genesis 16-21 (complete Hagar-Ishmael narrative)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 1|Isaiah 54:1]] (barren woman will have more children)
- **TO**: Exodus 19-20 (Mount Sinai / old covenant)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] (new covenant prophecy)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 6|Romans 9:6-9]] (Paul's other major treatment of Isaac-Ishmael typology)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 31|John 8:31-36]] (Jesus: "the slave does not remain in the house forever")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 18|Hebrews 12:18-24]] (Mount Sinai vs. Mount Zion contrast)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 6|2 Corinthians 3:6-18]] (old covenant letter vs. new covenant Spirit)

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** Providential Type (both positive [Isaac/Sarah] and negative [Ishmael/Hagar]). The typological significance was recognized only from the NT vantage point, though the events were sovereignly arranged by God.

**Christological Connection**: The Hagar-Ishmael vs. Sarah-Isaac typology points to Christ as the ultimate Seed through whom the promise is fulfilled. Just as Isaac was born miraculously (supernatural promise-fulfillment), Christ was conceived by the Holy Spirit ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 20|Matthew 1:20]]). Just as Isaac was the beloved only son whom Abraham offered (Genesis 22), Christ is the beloved only Son whom the Father gave ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]]). Just as Isaac's line leads to covenant blessing for all nations ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Genesis 22:18]]), Christ's death and resurrection secures covenant blessing for all who believe ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 14|Galatians 3:14]], 29). Those united to Christ by faith become "children of promise, like Isaac" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 28|Galatians 4:28]])—born not κατὰ σάρκα (according to flesh) but κατὰ πνεῦμα (according to Spirit). The distinction between Ishmael (excluded) and Isaac (inheriting) prefigures the final distinction between those trusting in works (condemned) and those trusting in Christ (justified).
