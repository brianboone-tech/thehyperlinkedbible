### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 30|Galatians 4:30-31]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1559|G1559]] ἐκβάλλω (ekballō) - to cast out, expel, drive out
- [[Lexicon/G3814|G3814]] παιδίσκη (paidiskē) - slave girl, maidservant
- [[Lexicon/G1658|G1658]] ἐλεύθερος (eleutheros) - free
- [[Lexicon/G2816|G2816]] κληρονομέω (klēronomeō) - to inherit
- [[Lexicon/G3361|G3361]] μή (mē) - not, never (emphatic negation with οὐ μή, ou mē - strong double negative)
- [[Lexicon/G3767|G3767]] ἄρα (ara) - therefore, then, consequently

**Context**: Paul quotes Genesis 21:10 verbatim (LXX): "**Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the slave woman's son will never share in the inheritance with the free woman's son**." This is the climactic application of his Hagar-Ishmael typology—the Judaizers who preach circumcision and law-keeping must be expelled from the church. Paul then concludes: "**Therefore, brothers, we are not children of the slave woman, but of the free woman**" (v. 31).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Sarah's command ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 10|Genesis 21:10]]) was confirmed by God as divine instruction ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 12|Genesis 21:12]])—making it not merely human anger but divine decree
- The exclusion was absolute and permanent—Ishmael would "never" (οὐ μή, ou mē - strongest Greek negation) share inheritance with Isaac

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 10|Genesis 21:10]] (Sarah's command, divinely confirmed)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 12|Genesis 21:12]] (God confirms: "Listen to everything Sarah tells you")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 1|Galatians 5:1]] ("For freedom Christ has set us free; stand firm therefore")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 1#Galatians 1 . 8|Galatians 1:8-9]] (Paul pronounces anathema on those preaching another gospel)
- **FROM NT**: 2 John 10-11 (do not receive those who bring false teaching)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 10|Titus 3:10]] (reject divisive person after warning)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 21|Matthew 7:21-23]] (many will claim Lord, but hear "Depart from me")

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** Providential Type (negative). Paul recognizes that God sovereignly arranged the Genesis events to foreshadow the separation between works-religion and grace-religion.

**Christological Connection**: The command to "cast out the slave woman and her son" points to the final judgment where Christ will separate sheep from goats ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|Matthew 25:31-46]]). Just as Ishmael (flesh-born) was permanently excluded from Abraham's household, those trusting in works ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 22|Matthew 7:22-23]], "many will say to me on that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not...'") will be excluded from the kingdom: "**Depart from me, I never knew you**" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 23|Matthew 7:23]]). The exclusion is absolute: "will **never** share in the inheritance." Conversely, those who are "in Christ" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]]) by faith are "children of the free woman" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 31|Galatians 4:31]]) and **will** inherit eternal life. The Hagar-Ishmael trajectory teaches that salvation is exclusively by grace through faith in Christ—no human works can secure or supplement it. Those who add works to grace will hear the final "Cast out," while those trusting in Christ alone will hear "Come, you who are blessed by my Father, **inherit** the kingdom" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 34|Matthew 25:34]]).
