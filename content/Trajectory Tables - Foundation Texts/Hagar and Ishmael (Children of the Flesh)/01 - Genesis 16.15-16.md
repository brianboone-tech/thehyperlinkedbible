### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16#Genesis 16 . 15|Genesis 16:15-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3205|H3205]] יָלַד (yālaḏ) - to bear, bring forth, beget
- [[Lexicon/H3458|H3458]] יִשְׁמָעֵאל (yišmāʿēl) - Ishmael ("God hears")
- [[Lexicon/H8084|H8084]] שְׁמֹנִים (šəmōnîm) - eighty
- [[Lexicon/H8337|H8337]] שֵׁשׁ (šēš) - six

**Context**: Hagar bore Ishmael when Abram was eighty-six years old—still fourteen years before Isaac's birth (Genesis 21:5, when Abraham was 100). The name "Ishmael" means "God hears," commemorating God's hearing of Hagar's affliction (16:11), yet this son born through human effort would not be the covenant heir.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The fourteen-year gap between Ishmael's birth (Abram age 86) and Isaac's birth (Abraham age 100) demonstrates God's patience in waiting for the promised seed, not accepting human substitutes
- God's angel prophesied Ishmael's character: "a wild donkey of a man" living in hostility (16:12), which later narratives confirm

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16#Genesis 16 . 11|Genesis 16:11-12]] (prophecy about Ishmael's nature)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 18|Genesis 17:18-21]] (Abraham pleads for Ishmael; God confirms Isaac as covenant heir)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 8|Genesis 21:8-21]] (Ishmael at ~16 years old when expelled)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 12|Genesis 25:12-18]] (Ishmael's descendants—twelve princes as God promised, but outside covenant line)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 23|Galatians 4:23]] ("born according to the flesh")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 7|Romans 9:7-8]] ("not all... are children of Abraham because they are his offspring")

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** Providential Type (negative).

**Christological Connection**: Ishmael, as a negative type, demonstrates by contrast the necessity of the true Seed—Jesus Christ. Where Ishmael was born κατὰ σάρκα (according to flesh), Isaac was born κατὰ ἐπαγγελίαν (according to promise), pointing forward to the ultimate Promise-Child, Jesus, conceived by the Holy Spirit ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 20|Matthew 1:20]]). Ishmael's exclusion highlights that only those born "from above" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 3|John 3:3]]) through faith-union with Christ are true children of Abraham.
