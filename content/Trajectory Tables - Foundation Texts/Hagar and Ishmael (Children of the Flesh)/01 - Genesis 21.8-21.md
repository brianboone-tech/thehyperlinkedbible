### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 8|Genesis 21:8-21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1580|H1580]] גָּמַל (gāmal) - to wean
- [[Lexicon/H6711|H6711]] צָחַק (ṣāḥaq) - to laugh, play, mock (מְצַחֵק, metzacheq - participle)
- [[Lexicon/H1644|H1644]] גָּרַשׁ (gāraš) - to drive out, cast out, expel
- [[Lexicon/H519|H519]] אָמָה (ʾāmâ) - maidservant, female slave
- [[Lexicon/H3423|H3423]] יָרַשׁ (yāraš) - to inherit, possess
- [[Lexicon/H7121|H7121]] קָרָא (qārāʾ) - to call, name, proclaim

**Context**: When Isaac was weaned (probably around age 3), Abraham held a feast. Sarah observed teenage Ishmael (about 16 years old) "mocking" (מְצַחֵק) Isaac and demanded the permanent expulsion of Hagar and her son. Though Abraham was distressed, God confirmed Sarah's command, establishing that covenant succession passes exclusively through Isaac.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 10|Genesis 21:10]]'s command "Cast out the slave woman and her son" becomes paradigmatic for excluding those who rely on flesh-works from covenant inheritance
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 12|Genesis 21:12]]'s principle "through Isaac shall your offspring be named" (בְיִצְחָק יִקָּרֵא לְךָ זָרַע) is quoted verbatim by Paul in [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 7|Romans 9:7]] and [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 18|Hebrews 11:18]] as the foundation of election theology
- The "mocking" (מְצַחֵק, from same root as Isaac's name יִצְחָק) represents the perpetual hostility between flesh-born and Spirit-born children

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16#Genesis 16 . 1|Genesis 16:1-16]] (Hagar's initial introduction and Ishmael's birth)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 18|Genesis 17:18-21]] (God's rejection of Ishmael as covenant heir)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 12|Genesis 25:12-18]] (Ishmael's descendants fulfilling God's promise of natural blessing outside covenant line)

- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 30|Galatians 4:30]] (direct quotation: "Cast out the slave woman and her son")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 7|Romans 9:7-8]] ("through Isaac shall your offspring be named")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 35|John 8:35]] ("the slave does not remain in the house forever")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 18|Hebrews 11:18]] (quoting [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 12|Genesis 21:12]] in context of Abraham's faith)

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** Providential Type (negative).

**Christological Connection**: The expulsion of Ishmael demonstrates the absolute necessity of spiritual birth for covenant membership. Just as Ishmael (natural son) could not inherit alongside Isaac (promise-son), no amount of human effort, religious credentials, or ethnic identity can secure salvation. Only those born "from above" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 3|John 3:3]]) through the Spirit, united to Christ by faith, receive the inheritance. The separation between Ishmael and Isaac foreshadows the final judgment where those trusting in works will hear "Depart from me" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 23|Matthew 7:23]]) while those trusting in Christ will "inherit the kingdom prepared for you" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 34|Matthew 25:34]]).
