### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16#Genesis 16 . 1|Genesis 16:1-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8198|H8198]] שִׁפְחָה (šip̄ḥâ) - maidservant, female slave, handmaid
- [[Lexicon/H4713|H4713]] מִצְרִית (miṣrîṯ) - Egyptian (feminine)
- [[Lexicon/H1129|H1129]] בָּנָה (bānâ) - to build (here used idiomatically: "build a family")
- [[Lexicon/H8085|H8085]] שָׁמַע (šāmaʿ) - to hear, listen to, obey
- [[Lexicon/H6963|H6963]] קוֹל (qôl) - voice
- [[Lexicon/H7043|H7043]] קָלַל (qālal) - to be light, trifling, despise

**Context**: After ten years in Canaan with no child from Sarai, she took matters into her own hands by giving her Egyptian servant Hagar to Abram to produce an heir through human effort. This action demonstrated lack of faith in God's promise and initiated a pattern of "works of the flesh" that would have devastating consequences.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Genesis 16's introduction of Hagar-Ishmael is developed in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 8|Genesis 21:8-21]] with the expulsion narrative
- The phrase "listened to the voice of Sarai" (16:2) echoes Adam "listening to the voice" of Eve ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17]])—repeating the pattern of human autonomy over divine command
- The Egyptian identity of Hagar connects to the later Exodus narrative where Egypt represents bondage

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 1|Genesis 15:1-6]] (Abram's faith counted as righteousness, contrasting with Sarai's unbelief in ch. 16)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17]] (Adam "listened to the voice of his wife" - parallel failure)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 8|Genesis 21:8-21]] (expulsion of Hagar and Ishmael)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 12|Genesis 25:12-18]] (Ishmael's descendants, fulfilling natural blessing but outside covenant)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 23|Galatians 4:23]] ("son by the slave woman was born according to the flesh")
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 7|Romans 9:7-8]] ("not the children of the flesh who are the children of God")

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** (recognized as typological only from NT vantage - Galatians 4:24) and **Providential** (sovereignly arranged events, not divinely commanded institution).

**Christological Connection**: While this is a **negative type** (showing what must be excluded), it points to Christ indirectly by demonstrating the absolute necessity of divine intervention for covenant blessing. Just as Isaac's birth required a miracle (supernatural promise-fulfillment), so salvation requires the miracle of new birth by the Spirit through faith in Christ. The flesh profits nothing ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 63|John 6:63]]); only what God does in fulfilling His promises brings true sonship and inheritance.
