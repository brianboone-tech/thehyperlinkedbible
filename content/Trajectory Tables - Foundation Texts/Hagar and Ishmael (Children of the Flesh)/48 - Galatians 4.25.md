### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 25|Galatians 4:25]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4614|G4614]] Σινᾶ (Sina) - Sinai
- [[Lexicon/G688|G688]] Ἀραβία (Arabia) - Arabia
- [[Lexicon/G4960|G4960]] συστοιχέω (systoicheō) - to correspond to, stand in the same row/line
- [[Lexicon/G3568|G3568]] νῦν (nyn) - now, present
- [[Lexicon/G2419|G2419]] Ἰερουσαλήμ (Hierousalēm) - Jerusalem
- [[Lexicon/G1398|G1398]] δουλεύω (douleuō) - to be enslaved, serve as a slave
- [[Lexicon/G5043|G5043]] τέκνον (teknon) - child, children

**Context**: Paul makes the shocking typological connection: "Hagar stands for (ἐστίν, estin) Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to (συστοιχεῖ, systoichei) the present-day Jerusalem, because she is in slavery (δουλεύει, douleuei) with her children." This revolutionary interpretation equates Hagar (slave woman) with Sinai (law-giving) and first-century Judaism (law-observance for righteousness).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Exodus 19-20 (Sinai covenant giving) connected typologically to Genesis 16-21 (Hagar narrative) through the common theme of **slavery**
- The Mosaic covenant, though glorious and divinely given, could not liberate from sin—it was preparatory (παιδαγωγός, [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 24|Galatians 3:24]])

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Exodus 19-20 (law given at Mount Sinai)
- **TO**: Genesis 16-21 (Hagar the slave woman)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] (new covenant prophecy—the old covenant will be replaced)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 6|2 Corinthians 3:6-18]] (ministry of letter kills; ministry of Spirit gives life)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6-13]] (old covenant obsolete; new covenant superior)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 7#Romans 7 . 1|Romans 7:1-6]] (believers died to the law; released from its bondage)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 23|Galatians 3:23-25]] (law was custodian until Christ; now faith has come)

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** Providential Type (negative). Paul recognizes typological significance only from the NT vantage point—the OT text itself did not indicate Hagar as a type of the Mosaic covenant.

**Christological Connection**: The Hagar-Sinai connection demonstrates that even divinely given religious institutions cannot save apart from Christ. The Mosaic law was "holy and righteous and good" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 7#Romans 7 . 12|Romans 7:12]]) yet could not justify because of human sin and weakness ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 3|Romans 8:3]]). Christ fulfilled the law's requirements ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 17|Matthew 5:17]]), bore its curse ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]]), and established a new covenant by His blood ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]]). Those united to Christ by faith are freed from law's bondage and receive the Spirit, becoming children of the free woman (Sarah/heavenly Jerusalem) rather than the slave woman (Hagar/earthly Jerusalem).
