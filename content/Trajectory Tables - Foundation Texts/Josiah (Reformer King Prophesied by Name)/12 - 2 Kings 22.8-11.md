### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 22#2 Kings 22 . 8|2 Kings 22:8-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה (sefer haTorah) - "the Book of the Law" - likely Deuteronomy
- מָצָאתִי (matzati) - "I have found" - discovery after long loss
- וַיִּקְרַע (vayyiqra') - "and he tore" - covenant mourning gesture

**Context**: In Josiah's 18th year (622 BC), during temple repairs, Hilkiah discovers the long-lost Law scroll. When read to the king, Josiah tears his garments in repentance.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Manasseh's wickedness (2 Kgs 21) had led to the Law's neglect and loss
- The book's rediscovery sparks covenant renewal (2 Kgs 23:1-3)
- Contrasts with Jehoiakim who burns the scroll without fear (Jer 36:24)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 24|Deuteronomy 31:24-26]] - Moses' command to keep the Law beside the ark
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 1|2 Kings 23:1-3]] - covenant renewal follows the discovery
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 36#Jeremiah 36 . 24|Jeremiah 36:24]] - Jehoiakim's opposite response
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 27|Luke 24:27]] - , 45 - Christ opens the Scriptures
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Christ IS the Word
**Type Classification**: Providential Type (sovereignly orchestrated discovery)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah discovers the written Word and responds with broken heart; Christ IS the living Word ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) who opens hearts to understand Scripture ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 45|Luke 24:45]]). The pattern: true reformation begins with recovering God's Word and responding in humility.
