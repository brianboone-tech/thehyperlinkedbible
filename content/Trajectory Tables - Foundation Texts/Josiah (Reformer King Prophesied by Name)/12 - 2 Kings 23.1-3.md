### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 1|2 Kings 23:1-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כָּרַת בְּרִית (karat berit) - "cut/made a covenant" - formal covenant renewal ceremony
- עַל־הָעַמּוּד (al-ha'ammud) - "by the pillar" - likely the pillar where kings stood (2 Kgs 11:14)
- בְּכָל־לִבּוֹ וּבְכָל־נַפְשׁוֹ (bekhol-libbo uvekhol-nafsho) - "with all his heart and all his soul" - total devotion

**Context**: After hearing the Law and receiving Huldah's prophecy, Josiah assembles all the people for public covenant renewal at the temple.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Echoes Moses' covenant ceremony at Sinai (Exod 24:3-8)
- Recalls Joshua's covenant renewal at Shechem (Josh 24:24-25)
- Parallels Jehoiada's covenant renewal after Athaliah (2 Kgs 11:17)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 3|Exodus 24:3-8]] - Moses' covenant ceremony
- **TO**: Deuteronomy 29-30 - covenant renewal in Moab
- **FROM OT**: Nehemiah 8-10 - post-exilic covenant renewal
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]] - "This cup is the new covenant in My blood"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (divinely orchestrated renewal ceremony)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah renews the old covenant at the temple pillar; Christ inaugurates the New Covenant with His own blood ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]]). Where Josiah could only call for renewed obedience, Christ provides both the requirement (perfect obedience) and the means (substitutionary atonement). The pattern: covenant renewal requires a mediator who represents both God and people.
