### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4-20]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- הוֹצִיא (hotzi) - "brought out" - active removal of idolatrous articles
- שָׂרַף (saraf) - "burned" - complete destruction by fire
- טִמֵּא (timme) - "defiled/desecrated" - made ritually unclean and unusable
- עֲצָמוֹת אָדָם ('atzamot adam) - "human bones" - fulfilling 1 Kgs 13:2 prophecy

**Context**: Josiah's comprehensive reformation extends from Judah to the former northern kingdom, destroying all traces of idolatry and fulfilling the 300-year-old prophecy.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Reverses Manasseh's abominations (2 Kgs 21:3-7)
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 13#1 Kings 13 . 2|1 Kings 13:2]] prophecy by burning bones on Jeroboam's altar (v. 15-16)
- Extends beyond Judah into Samaria (v. 19-20), reclaiming the whole land

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 13#1 Kings 13 . 2|1 Kings 13:2]] - prophecy of burning bones on the altar
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 21#2 Kings 21 . 3|2 Kings 21:3-7]] - Manasseh's idolatries that Josiah reversed
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 34#2 Chronicles 34 . 3|2 Chronicles 34:3-7]] - Chronicles' parallel account emphasizes thoroughness
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 13|John 2:13-17]] - Christ cleanses the temple
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 7|1 John 1:7]] - Christ purifies from all sin
**Type Classification**: Providential Type (divinely empowered reformation)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah destroyed physical idols and cleansed physical spaces; Christ destroys sin itself and purifies hearts as living temples. Where Josiah's reform was external and temporary (Judah still fell), Christ's purification is internal and eternal. The pattern: true reformation requires not just removing false gods but establishing true worship through a perfect mediator.
