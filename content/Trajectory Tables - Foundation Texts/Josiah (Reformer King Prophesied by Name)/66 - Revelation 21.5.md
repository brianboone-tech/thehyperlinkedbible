### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 5|Revelation 21:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- καθήμενος ἐπὶ τῷ θρόνῳ (kathēmenos epi tō thronō) - "the One seated on the throne" - divine sovereignty
- καινὰ (kaina) - "new" - qualitatively new, not just chronologically
- πάντα (panta) - "all things" - comprehensive, universal scope
- πιστοὶ καὶ ἀληθινοί (pistoi kai alēthinoi) - "faithful and true" - absolutely reliable

**Context**: From the heavenly throne, God declares the new creation where all things are made new - the ultimate and eternal reformation, transcending all previous renewals.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Isaiah prophesied "new heavens and new earth" (Isa 65:17; 66:22)
- Josiah's reformation was glorious but temporary - Judah still fell into exile
- Every OT reformation (Moses, Joshua, Samuel, Hezekiah, Josiah) pointed beyond itself

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] - "new heavens and new earth" prophecy
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 25|2 Kings 23:25]] - Josiah's unmatched reformation that still couldn't save Judah
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 13|2 Peter 3:13]] - new heavens and earth where righteousness dwells
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]] - "No longer will there be any curse"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Fulfillment (all reformation types culminate here)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah made things new in Israel - but only temporarily. Christ makes ALL THINGS new - eternally. Where Josiah removed idols from the land, in the New Jerusalem "nothing impure will ever enter" (Rev 21:27). Where Josiah restored covenant fellowship, in the new creation "God's dwelling is with man" forever (Rev 21:3). Where Josiah's reform delayed judgment, Christ's new creation removes the curse entirely (Rev 22:3). The consummation: what every reformer attempted partially, Christ accomplishes perfectly and eternally.
