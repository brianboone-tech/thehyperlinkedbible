### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 13#1 Kings 13 . 2|1 Kings 13:2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- יוֹשִׁיָּהוּ (Yoshiyahu) - "Josiah" = "Yahweh supports/heals"
- נוֹלָד (nolad) - "will be born" - prophetic future tense
- בֵּן (ben) - "son" - heir of David's house

**Context**: During Jeroboam's apostasy at the golden calf altar in Bethel, a prophet from Judah delivers this stunning prophecy naming Josiah three centuries before his birth.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The prophecy's fulfillment in [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 15|2 Kings 23:15-16]] demonstrates divine sovereignty over history
- The 300-year gap emphasizes God's patience and long-term redemptive planning
- Naming the deliverer in advance parallels Isaiah's prophecy of Cyrus (Isa 44:28; 45:1)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Davidic covenant (2 Sam 7:12-16) - Josiah comes from David's house
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 15|2 Kings 23:15-16]] - precise fulfillment of the prophecy
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 10|Matthew 1:10-11]] - Josiah in Christ's genealogy
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 31|Luke 1:31]] - angel names Jesus before birth
**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (explicit prophecy) Providential Type (sovereignly appointed deliverer)

**Christological Connection**: As Josiah was named centuries before his birth to be Israel's reformer, so Christ was prophesied by titles (Immanuel, Branch, Wonderful Counselor) and would accomplish ultimate reformation. The pattern: God announces the deliverer before he comes, validating his divine mission when he arrives.
