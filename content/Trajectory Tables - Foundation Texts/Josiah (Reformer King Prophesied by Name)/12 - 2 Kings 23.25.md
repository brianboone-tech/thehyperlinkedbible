### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 25|2 Kings 23:25]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- שָׁב אֶל־יְהוָה (shav el-YHWH) - "turned/returned to the LORD" - covenant repentance language
- בְּכָל־לְבָבוֹ וּבְכָל־נַפְשׁוֹ וּבְכָל־מְאֹדוֹ (bekhol-levavo uvekhol-nafsho uvekhol-me'odo) - "with all his heart, soul, and strength"
- כְּכֹל תּוֹרַת מֹשֶׁה (kekhol torat Moshe) - "according to all the Law of Moses"

**Context**: Scripture's final verdict on Josiah uses the very language of the Shema (Deut 6:5), declaring him unmatched in covenant devotion before or after.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Echoes the Shema's call to total love (Deut 6:5)
- Surpasses even David and Hezekiah in explicit praise
- Contrasts tragically with his sons' wickedness (Jer 22:13-17)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 5|Deuteronomy 6:5]] - the Shema command
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 5|2 Kings 18:5]] - Hezekiah's praise (different emphasis
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 22#Jeremiah 22 . 15|Jeremiah 22:15-16]] - Jeremiah uses Josiah as standard
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 37|Matthew 22:37]] - Jesus quotes Shema as greatest commandment
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 15|Matthew 3:15]] - Christ fulfills all righteousness
**Type Classification**: Providential Type, Forward-looking (points beyond itself to perfect king)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah loved God with all his heart/soul/strength, yet could not save Judah from exile. Only Christ perfectly fulfills the Shema (Matt 22:37) AND provides the righteousness His people lack (Matt 3:15; 2 Cor 5:21). The pattern: even the best human obedience cannot atone for sin or secure salvation - only the God-man can accomplish redemption.
