### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 9|Hebrews 9:9-10]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- παραβολή (parabolē) - "illustration/parable" - symbolic representation
- συνείδησιν (syneidēsin) - "conscience" - inner moral awareness
- διορθώσεως (diorthōseōs) - "reformation/setting things right" - comprehensive restoration
- δικαιώματα σαρκός (dikaiōmata sarkos) - "regulations for the body/flesh" - external ordinances

**Context**: Hebrews contrasts the old covenant's external regulations with Christ's internal, eternal work, describing the entire Levitical system as temporary "until the time of reformation."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Levitical system was "a shadow of the good things to come" (Heb 10:1)
- Even at its best (under Josiah), the old covenant could not perfect the conscience
- Josiah's reformation, though exemplary, was still within the "time until reformation"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4-25]] - Josiah's reformation of external worship
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 1|Leviticus 16:1-34]] - Day of Atonement rituals (context of Heb 9)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 1|Hebrews 10:1-4]] - old sacrifices cannot take away sins
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6-13]] - new covenant superior to old

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (old covenant reformation prefigures new covenant reality)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah's reformation was the best the old covenant could produce - yet it was still "external regulations" that couldn't perfect the conscience. Christ brings "the time of reformation" (διορθώσεως) - not just reforming the old system but establishing a new covenant (Heb 8:6-13) through His perfect sacrifice (Heb 10:10-14). The escalation: Josiah reformed external worship; Christ reforms internal reality, writing the law on hearts (Heb 10:16) and perfecting forever those being sanctified (Heb 10:14).
