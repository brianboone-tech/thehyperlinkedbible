### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 13|John 2:13-17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἱερόν (hieron) - "temple" - the temple complex/courts
- ἐμπόριον (emporion) - "house of merchandise" - commercial enterprise
- ζῆλος (zēlos) - "zeal" - consuming passion for God's honor
- καταφάγεται (kataphagetai) - "will consume" - future prophetic fulfillment

**Context**: At Passover, Jesus drives merchants and money changers from the temple courts, claiming authority over God's house as the disciples remember Psalm 69:9.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 9|Psalm 69:9]] ("Zeal for Your house consumes me") is Davidic passion for pure worship
- Josiah's temple cleansing (2 Kgs 23:4-7) removed idolatrous articles
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1-3]] prophesied the Lord suddenly coming to His temple to purify

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4-7]] - Josiah cleanses the temple
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 9|Psalm 69:9]] - zeal for God's house
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1-3]] - sudden coming to purify the temple
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] - Jesus IS the temple
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16-17]] - believers are God's temple
**Type Classification**: Direct Type (Christ fulfills the reformer-king pattern)

**Christological Connection**: Where Josiah cleansed the physical temple of idols, Christ cleanses it of commerce - but ultimately reveals HE IS the temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]). Josiah's reformation was external (removing objects); Christ's is internal (purifying hearts). Josiah made God's house a place of sacrifice; Christ offers Himself as the once-for-all sacrifice. The escalation: Josiah reformed temple worship; Christ replaces temple worship with Himself.
