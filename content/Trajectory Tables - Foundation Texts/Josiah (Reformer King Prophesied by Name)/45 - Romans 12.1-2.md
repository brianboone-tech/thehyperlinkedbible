### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1-2]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- λογικὴν λατρείαν (logikēn latreian) - "spiritual/reasonable worship" - worship fitting for rational beings
- μεταμορφοῦσθε (metamorphousthe) - "be transformed" - ongoing transformation (present imperative)
- ἀνακαινώσει τοῦ νοός (anakainōsei tou noos) - "renewing of the mind" - internal renovation
- δοκιμάζειν (dokimazein) - "discern/test/approve" - ability to evaluate God's will

**Context**: After expounding justification by faith (Rom 1-11), Paul calls believers to present their bodies as living sacrifices and be transformed by mind renewal, not conforming to this age.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Josiah called Israel to covenant renewal and reform (2 Kgs 23:1-3)
- The pattern: hear God's Word → repent → remove idols → restore true worship
- External reformation (Josiah) prefigures internal transformation (gospel)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 1|2 Kings 23:1-25]] - Josiah's comprehensive reformation
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 16|Deuteronomy 10:16]]; 30:6 - circumcision of the heart
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]] - transformation from glory to glory
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 22|Ephesians 4:22-24]] - put off old self, be renewed, put on new self

**Type Classification**: Application Typology (believers participate in Christ's reforming work)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah reformed external worship by destroying idols and restoring ceremonies; believers are called to internal reformation - minds renewed, lives transformed, becoming "living sacrifices." Where Josiah's reform was national and temporary, the gospel's reformation is personal and eternal. Through union with Christ (Rom 6:1-11), believers participate in His death to sin and resurrection to new life, embodying the "time of reformation" (Heb 9:10) in their transformed existence.
