### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 22#Jeremiah 22 . 15|Jeremiah 22:15-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- אָבִיךָ (avikha) - "your father" - Josiah as standard for Jehoiakim
- עָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה ('asah mishpat utzedaqah) - "did justice and righteousness"
- דָּן דִּין־עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן (dan din-'ani ve'evyon) - "judged the cause of the poor and needy"
- הֲלוֹא־הִיא הַדַּעַת אֹתִי (halo-hi hadda'at oti) - "Is not this to know Me?"

**Context**: Jeremiah condemns Jehoiakim's oppression by contrasting him with his righteous father Josiah, defining true knowledge of God as justice for the afflicted.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Josiah becomes the standard against which his sons are judged (cf. Jer 22:11-12, 24-30)
- Connects covenant faithfulness with social justice (cf. Deut 15:7-11)
- Anticipates the "Righteous Branch" prophecy (Jer 23:5-6)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 25|2 Kings 23:25]] - Josiah's unmatched devotion
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 7|Deuteronomy 15:7-11]] - Torah's justice requirements
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5-6]] - the coming righteous king
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|Matthew 25:31-46]] - Christ as judge who identifies with the poor
  - [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 27|James 1:27]] - pure religion cares for widows and orphans
**Type Classification**: Providential Type (Josiah as righteous standard)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah "knew God" through justice and righteousness; Christ IS "THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" (Jer 23:6) who perfectly embodies justice. Where Josiah practiced righteousness imperfectly, Christ IS righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and becomes sin for us (2 Cor 5:21). The pattern: human righteousness, even at its best, points to the need for divine righteousness incarnate.
