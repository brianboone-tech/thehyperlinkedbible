### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 21|2 Kings 23:21-23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- עֲשׂוּ פֶסַח ('asu pesach) - "keep/make Passover" - covenant meal of deliverance
- כַּכָּתוּב (kakatuv) - "as it is written" - according to Torah prescription
- לֹא־נַעֲשָׂה כַּפֶּסַח הַזֶּה (lo-na'asah kappesach hazzeh) - "no Passover like this had been kept"

**Context**: The climax of Josiah's reformation is the greatest Passover celebration since the judges' era, restoring covenant worship "as written" in the rediscovered Law.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- First proper Passover since Hezekiah's (2 Chr 30), surpassing it in scope
- Restores the feast neglected since Joshua's time (Josh 5:10-12)
- Links to original Passover in Egypt (Exod 12) and Sinai legislation (Num 9)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 1|Exodus 12:1-28]] - original Passover institution
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 30#2 Chronicles 30 . 1|2 Chronicles 30:1-27]] - Hezekiah's Passover
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 1|2 Chronicles 35:1-19]] - Chronicles' expanded account
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 . 7|1 Corinthians 5:7]] - "Christ our Passover has been sacrificed"
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 15|Luke 22:15-20]] - Last Supper
**Type Classification**: Providential Type (divinely ordained feast restored)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah restored the greatest Passover feast celebrating past deliverance; Christ IS the Passover Lamb whose sacrifice secures eternal redemption (1 Cor 5:7). Where Josiah's Passover memorialized exodus from Egypt, Christ's death accomplishes exodus from sin. The Last Supper transforms Passover from memorial to proclamation: "This cup is the new covenant in My blood" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]]).
