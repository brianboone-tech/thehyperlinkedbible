### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 6|Matthew 12:6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- μεῖζον (meizon) - "something greater" - neuter comparative (not "someone")
- τοῦ ἱεροῦ (tou hierou) - "than the temple" - the entire temple complex/institution
- ὧδε (hōde) - "here" - present in this place

**Context**: Defending His disciples' Sabbath activity, Jesus declares Himself greater than the temple, the sacrificial system, and all reformer kings who restored temple worship.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- David ate showbread reserved for priests (1 Sam 21:6, cited in Matt 12:3-4)
- Priests work on Sabbath in temple service yet are guiltless (Matt 12:5)
- Josiah restored the temple and its worship to unprecedented purity (2 Kgs 23:4-7)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4-7]] - Josiah cleanses and restores the temple
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 21#1 Samuel 21 . 6|1 Samuel 21:6]] - David's authority transcends ceremonial law
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] - Jesus IS the temple
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 41|Matthew 12:41-42]] - greater than Jonah and Solomon
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 3|Hebrews 3:3]] - Christ worthy of more glory than Moses
**Type Classification**: Superiority Typology (Christ transcends all types)

**Christological Connection**: Josiah restored the temple; Jesus claims to BE greater than the temple itself. Where Josiah cleansed a building, Christ IS the living temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]). Where Josiah made the physical temple pure, Christ makes believers living stones in God's spiritual temple (1 Pet 2:5). The escalation: Josiah reformed temple worship (shadow); Christ replaces the temple with Himself (substance) and His body, the church (Eph 2:19-22).
