### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 32#Psalm 32 . 1|Psalm 32:1-2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H835|H835]] אַשְׁרֵי (*ʾašrê*) - blessed, happy
- [[Lexicon/H5375|H5375]] נָשָׂא (*nāśāʾ*) - to lift, carry, forgive
- [[Lexicon/H3680|H3680]] כָּסָה (*kāsâ*) - to cover
- [[Lexicon/H2803|H2803]] חָשַׁב (*ḥāšaḇ*) - to reckon, count, impute

**Context**: "Blessed is the one whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered. Blessed is the man against whom the LORD counts no iniquity." David writes this psalm after his sin with Bathsheba and subsequent forgiveness (cf. Psalm 51). It celebrates the blessedness of imputed righteousness.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- 2 Samuel 11-12 - David's sin and Nathan's confrontation
- Psalm 51 - David's confession
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]] - Abraham's faith counted as righteousness

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]] - Righteousness imputed
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 12#2 Samuel 12 . 13|2 Samuel 12:13]] - Nathan: "The LORD has put away your sin"
- **FROM OT**: Psalm 51 - David's confession
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 6|Romans 4:6-8]] - Paul quotes as witness to justification
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 1|Romans 4:1-5]] - Abraham's faith paralleled

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Forward-looking (David's experience illustrates gospel)

**Christological Connection**: Paul quotes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 32#Psalm 32 . 1|Psalm 32:1-2]] in [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 6|Romans 4:6-8]] as David's witness to "the blessing of the one to whom God counts righteousness apart from works." David sinned grievously (adultery, murder), yet experienced full forgiveness—not by works but by God's grace through faith (confession). The verb "count/reckon" (חָשַׁב/λογίζομαι) is the same used for Abraham in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]]. David provides a second witness: justification is not by law-keeping but by faith receiving God's gracious imputation. Christ is the ground of this imputation—David's forgiveness was secured by the future atonement Christ would make; our forgiveness is secured by Christ's accomplished work.
