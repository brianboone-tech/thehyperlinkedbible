**Context**: In Revelation's throne room vision, no one is found worthy to open the sealed scroll until an elder announces: "Weep no more; behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals." The dual title—Lion of Judah and Root of David—identifies Christ through messianic prophecies. The conquering refers to His death and resurrection, qualifying Him to execute judgment and redemption (scroll's contents). When John looks, he sees not a lion but "a Lamb standing, as though it had been slain" (v. 6)—victory came through sacrifice.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 5|Revelation 5:5]] announces: "The Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered." This dual title identifies Christ through foundational messianic prophecies. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 9|Genesis 49:9-10]] prophesied: "Judah is a lion's cub... The scepter shall not depart from Judah." Christ, descended from Judah ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 14|Hebrews 7:14]]), fulfills this royal promise. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1]], 10 prophesied: "There shall come forth a shoot from the stump of Jesse... the root of Jesse shall stand as a signal for the peoples." [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 16|Revelation 22:16]] declares Christ is both "root and descendant of David"—David's source (as eternal Word, [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1-3]]) and David's son (through incarnation, [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 3|Romans 1:3]]). The conquering refers to Christ's death and resurrection—[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]] states Christ "disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, by triumphing over them in him." [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 33|John 16:33]] declares: "I have overcome the world." The dramatic reversal—Lion announced, Lamb revealed (v. 6)—teaches victory's method. The Lion conquers not through military might but through sacrificial death. First Corinthians 1:23-24 declares: "we preach Christ crucified... Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God." The Lamb "standing, as though it had been slain" shows resurrection victory—death defeated, life secured. The worthiness to "open the scroll and its seven seals" demonstrates Christ's authority over history and judgment. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9-10]] celebrates: "Worthy are you to take the scroll and to open its seals, for you were slain, and by your blood you ransomed people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation, and you have made them a kingdom and priests to our God." The trajectory shows: Judah prophesied as royal lion → David's line established as eternal kingdom → Isaiah prophesies root of Jesse → Christ descends from both Judah and David → He conquers through cross → resurrection vindicates → He opens scroll determining history → universal worship results → eternal kingdom established.
