### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6001-6100#H6051|עָנָן]] (*ʿānān*) - "cloud"
- [[Lexicon/H4301-4400#H4390|מָלֵא]] (*mālēʾ*) - "filled, was full"
- [[Lexicon/H1201-1300#H1004|בֵּית יְהוָה]] (*bêt YHWH*) - "house of the LORD"
- [[Lexicon/H3601-3700#H3519|כָּבוֹד]] (*kābôd*) - "glory, weightiness, splendor"
- [[Lexicon/H3601-3700#H3547|כֹּהֵן]] (*kōhēn*) - "priest"
- [[Lexicon/H8401-8500#H8334|לְשָׁרֵת]] (*ləšārēt*) - "to minister, serve"

**Context**: At the temple's dedication, when the priests bring the ark into the Most Holy Place, God's visible presence—the cloud of glory—fills the temple so completely that the priests cannot continue their service. This dramatic theophany validates the temple as God's dwelling place and marks the transition from portable tabernacle to permanent temple.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]] (cloud fills tabernacle), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 15|Exodus 24:15-18]] (glory cloud on Sinai)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 5#2 Chronicles 5 . 13|2 Chronicles 5:13-14]] (parallel account), [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1-3]] (fire and glory), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 4|Ezekiel 10:4, 18-19]] (glory departs from temple)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 9|Luke 2:9]] (glory of the Lord shone), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 17#Matthew 17 . 5|Matthew 17:5]] (bright cloud overshadowed), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 15#Revelation 15 . 8|Revelation 15:8]] (temple filled with smoke from God's glory)

**Christological Connection**: The glory-cloud filling Solomon's temple prefigures Christ's incarnation—the ultimate filling of earthly structure with divine presence. Where the cloud prevented priests from continuing service, Christ's presence fulfills and ends the old covenant priesthood. The pattern moves from glory filling tabernacle (Exodus 40) to glory filling temple (1 Kings 8) to glory tabernacling in flesh ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). Isaiah sees the Lord's glory filling the temple ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1-4]]), which John identifies as a vision of Christ's glory ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 41|John 12:41]]). At the transfiguration, "a bright cloud overshadowed them" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 17#Matthew 17 . 5|Matthew 17:5]]), revealing Christ as the true temple where God's glory dwells. The shepherds experience "the glory of the Lord" shining around them ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 9|Luke 2:9]]) at Christ's birth—the glory has come to dwell permanently in human flesh. Where priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, Christ "entered once for all into the holy places" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]), accomplishing what repeated priestly service could never achieve. Paul declares that believers "with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]])—no cloud prevents access now. Ultimately, Revelation sees "the temple filled with smoke from the glory of God" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 15#Revelation 15 . 8|Revelation 15:8]]) and the new creation where "the city has no need of sun or moon... for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). What [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] foreshadowed temporarily—God's manifest presence overwhelming human capacity—Revelation 21-22 realizes eternally in Christ and His glorified people.
