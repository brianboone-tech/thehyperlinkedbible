### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 1|Ezekiel 43:1-7]]

**Context**: After witnessing the glory depart from the temple ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]]), Ezekiel sees a vision of glory returning from the east to fill the eschatological temple. The voice like many waters, the earth shining with God's radiance, and the declaration that this is God's eternal throne all point to ultimate restoration when God's presence will dwell among His people forever.

**Christological Connection**: Ezekiel's vision of glory returning from the east finds fulfillment in Christ, who ascended from the Mount of Olives ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 9|Acts 1:9-12]]) and will return the same way ([[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 4|Zechariah 14:4]]). The incarnation is the glory's first return—John announces "the Word became flesh and dwelt [tabernacled] among us, and we have seen his glory" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). The voice "like many waters" reappears in Revelation as Christ's voice ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 15|1:15]]), identifying Him as the returning glory. Where Ezekiel saw "the earth shone with his glory" (v. 2), Revelation declares "the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). The throne declaration—"This is the place of my throne... where I will dwell in the midst of the people of Israel forever" (v. 7)—echoes in Revelation: "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]). Christ is both the glory returning and the temple receiving glory—He is "the place of God's throne," the true temple where heaven and earth meet. The eastern approach connects to Christ's olivet ascension and promised return, when "every eye will see him" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 7|Revelation 1:7]]) and God's glory will fill the earth as waters cover the sea. What Ezekiel saw in vision—glory returning, earth shining, voice thundering, eternal dwelling established—finds progressive fulfillment: first coming (incarnation), present age (Spirit in church), second coming (return in glory), and eternal state (new creation where "the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]])).
