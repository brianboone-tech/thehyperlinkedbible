### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]]

- [[Lexicon/H3068-3069#H3068|יְהוָה]] (*YHWH*) - "LORD, covenant name of God"

**Context**: After Israel's acceptance of covenant obligations at Mount Sinai, Moses ratifies the covenant through blood ritual. He builds an altar representing God and twelve pillars representing Israel's tribes. Young men offer burnt and peace offerings. Moses takes half the blood in basins and sprinkles the other half on the altar. After reading the Book of the Covenant and receiving the people's pledge ("All that the LORD has spoken we will do"), Moses sprinkles the blood on the people, declaring "Behold the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you."

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]]'s "blood of the covenant" finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ's Last Supper declaration: "This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 28|Matthew 26:28]]). Where Moses' words inaugurated the Sinai covenant, Christ's words inaugurate the new covenant prophesied by Jeremiah ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]]). The connection between Passover lamb blood (Exodus 12) and Sinai covenant blood (Exodus 24) reveals that redemption from judgment (Passover) leads to covenant relationship (Sinai), both requiring blood sacrifice. Christ fulfills both: His Passover death ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 . 7|1 Corinthians 5:7]]: "Christ, our Passover lamb") establishes new covenant relationship. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 18|Hebrews 9:18-22]] makes the typological connection explicit: just as Moses sprinkled blood to inaugurate the first covenant, so Christ's blood inaugurates the superior covenant. But whereas Moses' sprinkling required repeated annual sacrifices, Christ's blood is shed "once for all" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26]]), achieving "eternal redemption" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]). The sprinkled blood that cleansed externally under Moses gives way to Christ's blood that cleanses "our conscience from dead works to serve the living God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]). [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]] contrasts Abel's blood that cried out for vengeance with Jesus' "sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel"—Christ's blood speaks peace, forgiveness, and eternal covenant security. Where Sinai covenant was conditional ("if you will indeed obey," [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5]]) and therefore breakable ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 32|Jeremiah 31:32]]), the new covenant in Christ's blood is unconditional and unbreakable, founded not on Israel's promise ("we will do") but on Christ's finished work ("It is finished," [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]).
