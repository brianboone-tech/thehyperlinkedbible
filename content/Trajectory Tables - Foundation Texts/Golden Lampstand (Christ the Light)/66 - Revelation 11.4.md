### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 4|Revelation 11:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1501-1600#G1640|ἐλαῖαι]] (*elaiai*) - "olive trees" (nominative plural feminine)
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3087|λυχνίαι]] (*lychniai*) - "lampstands" (nominative plural feminine)
- [[Lexicon/G1701-1800#G1799|ἐνώπιον]] (*enōpion*) - "before, in the presence of"
- [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2962|κυρίου]] (*kyriou*) - "of Lord" (genitive)
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1093|γῆς]] (*gēs*) - "of earth" (genitive)
- [[Lexicon/G2476|ἑστῶτες]] (*hestōtes*) - "standing" (perfect active participle)

**Context**: Revelation 11:4 identifies the two witnesses: "These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands that stand before the Lord of the earth." This echoes Zechariah 4:2-3, where the prophet saw a lampstand with two olive trees feeding it oil. In Revelation, the imagery expands—the witnesses themselves are both olive trees (supplying oil/Spirit) and lampstands (shining light). This shows God's witnesses are Spirit-empowered (olive trees) light-bearers (lampstands) who testify before the world. The golden lampstand's singular form becomes multiple—many believers scattered as lampstands, fed by the Spirit, shining Christ's truth until He returns.

- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 20|Revelation 1:20]] (lampstands are churches), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 14|Matthew 5:14]] (you are light of world), [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]] (you will be my witnesses)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 4|Revelation 11:4]]'s identification of witnesses as "two olive trees and the two lampstands" echoes [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 2|Zechariah 4:2-3]], showing Spirit-empowered witness. The olive trees supply oil (Spirit); the lampstands shine light (testimony). Believers function both ways—receiving Spirit's power, bearing Christ's light. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]] promises: "you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses." The Spirit enables witness; witness manifests Spirit. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 6|Zechariah 4:6]] declares: "Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit, says the LORD of hosts." [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 18|Ephesians 5:18]] commands: "be filled with the Spirit"—continuous oil replenishment like olive trees feeding lampstand. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 14|Matthew 5:14]] declares: "You are the light of the world"—believers are lampstands collectively. The two witnesses represent all faithful testimony—Moses-like and Elijah-like power (v. 6) demonstrating Spirit's empowerment. The phrase "stand before the Lord of the earth" (v. 4) shows dual positioning: before God (receiving revelation, drawing power) and before earth (declaring truth, shining light). First Corinthians 2:4-5 describes Paul's ministry: "my speech and my message were not in plausible words of wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power, so that your faith might not rest in the wisdom of men but in the power of God." The trajectory shows: Zechariah's vision of lampstand with olive trees (OT prophecy) → Christ sends Spirit-empowered witnesses (NT fulfillment) → witnesses shine as lampstands fed by Spirit (ongoing ministry) → final witnesses testify in tribulation (end-times consummation) → eternal witness in new creation (glorified state).
