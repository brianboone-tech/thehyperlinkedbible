**Context**: Revelation 1:12-13, 20 describes John's vision of Christ among seven golden lampstands: "I saw seven golden lampstands, and in the midst of the lampstands one like a son of man" (vv. 12-13). Christ explains: "the seven lampstands are the seven churches" (v. 20). This fulfills and transforms the golden lampstand symbolism—the singular tabernacle lampstand becomes multiple church-lampstands, Christ tends them as priests tended the original. Churches are lampstands only as Christ dwells among them and the Spirit empowers them. This vision establishes Christ's ongoing ministry to His churches.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 12|Revelation 1:12-13]], 20 presents Christ among seven golden lampstands, identified as churches. This transforms the tabernacle's singular lampstand into multiple church-lampstands, Christ tending them as priests once tended the original. The vision fulfills [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 27#Exodus 27 . 20|Exodus 27:20-21]]'s priestly ministry—priests trimmed wicks, replenished oil; Christ tends churches, ensuring they shine. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 1|Revelation 2:1]] states Christ "holds the seven stars in his right hand" and "walks among the seven golden lampstands"—ongoing ministry. This fulfills [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 18#Matthew 18 . 20|Matthew 18:20]]: "where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I among them." The "son of man" designation (v. 13) echoes [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]], identifying Christ as divine Messiah. The high priestly garments—"clothed with a long robe and with a golden sash" (v. 13)—show His priestly function. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]] declares Christ "always lives to make intercession" for believers. The lampstand-church identification shows churches bear Christ's light to the world—not inherently but derivatively. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 14|Matthew 5:14]] declares: "You are the light of the world." [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 15|Philippians 2:15]] commands believers to "shine as lights in the world." The golden material represents divine calling and value. The midst-positioning shows Christ's nearness—not distant observer but present Lord. The trajectory shows: priests tend golden lampstand (OT type) → Christ is the light of the world (fulfillment) → Christ walks among church-lampstands (ongoing ministry) → churches shine His light collectively (participation) → eternal light in new creation (consummation).
