### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 33|Exodus 25:33-34]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1375|גְּבִעִים]] (*gəbiʿîm*) - "cups, calyxes" (plural)
- [[Lexicon/H8246|מְשֻׁקָּדִים]] (*məšuqqādîm*) - "shaped like almonds"
- [[Lexicon/H3730|כַּפְתֹּר]] (*kaptōr*) - "capital, knob"
- [[Lexicon/H6525|וָפֶרַח]] (*wāperaḥ*) - "and flower, blossom"
- [[Lexicon/H7070|קָנֶה]] (*qāneh*) - "branch, shaft"
- [[Lexicon/H8246|שָׁקֵד]] (*šāqēd*) - "almond tree"
- [[Lexicon/H4501|בַּמְּנֹרָה]] (*bammənōrāh*) - "on the lampstand"

**Context**: Exodus 25:33-34 describes the lampstand's almond-blossom ornamentation—each of the six branches decorated with three almond-shaped cups with calyxes and flowers, the central shaft with four. The almond tree (*šāqēd*, meaning "awakener" or "watcher") blooms first in spring, symbolizing resurrection and vigilance. Aaron's rod that budded was almond (Numbers 17:8), confirming God's choice through resurrection power. The lampstand's almond design points to Christ's resurrection—the light that death could not extinguish—and to believers who shine with resurrection life through the Spirit.

- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 17#Numbers 17 . 8|Numbers 17:8]] (Aaron's rod budded, produced almonds), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 11|Jeremiah 1:11-12]] (branch of almond tree, I am watching), [[Readable Bible/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 2#Song of Solomon 2 . 12|Song of Solomon 2:12]] (flowers appear on earth)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 25|John 11:25]] (I am resurrection and life), [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 20|1 Corinthians 15:20]] (Christ firstfruits of those who have slept), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 18|Revelation 1:18]] (I died, and behold I am alive forevermore)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 33|Exodus 25:33-34]]'s almond-blossom design symbolizes resurrection life. The almond tree blooms first in spring, awakening from winter dormancy; Christ rose first from death, the "firstfruits of those who have slept" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 20|1 Corinthians 15:20]]). [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 17#Numbers 17 . 8|Numbers 17:8]] records Aaron's dead rod miraculously budding, blossoming, producing almonds—authenticating priesthood through resurrection power. This prefigures Christ's resurrection authenticating His claims. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 25|John 11:25]] declares: "I am the resurrection and the life." [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 18|Revelation 1:18]] announces: "I died, and behold I am alive forevermore." The lampstand's light symbolizes illumination; the almond blossoms symbolize resurrection—together picturing Christ who said: "I am the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]) and "I am the resurrection and the life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 25|John 11:25]]). Light and life converge in Him. The almond's early blooming represents hope—death's winter ending, resurrection's spring beginning. [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]] declares Christ "is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, that in everything he might be preeminent." [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 4|Romans 6:4]] applies to believers: "as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too might walk in newness of life." The trajectory shows: almond blossoms ornament lampstand (OT symbol) → Aaron's rod buds with almonds (authentication) → Christ rises from death (fulfillment) → believers walk in newness of life (participation) → resurrection to glory (consummation).
