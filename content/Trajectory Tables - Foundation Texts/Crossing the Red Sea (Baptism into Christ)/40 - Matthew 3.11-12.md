### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 11|Matthew 3:11-12]]

**Greek Key Terms**:

- ὕδωρ (hydōr) - water
- μετάνοια (metanoia) - repentance, change of mind
- ἰσχυρότερος (ischyroteros) - mightier, stronger (comparative)
- πνεῦμα ἅγιον (pneuma hagion) - Holy Spirit
- πῦρ (pyr) - fire
- πτύον (ptyon) - winnowing fork, shovel
- ἅλων (halōn) - threshing floor
- σῖτος (sitos) - wheat, grain
- ἀποθήκη (apothēkē) - barn, storehouse
- ἄχυρον (achyron) - chaff
- κατακαίω (katakaiō) - to burn up, consume
- ἄσβεστος (asbestos) - unquenchable, inextinguishable

**Context**: Matthew 3 introduces John the Baptist, the forerunner prophesied in Isaiah 40:3 and Malachi 3:1. John preached in the wilderness of Judea, calling Israel to repentance and baptizing in the Jordan River. Verses 11-12 distinguish John's preparatory water baptism from Christ's baptism with the Holy Spirit and fire.

**Christological Connection**: John's water baptism prefigures Christian baptism, which signifies union with Christ in His death and resurrection. **John's baptism**: External water, symbolizing repentance and readiness for Messiah. **Christ's baptism (death/resurrection)**: The ultimate passage through judgment—Christ died (passed through the waters of wrath) and rose (emerged victorious). **Christian baptism**: Identifies believers with Christ's death, burial, and resurrection ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 3|Romans 6:3-4]]). **Spirit baptism**: Internal transformation—believers receive the Holy Spirit at conversion ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 12#1 Corinthians 12 . 13|1 Corinthians 12:13]]). The trajectory: John prepared the way → Christ accomplished salvation → believers receive the Spirit and are baptized into Christ. The escalation: John's baptism was symbolic; Christ's death is real. Water baptism is external; Spirit baptism is internal. John pointed to Christ; Christ is the substance. The winnowing fork imagery (v. 12) is fulfilled eschatologically: Christ separates believers (wheat) from unbelievers (chaff). [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]]: "There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus." Believers pass through judgment (Christ bore it) and enter rest (gathered into the barn = eternal life).

**Application**: **Water baptism without Spirit baptism is insufficient.** John baptized with water; Christ baptizes with the Spirit. Have you been baptized into Christ by the Spirit? Acts 1:5: "You will be baptized with the Holy Spirit." This happens at conversion (1 Corinthians 12:13). If you have trusted Christ, you have the Spirit. If not, repent and believe. Water baptism is important (obedience to Christ's command, Matthew 28:19), but it doesn't save you—only the Spirit regenerates. **Christ's coming brings both blessing and judgment.** Verse 12: wheat gathered (blessing) vs. chaff burned (judgment). Which are you? If you are in Christ, you are wheat—gathered into the Father's barn. If you reject Christ, you are chaff—facing unquenchable fire. The winnowing is coming. Are you ready? **Prepare to meet your God.** John's message was "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand" (Matthew 3:2). The same message applies today. Christ is coming to judge the living and the dead (Acts 10:42). Repent and trust Him before it's too late.
