### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 15|Isaiah 59:15-19]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 1|Isaiah 63:1-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8302|H8302]] שִׁרְיוֹן (*shiryon*) - coat of mail, breastplate
- [[Lexicon/H3468|H3468]] יֵשַׁע (*yesha*) - salvation (helmet of)
- [[Lexicon/H5360|H5360]] נְקָמָה (*neqamah*) - vengeance
- [[Lexicon/H1660|H1660]] גַּת (*gath*) - winepress

**Context**: Isaiah 59:16-17: "He saw that there was no man, and wondered that there was no one to intercede; then his own arm brought him salvation... He put on righteousness as a breastplate, and a helmet of salvation on his head; he put on garments of vengeance for clothing." Isaiah 63:1-3: "Who is this who comes from Edom... I have trodden the winepress alone... I trampled them in my anger."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- When no human deliverer exists, God Himself becomes warrior
- Armor imagery → [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 10|Ephesians 6:10-17]] (believer's armor)
- Winepress imagery → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 19|Revelation 14:19-20]]; 19:15

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 3|Exodus 15:3]] - God as man of war
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 24#Psalm 24 . 8|Psalm 24:8]] - mighty in battle
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 14|Ephesians 6:14-17]] - armor of God (quotes Isaiah 59)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 13|Revelation 19:13-15]] - "robe dipped in blood... treads the winepress"

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - prophetic anticipation of final victory

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the one clothed in Isaiah's armor - righteousness, salvation, vengeance. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 13|Revelation 19:13-15]] explicitly draws on Isaiah 63: Christ's robe is "dipped in blood" and He "treads the winepress of the fury of the wrath of God." What Isaiah prophesied, Christ fulfills. The armor He wore at the cross, He shares with His people (Eph 6). He fought alone at Calvary; we now fight in His victory.
