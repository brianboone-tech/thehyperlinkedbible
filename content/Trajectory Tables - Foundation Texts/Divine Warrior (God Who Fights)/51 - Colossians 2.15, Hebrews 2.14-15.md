### [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14-15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G554|G554]] ἀπεκδύομαι (*apekduomai*) - to strip off, disarm
- [[Lexicon/G2358|G2358]] θριαμβεύω (*thriambeuo*) - to triumph, lead in triumph
- [[Lexicon/G2673|G2673]] καταργέω (*katargeo*) - to render powerless, destroy
- [[Lexicon/G1228|G1228]] διάβολος (*diabolos*) - devil

**Context**: Colossians 2:15: "He disarmed (ἀπεκδυσάμενος) the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, by triumphing (θριαμβεύσας) over them in him." Hebrews 2:14-15: "That through death he might destroy (καταργήσῃ) the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil, and deliver all those who through fear of death were subject to lifelong slavery."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- OT Divine Warrior defeats earthly enemies
- Christ as Divine Warrior defeats cosmic enemies
- Victory through apparent defeat (cross)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - crushing the serpent's head
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 3|Exodus 15:3]] - the LORD is a man of war
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 1|Isaiah 63:1-6]] - God's victory alone
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 8|1 John 3:8]] - "to destroy the works of the devil"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]] - devil thrown into lake of fire

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - cross as decisive battle

**Christological Connection**: The cross is Christ's battlefield and His victory. Roman triumph imagery (θριαμβεύω) depicts Christ leading captive powers in His victory parade. What appears as defeat (crucifixion) is actually conquest. Through His death, He destroys death's power. The Divine Warrior wins not by superior force but by absorbing evil's worst and emerging victorious through resurrection. This is the "already" of spiritual warfare.
