### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 20#Deuteronomy 20 . 4|Deuteronomy 20:4]]; [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 10#Joshua 10 . 14|Joshua 10:14, 42]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3898|H3898]] לָחַם (*lacham*) - to fight
- [[Lexicon/H3467|H3467]] יָשַׁע (*yasha*) - to save, deliver
- [[Lexicon/H8085|H8085]] שָׁמַע (*shama*) - to hear (God heard Joshua's voice)

**Context**: Deuteronomy 20:4: "The LORD your God is he who goes with you to fight for you against your enemies, to give you the victory." Joshua 10:14: "There has been no day like it before or since, when the LORD heeded the voice of a man, for the LORD fought for Israel."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Red Sea paradigm → conquest application
- God fights WITH Israel (they participate but don't achieve victory)
- Supernatural intervention: sun standing still, hailstones

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 14|Exodus 14:14]]; 15:3 - foundational Divine Warrior texts
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 15|2 Chronicles 20:15]] - "The battle is not yours but God's"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 10|Ephesians 6:10]] - "Be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 31|Romans 8:31]] - "If God is for us, who can be against us?"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - conquest as divine victory pattern

**Christological Connection**: Christ leads His people into their inheritance. As Joshua led Israel into Canaan with God fighting for them, so Christ leads believers into spiritual inheritance with divine power securing victory. The cosmic signs (sun standing still) point to Christ's cosmic authority - creation obeys Him who made it.
