### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 10|Ephesians 6:10-17]]; [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 10#2 Corinthians 10 . 3|2 Corinthians 10:3-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3833|G3833]] πανοπλία (*panoplia*) - full armor
- [[Lexicon/G3823|G3823]] πάλη (*pale*) - wrestling, struggle
- [[Lexicon/G3794|G3794]] ὀχύρωμα (*ochuroma*) - stronghold, fortress
- [[Lexicon/G2507|G2507]] καθαίρεσις (*kathairesis*) - destruction, demolition

**Context**: Ephesians 6:10-17: "Put on the whole armor of God... we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against... spiritual forces of evil." Each piece: truth, righteousness, gospel, faith, salvation, word of God, prayer. 2 Corinthians 10:4: "The weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh but have divine power to destroy strongholds."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 17|Isaiah 59:17]] - God wears the armor
- Ephesians 6 - believers wear God's armor
- Sharing in divine warfare

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 17|Isaiah 59:17]] - God's breastplate and helmet
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 4|Isaiah 11:4-5]] - Messiah's righteousness and faithfulness
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 8|1 Thessalonians 5:8]] - faith, love, hope as armor
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 4#James 4 . 7|James 4:7]] - resist the devil

**Type Classification**: NT Application - believers join the fight

**Christological Connection**: The armor is "of God" - it belongs to Christ first. He wore truth, righteousness, salvation. Now He shares His armor with us. We don't fight in our own strength but "in the Lord and in the strength of his might" (6:10). Every piece of armor points to Christ: He is our truth, righteousness, peace, faith, salvation, and the living Word. We fight "in Christ," wearing Christ.
