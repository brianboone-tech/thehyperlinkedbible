### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11-21]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4170|G4170]] πολεμέω (*polemeo*) - to make war
- [[Lexicon/G4501|G4501]] ῥομφαία (*rhomphaia*) - sword (large, broad)
- [[Lexicon/G3025|G3025]] ληνός (*lenos*) - winepress
- [[Lexicon/G3041|G3041]] λίμνη (*limne*) - lake (of fire)

**Context**: Revelation 19:11-16: Christ appears on a white horse, "Faithful and True, and in righteousness he judges and makes war." His eyes flame, He wears many crowns, His robe is "dipped in blood," and "from his mouth comes a sharp sword." He "treads the winepress of the fury of the wrath of God the Almighty." The beast and false prophet are thrown into the lake of fire (19:20); Satan follows in 20:10.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 3|Exodus 15:3]] "man of war" → Revelation 19 conquering King
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 1|Isaiah 63:1-6]] winepress → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 15|Revelation 19:15]]
- Every Divine Warrior text finds consummation here

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 3|Exodus 15:3]] - LORD is a man of war
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 1|Isaiah 63:1-6]] - treading the winepress
- **TO**: Psalm 2 - nations rage; God's Anointed rules
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 3|Zechariah 14:3]] - LORD fights in day of battle
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]] - "already" victory at cross → "not yet" completion

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - ultimate fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: This IS Christ - King of kings and Lord of lords. Every Divine Warrior image finds its ultimate expression in the returning Christ. The battle is not close - with a word (sword from mouth) He defeats all enemies. The winepress of Isaiah 63 is fulfilled. The serpent of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] meets his final doom (20:10). From Exodus 15 to Revelation 19, the Divine Warrior trajectory culminates in Christ's eternal victory. His "robe dipped in blood" recalls both His cross-work and His judgment-work. The Lamb who was slain is the Lion who conquers.
