### [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 9|Joel 3:9-16]]; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 3|Zechariah 14:3-5]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4421|H4421]] מִלְחָמָה (*milchamah*) - war, battle
- [[Lexicon/H3117|H3117]] יוֹם (*yom*) - day (Day of the LORD)
- [[Lexicon/H3898|H3898]] לָחַם (*lacham*) - to fight
- [[Lexicon/H6944|H6944]] קֹדֶשׁ (*qodesh*) - holy (holy ones with Him)

**Context**: Joel 3:9-16: "Prepare war! Wake up the mighty men... Beat your plowshares into swords... Multitudes, multitudes, in the valley of decision! For the day of the LORD is near." Zechariah 14:3-5: "Then the LORD will go out and fight against those nations as when he fights on a day of battle. On that day his feet shall stand on the Mount of Olives."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Historical divine warfare → eschatological divine warfare
- Day of the LORD as final battle
- Reversal of Isaiah's plowshares (Isa 2:4) - war before peace

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Exodus 14-15 - paradigm of divine warfare
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 4|Isaiah 2:4]]/[[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 3|Micah 4:3]] - swords to plowshares (reversed in Joel)
- **FROM OT**: Ezekiel 38-39 - Gog and Magog final battle
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 11|Acts 1:11-12]] - Christ's return to Mount of Olives
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 16#Revelation 16 . 12|Revelation 16:12-16]] - Armageddon; 19:11-21 - final battle

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - explicit eschatological prophecy

**Christological Connection**: Christ returns to the Mount of Olives ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 11|Acts 1:11-12]]; Zech 14:4) as the Divine Warrior to fight the final battle. The "day of battle" in Zechariah anticipates Christ's Parousia. Joel's "valley of decision" is where Christ judges the nations. The eschatological battle is Christ's victory over all who oppose God's kingdom.
