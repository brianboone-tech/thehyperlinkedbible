### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 14|Exodus 14:14]]; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 3|Exodus 15:3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4421|H4421]] מִלְחָמָה (*milchamah*) - war, battle
- [[Lexicon/H376|H376]] אִישׁ (*ish*) - man (in "man of war")
- [[Lexicon/H3898|H3898]] לָחַם (*lacham*) - to fight, make war
- [[Lexicon/H2790|H2790]] חָרַשׁ (*charash*) - to be silent

**Context**: Exodus 14:14: "The LORD will fight for you, and you have only to be silent (תַּחֲרִישׁוּן)." Exodus 15:3: "The LORD is a man of war (אִישׁ מִלְחָמָה); the LORD is his name." These texts form the foundation of Divine Warrior theology following the Red Sea deliverance.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- First explicit declaration of God as warrior
- Red Sea victory becomes paradigm for all future deliverances
- "Be silent" - salvation is God's work, not Israel's

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - God's enmity against the serpent
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 10#Joshua 10 . 14|Joshua 10:14]] - "The LORD fought for Israel"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 24#Psalm 24 . 8|Psalm 24:8]] - "LORD, mighty in battle"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11]] - "In righteousness he judges and makes war"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]] - triumphing over enemies

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - foundational historical event

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the ultimate Divine Warrior. What God did visibly at the Red Sea - defeating enemies, bringing salvation through water - Christ accomplishes spiritually through cross and resurrection. The command to "be silent" anticipates salvation by grace through faith, not human effort. Jesus "the LORD" fights for His people against sin, Satan, and death.
