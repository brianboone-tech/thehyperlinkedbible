### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 13#Judges 13 . 5|Judges 13:5]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5139|H5139]] נָזִיר (*nāzîr*) - Nazirite, one separated/consecrated
- [[Lexicon/H3467|H3467]] יָשַׁע (*yāshaʿ*) - to save, deliver
- [[Lexicon/H990|H990]] בֶּטֶן (*beṭen*) - womb, belly

**Context**: The angel announces to Manoah's wife: "Behold, you shall conceive and bear a son. No razor shall come upon his head, for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb, and he shall begin to save Israel from the hand of the Philistines." Samson's Nazirite status from conception sets him apart as specially consecrated—yet the narrative ironically traces his systematic violation of every Nazirite stipulation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 1|Numbers 6:1-21]] - Nazirite vow regulations
- [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 14#Judges 14 . 8|Judges 14:8-9]] - Samson touches corpse (violation)
- [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 16#Judges 16 . 17|Judges 16:17-19]] - Samson's hair cut (final violation)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 1|Numbers 6:1-21]] - Nazirite vow instituted
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 1#1 Samuel 1 . 11|1 Samuel 1:11]] - Samuel as Nazirite
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 23|Matthew 2:23]] - Jesus as "Nazarene" (wordplay)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 32|Hebrews 11:32]] - Samson in faith chapter

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Anti-type (ironic contrast to Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Samson as failed Nazirite creates a dramatic anti-type to Christ. Samson was set apart from the womb yet violated his vows; Christ was set apart from eternity and perfectly fulfilled all righteousness. Samson's strength came and went with his hair; Christ's power is eternal. Samson's final victory came through his own death—pulling down the temple on the Philistines; Christ's victory came through His death—destroying the works of the devil. Yet [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 32|Hebrews 11:32]] includes Samson among the heroes of faith, celebrating the faith that persisted despite failures. The anti-type heightens the glory of the true type.
