### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 6#Judges 6 . 34|Judges 6:34]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (*rûaḥ*) - spirit, wind, breath
- [[Lexicon/H3847|H3847]] לָבַשׁ (*lāḇaš*) - to clothe, put on
- [[Lexicon/H1439|H1439]] גִּדְעוֹן (*Gidʿôn*) - Gideon, "hewer, feller"

**Context**: "The Spirit of the LORD clothed Gideon" (literally, "the Spirit of Yahweh clothed itself with Gideon"). This dramatic imagery—the Spirit wearing Gideon like a garment—describes divine empowerment for deliverance. Similar language appears for Othniel (3:10), Jephthah (11:29), and Samson (14:6, 19; 15:14).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 25|Numbers 11:25]] - Spirit resting on elders
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 10#1 Samuel 10 . 6|1 Samuel 10:6]] - Spirit rushing on Saul
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 13|1 Samuel 16:13]] - Spirit rushing on David

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 25|Numbers 11:25]] - Spirit empowerment pattern
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 13|1 Samuel 16:13]] - Spirit on David
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18]] - Spirit on Christ
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]] - Spirit poured out on all flesh

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Forward-looking (Spirit pattern develops toward Messiah)

**Christological Connection**: The Spirit's empowerment of the judges was episodic and task-specific; Christ receives the Spirit "without measure" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 34|John 3:34]]) permanently. The judges were "clothed" with the Spirit temporarily; Christ is anointed with the Spirit eternally. Where the Spirit came upon the judges for specific deliverances, the Spirit remains on Christ and flows through Him to all believers ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]]). The pattern establishes that true deliverance requires divine empowerment, fulfilled ultimately in the Spirit-anointed Messiah.
