### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 4|Isaiah 9:4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5923|H5923]] עֹל (*ʿōl*) - yoke
- [[Lexicon/H4294|H4294]] מַטֶּה (*maṭṭeh*) - rod, staff
- [[Lexicon/H4080|H4080]] מִדְיָן (*Midyān*) - Midian

**Context**: "For the yoke of his burden, and the staff for his shoulder, the rod of his oppressor, you have broken as on the day of Midian." Isaiah invokes Gideon's victory over Midian (Judges 7) as the paradigm for messianic deliverance. This verse appears within the great messianic prophecy leading to "For to us a child is born, to us a son is given" (9:6).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 1|Judges 7:1-25]] - Gideon defeats Midian with 300 men
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 26|Isaiah 10:26]] - Again invokes "day of Midian"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 83#Psalm 83 . 9|Psalm 83:9-11]] - Recollection of Midian's defeat

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 1|Judges 7:1-25]] - Original Midian victory
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 26|Isaiah 10:26]] - Second reference to Midian
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 15|Matthew 4:15-16]] - Quotes [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 1|Isaiah 9:1-2]]
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 79|Luke 1:79]] - Echoes light/darkness imagery

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - Prophetic anticipation uses judges pattern for Messiah

**Christological Connection**: Isaiah elevates Gideon's victory into messianic prophecy. The "day of Midian" was miraculous—300 men defeating a vast army by trumpets and torches, not swords. So the messianic deliverance will be supernatural, accomplished not by human might but by divine power. Yet Gideon himself was flawed ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 8#Judges 8 . 27|Judges 8:27]]); the Messiah will be perfect. Gideon's victory was local and temporary; the Messiah's victory will be universal and eternal. The prophecy uses the judges' pattern while transcending the judges' limitations.
