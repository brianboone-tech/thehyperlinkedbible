### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 26|Romans 11:26]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4506|G4506]] ῥύομαι (*rhyomai*) - to deliver, rescue
- [[Lexicon/G763|G763]] ἀσέβεια (*asebeia*) - ungodliness, impiety
- [[Lexicon/G654|G654]] ἀποστρέφω (*apostrephō*) - to turn away

**Context**: "And in this way all Israel will be saved, as it is written, 'The Deliverer will come from Zion, he will banish ungodliness from Jacob.'" Paul quotes Isaiah 59:20-21 and 27:9, identifying Christ as the ultimate Deliverer who surpasses all the judges.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 20|Isaiah 59:20-21]] - Redeemer comes to Zion
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 9|Isaiah 27:9]] - Jacob's sin removed
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]] - Looking on pierced one

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 20|Isaiah 59:20-21]] - Redeemer coming to Zion
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 14#Psalm 14 . 7|Psalm 14:7]] - "Oh, that salvation would come out of Zion!"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 25|Romans 11:25]] - Mystery of Israel's hardening
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 32|Romans 11:32]] - God's mercy on all

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Christ as ultimate Deliverer

**Christological Connection**: The judges delivered Israel from physical oppressors—Midianites, Philistines, Moabites. Christ delivers from sin itself, "banishing ungodliness from Jacob." The escalation is profound: the judges' deliverance was external and temporary; Christ's deliverance is internal and eternal. The judges could defeat enemies but not transform hearts; Christ removes sin and writes His law on hearts ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]]). Where the judges' salvation required repeated deliverances, Christ's salvation is "once for all" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10]]). He is the Deliverer to whom all the judges pointed.
