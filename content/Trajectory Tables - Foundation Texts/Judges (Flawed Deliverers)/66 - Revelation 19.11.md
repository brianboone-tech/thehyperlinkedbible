### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4103|G4103]] πιστός (*pistos*) - faithful, trustworthy
- [[Lexicon/G228|G228]] ἀληθινός (*alēthinos*) - true, genuine
- [[Lexicon/G1343|G1343]] δικαιοσύνη (*dikaiosynē*) - righteousness, justice
- [[Lexicon/G2919|G2919]] κρίνω (*krinō*) - to judge

**Context**: "Then I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse! The one sitting on it is called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he judges and makes war." Christ appears as the Divine Warrior-Judge, executing final judgment on God's enemies.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 96#Psalm 96 . 13|Psalm 96:13]] - Yahweh comes to judge the earth
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 4|Isaiah 11:4]] - Messiah judges with righteousness
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] - Son of Man given dominion

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 4|Isaiah 11:4]] - Messianic judgment with righteousness
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] - Son of Man's authority
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 17#Acts 17 . 31|Acts 17:31]] - God appointed day of judgment
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 15|Revelation 19:15-16]] - King of kings and Lord of lords

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Christ as Final Judge

**Christological Connection**: The OT judges administered justice imperfectly for a time; Christ administers justice perfectly forever. The judges rode into battle against Midianites and Philistines; Christ rides against all God's enemies, including death and Satan. "Faithful and True" contrasts with the judges' unfaithfulness (Gideon's ephod, Samson's lust, Jephthah's rash vow). "In righteousness he judges" contrasts with the moral chaos when "everyone did what was right in his own eyes." The trajectory culminates: imperfect human judges → prophetic promise of righteous King → Christ's first coming (suffering servant) → Christ's second coming (conquering King). The Judge of all the earth does right ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 25|Genesis 18:25]]), and He is Jesus.
