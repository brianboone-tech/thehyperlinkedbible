### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 2#Judges 2 . 16|Judges 2:16-19]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8199|H8199]] שָׁפַט (*shāphaṭ*) - to judge, govern, deliver
- [[Lexicon/H3467|H3467]] יָשַׁע (*yāshaʿ*) - to save, deliver
- [[Lexicon/H6965|H6965]] קוּם (*qûm*) - to rise up, establish

**Context**: Following Joshua's death and the passing of his generation, Israel fell into the recurring cycle of apostasy, oppression, crying out, and deliverance. God raised up judges—Spirit-empowered deliverers—who saved Israel from their enemies. Yet the cycle repeated: "Whenever the judge died, they turned back and were more corrupt than their fathers."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Deuteronomy 28 - Covenant blessings and curses
- [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 24#Joshua 24 . 31|Joshua 24:31]] - Israel served Yahweh during Joshua's generation
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 8#1 Samuel 8 . 1|1 Samuel 8:1-5]] - Israel's demand for a king

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Deuteronomy 28 - Covenant curses for disobedience
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 12#1 Samuel 12 . 11|1 Samuel 12:11]] - Samuel recalls the judges
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 20|Acts 13:20]] - Paul traces salvation history through judges
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 32|Hebrews 11:32]] - Judges included in hall of faith

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Backward-looking (pattern recognized from NT vantage)

**Christological Connection**: The judges were charismatic deliverers raised up by God's Spirit, yet their salvation was temporary and their character flawed. The very cycle—sin, oppression, cry, deliverance, sin again—demonstrates humanity's need for a Savior who delivers permanently and transforms hearts. Where the judges' deliverance ended at death, Christ's deliverance is eternal. Where the judges could not break the sin cycle, Christ breaks the power of sin. The institution of judgeship anticipates kingship, and kingship anticipates Christ's eternal reign.
