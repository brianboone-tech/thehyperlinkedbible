### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 23|Exodus 21:23-25]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5315|H5315]] נֶפֶשׁ (*nepeš*) - "life, soul"
- [[Lexicon/H5869|H5869]] עַיִן (*ʿayin*) - "eye"
- [[Lexicon/H8127|H8127]] שֵׁן (*šēn*) - "tooth"
- [[Lexicon/H3027|H3027]] יָד (*yāḏ*) - "hand"
- [[Lexicon/H7272|H7272]] רֶגֶל (*reḡel*) - "foot"
- [[Lexicon/H3555|H3555]] כְּוִיָּה (*kᵉwiyyâ*) - "burn"
- [[Lexicon/H6482|H6482]] פֶּצַע (*peṣaʿ*) - "wound"
- [[Lexicon/H2250|H2250]] חַבּוּרָה (*ḥabbûrâ*) - "bruise, stripe"

**Context**: Exodus 21:22-25 addresses injury to a pregnant woman during a fight. If the woman is harmed beyond the loss of the child, the law prescribes: "life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot, burn for burn, wound for wound, stripe for stripe." This lex talionis (law of retaliation) appears also in Leviticus 24:19-20 and Deuteronomy 19:21. Crucially, this law LIMITS vengeance—against the Lamechian pattern of escalating retaliation, the law demands only proportional response.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 23|Genesis 4:23-24]]** shows Lamechian vengeance: death for a wound, 77x for 7x.
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 23|Exodus 21:23-25]]** limits vengeance: wound for wound, no more.
- **[[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 21|Deuteronomy 19:21]]** emphasizes judicial administration: "Show no pity: life for life, eye for eye..."
- **[[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 19|Leviticus 24:19-20]]** applies the principle: "As he has done, so shall it be done to him."

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 4:23-24 (Lamechian excess), Leviticus 24:19-20 (parallel formulation), Deuteronomy 19:21 (judicial context)
- **TO NT**: Matthew 5:38-42 (Christ transcends lex talionis)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (law's limitation prepares for Christ's transcendence)

**Christological Connection**: The lex talionis serves as a middle term in the trajectory from Lamech to Christ. Lamech demanded disproportionate vengeance (77x); the law demanded proportional justice (1:1); Christ demands non-retaliation and unlimited forgiveness (70x7). Jesus explicitly addresses this trajectory in [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 38|Matthew 5:38-42]]: "You have heard that it was said, 'Eye for eye and tooth for tooth.' But I tell you, do not resist an evil person." Christ does not abolish justice but transcends it—His followers absorb wrong rather than retaliate, trusting God to vindicate.
