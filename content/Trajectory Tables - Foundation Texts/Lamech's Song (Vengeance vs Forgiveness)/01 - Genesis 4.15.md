### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 15|Genesis 4:15]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5358|H5358]] נָקַם (*nāqam*) - "to avenge, take vengeance"
- [[Lexicon/H7651|H7651]] שֶׁבַע (*sheḇaʿ*) - "seven"
- [[Lexicon/H226|H226]] אוֹת (*ʾôṯ*) - "sign, mark"
- [[Lexicon/H5221|H5221]] נָכָה (*nāḵâ*) - "to strike, kill"

**Context**: After Cain murdered Abel, God pronounced judgment: Cain would be a fugitive and wanderer, cursed from the ground. Cain complained that his punishment was unbearable and that anyone finding him would kill him. God responds with unexpected mercy: "Not so! If anyone slays Cain, then Cain will be avenged sevenfold." God placed a mark on Cain as a sign of this protection. This sevenfold vengeance is divine, not human—God reserves to Himself the right to avenge His own decree. The number seven signifies completeness; God's retribution would be total.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 24|Genesis 4:24]]** shows Lamech perverting this divine protection into human boast: "If Cain is avenged sevenfold, then Lamech seventy-sevenfold."
- **[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 19|Numbers 35:19]]** establishes the avenger of blood role, channeling vengeance through legal process rather than vigilante action.
- **[[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35]]** reserves vengeance explicitly to God: "Vengeance is Mine, and recompense."

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 4:24 (Lamech's perversion), Deuteronomy 32:35 (divine vengeance reserved)
- **TO NT**: Matthew 18:21-22 (seventy times seven forgiveness), Romans 12:19 (leave room for God's wrath)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (establishes the sevenfold pattern that Christ will transform)

**Christological Connection**: God's protection of Cain illustrates divine mercy to the undeserving—even murderers receive grace. This prepares for Christ's teaching that His followers extend mercy rather than demand vengeance. The sevenfold divine vengeance becomes the baseline that Lamech perverts and Christ transforms.
