### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5359|H5359]] נָקָם (*nāqām*) - "vengeance"
- [[Lexicon/H8005|H8005]] שִׁלֵּם (*šillēm*) - "recompense, repayment"
- [[Lexicon/H7272|H7272]] רֶגֶל (*reḡel*) - "foot"
- [[Lexicon/H4131|H4131]] מוֹט (*môṭ*) - "to slip, totter"
- [[Lexicon/H343|H343]] אֵיד (*ʾēḏ*) - "calamity, disaster"
- [[Lexicon/H7138|H7138]] קָרוֹב (*qārôḇ*) - "near, at hand"

**Context**: Deuteronomy 32 is Moses' Song, a prophetic poem recounting Israel's history and warning of judgment for unfaithfulness. Verse 35 declares: "Vengeance is Mine, and recompense; their foot shall slip in due time; for the day of their calamity is at hand, and their doom is coming quickly." This verse explicitly reserves vengeance to God alone—removing it from human prerogative. This directly contradicts Lamech's claim to personal vengeance (Gen 4:24) and establishes the principle Paul will cite in Romans 12:19.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 24|Genesis 4:24]]** shows Lamech claiming vengeance for himself—77-fold retaliation.
- **[[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35]]** reserves vengeance exclusively to God—humans must not usurp this divine prerogative.
- **[[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20#Proverbs 20 . 22|Proverbs 20:22]]** applies this practically: "Do not say, 'I will repay evil'; wait for the LORD, and He will deliver you."
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 94#Psalm 94 . 1|Psalm 94:1]]** addresses God as "God of vengeance"—this attribute belongs to Him alone.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 4:24 (Lamech's claim), Proverbs 20:22 (practical application), Psalm 94:1 (God of vengeance)
- **TO NT**: Romans 12:19 (Paul's quotation), Hebrews 10:30 (author's quotation)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (establishes the principle Christ's followers will embody)

**Christological Connection**: God's reservation of vengeance provides the theological foundation for Christ's teaching on non-retaliation. Paul explicitly quotes [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35]] in [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 19|Romans 12:19]]: "Do not avenge yourselves, beloved, but leave room for God's wrath. For it is written: 'Vengeance is Mine; I will repay,' says the Lord." [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 30|Hebrews 10:30]] similarly applies this verse. The trajectory from Lamech (claiming vengeance) through Moses (reserving vengeance to God) to Christ (commanding forgiveness and non-retaliation) shows progressive revelation. Christians do not take vengeance not because evil doesn't matter, but because God will judge perfectly—freeing believers to forgive as they have been forgiven.
