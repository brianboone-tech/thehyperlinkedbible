### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 18#Matthew 18 . 21|Matthew 18:21-22]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G264|G264]] ἁμαρτάνω (*hamartanō*) - "to sin"
- [[Lexicon/G863|G863]] ἀφίημι (*aphiēmi*) - "to forgive, release"
- [[Lexicon/G2034|G2034]] ἑπτάκις (*heptakis*) - "seven times"
- [[Lexicon/G1441|G1441]] ἑβδομηκοντάκις (*hebdomēkontakis*) - "seventy times"

**Context**: Matthew 18 addresses community life in the kingdom. After teaching on handling sin within the church (vv. 15-20), Peter asks: "Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me and I forgive him? Up to seven times?" Peter's suggestion of seven times was generous by rabbinic standards (some taught three times). Jesus' response astonishes: "I do not say to you up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven" (ἑβδομηκοντάκις ἑπτά). The phrase deliberately echoes and inverts Lamech's "seventy-sevenfold" vengeance (Gen 4:24). Where Lamech demanded 77x retaliation, Christ commands 70x7 (490) fold forgiveness.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 15|Genesis 4:15]]** established God's sevenfold protection of Cain.
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 24|Genesis 4:24]]** records Lamech's boast of 77-fold vengeance.
- **[[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35]]** reserved vengeance to God alone.
- Jesus deliberately inverts the Lamechian mathematics, transforming the vengeance trajectory into unlimited forgiveness.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 4:24 (Lamech's 77x vengeance—direct verbal echo), Deuteronomy 32:35 (vengeance reserved to God)
- **FROM MATTHEW**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 18#Matthew 18 . 23|Matthew 18:23-35]] (parable of unmerciful servant illustrates)
- **NT PARALLELS**: Luke 17:3-4 (seven times daily), Colossians 3:13 (forgive as forgiven)

**Type Classification**: Backward-Looking (recognizes Genesis 4:24 as negative type that Christ inverts)

**Christological Connection**: Christ's "seventy times seven" is the definitive reversal of the Cainite trajectory. Several dimensions emerge: (1) **Mathematics Inverted**: 77x vengeance becomes 70x7 forgiveness—the curse-line's numbers are transformed into grace. (2) **Posture Reversed**: Lamech demanded retaliation for wounds received; Christ's followers absorb wounds and extend forgiveness. (3) **Identity Transformed**: Lamech identified with Cain's protection to claim greater privilege; Christians identify with Christ's forgiveness to extend greater mercy. (4) **Parable Application**: The unmerciful servant (Matt 18:23-35) who demanded repayment after being forgiven millions illustrates the Lamechian spirit persisting in someone who has received grace—and faces severe judgment. (5) **Ground of Forgiveness**: The servant was forgiven a debt of 10,000 talents (millions); his fellow servant owed 100 denarii (months' wages). Christians forgive others' finite sins because God has forgiven their infinite debt.
