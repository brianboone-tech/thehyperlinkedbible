### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 27#Deuteronomy 27 . 26|Deuteronomy 27:26]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H0779|H0779]] אָרוּר (arur) - cursed
- [[Lexicon/H6965|H6965]] קוּם (qum) - confirm, establish, uphold
- [[Lexicon/H1697|H1697]] דָּבָר (dabar) - word, commandment
- [[Lexicon/H6213|H6213]] עָשָׂה (asah) - do, perform

**Context**: The final curse in the twelve curses recited at Mount Ebal (Deut 27:15-26). This comprehensive curse pronounces judgment on "everyone who does not confirm all the words of this law by doing them." The "all" is critical - partial obedience does not escape the curse.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This curse becomes the basis for covenant lawsuit oracles in the prophets (Isa 24:5-6; Jer 11:3-8; Mal 2:2)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 28#Deuteronomy 28 . 15|Deuteronomy 28:15-68]] elaborates the specific curses for covenant violation in horrifying detail
- Israel's exile is interpreted as the execution of this covenant curse ([[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 17#2 Kings 17 . 7|2 Kings 17:7-23]]; Lam 1-5)
- No OT text offers escape from the curse through human effort; only divine intervention can redeem (Deut 30:1-6)

  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 11#Jeremiah 11 . 3|Jeremiah 11:3]] - prophetic covenant lawsuit); Lamentations 1-5 (curse executed in exile
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 10|Galatians 3:10]] - all under law are under curse

**Type Classification**: FORWARD-LOOKING DIRECT TYPE - God commanded this curse formula as part of the Mosaic covenant, anticipating its fulfillment on the true covenant mediator who would bear the curse for His people

**Christological Connection**: Christ "became a curse for us" (Gal 3:13) by enduring the comprehensive covenant curse pronounced in [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 27#Deuteronomy 27 . 26|Deuteronomy 27:26]]. His substitutionary death on the cross satisfied the law's demand that curse fall on covenant-breakers. Because no human "confirms all the words of this law by doing them," all are under the curse (Rom 3:19-20; Gal 3:10). Christ alone bore the full weight of God's covenant wrath, redeeming His people from the curse "so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles" (Gal 3:14).
