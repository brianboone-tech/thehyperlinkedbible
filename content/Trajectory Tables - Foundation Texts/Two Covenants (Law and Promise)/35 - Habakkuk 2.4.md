### [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 4|Habakkuk 2:4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6662|H6662]] צַדִּיק (tsaddiq) - righteous, just
- [[Lexicon/H0530|H0530]] אֱמוּנָה (emunah) - faith, faithfulness, steadfastness
- [[Lexicon/H2421|H2421]] חָיָה (chayah) - live, have life

**Context**: In the midst of prophesying Babylon's invasion of Judah, Habakkuk receives God's answer: the proud oppressor will fall, but "the righteous shall live by his faith." This verse becomes one of the most quoted OT texts in the NT, foundational to Paul's doctrine of justification.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This verse echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]] (Abraham's faith counted as righteousness) and anticipates the faith-principle of the new covenant
- Contrasts with [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 5|Leviticus 18:5]] ("do this and live") - here life comes not by doing but by faith
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]] anticipates God circumcising hearts to love Him - the internal transformation enabling faith
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] promises the new covenant where God writes law on hearts, enabling faithful obedience

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]] - Abraham believed and it was counted as righteousness
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]] - heart transformation
- **FROM OT**:
  - No direct OT quotations, but principle assumed in Psalms of trust (Ps 37:3-5; 62:8)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 17|Romans 1:17]] - gospel reveals righteousness by faith
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 11|Galatians 3:11]] - no one justified by law
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 38|Hebrews 10:38]] - righteous live by faith, not shrink back
**Type Classification**: FORWARD-LOOKING PROVIDENTIAL TYPE - God providentially arranged Habakkuk's crisis to reveal the faith-principle that would characterize new covenant salvation; faith in crisis points forward to saving faith in Christ

**Christological Connection**: The "righteous by faith shall live" principle finds ultimate fulfillment in union with Christ. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 17|Romans 1:17]] identifies the gospel as revealing "the righteousness of God... from faith for faith, as it is written, 'The righteous shall live by faith.'" [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 11|Galatians 3:11]] uses this verse to prove justification is by faith in Christ, not law-works. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 38|Hebrews 10:38]] applies it to perseverance in faith, not shrinking back. Christ is both the object of saving faith and the one who perfectly demonstrated faith/faithfulness to the Father. United to Christ by faith, believers receive His righteousness and live by His life (Gal 2:20: "The life I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God").
