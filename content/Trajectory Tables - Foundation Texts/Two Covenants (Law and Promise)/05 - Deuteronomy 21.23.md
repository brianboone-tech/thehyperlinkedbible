### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 23|Deuteronomy 21:23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7045|H7045]] קְלָלָה (qelalah) - curse, cursed thing
- [[Lexicon/H0430|H0430]] אֱלֹהִים (elohim) - God
- [[Lexicon/H8518|H8518]] תָּלָה (talah) - hang, suspend
- [[Lexicon/H6086|H6086]] עֵץ (ets) - tree, wood

**Context**: The law requires that the body of an executed criminal hung on a tree must be buried the same day, "for a hanged man is cursed by God." The public display of hanging signifies the person died under God's judicial curse. This provides the typological framework for understanding Christ's crucifixion.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This law establishes hanging on a tree as the visible sign of God's covenant curse
- [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 8#Joshua 8 . 29|Joshua 8:29]] and 10:26-27 show this law applied to covenant enemies (king of Ai, five Amorite kings)
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 21#2 Samuel 21 . 6|2 Samuel 21:6-9]] records Saul's sons hung before the LORD to atone for covenant violation
- [[Readable Bible/17 - Esther/Esther 7#Esther 7 . 10|Esther 7:10]] (Haman hung on gallows) and [[Readable Bible/17 - Esther/Esther 9#Esther 9 . 13|Esther 9:13-14]] show execution by hanging as divine judgment
- No OT text anticipates the righteous one would be "hung on a tree" - this is revealed only in the NT

**Type Classification**: BACKWARD-LOOKING DIRECT TYPE - The law commanded burial of the cursed one hung on a tree, but only from the NT vantage point is it seen that the Righteous One would hang on a tree, bearing the curse for covenant-breakers

**Christological Connection**: Christ's crucifixion was not merely Roman execution but divine curse-bearing. By dying "hung on a tree," Christ fulfilled [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 23|Deuteronomy 21:23]]'s curse formula (Gal 3:13). The One who knew no sin became sin for us (2 Cor 5:21), bearing the full weight of the covenant curse pronounced on those who fail to "confirm all the words of this law" (Deut 27:26). The crucifixion was both Roman political execution and divine judicial sentence - the curse of God executed on God's Son as substitute for cursed sinners. Through this "curse for us," Christ "redeemed us from the curse of the law, so that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles" (Gal 3:13-14).
