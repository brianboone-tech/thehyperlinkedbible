### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13-14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1805|G1805]] ἐξαγοράζω (exagorazo) - redeem, buy back, ransom
- [[Lexicon/G2671|G2671]] κατάρα (katara) - curse, cursed thing
- [[Lexicon/G1096|G1096]] γίνομαι (ginomai) - become, come to be
- [[Lexicon/G2910|G2910]] κρεμάννυμι (kremannymi) - hang, suspend
- [[Lexicon/G3586|G3586]] ξύλον (xylon) - tree, wood, cross
- [[Lexicon/G2129|G2129]] εὐλογία (eulogia) - blessing

**Context**: The climax of Paul's argument that justification is by faith, not law-works. Having proven all under law are under curse (v. 10) and that no one is justified by law (v. 11), Paul now reveals the solution: Christ "redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Paul synthesizes three OT texts in this argument:
  1. Deuteronomy 27:26 - "Cursed is everyone who does not abide by all things written in the Book of the Law" (Gal 3:10)
  2. Habakkuk 2:4 - "The righteous shall live by faith" (Gal 3:11)
  3. Deuteronomy 21:23 - "Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree" (Gal 3:13)
- This interpretive synthesis follows the OT pattern of "interpretive blends" where later texts combine earlier texts to reveal deeper theological truth

**Type Classification**: BACKWARD-LOOKING PROVIDENTIAL TYPE - Christ's crucifixion was sovereignly arranged to fulfill the curse formula of Deuteronomy 21:23; recognized as substitutionary curse-bearing only from NT vantage point

**Christological Connection**: Christ's death on the cross was not merely Roman execution but divine penal substitution. By being "hanged on a tree," Christ became the cursed one of [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 23|Deuteronomy 21:23]], bearing the comprehensive covenant curse of [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 27#Deuteronomy 27 . 26|Deuteronomy 27:26]] that falls on all who fail to keep "all things written in the Book of the Law." The infinitely valuable person of the God-man could bear in finite time the infinite curse deserved by all who would believe. The purpose clause (v. 14) reveals the double result: (1) "that in Christ Jesus the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles" (fulfilling Gen 12:3), and (2) "so that we might receive the promised Spirit through faith" (fulfilling Jer 31:33; Ezek 36:27). Christ's curse-bearing is the hinge between the Mosaic covenant of curse and the Abrahamic/New covenant of blessing.
