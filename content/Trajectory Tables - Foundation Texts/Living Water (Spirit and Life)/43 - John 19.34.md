### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3057|G3057]] λόγχη *lonche* - spear, lance
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4125|G4125]] πλευρά *pleura* - side, rib
- [[Lexicon/G3501-3600#G3572|G3572]] νύσσω *nysso* - to pierce, prick
- [[Lexicon/G129|G129]] αἷμα *haima* - blood
- [[Lexicon/G5201-5300#G5204|G5204]] ὕδωρ *hydor* - water

**Context**: After Jesus' death on the cross, soldiers came to break the legs of the crucified to hasten death. Finding Jesus already dead, one soldier pierced His side with a spear, and immediately blood and water flowed out. John emphasizes eyewitness testimony (19:35) and scriptural fulfillment (19:36-37).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1]]** - Water flows from the threshold of the temple toward the east → [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] identifies Jesus' body as the temple → water flows from Jesus' side
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]]** - "They will look on Me, the One they have pierced" (quoted in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 37|John 19:37]])
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]]** - "On that day a fountain will be opened to the house of David... to cleanse them from sin and impurity" → fountain opened by piercing Christ's side

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Ezekiel 47:1 (water from temple); Zechariah 12:10 (pierced One); Zechariah 13:1 (fountain opened for cleansing)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]] - rivers of living water = Spirit, given after glorification
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 6|1 John 5:6-8]] - Jesus came by water and blood; Spirit testifies
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] - river from throne of Lamb
**Type Classification**: This is **fulfillment** of multiple OT types converging in one event:
- **Temple type** (Ezek 47:1) - **Direct type** fulfilled
- **Cleansing fountain** (Zech 13:1) - **Forward-looking prophecy** fulfilled
- **Pierced One** (Zech 12:10) - **Direct prophecy** fulfilled

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] is the physical, historical event that fulfills the entire living water trajectory:

1. **Jesus as Temple**: John already established Jesus' body as the temple (2:19-21). [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1]] saw water flowing from under the temple threshold. Now water flows from Jesus' pierced side.

2. **Timing = Glorification**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]] explained the Spirit couldn't be given "because Jesus had not yet been glorified." His death IS His glorification ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 23|John 12:23-24]]; 13:31-32; 17:1). The water flowing from His side signals the Spirit is now available.

3. **Blood and Water Symbolism**:
   - **Blood**: Atonement (Lev 17:11; Heb 9:22)
   - **Water**: Cleansing/regeneration/Spirit (Ezek 36:25-27; [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]]; [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 6|1 John 5:6-8]])
   - Together: Complete salvation (justification + sanctification)

4. **Zechariah 12-13 Fulfilled**:
   - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]]: "They will look on Me, the One they have pierced" (quoted [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 37|John 19:37]])
   - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]]: "On that day a fountain will be opened... to cleanse them from sin" - The piercing OPENS the fountain

5. **Ezekiel 47 Escalated**:
   - Ezekiel: Water flows from temple building
   - John: Water flows from Christ's body (the true temple)
   - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]: Water flows from throne of "God and of the Lamb"

6. **Connection to Pentecost**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]] says the exalted Jesus "has received from the Father the promised Holy Spirit and has poured out what you now see and hear." The water from Jesus' side at crucifixion anticipates the Spirit poured out at Pentecost.

The escalation: Physical temple building → Christ's physical body → Spiritual temple (church, 1 Cor 3:16) → Eternal city (Rev 21-22). Water from threshold → water from pierced side → Spirit poured out → river of life from Lamb's throne.
