### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4215|G4215]] ποταμός *potamos* - river
- [[Lexicon/G5201-5300#G5204|G5204]] ὕδωρ *hydor* - water
- [[Lexicon/G2222|G2222]] ζωή *zoe* - life (genitive: "of life")
- [[Lexicon/G2362|G2362]] θρόνος *thronos* - throne
- [[Lexicon/G721|G721]] ἀρνίον *arnion* - lamb, Lamb (of God)
- [[Lexicon/G3586|G3586]] ξύλον *xylon* - tree, wood (here: tree of life)

**Context**: Revelation 22:1-5 concludes the vision of the New Jerusalem. After describing the city's glory (Rev 21:9-27), John sees the river of life flowing from God's throne and the tree of life bearing monthly fruit with healing leaves. This deliberately echoes Genesis 2-3 (Eden) and Ezekiel 47 (temple river), showing the consummation brings full-circle return to paradise, now glorified and eternal.

**OT-to-OT Development** (Revelation as culmination):
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 9|Genesis 2:9-10]]** - Tree of life and river in Eden → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] restores both
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|Genesis 3:22-24]]** - Access to tree of life barred after fall → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 14|Revelation 22:14]], 19 access restored
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1]], 7, 12** - River from temple, trees on both banks, fruit monthly, leaves for healing → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] quotes nearly verbatim
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]]** - Living waters from Jerusalem → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] from God's throne

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 2:9-10 (Eden's river and tree); Ezekiel 47:1-12 (temple river); Zechariah 14:8 (living waters from Jerusalem); Psalm 46:4 (river making glad God's city)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10-14]] - living water to eternal life
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - rivers of living water = Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 17|Revelation 7:17]] - Lamb leads to springs of living water
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 6|Revelation 21:6]] - spring of water of life
**Type Classification**: This is **eschatological consummation** of all prior types:
- Eden = **protological type** (beginning) → New Jerusalem = **eschatological antitype** (ending)
- Temple = **provisional type** (temporary dwelling) → Throne = **eternal reality** (God dwelling with man forever, Rev 21:3)
- All prior types were **shadows** (Col 2:17; Heb 8:5; 10:1) → Revelation 22 is **substance**

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] is the consummation of the entire living water trajectory, centering on Christ:

1. **Christ as Co-Source**: "Throne of God AND of the Lamb" (Rev 22:1, 3)
   - Not two thrones but ONE throne shared by Father and Son
   - Yahweh = fountain of living waters (Jer 2:13)
   - Christ = source of living water ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-38]])
   - Revelation unites them: river flows from THEIR throne

2. **Eden Restored and Glorified**:
   - **Genesis 2**: River flows FROM Eden TO world
   - **Revelation 22**: River flows FROM throne (in city) TO nations (Rev 22:2 "healing of the nations")
   - **Escalation**: Physical → Spiritual → Eternal
   - **Access**: Tree of life barred (Gen 3:24) → Tree accessible (Rev 22:14)

3. **Ezekiel 47 Fulfilled**:
   - **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 12|Ezekiel 47:12]]**: Trees with monthly fruit, leaves for healing
   - **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]]**: Direct quote - "tree of life bearing twelve crops of fruit, yielding its fruit every month. And the leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations"
   - **Fulfillment**: Not physical temple in earthly Jerusalem but God's throne in New Jerusalem

4. **Living Water = Eternal Life**:
   - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 14|John 4:14]]: "The water I give will become a spring welling up to eternal life"
   - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]]: Living water = Holy Spirit
   - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]: River of water of LIFE
   - Integration: The Spirit gives eternal life; in consummation, the river of life flows perpetually

5. **No More Thirst**:
   - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 16|Revelation 7:16-17]]: "Never again will they hunger or thirst... the Lamb will lead them to springs of living water"
   - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 6|Revelation 21:6]]: "To the thirsty I will give to drink without cost from the spring of the water of life"
   - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 17|Revelation 22:17]]: "Let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who desires take the water of life without price"
   - Fulfillment: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 10|Isaiah 49:10]] ("They will not hunger or thirst"); [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 35|John 6:35]] ("will never thirst")

6. **Trinitarian Consummation**:
   - **Father**: God on the throne (Rev 22:1, 3)
   - **Son**: The Lamb on the throne (Rev 22:1, 3); the Lamb is the lamp (Rev 21:23)
   - **Spirit**: The river of life (implicit - cf. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]])
   - Unity: ONE throne, ONE river, ONE tree, ONE city

7. **Superiority and Escalation**:
   - **Eden**: One river → four headwaters | Expelled from garden
   - **Rock**: Water for 40 years | Temporary provision
   - **Temple**: Vision of future | Never literally fulfilled
   - **Christ**: Living water = Spirit | "Not yet given" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]])
   - **Pentecost**: Spirit poured out | Firstfruits
   - **New Jerusalem**: River of life FOREVER | Eternal reality, no sun needed (Rev 22:5)

The trajectory completes Fairbairn's principle: "The far distant ends of revelation embrace each other." Genesis 2 (protology) meets Revelation 22 (eschatology). The river flows from Eden's past through redemptive history to New Jerusalem's eternal future, always sourced in God's presence, mediated through Christ the Lamb, empowered by the Spirit, bringing LIFE to all who come and drink.

**Final Invitation**: Revelation 22:17 - "The Spirit and the bride say, 'Come!' And let the one who hears say, 'Come!' Let the one who is thirsty come; and let the one who wishes take the free gift of the water of life."
