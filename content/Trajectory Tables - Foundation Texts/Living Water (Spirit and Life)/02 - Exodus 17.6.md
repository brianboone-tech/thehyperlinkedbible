### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4301-4400#H4325|H4325]] מַיִם *mayim* - water
- [[Lexicon/H6501-6600#H6697|H6697]] צוּר *tsur* - rock
- [[Lexicon/H5201-5300#H5221|H5221]] נָכָה *nakah* - to strike, smite
- [[Lexicon/H2701-2800#H2722|H2722]] חֹרֵב *Choreb* - Horeb (the mountain of God)

**Context**: At Rephidim, the Israelites complained against Moses for lack of water. God commanded Moses to strike the rock at Horeb with his staff, and water gushed out to provide for the people. This miracle demonstrates God's provision in the wilderness and His presence accompanying Israel (the rock at Horeb = the mountain of God).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 11|Numbers 20:11]]** - Second water-from-rock incident at Kadesh. Moses strikes the rock (against God's command to speak to it), suggesting the rock shouldn't be struck twice (Heb 10:10).
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 15|Psalm 78:15-16]], 20** - Recounts the miracle: "He split the rocks in the wilderness and gave them abundant drink... streams gushed out"
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 41|Psalm 105:41]]** - "He opened the rock and water gushed out; it flowed like a river in the desert"
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 21|Isaiah 48:21]]** - "He made water flow for them from the rock; He split the rock and water gushed out"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Garden of Eden (Gen 2:10 - river from God's presence); God's provision of manna (Ex 16)
- **FROM OT**:

  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - Christ the source of living water
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] - water from Christ's pierced side
**Type Classification**: **Providential** (sovereignly arranged miracle, not commanded institution) and **Backward-looking** (recognized as Christological type from Paul's interpretation in 1 Cor 10:4)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]] provides the definitive interpretation: "They drank from the spiritual rock that accompanied them, and that rock was Christ." The physical rock struck by Moses prefigures Christ smitten on the cross. The water that flowed represents the Holy Spirit poured out through Christ's death ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]]: "the Spirit had not yet been given, because Jesus had not yet been glorified"). The escalation: physical water satisfying temporary thirst → Holy Spirit providing eternal life ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 13|John 4:13-14]]).
