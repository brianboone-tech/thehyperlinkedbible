### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4095|G4095]] πίνω *pino* - to drink
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4152|G4152]] πνευματικός *pneumatikos* - spiritual
- [[Lexicon/G4073|G4073]] πέτρα *petra* - rock, large stone
- [[Lexicon/G189|G189]] ἀκολουθέω *akoloutheo* - to follow, accompany
- [[Lexicon/G5501-5600#G5547|G5547]] Χριστός *Christos* - Christ, Messiah, Anointed One

**Context**: 1 Corinthians 10:1-13 uses Israel's wilderness experience as typological warning to the Corinthian church. Paul lists privileges Israel enjoyed (baptism in cloud and sea, spiritual food and drink) yet most perished due to idolatry and immorality. Verse 4 provides explicit Christological interpretation of the water-from-rock miracle (Ex 17:6; Num 20:11).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]** - God commands Moses to strike the rock at Horeb; water gushes out
- **[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 11|Numbers 20:11]]** - Moses strikes the rock at Kadesh (against command to speak to it); water flows abundantly
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 15|Psalm 78:15-16]], 20** - "He split the rocks in the wilderness... streams gushed out"
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 41|Psalm 105:41]]** - "He opened the rock and water gushed out; it flowed like a river in the desert"
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 21|Isaiah 48:21]]** - "He made water flow for them from the rock"

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Exodus 17:6; Numbers 20:11 (water from rock); Jewish tradition of rock following Israel
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - Christ offers living water = Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] - water from Christ's pierced side
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11-12]] - Christ entered the Most Holy Place with His own blood
**Type Classification**: Paul explicitly identifies this as **typological** (1 Cor 10:6, 11 uses τύπος *typos*). The rock is:
- **Providential type** (sovereignly arranged event, not commanded institution)
- **Backward-looking** (recognized as Christological only from NT perspective)
- **Pre-incarnate Christophany** (Christ present with Israel before incarnation)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]] provides the clearest OT-to-NT typological equation in the living water trajectory:

1. **Explicit Identification**: "The rock WAS Christ" - Not merely symbolized or prefigured, but Paul says the rock WAS (ἦν *en*) Christ. This implies:
   - Christ's **pre-existence**
   - Christ's **presence** with Israel in wilderness
   - Christ's **provision** of spiritual sustenance

2. **Physical → Spiritual Escalation**:
   - **OT**: Physical rock struck → physical water → sustained physical life
   - **NT**: Christ crucified (struck) → Holy Spirit poured out → imparts eternal life
   - Paul calls it "spiritual drink" (πνευματικὸν πόμα *pneumatikon poma*), not merely metaphorical but indicating the Spirit

3. **Rock Struck Once**:
   - Exodus 17: Rock struck once at Horeb (legitimate command)
   - Numbers 20: Moses strikes rock again at Kadesh (disobedient - should have spoken to it)
   - Typology: Christ dies once for all (Heb 10:10, 12, 14). Striking the rock twice violated the type.

4. **Following Rock**:
   - Jewish tradition: Rock/well followed Israel physically through wilderness
   - Paul's interpretation: Christ accompanied Israel spiritually
   - NT reality: Christ is with His people always (Matt 28:20; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 23|John 14:23]])

5. **Living Water Connection**:
   - Exodus 17/Numbers 20: Water from rock
   - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]: Rivers of living water from believers = Holy Spirit
   - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]: Rock = Christ; spiritual drink = Spirit from Christ
   - Integration: The rock smitten = Christ crucified; water flowing = Spirit poured out

6. **Superiority**:
   - Israel drank physical water and still died in wilderness
   - Believers drink spiritual water (receive the Spirit) and have eternal life
   - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 13|John 4:13-14]]: "Everyone who drinks this water [physical] will be thirsty again, but whoever drinks the water I give him [spiritual] will never thirst"

The trajectory completes: Eden's river → water from rock (Christ with Israel) → rivers from Christ/believers ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]]) → river from throne of God and Lamb (Rev 22:1). Paul's interpretation establishes that Christ was ALWAYS the source, even in OT times, progressively revealed until fully manifested in incarnation, crucifixion, and Pentecost.
