### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4701-4800#H4726|H4726]] מָקוֹר *maqor* - fountain, spring, source
- [[Lexicon/H4301-4400#H4325|H4325]] מַיִם *mayim* - water
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2416|H2416]] חַיִּים *chayyim* - living, alive (plural construct: "living waters")
- [[Lexicon/H953|H953]] בּוֹר *bor* - cistern, pit (appears twice: "cisterns, broken cisterns")

**Context**: Jeremiah 2:13 is the hinge verse in Yahweh's covenant lawsuit against Judah. God indicts His people for double evil: (1) forsaking Him, "the fountain of living waters," and (2) digging their own cisterns, which are broken and cannot hold water. This is the first occurrence of the phrase "living waters" (מְקוֹר מַיִם חַיִּים *meqor mayim chayyim*) in Scripture, establishing the verbal link that John 7:38 will later quote.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 13|Jeremiah 17:13]]** - Jeremiah repeats the phrase: "O LORD... all who forsake You will be put to shame... for they have forsaken the LORD, the fountain of living water"
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]]** - "On that day a fountain will be opened to the house of David... to cleanse them from sin and impurity" - develops the fountain imagery toward eschatological cleansing
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]]** - "On that day living water will flow out from Jerusalem" - uses Jeremiah's exact phrase מַיִם חַיִּים *mayim chayyim* but shifts from fountain to flowing river

**Type Classification**: Not a predictive prophecy but **typological identification**. God as "fountain of living waters" is **forward-looking** in the sense that it anticipates eschatological fulfillment (Zech 14:8; John 7:37-39), and **backward-looking** from NT perspective which sees Christ as the incarnate fountain.

**Christological Connection**: Jeremiah identifies **Yahweh** as the fountain of living waters. John's Gospel reveals that Jesus claims this divine identity for Himself ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10]]; 7:37). The progression:
- OT: Yahweh = fountain of living waters (Jer 2:13; 17:13)
- Gospels: Jesus = source of living water ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10]], 14; 7:37-38)
- Interpretive key: "He was speaking about the Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]])

The escalation: Physical fountain → Yahweh (spiritual fountain) → Christ (incarnate fountain) → Holy Spirit (living water Christ gives). Jeremiah's accusation ("you have forsaken Me") becomes Jesus' invitation ("Come to Me and drink"). The broken cisterns (idols) are replaced by the infinite fountain (Christ and the Spirit He gives).
