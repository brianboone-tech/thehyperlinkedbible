### [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4301-4400#H4325|H4325]] מַיִם *mayim* - water
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2416|H2416]] חַיִּים *chayyim* - living (plural: "living waters")
- [[Lexicon/H3389|H3389]] יְרוּשָׁלַםִ *Yerushalaim* - Jerusalem
- [[Lexicon/H6931|H6931]] קַדְמוֹנִי *qadmoni* - eastern (the eastern sea = Dead Sea)
- [[Lexicon/H314|H314]] אַחֲרוֹן *acharon* - western, latter (the western sea = Mediterranean)

**Context**: Zechariah 14 describes "the day of the LORD" when Yahweh will fight for Jerusalem against the nations. Verse 8 promises that "on that day" living waters will flow out from Jerusalem, divided between the eastern sea (Dead Sea) and western sea (Mediterranean), flowing continuously in both summer and winter. This connects to Zechariah 13:1's fountain opened for cleansing.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]]; 17:13** - Yahweh identified as "fountain of living waters" (same Hebrew phrase מַיִם חַיִּים *mayim chayyim*). Zechariah now locates this fountain geographically in Jerusalem.
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]]** - River from temple flowing eastward. Zechariah expands: water flows BOTH east and west, covering all directions.
- **[[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 18|Joel 3:18]]** - "A fountain will flow from the house of the LORD and water the valley of acacias" - Zechariah provides fuller eschatological development.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]] - ; 17:13 (living waters
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] - temple river
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]] - fountain opened for cleansing
- **FROM OT**:
  - No further OT development; Zechariah represents the culmination of OT living water prophecy
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - Jesus offers living water at Feast of Tabernacles, likely alluding to Zech 14:8 in context of Zech 14:16-19
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] - river of life from God's throne
**Type Classification**: **Direct type** (divinely revealed eschatological prophecy) and **Forward-looking** (explicit future indicators: "on that day," millennial kingdom context)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] provides the key. Jesus offers living water on the last day of Tabernacles, the very feast [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 16|Zechariah 14:16]] says nations will celebrate when living waters flow from Jerusalem. The connections:

1. **Timing**: Last day of Tabernacles ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37]]) = fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 16|Zechariah 14:16]]'s Tabernacles prophecy
2. **Source**: Living waters from Jerusalem (Zech 14:8) → Living water from Jesus ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-38]])
3. **Identity**: Yahweh as source (implied in Zech 14) → Jesus as source ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37]]) = Jesus is Yahweh
4. **Interpretation**: "He was speaking about the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were later to receive" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]])

The escalation:
- **Zechariah**: Living waters flow from geographic Jerusalem on the Day of the LORD
- **Jesus**: Living waters flow from Christ (the true temple) and from believers indwelt by the Spirit
- **Revelation**: Living waters flow from the throne of God and the Lamb in New Jerusalem (Rev 22:1)

Zechariah's perpetual flow (summer and winter) is realized in the Spirit's permanent indwelling ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 16|John 14:16-17]]). Zechariah's universal reach (east and west) is fulfilled in the Gospel going to all nations (Matt 28:19; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]). The fountain opened for cleansing (Zech 13:1) merges with the living waters (Zech 14:8) in the blood and water from Jesus' side ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]).
