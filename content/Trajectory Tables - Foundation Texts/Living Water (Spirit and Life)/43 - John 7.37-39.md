### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5201-5300#G5204|G5204]] ὕδωρ *hydor* - water
- [[Lexicon/G2101-2200#G2198|G2198]] ζάω *zao* - to live (participle ζῶν *zon* = living)
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4100|G4100]] πιστεύω *pisteuo* - to believe, trust
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα *pneuma* - spirit, Spirit (capitalized: Holy Spirit)
- [[Lexicon/G1301-1400#G1392|G1392]] δοξάζω *doxazo* - to glorify (aorist passive: "was glorified")

**Context**: Jesus speaks on the last and greatest day of the Feast of Tabernacles. The feast included a water-pouring ceremony (Mishnah Sukkah 4:9) where priests poured water from the Pool of Siloam at the altar, commemorating water from the rock (Ex 17; Num 20) and anticipating eschatological living waters (Ezek 47; Zech 14:8). Into this context, Jesus stands and cries out His invitation.

**OT-to-OT Development** (traced through John's Gospel):
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10]]** - River from Eden → [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]] "rivers will flow from within"
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]; [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 11|Numbers 20:11]]** - Water from rock → John identifies Christ as the rock (cf. 1 Cor 10:4)
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 3|Isaiah 44:3]]; 55:1** - "Come to the waters" → Jesus: "Come to Me and drink"
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]]** - Yahweh as "fountain of living waters" → Jesus claims this divine title
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]]** - River from temple → Jesus as the temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]); living water from Christ

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Jeremiah 2:13; 17:13 (fountain of living waters); Isaiah 55:1 (invitation to waters); Ezekiel 47:1-12 (temple river); Zechariah 14:8 (living waters from Jerusalem on Day of LORD)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10-14]] - living water becomes spring within
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] - water from Jesus' side
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 20#John 20 . 22|John 20:22]] - Jesus breathes Spirit on disciples
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]] - Jesus pours out Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 17|Revelation 22:17]] - Spirit and bride say "Come... take water of life"
**Type Classification**: This is **fulfillment** of the OT types, not a type itself. Jesus is the **antitype** to whom all prior water-from-God's-presence texts pointed. The typological categories:
- **Providential types fulfilled**: Eden's river, water from rock
- **Direct types fulfilled**: Ezekiel's temple river, Zechariah's living waters
- **Forward-looking prophecies**: Isaiah 44:3 (pour out Spirit), Joel 2:28 (Spirit on all flesh)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] is the apex of living water Christology:

1. **Christ claims Yahweh's identity**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]] calls Yahweh "fountain of living waters." Jesus says "come to ME and drink." This is a divine claim.

2. **Christ as the source**: Not merely a channel but the fountain itself. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 1|Isaiah 55:1]] invites "Come to the waters"; Jesus personalizes it: "Come to ME."

3. **Christ gives the Spirit**: Verse 39 specifies "the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were later to receive." The Spirit was not yet given "because Jesus had not yet been glorified" (7:39). Glorification = crucifixion/resurrection/ascension ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 23|John 12:23-24]], 32-33; 13:31-32; 17:1, 5). [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]] confirms: "Exalted to the right hand of God, He has received from the Father the promised Holy Spirit and has poured out what you now see and hear."

4. **Escalation from OT types**:
   - **Eden**: River flows FROM one geographic location
   - **Rock**: Water flows FROM one struck rock
   - **Temple**: River flows FROM one building in Jerusalem
   - **Christ**: Living water flows FROM every believer ("out of his heart")

5. **Trinitarian structure**:
   - **Father**: Original source ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]] "Jesus had not yet been glorified" by Father)
   - **Son**: Mediator who receives and gives the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]])
   - **Spirit**: The "living water" poured out on believers

The ultimate fulfillment: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] depicts "the river of the water of life... flowing from the throne of God **and of the Lamb**" - uniting Yahweh (OT fountain) with Christ (NT fulfillment) in eternal, life-giving flow.
