### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4301-4400#H4325|H4325]] מַיִם *mayim* - water
- [[Lexicon/H1961-2000#H1964|H1964]] הֵיכָל *heykal* - temple, palace
- [[Lexicon/H4665-4700#H4670|H4670]] מִפְתָּן *miptan* - threshold, sill
- [[Lexicon/H5027|H5027]] נַחַל *nachal* - river, stream (verse 5: becomes a river)
- [[Lexicon/H7495|H7495]] רָפָא *rapha* - to heal

**Context**: Ezekiel 47:1-12 is the climax of Ezekiel's temple vision (chapters 40-48). Water flows from under the threshold of the temple toward the east, progressively deepening (ankle → knee → waist → swimming depth). Trees line both banks, bearing fruit monthly, with leaves for healing. The river transforms the Dead Sea from salt to fresh water, bringing life wherever it flows.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10]]** - River from Eden: Ezekiel reverses the pattern (water flows FROM temple rather than TO garden), but the theology is identical (life from God's presence)
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 46#Psalm 46 . 4|Psalm 46:4]]** - "River whose streams make glad the city of God" - Ezekiel provides the vision this psalm anticipated
- **[[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 18|Joel 3:18]]** - "A fountain will flow from the house of the LORD and water the valley of acacias" - echoes Ezekiel's temple-source river
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]]** - "Living waters will flow out from Jerusalem, half to the eastern sea, half to the western sea" - develops Ezekiel's vision eschatologically

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Eden's river (Gen 2:10); tree of life (Gen 2:9); tabernacle/temple as God's dwelling
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]] - living waters from Jerusalem
  - [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 18|Joel 3:18]] - fountain from house of the LORD
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - living water from Christ = Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] - water from Jesus' side
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] - river of life from God's throne
**Type Classification**: **Direct type** (divinely revealed vision, commanded to be written down for Israel, Ezek 43:10-11) and **Forward-looking** (contains textual indicators of future fulfillment: "in the latter years" context of Ezek 38-48)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus fulfills Ezekiel 47 in multiple layers:
1. **Christ as Temple**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] identifies Jesus' body as the true temple. Water flows from the temple threshold = water flows from Christ's pierced side ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]).
2. **Christ as Source**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-38]] applies the river imagery to believers: "Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water" (ESV). John explains: "this He said about the Spirit" (7:39).
3. **Progressive Deepening**: Ezekiel's ankle → knee → waist → river symbolizes the Spirit's increasing work from Pentecost through church history.
4. **Life-Giving Power**: The Dead Sea made fresh = spiritual death to life transformation (Eph 2:1-5).
5. **Monthly Fruit, Healing Leaves**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]] directly quotes [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 12|Ezekiel 47:12]], placing the fulfillment in the New Jerusalem with the river flowing "from the throne of God and of the Lamb."

The escalation: Ezekiel's eschatological temple → Christ the true temple → Church as living temples (1 Cor 3:16; Eph 2:21-22) → New Jerusalem (Rev 21-22).
