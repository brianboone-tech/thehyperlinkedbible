### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 36#Psalm 36 . 8|Psalm 36:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1801-1900#H1880|H1880]] דֶּשֶׁן *deshen* - fatness, abundance
- [[Lexicon/H5001-5100#H5027|H5027]] נַחַל *nachal* - stream, river, valley
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5730|H5730]] עֵדֶן *eden* - delight, pleasure (same word as Eden in Gen 2:8)
- [[Lexicon/H4701-4800#H4726|H4726]] מָקוֹר *maqor* - fountain, spring, source
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2416|H2416]] חַיִּים *chayyim* - life, living

**Context**: Psalm 36 contrasts the wicked (vv. 1-4) with God's steadfast love and faithfulness (vv. 5-9). Verses 8-9 describe intimate fellowship with God using temple and Eden imagery: feasting in God's house, drinking from His river of delights, and finding the fountain of life in His presence.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10]]** - Eden's river echoed here as "river of delights" (note the Hebrew *eden* = delight)
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 46#Psalm 46 . 4|Psalm 46:4]]** - "There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God" - no literal river in Jerusalem; must be spiritual/eschatological
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 1|Isaiah 55:1]]** - "Come, all you who are thirsty, come to the waters... without money and without cost"
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]]** - "They have forsaken Me, the fountain of living waters"
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 13|Jeremiah 17:13]]** - "O LORD, the hope of Israel... the fountain of living water"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Garden of Eden (Gen 2:10); temple worship (abundance in God's house)
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 46#Psalm 46 . 4|Psalm 46:4]] - river making glad God's city
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 13|Jeremiah 2:13]] - ; 17:13 (Yahweh as fountain of living water
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] - river from temple
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10-14]] - Jesus offers living water
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - invitation to drink; living water = Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] - river of life from God's throne
**Type Classification**: Not strictly a type but **typological language** drawing on Eden and anticipating eschatological fulfillment. The imagery is **forward-looking** (prophetic hope for future satisfaction) and **backward-looking** (echoes Eden paradise).

**Christological Connection**: Jesus fulfills this psalm's promise. He declares: "If anyone is thirsty, let him come to Me and drink" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37]]), positioning Himself as the fountain of life promised in [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 36#Psalm 36 . 9|Psalm 36:9]]. The "river of delights" flows from Christ through the Holy Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]). Believers feast on the abundance of God's house by feeding on Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 35|John 6:35]], 51-58) and drink from the fountain of life by receiving the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 14|John 4:14]]). The ultimate fulfillment: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] depicts the river of life flowing from the throne of God **and of the Lamb**, uniting the OT promise with the Christological reality.
