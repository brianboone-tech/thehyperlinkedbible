### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 3|Isaiah 44:3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4301-4400#H4325|H4325]] מַיִם *mayim* - water
- [[Lexicon/H6601-6700#H6771|H6771]] צָמֵא *tsame* - thirsty
- [[Lexicon/H5001-5100#H5140|H5140]] נָזַל *nazal* - to flow, stream, pour out
- [[Lexicon/H7301-7400#H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ *ruach* - spirit, breath, wind (here: the Spirit of God)
- [[Lexicon/H1201-1300#H1293|H1293]] בְּרָכָה *berakah* - blessing

**Context**: Isaiah 44:1-5 is a salvation oracle promising restoration to Israel. Verse 3 contains the pivotal equation that unlocks the entire living water typology: parallelism explicitly equates pouring out water with pouring out the Spirit. This provides the hermeneutical key for interpreting all prior and subsequent water-from-God's-presence texts.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10]]** - River watering Eden → Isaiah interprets spiritually as God's Spirit
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]; [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 11|Numbers 20:11]]** - Water from rock → Isaiah's parallel suggests this prefigured the Spirit
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]]** - "I will sprinkle clean water on you... I will put My Spirit within you" - similar water/Spirit parallelism
- **[[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-29]]** - "I will pour out My Spirit on all people" - directly echoes [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 3|Isaiah 44:3]]'s pouring language

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Covenant promises to Abraham (Isa 44:3 "your offspring"); wilderness provision (water imagery)
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] - water and Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-32]] - outpouring of Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]] - Spirit of grace poured out
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - living water explicitly interpreted as Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]] - Peter quotes Joel's fulfillment
  - [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5-6]] - washing of regeneration and renewal by Holy Spirit
**Type Classification**: **Direct type** (divinely revealed prophecy, not merely providential arrangement) and **Forward-looking** (contains textual indicators pointing to future eschatological fulfillment)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 3|Isaiah 44:3]] establishes that "water" symbolizes "the Spirit," which [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]] confirms: "He was speaking about the Spirit, whom those who believed in Him were later to receive." The trajectory:
1. **OT Foundation**: Isaiah promises God will pour out water/Spirit on offspring
2. **NT Fulfillment**: Jesus offers living water ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10]]; 7:37-38) = the Holy Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]])
3. **NT Application**: Pentecost fulfills the promise ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17]], 33)
4. **Christological Mediation**: Christ receives the Spirit from the Father and pours Him out on believers ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]])

The escalation: Promise of Spirit (Isa 44:3) → Christ gives the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]]; 20:22) → Spirit flows from believers ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]]). Isaiah's thirsty land becomes the thirsty soul; Isaiah's offspring becomes those who believe in Christ; Isaiah's outpouring occurs through Christ's glorification (crucifixion/resurrection/ascension).
