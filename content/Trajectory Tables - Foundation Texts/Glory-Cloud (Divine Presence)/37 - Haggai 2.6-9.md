### [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 6|Haggai 2:6-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מְעַט (*me'at*) - "a little while" - short time (2:6)
- רָעַשׁ (*ra'ash*) - "shake" - earthquake imagery (2:6, 7, 21)
- חֶמְדַּת (*chemdat*) - "treasures/desired things" - precious objects or "Desired One" (Messiah) (2:7)
- כָּבוֹד (*kavod*) - "glory" - splendor, weightiness (2:7, 9)
- שָׁלוֹם (*shalom*) - "peace" - wholeness, prosperity (2:9)

**Context**: Post-exilic prophecy (c. 520 BC). Returned exiles rebuilding temple under Zerubbabel's leadership. Older generation remembers Solomon's temple glory; current structure seems pathetic by comparison (2:3). Haggai encourages: future temple will surpass former in glory.

  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]] - glory departed

**Christological Connection**: Christ is "greater glory" fulfilling Haggai's prophecy. **Escalation beyond imagination**: Zerubbabel's temple built of wood and stone; Christ is "living stone" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4]]). Solomon's temple contained gold/silver; Christ surpasses: "something greater than the temple is here" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 6|Matthew 12:6]]). First Temple had visible Shekinah cloud; Second Temple lacked it (causing grief); but Christ embodies Shekinah glory: "we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 7|Haggai 2:7]]: "I will fill this house with glory" - fulfilled when Christ enters temple courts, teaching and performing miracles. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 12|Matthew 21:12-16]]: Christ cleanses temple, heals blind and lame there - **greater glory** transforming sacred space. **Treasures/Desired One**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 7|Haggai 2:7]]'s חֶמְדַּת (*chemdat*) can mean "precious things" or "Desired One." Christ is both: He is the **treasure** Paul speaks of ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 7|2 Corinthians 4:7]]: "we have this treasure in jars of clay"); He is the **Desired One** of nations ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]]: ransomed from every tribe, language, people, nation). **Shaking of nations** ([[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 6|Haggai 2:6-7]]): [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 26|Hebrews 12:26-28]] shows this fulfilled eschatologically at Christ's return when old order removed, unshakeable kingdom remains. **Peace** ([[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 9|Haggai 2:9]]): Christ is peace-bringer - [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14-18]] shows Christ reconciling Jew and Gentile, making peace through cross. **Trajectory**: Solomon's temple glory (1 Kings 8) → glory departed (Ezekiel 10) → discouraged rebuilding (Haggai 1-2) → prophecy of greater glory ([[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 9|Haggai 2:9]]) → Christ as embodiment of greater glory ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) → believers as living temple ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]]) → eternal glory ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). Haggai's "latter glory" finds ultimate fulfillment not in renovated building but in Christ, then in glorified church, finally in New Jerusalem where "the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]).
