### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 10|Revelation 21:10-11]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- πνεύματι (*pneumati*) - "in the Spirit" - ecstatic visionary state (21:10)
- ὄρος (*oros*) - "mountain" - elevated vantage point (21:10)
- πόλιν (*polin*) - "city" - New Jerusalem (21:10)
- δόξαν τοῦ θεοῦ (*doxan tou theou*) - "glory of God" - divine radiance (21:11)
- φωστὴρ (*phōstēr*) - "radiance, light-giver" - luminous quality (21:11)
- ἴασπις (*iaspis*) - "jasper" - precious stone, crystal-clear (21:11)

**Context**: Vision of New Jerusalem (Revelation 21:9-22:5). Follows new heaven/earth announcement (21:1-8), one of seven bowl angels shows bride (21:9). Parallels Ezekiel's temple vision (Ezekiel 40-48 - also taken to high mountain, shown eschatological sanctuary). Climax of biblical revelation - consummation of all redemptive history.

**Christological Connection**: New Jerusalem radiating God's glory is **consummation** of glory-cloud trajectory centered on Christ. **Ultimate escalation**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]] - pillar of cloud/fire **led** Israel externally; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - glory **filled** tabernacle (localized); [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10]] - glory **filled** temple (permanent building but temporary presence); Ezekiel 10 - glory **departed**; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - glory **incarnate** in Christ; Acts 2 - glory **indwells** believers; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 11|Revelation 21:11]] - glory **radiates** from entire city, filling new creation. Verse 21:23 explains source: "**the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb**" - Christ is eternal radiance. Where tabernacle required external illumination ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 27#Exodus 27 . 20|Exodus 27:20-21]]: oil lamps), New Jerusalem needs no sun/moon (21:23) because **Christ Himself is light**. This fulfills [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]: "I am the light of the world" - from localized ministry in Galilee/Judea to cosmic illumination. **Trajectory's consummation**: External → localized → departed → incarnate → indwelling → **eternal, unmediated, cosmic glory**. Jasper's crystal clarity (21:11) suggests transparency - no veil (contrast Moses's veil, [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 33|Exodus 34:33-35]]; temple veil, [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 26#Exodus 26 . 31|Exodus 26:31-33]]). Where Moses could not enter glory-filled tabernacle ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 35|Exodus 40:35]]), believers **dwell** in glory-filled city eternally. Where ancient Israel beheld glory from distance ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 17|Exodus 24:17]]: "appearance like devouring fire"), glorified saints "will see his face" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]]) - beatific vision, unshielded glory producing no harm because believers perfectly holy. **Christological center**: "Its lamp is the Lamb" (21:23) - Christ crucified and glorified is eternal light-source. His redemptive work makes possible unmediated glory-access. Cross absorbed judgment-fire ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 29|Hebrews 12:29]]: "our God is a consuming fire"); resurrection released life-light. **From Adam to consummation**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]] - cherubim **guard** Eden, preventing access; Revelation 21-22 - cherubim absent, perfect access restored. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - fill earth with God's presence; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 11|Revelation 21:11]] - **fulfilled** as glory radiates from city to renewed creation. "The nations will walk by its light" (21:24) - universal scope. Glory-cloud trajectory complete: from temporary external manifestations → to eternal internal radiation → to cosmic glory filling all reality where "the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" forever.
