### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כְּבוֹד יְהוָה (*kevod YHWH*) - "glory of the LORD" - divine presence departing (10:18, 19)
- יָצָא (*yatsa*) - "went out" - left, departed (10:18)
- מִפְתַּן (*miftan*) - "threshold" - entrance/doorway (10:18)
- כְּרוּבִים (*keruvim*) - "cherubim" - angelic beings bearing God's throne-chariot (10:18, 19)
- עָמַד (*amad*) - "stood" - stationary position during departure (10:18, 19)

**Context**: Vision during Babylonian exile (c. 592 BC, five years after first deportation). Ezekiel sees abominations in temple (chapter 8), divine judgment executed (chapter 9), then glory's reluctant departure (chapters 10-11). Departure occurs in stages: from Most Holy Place → to threshold → to cherubim → to east gate → to Mount of Olives (11:23).

**Christological Connection**: Glory's departure from temple anticipates Christ's coming as true temple. **Escalation through reversal**: Ezekiel 10 - glory **departs** due to sin; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - glory **returns** in Christ ("we have seen his glory"). Where First Temple lost glory through Israel's unfaithfulness, Christ embodies glory permanently through perfect faithfulness. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23#Matthew 23 . 38|Matthew 23:38]]: Jesus pronounces "your house is left to you desolate" - echoing Ezekiel; then [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 61|Matthew 26:61]]: "I am able to destroy the temple of God and rebuild it in three days" (cf. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]) - Christ replacing temple. **Profound reversal**: Ezekiel 10 shows glory leaving Jerusalem via Mount of Olives; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 9|Acts 1:9-12]] shows glorified Christ ascending from same mountain; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 4|Zechariah 14:4]] prophesies Christ returning to Mount of Olives - **reversal of departure route**. Glory that departed reluctantly will return triumphantly. **Trajectory**: Glory fills Solomon's temple (1 Kings 8) → glory departs due to sin (Ezekiel 10) → glory returns in Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) → glory indwells believers (Acts 2) → glory fills new creation ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). Ezekiel 10 is **nadir** of trajectory - lowest point when visible glory leaves - creating longing for messianic restoration. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 1|Ezekiel 43:1-5]] prophesies: "the glory of the LORD entered the temple by the gate facing east... And behold, the glory of the LORD filled the temple" - partially fulfilled when Christ (glory incarnate) enters Jerusalem from east ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 1|Matthew 21:1-11]]), ultimately fulfilled when "the glory of God" lights New Jerusalem ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). From departed glory → to incarnate glory → to eternal glory. What Israel lost through sin, Christ restores through righteousness.
