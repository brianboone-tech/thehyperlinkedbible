### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- λόγος (*logos*) - "Word" - divine reason/revelation personified (1:1, 14)
- σὰρξ (*sarx*) - "flesh" - human nature, humanity (1:14)
- ἐσκήνωσεν (*eskēnōsen*) - "tabernacled, dwelt in tent" - from σκηνή (tent/tabernacle) (1:14)
- δόξαν (*doxan*) - "glory" - Shekinah presence, divine radiance (1:14)
- μονογενοῦς (*monogenous*) - "only begotten, unique" - one of a kind Son (1:14)
- πλήρης (*plērēs*) - "full" - complete, lacking nothing (1:14)

**Context**: Climax of Johannine prologue (1:1-18). Follows creation parallels (1:1-5: "In the beginning"), John Baptist's testimony (1:6-8), Light coming into world (1:9-13). Verse 1:14 is theological hinge: moves from eternal Logos (1:1-13) to incarnate Jesus (1:14-18). Sets stage for entire Gospel narrative.

**Christological Connection**: Christ is **perfect fulfillment** of glory-cloud trajectory. **Ultimate escalation**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]] - pillar of cloud/fire **led** Israel externally; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - glory **filled** tabernacle (localized building); [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - glory **filled** temple (permanent structure); [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - glory **incarnate** in Christ (personal embodiment). From external → localized → incarnate. Where Moses could not enter glory-filled tent ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 35|Exodus 40:35]]), disciples behold glory in Christ's face and fellowship with Him ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 39|John 1:39]]: "they stayed with him that day"). Where Solomon's temple contained glory temporarily (glory later departed, Ezekiel 10), Christ **is** glory permanently - "the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). **[[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 9|Haggai 2:9]] fulfilled**: "The latter glory of this house shall be greater than the former" - not architectural but **Christological**. Greater glory is Christ entering Second Temple ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 12|Matthew 21:12-17]]). **Escalation of access**: Tabernacle/temple - only high priest enters Most Holy Place annually (Leviticus 16); Christ - all believers have access to Father through Him ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 18|Ephesians 2:18]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-22]]). **Trajectory consummation**: External cloud → localized glory → incarnate glory (Christ) → indwelling Spirit (Acts 2) → eternal radiance. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 7|2 Corinthians 3:7-11]] contrasts: Moses's face shone temporarily with reflected glory; believers behold Christ's glory permanently, being "transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another" (3:18). [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] is **apex** of OT glory-cloud trajectory and **foundation** for NT indwelling glory. Consummation: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]] - "the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" - Christ who tabernacled among us eternally radiating divine glory in new creation.
