### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- עָנָן (*anan*) - "cloud" - theophanic presence (8:10, 11, 12)
- כְּבוֹד יְהוָה (*kevod YHWH*) - "glory of the LORD" - divine radiance (8:11)
- מָלֵא (*male*) - "filled" - completely pervaded (8:10, 11)
- עֲרָפֶל (*arafel*) - "thick darkness" - dense cloud obscuring God (8:12)
- זְבֻל (*zevul*) - "exalted house" - lofty dwelling (8:13)

**Context**: Temple dedication ceremony (1 Kings 8:1-66). Ark brought into Most Holy Place (8:1-9); priests exit holy place (8:10); Solomon addresses people (8:14-21), then prays (8:22-53). Mirrors tabernacle dedication (Exodus 40:34-38) with intentional parallels showing continuity.

**Christological Connection**: Temple glory foreshadows Christ as perfect temple. **Escalation**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 11|1 Kings 8:11]] - priests cannot stand before glory; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - disciples behold glory incarnate and abide with Him. Where temple glory was localized to Jerusalem, Christ embodies glory **personally**. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 6|Matthew 12:6]]: "something greater than the temple is here" - Christ surpasses temple in every dimension. Transfiguration ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 17#Matthew 17 . 1|Matthew 17:1-5]]) reveals Christ's essential glory: "his face shone like the sun, and his clothes became white as light. And behold... a bright cloud overshadowed them" - Shekinah glory testifying to Christ. **From temple to Christ**: Solomon's temple = permanent building but temporary glory (departed in 586 BC); Christ = eternal person with permanent glory ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 8|Hebrews 13:8]]). Temple required repeated sacrifices; Christ offered Himself once for all ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]). Temple excluded Gentiles (outer court only); Christ includes all nations ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14-18]]). **Trajectory of glory**: Temporary tent (Exodus 40) → permanent temple (1 Kings 8) → incarnate glory ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) → indwelling Spirit (Acts 2) → eschatological radiance ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). From glory filling localized building → to glory filling entire cosmos. Solomon: "I have built you an exalted house, a place for you to dwell in forever" (8:13) finds ultimate fulfillment in New Jerusalem where "the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]) and "the glory of God gives it light" (21:23). From building-centered glory → to Christ-centered glory filling new creation eternally.
