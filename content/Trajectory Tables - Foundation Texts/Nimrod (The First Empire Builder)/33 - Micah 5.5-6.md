### [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 5|Micah 5:5-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7965|H7965]] שָׁלוֹם (*shalom*) - "peace, completeness, welfare"
- [[Lexicon/H804|H804]] אַשּׁוּר (*ʾashshûr*) - "Assyria"
- [[Lexicon/H5248|H5248]] נִמְרֹד (*Nimrōd*) - "Nimrod"
- [[Lexicon/H7462|H7462]] רָעָה (*raʿah*) - "to shepherd, tend, rule"
- [[Lexicon/H2719|H2719]] חֶרֶב (*ḥereb*) - "sword"
- [[Lexicon/H5337|H5337]] נָצַל (*natsal*) - "to deliver, rescue"

**Context**: Micah 5 presents one of the clearest messianic prophecies in the OT. The Bethlehem-born Ruler (v. 2) will shepherd Israel in the LORD's strength (v. 4) and "be our peace" (שָׁלוֹם) when Assyria invades (v. 5). Verses 5-6 promise that Israel will raise "seven shepherds, even eight leaders of men" who will "rule the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod with the blade drawn." The explicit naming of "the land of Nimrod" (אֶרֶץ נִמְרֹד) as parallel to Assyria is unique in the OT—connecting the contemporary threat (Assyria) to its primordial founder.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Micah explicitly links Assyria to Nimrod (Gen 10:11), showing prophetic awareness that Nimrod founded both Babylon and Nineveh/Assyria.
- The promise of deliverance from "the land of Nimrod" encompasses all empires descended from Nimrod's rebellious spirit.
- Isaiah 10-11 similarly prophesies Messiah's defeat of Assyria, followed by worldwide peace.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 10:8-12 (Nimrod founds Assyria/Nineveh), Isaiah 10:5-19 (Assyria as God's rod, then judged)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1-10]] (Davidic Branch brings peace)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 6|Matthew 2:6]] (quotes [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14]] (Christ "is our peace")

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (explicit prophecy of Messiah defeating Nimrod's heirs); Providential (Nimrod as paradigm of all empire Christ conquers)

**Christological Connection**: Christ fulfills [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 5|Micah 5:5-6]] as the conqueror of all "Nimrods." (1) **Prince of Peace vs. Empire Builder**: "He will be our peace" (v. 5) contrasts with Nimrod's violence. Where empire brings oppression, Messiah brings shalom. (2) **Shepherd vs. Hunter**: The Bethlehem Ruler "will shepherd His flock" (v. 4); Nimrod was "a mighty hunter" (Gen 10:9). Christ gathers; Nimrod conquered. (3) **Spiritual Victory**: Christ defeats "the land of Nimrod" not by physical sword but by the "sword of His mouth" (Rev 19:15). The seven/eight shepherds may represent the church militant under Christ's headship. (4) **Comprehensive Deliverance**: "He will deliver us when Assyria invades" (v. 6)—Christ delivers His people from every empire that arises in Nimrod's spirit. [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]] declares Christ has "disarmed the rulers and authorities."
