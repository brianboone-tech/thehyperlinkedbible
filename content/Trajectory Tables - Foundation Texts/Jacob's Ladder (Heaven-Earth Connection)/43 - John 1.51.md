### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 51|John 1:51]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G281|G281]] ἀμήν (amēn) - "truly" - doubled for emphasis
- [[Lexicon/G3772|G3772]] οὐρανός (ouranos) - "heaven" - opened
- [[Lexicon/G455|G455]] ἀνοίγω (anoigō) - "open" - heaven opened
- [[Lexicon/G32|G32]] ἄγγελος (angelos) - "angels" - ascending and descending
- [[Lexicon/G305|G305]] ἀναβαίνω (anabainō) - "ascending" - going up
- [[Lexicon/G2597|G2597]] καταβαίνω (katabainō) - "descending" - coming down
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου (huios tou anthrōpou) - "Son of Man" - replacing "ladder"

**Context**: Jesus responds to Nathanael's confession ("You are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!") with an even greater promise: "You will see heaven open and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man." This directly quotes Genesis 28:12 but substitutes "the Son of Man" for "the ladder" (sullām)—explicitly identifying Jesus as the true Jacob's ladder, the genuine connection between heaven and earth.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - The original vision: ladder set on earth, top reaching heaven, angels ascending and descending
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] - "Son of Man" coming with clouds of heaven, approaching the Ancient of Days
- The combination of Genesis 28 and Daniel 7 is significant: the ladder connects to the eschatological Son of Man

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - Direct typological fulfillment; Jesus explicitly applies the ladder to Himself
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] - supplies the "Son of Man" title; Ezekiel 1 provides the opened-heaven context
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]] - "I am the way"
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-20]] - The "new and living way" through Christ
**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Jesus' explicit self-application of the Jacob's ladder type

**Christological Connection**: This verse is the definitive interpretive key for the entire trajectory. Jesus identifies Himself as the true and living ladder. His humanity (Son of Man) touches earth; His deity reaches heaven. In one Person, two natures unite to bring heaven and earth together. Mather wrote: "There is no ascending to Heaven, but by the spiritual Ladder Jesus Christ, no Salvation but by Christ, no comfortable Intercourse and Communion between God and us, but only in and through him." The angels ascending and descending "on" (ἐπί) the Son of Man indicates that all divine ministry flows through Christ—He is the sole point of heaven-earth contact.
