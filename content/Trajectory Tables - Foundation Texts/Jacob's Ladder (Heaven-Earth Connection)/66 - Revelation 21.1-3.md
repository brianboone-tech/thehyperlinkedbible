### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3772|G3772]] οὐρανός (ouranos) - "heaven" - new heaven
- [[Lexicon/G1093|G1093]] γῆ (gē) - "earth" - new earth
- [[Lexicon/G2537|G2537]] καινός (kainos) - "new" - qualitatively new, renewed
- [[Lexicon/G4172|G4172]] πόλις (polis) - "city" - the New Jerusalem
- [[Lexicon/G2597|G2597]] καταβαίνω (katabainō) - "descending" - coming down from heaven
- [[Lexicon/G4633|G4633]] σκηνή (skēnē) - "dwelling/tabernacle" - God's dwelling among humanity
- [[Lexicon/G2992|G2992]] λαός (laos) - "people" - God's covenant people

**Context**: Revelation's climax: the new creation where heaven and earth are united. John sees "a new heaven and a new earth" and the Holy City "coming down out of heaven from God." A voice proclaims: "Look! God's dwelling place is now among the people, and he will dwell with them." The ladder is no longer needed—heaven and earth have become one; God permanently dwells with humanity.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12-17]] - Jacob saw heaven touching earth at Bethel; named it "house of God"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] - "I will create new heavens and a new earth"
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - "My dwelling place will be with them; I will be their God, and they will be my people"
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 10|Zechariah 2:10]] - "I am coming, and I will live among you"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12-17]] - The ladder connected heaven and earth temporarily; now they are united permanently
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]], [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - New creation and divine dwelling promises
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - The Word "tabernacled" among us; Rev 21:22 - "The Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Heaven and earth permanently united; the ladder's purpose fully realized

**Christological Connection**: The trajectory culminates not in the abolition of Christ's mediatorial role but in its eternal fruition. "The Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" (21:22)—Christ remains central to divine-human fellowship forever. The ladder is no longer needed because what it symbolized (God with humanity) is now perfectly achieved. The New Jerusalem "comes down from heaven"—the final descent, heaven permanently joining earth. God "will dwell with them"—the Bethel promise ("house of God") becomes cosmic reality. What Jacob glimpsed at a lonely stone pillar becomes the eternal city where God and the Lamb are enthroned among their people forever.
