### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4720|H4720]] מִקְדָּשׁ (miqdāš) - "sanctuary" - holy place for God's dwelling
- [[Lexicon/H7931|H7931]] שָׁכַן (šāḵan) - "dwell/tabernacle" - God dwelling among His people
- [[Lexicon/H8403|H8403]] תַּבְנִית (taḇnîṯ) - "pattern/model" - heavenly design shown to Moses
- [[Lexicon/H4908|H4908]] מִשְׁכָּן (miškān) - "tabernacle/dwelling" - the tent of meeting

**Context**: After the Sinai theophany and covenant ratification, God commands Moses to collect offerings for constructing a sanctuary. The purpose is explicit: "Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them" (25:8). Moses must follow exactly the pattern (tabnît) shown him on the mountain—indicating a heavenly original that the earthly structure copies.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 17|Genesis 28:17]] - Jacob's declaration "This is the house of God" anticipates a permanent dwelling
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-38]] - God's glory fills the completed tabernacle, confirming it as the heaven-earth meeting point
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - Solomon's temple similarly filled with glory
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1-4]] - Isaiah's vision shows the temple as heaven's throne room on earth

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 10|Genesis 28:10-17]] - Jacob's ladder established the pattern; tabernacle institutionalizes it
- **FROM OT**:
  - The prophets critique empty formalism while affirming God's presence in His house
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - The Word "tabernacled" among us
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11-12]] - Christ entered the greater tabernacle
**Type Classification**: Direct (divinely commanded), Forward-Looking - The tabernacle was explicitly designed as a copy of heavenly reality (Heb 8:5), indicating its typological purpose

**Christological Connection**: The tabernacle was the institutionalized ladder—the place where heaven and earth met, where God dwelt with humanity, where prayers ascended and blessings descended. Christ is the true tabernacle ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]), the real dwelling of God among humanity. His body is the temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]). He perfectly fulfills what the tabernacle shadowed: permanent, personal, complete union of God with His people. The tabernacle required sacrifices, priests, rituals; Christ Himself is sacrifice, priest, and meeting place in one Person.
