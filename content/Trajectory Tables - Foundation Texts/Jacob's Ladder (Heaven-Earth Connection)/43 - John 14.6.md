### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3598|G3598]] ὁδός (hodos) - "way" - the path/road
- [[Lexicon/G225|G225]] ἀλήθεια (alētheia) - "truth" - reality, genuineness
- [[Lexicon/G2222|G2222]] ζωή (zōē) - "life" - divine, eternal life
- [[Lexicon/G3762|G3762]] οὐδείς (oudeis) - "no one" - absolute exclusion
- [[Lexicon/G2064|G2064]] ἔρχομαι (erchomai) - "come" - approach, arrive
- [[Lexicon/G3962|G3962]] πατήρ (patēr) - "Father" - the destination

**Context**: In the Upper Room, Jesus prepares His disciples for His departure. Thomas asks, "Lord, we do not know where you are going. How can we know the way?" Jesus responds with the sixth "I am" statement: "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me." This is the explicit claim to exclusive mediation—Christ alone is the ladder to the Father.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - The ladder was the only way between heaven and earth
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 26#Exodus 26 . 33|Exodus 26:33]] - The veil separated; only the high priest could pass through
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 14#Proverbs 14 . 12|Proverbs 14:12]] - "There is a way that appears to be right, but in the end it leads to death"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 35#Isaiah 35 . 8|Isaiah 35:8]] - "A highway will be there... It will be called the Way of Holiness"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - The ladder was the designated way; Christ IS the way
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 35#Isaiah 35 . 8|Isaiah 35:8]] - The "Way of Holiness" anticipates Christ as the way
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 12|Acts 4:12]] - "Salvation is found in no one else"
  - [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 5|1 Timothy 2:5]] - "One mediator between God and men"
**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - Christ surpasses the ladder by being the permanent, living way

**Christological Connection**: Mather noted that by Jacob's ladder "there is no ascending to Heaven, but by the spiritual Ladder Jesus Christ." [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]] makes this explicit: Christ is THE way, not A way. The ladder in Jacob's dream was temporary, localized, and visionary; Christ is eternal, universal, and real. He is not merely the means to God but also the truth about God (revealing the Father, 14:9) and the life from God (giving eternal life, 10:28). As the ladder connected heaven and earth, Christ connects humanity to the Father—and He IS that connection.
