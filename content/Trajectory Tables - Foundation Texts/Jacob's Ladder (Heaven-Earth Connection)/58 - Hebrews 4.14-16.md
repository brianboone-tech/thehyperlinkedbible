### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 14|Hebrews 4:14-16]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G749|G749]] ἀρχιερεύς (archiereus) - "high priest" - the great high priest
- [[Lexicon/G1330|G1330]] διέρχομαι (dierchomai) - "passed through" - traversed
- [[Lexicon/G3772|G3772]] οὐρανός (ouranos) - "heavens" - the plural (multiple heavens)
- [[Lexicon/G2902|G2902]] κρατέω (krateō) - "hold fast" - cling to the confession
- [[Lexicon/G4334|G4334]] προσέρχομαι (proserchomai) - "draw near/approach" - access to God
- [[Lexicon/G2362|G2362]] θρόνος (thronos) - "throne" - the throne of grace
- [[Lexicon/G3954|G3954]] παρρησία (parrēsia) - "confidence/boldness" - access without fear

**Context**: Hebrews argues for Christ's superiority over all OT mediators. Here the author summarizes: Jesus is the great high priest who has "passed through the heavens." Because of who He is and what He has done, believers can "approach God's throne of grace with confidence." The ladder imagery is implicit: Christ has ascended through the heavens and opened the way for believers to follow.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - The ladder reached from earth to heaven; angels ascended and descended
- Leviticus 16 - The high priest alone entered the Most Holy Place once a year
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 24#Psalm 24 . 3|Psalm 24:3-4]] - "Who may ascend the mountain of the LORD?"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1-4]] - The Lord enthroned in heaven, with access requiring cleansing

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - Christ as ladder; ascending through the heavens
- **FROM OT**: Leviticus 16's Day of Atonement access through the veil typifies Christ's work
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-22]] - "Confidence to enter the Most Holy Place by the blood of Jesus"

**Type Classification**: NT Application - Believers now have access to God through Christ the true ladder/high priest

**Christological Connection**: Christ has "passed through the heavens"—He has ascended the ladder from earth to God's throne. But unlike Jacob's ladder, this is not merely a vision; Christ is there permanently as believers' representative. And unlike the Levitical high priest who entered momentarily, Christ "always lives to intercede" (7:25). The ladder is not just for looking at but for using—believers "draw near" through Christ. He has opened the way by His blood (10:19-20), and He remains the way as the living high priest. What Jacob glimpsed, believers access. The ladder has become a highway of grace.
