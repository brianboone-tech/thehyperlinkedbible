### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 16|Matthew 3:16-17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G455|G455]] ἀνοίγω (anoigō) - "open" - heavens were opened
- [[Lexicon/G3772|G3772]] οὐρανός (ouranos) - "heaven" - the realm above
- [[Lexicon/G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα (pneuma) - "Spirit" - descending like a dove
- [[Lexicon/G2597|G2597]] καταβαίνω (katabainō) - "descend" - Spirit coming down
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (huios) - "Son" - the beloved Son

**Context**: At Jesus' baptism in the Jordan River, heaven is opened, the Spirit descends like a dove and rests on Jesus, and the Father's voice declares: "This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased." This trinitarian theophany marks Jesus' public ministry inauguration and demonstrates that He is the place where heaven and earth meet—the true Jacob's ladder.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - Heaven connected to earth through the ladder; angels ascending and descending
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 64#Isaiah 64 . 1|Isaiah 64:1]] - "Oh, that you would rend the heavens and come down" - prophetic longing fulfilled
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 1#Ezekiel 1 . 1|Ezekiel 1:1]] - "The heavens were opened and I saw visions of God" - prophetic precedent

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] - The ladder connected heaven and earth; now heaven opens upon Jesus
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1]] - "Here is my servant...my chosen one in whom I delight; I will put my Spirit on him"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 51|John 1:51]] - Jesus explicitly identifies Himself as the true ladder with heaven opened

**Type Classification**: NT Anticipation - Heavens opened upon Jesus prepares for His explicit self-identification as Jacob's ladder (John 1:51)

**Christological Connection**: The opened heavens at baptism reveal Jesus as the true Jacob's ladder. Where Jacob saw a vision of heaven-earth connection, Jesus IS that connection. The Spirit descending mirrors angels descending the ladder—all divine ministry flows through the Son. The Father's voice from above corresponds to God at the top of the ladder speaking to earth. Jesus' baptism demonstrates that in His Person, heaven and earth are permanently united. He stands in the Jordan (earth) while heaven opens upon Him—two realms meeting in one Person.
