### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3678|H3678]] כִּסֵּא (kissēʾ) - "throne" - God enthroned
- [[Lexicon/H7311|H7311]] רוּם (rûm) - "high/exalted" - the Lord's position
- [[Lexicon/H8193|H8193]] שׁוּל (šûl) - "hem/train" - filling the temple
- [[Lexicon/H8314|H8314]] שָׂרָף (śārāp̄) - "seraph" - burning/fiery beings
- [[Lexicon/H6918|H6918]] קָדוֹשׁ (qādôš) - "holy" - the threefold ascription
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (kāḇôḏ) - "glory" - filling the earth

**Context**: In the year King Uzziah died, Isaiah received a throne-room vision. He saw the Lord "high and exalted, seated on a throne" with the train of His robe filling the temple. Seraphim called out: "Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory." The temple shook and filled with smoke. This vision commissions Isaiah as prophet but also reveals heaven and earth overlapping in the temple space.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12-17]] - Jacob's ladder showed heaven-earth connection; Isaiah sees heaven's throne room in earth's temple
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]] - Glory filling the tabernacle prepared for Isaiah's temple vision
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 22#1 Kings 22 . 19|1 Kings 22:19]] - Micaiah's similar throne vision: "I saw the LORD sitting on his throne"
- Ezekiel 1, 10 - Ezekiel's throne-chariot vision develops the imagery further

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12-17]] - The ladder connected heaven and earth; here heaven's court appears in earth's temple
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 1|Isaiah 66:1]] - develops this: "Heaven is my throne, the earth is my footstool"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 41|John 12:41]] - "Isaiah saw Jesus' glory"
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 4#Revelation 4 . 8|Revelation 4:8]] - The heavenly beings echo the Trisagion
**Type Classification**: Prophetic development of the heaven-earth connection theme

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 41|John 12:41]] declares that Isaiah "saw Jesus' glory" in this vision. The exalted Lord on the throne is the pre-incarnate Christ. This anticipates the fuller revelation: the One on heaven's throne will descend the ladder to become flesh ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]), and then ascend again to the throne ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]]). The temple vision shows heaven touching earth in God's presence; in Christ, heaven permanently indwells earth as Emmanuel, God with us.
