### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 10|Genesis 28:10-17]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5551|H5551]] סֻלָּם (sullām) - "ladder/stairway" - connecting heaven and earth
- [[Lexicon/H4397|H4397]] מַלְאָךְ (malʾāḵ) - "angel/messenger" - ascending and descending
- [[Lexicon/H8064|H8064]] שָׁמַיִם (šāmayim) - "heaven" - the top of the ladder
- [[Lexicon/H776|H776]] אֶרֶץ (ʾereṣ) - "earth" - the base of the ladder
- [[Lexicon/H1004|H1004]] בֵּית אֵל (bêṯ ʾēl) - "house of God" - Bethel
- [[Lexicon/H8179|H8179]] שַׁעַר (šaʿar) - "gate" - "gate of heaven"

**Context**: Jacob, fleeing from Esau after deceiving their father Isaac, stops for the night at Luz. In a dream, he sees a ladder (or stairway) set on earth with its top reaching heaven, with angels ascending and descending. The LORD stands above (or beside) it and renews the Abrahamic covenant promises to Jacob. Upon waking, Jacob declares the place "the house of God" and "the gate of heaven," renaming it Bethel.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 5|Hosea 12:5]] references this encounter: "He met God at Bethel, and there God spoke with us"
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 1|Genesis 35:1-15]] records Jacob's return to Bethel where God reaffirms the name-change and covenant
- The tabernacle/temple later institutionalizes the Bethel principle—a designated heaven-earth meeting place

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - This is the foundational type—the first explicit vision of heaven-earth connection through a specific means
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1-4]] - develops the throne-room vision
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 1|Isaiah 66:1]] - affirms heaven as God's throne
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 51|John 1:51]] - explicitly applies this vision to Christ as the true ladder
**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-Looking - Jesus explicitly applies this vision to Himself (John 1:51), indicating divine intent in the original revelation

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the true ladder—His feet touched earth (incarnation) while His head reaches heaven (deity). In one Person, He unites both natures and both realms. Samuel Mather noted: "As in this Ladder, the top and the foot of it made but one Ladder; so both the Natures of Christ are but one Person; and by this Union of his two Natures he brings Heaven and Earth together, unites God and Man." The angels ascending and descending "on the Son of Man" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 51|John 1:51]]) indicate that all divine ministry flows through Christ—He is the only mediator, the only way, the true gate of heaven.
