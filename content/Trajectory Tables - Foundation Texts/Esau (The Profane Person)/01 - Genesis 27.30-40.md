### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 27#Genesis 27 . 30|Genesis 27:30-40]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- בְּרָכָה (berakhah) - "blessing" (H1293) - [[Lexicon/B1/B1293|benediction, prosperity]]
- חָרַד (kharad) - "tremble" (H2729) - [[Lexicon/K2/K2729|to tremble greatly]]
- וַיִּצְעַק (vayitz'ak) - "cried out" (H6817) - [[Lexicon/V6/V6817|to cry, call out loudly]]
- מָרָה (marah) - "bitter" (H4751) - [[Lexicon/M4/M4751|bitter, distressed]]

**Context**: Isaac intends to bless Esau despite divine oracle (Gen 25:23). Rebekah and Jacob deceive Isaac. Jacob receives irrevocable covenant blessing. Esau arrives moments too late.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Demonstrates principle that God's elective decree overrules human intention
- Rebekah acts to ensure God's word (Gen 25:23) is fulfilled, though through morally questionable means
- Isaac's attempt to reverse God's election fails—blessing cannot be recalled

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 23|Genesis 25:23]] - oracle—"older will serve younger"
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 27#Genesis 27 . 1|Genesis 27:1-29]] - deception preceding this passage
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]] - God loved Jacob, hated Esau—covenantal election
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 12|Romans 9:12-13]] - election not by works but by God's purpose
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 20|Hebrews 11:20]] - Isaac blessed Jacob/Esau "concerning things to come"—faith despite initial error
**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. God sovereignly arranged events to demonstrate elective purpose. Negative type—Esau's rejection foreshadows reprobates' final loss.

**Christological Connection**: Isaac's blessing transfers covenant headship to Jacob. Prefigures Father's election of Christ as covenant head (Isa 42:1, "My chosen one in whom I delight"). Esau's rejection foreshadows Israel's rejection of Messiah—losing kingdom privileges transferred to believing remnant (Matt 21:43). Final judgment: "Depart from me, I never knew you" (Matt 7:23)—too late for those who presumed on privileges without genuine faith.
