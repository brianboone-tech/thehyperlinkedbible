### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 10|Romans 9:10-13]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἐκλογή (eklogē) - "election, choice" (G1589) - [[Lexicon/E1/E1589|divine selection]]
- πρόθεσις (prothesis) - "purpose, plan" (G4286) - [[Lexicon/P4/P4286|deliberate intention]]
- ἔργων (ergōn) - "works, deeds" (G2041) - [[Lexicon/E2/E2041|actions, achievements]]
- καλοῦντος (kalountos) - "the one calling" (G2564) - [[Lexicon/K2/K2564|to call, summon]]
- ἠγάπησα (ēgapēsa) - "I loved" (G25) - [[Lexicon/A25|to love, elect in covenant]]
- ἐμίσησα (emisēsa) - "I hated" (G3404) - [[Lexicon/M3/M3404|to hate, reject]]

**Context**: Romans 9 addresses theodicy: Why did Israel (natural descendants) reject Messiah while Gentiles believe? Paul's answer: Not all Israel are Israel (9:6). God's elective purpose, not ethnic descent, determines who inherits promise. Jacob/Esau example proves election independent of works.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Paul quotes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 23|Genesis 25:23]] ("older will serve younger") and [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]] ("Jacob I loved, Esau I hated")
- Demonstrates that election precedes and produces works, not vice versa
- God's sovereignty in choosing covenant line established from Genesis onward

**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. Paul explicitly interprets Jacob/Esau as typological of elect/reprobate. God sovereignly arranged historical events to display elective principle.

**Christological Connection**: Ultimate expression of election is Christ—God's "Elect One" (Isa 42:1; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 35|Luke 9:35]]). All election is "in Christ" (Eph 1:4). Jacob chosen prefigures believers chosen in Christ before foundation of world. Esau rejected prefigures those passed over in God's elective decree. Christ is both elect head (chosen) and rejected stone (despised, Ps 118:22). In Him, elect receive all blessings Jacob typifies; outside Him, none escape rejection Esau exemplifies.
