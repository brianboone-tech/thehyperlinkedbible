### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 26#Genesis 26 . 34|Genesis 26:34-35]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- חִתִּי (khitti) - "Hittite" (H2850) - [[Lexicon/K2/K2850|Hittite, Canaanite people]]
- מֹרַת רוּחַ (morat ruakh) - "grief of spirit" (H4786, H7307) - [[Lexicon/M4/M4786|bitterness, grief]] + [[Lexicon/R7/R7307|spirit]]

**Context**: Esau, age forty, marries two Hittite (Canaanite) women: Judith and Basemath. Genesis 26:35 states they "brought grief of spirit to Isaac and Rebekah." Later (Gen 28:8-9) Esau realizes his parents' displeasure and attempts to remedy situation by marrying Ishmael's daughter—but damage already done.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Pattern: Covenant people warned against intermarriage with Canaanites (Gen 24:3; Ex 34:16; Deut 7:3-4)
- Esau's marriages demonstrate disregard for covenant holiness and parents' values
- Contrast with Abraham's careful selection of Rebekah from his own kindred (Gen 24)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 24#Genesis 24 . 3|Genesis 24:3]] - Abraham forbids Isaac marrying Canaanite
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 34|Genesis 25:34]] - despised birthright
- **FROM OT**:
  - Ezra 9-10 (intermarriage crisis post-exile)
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 11|Malachi 2:11-15]] - profaning covenant through marriage to foreign women
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 14|2 Corinthians 6:14]] - "Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers"
**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. Negative type—pattern of profane person disregarding covenant holiness.

**Christological Connection**: Esau chose Canaanite wives, joining with ungodly; Christ chose His Bride (Church) from among sinners but sanctified her (Eph 5:25-27). Esau's marriages brought grief to parents; Christ's marriage to Church brings joy to Father (Isa 62:5). Esau's alliances defiled; Christ's Bride is "without spot or wrinkle" (Eph 5:27). Warning: "Be holy, for I am holy" (1 Pet 1:16). Don't despise call to separation.
