### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 15|Revelation 20:15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- βίβλος (biblos) - "book" (G976) - [[Lexicon/B976|scroll, written record]]
- ζωή (zōē) - "life" (G2222) - [[Lexicon/Z2/Z2222|eternal life]]
- λίμνη (limnē) - "lake" (G3041) - [[Lexicon/L3/L3041|lake, pool]]
- πυρός (pyros) - "fire" (G4442) - [[Lexicon/P4/P4442|fire]]
- ἐβλήθη (eblēthē) - "was cast, thrown" (G906) - [[Lexicon/B906|to throw, cast]]

**Context**: Revelation 20:11-15 describes final judgment before Great White Throne. Books opened (20:12)—record of deeds. Book of Life opened—record of elect. Those not found in Book of Life cast into lake of fire (second death).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Book of Life concept rooted in OT: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 32|Exodus 32:32-33]] (Moses willing to be blotted out); [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 28|Psalm 69:28]] (wicked blotted from book of life); [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|Daniel 12:1]] (everyone found written in book delivered)
- Final separation between elect/reprobate foreshadowed throughout OT history

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 23|Genesis 25:23]] - election before birth
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]] - Jacob loved, Esau hated
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|Daniel 12:1-2]] - resurrection to everlasting life or everlasting contempt
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 24|Isaiah 66:24]] - worm does not die, fire not quenched
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 8|Revelation 21:8]] - cowardly, unbelieving, abominable, etc. in lake of fire
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|Matthew 25:41]] - "Depart from me, you cursed, into eternal fire"
**Type Classification**: Eschatological fulfillment of elect/reprobate typology. Jacob/Esau pattern reaches final, eternal expression.

**Christological Connection**: Christ is Judge at Great White Throne ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 22|John 5:22-27]]). Those "in Christ" found in Book of Life (Phil 4:3). Those outside Christ cast into lake of fire. Esau's rejection prefigures final rejection: "Depart from me, I never knew you" (Matt 7:23). Jacob's election prefigures believers' security: Names "written in heaven" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 20|Luke 10:20]]). Christ Himself is the Book of Life—all in Him are written, all outside Him are blotted out. Warning to professing Christians: Examine whether you are in the faith (2 Cor 13:5). "Work out your salvation with fear and trembling" (Phil 2:12). "Make your calling and election sure" (2 Pet 1:10).
