### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 29|Genesis 25:29-34]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- נָזִיד (nazid) - "stew, pottage" (H5138) - [[Lexicon/N5/N5138|boiled food, pottage]]
- עָיֵף (ayef) - "famished, weary" (H5889) - [[Lexicon/A5/A5889|exhausted, faint]]
- בְּכֹרָה (bekorah) - "birthright" (H1062) - [[Lexicon/B1/B1062|right of firstborn]]
- מִכְרָה (mikhrah) - "sell" (H4376) - [[Lexicon/M4/M4376|to sell, exchange]]
- וַיִּבֶז (vayyivez) - "despised" (H959) - [[Lexicon/V959|to despise, hold in contempt]]

**Context**: Esau returns from field, famished. Jacob cooking lentil stew. Esau demands food. Jacob demands birthright as price. Esau agrees: "I am about to die, so what good is a birthright to me?" (25:32). Transaction completed.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- No direct OT quotations of this event, but patriarchal narratives consistently show spiritual versus carnal choices
- Later prophets contrast Edom (Esau's descendants) with Israel (Jacob's line) as covenant versus rejection

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 23|Genesis 25:23]] (divine oracle—older serves younger foreshadows this exchange)
- **FROM OT**: Obadiah 1-21 (judgment on Edom for pride and violence against Jacob)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 16|Hebrews 12:16]] (Esau labeled "profane" for selling birthright for single meal)

**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. Negative type—warning of what not to be. Profane person who despises spiritual inheritance.

**Christological Connection**: Esau despised birthright; Israel despised Messiah (Isa 53:3, "despised and rejected"). Many in Israel forfeited kingdom blessing for earthly advantage (Matt 19:23-26). Christ valued Father's will above all (Heb 10:7). Believers warned: "Let us not be like Esau" (Heb 12:16)—cherish what Christ purchased at infinite cost.
