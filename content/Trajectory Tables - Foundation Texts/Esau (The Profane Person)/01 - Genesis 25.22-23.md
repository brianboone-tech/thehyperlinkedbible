### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 22|Genesis 25:22-23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- יִתְרֹצֲצוּ (yitrotzatzu) - "struggled together" (H7533) - [[Lexicon/H7/H7533|crushed, struggled violently]]
- לִדְרֹשׁ (lidrosh) - "to inquire" (H1875) - [[Lexicon/H1/H1875|to seek, consult]]
- לְאֹם (le'om) - "nation, people" (H3816) - [[Lexicon/L3/L3816|ethnic group, people]]
- רַב (rav) - "older, greater" (H7227) - [[Lexicon/H7/H7227|many, great, elder]]
- צָעִיר (tza'ir) - "younger, smaller" (H6810) - [[Lexicon/T6/T6810|small, young]]
- יַעֲבֹד (ya'avod) - "will serve" (H5647) - [[Lexicon/Y5/Y5647|to serve, work]]

**Context**: Isaac prays for barren Rebekah (25:21). She conceives twins who struggle violently in her womb—so intense she inquires of the LORD. God's oracle declares sovereign election before birth.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 3|Hosea 12:3]] recalls Jacob's prenatal struggle, interpreting it as early evidence of God's elective purposes
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]] uses "Jacob I loved, Esau I hated" to explain God's covenant faithfulness to Israel despite unworthiness

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - seed promise—enmity between seeds
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Genesis 12:2-3]] - covenant narrowing through Abraham's line
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]] - covenantal love/hate
  - [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 3|Hosea 12:3]] - prenatal struggle remembered
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 10|Romans 9:10-13]] - election before birth or works
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 16|Hebrews 12:16-17]] - Esau as negative example
**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. Recognized as typological from NT (Romans 9, Hebrews 12). God sovereignly arranged historical events to foreshadow elect/reprobate distinction.

**Christological Connection**: Esau (older, natural man) rejected; Jacob (younger, elect) chosen. Prefigures Christ as true Israel—elected head of new humanity. First Adam (natural) rejected; Last Adam (spiritual) chosen. Pattern: God's elective purpose establishes spiritual over natural lineage.
