### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 16|Hebrews 12:16]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- πόρνος (pornos) - "sexually immoral" (G4205) - [[Lexicon/P4/P4205|fornicator, immoral person]]
- βέβηλος (bebēlos) - "profane, godless" (G952) - [[Lexicon/B952|treating sacred as common]]
- πρωτοτόκια (prōtotokia) - "birthright" (G4415) - [[Lexicon/P4/P4415|rights of firstborn]]
- βρῶσις (brōsis) - "meal, food" (G1035) - [[Lexicon/B1/B1035|eating, food]]
- ἀπέδοτο (apedoto) - "sold" (G591) - [[Lexicon/A591|to sell away]]

**Context**: Hebrews 12 exhorts persecuted believers to endure discipline. Verses 14-17 warn against falling short of God's grace. Two dangers cited: sexual immorality and godlessness like Esau. Connection: Both trade eternal for temporal.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Hebrews quotes no specific OT text but alludes to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 29|Genesis 25:29-34]] (selling birthright) and 27:30-40 (seeking blessing with tears)
- Interprets historical narrative as moral/spiritual warning for NT believers
- Labels Esau with strongest condemnation: βέβηλος (profane)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 29|Genesis 25:29-34]] - sold birthright for stew
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 27#Genesis 27 . 34|Genesis 27:34-38]] - sought blessing with tears
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 24|Psalm 106:24]] - Israel "despised the pleasant land"—parallel to despising birthright
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 17|Hebrews 12:17]] - no place for repentance
  - [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 19|Philippians 3:19]] - "their god is their belly"
**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. Negative type—warning against profane character. Esau represents all who despise spiritual inheritance.

**Christological Connection**: Esau despised birthright; many despise Christ (Heb 10:29, "trampled the Son of God underfoot"). Esau valued food; Christ valued Father's will ("My food is to do the will of Him who sent me," [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 34|John 4:34]]). Esau sold inheritance for temporary pleasure; Christ "for the joy set before Him endured the cross" (Heb 12:2). Warning to professing Christians: Don't be like Esau—cherish what Christ purchased. "What does it profit to gain the world and forfeit your soul?" ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 8#Mark 8 . 36|Mark 8:36]]).
