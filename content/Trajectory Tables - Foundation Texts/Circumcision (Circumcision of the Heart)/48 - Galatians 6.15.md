### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 6#Galatians 6 . 15|Galatians 6:15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4061|περιτομή]] (*peritomē*) - "circumcision"
- [[Lexicon/G0201-0300#G0203|ἀκροβυστία]] (*akrobystia*) - "uncircumcision, foreskin"
- [[Lexicon/G2401-2500#G2480|ἰσχύει]] (*ischyei*) - "has power, counts for anything" (present active)
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2537|καινή]] (*kainē*) - "new" (qualitatively new)
- [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2937|κτίσις]] (*ktisis*) - "creation, creature"

**Context**: Paul concludes his argument against the Judaizers: "For neither circumcision counts for anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation." This radical statement nullifies circumcision's importance—not partially but absolutely. What mattered supremely in old covenant becomes utterly irrelevant in new covenant. Only one thing counts: new creation accomplished through union with Christ. This represents complete redemptive-historical shift from external ritual to internal transformation, from ethnic marker to spiritual reality.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 10|Genesis 17:10-14]] (circumcision as covenant sign), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] (new covenant promise)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 6|Galatians 5:6]] (neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts, but faith working through love), [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]] (if anyone is in Christ, new creation), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 15|Ephesians 2:15]] (abolishing law of commandments to create one new man)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 6#Galatians 6 . 15|Galatians 6:15]] declares "neither circumcision counts for anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation," showing circumcision's complete obsolescence in Christ. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 10|Genesis 17:10-14]] established circumcision as perpetual covenant sign—those uncircumcised were "cut off" from God's people. For two millennia, this marked covenant membership. But circumcision was always shadow pointing to substance. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 11|Romans 4:11]] calls it "sign and seal" of Abraham's faith—the reality it signified (righteousness by faith) mattered more than the sign itself. Christ's coming inaugurated the new creation prophesied in [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] and 66:22. Second Corinthians 5:17 announces: "if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." This isn't moral improvement but ontological transformation—death to old Adamic existence, resurrection to new Christ-life. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 15|Ephesians 2:15]] explains Christ "abolished the law of commandments expressed in ordinances, that he might create in himself one new man in place of the two"—Jew and Gentile united in one body through the cross. Circumcision distinguished these two; new creation unites them. The Spirit regenerates ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 3|John 3:3-8]]), creating what circumcision could only symbolize—new hearts loving God ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]]). Physical circumcision removed flesh; spiritual new creation removes old self and imparts Christ's life ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 9|Colossians 3:9-10]]). The trajectory shows progression: circumcision as covenant sign (Genesis 17) → prophetic promise of transformation ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]]) → Christ accomplishing new creation through death and resurrection → Spirit applying new creation to believers → physical circumcision obsolete because the reality has arrived. What mattered as marker becomes irrelevant in Christ; what was commanded finds fulfillment; what symbolized transformation is superseded by actual transformation. Only new creation counts—being crucified with Christ ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 2#Galatians 2 . 20|Galatians 2:20]]), raised with Christ ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 1|Colossians 3:1]]), transformed by the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]), conformed to Christ's image ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]), united to Christ in His death and resurrection producing entirely new existence where old covenant markers no longer apply.
