**Context**: Moses prophesies divine heart-circumcision: "The LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your offspring, so that you will love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul, that you may live." This moves beyond external ritual to internal transformation—God himself will perform the surgery, enabling covenant love and obedience. What Genesis 17 commanded externally (physical circumcision), Deuteronomy 30 promises internally (heart transformation), pointing to new covenant fulfillment through the Spirit.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 10|Genesis 17:10-11]] (circumcision as covenant sign), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 16|Deuteronomy 10:16]] (circumcise the foreskin of your heart)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 9#Jeremiah 9 . 25|Jeremiah 9:25-26]] (uncircumcised in heart), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26]] (new heart, new spirit)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 29|Romans 2:29]] (circumcision is of the heart, by the Spirit), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 11|Colossians 2:11]] (circumcision made without hands), [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 3|Philippians 3:3]] (we are the circumcision who worship by Spirit)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]] prophesies heart transformation fulfilled through Christ and the Spirit. Moses commands "circumcise the foreskin of your heart" (10:16)—human obligation. But [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]] promises "the LORD your God will circumcise your heart"—divine action. What law demands, grace provides. Physical circumcision removed foreskin; spiritual circumcision removes heart's rebellion. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 28|Romans 2:28-29]] announces fulfillment: "he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, nor is circumcision outward and physical. But... circumcision is of the heart, by the Spirit." The Spirit accomplishes what [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]] promised—internal transformation enabling covenant love. [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 11|Colossians 2:11]] describes this as "circumcision made without hands, by putting off the body of the flesh, by the circumcision of Christ." Christ's death and resurrection accomplish the surgery; the Spirit applies it to believers' hearts. The trajectory shows: Genesis 17 (external sign) → [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 16|Deuteronomy 10:16]] (internal demand) → [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 6|Deuteronomy 30:6]] (divine promise) → [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 29|Romans 2:29]]/[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 11|Colossians 2:11]] (spiritual fulfillment through Christ and Spirit). What began as physical ritual points to spiritual reality; what law commanded, Christ accomplishes; what was promised, the Spirit applies, enabling believers to "love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul."
