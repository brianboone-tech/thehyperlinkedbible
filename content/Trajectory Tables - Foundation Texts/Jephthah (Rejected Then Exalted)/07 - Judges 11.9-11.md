### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 11#Judges 11 . 9|Judges 11:9-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7218|H7218]] רֹאשׁ (rōʾš) - "head" - will I be your head?
- [[Lexicon/H7101|H7101]] קָצִין (qāṣîn) - "commander" - head and commander
- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (nāṯan) - "give/deliver" - if the LORD delivers them to me
- [[Lexicon/H5707|H5707]] עֵד (ʿēḏ) - "witness" - The LORD is witness

**Context**: Jephthah negotiates terms for his return: "If you bring me home again to fight against the Ammonites, and the LORD delivers them to me, will I really be your head (לְרֹאשׁ)?" The elders solemnly confirm with an oath: "The LORD is witness between us; we will do according to your word." The people then formally installed Jephthah as "head and commander" (לְרֹאשׁ וּלְקָצִין). The term רֹאשׁ ("head") carries significant typological weight, connecting to Psalm 118:22's "head of the corner" (לְרֹאשׁ פִּנָּה).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The term רֹאשׁ ("head") connects to [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]]—the rejected stone becomes the head/cornerstone
- Jephthah's exaltation parallels Joseph's rise from prison to vice-regent
- The confirmation "before the LORD at Mizpah" (11:11) gives covenant weight to the arrangement

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]] - "The stone the builders rejected has become the head of the corner"
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 40|Genesis 41:40]] - Pharaoh exalts Joseph
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 10#1 Samuel 10 . 24|1 Samuel 10:24]] - Samuel presents Saul: "Do you see him whom the LORD has chosen?"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 11|Acts 4:11]] - "the stone...rejected...has become the cornerstone"
  - [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 9|Philippians 2:9]] - "God highly exalted him"
**Type Classification**: Providential (circumstances sovereignly arranged for this reversal), Backward-Looking (typological significance clearer from NT)

**Christological Connection**: Jephthah's installation as "head" directly anticipates Christ's exaltation. Jesus quoted [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]] to religious leaders: "The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 42|Matthew 21:42]]). Peter echoes: "This Jesus is the stone that was rejected by you, the builders, which has become the cornerstone" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 11|Acts 4:11]]). The rejected one becomes the רֹאשׁ—the head to whom all must submit. [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 9|Philippians 2:9-11]]: "Therefore God has highly exalted him and bestowed on him the name that is above every name, so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow." Like Gilead's elders bowing before the expelled Jephthah, all creation will acknowledge the crucified Christ as Lord.
