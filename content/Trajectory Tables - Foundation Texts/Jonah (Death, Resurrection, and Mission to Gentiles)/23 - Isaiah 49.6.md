### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 6|Isaiah 49:6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- **נָקֵל** (*naqel*) = "too light, too small" - God declares restoring Jacob insufficient; Servant's mission must be global
- **עֶבֶד** (*eved*) = "servant" - The Servant of Yahweh, ultimately fulfilled in Christ
- **אוֹר גּוֹיִם** (*or goyim*) = "light to the nations/Gentiles" - Servant's mission extends beyond Israel to all peoples
- **יְשׁוּעָה** (*yeshu'ah*) = "salvation" - Cognate with Jesus' name (Yeshua); Servant brings salvation to earth's ends
- **קְצֵה הָאָרֶץ** (*qetseh ha'aretz*) = "end of the earth" - Universal scope of Servant's saving work
- **שֵׁבֶט יִשְׂרָאֵל** (*shevet Yisra'el*) = "tribes of Israel" - Servant first restores Israel, then extends to Gentiles
- **נְצוּרֵי** (*netsurei*) = "preserved ones" - Those preserved/protected by God, referring to faithful remnant

**Context**: Second Servant Song (Isaiah 49:1-13) where Servant speaks autobiographically. After describing initial discouragement (v. 4), God responds with expanded commission. Restoring Israel's tribes is "too light a thing" - Servant must be "light to the nations" bringing salvation to earth's ends. This radically expands messianic mission beyond ethnic Israel to include all Gentiles. Jonah's reluctant mission to Nineveh prefigures this global scope.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - God's promise to Abraham: "in you all families of earth will be blessed" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]], 22:18); Israel called to be kingdom of priests and holy nation ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5-6]]); Solomon's temple as house of prayer for all peoples ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 41|1 Kings 8:41-43]]); Psalms calling nations to praise Yahweh ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 67#Psalm 67 . 1|Psalm 67:1-2]], 96:3, 117:1)
- **FROM OT**:
  - Isaiah's later Servant passages ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 6|Isaiah 42:6]] - covenant to people, light to nations
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 10|Isaiah 52:10]] - all ends of earth see salvation
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 15|Isaiah 52:15]] - Servant sprinkles many nations); Simeon quotes this passage ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 30|Luke 2:30-32]]); Philip applies to Ethiopian ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 8#Acts 8 . 32|Acts 8:32-35]]
- **FROM NT**:
  - Paul and Barnabas explicitly cite [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 6|Isaiah 49:6]] to justify Gentile mission: "I have made you a light for the Gentiles, that you may bring salvation to the ends of the earth" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 47|Acts 13:47]]); Jesus commissions disciples to all nations ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]], [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]])
**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ fulfills the Servant's dual mission perfectly. He came "to the lost sheep of the house of Israel" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 15#Matthew 15 . 24|Matthew 15:24]]), restoring the remnant who believed. But his mission extended far beyond: "I have other sheep that are not of this fold" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 16|John 10:16]]). After resurrection, he commissions disciples: "You will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]) - directly echoing [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 6|Isaiah 49:6]]. Paul and Barnabas quote this verse when Jewish opposition forces them to turn to Gentiles: "The Lord has commanded us: 'I have made you a light for the Gentiles, that you may bring salvation to the ends of the earth'" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 47|Acts 13:47]]). Christ is the true Light who enlightens every man ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 9|John 1:9]]), who declared "I am the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]). Whereas Jonah reluctantly went to one Gentile city, Christ eagerly sends his disciples to all nations ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]]). The Servant's "too light a thing" becomes gospel reality: salvation of Jews alone would be insufficient - Christ's mission encompasses every tribe, tongue, and nation ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]], 7:9). The "ends of the earth" now worship the Jewish Messiah who is also Savior of the world ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 42|John 4:42]], [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 4#1 John 4 . 14|1 John 4:14]]).
