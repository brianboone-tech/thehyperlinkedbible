### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 87#Psalm 87 . 6|Psalm 87:6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3789|H3789]] כָּתַב (kathab) - to write, record
- [[Lexicon/H5608|H5608]] סָפַר (saphar) - to count, number, recount

**Context**: The Psalmist celebrates Zion as the city of God where peoples from all nations will be registered as born there. God Himself "records as he registers the peoples." This prophetic vision anticipates the universal scope of God's people and His sovereign right to enumerate them.

**Type Classification**: Providential Type | Forward-Looking

**Christological Connection**: God's sovereign registration of His people anticipates the Lamb's Book of Life ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]]). Christ conducts the ultimate census—not for exploitation but for salvation, ensuring all His sheep are accounted for and none lost.
