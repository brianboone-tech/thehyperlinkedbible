### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 3|John 10:3, 14, 27]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2564|G2564]] καλέω (kaleo) - to call
- [[Lexicon/G3686|G3686]] ὄνομα (onoma) - name
- [[Lexicon/G1097|G1097]] γινώσκω (ginosko) - to know intimately

**Context**: Jesus presents Himself as the Good Shepherd who knows His sheep by name, calls them, and they follow His voice. This intimate knowledge contrasts with census-taking for exploitation; Christ counts His sheep to protect and save them.

**Connections**:

  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 11|Exodus 30:11-16]] - God's ownership
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 87#Psalm 87 . 6|Psalm 87:6]] - God's registry
- **FROM OT**:
  - Ezekiel 34 (shepherd imagery)
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 11|Isaiah 40:11]] - gentle shepherd
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 39|John 6:39]] - losing none
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]] - Book of Life
**Type Classification**: Fulfillment text

**Christological Connection**: Christ conducts the ultimate census—not for military power but for salvation. Unlike David who counted Israel for his own purposes, Christ counts His sheep to ensure none are lost. The Shepherd's intimate knowledge of each sheep by name fulfills what the census ransom anticipated: God's personal ownership of His people.
