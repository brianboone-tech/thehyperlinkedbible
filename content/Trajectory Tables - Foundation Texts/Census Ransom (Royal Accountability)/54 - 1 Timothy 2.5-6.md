### [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 5|1 Timothy 2:5-6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3316|G3316]] μεσίτης (mesites) - mediator
- [[Lexicon/G487|G487]] ἀντίλυτρον (antilytron) - ransom, substitute payment
- [[Lexicon/G3956|G3956]] πᾶς (pas) - all

**Context**: Paul declares that Christ Jesus "gave himself as a ransom for all." The unique compound ἀντίλυτρον (only here in NT) emphasizes substitutionary payment—Christ paying in place of all. This extends the census ransom trajectory's scope from Israel to all humanity.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 11|Exodus 30:11-16]] - census ransom law
  - [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 45|Mark 10:45]] - ransom for many
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 6|Isaiah 53:6]] - LORD laid on Him iniquity of all
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 14|Titus 2:14]] - gave Himself to redeem us
**Type Classification**: Fulfillment text

**Christological Connection**: Where the census ransom was paid by each individual Israelite, Christ pays the ransom in place of all (ἀντίλυτρον). The universal scope ("for all") extends beyond ethnic Israel to encompass all who will believe, fulfilling the trajectory's implication that God's true people transcend national boundaries.
