### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 12|Revelation 20:12, 15]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G976|G976]] βίβλος (biblos) - book, scroll
- [[Lexicon/G2222|G2222]] ζωή (zoe) - life
- [[Lexicon/G721|G721]] ἀρνίον (arnion) - Lamb

**Context**: John's apocalyptic vision presents the final judgment where "books were opened... the book of life." Only those "written in the Lamb's book of life" enter the New Jerusalem. This is the ultimate census—God's final enumeration determining eternal destiny.

  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]] - firstborn enrolled in heaven
**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation

**Christological Connection**: The census ransom trajectory reaches its consummation: Christ's ransom payment secures eternal inclusion in God's register for all who trust in Him. Where David's unauthorized census brought death, the Lamb's authorized census brings life. God alone numbers His people, and He numbers them by the blood of the Lamb. The "Lamb's book of life" directly connects the census to Christ's atoning sacrifice.
