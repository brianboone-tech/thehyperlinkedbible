### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 11|Exodus 30:11-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3724|H3724]] כֹּפֶר (kopher) - ransom, atonement price
- [[Lexicon/H5315|H5315]] נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh) - soul, life
- [[Lexicon/H3722|H3722]] כָּפַר (kaphar) - to cover, make atonement

**Context**: God instructs Moses regarding the census ransom that must be collected whenever Israel is numbered. Each person, rich or poor, must give a half-shekel as "a ransom for his life to the LORD" to prevent plague. This establishes the foundational principle that Israel's lives belong to God, and any enumeration must acknowledge His ownership through ransom payment.

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded ordinance) | Forward-Looking (points to ultimate ransom)

**Christological Connection**: The half-shekel ransom demonstrates that life belongs to God and must be "purchased back." Christ fulfills this as the λύτρον (ransom) for many ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 45|Mark 10:45]]), paying with His own life what the half-shekel could only symbolize. Where the census ransom prevented temporal plague, Christ's ransom prevents eternal death.
