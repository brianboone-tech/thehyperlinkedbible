### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 1#Numbers 1 . 2|Numbers 1:2-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5375|H5375]] נָשָׂא (nasa) - to lift up, take (census)
- [[Lexicon/H7218|H7218]] רֹאשׁ (rosh) - head (counted by heads)
- [[Lexicon/H6635|H6635]] צָבָא (tsaba) - army, host

**Context**: God commands Moses to take a census of all Israel by their families. This divinely authorized enumeration demonstrates that census-taking itself is not sinful when conducted according to God's command and with proper ransom payment. The contrast with David's later unauthorized census highlights the importance of divine sanction.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 11|Exodus 30:11-16]] (census ransom law)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 1|2 Samuel 24:1]] (David's violation of proper census procedure)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 3|John 10:3]] (Christ knows His sheep by name)

**Type Classification**: Direct Type | Forward-Looking

**Christological Connection**: God's commanded census shows His intimate knowledge and ordering of His people. Christ fulfills this as the Good Shepherd who "calls his own sheep by name" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 3|John 10:3]]) and ensures "I should lose nothing of all that he has given me" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 39|John 6:39]]).
