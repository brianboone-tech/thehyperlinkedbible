### [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 1|2 Samuel 24:1-17]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4487|H4487]] מָנָה (manah) - to count, number
- [[Lexicon/H5062|H5062]] נֶגֶף (negeph) - plague, blow
- [[Lexicon/H2398|H2398]] חָטָא (chata) - to sin, miss the mark

**Context**: David's census of Israel constitutes a grave sin resulting in plague upon the nation. Though the text shows complexity (God's anger vs. Satan's incitement in Chronicles), David's fundamental error was numbering God's people without proper ransom payment, treating them as his military resource rather than God's possession.

- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 11|Exodus 30:11-16]] - census ransom law violated
  - [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 1#Numbers 1 . 2|Numbers 1:2]] - proper census precedent
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 21#1 Chronicles 21 . 1|1 Chronicles 21:1-17]] - parallel account emphasizing Satan's role
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 45|Mark 10:45]] - Christ pays the ransom David failed to require
**Type Classification**: Providential Type | Backward-Looking (recognized as typological from NT perspective)

**Christological Connection**: David's sin in counting Israel for his own purposes contrasts with Christ who "came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many" ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 45|Mark 10:45]]). Where David brought plague by unauthorized enumeration, Christ brings life by paying the ransom David neglected.
