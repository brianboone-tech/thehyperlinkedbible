### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 21|Numbers 18:21-24]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4643|H4643]] מַעֲשֵׂר (*ma'aser*) - tithe, tenth part
- [[Lexicon/H5159|H5159]] נַחֲלָה (*nachalah*) - inheritance, possession
- [[Lexicon/H2506|H2506]] חֵלֶק (*cheleq*) - portion, share

**Context**: Numbers 18:21-24: "To the Levites I have given every tithe (מַעֲשֵׂר) in Israel for an inheritance (נַחֲלָה), in return for their service... Among the people of Israel they shall have no inheritance (נַחֲלָה). For the tithe of the people of Israel... I have given to the Levites for an inheritance."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 27#Leviticus 27 . 30|Leviticus 27:30-33]] - Tithes belong to the LORD
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 9|Deuteronomy 10:9]]; 18:1-2 - "The LORD is their inheritance"
- [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 10|Nehemiah 13:10-12]] - Restoration of tithes after neglect

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 27#Leviticus 27 . 30|Leviticus 27:30-33]] - Tithe regulations
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 20|Genesis 14:20]] - Abraham tithes to Melchizedek
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 8|Malachi 3:8-10]] - Robbing God by withholding tithes
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 10|Nehemiah 13:10-12]] - Levites scattered due to unpaid tithes
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 5|Hebrews 7:5-9]] - Levitical tithing system discussed

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Divinely commanded economic arrangement

**Christological Connection**: The Levites had no earthly inheritance because "the LORD is their inheritance" ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 9|Deuteronomy 10:9]]). This points to Christ who, though he was rich, became poor ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 8#2 Corinthians 8 . 9|2 Corinthians 8:9]]). The Levites' dependence on Israel's faithfulness in tithing mirrors Christ's vulnerability in incarnation - he entrusted himself to the Father's care. As Levites served full-time in worship because they were freed from agricultural labor, so believers are called to present their whole selves as living sacrifices, trusting God for provision. The neglect of tithes ([[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 8|Malachi 3:8-10]]; [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 10|Nehemiah 13:10]]) caused Levites to abandon their posts - spiritual service requires material support.
