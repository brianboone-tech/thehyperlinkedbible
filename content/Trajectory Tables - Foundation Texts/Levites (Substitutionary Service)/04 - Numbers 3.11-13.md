### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 11|Numbers 3:11-13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3881|H3881]] לֵוִי (*Levi*) - Levite
- [[Lexicon/H1060|H1060]] בְּכוֹר (*bekor*) - firstborn
- [[Lexicon/H6299|H6299]] פָּדָה (*padah*) - to ransom, redeem
- [[Lexicon/H8478|H8478]] תַּחַת (*tachat*) - in place of, instead of

**Context**: Numbers 3:11-13: "And the LORD spoke to Moses, saying, 'Behold, I have taken the Levites from among the people of Israel instead of (תַּחַת) every firstborn who opens the womb among the people of Israel. The Levites shall be mine, for all the firstborn are mine. On the day that I struck down all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, I consecrated for my own all the firstborn in Israel, both man and beast. They shall be mine: I am the LORD.'"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]], 12-15 - Firstborn consecrated after Passover
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 8#Numbers 8 . 16|Numbers 8:16-18]] - Restatement of substitutionary principle
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 40|Numbers 3:40-51]] - Precise count: 22,000 Levites for 22,273 firstborn

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]] - Original consecration of firstborn
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 29|Exodus 12:29]] - Death of Egyptian firstborn
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 8#Numbers 8 . 16|Numbers 8:16-18]] - Restatement of substitution
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]] - Church as "firstborn enrolled in heaven"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]] - Christ as "firstborn of all creation"

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (Providential) - Divinely commanded substitution foreshadowing ultimate substitution

**Christological Connection**: The Levites served "in place of" (תַּחַת) Israel's firstborn - the same preposition used for substitutionary sacrifice. This structural principle in Israel's worship anticipates the ultimate substitution: Christ, the true Firstborn ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]], 18), dies "in place of" all who believe. As one tribe was taken so all tribes could worship, one Man was given so all nations could be redeemed. The mathematical precision (22,000 for 22,273, with redemption price for the remainder) shows God takes substitution seriously - exact accounting, not approximate exchange.
