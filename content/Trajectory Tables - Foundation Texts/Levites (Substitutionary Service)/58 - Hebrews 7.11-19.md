### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 11|Hebrews 7:11-19]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5050|G5050]] τελείωσις (*teleiosis*) - perfection, completion
- [[Lexicon/G3331|G3331]] μετάθεσις (*metathesis*) - change, removal
- [[Lexicon/G3551|G3551]] νόμος (*nomos*) - law
- [[Lexicon/G2420|G2420]] ἱερωσύνη (*hierosyne*) - priesthood
- [[Lexicon/G770|G770]] ἀσθενής (*asthenes*) - weak, powerless

**Context**: Hebrews 7:11-12, 18-19: "Now if perfection (τελείωσις) had been attainable through the Levitical priesthood (for under it the people received the law), what further need would there have been for another priest to arise after the order of Melchizedek, rather than one named after the order of Aaron? For when there is a change (μετάθεσις) in the priesthood, there is necessarily a change in the law as well... For on the one hand, a former commandment is set aside because of its weakness (ἀσθενής) and uselessness (for the law made nothing perfect), but on the other hand, a better hope is introduced, through which we draw near to God."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - "You are a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek"
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 18|Genesis 14:18-20]] - Melchizedek blesses Abraham

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - Prophetic announcement of Melchizedekian priest
- **TO**: Exodus 28-29 - Levitical/Aaronic priesthood institution
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 22|Hebrews 7:22]] - Jesus guarantor of better covenant
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6]] - Christ mediator of better covenant

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Explicit typological argument

**Christological Connection**: The rhetorical question exposes the Levitical system's limitation: "if perfection had been attainable through the Levitical priesthood... what further need?" The very fact that [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] promises another priest proves the first system was provisional. The Levites served faithfully within their limitations, but could never achieve "perfection" (τελείωσις) - complete, permanent, effectual salvation. Christ's priesthood is "after the order of Melchizedek" - without beginning or end, without genealogical qualification, without repeated sacrifice. The law is "changed" because the priesthood is changed; the entire Levitical system was a shadow pointing to the substance now revealed in Christ.
