### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 1|Numbers 18:1-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5656|H5656]] עֲבֹדָה (*avodah*) - service, labor, ministry
- [[Lexicon/H4979|H4979]] מַתָּנָה (*mattanah*) - gift
- [[Lexicon/H6944|H6944]] קֹדֶשׁ (*qodesh*) - holy thing, sanctuary
- [[Lexicon/H2114|H2114]] זוּר (*zur*) - stranger, outsider

**Context**: Numbers 18:1-7: God speaks to Aaron directly: "You and your sons and your father's house with you shall bear iniquity connected with the sanctuary... I have given your brothers the Levites from among the people of Israel as a gift (מַתָּנָה) to you, given to the LORD, to do the service of the tent of meeting... the outsider (זָר) who comes near shall be put to death."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]] - Aaron and sons set apart as priests
- Leviticus 8-9 - Consecration and inauguration of priesthood
- Numbers 16 - Korah's rebellion challenges this hierarchy

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]] - Original priestly appointment
- **TO**: Numbers 16 - Korah challenges Levitical hierarchy
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 29#2 Chronicles 29 . 11|2 Chronicles 29:11]] - Hezekiah affirms divine election of Levites
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 6|John 17:6]] - Believers "given" to Christ by the Father
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 8|Ephesians 4:8]], 11 - Christ gives gifts to church

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Divinely commanded service structure

**Christological Connection**: The Levites are called a "gift" (מַתָּנָה) given to Aaron. This gift language resonates with [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 6|John 17:6]] where Jesus says of believers: "They were yours, and you gave them to me." As the Father gave the Levites to serve Aaron, so the Father gives believers to serve Christ. The strict hierarchy (priests at altar, Levites assisting, outsiders excluded) points to the exclusive mediation of Christ - no one comes to the Father except through him. The "outsider who comes near shall be put to death" warns that approach to God requires the divinely appointed mediator.
