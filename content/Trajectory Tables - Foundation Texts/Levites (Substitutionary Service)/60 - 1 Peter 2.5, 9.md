**Context**: 1 Peter 2:5: "You yourselves like living stones are being built up as a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood (ἱεράτευμα ἅγιον), to offer spiritual sacrifices (πνευματικὰς θυσίας) acceptable to God through Jesus Christ."

1 Peter 2:9: "But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood (βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα), a holy nation, a people for his own possession, that you may proclaim the excellencies of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light."

**Type Classification**: NT Application - Levitical priesthood extended to all believers

**Christological Connection**: The Levites served "in place of" Israel's firstborn so that all Israel might worship. Now, through Christ's once-for-all sacrifice, the substitutionary principle reaches its goal: all believers become priests. What was restricted to one tribe is now extended to all who are "in Christ." The "spiritual sacrifices" believers offer are not atoning (Christ accomplished that) but responsive - praise, service, good works. The language "royal priesthood" (βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα) combines kingly and priestly roles, which were kept separate in Israel but are united in Christ and, through him, in his people. The Levites proclaimed Torah; believers "proclaim the excellencies" of Christ.
