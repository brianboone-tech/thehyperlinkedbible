### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 10|Ezekiel 44:10-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8582|H8582]] תָּעָה (*ta'ah*) - to go astray, wander
- [[Lexicon/H5771|H5771]] עָוֹן (*avon*) - iniquity, guilt
- [[Lexicon/H8104|H8104]] שָׁמַר (*shamar*) - to keep, guard, watch
- [[Lexicon/H4931|H4931]] מִשְׁמֶרֶת (*mishmereth*) - charge, guard duty
- [[Lexicon/H7126|H7126]] קָרַב (*qarab*) - to draw near, approach

**Context**: Ezekiel 44:10-16: "But the Levites who went far from me, going astray (תָּעָה) from me after their idols when Israel went astray... they shall bear their punishment (עָוֹן)... they shall not come near (קָרַב) to me to serve as priest... But the Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept (שָׁמַר) the charge of my sanctuary when the people of Israel went astray from me, shall come near to me to minister to me."

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking Type - Prophetic vision of eschatological reward/judgment

**Christological Connection**: In Ezekiel's vision, faithfulness in the present determines service in the future. Those who "went astray" are demoted; those who "kept the charge" are elevated to minister before God. This principle finds ultimate expression in Christ's teaching: "Well done, good and faithful servant... enter into the joy of your master" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 21|Matthew 25:21]]). The Zadokites' reward for faithfulness foreshadows the reward Christ promises to faithful servants. Yet even Zadokite faithfulness was imperfect; only Christ was perfectly faithful, and only through union with him do believers become a "royal priesthood" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]) qualified to serve forever.
