### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25#Numbers 25 . 12|Numbers 25:12-13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1285|H1285]] בְּרִית (*berit*) - covenant
- [[Lexicon/H7965|H7965]] שָׁלוֹם (*shalom*) - peace, wholeness
- [[Lexicon/H3550|H3550]] כְּהֻנָּה (*kehunnah*) - priesthood
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (*olam*) - forever, perpetual
- [[Lexicon/H7065|H7065]] קָנָא (*qana*) - to be jealous, zealous

**Context**: Numbers 25:12-13: "Therefore say, 'Behold, I give to him my covenant of peace (בְּרִית שָׁלוֹם), and it shall be to him and to his descendants after him the covenant of a perpetual (עוֹלָם) priesthood (כְּהֻנָּה), because he was jealous (קָנָא) for his God and made atonement for the people of Israel.'"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 25|Exodus 32:25-29]] - Levites' zeal at golden calf
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 8|Deuteronomy 33:8-11]] - Moses blesses Levi for covenant loyalty
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 4|Malachi 2:4-5]] - "Covenant with Levi" recalled

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 26|Exodus 32:26-29]] - Levites' earlier zeal against idolaters
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 4|Malachi 2:4-5]] - Covenant of life and peace with Levi
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 30|Psalm 106:30-31]] - Phinehas "intervened" and plague stopped
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 17|John 2:17]] - "Zeal for your house will consume me"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 3|Romans 4:3]] - "Counted to him as righteousness" (cf. Ps 106:31)

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Historical figure with typological significance

**Christological Connection**: Phinehas "made atonement" through his zealous action, stopping the plague and receiving a "covenant of peace." This foreshadows Christ who, in holy zeal ("zeal for your house will consume me," [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 17|John 2:17]]), makes atonement and secures an eternal priesthood. The "perpetual priesthood" granted to Phinehas finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ's Melchizedekian priesthood "after the order of an endless life" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 16|Hebrews 7:16]]). Phinehas's action was "counted to him as righteousness" ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 31|Psalm 106:31]]) - language echoing Abraham's faith - pointing to the righteousness that comes through faith in Christ's atoning work.
