**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Historical event with typological significance

**Christological Connection**: David's first attempt failed because he used a Philistine method (cart) rather than the Levitical method (shoulders). Good intentions do not excuse disobedience. The Levites "carried the ark on their shoulders" as prescribed - the burden was personal, not delegated to animals or machinery. This pictures Christ who personally bore the weight of our salvation: "He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 24|1 Peter 2:24]]). No substitute method, no shortcut - salvation required the personal bearing of sin by the God-appointed mediator. The phrase "we did not seek him according to the prescribed manner" warns that approach to God must be on his terms, through his appointed way - ultimately, through Christ alone.
