### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6]]

- [[Lexicon/G3009|G3009]] λειτουργία (*leitourgia*) - ministry, service

- [[Lexicon/G1242|G1242]] διαθήκη (*diatheke*) - covenant, testament
- [[Lexicon/G1861|G1861]] ἐπαγγελία (*epangelia*) - promise

**Context**: Hebrews 8:6: "But as it is, Christ has obtained a ministry (λειτουργία) that is as much more excellent (διαφορωτέρας) than the old as the covenant (διαθήκη) he mediates (μεσίτης) is better (κρείττων), since it is enacted on better promises (ἐπαγγελίαις)."

**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - Explicit comparison showing Christ's excellence

**Christological Connection**: The Levites' ministry (λειτουργία) was genuine but provisional; Christ's ministry is "more excellent" (διαφορωτέρας). The comparison is threefold: (1) more excellent ministry, (2) better covenant, (3) better promises. The Levites served under a covenant that could not finally deal with sin; Christ mediates a covenant that puts God's laws on hearts and remembers sins no more. The word "mediator" (μεσίτης) is key - the Levites served as go-betweens, standing between God and people. Christ is the ultimate Mediator, uniquely qualified because he is both God and man. What the Levitical system typified in shadow, Christ accomplishes in substance.
