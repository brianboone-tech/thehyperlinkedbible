**Context**: Revelation 5:10: "...and you have made them a kingdom (βασιλείαν) and priests (ἱερεῖς) to our God, and they shall reign (βασιλεύσουσιν) on the earth."

Revelation 20:6: "Blessed (μακάριος) and holy is the one who shares in the first resurrection! Over such the second death has no power, but they will be priests (ἱερεῖς) of God and of Christ, and they will reign (βασιλεύσουσιν) with him for a thousand years (χίλια ἔτη)."

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Priestly vocation reaches eternal fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: The Levitical priesthood was temporary, tribal, and earthly. The eschatological priesthood is eternal, universal, and heavenly-yet-reigning-on-earth. The substitutionary principle that began with Levites serving for Israel's firstborn now encompasses "every tribe and language and people and nation" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]]). What was privilege for one tribe becomes inheritance for all the redeemed. The combination of "kingdom" and "priests" (separated in Israel's history) is united in Christ and, through him, in his people forever. They "reign" because they are united to the reigning Christ; they serve as "priests" because they are united to the eternal High Priest. The Levites' service pointed forward to this glorious consummation.
