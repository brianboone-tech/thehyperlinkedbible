### [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 8|Malachi 2:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5493|H5493]] סוּר (*sur*) - to turn aside, depart
- [[Lexicon/H1870|H1870]] דֶּרֶךְ (*derek*) - way, path
- [[Lexicon/H7843|H7843]] שָׁחַת (*shachat*) - to corrupt, destroy
- [[Lexicon/H3782|H3782]] כָּשַׁל (*kashal*) - to stumble, stagger
- [[Lexicon/H8451|H8451]] תּוֹרָה (*torah*) - instruction, law

**Context**: Malachi 2:8-9: "But you have turned aside (סוּר) from the way (דֶּרֶךְ). You have caused many to stumble (כָּשַׁל) by your instruction (תּוֹרָה). You have corrupted (שָׁחַת) the covenant of Levi, says the LORD of hosts. So I make you despised and abased before all the people, inasmuch as you do not keep my ways but show partiality in your instruction."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 4|Malachi 2:4-7]] - The ideal "covenant with Levi" described
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 22#Ezekiel 22 . 26|Ezekiel 22:26]] - Priests "do violence to my law"
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 4#Hosea 4 . 6|Hosea 4:6]] - "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 4|Malachi 2:4-7]] - Ideal priestly instruction recalled
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25#Numbers 25 . 12|Numbers 25:12-13]] - Original Phinehas covenant
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 22#Ezekiel 22 . 26|Ezekiel 22:26]] - Priests fail to distinguish holy/common
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 4#Hosea 4 . 6|Hosea 4:6-9]] - Priests rejected for rejecting knowledge
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23#Matthew 23 . 2|Matthew 23:2-4]] - Jesus critiques teachers who don't practice what they teach

**Type Classification**: Antitype Contrast - Priestly failure heightens need for true High Priest

**Christological Connection**: The priests "turned aside from the way" - but Christ IS the Way ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]). They "caused many to stumble by instruction" - but Christ's teaching amazed crowds because he taught "with authority" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 28|Matthew 7:28-29]]). They "corrupted the covenant" - but Christ is mediator of a "better covenant" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6]]). They "showed partiality" - but Christ shows no favoritism ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 10#Acts 10 . 34|Acts 10:34]]). The prophetic indictment of failed priests creates longing for the faithful Priest who would perfectly fulfill what the Levitical priesthood could not. Where they were "despised and abased," Christ was "despised and rejected" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 3|Isaiah 53:3]]) - but for redemptive purposes, not as judgment for failure.
