### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 2|Judges 7:2-8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7227|H7227]] רַב (raḇ) - "many/too many" - You have too many people
- [[Lexicon/H6286|H6286]] פָּאַר (pāʾar) - "glorify" - lest Israel glorify themselves over Me
- [[Lexicon/H3027|H3027]] יָד (yāḏ) - "hand" - My own hand has saved me
- [[Lexicon/H3372|H3372]] יָרֵא (yārēʾ) - "fear/afraid" - Whoever is fearful and trembling
- [[Lexicon/H6884|H6884]] צָרַף (ṣārap̄) - "refine/sift" - I will sift them for you

**Context**: God deliberately reduced Gideon's army to ensure the victory could not be attributed to human strength. Starting with 32,000, the fearful were dismissed (22,000 left), then the water-lapping test reduced them to 300—against 135,000 Midianites. God's stated reason: "lest Israel glorify themselves over Me, saying, 'My own hand has saved me.'" Weakness was not just accepted but engineered.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 20#Deuteronomy 20 . 8|Deuteronomy 20:8]] provides legal basis for dismissing the fearful
- God fighting through impossible odds appears at Red Sea (Exodus 14), Jericho (Joshua 6), and later with Jonathan ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 14#1 Samuel 14 . 6|1 Samuel 14:6]])
- The principle "not by might nor by power" ([[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 6|Zechariah 4:6]]) is demonstrated

**Type Classification**: Providential (divinely engineered weakness), Forward-Looking (pattern for all divine work through human emptiness)

**Christological Connection**: God removing Gideon's army anticipates the cross. Jesus was "stripped" of disciples (all fled), of dignity, of life—reduced to apparent total weakness. Yet the resurrection proved that God's power is made perfect in weakness. The 300 defeating 135,000 prefigures the crucified Christ defeating sin, death, and Satan. What looks like defeat is actually victory when God is at work.
