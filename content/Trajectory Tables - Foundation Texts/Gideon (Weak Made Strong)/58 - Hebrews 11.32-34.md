### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 32|Hebrews 11:32-34]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4102|G4102]] πίστις (pistis) - "faith" - through faith
- [[Lexicon/G2610|G2610]] καταγωνίζομαι (katagōnizomai) - "conquer" - conquered kingdoms
- [[Lexicon/G1849|G1849]] ἐπιτυγχάνω (epitynchanō) - "obtain" - gained what was promised
- [[Lexicon/G772|G772]] ἀσθένεια (astheneia) - "weakness" - from weakness
- [[Lexicon/G1412|G1412]] δυναμόω (dynamoō) - "strengthen" - gained strength

**Context**: Hebrews 11 lists faith heroes, with Gideon named first among the judges (before Barak, Samson, Jephthah). The compressed list then describes what "through faith" they accomplished. The phrase "gained strength from weakness" (ἐδυναμώθησαν ἀπὸ ἀσθενείας) particularly fits Gideon's story—the weak man made strong, the reduced army achieving victory.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Gideon is listed first among the judges, suggesting his paradigmatic significance
- The Hebrews author reads the judges through the lens of faith despite their flaws
- The pattern of weakness → strength appears throughout OT (Moses, David, Elijah)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Judges 6-8 - Gideon's full narrative
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 1|Hebrews 11:1-2]] - Definition of faith framing this list
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 18#Psalm 18 . 32|Psalm 18:32-39]] - "God who equipped me with strength... you have armed me with strength for battle"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 12#2 Corinthians 12 . 9|2 Corinthians 12:9-10]] - Paul's articulation of the same principle
**Type Classification**: NT Commendation (Gideon's faith explicitly validated and held up as exemplary)

**Christological Connection**: The faith of Gideon and others looked forward to Christ. They "did not receive what was promised" fully (11:39) because God planned fulfillment in Christ. Gideon's "strength from weakness" finds its ultimate expression in Christ, who "was crucified in weakness, but lives by God's power" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 4|2 Corinthians 13:4]]). The pattern Gideon illustrated is the pattern Christ embodied and believers now share.
