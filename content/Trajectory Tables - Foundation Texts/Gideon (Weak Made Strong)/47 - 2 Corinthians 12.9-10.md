### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 12#2 Corinthians 12 . 9|2 Corinthians 12:9-10]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5485|G5485]] χάρις (charis) - "grace" - My grace is sufficient for you
- [[Lexicon/G1411|G1411]] δύναμις (dynamis) - "power" - My power is made perfect
- [[Lexicon/G769|G769]] ἀσθένεια (astheneia) - "weakness" - in weakness
- [[Lexicon/G5048|G5048]] τελειόω (teleioō) - "perfect/complete" - is made perfect
- [[Lexicon/G1981|G1981]] ἐπισκηνόω (episkēnoō) - "rest upon/tabernacle" - that Christ's power may rest on me
- [[Lexicon/G2744|G2744]] καυχάομαι (kauchaomai) - "boast" - I will boast all the more gladly

**Context**: Paul describes his "thorn in the flesh" and his three prayers for its removal. God's response: "My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is made perfect in weakness." Paul's conclusion: "Therefore I will boast all the more gladly about my weaknesses, so that Christ's power may rest on me. That is why, for Christ's sake, I delight in weaknesses... For when I am weak, then I am strong."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Paul articulates the theological principle that Gideon's story illustrates
- "Power made perfect in weakness" is the [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 2|Judges 7:2]] principle in theological form
- The OT pattern (Moses' stutter, Gideon's fear, David's youth) becomes explicit doctrine

**Type Classification**: NT Application (the Gideon pattern universalized for all believers)

**Christological Connection**: The "power made perfect in weakness" culminates in Christ crucified and risen. Christ "was crucified in weakness, but lives by God's power" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 4|2 Corinthians 13:4]]). The cross is the ultimate demonstration of divine power through apparent weakness. Gideon's 300 defeating 135,000 foreshadows the crucified Christ defeating sin, death, and hell. Believers now participate in this pattern: when we are weak, Christ's power rests on us.
