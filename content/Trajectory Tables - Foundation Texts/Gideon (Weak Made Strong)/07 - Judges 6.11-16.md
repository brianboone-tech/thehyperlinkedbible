### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 6#Judges 6 . 11|Judges 6:11-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4397|H4397]] מַלְאַךְ (malʾāḵ) - "angel/messenger" - the angel of the LORD
- [[Lexicon/H1368|H1368]] גִּבּוֹר (gibbôr) - "mighty man" - O mighty man of valor
- [[Lexicon/H2428|H2428]] חַיִל (ḥayil) - "valor/strength" - man of valor
- [[Lexicon/H1800|H1800]] דַּל (dal) - "weak/poor" - my clan is the weakest
- [[Lexicon/H6810|H6810]] צָעִיר (ṣāʿîr) - "youngest/smallest" - I am the youngest

**Context**: The angel of the LORD (a theophany) found Gideon threshing wheat in a winepress—an absurd location chosen to hide from Midianites. Yet God addressed him: "The LORD is with you, O mighty man of valor." Gideon's protest exposes his self-assessment: "My clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest in my father's house." God's response was not to find someone stronger but to promise His presence: "Surely I will be with you."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The pattern of calling the weak: Moses ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 11|Exodus 3:11]]), Jeremiah (1:6), David ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 11|1 Samuel 16:11]])
- "I will be with you" echoes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 12|Exodus 3:12]]; [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 5|Joshua 1:5]]
- The "angel of the LORD" as pre-incarnate Christ appears also with Abraham (Genesis 18), Jacob (Genesis 32), Moses (Exodus 3)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 11|Exodus 3:11-12]] - Moses' similar inadequacy and God's "I will be with you" promise
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 11|1 Samuel 16:11]] - David, the youngest, overlooked
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 6|Jeremiah 1:6-7]] - "I am only a child" yet called
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 5|Isaiah 6:5-8]] - "Woe is me" yet commissioned
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 27|1 Corinthians 1:27-29]] - God chooses the weak
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 12#2 Corinthians 12 . 9|2 Corinthians 12:9]] - Power perfected in weakness
**Type Classification**: Providential (sovereign call of the weak), Forward-Looking (pattern for all whom God calls)

**Christological Connection**: The angel of the LORD calling the hiding, fearful Gideon "mighty man of valor" pictures Christ's declaration over sinners. In Christ, the weak are made strong; the fearful become courageous. "I will be with you" becomes "I am with you always" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 20|Matthew 28:20]]). Christ calls not the righteous but sinners, not the strong but the weak—and His presence transforms identity.
