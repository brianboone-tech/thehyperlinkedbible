### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 19|Judges 7:19-22]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7782|H7782]] שׁוֹפָר (šôp̄ār) - "horn/trumpet" - blew their horns
- [[Lexicon/H3537|H3537]] כַּד (kaḏ) - "jar/pitcher" - broke the jars
- [[Lexicon/H3940|H3940]] לַפִּיד (lappîḏ) - "torch" - torches in their left hands
- [[Lexicon/H2719|H2719]] חֶרֶב (ḥereḇ) - "sword" - A sword for the LORD and for Gideon
- [[Lexicon/H7760|H7760]] שִׂים (śîm) - "set/put" - the LORD set all the men against one another

**Context**: The attack used unconventional weapons: torches hidden in jars, trumpets, and shouts. At the beginning of the middle watch, the 300 surrounded the camp, blew trumpets, smashed jars, revealed torches, and shouted: "A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!" The Midianites panicked; "the LORD set all the men in the camp against one another with their swords." Israel's 300 stood still while the enemy self-destructed.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The pattern of standing still while God fights echoes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 13|Exodus 14:13-14]] ("Stand still and see the salvation of the LORD") and [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 17|2 Chronicles 20:17]]
- Enemy self-destruction appears also in [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 14#1 Samuel 14 . 20|1 Samuel 14:20]] and [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 22|2 Chronicles 20:22-24]]
- The shofar (ram's horn) connects to Jericho's walls falling (Joshua 6)

**Type Classification**: Providential (victory through unconventional means), Forward-Looking (pattern of divine power through human weakness)

**Christological Connection**: The victory through apparent weakness anticipates the cross. As torches, jars, and trumpets (not swords) defeated Midian, so the cross (not military power) defeated Satan. Christ "disarmed rulers and authorities... triumphing over them" through what appeared to be defeat ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]]). The 300 standing still while God destroyed their enemies pictures believers who stand firm in Christ's finished victory.
