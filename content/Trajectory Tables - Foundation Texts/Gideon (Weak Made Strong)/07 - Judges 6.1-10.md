### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 6#Judges 6 . 1|Judges 6:1-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (nāṯan) - "give/deliver" - He delivered them into the hand of Midian
- [[Lexicon/H1809|H1809]] דָּלַל (dālal) - "bring low/impoverish" - Israel was greatly impoverished
- [[Lexicon/H2199|H2199]] זָעַק (zāʿaq) - "cry out" - the Israelites cried out to the LORD
- [[Lexicon/H5030|H5030]] נָבִיא (nāḇîʾ) - "prophet" - He sent them a prophet

**Context**: The Judges cycle reaches its fifth iteration with Midianite oppression. For seven years, Israel lived in caves, their crops destroyed by raiders "like a swarm of locusts" with "innumerable" camels. When Israel cried out, God first sent a prophet who indicted them: "You did not obey Me." This establishes the context of complete weakness and covenant failure before Gideon's call.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The Judges cycle (sin → oppression → cry → deliverance) repeats but with escalating severity
- The prophet's message recalls [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 2|Exodus 20:2-3]] (first commandment) and [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 4|Deuteronomy 6:4-5]]
- Israel's hiding in caves echoes [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 13#1 Samuel 13 . 6|1 Samuel 13:6]] under Philistine oppression

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 2|Exodus 20:2-3]] - The covenant commands violated
  - [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 28#Deuteronomy 28 . 49|Deuteronomy 28:49-51]] - Covenant curses fulfilled
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 13#1 Samuel 13 . 6|1 Samuel 13:6]] - Similar hiding under Philistine threat
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 19|Isaiah 2:19]] - Hiding in caves from God's judgment
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 38|Hebrews 11:38]] - Faith heroes "wandered in deserts and mountains, and hid in caves"
**Type Classification**: Providential (context establishing weakness), Forward-Looking (weakness as platform for divine power)

**Christological Connection**: Israel's desperate weakness typifies humanity's bondage to sin. The prophet's indictment ("you did not obey Me") exposes the spiritual root of the problem. Just as Israel needed both prophetic word and divine deliverance, humanity needs both conviction of sin and salvation in Christ. The caves of hiding anticipate the darkness from which Christ delivers His people.
