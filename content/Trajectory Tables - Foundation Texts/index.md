## Overview

**Foundation Texts** provide detailed analysis of the key passages referenced in each Trajectory Table. While the main trajectory tables give an overview of typological development, these supporting files offer verse-by-verse examination of the foundational texts.

---

## How to Use

1. Start with a [[Trajectory Tables/index|Trajectory Table]] to see the overall typological pattern
2. Click on "Text Analysis" links within the table to access Foundation Text files
3. Each Foundation Text examines the passage's context, structure, and theological significance

---

## Browse by Trajectory

Foundation texts are organized by their parent trajectory table. Access them through the main [[Trajectory Tables/index|Trajectory Tables Index]].
