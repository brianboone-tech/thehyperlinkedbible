### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- נֶפֶשׁ (nep̄eš) - soul, life, living being
- בָּשָׂר (bāśār) - flesh
- דָּם (dām) - blood
- מִזְבֵּחַ (mizbēaḥ) - altar
- כָּפַר (kāp̄ar) - to cover, atone, make atonement

**Context**: Leviticus 17 regulates sacrifices and prohibits consuming blood. Verse 11 provides the theological explanation for blood's sacredness.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]] articulates the principle enacted in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]: **covering for sin requires blood (life given)**. "For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it for you on the altar to make atonement [כַּפֵּר, kappēr, "to cover"] for your souls, for it is the blood that makes atonement by the life."

This is the Old Covenant system's central truth: אtonement = covering through blood. Animals' blood covered sin temporarily, requiring endless repetition ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 1|Hebrews 10:1-4]]). Christ's blood atones perfectly and permanently: "The blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin" ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 7|1 John 1:7]]). [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]: Christ "entered once for all into the holy places, not by means of the blood of goats and calves but by means of his own blood, thus securing eternal redemption."

The trajectory: Animal skins ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]) → Animal blood on altar ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]) → Christ's blood ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]; [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 18|1 Peter 1:18-19]]) → Robes washed in the Lamb's blood ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 14|Revelation 7:14]]).

**Application**: Every OT sacrifice pointed to Christ. The blood that made temporary covering prefigured Christ's blood that makes eternal covering. "Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness of sins" (Hebrews 9:22)—this principle, established in Genesis 3:21 and articulated in Leviticus 17:11, finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ's cross.
