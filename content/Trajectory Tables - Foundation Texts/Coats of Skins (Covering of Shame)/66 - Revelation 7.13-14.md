### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 13|Revelation 7:13-14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- στολή (stolē) - robe, long garment
- λευκός (leukos) - white, bright
- περιβάλλω (periballō) - to clothe, wrap around (perfect passive participle: "clothed")
- θλῖψις (thlipsis) - tribulation, affliction
- μέγας (megas) - great
- πλύνω (plynō) - to wash (aorist active)
- λευκαίνω (leukainō) - to make white, whiten (aorist active)
- αἷμα (haima) - blood
- ἀρνίον (arnion) - lamb, little lamb (diminutive of ἀρήν, arēn)

**Context**: Revelation 7:9-17 describes the multitude before God's throne—the redeemed from every nation, tribe, people, and language. One of the elders asks John about the white-robed throng, then explains their identity.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 13|Revelation 7:13-14]] is the eschatological fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]'s coats of skins. "These are the ones coming out of the great tribulation. They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb."

The stunning paradox—**robes washed white in blood**—captures the entire trajectory:
1. **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]**: Animal skins provided through death
2. **[[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]**: Blood makes atonement
3. **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 10|Isaiah 61:10]]**: Robes of righteousness (prophetic promise)
4. **[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]]**: Christ's righteousness imputed
5. **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 14|Revelation 7:14]]**: White robes washed in the Lamb's blood (consummation)

The blood that should condemn cleanses. The Lamb slain provides the covering, just as animals were slain in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]] to provide skins. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 8|Revelation 19:8]] adds: "Fine linen, bright and pure—for the fine linen is the righteous deeds of the saints" (both imputed and imparted righteousness).

**Application**: Every believer will stand before God's throne clothed in white robes washed in Christ's blood. Not our righteousness (filthy rags, Isaiah 64:6); not our fig leaves (Genesis 3:7); not our works (Ephesians 2:9)—but Christ's blood-bought righteousness. The trajectory from Eden to glory is complete: expelled naked and ashamed → clothed by God with animal skins → covered by sacrificial blood → clothed with Christ's righteousness by faith → glorified in white robes forever.
