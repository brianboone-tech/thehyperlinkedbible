### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10-14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- θέλημα (thelēma) - will
- ἁγιάζω (hagiazō) - to sanctify, make holy (perfect passive: "we have been sanctified")
- προσφορά (prosphora) - offering
- σῶμα (sōma) - body
- ἐφάπαξ (ephapax) - once for all
- τελειόω (teleioō) - to perfect, complete (perfect tense: "has perfected")
- ἁγιάζω (hagiazō) - those being sanctified (present passive participle)

**Context**: Hebrews 10:1-18 contrasts the Old Covenant's repeated, inadequate sacrifices with Christ's once-for-all, perfect sacrifice. Verses 10-14 are the climax.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10-14]] declares Christ's superiority over [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]'s animal skins and [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]'s animal blood. Animal skins covered temporarily; Christ's righteousness covers eternally. Animal blood was repeated endlessly (daily, yearly); Christ's sacrifice was "once for all" (ἐφάπαξ, ephapax, v. 10). Animal sacrifices "can never... perfect those who draw near" (10:1); Christ "by a single offering... has perfected for all time those who are being sanctified" (10:14).

The escalation:
- Animal skins ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]): Adequate but temporary
- Sacrificial blood (Leviticus): Repeated endlessly, proving inadequacy
- Christ's body offered "once for all" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10]]): Perfect, permanent, complete

"We have been sanctified" (ἡγιασμένοι ἐσμέν, hēgiasmenoi esmen, perfect passive)—completed action with ongoing results. Christ's one sacrifice accomplished what countless animal sacrifices could not.

**Application**: Rest in Christ's finished work. He has "perfected for all time those who are being sanctified" (10:14). You are not becoming righteous through repeated efforts (like repeated sacrifices); you are already righteous through Christ's one sacrifice. Live from this security, not toward it.
