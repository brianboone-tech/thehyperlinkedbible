### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 10|Isaiah 61:10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- שׂוֹשׂ (śôś) - to rejoice greatly (infinitive absolute + imperfect = emphatic)
- יְהוָה (YHWH) - the LORD
- לָבַשׁ (lāḇaš) - to clothe, put on
- בִּגְדֵי (biḡdê) - garments of
- יֶשַׁע (yešaʿ) - salvation, deliverance
- מְעִיל (məʿîl) - robe, cloak
- צְדָקָה (ṣəḏāqâ) - righteousness
- יָעָטַנִי (yəʿāṭanî) - He has covered me, wrapped me

**Context**: Isaiah 61 is a messianic prophecy describing the Anointed One's ministry (quoted by Jesus in Luke 4:18-21). Verse 10 is the prophet's (or Messiah's) joyful response to God's salvation.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 10|Isaiah 61:10]] prophetically anticipates what [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]] foreshadowed: **God clothing His people with righteousness**. "He has clothed me with the garments of salvation; he has covered me with the robe of righteousness."

The imagery shifts from literal skins ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]) to spiritual robes—righteousness itself as clothing. This is fulfilled in Christ: "Not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law, but that which comes through faith in Christ, the righteousness from God that depends on faith" ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 9|Philippians 3:9]]). [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 3|Zechariah 3:3-5]] enacts this: filthy garments removed, pure vestments provided by God.

The trajectory: Animal skins (literal covering, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]) → Robes of righteousness (prophetic promise, [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 10|Isaiah 61:10]]) → Christ's righteousness imputed ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 5|Romans 4:5-6]]) → White robes in glory ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 14|Revelation 7:14]]; 19:8).

**Application**: Believers have no righteousness of their own (Romans 3:10; Isaiah 64:6). But Christ clothes us with His righteousness. "As many of you as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ" (Galatians 3:27). We wear His robe, not our fig leaves.
