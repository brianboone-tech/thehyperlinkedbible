### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἁμαρτία (hamartia) - sin
- ποιέω (poieō) - to make
- γινώσκω (ginōskō) - to know (participle: "knowing")
- γινώμεθα (ginōmetha) - we might become (subjunctive)
- δικαιοσύνη (dikaiosynē) - righteousness
- θεός (theos) - God

**Context**: 2 Corinthians 5:14-21 describes the ministry of reconciliation. Verse 21 is the theological climax, explaining how God reconciles sinners to Himself.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]] is the NT fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]'s principle: **the great exchange**. "For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God."

Christ wore our filthy garments (sin); we receive His robe (righteousness). He took our covering of shame ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 7|Genesis 3:7]]'s fig leaves, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 10|Genesis 3:10]]'s guilt); we receive His covering of glory ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]'s pattern fulfilled perfectly). This is imputed righteousness: Christ's perfect obedience credited to our account by faith alone ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 5|Romans 4:5-6]], 22-24).

The trajectory: God clothed Adam/Eve with animal skins ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]]) → Sacrificial blood covered sin provisionally ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]) → Christ became sin for us; we become God's righteousness in Him ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]]) → We are clothed with Christ ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 27|Galatians 3:27]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 13#Romans 13 . 14|Romans 13:14]]).

**Application**: Do not trust in your own righteousness (fig leaves). Christ has given you His righteousness. You stand before God clothed in Christ's perfect obedience, not your failed attempts at goodness. "There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus" (Romans 8:1).
