### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 16|John 1:16-17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5485|G5485]] χάρις (*charis*) - grace
- [[Lexicon/G225|G225]] ἀλήθεια (*alētheia*) - truth
- [[Lexicon/G4138|G4138]] πλήρωμα (*plērōma*) - fullness
- [[Lexicon/G2983|G2983]] λαμβάνω (*lambanō*) - to receive

**Context**: "For from his fullness we have all received, grace upon grace. For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ." John declares that believers receive from Christ's inexhaustible fullness—grace in waves, one blessing after another. The law came through Moses, but grace and truth (the reality of what the law anticipated) came through Christ.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 30|Exodus 28:30]] - Urim (אוּרִים, "Lights") and Thummim (תֻּמִּים, "Perfections/Truth")
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]] - LORD abounding in חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת (steadfast love and faithfulness)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 85#Psalm 85 . 10|Psalm 85:10]] - "Steadfast love and faithfulness meet"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 30|Exodus 28:30]] - Urim (Lights) and Thummim (Perfections)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]] - God's self-revelation of grace and truth
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]] - "I am the way, the truth, and the life"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 7|Ephesians 1:7-8]] - Riches of grace lavished on us

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Forward-looking (priestly elements anticipate Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Mather connects [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 16|John 1:16-17]] to the Urim and Thummim: "Our Lights are from him... our Graces are from him. Grace instead of legal terror and rigour. Truth, that is, accomplishments and performances instead of shadows and promises." The Hebrew אוּרִים (Urim) means "lights," and תֻּמִּים (Thummim) means "perfections" or "truth." The LXX renders them δήλωσις καὶ ἀλήθεια ("manifestation and truth"). John's "grace and truth" (χάρις καὶ ἀλήθεια) may echo this, showing Christ as the fulfillment of what the Urim and Thummim symbolized. Where they provided partial light and limited revelation, Christ provides the fullness of grace upon grace. Where they gave answers to specific questions, Christ IS the answer—the full manifestation of God's character ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]]) in incarnate form. "Grace upon grace" (χάριν ἀντὶ χάριτος) suggests successive waves of blessing, each grace replaced by yet more grace from Christ's inexhaustible fullness.
