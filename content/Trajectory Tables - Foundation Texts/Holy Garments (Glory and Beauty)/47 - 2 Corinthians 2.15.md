### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 2#2 Corinthians 2 . 15|2 Corinthians 2:15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2175|G2175]] εὐωδία (*euōdia*) - fragrance, sweet aroma
- [[Lexicon/G2316|G2316]] θεός (*theos*) - God
- [[Lexicon/G4982|G4982]] σῴζω (*sōzō*) - to save

**Context**: "For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing." Paul describes believers as the fragrance of Christ, a pleasing aroma to God. The gospel ministry spreads like incense—a savour of life to those being saved, of death to those perishing.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 33|Exodus 28:33-35]] - Pomegranates on the high priest's robe (fragrant fruit)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 34|Exodus 30:34-38]] - Holy incense, a pleasing aroma before the LORD
- [[Readable Bible/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 4#Song of Solomon 4 . 13|Song of Solomon 4:13]] - Garden with pomegranates and pleasant fruits

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 33|Exodus 28:33-35]] - Pomegranates on the priest's robe
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 34|Exodus 30:34-38]] - Sweet incense in the tabernacle
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 4#Song of Solomon 4 . 13|Song of Solomon 4:13]] - Pomegranates and fragrant spices
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 2|Ephesians 5:2]] - Christ's sacrifice as fragrant offering
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 4#Philippians 4 . 18|Philippians 4:18]] - Gifts as fragrant offering acceptable to God

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Forward-looking (priestly garment element points to Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Mather connects the pomegranates on the high priest's robe to the sweet fruits of Christ's ministry: "The Pomegranate... seems to shadow forth the sweet refreshing fruits and effects of the voice of Christ, both in his Mediation with the Lord, and the Preaching of his Gospel in the Church." The pomegranate, bursting with juice and seeds, symbolizes fruitfulness and sweetness. As pomegranates adorned the high priest alongside the golden bells, so the sweet fruits of Christ's mediation accompany the sound of the gospel. Paul says "we are the aroma of Christ to God"—believers themselves become fragrant offerings, reflecting Christ's sacrifice which was "a fragrant offering" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 2|Ephesians 5:2]]). The priestly vestments anticipated this: where Aaron bore decorative pomegranates, believers bear actual fruit of Christ's Spirit, becoming a pleasing aroma before God.
