### [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 3|Colossians 2:3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G614|G614]] ἀπόκρυφος (*apokryphos*) - hidden, secret
- [[Lexicon/G2344|G2344]] θησαυρός (*thēsauros*) - treasure, storehouse
- [[Lexicon/G4678|G4678]] σοφία (*sophia*) - wisdom
- [[Lexicon/G1108|G1108]] γνῶσις (*gnōsis*) - knowledge

**Context**: "...in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge." Paul counters the Colossian heresy by declaring that Christ, not secret philosophies or angelic mediators, contains all wisdom and knowledge. What Israel sought through Urim and Thummim is found fully in Christ.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 30|Exodus 28:30]] - Urim and Thummim ("Lights and Perfections") for divine guidance
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 21|Numbers 27:21]] - Joshua to inquire of Urim before the LORD
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 28#1 Samuel 28 . 6|1 Samuel 28:6]] - Saul inquires but LORD does not answer
- [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 2#Ezra 2 . 63|Ezra 2:63]] - Wait until priest with Urim and Thummim arises

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 30|Exodus 28:30]] - Urim and Thummim for divine revelation
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 2#Proverbs 2 . 3|Proverbs 2:3-6]] - Treasure of wisdom from the LORD
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 24|1 Corinthians 1:24]], 30 - Christ the wisdom of God
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15-17]] - Christ as creator and sustainer

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Forward-looking (divinely prescribed means of revelation)

**Christological Connection**: Mather connects [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 3|Colossians 2:3]] to the Urim and Thummim: "All this pointed them to, and was most eminently fulfilled in Jesus Christ the only true High Priest, in and by whom alone God speaks his mind." The Urim ("Lights") and Thummim ("Perfections") provided divine guidance through the high priest—how they functioned is mysterious, but their purpose was clear: to reveal God's will. When the priestly line was corrupted, God ceased speaking through them ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 28#1 Samuel 28 . 6|1 Samuel 28:6]]), and post-exilic Israel awaited their restoration ([[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 2#Ezra 2 . 63|Ezra 2:63]]). Christ is the fulfillment: in Him are hidden ALL treasures of wisdom and knowledge—not partial guidance but complete revelation. The Urim gave limited answers to specific questions; Christ is the full revelation of God's mind and will. "Lights and Perfections" find their ultimate meaning in the One who is "the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]) and "the truth" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]).
