### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 33|Exodus 28:33-35]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7416|H7416]] רִמּוֹן (*rimmôn*) - pomegranate
- [[Lexicon/H6472|H6472]] פַּעֲמֹן (*paʿămōn*) - bell
- [[Lexicon/H2091|H2091]] זָהָב (*zāhāḇ*) - gold
- [[Lexicon/H8334|H8334]] שָׁרַת (*šāraṯ*) - to minister

**Context**: "On its hem you shall make pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet yarns, around its hem, with bells of gold between them, a golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate, around the hem of the robe. And it shall be on Aaron when he ministers, and its sound shall be heard when he goes into the Holy Place before the LORD, and when he comes out, so that he does not die."

- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 2#2 Corinthians 2 . 15|2 Corinthians 2:15]] - Sweet aroma of Christ (Mather: pomegranates = fruits)

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Forward-looking (divinely prescribed garment element)

**Christological Connection**: The golden bells and pomegranates on the high priest's robe hem carried rich symbolism. The bells sounded as Aaron ministered, signaling that the high priest was alive and active in the holy place—assurance that mediation was ongoing. Mather spiritualizes: "The Voice of Christ in his Prayers and Mediation for us... The Voice of Christ in the Gospel, which is heard of his Church, the sound thereof rings in his Church, yea throughout the world" (cf. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 18|Romans 10:18]]). The pomegranates, bursting with seeds and sweet juice, symbolize the fruitful effects of Christ's mediation—salvation, righteousness, and life. Paul writes "we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 2#2 Corinthians 2 . 15|2 Corinthians 2:15]])—the fragrant fruit of Christ's ministry. As Aaron's bells announced his living ministry, Christ's gospel sounds forth announcing His eternal intercession; as pomegranates adorned the robe with fruitfulness, believers display the fruit of Christ's work.
