### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1201-1300#H1254|H1254]] בָּרָא (bara) - to create, shape, form (always with God as subject; used of heaven and earth, individual humanity, new conditions)
- [[Lexicon/H8001-8100#H8064|H8064]] שָׁמַיִם (shamayim) - heavens, sky
- [[Lexicon/H701-800#H776|H776]] אֶרֶץ (erets) - earth, land
- [[Lexicon/H201-300#H216|H216]] אוֹר (or) - light

**Context**: Genesis 1:1-3 opens Scripture with God's sovereign creation of the heavens and earth ex nihilo. The earth begins in formless chaos (tohu wabohu), darkness covers the deep, and the Spirit hovers over the waters. Then God speaks the first creative fiat: "Let there be light," and light comes into existence. This establishes the pattern of creation by divine speech and the separation of light from darkness—foundational themes that reappear throughout Scripture's redemptive narrative.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 1|Isaiah 43:1-2]], 7, 14-21 - Isaiah uses creation language ("He who created you, O Jacob") to describe Israel's redemption, connecting creation and new creation
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17-18]] - Isaiah explicitly prophesies "new heavens and a new earth" using the same verb (bara) as [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]]
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 33#Psalm 33 . 6|Psalm 33:6]], 9 - "By the word of the LORD the heavens were made... For he spoke, and it came to be"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 104#Psalm 104 . 29|Psalm 104:29-30]] - God's Spirit gives life and renews creation

**Type Classification**: **Providential** (God sovereignly arranged creation as prefiguration) and **Forward-looking** (though not explicitly predictive in Genesis 1 itself, Isaiah later uses Genesis 1 creation language to prophesy eschatological new creation)

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the eternal Word through whom all things were created ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 3|John 1:3]]; [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 16|Colossians 1:16-17]]). The light that God spoke into existence in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]] prefigures Christ as "the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]) and the gospel illumination that shines "in the face of Jesus Christ" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]]). Just as God created the first heavens and earth, so He creates "new heavens and a new earth" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]]) through Christ's redemptive work. The original creation was "very good"; the new creation in Christ is glorified and eternal. This demonstrates Fairbairn's principle of escalation: the antitype (new creation) infinitely surpasses the type (original creation).
