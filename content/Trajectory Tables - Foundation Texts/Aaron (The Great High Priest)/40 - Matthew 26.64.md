### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 64|Matthew 26:64]]

**Context**: At His trial before the Sanhedrin, Jesus combines Psalm 110:1 with Daniel 7:13, claiming messianic status and the divine office. This declaration connects to Aaron's priesthood through Psalm 110:4, which assigns the office of "priest forever after the order of Melchizedek" to the one seated at God's right hand—a priesthood superior to Aaron's.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἀρχιερεύς (*archiereus*) - "high priest" - the office Aaron held, now judging the true High Priest
- δεξιῶν (*dexion*) - "right hand" - position of authority and priestly ministry (Psalm 110:1)
- δυνάμεως (*dynameos*) - "power" - the divine authority from which Christ exercises His priesthood
- ἐρχόμενον (*erchomenon*) - "coming" - the eschatological arrival of the great High Priest-King

**Christological Connection**: Jesus' trial testimony before Caiaphas the high priest reveals the supreme irony of redemptive history—the earthly high priest interrogates the eternal High Priest. When Jesus claims He will be "seated at the right hand of Power," He invokes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]], which in verse 4 declares this enthroned figure to be "a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek." The very position Jesus claims is the position from which He exercises a priesthood that supersedes Aaron's line. Caiaphas tears his robes in response ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 65|Matthew 26:65]]), unwittingly demonstrating that the Aaronic priesthood is being rent apart, making way for the superior priestly order Christ inaugurates. The one Aaron's office prefigured now stands before Aaron's successor, and the shadow confronts the substance.
