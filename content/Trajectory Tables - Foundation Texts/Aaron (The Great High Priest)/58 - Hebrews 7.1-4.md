### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 1|Hebrews 7:1-4]]

**Context**: The author provides detailed exposition of Genesis 14:17-20 to demonstrate Melchizedek's superiority to Levi: Abraham gave Melchizedek tithes, Melchizedek blessed Abraham ("the inferior is blessed by the superior"), and Melchizedek's priesthood preceded and transcends Mosaic law.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἱερεύς (*hiereus*) - "priest"
- βασιλεύς (*basileus*) - "king"
- εἰρήνη (*eirēnē*) - "peace" (Salem = peace)
- δικαιοσύνη (*dikaiosynē*) - "righteousness" (Melchizedek = king of righteousness)
- δεκάτη (*dekatē*) - "tithe, tenth"

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 17|Genesis 14:17-20]] - is the OT source being expounded
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - links this priesthood to Messiah
- **FROM OT**:
  - The Levitical priesthood (Exodus 28-29; Leviticus 8-9) is contrasted as inferior
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 11|Hebrews 7:11-28]] - continues arguing Christ's Melchizedekian priesthood surpasses Aaron's

**Christological Connection**: This exposition establishes Christ's priesthood as superior to Aaron's through three arguments: (1) Abraham tithed to Melchizedek, meaning Levi "in Abraham's loins" acknowledged Melchizedek's greater status (7:9-10); (2) Melchizedek blessed Abraham, and "without dispute the inferior is blessed by the superior" (7:7); (3) Melchizedek's priesthood operated independently of Mosaic law and tribal descent, pointing to a priesthood based on "the power of an indestructible life" (7:16). Christ fulfills this pre-Mosaic, superior priesthood: He is both "king of righteousness" and "king of peace" (7:2), titles that describe His saving work. The argument proves God's ultimate priestly plan always transcended the Aaronic system—a temporary shadow pointing to the eternal substance.
