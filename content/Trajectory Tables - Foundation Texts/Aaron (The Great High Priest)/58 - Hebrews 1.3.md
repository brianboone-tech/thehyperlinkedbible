### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]

**Context**: After "making purification for sins" (καθαρισμὸν τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν ποιησάμενος), Christ "sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high"—the first of five allusions to Psalm 110:1 in Hebrews. This establishes the book's central thesis: Christ's priestly work is completed and effective.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- καθαρισμός (*katharismos*) - "purification, cleansing"
- ἁμαρτία (*hamartia*) - "sin"
- ἐκάθισεν (*ekathisen*) - "he sat down" (aorist—completed action)
- δεξιά (*dexia*) - "right hand"
- μεγαλωσύνη (*megalōsynē*) - "majesty, greatness"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] provides the OT foundation for Christ's session at God's right hand
- **FROM OT**: Leviticus 16 describes Aaron's purification work that Christ fulfills and surpasses
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1]]; 10:12-13; 12:2 continue developing this theme of Christ's seated priesthood

**Christological Connection**: This verse is the theological engine of Hebrews' argument for Christ's superior priesthood. The contrast with Aaron is explicit and foundational: Aaron never sat because his work was never finished—he stood daily, offering the same sacrifices repeatedly ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]). Christ sat down because His sacrifice accomplished what Aaron's countless offerings never could. The purification language (καθαρισμός) connects directly to Levitical cleansing rituals, but Christ's purification is internal (cleansing conscience, [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]) not merely external. His session at "the right hand of the Majesty" fulfills [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] and positions Him to exercise eternal intercession ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]) from the place of supreme authority—something Aaron could never do.
