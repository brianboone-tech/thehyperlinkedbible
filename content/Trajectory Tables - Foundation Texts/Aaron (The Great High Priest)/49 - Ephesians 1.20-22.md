### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 20|Ephesians 1:20-22]]

**Context**: Paul combines Psalm 110:1 with Psalm 8:6, presenting Christ as the Last Adam at God's right hand. This enthronement positions Christ to minister as high priest—not merely over Israel, but over all creation, exercising the dominion Adam lost and the mediatorial authority Aaron's limited priesthood foreshadowed.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- δεξιᾷ (*dexia*) - "right hand" - position of supreme authority and priestly intercession
- ὑπέταξεν (*hypetaxen*) - "subjected" - all things placed under Christ's feet (Psalm 8:6)
- κεφαλὴν (*kephalen*) - "head" - Christ's headship over the church as priestly mediator
- ἐκκλησίας (*ekklesias*) - "church" - the new covenant community under Christ's high priesthood

**Christological Connection**: Paul's fusion of [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] and [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 6|Psalm 8:6]] reveals Christ as both the enthroned priest-king and the Last Adam who recovers what the first Adam lost. Where Adam was created to exercise dominion over creation as God's priestly representative ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]]; 2:15), he forfeited that role through sin. Aaron's priesthood provided mediation for fallen humanity, but Aaron himself was fallen—he needed atonement before he could offer it ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 6|Leviticus 16:6]]). Christ combines the roles: He is the perfect Last Adam who succeeds where the first failed ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 12|Romans 5:12-21]]; [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 45|1 Corinthians 15:45-49]]), and the great High Priest who mediates between God and humanity ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-6]]). His position "at the right hand" ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]) is the throne from which He exercises both Adamic dominion ("all things under his feet," [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 6|Psalm 8:6]]) and priestly intercession. The church, His body, participates in both dimensions—sharing His reign ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]]) and His priesthood ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]).
