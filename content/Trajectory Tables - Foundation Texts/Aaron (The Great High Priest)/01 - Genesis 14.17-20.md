### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 17|Genesis 14:17-20]]

**Context**: Melchizedek appears as king of Salem and "priest of God Most High" (כֹּהֵן לְאֵל עֶלְיוֹן), blessing Abraham and receiving tithes from him. This pre-Levitical priesthood operates independently of Aaronic succession, establishing that legitimate priesthood existed before Aaron and pointing to a superior priesthood that transcends tribal boundaries and mortality.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כֹּהֵן (*kohen*) - "priest"
- אֵל עֶלְיוֹן (*El Elyon*) - "God Most High"
- בָּרַךְ (*barak*) - "to bless"
- מַעֲשֵׂר (*ma'aser*) - "tithe, tenth part"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: This is the first mention of priesthood in Scripture, establishing the pattern
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] explicitly connects Messiah's eternal priesthood to Melchizedek's order
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 1|Hebrews 7:1-10]] provides extensive exposition demonstrating Melchizedek's superiority to Levitical priesthood

**Christological Connection**: Christ fulfills Melchizedek's priesthood as the eternal priest-king who combines royal and priestly offices. Unlike Aaron's priesthood which was temporary, tribal (Levitical), and mortal, Christ's priesthood after Melchizedek's order is eternal ("priest forever," [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 17|Hebrews 7:17]]), universal (not restricted to one tribe), and indestructible ("by the power of an indestructible life," [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 16|Hebrews 7:16]]). Abraham's act of giving tithes to Melchizedek demonstrates that even Levi, "still in the loins of his ancestor," acknowledged Melchizedek's superior priesthood ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 9|Hebrews 7:9-10]]), proving God's ultimate priestly plan transcends the Aaronic system.
