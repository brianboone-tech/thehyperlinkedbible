### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1]]

**Context**: The writer declares the "main point" (κεφάλαιον) of his entire argument: "We have such a high priest, one who is seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven." Christ surpasses Aaron in person (divine Son), position (heavenly throne), and performance (eternal salvation).

**Greek Key Terms**:
- κεφάλαιον (*kephalaion*) - "main point/sum" - the thesis that everything in Hebrews supports
- ἀρχιερέα (*archierea*) - "high priest" - the office Aaron held, Christ perfects
- ἐκάθισεν (*ekathisen*) - "sat down" - completed work, contrast Aaron's standing service (Hebrews 10:11)
- δεξιᾷ (*dexia*) - "right hand" - position of supreme authority and perpetual intercession

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1]] functions as the hinge of the entire epistle—everything before builds toward it, everything after flows from it. The "main point" (κεφάλαιον) is not merely that Christ is a high priest, but that He is "such a high priest"—one whose person, position, and performance infinitely transcend Aaron's. In person, Christ is the divine Son ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]), not a mortal descendant of Levi. In position, He is "seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven," not standing daily in an earthly tent ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]). In performance, He entered "the true tent that the Lord set up, not man" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 2|Hebrews 8:2]]), offering His own blood to secure eternal redemption ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]). Aaron's priesthood was "a copy and shadow of the heavenly things" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 5|Hebrews 8:5]]); Christ's priesthood is the heavenly reality. Aaron ministered in a tent Moses built according to the pattern shown on Sinai ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 40|Exodus 25:40]]); Christ ministers in the sanctuary that pattern was copying. The session at God's right hand declares His priestly work of atonement complete and His priestly ministry of intercession perpetual—"He always lives to make intercession" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]).
