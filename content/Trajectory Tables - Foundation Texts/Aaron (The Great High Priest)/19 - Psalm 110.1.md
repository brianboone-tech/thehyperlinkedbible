### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]

**Context**: The LORD (יהוה) addresses David's Lord (אֲדֹנִי), inviting Him to sit at His right hand until His enemies become His footstool. This messianic enthronement establishes the position from which Christ exercises His eternal priesthood—the most frequently cited OT text in the NT.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- יהוה (*YHWH*) - "the LORD" (God the Father)
- אֲדֹנִי (*Adonai*) - "my Lord" (the Messiah)
- יָמִין (*yamin*) - "right hand" (place of honor and authority)
- הֲדֹם (*hadom*) - "footstool" (symbol of complete subjugation)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 17|Genesis 14:17-20]] - establishes pre-Aaronic priesthood
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - connects this enthroned one to that priestly order
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - declares this enthroned Lord "a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 64|Matthew 26:64]]
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 34|Acts 2:34-35]]
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]] - , 13; 8:1; 10:12-13; 12:2 all cite this verse for Christ's session

**Christological Connection**: Jesus claimed this verse at His trial ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 64|Matthew 26:64]]), identifying Himself as the enthroned Lord who would sit at God's right hand. Peter at Pentecost argued this verse applies to Jesus, not David, proving His resurrection and exaltation ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 34|Acts 2:34-35]]). Hebrews uses [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] five times to establish Christ's finished priestly work: unlike Aaron who stood daily because his work was never complete, Christ "sat down at the right hand of God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 12|Hebrews 10:12]]) because His single sacrifice accomplished eternal redemption. The seated position proves the superiority of Christ's priesthood—His session signals completed atonement and ongoing intercession from the place of supreme authority.
