### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 16|Hebrews 10:16-17]]

**Context**: The writer's second citation of Jeremiah 31:33-34 emphasizes the result of Christ's high priestly work—complete forgiveness making additional sacrifices unnecessary. Where Aaron's repeated offerings demonstrated their inadequacy (Hebrews 10:1-4), Christ's once-for-all sacrifice produces total remission: "I will remember their sins...no more."

**Greek Key Terms**:
- διαθήκη (*diatheke*) - "covenant" - the new covenant Christ mediates (Hebrews 9:15)
- καρδίας (*kardias*) - "hearts" - inward transformation impossible under Aaron's external rituals
- ἁμαρτιῶν (*hamartion*) - "sins" - what Aaron covered annually, Christ removed eternally
- μνησθήσομαι (*mnesthsomai*) - "I will remember" - God's complete forgetting of sin (contrast Leviticus 16:21)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 16|Hebrews 10:16-17]]'s repetition of [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33-34]] (previously cited in [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 8|Hebrews 8:8-12]]) underscores the revolutionary transformation Christ's priesthood accomplishes. Under Aaron's ministry, sins required annual acknowledgment on the Day of Atonement—the high priest confessed "all the iniquities of the people of Israel, and all their transgressions, all their sins" ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 21|Leviticus 16:21]]), transferring them symbolically to the scapegoat. This yearly ritual proved that "it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 4|Hebrews 10:4]]); the sacrifices provided covering, not cleansing. But Christ's superior priesthood in the superior covenant produces what Aaron's could not: "I will remember their sins and their lawless deeds no more" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 17|Hebrews 10:17]]). Divine amnesia! The perfect tense "I will remember...no more" indicates permanent, irreversible forgetting. The immediate conclusion: "Where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer any offering for sin" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 18|Hebrews 10:18]]). Aaron's priesthood is obsolete not because it failed on its own terms, but because Christ's priesthood succeeded on ultimate terms—securing eternal redemption ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]), perfecting the conscience ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]), and guaranteeing that God remembers sins no more.
