### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- μονογενής (*monogenēs*) - "only begotten" - Unique, one-of-a-kind Son
- ἀγαπάω (*agapaō*) - "to love" - Self-sacrificial, covenant love
- δίδωμι (*didōmi*) - "to give" - To hand over, sacrifice
- ζωή αἰώνιος (*zōē aiōnios*) - "eternal life" - The life of the age to come

**Context**: Jesus explains to Nicodemus the heart of the gospel: God's love for the world is demonstrated by giving His only Son so that whoever believes in Him will have eternal life. The language deliberately echoes Genesis 22:2 ('your son, your only son, whom you love'), showing Jesus as the ultimate beloved Son offered for sacrifice.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]] presents Christ as the ultimate fulfillment of the Isaac type - the beloved only Son whom the Father gives up for sacrifice. The escalation is dramatic: Abraham's faith was tested by the command to sacrifice Isaac, but Isaac was spared and a ram substituted. God the Father actually gives His Son, and no substitute is provided - Christ is both the Isaac (beloved son) and the ram (substitutionary sacrifice). The language 'God so loved the world' universalizes the promise: whereas Isaac's birth blessed Abraham's family and (through promise) all nations, Christ's death and resurrection actually accomplish salvation for believers from every nation. Believers receive 'eternal life' - the ultimate escalation of the promise of 'seed.' Just as Isaac's birth seemed impossible to Abraham and Sarah, so eternal life seems impossible to sinners, but God provides what He promises through Christ.
