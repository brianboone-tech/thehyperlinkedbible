### [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5-6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3056|λουτρόν]] (*loutron*) - "washing, bathing"
- [[Lexicon/G3801-3900#G3824|παλιγγενεσία]] (*palingenesia*) - "regeneration, new birth, renewal"
- [[Lexicon/G0301-0400#G0342|ἀνακαίνωσις]] (*anakainōsis*) - "renewal, renovation"
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4151|πνεῦμα]] (*pneuma*) - "Spirit"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0040|ἅγιος]] (*hagios*) - "holy"
- [[Lexicon/G1501-1600#G1632|ἐκχέω]] (*ekcheō*) - "to pour out, shed forth"
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4145|πλουσίως]] (*plousiōs*) - "richly, abundantly"
- [[Lexicon/G4901-5000#G4990|σωτήρ]] (*sōtēr*) - "Savior"

**Context**: Paul instructs Titus regarding God's salvation, emphasizing its gracious nature. Salvation doesn't come through righteous works we've done but through God's mercy, manifested in "the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit." This washing isn't water baptism itself but the spiritual reality baptism signifies—the Holy Spirit's cleansing and transforming work.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] (clean water, new heart, new spirit), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 51#Psalms 51 . 10|Psalm 51:10]] (create in me a clean heart)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 18|Exodus 30:18-20]] (bronze basin for washing), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 14#Leviticus 14 . 8|Leviticus 14:8]] (washing for purification)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 5|John 3:5]] (born of water and Spirit), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]] (cleansed by washing of water with word), [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 11|1 Corinthians 6:11]] (washed, sanctified, justified)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5-6]]'s "washing of regeneration" fulfills all Old Testament purification rituals. Where the bronze basin provided water for priestly cleansing ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 18|Exodus 30:18-20]]), Christ provides the Holy Spirit for spiritual cleansing. The phrase "through Jesus Christ our Savior" identifies Christ as the mediator through whom the Spirit comes. Christ's death and resurrection made the Spirit's outpouring possible—only after His glorification did the Spirit come in new covenant fullness ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]]). The "regeneration" (palingenesia) Jesus promised ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 19#Matthew 19 . 28|Matthew 19:28]]) begins in individual conversion and culminates in cosmic renewal. The Spirit's "renewal" continues what regeneration begins—progressive transformation into Christ's image ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]). Where Levitical washings were repeated daily, the Spirit's regenerating work is once-for-all, never requiring repetition. Where Old Testament washings cleansed externally, the Spirit cleanses internally—conscience, heart, affections. Paul connects this washing to Ezekiel's prophecy: God promised to "sprinkle clean water" and give "a new heart" and "a new spirit" ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]])—precisely what the Holy Spirit accomplishes in regeneration. The Spirit's cleansing flows from Christ's blood: "how much more will the blood of Christ... purify our conscience from dead works to serve the living God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]). The "richly poured out" Spirit is Christ's gift to His church, purchased by His blood, ensuring perpetual cleansing for all who believe. What ceremonial washings could never accomplish—true heart purification—Christ achieves through the Spirit's regenerating, renewing work.
