### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0037|ἁγιάζω]] (*hagiazō*) - "to sanctify, consecrate, make holy"
- [[Lexicon/G2401-2500#G2511|καθαρίζω]] (*katharizō*) - "to cleanse, purify, make clean"
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3067|λουτρόν]] (*loutron*) - "washing, bathing"
- [[Lexicon/G5201-5300#G5204|ὕδωρ]] (*hydōr*) - "water"
- [[Lexicon/G4401-4500#G4487|ῥῆμα]] (*rhēma*) - "word, saying, utterance"

**Context**: Paul uses Christ's love for the church to model husbands' love for wives. Christ's sanctifying work includes cleansing the church "by the washing of water with the word." This washing refers to baptism accompanied by the proclamation of the gospel—the visible sign joined to the audible word, together signifying and sealing the Spirit's cleansing work.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]] (I will sprinkle clean water), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 18|Exodus 30:18-20]] (bronze basin for washing)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119#Psalms 119 . 9|Psalm 119:9]] (How can young man keep his way pure? By guarding according to your word)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 3|John 15:3]] (you are already clean because of the word), [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]] (washing of regeneration), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 17|John 17:17]] (sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]]'s "washing of water with the word" fulfills Ezekiel's prophecy of spiritual cleansing. Where the bronze basin provided water for priestly purification ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 18|Exodus 30:18-20]]), Christ provides the Holy Spirit's cleansing applied through baptism and the word. The phrase "with the word" (en rhēmati) identifies the instrument—the gospel creates faith, and baptism visibly signifies the believer's union with Christ in His death and resurrection ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 3|Romans 6:3-4]]). Jesus earlier declared, "Already you are clean because of the word that I have spoken to you" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 3|John 15:3]]), showing the word's cleansing power. The word doesn't work magically but as the Spirit applies it, creating faith and transforming hearts. Paul describes God's saving work as "the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]]), paralleling this passage. The washing isn't water baptism per se but the Spirit's work baptism represents. Christ's purpose—"that he might present the church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle or any such thing, that she might be holy and without blemish" (v. 27)—shows cleansing's goal: perfect holiness. This echoes the Levitical requirement that priests be without blemish ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 21#Leviticus 21 . 17|Leviticus 21:17-21]]) and sacrifices without defect ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 22#Leviticus 22 . 20|Leviticus 22:20-21]]). Christ's cleansing accomplishes what Levitical washings symbolized—true, complete, permanent purification. The church's ultimate presentation "in splendor, without spot or wrinkle" fulfills Revelation's vision: "Let us rejoice and exult and give him the glory, for the marriage of the Lamb has come, and his Bride has made herself ready; it was granted her to clothe herself with fine linen, bright and pure" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 7|Revelation 19:7-8]]). What Levitical washings could never achieve, Christ accomplishes through His word and Spirit—a bride perfectly cleansed, spotlessly holy, fit for eternal union with the Lamb.
