### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2101-2200#H2236|זָרַק]] (*zāraq*) - "to sprinkle, scatter, toss"
- [[Lexicon/H4401-4500#H4325|מַיִם]] (*mayim*) - "water"
- [[Lexicon/H2901-3000#H2889|טָהוֹר]] (*ṭāhôr*) - "clean, pure"
- [[Lexicon/H2901-3000#H2891|טָהַר]] (*ṭāhar*) - "to be clean, be pure" (Qal perfect)
- [[Lexicon/H2801-2900#H2932|טֻמְאָה]] (*ṭumʾâ*) - "uncleanness, defilement"
- [[Lexicon/H1401-1500#H1544|גִּלּוּלִים]] (*gillûlîm*) - "idols, detestable things"

**Context**: Speaking through Ezekiel to exiled Israel, God promises a future cleansing that transcends ceremonial purification. This prophecy follows the valley of dry bones vision (Ezekiel 37), both depicting Israel's restoration. God promises to sprinkle clean water on His people, cleansing them from all uncleannesses and idols—not merely external ritual but internal spiritual transformation.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 14#Leviticus 14 . 7|Leviticus 14:7]] (ceremonial sprinkling), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 18|Numbers 19:18-19]] (water of purification)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]] (new heart and Spirit), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]] (fountain opened for sin and uncleanness)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]] (washing of regeneration), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 22|Hebrews 10:22]] (bodies washed with pure water), [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 2|1 Peter 1:2]] (sprinkling of Jesus Christ's blood)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]]'s promise of sprinkling with clean water finds fulfillment in Christ's provision of spiritual cleansing. What Levitical sprinkling accomplished ceremonially and temporarily, Christ's death accomplishes spiritually and eternally. Paul explicitly connects this prophecy to Christian conversion: God saved us "by the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit, whom he poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Savior" ([[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5-6]]). The prophesied cleansing from "all your uncleannesses" is realized through Christ's blood: "the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin" ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 7|1 John 1:7]]). The promise to cleanse from "all your idols" is fulfilled as the Spirit applies Christ's work, turning hearts from false worship to true: "you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God" ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 1#1 Thessalonians 1 . 9|1 Thessalonians 1:9]]). Hebrews connects Ezekiel's promise to Christian assurance: "let us draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith, with our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 22|Hebrews 10:22]]). The "pure water" is the Holy Spirit's cleansing work applied through faith in Christ. Peter describes believers as "elect... for obedience to Jesus Christ and for sprinkling with his blood" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 2|1 Peter 1:2]]), directly fulfilling Ezekiel's prophecy. Where ceremonial sprinkling cleansed from external defilement, Christ's blood sprinkles conscience, removing guilt's paralyzing power. The sequence Ezekiel prophesies—sprinkling (v. 25), new heart (v. 26), Spirit indwelling (v. 27)—describes Christian conversion: cleansing through Christ's blood, regeneration by the Spirit, ongoing sanctification through the Spirit's presence. What God promised through Ezekiel, He accomplished through Christ.
