### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3057|λόγχη]] (*lonchē*) - "spear, lance"
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4125|πλευρά]] (*pleura*) - "side, rib"
- [[Lexicon/G3501-3600#G3572|νύσσω]] (*nyssō*) - "to pierce, prick, stab"
- [[Lexicon/G2101-2200#G2117|εὐθύς]] (*euthys*) - "immediately, at once"

- [[Lexicon/G5201-5300#G5204|ὕδωρ]] (*hydōr*) - "water"

**Context**: After Jesus died on the cross, a Roman soldier pierced His side with a spear to confirm death. John records the extraordinary detail: "at once there came out blood and water." This wasn't physiological trivia but theological testimony—Jesus is the true source of purification, fulfilling all Levitical cleansing rituals that required both blood and water.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 14#Leviticus 14 . 6|Leviticus 14:6-7]] (blood and water for cleansing leper), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 9|Numbers 19:9]] (water for purification mixed with ashes)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 22#Psalms 22 . 16|Psalm 22:16]] (they have pierced my hands and feet), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]] (they shall look on him whom they have pierced)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 6|1 John 5:6-8]] (came by water and blood), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 13|Hebrews 9:13-14]] (blood of Christ purifies conscience), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 7|Revelation 1:7]] (every eye will see him, even those who pierced him)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]'s blood and water flowing from Christ's pierced side is the New Testament's clearest fulfillment of Levitical purification typology. Where the leper's cleansing required blood and water applied separately ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 14#Leviticus 14 . 6|Leviticus 14:6-7]]), Christ's body provides both simultaneously. The blood accomplishes atonement—satisfying God's justice, removing sin's penalty. The water accomplishes cleansing—purifying conscience, removing sin's pollution. Together they provide complete salvation. John emphasizes this in his epistle: "This is he who came by water and blood—Jesus Christ; not by the water only but by the water and the blood. And the Spirit is the one who testifies, because the Spirit is the truth" ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 6|1 John 5:6-7]]). The threefold witness—Spirit, water, blood—all testify to Christ's saving work. The blood declares, "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]])—atonement accomplished. The water declares believers cleansed—"having our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 22|Hebrews 10:22]]). The bronze basin provided water for priestly service; Christ's pierced side provides cleansing enabling all believers to serve as "a royal priesthood" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]). Zechariah prophesied a fountain opened for cleansing: "On that day there shall be a fountain opened for the house of David and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, to cleanse them from sin and uncleanness" ([[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]]). Christ's pierced side is that fountain, flowing with blood and water, providing complete, perpetual purification for all who come. What the Levitical system required but couldn't fully provide, Christ's death supplies abundantly—cleansing from all sin, access to God's presence, transformation by the Spirit.
