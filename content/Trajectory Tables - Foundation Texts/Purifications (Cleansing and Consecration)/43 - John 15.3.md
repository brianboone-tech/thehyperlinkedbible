### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 3|John 15:3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2201-2300#G2235|ἤδη]] (*ēdē*) - "already, now"
- [[Lexicon/G5101-5200#G5210|ὑμεῖς]] (*hymeis*) - "you" (emphatic pronoun)
- [[Lexicon/G2401-2500#G2513|καθαρός]] (*katharos*) - "clean, pure"
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3056|λόγος]] (*logos*) - "word, message"
- [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2980|λαλέω]] (*laleō*) - "to speak, utter, tell"

**Context**: During the upper room discourse before His crucifixion, Jesus uses the vine-and-branches metaphor to teach about abiding in Him. Before developing the metaphor, He declares the disciples already clean—not through their own effort but through the word He has spoken to them. This cleansing precedes and enables fruitful abiding.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 119#Psalms 119 . 9|Psalm 119:9]] (How can young man keep way pure? By guarding according to your word)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]] (I will sprinkle clean water on you), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 51#Psalms 51 . 7|Psalm 51:7]] (purge me with hyssop)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]] (cleansed by washing of water with the word), [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 22|1 Peter 1:22-23]] (purified souls through the word), [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 21|James 1:21]] (receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 3|John 15:3]]'s declaration—"you are already clean because of the word that I have spoken to you"—reveals Christ as the ultimate purifying agent. Where Levitical washings used water to cleanse ceremonially, Christ uses His word to cleanse spiritually. The word creates faith, and faith unites believers to Christ, who is the source of all cleansing. Jesus earlier promised, "If you abide in my word, you are truly my disciples, and you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 31|John 8:31-32]]). This freedom includes liberation from sin's defilement. Peter later describes this cleansing: "Having purified your souls by your obedience to the truth for a sincere brotherly love, love one another earnestly from a pure heart, since you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and abiding word of God" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 22|1 Peter 1:22-23]]). The "word of God" is the instrument of new birth and soul purification. Paul similarly describes the church being "sanctified, having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|Ephesians 5:26]])—baptism's water joined to the gospel's word. The word accomplishes what Levitical washings could never do: "For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 12|Hebrews 4:12]]). Christ's word penetrates to the heart, exposing sin, creating faith, transforming affections. The disciples' cleansing through Jesus's word prepared them for the greater cleansing coming—the Holy Spirit's outpouring after Christ's ascension. Jesus promised, "the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 26|John 14:26]]). The Spirit continues the word's cleansing work, applying Christ's teaching to believers' hearts.
