### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 9|John 17:9-11]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2065|G2065]] ἐρωτάω (*erōtaō*) - "to ask, request, pray"
- [[Lexicon/G1325|G1325]] δίδωμι (*didōmi*) - "to give" (those given to Christ)
- [[Lexicon/G3686|G3686]] ὄνομα (*onoma*) - "name"
- [[Lexicon/G5083|G5083]] τηρέω (*tēreō*) - "to keep, guard, preserve"
- [[Lexicon/G40|G40]] ἅγιος (*hagios*) - "holy"

**Context**: John 17 records Jesus' high priestly prayer before His arrest. Verses 9-11 focus on His intercession for the disciples (distinguished from the world). Jesus prays specifically for "those whom you have given me" (v. 9), asking the Father to "keep them in your name" (v. 11). The language echoes priestly bearing and protection.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Alludes to [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 9|Exodus 28:9-12]], 29 (high priest bearing Israel's names before the LORD)
- Develops the Good Shepherd imagery from Ezekiel 34 (knowing sheep by name)
- Fulfills the intercessory role of the high priest on the Day of Atonement

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Exodus 28:12, 29 (bearing names); Ezekiel 34:11-16 (shepherd seeking sheep); Isaiah 53:12 (intercession for transgressors)
- **FROM OT**:
  - None (fulfillment)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 3|John 10:3]] - , 14 (calls sheep by name, knows His own
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]] - always lives to intercede
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 34|Romans 8:34]] - Christ intercedes
**Type Classification**: Fulfillment (Christ is the true High Priest bearing His people's names before the Father).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 9|John 17:9-11]] is the direct NT fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 12|Exodus 28:12]]. Aaron bore Israel's twelve tribal names on onyx stones; Christ bears individual names of the elect given Him by the Father. The comparison reveals escalation: Aaron's bearing was external (stones on garment); Christ's is internal (He knows His own intimately, [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 14|John 10:14]]). Aaron bore a nation; Christ bears "those whom you have given me" from every nation. Aaron's memorial was annual (Day of Atonement); Christ's intercession is perpetual ("always lives," [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]). The ephod stones were engraved permanently, symbolizing God's unchanging covenant with Israel; Christ's knowledge of His elect is eternal—"I give them eternal life, and they will never perish" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 28|John 10:28]]). The shoulder bearing (strength) finds fulfillment in Christ's omnipotent preservation: "Holy Father, keep them in your name" (v. 11).
