### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 5|Exodus 29:5]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H0899|H0899]] בֶּגֶד (*beḡeḏ*) - "garment, clothing"
- [[Lexicon/H0646|H0646]] אֵפוֹד (*'ēp̄ôḏ*) - "ephod"
- [[Lexicon/H04598|H04598]] מְעִיל (*mə'îl*) - "robe" (robe of the ephod)
- [[Lexicon/H02805|H02805]] חֵשֶׁב (*ḥēšeḇ*) - "skillfully woven band"

**Context**: Exodus 29 prescribes the seven-day consecration ceremony for Aaron and his sons. The garments are put on Aaron in specific sequence (v. 5): tunic, then robe of the ephod, then ephod itself, then breastpiece, with the woven waistband securing the ephod. This order emphasizes the ephod's centrality to priestly identity—Aaron cannot function as high priest without wearing the garment that bears Israel's names before the LORD.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 7|Leviticus 8:7]] records Moses executing this command at Aaron's actual consecration
- The ephod becomes inseparable from high priestly office throughout OT
- When the ephod is mentioned, legitimate priesthood is implied ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 28|1 Samuel 2:28]]; 14:3; 22:18)
- Illegitimate ephods ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 8#Judges 8 . 27|Judges 8:27]]; 17:5) demonstrate corruption when divorced from Aaronic priesthood

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded ceremony) and Forward-Looking (Aaron's consecration points to Christ's eternal appointment).

**Christological Connection**: Aaron's investiture with the ephod at consecration points to Christ's appointment as eternal High Priest. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 5|Hebrews 5:5]] declares: "So also Christ did not exalt himself to be made a high priest, but was appointed by him who said to him, 'You are my Son, today I have begotten you.'" Aaron received the ephod from Moses; Christ receives His priesthood directly from the Father. Aaron's consecration lasted seven days; Christ's priesthood is "forever, after the order of Melchizedek" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 6|Hebrews 5:6]]). The ephod's investiture signified Aaron beginning to bear Israel; Christ's appointment means He eternally bears His people before God.
