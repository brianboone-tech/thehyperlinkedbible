### [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 23#1 Samuel 23 . 9|1 Samuel 23:9-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H0646|H0646]] אֵפוֹד (*'ēp̄ôḏ*) - "ephod"
- [[Lexicon/H05066|H05066]] נָגַשׁ (*nāḡaš*) - "bring near, approach"
- [[Lexicon/H03068|H03068]] יְהוָה (*YHWH*) - "LORD" (covenant name of God)

**Context**: David learns that Saul is plotting against him in Keilah. He calls for Abiathar the priest, who had fled to David with the ephod after Saul massacred the priests at Nob (1 Samuel 22:20-23). David commands: "Bring the ephod here" (v. 9), then inquires of the LORD twice (vv. 10-11, 12), receiving clear answers both times that enable him to escape Saul's trap.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Associates the ephod with seeking divine guidance and revelation
- Demonstrates proper use of ephod—through legitimate Aaronic priest (Abiathar son of Ahimelech)
- Contrasts with Gideon's ephod ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 8#Judges 8 . 27|Judges 8:27]])—David seeks the LORD through proper channels
- The ephod here functions alongside Urim and Thummim for divine inquiry (implied by "inquired of the LORD")
- Foreshadows Christ as the One who perfectly reveals the Father's will

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely authorized means of inquiry) and Forward-Looking (points to perfect revelation through Christ).

**Christological Connection**: The ephod as instrument of divine guidance foreshadows Christ as the perfect revelation of the Father's will. Where David inquired through the ephod via Abiathar, believers have direct access to the Father through Christ the High Priest. Jesus declares, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]). The ephod mediated limited revelation (yes/no answers); Christ reveals the Father fully ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 18|John 1:18]]). David needed the ephod brought to him; Christ is Emmanuel—God with us always ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 23|Matthew 1:23]]; 28:20). The ephod's revelatory function pointed to the One in whom "are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 3|Colossians 2:3]]).
