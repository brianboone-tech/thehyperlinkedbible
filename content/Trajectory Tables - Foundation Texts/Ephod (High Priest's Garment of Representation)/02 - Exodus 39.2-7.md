### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 39#Exodus 39 . 2|Exodus 39:2-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H0646|H0646]] אֵפוֹד (*'ēp̄ôḏ*) - "ephod"
- [[Lexicon/H07718|H07718]] שֹׁהַם (*šōham*) - "onyx"
- [[Lexicon/H02146|H02146]] זִכָּרוֹן (*ziḵārôn*) - "memorial, remembrance"
- [[Lexicon/H01121|H01121]] בֵּן (*bēn*) - "son" (sons of Israel)

**Context**: Exodus 39 describes the actual construction of the priestly garments by Bezalel and the skilled craftsmen. This chapter parallels Exodus 28 (divine command) with fulfillment language: "as the LORD commanded Moses" appears repeatedly (vv. 1, 5, 7, 21, 26, 29, 31, 42, 43). The ephod's construction (vv. 2-7) exactly follows the pattern given in 28:6-12, with special emphasis on the memorial stones (v. 7).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Demonstrates faithful obedience—divine pattern → human execution
- The repetition of "stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel" (v. 7) emphasizes this function
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 39#Exodus 39 . 43|Exodus 39:43]] records Moses blessing the completed work, echoing God's blessing of creation ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]]; 2:3)
- The ephod now exists as physical reality, ready for Aaron's consecration ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 7|Leviticus 8:7]])

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded) and Forward-Looking (the careful execution of pattern points to perfect heavenly reality).

**Christological Connection**: The move from divine pattern (Exodus 28) to faithful execution (Exodus 39) prefigures Christ's perfect fulfillment of the Father's will. Where Bezalel executed Moses' instructions, Christ perfectly accomplishes the Father's redemptive plan. The ephod constructed according to pattern points to Christ as the true High Priest who perfectly fulfills God's design for bearing His people before Him. The "stones of remembrance" fabricated in Exodus 39 were used temporarily; Christ's eternal intercession is the reality they foreshadowed.
