### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1085|G1085]] γένος (*genos*) - "race, kind, offspring"
- [[Lexicon/G1588|G1588]] ἐκλεκτός (*eklektos*) - "chosen, elect"
- [[Lexicon/G934|G934]] βασίλειος (*basileios*) - "royal"
- [[Lexicon/G2406|G2406]] ἱεράτευμα (*hierateuma*) - "priesthood"
- [[Lexicon/G40|G40]] ἅγιος (*hagios*) - "holy"

**Context**: 1 Peter 2:4-10 applies OT temple and priesthood imagery to the church. Verse 9 uses four titles from Exodus 19:5-6 and Isaiah 43:20-21 to describe believers' corporate identity: chosen race, royal priesthood, holy nation, people for God's possession. The purpose clause explains: "that you may proclaim the excellencies of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Directly quotes/alludes to [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]] (kingdom of priests and holy nation)
- Incorporates [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 20|Isaiah 43:20-21]] (my chosen people, formed for myself to proclaim my praise)
- Applies Israel's corporate priestly vocation to the church

**Connections**:

**Type Classification**: Application (believers participate in Christ's priesthood corporately).

**Christological Connection**: The ephod bearing Israel's names before God points to Christ bearing His people, and by extension, to the church's corporate priestly identity. Aaron wore the ephod as representative for Israel; Christ bears His church as the true High Priest; the church, united to Christ, becomes a "royal priesthood" sharing in His mediatorial work. The progression is clear: Aaron bore twelve tribes → Christ bears the elect from every nation → the church corporately bears witness to the nations. The ephod's function was memorial (keeping Israel before God); the church's function is proclamation (declaring God's excellencies to the world). Where Aaron's priesthood was exclusive (only Levites), Christ's priesthood brings an inclusive sharing (all believers are priests). The shoulder stones on the ephod symbolized strength; the church's priestly witness is empowered by union with Christ, the true bearer of His people.
