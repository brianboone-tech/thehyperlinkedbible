### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4982|G4982]] σῴζω (*sōzō*) - "to save"
- [[Lexicon/G3838|G3838]] παντελής (*pantelēs*) - "complete, perfect, to the uttermost"
- [[Lexicon/G1793|G1793]] ἐντυγχάνω (*entynchanō*) - "to intercede, make intercession"
- [[Lexicon/G3842|G3842]] πάντοτε (*pantote*) - "always, at all times"
- [[Lexicon/G2198|G2198]] ζάω (*zaō*) - "to live"

**Context**: Hebrews 7 establishes Christ's superiority over the Levitical priesthood by comparing Him to Melchizedek. Verse 25 forms the climactic conclusion: Christ's eternal priesthood (vv. 23-24) results in perfect salvation because "he always lives to make intercession for them." The contrast is stark—Levitical priests died and needed successors; Christ lives forever.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 12|Exodus 28:12]] (Aaron bearing names before the LORD)
- Develops [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] (You are a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek)
- Fulfills the pattern of high priestly intercession on the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16)

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Exodus 28:12 (Aaron bears names as memorial); Leviticus 16:11-17 (high priest's intercessory work); Psalm 110:4 (eternal priesthood); Isaiah 53:12 (made intercession for transgressors)
- **FROM OT**:
  - None (fulfillment, not anticipation)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]] - Christ entered heaven to appear in God's presence for us
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 34|Romans 8:34]] - Christ intercedes for us
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 2#1 John 2 . 1|1 John 2:1]] - we have an advocate with the Father
**Type Classification**: Fulfillment (Christ is the antitype to Aaron's type).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]] is the direct fulfillment of the ephod's typology. Aaron bore Israel's names on stones on his shoulders "as a memorial before the LORD" ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 12|Exodus 28:12]]). This was symbolic, temporary, and limited. Christ's intercession is substantial (He personally knows and presents His elect), eternal (He "always lives"), and effectual (He "is able to save to the uttermost"). The ephod's shoulder stones represented strength; Christ bears His people in the omnipotent strength of His divine-human person. Aaron entered the earthly Most Holy Place yearly; Christ entered heaven itself, "now to appear in the presence of God on our behalf" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]]). The memorial function of the ephod—keeping Israel's names before God—is perfectly fulfilled in Christ's ceaseless intercession, presenting His own to the Father based on His once-for-all sacrifice.
