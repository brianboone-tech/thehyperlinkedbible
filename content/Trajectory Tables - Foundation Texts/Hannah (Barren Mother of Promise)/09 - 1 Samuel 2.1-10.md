### [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 1|1 Samuel 2:1-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7311|H7311]] רוּם (rûm) - "exalted" - my horn is exalted
- [[Lexicon/H3444|H3444]] יְשׁוּעָה (yəšûʿâ) - "salvation" - I rejoice in Your salvation
- [[Lexicon/H6135|H6135]] עֲקָרָה (ʿăqārâ) - "barren" - the barren gives birth to seven
- [[Lexicon/H1800|H1800]] דַּל (dal) - "poor" - He raises the poor from the dust
- [[Lexicon/H34|H34]] אֶבְיוֹן (ʾeḇyôn) - "needy" - lifts the needy from the ash heap
- [[Lexicon/H4899|H4899]] מָשִׁיחַ (māšîaḥ) - "anointed" - exalt the horn of His anointed

**Context**: Hannah's song transcends her personal situation to prophesy cosmic reversal and the coming King. This is the first occurrence in Scripture of "anointed" (מָשִׁיחַ, Messiah) applied to Israel's king—remarkable since Israel has no king yet. Hannah sees beyond Samuel to David, and beyond David to great David's greater Son.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Hannah's song introduces messianic royal language
- Psalm 113 echoes Hannah: "He raises the poor from the dust"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 1|Isaiah 54:1]] applies the barren mother image to Zion
- [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 46|Luke 1:46-55]] (Mary's Magnificat) systematically echoes Hannah's song

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 1#1 Samuel 1 . 10|1 Samuel 1:10-20]] - Hannah's prayer answered
- **FROM OT**: The messianic hope develops through David (2 Samuel 7), the Psalms, and prophets
- **FROM NT**: Mary's Magnificat ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 46|Luke 1:46-55]]) is structured after Hannah's song

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (explicitly prophesies the anointed King) and Providential (the barren mother pattern as vehicle for prophecy)

**Christological Connection**: Hannah's song is the first to speak of "His anointed" (מְשִׁיחוֹ)—the Messiah. Mary's Magnificat shows she understood: her Son is the anointed One Hannah anticipated. The reversals Hannah celebrated find ultimate fulfillment in Christ: He became poor that we might become rich ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 8#2 Corinthians 8 . 9|2 Corinthians 8:9]]); He humbled Himself and was exalted ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 6|Philippians 2:6-11]]); He brings down the proud and lifts up the humble ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 51|Luke 1:51-53]]). Hannah's barren womb produced the anointer of kings; Mary's virgin womb produced the King of kings.
