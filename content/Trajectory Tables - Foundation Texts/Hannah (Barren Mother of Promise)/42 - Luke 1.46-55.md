### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 46|Luke 1:46-55]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3170|G3170]] μεγαλύνω (megalynō) - "magnify/extol" - my soul magnifies the Lord
- [[Lexicon/G21|G21]] ἀγαλλιάω (agalliaō) - "rejoice greatly" - my spirit rejoices
- [[Lexicon/G4990|G4990]] σωτήρ (sōtēr) - "Savior" - God my Savior
- [[Lexicon/G5014|G5014]] ταπείνωσις (tapeinōsis) - "lowly estate" - the lowly estate of His servant
- [[Lexicon/G1415|G1415]] δυνατός (dynatos) - "mighty" - the Mighty One
- [[Lexicon/G5244|G5244]] ὑπερήφανος (hyperēphanos) - "proud" - scattered the proud

**Context**: Mary visits Elizabeth; the baby John leaps in Elizabeth's womb; Elizabeth pronounces blessing; Mary responds with the Magnificat. This song deliberately echoes Hannah's song (1 Samuel 2:1-10) throughout. Mary stands in the barren mother tradition as its climax—the virgin through whom the ultimate Child of Promise comes.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 1|1 Samuel 2:1-10]] - Hannah's song (the primary source)
- Psalm 113 - Praises God who raises the poor and gives children to the barren
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 1|Isaiah 54:1]] - The barren rejoices over many children
- [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 46|Luke 1:46-55]] - Mary's Magnificat fulfills the tradition

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 1|1 Samuel 2:1-10]] - The structural and thematic source
- **FROM OT**: The entire barren mother tradition
- **FROM NT**: Mary's song anticipates Jesus' teaching on reversal (Beatitudes, parables)

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment (Mary as the climax of the barren mother tradition)

**Christological Connection**: Mary's Magnificat celebrates Christ before His birth as the fulfillment of God's promises to Abraham (1:55). The barren mother tradition finds its goal in the virgin birth: the ultimate impossibility (conception without a father) produces the ultimate Child (the Son of God). Hannah's "anointed" ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 10|1 Samuel 2:10]]) is now identified: He is the one Mary carries. The reversals Hannah prophesied—the proud scattered, the humble exalted—are accomplished in Christ who "emptied Himself" ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 7|Philippians 2:7]]) and was "highly exalted" (2:9). Mary, the ultimate barren mother, bears the Savior of the world.
