### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]]

**Context**: On Mount Sinai, after giving the Ten Commandments and the Book of the Covenant, God instructs Moses to build a sanctuary "so that I may dwell among them." The tabernacle's design must follow precisely "the pattern" (תַּבְנִית, tabnit) shown on the mountain. This establishes that the earthly sanctuary, including the ark, is not human design but divine revelation—a copy of heavenly realities. The ark, described first among the furnishings (vv. 10-22), would be the focal point where God's presence dwelt.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מִקְדָּשׁ (*miqdash*) - "sanctuary" (holy place set apart for God)
- וְשָׁכַנְתִּי (*veshakhanti*) - "that I may dwell" (from שָׁכַן, shakan, the root of Shekinah glory)
- תַּבְנִית (*tabnit*) - "pattern/model" (divinely revealed blueprint)
- בְּתוֹכָם (*betokham*) - "among them" (God's presence in the midst)

**Christological Connection**: The command to build a sanctuary "so that I may dwell among them" finds ultimate fulfillment in the incarnation. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] deliberately echoes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]], using σκηνόω ("to tabernacle") to describe Christ's dwelling among us. Where the ark represented God's throne-presence in the tabernacle, Christ is "the image of the invisible God" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]])—God dwelling bodily among His people. The "pattern" shown to Moses was shadow; Christ is substance. Now, through the Spirit, believers themselves become God's temple ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]), and in the consummation, "the dwelling place of God is with man" forever ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]).
