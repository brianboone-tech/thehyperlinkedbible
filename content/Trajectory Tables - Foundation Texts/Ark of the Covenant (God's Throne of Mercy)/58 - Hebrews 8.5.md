### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 5|Hebrews 8:5]]

**Context**: Hebrews 8 argues that Christ's heavenly ministry surpasses the earthly Levitical priesthood. Verse 5 explains why: earthly priests "serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things," quoting Exodus 25:40 where Moses was told to build according to "the pattern shown you on the mountain." The earthly sanctuary—including the ark—was derivative, not ultimate. Christ ministers in "the true tent" (8:2), the heavenly reality of which Moses saw only the shadow.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ὑποδείγματι (*hypodeigmati*) - "copy/example" (derived from an original)
- σκιᾷ (*skia*) - "shadow" (outline, not substance)
- τῶν ἐπουρανίων (*tōn epouraniōn*) - "of the heavenly things" (celestial realities)
- τύπον (*typon*) - "pattern/type" (model shown to Moses)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 5|Hebrews 8:5]] provides the interpretive key for all tabernacle/ark typology: the earthly was "copy and shadow." The ark on which blood was sprinkled was copy; the heavenly throne where Christ offered His blood is original. The mercy seat where atonement was made annually was shadow; Christ is "the propitiation (ἱλαστήριον) for our sins" ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 2#1 John 2 . 2|1 John 2:2]]). This verse establishes escalation: copy → original, shadow → substance, earthly → heavenly. Christ's priesthood does not merely improve upon Aaron's—it operates in an entirely different and superior realm. The earthly ark pointed upward; Christ's sacrifice was offered in heaven itself.
