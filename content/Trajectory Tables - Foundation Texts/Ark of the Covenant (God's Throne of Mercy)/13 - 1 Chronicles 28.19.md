### [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 28#1 Chronicles 28 . 19|1 Chronicles 28:19]]

**Context**: David, having assembled the leaders of Israel, gives Solomon the detailed plans for the temple. Remarkably, David claims the pattern came "by the hand of the LORD upon me"—divine revelation comparable to Moses receiving the tabernacle pattern on Sinai. The plans include the Most Holy Place where the ark would reside and the "chariot of the cherubim" (v. 18) overshadowing the mercy seat. This establishes continuity: tabernacle → temple, both divinely designed, both housing the ark as God's throne.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- הַכֹּל בִּכְתָב (*hakkol bikhtav*) - "all this in writing" (documented divine revelation)
- מִיַּד יְהוָה עָלַי (*miyad YHWH 'alay*) - "by the hand of the LORD upon me" (prophetic inspiration)
- הִשְׂכִּיל (*hiskil*) - "gave understanding" (hiphil, caused to comprehend)
- מַלְאֲכוֹת הַתַּבְנִית (*mal'akhot hattavnit*) - "works of the pattern" (specific design details)

**Christological Connection**: The pattern principle bridges tabernacle and temple, establishing that both were divinely designed copies of heavenly realities. What Moses saw on Sinai, David received "by the hand of the LORD"—the same heavenly pattern, adapted for permanent dwelling. This continuity points forward: tabernacle → temple → Christ. If earthly sanctuaries housing the ark were copies of heaven, Christ is "the exact imprint of [God's] nature" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]), not copy but original. The ark dwelt in the Most Holy Place of both tabernacle and temple; Christ sits at God's right hand in the heavenly sanctuary itself. David's Spirit-given plans for the ark's resting place anticipated the eternal resting place: Christ enthroned in the true Holy of Holies.
