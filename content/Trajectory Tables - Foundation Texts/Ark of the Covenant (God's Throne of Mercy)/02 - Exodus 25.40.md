### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 40|Exodus 25:40]]

**Context**: After detailing the construction of the lampstand, God reiterates the command that Moses must make everything "according to the pattern shown you on the mountain." This verse is quoted in Hebrews 8:5 as proof that the earthly tabernacle was merely a copy of heavenly realities. The ark, mercy seat, table, and lampstand are not arbitrary designs but reflections of heavenly archetypes. Moses saw something on the mountain—the heavenly sanctuary—and was to replicate it on earth.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- וּרְאֵה (*ure'eh*) - "see to it, behold" (imperative, emphasizing careful attention)
- וַעֲשֵׂה (*va'aseh*) - "and make" (imperative, precise execution required)
- בְּתַבְנִיתָם (*betavnitam*) - "according to their pattern" (heavenly model)
- מָרְאֶה (*mor'eh*) - "shown" (hophil participle, caused to see by God)

**Christological Connection**: The pattern principle establishes a hermeneutical key: the earthly sanctuary system was always derivative, pointing to heavenly realities now revealed in Christ. Hebrews argues that Christ ministers in "the true tent" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 2|Hebrews 8:2]]), the heavenly sanctuary of which Moses' tabernacle was mere shadow. The earthly ark/mercy seat was a copy; Christ is the original. The pattern shown on Sinai anticipated the reality now disclosed: Christ's blood offered at the heavenly throne. The earthly was temporary and repeated; the heavenly is permanent and once-for-all. What Moses saw in shadow, we see fulfilled in Christ "the exact imprint of [God's] nature" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]).
