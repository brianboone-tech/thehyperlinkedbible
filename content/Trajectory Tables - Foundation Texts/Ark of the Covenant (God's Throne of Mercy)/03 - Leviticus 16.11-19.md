### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 11|Leviticus 16:11-19]]

**Context**: This passage describes the heart of the Day of Atonement ritual. After slaughtering the bull for his own sin offering, Aaron takes burning coals and incense behind the veil, then sprinkles the bull's blood on and before the mercy seat. He repeats this with the goat's blood for the people's sins. The blood on the mercy seat makes atonement for the Most Holy Place itself, purifying it from Israel's uncleanness. This is the only time annually that the high priest enters behind the veil to the ark.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כַּפֹּרֶת (*kapporet*) - "mercy seat" (from כָּפַר, to cover/atone; the place of propitiation)
- וְהִזָּה (*vehizzah*) - "and he shall sprinkle" (hiphil, ritual application of blood)
- לְכַפֵּר (*lekapper*) - "to make atonement" (piel infinitive, intensive covering of sin)
- מִטֻּמְאֹת (*mittum'ot*) - "from the uncleanness" (defilement requiring purification)

**Christological Connection**: The Day of Atonement ritual reveals Christ's work in miniature. The earthly high priest entered with animal blood; Christ entered "once for all into the holy places... by means of his own blood" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]). The earthly mercy seat was sprinkled annually; Christ's blood was offered once "to put away sin" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26]]). Paul identifies Christ Himself as the ἱλαστήριον ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 25|Romans 3:25]])—the mercy seat where God's wrath is propitiated. Where Aaron entered trembling behind the veil, Christ "appeared in the presence of God on our behalf" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]]). The annual repetition proved the ritual's insufficiency ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 1|Hebrews 10:1-4]]); Christ's once-for-all sacrifice proves its efficacy. The earthly ark with its blood-sprinkled mercy seat was shadow; Christ is the reality where God's justice and mercy kiss.
