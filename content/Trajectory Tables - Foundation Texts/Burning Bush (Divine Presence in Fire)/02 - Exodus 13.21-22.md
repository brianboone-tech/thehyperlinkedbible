### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21-22]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5982|H5982]] עַמּוּד (ʿammûḏ) - "pillar" - column of cloud/fire
- [[Lexicon/H6051|H6051]] עָנָן (ʿānān) - "cloud" - by day
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (ʾēš) - "fire" - by night
- [[Lexicon/H5148|H5148]] נָחָה (nāḥâ) - "lead/guide" - directing the journey
- [[Lexicon/H215|H215]] אוֹר (ʾôr) - "light" - the fire gave light at night

**Context**: As Israel departs Egypt, God provides visible guidance: a pillar of cloud by day and pillar of fire by night. This continuous presence enabled travel "by day or by night" (13:21). The pillar never departed from before the people (13:22). This institutionalizes the burning bush experience for the entire nation throughout their wilderness journey.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]] - The burning bush was the personal experience; the pillar extends it nationally
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 19|Exodus 14:19-20]] - The pillar protects Israel from Egypt at the Red Sea
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 38|Exodus 40:38]] - The pillar rested on the tabernacle "throughout all their journeys"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 5|Isaiah 4:5]] - Prophesies future pillar of cloud/fire over restored Zion

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]] - The burning bush initiated the fire-presence theme
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 5|Isaiah 4:5]] applies the pillar imagery to eschatological restoration
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 1|1 Corinthians 10:1-2]] - "Our fathers were all under the cloud... baptized into Moses in the cloud"

**Type Classification**: Direct (divinely provided), Forward-Looking - The pillar pointed to Christ's continuous presence with His people and the Spirit's guidance

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the light of the world ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]])—the true pillar of fire illuminating spiritual darkness. He guides His people through the wilderness of this world to the heavenly Canaan. The pillar never departed; Christ promises "I am with you always" (Matt 28:20). What the pillar symbolized (divine presence, guidance, protection), Christ accomplishes permanently. The same presence that made the burning bush burn without consuming now indwells believers by the Holy Spirit, guiding through every wilderness trial.
