### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 2|Isaiah 43:2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5674|H5674]] עָבַר (ʿāḇar) - "pass through" - crossing through water and fire
- [[Lexicon/H4325|H4325]] מַיִם (mayim) - "waters" - first element of trial
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (ʾēš) - "fire" - second element of trial
- [[Lexicon/H3857|H3857]] לָהַט (lāhaṭ) - "scorch/burn" - what will NOT happen
- [[Lexicon/H7857|H7857]] שָׁטַף (šāṭap̄) - "overflow" - what rivers will NOT do
- [[Lexicon/H3680|H3680]] כָּבָה (kāḇâ) - "extinguish" - the fire will not consume

**Context**: In the context of new exodus promises, God declares to Israel: "When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and when you pass through the rivers, they will not sweep over you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be burned; the flames will not set you ablaze." This prophetic promise extends the burning bush principle to all covenant trials—God's preserving presence in every fire.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]] - The burning bush was not consumed
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 21|Exodus 14:21-22]] - Israel passed through the Red Sea waters
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 27|Daniel 3:27]] - The three passed through fire unburned
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 2|Isaiah 43:2]] draws these together as paradigm for all God's people in all trials

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]] - The burning bush promise extended to future experience
- **FROM OT**: Daniel 3's fiery furnace literally fulfills this promise
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 4#1 Peter 4 . 12|1 Peter 4:12]] - The "fiery ordeal" refines but does not destroy believers

**Type Classification**: Prophetic application of the burning bush principle to eschatological deliverance

**Christological Connection**: Christ passed through the "waters" of death and the "fire" of God's judgment—and emerged victorious in resurrection. His passage through these ultimate trials guarantees that His people will survive their lesser trials. The promise "I will be with you" is ultimately fulfilled in Emmanuel, God with us. Christ is the presence that makes fire preserving rather than destroying. Because He absorbed the consuming fire on the cross, believers pass through trials as refinement, not destruction.
