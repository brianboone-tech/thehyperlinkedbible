### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 28|Hebrews 12:28-29]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G932|G932]] βασιλεία (basileia) - "kingdom" - unshakable kingdom received
- [[Lexicon/G761|G761]] ἀσάλευτος (asaleutos) - "unshakable" - cannot be shaken
- [[Lexicon/G2999|G2999]] λατρεύω (latreuō) - "worship/serve" - acceptable service
- [[Lexicon/G2124|G2124]] εὐλάβεια (eulabeia) - "reverence/godly fear" - attitude in worship
- [[Lexicon/G1262|G1262]] δέος (deos) - "awe" - accompanying reverence
- [[Lexicon/G2654|G2654]] καταναλίσκω (katanaliskō) - "consume" - God is consuming fire

**Context**: Hebrews 12 contrasts the terror of Mount Sinai with the grace of Mount Zion. Believers have come not to the blazing mountain that made Israel beg for silence, but to the heavenly Jerusalem, to Jesus the mediator. Yet the conclusion is sobering: "Our God is a consuming fire." The same God who appeared in fire at Sinai is approached through Christ.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]] - Fire that does not consume (the bush)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 24|Deuteronomy 4:24]] - "The LORD your God is a consuming fire" (first occurrence)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 9#Deuteronomy 9 . 3|Deuteronomy 9:3]] - "The LORD your God is a consuming fire who will destroy them"
- [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 29|Hebrews 12:29]] quotes Deuteronomy to conclude the Sinai/Zion contrast

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]] - The burning bush introduced fire as divine presence
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 24|Deuteronomy 4:24]] declared God is consuming fire; Hebrews applies it
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 26|Hebrews 10:26-31]] - Fire awaits those who reject Christ; 12:28-29 applies to worship

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - The consuming fire is now the object of worship for those in Christ

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the One through whom believers approach the consuming fire unharmed. He bore the fire of God's judgment on the cross so that believers might dwell with the consuming fire in safety. The burning bush showed fire that does not destroy; Christ shows why—the destruction fell on Him. In the new creation, believers will dwell eternally with the consuming fire because Christ has made them fireproof through His sacrifice. What the bush symbolized (divine presence in fire that does not destroy) becomes eternal reality through Christ.
