### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 1|Exodus 3:1-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5572|H5572]] סְנֶה (seneh) - "bush/thornbush" - the specific shrub
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (ʾēš) - "fire" - divine manifestation
- [[Lexicon/H398|H398]] אָכַל (ʾāḵal) - "consume/eat" - the fire did NOT consume
- [[Lexicon/H4397|H4397]] מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה (malʾāḵ YHWH) - "Angel of the LORD" - theophany
- [[Lexicon/H6944|H6944]] קֹדֶשׁ (qōḏeš) - "holy" - the ground became holy
- [[Lexicon/H430|H430]] אֱלֹהִים (ʾĕlōhîm) - "God" - who called from the bush

**Context**: Moses, a fugitive shepherd in Midian for 40 years, encounters God at "Horeb, the mountain of God." The Angel of the LORD appears in a flame of fire within a bush. The bush burns but is not consumed—a paradox that captures Moses' attention. When he approaches, God calls him by name and identifies Himself as "the God of your fathers." This theophany commissions Moses to deliver Israel from Egypt.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The pillar of fire (Exod 13:21-22) extends the burning bush principle to the nation
- Sinai ablaze with fire (Exod 19:18) shows God's presence in fire at national scale
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 24|Deuteronomy 4:24]] interprets: "The LORD your God is a consuming fire"
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 16|Deuteronomy 33:16]] references "him who dwelt in the burning bush" in Moses' blessing

**Connections**:
- **TO**: This is the foundational type—God present in fire, preserving what should be consumed
- **FROM OT**: The pillar of fire, Sinai theophany, and Daniel 3's fiery furnace develop the theme
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 30|Acts 7:30-35]] (Stephen's exposition), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 32|Matthew 22:32]] (Jesus' use for resurrection proof)

**Type Classification**: Providential, Forward-Looking - The pattern explicitly applies to the Church's experience of persecution with divine preservation (1 Peter 4:12-14)

**Christological Connection**: The Angel of the LORD in the bush is a pre-incarnate appearance of Christ—He who would later dwell bodily among His people ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). Christ is the divine presence that transforms fire from destroyer to refiner. He passed through the fire of God's wrath on the cross (the bush truly burning) so that His people might pass through trials unharmed. The One who dwelt in the bush now dwells in His Church by His Spirit, and wherever He dwells, the fire preserves rather than destroys.
