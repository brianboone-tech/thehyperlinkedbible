**Context**: Hebrews 10:1-18 concludes the author's demonstration that Christ's sacrifice is superior to the Old Covenant sacrificial system. Verses 19-25 transition to application: "Therefore, brothers..." Based on Christ's accomplished work, believers now have confident access to God's presence. The passage contrasts the Old Covenant restrictions (only high priest, once per year, through animal blood) with New Covenant access (all believers, continually, through Christ's blood).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-22]] directly reverses [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]] and fulfills the Tree of Life typology:

1. **The Way Barred → The Way Opened**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]] describes cherubim and a flaming sword "to guard the way [דֶּרֶךְ dereḵ] to the tree of life." [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 20|Hebrews 10:20]] declares Christ has opened "a new and living way [ὁδὸν πρόσφατον καὶ ζῶσαν]" through the veil. The "way" to life, barred since the fall, is reopened through Christ's death. The cherubim no longer bar access; they are passed through. The flaming sword of judgment fell on Christ, not on us.

2. **The Veil = Christ's Flesh**: The author identifies the veil (καταπέτασμα) with Christ's flesh (σάρξ): "through the curtain, that is, his flesh" (διὰ τοῦ καταπετάσματος, τοῦτ' ἔστιν τῆς σαρκὸς αὐτοῦ). When the veil was torn at Christ's death ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 51|Matthew 27:51]]), it symbolized His body torn in crucifixion. The barrier separating humanity from God (the veil) was removed by Christ's sacrificial death. His torn flesh opens the way to the presence of God—the antitype to the Tree of Life.

3. **Blood of Jesus = Access to Life**: Verse 19 specifies access is "by the blood of Jesus" (ἐν τῷ αἵματι Ἰησοῦ). Just as the high priest entered the Most Holy Place with atoning blood ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 15|Leviticus 16:15]]), Christ entered with His own blood ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12]]), securing eternal redemption. The blood satisfies God's justice, cleanses our conscience (v. 22), and grants us access to the life-giving presence of God. The Tree of Life mediated life; Christ's blood mediates eternal life.

4. **From One Priest Once a Year → All Believers Continually**: Old Covenant access was restricted to one man (high priest), one day per year (Day of Atonement), with repeated sacrifices. New Covenant access is for all believers ("we have confidence," ἔχομεν παρρησίαν), at all times ("let us draw near," present tense exhortation to continuous action), based on one perfect sacrifice ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10-14]]). The escalation is complete: from limited to universal access; from annual to perpetual; from external ritual to heart transformation.

5. **Cleansed Hearts = Internal Purity**: Verse 22 describes "hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water." This alludes to the priestly consecration rituals ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 4|Exodus 29:4]]; [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 6|Leviticus 8:6]]) and the sprinkling of blood on the Day of Atonement ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 14|Leviticus 16:14-15]]). But the New Covenant cleansing is internal: hearts, not merely bodies; conscience, not merely ritual purity. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] promised God would sprinkle clean water and give a new heart and Spirit. Hebrews applies this: believers are cleansed inwardly, fitted to enter God's presence.

6. **The Escalation from Tree of Life to Throne of Grace**:
   - **Eden**: Tree of Life in garden; cherubim bar the way after sin ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 9|Genesis 2:9]]; 3:24)
   - **Tabernacle/Temple**: Most Holy Place with ark (mercy seat) between cherubim; veil bars access except for high priest once per year (Exodus 25-26; Leviticus 16)
   - **Christ's Death**: Veil torn; way opened through His flesh and blood ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 51|Matthew 27:51]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-20]])
   - **New Covenant**: All believers have confidence to enter; continuous access to throne of grace ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 16|Hebrews 4:16]]; 10:19-22)
   - **New Creation**: Tree of Life freely accessible; God and the Lamb are the temple; no veil; nations healed ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]; 22:2, 14)

**Quote (John Owen)**: "The veil was the greatest obstruction to our access unto God. This was removed by the rending of it... And this was done in the death of Christ, when the veil of the temple was rent."

**Application**: Believers should appropriate the confidence Christ has purchased. We are invited—commanded—to "draw near" (προσερχώμεθα). This is not presumption but faith. We come not based on our worthiness but Christ's work. We approach the throne of grace with confidence (Hebrews 4:16), enter the holy places by His blood (10:19), and have access to the Father through the Spirit (Ephesians 2:18). This is the gospel: the way to life, barred in Genesis 3:24, is opened in Christ.

The Tree of Life trajectory culminates: Genesis 2:9 (tree offers life) → Genesis 3:24 (way barred) → Leviticus 16 (limited access through blood) → Matthew 27:51 (veil torn) → Hebrews 10:19-22 (way opened through Christ's flesh) → Revelation 22:2 (tree freely accessible in new creation). Christ is the fulfillment and substance of every type.
