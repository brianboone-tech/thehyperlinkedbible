### [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15-20]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- εἰκών (*eikōn*) - "image" - exact representation, visible manifestation (1:15; cf. LXX Genesis 1:26 צֶלֶם)
- πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*) - "firstborn" - supreme rank, preeminence (1:15, 18)
- πλήρωμα (*plērōma*) - "fullness" - totality of divine attributes (1:19)
- ἀποκαταλλάξαι (*apokatallaxai*) - "to reconcile" - restore broken relationship (1:20, 22)

**Context**: Early Colossian hymn (likely pre-Pauline, adapted by Paul) celebrating Christ's cosmic supremacy. Follows thanksgiving (1:3-8) and prayer for knowledge (1:9-14); precedes warning against false teaching emphasizing angelic intermediaries (2:8-23).

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the ultimate Image-Bearer who fulfills [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]] perfectly. **Escalation**: Adam bore image as created being; Christ **is** image as eternal Son ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]: "radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature"). Adam's image was damaged by sin; Christ's image is perfect and unchanging. Adam was given dominion over creation ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]]); Christ is "firstborn of all creation" (1:15) with absolute supremacy - "all things were created through him and for him" (1:16). Adam's disobedience brought alienation; Christ's blood "reconciled" all things (1:20). The trajectory shows movement from **type** (Adam as image-bearer pointing forward) to **antitype** (Christ as perfect Image). Through union with Christ, believers are being "renewed in knowledge after the image of [their] creator" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 10|Colossians 3:10]]) - progressive restoration of Adamic glory. Final goal: "we shall bear the image of the man of heaven" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 49|1 Corinthians 15:49]]). From damaged image → to perfect Image → to glorified image-community.
