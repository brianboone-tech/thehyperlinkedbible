### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- צֶלֶם (*tselem*) - "image" - physical representation, statue (cf. ANE kings placing images in distant territories)
- דְּמוּת (*demut*) - "likeness" - similarity, resemblance (safeguards against crude identification)
- כָּבַשׁ (*kavash*) - "subdue" - royal conquest language, exercising dominion
- רָדָה (*radah*) - "have dominion" - rule, govern (kingly authority)

**Context**: Climax of creation week; sixth day after all other creatures created. Divine council language ("Let us make") suggests trinitarian consultation. Humanity created last as crown of creation.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1-2]] - Spirit hovering over waters - divine presence initiating creation
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 1|Genesis 5:1-3]] - image transmitted to Seth despite Fall
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 6|Genesis 9:6]] - murder forbidden because of image
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 3|Psalm 8:3-8]] - humans crowned with glory and honor
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 45|1 Corinthians 15:45-49]] - first Adam vs. last Adam
  - [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]] - Christ as image of invisible God
  - [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 3#James 3 . 9|James 3:9]] - humans made in likeness of God
**Christological Connection**: Christ is "the image of the invisible God" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]) - what Adam was called to be but failed to achieve. Where first Adam distorted the image through sin, last Adam perfectly reflects the Father: "He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). The trajectory moves from Adam as type (image-bearer) to Christ as antitype (perfect Image). Believers are now being "conformed to the image of his Son" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]), progressively restored to original glory. Final escalation occurs at resurrection when "we shall bear the image of the man of heaven" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 49|1 Corinthians 15:49]]) - from damaged image → to perfect Image → to corporate body of glorified image-bearers. The commission to "fill the earth" finds fulfillment in Great Commission to make disciples of all nations ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18-20]]).
