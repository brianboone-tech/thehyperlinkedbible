### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 1|Genesis 3:1-24]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- עָרוּם (*arum*) - "crafty, shrewd" - serpent's deceptive wisdom (wordplay with עֵירֹם *eyrom* "naked" in 2:25)
- תַּאֲוָה (*ta'avah*) - "desire" - Eve's threefold attraction to forbidden fruit (3:6)
- קוֹל (*qol*) - "sound, voice" - God walking in garden (3:8), seeking broken fellowship
- אֵיבָה (*eyvah*) - "enmity" - cosmic conflict between serpent's seed and woman's seed (3:15)

**Context**: Immediately follows creation account and placement in Eden. Serpent appears without introduction, suggesting prior fall (cf. Ezekiel 28:13-17; Revelation 12:9). Woman created as helper (Genesis 2:18-25); now both tempted and fall together.

  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 21|1 Corinthians 15:21-22]] - in Adam all die
**Christological Connection**: Where first Adam failed in garden-sanctuary, last Adam succeeds. Christ faces temptation in wilderness ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]]) and Gethsemane garden ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 36|Matthew 26:36-46]]), but unlike Adam, He obeys perfectly. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]'s promise of serpent-crusher finds fulfillment in Christ, who through death destroys "the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14]]). The trajectory shows **escalation through reversal**: Adam's disobedience → Christ's obedience; Adam's hiding from God → Christ's face-to-face fellowship with Father; Adam expelled from garden → Christ opens paradise to repentant thief ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 43|Luke 23:43]]). [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 19|Romans 5:19]]: "as by the one man's disobedience the many were made sinners, so by the one man's obedience the many will be made righteous." Image damaged in Adam is fully restored in Christ, the perfect Image who enables our transformation "from one degree of glory to another" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]).
