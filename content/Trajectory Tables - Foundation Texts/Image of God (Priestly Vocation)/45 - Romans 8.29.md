### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- προέγνω (*proegnō*) - "foreknew" - intimate covenantal knowledge, not mere intellectual awareness (8:29)
- προώρισεν (*proōrisen*) - "predestined" - determined beforehand according to divine purpose (8:29)
- συμμόρφους (*symmorphous*) - "conformed to" - sharing same form/shape (8:29)
- εἰκόνος (*eikonos*) - "image" - exact representation (8:29; cf. Genesis 1:26 צֶלֶם)
- πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*) - "firstborn" - preeminence, priority (8:29)

**Context**: Golden chain of salvation (Romans 8:28-30). Follows assurance that "all things work together for good" (8:28); precedes link from foreknowledge to glorification (8:30). Part of larger section on life in the Spirit and future glory (8:1-39).

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the perfect Image to which all believers are being conformed. Where Adam bore God's image but fell, Christ **is** God's image eternally ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). Believer's transformation into Christ's image is **progressive** now ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]: "being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another") and will be **complete** at resurrection ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 2|1 John 3:2]]: "when he appears we shall be like him"). **Escalation**: Adam's image-bearing was created and mutable; Christ's is eternal and immutable. Adam was appointed to represent God; Christ represents God perfectly as "the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). Adam's failure corrupted image in all descendants; Christ's success restores image in all united to Him. The trajectory reveals God's eternal purpose: from damaged image in Adam → to perfect Image in Christ → to corporate body of image-bearers conformed to Christ. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]] places this within Trinitarian framework: Father foreknows and predestines; Spirit transforms ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]); goal is conformity to Son. Believers become πρωτότοκος "firstborn" (plural) by union with **the** πρωτότοκος (singular), sharing His inheritance and glory ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 17|Romans 8:17]]).
