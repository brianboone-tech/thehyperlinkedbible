### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-5]] (Context for v. 5)

**Note**: This file provides context for Revelation 21:5. The full analysis is in the separate file for verse 5.

See: [[66 - Revelation 21.5|Revelation 21:5 - Full Analysis]]

**Context Summary**: John sees a new heaven and new earth, the first having passed away. The holy city, new Jerusalem, descends from heaven. God dwells with His people, wiping away every tear. No more death, mourning, crying, or pain—the former things have passed away. Then God declares from His throne: "Behold, I am making all things new" (v. 5), commanding John to write these faithful and true words.

This consummates the new moon theme—what monthly renewal symbolized (fresh start, renewed commitment, temporal reset) finds eternal fulfillment when God renews all creation permanently.
