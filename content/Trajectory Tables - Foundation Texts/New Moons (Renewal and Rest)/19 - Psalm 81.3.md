### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 81#Psalm 81 . 3|Psalm 81:3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8601-8700#H8628|תִּקְעוּ]] (*tiqʿû*) - "blow, sound" (trumpet)
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2320|בַּחֹדֶשׁ]] (*baḥōdeš*) - "at the new moon"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7782|שׁוֹפָר]] (*šôpār*) - "ram's horn, trumpet"
- [[Lexicon/H3501-3600#H3677|בַּכֵּסֶה]] (*bakkēseh*) - "at the full moon" (or "at the covering/concealment")
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2282|לְיוֹם חַגֵּנוּ]] (*ləyôm ḥāgēnû*) - "on our feast day"

**Context**: The psalmist calls for trumpet blasts at new moon and full moon on Israel's feast day. This liturgical instruction links lunar cycles to worship, using trumpet to announce monthly and festival celebrations. The new moon marked time for joyful assembly, renewed covenant commitment, and fresh worship.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 10|Numbers 10:10]] (blow trumpets on appointed feasts and new moons), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28#Numbers 28 . 11|Numbers 28:11-15]] (new moon offerings), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 24|Leviticus 23:24]] (seventh month new moon)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 5#2 Chronicles 5 . 13|2 Chronicles 5:13]] (trumpeters and singers praised LORD), [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 33|Nehemiah 10:33]] (new moons and appointed feasts)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16-17]] (new moon is shadow), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22-24]] (come to festal gathering)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 81#Psalm 81 . 3|Psalm 81:3]]'s new moon trumpet finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ who provides perpetual renewal and eternal feast. The monthly trumpet gathered Israel temporarily; Christ gathers believers eternally: "You have come... to the assembly of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]]). The new moon marked fresh start monthly; Christ inaugurates new creation transcending monthly cycles: "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]]). The trumpet announced "feast day" (ḥāgēnû); believers participate in eternal feast—"Blessed are those who are invited to the marriage supper of the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 9|Revelation 19:9]]). Where new moons provided monthly renewal, Christ provides continual renewal: "Though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 16|2 Corinthians 4:16]]). The lunar cycle's visibility made God's faithfulness observable monthly; Christ's resurrection makes it permanent—"Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 8|Hebrews 13:8]]). Paul warns believers not to return to "shadow" observances like new moons ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16]]) because the substance—Christ—has arrived. The joy of new moon celebration finds complete expression in Christ: "These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may be in you, and that your joy may be full" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 11|John 15:11]]). The trumpet will sound finally when Christ returns ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16]]), gathering all to eternal feast where "the Lamb will be their shepherd, and he will guide them to springs of living water" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 17|Revelation 7:17]]). No more monthly renewals needed—permanent newness reigns.
