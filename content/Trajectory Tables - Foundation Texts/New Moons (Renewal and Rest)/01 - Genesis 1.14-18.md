### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 14|Genesis 1:14-18]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3401-3500#H3974|מְאֹרֹת]] (*məʾōrōt*) - "lights, luminaries"
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7549|בִּרְקִיעַ]] (*birqîaʿ*) - "in the expanse, firmament"
- [[Lexicon/H0201-0300#H0226|לְאֹתֹת]] (*ləʾōtōt*) - "for signs"
- [[Lexicon/H4101-4200#H4150|וּלְמוֹעֲדִים]] (*ûləmôʿădîm*) - "and for appointed times, seasons"
- [[Lexicon/H3101-3200#H3117|וּלְיָמִים וְשָׁנִים]] (*ûləyāmîm wəšānîm*) - "and for days and years"
- [[Lexicon/H4401-4500#H4475|הַמָּאוֹר הַגָּדֹל]] (*hammāʾôr haggādōl*) - "the greater light" (sun)
- [[Lexicon/H4401-4500#H4475|הַמָּאוֹר הַקָּטֹן]] (*hammāʾôr haqqāṭōn*) - "the lesser light" (moon)

**Context**: God creates sun, moon, and stars on day four, establishing them "for signs and for seasons, and for days and years." These luminaries govern day and night, mark time's passage, and structure creation's rhythms. The moon's monthly cycle provides the foundation for Israel's lunar calendar and new moon observances, demonstrating God's sovereignty over time itself.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 104#Psalm 104 . 19|Psalm 104:19]] (He made the moon to mark the seasons), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 136#Psalm 136 . 7|Psalm 136:7-9]] (sun and moon thanksgiving)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28#Numbers 28 . 11|Numbers 28:11-15]] (new moon offerings), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 81#Psalm 81 . 3|Psalm 81:3]] (trumpet at new moon)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]] (new creation in Christ), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]] (no need of sun or moon)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 14|Genesis 1:14-18]]'s luminaries prefigure Christ who fulfills and transcends creation's temporal order. The sun governs day; Christ is "the sun of righteousness" ([[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 4#Malachi 4 . 2|Malachi 4:2]]) and "the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]). The moon marks seasons; Christ inaugurates "the fullness of time" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]]) when God sent His Son. The appointed times (môʿădîm) established through lunar cycles—new moons, Passover, Feast of Tabernacles—all prefigured Christ: "These are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance belongs to Christ" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16-17]]). The new moon's monthly renewal symbolized what Christ accomplishes permanently: "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]]). Where moon waxes and wanes, Christ's light shines constantly: "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]). The lunar cycle's repetition pointed to continual renewal now realized in believers: "being renewed day by day" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 16|2 Corinthians 4:16]]). The new creation eliminates dependence on created luminaries: "The city has no need of sun or moon... for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). Creation's temporal rhythms—day and night, months and years—yield to eternity where God's servants "will reign forever and ever" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 5|Revelation 22:5]]) in perpetual light. The "signs" (ʾōtōt) function finds ultimate expression in Christ as the sign par excellence: "This will be a sign for you" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 12|Luke 2:12]])—the incarnate God governing not just day and night but all creation.
