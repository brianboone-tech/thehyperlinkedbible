### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 23|Isaiah 66:23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1701-1800#H1767|מִדֵּי]] (*middê*) - "as often as, from... to..."
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2320|חֹדֶשׁ בְּחָדְשׁוֹ]] (*ḥōdeš bəḥodšô*) - "month by month, from new moon to new moon"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7676|שַׁבָּת בְּשַׁבַּתּוֹ]] (*šabbāt bəšabbattô*) - "Sabbath by Sabbath, from Sabbath to Sabbath"
- [[Lexicon/H3501-3600#H3605|כָּל־בָּשָׂר]] (*kol-bāśār*) - "all flesh, all humanity"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7812|לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֹת]] (*ləhištaḥăwōt*) - "to worship, bow down"
- [[Lexicon/H6401-6500#H6440|לְפָנַי]] (*ləpānay*) - "before me"

**Context**: Isaiah prophesies that in the new creation, from new moon to new moon and Sabbath to Sabbath, all humanity will come to worship before the LORD. This eschatological vision transforms temporary observances (new moons, Sabbaths) into perpetual worship pattern, extending Israel's worship rhythms to all people in God's renewed creation.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28#Numbers 28 . 11|Numbers 28:11-15]] (new moon offerings), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 81#Psalm 81 . 3|Psalm 81:3]] (trumpet at new moon)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 22|Isaiah 66:22]] (new heavens and new earth), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 16|Zechariah 14:16]] (nations come to worship)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]] (nations walk by city's light), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]] (his servants will worship him), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22-24]] (come to festal gathering)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 23|Isaiah 66:23]]'s perpetual worship finds fulfillment in Christ who mediates eternal access to God's presence. The new moons and Sabbaths, shadows pointing to Christ ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16-17]]), give way to substance—Christ Himself. In Him, believers "have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living God... and to innumerable angels in festal gathering... and to Jesus, the mediator of a new covenant" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22-24]]). The "from new moon to new moon" rhythm becomes continuous renewal: "Though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 16|2 Corinthians 4:16]]). The "all flesh" worshiping fulfills the Great Commission—disciples "from all nations" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]]) gathered to worship Christ. The new creation worship Isaiah prophesies occurs now partially (believers worship continually—[[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 17|1 Thessalonians 5:17]]: "pray without ceasing") and will occur perfectly in consummation. Revelation describes this: "His servants will worship him. They will see his face" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3-4]])—uninterrupted worship in God's unveiled presence. No literal new moons are needed because "the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). The monthly renewal cycle pointed to ultimate newness: "Behold, I am making all things new" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 5|Revelation 21:5]])—not cyclical but permanent. Isaiah's "from Sabbath to Sabbath" becomes eternal Sabbath rest where believers "rest from their labors" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]) perpetually. Christ enables this through His perfect work, granting continuous access to God's presence where worship never ends.
