### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 16|2 Corinthians 4:16]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1352|διό]] (*dio*) - "therefore, for this reason"
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1455|ἐγκακοῦμεν]] (*enkakοumen*) - "we do not lose heart, become discouraged"
- [[Lexicon/G1801-1900#G1854|ἔξω ἄνθρωπος]] (*exō anthrōpos*) - "outer person, outward man"
- [[Lexicon/G1301-1400#G1311|διαφθείρεται]] (*diaphtheiretai*) - "is being destroyed, decaying" (present passive)
- [[Lexicon/G2001-2100#G2081|ἔσω]] (*esō*) - "inner person, inward"
- [[Lexicon/G0301-0400#G0341|ἀνακαινοῦται]] (*anakainoutai*) - "is being renewed" (present passive)
- [[Lexicon/H2250|ἡμέρᾳ καὶ ἡμέρᾳ]] (*hēmera kai hēmera*) - "day by day, daily"

**Context**: Paul explains his perseverance despite suffering: though outward decay is occurring, inward renewal happens daily. Physical deterioration doesn't produce discouragement because simultaneous spiritual renewal occurs. This creates paradoxical Christian existence—external wasting, internal renewing—sustained by hope in eternal glory outweighing temporary affliction.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 103#Psalm 103 . 5|Psalm 103:5]] (renews your youth like eagle), [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 3#Lamentations 3 . 22|Lamentations 3:22-23]] (mercies new every morning)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]] (new creation), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 23|Ephesians 4:23-24]] (renewed in spirit of minds), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 10|Colossians 3:10]] (new self being renewed), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 2|Romans 12:2]] (transformed by renewal of mind)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 16|2 Corinthians 4:16]]'s daily renewal fulfills what new moons symbolized monthly—fresh mercy, renewed strength. The monthly cycle acknowledged time's passage and ongoing need; Christ provides daily, even moment-by-moment renewal. Paul's "day by day" echoes [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 3#Lamentations 3 . 22|Lamentations 3:22-23]]: "The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning"—what was God's general faithfulness becomes believers' specific experience in Christ. The Spirit continuously renews: "Do not get drunk with wine... but be filled with the Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 18|Ephesians 5:18]])—present imperative suggesting ongoing filling. The outer person's decay results from Adam's curse ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 19|Genesis 3:19]]: "to dust you shall return"); the inner person's renewal flows from Christ's resurrection life. Union with Christ means participating in His resurrection power now: "If the Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, he who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through his Spirit who dwells in you" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 11|Romans 8:11]]). The daily renewal anticipates bodily renewal at resurrection when "the perishable puts on the imperishable, and the mortal puts on immortality" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 54|1 Corinthians 15:54]]). Present inner renewal is firstfruits of complete future renewal. Paul elsewhere describes this transformation: "We all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another. For this comes from the Lord who is the Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]]). The new moon's monthly renewal pointed to what Christ provides continuously—new mercies ([[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 3#Lamentations 3 . 22|Lamentations 3:22-23]]), new strength ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 31|Isaiah 40:31]]), new life. Believers don't need monthly renewal rituals because Christ renews them daily through His indwelling Spirit. The ultimate renewal arrives when Christ returns and believers receive glorified bodies ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 21|Philippians 3:21]]), ending all decay permanently. New moons marked temporal renewal; Christ provides eternal renewing.
