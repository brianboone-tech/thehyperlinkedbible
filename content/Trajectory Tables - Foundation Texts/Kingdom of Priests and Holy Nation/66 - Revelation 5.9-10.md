**Context**: The throne room vision of Revelation 4-5 reaches its climax as the Lamb, slain yet standing, takes the scroll from the Father's right hand. The "new song" celebrates the Lamb's worthiness based on His redemptive work: He was slain and "purchased" (ἠγόρασας) people "for God" (τῷ θεῷ) from the universal fourfold expression (tribe, tongue, people, nation). The result: He "made them" (ἐποίησας) a kingdom and priests to our God, and "they shall reign" (βασιλεύσουσιν) upon the earth. This parallels Revelation 1:5-6 but expands the scope explicitly to universal humanity and adds the future reign.

**Type Classification**: **Forward-looking fulfillment**—Exodus 19:6 universalized through Lamb's sacrifice; **Already/Not Yet**—constituted as kingdom-priests (aorist), will reign (future)

**Christological Connection**: The Lamb's worthiness to open the scroll derives entirely from His redemptive death. The fourfold universal formula ("tribe, tongue, people, nation") fulfills and surpasses [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]'s single-nation scope—Christ's blood purchases not one ethnic nation but a transnational people. The "kingdom of priests" promised to Israel at Sinai, conditional upon obedience, is now constituted unconditionally through the Lamb's sacrifice. The future reign "upon the earth" (ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς) indicates that creation itself will be reclaimed—the priestly-royal vocation of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]], lost in Adam, is restored in the Last Adam. Christ's reign becomes the believers' reign; His priestly access becomes their priestly access; His kingdom becomes their inheritance.
