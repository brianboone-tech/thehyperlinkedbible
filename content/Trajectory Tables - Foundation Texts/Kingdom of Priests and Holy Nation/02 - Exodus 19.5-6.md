### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4467|H4467]] מַמְלֶכֶת (mamleket) - "kingdom, dominion, royal power"
- [[Lexicon/H3548|H3548]] כֹּהֵן (kohen) - "priest" (one who mediates between God and man)
- [[Lexicon/H1471|H1471]] גּוֹי (goy) - "nation, people" (corporate identity)
- [[Lexicon/H6918|H6918]] קָדוֹשׁ (qadosh) - "holy, set apart, sacred"
- [[Lexicon/H5459|H5459]] סְגֻלָּה (segullah) - "treasured possession, special property"
- [[Lexicon/H8085|H8085]] שָׁמַע (shama) - "hear, obey" (conditional element)

**Context**: At Mount Sinai, three months after the Exodus, God constitutes Israel as His covenant people. Before giving the law (ch. 20), God establishes Israel's unique corporate identity. The eagle's wings metaphor (v. 4) recalls God's redemptive deliverance from Egypt—grace precedes law. The conditional "if you obey" introduces covenant obligations, but the identity promised is unconditional in God's purpose. Israel is to be: (1) treasured possession—unique relationship among all nations; (2) kingdom of priests—royal authority combined with mediatorial function; (3) holy nation—set apart for God's purposes among all peoples.

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (divinely commanded institutional identity) + **Forward-looking** (contains expectational force toward missionary vocation and eschatological fulfillment)

**Christological Connection**: Christ fulfills Israel's priestly-royal calling perfectly where Israel failed. He is the true High Priest who mediates God's presence ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 14|Hebrews 4:14-16]]), the King who exercises dominion ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18]]), and the Holy One who consecrates His people ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 10|Hebrews 10:10]]). Through union with Christ, believers share in His priestly-royal identity—offered as spiritual sacrifices ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]]), declaring God's excellencies ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]), and reigning with Him ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 6|Revelation 20:6]]; 22:5). The conditional promise ("if you obey") finds its unconditional fulfillment in Christ's perfect obedience imputed to His people.
