**Context**: In Romans 11, Paul uses the olive tree metaphor to explain Gentile inclusion: Gentiles are "wild olive shoots" grafted into the cultivated tree of Israel. In Ephesians 2, Paul describes Gentiles who were "strangers to the covenants of promise" now being "brought near by the blood of Christ" and made "fellow citizens with the saints and members of God's household." Both passages describe Gentile inclusion in Israel's blessings.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- These passages fulfill Noah's prophecy that Japheth would "dwell in the tents of Shem" (Gen 9:27).
- The olive tree imagery connects to OT Israel as God's planting (Jer 11:16-17; [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 14#Hosea 14 . 6|Hosea 14:6]]).
- "Fellow citizens" fulfills the Abrahamic promise that "all families of the earth" would be blessed.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 9:27 (Japheth in Shem's tents), Genesis 12:3 (all families blessed), Isaiah 56:3-8 (foreigners joining Israel)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 28|Galatians 3:28-29]] (all one in Christ, Abraham's offspring), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 11|Colossians 3:11]] (no Greek or Jew distinction)
- **PARALLEL**: Acts 15:14-17 (James cites Amos re: Gentile inclusion), 1 Peter 2:9-10 (once not a people, now God's people)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking Fulfillment (Japheth's dwelling in Shem's tents now explained); Direct (explicit teaching on Gentile inclusion).

**Christological Connection**: These passages show Christ as the means by which Japheth dwells in Shem's tents. (1) **Grafted into Israel's Tree**: Gentiles don't start a new tree but are grafted into the "rich root of the olive tree" (Rom 11:17)—the patriarchs, including Shem. Through Christ, Gentiles share Shem's blessings. (2) **Brought Near by Blood**: Gentiles who were "far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ" (Eph 2:13). Christ's death is the mechanism by which Japheth enters Shem's tents. (3) **Fellow Citizens**: Gentiles are no longer "strangers to the covenants of promise" (Eph 2:12) but "fellow citizens with the saints" (2:19). The covenants made with Shem's descendants now belong to all who believe. (4) **One New Humanity**: Christ "has made us both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility" (Eph 2:14). Shem and Japheth are now one in Christ. (5) **Household of God**: Gentiles are "members of the household of God" (Eph 2:19). The "tents of Shem" have become the Father's house, and all believers—from every ethnicity—dwell there through Christ.
