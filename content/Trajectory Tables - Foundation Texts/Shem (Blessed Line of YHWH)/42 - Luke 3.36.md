### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 36|Luke 3:36]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4590|G4590]] Σήμ (*Sēm*) - "Shem"
- [[Lexicon/G3575|G3575]] Νῶε (*Nōe*) - "Noah"
- [[Lexicon/G742|G742]] Ἀρφαξάδ (*Arphaxad*) - "Arphaxad"
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (*huios*) - "son"
- [[Lexicon/G2316|G2316]] θεός (*theos*) - "God"

**Context**: Luke's genealogy traces Jesus' ancestry from Joseph back through David, Abraham, Shem, Noah, Adam, and ultimately to God (Luke 3:38: "the son of Adam, the son of God"). This is the only NT genealogy that extends back past Abraham to the patriarchs before the flood. The inclusion of Shem demonstrates Jesus' connection to the blessed line established in Genesis 9:26.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Luke's genealogy validates the OT genealogical records from Genesis 5, 11, and beyond.
- By tracing through Shem, Luke connects Jesus to Noah's prophetic blessing and the narrowing covenant line.
- The genealogy culminates in "the son of God," bringing the line full circle from Adam (who was created as God's son) to Jesus (who is the eternal Son of God).

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 9:26-27 (blessing on Shem), Genesis 11:10-26 (Shem's genealogy), Genesis 5:1-32 (Adam to Noah)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 1|Matthew 1:1-17]] (complementary genealogy through David), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 3|Romans 1:3]] (descended from David)
- **PARALLEL**: 1 Chronicles 1:1-27 (OT genealogy Adam to Abraham)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking Fulfillment (demonstrates Jesus as heir of Shem's blessing); Direct (genealogical verification of OT connections).

**Christological Connection**: Luke's genealogy through Shem is theologically significant. (1) **Son of Shem**: Jesus is literally "the son of Shem" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 36|Luke 3:36]]). The blessing pronounced in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 26|Genesis 9:26]]—"Blessed be the LORD, the God of Shem"—reaches its climax: the God of Shem is now incarnate as a son of Shem. (2) **Universal Scope**: By tracing to Adam (and to God), Luke shows Jesus' universal significance. He is not merely Israel's Messiah but humanity's Savior. Through Shem comes the one who saves all Adam's children. (3) **Completing the Line**: Adam was created "the son of God" (3:38). Through sin, humanity lost that status. Jesus, eternally the Son of God, enters the line of Adam to restore what was lost. Shem's line is the channel through which restoration comes. (4) **Japheth Fulfilled**: The promise that Japheth would "dwell in Shem's tents" is fulfilled when Gentiles (Japheth's descendants) believe in Jesus (Shem's descendant). Luke, likely a Gentile, writes this genealogy—a Japhethite recording Shem's most important son. (5) **Name Above Every Name**: Shem means "name." Christ, who descends from Shem, receives "the name that is above every name" (Phil 2:9). The line of "Name" produces the One with the ultimate Name.
