### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 10|Genesis 11:10-26]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8435|H8435]] תּוֹלְדֹת (*toledot*) - "generations, account"
- [[Lexicon/H8035|H8035]] שֵׁם (*shem*) - "Shem"
- [[Lexicon/H775|H775]] אַרְפַּכְשַׁד (*arpakshad*) - "Arphaxad"
- [[Lexicon/H5677|H5677]] עֵבֶר (*ʿeber*) - "Eber" (ancestor of "Hebrews")
- [[Lexicon/H6389|H6389]] פֶּלֶג (*peleg*) - "Peleg, division"
- [[Lexicon/H8646|H8646]] תֶּרַח (*terach*) - "Terah"
- [[Lexicon/H87|H87]] אַבְרָם (*ʾabram*) - "Abram, exalted father"

**Context**: After the Babel narrative (Gen 11:1-9), Genesis returns to genealogy. The toledot of Shem traces his descendants over ten generations to Terah and his sons, including Abram. This genealogy bridges the primeval history (Gen 1-11) with the patriarchal narratives (Gen 12-50), showing how the blessing on Shem flows toward Abraham.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The genealogy parallels the genealogy from Adam to Noah (Gen 5), both covering ten generations with similar declining lifespans.
- Eber (עֵבֶר) is the ancestor from whom "Hebrews" derive their name—the people of Shem through whom blessing comes.
- Peleg's name ("division") may reference the Babel division (11:9) that occurs in his lifetime.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 5:1-32 (parallel genealogy from Adam to Noah), Genesis 9:26-27 (blessing on Shem)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]] (Abrahamic covenant), [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1#1 Chronicles 1 . 17|1 Chronicles 1:17-27]] (genealogy repeated)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 34|Luke 3:34-36]] (Jesus' genealogy through this line)

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (genealogical preservation sovereignly arranged); Forward-Looking (each generation moves toward the promised blessing through Abraham).

**Christological Connection**: This genealogy is part of Christ's ancestry. (1) **Ten Generations**: Like Adam-to-Noah, Shem-to-Abraham covers ten generations. Ten symbolizes completeness—the genealogical countdown toward Abraham's covenant. (2) **Eber to Hebrew**: Christ is called "Hebrew of Hebrews" through Paul's lineage (Phil 3:5, indirectly). The Hebrews, children of Eber, are Shem's blessed descendants. (3) **Declining Lifespans**: The decreasing ages (Shem: 600 years; Abraham: 175 years) show humanity's ongoing need for the One who conquers death. (4) **Terah to Abraham**: Terah's family in Ur of the Chaldeans represents idolatrous humanity from whom God calls Abraham. From this unlikely beginning, God produces the Messiah. (5) **Luke's Genealogy**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 34|Luke 3:34-36]] traces Jesus through "Shelah, the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah." Christ is Shem's son—heir of the blessed line, the one in whom Japheth truly dwells.
