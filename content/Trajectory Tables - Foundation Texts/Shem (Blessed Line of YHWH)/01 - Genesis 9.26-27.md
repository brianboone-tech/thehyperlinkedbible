### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 26|Genesis 9:26-27]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1288|H1288]] בָּרַךְ (*barak*) - "to bless, kneel"
- [[Lexicon/H3068|H3068]] יְהוָה (*YHWH*) - "the LORD" (divine name)
- [[Lexicon/H430|H430]] אֱלֹהִים (*ʾelohim*) - "God"
- [[Lexicon/H8035|H8035]] שֵׁם (*shem*) - "Shem, name" (pun on "name/renown")
- [[Lexicon/H6601|H6601]] פָּתָה (*patah*) - "to enlarge, persuade" (wordplay with Japheth)
- [[Lexicon/H3315|H3315]] יֶפֶת (*yepheth*) - "Japheth"
- [[Lexicon/H7931|H7931]] שָׁכַן (*shakan*) - "to dwell, tabernacle"
- [[Lexicon/H168|H168]] אֹהֶל (*ʾohel*) - "tent"

**Context**: After Noah's drunkenness and nakedness, Ham sins by looking on his father and telling his brothers, while Shem and Japheth reverently cover their father without looking. Noah awakes and speaks prophetically: cursing Canaan (Ham's son), blessing Shem, and prophesying enlargement for Japheth. The blessing on Shem is unique—rather than blessing Shem directly, Noah blesses YHWH as "the God of Shem."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "The LORD, the God of Shem" anticipates the covenant formula: "I will be your God, and you will be My people" (Lev 26:12).
- "Japheth shall dwell in the tents of Shem" foreshadows Gentile inclusion in the covenant community.
- The genealogy from Shem to Abraham (Gen 11:10-26) demonstrates how the blessing narrows to one family.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 3:15 (seed promise narrowed through Shem), Genesis 6:9-10 (Noah's three sons)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 10|Genesis 11:10-26]] (Shem's genealogy to Abraham), [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]] (Abrahamic covenant)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 36|Luke 3:36]] (Jesus through Shem), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 17|Romans 11:17]] (Gentiles grafted in)

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (Shem's righteous action rewarded); Forward-Looking (explicit prophecy of Japheth's future dwelling in Shem's tents).

**Christological Connection**: Noah's blessing establishes the theological trajectory fulfilled in Christ. (1) **YHWH as Shem's God**: This is the first identification of YHWH with a specific people. Through Shem comes Abraham, Israel, and Christ—in whom "all the fullness of the Godhead dwells bodily" (Col 2:9). The God of Shem becomes incarnate through Shem's descendants. (2) **Name**: שֵׁם means "name." Christ bears "the name that is above every name" (Phil 2:9). The line of Shem ("Name") produces the One with the supreme Name. (3) **Dwelling in Shem's Tents**: Japheth's descendants (traditionally, the European peoples) would find blessing through Shem's line. In Christ, Gentiles are "fellow heirs, members of the same body" (Eph 3:6)—dwelling in Shem's tents. (4) **Covering Nakedness**: Shem reverently covered his father's shame. Christ covers our shame with His righteousness—"clothed...with garments of salvation" (Isa 61:10). The one who covered nakedness produces the One who covers sin.
