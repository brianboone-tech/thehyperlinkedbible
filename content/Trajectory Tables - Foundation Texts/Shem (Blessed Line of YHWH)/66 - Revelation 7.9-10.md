### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9-10]]

**Context**: After sealing the 144,000 from Israel's tribes, John sees "a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb." They wear white robes and hold palm branches, crying: "Salvation belongs to our God who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!" This vision portrays the ultimate fulfillment of Gentile inclusion.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This multitude fulfills [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]]: "In you all the families of the earth shall be blessed."
- The "every nation, tribe, people, language" formula reverses Babel's scattering (Gen 11) and fulfills the nations flowing to Zion (Isa 2:2-3).
- The worship before the throne fulfills [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 18|Isaiah 66:18-23]]'s vision of all nations coming to see God's glory.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 9:27 (Japheth in Shem's tents), Genesis 12:3 (all families blessed), Genesis 11:1-9 (Babel reversed)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] (purchased from every tribe), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 6|Revelation 14:6]] (eternal gospel to every nation), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24]] (nations walk by its light)
- **PARALLEL**: Matthew 24:14 (gospel to all nations), Acts 1:8 (witnesses to the end of the earth)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking Fulfillment (eschatological consummation of Japheth dwelling in Shem's tents); Direct (explicit vision of universal ingathering).

**Christological Connection**: This vision is the ultimate fulfillment of Shem's blessing. (1) **Japheth's Multitude**: "From every nation, tribe, people, and language"—this includes all of Japheth's descendants and Ham's, along with Shem's. All dwell together in Shem's tent, now revealed as the presence of the Lamb. (2) **Through the Lamb**: "Salvation belongs...to the Lamb!" All these from every nation are saved through Jesus Christ, Shem's descendant. The blessing promised to Shem flows to the whole world through His seed. (3) **White Robes**: "These are the ones coming out of the great tribulation. They have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb" (Rev 7:14). Shem covered his father's nakedness with a garment; Christ covers all nations' sin with His blood. (4) **Universal Worship**: The multitude joins angels, elders, and living creatures in worship. The "God of Shem" (Gen 9:26) now receives worship from all peoples—Shem's blessing reaches cosmic scope. (5) **Never Hunger or Thirst**: "The Lamb in the midst of the throne will be their shepherd" (Rev 7:17). The shepherd of all nations is from Shem's line. The blessing that began with one man now feeds countless millions forever.
