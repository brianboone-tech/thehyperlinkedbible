### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3056|ὁ λόγος]] (*ho logos*) - "the Word"
- [[Lexicon/G4401-4500#G4561|σὰρξ]] (*sarx*) - "flesh, human nature"
- [[Lexicon/G1001-1100#G1096|ἐγένετο]] (*egeneto*) - "became" (not "appeared" but "came to be")
- [[Lexicon/G4601-4700#G4637|ἐσκήνωσεν]] (*eskēnōsen*) - "tabernacled, pitched tent, dwelt"
- [[Lexicon/G1301-1400#G1391|δόξα]] (*doxa*) - "glory"
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5485|χάρις καὶ ἀλήθεια]] (*charis kai alētheia*) - "grace and truth"

**Context**: John's prologue climaxes with the incarnation announcement. The eternal Word, who was with God and was God (vv. 1-2), becomes flesh and dwells among humanity. John uses tabernacle imagery to describe Christ's incarnational presence, evoking Israel's wilderness experience when God dwelt in their midst through the tabernacle.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (tabernacle command), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 18|Exodus 33:18-23]] (Moses sees glory), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]] (gracious and merciful), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] (Immanuel prophecy)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] (body as temple), [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 6|Philippians 2:6-7]] (taking form of servant), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 19|Colossians 1:19]] (all fullness dwells), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 9|Colossians 2:9]] (fullness of deity bodily)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] declares the theological center of the Feast of Tabernacles' typology—the Word tabernacled (ἐσκήνωσεν) among us. What Israel's booths symbolized (temporary dwelling in anticipation of permanent presence), Christ accomplishes in the incarnation. The portable tabernacle that accompanied Israel through the wilderness finds fulfillment in the incarnate Son who accompanies His people through the wilderness of this world. The glory Moses glimpsed ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 18|Exodus 33:18-23]]) is now "beheld" fully in Christ ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]]). The grace and truth (χάρις καὶ ἀλήθεια) that define God's covenant character ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]]) are perfectly embodied in Jesus. During the Feast of Tabernacles, Jesus proclaimed Himself the source of living water ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]]) and the light of the world ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]), fulfilling what the feast's ceremonies symbolized. His body is the true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]), the place where God dwells. Through the Spirit, this dwelling extends to believers ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 20|John 14:20-23]]; [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]), and reaches consummation when "the dwelling place of God is with man" eternally ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]).
