### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5301-5400#G5457|τὸ φῶς τοῦ κόσμου]] (*to phōs tou kosmou*) - "the light of the world"
- [[Lexicon/G0101-0200#G0190|ἀκολουθέω]] (*akolouthō*) - "follow"
- [[Lexicon/G4601-4700#G4653|σκοτία]] (*skotia*) - "darkness"
- [[Lexicon/G5301-5400#G5457|τὸ φῶς τῆς ζωῆς]] (*to phōs tēs zōēs*) - "the light of life"

**Context**: During or immediately after the Feast of Tabernacles, when the temple courts were still illuminated by the torch-lighting ceremony, Jesus declares, "I am the light of the world." This second "I AM" statement claims to be the reality to which the feast's lampstands pointed.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]] (let there be light), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]] (pillar of fire), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 27#Psalm 27 . 1|Psalm 27:1]] (LORD is my light), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 2|Isaiah 9:2]] (people in darkness see great light), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 6|Isaiah 42:6]] (light to the nations), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 60#Isaiah 60 . 1|Isaiah 60:1-3]] (arise, shine, your light has come)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 4|John 1:4-5]] (life was light of men), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 9#John 9 . 5|John 9:5]] (light of the world while in the world), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 46|John 12:46]] (I have come as light), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 8|Ephesians 5:8]] (you are light in the Lord)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]] presents Christ as the fulfillment of the Feast of Tabernacles' torch-lighting ceremony. What the lampstands symbolized—God's guidance through the pillar of fire, the hope of messianic illumination—Christ provides in Himself. He is the "true light, which gives light to everyone" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 9|John 1:9]]). The pillar of fire that guided Israel through the wilderness prefigured Christ who guides His followers through the darkness of this world. Isaiah's prophecy that the Servant would be "a light for the nations" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 6|Isaiah 42:6]]) finds fulfillment as Christ brings revelation to Gentiles ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 32|Luke 2:32]]). Believers participate in Christ's light—"you are the light of the world" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 14|Matthew 5:14]])—by reflecting His illumination. This trajectory reaches consummation in the new Jerusalem where "the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). No torches, no lampstands, no sun required—Christ Himself is the eternal light in God's dwelling place with His people.
