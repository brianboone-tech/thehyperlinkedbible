### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4701-4800#G4983|σῶμα]] (*sōma*) - "body"
- [[Lexicon/G3401-3500#G3485|ναὸς]] (*naos*) - "temple, sanctuary"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0040|ἁγίου πνεύματος]] (*hagiou pneumatos*) - "Holy Spirit"
- [[Lexicon/G1701-1800#G1722|ἐν ὑμῖν]] (*en hymin*) - "in you" (plural)
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1438|ἑαυτῶν]] (*heautōn*) - "your own, of yourselves"

**Context**: Paul addresses sexual immorality in Corinth by reminding believers that their individual bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit. This personalizes the corporate temple theology of 1 Corinthians 3:16, applying sacred-space categories to each believer's physical body.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (dwell among them), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 2|Leviticus 19:2]] (be holy for I am holy)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]] (corporate temple), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1]] (present bodies as living sacrifice), [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16]] (we are temple of living God)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]] represents the culmination of the tabernacling trajectory. God dwelt in the tabernacle ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]]), then in Christ's body ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]; [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 9|Colossians 2:9]]: "in him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily"), then in the church corporately ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]), and now in each believer individually ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]]). The Holy Spirit—given by Christ after His glorification ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 39|John 7:39]])—mediates Christ's presence in believers. Union with Christ ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 17|1 Corinthians 6:17]]: "joined to the Lord, one spirit") makes each believer a temple. The body purchased by Christ's blood (v. 20) becomes sanctified space where the Spirit dwells. This anticipates the resurrection when bodies will be glorified ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 42|1 Corinthians 15:42-44]]), perfecting the temple analogy. Ultimately, in the new creation, redeemed bodies will dwell in God's presence ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]), bringing together individual (body-temples), corporate (church-temple), and cosmic (new creation as God's dwelling) dimensions into perfect, eternal unity.
