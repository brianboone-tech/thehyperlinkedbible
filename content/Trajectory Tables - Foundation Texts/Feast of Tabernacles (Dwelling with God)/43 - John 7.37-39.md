### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2001-2100#G2078|ἐσχάτῃ ἡμέρᾳ τῇ μεγάλῃ]] (*eschatē hēmera tē megalē*) - "the last day, the great day"
- [[Lexicon/G1301-1400#G1372|διψᾷ]] (*dipsaō*) - "thirst"
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4095|πινέτω]] (*pinetō*) - "let him drink"
- [[Lexicon/G4201-4300#G4215|ποταμοὶ ὕδατος ζῶντος]] (*potamoi hydatos zōntos*) - "rivers of living water"
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4151|πνεύματος]] (*pneumatos*) - "of the Spirit"

**Context**: On the climactic final day of the Feast of Tabernacles, when the water-pouring ceremony reached its peak, Jesus stands and dramatically proclaims Himself the source of living water. John explains this refers to the Spirit, who would be given after Jesus' glorification.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]] (water from rock), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 11|Numbers 20:11]] (Moses strikes rock), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 12#Isaiah 12 . 3|Isaiah 12:3]] (draw water from wells of salvation), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] (water from temple), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]] (living waters from Jerusalem)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 14|John 4:14]] (living water to Samaritan woman), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] (water from Christ's side), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]] (river of life from throne)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] reveals Christ as the fulfillment of the Feast of Tabernacles' water-pouring ceremony. What the ceremony symbolized—God's provision of water in the wilderness and hope for messianic abundance—Christ provides in reality through the Spirit. The connection to Exodus 17 is explicit in [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]: "they drank from the spiritual Rock that followed them, and the Rock was Christ." The rock that provided water in the wilderness prefigured Christ who provides living water through the Spirit. Jesus' glorification (His death, resurrection, ascension) was necessary before the Spirit could be given (v. 39), linking the water provision to His completed redemptive work. When His side was pierced on the cross, "blood and water" flowed out ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]]), symbolizing the life-giving Spirit released through His death. Ezekiel's vision of water flowing from the temple ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1]]) finds fulfillment as the Spirit flows from Christ, the true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]). This reaches ultimate consummation in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]: "the river of the water of life, bright as crystal, flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb"—eternal living water from the glorified Christ in the new creation.
