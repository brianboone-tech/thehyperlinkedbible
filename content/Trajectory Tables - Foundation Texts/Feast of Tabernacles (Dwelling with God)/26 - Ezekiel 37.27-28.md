### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27-28]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4801-4900#H4908|מִשְׁכָּנִי]] (*miškānî*) - "my dwelling place, my tabernacle"
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5921|עֲלֵיהֶם]] (*ʿălêhem*) - "over them, upon them"
- [[Lexicon/H1901-2000#H1961|וְהָיִיתִי]] (*wəhāyîtî*) - "and I will be"
- [[Lexicon/H0401-0500#H0430|לֵאלֹהִים]] (*lēʾlōhîm*) - "as God"
- [[Lexicon/H5601-5700#H5971|לְעָם]] (*ləʿām*) - "as people"

**Context**: In the culmination of Ezekiel's vision of national restoration (the dry bones coming to life, the reunited kingdom), God promises that His dwelling place will be with restored Israel forever. This prophetic anticipation looks beyond return from Babylonian exile to the ultimate fulfillment when God dwells permanently with His people.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (original tabernacle command), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] (covenant formula)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 7|Ezekiel 43:7]] (temple vision continues), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 10|Zechariah 2:10-11]] (I will dwell in your midst)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] (Word tabernacled among us), [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16]] (temple of living God), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] (dwelling of God with man)

**Christological Connection**: Ezekiel's prophecy finds fulfillment in three stages through Christ. First, in the incarnation: "The Word became flesh and tabernacled [ἐσκήνωσεν] among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]), fulfilling "my dwelling place shall be with them" as God dwells in human flesh. Jesus identifies His body as the temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]), locating God's dwelling in Himself. Second, in the church age: believers corporately become "God's temple" through the Spirit's indwelling ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]; [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16]]), with God's dwelling "among them" realized through the Spirit. Third, in the new creation: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] explicitly echoes Ezekiel's language: "Behold, the dwelling place [σκηνή] of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God." The "forevermore" of [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 28|Ezekiel 37:28]] reaches ultimate consummation when God and the Lamb dwell eternally with redeemed humanity in the new Jerusalem, with no temple needed because "the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]).
