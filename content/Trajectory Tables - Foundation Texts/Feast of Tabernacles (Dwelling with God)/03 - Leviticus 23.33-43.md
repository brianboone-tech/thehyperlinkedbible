### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 33|Leviticus 23:33-43]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2201-2300#H2282|חַג הַסֻּכֹּת]] (*ḥag hassukkōt*) - "Feast of Booths/Tabernacles"
- [[Lexicon/H5401-5500#H5521|סֻכָּה]] (*sukkāh*) - "booth, tabernacle, shelter"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7651|שִׁבְעַת יָמִים]] (*šibʿat yāmîm*) - "seven days"
- [[Lexicon/H3301-3400#H3427|תֵּשְׁבוּ]] (*tēšəbû*) - "you shall dwell, remain"
- [[Lexicon/H4901-5000#H4940|לְמַעַן יֵדְעוּ]] (*ləmaʿan yēdəʿû*) - "that they may know"

**Context**: God commands Israel to observe the Feast of Tabernacles for seven days in the seventh month, dwelling in temporary booths to commemorate their wilderness wanderings when God sheltered them in tents. The feast combines harvest celebration with historical remembrance, teaching dependence on God's provision and foreshadowing God's ultimate tabernacling among His people.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 3|Exodus 13:3-10]] (remembering the Exodus), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 13|Deuteronomy 16:13-15]] (feast observance expanded)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8#Nehemiah 8 . 14|Nehemiah 8:14-18]] (post-exilic observance), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 16|Zechariah 14:16-19]] (eschatological feast)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] (Word tabernacled), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 2|John 7:2-39]] (Jesus at the feast), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 9|Hebrews 11:9-10]] (dwelling in tents by faith)

**Christological Connection**: The Feast of Tabernacles finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ's incarnation. John announces, "The Word became flesh and tabernacled [ἐσκήνωσεν, *eskēnōsen*] among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]), using a verb derived from the same root as "tabernacle" (σκηνή, *skēnē*). What Israel's temporary booths symbolized—God dwelling among His people—Christ accomplishes in His incarnation. During the Feast of Tabernacles, Jesus declares Himself the source of living water ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]]) and the light of the world ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 12|John 8:12]]), fulfilling the feast's water-pouring and torch-lighting ceremonies. The feast's eschatological dimension finds consummation in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]: "Behold, the dwelling place [σκηνή, *skēnē*] of God is with man. He will dwell [σκηνώσει, *skēnōsei*] with them." The temporary booths, fulfilled in Christ's earthly tabernacling, reach eternal fulfillment when God dwells with His people forever in the new creation.
