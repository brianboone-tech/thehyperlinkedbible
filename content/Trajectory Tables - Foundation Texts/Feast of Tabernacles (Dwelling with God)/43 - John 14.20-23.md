### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 20|John 14:20-23]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1701-1800#G1722|ἐν ἐμοί... ἐν ὑμῖν... ἐν αὐτῷ]] (*en emoi... en hymin... en autō*) - "in me... in you... in him" (mutual indwelling)
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2532|κἀγὼ ἐν αὐτῷ]] (*kagō en autō*) - "and I in him"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0025|ἀγαπᾷ]] (*agapaō*) - "love"
- [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3438|μονὴν]] (*monēn*) - "dwelling place, room, abode"
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4160|ποιησόμεθα]] (*poiēsometha*) - "we will make"

**Context**: In the Upper Room discourse, Jesus promises the disciples that mutual indwelling will characterize the new covenant relationship. The Father and Son will come and make their dwelling (μονή) with believers who love and obey Jesus.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (tabernacle among them), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] (I will make my dwelling among you), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] (my dwelling place shall be with them)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 4|John 15:4-5]] (abide in me), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 21|John 17:21-23]] (that they may be one as we are one), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 9|Romans 8:9-11]] (Spirit dwells in you), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 17|Ephesians 3:17]] (Christ dwell in your hearts)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 20|John 14:20-23]] reveals how the Feast of Tabernacles' dwelling motif extends from incarnation to indwelling. Christ tabernacled among us ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]); now Father and Son tabernacle within us through the Spirit. What the feast's booths symbolized—God dwelling with His people—reaches new intensity as the Triune God makes His abode in individual believers. Paul develops this: "Do you not know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in you?" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]). The earthly tabernacle prefigured Christ's incarnation; the indwelling Spirit fulfills God's promise "I will make my dwelling among you" ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11]]) in each believer. This mutual indwelling—"I in my Father, and you in me, and I in you"—constitutes participation in the divine life, a mystery "hidden for ages" now revealed ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 27|Colossians 1:27]]): "Christ in you, the hope of glory." The trajectory culminates in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]], where tabernacle imagery reaches consummation: God dwells with His people eternally, and they see His face ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]]), enjoying unmediated communion forever.
