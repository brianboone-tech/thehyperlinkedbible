### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6201-6300#H6213|וְעָשׂוּ לִי]] (*wəʿāśû lî*) - "and they shall make for me"
- [[Lexicon/H4801-4900#H4720|מִקְדָּשׁ]] (*miqdāš*) - "sanctuary, holy place"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7931|וְשָׁכַנְתִּי]] (*wəšākantî*) - "and I will dwell, tabernacle"
- [[Lexicon/H8401-8500#H8432|בְּתוֹכָם]] (*bətôkām*) - "in their midst, among them"
- [[Lexicon/H8401-8500#H8403|תַּבְנִית]] (*tabnît*) - "pattern, model, blueprint"

**Context**: God commands Moses to build a sanctuary—the tabernacle—so that He may dwell among His people. This portable tent-sanctuary will accompany Israel through the wilderness, making God's presence accessible and mobile. The command establishes the theological foundation for the Feast of Tabernacles.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 45|Exodus 29:45-46]] (purpose of tabernacle), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-38]] (glory fills tabernacle)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] (covenant promise of dwelling), [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-13]] (temple dedication), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27-28]] (future dwelling promise)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] (Word tabernacled among us), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 2|Hebrews 8:2]] (true tent not made with hands), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] (dwelling of God with man)

**Christological Connection**: The tabernacle's purpose—"that I may dwell among them"—finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ's incarnation. John announces: "The Word became flesh and dwelt [ἐσκήνωσεν, *eskēnōsen*, lit. 'tabernacled'] among us, and we have seen his glory" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). Where the tabernacle was a portable tent allowing God's presence to accompany Israel through the wilderness, Christ's incarnation is God dwelling in human flesh, accompanying His people through the wilderness of this world. Hebrews interprets the earthly tabernacle as "a copy and shadow of the heavenly things" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 5|Hebrews 8:5]]), with Christ serving "in the holy places, in the true tent that the Lord set up, not man" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 2|Hebrews 8:2]]). The tabernacle's glory-filled interior ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]]) prefigures Christ who manifests God's glory ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 11|John 2:11]]). Presently, believers corporately become God's temple through the indwelling Spirit ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]). Ultimately, in the new creation, "the dwelling place of God is with man" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]), fulfilling [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]]'s promise perfectly and eternally—no tent required, for "the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]).
