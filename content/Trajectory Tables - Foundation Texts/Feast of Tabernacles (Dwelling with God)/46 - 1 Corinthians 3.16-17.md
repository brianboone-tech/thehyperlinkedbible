### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16-17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3401-3500#G3485|ναὸς θεοῦ]] (*naos theou*) - "temple of God" (inner sanctuary, not ἱερόν)
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4151|πνεῦμα τοῦ θεοῦ]] (*pneuma tou theou*) - "Spirit of God"
- [[Lexicon/G3601-3700#G3611|οἰκεῖ]] (*oikei*) - "dwells, makes home"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0040|ἅγιος]] (*hagios*) - "holy"

**Context**: Paul addresses divisions in the Corinthian church by reminding them of their corporate identity as God's temple. The Spirit's indwelling makes the church community sacred, requiring unity and purity. This is corporate temple theology—the church collectively is God's dwelling place.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (make me a sanctuary), [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-13]] (glory fills temple), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] (my dwelling place with them)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]] (individual temple), [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16]] (temple of living God), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]] (built together as dwelling place), [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]] (spiritual house)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16-17]] demonstrates how Christ's incarnational tabernacling ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]) extends to the church through the Spirit. What the tabernacle and temple prefigured—God dwelling among His people—finds fulfillment first in Christ's body (the true temple, [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]), then in the church (the corporate body of Christ). The progression is: (1) OT tabernacle/temple (shadow), (2) Christ's body (substance), (3) Church as Christ's body (extension). The Spirit who filled the tabernacle ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]]) now fills the church ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 4|Acts 2:4]]). Paul's warning against destroying the temple (v. 17) echoes Jesus' promise to rebuild the temple in three days ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19]])—both Christ's resurrection and the church's resurrection identity are in view. Ephesians expands this: Christ is the cornerstone, and believers are "being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20-22]]). This corporate dwelling reaches eschatological fulfillment in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]], where "the dwelling place of God is with man" eternally—no physical temple needed because "the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]).
