### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4601-4700#G4633|ἡ σκηνὴ τοῦ θεοῦ]] (*hē skēnē tou theou*) - "the tabernacle of God"
- [[Lexicon/G3201-3300#G3326|μετὰ τῶν ἀνθρώπων]] (*meta tōn anthrōpōn*) - "with mankind"
- [[Lexicon/G4601-4700#G4637|σκηνώσει]] (*skēnōsei*) - "will dwell, will tabernacle"
- [[Lexicon/G0801-0900#G0846|αὐτοὶ λαὸς αὐτοῦ]] (*autoi laos autou*) - "they his people"

**Context**: In John's vision of the new creation, a voice from the throne announces the ultimate fulfillment of God's desire to dwell with humanity. This is the consummation of the entire biblical narrative of divine presence—from Eden, through tabernacle/temple, incarnation, and church, to eternal dwelling.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 8|Genesis 3:8]] (God walking in garden), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] (I will dwell among them), [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] (covenant formula), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] (my dwelling place with them)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] (Word tabernacled among us), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]] (no temple needed), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3-4]] (they will see his face)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] consummates the Feast of Tabernacles' entire trajectory. The temporary booths commemorating wilderness wandering pointed to God's ultimate tabernacling with His people. This finds initial fulfillment in Christ's incarnation—"the Word tabernacled [ἐσκήνωσεν] among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). The same verb returns in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]: God "will tabernacle [σκηνώσει] with them." What was temporary in the wilderness, temporary in the incarnation (Christ's earthly ministry), now becomes permanent and eternal. The progression is complete: (1) Israel's temporary booths (shadow), (2) Portable tabernacle (type), (3) Solomon's temple (anticipation), (4) Christ's incarnation (substance), (5) Church as Spirit's temple (participation), (6) New creation (consummation). Remarkably, verse 22 declares "I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb." The Lamb—the crucified and risen Christ—is the eternal temple. God's dwelling with humanity is mediated through the incarnate, glorified Son forever. The face-to-face vision promised ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]]) fulfills Moses' unrealized desire ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 20|Exodus 33:20]]), made possible through Christ who reveals the Father ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 9|John 14:9]]). The Feast of Tabernacles' water and light find eternal reality in the river of life and the Lamb's illumination ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). All that the feast symbolized reaches perfect, eternal fulfillment when God tabernacles with redeemed humanity forever.
