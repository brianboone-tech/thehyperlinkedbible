### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 1|Joshua 1:1-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- **יְהוֹשֻׁעַ** (Yehoshua/Joshua) - "Yahweh saves" - same name as Jesus (Ἰησοῦς), foreshadowing the greater Joshua
- **מְנוּחָה** (menuchah) - "Rest" - cessation from enemies, peace in the land, sabbatical fulfillment
- **חָזַק וֶאֱמָץ** (chazaq ve'ematz) - "Be strong and courageous" - repeated command, divine empowerment for conquest

**Context**: Moses has died. God commissions Joshua to lead Israel into the promised land. The repeated command "be strong and courageous" (vv. 6, 7, 9) emphasizes that success depends not on military prowess but on faithfulness to God's word (meditate on the law day and night, v. 8). The conquest is about to begin.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 34#Deuteronomy 34 . 5|Deuteronomy 34:5-9]] (Moses dies, Joshua succeeds him), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 7|Deuteronomy 31:7-8]], 23 (Moses commissions Joshua), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 18|Numbers 27:18-23]] (Joshua filled with spirit of wisdom)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 11#Joshua 11 . 23|Joshua 11:23]] (Joshua took the whole land, gave it as inheritance), [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]] (all promises fulfilled, not one word failed), [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 23#Joshua 23 . 1|Joshua 23:1]] (the LORD gave rest to Israel)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] (if Joshua had given them rest, God would not speak of another day), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]] (Jesus: Come to me, I will give you rest), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-4]] (no more death or mourning, God dwelling with His people)

**Christological Connection**: Joshua is the most direct OT type of Jesus Christ, sharing His very name. As Joshua led Israel into earthly rest (the land), Jesus leads believers into heavenly rest (eternal life). The escalation: Joshua conquered physical enemies and gave temporary rest; Jesus conquered sin, death, and Satan, giving eternal rest. Joshua's rest was incomplete—enemies remained in the land (Judges 1); Jesus's rest is complete—He has "disarmed the rulers and authorities" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]]). Joshua's commission to meditate on the law parallels Jesus's perfect obedience to the Father's word ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 19|John 5:19]]). Joshua's name means "Yahweh saves"; Jesus fulfills this perfectly—"You shall call His name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 21|Matthew 1:21]]). The crossing of the Jordan under Joshua prefigures baptism into Christ ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 1|1 Corinthians 10:1-4]])—passing through water into new life. Joshua divided the land as inheritance; Christ grants believers an "inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 4|1 Peter 1:4]]). What Joshua inaugurated, Jesus consummates—true and lasting rest for the people of God.
