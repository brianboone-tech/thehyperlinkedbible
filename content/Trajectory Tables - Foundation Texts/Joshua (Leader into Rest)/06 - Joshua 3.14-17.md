### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 3#Joshua 3 . 14|Joshua 3:14-17]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- אֲרוֹן הַבְּרִית (*aron habrit*) - "ark of the covenant" - God's throne and presence leading His people through death waters
- הַיַּרְדֵּן (*haYarden*) - "the Jordan" - Boundary river separating wilderness wandering from promised rest
- עָמְדוּ נֵד (*'amdu ned*) - "stood in a heap" - Waters miraculously stopped, echoing Red Sea exodus
- עַל־יְרֵכוֹ (*'al-Yerekho*) - "opposite Jericho" - Strategic entry point into Canaan's fortified cities

**Context**: Israel's crossing of the Jordan River marks the culmination of 40 years of wilderness wandering and the beginning of conquest. The priests bearing the ark step into the flooding Jordan, and the waters miraculously stop, allowing all Israel to cross on dry ground into the promised land.

**Christological Connection**: Joshua leading Israel through the Jordan into the promised land types Jesus leading His people through death into resurrection rest. The ark of God's presence must first enter the judgment waters, standing in the midst of the Jordan while all Israel passes safely through—a vivid picture of Christ bearing the full weight of divine judgment, holding back the waters of wrath, while His people pass safely through death to life. Jesus, the true Yehoshua, was baptized in this same Jordan, identifying with Israel's crossing and foreshadowing His own passage through death's waters in crucifixion and resurrection. Where Joshua's crossing brought Israel into temporal, physical rest in Canaan, Christ's resurrection brings His people into eternal, spiritual rest in the New Creation—the true promised land that will never fail.
