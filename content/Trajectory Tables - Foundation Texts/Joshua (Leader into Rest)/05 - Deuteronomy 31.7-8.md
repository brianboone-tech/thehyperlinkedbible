### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 7|Deuteronomy 31:7-8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- יְהוֹשֻׁעַ (*Yehoshua*) - "Yahweh saves" - The name shared by Joshua and Jesus, revealing the ultimate Savior
- חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ (*chazaq ve'emats*) - "Be strong and courageous" - Divine empowerment for covenant leadership
- נַחֲלָה (*nachalah*) - "inheritance" - The promised rest given as God's gift to His people
- לֹא יַרְפְּךָ וְלֹא יַעַזְבֶךָּ (*lo yarpeka velo ya'azveka*) - "He will not leave you or forsake you" - God's covenant faithfulness

**Context**: Moses' final acts before death, publicly commissioning Joshua as his successor before all Israel. The law has brought Israel to the border of the promised land, but Moses cannot enter. A new leader must bring God's people into the inheritance.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 11|Exodus 33:11]] - Joshua as Moses' young assistant
  - [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 18|Numbers 27:18-23]] - Initial commissioning of Joshua
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 5|Joshua 1:5-9]] - God repeats promise to Joshua
  - [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 44|Joshua 21:44-45]] - Joshua accomplishes the conquest
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] - "For if Joshua had given them rest..."
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 20|Matthew 28:20]] - Jesus: "I am with you always"
**Christological Connection**: Joshua's commissioning as Moses' successor reveals the pattern of redemptive history: the law (Moses) brings God's people to the threshold of rest but cannot bring them in. A new leader named "Yahweh saves" must arise to accomplish what the law could not. Christ is the true Yehoshua who succeeds where Moses failed, leading His people into the eternal rest promised from the foundation of the world. The promise "I will not leave you or forsake you" reaches its ultimate fulfillment in Jesus, who is "with us always, to the end of the age" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 20|Matthew 28:20]]), never requiring a successor because He lives forever to lead His people into God's eternal Sabbath rest.
