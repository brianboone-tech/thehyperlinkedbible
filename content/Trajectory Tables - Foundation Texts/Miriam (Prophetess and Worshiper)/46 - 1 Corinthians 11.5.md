### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11#1 Corinthians 11 . 5|1 Corinthians 11:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4336|G4336]] προσεύχομαι (*proseuchomai*) - "to pray"
- [[Lexicon/G4395|G4395]] προφητεύω (*prophēteuō*) - "to prophesy"
- [[Lexicon/G2776|G2776]] κεφαλή (*kephalē*) - "head" (both literal and metaphorical)
- [[Lexicon/G2619|G2619]] κατακαλύπτω (*katakalyptō*) - "to cover, veil"
- [[Lexicon/G2617|G2617]] καταισχύνω (*kataischynō*) - "to dishonor, shame"

**Context**: Paul addresses worship practices in Corinthian church, specifically head covering for women who pray or prophesy publicly. Critical for our purposes: Paul assumes women DO pray and prophesy in gathered worship - the question is HOW (with head covered), not WHETHER. This confirms Acts 2:17-18's fulfillment continues in church practice. The passage balances two truths: (1) women exercise prophetic gifts publicly, and (2) they do so in way that honors created order and headship principle.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- From Miriam leading women separately ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 20|Exodus 15:20-21]]) to women participating in mixed corporate worship
- From exceptional prophetesses (Miriam, Deborah, Huldah) to normative spiritual gift ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17-18]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 6|Romans 12:6]])
- From gender-segregated worship patterns to unified yet distinct participation

**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. Miriam's prophetic worship leadership anticipates normative NT pattern, though with different cultural expressions.

**Christological Connection**: Christ poured out His Spirit on the church ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]]), gifting both men and women for prophetic witness. As Miriam led worship celebrating Red Sea deliverance, Spirit-empowered women lead worship celebrating Christ's greater redemption. The trajectory: **Miriam leads women** (segregated) → **Joel promises daughters will prophesy** → **Pentecost fulfills promise** → **Women prophesy in church worship** (1 Cor 11:5) → **Perfect worship in new creation** (Rev 15:3). Christ as head of church ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 23|Ephesians 5:23]]) delegates prophetic ministry to entire body, male and female, while maintaining loving order that reflects His relationship with church.
