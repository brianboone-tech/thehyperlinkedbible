### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 20|Exodus 15:20-21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5031|H5031]] נְבִיאָה (*nebi'ah*) - "prophetess" (feminine form of prophet)
- [[Lexicon/H8596|H8596]] תֹּף (*toph*) - "tambourine, hand drum"
- [[Lexicon/H4246|H4246]] מְחֹלָה (*mecholah*) - "dancing, dance"
- [[Lexicon/H7891|H7891]] שִׁיר (*shir*) - "to sing"
- [[Lexicon/H1342|H1342]] גָּאָה (*ga'ah*) - "to be exalted, rise up, triumph"

**Context**: Immediately after Moses' song celebrating Israel's deliverance through the Red Sea (Exodus 15:1-19), Miriam leads the women in antiphonal worship. She is identified by three titles: "Miriam the prophetess" (first occurrence of this designation for a woman), "Aaron's sister" (connecting her to priestly line), and leader of women's worship. Taking tambourine in hand, she leads responsive singing and dancing - a pattern of corporate worship celebration.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This is the first explicit designation of a woman as "prophetess" in Scripture
- Miriam's song echoes Moses' opening line (15:1), establishing pattern of antiphonal worship
- Later prophetesses include Deborah ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 4#Judges 4 . 4|Judges 4:4]]), Huldah ([[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 22#2 Kings 22 . 14|2 Kings 22:14]]), and Isaiah's wife ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 8#Isaiah 8 . 3|Isaiah 8:3]])
- Women leading worship with tambourines and dancing becomes recurring pattern ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 18#1 Samuel 18 . 6|1 Samuel 18:6-7]]; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 68#Psalm 68 . 25|Psalm 68:25]])

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - Women's role in worship anticipated in Eve's naming as "mother of all living" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 20|Genesis 3:20]]); Hannah's song ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 1|1 Samuel 2:1-10]]) follows similar pattern
- **FROM OT**:
  - Deborah's victory song (Judges 5); women celebrating David's victories ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 18#1 Samuel 18 . 6|1 Samuel 18:6-7]])
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 68#Psalm 68 . 11|Psalm 68:11]] - "The Lord gives the command; the women who proclaim the good tidings are a great host"
- **FROM NT**:
  - Mary's Magnificat ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 46|Luke 1:46-55]]) echoes structure
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17-18]] - fulfills prophesying daughters; women praying/prophesying in Corinth ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11#1 Corinthians 11 . 5|1 Corinthians 11:5]]
**Type Classification**: Backward-looking Providential Type. While Miriam's prophetic office was genuine in her time, its typological significance pointing to NT prophesying women is recognized from apostolic interpretation (Acts 2:17-18).

**Christological Connection**: Miriam's song celebrates God's victory over enemies through water of judgment/deliverance. This points to Christ's victory through baptismal waters of death and resurrection. As Miriam led Israel's women in praise after Red Sea deliverance, the church (bride of Christ) leads redeemed humanity in worship after Christ's greater exodus. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 15#Revelation 15 . 3|Revelation 15:3]] merges "song of Moses" (which Miriam sang) with "song of the Lamb" - uniting exodus deliverance with Christ's redemption in one eternal chorus.
