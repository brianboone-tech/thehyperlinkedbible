### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 17|Acts 2:17-18]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4396|G4396]] προφήτης (*prophētēs*) - "prophet"
- [[Lexicon/G4395|G4395]] προφητεύω (*prophēteuō*) - "to prophesy"
- [[Lexicon/G2364|G2364]] θυγάτηρ (*thugatēr*) - "daughter"
- [[Lexicon/G1632|G1632]] ἐκχέω (*ekcheō*) - "to pour out"
- [[Lexicon/G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα (*pneuma*) - "Spirit"

**Context**: On Day of Pentecost, after Holy Spirit's visible descent and disciples' speaking in tongues, Peter explains the phenomenon by quoting Joel 2:28-29. He declares "this is what was spoken by the prophet Joel" - identifying Pentecost as fulfillment of OT promise. The key expansion from Joel: "in the last days" (eschatological timeframe), and emphasis on universal Spirit outpouring across gender, age, and social status. "Your sons and daughters will prophesy" validates women's prophetic ministry in church age.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-29]] promised Spirit outpouring on "all flesh" - democratizing prophecy beyond select individuals
- OT prophetesses were exceptional (Miriam, Deborah, Huldah) - now becomes normative
- What was rare gift for few women becomes common reality for many

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking Direct Type. Joel explicitly predicted this outpouring; Peter declares its fulfillment at Pentecost.

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the Spirit-baptizer ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 11|Matthew 3:11]]; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 5|Acts 1:5]]). His ascension and exaltation led to Spirit's outpouring ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33]]). The Spirit's work is to glorify Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 14|John 16:14]]), so prophecy in church age testifies to Jesus ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 10|Revelation 19:10]] - "testimony of Jesus is spirit of prophecy"). Miriam's isolated prophetic ministry finds fulfillment in Christ's gift of Spirit to all believers - making entire church a prophetic community bearing witness to Him. The pattern escalates: **one prophetess** (Miriam) → **promise of many** (Joel) → **fulfillment for all** (Pentecost) → **ongoing reality** (church age) → **perfect worship** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 15#Revelation 15 . 3|Revelation 15:3]]).
