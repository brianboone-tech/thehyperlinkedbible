### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 11|Hebrews 13:11-12]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3901-4000#G3925|G3925]] παρεμβολή (parembolē) - camp, barracks, encampment
- [[Lexicon/G4301-4400#G4439|G4439]] πύλη (pylē) - gate, door
- [[Lexicon/G2601-2700#G2618|G2618]] κατακαίω (katakaiō) - to burn up, consume entirely
- [[Lexicon/G1801-1900#G1854|G1854]] ἔξω (exō) - outside, without

**Context**: The author of Hebrews argues that Jesus fulfilled the Levitical typology by suffering "outside the gate" of Jerusalem. Just as sin offering bodies were burned outside Israel's camp, so Jesus died outside the city walls—in the place of rejection and judgment.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 27|Leviticus 16:27]] - Day of Atonement bodies burned outside the camp (directly cited)
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 12|Leviticus 4:12]] - Regular sin offering bodies burned outside
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 3|Numbers 19:3]] - Red heifer burned outside the camp

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 27|Leviticus 16:27]] - Explicit typological reference
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 12|Leviticus 4:12]] - Sin offering procedure
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 20|John 19:17, 20]] - "The place where Jesus was crucified was near the city"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 58|Acts 7:58]] - Stephen stoned "outside the city"

**Type Classification**: **Direct/Fulfilled** - The NT explicitly identifies Jesus' crucifixion location as the antitype of Levitical burning outside the camp

**Christological Connection**: **CRITICAL NT FULFILLMENT** - This is Hebrews' definitive interpretation of the "outside the camp" typology. The author makes several key moves:

1. **Location Equivalence**: The "camp" (παρεμβολή) becomes Jerusalem's "gate" (πύλη)—the sacred geography transfers from tabernacle/camp to temple/city
2. **Purpose Continuity**: "In order to sanctify the people through his own blood" (v.12)—same sanctifying purpose as Levitical sacrifices
3. **Typological Clarity**: The Levitical bodies were burned "outside the camp"; Jesus "suffered outside the gate"—the same spatial theology of rejection

The implication is profound: Jesus occupied the place of maximum rejection from God's presence—the ash heap where sin-bearing victims were consumed—so that His blood could bring maximum access to God's presence (10:19-22).
