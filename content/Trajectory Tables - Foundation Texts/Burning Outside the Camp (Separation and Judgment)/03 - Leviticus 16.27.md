### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 27|Leviticus 16:27-28]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4201-4300#H4264|H4264]] מַחֲנֶה (machaneh) - camp, encampment
- [[Lexicon/H2301-2400#H2351|H2351]] חוּץ (chuts) - outside, abroad
- [[Lexicon/H8201-8300#H8313|H8313]] שָׂרַף (saraph) - to burn completely
- [[Lexicon/H5601-5700#H5638|H5638]] עוֹר (or) - skin, hide

**Context**: On the annual Day of Atonement, after the high priest makes atonement in the Most Holy Place with the blood of the bull (for himself) and goat (for the people), the bodies of these animals are taken outside the camp and burned entirely—skin, flesh, and dung.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 14|Leviticus 16:14-15]] - Blood sprinkled on and before the mercy seat (WITHIN)
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 12|Leviticus 4:12]] - Same pattern established for regular sin offerings
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 28|Leviticus 16:28]] - The one who burns the bodies must wash and is unclean until evening

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 11|Hebrews 13:11-12]] - Hebrews explicitly cites this text, applying it to Christ
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:21-22]] - The scapegoat sent to the wilderness (another "outside" element)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 17|John 19:17, 20]] - Jesus crucified "near the city" at Golgotha

**Type Classification**: **Direct** - The most solemn day of Israel's calendar combined acceptance (blood within) with rejection (body without)

**Christological Connection**: The Day of Atonement perfectly illustrates the inside/outside dynamic. The high priest enters the Most Holy Place (the closest approach to God) with blood that secures acceptance. Meanwhile, the carcasses are burned outside the camp (the farthest from God's presence) in rejection. Christ's work unites both: His blood speaks in heaven (Hebrews 12:24), while His body was given over to the place of rejection outside Jerusalem. Propitiation within, judgment without—both accomplished by one Savior.
