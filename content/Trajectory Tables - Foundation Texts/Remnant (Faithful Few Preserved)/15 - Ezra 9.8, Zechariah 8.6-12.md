### [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 9#Ezra 9 . 8|Ezra 9:8, 13-15]]; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 6|Zechariah 8:6, 11-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7611|H7611]] שְׁאֵרִית (*she'erit*) - remnant, remainder
- [[Lexicon/H6413|H6413]] פְּלֵיטָה (*peletah*) - escape, deliverance
- [[Lexicon/H8281|H8281]] שָׁרָה (*sharah*) - to let loose, release
- [[Lexicon/H1293|H1293]] בְּרָכָה (*berakah*) - blessing

**Context**: Ezra 9:8: "Now for a brief moment favor has been shown by the LORD our God, to leave us a remnant (פְּלֵיטָה) and to give us a secure hold within his holy place." Zechariah 8:6, 11-12: "If it is marvelous in the eyes of the remnant of this people... I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Pre-exilic remnant prophecies → post-exilic remnant reality
- Prophetic promise becomes historical fulfillment (partial)
- Small returnee community experiences promises in miniature

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Jeremiah's promise of 70-year restoration (Jer 25:11-12)
- **TO**: Isaiah's second exodus (Isa 11:11)
- **FROM OT**: Post-exilic community as remnant fulfillment
- **FROM NT**: Jewish believers as continuation of faithful remnant
- **FROM NT**: Church as spiritual remnant community

**Type Classification**: Partial fulfillment pointing to ultimate fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: The post-exilic community, though physically returned, awaited a greater restoration. Jesus comes to this remnant community ("I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel," Matt 15:24). The believing Jews who receive Him become the nucleus of the expanded remnant. The "brief moment" of favor in Ezra becomes "the fullness of time" in Christ (Gal 4:4). The partial return points to the ultimate return to God through the Messiah.
