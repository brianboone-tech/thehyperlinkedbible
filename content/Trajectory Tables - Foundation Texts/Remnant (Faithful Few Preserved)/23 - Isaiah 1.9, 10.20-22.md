### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 1#Isaiah 1 . 9|Isaiah 1:9]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 20|Isaiah 10:20-22]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8300|H8300]] שָׂרִיד (*sarid*) - survivor, remnant
- [[Lexicon/H7605|H7605]] שְׁאָר (*she'ar*) - remnant, remainder
- [[Lexicon/H7725|H7725]] שׁוּב (*shuv*) - to return
- [[Lexicon/H410|H410]] אֵל (*El*) - God (in "El Gibbor" - Mighty God)

**Context**: Isaiah 1:9: "Unless the LORD of hosts had left us a few survivors (שָׂרִיד), we should have been like Sodom." Isaiah 10:20-22: "A remnant (שְׁאָר) will return (יָשׁוּב), the remnant of Jacob, to the mighty God (אֵל גִּבּוֹר)." Isaiah's son is named שְׁאָר יָשׁוּב (*Shear-jashub*) - "A Remnant Shall Return" (7:3).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Elijah's 7,000 → Isaiah's "shear-jashub"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 1#Isaiah 1 . 9|Isaiah 1:9]] directly connects to Sodom tradition
- Remnant concept becomes central prophetic theme

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Sodom's destruction (Gen 19) - total judgment without remnant
- **TO**: Elijah's 7,000 ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 18|1 Kings 19:18]])
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 13|Isaiah 6:13]] - "holy seed is its stump"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 11|Isaiah 11:11-16]] - second exodus of remnant
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 27|Romans 9:27-29]] - Paul quotes [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 22|Isaiah 10:22-23]] and 1:9

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - explicit prophetic announcement

**Christological Connection**: The "mighty God" (אֵל גִּבּוֹר) to whom the remnant returns is the same title given to the Messianic child in [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6]]. The remnant returns to their Messiah, the divine King. Paul interprets this as fulfilled in Jewish believers who receive Jesus - they are the "shear-jashub" who have returned to God through Christ. The remnant concept is inseparable from Messianic hope.
