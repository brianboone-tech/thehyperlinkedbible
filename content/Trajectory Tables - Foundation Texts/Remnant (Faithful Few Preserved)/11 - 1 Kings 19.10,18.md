### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 10|1 Kings 19:10]]; [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 18|1 Kings 19:18]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7604|H7604]] שָׁאַר (*sha'ar*) - to remain, be left
- [[Lexicon/H3498|H3498]] יָתַר (*yatar*) - to leave remaining
- [[Lexicon/H7651|H7651]] שֶׁבַע (*sheva*) - seven (number of completeness)
- [[Lexicon/H505|H505]] אֶלֶף (*eleph*) - thousand

**Context**: After Mount Carmel, Elijah flees Jezebel and despairs: "I alone am left, and they seek my life" (19:10). God reveals: "I will leave seven thousand in Israel, all the knees that have not bowed to Baal" (19:18). The prophet perceives total apostasy, but God has preserved a faithful remnant invisible to human observation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Noah's 8 → 7,000 - remnant grows
- Elijah's hidden remnant → Isaiah's visible "holy seed" (6:13)
- Pattern of divine preservation through apostasy

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Noah's family (Gen 7:23) - first remnant
- **TO**: Lot's family (Gen 19) - preserved from Sodom
- **FROM OT**: Isaiah's remnant theology (Isa 1:9; 10:20-22)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 4|Romans 11:4]] - Paul quotes this exact passage
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 5|Romans 11:5]] - "So too at the present time there is a remnant"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - God's hidden work of preservation

**Christological Connection**: The 7,000 point forward to the believing remnant who recognize Jesus as Messiah. Paul's use in Romans 11 is explicit: just as God preserved 7,000 in Elijah's day, so now He has preserved a remnant of Jewish believers who have not rejected Christ. The church's Jewish nucleus (apostles, early Jerusalem believers) represents this remnant, and through them the gospel goes to all nations.
