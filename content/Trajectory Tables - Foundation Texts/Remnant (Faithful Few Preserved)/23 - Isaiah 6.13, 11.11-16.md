### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 13|Isaiah 6:13]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 11|Isaiah 11:11-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (*zera*) - seed, offspring
- [[Lexicon/H6944|H6944]] קֹדֶשׁ (*qodesh*) - holy
- [[Lexicon/H7605|H7605]] שְׁאָר (*she'ar*) - remnant
- [[Lexicon/H8145|H8145]] שֵׁנִי (*sheni*) - second (second exodus)

**Context**: Isaiah 6:13: After judgment reduces the land to a tenth, and even that is burned, "The holy seed (זֶרַע קֹדֶשׁ) is its stump." Life emerges from apparent death. Isaiah 11:11-16: "The Lord will extend his hand yet a second time (שֵׁנִית) to recover the remnant... He will raise a signal for the nations and will assemble the banished of Israel."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "Holy seed" connects to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] seed promise
- "Second time" explicitly recalls first exodus
- New exodus as remnant-gathering motif

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - seed of the woman
- **TO**: Exodus - first deliverance from Egypt
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 14|Isaiah 43:14-21]] - new exodus theme
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 26|Romans 11:26]] - "all Israel will be saved"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 4|Revelation 7:4-9]] - ingathering from all nations

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - explicit eschatological promise

**Christological Connection**: The "holy seed" from the stump points to Christ, the Branch from Jesse's stump (11:1). The "second exodus" is accomplished through Christ who leads His people out of spiritual bondage greater than Egypt. The worldwide gathering of the remnant "from the four corners of the earth" (11:12) is fulfilled as the gospel goes to all nations and gathers the elect from everywhere into one body in Christ.
