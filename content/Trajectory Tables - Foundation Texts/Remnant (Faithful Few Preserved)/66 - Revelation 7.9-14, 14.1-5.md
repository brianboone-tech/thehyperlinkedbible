### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9-10, 14]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 1|Revelation 14:1-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3793|G3793]] ὄχλος (*ochlos*) - crowd, multitude
- [[Lexicon/G4973|G4973]] σφραγίς (*sphragis*) - seal (144,000 sealed)
- [[Lexicon/G5443|G5443]] φυλή (*phyle*) - tribe
- [[Lexicon/G536|G536]] ἀπαρχή (*aparche*) - firstfruits

**Context**: Revelation 7 presents two groups: the 144,000 sealed from the twelve tribes of Israel (7:4-8) and "a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages" (7:9). They are those "coming out of the great tribulation" who have "washed their robes... in the blood of the Lamb" (7:14). Revelation 14:1-5 shows the 144,000 as "firstfruits (ἀπαρχή) for God and the Lamb."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Noah's 8 → 7,000 → 144,000 → innumerable multitude
- Remnant expands from family to worldwide gathering
- Final preservation through ultimate judgment

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Noah's family (Gen 7:23) - first preserved through judgment
- **TO**: Isaiah's remnant from all nations (Isa 11:11-12)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 32|Joel 2:32]] - "everyone who calls on the name of the LORD shall be saved"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 13|Ephesians 1:13-14]] - sealed with the Holy Spirit
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 19|2 Timothy 2:19]] - "The Lord knows those who are his"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - remnant finally complete

**Christological Connection**: The innumerable multitude stands "before the throne and before the Lamb" (7:9) - their preservation is entirely because of Christ. They "have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb" (7:14). The remnant's purity is Christ's righteousness imputed and applied. The 144,000 "follow the Lamb wherever he goes" (14:4). From Noah's family to this vast assembly, every remnant finds its unity in the Lamb who was slain.
