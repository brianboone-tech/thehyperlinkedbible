### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 25|Romans 11:25-26]]; [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|Galatians 3:29]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4138|G4138]] πλήρωμα (*pleroma*) - fullness, full number
- [[Lexicon/G4690|G4690]] σπέρμα (*sperma*) - seed, offspring
- [[Lexicon/G2818|G2818]] κληρονόμος (*kleronomos*) - heir
- [[Lexicon/G1484|G1484]] ἔθνος (*ethnos*) - nation, Gentile

**Context**: Romans 11:25-26: "A partial hardening has come upon Israel, until the fullness of the Gentiles (τὸ πλήρωμα τῶν ἐθνῶν) has come in. And in this way all Israel will be saved." Galatians 3:29: "If you are Christ's, then you are Abraham's offspring (σπέρμα), heirs according to promise."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Abrahamic promise of "all nations blessed" (Gen 12:3)
- Isaiah's "light to the nations" (Isa 49:6)
- Now fulfilled as Gentiles become Abraham's seed

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]] - all families blessed
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 6|Isaiah 49:6]] - light to the nations
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 10|Isaiah 11:10]] - root of Jesse, signal for peoples
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 11|Ephesians 2:11-22]] - Gentiles brought near, one new man
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 3#Ephesians 3 . 6|Ephesians 3:6]] - Gentiles fellow heirs

**Type Classification**: NT Superiority - remnant expanded beyond ethnic Israel

**Christological Connection**: The expansion of the remnant happens "in Christ" (Gal 3:28-29). Christ is THE seed of Abraham (Gal 3:16), and all who belong to Christ share His identity as Abraham's offspring. The remnant, once limited to faithful Israelites, now includes believers from every nation - all united by faith in Christ. The "all Israel" saved (Rom 11:26) is the full number of elect from Jew and Gentile.
