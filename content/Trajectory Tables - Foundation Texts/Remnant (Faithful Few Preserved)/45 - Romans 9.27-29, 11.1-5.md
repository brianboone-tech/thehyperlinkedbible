### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 27|Romans 9:27-29]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 1|Romans 11:1-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3005|G3005]] λεῖμμα (*leimma*) - remnant
- [[Lexicon/G5259|G5259]] ὑπόλειμμα (*hypoleimma*) - what is left, remnant
- [[Lexicon/G1589|G1589]] ἐκλογή (*ekloge*) - election, choice
- [[Lexicon/G5485|G5485]] χάρις (*charis*) - grace

**Context**: Romans 9:27-29 quotes Isaiah: "Though the number of the sons of Israel be as the sand of the sea, only a remnant (ὑπόλειμμα) of them will be saved." Romans 11:4-5 quotes 1 Kings 19:18 about the 7,000, concluding: "So too at the present time there is a remnant (λεῖμμα), chosen by grace (κατ᾽ ἐκλογὴν χάριτος)."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Paul draws Isaiah and Elijah remnant traditions together
- OT remnant → Jewish believers in Jesus = present remnant
- Election by grace as basis of remnant identity

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 22|Isaiah 10:22-23]] - only remnant saved (quoted in 9:27)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 1#Isaiah 1 . 9|Isaiah 1:9]] - survivors left (quoted in 9:29)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 18|1 Kings 19:18]] - 7,000 preserved (quoted in 11:4)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 25|Romans 11:25-26]] - fullness of Gentiles; all Israel saved
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 6#Galatians 6 . 16|Galatians 6:16]] - "the Israel of God"

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - OT remnant finds present expression

**Christological Connection**: Paul's argument is Christological: the remnant are those who have received Jesus as Messiah. "Election by grace" (11:5) is election in Christ. Paul himself exemplifies the remnant - a zealous Israelite who now believes in Jesus (11:1). The Jewish believers in Jesus are direct continuation of the OT faithful remnant. Christ is both the criterion and the gatherer of the remnant.
