### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9-10]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22-24]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1085|G1085]] γένος (*genos*) - race, people
- [[Lexicon/G1588|G1588]] ἐκλεκτός (*eklektos*) - chosen, elect
- [[Lexicon/G2992|G2992]] λαός (*laos*) - people
- [[Lexicon/G1577|G1577]] ἐκκλησία (*ekklesia*) - assembly, church

**Context**: 1 Peter 2:9-10: "You are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for his own possession... Once you were not a people, but now you are God's people (λαὸς θεοῦ)." Hebrews 12:22-24: "You have come to Mount Zion... and to the assembly (ἐκκλησίᾳ) of the firstborn."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5-6]] - Israel as kingdom of priests
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 9|Hosea 1:9]]; 2:23 - "not my people" → "my people"
- Peter applies both to the church

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5-6]] - treasured possession, kingdom of priests
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 20|Isaiah 43:20-21]] - "my chosen people... that they might declare my praise"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 9|Hosea 1:9-10]]; 2:23 - "Not My People" → "My People"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 6|Revelation 1:6]] - "a kingdom, priests to his God"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9-10]] - "from every tribe and language"

**Type Classification**: NT Application - believers as remnant community

**Christological Connection**: The church's identity as "chosen race" and "holy nation" is "in Christ." We are elect because we are united to the Elect One ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4]], "living stone, rejected... but in God's sight chosen"). Hebrews emphasizes that we approach God through Jesus the mediator (12:24). The remnant community exists only because of its connection to Christ. As He is the cornerstone ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 6|1 Peter 2:6]]), we are living stones built on Him.
