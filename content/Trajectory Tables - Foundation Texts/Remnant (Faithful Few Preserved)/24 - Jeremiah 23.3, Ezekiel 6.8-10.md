### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 3|Jeremiah 23:3]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 6#Ezekiel 6 . 8|Ezekiel 6:8-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6908|H6908]] קָבַץ (*qabats*) - to gather, assemble
- [[Lexicon/H7605|H7605]] שְׁאָר (*she'ar*) - remnant
- [[Lexicon/H6412|H6412]] פָּלִיט (*palit*) - fugitive, escaped one
- [[Lexicon/H2719|H2719]] חֶרֶב (*cherev*) - sword

**Context**: Jeremiah 23:3: "I will gather the remnant of my flock out of all the countries where I have driven them, and I will bring them back to their fold." Ezekiel 6:8-10: "Yet I will leave some of you alive. When you have among the nations some who escape the sword, then those of you who escape will remember me."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Isaiah's remnant → Jeremiah/Ezekiel's exilic remnant
- Judgment becomes the means of purification
- Scattering among nations → gathering from nations

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Isaiah's remnant theology (Isa 10:20-22)
- **FROM OT**: Ezekiel 37 - valley of dry bones (remnant restored)
- **FROM OT**: Ezra-Nehemiah - actual return of remnant
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 16|John 10:16]] - "other sheep... one flock, one shepherd"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 12|Ephesians 2:12-19]] - Gentiles brought near; one new man

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - prophetic promise of restoration

**Christological Connection**: God's promise to "gather" (קָבַץ) the remnant is fulfilled when Jesus declares He came "to seek and save the lost" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 19#Luke 19 . 10|Luke 19:10]]) and speaks of "gathering" His elect (Matt 24:31). The good shepherd who gathers scattered sheep (Jer 23:3-4) is explicitly Christ (John 10; Ezek 34:23). The exilic refinement points to how God uses trials to purify His people ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 6|1 Peter 1:6-7]]).
