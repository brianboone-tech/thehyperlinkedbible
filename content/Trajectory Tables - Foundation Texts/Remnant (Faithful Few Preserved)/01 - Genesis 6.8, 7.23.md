### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 8|Genesis 6:8]]; [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 23|Genesis 7:23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2580|H2580]] חֵן (*chen*) - grace, favor
- [[Lexicon/H7604|H7604]] שָׁאַר (*sha'ar*) - to remain, be left over
- [[Lexicon/H6413|H6413]] פְּלֵיטָה (*peletah*) - escape, remnant

**Context**: Genesis 6:8 states "Noah found favor (חֵן) in the eyes of the LORD" amidst universal corruption. Genesis 7:23 declares "Only Noah was left (וַיִשָּׁאֶר), and those who were with him in the ark." This is the first remnant - 8 persons preserved through judgment while all else perishes.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Noah pattern → Lot's family preserved from Sodom (Gen 19)
- Noah pattern → Elijah's 7,000 ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 18|1 Kings 19:18]])
- Noah's "favor" (חֵן) → concept of grace that preserves

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Creation (Gen 1) - Noah represents new beginning after judgment
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 1#Isaiah 1 . 9|Isaiah 1:9]] - "unless the LORD had left us survivors"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 18|1 Kings 19:18]] - 7,000 who have not bowed to Baal
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20]] - "eight persons were brought safely through water"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 5|2 Peter 2:5]] - "preserved Noah, a herald of righteousness, with seven others"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - God sovereignly arranges preservation

**Christological Connection**: Noah prefigures Christ as the one through whom others are saved. As Noah's family was preserved through judgment waters by being "in the ark," so believers are preserved through judgment by being "in Christ." Peter explicitly connects baptism to Noah ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20-21]]). The first remnant points forward to the ultimate remnant - all who trust in Christ are preserved through divine judgment.
