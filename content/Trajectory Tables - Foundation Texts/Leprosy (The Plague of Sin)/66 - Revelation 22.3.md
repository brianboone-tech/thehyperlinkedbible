### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2652-2653#G2652|κατάθεμα]] (*katathema*) - "curse, accursed thing" (nominative neuter)
- [[Lexicon/G2089-2090#G2089|ἔτι]] (*eti*) - "still, any longer" (adverb)
- [[Lexicon/G2362-2363#G2362|θρόνος]] (*thronos*) - "throne" (nominative)
- [[Lexicon/G0721-0722#G0721|ἀρνίου]] (*arniou*) - "Lamb" (genitive)
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1402#G1401|δοῦλοι]] (*douloi*) - "servants, slaves" (nominative plural)
- [[Lexicon/G3000-3001#G3000|λατρεύσουσιν]] (*latreusousin*) - "they will worship, serve" (future active)

**Context**: Revelation 22:3 describes new creation's defining characteristic: "No longer will there be anything accursed, but the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will worship him." The Greek *katathema* ("accursed thing," "curse") recalls Genesis 3's curse and Leviticus' ceremonial uncleanness. Zechariah 14:11 prophesied restored Jerusalem: "there shall never again be a decree of utter destruction. Jerusalem shall dwell in security." Revelation fulfills this globally—not merely Jerusalem but entire new creation free from curse. The contrast is stark: Genesis 3 introduced curse; Revelation 22:3 removes it permanently. Leprosy, the ultimate "accursed" (*ḥērem*) condition requiring isolation (Leviticus 13:46), will not exist. The positive statement—"throne of God and Lamb... servants will worship"—depicts unhindered fellowship. What sin separated (Genesis 3), Christ reunites (Revelation 22).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]'s declaration—"No longer will there be anything accursed, but the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will worship him"—reverses [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]]'s curse. God pronounced: "cursed is the ground because of you... thorns and thistles it shall bring forth... to dust you shall return." The curse pervaded existence: spiritual (separation from God, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]]—cherubim guarding Eden), physical (disease, death), social (isolation—epitomized by lepers dwelling "outside the camp," [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 13#Leviticus 13 . 46|Leviticus 13:46]]), environmental (ground cursed, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17]]). Leprosy represented curse's comprehensive effects: defiling, spreading, isolating, deadly. The leper crying "Unclean! Unclean!" ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 13#Leviticus 13 . 45|Leviticus 13:45]]) dramatized curse's shame and separation. [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 27#Deuteronomy 27 . 26|Deuteronomy 27:26]] expanded: "Cursed be anyone who does not confirm all the words of this law by doing them"—comprehensive condemnation on law-breakers. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 10|Galatians 3:10]] applies: "For all who rely on works of the law are under a curse." Christ redeems by "becoming a curse for us" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]])—bearing curse on cross ("Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree," quoting [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 23|Deuteronomy 21:23]]). His substitutionary death removes curse, enables blessing. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21-22]] anticipates result: "the creation itself will be set free from its bondage to corruption and obtain the freedom of the glory of the children of God. For we know that the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until now." [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]] consummates this: "no longer anything accursed" (*ou... pan katathema eti*). The Greek *katathema* (curse, devoted to destruction) parallels Hebrew *ḥērem* (ban, curse). [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 11|Zechariah 14:11]] prophesied: "there shall never again be a decree of utter destruction (*ḥērem*). Jerusalem shall dwell in security." Revelation applies this globally—entire new creation free from curse. The positive statement—"throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will worship him"—depicts unrestricted fellowship. What curse separated (cherubim guarding Eden, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]]; lepers isolated outside camp, [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 13#Leviticus 13 . 46|Leviticus 13:46]]), Christ reunites. The throne's presence (not hidden in Holy of Holies behind veil) indicates unrestricted access. All believers function as priests (*latreusousin*, "they will serve/worship"—priestly term), fulfilling [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]: "you shall be to me a kingdom of priests." The trajectory shows: Genesis 3 introduces curse (separation, death, environmental corruption) → Leviticus 13-14 epitomizes curse through leprosy (defilement, isolation) → [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 11|Zechariah 14:11]] promises curse's removal → Christ bears curse on cross ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]]) → inaugurates new creation ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]]) → [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21-22]] anticipates creation's liberation → [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]] consummates: curse permanently removed, unhindered worship established. No more leprosy, no more "Unclean!" cries, no more isolation—only eternal fellowship with God and Lamb.
