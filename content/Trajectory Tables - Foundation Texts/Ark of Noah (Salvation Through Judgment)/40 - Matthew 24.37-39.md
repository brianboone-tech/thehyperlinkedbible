### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 37|Matthew 24:37-39]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3952|G3952]] παρουσία (*parousia*) - "coming, arrival, presence" (the coming of the Son of Man)
- [[Lexicon/G2250|G2250]] ἡμέρα (*hēmera*) - "day, time" (days of Noah, day Noah entered ark)
- [[Lexicon/G2627|G2627]] κατακλυσμός (*kataklysmos*) - "flood, deluge, inundation" (the flood came)
- [[Lexicon/G142|G142]] αἴρω (*airō*) - "to take away, remove, destroy" (took them all away)
- [[Lexicon/G1097|G1097]] γινώσκω (*ginōskō*) - "to know, perceive, understand" (they knew nothing)

**Context**: Matthew 24:37-39 appears in Jesus' Olivet Discourse answering the disciples' question: "What will be the sign of your coming and of the end of the age?" (v. 3). Jesus warns of false messiahs (vv. 4-5), wars (vv. 6-7), persecution (vv. 9-14), tribulation (vv. 15-28), cosmic signs (vv. 29-31), and then addresses the suddenness and unexpectedness of His return using Noah's flood as the paradigm: "As it was in the days of Noah, so it will be at the coming of the Son of Man." The comparison focuses on normalcy bias (eating, drinking, marrying), ignorance despite warning, and sudden inescapable judgment.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 11|Genesis 7:11-23]] describes the flood's sudden onset: "On that very day" (v. 13) the flood came, and "everything on dry land that had the breath of life in its nostrils died" (v. 22).
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 3|Genesis 6:3]] records God's warning: "My Spirit will not contend with man forever... his days shall be 120 years"—a 120-year warning period that the wicked ignored.
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 20|Isaiah 26:20-21]] uses similar language: "Hide yourselves for a little while until the wrath has passed by. For behold, the LORD is coming out from his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth."

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 6:3 (120-year warning); Genesis 7:1-4 (seven-day final warning); Genesis 7:11-23 (sudden flood, universal destruction); Isaiah 26:20-21 (hiding until wrath passes)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 17#Luke 17 . 26|Luke 17:26-27]] - parallel account
  - [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 3|2 Peter 3:3-10]] - scoffers saying "where is the promise?"
  - [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 2|1 Thessalonians 5:2-3]] - day of the Lord comes like thief in night
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 3|Revelation 3:3]] - I will come like a thief
- **RELATED NT**: Matthew 24:42-44 (watch therefore, for you do not know what day your Lord is coming); Matthew 25:1-13 (parable of ten virgins—door shut)
**Type Classification**: **Providential Type** (sovereignly arranged event) explicitly identified by Jesus as **Forward-Looking** (eschatological pattern).

**Christological Connection**: (1) **Christ as Judge** - As God judged in Noah's day, Christ (Son of Man) will judge at His parousia—asserting deity and judicial authority; (2) **Ark prefigures Christ** - Those who entered ark were saved; those who enter Christ (believe) will be saved from coming wrath ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 1#1 Thessalonians 1 . 10|1 Thessalonians 1:10]]); (3) **Door shut by God** - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 16|Genesis 7:16]] records "the LORD shut him in"—once shut, entrance was impossible. Jesus warns: "I will shut the door" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 10|Matthew 25:10]]; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 13#Luke 13 . 25|Luke 13:25]]). The time of opportunity ends; (4) **Normalcy before catastrophe** - The world's complacency before Christ's return mirrors pre-flood complacency, demonstrating humanity's unchanged nature; (5) **Sudden, discriminating judgment** - The flood "took them all away" (v. 39) suddenly and universally, prefiguring Christ's return when "one will be taken and the other left" (vv. 40-41)—final discrimination between righteous and wicked.
