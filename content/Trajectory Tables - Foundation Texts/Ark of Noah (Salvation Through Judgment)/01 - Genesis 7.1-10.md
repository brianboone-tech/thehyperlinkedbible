### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 1|Genesis 7:1-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H935|H935]] בּוֹא (*bôʾ*) - "come, enter" (divine invitation to enter the ark)
- [[Lexicon/H6662|H6662]] צַדִּיק (*ṣaddîq*) - "righteous, just" (Noah found righteous before God)
- [[Lexicon/H1755|H1755]] דּוֹר (*dôr*) - "generation" (Noah righteous in his generation)
- [[Lexicon/H5462|H5462]] סָגַר (*sāḡar*) - "to shut, close" (the LORD shut him in, v. 16)
- [[Lexicon/H1004|H1004]] בַּיִת (*bayiṯ*) - "house, household" (all your household)

**Context**: Genesis 7:1-10 records the critical transition from ark construction to ark entrance. God commands Noah: "Enter the ark, you and all your household" (v. 1), declaring the reason: "because I have found you righteous in this generation." Seven days remain before judgment (v. 4). Noah obeys, entering with his wife, three sons, and their wives—eight souls total (v. 7). Clean and unclean animals enter in prescribed numbers (vv. 2-3, 8-9). The climactic verse: "Then the LORD shut him in" (v. 16). This divine sealing secures those inside while excluding those outside.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 20|Isaiah 26:20]] directly echoes this pattern: "Come, my people, enter your chambers, and shut your doors behind you; hide yourselves for a little while until the wrath has passed by." The vocabulary matches: בּוֹא (enter), סָגַר (shut), עַד (until wrath passes).
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 91#Psalm 91 . 1|Psalm 91:1-4]] develops the refuge theme: "He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High... He will cover you with his feathers, and under his wings you will find refuge."
- The seven-day warning (v. 4) becomes pattern for final warning before judgment throughout Scripture.

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (divinely commanded entrance) and **Forward-Looking** (the temporal urgency and divine sealing point forward to eschatological realities).

**Christological Connection**: (1) **Divine invitation** - "Come" (בּוֹא) anticipates Christ's gospel call: "Come to me, all you who are weary" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]); (2) **Righteousness required** - Noah found righteous through faith ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 7|Hebrews 11:7]]) prefigures believers declared righteous through faith in Christ ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 1|Romans 5:1]]); (3) **One door shut by God** - Just as the LORD shut Noah in (v. 16), God seals believers in Christ ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 13|Ephesians 1:13-14]], "sealed with the Holy Spirit"); (4) **Household salvation** - Noah's family saved with him prefigures covenant promise "you and your household" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 16#Acts 16 . 31|Acts 16:31]]); (5) **Seven-day warning** - The final warning period before inescapable judgment prefigures the gospel age as time of patience before Christ's return ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 9|2 Peter 3:9]], "The Lord is not slow... but is patient").
