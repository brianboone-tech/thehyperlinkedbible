### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 13|Genesis 6:13-22]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8392|H8392]] תֵּבָה (*tēḇâ*) - "ark, box, chest" (used only of Noah's ark and Moses' basket)
- [[Lexicon/H1613|H1613]] גֹּפֶר (*gōp̄er*) - "gopher wood" (wood type for ark construction, possibly cypress)
- [[Lexicon/H3722|H3722]] כָּפַר (*kāp̄ar*) - "to cover, make atonement" (used for pitching ark and atoning for sin)
- [[Lexicon/H6607|H6607]] פֶּתַח (*p̄eṯaḥ*) - "door, entrance" (the ark's one door)
- [[Lexicon/H6680|H6680]] צָוָה (*ṣāwâ*) - "to command" (God's authoritative instruction)

**Context**: Genesis 6:13-22 records God's command to Noah to build an ark as the sole means of deliverance from the coming flood judgment. This follows the account of universal human wickedness (6:5-7) and God's grief over creation's corruption (6:6), contrasted with Noah who "found favor in the eyes of the LORD" (6:8) and was "a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time" (6:9). God reveals His plan: "I am going to bring floodwaters on the earth to destroy all life under the heavens" (6:17), but provides escape: "Make yourself an ark" (6:14). The passage emphasizes precise obedience: "Noah did everything just as God commanded him" (6:22).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 9|Isaiah 54:9]] later references "the waters of Noah" and God's oath "never again," establishing the flood as covenant precedent
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 20|Isaiah 26:20]] echoes the ark pattern: "Come, my people, enter your chambers and shut your doors behind you; hide yourselves for a little while until the wrath has passed by"
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 14#Ezekiel 14 . 14|Ezekiel 14:14]], 20 invokes Noah alongside Daniel and Job as examples of personal righteousness that saves the individual but cannot avert corporate judgment

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (explicitly commanded by God) and **Forward-Looking** (Hebrews 11:7 shows Noah acted by faith with eschatological vision; 1 Peter 3:20-21 uses technical typological language ἀντίτυπον).

**Christological Connection**: The ark points to Christ in multiple ways: (1) **One door** (v. 16) - Christ declares "I am the door" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 9|John 10:9]]), exclusive entrance to salvation; (2) **Divine design** - salvation is God's provision, not human invention; (3) **Refuge from wrath** - as the ark sheltered from judgment waters, Christ shelters from God's wrath against sin; (4) **Pitched/covered with atonement** - the verb כָּפַר used for waterproofing the ark is the same word used for "make atonement," foreshadowing Christ's atoning work that makes believers secure from judgment; (5) **Perfect obedience** - Noah's obedience to God's command prefigures Christ's perfect obedience, and believers' faith-response to God's gospel command.
