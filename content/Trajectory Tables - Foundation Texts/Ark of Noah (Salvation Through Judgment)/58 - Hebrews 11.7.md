### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 7|Hebrews 11:7]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4102|G4102]] πίστις (*pistis*) - "faith, trust, belief" (by faith Noah...)
- [[Lexicon/G5537|G5537]] χρηματίζω (*chrēmatizō*) - "to warn, admonish, receive divine communication" (being warned by God)
- [[Lexicon/G2680|G2680]] κατασκευάζω (*kataskeuazō*) - "to construct, prepare, build" (constructed an ark)
- [[Lexicon/G4991|G4991]] σωτηρία (*sōtēria*) - "salvation, deliverance, preservation" (for the saving of his household)
- [[Lexicon/G2632|G2632]] κατακρίνω (*katakrinō*) - "to condemn, judge against" (condemned the world)
- [[Lexicon/G2818|G2818]] κληρονόμος (*klēronomos*) - "heir, inheritor" (became heir)
- [[Lexicon/G1343|G1343]] δικαιοσύνη (*dikaiosynē*) - "righteousness, justice" (of the righteousness that is by faith)

**Context**: Hebrews 11:7 provides THE definitive NT interpretation of Noah's ark-building. It appears in the "hall of faith" chapter, between Abel's sacrifice by faith (v. 4) and Abraham's obedience by faith (vv. 8-10). The verse unpacks the theological meaning: "By faith Noah, being warned by God concerning events as yet unseen, in reverent fear constructed an ark for the saving of his household. By this he condemned the world and became an heir of the righteousness that comes by faith." Five key elements: (1) divine warning about unseen judgment, (2) faith-response in reverent fear, (3) obedient construction of salvation-means, (4) discriminating result (household saved, world condemned), (5) inheritance of faith-righteousness.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 13|Genesis 6:13-22]] records God's warning and Noah's obedient ark-construction: "Noah did everything just as God commanded him" (v. 22).
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 9|Genesis 6:9]] describes Noah as "a righteous man, blameless in his generation; Noah walked with God." Hebrews interprets this righteousness as "by faith" (11:7), connecting to [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 4|Habakkuk 2:4]], "the righteous shall live by faith."
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 1|Genesis 7:1]] records God's declaration: "I have seen that you are righteous before me in this generation"—Hebrews identifies this as inherited righteousness through faith, not earned merit.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 6:9 (Noah was righteous); Genesis 6:13-22 (God warned, Noah built); Genesis 7:1 (you are righteous); Habakkuk 2:4 (righteous by faith)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 3|Romans 4:3-5]] - Abraham's faith counted as righteousness, pattern for all who believe
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 1|Romans 5:1]] - justified by faith
  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20]] - eight souls saved through water
  - [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 5|2 Peter 2:5]] - Noah, herald of righteousness
- **RELATED NT**: Hebrews 11:1 (faith is assurance of things hoped for, conviction of things not seen); Hebrews 11:6 (without faith impossible to please God)
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (divinely commanded ark) and **Forward-Looking** (Hebrews explicitly identifies faith-obedience as the essential element, pointing forward to faith in Christ).

**Christological Connection**: (1) **Divine warning about judgment** - As God warned Noah about coming flood, gospel warns of coming wrath ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 5|Romans 2:5]]; [[Readable Bible/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 1#2 Thessalonians 1 . 7|2 Thessalonians 1:7-9]]); (2) **Faith in God's provision** - Noah's faith in the ark prefigures faith in Christ, God's appointed means of salvation; (3) **Condemned the world** - As Noah's obedience condemned unbelieving generation, Christ's obedience condemns those who reject Him ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 18|John 3:18-19]]); (4) **Heir of righteousness** - Noah inherited righteousness by faith, prefiguring believers' imputed righteousness through faith in Christ ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 3|Romans 4:3-5]], 22-24); (5) **Reverent fear** - Noah's εὐλαβηθείς (godly fear) prefigures believers' response to grace ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 28|Hebrews 12:28]], "let us offer to God acceptable worship, with reverence and awe [μετὰ εὐλαβείας]").
