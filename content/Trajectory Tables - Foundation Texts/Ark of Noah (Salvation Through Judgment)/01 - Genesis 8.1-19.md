### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 1|Genesis 8:1-19]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2142|H2142]] זָכַר (*zāḵar*) - "to remember" (God remembered Noah, v. 1)
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (*rûaḥ*) - "wind, spirit, breath" (God made a wind blow over the earth)
- [[Lexicon/H5117|H5117]] נוּחַ (*nûaḥ*) - "to rest, settle" (the ark came to rest on Ararat, v. 4)
- [[Lexicon/H3123|H3123]] יוֹנָה (*yônâ*) - "dove" (clean bird sent out to test for dry land)
- [[Lexicon/H6213|H6213]] עָשָׂה (*ʿāśâ*) - "to make, do" (Noah built an altar, v. 20)

**Context**: Genesis 8:1-19 describes the flood's recession and Noah's emergence into a cleansed world. The turning point: "God remembered Noah" (v. 1). God makes wind blow, waters recede, fountains of deep and floodgates of heaven close (vv. 1-2). The ark rests on Mount Ararat (v. 4). Noah sends out raven and dove to test for dry land (vv. 6-12). God commands: "Come out of the ark" (v. 16). Noah emerges and immediately builds an altar, offering burnt offerings (v. 20). The Lord smells the pleasing aroma and promises never again to curse the ground (v. 21).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 2#Exodus 2 . 24|Exodus 2:24]] uses identical language: "God remembered his covenant" when hearing Israel's groaning in Egypt—establishing "God remembered" as covenantal language of deliverance.
- The dove returning with olive leaf (v. 11) becomes symbol of peace and new beginning throughout Scripture ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 55#Psalm 55 . 6|Psalm 55:6]]; [[Readable Bible/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 2#Song of Solomon 2 . 14|Song of Solomon 2:14]]).
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 1#Leviticus 1 . 9|Leviticus 1:9]], 13, 17 establishes burnt offerings as "pleasing aroma to the LORD," connecting sacrifice to reconciliation.

**Type Classification**: **Providential Type** (sovereignly arranged deliverance and new beginning) and **Forward-Looking** (resurrection pattern and new creation imagery).

**Christological Connection**: (1) **God remembered** - Divine initiative in salvation; God remembered His covenant in Christ ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 72|Luke 1:72]]); (2) **Ark rested** - Christ's work is finished ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]), providing rest for believers ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-10]]); (3) **New beginning through sacrifice** - Noah's burnt offerings establish new order on atonement, prefiguring Christ's sacrifice establishing new covenant ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11-14]]); (4) **Pleasing aroma** - Noah's sacrifice was "pleasing aroma to the LORD" (v. 21), prefiguring Christ who "gave himself up for us as a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 2|Ephesians 5:2]]); (5) **From death to life** - Emerging from ark to new world prefigures resurrection—believers pass through death (in Christ) to new creation ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]]).
