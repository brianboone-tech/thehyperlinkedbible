### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 17|John 1:17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3551|G3551]] νόμος (*nomos*) - law
- [[Lexicon/G5485|G5485]] χάρις (*charis*) - grace
- [[Lexicon/G225|G225]] ἀλήθεια (*alētheia*) - truth

**Context**: "For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ." John contrasts the two covenants while honoring both. Moses was the instrument ("through," διά) of law-giving; Christ is the source and substance of grace and truth. This is not abolition but escalation—Moses prepared for what Christ brings.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 31#Exodus 31 . 18|Exodus 31:18]] - God writes law on tablets through Moses
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]] - LORD proclaims Himself "abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness" (חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 34#Deuteronomy 34 . 10|Deuteronomy 34:10]] - No prophet like Moses arose... until now

**Connections**:

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Forward-looking (Moses as covenant mediator anticipates Christ)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 17|John 1:17]] establishes the governing contrast of John's Gospel: Moses mediated law, but Christ brings grace and truth. The phrase "grace and truth" (χάρις καὶ ἀλήθεια) translates the Hebrew חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת from [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]]—God's self-revelation to Moses. What Moses glimpsed partially at Sinai is now embodied fully in Christ. The law was not bad but preparatory; it revealed sin but could not save. Christ brings what the law pointed toward: covenant faithfulness (חֶסֶד) now accessible through incarnate grace, and divine reliability (אֱמֶת) now revealed as ultimate truth in the Word made flesh. Moses gave the shadow; Christ is the substance.
