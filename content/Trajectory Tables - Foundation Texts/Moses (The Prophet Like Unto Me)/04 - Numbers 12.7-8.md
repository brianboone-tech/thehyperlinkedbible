### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 12#Numbers 12 . 7|Numbers 12:7-8]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H539|H539]] אָמַן (*ʾāman*) - to be faithful, trustworthy
- [[Lexicon/H1004|H1004]] בַּיִת (*bayiṯ*) - house, household
- [[Lexicon/H5650|H5650]] עֶבֶד (*ʿeḇeḏ*) - servant
- [[Lexicon/H6310|H6310]] פֶּה (*peh*) - mouth (face to face = פֶּה אֶל־פֶּה)

**Context**: "Not so with my servant Moses. He is faithful in all my house. With him I speak mouth to mouth, clearly, and not in riddles, and he beholds the form of the LORD." God vindicates Moses against Aaron and Miriam's challenge, declaring Moses's unique status among prophets—faithful in God's entire household, receiving direct revelation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 11|Exodus 33:11]] - LORD spoke to Moses face to face as friend
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 34#Deuteronomy 34 . 10|Deuteronomy 34:10]] - No prophet like Moses arose (face to face)
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 35|1 Samuel 2:35]] - Faithful priest who will walk before my anointed

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 11|Exodus 33:11]] - Face to face relationship
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 34#Deuteronomy 34 . 10|Deuteronomy 34:10]] - Canonical epitaph echoing this text
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 2|Hebrews 3:2]], 5-6 - "Moses was faithful... as a servant"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 18|John 1:18]] - "No one has seen God... only Son has made him known"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Forward-looking (Moses's faithfulness points to Christ's greater faithfulness)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 12#Numbers 12 . 7|Numbers 12:7-8]] provides the OT foundation for Hebrews 3. Moses is "faithful in all my house" (בְּכָל־בֵּיתִי נֶאֱמָן)—a remarkable commendation. He alone speaks with God "mouth to mouth" (פֶּה אֶל־פֶּה), not through dreams or visions. Yet Hebrews draws the crucial distinction: Moses was faithful AS A SERVANT (θεράπων) in God's house, but Christ is faithful AS A SON (υἱός) over God's house. The builder of a house has greater honor than the house itself ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 3|Hebrews 3:3]]). Moses testified to what would be spoken later (3:5); Christ is the One spoken of. Moses saw God's form (תְּמֻנָה) but not His face ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 20|Exodus 33:20]]); Christ IS "the radiance of God's glory and exact imprint of his nature" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). Moses's unique intimacy with God was unparalleled among prophets—until the Prophet like Moses came, the Son who dwells in the Father's bosom and makes Him fully known ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 18|John 1:18]]).
