### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 17#Matthew 17 . 1|Matthew 17:1-8]]

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Backward-looking (Moses and Elijah as witnesses to Christ)

**Christological Connection**: The Transfiguration is the climax of Moses typology. The parallels to Exodus 24 are unmistakable: high mountain, six days, cloud of glory, divine voice. But the escalation is dramatic: Moses's face shone with reflected glory after being with God; Jesus's face shines with inherent divine glory—He IS God. Moses and Elijah appear as witnesses representing the Law and the Prophets, both testifying to Christ. Peter's suggestion to build three tents (placing Jesus alongside Moses and Elijah) is rebuked by the divine voice: "This is my beloved Son... listen to HIM." The command echoes [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 18#Deuteronomy 18 . 15|Deuteronomy 18:15]] ("to him you shall listen"), confirming Jesus as the Prophet like Moses. But He is greater than Moses—Moses received the law, Christ transcends it; Moses's glory faded, Christ's is eternal; Moses served as witness, Christ is the Subject witnessed to. Luke uniquely records they spoke of Jesus's "exodus" (ἔξοδος) He would accomplish at Jerusalem ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 31|Luke 9:31]])—His death and resurrection as the true deliverance that Moses's exodus prefigured.
