### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4561|G4561]] σάρξ (*sarx*) - flesh
- [[Lexicon/G4637|G4637]] σκηνόω (*skēnoō*) - to dwell, tabernacle
- [[Lexicon/G1391|G1391]] δόξα (*doxa*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/G3439|G3439]] μονογενής (*monogenēs*) - only begotten, unique

**Context**: "And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth." John uses tabernacle language (σκηνόω = "tabernacled") to describe the incarnation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - "Let them make me a sanctuary"
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - Glory fills tabernacle
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 4|Ezekiel 43:4-5]] - Glory returns to temple

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - Tabernacle glory
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 4|Ezekiel 43:4]] - Glory returns
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] - Jesus' body as temple
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 9|Colossians 2:9]] - Fullness of deity bodily

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Christ as true tabernacle

**Christological Connection**: The verb σκηνόω ("tabernacled") directly echoes the Hebrew שָׁכַן (shākan), root of Shekinah. John declares that the glory which once filled the tabernacle now dwells in human flesh—Jesus Christ. The glory "we have seen" echoes Moses' request to see God's glory ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 18|Exodus 33:18]]) and the glory that filled tabernacle and temple. The incarnation is the ultimate localization of divine presence: not in a tent, not in a building, but in a Person. The escalation is complete: cloud-pillar → tabernacle → temple → Christ.
