### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 19|Exodus 14:19-20]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4397|H4397]] מַלְאָךְ (*malʾāḵ*) - angel, messenger
- [[Lexicon/H2822|H2822]] חֹשֶׁךְ (*ḥōšeḵ*) - darkness
- [[Lexicon/H215|H215]] אוֹר (*ʾôr*) - to be light, give light

**Context**: "The angel of God who was going before the host of Israel moved and went behind them, and the pillar of cloud moved from before them and stood behind them, coming between the host of Egypt and the host of Israel. And there was the cloud and the darkness. And it lit up the night without one coming near the other all night."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]] - Pillar originally before Israel
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 24|Exodus 14:24]] - LORD looks down from pillar
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 5|Isaiah 4:5]] - Future cloud and fire over Zion

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]] - Pillar's guiding function
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 39|Psalm 105:39]] - Cloud for covering
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 1|1 Corinthians 10:1-2]] - Under the cloud
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 29|Hebrews 11:29]] - Crossing by faith

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Divine protection pattern

**Christological Connection**: The pillar's repositioning demonstrates that God's presence is not merely decorative but defensive. The same cloud that guided Israel now shields Israel. To Egypt, darkness; to Israel, light. This dual function anticipates Christ, who is salvation to those who believe but judgment to those who reject. The pillar stood "between"—a mediating presence. Christ stands between His people and their enemies, between humanity and divine judgment, as the one Mediator between God and man ([[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 5|1 Timothy 2:5]]).
