### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kāḇôḏ*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H3318|H3318]] יָצָא (*yāṣāʾ*) - to go out, depart
- [[Lexicon/H4670|H4670]] מִפְתָּן (*mip̄tān*) - threshold

**Context**: "Then the glory of the LORD went out from the threshold of the house, and stood over the cherubim. And the cherubim lifted up their wings and mounted up from the earth before my eyes as they went out." Ezekiel witnesses the Shekinah departing from the temple due to Israel's persistent sin.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - Glory enters temple
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 8#Ezekiel 8 . 6|Ezekiel 8:6]] - Abominations driving glory away
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 23|Ezekiel 11:23]] - Glory departs to Mount of Olives

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - Glory originally entered
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10]] - Glory filled temple
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23#Matthew 23 . 38|Matthew 23:38]] - "Your house is left desolate"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 1|Matthew 24:1-2]] - Temple destruction predicted

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Crisis in presence pattern

**Christological Connection**: The glory's departure was tragic yet necessary—God's holiness cannot coexist with persistent sin. The glory moved from the Holy of Holies to the threshold, then to the east gate, then to the Mount of Olives ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 23|Ezekiel 11:23]]), and departed. This trajectory is reversed in Christ: Jesus ascended from the Mount of Olives ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 9|Acts 1:9-12]]) and will return to the same place ([[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 4|Zechariah 14:4]]). The glory that departed has returned in Christ and will return bodily at the Second Coming. The departure was not final but preparatory.
