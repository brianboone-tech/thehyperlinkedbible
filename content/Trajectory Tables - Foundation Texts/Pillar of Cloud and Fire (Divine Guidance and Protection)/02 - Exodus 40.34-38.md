### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-38]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kāḇôḏ*) - glory, honor, weight
- [[Lexicon/H4390|H4390]] מָלֵא (*mālēʾ*) - to fill, be full
- [[Lexicon/H7931|H7931]] שָׁכַן (*šāḵan*) - to dwell, settle, tabernacle

**Context**: "The cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle. And Moses was not able to enter the tent of meeting because the cloud settled on it, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle." The pillar that led through the wilderness now localizes in the sanctuary.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]] - Pillar leads in wilderness
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 15|Numbers 9:15-23]] - Cloud governs movements
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - Glory fills Solomon's temple

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - "Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - Temple filled with glory
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word tabernacled among us
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - God dwells with man

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Forward-looking (presence localized, pointing to incarnation)

**Christological Connection**: The verb "settled" (שָׁכַן, shākan) is the root of Shekinah—God's dwelling presence. John uses the cognate Greek term when he writes "the Word became flesh and tabernacled (ἐσκήνωσεν) among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). The glory that filled the tabernacle is the same glory John saw in Christ: "we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father." The tabernacle was provisional; Christ is the permanent dwelling of God with humanity.
