### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4633|G4633]] σκηνή (*skēnē*) - tent, tabernacle, dwelling
- [[Lexicon/G4637|G4637]] σκηνόω (*skēnoō*) - to dwell, tabernacle
- [[Lexicon/G2992|G2992]] λαός (*laos*) - people

**Context**: "And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying, 'Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God.'" The trajectory reaches its consummation—God's presence unmediated and eternal.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - "Let them make me a sanctuary"
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] - "I will make my dwelling among you"
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - "My dwelling place shall be with them"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - Original tabernacle purpose
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - Prophetic promise
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word tabernacled
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22-23]] - No temple; God is the temple

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Eternal divine presence

**Christological Connection**: The trajectory reaches its goal: the cloud-pillar was temporary and external; the tabernacle and temple were provisional; Christ's incarnate presence was limited to one place at a time; the Spirit's indwelling is internal but incomplete. In the new creation, all limitations are removed. "I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb" (21:22). No more mediated presence; God Himself dwells directly with His people. "The city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" (21:23). The pillar of fire that gave light at night is now eternal light from God's glory. The far distant ends embrace: the cloud that led from Egypt to Canaan finds its eternal fulfillment in God's immediate presence in the New Jerusalem.
