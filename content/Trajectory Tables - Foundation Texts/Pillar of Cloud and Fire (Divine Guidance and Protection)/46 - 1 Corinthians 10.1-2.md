**Context**: "For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea, and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea." Paul interprets the Exodus as Israel's "baptism"—identification with Moses through cloud and sea.

**Type Classification**: NT Application - Exodus as type of Christian baptism

**Christological Connection**: Paul explicitly identifies the Exodus as typological for Christian experience. "Baptized into Moses" parallels "baptized into Christ" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 3|Romans 6:3]]; [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 27|Galatians 3:27]]). The cloud and sea together effected Israel's deliverance and identified them with their mediator. Christian baptism identifies believers with Christ and His death-resurrection. The pattern: under the cloud + through the sea = baptism into Moses; under Christ's blood + through the Spirit = baptism into Christ. The Exodus was a corporate redemption; baptism initiates into the new covenant community.
