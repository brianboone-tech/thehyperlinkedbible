### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kāḇôḏ*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H6051|H6051]] עָנָן (*ʿānān*) - cloud
- [[Lexicon/H5975|H5975]] עָמַד (*ʿāmaḏ*) - to stand, remain

**Context**: "When the priests came out of the Holy Place, a cloud filled the house of the LORD, so that the priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD." Solomon's temple receives the same glory that filled the tabernacle.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]] - Glory fills tabernacle
- [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1-3]] - Fire and glory at dedication
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18]] - Glory departs

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - Tabernacle precedent
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 5#2 Chronicles 5 . 13|2 Chronicles 5:13-14]] - Parallel account
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] - Jesus' body as temple
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]] - Church as temple

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Continuation of presence pattern

**Christological Connection**: The cloud-glory that led Israel through the wilderness, that filled the tabernacle, now fills the permanent temple. Yet even Solomon recognized the temple's limitations: "But will God indeed dwell on the earth? Behold, heaven and the highest heaven cannot contain you; how much less this house that I have built!" ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 27|1 Kings 8:27]]). The temple was a shadow; Christ is the substance. Jesus declared, "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up"—speaking of His body ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]). The glory that filled Solomon's temple dwelt bodily in Christ.
