### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 2|Ezekiel 43:2-5]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kāḇôḏ*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H6921|H6921]] קָדִים (*qāḏîm*) - east
- [[Lexicon/H935|H935]] בּוֹא (*bôʾ*) - to come, enter

**Context**: "Behold, the glory of the God of Israel was coming from the east. And the sound of his coming was like the sound of many waters, and the earth shone with his glory... As the glory of the LORD entered the temple by the gate facing east, the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into the inner court; and behold, the glory of the LORD filled the temple."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]] - Glory departed eastward
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 23|Ezekiel 11:23]] - Glory on Mount of Olives
- [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 7|Haggai 2:7]], 9 - Latter glory promised

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]] - Glory departed
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 9|Haggai 2:9]] - Greater glory coming
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Glory in Christ
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 11|Revelation 21:11]] - City has God's glory

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking - Prophetic anticipation of Messiah/new creation

**Christological Connection**: The glory that departed eastward returns from the east. Ezekiel's vision anticipates multiple fulfillments: (1) partially in Christ's first coming—"the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us, and we have seen his glory" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]); (2) fully in the new creation—"the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). The eastward return connects to Christ's return: "For as the lightning comes from the east and shines as far as the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 27|Matthew 24:27]]).
