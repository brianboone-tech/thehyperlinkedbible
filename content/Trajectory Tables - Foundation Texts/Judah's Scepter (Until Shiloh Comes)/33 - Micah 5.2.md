### [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1035|H1035]] בֵּית לֶחֶם (*beth lechem*) - "Bethlehem, house of bread"
- [[Lexicon/H672|H672]] אֶפְרָתָה (*ʾephratah*) - "Ephrathah, fruitful"
- [[Lexicon/H6996|H6996]] צָעִיר (*tsaʿir*) - "small, insignificant"
- [[Lexicon/H4910|H4910]] מָשַׁל (*mashal*) - "to rule"
- [[Lexicon/H4163|H4163]] מוֹצָאָה (*motsaʾah*) - "origin, going forth"
- [[Lexicon/H6924|H6924]] קֶדֶם (*qedem*) - "ancient times, east"
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (*ʿolam*) - "eternity, everlasting"

**Context**: Micah prophesies during Assyria's threat to Judah (8th century BC). After depicting Israel's crisis (5:1—the judge of Israel struck on the cheek), the prophet pivots with "But you, Bethlehem." From the smallest clan comes the greatest ruler. His "origins are of old, from the days of eternity"—language implying divine preexistence. This is the most geographically specific messianic prophecy in the OT.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Bethlehem is David's hometown (1 Sam 16:1), tying this to the Davidic covenant.
- The "small among the clans" echoes David's insignificance—the youngest son of Jesse.
- "Days of eternity" goes beyond David's human origin to suggest the ruler's divine nature.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 35:19 (Rachel buried near Bethlehem), Ruth 4:11 (Bethlehem as setting), 1 Samuel 16:1-13 (David anointed there)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] (Davidic covenant), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6-7]] (child born, son given)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 4|Matthew 2:4-6]] (fulfilled at Jesus' birth), [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 42|John 7:42]] (Christ comes from Bethlehem)

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (explicit prophetic prediction); Forward-Looking (clearly anticipates a future ruler with eternal origins).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]] is directly fulfilled in Jesus' birth. (1) **Geographic Precision**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 4|Matthew 2:4-6]] records that when Herod asked where the Christ was to be born, the chief priests and scribes quoted this very verse. Providence brought Mary and Joseph to Bethlehem precisely at the right moment. (2) **Smallness and Greatness**: "Small among the clans of Judah"—yet from this small town comes the universal Ruler. God's pattern: the youngest son (David), the smallest town (Bethlehem), the baby in a manger. Humility before exaltation. (3) **Eternal Origins**: "His origins are of old, from the days of eternity." No mere human has "origins from eternity." This is Christ's divine preexistence—"In the beginning was the Word" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1]]). The ruler is both born in Bethlehem (human) and exists from eternity (divine). (4) **For Me**: "Out of you will come forth for Me"—God speaks. The ruler is God's own provision, sent by the Father, coming forth for YHWH's purposes. (5) **Ruler over Israel**: Yet Christ's rule extends far beyond ethnic Israel—"from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth" (Ps 72:8).
