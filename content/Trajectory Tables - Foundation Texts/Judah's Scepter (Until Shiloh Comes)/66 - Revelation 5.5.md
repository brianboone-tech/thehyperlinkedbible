### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 5|Revelation 5:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3023|G3023]] λέων (*leōn*) - "lion"
- [[Lexicon/G5443|G5443]] φυλή (*phylē*) - "tribe"
- [[Lexicon/G2455|G2455]] Ἰούδας (*Ioudas*) - "Judah"
- [[Lexicon/G4491|G4491]] ῥίζα (*rhiza*) - "root"
- [[Lexicon/G1138|G1138]] Δαυίδ (*Dauid*) - "David"
- [[Lexicon/G3528|G3528]] νικάω (*nikaō*) - "to conquer, overcome"
- [[Lexicon/G455|G455]] ἀνοίγω (*anoigō*) - "to open"
- [[Lexicon/G4973|G4973]] σφραγίς (*sphragis*) - "seal"

**Context**: John weeps in heaven because no one is found worthy to open the scroll—the book of God's redemptive purposes for history. An elder intervenes: "Weep no more; behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals." John then sees not a lion but a Lamb "standing, as though it had been slain" (v. 6). The Lion-Lamb conquers through sacrifice.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "Lion of the tribe of Judah" directly quotes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 9|Genesis 49:9]] ("Judah is a young lion").
- "Root of David" connects to [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1]], 10 (shoot from the stump of Jesse; root of Jesse).
- The conquest language echoes Psalm 2's victory and [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]'s bruising of the serpent.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 49:9-10 (Judah as lion, scepter promise), Isaiah 11:1, 10 (root of Jesse), 2 Samuel 7:12-16 (Davidic covenant)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 16|Revelation 22:16]] (root and offspring of David), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11-16]] (conquering King)
- **PARALLEL**: Revelation 5:6-14 (Lamb receives worship), John 1:29 (Lamb of God)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking Fulfillment (explicit identification of Christ as Genesis 49:9's Lion); Direct (the elder's announcement is revelatory).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 5|Revelation 5:5]] is the climax of the Judahite scepter trajectory. (1) **Lion of Judah**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 9|Genesis 49:9]]'s prophecy—"Judah is a young lion"—finds its ultimate fulfillment. Christ is the conquering Lion who rules all creation. (2) **Root of David**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 10|Isaiah 11:10]]'s "root of Jesse" stands—He is both David's root (eternal origin) and David's descendant (human birth). Christ is both source and product of the Davidic line. (3) **Has Conquered**: The perfect tense indicates completed victory. How? The Lion conquered by becoming the slain Lamb (v. 6). The cross is His conquest; resurrection is His coronation. (4) **Opening the Scroll**: Only the Lion-Lamb can open God's purposes for history. The scepter of Judah now controls all that unfolds. Christ is Lord of history. (5) **Worship Follows**: After the Lamb takes the scroll, all creation worships (vv. 8-14). "To him shall be the obedience of the peoples" (Gen 49:10) is fulfilled as "every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea" praises the Lamb. The scepter promised to Judah now receives universal homage. The "until Shiloh comes" has arrived.
