### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 15|Genesis 17:15-21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8297|H8297]] שָׂרַי (Śāray) - "my princess" - her original name
- [[Lexicon/H8283|H8283]] שָׂרָה (Śārâ) - "princess" - her new name
- [[Lexicon/H1288|H1288]] בָּרַךְ (bāraḵ) - "bless" - I will bless her
- [[Lexicon/H1471|H1471]] גּוֹי (gôy) - "nation" - mother of nations
- [[Lexicon/H4428|H4428]] מֶלֶךְ (meleḵ) - "king" - kings shall come from her
- [[Lexicon/H6711|H6711]] צָחַק (ṣāḥaq) - "laugh" - Abraham laughed; root of Isaac's name

**Context**: This is the covenant-making chapter where God establishes circumcision as the sign of His covenant with Abraham. God changes Abram to Abraham (father of a multitude) and now changes Sarai to Sarah. Crucially, God specifies that the covenant son will come through Sarah, not Hagar's son Ishmael. Abraham's laughter expresses both wonder and incredulity: "Will a son be born to a man a hundred years old? Will Sarah bear a child at the age of ninety?"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Genesis 17 builds on [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]] and 15:1-6, now specifying Sarah as the mother of the promised seed
- The name change parallels Abram→Abraham, indicating new identity and destiny
- The promise of "kings" from Sarah (17:16) connects to the later Davidic covenant (2 Samuel 7)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Genesis 12:2-3]] - Original promise of blessing and nations
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 4|Genesis 15:4-6]] - Promise of offspring
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] - Davidic line from Sarah's descendants; Psalm 72 - The king who fulfills the promises
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 23|Galatians 4:23]] - "born through the promise"
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 9|Romans 9:9]] - "At the appointed time I will return, and Sarah will have a son"
**Type Classification**: Providential (divine arrangement of Sarah's role), Forward-Looking (explicit promises pointing to future fulfillment including kings)

**Christological Connection**: The "kings" who will come from Sarah reach their apex in Christ, the King of kings. The promise that Sarah will be "mother of nations" is fulfilled as Gentiles are grafted into the Abrahamic family through Christ. Jesus is the ultimate descendant of Sarah through whom all nations are blessed. The supernatural conception promised here anticipates the virgin conception—both are God overcoming natural impossibility to bring the Child of Promise.
