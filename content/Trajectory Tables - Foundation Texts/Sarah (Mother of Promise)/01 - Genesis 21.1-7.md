### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 1|Genesis 21:1-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6485|H6485]] פָּקַד (pāqaḏ) - "visit/attend to" - The LORD attended to Sarah
- [[Lexicon/H1696|H1696]] דָּבַר (dāḇar) - "speak/promise" - as He had spoken
- [[Lexicon/H4150|H4150]] מוֹעֵד (môʿēḏ) - "appointed time" - at the set time
- [[Lexicon/H3327|H3327]] יִצְחָק (Yiṣḥāq) - "Isaac/laughter" - And she called his name Isaac
- [[Lexicon/H6712|H6712]] צְחֹק (ṣĕḥōq) - "laughter" - God has made me laugh
- [[Lexicon/H4448|H4448]] מָלַל (mālal) - "speak" - Who would have said

**Context**: The promise is fulfilled. The LORD "attended to" Sarah (a tender term suggesting careful attention to her need) and "did for Sarah what He had promised." At age 100, Abraham fathers Isaac; at 90, Sarah bears him. The child is named Isaac ("laughter") fulfilling both Abraham's laugh of wonder (17:17) and Sarah's laugh of doubt turned joy (18:12; 21:6). Sarah's exclamation—"Who would have told Abraham that Sarah would nurse children?"—expresses holy astonishment.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Genesis 21 fulfills [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Genesis 12:2-3]], 15:4-6, 17:15-21, and 18:10
- The phrase "at the set time" (למועד) connects to Israel's festivals (appointed times) where God acts
- The pattern of promised son → fulfillment recurs with Hannah ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 1#1 Samuel 1 . 20|1 Samuel 1:20]])

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - All prior promises to Abraham regarding offspring
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - Seed promise
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 1#1 Samuel 1 . 19|1 Samuel 1:19-20]] - Hannah's Samuel follows the pattern
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 126#Psalm 126 . 2|Psalm 126:2]] - "Then our mouth was filled with laughter"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 19|Romans 4:19-22]] - Abraham's faith despite impossible circumstances
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 11|Hebrews 11:11-12]] - Sarah's faith commended
**Type Classification**: Providential (Isaac's birth sovereignly arranged), Forward-Looking (Isaac himself becomes a type—offered on Moriah, father of Jacob/Israel)

**Christological Connection**: Isaac's birth from Sarah's dead womb typifies Christ's birth from a virgin and His resurrection from the dead. Both involve supernatural intervention in what is naturally impossible. As Isaac was the long-promised son through whom blessing came, Christ is the ultimate Son through whom salvation comes. Sarah's laughter of joy anticipates the eschatological joy when God fulfills all His promises in Christ. The church, like Sarah, moves from doubt to delight as it witnesses God's faithfulness in Christ.
