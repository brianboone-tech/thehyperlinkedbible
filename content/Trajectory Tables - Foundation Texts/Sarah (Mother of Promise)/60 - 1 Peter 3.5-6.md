### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 5|1 Peter 3:5-6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G40|G40]] ἅγιος (hagios) - "holy" - the holy women of the past
- [[Lexicon/G1679|G1679]] ἐλπίζω (elpizō) - "hope" - who put their hope in God
- [[Lexicon/G2885|G2885]] κοσμέω (kosmeō) - "adorn" - adorned themselves
- [[Lexicon/G5293|G5293]] ὑποτάσσω (hypotassō) - "submit" - submissive to their husbands
- [[Lexicon/G5219|G5219]] ὑπακούω (hypakouō) - "obey" - Sarah obeyed Abraham
- [[Lexicon/G2962|G2962]] κύριος (kyrios) - "lord" - called him lord
- [[Lexicon/G5043|G5043]] τέκνον (teknon) - "children" - you are her children
- [[Lexicon/G4422|G4422]] πτόησις (ptoēsis) - "fear" - refuse to give way to fear

**Context**: Peter is instructing Christian wives, especially those married to unbelieving husbands, on how to conduct themselves. He points to Sarah as the paradigm of godly womanhood: she "obeyed Abraham and called him lord." Peter then says that Christian wives become Sarah's spiritual daughters when they imitate her faith and fearlessness.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Peter alludes to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 12|Genesis 18:12]] where Sarah refers to Abraham as "my lord" (אֲדֹנִי)
- The broader context of Sarah's life—following Abraham from Ur, through Canaan, into Egypt, and back—demonstrates practical submission through uncertainty
- Sarah's trust was ultimately in God, not just Abraham

**Connections**:

  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-5]] - Sarah follows Abraham to unknown land

  - [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 9|1 Timothy 2:9-15]] - Godly adornment and conduct
**Type Classification**: NT Application (Sarah as model for Christian wives)

**Christological Connection**: Sarah's submission to Abraham while trusting God typifies the church's relationship to Christ. As Sarah called Abraham "lord," the church acknowledges Christ as Lord. As Sarah followed Abraham through unknown territory, believers follow Christ through this world. The "inner beauty" Peter commends (3:4) reflects Christ's character formed in believers. Sarah's fearlessness came from trusting God's promises—promises fulfilled in Christ. Christian wives are Sarah's "children" as they trust the same faithful God, now revealed fully in Jesus.
