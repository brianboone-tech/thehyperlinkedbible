### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 20#Ezekiel 20 . 41|Ezekiel 20:41]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7521|H7521]] רָצָה (*ratsah*) - "to be pleased with, accept favorably"
- [[Lexicon/H7381|H7381]] רֵיחַ (*reach*) - "scent, fragrance"
- [[Lexicon/H5207|H5207]] נִיחֹחַ (*nichoach*) - "pleasing, soothing"
- [[Lexicon/H6942|H6942]] קָדַשׁ (*qadash*) - "to be holy, sanctified, consecrated"
- [[Lexicon/H1471|H1471]] גּוֹיִם (*goyim*) - "nations, Gentiles"

**Context**: Ezekiel 20 recounts Israel's history of rebellion from Egypt through the exile. Despite repeated covenant unfaithfulness, God promises future restoration—not because of Israel's merit but for the sake of His holy name (20:44). Verse 41 prophesies that when God regathers His scattered people from the nations, He will accept them "as a pleasing aroma," and through this act He will display His holiness before the Gentiles. This echoes the Levitical sacrificial language but applies it eschatologically to the people themselves becoming acceptable to God. The prophet envisions a time when Israel's worship will finally be pure and their relationship with God fully restored.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Leviticus 1-7 established "pleasing aroma" as the hallmark of acceptable sacrifice
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 6#Jeremiah 6 . 20|Jeremiah 6:20]] declared Israel's sacrifices unacceptable due to disobedience
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 56#Isaiah 56 . 7|Isaiah 56:7]] promised that foreigners' offerings would be accepted on God's altar
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 22|Ezekiel 36:22-38]] elaborates on restoration for God's name's sake through spiritual renewal

**Christological Connection**: Ezekiel's prophecy of Israel accepted "as a pleasing aroma" finds fulfillment through Christ's mediating work. Where Israel's repeated sacrifices could not secure lasting acceptance due to ongoing sin, Christ's perfect sacrifice achieves what the prophets anticipated—a people fully acceptable to God. Paul applies this exact language to Christ: "a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 2|Ephesians 5:2]]), and to believers: "the aroma of Christ" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 2#2 Corinthians 2 . 15|2 Corinthians 2:15]]). The regathering from the nations anticipates both the restoration of believing Israel ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 26|Romans 11:26-27]]) and the ingathering of Gentiles into God's people through the gospel. What Ezekiel prophesied as future restoration is realized in Christ, who makes His people a "pleasing aroma" before the Father.
