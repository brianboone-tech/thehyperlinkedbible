### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 15#Leviticus 15 . 13|Leviticus 15:13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4325|H4325]] מַיִם (*mayim*) - "water"
- [[Lexicon/H2416|H2416]] חַיִּים (*chayyim*) - "living, running" (literally "alive")
- [[Lexicon/H2891|H2891]] טָהֵר (*taher*) - "to be clean, to purify"

**Context**: Leviticus 15 addresses ceremonial uncleanness from bodily discharges. Verse 13 prescribes the purification ritual: "When the one with a discharge is cleansed of his discharge, then he shall count for himself seven days for his cleansing, wash his clothes, and bathe his body in **living water** (מַיִם חַיִּים); and he shall be clean." The phrase "living water" refers to fresh, flowing water from a spring or stream, not stagnant water. This external washing addressed ceremonial defilement but could not cleanse the conscience or transform the heart.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 5#2 Kings 5 . 10|2 Kings 5:10-14]] applies this principle to Naaman's leprosy—he must wash in the Jordan River to be cleansed, though he initially resists the humble means
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]] transforms the concept: God promises "I will sprinkle clean water on you" (divine action) leading to internal cleansing ("new heart, new spirit")
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]] prophesies "a fountain opened... for sin and uncleanness"—escalating from ritual washing to eschatological cleansing

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking** (the prophets develop this into expectational promise) and **Direct Type** (divinely commanded institution pointing beyond itself)

**Christological Connection**: The "living water" required for purification points to Christ, who offers "rivers of living water" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]])—the Holy Spirit given to believers. What the law commanded externally (washing in living water), Christ accomplishes internally (cleansing the conscience through His blood and giving the life-giving Spirit). The Pool of Bethesda demonstrates the tragic inadequacy of ritual washing; Christ's word demonstrates the power of divine grace that needs no pool, no human effort, only faith in His sovereign power.
