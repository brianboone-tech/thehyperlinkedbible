### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2236|H2236]] זָרַק (*zaraq*) - "to sprinkle, scatter"
- [[Lexicon/H4325|H4325]] מַיִם (*mayim*) - "water"
- [[Lexicon/H2889|H2889]] טָהוֹר (*tahor*) - "clean, pure"
- [[Lexicon/H3820|H3820]] לֵב (*lev*) - "heart"
- [[Lexicon/H2319|H2319]] חָדָשׁ (*chadash*) - "new, fresh"
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (*ruach*) - "spirit, wind, breath"

**Context**: Ezekiel prophesies to exiled Israel, promising future restoration. God declares: "I will sprinkle clean water on you, and you shall be clean; I will cleanse you from all your filthiness and from all your idols. I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you; I will take the heart of stone out of your flesh and give you a heart of flesh. I will put My Spirit within you and cause you to walk in My statutes, and you will keep My judgments and do them" (vv. 25-27). This is the most comprehensive promise of divine transformation in the OT, moving from external ceremonial cleansing to internal spiritual regeneration.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 15#Leviticus 15 . 13|Leviticus 15:13]] commanded humans to wash themselves in living water—human action
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25]] promises God will sprinkle clean water on His people—divine action
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]] promised a new covenant with law written on hearts; Ezekiel adds the Spirit component
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|Zechariah 13:1]] echoes this with "a fountain opened for the house of David... for sin and for uncleanness"

The trajectory shows escalation: from commanded washing (Lev) → to promised divine cleansing (Ezek) → to opened fountain (Zech) → to Christ who IS the fountain (John 7:37-39).

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking** (contains explicit expectational promise pointing to future fulfillment) and **Direct Type** (divinely revealed promise)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] reveals what the Pool of Bethesda could never accomplish. The pool required human effort (getting in first) but provided uncertain, limited healing. Ezekiel promises divine initiative ("**I will** sprinkle," "**I will** give," "**I will** put") accomplishing comprehensive transformation (cleansing + new heart + Spirit). Christ fulfills this at two levels: (1) In His earthly ministry, He demonstrates divine power by healing the Bethesda invalid without ritual—His word alone accomplishes what the pool symbolized ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 8|John 5:8-9]]); (2) In His death and resurrection, He secures the new covenant realities Ezekiel prophesied—believers receive the "washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]]), precisely Ezekiel's threefold promise. The contrast is absolute: Bethesda = human striving for occasional healing; Christ = divine grace accomplishing eternal regeneration.
