### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 3|Romans 8:3-4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G102|G102]] ἀδύνατον (*adynaton*) - "impossible, powerless, unable"
- [[Lexicon/G3551|G3551]] νόμος (*nomos*) - "law"
- [[Lexicon/G770|G770]] ἀσθενέω (*astheneō*) - "to be weak, feeble, sick" (same root as Bethesda's ἀσθένεια)
- [[Lexicon/G4561|G4561]] σάρξ (*sarx*) - "flesh"
- [[Lexicon/G2632|G2632]] κατακρίνω (*katakrinō*) - "to condemn, judge against"
- [[Lexicon/G1345|G1345]] δικαίωμα (*dikaiōma*) - "righteous requirement, ordinance"

**Context**: Paul explains why the law could not save: "For what the law could not do (τὸ γὰρ ἀδύνατον τοῦ νόμου), in that it was weak through the flesh (ἐν ᾧ ἠσθένει διὰ τῆς σαρκός), God did by sending His own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, on account of sin: He condemned sin in the flesh, that the righteous requirement of the law might be fulfilled in us who do not walk according to the flesh but according to the Spirit" (vv. 3-4). The law was not defective in itself but "weak through the flesh"—it commanded righteousness but provided no power to fallen humans to obey.

**Type Classification**: **Backward-Looking** (explains typologically why law/ritual failed) and **Fulfillment Text** (shows Christ accomplishing what law symbolized)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 3|Romans 8:3-4]] is the theological commentary on John 5. The Pool of Bethesda illustrates "what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh." The 38-year invalid represents fallen humanity: he knew what was required (get into the pool) but lacked strength to achieve it. The word "weak" (ἀσθενέω) is the same root used for the invalid's "sickness" (ἀσθένεια) in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 5|John 5:5]]—linguistic connection highlighting the parallel. The law commanded washing (Lev 15:13), the pool offered occasional healing, but both were "weak through the flesh." Then Christ comes—"God did by sending His own Son." Jesus bypasses the ineffective pool and speaks the powerful word: "Rise, take up your bed, and walk." This is salvation: God accomplishing through Christ what law could not accomplish through human effort. The invalid walked immediately ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 9|John 5:9]]); believers are saved immediately by faith (Eph 2:8). The 38 years of helpless waiting end in a moment when divine power speaks.
