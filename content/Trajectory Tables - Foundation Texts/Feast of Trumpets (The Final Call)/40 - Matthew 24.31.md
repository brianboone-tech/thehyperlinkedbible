### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 31|Matthew 24:31]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G0601-0700#G0649|ἀποστελεῖ]] (*apostelei*) - "he will send out"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0032|ἀγγέλους αὐτοῦ]] (*angelous autou*) - "his angels"
- [[Lexicon/G3201-3300#G3173|μετὰ σάλπιγγος μεγάλης]] (*meta salpingos megalēs*) - "with great trumpet"
- [[Lexicon/G1901-2000#G1996|ἐπισυνάξουσιν]] (*episynaxousin*) - "they will gather together"
- [[Lexicon/G1501-1600#G1588|τοὺς ἐκλεκτοὺς]] (*tous eklektous*) - "the elect, the chosen ones"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0004|τῶν τεσσάρων ἀνέμων]] (*tōn tessarōn anemōn*) - "the four winds"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0206|ἀπ᾽ ἄκρων οὐρανῶν]] (*ap' akrōn ouranōn*) - "from ends of heavens"

**Context**: Jesus describes His return: after cosmic signs, the Son of Man appears in heaven's clouds with power and glory. He sends angels with great trumpet to gather His elect from earth's four corners—complete, universal gathering of all chosen by God. This climactic assembly fulfills OT trumpet-gathering prophecies, bringing scattered elect into Christ's presence.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 4|Deuteronomy 30:4]] (gather from ends of earth), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 13|Isaiah 27:13]] (great trumpet will blow), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 8|Jeremiah 31:8]] (gather them from farthest parts)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 13#Mark 13 . 27|Mark 13:27]] (parallel gathering account), [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16-17]] (trumpet gathers to Christ), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]] (great multitude from every nation)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 31|Matthew 24:31]]'s trumpet-gathering consummates Christ's redemptive mission. He came "to seek and to save the lost" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 19#Luke 19 . 10|Luke 19:10]]); the trumpet accomplishes final seeking and ultimate saving. The "elect" are those chosen "in him before the foundation of the world" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 4|Ephesians 1:4]]), purchased by Christ's blood ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]]: "ransomed people for God from every tribe and language and people and nation"), preserved through trials ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 28|John 10:28]]: "no one will snatch them out of my hand"), and finally gathered to glory. The angels serve Christ as reapers in harvest: "The harvest is the end of the age, and the reapers are angels" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13#Matthew 13 . 39|Matthew 13:39]]). The "four winds" gathering fulfills Jesus' promise: "Many will come from east and west and recline at table with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 8#Matthew 8 . 11|Matthew 8:11]]). The comprehensiveness ensures Christ's perfect shepherding—"I have other sheep that are not of this fold. I must bring them also... So there will be one flock, one shepherd" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 16|John 10:16]]). The trumpet's "great" designation surpasses all previous trumpets—Sinai's theophanic, jubilee's liberating, Feast of Trumpets' memorial—as the ultimate, eschatological summons. Revelation pictures the gathered multitude: "A great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]]). This is Christ's purchased possession ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 14|Ephesians 1:14]]), His bride gathered for eternal union, His sheep assembled in secure fold. No believer too remote (ends of heaven), no saint too long deceased (dead rise first), no location beyond reach (four winds)—the great trumpet gathers all to Christ who promised: "Where I am, there will my servant be also" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 26|John 12:26]]).
