### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 13|Isaiah 27:13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8601-8700#H8628|יִתָּקַע]] (*yittāqaʿ*) - "will be blown" (passive, divine action)
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7782|בְּשׁוֹפָר גָּדוֹל]] (*bəšôpār gādôl*) - "with great trumpet"
- [[Lexicon/H0001-0100#H0006|הָאֹבְדִים]] (*hāʾōbədîm*) - "the lost ones, the perishing"
- [[Lexicon/H5001-5100#H5080|הַנִּדָּחִים]] (*hanniddāḥîm*) - "the driven out, the dispersed"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7812|וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּ]] (*wəhištaḥăwû*) - "and they will worship"
- [[Lexicon/H2001-2100#H2022|הַר הַקֹּדֶשׁ]] (*har haqqōdeš*) - "the holy mountain"

**Context**: Isaiah prophesies that "in that day" a great trumpet will sound, gathering Israel's exiles from Assyria and Egypt to worship the LORD at Jerusalem. This eschatological trumpet reverses the scattering caused by judgment, gathering the lost and dispersed to Zion. The "great trumpet" (šôpār gādôl) emphasizes the summons's power and scope—reaching from east (Assyria) to south (Egypt), gathering all scattered Israel.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 3|Deuteronomy 30:3-4]] (God will gather from all peoples), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 147#Psalm 147 . 2|Psalm 147:2]] (LORD gathers outcasts of Israel)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 11|Isaiah 11:11-12]] (Lord will assemble from four corners), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 8|Jeremiah 31:8]] (I will bring them from north and gather)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 31|Matthew 24:31]] (gather elect from four winds), [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16-17]] (trumpet gathers to Christ), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]] (great multitude from every nation)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 13|Isaiah 27:13]]'s great trumpet finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ's return when He gathers His elect from all nations. Jesus declares: "He will send out his angels with a loud trumpet call, and they will gather his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 31|Matthew 24:31]]). Where Assyria and Egypt represented Israel's literal scattering, the church experiences global dispersion—believers scattered throughout every nation, culture, and geography. Christ's trumpet will summon all: "The Lord himself will descend from heaven with a cry of command, with the voice of an archangel, and with the sound of the trumpet of God" ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16]]). The "lost" (ʾōbədîm) and "driven out" (niddāḥîm) include those dead in Christ whom death scattered—the trumpet raises them first ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16]]). Then living believers are gathered. The result: all God's people assembled, none missing. Revelation pictures this: "A great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]]). The "great trumpet" surpasses all previous trumpets—Sinai's theophanic trumpet, jubilee's liberation trumpet, Feast of Trumpets' memorial trumpet. This is the ultimate, eschatological, resurrection trumpet. The worship goal ("worship the LORD on the holy mountain") finds fulfillment as believers worship eternally in God's presence ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]: "his servants will worship him"). Isaiah's Jerusalem becomes the new Jerusalem descending from heaven ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]]). The trumpet's blast is comprehensive—reaching dead in graves, living on earth, scattered across globe—gathering all to Christ in an instant. No believer too remote, no saint too long deceased—the great trumpet summons all to final assembly.
