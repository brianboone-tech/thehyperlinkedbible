### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 14|Ephesians 5:14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1453|ἔγειρε]] (*egeire*) - "awake, arise!" (imperative)
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2518|καθεύδων]] (*katheudōn*) - "one who sleeps"
- [[Lexicon/G0301-0400#G0450|ἀνάστα]] (*anasta*) - "rise up!" (imperative)
- [[Lexicon/G3401-3500#G3498|ἐκ τῶν νεκρῶν]] (*ek tōn nekrōn*) - "from the dead"
- [[Lexicon/G1901-2000#G2017|ἐπιφαύσει]] (*epiphausei*) - "will shine upon" (future)
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5547|Χριστός]] (*Christos*) - "Christ, Messiah"

**Context**: Paul quotes what appears to be an early Christian baptismal hymn or prophetic oracle: "Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you." This functions as trumpet-like wake-up call, summoning believers from spiritual sleep to walk in light. The eschatological trumpet's future summons creates present urgency—wake up now before final trumpet sounds.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 60#Isaiah 60 . 1|Isaiah 60:1]] (Arise, shine, for your light has come), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 19|Isaiah 26:19]] (Awake and sing, you who dwell in dust), [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 1|Joel 2:1]] (blow trumpet, wake up)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 13#Romans 13 . 11|Romans 13:11-12]] (time to wake from sleep, night is far gone), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 8|Ephesians 5:8]] (you were darkness, now light), [[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 6|1 Thessalonians 5:6-8]] (not sleep but be awake and sober)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 14|Ephesians 5:14]]'s wake-up call anticipates Christ's trumpet summons while applying it presently. The eschatological trumpet will literally "awake" the sleeping dead ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16]]: "the dead in Christ will rise"); the spiritual trumpet calls believers to wake from moral slumber now. Christ is both future resurrector and present illuminator—"Christ will shine on you" fulfills Isaiah's promise: "The LORD will be your everlasting light" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 60#Isaiah 60 . 19|Isaiah 60:19]]). The same power that will raise dead bodies ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 19|Ephesians 1:19-20]]) currently raises spiritually dead souls: "You were dead in the trespasses and sins... but God... made us alive together with Christ" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 1|Ephesians 2:1, 4-5]]). The trumpet's alarm function ([[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 1|Joel 2:1]]: "blow trumpet in Zion") finds ongoing application: "It is full time now for you to wake from sleep" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 13#Romans 13 . 11|Romans 13:11]]). Paul urges: "Let us not sleep, as others do, but let us keep awake and be sober" ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 6|1 Thessalonians 5:6]]). The final trumpet's certainty creates present watchfulness. Jesus warned: "Stay awake... for you do not know when the master of the house will come" ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 13#Mark 13 . 35|Mark 13:35]]). Believers live between two trumpets: conversion's call (already awakened) and resurrection's call (will be fully awakened). The present wake-up call prepares for final trumpet: those walking in light won't be caught sleeping when Christ returns. The promise "Christ will shine on you" consummates when "the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). The trumpet that will wake the dead confronts living believers now: Awake! Arise! Walk as children of light until the daybreak and Christ appears.
