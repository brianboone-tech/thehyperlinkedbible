### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 9|Leviticus 25:9-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5601-5700#H5674|וְהַעֲבַרְתָּ]] (*wəhaʿăbartā*) - "and you shall cause to pass through, sound"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7782|שׁוֹפַר תְּרוּעָה]] (*šôpar tərûʿâ*) - "trumpet of blast, loud trumpet"
- [[Lexicon/H3401-3500#H3725|בְּיוֹם הַכִּפֻּרִים]] (*bəyôm hakkippurîm*) - "on the Day of Atonement"
- [[Lexicon/H6901-7000#H6942|וְקִדַּשְׁתֶּם]] (*wəqiddaštem*) - "and you shall consecrate"
- [[Lexicon/H1701-1800#H1865|דְּרוֹר]] (*dərôr*) - "liberty, freedom, release"

**Context**: God commands that on the Day of Atonement of the fiftieth year, Israel shall sound the trumpet throughout the land, proclaiming jubilee—liberty for slaves, return of property, cancellation of debts. The trumpet announces comprehensive restoration and freedom, pointing to Christ who proclaimed "liberty to the captives" and accomplished ultimate jubilee through His atoning work.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 29|Leviticus 16:29-31]] (Day of Atonement observance), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 1|Deuteronomy 15:1-2]] (seventh year release)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18-19]] (proclaim liberty to captives), [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 17|2 Corinthians 3:17]] (where Spirit is, there is freedom), [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 1|Galatians 5:1]] (freedom in Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Leviticus 25's jubilee trumpet finds fulfillment in Christ who proclaimed and accomplished ultimate liberty. Jesus inaugurated His ministry by declaring: "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor. He has sent me to proclaim liberty to the captives and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty those who are oppressed, to proclaim the year of the Lord's favor" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18-19]]). This quotes Isaiah 61, a jubilee text, showing Christ as jubilee fulfillment. The trumpet announced: slaves freed, debts canceled, land restored. Christ accomplishes this spiritually: He frees those enslaved to sin ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 34|John 8:34-36]]: "If the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed"), cancels unpayable sin-debt ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 14|Colossians 2:14]]: "canceled the record of debt... nailing it to the cross"), restores lost inheritance ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 4|1 Peter 1:4]]: "inheritance that is imperishable"). The jubilee's trigger—Day of Atonement—prefigures Christ's atoning sacrifice that enables liberation. Forgiveness precedes freedom; atonement enables release. Paul declares: "Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 17|2 Corinthians 3:17]]). The trumpet sounded "throughout all your land" becomes gospel proclaimed "to all nations" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19]]). The fiftieth year's comprehensive restoration points to new creation when all things are restored ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 3#Acts 3 . 21|Acts 3:21]]). Believers experience jubilee proleptically now ("For freedom Christ has set us free"—[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 1|Galatians 5:1]]) while awaiting final trumpet that announces complete liberation from death itself. The jubilee trumpet was temporary (every fifty years); Christ's jubilee is eternal—liberation that never ends, freedom that cannot be revoked, inheritance that never diminishes.
