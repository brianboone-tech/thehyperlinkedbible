### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 13|Isaiah 52:13]]-[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|53:12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5375|H5375]] נָשָׂא (*nasa*) - "bore, carried" - The Servant bore our griefs and carried our sorrows
- [[Lexicon/H2470|H2470]] חָלָה (*chalah*) - "sickness, grief" - Our sicknesses and griefs laid upon the Servant
- [[Lexicon/H6586|H6586]] פֶּשַׁע (*pesha*) - "transgression, rebellion" - The Servant was pierced for our transgressions
- [[Lexicon/H5771|H5771]] עָוֹן (*avon*) - "iniquity, guilt" - The Servant was crushed for our iniquities
- [[Lexicon/H817|H817]] אָשָׁם (*asham*) - "guilt offering" - The Servant makes Himself a guilt offering
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (*zera*) - "seed, offspring" - The Servant will see His offspring despite death

**Context**: Isaiah 52:13-53:12 is the fourth and climactic Servant Song, providing unprecedented detail about vicarious atonement. The Servant's suffering is not incidental but purposeful—He dies as a guilt offering for sins, yet will see offspring and prolong days, creating a riddle solved only by resurrection.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **Leviticus 16**: Day of Atonement scapegoat "bears" (nasa) the people's iniquities into wilderness—the Servant bears sins permanently
- **Leviticus 4-5**: Guilt offering (asham) for unintentional sins—the Servant becomes the ultimate guilt offering
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 8|Genesis 22:8]]**: "God will provide the lamb"—the Servant is the Lamb God provides for sacrifice
- **Psalm 22**: Righteous sufferer's psalm of dereliction and vindication prefigures Servant's pattern

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier OT)**: Genesis 22:8 (God provides lamb); Leviticus 16 (scapegoat bears iniquity); Psalm 22 (suffering and vindication); Zechariah 12:10 (pierced one)
- **FROM OT (Later OT)**: No later OT development—this is the pinnacle of OT revelation about atonement

**Type Classification**: **Forward-looking** (explicit prophecy awaiting fulfillment) | **Direct Type** (divinely revealed blueprint of Messiah's atoning work)

**Christological Connection**: Every detail of Isaiah 53 finds fulfillment in Jesus Christ:
- **52:13-15**: Exalted yet marred—Jesus crucified yet risen and ascended
- **53:3**: Despised and rejected—Jesus rejected by His own ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 11|John 1:11]])
- **53:4-6**: Bore our sins—Jesus "bore our sins in his body on the tree" (1 Pet 2:24)
- **53:7**: Silent like a lamb—Jesus silent before accusers (Matt 26:63)
- **53:8**: Cut off from the land of the living—Jesus died
- **53:9**: Grave with wicked, with rich man—crucified with criminals, buried in Joseph's tomb (Matt 27:57-60)
- **53:10**: Makes Himself guilt offering, sees offspring—Jesus's death atones, His resurrection produces spiritual children
- **53:12**: Numbered with transgressors, bore sins, interceded—Jesus crucified between thieves ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 37|Luke 22:37]]), bore sins (1 Pet 2:24), interceded ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 34|Luke 23:34]])

The riddle of dying yet seeing offspring is resolved in resurrection: Jesus died, was buried, rose, and now sees countless spiritual children born through faith in His finished work.
