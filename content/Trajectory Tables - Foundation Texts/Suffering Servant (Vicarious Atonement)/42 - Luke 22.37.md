### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 37|Luke 22:37]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5048|G5048]] τελέω (*teleo*) - "to fulfill, accomplish, complete" - Scripture must be fulfilled in Jesus
- [[Lexicon/G3049|G3049]] λογίζομαι (*logizomai*) - "to reckon, count, number" - The Servant reckoned/numbered with transgressors
- [[Lexicon/G459|G459]] ἄνομος (*anomos*) - "lawless, transgressor" - Criminals, lawbreakers
- [[Lexicon/G5056|G5056]] τέλος (*telos*) - "end, goal" - What is written about Jesus reaches its fulfillment

**Context**: Luke 22:37 occurs during the Last Supper, just before Jesus's arrest. He instructs disciples to prepare for coming opposition, then explicitly quotes Isaiah 53:12, declaring this Scripture must be fulfilled in Him. This is Jesus's clearest pre-passion identification with the Suffering Servant.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] concludes the fourth Servant Song: "He was numbered with the transgressors; yet he bore the sin of many, and makes intercession for the transgressors." Jesus quotes this verse to predict His crucifixion between criminals.

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier OT)**: Isaiah 52:13-53:12 (full Servant Song); Psalm 22:6-8 (mocked as evildoer)
- **FROM OT (Later OT)**: N/A (Isaiah 53 is climactic)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 15#Mark 15 . 27|Mark 15:27-28]] - crucified with two robbers
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 32|Luke 23:32-33]] - led with criminals
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 18|John 19:18]] - crucified between two others
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]] - made to be sin
**Type Classification**: Jesus declares Isaiah 53:12 as **forward-looking Direct prophecy** that must find literal fulfillment in His passion

**Christological Connection**: Jesus was literally "numbered with the transgressors" when crucified between two criminals ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 15#Mark 15 . 27|Mark 15:27-28]]; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 32|Luke 23:32-33]]). But the theological significance runs deeper: He who knew no sin was "made to be sin" for us (2 Cor 5:21), treated as the ultimate transgressor though perfectly innocent. His numbering with transgressors was both literal (physical placement between criminals) and theological (bearing the legal guilt of sinners). Jesus's quotation of [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] demonstrates His conscious fulfillment of the Servant role. He understood that Messianic glory comes through shameful death, exaltation through humiliation, vindication through condemnation. By quoting this verse hours before His arrest, Jesus shows His death was not tragedy but triumph—the planned fulfillment of prophetic Scripture. The innocent Servant willingly takes the place assigned to transgressors.
