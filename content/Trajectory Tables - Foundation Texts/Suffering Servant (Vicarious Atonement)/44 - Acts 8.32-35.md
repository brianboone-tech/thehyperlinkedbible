### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 8#Acts 8 . 32|Acts 8:32-35]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4263|G4263]] πρόβατον (*probaton*) - "sheep" - Led as sheep to slaughter (Isa 53:7)
- [[Lexicon/G286|G286]] ἀμνός (*amnos*) - "lamb" - Silent as lamb before shearer (Isa 53:7)
- [[Lexicon/G2920|G2920]] κρίσις (*krisis*) - "judgment, justice" - In humiliation His justice was taken away (Isa 53:8)
- [[Lexicon/G1074|G1074]] γενεά (*genea*) - "generation, descendants" - Who can describe His generation? (Isa 53:8)
- [[Lexicon/G2097|G2097]] εὐαγγελίζω (*euangelizo*) - "to proclaim good news, gospel" - Philip preached Jesus from Isaiah 53

**Context**: Acts 8:32-35 records Philip's encounter with an Ethiopian eunuch reading Isaiah 53:7-8. When asked "About whom does the prophet say this?" Philip uses this very passage to proclaim the gospel of Jesus. This demonstrates apostolic conviction that Isaiah 53 is exclusively about Jesus.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 7|Isaiah 53:7-8]] forms the heart of the fourth Servant Song, describing the Servant's silence under oppression ("like a sheep... like a lamb... he did not open his mouth") and unjust death ("by oppression and judgment he was taken away... cut off from the land of the living").

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier OT)**: Isaiah 52:13-53:12 (complete Servant Song); Genesis 22:8 (God provides lamb); Exodus 12:3-5 (Passover lamb silent); Leviticus 16:21-22 (scapegoat bears iniquity)
- **FROM OT (Later OT)**: N/A (Isaiah 53 is climactic)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 29|John 1:29]] - Lamb of God
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 63|Matthew 26:63]] - silent before high priest
  - [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 15#Mark 15 . 5|Mark 15:5]] - Jesus silent
  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 23|1 Peter 2:23]] - did not retaliate
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 6|Revelation 5:6]] - Lamb slain
**Type Classification**: Philip interprets Isaiah 53:7-8 as **forward-looking Direct prophecy** finding exclusive fulfillment in Jesus Christ

**Christological Connection**: Philip's answer to "About whom does the prophet say this?"—beginning with [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 7|Isaiah 53:7-8]], he "told him the good news about Jesus" (8:35). This demonstrates apostolic conviction:

**Sheep to slaughter**: Jesus led to crucifixion, innocent yet condemned

**Lamb before shearer**: Jesus remained silent before Caiaphas (Matt 26:63), Herod (Luke 23:9), and Pilate (Mark 15:5), fulfilling "he did not open his mouth"

**Humiliation/justice taken away**: Jesus's trial was mockery of justice—false witnesses, illegal proceedings, predetermined verdict

**Cut off from land of living**: Jesus died, was buried

**Who can describe His generation?**: The LXX translation Philip uses emphasizes the mystery of the Servant's descendants—who can explain how one who dies will have offspring? Answer: resurrection and spiritual children born through faith

Philip's use of Isaiah 53 as evangelistic text shows this passage was recognized as the OT gospel. To proclaim Jesus is to explain how He fulfills the Servant's profile: innocent yet condemned, silent yet eloquent in suffering, killed yet producing countless descendants. The eunuch's question and Philip's answer establish that Isaiah 53 is about one person—Jesus Christ—not Israel collectively, not Isaiah personally, but the Suffering Servant who died and rose to save.
