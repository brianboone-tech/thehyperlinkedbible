**Context**: After Jesus died on the cross, a soldier pierced His side to confirm death. Unexpectedly, both blood and water flowed out. John emphasizes the eyewitness nature of this event (19:35: "He who saw it has borne witness") and its theological significance. This is the only Gospel to record this detail.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 21|Genesis 2:21-23]]: From Adam's opened side came Eve, the mother of all living. From Christ's opened side comes blood and water (the means of spiritual life for the new humanity).
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 17|Numbers 19:17]]: Blood (producing ashes) + living water = purification from death. From Christ's side flows both elements together.
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]]: Moses struck the rock, and water flowed. Paul identifies the rock as Christ ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 10#1 Corinthians 10 . 4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]). Christ, struck by God's judgment, releases living water (Spirit).

**Type Classification**: **Fulfillment** of **Direct Type** (Numbers 19's commanded dual-element purification) and **Providential Type** (water from struck rock).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 34|John 19:34]] is the climactic fulfillment of Numbers 19's water of purification typology. The red heifer ritual required two distinct elements: (1) ashes from the sacrificed heifer (representing blood atonement); (2) living water from a spring (representing the Spirit). These had to be mixed together—neither alone could purify. From Christ's pierced side, both elements flow simultaneously: blood and water. The symbolism is profound. The blood represents Christ's atoning sacrifice—the reality to which the red heifer's ashes pointed ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 13|Hebrews 9:13-14]]). The water represents the Holy Spirit—the living water Jesus promised ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]). What was separated in the OT type (sacrifice producing ashes, then water added) flows together from Christ's crucified body. He is both the sacrifice and the source of living water, both the Lamb slain and the fountain opened. Mather explains: "This water and blood, is the blood of Justification, and the water of Sanctification, both plentifully flowing from Christ our Purification." The dual flow teaches that salvation requires both: Christ's blood for forgiveness and the Spirit for regeneration, justification and sanctification, atonement and application. As [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 6|1 John 5:6]] insists: "not by water only but by water and blood"—both are necessary. The piercing of Christ's side releases what Numbers 19 required: the means of purification from death. Death pierced Christ; from that wound flows the antidote to death—cleansing blood and life-giving Spirit.
