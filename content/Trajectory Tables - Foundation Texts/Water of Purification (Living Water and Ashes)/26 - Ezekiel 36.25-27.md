### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2236|H2236]] זָרַק (*zaraq*) - "to sprinkle, scatter"
- [[Lexicon/H4325|H4325]] מַיִם (*mayim*) - "water"
- [[Lexicon/H2889|H2889]] טָהוֹר (*tahor*) - "clean, pure"
- [[Lexicon/H2891|H2891]] טָהֵר (*taher*) - "to be clean, to purify"
- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (*natan*) - "to give, put, place"
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (*ruach*) - "spirit, Spirit, breath"
- [[Lexicon/H3820|H3820]] לֵב (*lev*) - "heart"

**Context**: Ezekiel prophesies Israel's restoration from Babylonian exile using ceremonial purification language transformed into spiritual promise. God will sprinkle clean water (internal cleansing from sin), give a new heart (transformation of nature), and put His Spirit within them (indwelling presence). This reapplies Numbers 19's ritual language to new covenant reality.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- This passage directly develops [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 19#Numbers 19 . 17|Numbers 19:17-19]]'s sprinkling ritual, moving from external ceremonial cleansing to internal spiritual regeneration.
- The dual elements remain: water sprinkled (cleansing) + Spirit given (transformation). What was separated in the ceremonial law (ashes + living water) becomes unified in God's eschatological work.
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] (new covenant promise) and anticipates [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-29]] (Spirit poured out on all flesh).

**Type Classification**: **Forward-looking** (prophetic promise awaiting fulfillment in new covenant); reinterpretation of **Direct Type** (Numbers 19's commanded ritual).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 25|Ezekiel 36:25-27]] prophesies what Christ accomplishes through His death and resurrection: the dual cleansing of justification and sanctification. The "clean water" sprinkled corresponds to Christ's blood purifying the conscience ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]); the "Spirit" given corresponds to the living water flowing from Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38-39]]). Just as Numbers 19 required both ashes (representing sacrifice) and living water (representing life/Spirit), Ezekiel's promise requires both cleansing and Spirit-giving—both accomplished through Christ. Jesus alludes to this passage when He tells Nicodemus: "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 5|John 3:5]]). The "water" and "Spirit" are not two baptisms but the dual aspects of regeneration prefigured in Ezekiel's prophecy. Paul explicitly connects this text to Christian salvation: God "saved us... by the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit" ([[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]]). The trajectory is clear: Numbers 19's external ceremonial cleansing → Ezekiel's prophetic promise of internal spiritual cleansing → Christ's accomplishment of both through His sacrifice (providing cleansing) and sending the Spirit (providing transformation). What the water of purification symbolized externally, Christ effects internally through His blood and Spirit.
