**Context**: The vision of the New Jerusalem culminates with the river of the water of life flowing from God's throne. This synthesizes the entire biblical water trajectory: Eden's river (Genesis 2:10), the wilderness rock (Exodus 17:6), Ezekiel's temple river (Ezekiel 47:1), Zechariah's living waters (Zechariah 14:8), and Jesus' promise (John 7:38). The trajectory reaches its consummation—life-giving water flows eternally from God's presence.

**Type Classification**: **Consummation** of **Forward-looking** **Providential Type** (Eden's river) and **Direct Type** (Numbers 19's living water).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] consummates the water of purification typology by depicting the eternal reality toward which Numbers 19 pointed. The red heifer ritual required "living water" (מַיִם חַיִּים) mixed with ashes for purification from death-defilement. This temporary, external ceremony finds its ultimate fulfillment in the river of the water of life flowing from the Lamb's throne. The typological progression is complete: (1) **Source**: Numbers 19's living water came from springs; Revelation's water flows from God and the Lamb's throne—the ultimate source. (2) **Purity**: Numbers 19's water needed mixing with ashes (representing sacrifice); Revelation's water is "bright as crystal"—pure without admixture, because the Lamb's sacrifice is eternally complete. (3) **Purpose**: Numbers 19 cleansed from corpse-contact; Revelation needs no cleansing because "death shall be no more" (21:4). (4) **Accessibility**: Numbers 19's water required ritual preparation and application; Revelation's river flows freely—"let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who desires take the water of life without price" (22:17). (5) **Effect**: Numbers 19 restored temporary tabernacle access; Revelation grants eternal presence "they will see his face" (22:4). The dual elements of purification (ashes + living water) are transcended—the Lamb Himself (the ultimate sacrifice) is present, and the Spirit (the ultimate living water) flows unceasingly. What the ceremonial water accomplished externally and temporarily, the eschatological river accomplishes perfectly and eternally. The type is not discarded but glorified—living water remains, now flowing from the crucified and risen Lamb forever.
