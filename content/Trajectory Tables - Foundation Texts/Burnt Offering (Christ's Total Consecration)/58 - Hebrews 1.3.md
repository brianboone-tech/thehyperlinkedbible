**Context**: Hebrews' opening declaration presents Christ as the radiance of God's glory who, "when He had by Himself purged our sins, sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high." The burnt offering typology appears immediately—Christ's work of purification (the offering) followed by His session at God's right hand (the acceptance). The transition from work to rest, from offering to enthronement, signals completed burnt offering and divine vindication.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]] announces Christ's burnt offering in summary form: purification accomplished, session granted. The burnt offering pattern required (1) offering presented, (2) consumed on altar, (3) ascending to God, (4) accepted as sweet savor. Christ fulfilled each element. He presented Himself—"having made purification by Himself" emphasizes personal offering. He was consumed by divine wrath—bearing sin's full penalty. He ascended to the Father—resurrection and ascension following death. He was accepted—"sat down at the right hand of the Majesty" demonstrates supreme approval. The seated posture is crucial. Levitical priests stood continually because work never ceased; the tabernacle contained no chairs. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]] emphasizes this: "every priest stands daily ministering and offering repeatedly the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins." But Christ "sat down"—the aorist verb indicating completed action. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 9|Romans 6:9]] explains why sitting is appropriate: "knowing that Christ, having been raised from the dead, dies no more. Death no longer has dominion over Him." The daily burnt offerings needed constant repetition; Christ's offering requires no supplement. The location of Christ's session—"right hand of the Majesty on high"—fulfills [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] and indicates highest honor. The burnt offering's ashes were carried "to a clean place outside the camp" ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 6#Leviticus 6 . 11|Leviticus 6:11]]), honored but discarded. Christ's body was laid in Joseph's new tomb ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 59|Matthew 27:59-60]])—honored burial—but didn't remain there. He rose and ascended, receiving position of supreme authority. The trajectory from burnt offering typology to Christological reality shows escalation: temporary purification → eternal purification; priests standing → Christ seated; repeated offerings → singular offering; earthly tabernacle → heavenly throne; ashes to clean place → exaltation to God's right hand. What began in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 20|Genesis 8:20]] as Noah's sweet savor offering, continued through Levitical priesthood's daily burnt offerings, finds consummate fulfillment in Christ's self-offering vindicated by His exaltation. The seated Christ announces: burnt offering complete, purification accomplished, acceptance granted, work finished.
