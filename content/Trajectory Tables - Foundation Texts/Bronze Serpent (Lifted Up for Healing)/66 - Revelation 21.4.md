**Context**: The bronze serpent brought temporary healing from physical death; Christ's lifting up brings eternal healing from spiritual death. In the new creation, the ultimate fulfillment of the bronze serpent's promise arrives: "He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore, for the former things have passed away."

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]] reveals the bronze serpent typology's ultimate fulfillment. The bronze serpent granted temporary healing from snake venom—the bitten Israelite who looked lived longer but eventually died from other causes. Christ's cross accomplishes what the bronze serpent could only symbolize: eternal healing from sin's deadly poison. The fiery serpents brought physical death ([[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 6|Numbers 21:6]]); Christ defeats death itself. Paul declares this triumph: "Death is swallowed up in victory... thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 54|1 Corinthians 15:54-57]]). The progression from type to antitype shows escalating fulfillment: bronze serpent delayed death → Christ defeats death; temporary healing → eternal healing; physical life extended → spiritual life granted forever. Isaiah prophesied "he will swallow up death forever" (25:8); Revelation announces the prophecy's fulfillment: "death shall be no more" (21:4). This occurs because Christ, lifted up on the cross like the bronze serpent, bore sin's full penalty including death itself. He "abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the gospel" ([[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 1#2 Timothy 1 . 10|2 Timothy 1:10]]). The bronze serpent's healing was external (curing venom's effects); Christ's healing is comprehensive (removing death's cause—sin). [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 14|Revelation 20:14]] describes death's final defeat: "death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire"—the ancient serpent (12:9, the devil) and his ultimate weapon (death) are both destroyed. What began in Numbers 21 as God's gracious provision of temporary remedy culminates in Revelation 21 as complete eradication of the poison that brought death. The lifted serpent prefigured the lifted Christ; temporary healing foreshadowed eternal healing; looking in faith typified believing unto life everlasting. In the new creation, no more serpents bite because the ancient serpent is defeated; no more death reigns because Christ has conquered it through His death and resurrection.
