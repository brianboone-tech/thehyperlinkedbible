**Context**: Hebrews 12:1-2 exhorts believers to "lay aside every weight, and sin which clings so closely, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, looking to Jesus, the founder and perfecter of our faith." This transforms the Day of Atonement's external affliction (fasting, Leviticus 16:29-31) into internal mortification—putting sin to death by the Spirit's power. The motivation: Jesus "endured the cross, despising the shame, and is seated at the right hand of the throne of God" (v. 2). Christ's completed atonement and exaltation empower believers' sanctification.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 1|Hebrews 12:1-2]] transforms the Day of Atonement's external affliction ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 29|Leviticus 16:29-31]]) into internal mortification. Israel fasted; believers put sin to death. The power source differs—not self-discipline but Spirit-enablement. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 13|Romans 8:13]] states: "if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live." [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 5|Colossians 3:5]] commands: "Put to death therefore what is earthly in you: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and covetousness." The motivation: "looking to Jesus, the founder and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is seated at the right hand of the throne of God" (v. 2). Christ endured ultimate affliction—bearing God's wrath, suffering shame—accomplishing atonement. His exaltation—"seated at the right hand"—fulfills [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]], demonstrating suffering's end is glory. [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 8|Philippians 2:8-9]] declares: "he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. Therefore God has highly exalted him." Believers follow this pattern—laying aside sin (v. 1), enduring trials (v. 1), fixing eyes on Christ (v. 2), pressing toward glory ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 14|Philippians 3:14]]). The "lay aside every weight, and sin which clings so closely" (v. 1) pictures stripping for race—runners removed hindering garments. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 22|Ephesians 4:22-24]] commands: "put off your old self... and to put on the new self." The trajectory shows: Israel afflicts flesh through fasting (external) → Christ endures cross (substitutionary) → believers mortify sin by Spirit (internal) → final perfection in glory (consummation).
