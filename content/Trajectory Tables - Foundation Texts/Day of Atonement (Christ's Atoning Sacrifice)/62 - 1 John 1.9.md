### [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 9|1 John 1:9]]

**Context**: First John 1:9 promises: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness." This transforms the Day of Atonement's annual cycle into continual cleansing—believers don't wait yearly for forgiveness but receive it immediately through confession based on Christ's finished work. The confession isn't meritorious but acknowledges what Christ accomplished. God's faithfulness and justice guarantee forgiveness because Christ's blood satisfied divine wrath. The dual promise—forgiveness (legal) and cleansing (relational)—addresses both guilt and pollution.

**Christological Connection**: First [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 9|John 1:9]]'s promise—"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness"—transforms the Day of Atonement's annual cleansing into continual forgiveness. [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 30|Leviticus 16:30]] promised yearly cleansing: "on this day shall atonement be made for you to cleanse you." Christ's finished work enables immediate cleansing through confession. First [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 1|John 2:1-2]] explains the basis: "if anyone does sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous. He is the propitiation for our sins." God is "faithful" (keeps covenant promises) and "just" (Christ's blood satisfied legal demands)—forgiveness isn't mercy ignoring justice but justice satisfied through substitution. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 25|Romans 3:25-26]] declares God put forward Christ "as a propitiation... to show his righteousness... so that he might be just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus." The dual promise—forgive and cleanse—addresses guilt (forensic) and pollution (experiential). [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 103#Psalm 103 . 12|Psalm 103:12]] celebrates: "as far as the east is from the west, so far does he remove our transgressions from us." [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 19|Micah 7:19]] promises God will "cast all our sins into the depths of the sea." [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 12|Hebrews 8:12]] quotes new covenant promise: "I will remember their sins no more." The comprehensive scope—"all unrighteousness"—excludes no sin. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]] announces: "There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus." The trajectory shows: Day of Atonement provides annual cleansing (temporary, external) → Christ accomplishes eternal atonement (permanent, internal) → believers confess sins (ongoing practice) → God forgives immediately (continual cleansing) → perfect holiness in glory (consummated purity).
