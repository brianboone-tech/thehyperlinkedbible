**Context**: Revelation 21:4, 27 describes the new creation where "death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore, for the former things have passed away" (v. 4), and "nothing unclean will ever enter it" (v. 27). The Day of Atonement addressed sin annually, covering but not eradicating it. The new creation removes sin eternally—not merely atoned for but abolished, not just forgiven but eradicated. What the scapegoat symbolized—sins carried into wilderness never to return—becomes permanent reality. The redeemed stand before God perfectly clean, sins remembered no more, the fall's effects reversed forever.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]], 27 describes Christ's atonement's consummation—sin not merely atoned for but eradicated, death not covered but destroyed. "Death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore, for the former things have passed away" (v. 4). The Day of Atonement addressed sin annually; the new creation removes sin eternally. What the scapegoat symbolized—sins carried to remote wilderness never to return ([[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 22|Leviticus 16:22]])—becomes permanent reality. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 12|Hebrews 8:12]] promised: "I will remember their sins no more"—complete forgiveness. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]] states: "nothing unclean will ever enter it"—absolute holiness. The trajectory reaches completion: Day of Atonement provides annual covering (temporary) → Christ accomplishes eternal redemption (permanent) → believers experience progressive sanctification (ongoing) → new creation brings sinless perfection (consummated). First Corinthians 15:26 declares: "The last enemy to be destroyed is death." [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]], quoted in [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 54|1 Corinthians 15:54]], promises: "He will swallow up death forever." [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]] announces: "No longer will there be anything accursed"—Genesis 3's curse removed. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21]] promises creation will "be set free from its bondage to corruption." Second Peter 3:13 anticipates "new heavens and a new earth in which righteousness dwells." The fourfold elimination—death, mourning, crying, pain (v. 4)—reverses the fall's effects comprehensively. Genesis brought curse; Christ brings blessing. Adam brought death; Christ brings life. Sin's reign ends; righteousness reigns. Christ's atonement accomplishes what Day of Atonement foreshadowed—permanent, complete, eternal removal of sin and its effects, establishing new creation where God dwells with His people in unbroken fellowship forever.
