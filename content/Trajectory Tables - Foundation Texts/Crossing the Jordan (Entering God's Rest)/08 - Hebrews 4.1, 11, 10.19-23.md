### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 1|Hebrews 4:1, 11]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19-23]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4704|G4704]] σπουδάζω (*spoudazo*) - to be eager, strive earnestly
- [[Lexicon/G3954|G3954]] παρρησία (*parresia*) - confidence, boldness
- [[Lexicon/G1529|G1529]] εἴσοδος (*eisodos*) - entrance, way in
- [[Lexicon/G4314|G4314]] προσέρχομαι (*proserchomai*) - to draw near, approach

**Context**: Hebrews 4:1, 11 exhorts believers to "strive to enter that rest" while the promise remains. Hebrews 10:19-23 provides the means: "we have confidence to enter the holy places by the blood of Jesus" through "the new and living way." Believers are to "draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Israel had to actively enter Canaan - not passive reception
- Contrast: wilderness generation "failed to enter" due to unbelief
- Jordan crossing required stepping into the water (priests) and walking through (people)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 3#Joshua 3 . 14|Joshua 3:14-17]] - priests step in, people cross
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 7|Hebrews 3:7-4]]:11 - theological foundation for these exhortations
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 1|Hebrews 12:1-2]] - "run with endurance...looking to Jesus"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 4#James 4 . 8|James 4:8]] - "Draw near to God and he will draw near to you"

**Type Classification**: NT Application of Jordan crossing typology

**Christological Connection**: The blood of Jesus opens access to God's presence (holy places) just as the ark opened access across the Jordan. Believers now have "confidence to enter" - not through physical crossing but through Christ's finished work. The exhortation to "strive to enter" echoes Israel's need to actively cross Jordan and possess the land, not merely observe from the wilderness.
