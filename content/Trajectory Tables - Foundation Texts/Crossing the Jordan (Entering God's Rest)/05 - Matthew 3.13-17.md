### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 13|Matthew 3:13-17]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 3|Romans 6:3-4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G907|G907]] βαπτίζω (*baptizo*) - to baptize, immerse
- [[Lexicon/G2424|G2424]] Ἰησοῦς (*Iesous*) - Jesus (= Hebrew Joshua)
- [[Lexicon/G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα (*pneuma*) - Spirit
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (*huios*) - son

**Context**: Jesus comes to John at the Jordan River to be baptized. John protests, but Jesus insists "to fulfill all righteousness." As Jesus emerges from the water, the heavens open, the Spirit descends like a dove, and the Father declares "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Jordan = site of Israel's entrance into promised land
- Jesus' baptism inaugurates His public ministry (parallel to Joshua crossing into conquest)
- "Beloved Son" echoes Isaac (Gen 22:2) and Davidic king (Ps 2:7)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's Jordan crossing (Josh 3-4) - same location, new Joshua
- **TO**: Elijah/Elisha at Jordan (2 Kgs 2) - prophetic succession
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1]] - "my servant...my chosen, in whom my soul delights"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 3|Romans 6:3-4]] - baptism as death and resurrection with Christ
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 12|Colossians 2:12]] - "buried with him in baptism"

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment of Providential Type

**Christological Connection**: Jesus descends into the Jordan (type of death), emerges from the waters (type of resurrection), and is anointed by the Spirit for ministry. The Jordan becomes the symbol of death and resurrection with Christ. As Joshua crossed Jordan to begin the conquest, Jesus is baptized in Jordan to begin His conquest of sin, Satan, and death.
