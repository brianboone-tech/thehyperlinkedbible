### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G373|G373]] ἀναπαύω (*anapauo*) - to rest, refresh
- [[Lexicon/G2873|G2873]] κόπος (*kopos*) - labor, toil
- [[Lexicon/G2288|G2288]] θάνατος (*thanatos*) - death
- [[Lexicon/G3956|G3956]] πᾶς (*pas*) - all, every

**Context**: Revelation 14:13 pronounces blessing on those who "die in the Lord" - "they may rest from their labors, for their deeds follow them." Revelation 21:4 describes the new creation: "He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Joshua's rest was temporary - enemies returned in Judges
- Sabbath rest pointed forward to eternal rest
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]] promised God would "swallow up death forever"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]] - rest given, but incomplete
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]] - death swallowed up
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] - creation Sabbath as prototype of eternal rest
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 54|1 Corinthians 15:54-55]] - "Death is swallowed up in victory"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-10]] - "Sabbath rest remains for the people of God"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - ultimate fulfillment of rest typology

**Christological Connection**: Physical death for believers is the final Jordan crossing - not a barrier to fear but a passage to ultimate rest. Just as Joshua's generation crossed Jordan to enter the land of promise, believers cross through death (or Christ's return) to enter eternal rest in the new creation. The trajectory completes: wilderness wandering → Jordan crossing → Canaan rest → eternal rest in God's presence. Death itself is abolished; the rest is perfect and unending.
