### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 2|Joshua 1:2-3]]; [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 3#Joshua 3 . 14|Joshua 3:14-17]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5674|H5674]] עָבַר (*'abar*) - to cross over, pass through
- [[Lexicon/H3383|H3383]] יַרְדֵּן (*Yarden*) - Jordan ("descender")
- [[Lexicon/H5159|H5159]] נַחֲלָה (*nachalah*) - inheritance, possession
- [[Lexicon/H727|H727]] אָרוֹן (*'aron*) - ark (of the covenant)

**Context**: After Moses' death, God commissions Joshua to lead Israel across the Jordan into the promised land. The crossing occurs at flood stage (harvest time), making it humanly impossible. The priests carrying the ark step into the water first, and the waters are cut off upstream at Adam, allowing Israel to cross on dry ground.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Echoes the Red Sea crossing (Exod 14) - same divine pattern of waters parting
- [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 4#Joshua 4 . 23|Joshua 4:23]] explicitly connects: "as he did to the Red Sea"
- Elijah and Elisha later cross the Jordan miraculously (2 Kgs 2:8, 14)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Red Sea crossing (Exod 14:21-22) - waters parted for deliverance
- **TO**: Abrahamic promise of land (Gen 15:18-21)
- **FROM OT**: Memorial stones at Gilgal (Josh 4:19-24)
- **FROM OT**: Rest achieved (Josh 21:43-45)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] - "For if Joshua had given them rest..."

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (sovereignly arranged historical event)

**Christological Connection**: As Joshua (יְהוֹשֻׁעַ = Jesus in Greek) led Israel through the Jordan into Canaan, so Jesus leads His people through death into eternal rest. The ark going first pictures Christ entering death before us. The waters being cut off pictures judgment removed. Crossing on dry ground pictures safe passage through death for those united to Christ.
