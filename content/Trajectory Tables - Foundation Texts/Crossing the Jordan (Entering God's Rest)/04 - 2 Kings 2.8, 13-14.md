### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 2#2 Kings 2 . 8|2 Kings 2:8, 13-14]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H155|H155]] אַדֶּרֶת (*'addereth*) - mantle, cloak (prophetic garment)
- [[Lexicon/H5221|H5221]] נָכָה (*nakah*) - to strike
- [[Lexicon/H2673|H2673]] חָצָה (*chatsah*) - to divide
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (*ruach*) - spirit

**Context**: Elijah strikes the Jordan with his mantle, the waters part, and he and Elisha cross on dry ground. After Elijah's ascension in the whirlwind, Elisha strikes the waters with Elijah's mantle asking "Where is the LORD, the God of Elijah?" The waters part again, confirming the transfer of prophetic authority.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Echoes Joshua's Jordan crossing (Josh 3-4)
- Elijah's crossing precedes his "exodus" (ascension) - like Moses before death
- Elisha's double portion (2 Kgs 2:9) parallels Joshua receiving Moses' spirit (Num 27:18)

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's Jordan crossing (Josh 3:14-17) - waters part
- **TO**: Moses parting Red Sea (Exod 14:21) - paradigmatic water miracle
- **FROM OT**: Elisha's ministry of miracles follows (2 Kgs 2-13)
- **FROM NT**: Transfiguration - Moses and Elijah with Jesus (Matt 17:3)
- **FROM NT**: John the Baptist "in spirit and power of Elijah" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 17|Luke 1:17]])

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (sovereignly arranged prophetic event)

**Christological Connection**: Elijah's crossing the Jordan before his ascension (type of resurrection/glorification) and Elisha's crossing back with double portion (type of Spirit-empowerment) prefigure Christ's death, resurrection, ascension, and the subsequent outpouring of the Spirit at Pentecost. The Jordan symbolizes the threshold between earthly ministry and heavenly glory.
