### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]]; [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 14#Numbers 14 . 22|Numbers 14:22-23]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (*natan*) - to give
- [[Lexicon/H5117|H5117]] נוּחַ (*nuach*) - to rest, settle
- [[Lexicon/H3423|H3423]] יָרַשׁ (*yarash*) - to possess, inherit
- [[Lexicon/H539|H539]] אָמַן (*'aman*) - to believe, be faithful

**Context**: Joshua 21:43-45 summarizes the conquest: God gave Israel all the land He swore to their fathers; they possessed it and settled there; God gave them rest on every side; not one word of His promises failed. This stands in contrast to Numbers 14:22-23 where the wilderness generation was excluded for unbelief.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fulfillment of Abrahamic land promise (Gen 15:18-21)
- Contrast with wilderness generation's failure (Num 14; Deut 1:34-40)
- Foundation for Hebrews 3-4's warning about unbelief preventing entrance

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Abrahamic covenant (Gen 15:18-21) - land promise
- **TO**: Wilderness rebellion (Num 14:22-23) - unbelief excludes
- **FROM OT**: Incomplete conquest noted (Judg 1:27-36)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 18|Hebrews 3:18-19]] - "they were unable to enter because of unbelief"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] - "if Joshua had given them rest"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type with forward-looking significance (rest language anticipates eschatological fulfillment)

**Christological Connection**: Joshua gave Israel earthly rest from enemies, but [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] notes "if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken of another day afterward." The incomplete, temporary rest under Joshua points to the complete, eternal rest in Christ. Believers who trust Christ enter the rest that Joshua's generation only typified.
