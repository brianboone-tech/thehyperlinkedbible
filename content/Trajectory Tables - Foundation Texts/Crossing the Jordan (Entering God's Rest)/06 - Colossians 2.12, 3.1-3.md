### [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 12|Colossians 2:12]]; [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 1|Colossians 3:1-3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4916|G4916]] συνθάπτω (*sunthapto*) - to bury together with
- [[Lexicon/G4891|G4891]] συνεγείρω (*sunegeiro*) - to raise together with
- [[Lexicon/G4102|G4102]] πίστις (*pistis*) - faith
- [[Lexicon/G2928|G2928]] κρύπτω (*krupto*) - to hide

**Context**: Paul teaches that believers have been "buried with him in baptism" and "raised with him through faith in the powerful working of God." Building on this, Colossians 3:1-3 declares believers have "been raised with Christ" and should "seek the things that are above" because their "life is hidden with Christ in God."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Jordan crossing = transition from wilderness wandering to promised land rest
- Believers have already "crossed over" - death to life with Christ
- "Hidden with Christ" echoes ark hidden in Jordan's depths, then emerging

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Joshua's Jordan crossing (Josh 3-4) - transition from death to life
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 3|Romans 6:3-4]] - parallel baptism theology
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]] - "seated with him in heavenly places"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 3|Hebrews 4:3]] - "we who have believed enter that rest"

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Jordan crossing fulfilled in baptismal union with Christ

**Christological Connection**: Just as Israel crossed Jordan to enter Canaan's rest, believers have already crossed through death and resurrection with Christ. The crossing is complete; the rest has begun. Believers now live in the "already" of the promised land while awaiting the "not yet" of its full consummation. Their life is "hidden with Christ" as they await His appearing.
