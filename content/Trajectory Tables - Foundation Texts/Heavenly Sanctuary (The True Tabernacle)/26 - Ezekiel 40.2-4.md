### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 40#Ezekiel 40 . 2|Ezekiel 40:2-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4ראות|H4758]] מַרְאוֹת (*mar'ot*) - "visions" (plural of מַרְאֶה); divine revelations granted to the prophet
- [[Lexicon/H8508|H8508]] תָּבְנִית (*tavnit*) - "structure, pattern, plan" (from same root as Exod 25:9's תַּבְנִית)
- [[Lexicon/H4060|H4060]] מִדָּה (*middah*) - "measure, measurement"; precise dimensions revealed in the vision

**Context**: Ezekiel 40-48 presents the prophet's climactic vision of a restored temple, received in the 25th year of exile (c. 573 BC), exactly 14 years after Jerusalem's destruction. The vision begins with Ezekiel transported "in visions of God" (v. 2) to a high mountain where he sees "a structure like a city" (v. 2). A bronze-like figure (likely an angel) appears with measuring instruments (v. 3), commanding Ezekiel to observe carefully and declare everything to Israel (v. 4). The ensuing chapters provide extraordinarily detailed measurements of gates, courts, chambers, and the temple itself. The vision climaxes with God's glory entering this temple (43:1-5), promising eternal dwelling with purified Israel.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 9|Exodus 25:9]], 40 establishes precedent of Moses being shown a heavenly pattern for the earthly tabernacle
- [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 28#1 Chronicles 28 . 11|1 Chronicles 28:11-19]] records David receiving temple plans "in writing from the hand of the LORD"
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 10|Ezekiel 43:10-11]] explicitly commands the prophet to show Israel the temple's design "that they may be ashamed of their iniquities" and "keep the whole form of it and all its statutes"
- Zechariah 1-6 contains visions of heavenly realities related to post-exilic restoration
- [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 10|Revelation 21:10-27]] parallels this vision: John "in the Spirit" taken to a high mountain to see the new Jerusalem descending from heaven

**Type Classification**: **Providential Type** (God sovereignly granted Ezekiel this vision, not a commanded structure to build) and **Forward-Looking** (the vision explicitly points beyond current reality to eschatological fulfillment when God dwells with purified Israel)

**Christological Connection**: Ezekiel's temple vision finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ and the new creation. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] identifies Christ's body as the true temple: "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up." [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 19|Ephesians 2:19-22]] applies temple imagery to the church: believers are "built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit." [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]] states the final reality: "I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb." The trajectory moves from Ezekiel's visionary temple → Christ as temple → church as temple → God himself as temple. Christ entered the heavenly sanctuary (Heb 9:24) that Ezekiel glimpsed in prophetic vision, and believers now have access through him (Heb 4:14-16).
