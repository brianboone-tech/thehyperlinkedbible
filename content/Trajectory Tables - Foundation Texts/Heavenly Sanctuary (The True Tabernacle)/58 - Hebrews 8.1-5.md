### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3588|G2233]] ὑπόδειγμα (*hypodeigma*) - "copy, example, pattern"; that which shows forth a pattern or serves as a model
- [[Lexicon/G4639|G4639]] σκιά (*skia*) - "shadow, foreshadowing"; the outline cast by a substance, inferior to the reality
- [[Lexicon/G228|G228]] ἀληθινός (*alethinos*) - "true, genuine, real"; opposed to symbolic or counterfeit

**Context**: Hebrews 8:1-5 serves as the "main point" (κεφάλαιον, v. 1) summarizing chapters 5-7's argument for Christ's superior priesthood. The author declares: "We have such a high priest, one who is seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven" (v. 1). This high priest ministers in "the true tent" (τῆς σκηνῆς τῆς ἀληθινῆς) that the Lord erected, not man (v. 2). In contrast, Levitical priests serve in a sanctuary that is merely a "copy and shadow" (ὑπόδειγμα καὶ σκιά) of the heavenly things (v. 5). To prove this, the author quotes Exodus 25:40, where Moses was warned to make everything according to the pattern shown on the mountain. The argument is straightforward: if the earthly sanctuary was built according to a heavenly pattern, then the heavenly is the original and the earthly the inferior copy.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 9|Exodus 25:9]], 40; 26:30 establish the heavenly pattern principle—Moses shown the τύπος and commanded to replicate it exactly
- [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 28#1 Chronicles 28 . 11|1 Chronicles 28:11-19]] shows David receiving temple plans "in writing from the hand of the LORD," demonstrating continuity of heavenly blueprint revelation
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]], 4 prophesies a priest-king who sits at God's right hand and reigns as "priest forever after the order of Melchizedek"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1-4]] grants the prophet a vision of the Lord enthroned in the heavenly temple with seraphim worshiping

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Exodus 25:40 (quoted in v. 5); Psalm 110:1 (Christ seated at right hand); Numbers 12:7-8 (Moses faithful in all God's house)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11]] - Christ entered "the greater and more perfect tent not made with hands"
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]] - Christ entered "heaven itself," not copies
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22-24]] - believers come to Mount Zion, the heavenly Jerusalem, and the assembly of the firstborn enrolled in heaven
**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (earthly sanctuary divinely commanded as copy of heavenly) and **Forward-Looking** (Exodus itself testifies to a greater reality beyond the earthly copy, as Hebrews demonstrates by quoting Exod 25:40)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-5]] explicitly identifies Christ as the High Priest who ministers in the heavenly sanctuary. He is "seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven" (v. 1), fulfilling [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]'s prophecy. Unlike Levitical priests who stood daily offering repeated sacrifices, Christ sat down because his work is finished (cf. Heb 10:11-12). His ministry location—"heaven itself" (9:24)—proves the superiority of his priesthood. The earthly tabernacle pointed to this reality from its inception. Moses was shown the heavenly pattern (Exod 25:40); Christ entered the heavenly original. Owen's exposition emphasizes: "The entrance of Christ into heaven as our high priest was into it as the temple of God; wherein the chief thing considerable is the throne of grace."
