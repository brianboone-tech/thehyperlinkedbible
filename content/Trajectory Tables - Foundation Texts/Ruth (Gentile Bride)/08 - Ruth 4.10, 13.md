### [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 10|Ruth 4:10, 13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7069|H7069]] קָנָה (*qanah*) - "to acquire, buy"
- [[Lexicon/H802|H802]] אִשָּׁה (*ʾishah*) - "wife, woman"
- [[Lexicon/H4125|H4125]] מוֹאֲבִיָּה (*moʾaviyyah*) - "Moabitess"
- [[Lexicon/H3947|H3947]] לָקַח (*laqach*) - "to take, marry"
- [[Lexicon/H2030|H2030]] הֵרָיוֹן (*herayon*) - "conception"
- [[Lexicon/H3205|H3205]] יָלַד (*yalad*) - "to bear, give birth"

- [[Lexicon/H5414|H5414]] נָתַן (*nathan*) - "to give"

**Context**: After the nearer kinsman refuses to redeem, Boaz publicly declares: "I have acquired Ruth the Moabitess, Mahlon's widow, as my wife, to raise up the name of the deceased through his inheritance" (v. 10). The narrative then reports the marriage and its fruit: "So Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife. And when he had relations with her, the LORD enabled her to conceive, and she gave birth to a son" (v. 13). The foreign widow becomes the redeemer's bride; the barren womb receives divine life.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "Ruth the Moabitess" appears one final time in the public declaration—her ethnic origin acknowledged even as her status transforms. She is no longer "foreigner" but "wife."
- "The LORD enabled her to conceive" (lit. "YHWH gave her conception") echoes divine intervention in barren matriarchs: Sarah (Gen 21:1-2), Rebekah (Gen 25:21), Rachel (Gen 30:22-23), Hannah (1 Sam 1:19-20). Ruth joins this company.
- The acquired bride producing a son parallels the church's fruitfulness in Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 5|John 15:5]], 16).

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 21:1-2 (Sarah conceives), Genesis 30:22 (God remembered Rachel), 1 Samuel 1:19-20 (Hannah conceives)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 14|Ruth 4:14-17]] (the women bless the child), [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12]] (Davidic offspring promised)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 25|Ephesians 5:25-32]] (Christ and the church as bridegroom and bride), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 7|Revelation 19:7-9]] (marriage of the Lamb)

**Type Classification**: Providential (sovereignly arranged marriage); Forward-Looking (bride acquired by redeemer anticipates church united to Christ).

**Christological Connection**: Ruth's marriage to Boaz prefigures the church's union with Christ. (1) **"I Have Acquired"**: Boaz acquires Ruth; Christ "gave Himself up for" the church to "present her to Himself" (Eph 5:25-27). (2) **"The Moabitess"**: Ruth's foreign origin is acknowledged but overcome. The church is composed of those "once far off" (Eph 2:13). (3) **"She Became His Wife"**: The marriage represents covenant union. "The two shall become one flesh...I am saying this about Christ and the church" (Eph 5:31-32). (4) **"The LORD Gave Conception"**: Divine intervention produces fruit. "Apart from Me you can do nothing" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 5|John 15:5]]). The church's fruitfulness depends on Christ. (5) **"Gave Birth to a Son"**: The marriage produces offspring. The church produces "children of God" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 12|John 1:12]]). Ruth's transformation from foreign widow to fruitful wife is the pattern: outsiders become the Bride, and the Bride bears fruit through the Bridegroom's power.
