**Context**: Revelation 19:7-8: "Let us rejoice and be glad and give Him the glory. For the marriage of the Lamb has come, and His Bride has made herself ready. She was given to wear fine linen, bright and clean, for the fine linen is the righteous deeds of the saints." Revelation 21:2, 9: "I saw the Holy City, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband...Come, I will show you the Bride, the wife of the Lamb." The church—composed of redeemed from every nation—is presented as Christ's Bride in glorious consummation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The marriage metaphor develops from OT prophetic imagery: Israel as YHWH's bride (Isa 54:5-6; 62:5; [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 14|Hosea 2:14-20]]; Ezek 16:8-14).
- Ruth's marriage to Boaz is narrative embodiment of this metaphor: the destitute foreigner becoming the redeemer's bride parallels the church's story.
- "Fine linen, bright and clean" echoes Ruth's preparation on Naomi's instruction: "Wash yourself, put on perfume, and wear your best clothes" ([[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 3#Ruth 3 . 3|Ruth 3:3]]).

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Ruth 3:3 (Ruth's bridal preparation), Ruth 4:13 (Ruth becomes Boaz's wife), Isaiah 61:10 (garments of salvation), Isaiah 62:5 (as bridegroom rejoices over bride), Hosea 2:19-20 (I will betroth you to Me forever)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 2|2 Corinthians 11:2]] (presented as pure virgin to Christ), [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 25|Ephesians 5:25-32]] (Christ and the church), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 2|Matthew 22:2-14]] (wedding feast parable)
- **PARALLEL**: Revelation 21:9-27 (new Jerusalem described in bridal terms)

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation (final fulfillment of bridal imagery); Forward-Looking from Ruth's perspective.

**Christological Connection**: Ruth's marriage to Boaz finds its ultimate counterpart in the Lamb's marriage. (1) **Bride Made Ready**: Ruth prepared herself ([[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 3#Ruth 3 . 3|Ruth 3:3]]); the Bride "has made herself ready" (Rev 19:7). Both preparation and divine provision combine. (2) **Fine Linen**: Ruth wore her best clothes; the Bride wears "fine linen, bright and clean." The wedding garments are righteousness. (3) **From Poverty to Glory**: Ruth was a destitute widow; the church was "dead in trespasses." Both are transformed by the Redeemer. (4) **Marriage Consummation**: "Boaz took Ruth, and she became his wife" ([[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 13|Ruth 4:13]]); "The marriage of the Lamb has come" (Rev 19:7). The consummation is eternal. (5) **City and Bride**: The new Jerusalem is both city and Bride—the redeemed community in its glorified state. Ruth joined Bethlehem's community through marriage; believers join the heavenly city through Christ. The Moabitess bride anticipates the countless multitude from every nation who comprise the Lamb's Bride.
