**Context**: After the sealing of 144,000 from Israel's tribes, John sees the eschatological harvest: "After this I looked, and behold, a great multitude that no one could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and languages, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed in white robes, with palm branches in their hands, crying out with a loud voice, 'Salvation belongs to our God who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb!'" This vision portrays the ultimate fulfillment of Gentile inclusion—Ruth's story multiplied to cosmic scale.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The fourfold formula "every nation, tribe, people, language" emphasizes universality. Ruth's Moabite origin is just one of countless ethnicities represented.
- "No one could number" contrasts with the precise 144,000 from Israel. The Gentile ingathering exceeds calculation.
- Palm branches echo the Feast of Tabernacles (Lev 23:40), connecting to Israel's worship while expanding it to all nations.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 12:3 (all families blessed), Genesis 22:17 (offspring like stars—innumerable), Isaiah 2:2-3 (all nations streaming to Zion), Isaiah 56:7 (house of prayer for all peoples)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 1#Ruth 1 . 16|Ruth 1:16-17]] (Ruth's confession), [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 16|Zechariah 14:16]] (nations coming to worship)
- **PARALLEL**: Revelation 5:9 (purchased from every tribe), Revelation 14:6 (eternal gospel to every nation), Revelation 21:24 (nations walking by its light)

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation (final fulfillment of Gentile inclusion promises); Forward-Looking from all OT perspectives.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9-10]] is the ultimate fulfillment of Ruth's trajectory. (1) **One Becomes Multitude**: Ruth was one foreigner finding refuge; here are countless millions "from every nation." Her story anticipated the harvest. (2) **"No One Could Number"**: Ruth was individually numbered in David's genealogy; the Gentile ingathering exceeds counting. Grace multiplies exponentially. (3) **"Before the Lamb"**: Ruth found refuge "under YHWH's wings" ([[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 2#Ruth 2 . 12|Ruth 2:12]]); the multitude stands "before the Lamb." The Lamb IS the refuge. (4) **"Salvation Belongs"**: The multitude cries: "Salvation belongs to our God...and to the Lamb!" Ruth's experience of redemption (גְּאֻלָּה) through Boaz finds cosmic expression in σωτηρία through Christ. (5) **White Robes**: Ruth was clothed for her wedding ([[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 3#Ruth 3 . 3|Ruth 3:3]]); the multitude wears "white robes"—righteousness given by the Lamb. (6) **Palm Branches**: Ruth participated in Bethlehem's harvest festival; the multitude celebrates the eternal Tabernacles. The Moabitess who gleaned in Boaz's field joins the harvest of the ages—one among the innumerable multitude praising the Lamb forever.
