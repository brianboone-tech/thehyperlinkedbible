### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|Galatians 3:29]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5547|G5547]] Χριστός (Christos) - Christ, Messiah, Anointed One
- [[Lexicon/G4601-4700#G4690|G4690]] σπέρμα (sperma) - seed, offspring, descendants
- [[Lexicon/G2701-2800#G2818|G2818]] κληρονόμος (klēronomos) - heir, inheritor
- [[Lexicon/G1801-1900#G1860|G1860]] ἐπαγγελία (epangelia) - promise, pledge

**Context**: Galatians 3:23-29 concludes Paul's argument that justification is by faith alone, not works of the law. Verses 26-29 explain that all believers - Jew and Gentile, slave and free, male and female - are "sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus" (v. 26) and therefore "Abraham's seed, heirs according to the promise" (v. 29). Union with Christ makes Gentiles children of Abraham without physical circumcision.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 7|Genesis 12:7]]** - "To your offspring (seed) I will give this land" - original Abrahamic promise
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 13#Genesis 13 . 15|Genesis 13:15-16]]** - "To you and your offspring (seed) forever... like the dust of the earth" - land and numerical increase promised
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 17|Genesis 22:17-18]]** - "Your offspring (seed) will possess the gates of their enemies, and through your offspring (seed) all nations will be blessed" - universal blessing through Abraham's seed
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]]** - Paul identifies the singular "seed" as Christ: "The Scripture does not say 'and to seeds,' meaning many, but 'and to your seed,' meaning One, who is Christ"

The OT development shows promise to Abraham's seed (Genesis) → narrowing to one Seed, Christ (Paul's interpretation) → expansion to all who are "in Christ" (Gal 3:26-29). This is narrowing to one (Christ) then expansion through union with the One.

**Type Classification**: **Prophetic-Typological Fulfillment** - Abraham's seed/offspring is ultimately Christ, with believers participating in Him. **Forward-looking** - The Abrahamic promises always anticipated Christ and the multiethnic people of God (Gen 22:18: "all nations will be blessed").

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the true Seed of Abraham (Gal 3:16), the ultimate heir of all the covenant promises. He alone perfectly fulfills what it means to be Abraham's offspring - faithful, obedient, blessing the nations. Believers become Abraham's seed only through union with Christ by faith. As Paul argues, "If you belong to Christ, then you are Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise." The church's identity as spiritual Israel is grounded entirely in Christ, the true Israel who embodies and fulfills all the covenant promises made to the patriarchs.
