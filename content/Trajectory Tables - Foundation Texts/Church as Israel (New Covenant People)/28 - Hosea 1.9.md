### [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 9|Hosea 1:9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3801-3900#H3818|H3818]] לֹא עַמִּי (Lo-Ammi) - "Not My People" (covenant divorce formula)
- [[Lexicon/H5901-6000#H5971|H5971]] עַם (am) - people, nation
- [[Lexicon/H1-100#H1961|H1961]] הָיָה (hayah) - to be, become (covenant relationship)

**Context**: Hosea's third child is named "Lo-Ammi" (Not My People), symbolizing God's rejection of unfaithful Israel. This reverses the covenant formula "You will be My people, and I will be your God" (Exodus 6:7; Leviticus 26:12). The northern kingdom of Israel has broken covenant through idolatry, and God declares the covenant relationship severed.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 12|Leviticus 26:12]]** - Original covenant formula: "I will walk among you and be your God, and you will be My people"
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 7#Jeremiah 7 . 16|Jeremiah 7:16]]; 11:14** - God tells Jeremiah not to pray for "this people" - the covenant relationship is broken
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]]** - New covenant promise: "I will be their God, and they will be My people" - restoration beyond judgment
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 28|Ezekiel 36:28]]** - "You will be My people, and I will be your God" - eschatological renewal
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 9|Zechariah 13:9]]** - "They will call on My name, and I will answer them; I will say, 'They are My people,' and they will say, 'The LORD is our God'" - covenantal restoration

The OT trajectory shows covenant relationship broken (Hosea) → judgment announced (Jeremiah) → restoration promised (Jeremiah 31; Ezekiel 36) → eschatological renewal (Zechariah).

**Type Classification**: **Providential Type** - God sovereignly arranged Israel's covenant breaking to foreshadow the inclusion of Gentiles in the new covenant. **Forward-looking** - Hosea 1:10 immediately promises reversal, anticipating eschatological restoration.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 9|Hosea 1:9]] ultimately highlights humanity's covenant-breaking sinfulness, which Christ came to remedy. Through His blood, Christ establishes the new covenant ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]]), transforming "Not My People" into "My People." The church's identity as God's people is grounded not in ethnic descent or covenant obedience, but in Christ's faithful covenant-keeping on our behalf. He who is the true Israel (Matt 2:15) brings covenant outsiders into the family of God.
