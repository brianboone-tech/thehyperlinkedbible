### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9-10]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5485|G5485]] χάρις (charis) - grace (implied in "by Your blood You purchased")
- [[Lexicon/G59|G59]] ἀγοράζω (agorazō) - to purchase, redeem, buy (slave market imagery)
- [[Lexicon/G5401-5500#G5443|G5443]] φυλή (phylē) - tribe
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1100|G1100]] γλῶσσα (glōssa) - tongue, language
- [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2992|G2992]] λαός (laos) - people
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1484|G1484]] ἔθνος (ethnos) - nation, Gentiles
- [[Lexicon/G901-1000#G932|G932]] βασιλεία (basileia) - kingdom
- [[Lexicon/G2401-2500#G2409|G2409]] ἱερεύς (hiereus) - priest

**Context**: Revelation 5 depicts the heavenly throne room where only the Lamb who was slain is worthy to open the scroll of God's redemptive purposes. The four living creatures and twenty-four elders sing a new song (vv. 9-10), celebrating Christ's redemptive work: He purchased people "from every tribe and tongue and people and nation" and made them "a kingdom and priests to serve our God." This echoes Exodus 19:6 and fulfills it on a global, multiethnic scale.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]** - "You shall be to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation" - original covenant identity for Israel
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 6|Isaiah 61:6]]** - "You will be called priests of the LORD, ministers of our God" - renewed priestly identity for restored Israel
- **[[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 14|Daniel 7:14]], 27** - "His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom... The kingdom and dominion... will be given to the saints of the Most High" - eschatological kingdom ruled by God's people
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 16|Zechariah 14:16]]** - "All the survivors from all the nations... will go up year after year to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts" - nations worshiping the LORD

The OT trajectory shows covenant identity given to Israel (Exodus 19) → priestly identity renewed (Isaiah 61) → kingdom promised to saints (Daniel 7) → nations included (Zechariah 14) → multiethnic kingdom and priesthood fulfilled (Revelation 5).

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** - God explicitly commanded Israel to be His "kingdom of priests" (Exodus 19:6), which directly prefigures the church's eschatological royal-priestly identity. **Forward-looking** - The prophets anticipated this expanded to include the nations (Isaiah 61:6; Zechariah 14:16-21).

**Christological Connection**: Christ the Lamb is the ultimate fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]. He is the true King-Priest ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 13|Zechariah 6:13]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 1|Hebrews 7:1-3]]), who through His blood purchases a people and shares His royal-priestly identity with them. The church's destiny to reign as kingdom and priests is grounded entirely in union with Christ, the Lion of Judah and Lamb who was slain. [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]] finds its "Yes" in Christ ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 1#2 Corinthians 1 . 20|2 Corinthians 1:20]]): through Him, believers from **every** nation inherit Israel's covenant calling and are constituted as a multiethnic kingdom of priests, fulfilling God's original purpose to fill the earth with His image-bearing, glory-mediating people ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 3|Isaiah 6:3]]; [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]).
