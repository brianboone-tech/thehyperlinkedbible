### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 20|Isaiah 43:20-21]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5901-6000#H5971|H5971]] עַמִּי (ammi) - "My people"
- [[Lexicon/H1-100#H972|H972]] בָּחִיר (bachir) - chosen, elect
- [[Lexicon/H3301-3400#H3335|H3335]] יָצַר (yatsar) - to form, fashion, create
- [[Lexicon/H5401-5500#H5608|H5608]] סָפַר (saphar) - to declare, recount, proclaim
- [[Lexicon/H8101-8200#H8416|H8416]] תְּהִלָּה (tehillah) - praise, song of praise

**Context**: In Isaiah 43, God promises restoration from Babylonian exile, comparing it to a new exodus. In verses 20-21, He declares His purpose for Israel: "My chosen people, the people I formed for Myself, that they might declare My praise." This restates Israel's covenant purpose from Exodus 19:5-6, now renewed in exile.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5-6]]** - Original covenant purpose: treasured possession, kingdom of priests, holy nation
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 6|Isaiah 42:6-7]]** - Servant called to be "light for the Gentiles," extending covenant purpose to the nations
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 1|Isaiah 43:1]]** - "I have called you by your name; you are Mine" - covenant ownership and intimate relationship
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 7|Isaiah 43:7]]** - "Everyone called by My name and created for My glory, whom I have indeed formed and made" - creation language for covenant people
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 1|Isaiah 44:1-2]]** - "Jacob My servant... Israel whom I have chosen" - election reaffirmed
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 6|Isaiah 61:6]]** - "You will be called priests of the LORD, ministers of our God" - priestly purpose renewed
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 102#Psalm 102 . 18|Psalm 102:18]]** - "A people not yet created will praise the LORD" - future generation formed to praise God

The canonical trajectory shows covenant purpose established (Exodus 19) → crisis of exile → purpose reaffirmed (Isaiah 43) → priestly identity renewed (Isaiah 61) → ultimate fulfillment in multiethnic church.

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** - God explicitly stated Israel's purpose as declaring His praise, which the NT applies to the church. **Forward-looking** - Isaiah anticipated restoration beyond exile, which finds ultimate fulfillment in the new covenant community.

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the ultimate chosen Servant ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1]]; Matt 12:18) who perfectly declares God's praise through His obedient life, sacrificial death, and glorious resurrection. He is the true Israel who fulfills the covenant purpose. The church, united to Christ, shares in His mission to declare God's praise to the nations (Matt 28:19-20). Through the Spirit, believers are God's new creation (2 Cor 5:17), formed in Christ to display the glories of redeeming grace (Eph 2:10; 1 Pet 2:9).
