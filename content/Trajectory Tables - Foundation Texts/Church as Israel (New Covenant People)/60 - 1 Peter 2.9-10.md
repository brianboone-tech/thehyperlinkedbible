### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9-10]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1085|G1085]] γένος ἐκλεκτόν (genos eklekton) - chosen race, elect people
- [[Lexicon/G901-1000#G934|G934]] βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα (basileion hierateuma) - royal priesthood
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1484|G1484]] ἔθνος ἅγιον (ethnos hagion) - holy nation
- [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2992|G2992]] λαὸς εἰς περιποίησιν (laos eis peripoiēsin) - people for God's own possession
- [[Lexicon/G1801-1900#G1804|G1804]] ἐξαγγείλητε (exangeilēte) - proclaim, declare, announce

**Context**: 1 Peter 2:4-10 describes the church as God's new temple (vv. 4-5) and living stones built on Christ the cornerstone (vv. 6-8). Verse 9 then applies four OT Israel titles to the church - chosen race, royal priesthood, holy nation, people for possession - followed by the purpose: "that you may proclaim the virtues of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light." Verse 10 quotes Hosea 1-2 to emphasize the grace of becoming God's people.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5-6]]** - Original covenant identity: "treasured possession... kingdom of priests... holy nation"
- **[[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 6|Deuteronomy 7:6]]** - "Holy to the LORD your God... chosen... treasured possession"
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 20|Isaiah 43:20-21]]** - "My chosen people, the people I formed for Myself, that they might declare My praise"
- **[[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 9|Hosea 1:9-10]]; 2:23** - "Not My People" becomes "My People" and "sons of the living God"

Peter weaves together Exodus 19, Deuteronomy 7, Isaiah 43, and Hosea 2 to show the church inheriting all of Israel's covenant privileges through Christ.

**Type Classification**: **Direct and Corporate Typology** - God explicitly commanded Israel to be His kingdom of priests and holy nation (Exodus 19), which directly prefigures the church's priestly calling. **Backward-looking** - The NT retrospectively identifies this as pointing to the church's royal-priestly identity in Christ.

**Christological Connection**: All four titles ultimately point to Christ and are received through union with Him:
- **Chosen race**: Christ is God's Chosen One ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 35|Luke 9:35]]; Isa 42:1); believers are "chosen in Him" (Eph 1:4)
- **Royal priesthood**: Christ is King of kings (Rev 19:16) and Great High Priest (Heb 4:14); we reign and intercede through Him
- **Holy nation**: Christ is the Holy One of God ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 24|Mark 1:24]]); we are holy in Him (1 Cor 1:2, 30)
- **People for possession**: Christ is God's beloved Son (Matt 3:17); we are God's treasured possession in Christ (Eph 1:14)

The church does not replace Israel but fulfills Israel's calling through Christ, the true and faithful Israel, into whom Gentiles are graciously grafted (Rom 11:17-24).
