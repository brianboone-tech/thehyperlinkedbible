### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 13#Psalm 13 . 1|Psalm 13:1-6]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- שִׁיר (*shir*) - "sing" (v.6)
- עַד־אָנָה (*ad-anah*) - "how long?" (v.1-2, 4x repetition)
- יְהוָה (*YHWH*) - "LORD" (vv.1, 3, 6)

**Context**: A psalm of David expressing deep anguish over God's apparent hiddenness, yet resolving in confident praise. The fourfold "how long?" intensifies the lament before the dramatic pivot to trust and song.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Establishes the archetypal lament-to-praise structure found throughout the Psalter
- Psalm 22 follows this same pattern but with intensified suffering and broader resolution
- The pattern of crying out followed by singing becomes a template for righteous suffering

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Psalm 22:1-31 (expanded lament-to-praise), Jonah 2:1-9 (thanksgiving from depths)
- **FROM OT**: Similar structure in Psalms 6, 10, 35, 42, 74, 79, 80, 89, 90, 94
- **FROM NT**: Hebrews 2:12 reveals Christ as the ultimate singer of these psalms

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking | Providential

**Christological Connection**: Christ experienced the "How long?" of divine hiddenness on the cross, yet His suffering resolved in the ultimate "singing to the LORD"—first in resurrection triumph, then in leading the eternal congregation in praise.
