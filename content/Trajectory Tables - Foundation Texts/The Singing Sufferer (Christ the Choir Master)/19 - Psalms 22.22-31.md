### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 22|Psalm 22:22-31]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- אֲסַפְּרָה (*asapperah*) - "I will declare" (from נָגַד *nagad*) (v.22)
- קָהָל (*qahal*) - "congregation/assembly" (v.22) → LXX: ἐκκλησία
- אֲהַלֶּלְךָּ (*ahalelka*) - "I will praise you" (from הָלַל *halal*) (v.22)
- יִרְאֵי יְהוָה (*yir'e YHWH*) - "those who fear the LORD" (v.23)

**Context**: The dramatic resolution of Psalm 22. After 21 verses of intense suffering, the psalmist pivots to triumphant praise—declaring God's name in the congregation and calling all nations to worship. The shift is so sudden that some interpreters treat vv.22-31 as a separate psalm, but the unity is crucial: the same voice that cried "forsaken" now leads worship.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The resolution extends beyond personal deliverance to universal scope
- "All the ends of the earth" (v.27) and "all the families of the nations" (v.27) anticipate Gentile inclusion
- "Posterity will serve him" (v.30) projects the praise into future generations
- The suffering-to-praise pattern becomes a template for messianic expectation

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Psalm 18:49 (praise among nations), Isaiah 52:15 (nations/kings)
- **FROM OT**: Psalm 13:6 (singing after lament), Jonah 2:9 (thanksgiving from depths)
- **FROM NT**: Hebrews 2:12 (quotes v.22 directly, identifying Jesus as speaker)

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking | Providential

**Christological Connection**: Hebrews 2:12 makes the identification explicit: Jesus is the one who declares God's name to His brothers and sings praise in the midst of the congregation. The ἐκκλησία Christ addresses is the church—the assembly of those He has sanctified (Heb 2:11). Christ is not only the psalm's subject but its singer; the suffering One becomes the worship leader.
