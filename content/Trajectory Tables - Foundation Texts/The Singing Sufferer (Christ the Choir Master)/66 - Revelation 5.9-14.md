### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9-14]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- ᾠδὴν καινήν (*oden kainen*) - "new song" (v.9)
- ἐσφάγης (*esphages*) - "you were slain" (v.9, 12)
- ἀρνίον (*arnion*) - "Lamb" (v.12, 13—Johannine distinctive, 28x in Revelation)
- ἄξιος (*axios*) - "worthy" (v.9, 12)
- μυριάδες μυριάδων (*myriades myriadon*) - "myriads of myriads" (v.11)

**Context**: The heavenly throne room scene where the Lamb alone is worthy to open the sealed scroll. The slain-yet-standing Lamb receives the worship of the four living creatures, the twenty-four elders, myriads of angels, and all creation. This is the eschatological culmination of the singing sufferer pattern—the crucified One leads eternal worship.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "New song" echoes Psalm 33:3; 40:3; 96:1; 98:1; 144:9; 149:1
- Universal worship ("every creature") fulfills Psalm 22:27-29
- The "Lamb slain" imagery connects Passover, Isaiah 53, and Day of Atonement
- Daniel 7's "myriads" serve the Son of Man; here they worship the Lamb

**Connections**:
- **TO**: Revelation 14:3; 15:3-4 (more heavenly songs); 19:1-8 (Hallelujah chorus)
- **FROM OT**: Psalm 22:22-31 (universal praise following suffering), Isaiah 53:12 (slain servant)
- **FROM NT**: Hebrews 2:12 (Christ singing in congregation—now cosmically expanded)

**Type Classification**: Backward-looking | Providential

**Christological Connection**: The singing sufferer trajectory reaches its climax: the Lamb who was slain now receives the worship of all creation. The One who cried "Why have you forsaken me?" is now surrounded by myriads singing "Worthy is the Lamb!" The congregation (ἐκκλησία) He addressed in Hebrews 2:12 has expanded to include angels and all creatures. Christ is the eternal choir master—His cross-initiated song continues forever in the new creation.
