**Context**: In Revelation 5:5, Jesus is introduced as "the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David" who has conquered and can open the scroll. In 22:16, Jesus speaks directly: "I am the Root and the Offspring of David, the bright Morning Star." These are among Christ's final self-declarations in Scripture.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "Root of David" (ῥίζα Δαυίδ) parallels [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 10|Isaiah 11:10]]'s "Root of Jesse"
- The paradox of being both "Root" (ancestor/source) and "Offspring" (descendant) resolves only in Christ's divine-human nature
- The Branch who sprouted in humility now reigns in eternal glory
- The conquering Lion is revealed as a slain Lamb (5:6) - victory through sacrifice

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (all Branch prophecies anticipated this), Eschatological Consummation

**Christological Connection**: Christ's self-identification as both "Root and Offspring of David" (22:16) resolves the paradox implicit in Isaiah's prophecy. As eternal God, He is David's Root - the source from whom David derived his kingship. As incarnate man, He is David's Offspring - the descendant who fulfills the Davidic covenant. The Branch who emerged from Jesse's "stump" (apparent death of the dynasty) through humble birth in Nazareth now reigns eternally, and every tongue confesses Him Lord. The trajectory from [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] through 2 Samuel 7, Isaiah 4 and 11, Jeremiah 23 and 33, Zechariah 3 and 6, through Matthew and Romans, reaches its glorious terminus: the Righteous Branch has conquered, and His kingdom shall never end.
