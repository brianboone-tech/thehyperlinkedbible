### [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 12|Zechariah 6:12-13]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6780|H6780]] צֶמַח (tsemach) - branch, sprout
- [[Lexicon/H1129|H1129]] בָּנָה (banah) - to build
- [[Lexicon/H1964|H1964]] הֵיכָל (heykal) - temple, palace
- [[Lexicon/H3548|H3548]] כֹּהֵן (kohen) - priest
- [[Lexicon/H3678|H3678]] כִּסֵּא (kisse) - throne
- [[Lexicon/H7965|H7965]] שָׁלוֹם (shalom) - peace, counsel of peace

**Context**: Zechariah is commanded to crown Joshua the high priest with gold and silver crowns - a stunning symbolic action since priests were never crowned (only kings were). This deliberate violation of the Mosaic order points forward to one who will legitimately hold both offices: "He will be a priest on His throne."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Culmination of the Branch prophecies: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 2|Isaiah 4:2]] (divine origin) → [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5]] (royal righteousness) → [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 8|Zechariah 3:8]] (servant-branch) → 6:12-13 (priest-king)
- Resolves [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 17|Jeremiah 33:17-18]]'s dual promise of eternal Davidic and Levitical lines
- "Branch out from His place" suggests both growth and origin from obscurity
- "Peaceful counsel between the two" (priesthood and kingship) ends their historical tension

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14-18]] - parallel promise of Davidic king and Levitical priest
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - priest forever after order of Melchizedek
- **FROM OT**:
  - No further OT development - this is the climax of Branch prophecy
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 14|Hebrews 4:14-5]] - :10; 7:1-28; 8:1-2 (Christ as priest-king
**Type Classification**: Forward-looking, Direct Type (prophetic symbolic action with explicit interpretation)

**Christological Connection**: Hebrews presents Christ as the fulfillment of this priest-king prophecy. He is "a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek" (5:6; 7:17, quoting [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]), seated "at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven" (8:1). Christ builds the true temple - not made with hands but composed of living stones ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]]). The "peaceful counsel between the two" finds its resolution in Christ alone: His priestly sacrifice satisfies divine justice, enabling His kingly rule to be one of grace. The historical tension between Israel's kings and priests - often in conflict - is eternally resolved in the one Mediator.
