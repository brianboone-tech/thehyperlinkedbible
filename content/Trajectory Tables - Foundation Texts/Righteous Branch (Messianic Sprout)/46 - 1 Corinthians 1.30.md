### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 30|1 Corinthians 1:30]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4678|G4678]] σοφία (sophia) - wisdom
- [[Lexicon/G1343|G1343]] δικαιοσύνη (dikaiosynē) - righteousness
- [[Lexicon/G38|G38]] ἁγιασμός (hagiasmos) - sanctification, holiness
- [[Lexicon/G629|G629]] ἀπολύτρωσις (apolytrōsis) - redemption
- [[Lexicon/G1096|G1096]] γίνομαι (ginomai) - to become (ἐγενήθη - "became for us")

**Context**: Paul contrasts worldly wisdom with God's wisdom displayed in the cross. The Corinthians boasted in human wisdom and eloquence; Paul redirects their boasting to Christ alone (v. 31). Christ "became for us" (ἐγενήθη ἡμῖν) what we could never achieve ourselves: wisdom, righteousness, sanctification, and redemption.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 6|Jeremiah 23:6]] names the Branch "The LORD Our Righteousness" (יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 16|Jeremiah 33:16]] extends this name to Jerusalem/God's people
- Paul's declaration that Christ "became for us... righteousness" fulfills this prophetic name
- The imputation implied in "The LORD Our Righteousness" is made explicit in Christ

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (the Branch's name anticipated this reality), fulfillment through union with Christ

**Christological Connection**: The Branch prophesied in Jeremiah as "The LORD Our Righteousness" finds His identity fully revealed in [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 30|1 Corinthians 1:30]]. Christ does not merely provide righteousness as an external gift - He Himself "became" (ἐγενήθη) righteousness for us. This reflects Spurgeon's emphasis in his sermon on [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 6|Jeremiah 23:6]]: believers receive Christ's righteousness through imputation - God credits His perfect obedience to those who trust Him. The justified believer can say with Jeremiah's words, "The LORD is our righteousness," because through union with Christ, His righteousness is truly ours. This is the gospel reality to which all the Branch prophecies pointed.
