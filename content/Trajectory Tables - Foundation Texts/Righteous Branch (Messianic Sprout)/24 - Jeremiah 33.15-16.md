### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 15|Jeremiah 33:15-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6780|H6780]] צֶמַח (tsemach) - branch, sprout
- [[Lexicon/H6666|H6666]] צְדָקָה (tsedaqah) - righteousness
- [[Lexicon/H6779|H6779]] צָמַח (tsamach) - to sprout, spring up (verb)
- [[Lexicon/H3389|H3389]] יְרוּשָׁלִַם (Yerushalayim) - Jerusalem
- [[Lexicon/H983|H983]] בֶּטַח (betach) - security, safety

**Context**: Jeremiah is imprisoned in the courtyard of the guard (v. 1) when God reaffirms the Branch promise. This passage repeats Jeremiah 23:5-6 with significant additions: (1) explicit connection to God's "gracious promise" (v. 14), (2) grounding in creation covenant (vv. 20-21), and (3) extending the name "The LORD Our Righteousness" to Jerusalem itself (v. 16).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Reaffirms [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5-6]] with greater covenant emphasis
- The verb צָמַח ("cause to sprout") emphasizes God's sovereign action
- Jerusalem bearing the divine name extends the blessing from king to city/people
- Verses 17-22 ground the promise in eternal Davidic and Levitical covenants

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5-6]] - original Branch prophecy); 2 Samuel 7 (Davidic covenant
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 11|Zechariah 6:11-14]] - priest-king union fulfills vv. 17-18
- **FROM NT**:
  - Hebrews 7-8 (Christ as eternal priest-king)
**Type Classification**: Forward-looking, Providential Type

**Christological Connection**: The extension of "The LORD Our Righteousness" from the Branch (23:6) to Jerusalem (33:16) foreshadows the New Testament teaching that Christ's righteousness is imputed to His people. The church, as the new Jerusalem ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 26|Galatians 4:26]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]]), bears Christ's righteousness. The dual promise of eternal Davidic kingship and Levitical priesthood finds resolution in Christ alone, who as Hebrews demonstrates, holds both offices eternally - King after the order of David, Priest after the order of Melchizedek.
