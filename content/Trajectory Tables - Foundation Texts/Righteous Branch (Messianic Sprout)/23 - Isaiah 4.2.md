### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 2|Isaiah 4:2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6780|H6780]] צֶמַח (tsemach) - branch, sprout, growth
- [[Lexicon/H3068|H3068]] יְהוָה (YHWH) - the LORD
- [[Lexicon/H6643|H6643]] צְבִי (tsebi) - beauty, glory, honor
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (kabod) - glory, honor, splendor
- [[Lexicon/H6413|H6413]] פְּלֵיטָה (peletah) - remnant, escaped ones

**Context**: Following Isaiah's judgment oracle against Judah's proud women (3:16-4:1), this verse introduces an abrupt shift to eschatological hope. "In that day" (בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא) signals the Day of the LORD when judgment gives way to restoration for the remnant.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- First occurrence of צֶמַח (tsemach) as a messianic title
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5]] and 33:15 develop this into "righteous Branch" (צֶמַח צַדִּיק)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 8|Zechariah 3:8]] and 6:12 further develop the Branch as priest-king
- The "fruit of the land" may parallel the Branch, or refer to material blessing accompanying His reign

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking, Providential Type

**Christological Connection**: The "Branch of the LORD" points to Christ's divine nature - He is not merely David's descendant but the LORD's own Branch. The description "beautiful and glorious" (לִצְבִי וּלְכָבוֹד) anticipates the glory believers behold in Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]: "We have seen His glory"). That this glory is "for the survivors of Israel" points to the remnant theology that Paul develops in Romans 9-11, where Gentiles are grafted into Israel's olive tree through faith in Christ, the true Branch.
