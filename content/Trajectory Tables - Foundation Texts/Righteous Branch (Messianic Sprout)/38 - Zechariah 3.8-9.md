### [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 8|Zechariah 3:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6780|H6780]] צֶמַח (tsemach) - branch, sprout
- [[Lexicon/H5650|H5650]] עֶבֶד (ebed) - servant
- [[Lexicon/H226|H226]] אוֹת (ot) - sign, symbol, portent
- [[Lexicon/H68|H68]] אֶבֶן (eben) - stone
- [[Lexicon/H5869|H5869]] עַיִן (ayin) - eye (seven eyes on the stone)
- [[Lexicon/H5771|H5771]] עָוֹן (avon) - iniquity, guilt

**Context**: Fourth night vision - Joshua the high priest stands accused by Satan, clothed in filthy garments (sin). The Angel of the LORD removes Joshua's iniquity and reclothes him in pure vestments. Joshua and his fellow priests are declared "men of sign" (אַנְשֵׁי מוֹפֵת) - symbolic figures pointing to the coming "Servant, the Branch."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Combines two messianic titles: "Servant" (Isaiah 42-53) and "Branch" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 2|Isaiah 4:2]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5]])
- The seven-eyed stone anticipates [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 10|Zechariah 4:10]] (eyes of the LORD)
- Iniquity removed "in a single day" points to once-for-all atonement
- Joshua the high priest typologically represents the coming priest-king

- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 29|John 1:29]] - Lamb takes away sin

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking, Direct Type (prophetic vision with explicit messianic identification)

**Christological Connection**: The removal of iniquity "in a single day" (בְּיוֹם אֶחָד) points directly to the cross. John the Baptist identifies Jesus as "the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 29|John 1:29]]), and Hebrews emphasizes that Christ "appeared once for all at the end of the ages to put away sin by the sacrifice of Himself" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26]]). Joshua's symbolic cleansing - filthy garments removed, pure vestments given - pictures justification: believers' sins removed and Christ's righteousness imputed. The Branch is both the priest who offers sacrifice and the sacrifice itself.
