### [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zera) - seed, offspring, descendant
- [[Lexicon/H4467|H4467]] מַמְלָכָה (mamlakah) - kingdom, sovereignty
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (olam) - forever, everlasting

**Context**: Nathan's oracle to David establishing the Davidic covenant. David desired to build God a house (temple), but God reverses this: He will build David a "house" (dynasty). The promise includes an immediate son (Solomon) who will build the temple, but extends beyond to an eternal descendant whose kingdom will never end.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The "seed" (זֶרַע) language connects to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] and the Abrahamic promises
- Later prophets develop this into explicit "Branch" (צֶמַח/נֵצֶר) terminology
- Psalm 89 and 132 expand on the covenant's unconditional nature
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14-22]] grounds the Branch promise in this Davidic covenant

**Christological Connection**: Jesus is identified as "Son of David" throughout the Gospels ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 1|Matthew 1:1]]; 9:27; 12:23; 21:9). The angel Gabriel explicitly connects Jesus' birth to this promise: "The Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David, and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]]). Peter at Pentecost declares that David foresaw Christ's resurrection as the fulfillment of God's oath ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 30|Acts 2:30-31]]). The "seed" that would build an eternal house is ultimately Christ, who builds the true temple - His church composed of living stones.
