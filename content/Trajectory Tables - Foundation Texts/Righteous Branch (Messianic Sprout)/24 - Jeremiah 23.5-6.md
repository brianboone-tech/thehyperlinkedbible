### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5-6]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6780|H6780]] צֶמַח (tsemach) - branch, sprout
- [[Lexicon/H6662|H6662]] צַדִּיק (tsaddiq) - righteous, just
- [[Lexicon/H4428|H4428]] מֶלֶךְ (melek) - king
- [[Lexicon/H7919|H7919]] שָׂכַל (sakal) - to act wisely, prosper
- [[Lexicon/H6666|H6666]] צְדָקָה (tsedaqah) - righteousness, justice
- [[Lexicon/H3072|H3072]] יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ (YHWH Tsidqenu) - The LORD Our Righteousness

**Context**: Jeremiah indicts Judah's shepherds (kings) who have scattered the flock (vv. 1-2). God promises to gather the remnant Himself (v. 3) and raise up faithful shepherds (v. 4). The ultimate shepherd is the "righteous Branch" - a Davidic king who will reign in wisdom, justice, and righteousness. His divine name identifies Him with Yahweh Himself.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Develops [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 2|Isaiah 4:2]]'s "Branch of the LORD" into "righteous Branch for David"
- Explicitly royal: "a King shall reign and prosper"
- The name "The LORD Our Righteousness" identifies the Branch with Yahweh
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 15|Jeremiah 33:15-16]] reaffirms this prophecy with covenant certainty

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking, Providential Type

**Christological Connection**: Paul declares that Christ "became for us wisdom from God—that is, our righteousness" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 30|1 Corinthians 1:30]]), fulfilling the name "The LORD Our Righteousness." The imputation of Christ's righteousness to believers ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]]; [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 21|Romans 3:21-26]]) is the gospel reality to which this title points. Jesus is not merely a righteous king but righteousness itself, credited to all who trust in Him. As Spurgeon preached, believers "receive Christ's righteousness through imputation—God credits His perfect life and obedience to those who trust Him."
