### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 45#Psalm 45 . 6|Psalm 45:6-7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- כִּסְאֲךָ אֱלֹהִים (kis'akha elohim) - "Your throne, O God" (vocative address)
- עוֹלָם וָעֶד (olam va'ed) - "Forever and ever" - Eternal duration
- מָשַׁח (mashach) - "Anointed" - Root of "Messiah"
- אֱלֹהִים אֱלֹהֶיךָ (elohim elohekha) - "God, your God" - God addressing the king as "God"

**Context**: Psalm 45 is a royal wedding psalm, celebrating the king's marriage. Verses 6-7 address the king in stunning terms: "Your throne, O God, is forever and ever... therefore God, your God, has anointed you." The psalm attributes divine honors to the Davidic king while acknowledging he is anointed by God. This created interpretive tension—how can the king be called "God" (אֱלֹהִים) while also having "your God" (אֱלֹהֶיךָ)? The NT resolves this by applying the psalm to Jesus, the ultimate Davidic king who is both God and man.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 13|2 Samuel 7:13-16]] - Davidic covenant: "I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever... Your throne shall be established forever"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalm 2:7]] - "You are my Son; today I have begotten you"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 5|Psalm 72:5]],17 - "May he live while the sun endures... May his name endure forever"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]],36-37 - "I will make him the firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth... His offspring shall endure forever, his throne as long as the sun before me"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] - "The LORD says to my Lord: 'Sit at my right hand'"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: 2 Samuel 7 - Davidic covenant establishing eternal throne
- **FROM OT**: Later Davidic psalms (72, 89, 110) expand messianic vision
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 8|Hebrews 1:8-9]] - Quoted in full, applied to the Son; establishes deity of Christ

**Type Classification**: **Forward-looking | Direct**
- Forward-looking: The psalm's hyperbolic language (eternal throne, divine address) points beyond any historical king to the ultimate Davidic Messiah.
- Direct: Davidic covenant promises (2 Sam 7) directly establish the expectation of an eternal king.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 8|Hebrews 1:8-9]] applies [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 45#Psalm 45 . 6|Psalm 45:6-7]] to Jesus with full force: "But of the Son he says, 'Your throne, O God, is forever and ever, the scepter of uprightness is the scepter of your kingdom. You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness beyond your companions.'" The author identifies Jesus as the one whom the Father addresses as "God" (ὁ θεός). This is not the Father speaking to Himself, but the Father addressing the Son as deity. The phrase "God, your God, has anointed you" indicates the Son's humanity (He is anointed) while affirming His deity (addressed as God). Jesus is the ultimate Davidic king whose throne endures forever because He is both God and man—the incarnate Son.
