### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 14|Exodus 3:14]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- אֶהְיֶה (ehyeh) - "I AM" / "I WILL BE" - First person singular imperfect of הָיָה (hayah, "to be")
- יהוה (YHWH) - The divine name, derived from third person form of same root

**Context**: At the burning bush, Moses asks God's name to tell the Israelites who sent him. God responds with "I AM WHO I AM" (אֶהְיֶה אֲשֶׁר אֶהְיֶה), commanding Moses to say "I AM" (אֶהְיֶה) has sent you. This is immediately connected to "the LORD" (יהוה) in v. 15 as God's eternal name. The name emphasizes God's self-existence, eternal presence, and covenant faithfulness—He is the self-existent one who will be with His people.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 6#Exodus 6 . 2|Exodus 6:2-3]] - "I am the LORD (יהוה). I appeared to Abraham... but by my name the LORD I did not make myself known to them"
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 39|Deuteronomy 32:39]] - "See now that I, even I, am he, and there is no god beside me"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 4|Isaiah 41:4]]; 43:10,13,25; 46:4; 48:12 - Repeated "I am he" (אֲנִי הוּא) declarations by Yahweh, echoing [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 14|Exodus 3:14]]
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 9|Hosea 1:9]] - Contrast: "You are not my people, and I am not 'I AM' to you" (wordplay on divine name)

**Type Classification**: **Forward-looking | Providential**
- Forward-looking: The name intrinsically points beyond the Exodus to God's eternal, unchanging presence with His people throughout redemptive history.
- Providential: God's self-revelation at this moment in history, establishing the name by which He will be known.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus' use of "I AM" (ἐγώ εἰμι) in John's Gospel applies this divine name directly to Himself. Most explicitly in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 58|John 8:58]] ("Before Abraham was, I AM"), Jesus claims pre-existence, eternality, and the divine name itself. The Jews immediately attempt to stone Him for blasphemy, recognizing the claim. Other instances ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 20|John 6:20]]; 18:5-6) also echo [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 14|Exodus 3:14]]. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 8|Hebrews 13:8]] declares "Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever"—the immutability inherent in "I AM."
