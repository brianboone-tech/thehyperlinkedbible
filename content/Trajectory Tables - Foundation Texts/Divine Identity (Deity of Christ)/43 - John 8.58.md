### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 58|John 8:58]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- πρὶν (prin) - "Before" - Temporal conjunction
- Ἀβραὰμ (Abraam) - Abraham
- γενέσθαι (genesthai) - "Was born" / "came into being" - Aorist infinitive of γίνομαι
- ἐγώ εἰμι (ego eimi) - "I AM" - Present tense, echoing LXX of Exodus 3:14

**Context**: John 8 records Jesus' escalating conflict with the Jews during the Feast of Tabernacles. The chapter builds to this climax: Jesus claims, "Before Abraham was, I AM." The contrast is stark—Abraham "came into being" (γενέσθαι, aorist, temporal existence), but Jesus simply "is" (ἐγώ εἰμι, present tense, eternal existence). The Jews immediately pick up stones to execute Him for blasphemy (v. 59), recognizing Jesus' claim to the divine name from Exodus 3:14. This is John's clearest statement of Jesus' pre-existence and deity.

**Type Classification**: **Fulfillment of Forward-looking | Direct type**
- The divine name "I AM" intrinsically pointed forward to Yahweh's eternal presence with His people. Jesus claims this name as His own.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 58|John 8:58]] is Jesus' explicit claim to the divine name "I AM" (ἐγώ εἰμι). The statement asserts:
1. **Pre-existence**: Jesus existed before Abraham (c. 2000 BC)
2. **Eternality**: Not "I was" but "I AM"—present tense, eternal existence
3. **Divine identity**: Direct claim to Yahweh's name from [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 14|Exodus 3:14]]

The Jews' immediate attempt to stone Jesus (v. 59) proves they understood His claim. Stoning was the prescribed penalty for blasphemy (Lev 24:16). If Jesus were merely claiming to be a prophet or angel, no blasphemy would exist. But claiming to be the eternal "I AM"—that is claiming to be Yahweh Himself. John presents this not as blasphemy but as revelation of Jesus' true identity. Throughout John's Gospel, Jesus uses ἐγώ εἰμι with predicates ("I am the bread of life," "I am the light of the world") and absolutely ("I AM"). The absolute usage ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 58|John 8:58]]; 18:5-6) claims the divine name, revealing Jesus as the incarnate Yahweh.
