### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 12|Revelation 1:12-18]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3664|G3664]] ὅμοιος (homoios) - like, similar to
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (huios) - son
- [[Lexicon/G444|G444]] ἄνθρωπος (anthrōpos) - man (as in "Son of Man")
- [[Lexicon/G4413|G4413]] πρῶτος (prōtos) - first
- [[Lexicon/G2078|G2078]] ἔσχατος (eschatos) - last

**Context**: John turns to see the voice speaking to him and beholds the risen Christ in unveiled glory—"one like a son of man" with white hair, eyes like flames of fire, feet like burnished bronze, and a voice like many waters. Christ identifies Himself: "Fear not, I am the first and the last, and the living one. I died, and behold I am alive forevermore."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] - "One like a son of man" coming with clouds
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 5|Daniel 10:5-6]] - Similar glorious figure with bronze limbs and flaming eyes
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 1#Ezekiel 1 . 26|Ezekiel 1:26-28]] - Likeness of a human form on the throne
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 12|Revelation 19:12]] - Eyes like flames of fire

**Type Classification**: Eschatological fulfillment; Direct theophany

**Christological Connection**: John's vision of the risen Christ synthesizes all OT theophanic imagery into a single, overwhelming revelation. The "one like a son of man" recalls Daniel 7; the fiery eyes and bronze feet echo Daniel 10; the glory surrounding Him parallels Ezekiel 1. When Christ declares "I am the first and the last"—a title YHWH claims exclusively ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 6|Isaiah 44:6]]; 48:12)—He confirms that all prior theophanies were appearances of the One who now stands revealed in resurrection glory.
