### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 17#Matthew 17 . 1|Matthew 17:1-8]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3339|G3339]] μεταμορφόω (metamorphoō) - transformed, transfigured
- [[Lexicon/G1391|G1391]] δόξα (doxa) - glory
- [[Lexicon/G5457|G5457]] φῶς (phōs) - light

**Context**: Jesus takes Peter, James, and John to a high mountain where He is transfigured before them—His face shining like the sun and His garments becoming white as light. Moses and Elijah appear, and a voice from the cloud declares, "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased; listen to him."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 29|Exodus 34:29-35]] - Moses' face shone from being in God's presence; Christ's glory is intrinsic, not derived
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 9|Daniel 7:9]] - Ancient of Days with garments white as snow
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 1#2 Peter 1 . 16|2 Peter 1:16-18]] - Peter recalls witnessing Christ's majesty on the holy mountain

**Type Classification**: Direct fulfillment (Jesus reveals His pre-existent glory)

**Christological Connection**: The Transfiguration unveils what the OT theophanies anticipated—God dwelling with humanity in visible glory. Where Moses' face merely reflected glory, Christ's glory shines from within, revealing Him as "the radiance of the glory of God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). The voice from heaven directly identifies Jesus as the Son, connecting this revelation to all prior divine appearances.
