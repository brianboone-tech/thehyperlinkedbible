### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 2#Leviticus 2 . 11|Leviticus 2:11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מִנְחָה (minḥâ) - grain offering, tribute offering
- שְׂאֹר (śəʾōr) - leaven, yeast
- דְּבַשׁ (dəḇaš) - honey
- אִשֶּׁה (ʾiššê) - food offering, offering made by fire to the LORD

**Context**: Leviticus 2 regulates the grain offering (מִנְחָה, minḥâ), an offering of fine flour, oil, and frankincense presented to the LORD. Unlike animal sacrifices, this offering came from the produce of the field, representing the fruit of human labor dedicated to God. Verse 11 prohibits two ingredients: leaven and honey.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 25|Exodus 34:25]] - prohibition of leavened bread with Passover sacrifice
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 3|Genesis 4:3-4]] - Cain's grain offering, Abel's blood sacrifice—foreshadowing inadequacy of works vs. substitutionary atonement
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 4#Amos 4 . 5|Amos 4:5]] - God sarcastically condemns Israel's corrupted worship: "Offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving of that which is leavened"—violating [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 2#Leviticus 2 . 11|Leviticus 2:11]] shows their worship is defiled
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 16#Matthew 16 . 6|Matthew 16:6-12]] - Jesus uses leaven as metaphor for corrupting influence
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 . 6|1 Corinthians 5:6-8]] - leaven symbolizes sin and malice
**Christological Connection**: The prohibition of leaven in offerings points to Christ, the perfectly pure offering. Unlike leavened bread (corrupted by fermentation), Christ is the unleavened bread—absolutely holy, undefiled, separate from sinners ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 26|Hebrews 7:26]]). His offering to the Father was without corruption: "He committed no sin, neither was deceit found in his mouth" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 22|1 Peter 2:22]]). The grain offerings Israel brought were types of the ultimate offering: Christ presenting Himself unblemished to God ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]). Now, as royal priests ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]), we offer "spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]])—but only because we are made acceptable in Christ, our unleavened offering.

**Application**: What offerings are you bringing to God? Worship with hidden sin is leavened worship—unacceptable. Prayer with unconfessed iniquity is corrupted prayer (Psalm 66:18). Service with impure motives is defiled service. God demands holiness in those who approach Him. Search your heart: Is there leaven (sin, hypocrisy, false teaching) corrupting your worship? Confess it, forsake it, and approach God through Christ alone—the unleavened offering that makes your worship acceptable.
