### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]]

**Context**: Moses sprinkles blood on the people saying, "Behold the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you." This inaugurates the Mosaic covenant through blood, establishing blood as mediator between God and His people. Where Abel's blood cried from the ground marking broken relationship (murder), covenant blood sprinkled on the people establishes restored relationship. Hebrews 9:20 quotes this verse to show Christ's blood inaugurates a better covenant.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- דַּם־הַבְּרִית (*dam-habberit*) - "blood of the covenant"—blood ratifies covenant
- וַיִּזְרֹק (*vayyizroq*) - "and he sprinkled"—application of blood to people
- כָּרַת (*karat*) - "cut" (covenant making involves cutting/blood)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]] transforms blood theology from Abel's cry for vengeance to covenant establishment. Where Abel's blood marked rupture, Moses' blood-sprinkling marked relationship. Jesus explicitly echoes this verse at the Last Supper: "This is my blood of the covenant" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 28|Matthew 26:28]]). [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 20|Hebrews 9:20]] quotes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]] to demonstrate that the old covenant required blood, then shows Christ's blood inaugurates the new covenant more effectively. The trajectory: Abel's blood (broken relationship) → Mosaic blood (covenant relationship) → Christ's blood (eternal covenant relationship). Each stage shows blood speaking—Abel's for justice, Moses' for covenant, Christ's for forgiveness.
