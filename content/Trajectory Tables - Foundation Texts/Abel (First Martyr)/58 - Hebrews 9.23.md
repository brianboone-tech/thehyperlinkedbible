### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 23|Hebrews 9:23]]

**Context**: "Thus it was necessary for the copies of the heavenly things to be purified with these rites, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these." The earthly tabernacle (copy) required animal blood for purification; the heavenly sanctuary required Christ's blood. Where Abel's blood polluted the ground (Genesis 4:11), Levitical blood purified earthly sanctuary, and Christ's blood purifies the heavenly reality itself.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- τὰ ὑποδείγματα (*ta hypodeigmata*) - "the copies/patterns"—earthly tabernacle as shadow
- τῶν ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς (*tōn en tois ouranois*) - "of the things in the heavens"—heavenly realities
- καθαρίζεσθαι (*katharizesthai*) - "to be purified"—cleansing through blood
- κρείττοσιν θυσίαις (*kreittosin thysiais*) - "better sacrifices"—Christ's superior offering

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 23|Hebrews 9:23]] reveals the full scope of Christ's blood efficacy. Abel's blood polluted the earth; Levitical blood purified earthly copies; Christ's blood purifies heaven itself. The escalation is dramatic: not merely cleansing earthly sacred space but purifying the heavenly reality of which the tabernacle was a shadow. Where innocent blood (Abel's) brought curse to the ground, and sacrificial blood (Levitical) annually cleansed symbolic space, Christ's blood accomplishes permanent purification of the true sanctuary—God's very presence. The trajectory: pollution (Abel) → annual purification (Leviticus) → eternal purification (Christ).
