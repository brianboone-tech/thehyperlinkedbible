### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 4|Hebrews 11:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4102|G4102]] πίστις (pistis) - faith, trust, belief
- [[Lexicon/G2201-2300#G2909|G2909]] κρείττων (kreittōn) - better, superior, more excellent
- [[Lexicon/G2301-2400#G2378|G2378]] θυσία (thysia) - sacrifice, offering
- [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2980|G2980]] λαλέω (laleō) - to speak, say, utter

**Context**: Opening the great faith chapter with examples from creation onward, Hebrews presents Abel as first human exemplar: "By faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain did. By faith he was commended as righteous when God gave approval to his gifts. And by faith he still speaks, even though he is dead."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 4|Genesis 4:4]] - "The LORD looked with favor on Abel and his offering" (but Genesis doesn't explicitly mention "faith")
- Hebrews interprets Abel's acceptable sacrifice as evidence of faith—brought what pleased God by trusting God's revelation
- Pattern established: faith expresses itself through obedient offering of what God requires

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 2|Genesis 4:2-4]] - Abel's offering of firstborn with fat portions accepted by God
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]] - Christ's blood "speaks a better word than the blood of Abel"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 12|1 John 3:12]] - "Do not be like Cain, who belonged to the evil one and murdered his brother... because his own actions were evil and his brother's were righteous"

**Type Classification**: **Providential** (sovereignly arranged example) / **Backward-Looking** (NT reveals faith principle operative in Genesis)

**Christological Connection**: Abel's faith-offering accepted by God foreshadows Christ's acceptable offering by which He enters God's presence. Where Abel "still speaks" through his faithful example, Christ speaks as living High Priest ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]) and through His blood ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]]). Escalation: Abel's faith-sacrifice pleased God temporarily; Christ's faith-obedience ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 7|Hebrews 5:7-9]]) pleased God eternally. Abel's "better sacrifice than Cain" anticipates Christ's "better sacrifice" than Levitical offerings ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 23|Hebrews 9:23]]). Abel's witness transcends death through memory; Christ's witness transcends death through resurrection. Abel's blood still cries; Christ's blood still speaks—but speaks a BETTER WORD.
