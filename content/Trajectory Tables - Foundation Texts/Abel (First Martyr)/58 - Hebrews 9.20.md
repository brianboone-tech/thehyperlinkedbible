### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 20|Hebrews 9:20]]

**Context**: The author quotes Moses at Sinai: "This is the blood of the covenant that God commanded for you" (from Exodus 24:8). This establishes that covenant relationship requires blood. The argument moves toward showing Christ's blood establishes a better covenant. In the Abel trajectory, this verse marks the transformation of blood from crying judgment (Abel) to establishing covenant relationship (Sinai) to providing eternal redemption (Christ).

**Greek Key Terms**:
- τοῦτο τὸ αἷμα (*touto to haima*) - "this is the blood"—demonstrative identification
- τῆς διαθήκης (*tēs diathēkēs*) - "of the covenant"—blood ratifies covenant
- ἧς ἐνετείλατο (*hēs eneteilato*) - "which commanded"—divine prescription

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 20|Hebrews 9:20]] quotes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]] to show blood's role in covenant establishment, then argues Christ's blood establishes a better covenant (v.15). The trajectory from Abel: (1) Abel's blood—broken relationship, crying for justice; (2) Mosaic blood—covenant relationship established through sprinkled blood; (3) Christ's blood—eternal covenant, better promises, once-for-all efficacy. Each stage shows blood "speaking": Abel's speaks judgment, Moses' speaks covenant obligation, Christ's speaks "better than Abel" (12:24)—forgiveness and eternal inheritance.
