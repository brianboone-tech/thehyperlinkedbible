### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 2|Hebrews 12:2]]

**Context**: "Looking to Jesus, the founder and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is seated at the right hand of the throne of God." This follows the "cloud of witnesses" including Abel (11:4) and applies Psalm 110:1 to Christ. The suffering-then-glory pattern: Abel suffered martyrdom and his testimony continues (11:4); Christ endured the cross and is now enthroned.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἀρχηγὸν καὶ τελειωτήν (*archēgon kai teleiōtēn*) - "founder and perfecter"—origin and completion
- ὑπέμεινεν σταυρόν (*hypemeinen stauron*) - "endured the cross"—patient suffering
- αἰσχύνης καταφρονήσας (*aischynēs kataphronēsas*) - "despising the shame"—rejection of disgrace
- κεκάθικεν ἐν δεξιᾷ (*kekathiken en dexia*) - "has sat down at the right hand"—Psalm 110:1 fulfillment

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 2|Hebrews 12:2]] presents Christ as the climax of the faithful sufferers catalog (ch. 11) that begins with Abel. Abel "through his faith... still speaks, even though he died" (11:4)—his testimony continues. Christ "endured the cross" then "sat down at the right hand"—His testimony is complete and He is vindicated. The trajectory: Abel (suffered → testimony continues) → Christ (suffered → enthroned). Where Abel's vindication awaits final judgment, Christ's vindication is accomplished. Believers look to Jesus as the "perfecter" who completed what Abel exemplified imperfectly.
