### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 2|Genesis 4:2-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1-100#H7462|H7462]] רֹעֵה (rō'eh) - shepherd, keeper of sheep
- [[Lexicon/H1-100#H6629|H6629]] צֹאן (tsō'n) - flock, sheep, goats
- [[Lexicon/H1-100#H1060|H1060]] בְּכוֹר (bekōr) - firstborn, firstling
- [[Lexicon/H2401-2500#H2459|H2459]] חֵלֶב (ḥēleb) - fat, best portions

**Context**: After the Fall, Adam's sons bring offerings to the LORD. Abel, the shepherd, brings firstborn of his flock with their fat portions. Cain, the farmer, brings "some of the fruit" without specification of quality. The LORD regards Abel and his offering favorably but not Cain's.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 3#Leviticus 3 . 16|Leviticus 3:16]] - "All the fat is the LORD's" (Abel gave God's portion)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]], 12-13 - Firstborn consecrated to LORD (Abel gave firstfruits)
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 9|Proverbs 3:9]] - "Honor the LORD with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops"
- The pattern: acceptable worship requires giving God the BEST (firstborn, fat portions), not merely SOME

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]] - God clothes Adam and Eve with animal skins (first blood shed for covering)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 22|Hebrews 9:22]] - "Without shedding of blood there is no forgiveness"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 4|Hebrews 11:4]] - "By faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain"

**Type Classification**: **Providential** (sovereignly arranged, not commanded) / **Backward-Looking** (NT reveals typological significance)

**Christological Connection**: Abel's blood sacrifice accepted by faith foreshadows Christ's offering. Just as Abel brought firstborn with fat portions (the best), Christ is God's firstborn ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]) offering Himself as the perfect sacrifice. Abel's acceptance by faith points to justification by faith in Christ's blood. The acceptable worship through blood sacrifice anticipates the once-for-all blood of Christ that speaks a better word than Abel's ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]]).
