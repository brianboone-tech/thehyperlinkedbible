### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 110#Psalms 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]

**Context**: "The LORD says to my Lord: 'Sit at my right hand, until I make your enemies your footstool.'" This most-quoted OT verse in the NT establishes the Messiah's enthronement at God's right hand after completing His work. Hebrews 12:2 applies this to Jesus who "endured the cross... and is seated at the right hand of the throne of God." This connects to Abel trajectory through the suffering-then-glory pattern: Abel suffered martyrdom, Christ endured the cross, both are vindicated—Abel's testimony speaks eternally (Hebrews 11:4), Christ sits enthroned.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- נְאֻם יְהוָה (*ne'um YHWH*) - "oracle of the LORD"—divine declaration
- לַאדֹנִי (*la'adoni*) - "to my Lord"—David's acknowledgment of greater Lord
- שֵׁב לִימִינִי (*shev limini*) - "sit at my right hand"—place of honor/authority
- הֲדֹם לְרַגְלֶיךָ (*hadom leragleykha*) - "footstool for your feet"—complete victory

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] represents the vindication of the suffering righteous one. Abel suffered and died with no visible vindication—yet his faith "still speaks" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 4|Hebrews 11:4]]). Christ suffered the cross (ultimate martyrdom) but was vindicated through resurrection and enthronement. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 2|Hebrews 12:2]] bridges these: Jesus "endured the cross" (like Abel's death) but is now "seated at the right hand" (unlike Abel, fully vindicated). The trajectory: Abel (suffering → testimony continues) → Christ (suffering → enthronement). Where Abel's vindication awaits final judgment, Christ's vindication is already accomplished and visible.
