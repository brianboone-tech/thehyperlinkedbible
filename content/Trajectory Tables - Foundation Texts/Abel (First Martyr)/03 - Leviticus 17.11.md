### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5001-5100#H5315|H5315]] נֶפֶשׁ (nepeš) - soul, life, person
- [[Lexicon/H1801-1900#H1818|H1818]] דָּם (dām) - blood
- [[Lexicon/H1201-1300#H1320|H1320]] בָּשָׂר (bāśār) - flesh, meat
- [[Lexicon/H3601-3700#H3722|H3722]] כָּפַר (kāpar) - to atone, make atonement, cover over

**Context**: Within regulations for sacrifices and eating meat, God establishes theological foundation: "For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it to you to make atonement for your souls upon the altar; for it is the blood that makes atonement for the soul."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 4|Genesis 9:4]] - "You must not eat meat with its lifeblood still in it" (blood = life principle)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 23|Deuteronomy 12:23]] - "Be sure not to eat the blood, because the blood is the life"
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 11|Leviticus 16:11-16]] - Day of Atonement: blood sprinkled seven times for national atonement
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]] - Moses sprinkles blood on people: "This is the blood of the covenant"
- [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 22|Hebrews 9:22]] - "Without shedding of blood there is no forgiveness"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 10|Genesis 4:10]] - Abel's blood cries from ground (life in blood has voice before God)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 13|Hebrews 9:13-14]] - "If blood of goats... sanctifies... how much more shall blood of Christ"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 18|1 Peter 1:18-19]] - "Redeemed not with perishable things... but with precious blood of Christ"

**Type Classification**: **Direct Type** (divinely commanded in Mosaic law) / **Forward-Looking** (text itself indicates provisional nature—"I have given it")

**Christological Connection**: Levitical blood atonement is the God-ordained shadow pointing to Christ's blood. Where animal blood merely covered sins annually, Christ's blood removes sins eternally ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 4|Hebrews 10:4]], 11-14). The principle established here—life for life, blood for soul—finds perfect fulfillment when the sinless Life (Christ) gives His blood for sinful souls. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12-14]] contrasts: if Levitical blood sanctifies flesh, how much more does Christ's blood purify conscience? The escalation: temporary covering becomes permanent cleansing; external ritual becomes internal transformation; repeated sacrifices become once-for-all efficacy.
