### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 8|Genesis 4:8-10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1801-1900#H1818|H1818]] דָּם (dām) - blood (as that which when shed causes death)
- [[Lexicon/H6601-6700#H6817|H6817]] צָעַק (tsā'aq) - to cry out, call for help (blood "cries")
- [[Lexicon/H101-200#H127|H127]] אֲדָמָה ('ădāmāh) - ground, earth, soil
- [[Lexicon/H6601-6700#H6662|H6662]] צַדִּיק (tsaddîq) - righteous, just

**Context**: Cain murders Abel in the field out of jealous rage. When God confronts Cain ("Where is your brother?"), Cain's evasion ("Am I my brother's keeper?") reveals hard heart. God declares: "What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood cries out to Me from the ground."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 5|Genesis 9:5-6]] - "From each man I will demand an accounting for his brother's life... whoever sheds man's blood, by man his blood shall be shed"
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 33|Numbers 35:33]] - "Blood pollutes the land, and atonement cannot be made for the land except by the blood of the one who shed it"
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 1|Deuteronomy 21:1-9]] - Unsolved murder requires atonement ritual; innocent blood cries from ground
- [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 24#2 Chronicles 24 . 22|2 Chronicles 24:22]] - Zechariah murdered between temple and altar, cries "May the LORD see this and call you to account"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 9#Psalm 9 . 12|Psalm 9:12]] - "He who avenges blood remembers; He does not ignore the cries of the afflicted"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17]] - Ground cursed because of Adam's sin (now receives blood of righteous)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23#Matthew 23 . 35|Matthew 23:35]] - "Upon you will come all the righteous blood shed on earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zechariah"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]] - Christ's "sprinkled blood speaks a better word than the blood of Abel"

**Type Classification**: **Providential** (sovereignly arranged event) / **Backward-Looking** (Hebrews 12:24 reveals contrast with Christ's blood)

**Christological Connection**: Abel as first righteous martyr whose blood cried for VENGEANCE prefigures Christ, the ultimate righteous martyr whose blood speaks FORGIVENESS ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 24|Hebrews 12:24]]). This is the critical escalation—the antitype transforms the type's meaning. Both are innocent blood shed by wicked brothers. But where Abel's blood demands justice, Christ's blood procures mercy. Where Abel's blood cries from the ground in death, Christ's blood speaks from heaven in resurrection. Abel's one cry is answered in Christ's "better word" (kreittona lalounta) that silences vengeance with grace.
