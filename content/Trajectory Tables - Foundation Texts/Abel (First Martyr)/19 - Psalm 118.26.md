### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 118#Psalms 118 . 26|Psalm 118:26]]

**Context**: "Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD! We bless you from the house of the LORD." This pilgrimage psalm blessing welcomes one who comes in YHWH's name. Jesus applied this to Himself, prophesying Jerusalem will not see Him again until they say these words (Matthew 23:39). Significantly, this prophecy immediately follows Jesus' declaration about "all righteous blood from Abel to Zechariah" (v.35). The Abel connection: Jerusalem's rejection culminates the pattern of killing righteous messengers.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- בָּרוּךְ הַבָּא (*barukh habba'*) - "blessed is the one who comes"—pilgrim/royal welcome
- בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה (*beshem YHWH*) - "in the name of the LORD"—divine authority
- בֵּרַכְנוּכֶם (*berakhnukhem*) - "we bless you"—priestly blessing
- מִבֵּית יְהוָה (*mibbet YHWH*) - "from the house of the LORD"—temple as source

**Christological Connection**: Jesus quotes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 26|Psalm 118:26]] immediately after indicting Jerusalem for killing prophets from Abel to Zechariah. The sequence is crucial: (1) Jerusalem kills righteous messengers (Abel pattern), (2) Jerusalem will kill Jesus (ultimate righteous one), (3) Jerusalem is left desolate, (4) Jesus returns when they welcome Him with this blessing. The Abel trajectory shows consistent rejection of God's righteous messengers; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 26|Psalm 118:26]] anticipates ultimate acceptance. Where Abel was rejected and murdered, Christ was rejected, murdered, but will return to Israel's welcome. The pattern transforms from rejection to reception.
