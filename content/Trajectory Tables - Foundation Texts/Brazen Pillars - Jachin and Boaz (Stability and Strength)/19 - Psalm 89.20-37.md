### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 20|Psalm 89:20-37]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3559|H3559]] כּוּן (*kûn*) - "to establish, make firm" (same root as Jachin)
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (*ʿôlām*) - "forever, eternity"
- [[Lexicon/H1285|H1285]] בְּרִית (*bᵉrîṯ*) - "covenant"
- [[Lexicon/H7650|H7650]] שָׁבַע (*šāḇaʿ*) - "to swear, take oath"
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (*zeraʿ*) - "seed, offspring"
- [[Lexicon/H3678|H3678]] כִּסֵּא (*kissēʾ*) - "throne"
- [[Lexicon/H8121|H8121]] שֶׁמֶשׁ (*šemeš*) - "sun"
- [[Lexicon/H3394|H3394]] יָרֵחַ (*yārēaḥ*) - "moon"

**Context**: Psalm 89 celebrates the Davidic covenant (2 Samuel 7) in the first 37 verses, then laments its apparent failure in verses 38-51. Verses 20-29 recount God's covenant promises to David: "I have found David My servant... I will establish (כּוּן) his line forever, his throne as long as the heavens endure" (vv. 20, 29). Verses 30-37 address covenant conditionality for descendants yet unconditional permanence for the dynasty: "My covenant I will not violate... His line will continue forever; his throne will endure before Me like the sun" (vv. 34, 36). The verb כּוּן (establish) appears repeatedly, connecting directly to the pillar name Jachin.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]]** is the foundational Davidic covenant text, promising eternal establishment of David's throne.
- **[[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 21|1 Kings 7:21]]** names the pillar Jachin (יָכִין, "He will establish")—same root as Psalm 89's repeated promise.
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132:11-12]]** parallels: "The LORD swore an oath to David... If your sons keep My covenant... their sons will sit on your throne forever."
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 7|Isaiah 9:7]]** promises: "Of the increase of His government and peace there will be no end... establishing (כּוּן) it with justice and righteousness."

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: 2 Samuel 7:12-16 (Davidic covenant), 1 Kings 7:21 (Jachin pillar), Isaiah 9:6-7 (eternal establishment)
- **WITHIN PSALM**: Psalm 89:38-51 (lament over apparent failure)
- **TO NT**: Luke 1:32-33 (Gabriel's announcement), Acts 2:30-31 (Peter's Pentecost sermon)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (the establishment language points to Christ's eternal throne)

**Christological Connection**: Psalm 89's establishment promises find fulfillment in Christ: (1) **Gabriel's Announcement**: "The Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David. He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and His kingdom will have no end" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]]). The establishment (כּוּן) reaches eternal fulfillment. (2) **Resurrection Fulfillment**: Peter interprets Psalm 89 alongside Psalm 132 as fulfilled in Christ's resurrection: "God had promised him on oath that He would place one of his descendants on his throne" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 30|Acts 2:30]]). (3) **Pillar Connection**: The pillar Jachin proclaimed "He will establish"—Christ IS that establishment. His throne is "forever and ever" (Heb 1:8). (4) **Covenant Preserved**: The psalm laments apparent covenant failure (vv. 38-51); Christ resolves the tension. Through Him, David's line continues eternally. (5) **Both Pillars**: Psalm 89 encapsulates both pillar names: Jachin (God's establishment, vv. 21, 29) and Boaz (God's strength, vv. 21: "My arm will strengthen him"). In Christ, both promises are perfectly realized.
