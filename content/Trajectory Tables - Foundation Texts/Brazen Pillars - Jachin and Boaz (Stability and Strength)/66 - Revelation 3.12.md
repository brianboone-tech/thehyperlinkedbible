### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 12|Revelation 3:12]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4769|G4769]] στῦλος (*stylos*) - "pillar, column"
- [[Lexicon/G3485|G3485]] ναός (*naos*) - "temple, sanctuary"
- [[Lexicon/G1854|G1854]] ἔξω (*exō*) - "outside, out"
- [[Lexicon/G3686|G3686]] ὄνομα (*onoma*) - "name"
- [[Lexicon/G1125|G1125]] γράφω (*graphō*) - "to write"
- [[Lexicon/G2537|G2537]] καινός (*kainos*) - "new"

**Context**: Revelation 3:7-13 contains Christ's letter to the church in Philadelphia. After promising the open door no one can shut (vv. 7-8) and protection from the hour of testing (v. 10), Christ gives the overcomer promise: "I will make him a pillar in the temple of My God, and he will never again leave it. I will write on him the name of My God, the name of the city of My God—the new Jerusalem that comes down out of heaven from My God—and My new name" (v. 12). This promise directly answers the destruction of Jachin and Boaz: those bronze pillars were carried to Babylon (2 Kings 25:13), but living pillars in Christ's temple "will never again leave."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 21|1 Kings 7:21-22]]** records the naming of Jachin and Boaz—pillars bearing meaningful names.
- **[[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 25#2 Kings 25 . 13|2 Kings 25:13]]** describes their destruction and removal to Babylon—pillars "going out" from the temple.
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 18|Jeremiah 1:18-19]]** presents the prophet as an iron pillar that will not be overcome.
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 56#Isaiah 56 . 5|Isaiah 56:5]]** promises the faithful "a memorial and a name (שֵׁם, šēm)... that will not be cut off."

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: 1 Kings 7:21 (named pillars), 2 Kings 25:13 (pillars removed), Isaiah 56:5 (everlasting name)
- **FROM REVELATION**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]] (no temple in New Jerusalem), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]] (His name on foreheads)
- **NT PARALLELS**: Galatians 2:9 (apostles as pillars), 1 Timothy 3:15 (church as pillar of truth)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (the temple pillars pointed to believers' permanent status in Christ), Direct (deliberate contrast with pillar destruction establishes typological fulfillment)

**Christological Connection**: Christ's promise completes the pillar trajectory: (1) **Christ Makes Pillars**: "I will make him a pillar"—Christ actively transforms believers. Unlike Solomon who commissioned Hiram to cast bronze pillars, Christ Himself fashions living pillars. (2) **Permanent Residence**: "Will never again leave" directly contrasts the Babylon exile of Jachin and Boaz. What was removed cannot be re-removed in Christ's temple. (3) **Triple Naming**: The bronze pillars bore two names (Jachin, Boaz); the overcomer receives three names—Father, City, Son. The multiplication indicates escalation and comprehensive belonging. (4) **Mather's Application**: "Living Pillars in the spiritual Temple shall go out no more"—permanence secured through Christ's victory over death. (5) **New Name**: Christ's "new name" (v. 12) suggests post-resurrection identity. Believers share in Christ's glorified status. (6) **Temple Paradox**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]] says there's no temple in the New Jerusalem ("the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple"). Yet believers are still "pillars." The resolution: believers support and constitute the temple that IS God's presence. The pillar becomes one with the temple.
