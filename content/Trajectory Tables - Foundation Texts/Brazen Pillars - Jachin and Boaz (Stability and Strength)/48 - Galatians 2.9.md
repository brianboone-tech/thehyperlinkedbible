### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 2#Galatians 2 . 9|Galatians 2:9]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4769|G4769]] στῦλος (*stylos*) - "pillar, column"
- [[Lexicon/G1380|G1380]] δοκέω (*dokeō*) - "to seem, to be reputed"
- [[Lexicon/G5485|G5485]] χάρις (*charis*) - "grace"
- [[Lexicon/G1188|G1188]] δεξιός (*dexios*) - "right (hand)"
- [[Lexicon/G2842|G2842]] κοινωνία (*koinōnia*) - "fellowship, sharing"

**Context**: Galatians 2:1-10 recounts Paul's visit to Jerusalem to present his gospel to the apostolic leaders. After describing his private meeting and the outcome, Paul names "James, Cephas [Peter], and John, those reputed to be pillars" (οἱ δοκοῦντες στῦλοι εἶναι, v. 9). These leaders "gave me and Barnabas the right hand of fellowship," recognizing the grace given to Paul for Gentile ministry. The "pillar" designation identifies these apostles as foundational supports of the church—living fulfillments of what Jachin and Boaz symbolized architecturally.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 21|1 Kings 7:21-22]]** established the pillar typology with Jachin and Boaz at the temple entrance.
- **[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 18|Jeremiah 1:18-19]]** developed the metaphor: God made Jeremiah "an iron pillar" against the whole land.
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 22#Isaiah 22 . 23|Isaiah 22:23]]** uses similar imagery for Eliakim: "I will drive him like a peg into a firm place"—though peg rather than pillar, the concept of stable support is parallel.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: 1 Kings 7:21-22 (temple pillars), Jeremiah 1:18 (prophet as pillar)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 15|1 Timothy 3:15]] (church as pillar of truth), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 12|Revelation 3:12]] (overcomer as pillar)
- **WITHIN GALATIANS**: Galatians 1:17-19 (Paul's earlier Jerusalem visit), Galatians 2:11-14 (later Antioch incident)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (temple pillars pointed to living supports of God's spiritual house)

**Christological Connection**: The apostles as "pillars" reflect Christ's work in building His church: (1) **Derivative Authority**: The apostles are pillars, but Christ is the foundation/cornerstone (1 Cor 3:11; Eph 2:20). Their pillar status derives from their relationship to Christ. (2) **Testimony Function**: Jachin and Boaz proclaimed God's establishment and strength silently through their names. The apostles proclaim Christ actively through gospel witness. They support the church by their testimony to Christ. (3) **Mather's Qualifications**: A good pillar is "strait and strong"—upright in doctrine, unmoved by opposition. The Jerusalem pillars demonstrated this by affirming Paul's gospel while maintaining their own Jewish mission. (4) **Living vs. Bronze**: The temple pillars were eventually destroyed ([[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 25#2 Kings 25 . 13|2 Kings 25:13]]); the apostolic pillar-testimony endures in Scripture. The living pillar concept prepares for [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 12|Revelation 3:12]]'s promise. (5) **Multiple Pillars**: The temple had two pillars; the church has multiple pillar-leaders. Unity in diversity supports the structure—Paul and Peter, James and John, each with distinct callings yet recognizing one another's grace.
