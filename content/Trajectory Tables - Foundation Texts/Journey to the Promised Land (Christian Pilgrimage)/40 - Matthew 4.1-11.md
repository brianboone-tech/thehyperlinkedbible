**Context**: Matthew 4:1-11 records Jesus' temptation in the wilderness immediately after His baptism (Matthew 3:13-17). The Spirit led Jesus into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. He fasted forty days and nights, then was tempted three times. Each time, Jesus responded with Scripture, specifically quoting Deuteronomy 6 and 8—texts about Israel's wilderness experience.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]] demonstrates Jesus as the true Israel who succeeded where Israel failed. **Israel** spent forty years in the wilderness, tested by hunger, and failed (grumbled, demanded bread, worshiped idols). **Jesus** spent forty days in the wilderness, tested by hunger, and succeeded (trusted God, obeyed His word, worshiped God alone). The parallels are precise: **Israel**: Tested by hunger → demanded bread → God provided manna → later grumbled and tested God → worshiped golden calf. **Jesus**: Tested by hunger → refused to turn stones to bread → quoted "Man shall not live by bread alone" → refused to test God → refused to worship Satan. The escalation: Israel's test was corporate and prolonged (forty years); Jesus' test was individual and intense (forty days). Israel had physical manna; Jesus fed on God's word. Israel failed and died in the wilderness; Jesus succeeded and emerged victorious to begin His ministry. Jesus recapitulates Israel's history but does so perfectly. He is the obedient Son, the faithful Israel, qualifying Him to be the Savior who redeems disobedient Israel (and all who believe).

**Application**: **Resist temptation with Scripture.** Jesus responded to each temptation with "It is written..." He defeated Satan with God's word. Are you armed with Scripture? Memorize God's word so you can wield it when tempted (Psalm 119:11: "I have stored up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you"). **Man does not live by bread alone.** Verse 4: "Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God." Physical food sustains the body; God's word sustains the soul. Are you feeding on Scripture daily? Neglect God's word and you will starve spiritually. **Do not test God.** Verse 7: "You shall not put the Lord your God to the test." Don't presume on God's protection while living recklessly. Don't demand that God prove Himself. Trust Him; obey Him. **Worship God alone.** Verse 10: "You shall worship the Lord your God and him only shall you serve." Idolatry is not merely bowing to statues; it's loving anything more than God. What do you worship? Money? Success? Comfort? Relationships? Worship God alone.
