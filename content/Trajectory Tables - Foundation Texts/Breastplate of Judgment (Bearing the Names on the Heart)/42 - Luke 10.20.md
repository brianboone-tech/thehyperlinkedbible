### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 20|Luke 10:20]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5463|G5463]] χαίρω (*chairō*) - "to rejoice, be glad" - Deep, abiding joy rooted in spiritual reality
- [[Lexicon/G3686|G3686]] ὄνομα (*onoma*) - "name" - Personal identity and character
- [[Lexicon/G1125|G1125]] γράφω (*graphō*) - "to write, engrave, record" - Permanent inscription (cf. engraving on breastplate stones)
- [[Lexicon/G3772|G3772]] οὐρανός (*ouranos*) - "heaven" - The dwelling place of God; the realm of eternal, unchanging reality

**Context**: Luke 10:17-20 records the seventy-two disciples' return from their mission, rejoicing that "even the demons are subject to us in Your name!" Jesus responds by reframing their joy: "Nevertheless, do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rejoice that your names are written in heaven" (v. 20). Jesus redirects them from TEMPORARY, VISIBLE success (exorcisms) to ETERNAL, INVISIBLE reality (heavenly enrollment). This statement presupposes the Jewish concept of the "Book of Life" (Exod 32:32; Ps 69:28; Dan 12:1; Mal 3:16) and transforms it—believers' names are permanently recorded in heaven.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 29|Exodus 28:29]]: Aaron bore "the names of the sons of Israel over his heart on the breastpiece of judgment, as a continual reminder before the LORD." The engraved names on stones represented Israel's permanent standing before God.
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 32|Exodus 32:32]]: Moses pleads, "If You will forgive their sin—but if not, please blot me out of Your book that You have written." God's "book" contains names of the living/righteous.
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 28|Psalm 69:28]]: "Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; let them not be enrolled among the righteous." Removal from the book = divine judgment; retention = salvation.
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 16|Malachi 3:16]]: "A book of remembrance was written before Him for those who feared the LORD and esteemed His name." God keeps a record of His faithful ones.
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|Daniel 12:1]]: "At that time your people shall be delivered, everyone whose name shall be found written in the book." Eschatological deliverance linked to heavenly enrollment.

**Type Classification**: This text FULFILLS and TRANSCENDS the breastplate typology. Aaron bore names on stones (external, physical); God writes names in heaven (internal, spiritual, eternal).

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 20|Luke 10:20]] consummates the breastplate's typology by revealing that what Aaron did temporarily and externally, God does eternally and internally. The trajectory:

**EXODUS 28:29 (Type)**: Aaron bore the names of Israel's twelve tribes on twelve stones over his heart as "a continual reminder before the LORD." The engraving was PERMANENT (could not be erased), PRECIOUS (on valuable stones), and POSITIONED near the heart (intimate representation).

**LUKE 10:20 (Fulfillment)**: Jesus declares that believers' names are "written in heaven." The engraving is now ETERNAL (heavenly records never fade), PRECIOUS (names of God's elect), and POSITIONED in God's very presence (not on a priest's chest but in heaven itself).

**ESCALATION**:
- Aaron bore names ON STONES; God writes names IN A BOOK (and ultimately engraves them on the new Jerusalem, Rev 21:12)
- Aaron's breastplate was VISIBLE and EXTERNAL; heaven's record is INVISIBLE and ETERNAL
- Aaron bore TWELVE tribal names; Jesus speaks of INDIVIDUAL disciples' names—personal, not just corporate
- Aaron's intercession was TEMPORARY (he died); Christ's advocacy is PERPETUAL ("He always lives to make intercession," Heb 7:25)
- Aaron represented Israel BEFORE GOD; Christ writes names IN HEAVEN (direct access, not mediated representation)

The breastplate's engraved stones prefigured the permanence and preciousness of God's knowledge of His people. Luke 10:20 reveals the reality: believers' names are eternally secure, not on stones worn by a mortal priest, but written in heaven by the decree of the eternal God. This is why Jesus says "REJOICE" (v. 20)—the assurance that one's name is written in heaven transcends all earthly success or failure. As Fairbairn noted, Aaron bore names "in their name and behalf... before the Lord"—a continual memorial. Christ surpasses this by ensuring that believers' names are never blotted out (Rev 3:5), securing eternal life (Rev 21:27).
