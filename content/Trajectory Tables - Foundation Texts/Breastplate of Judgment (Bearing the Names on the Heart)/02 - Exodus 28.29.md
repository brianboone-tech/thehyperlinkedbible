### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 29|Exodus 28:29]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5375|H5375]] נָשָׂא (*nasa*) - "to bear, carry, lift" - Used of bearing a burden or responsibility; here, bearing names as a representative
- [[Lexicon/H3820|H3820]] לֵב (*lev*) - "heart" - The inner person, seat of mind, will, and emotions
- [[Lexicon/H8548|H8548]] תָּמִיד (*tamid*) - "continually, perpetually, regularly" - Without interruption or cessation
- [[Lexicon/H2146|H2146]] זִכָּרוֹן (*zikaron*) - "memorial, reminder" - Something that keeps memory alive before God

**Context**: This verse articulates the PURPOSE of the breastplate described in verses 15-21. Aaron's wearing of the breastplate is not mere decoration but a perpetual act of intercession. Whenever he enters the Holy Place (not just the Most Holy Place, but the daily service in the sanctuary), he bears the names of all twelve tribes "over his heart." The heart location is crucial—not on his shoulders (strength), nor on his head (authority), but on his heart (affection, love, intimate concern). The phrase "continual reminder before the LORD" establishes this as an ongoing, uninterrupted ministry of representation.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/22 - Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 8#Song of Solomon 8 . 6|Song of Solomon 8:6]]: "Set me as a seal upon your heart, as a seal upon your arm." The bride's plea for permanent, intimate union echoes the breastplate's function. Aaron bore Israel as a seal on his heart; the lover seeks the same inseparable bond.
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 16|Isaiah 49:16]]: "Behold, I have engraved you on the palms of My hands." God reverses the image—He bears His people as permanently as Aaron bore the stones. The engraving metaphor links both texts.
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]]: "I will put My law within them and write it on their hearts." The trajectory moves from external stones to internal heart transformation, anticipating the new covenant.

**Type Classification**: Direct Type (divinely commanded) and Forward-Looking (the language of perpetual intercession and continual remembrance points beyond the temporal to the eternal).

**Christological Connection**: Aaron bearing Israel's names on his heart before the LORD is the OT shadow of Christ's eternal intercession. The escalation is profound: Aaron's ministry was DAILY (he had to re-enter); Christ's is ETERNAL ("He always lives"). Aaron's was LIMITED (he could die, fail, sin); Christ's is PERFECT (sinless, immortal, efficacious). Aaron bore names on STONES (external); Christ knows His sheep INTIMATELY (internal). [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 34|Romans 8:34]] and [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]] explicitly present Christ's intercession as the fulfillment of the high priestly type. Because Christ bears believers on His heart continually, no condemnation can stand (Rom 8:33-34), and salvation is complete (Heb 7:25).
