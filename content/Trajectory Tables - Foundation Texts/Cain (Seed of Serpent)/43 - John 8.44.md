### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 44|John 8:44]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3962|G3962]] πατήρ (patēr) - "father" - your father the devil
- [[Lexicon/G1228|G1228]] διάβολος (diabolos) - "devil/slanderer" - the accuser
- [[Lexicon/G443|G443]] ἀνθρωποκτόνος (anthrōpoktonos) - "murderer" - murderer from the beginning
- [[Lexicon/G5583|G5583]] ψεύστης (pseustēs) - "liar" - the father of lies
- [[Lexicon/G1939|G1939]] ἐπιθυμία (epithymia) - "desire" - desires of your father

**Context**: In a heated exchange with Jewish leaders who claim Abraham as their father, Jesus declares: "You are of your father the devil, and your will is to do your father's desires. He was a murderer from the beginning, and does not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he lies, he speaks out of his own character, for he is a liar and the father of lies."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Jesus identifies the devil as "a murderer from the beginning"—alluding to the serpent's role in humanity's fall and Cain's murder of Abel
- The serpent's deception in Eden (Gen 3:1-5) establishes him as "father of lies"
- The spiritual lineage announced in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] is explicitly identified

**Type Classification**: NT Identification (Jesus explicitly identifies the serpent's seed pattern)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus identifies Himself as the one who speaks truth (contrasted with the devil's lies) and brings life (contrasted with the devil's murderous nature). The seed-conflict of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] reaches its climax in Christ. Those who reject Jesus demonstrate their spiritual lineage from the serpent; those who receive Him become children of God ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 12|John 1:12]]). Christ crushes the serpent's head (Gen 3:15) and liberates those held captive by the devil's lies.
