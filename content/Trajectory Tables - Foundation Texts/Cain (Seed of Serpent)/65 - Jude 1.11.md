### [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 11|Jude 1:11]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3598|G3598]] ὁδός (hodos) - "way" - the way of Cain
- [[Lexicon/G4198|G4198]] πορεύομαι (poreuomai) - "go/walk" - they walked in Cain's way
- [[Lexicon/G4106|G4106]] πλάνη (planē) - "error/deception" - Balaam's error
- [[Lexicon/G3408|G3408]] μισθός (misthos) - "reward/wages" - for reward (Balaam)
- [[Lexicon/G485|G485]] ἀντιλογία (antilogia) - "rebellion" - Korah's rebellion

**Context**: Jude warns against false teachers who have infiltrated the church. He pronounces a threefold woe: "Woe to them! For they walked in the way of Cain and abandoned themselves for the sake of gain to Balaam's error and perished in Korah's rebellion." This triad—Cain, Balaam, Korah—represents different aspects of opposition to God and His people.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- "The way of Cain" becomes a recognized category for a pattern of behavior
- The triad combines: murder of the righteous (Cain), corruption for profit (Balaam), rebellion against authority (Korah)
- All three OT figures opposed God's purposes and met judgment

  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 14|Revelation 2:14]] - teaching of Balaam
**Type Classification**: NT Warning (the pattern is identified as ongoing threat to the church)

**Christological Connection**: Christ is "the way, the truth, and the life" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]])—the antithesis of "the way of Cain." Where Cain's way is characterized by faithless worship, hatred, and violence, Christ's way is characterized by perfect obedience, love for enemies, and self-sacrifice. False teachers who follow Cain's way ultimately oppose Christ, and their judgment is certain. Believers are called to follow Christ's way, not Cain's—loving brothers rather than hating them ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 11|1 John 3:11-15]]).
