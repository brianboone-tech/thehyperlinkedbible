### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10-15]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1228|G1228]] διάβολος (diabolos) - "devil" - the devil who deceived
- [[Lexicon/G3041|G3041]] λίμνη (limnē) - "lake" - lake of fire
- [[Lexicon/G4442|G4442]] πῦρ (pyr) - "fire" - fire and sulfur
- [[Lexicon/G976|G976]] βίβλος (biblos) - "book" - book of life
- [[Lexicon/G2288|G2288]] θάνατος (thanatos) - "death" - second death

**Context**: Revelation 20 describes the final judgment. The devil is cast into the lake of fire (v. 10). The dead are judged according to their works, and "if anyone's name was not found written in the book of life, he was thrown into the lake of fire" (v. 15). This is called "the second death" (v. 14).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The judgment promised in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] (serpent's head crushed) reaches final fulfillment
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 9|Daniel 7:9-12]] depicts final judgment with thrones and books opened
- The imagery of fire and sulfur echoes Sodom's judgment (Gen 19:24)

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation (the trajectory reaches its final resolution)

**Christological Connection**: The Lamb's book of life (Rev 21:27) determines eternal destiny. Christ, the seed of the woman, has crushed the serpent's head through His death and resurrection. Those who belong to Christ—whose names are in His book—share His victory. Those who followed "the way of Cain" share the serpent's fate. The enmity announced in Eden, manifested first in Cain, and traced through all of Scripture, reaches its final resolution: complete victory for Christ and His people, complete judgment for the serpent and his seed.
