### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 8|Genesis 4:8-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6965|H6965]] קוּם (qûm) - "rise up" - Cain rose up against Abel
- [[Lexicon/H2026|H2026]] הָרַג (hārag) - "kill/murder" - the first murder
- [[Lexicon/H1818|H1818]] דָּם (dām) - "blood" - Abel's blood cries from the ground
- [[Lexicon/H6817|H6817]] צָעַק (ṣāʿaq) - "cry out" - the blood's testimony
- [[Lexicon/H5128|H5128]] נוּעַ (nûaʿ) - "wander" - Cain's restless existence

**Context**: Despite God's warning, Cain gives in to sin's mastery. He rises up against his brother Abel in the field and kills him—the first murder in human history. When God asks "Where is Abel your brother?", Cain responds with defiance: "Am I my brother's keeper?" God pronounces judgment: the ground that received Abel's blood will no longer yield its strength for Cain; he will be a wanderer on the earth.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The pattern of righteous blood crying out continues: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 24#2 Chronicles 24 . 22|2 Chronicles 24:22]] (Zechariah's blood)
- The image of innocent blood polluting the land develops in [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 33|Numbers 35:33]] and [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 10|Deuteronomy 19:10]]
- The "voice of blood" concept appears in [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 16#Job 16 . 18|Job 16:18]] and [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 21|Isaiah 26:21]]

**Type Classification**: Backward-looking (Hebrews and 1 John interpret Cain's murder typologically)

**Christological Connection**: Abel's blood cried out for vengeance; Christ's blood "speaks a better word than the blood of Abel" (Heb 12:24)—it speaks forgiveness and reconciliation. Where Cain murdered the righteous, Christ was murdered by the unrighteous yet prayed "Father, forgive them" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 34|Luke 23:34]]). The serpent's seed kills; the woman's seed dies to save His killers. Abel's blood inaugurates the testimony of martyrs that finds its fullest expression in Christ, "the faithful witness" (Rev 1:5).
