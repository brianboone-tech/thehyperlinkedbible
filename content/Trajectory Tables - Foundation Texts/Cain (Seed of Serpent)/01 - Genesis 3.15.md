### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H342|H342]] אֵיבָה (ʾêbâ) - "enmity" - hostility between the two seeds
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zeraʿ) - "seed/offspring" - used for both serpent's and woman's seed
- [[Lexicon/H7779|H7779]] שׁוּף (šûp̄) - "bruise/strike" - the mutual conflict between seeds

**Context**: After the serpent deceives Eve and Adam sins, God pronounces judgment. Before cursing the ground and announcing the consequences for Adam and Eve, God addresses the serpent with this foundational prophecy: "I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring; he shall bruise your head, and you shall bruise his heel."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The "seed of the woman" concept develops through the patriarchal promises (Gen 12:7, 22:17-18)
- The royal seed line narrows through Judah (Gen 49:10) and David (2 Sam 7:12-16)
- The serpent's seed manifests in those who oppose God's purposes throughout the OT

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (explicitly prophetic), Providential (establishing the framework for all subsequent types)

**Christological Connection**: This verse establishes the framework within which Cain's role as "seed of the serpent" must be understood. The woman's seed will ultimately crush the serpent's head—a victory accomplished at the cross where Christ defeated Satan (Col 2:15; Heb 2:14). Every manifestation of the serpent's seed (including Cain and all who follow his "way") stands under this prophesied judgment. Christ, the true seed of the woman, triumphs over all who belong to the evil one.
