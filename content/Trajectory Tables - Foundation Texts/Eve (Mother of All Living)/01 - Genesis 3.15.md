### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H0802|H0802]] אִשָּׁה (ishshah) - woman
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zera) - seed, offspring
- [[Lexicon/H0342|H0342]] אֵיבָה (eybah) - enmity, hostility
- [[Lexicon/H7779|H7779]] שׁוּף (shuph) - crush, bruise, strike

**Context**: Immediately following the fall, God pronounces judgment on the serpent. Within this curse, God embeds the first gospel promise—the protoevangelium. This is the foundational promise of redemption, establishing that deliverance will come through the "seed of the woman."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Intertextuality Pairs/OT to OT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3.14-15 to Isaiah 25.10|Isaiah 25:10]] applies the serpent-trampling imagery to God's enemies
- The seed promise continues through Seth ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 25|Genesis 4:25]]), Abraham ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Genesis 22:18]]), David ([[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-14]]), culminating in prophetic expectations

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|Genesis 2:15-17]] - man's original covenant with God, broken in fall
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] - virgin's seed
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 10|Isaiah 53:10]] - suffering servant's seed
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 16#Romans 16 . 20|Romans 16:20]] - Satan crushed under believers' feet
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]] - born of woman
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 1|Revelation 12:1-5]] - woman and male child
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 8|1 John 3:8]] - Son of God destroys devil's works
**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (contains explicit predictive element pointing to future victory) and Direct (spoken by God Himself as prophecy)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is THE seed of the woman who crushes the serpent's head through His death and resurrection. His heel was struck (crucifixion), but He delivered the fatal blow to Satan ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14]]). The enmity between the two seeds plays out in Cain vs. Abel, continuing through redemptive history until Christ's final victory.
