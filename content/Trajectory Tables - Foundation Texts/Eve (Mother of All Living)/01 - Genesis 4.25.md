### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 25|Genesis 4:25]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8352|H8352]] שֵׁת (Sheth) - Seth, "appointed" or "granted"
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (zera) - seed, offspring
- [[Lexicon/H0430|H0430]] אֱלֹהִים (Elohim) - God

**Context**: After Abel's murder and Cain's exile, Eve bears another son. She recognizes Seth as the divinely appointed replacement seed—the godly line through whom the promise will continue.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Seth's line produces the godly genealogy (Genesis 5) leading to Noah, Abraham, David, and ultimately Christ
- Contrasts with Cain's ungodly line ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 17|Genesis 4:17-24]]) which epitomizes the serpent's seed
- Seth becomes the appointed heir, foreshadowing the pattern of divine election over natural primogeniture

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - seed promise
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 1|Genesis 4:1-8]] - Cain proves false seed
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 3|Genesis 5:3]] - Adam fathers Seth in his likeness—image transmission
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 38|Luke 3:38]] - genealogy of Jesus traces back through Seth to Adam, "son of God"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 23|Luke 3:23-38]] - Christ's genealogy goes through Seth, not Cain
**Type Classification**: Providential (divinely arranged as the appointed seed-line bearer), Forward-Looking (points to the coming appointed Seed)

**Christological Connection**: Seth is appointed by God to carry the seed of promise after Abel's death and Cain's disqualification. Similarly, Christ is the ultimately appointed One—foreordained before the foundation of the world ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 20|1 Peter 1:20]]), set apart by the Father ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 36|John 10:36]]). Just as Seth replaced Abel (the righteous one killed by wicked Cain), Christ came as the righteous One who died at the hands of wicked men, but rose again to secure the seed-line of faith. All who are "in Christ" are the true seed of the woman ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|Galatians 3:29]]).
