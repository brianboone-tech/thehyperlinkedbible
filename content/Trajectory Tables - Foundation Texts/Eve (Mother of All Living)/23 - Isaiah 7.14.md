### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5959|H5959]] עַלְמָה (almah) - virgin, young woman of marriageable age
- [[Lexicon/H0410|H0410]] אֵל (el) - God, mighty one
- [[Lexicon/H6005|H6005]] עִמָּנוּ (immanu) - with us

**LXX**: παρθένος (parthenos) - virgin, rendering the Hebrew almah with the unambiguous Greek term for virgin

**Context**: Isaiah addresses King Ahaz during the Syro-Ephraimite crisis (735 BC). Ahaz refuses to ask God for a sign, so God gives one anyway: a virgin will conceive and bear a son named Immanuel. Near fulfillment may involve Isaiah's son, but ultimate fulfillment awaits the virgin-born Christ.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Builds on [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] (seed of the woman) by specifying virginal conception
- Connects to Davidic covenant ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6-7]]; 11:1-5)—the virgin's son will sit on David's throne forever
- Later prophets continue the royal-messianic expectation ([[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2-3]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5-6]])

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - seed of woman
  - [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-14]] - Davidic covenant
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6]] - child born, son given—divine child
  - [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]] - ruler from Bethlehem
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 22|Matthew 1:22-23]] - explicit fulfillment in Jesus' virgin birth
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 26|Luke 1:26-35]] - Gabriel announces virgin conception
**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (explicit prophecy pointing to future virgin-born Messiah), Direct (spoken by God through Isaiah as prophecy)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] specifies HOW the seed of the woman ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]) will come—through virginal conception. Jesus, born of the Virgin Mary, fulfills this precisely ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 18|Matthew 1:18-25]]; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 26|Luke 1:26-38]]). His virgin birth demonstrates He is uniquely the seed of woman (not descended from Adam through male line), breaking the curse transmitted through Adam's headship. As Immanuel, He is "God with us"—deity taking on humanity to crush the serpent's head.
