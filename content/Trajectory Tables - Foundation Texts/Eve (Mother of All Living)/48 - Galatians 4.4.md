### [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4138|G4138]] πλήρωμα (pleroma) - fullness, completion
- [[Lexicon/G1096|G1096]] γίνομαι (ginomai) - to be born, become
- [[Lexicon/G1135|G1135]] γυνή (gyne) - woman
- [[Lexicon/G3551|G3551]] νόμος (nomos) - law

**Context**: Paul explains the gospel of adoption as sons. Before Christ came, we were under the law as children under guardians. When the time was fully ripe, God sent His Son to redeem those under law.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Direct fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] (seed of the woman)
- Fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] (virgin conception—implied by "born of woman" without mention of human father)
- Completion of the Davidic covenant ([[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-14]])

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 3:15 (seed of woman); Isaiah 7:14 (virgin conception); Daniel 9:24-27 (timing—seventy weeks)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 12|Romans 5:12-21]] - second Adam
  - [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 6|Philippians 2:6-7]] - taking form of servant
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14-17]] - partaking of flesh and blood
**Type Classification**: Direct fulfillment of forward-looking prophecy (Genesis 3:15; Isaiah 7:14)

**Christological Connection**: "Born of a woman" is Paul's explicit citation of the protoevangelium ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]). Jesus Christ is THE seed of the woman promised in Eden. His birth "when the time had fully come" shows God's sovereign timing in redemptive history. Born of Mary (daughter of Eve), born under law (to fulfill it perfectly), Christ accomplishes what Adam and Eve failed to do—perfect obedience. He then crushes the serpent's head through His death and resurrection, redeeming all who are under the curse. We become children of the woman's seed through faith-union with Christ.
