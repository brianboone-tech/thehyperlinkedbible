### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 30|Luke 1:30-32]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3137|G3137]] Μαρία (Maria) - Mary
- [[Lexicon/G5485|G5485]] χάρις (charis) - grace, favor
- [[Lexicon/G4815|G4815]] συλλαμβάνω (syllambano) - to conceive
- [[Lexicon/G5207|G5207]] υἱός (huios) - son
- [[Lexicon/G2362|G2362]] θρόνος (thronos) - throne

**Context**: The angel Gabriel announces to Mary that she will conceive and bear the long-awaited Messiah. This is the moment Genesis 3:15 begins its final fulfillment—the seed of the woman is about to arrive.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] (seed of woman)
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] (virgin conception—[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 34|Luke 1:34-35]] makes this explicit)
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-14]] (Davidic covenant—"throne of His father David")
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 9#Daniel 9 . 24|Daniel 9:24-27]] (timing of Messiah's coming)

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 3:15 (protoevangelium); Isaiah 7:14 (virgin conception); 2 Samuel 7:12-16 (Davidic throne); Psalm 132:11 (fruit of David's body on throne)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 18|Matthew 1:18-23]] - parallel virgin birth announcement to Joseph
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]] - born of woman
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 5|Revelation 12:5]] - woman gives birth to male child who will rule nations
**Type Classification**: Direct fulfillment of forward-looking prophecies (Genesis 3:15; Isaiah 7:14)

**Christological Connection**: Mary is the ultimate "woman" of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]], the virgin of [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]], and the Davidic mother of the eternal King. Her son Jesus is THE seed who crushes the serpent's head. What Eve lost through disobedience, Mary's obedience helps restore: "Behold, I am the servant of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 38|Luke 1:38]]). Mary as daughter of Eve becomes mother of the Second Adam. All believers become spiritual children of this woman's seed through faith in Christ ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|Galatians 3:29]]).
