### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 1|Genesis 4:1]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7069|H7069]] קָנָה (qanah) - to get, acquire, create
- [[Lexicon/H0376|H0376]] אִישׁ (ish) - man, husband
- [[Lexicon/H3068|H3068]] יְהוָה (YHWH) - the LORD

**Context**: Eve conceives and bears Cain, the first human born (as opposed to created). Her exclamation reveals her hope that Cain might be the promised seed of Genesis 3:15.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Eve's premature hope in Cain as deliverer parallels later mistaken hopes in false messiahs
- Cain proves to be of the serpent's seed (murderer, liar), not the promised seed
- The pattern of firstborn rejection continues (Cain rejected, Seth chosen; Ishmael rejected, Isaac chosen; Esau rejected, Jacob chosen)

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - protoevangelium creates expectation
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 20|Genesis 3:20]] - Eve named "life-giver"
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 8|Genesis 4:8]] - Cain murders Abel—proof he's of serpent's seed
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 12|1 John 3:12]] - Cain identified as "of the evil one"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 12|1 John 3:12]] - Cain belonged to the evil one); Jude 11 (way of Cain
**Type Classification**: Backward-Looking (typological significance seen retrospectively from NT), Providential (sovereignly arranged to demonstrate the seed conflict)

**Christological Connection**: Eve's mistake with Cain demonstrates that the promised seed would NOT come through natural expectation or firstborn rights. Just as Cain was rejected in favor of Seth, so natural Israel must give way to Christ and His church. The true "man from the LORD" is Jesus, the second Adam, who undoes Cain's murder by laying down His life willingly. Where Cain killed his brother, Christ dies for His brothers.
