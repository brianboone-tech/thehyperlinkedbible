### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 1|Revelation 12:1-5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4592|G4592]] σημεῖον (semeion) - sign, wonder
- [[Lexicon/G1135|G1135]] γυνή (gyne) - woman
- [[Lexicon/G5088|G5088]] τίκτω (tikto) - to give birth, bear
- [[Lexicon/G0730|G0730]] ἄρρην (arsen) - male, masculine
- [[Lexicon/G4464|G4464]] ῥάβδος (rhabdos) - rod, scepter, staff
- [[Lexicon/G1404|G1404]] δράκων (drakon) - dragon, serpent

**Context**: John sees apocalyptic vision of the cosmic conflict between the woman and the dragon (ancient serpent). This is the ultimate unveiling of Genesis 3:15—the woman's seed versus the serpent's enmity, played out on cosmic scale.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fulfills [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] in eschatological imagery (woman, seed, serpent, crushing victory)
- The "male child who will rule all nations with iron scepter" quotes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 9|Psalm 2:9]] (messianic psalm)
- Woman clothed with sun, moon, twelve stars = Israel/church as covenant people bearing Messiah

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 3:15 (woman, seed, serpent conflict); Psalm 2:9 (iron scepter rule); Isaiah 7:14 (virgin bearing son); Isaiah 66:7-8 (Zion giving birth)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 9|Revelation 12:9]] - identifies dragon as "ancient serpent" (Genesis 3
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 10|Revelation 12:10-11]] - victory through Christ's blood
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 17|Revelation 12:17]] - dragon makes war on rest of woman's offspring—believers
**Type Classification**: Eschatological fulfillment of forward-looking prophecy (Genesis 3:15)

**Christological Connection**: Revelation 12 is the grand finale of the Eve trajectory. The woman (representing both Mary and the church) gives birth to the male child who will rule all nations—Jesus Christ, the seed of the woman. The ancient serpent (dragon, devil, Satan—[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 9|Revelation 12:9]]) attempts to devour the child, but the child is caught up to God's throne (resurrection and ascension). The dragon's defeat is proclaimed: "They have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 11|Revelation 12:11]]). [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]]'s promise finds complete, cosmic fulfillment. The serpent's head is crushed; the woman's seed reigns forever. Believers are "the rest of her offspring" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 17|Revelation 12:17]]), children of the woman's seed through faith in Christ.
