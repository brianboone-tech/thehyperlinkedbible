### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 20|Genesis 3:20]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2332|H2332]] חַוָּה (Havvah) - Eve, "life" or "living"
- [[Lexicon/H0517|H0517]] אֵם (em) - mother
- [[Lexicon/H2416|H2416]] חַי (chay) - living, alive

**Context**: Adam names his wife after God's pronouncement of both curse and promise. Despite the sentence of death upon humanity, Adam names her "Life" or "Living One," demonstrating faith that life (not death) will prevail through her offspring.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The pattern of "mother of promise" continues through Sarah (barren yet bearing Isaac), Rebekah, Rachel, Hannah, and ultimately Mary
- Eve as "mother of all living" points to both physical descent and spiritual seed-line

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - the protoevangelium immediately precedes this naming
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 1|Genesis 4:1]] - Eve's hope in Cain
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 25|Genesis 4:25]] - Eve recognizes Seth as appointed seed
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]] - Christ born of a woman
  - [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 15|1 Timothy 2:15]] - saved through childbearing—likely reference to Christ's birth
**Type Classification**: Providential (Adam's naming inspired by divine promise), Forward-Looking (points to the woman who will bear the seed of promise)

**Christological Connection**: Eve's naming as "mother of all the living" finds ultimate fulfillment in Mary, who bears Jesus Christ—the Life of the world ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]; 11:25). What Eve lost (access to tree of life), Christ restores. Eve is mother of all who physically live; Mary is mother of Him who gives eternal life. The church, as bride of Christ, continues this motherhood by bearing spiritual children through gospel proclamation.
