### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7650|נִשְׁבַּע יְהוָה]] (*nišbaʿ YHWH*) - "the LORD has sworn"
- [[Lexicon/H5101-5200#H5162|וְלֹא יִנָּחֵם]] (*wəlōʾ yinnāḥēm*) - "and will not change his mind"
- [[Lexicon/H3501-3600#H3548|כֹּהֵן לְעוֹלָם]] (*kōhēn ləʿôlām*) - "priest forever"
- [[Lexicon/H1701-1800#H1700|דִּבְרָתִי]] (*dibrātî*) - "manner, order"
- [[Lexicon/H4401-4500#H4442|מַלְכִּי־צֶדֶק]] (*malkî-ṣedeq*) - "Melchizedek" (meaning "king of righteousness")

**Context**: In this royal Davidic psalm, God swears an irrevocable oath that David's Lord will be "a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek." This prophecy is revolutionary—the Messiah's priesthood will not derive from Aaron's Levitical line but from Melchizedek's superior, mysterious priesthood. The oath formula demonstrates God's unbreakable commitment to establish this eternal priestly king.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 18|Genesis 14:18-20]] (Melchizedek priest of God Most High), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalm 2:7]] (You are my Son), [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]] (scepter shall not depart from Judah)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 6|Hebrews 5:6, 10]] (Christ addressed as Melchizedekan priest), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 20|Hebrews 6:20]] (forerunner entered as high priest after Melchizedek), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 11|Hebrews 7:11-28]] (extended exposition of Melchizedekan priesthood)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] finds exclusive fulfillment in Christ, whose priesthood after Melchizedek's order surpasses the Levitical. Jesus quotes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] to prove Messiah's deity ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 44|Matthew 22:44]]), and Hebrews extensively develops verse 4's priesthood. Christ's appointment differs from Aaron's: "The former priests were made such without an oath, but this one was made a priest with an oath by the one who said to him: 'The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind, "You are a priest forever"'" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 21|Hebrews 7:21]]). The oath guarantees permanence—what God swears is unbreakable. Christ's priesthood isn't based on "a legal requirement concerning bodily descent" (Levitical genealogy) but "the power of an indestructible life" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 16|Hebrews 7:16]])—resurrection life that cannot die. The Melchizedek typology is profound: Melchizedek received tithes from Abraham, proving superiority over Abraham's descendant Levi ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 9|Hebrews 7:9-10]]); Melchizedek blessed Abraham, and "the inferior is blessed by the superior" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 7|Hebrews 7:7]]); Melchizedek's priesthood existed apart from Mosaic law, demonstrating a priesthood "on a different legal basis" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 11|Hebrews 7:11]]). Christ's dual role as king (Davidic descent) and priest (Melchizedekan order) unites offices Israel separated. He is "King of righteousness" and "King of peace" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 2|Hebrews 7:2]]), fulfilling Melchizedek's name and title. The "forever" priesthood ensures believers have perpetual intercession—"he always lives to make intercession for them" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]). [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]]'s oath establishes what Levitical priesthood couldn't provide: permanent, perfect mediation through the eternal priest-king who sits at God's right hand until all enemies submit.
