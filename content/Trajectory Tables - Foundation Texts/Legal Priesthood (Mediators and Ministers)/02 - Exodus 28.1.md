### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7101-7200#H7126|הַקְרֵב]] (*haqrēb*) - "bring near" (causative, Hiphil imperative)
- [[Lexicon/H0001-0100#H0175|אַהֲרֹן אָחִיךָ]] (*ʾahărōn ʾāḥîkā*) - "Aaron your brother"
- [[Lexicon/H1101-1200#H1121|בָּנָיו]] (*bānāyw*) - "his sons"
- [[Lexicon/H3401-3500#H3547|לְכַהֲנוֹ לִי]] (*ləkahănô lî*) - "to serve me as priest"

**Context**: God commands Moses to bring Aaron and his sons near for priestly service. This divine appointment establishes the Aaronic priesthood, restricting priestly ministry to one family from Levi's tribe. The command to "bring near" carries sacrificial connotations—the same verb used for bringing offerings to the altar. Aaron is brought near to serve as mediator between holy God and sinful Israel.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]] (kingdom of priests), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 10|Numbers 3:10]] (Aaron and sons minister as priests), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 7|Numbers 18:7]] (priesthood as gift)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 1|Hebrews 5:1-4]] (every high priest chosen from among men), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 5|Hebrews 5:5-6]] (Christ appointed by God), [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]] (royal priesthood)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1]]'s appointment of Aaron prefigures Christ's superior priestly appointment. Hebrews declares Christ "did not exalt himself to be made a high priest, but was appointed by him who said to him, 'You are my Son, today I have begotten you'" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 5|Hebrews 5:5]]). As Aaron was sovereignly chosen from among his brothers, Christ was "made like his brothers in every respect, so that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 17|Hebrews 2:17]]). Yet Christ's priesthood surpasses Aaron's: Aaron was appointed by Moses' command, Christ by the Father's oath ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 20|Hebrews 7:20-21]]); Aaron's priesthood was temporary, Christ's eternal ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 24|Hebrews 7:24]]); Aaron needed successors due to death, Christ "continues forever" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 23|Hebrews 7:23]]); Aaron's lineage was Levitical, Christ's Melchizedekan ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 11|Hebrews 7:11]]). Aaron served in earthly copy, Christ in heavenly reality ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 5|Hebrews 8:5]]). The exclusive nature of Aaron's appointment—only his descendants—points to Christ's unique qualification as the only mediator ([[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 5|1 Timothy 2:5]]). Aaron was "brought near" to serve; Christ brings believers near to God ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 13|Ephesians 2:13]]: "brought near by the blood of Christ"). The democratization occurs not through abolishing priesthood but through Christ's perfect priestly work: believers become "a royal priesthood" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]) because Christ's mediation grants all access to God. Aaron's appointment was type; Christ's is antitype. Aaron pointed forward; Christ fulfills perfectly.
