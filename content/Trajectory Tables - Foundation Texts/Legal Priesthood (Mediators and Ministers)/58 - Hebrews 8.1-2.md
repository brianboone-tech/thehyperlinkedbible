### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-2]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2801-2900#G2774|κεφάλαιον]] (*kephalaion*) - "main point, summary"
- [[Lexicon/G5601-5700#G5108|τοιοῦτον]] (*toiouton*) - "such a one" (referring to Christ)
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2523|ἐκάθισεν]] (*ekathisen*) - "sat down" (aorist, completed action)
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1188|δεξιᾷ τοῦ θρόνου]] (*dexia tou thronou*) - "right hand of the throne"
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3172|μεγαλωσύνης]] (*megalōsynēs*) - "Majesty" (divine greatness)
- [[Lexicon/G3001-3100#G3011|λειτουργός]] (*leitourgos*) - "minister, servant" (public service)
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0039|τῶν ἁγίων]] (*tōn hagiōn*) - "the holy places, the sanctuary"
- [[Lexicon/G4401-4500#G4633|σκηνῆς τῆς ἀληθινῆς]] (*skēnēs tēs alēthinēs*) - "the true tent/tabernacle"

**Context**: Hebrews declares the "main point" of its argument: believers have a High Priest who sat down at God's right hand in heaven, ministering in the true sanctuary. This contrasts decisively with Levitical priests who stood in earthly tabernacle. Christ's session demonstrates finished work; His heavenly location demonstrates superior ministry. He serves not in human-made copy but in the reality—"the true tent that the Lord set up, not man."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] (sit at my right hand), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]] (make me a sanctuary according to pattern)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]] (sat down at right hand), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 11|Hebrews 9:11]] (greater and more perfect tent), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]] (entered heaven itself), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 12|Hebrews 10:12]] (offered one sacrifice and sat down)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-2]] declares the "main point"—believers' High Priest sits enthroned at God's right hand, ministering in heaven's true sanctuary. This fulfills [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]'s invitation: "The LORD says to my Lord: 'Sit at my right hand.'" Christ's session is triumphant—having "made purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]). The sitting posture contrasts decisively with Levitical priests who stood daily because their work never finished ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 11|Hebrews 10:11]]). Christ sat because He "offered for all time a single sacrifice for sins" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 12|Hebrews 10:12]]), completing what repetitive offerings couldn't accomplish. His location is heavenly—"a minister in the holy places, in the true tent that the Lord set up, not man." This transcends earthly tabernacle and temple. Moses built according to heavenly pattern ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 40|Exodus 25:40]]); Solomon constructed magnificent temple ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 27|1 Kings 8:27]]: yet "will God indeed dwell on the earth? Behold, heaven and the highest heaven cannot contain you"); but Christ "entered... into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God on our behalf" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 24|Hebrews 9:24]]). The earthly sanctuary was copy (hypodeigma) and shadow (skia); Christ ministers in reality (alēthinē). This guarantees superior efficacy—His blood cleanses conscience ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9:14]]), His intercession is perpetual ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]), His covenant is better ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6]]). The "right hand of the throne" indicates supreme authority—Christ reigns as priest-king, fulfilling Melchizedekan pattern. Believers confidently approach God's throne of grace ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 16|Hebrews 4:16]]) because their High Priest sits there, representing them in God's immediate presence. Levitical priests entered earthly Most Holy Place once yearly with fear; Christ entered heavenly reality once for all with authority, opening permanent access for all who trust Him.
