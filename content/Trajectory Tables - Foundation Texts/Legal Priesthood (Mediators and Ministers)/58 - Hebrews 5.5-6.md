### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 5|Hebrews 5:5-6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1401-1500#G1392|ἐδόξασεν]] (*edoxasen*) - "glorified, exalted"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0058|ἀρχιερεὺς]] (*archiereus*) - "high priest"
- [[Lexicon/G2801-2900#G2980|λαλήσας]] (*lalēsas*) - "having spoken, said"
- [[Lexicon/G4901-5000#G5207|Υἱός μου εἶ σύ]] (*hyios mou ei sy*) - "You are my Son"
- [[Lexicon/G2401-2500#G2409|ἱερεὺς εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα]] (*hiereus eis ton aiōna*) - "priest forever"
- [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3198|Μελχισέδεκ]] (*Melchisedek*) - "Melchizedek"

**Context**: The author demonstrates that Christ did not appoint Himself as High Priest but was appointed by God the Father. Two divine declarations establish Christ's priestly qualification: Psalm 2:7 ("You are my Son") proves divine sonship, and Psalm 110:4 ("You are a priest forever") establishes eternal priesthood after Melchizedek's order. This divine appointment surpasses Levitical priests who inherited office through genealogy.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalm 2:7]] (divine sonship declaration), [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] (Melchizedekan priesthood oath)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 5|Hebrews 1:5]] ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalm 2:7]] quoted for sonship), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 10|Hebrews 5:10]] (designated high priest after Melchizedek), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 17|Hebrews 7:17, 21]] (oath formula repeated)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 5|Hebrews 5:5-6]] establishes Christ's priestly credentials through divine appointment, contrasting with self-appointment or merely human authorization. Where Levitical priests inherited office through Aaronic genealogy, Christ receives appointment by God's oath. The quotation of [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalm 2:7]] ("You are my Son, today I have begotten you") applies to Christ's resurrection-exaltation—God declares His divine sonship publicly through raising Him from the dead ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 33|Acts 13:33]]). This sonship uniquely qualifies Christ for priestly ministry—He mediates not as mere man but as God-man. The second quotation from [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] ("You are a priest forever, after the order of Melchizedek") establishes His eternal priesthood. The combination proves Christ's dual nature and dual role: as Son, He is divine; as priest, He mediates between God and humanity. His priesthood doesn't depend on Levitical genealogy (Christ descended from Judah, not Levi—[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 14|Hebrews 7:14]]) but on divine oath. This makes it "unchangeable" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 24|Hebrews 7:24]])—unable to pass to another. Christ didn't "exalt himself" but waited for the Father's appointment, modeling humble submission even in receiving priesthood. The "forever" priesthood guarantees perpetual intercession—unlike Aaronic priests who died and needed successors, Christ "always lives to make intercession" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]). Believers can approach God confidently because their High Priest holds office not by revocable genealogical claim but by God's irrevocable oath. Where Aaron's sons inherited priesthood by birth, Christ receives it by divine declaration. This oath-based priesthood is superior in every way: it's permanent (not temporary), perfect (not flawed by sin), powerful (based on indestructible life, not mortal succession). [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 5|Hebrews 5:5-6]] anchors Christ's priesthood firmly in God's sovereign purpose, proven by Scripture, establishing the foundation for all subsequent argument about His superior high priestly ministry.
