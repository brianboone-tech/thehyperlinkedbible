### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 28|Numbers 20:28]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6401-6500#H6584|וַיַּפְשֵׁט]] (*wayyapšēṭ*) - "and he stripped off" (Hiphil)
- [[Lexicon/H0801-0900#H0899|בְּגָדָיו]] (*bəgādāyw*) - "his garments" (priestly vestments)
- [[Lexicon/H3701-3800#H3847|וַיַּלְבֵּשׁ]] (*wayyalbēš*) - "and he clothed" (Hiphil)
- [[Lexicon/H0401-0500#H0499|אֶלְעָזָר]] (*ʾelʿāzār*) - "Eleazar" (meaning "God has helped")
- [[Lexicon/H4101-4200#H4191|וַיָּמָת]] (*wayyāmāt*) - "and he died"
- [[Lexicon/H7301-7400#H7218|רֹאשׁ הָהָר]] (*rōʾš hāhār*) - "top of the mountain"

**Context**: Aaron dies on Mount Hor at age 123 after forty years of priestly service. Before his death, Moses transfers Aaron's high priestly garments to his son Eleazar, symbolizing the priesthood's succession. Aaron's death demonstrates the Levitical priesthood's fundamental limitation—death prevents continuance in office, requiring constant replacement through genealogical succession.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 1|Exodus 28:1-4]] (Aaron's priestly garments), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 24|Numbers 20:24-26]] (Aaron gathered to his people)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25#Numbers 25 . 11|Numbers 25:11-13]] (Phinehas' perpetual priesthood), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 21|Jeremiah 33:21-22]] (Levitical priests never lack successor)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 23|Hebrews 7:23-24]] (death prevented continuing; Christ continues forever), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 8|Hebrews 7:8]] (mortal men receive tithes; Christ lives)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 28|Numbers 20:28]]'s priestly succession due to death highlights Christ's eternal priesthood's superiority. Hebrews argues: "The former priests were many in number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 23|Hebrews 7:23]]). Aaron died, requiring Eleazar; Eleazar died, requiring Phinehas—an unbroken chain of death and succession for fifteen centuries. Each death proved the priesthood's inadequacy to accomplish final redemption. But Christ "holds his priesthood permanently, because he continues forever" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 24|Hebrews 7:24]]). The Greek word aparabatos ("permanent, unchangeable") means unable to pass to another—Christ's priesthood cannot transfer because He never dies. Where Aaron was stripped of garments in death, Christ was "clothed with splendor and majesty" ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 104#Psalm 104 . 1|Psalm 104:1]]) in resurrection. His high priestly ministry continues eternally in heaven's true sanctuary ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-2]]). The resurrection conquered death that terminated every Aaronic priest ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 9|Romans 6:9]]: "Death no longer has dominion over him"). Therefore Christ "always lives to make intercession" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]) without interruption, succession, or replacement. Aaron's death on Mount Hor illustrates the limitation; Christ's resurrection on Easter morning demolishes it. Believers need no succession of mediators because Christ's priesthood never ends, securing eternal salvation for all who draw near through Him.
