### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 23|Hebrews 7:23-24]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4119|πλείονες]] (*pleiones*) - "many, numerous" (comparative)
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1096|γεγονότες]] (*gegonotēs*) - "having become" (perfect participle)
- [[Lexicon/G2301-2400#G2348|θανάτῳ]] (*thanatō*) - "by death" (dative of cause)
- [[Lexicon/G2701-2800#G2967|κωλύεσθαι]] (*kōlyesthai*) - "prevented, hindered"
- [[Lexicon/G3801-3900#G3887|παραμένειν]] (*paramenein*) - "continue, remain"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0165|εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα]] (*eis ton aiōna*) - "forever" (literally "unto the age")
- [[Lexicon/G0501-0600#G0531|ἀπαράβατον]] (*aparabaton*) - "permanent, unchangeable, unable to pass to another"

**Context**: Hebrews contrasts Levitical priests with Christ using the decisive categories of death versus life. Levitical priests were "many in number" because death prevented any single priest from continuing in office—each generation required replacements. But Christ "holds his priesthood permanently" because He "continues forever." Death that terminated every Aaronic priest cannot touch Christ's risen life, making His priesthood eternally effective.

- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 8|Hebrews 7:8]] (mortal men receive tithes; Christ lives), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 16|Hebrews 7:16]] (power of indestructible life), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]] (always lives to make intercession)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 23|Hebrews 7:23-24]] demonstrates Christ's eternal priesthood's superiority through the death-versus-life contrast. Where Levitical priests "were many in number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office," Christ "holds his priesthood permanently, because he continues forever." The resurrection is decisive—Christ "was delivered up for our trespasses and raised for our justification" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 25|Romans 4:25]]), and having been raised, "death no longer has dominion over him" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 9|Romans 6:9]]). This indestructible life qualifies Him uniquely for eternal priesthood. Aaron died on Mount Hor; Christ lives at God's right hand. Eleazar replaced Aaron; no one replaces Christ because He needs no replacement. The "many" priests over fifteen centuries testify to the system's inadequacy—if one could perfect believers, succession would cease. But Christ's single, eternal priesthood "perfects for all time those who are being sanctified" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 14|Hebrews 10:14]]). The word aparabaton ("permanent, unchangeable") is rich—it means Christ's priesthood cannot pass (parabainō) to another. There will be no successor to Christ, no "Christ II" needed when He fails or dies, because He neither fails nor dies. This permanence grounds believers' eternal security—their mediator's work isn't interrupted by death, requiring them to find new priest. Christ "always lives to make intercession for them" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 25|Hebrews 7:25]]), ensuring perpetual advocacy. The Levitical system's weakness was its practitioners' mortality; Christ's strength is His indestructible life. Every Aaronic priest's gravestone testified to limitation; Christ's empty tomb testifies to victory. The many needed priests reveal the problem; the one eternal Priest provides the solution.
