### [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 5|Numbers 16:5]]

**Context**: During Korah's rebellion, when 250 leaders challenged Moses and Aaron's unique authority, Moses declares that "tomorrow morning the LORD will reveal who belongs to Him and who is holy." This divine discernment would distinguish God's chosen ones from presumptuous rebels. The phrase "the LORD will reveal who belongs to Him" establishes that God—not human claim—determines who is truly His. Paul quotes this verse in 2 Timothy 2:19 as proof that "the Lord knows those who are his."

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- וְיֹדַע יְהוָה (*veyoda' YHWH*) - "the LORD will reveal/know" (hiphil, causative knowing)
- אֶת־אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ (*et-asher-lo*) - "who belongs to Him" (possessive relationship)
- הַקָּדוֹשׁ (*haqqadosh*) - "the holy one" (set apart by God, not self)
- וְהִקְרִיב (*vehiqriv*) - "He will bring near" (God chooses access to Himself)

**Christological Connection**: Moses' declaration that "the LORD will reveal who belongs to Him" points forward to Christ's teaching that the Father has given certain people to the Son ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 37|John 6:37]]), that Christ "knows his own" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 14|John 10:14]]), and that those written in the Lamb's book of life are secured by divine election. Korah's presumption—claiming that "everyone in the entire congregation is holy" (16:3)—was judged by fire and earthquake because God alone determines who is His. The book of life is not a democratic registry but a sovereign record. Paul's quotation in [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 19|2 Timothy 2:19]] applies this to the church: amid false teaching and apostasy, "the Lord knows those who are his"—names written before the foundation of the world.
