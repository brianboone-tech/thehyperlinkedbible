### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 62#Isaiah 62 . 2|Isaiah 62:2]]

**Context**: Isaiah proclaims Jerusalem's future restoration when "the nations shall see your righteousness, and all the kings your glory, and you shall be called by a new name that the mouth of the LORD will give." The divine bestowal of a new name signifies transformation and belonging—those named by God belong to God. This "new name" theme appears in Revelation 2:17 and 3:12 where overcomers receive Christ's own new name.

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- שֵׁם חָדָשׁ (*shem chadash*) - "new name" (transformed identity)
- פִּי יְהוָה יִקֳּבֶנּוּ (*pi YHWH yiqqevennu*) - "the mouth of the LORD will give/designate" (divine naming)
- צִדְקֵךְ (*tsidqek*) - "your righteousness" (vindicated status)
- כְּבוֹדֵךְ (*kevodek*) - "your glory" (radiant transformation)

**Christological Connection**: The promise of a divine "new name" connects directly to the book of life. To be named by God is to be claimed by God—one's name written in His book. In Revelation, overcomers receive Christ's "new name" (3:12), their names are in the book of life (3:5), and they bear the Father's and Lamb's names on their foreheads (14:1; 22:4). The new name given to restored Zion anticipates the greater naming when believers receive the name of the triune God Himself. Divine naming = divine ownership = divine security. Those whom God names, God keeps; those in His book bear His name forever.
