### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 48|Acts 13:48]]

**Context**: Paul preaches in Pisidian Antioch. When Jews reject the gospel, Paul turns to Gentiles who respond joyfully. Luke's explanation is striking: "as many as were appointed to eternal life believed" (ὅσοι ἦσαν τεταγμένοι εἰς ζωὴν αἰώνιον ἐπίστευσαν). The passive verb "appointed/ordained" (τεταγμένοι) indicates divine predetermination—they believed because they were already appointed to life.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- τεταγμένοι (*tetagmenoi*) - "appointed/ordained" (perfect passive participle, already determined)
- εἰς ζωὴν αἰώνιον (*eis zōēn aiōnion*) - "to eternal life" (destination predetermined)
- ἐπίστευσαν (*episteusan*) - "believed" (aorist, result of appointment)
- ὅσοι (*hosoi*) - "as many as" (precisely those appointed, no more, no less)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 48|Acts 13:48]] directly connects the book of life to the order of salvation. Those "appointed to eternal life" believed—their names were in the book before they exercised faith. This is not faith producing election but election producing faith. Luke's phrasing echoes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 32|Exodus 32:32-33]] where God's "book" determined destiny. The Lamb's book of life ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 13#Revelation 13 . 8|Revelation 13:8]]) contains names "written before the foundation of the world"—those appointed believed in Antioch, and those appointed believe today. Faith is the evidence of election, not its cause. Christ died for those the Father gave Him ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]], 15), and those given to Christ will come to Him ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 37|John 6:37]]).
