### [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 19|2 Timothy 2:19]]

**Context**: Amid warnings about false teachers (Hymenaeus and Philetus who taught that the resurrection had already happened), Paul provides assurance: "God's firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: 'The Lord knows those who are his.'" This quotation from Numbers 16:5 (LXX) applies Korah's rebellion typologically—false teachers claim to be God's people, but God knows who truly belongs to Him.

**Greek Key Terms**:
- στερεὸς θεμέλιος (*stereos themelios*) - "firm foundation" (unshakable divine decree)
- σφραγῖδα (*sphragida*) - "seal" (authentication, security)
- ἔγνω κύριος (*egnō kyrios*) - "the Lord knows" (perfect knowing, established fact)
- τοὺς ὄντας αὐτοῦ (*tous ontas autou*) - "those who are his" (possession, belonging)

**Christological Connection**: This verse is the clearest NT application of the book of life concept to pastoral assurance. Amid false teaching and apostasy, believers can trust that "the Lord knows those who are his." The foundation—God's sovereign election—stands firm; the seal—divine ownership—cannot be broken. What was true in Korah's rebellion (God distinguished His own from rebels) remains true in the church (God distinguishes genuine believers from false teachers). Those whose names are in the Lamb's book are secure not because they recognize themselves as God's but because God recognizes them. Christ knows His sheep; they cannot be snatched away ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 28|John 10:28-29]]). The book of life is God's firm foundation, sealed by His infallible knowledge.
