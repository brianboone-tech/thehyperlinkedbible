### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 8|Revelation 7:8]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5443|G5443]] φυλή (*phylē*) - "tribe"
- [[Lexicon/G958|G958]] Βενιαμίν (*Beniamin*) - "Benjamin"
- [[Lexicon/G4973|G4973]] σφραγίζω (*sphragizō*) - "to seal"
- [[Lexicon/G1427|G1427]] δώδεκα (*dōdeka*) - "twelve"
- [[Lexicon/G5505|G5505]] χιλιάς (*chilias*) - "thousand"

**Context**: The 144,000 sealed from the tribes of Israel are listed in Revelation 7:4-8. Benjamin appears last in this enumeration: "from the tribe of Benjamin 12,000." The list differs from traditional OT tribal lists—Dan is omitted, Joseph and Manasseh both appear, and the order is unusual. Benjamin's placement at the end may be significant, echoing his position as the youngest son who completed Jacob's family.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Benjamin was Jacob's twelfth and final son, completing the family (Gen 35:16-18). His position as the last sealed tribe mirrors this.
- The tribe nearly faced extinction in Judges 20-21 but was preserved—now 12,000 are sealed from this remnant.
- Benjamin remained loyal to Judah after the kingdom divided (1 Kgs 12:21), and here Benjamin is sealed alongside all tribes in restored unity.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 35:16-18 (youngest son), Genesis 49:28 (twelve tribes complete), Judges 20-21 (near extinction)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 1|Revelation 14:1-5]] (144,000 with the Lamb), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 12|Revelation 21:12]] (twelve gates named for tribes)
- **PARALLEL**: Romans 11:1 (Benjamite as proof of remnant), Philippians 3:5 (Paul of Benjamin)

**Type Classification**: Forward-Looking (eschatological consummation of tribal promises); Direct (literal tribal identification in apocalyptic vision).

**Christological Connection**: Benjamin's sealing among the 144,000 completes the trajectory. (1) **Last Becomes Complete**: As the youngest son, Benjamin completed Jacob's family. In Revelation's sealing, Benjamin appearing last suggests eschatological completion—the ingathering of all God's people is finished. (2) **Wolf Serving Lamb**: The "ravenous wolf" tribe (Gen 49:27) is now sealed for the Lamb (Rev 7:14). The transformation predicted in Paul's conversion becomes cosmic—all of Benjamin's sealed ones follow the Lamb. (3) **Preservation Through Judgment**: The sealing protects from coming wrath (Rev 7:1-3). Benjamin, once nearly exterminated in Judges, is now eternally secured. What nearly perished is eternally preserved. (4) **Unity Restored**: Benjamin remained with Judah when Israel split. In Revelation, all twelve tribes are reunited in the Lamb's service—the kingdom's division healed, the family made one. (5) **Son at the Right Hand**: Benjamin's name means "son of the right hand." These sealed ones will stand at Christ's right hand (Matt 25:33-34), sharing in His glory as beloved sons.
