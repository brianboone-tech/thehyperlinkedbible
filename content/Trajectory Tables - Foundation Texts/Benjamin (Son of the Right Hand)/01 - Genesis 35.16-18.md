### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 16|Genesis 35:16-18]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1144|H1144]] בִּנְיָמִין (*binyamin*) - "Benjamin, son of the right hand"
- [[Lexicon/H1126|H1126]] בֶּן־אוֹנִי (*ben-ʾoni*) - "son of my sorrow"
- [[Lexicon/H3225|H3225]] יָמִין (*yamin*) - "right hand"
- [[Lexicon/H205|H205]] אוֹן (*ʾon*) - "sorrow, trouble, wickedness"
- [[Lexicon/H672|H672]] אֶפְרָת (*ʾephrat*) - "Ephrath, fruitful" (= Bethlehem)
- [[Lexicon/H7185|H7185]] קָשָׁה (*qashah*) - "to be hard, difficult" (describing the labor)
- [[Lexicon/H4191|H4191]] מוּת (*mut*) - "to die"
- [[Lexicon/H5315|H5315]] נֶפֶשׁ (*nephesh*) - "soul, life, breath"

**Context**: Jacob's beloved wife Rachel gives birth on the road between Bethel and Ephrath (Bethlehem). The labor is severe, and Rachel dies in childbirth. With her dying breath, she names the child "Ben-oni" (son of my sorrow), but Jacob immediately renames him "Benjamin" (son of my right hand). This is the only child whose name the father changes, and the only patriarch's wife to die in childbirth. The location near Bethlehem and the transformation of the name from sorrow to honor create a rich typological matrix.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The birth near Bethlehem (Ephrath) connects to [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]], where Bethlehem Ephrathah produces the one whose origins are "from ancient times."
- Rachel's weeping for her children (Jer 31:15) develops the motif of maternal sorrow associated with this region.
- The "right hand" terminology echoes throughout Scripture as the position of honor (Ps 110:1; Ps 16:11).

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 30:22-24 (Joseph's birth to Rachel), Genesis 48:13-14 (right hand blessing)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]] (ruler from Bethlehem), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 15|Jeremiah 31:15]] (Rachel weeping)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 16|Matthew 2:16-18]] (Rachel weeping—fulfillment), [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 19|Mark 16:19]] (Christ at right hand)

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (sovereignly arranged birth circumstances); Forward-Looking (naming anticipates the "Son at the right hand" who comes through travail).

**Christological Connection**: Benjamin's birth prefigures Christ in multiple dimensions. (1) **Bethlehem Location**: Benjamin is born near Bethlehem-Ephrath, the very place where Christ would be born (Mic 5:2; Matt 2:1). (2) **Travail unto Death**: Rachel's life is given for her son's—maternal suffering producing new life. Mary would "pondered these things in her heart" as she watched her Son, and Simeon prophesied "a sword will pierce your own soul" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 35|Luke 2:35]]). (3) **Dual Names**: Ben-oni ("son of sorrow") reflects Christ as "a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief" (Isa 53:3), while Benjamin ("son of the right hand") anticipates Christ's exaltation to "the right hand of the Majesty on high" (Heb 1:3). (4) **Father's Authority**: Jacob's renaming demonstrates that the Father's declaration prevails over earthly circumstances—what appeared as tragedy is transformed to honor. So Christ's death was transformed to resurrection, humiliation to exaltation.
