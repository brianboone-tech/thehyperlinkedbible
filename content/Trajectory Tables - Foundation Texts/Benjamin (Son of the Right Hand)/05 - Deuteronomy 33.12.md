### [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 12|Deuteronomy 33:12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3032|H3032]] יְדִיד (*yedid*) - "beloved"
- [[Lexicon/H7931|H7931]] שָׁכַן (*shakan*) - "to dwell, rest, tabernacle"
- [[Lexicon/H983|H983]] בֶּטַח (*betach*) - "security, safely"
- [[Lexicon/H2653|H2653]] חָפַף (*chaphaph*) - "to cover, shield, protect"
- [[Lexicon/H3802|H3802]] כָּתֵף (*katheph*) - "shoulder"
- [[Lexicon/H3117|H3117]] יוֹם (*yom*) - "day"

**Context**: Moses' final blessings on Israel contrast with Jacob's. Where Jacob saw Benjamin as a "ravenous wolf," Moses sees him as "the beloved of the LORD" who dwells securely. God "shields him all day long" and Benjamin rests "upon His shoulders." This remarkably tender blessing describes a relationship of intimate protection. The reference to God's "shoulders" may allude to the temple's location in Benjamin's territory.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The temple mount sat on the border of Benjamin and Judah, with the altar possibly in Benjamin's territory. "Upon His shoulders" may refer to the hills where God would place His dwelling.
- The term יְדִיד ("beloved") echoes the name Jedidiah given to Solomon (2 Sam 12:25), connecting Benjamin's blessing to the temple-builder.
- The shepherd carrying sheep on shoulders ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 15#Luke 15 . 5|Luke 15:5]]) develops this imagery.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Genesis 35:16-18 (Benjamin's birth), Genesis 49:27 (Jacob's contrasting blessing)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 12#2 Samuel 12 . 25|2 Samuel 12:25]] (Solomon called Jedidiah/beloved), 1 Kings 6 (temple in Benjamin's region)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 15#Luke 15 . 5|Luke 15:5]] (shepherd carries lamb on shoulders), [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 11|Mark 1:11]] (beloved Son)

**Type Classification**: Providential Type (Moses' prophetic blessing); Forward-Looking (anticipates both temple location and Christ as the "Beloved" who carries His people).

**Christological Connection**: Moses' blessing of Benjamin directly anticipates Christ and His people. (1) **The Beloved**: Christ is explicitly called "the Beloved" (ὁ ἠγαπημένος, Eph 1:6). At His baptism, the Father declares, "This is My beloved Son" (Matt 3:17). Benjamin's title belongs supremely to Christ. (2) **Dwelling Secure**: The believer "in Christ" rests secure—nothing can separate us from God's love (Rom 8:38-39). As Benjamin dwelt safely, believers abide in Christ. (3) **Shielded All Day Long**: Christ is our shield (faith's shield in Eph 6:16 connects to Christ's protection). The continuous nature ("all day long") mirrors Christ's perpetual intercession (Heb 7:25). (4) **On His Shoulders**: The Good Shepherd "lays [the lost sheep] on his shoulders, rejoicing" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 15#Luke 15 . 5|Luke 15:5]]). Benjamin resting on God's shoulders prefigures believers carried by Christ—the government is "upon His shoulder" (Isa 9:6), and He bears His people to glory.
