### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 27|Romans 3:27-28]]; [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 27|1 Corinthians 1:27-29]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2746|G2746]] καύχησις (kauchēsis) - boasting, glorying
- [[Lexicon/G1576|G1576]] ἐκκλείω (ekkleiō) - to shut out, exclude
- [[Lexicon/G3474|G3474]] μωρός (mōros) - foolish
- [[Lexicon/G772|G772]] ἀσθενής (asthenēs) - weak
- [[Lexicon/G2617|G2617]] καταισχύνω (kataischynō) - to put to shame

**Context**: Romans 3:27—"Then what becomes of our boasting? It is excluded." Justification by faith, not works, removes all human boasting. 1 Corinthians 1:27-29—"God chose what is foolish... what is weak... what is low and despised... so that no human being might boast in the presence of God." God's method of salvation deliberately excludes human glory.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 2|Judges 7:2]] - "Lest Israel boast over me"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 9#Jeremiah 9 . 23|Jeremiah 9:23-24]] - Let him who boasts, boast in the LORD
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 7|2 Corinthians 4:7]] - Power belongs to God, not us

**Type Classification**: Theological principle rooted in Gideon pattern

**Christological Connection**: The "no boasting" principle of [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 2|Judges 7:2]] governs all of God's saving work. Just as God reduced Gideon's army from 32,000 to 300 "lest Israel boast," God designed salvation by grace through faith to exclude human boasting ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 27|Romans 3:27]]). He chose "what is weak in the world to shame the strong" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 27|1 Corinthians 1:27]]) so that salvation displays divine power alone. Christ crucified is the ultimate "foolish" and "weak" means by which God saves—"so that no human being might boast in the presence of God."
