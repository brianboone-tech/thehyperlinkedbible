### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 12|Matthew 4:12-17]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2194|G2194]] Ζαβουλών (Zaboulon) - Zebulun
- [[Lexicon/G3508|G3508]] Νεφθαλίμ (Nephthalim) - Naphtali
- [[Lexicon/G5457|G5457]] φῶς (phōs) - light
- [[Lexicon/G4655|G4655]] σκοτία (skotia) - darkness
- [[Lexicon/G2784|G2784]] κηρύσσω (kēryssō) - to proclaim, preach (trumpet-like proclamation)

**Context**: After John's arrest, Jesus deliberately moves from Nazareth to Capernaum "in the territory of Zebulun and Naphtali." Matthew explains this was "so that what was spoken by the prophet Isaiah might be fulfilled" and quotes Isaiah 9:1-2. Jesus then begins proclaiming (κηρύσσω—a herald's trumpet-like announcement): "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 1|Isaiah 9:1-2]] - The prophecy being fulfilled
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 6#Judges 6 . 35|Judges 6:35]] - Zebulun and Naphtali gathered with Gideon
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18-19]] - Jesus' Nazareth sermon on Isaiah 61

**Type Classification**: Direct fulfillment of Isaiah 9:1-2; completes Gideon typology

**Christological Connection**: Jesus' deliberate move to Zebulun and Naphtali—the exact region of Gideon's victory—completes the typological pattern. Where Gideon's torches shone in darkness to defeat Midian, Jesus the "great light" dawns to defeat spiritual darkness. His proclamation (κηρύσσω) echoes the trumpet blast of Gideon's 300. The "day of Midian" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 4|Isaiah 9:4]]) now arrives in its fullness: the Child born ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6]]) has come to break oppression through proclamation of the kingdom.
