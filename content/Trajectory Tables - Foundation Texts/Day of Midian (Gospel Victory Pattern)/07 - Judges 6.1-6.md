### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 6#Judges 6 . 1|Judges 6:1-6]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4080|H4080]] מִדְיָן (Midyan) - Midian, the oppressing nation
- [[Lexicon/H1809|H1809]] דָּלַל (dalal) - to be brought low, impoverished
- [[Lexicon/H4585|H4585]] מְעוֹנָה (me'onah) - dens, caves (where Israel hid)

**Context**: Israel did evil and God gave them into Midian's hand for seven years. The Midianites, Amalekites, and "people of the East" would destroy Israel's crops, leaving them "greatly impoverished." Israel hid in caves and cried out to the LORD. This darkness sets the stage for Gideon's unconventional deliverance.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 2#Judges 2 . 11|Judges 2:11-15]] - The cycle of sin, oppression, crying out, and deliverance
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 1|Isaiah 9:1]] - "The land of Zebulun and Naphtali" in darkness
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 16|Matthew 4:16]] - "The people dwelling in darkness have seen a great light"

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (Isaiah explicitly recalls this as typological); Providential

**Christological Connection**: The darkness of Midianite oppression prefigures the spiritual darkness from which Christ delivers. Just as Israel was "greatly impoverished" and hiding in caves, humanity apart from Christ dwells in "the region and shadow of death" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 16|Matthew 4:16]]). The geographic connection—Zebulun and Naphtali—links this oppression directly to Jesus' ministry location, where the "great light" would dawn.
