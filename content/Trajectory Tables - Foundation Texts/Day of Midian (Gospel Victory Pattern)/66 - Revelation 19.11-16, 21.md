### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 11|Revelation 19:11-16, 21]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4501|G4501]] ῥομφαία (rhomphaia) - sword (coming from His mouth)
- [[Lexicon/G4750|G4750]] στόμα (stoma) - mouth
- [[Lexicon/G3056|G3056]] λόγος (logos) - word (His name is "The Word of God")
- [[Lexicon/G935|G935]] βασιλεύς (basileus) - king
- [[Lexicon/G3960|G3960]] πατάσσω (patassō) - to strike, smite

**Context**: Christ returns as conquering King, riding a white horse. His weapon is not a conventional sword but "a sharp sword" coming from His mouth—the Word of God. He bears the name "King of kings and Lord of lords." Victory comes through proclamation: "the rest were slain by the sword that came from the mouth of him who was sitting on the horse."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 20|Judges 7:20]] - "A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 4|Isaiah 11:4]] - "He shall strike the earth with the rod of his mouth"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 4|Isaiah 9:4]] - Breaking oppression "as on the day of Midian"

**Type Classification**: Eschatological consummation of the "Day of Midian" pattern

**Christological Connection**: The "day of Midian" reaches its ultimate fulfillment. Gideon's men cried "A sword for the LORD and for Gideon!" but used no swords—only trumpets, torches, and broken jars. Similarly, Christ's final victory comes through "the sword that came from His mouth"—the Word of God. The proclamation pattern established at Midian, prophesied in [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 4|Isaiah 9:4]], inaugurated in Jesus' Galilean ministry, and extended through the church's gospel proclamation, reaches consummation when Christ returns and conquers all enemies by His Word alone. The trumpet of proclamation brings final victory.
