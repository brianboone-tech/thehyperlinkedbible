### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 5|2 Corinthians 4:5-7]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G2784|G2784]] κηρύσσω (kēryssō) - to proclaim, preach (herald's announcement)
- [[Lexicon/G5457|G5457]] φῶς (phōs) - light
- [[Lexicon/G2344|G2344]] θησαυρός (thēsauros) - treasure
- [[Lexicon/G3749|G3749]] ὀστράκινος (ostrakinos) - made of clay, earthen
- [[Lexicon/G4632|G4632]] σκεῦος (skeuos) - vessel, jar
- [[Lexicon/G5236|G5236]] ὑπερβολή (hyperbolē) - surpassing, extraordinary

**Context**: Paul describes gospel ministry: "We proclaim not ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord." The treasure is "the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." This treasure is contained in "jars of clay" (ὀστράκινος σκεῦος)—weak human vessels—"to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us."

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 16|Judges 7:16-20]] - Clay jars containing torches
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 2|Judges 7:2]] - "Lest Israel boast over me"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 4|Isaiah 9:4]] - "As on the day of Midian"

**Type Classification**: NT application of Gideon typology

**Christological Connection**: Paul's language directly echoes Gideon's victory. The parallel is precise: (1) **Torches → "light of the gospel of the glory of Christ"** (v.4); (2) **Clay jars → "jars of clay"** (v.7); (3) **"Lest Israel boast" → "to show that the surpassing power belongs to God and not to us"** (v.7). Gospel ministers are Gideon's 300—weak vessels carrying the light of Christ, whose brokenness reveals the treasure within. The "day of Midian" continues through the church's proclamation.
