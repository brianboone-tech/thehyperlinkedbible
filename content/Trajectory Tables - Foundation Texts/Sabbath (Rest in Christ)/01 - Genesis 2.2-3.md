### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H3601-3700#H3615|וַיְכַל]] (*wayəkal*) - "and he finished, completed"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7637|בַּיּוֹם הַשְּׁבִיעִי]] (*bayyôm haššəbîʿî*) - "on the seventh day"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7673|וַיִּשְׁבֹּת]] (*wayyišbōt*) - "and he rested, ceased"
- [[Lexicon/H1201-1300#H1288|וַיְבָרֶךְ]] (*wayəbārek*) - "and he blessed"
- [[Lexicon/H6801-6900#H6942|וַיְקַדֵּשׁ]] (*wayəqaddēš*) - "and he made holy, sanctified"

**Context**: God rests on the seventh day after completing creation and blesses the day, making it holy. This establishes the Sabbath as a creation ordinance, grounded in God's own pattern of work and rest, before any covenant or law is given.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1-31]] (six days of creation)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]] (Sabbath command in Decalogue), [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 31#Exodus 31 . 13|Exodus 31:13-17]] (Sabbath as covenant sign), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 12|Deuteronomy 5:12-15]] (Sabbath grounded in exodus)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 3|Hebrews 4:3-4]] (rest from creation), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-10]] (Sabbath rest remains), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]] (Christ gives rest)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] establishes the pattern of work-completion-rest that finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ. God's rest after creation prefigures Christ's rest after redemption. Jesus declares "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]) before resting in the tomb on the Sabbath, then rising on the first day—completing redemptive work as God completed creative work. Hebrews develops this typology extensively: "For he has somewhere spoken of the seventh day in this way: 'And God rested on the seventh day from all his works'" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 4|Hebrews 4:4]]), then applies it to believers entering God's rest by ceasing from works-righteousness ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 10|Hebrews 4:10]]). The "Sabbath rest" (σαββατισμός, *sabbatismos*) that remains for God's people ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9]]) flows from God's original seventh-day rest. What [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] established in creation—rest following completed work—Christ accomplishes in redemption, providing rest for souls weary with trying to earn salvation ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]). The pattern moves: God's rest (Genesis 2) → Israel's Sabbath (Exodus 20) → Christ's finished work (John 19) → Believer's present rest (Hebrews 4) → Eternal rest ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]). The seventh day's blessing and sanctification point forward to the eternal rest where God's people "rest from their labors" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]) in the new creation.
