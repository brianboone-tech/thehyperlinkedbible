### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 10|Isaiah 11:10]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H8401-8500#H8328|שֹׁרֶשׁ יִשַׁי]] (*šōreš yišay*) - "root of Jesse"
- [[Lexicon/H5901-6000#H5975|עֹמֵד]] (*ʿōmēd*) - "standing, will stand"
- [[Lexicon/H5201-5300#H5251|לְנֵס עַמִּים]] (*lənēs ʿammîm*) - "as a signal/banner for peoples"
- [[Lexicon/H4501-4600#H4496|מְנֻחָתוֹ]] (*mənuḥātô*) - "his resting place"
- [[Lexicon/H3501-3600#H3519|כָּבוֹד]] (*kābôd*) - "glory, honor"

**Context**: Isaiah prophesies that the Messianic shoot from Jesse's stump will stand as a banner for the nations, and His resting place will be glorious. This moves rest beyond Israel's geographical land to the Messiah's glory-filled dwelling, universalizing and eschatologizing rest.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1]] (shoot from Jesse's stump), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 17|Numbers 24:17]] (star from Jacob), [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]] (Shiloh prophecy)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 32#Isaiah 32 . 18|Isaiah 32:18]] (people dwell in peaceful resting places), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 1|Isaiah 66:1]] (heaven is my throne)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28-30]] (come to me and I will give you rest), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 12|Romans 15:12]] (root of Jesse risen to rule Gentiles), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 5|Revelation 5:5]] (Root of David has conquered)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 10|Isaiah 11:10]] finds explicit fulfillment in Christ. Paul quotes this verse: "The root of Jesse will come, even he who arises to rule the Gentiles; in him will the Gentiles hope" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 12|Romans 15:12]]). Christ is the root and offspring of David ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 16|Revelation 22:16]]), raised as banner for the nations through the cross ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 32|John 12:32]]: "I, when I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all people to myself"). The "glorious resting place" finds fulfillment in Christ who invites: "Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]). Christ Himself is the rest—not a location but a person. His glory is manifest in incarnation ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]), transfiguration ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 17#Matthew 17 . 2|Matthew 17:2]]), and will be fully revealed at His return ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 30|Matthew 24:30]]). The nations' seeking fulfills as Gentiles stream into the church ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 11|Ephesians 2:11-19]]). The glorious resting place reaches consummation in the new Jerusalem where "the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]). What Isaiah prophesied—the Messiah's glorious resting place drawing nations—Christ fulfills, offering rest from sin's burden now and eternal glory-rest in the age to come.
