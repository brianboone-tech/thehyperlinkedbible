### [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 13|Joshua 1:13-15]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4501-4600#H4496|הַמְּנוּחָה]] (*hammənûḥāh*) - "the rest"
- [[Lexicon/H5301-5400#H5414|נֹתֵן לָכֶם]] (*nōtēn lākem*) - "giving to you"
- [[Lexicon/H0001-0100#H0776|הָאָרֶץ]] (*hāʾāreṣ*) - "the land"
- [[Lexicon/H5601-5700#H5674|תַּעַבְרוּ]] (*taʿabrû*) - "you shall cross over"

**Context**: Joshua reminds the tribes settling east of the Jordan that Moses promised them rest (mənûḥāh) in the land God is giving. Yet they must still cross over and fight until their brothers also receive rest. This reveals rest's partial nature under Joshua—geographical but incomplete.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 3#Deuteronomy 3 . 20|Deuteronomy 3:20]] (Moses' promise of rest), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 9|Deuteronomy 12:9-10]] (rest not yet come)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]] (rest achieved), [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 22#Joshua 22 . 4|Joshua 22:4]] (rest given to brothers), [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 23#Joshua 23 . 1|Joshua 23:1]] (rest from enemies)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]] (if Joshua had given rest, God wouldn't speak of another day)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 13|Joshua 1:13-15]]'s incomplete rest points forward to Christ who provides comprehensive rest. Joshua (Hebrew Yehoshua = "YHWH saves," Greek Ἰησοῦς = Jesus) serves as type of Christ. As Joshua led Israel into Canaan-rest, Jesus leads believers into salvation-rest. Yet Joshua's rest was partial, territorial, and temporary—Israel never fully possessed the land nor enjoyed lasting peace. Hebrews argues: "if Joshua had given them rest, God would not speak later about another day" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8]]). Therefore "a Sabbath rest remains" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9]]) beyond Joshua's provision. Christ provides what Joshua couldn't—rest for souls weary with sin ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]), rest from works-righteousness ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 10|Hebrews 4:10]]), and eschatological rest in the new creation ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]). The corporate dimension of Joshua's rest—eastern tribes helping brothers—prefigures the church's mission: those who have entered Christ's rest serve to bring others into it ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 18|2 Corinthians 5:18-20]]). Joshua's conquest was type; Christ's redemption is antitype. Canaan was shadow; eternal rest in God's presence is substance ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3-4]]).
