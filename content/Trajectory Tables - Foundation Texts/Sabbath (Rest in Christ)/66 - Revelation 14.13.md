### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3107|μακάριοι]] (*makarioi*) - "blessed, happy"
- [[Lexicon/G3401-3500#G3498|νεκροὶ]] (*nekroi*) - "dead ones"
- [[Lexicon/G0001-0100#G0599|ἀποθνῄσκοντες]] (*apothnēskontes*) - "dying, who die"
- [[Lexicon/G1701-1800#G1722|ἐν κυρίῳ]] (*en kyriō*) - "in the Lord" (locative union)
- [[Lexicon/G0301-0400#G0373|ἀναπαήσονται]] (*anapāēsontai*) - "they will rest" (future middle)
- [[Lexicon/G2801-2900#G2873|κόπων]] (*kopōn*) - "labors, toils"
- [[Lexicon/G2001-2100#G2041|ἔργα]] (*erga*) - "works, deeds"

**Context**: In the midst of Revelation's judgment visions, a voice from heaven pronounces blessing on those who die in the Lord. They rest from their labors, yet their works follow them. This eschatological rest consummates the Sabbath principle—eternal cessation from toil in God's presence, while grace-wrought works endure as fruit, not merit.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] (God rested from his works), [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 57#Isaiah 57 . 2|Isaiah 57:2]] (they enter into peace; they rest)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-10]] (Sabbath rest remains), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 6#Revelation 6 . 11|Revelation 6:11]] (rest a little longer), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]] (no more pain or crying)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]] announces the Sabbath rest's eschatological consummation for those united to Christ. The phrase "who die in the Lord" is locational—only union with Christ secures this rest. Christ Himself entered rest after redemption's completion, crying "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]) and resting in the tomb before resurrection. Believers' death mirrors Christ's pattern: finished work (their earthly calling completed), rest (intermediate state peace), resurrection (glorification at Christ's return). The distinction between resting "from labors" yet works "following" reflects grace theology: believers cease from works-righteousness ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 8|Ephesians 2:8-9]]: "not a result of works") yet were "created in Christ Jesus for good works" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 10|Ephesians 2:10]]). In eternity, labors motivated by fear, duty, or merit-seeking cease—these were the toil's burden. But works flowing from grace, faith working through love ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 6|Galatians 5:6]]), follow as evidence of genuine faith and bring reward ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 14|1 Corinthians 3:14]]). The new creation features no temple because "the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]])—direct access replaces mediated worship. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]'s servants "will worship him" shows activity without toilsome labor. This is eternal Sabbath: rest characterizing existence, yet joyful service to the Lamb. What Genesis 2 anticipated after six days' work, what Israel experienced weekly but imperfectly, what Christ provides spiritually now through gospel rest, [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]] consummates eternally—the Sabbath principle fulfilled forever in God's unveiled presence where "they will rest from their labors" and enjoy unbroken communion with the Lamb who is their eternal rest.
