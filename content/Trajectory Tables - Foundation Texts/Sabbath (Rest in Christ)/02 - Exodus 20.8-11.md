### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2001-2100#H2142|זָכוֹר]] (*zākôr*) - "remember"
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7676|יוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת]] (*yôm haššabbāt*) - "the Sabbath day"
- [[Lexicon/H6801-6900#H6942|לְקַדְּשׁוֹ]] (*ləqaddəšô*) - "to keep it holy, to sanctify it"
- [[Lexicon/H4701-4800#H4399|מְלָאכָה]] (*məlāʾkāh*) - "work, labor"
- [[Lexicon/H3001-3100#G3068|יְהוָה]] (*YHWH*) - "the LORD"

**Context**: The Fourth Commandment requires Israel to remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy, grounding the command in God's creative pattern. Six days for labor, the seventh for rest—this structure mirrors God's own work rhythm and becomes a covenant obligation distinguishing Israel.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]] (God's rest on seventh day)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 31#Exodus 31 . 13|Exodus 31:13-17]] (Sabbath as perpetual sign), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 12|Deuteronomy 5:12-15]] (Sabbath grounded in exodus), [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 15|Nehemiah 13:15-22]] (Sabbath violations addressed)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 1|Matthew 12:1-8]] (Lord of the Sabbath), [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 2#Mark 2 . 27|Mark 2:27]] (Sabbath made for man), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16-17]] (Sabbath as shadow)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]] establishes the Sabbath pattern that Christ fulfills and transcends. Jesus declares, "The Son of Man is lord of the Sabbath" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 8|Matthew 12:8]]), claiming authority over the institution. His Sabbath healings ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 13#Luke 13 . 10|Luke 13:10-17]]; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 1|John 5:1-18]]) demonstrate that He gives the rest the Sabbath symbolized—liberation from bondage, including sin and sickness. Paul teaches that Sabbath regulations are "a shadow of the things to come, but the substance belongs to Christ" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16-17]]). The creation pattern—six days work, seventh rest—finds redemptive parallel in Christ's completed work ("It is finished," [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]) followed by Sabbath rest in the tomb, then resurrection on the first day. Hebrews interprets the Sabbath as pointing to rest from works-righteousness: "whoever has entered God's rest has also rested from his works as God did from his" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 10|Hebrews 4:10]]). What the weekly Sabbath provided externally (physical rest), Christ provides internally (soul rest, [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]). The Sabbath command's creation grounding points forward to new creation rest ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-4]]) where God's people rest from earthly labors eternally.
