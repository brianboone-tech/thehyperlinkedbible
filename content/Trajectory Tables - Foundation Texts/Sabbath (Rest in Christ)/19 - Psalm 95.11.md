### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 11|Psalm 95:11]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7650|נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי בְאַפִּי]] (*nišbaʿtî bəʾappî*) - "I swore in my wrath"
- [[Lexicon/H0501-0600#H0518|אִם]] (*ʾim*) - "surely, if" (in oath formula = "surely not")
- [[Lexicon/H0901-1000#H0935|יְבֹאוּן]] (*yəbôʾûn*) - "they shall enter"
- [[Lexicon/H4501-4600#H4496|מְנוּחָתִי]] (*mənûḥātî*) - "my rest"

**Context**: God swears that the wilderness generation, who hardened their hearts at Meribah and tested Him despite seeing His works, will not enter His rest. This divine oath links disobedience with forfeiture of rest, establishing the pattern that faith brings rest while unbelief excludes from it.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 14#Numbers 14 . 21|Numbers 14:21-23]] (oath that wilderness generation won't enter land), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 12|Numbers 20:12]] (Moses and Aaron excluded), [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 34|Deuteronomy 1:34-35]] (oath repeated)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]] (partial rest in Canaan)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 7|Hebrews 3:7-11]] (Holy Spirit warns), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 18|Hebrews 3:18-19]] (unbelief prevents entry), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 3|Hebrews 4:3-5]] (rest available to believers), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 11|Hebrews 4:11]] (strive to enter rest)

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 11|Psalm 95:11]] establishes that rest requires faith, not works—a principle fulfilled in Christ. Hebrews quotes this psalm to prove "a Sabbath rest remains for the people of God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9]]) beyond Canaan's rest. The wilderness generation's failure demonstrates that external Sabbath observance without internal faith provides no true rest. They kept Sabbaths weekly yet missed God's rest entirely. Christ provides what Canaan couldn't—rest for souls ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]). The oath "they shall not enter my rest" finds reversal in Christ's invitation: "Come to me... and I will give you rest" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]]). What unbelief excludes from, faith in Christ opens access to. Hebrews applies the psalm christologically: "whoever has entered God's rest has also rested from his works as God did from his" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 10|Hebrews 4:10]]), meaning believers rest from works-righteousness, trusting Christ's finished work. The wilderness generation tried to enter rest through obedience but failed; believers enter through faith in Christ who obeyed perfectly. The psalm's "Today" creates eschatological urgency—"as long as it is called 'today'" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 13|Hebrews 3:13]])—faith's opportunity remains open until Christ's return. The rest forfeited by the wilderness generation is now offered in Christ to all who believe.
