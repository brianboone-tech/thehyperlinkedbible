### [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 19|Hosea 2:19-20]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- אָרַשׂ (ʾāraś) - to betroth, espouse (repeated 3x: "I will betroth you")
- עוֹלָם (ʿôlām) - forever, everlasting
- צֶדֶק (ṣeḏeq) - righteousness
- מִשְׁפָּט (mišpāṭ) - justice
- חֶסֶד (ḥeseḏ) - steadfast love, covenant loyalty
- רַחֲמִים (raḥămîm) - mercy, compassion
- אֱמוּנָה (ʾĕmûnâ) - faithfulness
- יָדַע (yāḏaʿ) - to know (intimate knowledge)

**Context**: Hosea 2 describes Israel's unfaithfulness (playing the harlot with idols) and Yahweh's promised restoration. Despite Israel's adultery, Yahweh promises to betroth her again.

**Christological Connection**: Hosea's marriage to Gomer (a prostitute) enacted God's relationship with Israel. Israel was Yahweh's unfaithful bride; the church is Christ's purified bride. The three-fold "I will betroth you" emphasizes covenant permanence. Christ betroths His church "forever" (עוֹלָם, ʿôlām)—an eternal covenant secured by His blood.

The qualities of the betrothal:
- **Righteousness** (צֶדֶק): Christ's righteousness imputed
- **Justice** (מִשְׁפָּט): Satisfied at the cross
- **Steadfast Love** (חֶסֶד): Covenant loyalty that never fails
- **Mercy** (רַחֲמִים): Undeserved grace
- **Faithfulness** (אֱמוּנָה): Christ's unchanging commitment

**Application**: Unlike Israel (often unfaithful), the church will be presented pure (Ephesians 5:27) because Christ's sanctifying work ensures it. Our faithfulness rests on His faithfulness.
