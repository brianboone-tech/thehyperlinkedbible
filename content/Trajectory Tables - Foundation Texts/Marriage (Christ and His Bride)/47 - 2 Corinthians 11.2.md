### [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 2|2 Corinthians 11:2]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ζηλόω (zēloō) - to be jealous for, zealous for
- ζῆλος (zēlos) - jealousy, zeal
- θεοῦ (theou) - of God (genitive: divine jealousy)
- ἁρμόζω (harmozō) - to betroth, espouse (aorist middle)
- ἀνήρ (anēr) - husband, man
- εἷς (heis) - one
- παρίστημι (paristēmi) - to present
- παρθένος (parthenos) - virgin
- ἁγνός (hagnos) - pure, chaste

**Context**: Paul defends his apostleship against false teachers in Corinth. He expresses divine jealousy for the Corinthians' spiritual purity.

**Christological Connection**: Paul speaks as the "friend of the bridegroom" (cf. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 29|John 3:29]]), having betrothed the Corinthian church to Christ. "I betrothed you to one husband, to present you as a pure virgin to Christ."

The imagery:
- **Betrothal**: Already accomplished (aorist ἡρμοσάμην, "I betrothed")
- **One Husband**: Exclusive covenant with Christ alone
- **Pure Virgin**: The church's purity matters because she belongs to Christ
- **Presentation**: Future consummation at Christ's return

Paul's jealousy is "godly jealousy" (ζῆλος θεοῦ)—he guards the church's faithfulness to Christ as a best man guards the bride's purity for the groom.

**Application**: The church is betrothed to Christ now. We await the wedding supper (Revelation 19:7-9). Guard your purity; you belong to Christ alone.
