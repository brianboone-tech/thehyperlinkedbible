### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 31|Ephesians 5:31-33]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἀντὶ τούτου (anti toutou) - for this reason, therefore
- καταλείπω (kataleipō) - to leave, forsake
- προσκολλάω (proskollaō) - to join, cleave to, be united with
- σάρξ μία (sarx mia) - one flesh
- μυστήριον (mystērion) - mystery (hidden truth now revealed)
- μέγας (megas) - great, profound
- λέγω (legō) - I say, I mean
- εἰς (eis) - into, with reference to
- φοβέω (phobeō) - to fear, reverence (present middle subjunctive)

**Context**: Paul concludes his marriage instruction in Ephesians 5:22-33 by quoting Genesis 2:24 and revealing its ultimate meaning.

**Christological Connection**: Paul quotes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 24|Genesis 2:24]] ("Therefore a man shall leave... one flesh") and declares: "This mystery is profound, and I am saying that it refers to Christ and the church" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 32|Ephesians 5:32]]).

The "mystery" (μυστήριον, mystērion) is that **from the beginning, marriage was designed to prefigure Christ and the church**. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 24|Genesis 2:24]] has dual reference:
1. **Literal**: Instruction for human marriage
2. **Typological**: Ultimately pointing to Christ and the church

Every Christian marriage is meant to be:
- A **living parable** of the gospel
- A **visible sermon** displaying Christ's love
- A **drama** reenacting the greater reality

When husbands love sacrificially and wives respond joyfully, the watching world sees Christ and the church.

**Application**: Husbands and wives, your marriage is not ultimately about your happiness (though God desires that). It's about displaying the gospel. Make your marriage preach.
