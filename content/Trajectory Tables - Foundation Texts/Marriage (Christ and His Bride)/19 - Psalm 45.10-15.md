### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 45#Psalms 45 . 10|Psalm 45:10-15]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מֶלֶךְ (meleḵ) - king
- בַּת (baṯ) - daughter
- חָמַד (ḥāmaḏ) - to desire, delight in
- יֹפִי (yōp̄î) - beauty
- אָדוֹן (ʾāḏôn) - lord, master
- הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה (hištaḥăwâ) - to bow down, worship
- כְּבוּדָּה (kəḇûddâ) - glory, splendor
- בַּת־מֶלֶךְ (baṯ-meleḵ) - king's daughter, princess
- זָהָב (zāhāḇ) - gold
- רִקְמָה (riqmâ) - embroidered work, woven garments

**Context**: Psalm 45 is a royal wedding psalm for an Israelite king. Verses 1-9 praise the king; verses 10-17 address the bride. Hebrews 1:8-9 applies verses 6-7 to Christ, indicating Messianic interpretation.

**Christological Connection**: This psalm prophetically depicts Christ (the Messianic King) and His bride (the church). The bride is told: "Hear, O daughter... forget your people and your father's house, and the king will desire your beauty" ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 45#Psalm 45 . 10|Psalm 45:10-11]]). The church, drawn from all nations, forsakes former allegiances to belong to Christ. "The princess is decked... with gold-woven robes; in many-colored robes she is led to the king" (45:13-14)—echoing [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 8|Revelation 19:8]]: "fine linen, bright and pure."

**Application**: Christ desires His bride's beauty—not our beauty by nature (we were sinful), but beauty He creates through sanctification (Ephesians 5:26-27).
