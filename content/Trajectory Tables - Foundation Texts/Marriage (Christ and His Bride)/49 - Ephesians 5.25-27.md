### [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 25|Ephesians 5:25-27]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἀγαπάω (agapaō) - to love (present imperative: continuous command)
- παραδίδωμι (paradidōmi) - to give up, hand over, deliver
- ἁγιάζω (hagiazō) - to sanctify, make holy
- καθαρίζω (katharizō) - to cleanse
- λουτρόν (loutron) - washing, bathing
- ῥῆμα (rhēma) - word
- παρίστημι (paristēmi) - to present
- ἔνδοξος (endoxos) - glorious, splendid
- σπίλος (spilos) - spot, stain
- ῥυτίς (rhytis) - wrinkle
- ἅγιος (hagios) - holy
- ἄμωμος (amōmos) - blameless, without blemish

**Context**: Ephesians 5:22-33 uses marriage to teach about Christ and the church. Verses 25-27 command husbands to love their wives "as Christ loved the church."

**Christological Connection**: This is the NT's fullest exposition of marriage typology. Christ's love for the church has three stages:

1. **Past: Sacrifice** - "gave himself up for her" (παρέδωκεν ἑαυτὸν, paredōken heauton, aorist)—completed at the cross
2. **Present: Sanctification** - "that he might sanctify her, having cleansed her" (present ongoing work)
3. **Future: Presentation** - "that he might present the church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle... holy and without blemish"

This echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 22|Genesis 2:22]]: God "brought her to the man." Christ will present the church to Himself—the bride He purchased, purified, and perfected.

**Application**: Husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church: sacrificially (gave Himself), sanctifyingly (cleansing her), purposefully (presenting her glorified). This is the gospel pattern applied to marriage.
