### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]] and [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 15|Exodus 13:15]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6942|H6942]] קָדַשׁ (*qadash*) - "to consecrate, sanctify, set apart as holy"
- [[Lexicon/H1060|H1060]] בְּכוֹר (*bekhor*) - "firstborn, eldest"
- [[Lexicon/H6299|H6299]] פָּדָה (*padah*) - "to redeem, ransom, rescue"
- [[Lexicon/H7523|H7523]] רָצַח (*ratsach*) - "to kill, slay" (used of the tenth plague)

**Context**: Exodus 13 follows immediately after the Passover narrative (Exodus 12) and institutes two permanent memorials of the deliverance: the Feast of Unleavened Bread and the consecration of the firstborn. The consecration command comes directly from God through Moses as Israel prepares to leave Egypt. Verse 2 states the principle ("Consecrate to Me every firstborn"), while verse 15 provides the theological rationale ("when Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, the LORD killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt"). The firstborn belong to God because He claimed them through judgment and redemption.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 11|Numbers 3:11-13]]** directly fulfills and interprets [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]] by instituting the Levitical substitution. God accepts the Levites as corporate substitutes for all Israel's firstborn, establishing the substitutionary principle at the heart of Israelite worship.
- **[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 15|Numbers 18:15-18]]** elaborates the redemption mechanism: firstborn sons must be redeemed with five shekels of silver, while firstborn animals are sacrificed. This creates the two-track system: humans redeemed by payment, animals redeemed by sacrifice.
- **[[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 36|Nehemiah 10:36]]** shows post-exilic Israel renewing covenant commitment to present their firstborn at the temple, demonstrating the enduring nature of this consecration.

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 12|Exodus 12:12-13]], 29 (the tenth plague that established God's claim on the firstborn)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 11|Numbers 3:11-13]] (Levitical substitution), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 15|Numbers 18:15-18]] (redemption price), [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 36|Nehemiah 10:36]] (covenant renewal)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 22|Luke 2:22-23]] (Jesus presented as firstborn), [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]] (Christ as firstborn among many brothers), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]] (church as assembly of firstborn)

**Type Classification**: Direct (divinely commanded consecration), Forward-Looking (points explicitly to Christ who becomes "firstborn among many brothers" and redeems believers to be God's firstborn assembly)

**Christological Connection**: The firstborn consecration anticipates Christ in multiple ways: (1) Jesus is presented at the temple as Mary's firstborn, fulfilling the law ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 22|Luke 2:22-23]]); (2) Christ is "the firstborn among many brothers" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]), holding the preeminent position; (3) Christ is "the firstborn from the dead" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]]), the first to rise in resurrection glory; (4) Through Christ's redemption, believers become "the assembly of the firstborn enrolled in heaven" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]]). The pattern moves from Israel's firstborn consecrated through Passover blood to Christ consecrated as the ultimate Firstborn, to the church consecrated through Christ's blood as God's firstborn sons.
