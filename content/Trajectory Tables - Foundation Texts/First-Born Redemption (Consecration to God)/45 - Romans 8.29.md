### [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4267|G4267]] προγινώσκω (*proginōskō*) - "to foreknow, know beforehand"
- [[Lexicon/G4309|G4309]] προορίζω (*proorizō*) - "to predestine, determine beforehand"
- [[Lexicon/G4832|G4832]] σύμμορφος (*symmorphos*) - "conformed to, sharing the same form"
- [[Lexicon/G1504|G1504]] εἰκών (*eikōn*) - "image, likeness"
- [[Lexicon/G4416|G4416]] πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*) - "firstborn"

**Context**: Romans 8:28-30 forms the climax of Paul's exposition of the Spirit's work in believers' lives. Verse 28 declares God's providential care ("all things work together for good"), and verses 29-30 unveil the eternal chain of salvation: foreknowledge → predestination → calling → justification → glorification. Verse 29 reveals the PURPOSE of predestination: "to be conformed to the image of His Son, so that He would be the firstborn among many brothers." God's goal is not merely individual salvation but the creation of a family resembling Christ, with Christ holding preeminence as the eldest brother.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]]** established that Israel's firstborn belong to God through redemption. Paul reinterprets "firstborn" christologically—Jesus is THE firstborn, and believers become His brothers through adoption.
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 3|Genesis 49:3]]** calls Reuben "my firstborn, my might, the firstfruits of my strength, excelling in honor, excelling in power." The firstborn holds preeminence, which Reuben forfeited but Christ perfectly fulfills.
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]]** prophesies of the Davidic king: "I will appoint him to be My firstborn, the most exalted of the kings of the earth." This royal firstborn status finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ, the Son of David.

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Exodus 13:2 (firstborn consecration), Psalm 89:27 (Davidic firstborn), Genesis 49:3 (firstborn preeminence)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]] ("firstborn of all creation"), [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]] ("firstborn from the dead"), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 6|Hebrews 1:6]] ("when He brings the firstborn into the world"), [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]] ("assembly of the firstborn"), [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]] ("firstborn from the dead")
- **IMMEDIATE CONTEXT**: Romans 8:14-17 establishes believers as "sons of God" and "heirs with Christ." Verse 29 reveals that this sonship means conformity to Christ's image and brotherhood with Christ.

**Type Classification**: Direct (fulfillment of OT firstborn consecration), Forward-Looking (Psalm 89:27's promise of the Davidic "firstborn" explicitly points forward to Christ)

**Christological Connection**: Christ's firstborn status operates on multiple levels: (1) **Eternally**: "firstborn of all creation" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]])—He holds supremacy over creation as the eternal Son. (2) **Redemptively**: "firstborn from the dead" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]])—He is the first to rise in resurrection glory, the prototype of new creation. (3) **Familially**: "firstborn among many brothers" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]])—He holds preeminence in the redeemed family, the eldest brother who leads many sons to glory ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 10|Hebrews 2:10]]). (4) **Royally**: fulfilling [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]]'s promise of the Davidic firstborn as "most exalted of the kings of the earth." The OT trajectory from Israel's consecrated firstborn ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]]) to God's appointed Davidic firstborn ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]]) culminates in Christ, who is firstborn in every sense—eternal, redemptive, familial, and royal. Believers participate in His firstborn status by being "conformed to His image" and adopted as God's sons ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 15|Romans 8:15]], 23; [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 5|Galatians 4:5]]). The church becomes "the assembly of the firstborn enrolled in heaven" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]]) precisely because they are united to THE Firstborn.
