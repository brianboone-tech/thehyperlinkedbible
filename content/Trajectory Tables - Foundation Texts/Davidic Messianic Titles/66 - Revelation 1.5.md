### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3144|G3144]] μάρτυς (*martus*) - "witness" (also "martyr")
- [[Lexicon/G4401-4500#G4416|G4416]] πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*) - "firstborn"
- πιστός (*pistos*) - "faithful, reliable"
- ἄρχων (*archōn*) - "ruler, prince"
- βασιλεύς (*basileus*) - "king"

**Context**: John opens Revelation with a trinitarian greeting (vv. 4-5), introducing Jesus Christ with three messianic titles drawn from Psalm 89. This identification immediately establishes Christ's authority over the Roman emperors who persecuted the churches.

**Text**: "and from Jesus Christ, **the faithful witness** (ὁ μάρτυς ὁ πιστός), **the firstborn from the dead** (ὁ πρωτότοκος τῶν νεκρῶν), and **the ruler of the kings of the earth** (ὁ ἄρχων τῶν βασιλέων τῆς γῆς)."

**Christological Connection**: The modification "firstborn **from the dead**" (τῶν νεκρῶν) is John's decisive addition. Psalm 89's "firstborn" was a royal title; John transforms it into a resurrection title. Jesus' death was His faithful witness (μάρτυς → martyrdom), and His resurrection vindicated that testimony, making Him the first to rise in glorified humanity. His present reign as "ruler of the kings of the earth" demonstrates that the Davidic covenant did not fail—it found its true fulfillment in one who conquered death itself.
