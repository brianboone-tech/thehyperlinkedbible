### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 16|Revelation 19:16]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G935|G935]] βασιλεύς (*basileus*) - "king"
- [[Lexicon/G2962|G2962]] κύριος (*kyrios*) - "lord"

**Context**: Christ's return as conquering King, riding on a white horse to judge and make war in righteousness (vv. 11-16).

**Text**: "On his robe and on his thigh he has a name written, **KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS** (ΒΑΣΙΛΕΥΣ ΒΑΣΙΛΕΩΝ ΚΑΙ ΚΥΡΙΟΣ ΚΥΡΙΩΝ)."

**Connections**:
- **TO (OT Background)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]] - "highest of the kings of the earth"
  - [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 47|Daniel 2:47]] - "God of gods and Lord of kings"
- **FROM NT (Earlier in Revelation)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]] - "ruler of the kings of the earth"
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 17#Revelation 17 . 14|Revelation 17:14]] - "Lord of lords and King of kings"

**Christological Connection**: This is the eschatological consummation of [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]]'s promise. What was sworn to David's heir—"the highest of the kings of the earth"—is visibly realized at Christ's return. The title inscribed on His robe declares to all creation that the promises to David are eternally fulfilled. The Davidic throne endures forever because Christ, the true Firstborn, reigns as King of Kings.
