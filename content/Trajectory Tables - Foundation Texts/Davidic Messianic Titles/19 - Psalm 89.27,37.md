### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]]; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 37|Psalm 89:37]]

**Hebrew/Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1001-1100#H1060|H1060]] בְּכוֹר (*bekor*) - "firstborn" → LXX: [[Lexicon/G4401-4500#G4416|G4416]] πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*)
- [[Lexicon/H5701-5800#H5707|H5707]] עֵד (*ʻēd*) - "witness" → LXX: [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3144|G3144]] μάρτυς (*martus*)
- נֶאֱמָן (*neʼĕmān*) - "faithful, reliable" → LXX: πιστός (*pistos*)
- עֶלְיוֹן (*ʻelyôn*) - "highest, most high" (v. 27)

**Context**: Psalm 89 is a covenant psalm by Ethan the Ezrahite, celebrating God's eternal promises to David (vv. 1-37) before lamenting the apparent failure of those promises (vv. 38-51). Verses 27 and 37 contain the specific titles John echoes in Revelation 1:5.

**Verse 27**: "I will indeed appoint him as My **firstborn** (בְּכוֹר), the **highest of the kings of the earth** (עֶלְיוֹן לְמַלְכֵי־אָרֶץ)."
- "Firstborn" indicates preeminence and privilege, not birth order (cf. Exodus 4:22 where Israel is God's "firstborn")
- "Highest of kings" promises universal sovereignty over all earthly rulers

**Verse 37**: "like the moon, established forever, a **faithful witness** (עֵד נֶאֱמָן) in the sky."
- The moon as "faithful witness" guarantees the permanence of the Davidic dynasty
- The throne's endurance testifies to God's covenant faithfulness

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier OT)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] - Davidic covenant foundation
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 22|Exodus 4:22]] - Israel as God's firstborn (corporate type)
- **FROM OT (Later references)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 3|Isaiah 55:3-4]] - "witness to the peoples"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]] - All three titles applied to Christ
  - [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15, 18]] - "firstborn of all creation," "firstborn from the dead"

**Christological Connection**: Jesus fulfills these titles through His death and resurrection. The "faithful witness" becomes especially poignant since μάρτυς also means "martyr"—Jesus witnessed faithfully unto death. His resurrection makes Him "firstborn from the dead" (τῶν νεκρῶν added in Rev 1:5), transforming the royal title into a resurrection title. The one who died now reigns as "ruler of the kings of the earth," vindicating God's covenant faithfulness that Psalm 89 questioned.
