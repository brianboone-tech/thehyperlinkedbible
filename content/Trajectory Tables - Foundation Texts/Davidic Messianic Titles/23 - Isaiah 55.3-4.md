### [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 3|Isaiah 55:3-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H5707|H5707]] עֵד (*'ēd*) - "witness" (v. 4)
- [[Lexicon/H5057|H5057]] נָגִיד (*nāgîd*) - "leader, commander" (v. 4)
- [[Lexicon/H2617|H2617]] חֶסֶד (*ḥesed*) - "steadfast love" (v. 3) - "the sure mercies of David"

**Context**: Isaiah 55 invites all to come to the waters of salvation. Verses 3-4 connect this invitation to the Davidic covenant, extending its blessings to all who come.

**Text**:
- v. 3: "I will make with you an everlasting covenant, my steadfast, sure love for David"
- v. 4: "Behold, I have made him a **witness to the peoples** (עֵד לְאֻמִּים), a leader and commander for the peoples"

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier OT)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] - Davidic covenant
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 37|Psalm 89:37]] - "faithful witness in the sky"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]] - "the faithful witness"
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 13#Acts 13 . 34|Acts 13:34]] - Paul quotes this verse

**Christological Connection**: Isaiah extends the "witness" concept beyond Israel to the nations. The Davidic heir will not merely rule Israel but testify to all peoples. This prophetic expansion prepares for Christ, who commands His witnesses to go "to the ends of the earth" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]).
