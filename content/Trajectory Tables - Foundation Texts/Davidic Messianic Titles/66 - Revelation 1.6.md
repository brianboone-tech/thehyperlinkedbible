### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 6|Revelation 1:6]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G932|G932]] βασιλεία (*basileia*) - "kingdom"
- [[Lexicon/G2409|G2409]] ἱερεύς (*hiereus*) - "priest"

**Context**: Immediately following the threefold messianic titles (v. 5), John declares what Christ has done for believers—made them a kingdom and priests.

**Text**: "and made us a kingdom, priests to his God and Father—to him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen."

**Connections**:
- **TO (OT Background)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]] - "a kingdom of priests"
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalm 110:4]] - Messianic priest-king
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]] - "a royal priesthood"
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 10|Revelation 5:10]] - "a kingdom and priests... they shall reign"
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 6|Revelation 20:6]] - "priests of God and of Christ"

**Christological Connection**: Because Christ is the Faithful Witness, Firstborn, and Ruler of Kings (v. 5), believers share in His royal-priestly identity (v. 6). Union with the King makes us kings; union with the Priest makes us priests. The church's calling to witness (Rev 12:11) flows from the Faithful Witness Himself.
