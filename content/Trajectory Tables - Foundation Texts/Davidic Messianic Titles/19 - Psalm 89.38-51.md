### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 38|Psalm 89:38-51]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H4886|H4886]] מָשִׁיחַ (*māšîaḥ*) - "anointed one, messiah" (v. 38, 51)
- [[Lexicon/H2617|H2617]] חֶסֶד (*ḥesed*) - "steadfast love, loving devotion" (v. 49)
- [[Lexicon/H530|H530]] אֱמוּנָה (*'ĕmûnāh*) - "faithfulness" (v. 49)

**Context**: The lament section of Psalm 89, where the psalmist contrasts God's covenant promises (vv. 1-37) with the apparent reality of covenant failure—the Davidic throne in ruins, the crown in the dust.

**The Crisis**:
- v. 38: "You have spurned and rejected... your anointed"
- v. 39: "You have renounced the covenant... defiled his crown in the dust"
- v. 44: "You have made his splendor to cease and cast his throne to the ground"
- v. 49: "Lord, where is your steadfast love of old, which by your faithfulness you swore to David?"

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier in Psalm)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27, 37]] - The promises now seemingly broken
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]] - Christ's resurrection answers this lament

**Christological Connection**: This unanswered lament creates the theological tension that Christ's resurrection resolves. The 600 years between the exile and Christ represent the "silence" between v. 51 and the NT answer. When John calls Jesus "the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead," he proclaims: God kept His word to David.
