### [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]; [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4416|G4416]] πρωτότοκος (*prōtotokos*) - "firstborn" (vv. 15, 18)
- [[Lexicon/G2937|G2937]] κτίσις (*ktisis*) - "creation" (v. 15)
- [[Lexicon/G3498|G3498]] νεκρός (*nekros*) - "dead" (v. 18)

**Context**: Paul's Christ-hymn (1:15-20) declares Christ's supremacy over all creation and His headship of the church.

**Text**:
- v. 15: "He is the image of the invisible God, the **firstborn of all creation** (πρωτότοκος πάσης κτίσεως)"
- v. 18: "He is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning, the **firstborn from the dead** (πρωτότοκος ἐκ τῶν νεκρῶν)"

**Connections**:
- **TO (OT Background)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27]] - "I will make him my firstborn"
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 22|Exodus 4:22]] - Israel as God's "firstborn"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]] - "the firstborn from the dead"

**Christological Connection**: Paul expands the "firstborn" concept in two directions:
1. **Firstborn of creation** (v. 15) - Christ holds primacy over all created things (not that He is created, but that He has the rights of the firstborn over creation)
2. **Firstborn from the dead** (v. 18) - Christ is the first to rise in glorified humanity, guaranteeing our resurrection

The Davidic "firstborn" (Ps 89:27) was limited to royal preeminence among kings; Christ's firstborn status encompasses all reality.
