### [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H2233|H2233]] זֶרַע (*zera'*) - "seed, offspring"
- [[Lexicon/H3678|H3678]] כִּסֵּא (*kissē'*) - "throne"
- [[Lexicon/H5769|H5769]] עוֹלָם (*'ôlām*) - "forever, eternal"

**Context**: Nathan's oracle to David establishing the Davidic covenant—God's unconditional promise that David's dynasty would endure forever and that one of his descendants would build a house for God's name.

**Connections**:
- **TO (Earlier OT)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]] - Scepter promise to Judah
- **FROM OT (Later references)**:
  - [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 3|Psalm 89:3-4, 27-37]] - Davidic covenant elaborated
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 7|Isaiah 9:7]] - Throne established forever
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]] - Angel applies to Jesus
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 30|Acts 2:30-31]] - Peter's Pentecost sermon

**Christological Connection**: This covenant establishes the expectation of an eternal Davidic king. The promise "your throne shall be established forever" (v. 16) transcends any merely human fulfillment—only Christ, the God-man, can reign eternally.
