### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 5#1 Peter 5 . 1|1 Peter 5:1-4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4165|G4165]] ποιμαίνω (poimaino) - to shepherd, tend, feed (imperative: ποιμάνατε)
- [[Lexicon/G701-800#G750|G750]] ἀρχιποίμην (archipoimēn) - chief shepherd (only occurrence in NT)
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4168|G4168]] ποίμνιον (poimnion) - flock (diminutive of ποίμνη)

**Context**: Peter exhorts church elders (πρεσβυτέρους) to shepherd God's flock faithfully, not as domineering lords but as examples. He promises reward when "the Chief Shepherd appears." This follows exhortation to humble submission (5:5-7) and precedes warning about the devil prowling like roaring lion (5:8-9).

**OT-to-OT Development Fulfilled**:
- Ezekiel 34:2-4 (woe to shepherds who feed themselves, not flock) → Ezekiel 34:23 (one shepherd over them) → Jeremiah 3:15 (shepherds after God's own heart) → FULFILLED in 1 Peter 5:1-4 (shepherd the flock, Chief Shepherd will appear)

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Ezekiel 34:2-10 (judgment on false shepherds); Jeremiah 3:15 (shepherds after My heart); Jeremiah 23:1-4 (woe to shepherds)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 15|John 21:15-17]] - Peter's commission: Feed My sheep
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11-13]] - Good Shepherd vs. hired hand
  - [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 20#Acts 20 . 28|Acts 20:28]] - shepherd the church of God
**Type Classification**: Application of typology to church - Christ fulfills divine Shepherd role; elders fulfill undershepherd role.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is the Chief Shepherd (ἀρχιποίμην) over all human shepherds. This title, unique in Scripture, emphasizes His supremacy: all pastors/elders are under-shepherds accountable to Him. Peter's own commission in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 15|John 21:15-17]] ("Feed My lambs... Tend My sheep... Feed My sheep") demonstrates this relationship: the sheep remain Christ's ("MY sheep"); Peter serves as delegated shepherd. The exhortations echo Ezekiel 34's critique: elders must not "lord it over" (κατακυριεύοντες) those entrusted to them (contrast Ezek 34:4: "ruled them with violence and cruelty") but shepherd "being examples" (τύποι γινόμενοι). The promise "when the Chief Shepherd appears" (φανερωθέντος τοῦ ἀρχιποίμενος) points to Christ's return—eschatological accountability paralleling [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 10|Ezekiel 34:10]] ("I will demand My flock from them"). The "unfading crown of glory" contrasts earthly reward, fulfilling Ezekiel's promise of blessing under the true Shepherd.
