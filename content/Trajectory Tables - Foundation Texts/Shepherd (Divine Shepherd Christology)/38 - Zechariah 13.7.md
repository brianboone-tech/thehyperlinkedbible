### [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 7|Zechariah 13:7]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7462|H7462]] רֹעִי (ro'i) - my shepherd
- [[Lexicon/H5900-6000#H5997|H5997]] עֲמִית (amit) - companion, associate, fellow (implies equality/intimacy)
- [[Lexicon/H5200-5300#H5221|H5221]] נָכָה (nakah) - to strike, smite, kill
- [[Lexicon/H6300-6400#H6327|H6327]] פּוּץ (puts) - to scatter, disperse

**Context**: Zechariah 13:7-9 follows the "pierced one" prophecy (12:10) and cleansing fountain (13:1). This oracle introduces suffering shepherd typology: Yahweh commands His sword to strike "My shepherd, the man who is My companion." The scattering leads to refining remnant (13:8-9).

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking Direct Type** - Explicitly predictive. Jesus' quotation (Matt 26:31) confirms this is prophecy of His passion. The unique language ("My companion") indicates original audience should recognize extraordinary claim.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is the Shepherd whom Zechariah prophesied would be struck. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 31|Matthew 26:31]] and [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 27|Mark 14:27]] record Jesus quoting this text on the night of His betrayal: "I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered." The title "My companion" (עֲמִיתִי) reveals Christ's equality with the Father—only one who shares divine nature could be called God's "companion/fellow." The striking is substitutionary: God's sword (judgment) falls on the Shepherd instead of the sheep. This connects to [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]], 15 ("I lay down My life for the sheep") and [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 4|Isaiah 53:4]], 10 ("stricken, smitten by God... it pleased Yahweh to crush Him"). [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 7|Zechariah 13:7]] thus anticipates the penal substitutionary nature of Christ's death: the Good Shepherd willingly receives the blow meant for His flock.
