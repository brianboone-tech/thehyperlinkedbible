### [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7462|H7462]] רָעָה (ra'ah) - to shepherd, pasture, feed (v. 4: "he will stand and shepherd")
- [[Lexicon/H4700-4800#H4163|H4163]] מוֹצָאוֹת (motsa'ot) - origins, going forth, source
- [[Lexicon/H6800-6900#H6924|H6924]] קֶדֶם (qedem) - ancient times, eternity past
- [[Lexicon/H7900-8000#H7965|H7965]] שָׁלוֹם (shalom) - peace, wholeness, security

**Context**: Micah prophesies during Assyrian crisis (late 8th century BC). Chapter 5 contrasts Israel's present humiliation (5:1: "they strike the judge of Israel") with future exaltation through a ruler from Bethlehem. This ruler will "shepherd" (רָעָה) in Yahweh's strength and bring universal peace (שָׁלוֹם).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 2|2 Samuel 5:2]] (David from Bethlehem shepherds Israel) → [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 11#1 Chronicles 11 . 2|1 Chronicles 11:2]] (You shall shepherd My people) → [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2-4]] (Bethlehemite ruler whose origins are from eternity, will shepherd) → [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23]] (My servant David will shepherd)
- Micah combines Davidic lineage, divine preexistence, and shepherd imagery into messianic prophecy

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking Direct Type** - Contains explicit predictive content (birthplace, eternal origins, shepherd-ruler) recognized by original audience as messianic. Matthew 2:5-6 shows chief priests and scribes understood this as predicting Messiah's birthplace.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is the Bethlehemite shepherd-ruler Micah prophesied. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 6|Matthew 2:6]] applies this text to Jesus' birth, significantly adding shepherd language: "who will shepherd My people Israel" (drawn from 2 Sam 5:2). The phrase "whose origins are from of old, from ancient times" reveals Christ's eternal preexistence—He is both David's son (human descent from Bethlehem) and David's Lord (divine origin from eternity). [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]] fulfills the shepherd role: Jesus shepherds "in the strength of Yahweh" because He IS Yahweh incarnate. [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 4|Micah 5:4]]b promises "he will be their peace (שָׁלוֹם)"—fulfilled in [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14]]: "He Himself is our peace."
