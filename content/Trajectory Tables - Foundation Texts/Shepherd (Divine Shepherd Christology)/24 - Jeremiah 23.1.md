### [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 1|Jeremiah 23:1-4]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7462|H7462]] רָעָה (ra'ah) - to shepherd (participle: רֹעִים, shepherds)
- [[Lexicon/H0001-0100#H0006|H0006]] אָבַד (avad) - to destroy, perish, scatter
- [[Lexicon/H6300-6400#H6485|H6485]] פָּקַד (paqad) - to visit, attend to, punish, muster

**Context**: Jeremiah's oracle against Judah's false shepherds (kings and leaders) who scattered the flock through injustice and idolatry. This judgment precedes the promise of a righteous Branch from David's line (23:5-6). The shepherds' failure creates crisis requiring divine intervention.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 1|Jeremiah 23:1-4]] (woe to false shepherds) → [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 1|Ezekiel 34:1-10]] (expanded indictment of shepherds) → [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 11|Ezekiel 34:11-16]] (Yahweh Himself will shepherd) → [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23]] (raise up one shepherd, My servant David)
- Prophetic critique establishes need for true Shepherd

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking Direct Type** - Explicitly predictive: "I will raise up shepherds over them" (23:4). The immediate fulfillment is post-exilic restoration, but ultimate fulfillment is Messianic (23:5-6 follows).

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is the righteous Shepherd whom Jeremiah anticipated. Where Judah's kings scattered and destroyed, Jesus gathers and saves ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11-16]]). [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 9#Matthew 9 . 36|Matthew 9:36]] and [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 6#Mark 6 . 34|Mark 6:34]] depict Jesus seeing crowds "like sheep without a shepherd"—applying Jeremiah's indictment to first-century Israel's leadership. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 12|John 10:12-13]] contrasts hired hands (false shepherds) with the Good Shepherd who lays down His life. The promise "I will raise up shepherds" (23:4) finds ultimate fulfillment in Christ, who then commissions under-shepherds ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 15|John 21:15-17]]; 1 Pet 5:1-4).
