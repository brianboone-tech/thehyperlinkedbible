### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11-18]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4166|G4166]] ποιμήν (poimen) - shepherd
- [[Lexicon/G2501-2600#G2570|G2570]] καλός (kalos) - good, beautiful, noble, model
- [[Lexicon/G5400-5500#G5590|G5590]] ψυχή (psyche) - life, soul
- [[Lexicon/G5000-5100#G5087|G5087]] τίθημι (tithemi) - to lay down, place, put

**Context**: John 10:1-21 follows healing of blind man (John 9) and Pharisees' rejection. Jesus contrasts true shepherd (enters by gate, sheep know His voice) with thieves/robbers. The "I am the good shepherd" declaration (10:11, 14) is the fourth of seven "I am" (ἐγώ εἰμι) statements in John's Gospel, claiming divine identity and fulfilling Ezekiel 34.

**OT-to-OT Development Fulfilled**:
- Genesis 48:15 (God shepherded Jacob) → Psalm 23:1 (Yahweh my shepherd) → Ezekiel 34:11-16 (I Myself will shepherd) → Ezekiel 34:23 (My servant David will shepherd) → FULFILLED in John 10:11-18 (I am the good shepherd)

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Ezekiel 34:11-24 (primary background); Psalm 23; Isaiah 40:11; Zechariah 13:7
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 15|John 21:15-17]] - Feed My sheep
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 20|Hebrews 13:20]] - great Shepherd
  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 25|1 Peter 2:25]] - Shepherd and Overseer
  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 5#1 Peter 5 . 4|1 Peter 5:4]] - Chief Shepherd
**Type Classification**: Antitype fulfilling **Forward-Looking Direct Types** (Ezek 34:11-16, 23; Zech 13:7) and **Providential Types** (Gen 48:15; 2 Sam 5:2; Psalm 23).

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ IS the Good Shepherd. He fulfills Ezekiel 34's dual promise: (1) "I Myself will shepherd" (34:11-16)—Jesus claims divine identity through "I am" formula and exercises divine prerogatives (knows each sheep, gives eternal life, protects from perishing); (2) "My servant David will shepherd" (34:23)—Jesus is David's greater Son (Matt 1:1; Rom 1:3). The unprecedented element is voluntary death: "I lay down My life for the sheep" (10:11, 15). No OT shepherd, including David, could atone for sin through death. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 7|Zechariah 13:7]]'s struck shepherd finds fulfillment—Jesus willingly receives divine judgment as substitute. The phrase "I have authority to lay it down and authority to take it up again" (10:18) reveals deity: only God has power over life and death. This passage is the hermeneutical key demonstrating how Yahweh shepherds His flock: through incarnation in David's Son.
