### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 11|Ezekiel 34:11-16]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7462|H7462]] רָעָה (ra'ah) - to shepherd, feed, pasture
- [[Lexicon/H1800-1900#H1875|H1875]] דָּרַשׁ (darash) - to seek, search for, inquire
- [[Lexicon/H1200-1300#H1239|H1239]] בָּקַר (baqar) - to seek, search out, examine
- [[Lexicon/H6300-6400#H6485|H6485]] פָּקַד (paqad) - to attend to, care for, visit

**Context**: Following comprehensive indictment of false shepherds (34:1-10), Yahweh announces dramatic reversal: "I Myself" (הִנְנִי אָנִי, hineni ani—emphatic pronoun) will assume the shepherd role. This is one of the most significant Christological prophecies in Ezekiel: God personally intervenes to shepherd His scattered flock.

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking Direct Type** - Explicitly predictive: "I will search... I will seek... I will rescue... I will gather." Original audience expected future divine intervention. Ultimate fulfillment is Messianic.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ IS Yahweh shepherding His flock in human flesh. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11-16]] claims this divine prerogative: "I am the good shepherd" (ἐγώ εἰμι—divine name formula). Jesus fulfills each action: searches for lost ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 15#Luke 15 . 4|Luke 15:4-7]]); rescues from sin and death (Matt 1:21); gathers scattered sheep ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 52|John 11:52]]); feeds with living bread ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 35|John 6:35]]); binds up broken (Isa 61:1; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 18|Luke 4:18]]). The stunning move from [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 11|Ezekiel 34:11-16]] (Yahweh shepherds) to 34:23 (servant David shepherds) can only be resolved christologically: the Davidic shepherd IS Yahweh incarnate. This is the mystery of the Incarnation prefigured.
