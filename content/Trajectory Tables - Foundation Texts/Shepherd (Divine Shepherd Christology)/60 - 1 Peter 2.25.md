### [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 25|1 Peter 2:25]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4166|G4166]] ποιμήν (poimen) - shepherd
- [[Lexicon/G1901-2000#G1985|G1985]] ἐπίσκοπος (episkopos) - overseer, guardian, bishop
- [[Lexicon/G5400-5500#G5590|G5590]] ψυχή (psyche) - soul, life

**Context**: 1 Peter 2:21-25 follows Christ's suffering as example for slaves enduring unjust treatment. Peter quotes Isaiah 53 (suffering servant) extensively, then applies shepherd imagery: "You were like sheep going astray, but now you have returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls."

**OT-to-OT Development Fulfilled**:
- Isaiah 53:6 (all like sheep gone astray, each to his own way) → Ezekiel 34:5-6 (sheep scattered, went astray) → Ezekiel 34:11-12, 16 (I will seek the lost, bring back strays) → FULFILLED in 1 Peter 2:25 (returned to Shepherd)

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Isaiah 53:4-6 (wounded for transgressions, all like sheep gone astray); Ezekiel 34:11-12 (I will seek scattered sheep); Psalm 23:3 (He restores my soul)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11-16]] - Good Shepherd
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 15#Luke 15 . 4|Luke 15:4-7]] - seeking lost sheep
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 20|Hebrews 13:20]] - great Shepherd
  - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 5#1 Peter 5 . 4|1 Peter 5:4]] - Chief Shepherd
**Type Classification**: Antitype fulfilling **Forward-Looking Direct Type** (Ezek 34:11-12, 16: "I will seek the lost, bring back the strays").

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is the Shepherd and Overseer of souls. The dual title combines Ezekiel 34's promise (Yahweh will seek scattered sheep) with pastoral oversight. Where [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 6|Isaiah 53:6]] describes universal straying ("all like sheep"), Peter declares believers have "returned" (converted) to the Shepherd—applying [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 11|Ezekiel 34:11-12]]'s promise ("I will search... seek them out") to individual salvation. The title ἐπίσκοπος (Overseer) escalates beyond physical shepherding to spiritual care: He oversees "your souls," ensuring eternal well-being. This fulfills [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 23#Psalm 23 . 3|Psalm 23:3]] ("He restores my soul") at deeper level. Peter's own commission in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 15|John 21:15-17]] ("Feed My sheep") derives from this Christology: Jesus remains the Chief Shepherd (1 Pet 5:4); elders are under-shepherds caring for "God's flock" (5:2) under Christ's oversight.
