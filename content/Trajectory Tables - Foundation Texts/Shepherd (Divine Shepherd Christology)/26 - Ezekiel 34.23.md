### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23-24]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7462|H7462]] רָעָה (ra'ah) - to shepherd, feed, tend
- [[Lexicon/H0200-0300#H0259|H0259]] אֶחָד (echad) - one (emphatic singularity)
- [[Lexicon/H5600-5700#H5650|H5650]] עֶבֶד (eved) - servant, slave
- [[Lexicon/H5300-5400#H5387|H5387]] נָשִׂיא (nasi) - prince, leader, chief

**Context**: This verse is the Christological crescendo of Ezekiel 34. Immediately after promising "I Myself will shepherd My flock" (34:11-16), God announces: "I will set up over them one shepherd, My servant David." This juxtaposition creates the central mystery: How can Yahweh shepherd (34:11-16) AND servant David shepherd (34:23)? The only resolution is incarnational: the Davidic shepherd IS Yahweh.

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking Direct Type** - Explicitly predictive with clear messianic content. Original audience expected future Davidic ruler; NT reveals this is Yahweh incarnate.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ IS "My servant David" whom Ezekiel prophesied. He fulfills both the divine shepherd promise (34:11-16: "I Myself") and the Davidic shepherd promise (34:23: "My servant David"). [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]] claims the divine shepherd role: "I am the good shepherd." [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 16|John 10:16]] fulfills "one shepherd": "there will be one flock, one shepherd." The dual title "My servant David" fits Jesus perfectly: He is the Servant who came "not to be served but to serve" ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 45|Mark 10:45]]) AND David's greater Son (Matt 22:41-46). This text anticipated the mystery Paul articulates in [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 16|1 Timothy 3:16]]: "God was manifested in the flesh"—Yahweh shepherding through incarnate Davidic King.
