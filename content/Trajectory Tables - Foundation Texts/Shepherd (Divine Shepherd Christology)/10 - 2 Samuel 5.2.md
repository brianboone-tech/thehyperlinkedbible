### [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 2|2 Samuel 5:2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7401-7500#H7462|H7462]] רָעָה (ra'ah) - to shepherd, pasture, tend
- [[Lexicon/H5000-5100#H5057|H5057]] נָגִיד (nagid) - leader, ruler, prince

**Context**: The elders of Israel come to David at Hebron to anoint him king over all Israel (not just Judah). They recount Yahweh's word to David: "You shall shepherd My people Israel, and you shall be ruler over Israel." This marks the transition from Saul's failed kingship to the Davidic covenant established in 2 Samuel 7.

**Type Classification**: **Forward-Looking Providential Type** - God sovereignly established David as shepherd-king to prefigure the Messiah. While not explicitly predictive in this verse, the Davidic covenant (2 Sam 7) makes it forward-pointing. Later prophets (Ezek 34:23; Mic 5:2-4) develop this into clear messianic expectation.

**Christological Connection**: David's shepherding over Israel is fulfilled in Jesus Christ, the Son of David. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 6|Matthew 2:6]] explicitly applies shepherd imagery to Jesus, combining [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]] with this text. Where David shepherded with "integrity of heart" but failed (Bathsheba, census), Jesus shepherds perfectly. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]] escalates the type: the Good Shepherd not only protects but lays down His life for the sheep—something no human king, including David, could do. Jesus is the eternal Shepherd-King whom David's reign typologically anticipated.
