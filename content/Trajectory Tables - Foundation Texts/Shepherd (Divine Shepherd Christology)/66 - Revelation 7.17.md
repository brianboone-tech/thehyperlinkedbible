### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 17|Revelation 7:17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G0701-0800#G0721|G0721]] ἀρνίον (arnion) - Lamb (diminutive, used 29x in Revelation for Christ)
- [[Lexicon/G4101-4200#G4165|G4165]] ποιμαίνω (poimaino) - to shepherd, tend, feed
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4077|G4077]] πηγή (pege) - spring, fountain, source
- [[Lexicon/G2101-2200#G2198|G2198]] ζάω (zao) - to live (participle: ζώντων, living)

**Context**: Revelation 7:9-17 depicts the innumerable multitude from every nation before the throne, clothed in white robes, worshiping the Lamb. An elder explains these are those who came out of great tribulation, washed their robes in the Lamb's blood (7:14). Verse 17 describes their eternal provision by the Lamb.

**OT-to-OT Development Fulfilled**:
- Genesis 48:15 (God shepherded Jacob) → Psalm 23:1-2 (Yahweh my shepherd, leads to still waters) → Ezekiel 34:14-15 (feed in good pasture, by streams of water, make them lie down) → CONSUMMATED in Revelation 7:17 (Lamb will shepherd, guide to springs of living water)

**Connections**:
- **TO OT**: Psalm 23:1-2 (shepherd, still waters); Isaiah 49:10 (neither hunger nor thirst, springs of water); Ezekiel 34:14-15 (feed in good pasture, lie down in good grazing land); Ezekiel 47:1-12 (river from temple bringing life)
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 10|John 4:10-14]] - living water welling up to eternal life
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - rivers of living water = Holy Spirit
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]] - Good Shepherd
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 6|Revelation 21:6]] - ; 22:1 (river of water of life
**Type Classification**: Eschatological antitype consummating all preceding shepherd types from Genesis 48:15 through 1 Peter 5:4.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ, the Lamb who was slain, eternally shepherds the redeemed multitude. This paradoxical image brings shepherd typology to glorious consummation: the Shepherd who laid down His life as the Lamb ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11]], 15) now shepherds forever. Fairbairn's principle applies: "The far distant ends of revelation embrace each other"—from [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 48#Genesis 48 . 15|Genesis 48:15]] (God shepherded Jacob) to [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 17|Revelation 7:17]] (Lamb shepherds eternally). The "springs of living water" (πηγὰς ὑδάτων ζώντων) fulfill [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 23#Psalm 23 . 2|Psalm 23:2]] ("still waters"), [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 14|Ezekiel 34:14]] ("feed by streams of water"), and Jesus' own promise in [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 14|John 4:14]] ("spring of water welling up to eternal life") and 7:37-38 ("rivers of living water"). The phrase "God will wipe away every tear" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]]) shows complete reversal of shepherd typology's crisis stage: where false shepherds scattered and oppressed (Ezek 34:1-10), the Lamb-Shepherd brings eternal comfort, provision, and presence. This is the telos (goal) toward which all shepherd imagery pointed from the beginning.
