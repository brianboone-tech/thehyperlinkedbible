### [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 2|Hebrews 5:1-3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G1-100#G50|G50]] ἀγνοέω (agnoeō) - to be ignorant, not know
- [[Lexicon/G3101-3200#G3356|G3356]] μετριοπαθέω (metriopatheō) - to deal gently, have compassion
- [[Lexicon/G4001-4100#G4105|G4105]] πλανάω (planaō) - to lead astray, cause to wander
- [[Lexicon/G701-800#G769|G769]] ἀσθένεια (astheneia) - weakness, infirmity

**Context**: Hebrews describes the qualifications of a high priest: appointed by God, able to sympathize with human weakness, and offering sacrifices for sins—both his own and the people's. The phrase "have compassion on the ignorant" (μετριοπαθεῖν τοῖς ἀγνοοῦσιν) directly echoes the Levitical provision for sins of ignorance.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 2|Leviticus 4:2]] - Sin offering for sins "through ignorance" (LXX: ἀκουσίως)
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 16|Leviticus 16:16]] - Atonement for "uncleannesses" and "transgressions"
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 27|Numbers 15:27-28]] - Individual who sins "through ignorance" brings a sin offering

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 2|Leviticus 4:2]] - Levitical provision for ignorant sinners
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 15|Hebrews 4:15]] - Christ "in every respect tempted as we are, yet without sin"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 13|1 Timothy 1:13]] - Paul received mercy "because I did it ignorantly"

**Type Classification**: **Direct/Fulfilled** - The Levitical high priest's compassion for ignorant sinners is perfected in Christ

**Christological Connection**: **CRITICAL NT CONNECTION** - This text explicitly connects Leviticus 4's "sin of ignorance" to Christ's priestly ministry. Key insights:

1. **Vocabulary Shift**: The LXX translates שְׁגָגָה as ἀκουσίως ("unwillingly"), but Hebrews uses the ἀγνοέω word group ("ignorance")—emphasizing the cognitive dimension
2. **Priestly Sympathy**: Human high priests could sympathize because they shared the weakness; Christ sympathizes despite His sinlessness because He "learned obedience through suffering" (5:8)
3. **Dual Object**: Compassion extends to "the ignorant" (τοῖς ἀγνοοῦσιν) and those "going astray" (πλανωμένοις)—sins of confusion and sins of wandering

Christ's priesthood specializes in mercy to those who sin without realizing it—which is all of us, far more than we know.
