### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 2|Leviticus 4:1-2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7684|H7684]] שְׁגָגָה (shegagah) - error, inadvertence, sin of ignorance
- [[Lexicon/H7601-7700#H7683|H7683]] שָׁגָה (shagah) - to err, go astray, wander
- [[Lexicon/H2398-2500#H2398|H2398]] חָטָא (chata) - to sin, miss the mark
- [[Lexicon/H5301-5400#H5315|H5315]] נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh) - soul, person, life

**Context**: Leviticus 4 introduces the sin offering for sins committed "through ignorance" (בִשְׁגָגָה, bishgagah). The phrase addresses sins committed when the sinner did not realize the action was sinful. The law recognizes that sin can exist without conscious awareness—a terrifying truth met with gracious provision.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 4#Leviticus 4 . 13|Leviticus 4:13]] - Same provision for the congregation sinning ignorantly
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 15|Leviticus 5:15]] - Extended to trespasses in holy things through ignorance
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 27|Numbers 15:27-29]] - Confirmed for all Israel, native and sojourner

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 2|Hebrews 5:2]] - High priest "can have compassion on the ignorant"
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 19#Psalm 19 . 12|Psalm 19:12]] - "Cleanse me from secret faults"
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 13|1 Timothy 1:13]] - Paul received mercy "because I did it ignorantly"

**Type Classification**: **Direct** - Divinely instituted category of sin with specific sacrificial provision

**Christological Connection**: The provision for sins of ignorance reveals two critical truths: (1) Sin is not merely conscious rebellion but any failure to conform to God's will, whether known or unknown; (2) God's mercy extends even to sins we cannot identify. This anticipates Christ's priesthood which specializes in compassion for "the ignorant" (Hebrews 5:2). The category is terrifying (my sins exceed my awareness!) but the provision is comforting (God has already made provision for what I cannot confess).
