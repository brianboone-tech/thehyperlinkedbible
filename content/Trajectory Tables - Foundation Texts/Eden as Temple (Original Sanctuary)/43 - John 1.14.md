### [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- λόγος (*logos*) - "Word" - divine reason/revelation personified
- ἐσκήνωσεν (*eskēnōsen*) - "tabernacled, dwelt in tent" - from σκηνή (tent/tabernacle)
- δόξαν (*doxan*) - "glory" - Shekinah presence visible in Christ

**Context**: Prologue of John's Gospel; theological foundation for entire narrative. Follows creation language (1:1-5, echoing Genesis 1); precedes John Baptist's testimony (1:19ff).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]] - glory filled tabernacle; Moses could not enter
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - glory filled temple
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] - Immanuel, "God with us"
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 60#Isaiah 60 . 1|Isaiah 60:1-2]] - glory of LORD risen upon Zion
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 19|Colossians 1:19]] - fullness of God dwelt in Christ
  - [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 9|Colossians 2:9]] - fullness of deity dwells bodily
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 6|2 Corinthians 4:6]] - glory of God in face of Jesus Christ
**Christological Connection**: Christ **is** the true temple. Where Eden had God walking in garden, where tabernacle had glory-cloud filling tent, where Solomon's temple had Shekinah glory, Christ embodies all these realities perfectly. The Word who spoke creation into existence ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 3|John 1:3]]) now tabernacles in creation. This is culmination of temple trajectory - God's presence not external (pillar), not localized (temple), but incarnate in Jesus, anticipating indwelling in believers ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 23|John 14:23]]) and eternal dwelling in New Jerusalem.
