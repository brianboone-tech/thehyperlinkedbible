### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-17]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- עֵדֶן (*eden*) - "delight, pleasure" - suggests sanctuary/sacred space
- עָבַד (*avad*) - "to work, serve" - priestly service language (cf. Numbers 3:7-8)
- שָׁמַר (*shamar*) - "to keep, guard" - priestly guarding language (cf. Numbers 3:7-8)

**Context**: Creation narrative; God plants garden in Eden, places Adam there with commission to work/keep it. Contains tree of life at center, rivers flowing out, gold and precious stones.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]] - image-bearing mandate to fill earth
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]] - tabernacle as Eden replica
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 13|Ezekiel 28:13-14]] - Eden as holy mountain
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word tabernacled among us); Revelation 21-22 (New Jerusalem as restored Eden
**Christological Connection**: Christ is the true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]) who restores what Adam lost. As last Adam in true garden-sanctuary, He succeeds where first Adam failed, opening way for redeemed humanity to dwell with God in restored cosmic Eden (New Jerusalem). The commission to "work and keep" the garden-sanctuary finds fulfillment in Great Commission to expand God's presence to earth's ends.
