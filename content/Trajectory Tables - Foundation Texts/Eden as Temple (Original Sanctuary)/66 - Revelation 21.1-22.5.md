### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-22:5]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- καινὸν (*kainon*) - "new" - qualitatively new, not merely recent
- σκηνὴ τοῦ θεοῦ (*skēnē tou theou*) - "tabernacle/dwelling of God"
- ναὸν οὐκ εἶδον (*naon ouk eidon*) - "I saw no temple" - temple and city merged

**Context**: Climax of Revelation (AD 90s); follows final judgment (20:11-15); presents consummated new creation as eternal state.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-17]] - Eden
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|Genesis 3:22-24]] - tree of life guarded
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]] - glory filled tabernacle
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - glory filled temple
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]] - "I create new heavens and new earth"
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]] - river from temple, trees for healing
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]] - living waters from Jerusalem
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Word tabernacled among us
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]] - church as temple
  - [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]] - dwelling place for God
**Christological Connection**: Christ is "Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end" (21:6) - creation and new creation bookends. **Ultimate escalation of temple trajectory**: From localized garden → portable tabernacle → fixed temple → incarnate Christ → indwelling Spirit in believers → **cosmic sanctuary where throne of God and Lamb is at center (22:3), river flows from throne (22:1), servants see His face (22:4), reign forever (22:5)**. Adamic commission consummated: earth filled not just with humans but with glorified humanity dwelling in unbroken fellowship with triune God. Eden's purpose - God with man - achieved eternally. "The dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God" (21:3) - climax of all Scripture.
