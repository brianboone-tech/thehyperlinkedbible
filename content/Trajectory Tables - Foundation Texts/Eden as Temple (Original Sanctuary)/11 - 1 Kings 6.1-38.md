### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 6#1 Kings 6 . 1|1 Kings 6:1-38]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- בַּיִת (*bayit*) - "house" - temple as God's dwelling
- דְּבִיר (*devir*) - "inner sanctuary, Holy of Holies"
- כְּרוּבִים (*keruvim*) - "cherubim" - guardians recalling Eden (Genesis 3:24)

**Context**: Solomon builds permanent temple in Jerusalem (c. 966 BC), 480 years after exodus. Fulfills David's desire (2 Samuel 7:1-17) though God chose Solomon to build (1 Chronicles 22:9-10).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-17]] - Eden as original sanctuary
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]] - tabernacle pattern
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 3#2 Chronicles 3 . 1|2 Chronicles 3:1]] - built on Mount Moriah where Abraham offered Isaac); Ezekiel 10-11 (glory departs from this temple
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 6|Matthew 12:6]] - one greater than temple
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]] - Christ's body as true temple
**Christological Connection**: Solomon's temple, though magnificent, was destroyed due to Israel's sin. Christ is the true temple that cannot be destroyed - risen from death, He is eternal dwelling place of God. Believers corporately form living temple ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 19|Ephesians 2:19-22]]) being built on Christ the cornerstone, anticipating New Jerusalem where temple and city merge into one cosmic sanctuary.
