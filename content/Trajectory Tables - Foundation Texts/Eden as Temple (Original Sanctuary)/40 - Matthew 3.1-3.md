### [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 1|Matthew 3:1-3]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ἐρήμῳ (*erēmō*) - "wilderness, desert" - place of chaos, anti-Eden
- ἑτοιμάσατε (*hetoimasate*) - "prepare" - make ready for Lord's coming
- κύριου (*kyriou*) - "Lord" - divine presence returning

**Context**: John Baptist's ministry (c. AD 27-29) in Judean wilderness; precedes Jesus's public ministry. Quotes Isaiah 40:3 - restoration prophecy announcing new exodus.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 3|Isaiah 40:3-5]] - voice crying in wilderness; prepare way of LORD; glory revealed
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1]] - messenger preparing way
  - [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 4#Malachi 4 . 5|Malachi 4:5-6]] - Elijah before day of LORD
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 23|John 1:23]] - John identifies himself with [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 3|Isaiah 40:3]]
  - [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 10|Matthew 11:10]] - Jesus identifies John as prophesied messenger
**Christological Connection**: John prepares way for Christ who is both greater temple-builder and temple Himself. John witnesses Spirit descend on Jesus like dove ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 16|Matthew 3:16]]) - evoking [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] (Spirit hovering over waters) and inaugurating new creation. Where John baptizes with water in wilderness, Jesus baptizes with Holy Spirit (3:11), transforming hearts into living temples, beginning global Eden-expansion through gospel.
