### [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מִקְדָּשׁ (*miqdash*) - "sanctuary, holy place"
- שָׁכַן (*shakan*) - "to dwell, tabernacle" - root of Shekinah
- תַּבְנִית (*tavnit*) - "pattern, model" - heavenly archetype

**Context**: God commands Moses to build tabernacle according to pattern shown on Mount Sinai. Immediately follows covenant ratification (Exodus 24); precedes detailed tabernacle instructions (Exodus 25-31).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 8|Genesis 2:8-17]] - Eden as original sanctuary
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 6#1 Kings 6 . 1|1 Kings 6:1-38]] - Solomon's temple follows same pattern); Ezekiel 40-48 (eschatological temple vision
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 5|Hebrews 8:5]] - earthly sanctuary as copy/shadow of heavenly
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - ultimate dwelling of God with man
**Christological Connection**: Tabernacle foreshadows Christ in whom "all the fullness of God was pleased to dwell" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 19|Colossians 1:19]]). [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] uses tabernacle language: Word "tabernacled" (ἐσκήνωσεν) among us. What was localized in tent becomes incarnate in Person, then indwelling in believers ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]), finally cosmic in New Jerusalem where God dwells with humanity forever.
