### [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 40#Ezekiel 40 . 1|Ezekiel 40:1-48:35]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- מַיִם (*mayim*) - "waters" - river flowing from temple (47:1-12)
- עֵץ (*ets*) - "tree" - trees bearing fruit monthly with healing leaves (47:12)
- יְהוָה שָׁמָּה (*YHWH shammah*) - "The LORD Is There" - city's name (48:35)

**Context**: Ezekiel's vision (573 BC, during Babylonian exile) of eschatological temple and restored land. Follows vision of glory departing (Ezekiel 10-11); anticipates return of glory (43:1-5).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10-14]] - Eden's river); 1 Kings 6-8 (Solomon's temple
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 8|Zechariah 14:8]] - living waters from Jerusalem
  - [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 18|Joel 3:18]] - fountain from house of LORD
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 37|John 7:37-39]] - living water from Christ
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]] - river of life from throne
**Christological Connection**: Ezekiel's vision finds fulfillment not in rebuilt second temple (which fell short) but in Christ and New Jerusalem. Christ is living water source ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]]); the church is living temple being built ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]]); New Jerusalem descends as cosmic temple-city where "The LORD Is There" becomes "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]). Eden expanded to cosmic scale.
