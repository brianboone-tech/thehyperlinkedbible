### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16-17]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ναὸς θεοῦ (*naos theou*) - "temple of God" - inner sanctuary, Holy of Holies
- πνεῦμα τοῦ θεοῦ (*pneuma tou theou*) - "Spirit of God" - divine presence indwelling
- ἅγιος (*hagios*) - "holy" - set apart for God's presence

**Context**: Paul's letter to Corinthian church (c. AD 55) addressing divisions (1:10-4:21). This verse concludes section on church as God's building/field (3:5-17).

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]] - God dwelling in sanctuary among Israel
  - [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]] - glory filled temple
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] - "I will make my dwelling among you"
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] - "My dwelling place shall be with them"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]] - individual believer as temple
  - [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]] - church as dwelling place of God
  - [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]] - God's dwelling with man consummated
**Christological Connection**: Christ's body is true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 21|John 2:21]]); believers united to Christ corporately form His body, thus becoming living temple. **Escalation from OT**: Eden = one location; tabernacle = portable but singular; Solomon's temple = fixed in Jerusalem; **church = multiplying temples globally**. Adam's commission to expand Eden now fulfilled as gospel creates sacred communities worldwide. Anticipates New Jerusalem where entire city is temple and "dwelling place of God is with man" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]).
