### [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19-20]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- ναὸς τοῦ ἁγίου πνεύματος (*naos tou hagiou pneumatos*) - "temple of the Holy Spirit"
- σῶμα (*sōma*) - "body" - physical body as sacred space
- ἐδοξάσατε (*edoxasate*) - "glorify" - reflect God's glory through bodily holiness

**Context**: Paul addresses sexual immorality in Corinth (6:12-20). Follows discussion of lawsuits (6:1-11); application of gospel to sexual ethics.

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|Genesis 2:15]] - Adam to work/keep Eden-temple
  - [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 2|Leviticus 19:2]] - "You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy"
- **FROM OT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 45|Exodus 29:45-46]] - God dwelling among Israel in sanctuary
  - [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 27|Ezekiel 36:27]] - "I will put my Spirit within you"
- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1]] - present bodies as living sacrifice
  - [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16-18]] - catena of temple-presence promises
**Christological Connection**: Believers' bodies are temples because Christ purchased them (6:20), Spirit indwells them (6:19), and they are members of Christ's body (6:15). **Escalation**: Eden = God walked externally; tabernacle/temple = God dwelt in building; Christ = God incarnate in one body; **Pentecost = God indwells every believer's body**. Adam's priestly vocation to "work and keep" sanctuary now belongs to every Christian - guarding heart/body as temple. Ultimate consummation in resurrection when mortal bodies transformed to glorious bodies ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 42|1 Corinthians 15:42-44]], [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 21|Philippians 3:21]]), fully manifesting God's glory.
