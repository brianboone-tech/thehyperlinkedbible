### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H101-200#H120|H120]] אָדָם (adam) - mankind, humanity
- [[Lexicon/H6701-6800#H6754|H6754]] צֶלֶם (tselem) - image, likeness
- [[Lexicon/H7201-7300#H7287|H7287]] רָדָה (radah) - to rule, have dominion

**Context**: The climax of God's creative work on the sixth day. After creating the animal kingdom, God creates humanity in His own image and likeness, commissioning them to exercise dominion over the earth as His vice-regents. This is the foundational text for understanding humanity's original design and calling.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 4|Psalm 8:4-6]] reflects on this creation account, asking 'What is man... son of man?' and celebrating humanity's exalted position despite apparent insignificance
- The dominion mandate is renewed after the Flood ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1-7]]), though now with bloodshed and fear introduced
- The image of God concept recurs throughout Scripture as the basis for human dignity and sanctity of life ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 6|Genesis 9:6]]; [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 3#James 3 . 9|James 3:9]])

**Connections**:

**Type Classification**: Providential type - humanity's creation and dominion calling prefigures Christ as the true image-bearer and the last Adam who perfectly exercises dominion over all creation.

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the ultimate fulfillment of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]. He is the perfect image of God ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]; [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]), the 'last Adam' who succeeds where the first Adam failed ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 45|1 Corinthians 15:45]]). While the first Adam bore God's image and received dominion, he fell through disobedience, losing both. Christ, the second man from heaven, perfectly bears God's image and exercises righteous dominion, receiving 'all authority in heaven and on earth' ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18]]). Through His resurrection, Christ restores the dominion mandate: all things will be subjected under His feet ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 27|1 Corinthians 15:27]], quoting [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 6|Psalm 8:6]]). Believers, incorporated into Christ, are being renewed in the image of God ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 10|Colossians 3:10]]; [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 24|Ephesians 4:24]]) and will share in Christ's reign ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 10|Revelation 5:10]]; 22:5), fulfilling humanity's original calling to exercise dominion as God's image-bearers.
