### [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 17|Psalm 80:17]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H1101-1200#H1121|H121]] בֵּן (ben) - son
- [[Lexicon/H101-200#H120|H120]] אָדָם (adam) - man, mankind
- [[Lexicon/H553-600#H553|H553]] אָמַץ (amats) - to strengthen, make strong, fortify
- [[Lexicon/H3001-3100#H3225|H3225]] יָמִין (yamin) - right hand

**Context**: Psalm 80 is a national lament over Israel's devastation, using the metaphor of a vine brought from Egypt (Exodus), planted and flourishing (Conquest and Kingdom), but now broken down and ravaged (Exile or Assyrian destruction). The psalm repeatedly pleads 'restore us, O God' (vv. 3, 7, 19). Verse 17 prays for God's hand to rest upon 'the man of your right hand, the son of man you made strong for yourself.'

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Develops Psalm 8's 'son of man' theme from general humanity to a specific representative figure
- 'Man of your right hand' becomes messianic imagery: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] ('Sit at my right hand'), [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 64|Matthew 26:64]] (Jesus claims this position)
- The vine metaphor connects to later prophetic usage: Isaiah 5 (song of the vineyard), [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]], Ezekiel 15, 17, 19 (Israel as unfaithful vine)

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking type - the psalm explicitly looks forward to a future deliverer. The 'son of man whom you made strong' points beyond any historical king to the ultimate Son of Man who sits at God's right hand and exercises divine authority to restore God's people.

**Christological Connection**: Jesus fulfills [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 17|Psalm 80:17]] as the 'man of God's right hand' and 'son of man made strong.' At His trial, Jesus combines [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] (seated at right hand) with [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (Son of Man coming on clouds), claiming the very position [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 17|Psalm 80:17]] anticipates ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]]). Stephen's vision confirms this: he sees 'the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God' ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 56|Acts 7:56]]). While Israel as the vine failed and was judged ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 6|John 15:6]] alludes to Ezekiel's vine burned with fire), Jesus declares 'I am the true vine' ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1]]), fulfilling what Israel could not. He is the strengthened Son of Man who restores God's people, the Representative who succeeds where corporate Israel failed. Believers become branches in Him, sharing in His life and fruitfulness. The restoration Psalm 80 pleads for comes through Christ, the man of God's right hand, who exercises divine authority to deliver His people from devastation and bring them into the kingdom.
