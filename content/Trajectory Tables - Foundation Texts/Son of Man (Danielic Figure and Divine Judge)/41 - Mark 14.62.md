**Context**: Jesus' trial before the Sanhedrin. The high priest asks under oath: 'Are You the Christ, the Son of the Blessed One?' (v. 61). Jesus answers with ego eimi ('I am') and adds: 'you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power and coming with the clouds of heaven' (v. 62). The high priest tears his robes, declaring blasphemy (v. 63), and the council condemns Jesus to death (v. 64).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Combines [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] ('The LORD said to my Lord: "Sit at my right hand"') with [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] ('one like a son of man... coming with clouds')
- 'I am' (ego eimi) echoes YHWH's self-revelation in [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 14|Exodus 3:14]] (LXX: ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν)
- 'Power' (dynamis) is a Jewish circumlocution for God (avoiding direct use of divine name)

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (predicting vindication and return) and backward-looking (interpreting Psalm 110:1 and Daniel 7:13 as referring to Himself). Jesus reads OT typologically: the Davidic king and Danielic Son of Man converge in Him.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]] is the climax of Jesus' Son of Man claims. Throughout His ministry, He used this title strategically—affirming humanity while hinting at transcendence. Now, under oath, He explicitly identifies Himself as the Danielic Son of Man who will sit at God's right hand and return on clouds. This combines three staggering claims: (1) 'I am'—the divine name; (2) 'seated at the right hand of Power'—sharing God's throne ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]); (3) 'coming with clouds'—divine prerogative reserved for YHWH ([[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]]). The high priest's response confirms Jesus claimed deity: tearing robes was the prescribed response to blasphemy (m. Sanhedrin 7:5). What appeared as Jesus' defeat (condemnation, crucifixion) is reframed as prelude to vindication. The judges will 'see' (witness) Jesus exalted—fulfilled immediately through resurrection, partially through apostolic testimony ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 56|Acts 7:56]]), and ultimately at the Second Coming ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 7|Revelation 1:7]] 'every eye will see him, even those who pierced him'). Stephen's vision validates Jesus' claim: he sees 'the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God' ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 56|Acts 7:56]]). The ascension narratives ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 51|Luke 24:51]]; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 9|Acts 1:9-11]]) and epistolary affirmations ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]; 8:1; 10:12) confirm Jesus is enthroned at God's right hand, exercising the authority [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 14|Daniel 7:14]] promised: everlasting dominion over all nations.
