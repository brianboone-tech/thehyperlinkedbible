### [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 8#Mark 8 . 31|Mark 8:31]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G5201-5300#G5207|G5207]] υἱός (huios) - son
- [[Lexicon/G401-500#G444|G444]] ἄνθρωπος (anthropos) - man, human
- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1163|G1163]] δεῖ (dei) - it is necessary, must
- [[Lexicon/G3801-3900#G3958|G3958]] πάσχω (pascho) - to suffer, experience
- [[Lexicon/G593-600#G593|G593]] ἀποδοκιμάζω (apodokimazo) - to reject, repudiate after examination

**Context**: This is the first passion prediction, immediately following Peter's confession 'You are the Christ' (v. 29). Jesus warns them not to tell anyone (v. 30), then begins teaching that the Son of Man must suffer, be rejected by Jewish leaders, be killed, and rise after three days (v. 31). Peter rebukes Jesus for this (v. 32), and Jesus rebukes Peter as satanic (v. 33). This is a watershed moment in Mark: Jesus redefines messiahship through suffering.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Combines [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (Son of Man receiving glory) with Isaiah 53 (Suffering Servant rejected and killed)
- 'Rejected' (apodokimazo) echoes [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]] ('stone the builders rejected'), later applied to Jesus ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 12#Mark 12 . 10|Mark 12:10]]; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 11|Acts 4:11]]; [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 7|1 Peter 2:7]])
- The 'must' (dei) indicates divine necessity rooted in OT Scripture ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 26|Luke 24:26-27]], 44-47)

**Type Classification**: Forward-looking (predicting Jesus' own death and resurrection) and backward-looking (interpreting OT Suffering Servant as the Danielic Son of Man). This is hermeneutical innovation grounded in OT types.

**Christological Connection**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 8#Mark 8 . 31|Mark 8:31]] is the hinge of Jesus' ministry—He redefines the Son of Man not as bypassing suffering but embracing it. The glorious Danielic figure must first be the despised and rejected Suffering Servant. This is not plan B after a failed plan A; it is God's eternal plan ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 23|Acts 2:23]] 'delivered up according to the definite plan and foreknowledge of God'). Jesus teaches that suffering is the necessary pathway to glory: 'Was it not necessary that the Christ should suffer these things and enter into his glory?' ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 26|Luke 24:26]]). The elders, chief priests, and scribes who reject Him fulfill Scripture ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalm 118:22]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 3|Isaiah 53:3]]). The three-day resurrection echoes [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 6#Hosea 6 . 2|Hosea 6:2]] and Jesus' sign of Jonah ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 40|Matthew 12:40]]). [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 45|Mark 10:45]] expands this: 'The Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many,' directly quoting [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 11|Isaiah 53:11-12]]. The Danielic Son of Man who receives universal dominion ([[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 14|Daniel 7:14]]) does so by first becoming the sin-bearing servant who is 'numbered with the transgressors' ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]]; [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 15#Mark 15 . 28|Mark 15:28]]). This fusion of Daniel 7 and Isaiah 53 is Jesus' unique contribution to biblical theology, revealing that the pathway to the throne is through the cross.
