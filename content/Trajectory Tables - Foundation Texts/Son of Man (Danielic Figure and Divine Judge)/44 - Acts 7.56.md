### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 56|Acts 7:56]]

- [[Lexicon/G1101-1200#G1188|G1188]] δεξιός (dexios) - right hand

**Context**: Stephen's martyrdom. After delivering a lengthy sermon tracing Israel's history of rejecting God's messengers (Acts 7:2-53), Stephen is stoned by an enraged Sanhedrin. Before his death, filled with the Holy Spirit, he gazes into heaven and sees 'the glory of God, and Jesus standing at the right hand of God' (v. 55), then declares: 'Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of Man standing at the right hand of God' (v. 56). This is the only non-Gospel use of 'Son of Man' in the NT.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Echoes [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (Son of Man approaching Ancient of Days) and [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] (sit at my right hand)
- 'Heavens opened' recalls [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 1#Ezekiel 1 . 1|Ezekiel 1:1]] (prophet sees God's throne); [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 64#Isaiah 64 . 1|Isaiah 64:1]] (plea for God to rend heavens); [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 16|Matthew 3:16]] (Jesus' baptism)
- The standing posture (vs. sitting) may echo [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|Daniel 12:1]] (Michael 'shall stand up') or indicate active advocacy

**Type Classification**: Backward-looking (validates Jesus' claim at trial—Mark 14:62) and forward-looking (points to Christ's ongoing heavenly ministry and final return). Stephen's vision is historical confirmation and eschatological preview.

**Christological Connection**: Stephen's vision is the first post-ascension confirmation that Jesus is the Danielic Son of Man enthroned at God's right hand. At Jesus' trial ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 62|Mark 14:62]]), He told the Sanhedrin 'you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power.' Now, the same council stoning Stephen receives that very witness through Stephen's testimony. The standing posture (rather than seated) is theologically significant. Some interpret it as Jesus rising to welcome Stephen home (cf. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 16#Psalm 16 . 11|Psalm 16:11]] 'in your presence is fullness of joy'); others see it as Christ standing as advocate/witness for His martyred servant (cf. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 12#Luke 12 . 8|Luke 12:8]] 'the Son of Man will acknowledge before the angels of God'). The standing posture may also echo [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|Daniel 12:1]], where Michael 'shall stand up' to defend God's people in their time of distress. Whatever the precise meaning, Stephen's vision validates: (1) Jesus' trial claim was true—He is the Son of Man at God's right hand; (2) the exalted Christ is present with His suffering church; (3) the earthly condemnation (both Jesus' and Stephen's) is reversed in heaven's court. This is the only non-Gospel use of 'Son of Man,' and it's significant that it comes from a martyr's lips, confirming that the crucified Jesus is the vindicated, exalted, Danielic Son of Man who will return to judge ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 11|Acts 1:11]]; 17:31).
