### [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 14|Luke 4:14-21]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα (pneuma) - "Spirit" - in the power of the Spirit
- [[Lexicon/G1411|G1411]] δύναμις (dynamis) - "power" - in the power of the Spirit
- [[Lexicon/G5548|G5548]] χρίω (chriō) - "anoint" - he has anointed me
- [[Lexicon/G2097|G2097]] εὐαγγελίζω (euangelizō) - "preach good news" - to preach good news
- [[Lexicon/G859|G859]] ἄφεσις (aphesis) - "release/forgiveness" - to proclaim release

**Context**: After His baptism and temptation, "Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit (ἐν τῇ δυνάμει τοῦ πνεύματος) to Galilee" (4:14). In the Nazareth synagogue, He read Isaiah 61:1-2: "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he has anointed me (ἔχρισέν με) to proclaim good news to the poor. He has sent me to proclaim liberty to the captives and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty those who are oppressed, to proclaim the year of the Lord's favor" (4:18-19). He then declared: "Today this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing" (4:21).

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Jesus quotes [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 1|Isaiah 61:1-2]], the Messianic anointing prophecy
- The "year of the Lord's favor" connects to Jubilee (Leviticus 25)
- Spirit-anointing for ministry fulfills what judges' Spirit-empowerment prefigured

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment (Jesus is the ultimate Spirit-anointed deliverer), NT Superiority (Christ's anointing surpasses all OT figures)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus is the perfect fulfillment of the Spirit-empowered deliverer pattern. Where Samson's Spirit-empowerment was episodic (coming upon him at specific moments), Jesus was conceived by the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 35|Luke 1:35]]), baptized with the Spirit (3:22), filled with the Spirit (4:1), led by the Spirit (4:1), and ministered in the Spirit's power (4:14, 18). Where Samson's Spirit-empowerment could be forfeited through unfaithfulness, Jesus' Spirit-anointing was permanent—given "without measure" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 34|John 3:34]]). Where Samson "began" deliverance ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 13#Judges 13 . 5|Judges 13:5]]), Jesus completed it: "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]). Samson killed enemies; Jesus proclaimed release to captives. Samson's strength restored sight to none; Jesus gave sight to the blind. Every aspect of Spirit-empowered ministry that Samson illustrated imperfectly, Jesus embodied perfectly. The Spirit-empowered deliverer pattern finds its ultimate expression in Christ.
