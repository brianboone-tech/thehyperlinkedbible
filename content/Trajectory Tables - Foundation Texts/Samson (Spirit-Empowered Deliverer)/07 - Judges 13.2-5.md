### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 13#Judges 13 . 2|Judges 13:2-5]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H6135|H6135]] עָקָר (ʿāqār) - "barren" - whose wife was barren
- [[Lexicon/H4397|H4397]] מַלְאָךְ (malʾāḵ) - "angel/messenger" - the angel of the LORD appeared
- [[Lexicon/H5139|H5139]] נָזִיר (nāzîr) - "Nazirite/consecrated one" - a Nazirite to God
- [[Lexicon/H990|H990]] בֶּטֶן (beṭen) - "womb" - from the womb
- [[Lexicon/H2490|H2490]] חָלַל (ḥālal) - "begin" - he will begin to deliver

**Context**: The angel of the LORD appears to Manoah's barren wife with an annunciation: "You will conceive and give birth to a son" (13:3). The child would be "a Nazirite to God from the womb" (נְזִיר אֱלֹהִים יִהְיֶה הַנַּעַר מִן־הַבֶּטֶן). The Nazirite vow (Numbers 6) involved consecration through abstaining from wine, avoiding razor and corpse. For Samson, this was not a temporary vow but a lifelong calling from before birth. The commission: "he will begin to deliver Israel from the hand of the Philistines" (13:5). The verb יָחֵל ("begin") indicates partial, initiatory deliverance.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The barren-wife-bearing-deliverer pattern connects to Sarah (Isaac), Rebekah (Jacob), Rachel (Joseph), Hannah (Samuel)
- The Angel of the LORD theophany connects to similar appearances ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 16#Genesis 16 . 7|Genesis 16:7-13]]; 22:11-18; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2-6]])
- The Nazirite vow connects to Numbers 6 and anticipates Samuel's dedication ([[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 1#1 Samuel 1 . 11|1 Samuel 1:11]])

**Type Classification**: Providential (circumstances sovereignly arranged), Backward-Looking (typological significance recognized from NT)

**Christological Connection**: Samson's prenatal consecration anticipates Christ's unique status. Jesus was "holy" from conception ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 35|Luke 1:35]]), set apart for redemptive purpose. John the Baptist, also announced to barren parents with Nazirite elements ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 15|Luke 1:15]]), prepared for Christ. Where Samson was to "begin" deliverance, Jesus accomplished complete salvation: "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]). The Nazirite pattern of consecration finds its fulfillment in Christ, who was "made to be sin who knew no sin" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]])—the one truly set apart, holy, consecrated for the ultimate deliverance of His people.
