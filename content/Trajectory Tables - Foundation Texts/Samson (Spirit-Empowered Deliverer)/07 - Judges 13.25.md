### [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 13#Judges 13 . 25|Judges 13:25]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H7307|H7307]] רוּחַ (rûaḥ) - "Spirit" - the Spirit of the LORD
- [[Lexicon/H2490|H2490]] חָלַל (ḥālal) - "begin" - began to stir him
- [[Lexicon/H6470|H6470]] פָּעַם (pāʿam) - "stir/impel/trouble" - to stir him

**Context**: After noting Samson's birth and growth ("the boy grew, and the LORD blessed him," 13:24), the narrative records the first manifestation of the Spirit: "The Spirit of the LORD began to stir him (וַתָּחֶל רוּחַ יְהוָה לְפַעֲמוֹ) at Mahaneh-dan, between Zorah and Eshtaol." The verb פָּעַם means "to thrust, impel, disturb"—suggesting inner restlessness, divine prompting that would lead to action. This initial stirring preceded the more dramatic Spirit-empowerments.

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- The Spirit's role with judges: Othniel (3:10), Gideon (6:34), Jephthah (11:29)
- The verb "began" (חָלַל) echoes 13:5—Samson will "begin" to deliver; the Spirit "begins" to stir
- The Spirit's moving anticipates the messianic promise of [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 2|Isaiah 11:2]]

**Connections**:
- **TO**:
  - [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 3#Judges 3 . 10|Judges 3:10]] - "Spirit of the LORD came upon Othniel"
  - [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 6#Judges 6 . 34|Judges 6:34]] - "Spirit clothed Gideon"
- **FROM OT**:

- **FROM NT**:
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 2#Luke 2 . 40|Luke 2:40]] - Jesus "filled with wisdom"
  - [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 3#Luke 3 . 22|Luke 3:22]] - Spirit descends at baptism
**Type Classification**: Providential (Spirit-empowerment sovereignly given), Backward-Looking (pattern clarified by Christ's Spirit-anointed ministry)

**Christological Connection**: The Spirit's initial stirring in Samson anticipates the Spirit's presence in Jesus' early years. Luke records that Jesus "grew and became strong, filled with wisdom" (2:40), and at age twelve displayed supernatural understanding (2:46-47). The stirring of the Spirit prepared both Samson and Jesus for public ministry. Yet the differences are significant: Samson's Spirit-empowerment was episodic and eventually forfeited; Jesus was conceived by the Spirit ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 35|Luke 1:35]]), filled with the Spirit "without measure" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 34|John 3:34]]), and never lost divine presence. The Spirit's stirring in Samson shadows the Spirit's permanent, unlimited rest upon Christ.
