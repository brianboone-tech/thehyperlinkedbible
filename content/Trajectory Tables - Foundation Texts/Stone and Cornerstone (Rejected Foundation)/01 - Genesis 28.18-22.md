### [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 18|Genesis 28:18-22]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H68|H0068]] אֶבֶן (eben) - stone
- [[Lexicon/H4676|H4676]] מַצֵּבָה (matstsebah) - pillar, memorial stone
- [[Lexicon/H1004|H1004]] בֵּית (bayith) - house (Bethel = "house of God")
- [[Lexicon/H8179|H8179]] שַׁעַר (sha'ar) - gate ("gate of heaven")

**Context**: Jacob flees from Esau and stops at Bethel for the night. He has a vision of a ladder reaching to heaven with angels ascending and descending, and Yahweh standing above it, renewing the Abrahamic covenant promises. Upon waking, Jacob recognizes this as a sacred place—the "house of God" and "gate of heaven." He takes the stone he used as a pillow, sets it up as a memorial pillar (matstsebah), anoints it with oil, and names the place Bethel. He vows that if God brings him back safely, this stone will be "God's house" and he will give God a tenth of all.

**Type Classification**: **Providential** (God sovereignly arranged this encounter) and **Backward-looking** (recognized as typological only from NT perspective, though it contains forward-pointing elements like the anointing and the vision of mediation between heaven and earth)

**Christological Connection**: Christ is the true and greater Stone of Bethel. He is the place where heaven and earth meet ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 51|John 1:51]]), the true house of God ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]), the anointed One (Messiah/Christ), and the foundation stone upon which God builds His spiritual house ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4-5]]). Where Jacob saw angels ascending and descending on a ladder, Jesus promises His disciples will see angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man—He is the mediator, the bridge between God and humanity. The stone Jacob anointed pointed forward to the Stone God would anoint with the Spirit without measure.
