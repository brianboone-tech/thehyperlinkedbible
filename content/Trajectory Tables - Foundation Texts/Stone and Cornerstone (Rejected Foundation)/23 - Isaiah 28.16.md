**Context**: Isaiah 28:14-22 pronounces judgment on the scoffing rulers of Jerusalem who boast they have made a "covenant with death" and "agreement with Sheol"—likely referring to political alliances (possibly with Egypt) they believe will protect them from Assyria's invasion (the "overwhelming scourge"). Against their false refuge of lies, Yahweh declares He is laying a sure foundation in Zion: a tested stone, a precious cornerstone. Those who believe in this foundation will not be shaken or panic when judgment comes. But those who trust in lies will see their refuge swept away by hail and flooding waters.

**Type Classification**: **Forward-looking** (prophetic promise pointing to future Messianic fulfillment) and **Direct** (divinely ordained foundation explicitly stated in canonical prophecy)

**Christological Connection**: Jesus Christ is the Stone God laid in Zion—the tested, precious cornerstone, the sure foundation. He was tested through suffering and temptation and proved perfectly faithful ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 18|Hebrews 2:18]]; 4:15). He is precious beyond measure—the pearl of great price ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13#Matthew 13 . 46|Matthew 13:46]]), the beloved Son in whom the Father is well pleased ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 17|Matthew 3:17]]; 17:5). He is the cornerstone that determines the shape and alignment of the entire building—the church ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 20|Ephesians 2:20-22]]; [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 4|1 Peter 2:4-5]]). The promise "whoever believes in Him will never be shaken/put to shame" finds fulfillment in justification by faith in Christ ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 33|Romans 9:33]]; 10:11). Those who build their lives on Christ, the solid Rock, will not fall when storms come ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 24|Matthew 7:24-25]]). Those who trust in political power, military might, or human wisdom—modern "covenants with death"—will see their refuge swept away. But those who rest in Christ stand firm forever, for He is the foundation God Himself has laid, tested through death and resurrection, and declared to be the only sure hope for humanity. "For no one can lay a foundation other than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 11|1 Corinthians 3:11]]).
