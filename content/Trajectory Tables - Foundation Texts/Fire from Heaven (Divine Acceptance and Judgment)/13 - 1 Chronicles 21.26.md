### [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 21#1 Chronicles 21 . 26|1 Chronicles 21:26]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (*esh*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/H4196|H4196]] מִזְבֵּחַ (*mizbeach*) - altar
- [[Lexicon/H8064|H8064]] שָׁמַיִם (*shamayim*) - heaven
- [[Lexicon/H6030|H6030]] עָנָה (*anah*) - to answer
- [[Lexicon/H5930|H5930]] עֹלָה (*olah*) - burnt offering
- [[Lexicon/H8002|H8002]] שֶׁלֶם (*shelem*) - peace offering

**Context**: 1 Chronicles 21:26: "And David built there an altar to the LORD and presented burnt offerings and peace offerings and called on the LORD, and the LORD answered him (וַיַּעֲנֵהוּ) with fire from heaven (בְּאֵשׁ מִן־הַשָּׁמַיִם) upon the altar of burnt offering."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fire from heaven marks the future temple site
- Divine answer (עָנָה) connects prayer and fire
- Location validated: threshing floor of Ornan becomes temple mount

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]] - Fire at tabernacle inauguration
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 18|2 Samuel 24:18-25]] - Parallel account (without fire)
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 3#2 Chronicles 3 . 1|2 Chronicles 3:1]] - Solomon builds temple on this site
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1]] - Fire at temple dedication
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 22|Hebrews 12:22]] - Mount Zion, heavenly Jerusalem

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Fire sovereignly marks the place God chooses

**Christological Connection**: Fire from heaven validated the site where God's presence would dwell. This points to Christ as the true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]) - the place where God and humanity meet. Just as fire marked where God would accept sacrifice, Christ is the place where God accepts sinners. The threshing floor of judgment (David's sin bringing plague) becomes the place of mercy through sacrifice - as the cross becomes the place where judgment and mercy meet.
