### [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 3|Acts 2:3-4]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4442|G4442]] πῦρ (*pyr*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/G1100|G1100]] γλῶσσα (*glossa*) - tongue
- [[Lexicon/G1266|G1266]] διαμερίζω (*diamerizo*) - to divide, distribute
- [[Lexicon/G2523|G2523]] καθίζω (*kathizo*) - to sit, settle
- [[Lexicon/G4151|G4151]] πνεῦμα (*pneuma*) - spirit
- [[Lexicon/G4130|G4130]] πίμπλημι (*pimplemi*) - to fill

**Context**: Acts 2:3-4: "And divided tongues as of fire (γλῶσσαι ὡσεὶ πυρός) appeared to them and rested (ἐκάθισεν) on each one of them. And they were all filled (ἐπλήσθησαν) with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues as the Spirit gave them utterance."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fire no longer consumes the offering - it RESTS on the worshipers
- Believers become the living sacrifice on whom fire descends
- Tongues (languages) for mission = reversal of Babel

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]] - Fire consumes offering at tabernacle
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1]] - Fire + glory at temple
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 18|Exodus 19:18]] - Fire at Sinai (law given)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-32]] - Spirit poured out
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|Romans 12:1]] - Present bodies as living sacrifice

**Type Classification**: NT Fulfillment - Fire from heaven now indwelling believers

**Christological Connection**: At Pentecost, the fire pattern reaches its transformation. Fire descended at tabernacle, temple, and Carmel to consume offerings and validate worship. Now fire descends on BELIEVERS - they become the temple (1 Cor 3:16), they become the living sacrifice (Rom 12:1). This is possible because Christ, the ultimate sacrifice, has been accepted once for all. The fire that fell on Christ at the cross (divine judgment) now falls on believers as the Spirit (divine presence). Fire no longer consumes because Christ has been consumed in our place.
