### [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 1|Leviticus 10:1-2]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (*esh*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/H2114|H2114]] זוּר (*zur*) - to be strange, foreign
- [[Lexicon/H6680|H6680]] צָוָה (*tsavah*) - to command
- [[Lexicon/H3318|H3318]] יָצָא (*yatsa*) - to go out, come forth
- [[Lexicon/H398|H398]] אָכַל (*akal*) - to eat, consume

**Context**: Leviticus 10:1-2: "Now Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, each took his censer and put fire in it and laid incense on it and offered unauthorized fire (אֵשׁ זָרָה, "strange fire") before the LORD, which he had not commanded them. And fire came out (וַתֵּצֵא אֵשׁ) from before the LORD and consumed them (וַתֹּאכַל אוֹתָם), and they died before the LORD."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Immediately follows [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]] - same fire, opposite result
- "Strange fire" contrasts with divine fire just sent
- Establishes that human innovation in worship is fatal

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]] - Fire consumes offering (acceptance)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 9|Exodus 30:9]] - "You shall not offer strange incense"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 35|Numbers 16:35]] - Fire consumes Korah's 250 men
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 1#2 Kings 1 . 10|2 Kings 1:10-12]] - Fire consumes soldiers
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 29|Hebrews 12:29]] - "Our God is a consuming fire"

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Divine fire as judgment (shadow of final judgment fire)

**Christological Connection**: The same fire that accepted the offering consumed those who approached improperly. This illustrates why Christ's mediation is necessary - no human approach to God is acceptable apart from the divinely appointed way. Christ is the "authorized" access to God ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]). Those who reject Christ face the same consuming fire (Heb 10:27, 31). The fire that welcomes believers in Christ becomes judgment fire for those outside Christ.
