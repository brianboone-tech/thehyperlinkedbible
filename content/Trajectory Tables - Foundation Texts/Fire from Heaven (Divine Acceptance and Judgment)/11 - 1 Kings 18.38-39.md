### [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 18#1 Kings 18 . 38|1 Kings 18:38-39]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (*esh*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/H5307|H5307]] נָפַל (*naphal*) - to fall
- [[Lexicon/H398|H398]] אָכַל (*akal*) - to eat, consume
- [[Lexicon/H5930|H5930]] עֹלָה (*olah*) - burnt offering
- [[Lexicon/H3068|H3068]] יְהוָה (*YHWH*) - the LORD
- [[Lexicon/H430|H430]] אֱלֹהִים (*elohim*) - God

**Context**: 1 Kings 18:38-39: "Then the fire of the LORD fell (וַתִּפֹּל אֵשׁ־יְהוָה) and consumed the burnt offering and the wood and the stones and the dust, and licked up the water that was in the trench. And when all the people saw it, they fell on their faces and said, 'The LORD, he is God (יְהוָה הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים); the LORD, he is God.'"

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fire validates true worship against false (Baal worship)
- Consumes not just sacrifice but altar itself - total validation
- People's confession: "YHWH, he is THE God"

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]] - Fire at tabernacle (acceptance)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 24|Deuteronomy 4:24]] - "The LORD your God is a consuming fire"
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 1#2 Kings 1 . 10|2 Kings 1:10-12]] - Elijah calls fire as judgment
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 54|Luke 9:54]] - Disciples want to call fire (rebuked)
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 29|Hebrews 12:29]] - "Our God is a consuming fire"

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Fire sovereignly answers to vindicate true worship

**Christological Connection**: Fire from heaven proved that YHWH alone is the true God. This points to Christ's vindication through resurrection - God's ultimate "answer" proving Jesus is Lord. The people's confession "The LORD, he is God" anticipates every knee bowing and tongue confessing that Jesus Christ is Lord (Phil 2:10-11). Elijah's bold prayer was answered by fire; Christ's obedient sacrifice was answered by resurrection - the ultimate divine acceptance.
