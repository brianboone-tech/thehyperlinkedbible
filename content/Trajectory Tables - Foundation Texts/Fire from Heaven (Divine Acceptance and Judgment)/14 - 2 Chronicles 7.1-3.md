### [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1-3]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (*esh*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/H3381|H3381]] יָרַד (*yarad*) - to come down, descend
- [[Lexicon/H8064|H8064]] שָׁמַיִם (*shamayim*) - heaven
- [[Lexicon/H3519|H3519]] כָּבוֹד (*kavod*) - glory
- [[Lexicon/H4390|H4390]] מָלֵא (*male*) - to fill
- [[Lexicon/H7812|H7812]] שָׁחָה (*shachah*) - to bow down, worship

**Context**: 2 Chronicles 7:1-3: "As soon as Solomon finished his prayer, fire came down from heaven (וְהָאֵשׁ יָרְדָה מֵהַשָּׁמַיִם) and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the LORD (כְּבוֹד יְהוָה) filled the temple. And the priests could not enter the house of the LORD, because the glory of the LORD filled the LORD's house. When all the people of Israel saw the fire come down and the glory of the LORD on the temple, they bowed down (וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ) with their faces to the ground."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Climax of fire-from-heaven pattern: fire + glory together
- Echoes tabernacle inauguration (Lev 9:24) but surpasses it
- Temple becomes the permanent dwelling of divine presence

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]] - Fire at tabernacle inauguration
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 21#1 Chronicles 21 . 26|1 Chronicles 21:26]] - Fire at David's altar (same site)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]] - Glory fills tabernacle
- **FROM OT**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 10#Ezekiel 10 . 18|Ezekiel 10:18-19]] - Glory departs temple
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]] - Glory dwells in Christ

**Type Classification**: Direct Type - Fire from heaven as ultimate sign of divine acceptance of temple

**Christological Connection**: Fire and glory together mark the fullest OT expression of God dwelling with His people. This points to Christ: "The Word became flesh and dwelt (tabernacled) among us, and we have seen his glory" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]). The glory that filled Solomon's temple finds its antitype in Christ, in whom "the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily" (Col 2:9). At Pentecost, this glory-fire comes to rest on believers, making the church the new temple.
