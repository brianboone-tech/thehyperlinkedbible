### [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 1#2 Kings 1 . 10|2 Kings 1:10-12]]

**Hebrew Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/H784|H784]] אֵשׁ (*esh*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/H8064|H8064]] שָׁמַיִם (*shamayim*) - heaven
- [[Lexicon/H3381|H3381]] יָרַד (*yarad*) - to come down, descend
- [[Lexicon/H398|H398]] אָכַל (*akal*) - to eat, consume
- [[Lexicon/H376|H376]] אִישׁ (*ish*) - man
- [[Lexicon/H430|H430]] אֱלֹהִים (*elohim*) - God

**Context**: 2 Kings 1:10, 12: "But Elijah answered the captain of fifty, 'If I am a man of God (אִישׁ אֱלֹהִים), let fire come down from heaven (תֵּרֵד אֵשׁ מִן־הַשָּׁמַיִם) and consume you and your fifty.' Then fire came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Fire as judgment on those opposing God's prophet
- Twice repeated - confirms divine origin
- "Man of God" validated by heaven's response

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 18#1 Kings 18 . 38|1 Kings 18:38]] - Fire at Carmel (acceptance)
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 1|Leviticus 10:1-2]] - Fire consumes Nadab and Abihu
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 54|Luke 9:54-55]] - James and John want to call fire; Jesus rebukes
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 5|Revelation 11:5]] - Fire from two witnesses' mouths
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 9|Revelation 20:9]] - Fire consumes Gog and Magog

**Type Classification**: Providential Type - Fire as judgment on opposition to God's word

**Christological Connection**: Jesus explicitly addresses this passage when James and John want to call down fire on the Samaritan village ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 54|Luke 9:54]]). Jesus rebukes them: "You do not know what manner of spirit you are of." Christ came not to judge but to save ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 17|John 3:17]]). The fire of judgment is reserved for the final day (2 Thess 1:7-8). What Elijah did in judgment, Christ delays in mercy - but the final fire will come (Rev 20:9).
