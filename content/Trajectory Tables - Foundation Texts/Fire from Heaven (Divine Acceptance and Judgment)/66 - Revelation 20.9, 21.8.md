### [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 9|Revelation 20:9]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 8|Revelation 21:8]]

**Greek Key Terms**:
- [[Lexicon/G4442|G4442]] πῦρ (*pyr*) - fire
- [[Lexicon/G2597|G2597]] καταβαίνω (*katabaino*) - to come down, descend
- [[Lexicon/G3772|G3772]] οὐρανός (*ouranos*) - heaven
- [[Lexicon/G2719|G2719]] κατεσθίω (*katesthio*) - to eat up, devour, consume
- [[Lexicon/G3041|G3041]] λίμνη (*limne*) - lake
- [[Lexicon/G2303|G2303]] θεῖον (*theion*) - sulfur, brimstone

**Context**: Revelation 20:9: "And they marched up over the broad plain of the earth and surrounded the camp of the saints and the beloved city, but fire came down from heaven (πῦρ ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ) and consumed them (κατέφαγεν αὐτούς)."

Revelation 21:8: "But as for the cowardly, the faithless... their portion will be in the lake that burns with fire and sulfur (ἐν τῇ λίμνῃ τῇ καιομένῃ πυρὶ καὶ θείῳ), which is the second death."

**OT-to-OT Development**:
- Echoes fire on Sodom (Gen 19:24)
- Echoes fire consuming Gog's armies (Ezek 38:22; 39:6)
- Consummation of all judgment-fire imagery

**Connections**:
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 19#Genesis 19 . 24|Genesis 19:24]] - Fire and sulfur on Sodom
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 1|Leviticus 10:1-2]] - Fire consumes unauthorized approach
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 1#2 Kings 1 . 10|2 Kings 1:10-12]] - Fire consumes enemies
- **TO**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 38#Ezekiel 38 . 22|Ezekiel 38:22]] - Fire on Gog
- **FROM NT**: [[Readable Bible/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 1#2 Thessalonians 1 . 7|2 Thessalonians 1:7-8]] - Lord revealed with flaming fire

**Type Classification**: Eschatological Consummation - Final expression of divine fire

**Christological Connection**: The fire that fell to accept sacrifices and judge the wicked throughout redemptive history reaches its consummation. Those who rejected Christ's sacrifice face the fire that Christ bore for believers. The same fire that welcomes believers (Pentecost fire of the Spirit) becomes eternal judgment for unbelievers (lake of fire). The "second death" is permanent exposure to the consuming fire that Christ endured temporarily on the cross. For those in Christ, fire has already fallen - on their Substitute. For those outside Christ, fire is yet to fall - eternally.
