## The Heart of The Hyperlinked Bible

The **Readable Bible is the center of this entire site**. This is where you read Scripture with every verse connected to the rich web of cross-references, literary structures, and typological patterns that the biblical authors embedded in the text.

Every verse in the Readable Bible ends with **inline links** connecting you to:

| Link | What You'll Find |
|------|------------------|
| **Verse Number** | Hebrew/Greek interlinear in Reference Bible |
| **IP** | Intertextuality Pair showing quotations/allusions |
| **C** | Chiastic structure containing this verse |
| **TOSK** | Treasury of Scripture Knowledge cross-references |
| **TT** | Trajectory Table tracing typological fulfillment |

**Tip**: Just start reading and click the links that interest you. The connections will unfold naturally.
