# 4.2.1.3 - Commission - of Christ as eschatological Israel/Son of Man/Adam

## Overview

**The Fivefold Commission Perfectly Fulfilled:**

Christ receives and accomplishes the **Adamic Commission** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]) that all previous Adams failed to complete. This is the **eschatological commission**—not merely a task but an identity.

---

## 1. BLESSED - Divine Approval

**From Eternity:**
- **Beloved of the Father**: "Before the foundation of the world" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 4|Ephesians 1:4]])
- **Glory with the Father**: "The glory I had with you before the world existed" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 5|John 17:5]])

**In Time:**
- **Baptism**: "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 17|Matthew 3:17]])
- **Transfiguration**: "This is my beloved Son; listen to him" ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 9#Mark 9 . 7|Mark 9:7]])
- **Resurrection**: "Declared to be Son of God in power" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 4|Romans 1:4]])

**Post-Ascension:**
- **Seated in Heavenly Places**: "Far above all rule and authority" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 20|Ephesians 1:20-21]])
- **Name Above Every Name**: "At the name of Jesus every knee should bow" ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 9|Philippians 2:9-11]])
- **Crown of Glory**: "Crowned with glory and honor" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 9|Hebrews 2:9]])

**Beale**: Christ receives the escalated blessing that Adam forfeited—irreversible, eternal, cosmic in scope.

---

## 2. BE FRUITFUL AND MULTIPLY - Spiritual Offspring

**Seed Promise Fulfilled:**
- **Singular Seed**: "And to your offspring [seed, singular], who is Christ" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]])
- **Many Sons to Glory**: "Bringing many sons to glory" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 10|Hebrews 2:10]])
- **Grain of Wheat**: "Unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 24|John 12:24]])

**Multiplication of Disciples:**
- **Twelve Apostles**: New Israel, twelve tribes reconstituted
- **Pentecost**: 3,000 added ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 41|Acts 2:41]])—echoes [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 28|Exodus 32:28]] reversal (3,000 died at golden calf)
- **Gospel Spreads**: "In all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]])
- **Great Multitude**: "From every nation, tribe, people and language" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 9|Revelation 7:9]])

**Church as Christ's Body:**
- **Bride of Christ**: Fruitfulness in corporate union ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 25|Ephesians 5:25-27]])
- **Building Grows**: "Whole body... grows with a growth that is from God" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 19|Colossians 2:19]])
- **Branches Bear Fruit**: "I am the vine; you are the branches" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 5|John 15:5]])

## 3. FILL THE EARTH - Extending God's Presence

**Great Commission:**
"Go therefore and make disciples of **all nations**, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, **I am with you always**, to the end of the age." ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19-20]])

**Fulfilling [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]]:**
- **Son of Man Given Dominion**: "All peoples, nations, and languages should serve him"
- **Kingdom Extends Worldwide**: "His dominion is an everlasting dominion"
- **Stone Fills Earth**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 35|Daniel 2:35]], 44-45—kingdom becomes great mountain filling whole earth

**Temple Expands Globally** (Beale):
- **Eden = First Temple**: God's presence localized
- **Tabernacle/Temple**: Presence in one nation (Israel)
- **Christ = True Temple**: "The Word became flesh and **tabernacled** among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]])
- **Church = Temple Expanding**: "You are being built together into a dwelling place for God" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 22|Ephesians 2:22]])
- **New Jerusalem**: "The dwelling place of God is with man" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]])—fills new creation

**Gospel to the Ends:**
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]**: "You will be my witnesses... to the end of the earth"
- **[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 23|Colossians 1:23]]**: Gospel "has been proclaimed in all creation under heaven"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 18|Romans 10:18]]**: "Their voice has gone out to all the earth"

## 4. SUBDUE - Conquering All Enemies

**Christus Victor - Christ the Conqueror:**

**Satan Subdued:**
- **Wilderness**: "Get behind me, Satan!" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 10|Matthew 4:10]])—first victory
- **Exorcisms**: "If it is by the Spirit of God that I cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 28|Matthew 12:28]])
- **Cross**: "He disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, by triumphing over them" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]])
- **Resurrection**: Death's sting removed ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 55|1 Corinthians 15:55-57]])
- **Ascension**: "He led a host of captives" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 8|Ephesians 4:8]])

**Death Subdued:**
- **"O death, where is your victory?"** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 55|1 Corinthians 15:55]])
- **"The last enemy to be destroyed is death"** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 26|1 Corinthians 15:26]])—process begun, completion certain
- **"He destroyed the one who has the power of death, that is, the devil"** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14]])

**Sin Subdued:**
- **"The Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 29|John 1:29]])
- **"He appeared once for all at the end of the ages to put away sin by the sacrifice of himself"** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 26|Hebrews 9:26]])
- **"There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus"** ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]])

**Powers/Authorities Subdued:**
- **Cosmic Powers**: "Far above all rule and authority and power and dominion" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 21|Ephesians 1:21]])
- **Spiritual Forces**: Wrestling against principalities ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 12|Ephesians 6:12]]), but Christ has triumphed
- **All Creation**: "All things were created through him and for him... in him all things hold together" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 16|Colossians 1:16-17]])

**Intrusion Note** (Kline): Christ's subduing is the **eschatological judgment** breaking into history. The final day's victory is won NOW in the cross/resurrection, though its full manifestation awaits the Parousia.

## 5. RULE - Eternal Kingship

**Davidic Throne Established Forever:**

**Angelic Announcement:**
"He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. And the Lord God will give to him **the throne of his father David**, and he will reign over the house of Jacob **forever**, and of his kingdom there will be **no end**." ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 1#Luke 1 . 32|Luke 1:32-33]])

**Triumphal Entry:**
- **"Hosanna to the Son of David!"** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 9|Matthew 21:9]])
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 9|Zechariah 9:9]] Fulfilled**: "Behold, your king is coming to you, humble, and mounted on a donkey"
- **Rejected by His Own**: Israel refuses their King ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 15|John 19:15]]—"We have no king but Caesar")

**Crucifixion Kingship:**
- **Inscription**: "Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 19|John 19:19]])—ironic truth
- **Pilate's Question**: "Are you the King of the Jews?" Jesus: "You have said so" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 11|Matthew 27:11]])
- **Crown of Thorns**: Mockery becomes reality—He reigns from the tree

**Resurrection Enthronement:**
- **All Authority**: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18]])
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 33|Acts 2:33-36]]**: "Being therefore exalted at the right hand of God... God has made him both **Lord and Christ**, this Jesus whom you crucified"
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 110#Psalms 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]**: "The LORD says to my Lord: 'Sit at my right hand, until I make your enemies your footstool'"

**Present Reign** (Amillennial/Inaugurated Eschatology):
- **NOW Reigning**: "He must reign until he has put all his enemies under his feet" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 25|1 Corinthians 15:25]])
- **Mediatorial Kingdom**: Currently exercising dominion from heaven ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 20|Ephesians 1:20-23]])
- **Building His Church**: "I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 16#Matthew 16 . 18|Matthew 16:18]])

**Future Manifestation:**
- **"Every knee will bow"** ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 10|Philippians 2:10-11]])—universal recognition
- **"King of kings and Lord of lords"** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 19#Revelation 19 . 16|Revelation 19:16]])—visible, undeniable reign
- **Consummation**: "Then comes the end, when he delivers the kingdom to God the Father" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 24|1 Corinthians 15:24]])

**Beale's Son of Man Theology:**
Christ = [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] Son of Man receiving eternal dominion. This is not future only but **inaugurated NOW**—the kingdom has come ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 15|Mark 1:15]]), though not yet consummated.
