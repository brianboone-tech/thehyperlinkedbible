# 3.1 - Sin - at the tree of discerning of good and evil (Genesis 3:1-13)

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/3 - Fall|← Back to 3 - Fall]]

---

## Overview

The serpent's temptation and Adam and Eve's disobedience - the entrance of sin into God's good creation.

---

## The Temptation (Genesis 3:1-5)

**Serpent's Strategy**:
1. **Question God's Word**: "Did God actually say...?" (3:1)
2. **Deny God's Judgment**: "You will not surely die" (3:4)
3. **Promise False Autonomy**: "You will be like God, knowing good and evil" (3:5)

**Three-Fold Lure** ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 2#1 John 2 . 16|1 John 2:16]]):
- **Lust of the Flesh**: "Good for food" (physical appetite)
- **Lust of the Eyes**: "Delight to the eyes" (aesthetic desire)
- **Pride of Life**: "Desired to make one wise" (intellectual pride)

---

## The Fall (Genesis 3:6)

**"So when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was to be desired to make one wise, she took of its fruit and ate, and she also gave some to her husband who was with her, and he ate."**

**Adam's Failure**: Present but passive - fails to protect/guard the garden
**Eve's Deception**: Deceived by serpent's lie
**Covenant Breaking**: Direct violation of God's command

---

## The Aftermath (Genesis 3:7-13)

**Immediate Consequences**:
- Eyes opened - shame and awareness of nakedness
- Fig leaves - futile attempt at self-covering
- Hiding from God - broken fellowship
- Blame-shifting - Adam blames Eve (and implicitly God); Eve blames serpent

**Spiritual Death**: Immediate separation from God
**Physical Death**: Process begins ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 19|Genesis 3:19]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Federal Headship**: Adam represents all humanity - his sin imputes guilt to all ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 12|Romans 5:12-21]])

**Total Depravity**: Sin affects every part of human nature

**Enmity with God**: Relationship broken - hiding from presence

**Inability to Self-Atone**: Fig leaves cannot cover shame - pointing to need for God's provision (animal skins, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|Genesis 3:21]])

**Satan's Victory... Temporary**: The serpent wins the battle but God will win the war ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]])
