## Overview

**Pentecost: The Church as New Creation Temple**

The Church's "creation" moment is **Pentecost** ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 1|Acts 2:1-4]])—when the Spirit poured out and the new covenant community was born. This is the escalation of new creation beyond even Christ's individual resurrection.

---

## A. Pentecost as New Creation Event

**[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 1|Acts 2:1-4]] - The Spirit Descends:**
- **"A mighty rushing wind"**: Echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] (Spirit hovering over waters)
- **"Tongues of fire"**: Theophanic presence (cf. [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 2|Exodus 3:2]]; 19:18)
- **"They were all filled with the Holy Spirit"**: Temple presence now indwells people
- **Speaking in Tongues**: Babel reversed—scattered nations hear gospel in own languages

**Beale's Temple Theology:**
- **Eden = First Temple**: God walked with Adam
- **Tabernacle/Temple**: God's presence localized in Holy of Holies
- **Christ = True Temple**: "The Word became flesh and tabernacled among us" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]])
- **Church = Living Stones**: "You... are being built up as a spiritual house" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 5|1 Peter 2:5]])
- **Believers = Individual Temples**: "Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit?" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 19|1 Corinthians 6:19]])

**New Creation Language:**
- **"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation"** ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]])
- **"We are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus"** ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 10|Ephesians 2:10]])
- **"You have put on the new self, which is being renewed"** ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 10|Colossians 3:10]])

---

## B. The Church Born at Pentecost

**[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 41|Acts 2:41-47]] - First Community:**
- **3,000 Added**: Reverses [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 28|Exodus 32:28]] (3,000 died at golden calf)—grace triumphs over judgment
- **Devoted to Apostles' Teaching**: Word-centered community
- **Fellowship**: Koinonia—sharing in Christ's life together
- **Breaking of Bread**: Lord's Supper celebrated
- **Prayers**: Corporate worship
- **All Things in Common**: Economic generosity, care for poor
- **Glad and Generous Hearts**: Joy of new creation

**One New Man ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14-16]]):**
- **Jew and Gentile United**: "He has made us both one"
- **Dividing Wall Broken**: Ethnic barriers demolished in Christ
- **Hostility Killed**: "Putting to death the hostility"
- **One Body**: Corporate new humanity—neither Jew nor Greek ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 28|Galatians 3:28]])

---

## C. The Spirit's Work - Creating New Covenant People

**Regeneration (New Birth):**
- **[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 3|John 3:3-8]]**: "Unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom"
- **[[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 3#Titus 3 . 5|Titus 3:5]]**: "He saved us... by the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit"
- **[[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 18|James 1:18]]**: "Of his own will he brought us forth by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of firstfruits"

**Indwelling:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 9|Romans 8:9]]**: "You... are in the Spirit, if in fact the Spirit of God dwells in you"
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]**: "Do you not know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in you?"
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 6|Galatians 4:6]]**: "God has sent the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying, 'Abba! Father!'"

**Sealing:**
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 13|Ephesians 1:13-14]]**: "You were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit, who is the guarantee of our inheritance"
- **[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 1#2 Corinthians 1 . 21|2 Corinthians 1:21-22]]**: "He has... put his seal on us and given us his Spirit in our hearts as a guarantee"
- **Downpayment**: ἀρραβών (arrabon)—first installment guaranteeing full payment

**Baptism into One Body:**
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 12#1 Corinthians 12 . 13|1 Corinthians 12:13]]**: "In one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—Jews or Greeks, slaves or free"
- **Corporate Unity**: Not just individual salvation, but membership in Christ's body

## D. New Covenant Enacted

**[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] Fulfilled:**
- **"I will make a new covenant"** ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31]])—enacted at Last Supper ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]])
- **"I will put my law within them"** ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]])—Spirit writes on hearts ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 3|2 Corinthians 3:3]])
- **"I will be their God, and they shall be my people"** ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]])—covenant formula realized
- **"They shall all know me"** ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 34|Jeremiah 31:34]])—internal, immediate knowledge of God
- **"I will forgive their iniquity"** ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 34|Jeremiah 31:34]])—atonement accomplished

**[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]] Fulfilled:**
- **"I will give you a new heart"**: Regeneration
- **"I will put my Spirit within you"**: Indwelling
- **"Cause you to walk in my statutes"**: Empowerment for obedience

**Contrast with Old Covenant:**
| **Old Covenant** | **New Covenant** |
|---|---|
| External law (tablets) | Internal law (hearts) |
| Mediated (priests) | Immediate (Spirit) |
| Conditional (obey and live) | Unconditional promise (I will) |
| Temporary (shadows) | Eternal (reality) |
| National (Israel) | Universal (all nations) |

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6]]**: "But as it is, Christ has obtained a ministry that is as much more excellent than the old as the covenant he mediates is better, since it is enacted on better promises."

## E. The Church as Eschatological Community

**Already Tasting Age to Come:**
- **[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 4|Hebrews 6:4-5]]**: "Those who... have tasted the heavenly gift, and have shared in the Holy Spirit, and have tasted the goodness of the word of God and the powers of the age to come"
- **Not Merely Future**: Age to come has invaded present age—we experience it NOW
- **Miracles/Signs**: "Signs of a true apostle were performed... with signs and wonders and mighty works" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 12#2 Corinthians 12 . 12|2 Corinthians 12:12]])

**Corporate Last Adam:**
- **Christ = Head**: "He is the head of the body, the church" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]])
- **Church = Body**: "You are the body of Christ and individually members of it" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 12#1 Corinthians 12 . 27|1 Corinthians 12:27]])
- **One New Man**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 15|Ephesians 2:15]]—corporate humanity united in Christ

**Beale**: "The Church is not merely an organization but the **organism** of new creation—Christ's resurrection life pulsating through His body, making us partakers of divine nature ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 1#2 Peter 1 . 4|2 Peter 1:4]])."
