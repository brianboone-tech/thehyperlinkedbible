# 1.1 - God's Creation Cycle

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1 - Creation|← Back to 1 - Creation]]

---

## Overview

The eight-part structure of God's creation week, establishing the patterns and order that will be recapitulated throughout redemptive history. Each element anticipates the Adamic commission and its fulfillment in Christ.

---

## The Eight Elements

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.1 - Creation - Genesis 1.1|1.1.1 - Creation - Genesis 1:1]]
"In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth."

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.2 - Chaos - Genesis 1.2|1.1.2 - Chaos - Genesis 1:2]]
"The earth was without form and void, and darkness was over the face of the deep."

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.3 - Fruitful and Multiply - Genesis 1.11-25|1.1.3 - Fruitful and Multiply - Genesis 1:11-25 (2nd 3 days of Creation)]]
Plants, trees, sea creatures, birds, land animals - filling the earth with life.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.4 - Fill the earth - Genesis 1.11-25|1.1.4 - Fill the earth - Genesis 1:11-25 (2nd 3 days of Creation)]]
The second three days populate what the first three days prepared.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.5 - Rule over chaos - Genesis 1.3-10|1.1.5 - Rule over chaos - Genesis 1:3-10 (1st 3 days of Creation)]]
Light, sky/waters, land/seas - God exercises dominion over chaos.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.6 - Subdue the chaos - Genesis 1.3-10|1.1.6 - Subdue the chaos - Genesis 1:3-10 (1st 3 days of Creation)]]
God brings order to the formless void - establishing boundaries.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.7 - Image - Genesis 1.26-28|1.1.7 - Image (Reflecting God's Activities) - Genesis 1:26-28 (6th Day of Creation)]]
Humanity created in God's image - commissioned to reflect God's creative work.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1.8 - Rest - Genesis 2.1-2|1.1.8 - Rest - Genesis 2:1-2 (7th day of Creation)]]
God's Sabbath rest - the goal toward which all creation moves.

---

## Pattern Established

This creation pattern establishes the template for all subsequent redemptive cycles:
- **Chaos** → **Order** → **Filling** → **Subduing** → **Ruling** → **Rest**

The Adamic commission ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]]) calls humanity to continue this work:
- Be **blessed**
- Be **fruitful and multiply**
- **Fill** the earth
- **Subdue** it
- **Rule** over it

This pattern will be repeated and escalated in every redemptive intrusion until its final fulfillment in Christ and the new creation.
