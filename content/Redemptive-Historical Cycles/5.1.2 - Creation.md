# 5.1.2 - Creation - Last New Creation (Revelation 21:2-22:5)

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/5.1 - Intrusion - Return of the King|← Back to 5.1]]

---

## Overview

The final and ultimate new creation - new heavens, new earth, New Jerusalem. This is not merely renovation but eschatological transformation. What God began in Genesis 1-2 reaches its glorious consummation in Revelation 21-22.

---

## The Ultimate New Creation Event

**This is the FINAL new creation - no more cycles, no more "not yet," only eternal "already":**

After the chaos of the old order passing away (Revelation 21:1a), God creates **new heavens and a new earth** where righteousness dwells ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 13|2 Peter 3:13]]). This is the culmination of all redemptive history—Eden restored and escalated beyond measure.

### **A. New Heavens and New Earth - Revelation 21:1-5:**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-2]]**:
"Then I saw **a new heaven and a new earth**, for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away, and the sea was no more. And I saw **the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God**, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband."

**Continuity and Discontinuity:**

**Continuity** - Transformation, Not Annihilation:
- **[[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 10|2 Peter 3:10-13]]**: "The earth and the works that are done on it will be exposed... we are waiting for new heavens and a new earth"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21]]**: "The creation itself will be set free from its bondage to corruption and obtain the freedom of the glory of the children of God"
- **NOT** destroyed and replaced, but **purified and renewed** (like our resurrection bodies)
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]]; 66:22**: "For behold, I create new heavens and a new earth"—same verb (בָּרָא, bara) as [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]]

**Discontinuity** - Radically New Order:
- **No more sea** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]]): Chaos permanently removed
- **No more curse** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]): [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]] fully reversed
- **No more death, mourning, crying, pain** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]]): Former things passed away
- **Righteousness dwells** ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 13|2 Peter 3:13]]): Sin cannot exist here

**Beale**: "The new creation is both a restoration of Eden and an escalation beyond it. What Adam failed to accomplish—expanding Eden to cover the earth—is now achieved. The entire cosmos becomes God's temple-garden."

### **B. New Jerusalem Descends - The City-Temple:**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]], 9-27 - The Bride-City:**

"And I saw the holy city, **new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God**, prepared as **a bride adorned for her husband**."

**Key Features of New Jerusalem:**

**1. Descends from Heaven:**
- **Not built by human hands**: God's city, not man's Babel
- **From above**: Origin is divine, not earthly
- **Fulfills Abraham's hope**: "He was looking forward to the city that has foundations, whose designer and builder is God" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 10|Hebrews 11:10]])

**2. Cube Shape = Holy of Holies Expanded:**
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 16|Revelation 21:16]]**: "Its length and width and height are equal"—12,000 stadia cubed
- **[[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 6#1 Kings 6 . 20|1 Kings 6:20]]**: Holy of Holies was a perfect cube (20 cubits × 20 × 20)
- **The ENTIRE CITY is now the Most Holy Place**: All inhabitants are priests with full access to God's presence

**Beale's Temple Theology Culminates**: "Eden was the first temple, where God walked with Adam. The Tabernacle and Temple were types pointing forward. Christ's body was the true temple ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]]). The Church is the living temple ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]). Now the **entire new creation is the temple**, with no separate structure needed ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]])."

**3. Dimensions Echo Perfection:**
- **12,000 stadia**: 12 (Israel's tribes) × 1,000 (completeness) = fullness of God's people
- **144 cubits wall** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 17|Revelation 21:17]]): 12 × 12 = perfect security
- **12 gates, 12 foundations**: Named for tribes and apostles—OT and NT united
- **Precious stones**: Echoes Aaron's breastplate ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 17|Exodus 28:17-20]]) and Eden's garden ([[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 13|Ezekiel 28:13]])

**4. Gates Never Shut:**
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 25|Revelation 21:25]]**: "Its gates will never be shut by day—and there will be no night there"
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 60#Isaiah 60 . 11|Isaiah 60:11]]**: "Your gates shall be open continually; day and night they shall not be shut"
- **Eternal security**: No enemies to guard against
- **Perpetual access**: Come and go freely in God's presence

**5. Nations Bring Glory:**
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]]**: "The nations will walk by its light, and the kings of the earth will bring their glory into it... They will bring into it the glory and the honor of the nations"
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 72#Psalms 72 . 10|Psalm 72:10-11]]**: "May the kings of Tarshish and of the coastlands render him tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba bring gifts!"
- **Great Commission fulfilled**: All nations discipled, bringing their cultural glories redeemed
- **Not absorption but transformation**: Diverse peoples retain identity while united in worship

**6. No Temple in the City:**
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]**: "And I saw no temple in the city, for its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb"
- **Temple's PURPOSE fulfilled**: To mediate God's presence—now God dwells DIRECTLY with His people
- **No separation**: No veil, no court of Gentiles, no Most Holy Place accessible only to high priest once a year
- **Whole city is Holy of Holies**: Perfect, unmediated communion

### **C. God Dwells with His People - Immanuel Consummated:**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3-4]] - The Central Promise:**

"Behold, **the dwelling place of God is with man**. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and **God himself will be with them as their God**. He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and **death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore, for the former things have passed away**."

**The Tabernacle/Temple Pattern Fulfilled:**

**Old Testament Trajectory:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 8|Genesis 3:8]]**: God walked in the garden with Adam—intimate presence
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8]]**: "Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell in their midst"
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]]**: Glory cloud filled the Tabernacle
- **[[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 10|1 Kings 8:10-11]]**: Glory filled Solomon's Temple
- **Ezekiel 10**: Glory departed due to Israel's sin—exile
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 1|Ezekiel 43:1-5]]**: Vision of glory returning—future restoration

**New Testament Reality:**
- **[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]**: "The Word became flesh and **dwelt** (ἐσκήνωσεν, eskenosen = 'tabernacled') among us"
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 23|Matthew 1:23]]**: "They shall call his name Immanuel (which means, God with us)"
- **[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16]]**: "We are the temple of the living God; as God said, 'I will make my dwelling among them'"

**Consummation Arrival:**
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]**: σκηνή (skene = "dwelling/tabernacle") of God is with man
- **NO MORE SEPARATION**: What was mediated is now immediate
- **Face-to-face fellowship**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 12|1 Corinthians 13:12]], "Then I shall know fully, even as I have been fully known"
- **Eternal communion**: The goal of all creation history achieved

**"He Will Wipe Away Every Tear":**
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]]**: "He will swallow up death forever; and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from all faces"
- **Personal, tender care**: God Himself (not angels) wipes tears
- **All sorrow ended**: Every grief from the fall reversed
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 17|Revelation 7:17]]**: The Lamb will be their shepherd and guide them to springs of living water

### **D. No More Curse - Genesis 3 Fully Reversed:**

**Revelation 22:3a**:
"**No longer will there be anything accursed**, but the throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will worship him."

**The Curse of Genesis 3:14-19 Undone:**

**1. Ground No Longer Cursed:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-18]]**: "Cursed is the ground because of you; in pain you shall eat of it... thorns and thistles it shall bring forth"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]**: κατάθεμα (katathema = "curse") no longer exists
- **No more frustration in labor**: Work without toil ([[Readable Bible/21 - Ecclesiastes/Ecclesiastes 2#Ecclesiastes 2 . 22|Ecclesiastes 2:22-23]] reversed)

**2. Death Abolished:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 19|Genesis 3:19]]**: "By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread, till you return to the ground"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]]**: "Death shall be no more"
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 26|1 Corinthians 15:26]]**: "The last enemy to be destroyed is death"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 23|Romans 6:23]]**: "The wages of sin is death"—sin removed, therefore death removed

**3. Pain and Suffering Ended:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 16|Genesis 3:16]]**: "I will surely multiply your pain in childbearing; in pain you shall bring forth children"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]]**: "Neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 22|Romans 8:22]]**: "The whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth"—groaning ended

**4. Separation from God Healed:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 23|Genesis 3:23-24]]**: "The LORD God sent him out from the garden of Eden... He drove out the man, and at the east of the garden of Eden he placed the cherubim and a flaming sword"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 4|Revelation 22:4]]**: "They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads"
- **Access to God's presence fully restored—no cherubim guarding entry**

### **E. Tree of Life Accessible - Eden Restored and Escalated:**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1-2]]**:
"Then the angel showed me **the river of the water of life**, bright as crystal, flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb through the middle of the street of the city; also, on either side of the river, **the tree of life with its twelve kinds of fruit**, yielding its fruit each month. **The leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations**."

**Eden Imagery Fulfilled:**

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 9|Genesis 2:9-10]]**:
"And out of the ground the LORD God made to spring up every tree that is pleasant to the sight and good for food. **The tree of life was in the midst of the garden**, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. A river flowed out of Eden to water the garden."

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|Genesis 3:22-24]]**:
"Then the LORD God said, 'Behold, the man has become like one of us in knowing good and evil. Now, lest he reach out his hand and take also of the tree of life and eat, and live forever'—therefore the LORD God sent him out from the garden of Eden... He drove out the man, and at the east of the garden of Eden he placed the cherubim and a flaming sword that turned every way **to guard the way to the tree of life**."

**Restoration and Escalation:**

**1. River of Life:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 10|Genesis 2:10]]**: One river from Eden divided into four
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 1|Revelation 22:1]]**: River flows "from the throne of God and of the Lamb"—source is God Himself
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 1|Ezekiel 47:1-12]]**: Temple vision with river bringing life—now fulfilled
- **[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 7#John 7 . 38|John 7:38]]**: "Rivers of living water will flow from within him"—Christ is the source

**2. Tree of Life - No Longer Forbidden:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 24|Genesis 3:24]]**: Cherubim guarded access—humanity barred
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]]**: Tree accessible to all inhabitants—curse removed
- **[[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 18|Proverbs 3:18]]**: "She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her"—wisdom personified
- **Now LITERAL access**: What was lost in Eden is restored in new creation

**3. Twelve Kinds of Fruit:**
- **Monthly yield**: Perpetual abundance, no seasons of scarcity
- **12 fruits**: Corresponds to 12 tribes, 12 apostles, 12 gates—complete provision
- **[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 12|Ezekiel 47:12]]**: "Their fruit will be for food, and their leaves for healing"—fulfilled here

**4. Healing of the Nations:**
- **NOT** healing from sickness (no sickness exists, [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]])
- **θεραπείαν (therapeian)**: Health, vitality, flourishing
- **Cultural restoration**: Nations redeemed, not abolished
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24]]**: "The nations will walk by its light"—ethnic diversity preserved, sin purged

**Beale**: "The tree of life represents eternal, abundant life flowing from God's presence. In Eden, Adam and Eve had ACCESS but never ate—now the saints feast perpetually. This is escalation: Eden was good, but the new creation is glorious beyond measure."

### **F. No More Night, No Need of Sun or Moon:**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]; 22:5**:
"And the city has no need of sun or moon to shine on it, **for the glory of God gives it light, and its lamp is the Lamb**... And night will be no more. They will need no light of lamp or sun, **for the Lord God will be their light**, and they will reign forever and ever."

**God's Glory as Light:**

**Old Testament Background:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]]**: "Let there be light"—light created BEFORE sun/moon ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 14|Genesis 1:14-19]])
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 21|Exodus 13:21]]**: Pillar of fire guided Israel by night—God's presence as light
- **[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34-35]]**: Glory cloud filled the Tabernacle—Shekinah glory
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 27#Psalms 27 . 1|Psalm 27:1]]**: "The LORD is my light and my salvation"

**Prophetic Fulfillment:**
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 60#Isaiah 60 . 19|Isaiah 60:19-20]]**: "The sun shall be no more your light by day, nor for brightness shall the moon give you light; but the LORD will be your everlasting light, and your God will be your glory. Your sun shall no more go down, nor your moon withdraw itself; for the LORD will be your everlasting light"
- **[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 6|Zechariah 14:6-7]]**: "On that day there shall be no light, cold, or frost. And there shall be a unique day... at evening time there shall be light"

**New Creation Reality:**
- **No created light needed**: God's uncreated glory illuminates all
- **No darkness**: Night = absence of light; where God is, there is no absence
- **No shadows**: Perfect, unmediated light
- **The Lamb is the lamp**: Christ's glory radiates throughout the city

**Theological Significance:**
- **Unmediated glory**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 20|Exodus 33:20]], "You cannot see my face, for man shall not see me and live"—NOW we can endure His glory because we are glorified
- **Perpetual day**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 84#Psalms 84 . 11|Psalm 84:11]], "The LORD God is a sun and shield"—metaphor becomes reality
- **No fear of night**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 91#Psalms 91 . 5|Psalm 91:5]], "You will not fear the terror of the night"—night itself abolished

- **Perfect humanity**: What Adam was meant to be, we will be in Christ

**[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 6|Isaiah 11:6-9]] - Harmony Restored:**

**Perfect Ecosystem:**
- **No predation**: Death removed from all creation

**[[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]**:
"For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea."

## Beale's Temple Theology - The Consummation

**The Trajectory of God's Dwelling:**

1. **Eden** (Genesis 2-3): God walks in the garden-temple with Adam
2. **Tabernacle** (Exodus 25-40): God dwells in tent among Israel
3. **Temple** (1 Kings 6-8): Permanent house, but still localized
4. **Christ** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]): "The Word became flesh and tabernacled among us"
5. **Church** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]]; [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]]): Living temple of the Spirit
6. **New Creation** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]], 22): **ENTIRE CREATION is God's temple**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]]**:
"And I saw no temple in the city, for **its temple is the Lord God the Almighty and the Lamb**."

**Beale's Conclusion**: "The goal of redemptive history is not escape FROM creation but the TRANSFORMATION of creation into God's cosmic temple. Heaven and earth merge—no more separation. The new Jerusalem 'comes down' ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]]) because heaven invades earth permanently. This is the ultimate intrusion—the Consummation fully breaks in and remains forever."

**Eden Escalated:**
- **Eden**: Small garden, one couple, probation period, tree forbidden
- **New Jerusalem**: Cosmic city, innumerable multitudes, eternal security, tree accessible
- **Eden**: Potential for sin and fall
- **New Jerusalem**: Confirmed in righteousness, sin impossible

## Kline's Intrusion Framework - Common Grace Ended

**The Periphery of Delay Collapses:**

Throughout history, Kline argues, there's been a tension:
- **Core**: The eschatological reality—Consummation/Judgment Day
- **Periphery of Delay**: Common Grace period allowing coexistence of wheat/tares

**At the Parousia:**
- **Common Grace ENDS**: No more restraint on judgment ([[Readable Bible/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 2#2 Thessalonians 2 . 6|2 Thessalonians 2:6-7]])
- **Consummation OVERTAKES**: Becomes the ONLY reality
- **No more "not yet"**: The "already" swallows up all shadows

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 10#Revelation 10 . 6|Revelation 10:6]]**:
"There will be **no more delay**, but... the mystery of God will be fulfilled."

**In the New Creation:**
- **No probation**: Testing period permanently over
- **No mixed community**: Only the righteous dwell there ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]])
- **No temporal blessings for the wicked**: Lake of fire is their portion ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 15|Revelation 20:15]])
- **Only Consummation ethics**: Righteousness alone, no compromise with sin
