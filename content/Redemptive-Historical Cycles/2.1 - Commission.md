## Text

**"The LORD God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to work it and keep it. And the LORD God commanded the man, saying, 'You may surely eat of every tree of the garden, but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die.'"** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|Genesis 2:15-17]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Work and Keep** (עָבַד וְשָׁמַר):
- **עָבַד** (avad): To work, serve, till - same word used for priestly service in tabernacle
- **שָׁמַר** (shamar): To keep, guard, preserve - protect the garden from intrusion

**Priestly Commission**: Adam is placed in the garden-temple as priest-king

**Covenant Stipulation**: Command not to eat from the tree of knowledge of good and evil

**Test of Obedience**: Will Adam trust God's word or seek autonomy?

**Life or Death**: Clear consequences stated - obedience leads to life, disobedience brings death

**Probationary Period**: Adam is being tested for entrance into confirmed, irreversible life (Sabbath rest)

---

## Connection to Adamic Commission

This commission connects to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]]:
- **Blessed**: Adam enjoys fellowship with God
- **Be Fruitful/Multiply**: Will expand image-bearers
- **Fill**: Will extend Eden-temple throughout earth
- **Subdue**: Must guard against serpent intrusion (failed)
- **Rule**: Exercises dominion under God
