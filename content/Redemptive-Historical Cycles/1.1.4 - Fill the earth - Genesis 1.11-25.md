# 1.1.4 - Fill the earth - Genesis 1:11-25

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1 - God's Creation Cycle|← Back to 1.1]]

---

## Text

**Days 4-6**: Lights in the sky, fish in the seas, birds in the air, animals on the land.

---

## Theological Significance

**Correspondence**: Each realm prepared in days 1-3 is filled in days 4-6
  - Day 1 (light) → Day 4 (sun, moon, stars)
  - Day 2 (waters/sky) → Day 5 (fish, birds)
  - Day 3 (land) → Day 6 (land animals, humans)

**Divine Presence**: God's glory filling His creation

**Temple Pattern**: Like the tabernacle/temple, creation is ordered space filled with God's presence

**Adamic Commission**: Humanity will be commissioned to fill the earth with image-bearers
