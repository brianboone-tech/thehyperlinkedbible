## Overview

The saints' eternal commission in the new creation - the Adamic mandate finally and fully accomplished. What Adam failed to do, what Israel failed to do, what the Church has begun, the glorified saints will complete and sustain forever.

## The Fivefold Adamic Commission Finally Consummated

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-28]] - The Original Mandate:**

"Then God said, 'Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let them have **dominion** over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth.' So God created man in his own image, in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them. And God **blessed** them. And God said to them, '**Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it**, and have **dominion** over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that moves on the earth.'"

**Five Elements:**
1. **BLESSED** - Divine favor, flourishing, prosperity
2. **BE FRUITFUL AND MULTIPLY** - Expand God's image-bearers
3. **FILL THE EARTH** - Extend God's presence globally
4. **SUBDUE** - Bring creation under righteous rule
5. **RULE/HAVE DOMINION** - Exercise godly authority

**Adam's Failure:**
- **Failed to subdue**: Allowed serpent to invade and deceive
- **Failed to rule**: Abdicated responsibility, followed Eve into sin
- **Result**: Cursed, expelled from Eden, dominion lost

**Christ's Success:**
- **Second Adam** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 45|1 Corinthians 15:45-47]]): Where first Adam failed, last Adam succeeds
- **Resisted Satan**: Subdued the serpent in wilderness temptation ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]])
- **Exercised dominion**: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18]])
- **Began filling earth**: Great Commission ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19-20]])

**Church's Partial Fulfillment:**
- **Already**: Spiritual authority over demons ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 19|Luke 10:19]]), ruling with Christ ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]])
- **Not Yet**: Still resisted, still mortal, still incomplete

**Consummation - Full and Final Accomplishment:**

In the new creation, the fivefold commission reaches its eschatological culmination. The saints, as corporate Adam united to Christ the Last Adam, accomplish what the first Adam was meant to achieve.

## Work Without Toil - Edenic Labor Restored

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|Genesis 2:15]]**:
"The LORD God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden **to work it and keep it**."

**Original Work:**
- **Before the fall**: Work was joyful, purposeful, non-frustrating
- **Adam was gardener-priest**: Tending God's temple-garden
- **Cultivating beauty**: Creation under righteous stewardship

**Curse on Work:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]]**: "Cursed is the ground... in pain you shall eat of it... By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread"
- **Toil, frustration, thorns**: Work becomes burdensome
- **[[Readable Bible/21 - Ecclesiastes/Ecclesiastes 2#Ecclesiastes 2 . 22|Ecclesiastes 2:22-23]]**: "What has a man from all the toil and striving of heart with which he toils beneath the sun?... His work is full of pain"

**Restoration in New Creation:**
- **Curse removed** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]): No more thorns, sweat, frustration
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 3|Revelation 22:3]]**: "His servants will worship him"—λατρεύσουσιν (latreuousin = "serve/worship")
- **Purposeful activity**: Not idle rest but fulfilling labor
- **Perfect satisfaction**: Every task brings joy, no failure or disappointment

**Augustine**: "There we shall rest and see, see and love, love and praise. This is what shall be in the end without end."

## Eternal Sabbath Rest - The Goal Achieved

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-11]]**:
"So then, there remains a **Sabbath rest** for the people of God, for whoever has entered God's rest has also rested from his works as God did from his. Let us therefore strive to enter that rest."

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 1|Genesis 2:1-3]]**:
"Thus the heavens and the earth were finished... And on the seventh day God finished his work that he had done, and he rested on the seventh day... So God blessed the seventh day and made it holy."

**Sabbath Pattern:**
- **Creation**: Six days of work → Seventh day rest
- **Typology**: Weekly Sabbath → Eschatological Sabbath
- **Hebrews 4**: Canaan rest was type, Christ's rest is antitype

**Eternal Sabbath:**
- **Work completed**: Redemption finished ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]], "It is finished")
- **Rest entered**: Not cessation of activity but perfect fulfillment
- **No more striving**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 10|Hebrews 4:10]], "Rested from his works"—the battle is over
- **Worship as rest**: Joyful service without burden

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28-30]]**:
"Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and **you will find rest for your souls**."

**Consummation**: The rest Jesus promised is fully realized—souls at perfect peace, bodies glorified, work without weariness, joy without end.

## Commission Fulfilled - The Victory Complete

**The Pattern Across Scripture:**

1. **Creation**: God creates and commissions Adam
2. **Fall**: Adam fails the commission
3. **Redemption**: Christ accomplishes the commission as Last Adam
4. **Church**: Saints participate in commission (already/not yet)
5. **Consummation**: Saints complete commission eternally

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 11#Revelation 11 . 15|Revelation 11:15]]**:
"The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ, and **he shall reign forever and ever**."

**Beale's Conclusion**: "The new creation is the culmination of the creation mandate. Adam was to expand the borders of Eden until the glory of God filled the earth. Christ began this work, the Church continues it, and in the new creation it is eternally accomplished. The whole earth is now Eden—God's temple-presence fills all."

**Kline's Framework**: "The Adamic commission was given under probation—succeed and enter eternal life, fail and face exile. Christ succeeded where Adam failed. Now, in the Consummation, probation is over, and the saints enjoy the reward of the commission without possibility of failure."
