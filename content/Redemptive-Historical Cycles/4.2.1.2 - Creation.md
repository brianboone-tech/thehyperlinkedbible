# 4.2.1.2 - Creation - Escalated New Creation in Christ

---

## Overview

The ultimate new creation event - Christ's life, death, and resurrection inaugurate the age to come.

---

**The Ultimate New Creation Event:**

This is not merely another creation moment—this is the **eschatological new creation** breaking into history. What was only anticipated in previous intrusions now becomes reality.

### **A. Christ's Life as New Creation:**

**Incarnation = New Creation Begins:**
- **"The Word became flesh"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]): Divine presence tabernacles among us
- **Virgin Birth**: Creation without human agency (echoes Genesis 1 creation ex nihilo principle)
- **"Firstborn of all creation"** ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]): Not first created thing, but preeminent over creation as Last Adam

**Baptism = New Creation Commissioning:**
- **Spirit Descends** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 16|Matthew 3:16]]): Like [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]], Spirit hovers over waters
- **Father Declares**: "This is my beloved Son" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 17|Matthew 3:17]])—echoes Genesis 1 divine declarations
- **Heaven Opens**: Temple veil between heaven/earth torn open (prefiguring crucifixion)

**Wilderness Temptation = New Adam Succeeds Where First Adam Failed:**
- **40 Days** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]]): Recapitulates Israel's 40 years (and Adam's probation)
- **Tempted with Food**: Unlike Adam, Christ does not eat forbidden fruit
- **Satan Defeated**: "The prince of this world is cast out" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 31|John 12:31]])
- **Angels Minister** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 11|Matthew 4:11]]): Eden's harmony with creation restored

**Ministry = New Creation Manifestations:**
- **Healing the Sick**: Undoing curse effects ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 35#Isaiah 35 . 5|Isaiah 35:5-6]] fulfilled)
- **Raising the Dead**: Reversing death's reign ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 25|John 11:25]]—"I am the resurrection")
- **Calming Storms**: Authority over chaos ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 4#Mark 4 . 39|Mark 4:39]]—echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] ordering chaos)
- **Feeding Multitudes**: New manna in wilderness (John 6—greater than Moses)
- **Walking on Water**: Mastery over primordial chaos ([[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 9#Job 9 . 8|Job 9:8]]; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 77#Psalms 77 . 19|Psalm 77:19]])

### **B. Christ's Death as De-Creation/Re-Creation:**

**Beale**: "The crucifixion is simultaneously de-creation (judgment) and new creation (resurrection life)."

**Good Friday = Cosmic De-Creation:**
- **Darkness at Noon** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 45|Matthew 27:45]]): Reversal of Genesis 1:3 "Let there be light"
- **Temple Veil Torn** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 51|Matthew 27:51]]): Access to Holy of Holies opened
- **Earth Quakes**: Creation convulses ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 51|Matthew 27:51]])
- **Rocks Split**: Even geological order fractures
- **Tombs Open**: Death's grip begins to loosen ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 52|Matthew 27:52-53]])
- **"It is finished"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]]): Completion of new creation work (echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 1|Genesis 2:1-2]])

**Kline's Intrusion Ethics**: Christ experiences the **full judgment of the Consummation**—the holy wrath reserved for the final day is poured out on Him in history. "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 46|Matthew 27:46]])—He enters eschatological exile FOR us.

**The Great Exchange:**
- Christ becomes **curse** for us ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]])
- We become **righteousness** of God in Him ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]])
- He experiences **exile** (forsaken by Father)
- We receive **restoration** (brought near to God)

### **C. Christ's Resurrection as New Creation Accomplished:**

**Easter = New Creation Dawns:**

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a **new creation**. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]])

**Beale's Key Insight**: Resurrection is not resuscitation but **eschatological transformation**—the age to come has invaded this present evil age.

**Firstfruits Language:**
- **"Firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep"** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 20|1 Corinthians 15:20]]): Harvest has begun
- **"Firstborn from the dead"** ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 18|Colossians 1:18]]): Pioneer of new creation
- **"Beginning of God's new creation"** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 14|Revelation 3:14]]): Alpha of eschatological order

**New Creation Realities in Resurrection:**
- **Transformed Body**: Physical yet glorified ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 36|Luke 24:36-43]]; [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 21|Philippians 3:21]])
- **Death Defeated**: "Death is swallowed up in victory" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 54|1 Corinthians 15:54]])
- **Imperishable Life**: "He is risen indeed" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 34|Luke 24:34]])—irreversible transformation
- **Spirit Outpoured**: "He breathed on them and said, 'Receive the Holy Spirit'" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 20#John 20 . 22|John 20:22]])—echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 7|Genesis 2:7]]

**Temple Imagery** (Beale):
- **His Body is the Temple**: "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 19|John 2:19-21]])
- **Resurrection = Temple Rebuilt**: Where God dwells fully with humanity
- **Greater than Solomon's Temple**: Contains the fullness of deity bodily ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 9|Colossians 2:9]])

### **D. Christ's Ascension as New Creation Enthroned:**

**Exaltation = Messianic Reign Begins:**
- **Seated at Right Hand** ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 20|Ephesians 1:20]]): Davidic throne established in heaven
- **All Authority Given** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 18|Matthew 28:18]]): [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] Son of Man receives kingdom
- **Name Above All Names** ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 9|Philippians 2:9-11]]): Universal recognition (eschatological reality)
- **High Priest in Heavenly Temple** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1-2]]): Melchizedekian order

**Already/Not Yet**:
- **Already**: New creation has begun—resurrection inaugurated
- **Not Yet**: Full consummation awaits—creation still groans
- **Intrusion Intensity**: The "age to come" has broken into "this present age" with unprecedented force

**Core vs. Periphery Collapses**: In Christ, what was hidden core in OT types becomes visible reality. He IS the temple (not symbol), He IS the true Israel (not shadow), He IS the Sabbath rest (not pointer).
