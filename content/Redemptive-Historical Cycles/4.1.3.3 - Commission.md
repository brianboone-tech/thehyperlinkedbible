# 4.1.3.3 - Commission - Noah as New Adam (Genesis 9:1-7)

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.1.3 - Intrusion - The Flood and Tower of Babel|← Back to 4.1.3]]

---

## Overview

After the flood's re-creation, God commissions Noah exactly as He commissioned Adam in Genesis 1:28. Noah is a **new Adam** - the head of a renewed humanity, given the mandate to "be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth." Yet this commission comes with sobering additions: permission to eat meat, the institution of capital punishment, and a covenant promising never again to flood the earth. These new provisions recognize the post-fall, post-flood reality - humanity's hearts remain unchanged ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 21|Genesis 8:21]]), so God adds structures to restrain sin and sustain creation. The rainbow covenant establishes **common grace** - God's commitment to preserve the world despite ongoing rebellion.

**Key Theme**: Noah as corporate Adam; creation commission renewed; new provisions for restraining sin; covenant of common grace.

## The Adamic Commission Restated

### 1. Blessed

**Genesis 1:28 - Adam Blessed**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - "And God blessed them. And God said to them..."

**Genesis 9:1 - Noah Blessed**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]] - "And God blessed Noah and his sons and said to them..."

**Significance**: The language is nearly identical. Just as God blessed Adam and Eve at creation, He now blesses Noah and his sons at re-creation. This blessing is God's **empowerment** to fulfill the commission - it is not mere wish but divine enablement.

**Blessing Throughout Scripture**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Genesis 12:2]] - "I will bless you" (Abraham)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 26#Genesis 26 . 3|Genesis 26:3]] - "I will be with you and bless you" (Isaac)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 3|Genesis 28:3]] - "God Almighty bless you" (Jacob)
- [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 3|Ephesians 1:3]] - "Blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing"

God's blessing is His favor resting on His chosen representatives, enabling them to carry out His purposes.

### 2. Be Fruitful and Multiply

**Genesis 1:28 - First Commission**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - "And God said to them, 'Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth.'"

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]], 7 - Commission Repeated Twice**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]] - "Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth"
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 7|Genesis 9:7]] - "And you, be fruitful and multiply, increase greatly on the earth and multiply in it"

**Emphasis Through Repetition**: The doubling of this command (verses 1 and 7, bracketing the new provisions) emphasizes its importance. God wants the earth **filled** with humanity. The flood reduced humanity to eight people; now God commissions them to repopulate the planet.

**Beale's Insight**: This is the **creation mandate renewed**. Despite human sin necessitating the flood, God does not abandon His original purpose. He still wants the earth filled with His image-bearers, subduing creation and ruling for His glory.

**The Pattern Continues**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - Adam and Eve
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]], 7 - Noah and sons (repeated)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 2|Genesis 12:2]] - Abraham ("I will make of you a great nation")
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 17#Genesis 17 . 6|Genesis 17:6]] - Abraham ("I will make you exceedingly fruitful")
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 26#Genesis 26 . 4|Genesis 26:4]] - Isaac ("I will multiply your offspring")
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 3|Genesis 28:3]] - Jacob ("that you may...become a company of peoples")
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 11|Genesis 35:11]] - Jacob ("Be fruitful and multiply")

The commission to multiply is threaded through redemptive history, pointing ultimately to Christ and the multiplication of spiritual seed (the church) through the Great Commission ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19-20]]).

### 3. Fill the Earth

**Genesis 1:28 - Fill the Earth**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - "Fill the earth and subdue it"

**Genesis 9:1 - Fill the Earth Renewed**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]] - "Fill the earth"

**Genesis 11:4 - Babel's Refusal**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 4|Genesis 11:4]] - "Lest we be dispersed over the face of the whole earth" (direct disobedience)

**Significance**: God wants humanity spread across the globe, not concentrated in one rebellious city. The earth is designed to be **filled** with God's image, displaying His glory in every corner of creation. Babel will attempt to resist this command, leading to forced dispersion ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 8|Genesis 11:8-9]]).

### 4. Subdue and Rule Over Creation

**Genesis 1:28 - Subdue and Rule**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] - "And subdue it and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that moves on the earth."

**Genesis 9:2 - Dominion with Fear**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 2|Genesis 9:2]] - "The fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth and upon every bird of the heavens, upon everything that creeps on the ground and all the fish of the sea. Into your hand they are delivered."

**Critical Difference**: In Genesis 1, Adam's rule was harmonious. Now, post-fall and post-flood, animals **fear** humans. Dominion continues, but with **tension**. This is a degraded version of the original commission.

**Theological Significance**:
- **Pre-Fall**: Harmony between humanity and creation (Adam names animals, no violence)
- **Post-Fall**: Enmity introduced ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] - enmity between seed of woman and serpent)
- **Post-Flood**: Fear and dread govern the relationship

The creation groans under the weight of sin ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 19|Romans 8:19-22]]), waiting for final redemption when the curse is lifted and harmony is restored ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 6|Isaiah 11:6-9]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 25|Isaiah 65:25]]).

## New Elements Post-Flood: Restraining Sin

The post-flood world requires **additional provisions** to restrain humanity's wicked heart. God institutes three new measures:

### 1. Permission to Eat Meat (Genesis 9:3)

**Pre-Flood Diet - Vegetarian**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 29|Genesis 1:29]] - "Behold, I have given you every plant yielding seed that is on the face of all the earth, and every tree with seed in its fruit. You shall have them for food."
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 30|Genesis 1:30]] - Animals also ate plants

**Post-Flood Permission - Carnivorous**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 3|Genesis 9:3]] - "Every moving thing that lives shall be food for you. And as I gave you the green plants, I give you everything."

**Why the Change?**

Several interpretations:
1. **Accommodation to fallen world**: In a world scarred by sin and the flood's devastation, meat provides necessary nutrition
2. **Symbolic of death's reality**: Eating animals reminds humanity of the consequences of sin (death entered through the fall)
3. **Foreshadowing sacrifice**: The shedding of blood points to the sacrificial system and ultimately to Christ

**Restriction on Blood**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 4|Genesis 9:4]] - "But you shall not eat flesh with its life, that is, its blood."

**Significance of Blood**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]] - "For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it for you on the altar to make atonement for your souls, for it is the blood that makes atonement by the life."
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 14|Leviticus 17:14]] - "For the life of every creature is its blood"

Blood represents **life** - it is sacred because life belongs to God. This prohibition foreshadows the entire sacrificial system where blood is used for atonement. The restriction teaches reverence for life and prepares humanity for the ultimate blood sacrifice - Christ's.

### 2. Capital Punishment Instituted (Genesis 9:5-6)

**Genesis 9:5-6 - Life for Life**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 5|Genesis 9:5-6]] - "And for your lifeblood I will require a reckoning: from every beast I will require it and from man. From his fellow man I will require a reckoning for the life of man. Whoever sheds the blood of man, by man shall his blood be shed, for God made man in his own image."

**Three Key Elements**:

**1. God Requires a Reckoning**: Human life is so valuable that God Himself demands an accounting for every life taken. This is not vigilante justice but divinely mandated justice.

**2. By Man Shall His Blood Be Shed**: God delegates to human society the responsibility to execute murderers. This is the **foundation of civil government** - God authorizes humans to administer justice.

**3. Because God Made Man in His Image**: The basis for capital punishment is the **imago Dei**. To murder a human is to destroy God's image, an act of cosmic treason. Life is sacred because humans bear God's image.

**Contrast with Cain**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 15|Genesis 4:15]] - God protected Cain from being killed, despite his murder of Abel
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 6|Genesis 9:6]] - Now God institutes capital punishment for murder

**Why the Difference?** Before the flood, God restrained human vengeance (Cain's mark). After the flood, recognizing the depth of human wickedness ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Genesis 6:5]]), God establishes formal justice structures to **restrain evil**. Capital punishment serves as a deterrent and demonstrates that life is sacred.

**Theological Implications**:
- **Human government begins here** - God delegates judicial authority to humanity
- **Justice reflects God's character** - God is just; human justice mirrors divine justice
- **Life is sacred** - murder is the ultimate crime because it destroys God's image
- **Foreshadows Christ** - The innocent One will bear capital punishment for our sin ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 8|Isaiah 53:8]]; [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 16|John 19:16-18]])

**NT Affirmation of Civil Government**:
- [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 13#Romans 13 . 1|Romans 13:1-4]] - "Let every person be subject to the governing authorities... the authority does not bear the sword in vain. For he is the servant of God, an avenger who carries out God's wrath on the wrongdoer."
- [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 13|1 Peter 2:13-14]] - "Be subject for the Lord's sake to every human institution... to punish those who do evil and to praise those who do good"

Civil government is a **common grace institution** - established to restrain sin and promote order in a fallen world.

### 3. Sanctity of Blood Established (Genesis 9:4)

**Genesis 9:4 - Do Not Eat Blood**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 4|Genesis 9:4]] - "But you shall not eat flesh with its life, that is, its blood."

**Why Prohibit Eating Blood?**

**Theological Reason**: Blood represents **life**, and life belongs to God. To consume blood is to treat life casually, as if it were a mere commodity. God reserves blood for sacred purposes - atonement.

**Connections to Sacrificial System**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 11|Leviticus 17:11]] - "For the life of the flesh is in the blood, and I have given it for you on the altar to make atonement for your souls"
- [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 22|Hebrews 9:22]] - "Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness of sins"

**Fulfillment in Christ**:
- [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 28|Matthew 26:28]] - "This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins"
- [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 18|1 Peter 1:18-19]] - "You were ransomed... not with perishable things such as silver or gold, but with the precious blood of Christ"
- [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]] - "Worthy are you... for you were slain, and by your blood you ransomed people for God"

The prohibition on eating blood points to the ultimate shedding of blood - Christ's sacrifice on the cross.

## The Covenant Established (Genesis 9:8-17)

### God's Unconditional Promise

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 8|Genesis 9:8-11]] - Never Again a Flood**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 8|Genesis 9:8-9]] - "Then God said to Noah and to his sons with him, 'Behold, I establish my covenant with you and your offspring after you'"
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 11|Genesis 9:11]] - "I establish my covenant with you, that never again shall all flesh be cut off by the waters of the flood, and never again shall there be a flood to destroy the earth."

**Unconditional Covenant**: This is a **unilateral** covenant - God makes a promise with no conditions attached. Regardless of human behavior, God commits to never again destroy the earth by flood.

**Parties to the Covenant**:
- Noah and his sons
- Their offspring (all humanity)
- Every living creature ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 10|Genesis 9:10]])

This is a **cosmic** covenant - it includes all creation. God covenants not only with humanity but with the earth itself.

### The Rainbow Sign

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 12|Genesis 9:12-17]] - Sign of the Covenant**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 12|Genesis 9:12-13]] - "This is the sign of the covenant that I make between me and you and every living creature... I have set my bow in the cloud"
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 14|Genesis 9:14-16]] - "When I bring clouds over the earth and the bow is seen in the clouds, I will remember my covenant... When the bow is in the clouds, I will see it and remember the everlasting covenant"

**Significance of the Rainbow**:
- **Visible Sign**: The rainbow appears after storms, when rain clouds and sunlight interact - a reminder that God restrains judgment
- **God Remembers**: The language "I will remember" indicates God's covenant faithfulness (see [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 1|Genesis 8:1]])
- **Everlasting Covenant**: This covenant lasts as long as the earth endures

**Theological Depth**: Some commentators suggest the "bow" (קֶשֶׁת, *qeshet*) is the same word used for a warrior's bow. The imagery may be of God **hanging up His bow** - laying aside His weapon of judgment. After the flood, God symbolically declares He will not "shoot" His arrows of judgment again by global flood.

**Covenantal Pattern**:
- **Sign given**: Rainbow
- **Promise made**: Never again a flood
- **Parties named**: God, Noah, all living creatures
- **Duration**: Everlasting

This pattern will be repeated in subsequent covenants (circumcision for Abraham, Sabbath for Moses, etc.).

## Theological Themes

### 1. Noah as New Adam (Corporate Representation)

**Beale's Corporate Adam Framework**: Noah functions as a **federal head** - a representative whose actions affect those he represents. Just as Adam's sin brought death to all ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 12|Romans 5:12]]), Noah's obedience brings preservation to his household.

**Comparison Chart**:

| Adam (Genesis 1-3) | Noah (Genesis 6-9) | Christ (NT) |
|-------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Blessed (1:28) | Blessed (9:1) | Blessed (Eph 1:3) |
| Be fruitful, multiply (1:28) | Be fruitful, multiply (9:1, 7) | Spiritual multiplication (Matt 28:19) |
| Fill the earth (1:28) | Fill the earth (9:1) | Fill earth with disciples ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]) |
| Subdue, rule (1:28) | Dominion with fear (9:2) | All authority given (Matt 28:18) |
| Disobeyed, brought curse | Obeyed, preserved remnant | Obeyed perfectly, brought blessing (Rom 5:19) |
| Nakedness shameful (3:7) | Nakedness shameful (9:21-23) | Clothed us in righteousness (Isa 61:10) |

**Noah's Limitations**: Like Adam, Noah will fall ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 20|Genesis 9:20-27]] - drunkenness and nakedness). This demonstrates that **no human Adam can perfectly fulfill the commission**. Only Christ, the Last Adam, succeeds where all others fail.

### 2. Common Grace: Sustaining Creation Despite Sin

**Genesis 8:21-22 - God's Commitment**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 21|Genesis 8:21-22]] - "I will never again curse the ground because of man, for the intention of man's heart is evil from his youth... While the earth remains, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night, shall not cease."

**The Paradox**:
- **Before flood**: Hearts are evil → Therefore judge
- **After flood**: Hearts are evil → Therefore show patience

**What Is Common Grace?**

Common grace is God's **undeserved favor** shown to all humanity (believer and unbeliever) by:
- Sustaining creation (seasons, agriculture)
- Restraining evil (government, conscience)
- Providing temporal blessings (rain, sunshine)
- Delaying final judgment

**Kline's Intrusion Framework**: The flood was an **intrusion** of eschatological judgment into history. Now God withdraws that intrusion and establishes a period of common grace - a "delay" before the final judgment. This delay provides time for:
- The church to grow
- The gospel to spread
- Sinners to repent

**NT Confirmation**:
- [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 45|Matthew 5:45]] - "He makes his sun rise on the evil and on the good, and sends rain on the just and on the unjust"
- [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 9|2 Peter 3:9]] - "The Lord is not slow to fulfill his promise... but is patient toward you, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should reach repentance"

### 3. Image of God Remains Despite the Fall

**Genesis 9:6 - Image Reaffirmed**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 6|Genesis 9:6]] - "Whoever sheds the blood of man, by man shall his blood be shed, for God made man in his own image."

**Critical Theological Point**: Even after the fall, after millennia of sin, after the global judgment of the flood - **humans still bear God's image**. The *imago Dei* is marred but not erased.

**Implications**:
1. **Human dignity**: Every person has inherent worth as God's image-bearer
2. **Sanctity of life**: Murder is cosmic treason - destroying God's image
3. **Basis for justice**: Human government exists to protect image-bearers
4. **Hope for redemption**: The image can be restored through Christ

**NT Development**:
- [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11#1 Corinthians 11 . 7|1 Corinthians 11:7]] - "Man... is the image and glory of God"
- [[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 3#James 3 . 9|James 3:9]] - "People... are made in the likeness of God"
- [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 3#Colossians 3 . 10|Colossians 3:10]] - "The new self... is being renewed in knowledge after the image of its creator"

Christ is the perfect image of God ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 15|Colossians 1:15]]), and believers are being conformed to His image ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29]]).

## Christological Fulfillment

### 1. Christ as the True Adam

**Noah Failed**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 20|Genesis 9:20-23]] - Noah gets drunk, lies naked in his tent, brings shame on himself.

**Christ Succeeded**:
- [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]] - Christ resisted temptation perfectly
- [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 15|Hebrews 4:15]] - "Tempted as we are, yet without sin"
- [[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 8|Philippians 2:8]] - "He humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death"

**Romans 5:18-19 - The Contrast**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 18|Romans 5:18-19]] - "As one trespass led to condemnation for all men, so one act of righteousness leads to justification and life for all men. For as by the one man's disobedience the many were made sinners, so by the one man's obedience the many will be made righteous."

Adam (and Noah) failed; Christ succeeded. We receive the benefits of Christ's perfect obedience.

### 2. Christ's Blood Fulfills the Sacrificial System

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 4|Genesis 9:4]] - Blood Reserved**: Blood is sacred, reserved for atonement.

**Hebrews 9:12-14 - Christ's Blood**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 12|Hebrews 9:12-14]] - "He entered once for all into the holy places, not by means of the blood of goats and calves but by means of his own blood, thus securing an eternal redemption."

**1 John 1:7 - Cleansing**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 1#1 John 1 . 7|1 John 1:7]] - "The blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin."

All OT blood prohibitions and sacrifices point to the ultimate shedding of blood - Christ's death on the cross.

### 3. The Great Commission Fulfills the Creation Mandate

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]], 7 - Be Fruitful, Multiply, Fill**:
- "Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth"

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19-20]] - Make Disciples**:
- [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 19|Matthew 28:19-20]] - "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them... teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you"

**[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]] - Fill the Earth**:
- [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]] - "You will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth"

The **physical** multiplication commanded to Noah finds **spiritual** fulfillment in the church's multiplication. Christ's disciples are to fill the earth with the knowledge of God's glory as the waters cover the sea ([[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]).

## Summary: Commission Renewed, Yet Pattern Repeats

God graciously renews the creation mandate with Noah, giving humanity a fresh start. Yet the sobering additions - meat-eating, capital punishment, blood prohibitions - reveal that **sin has not been eradicated**. Human hearts remain wicked ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 21|Genesis 8:21]]). Noah himself will soon fall ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 20|Genesis 9:20-27]]), and Babel will demonstrate corporate rebellion ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 1|Genesis 11:1-4]]). The pattern continues: **commission given → sin committed → judgment follows**.

**Key Truths**:
1. **Noah is a new Adam** - commissioned to restart humanity
2. **Creation mandate renewed** - be fruitful, multiply, fill, rule
3. **New provisions restrain sin** - meat, government, blood prohibition
4. **Common grace sustains creation** - God's patience provides time for redemption
5. **Image of God remains** - human dignity and sanctity of life affirmed
6. **Christ fulfills the commission perfectly** - where all human Adams fail, the Last Adam succeeds

The rainbow covenant promises that God will sustain creation until the final judgment. But the deeper need remains: **humanity needs a new heart**, not just a new start. The commission can only be perfectly fulfilled by the One who obeys without fail - Jesus Christ, the true and final Adam.
