## Overview

The entrance of sin and judgment into God's good creation. Adam and Eve's rebellion brings curse, death, and exile from the garden-temple.

---

## Subsections

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/3.1 - Sin|3.1 - Sin - at the tree of discerning of good and evil (Genesis 3:1-13)]]
The serpent's temptation and Adam and Eve's disobedience.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/3.2 - Judgment|3.2 - Judgment]]
God's pronouncement of curse upon the serpent, the woman, the man, and the ground; exile from Eden.

---

## Key Themes

**Covenant Breaking**: Adam fails the probationary test and breaks the covenant of works

**Curse Pronounced**: Upon serpent, woman (pain in childbearing), man (toil), and ground (thorns and thistles)

**Death Enters**: Spiritual death immediate; physical death begins its reign

**Exile from Temple**: Cherubim guard the way back to the tree of life - humanity exiled from God's presence

**Proto-Evangelium**: First gospel promise - the seed of the woman will crush the serpent's head ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]])

**Decreation Begins**: The reversal of creation order - chaos reintroduced into God's good world
