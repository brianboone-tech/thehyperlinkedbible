## Overview

The ultimate intrusion where Core and Periphery merge. Christ is the climactic intrusion where the Consummation breaks through with unprecedented intensity.

**CRITICAL**: The pattern is **BROKEN** - Christ does not sin (4.2.1.4).

---

## The Fivefold Pattern

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1.1 - Chaos|4.2.1.1 - Chaos - oppression/destruction as continuing exile]]
Christ enters the chaos of continuing exile - Israel under Rome, spiritual darkness, no Davidic king, no glory cloud.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1.2 - Creation|4.2.1.2 - Creation - Escalated New Creation in Christ]]
Incarnation, baptism, wilderness victory, ministry miracles, death as de-creation/re-creation, resurrection, ascension.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1.3 - Commission|4.2.1.3 - Commission - of Christ as eschatological Israel/Son of Man/Adam]]
The fivefold Adamic commission perfectly fulfilled: Blessed, Be Fruitful, Fill Earth, Subdue Enemies, Rule Forever.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1.4 - Sin|4.2.1.4 - Sin - Christ resists sin]]
**PATTERN BROKEN**: Perfect obedience where all others failed. Active and passive obedience. Recapitulates Israel's story perfectly.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1.5 - Judgment|4.2.1.5 - Judgment - Already/Not Yet Tension]]
Spiritual restoration accomplished (Already) while physical exile continues (Not Yet). Inaugurated eschatology.
