# 5 - CONSUMMATION

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4 - Redemption|← 4 - Redemption]] | [[- Redempt[[- Redemptive-Historical Cycles|Main]]
The final return of the King and the new creation. The last intrusion when all delay ends, Common Grace ceases, and the Consummation arrives in fullness.

---

## Subsections

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/5.1 - Intrusion - Return of the King|5.1 - Intrusion - Return of the King]]
The Parousia - Christ's second coming and the final establishment of the new heavens and new earth.

---

## Key Themes

**No More Delay**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 10#Revelation 10 . 6|Revelation 10:6]] - "There will be no more delay"

**Pattern Completed**: The fivefold cycle reaches its ultimate and final fulfillment

**Common Grace Ends**: The age of gospel proclamation ceases; final separation occurs

**New Creation Consummated**: Revelation 21-22 - New heavens, new earth, New Jerusalem

**Presence Fully Restored**: "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]])

**Temple Fills All**: The entire new creation becomes God's dwelling - no temple needed ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 22|Revelation 21:22]])

**Eden Restored and Escalated**: Tree of life accessible again, but in glorified city-garden

**Sabbath Rest Entered**: The eternal rest that all previous Sabbaths pointed toward

**Commission Fulfilled**: God's glory fills the entire earth as the saints reign with Christ forever
