# 4.2 - The Delay - The Age of Fulfillment

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4 - Redemption|← Back to 4 - Redemption]]

---

## Overview

New Testament fulfillment in Jesus Christ and the Church. The pattern is broken in Christ who does not sin, inaugurating the age to come.

---

## The Two Intrusions

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1 - Intrusion - Jesus and the Kingdom|4.2.1 - Intrusion - Jesus and the Kingdom]]
Christ perfectly accomplishes what all previous Adams failed to do. **The pattern is broken** - He does not sin.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.2 - Intrusion - People of the Kingdom|4.2.2 - Intrusion - People of the Kingdom]]
The Church participates in Christ's already/not yet tension, continuing the Great Commission until He returns.

---

## Key Theme

**Pattern Broken**: For the first time in redemptive history, the cycle does not end in failure. Christ succeeds where all others failed, inaugurating the Consummation while the delay continues for gospel advance.
