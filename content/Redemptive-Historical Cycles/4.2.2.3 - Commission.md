## Overview

**The Church's Adamic Commission: Fulfilling the Great Commission**

Christ delegates His authority to the Church to continue the mission of filling the earth with God's glory. This is the **fivefold Adamic commission** reapplied to the new covenant people.

---

## The Great Commission (Matthew 28:18-20)

**"All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age."**

**Key Elements:**
1. **Authority Established**: Christ has received [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] dominion
2. **Mission Given**: Go, make disciples, baptize, teach
3. **Scope Defined**: **All nations**—universal, not just Israel
4. **Presence Promised**: "I am with you always"—Immanuel continues
5. **Duration Specified**: "To the end of the age"—until Parousia

---

## Fivefold Commission Applied to the Church

### 1. BLESSED - Divine Approval

**Adopted as Sons:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 15|Romans 8:15-17]]**: "You have received the Spirit of adoption as sons, by whom we cry, 'Abba! Father!' The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God, and if children, then heirs—heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ"
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 26|Galatians 3:26]]**: "In Christ Jesus you are all sons of God, through faith"
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 5|Ephesians 1:5]]**: "He predestined us for adoption to himself as sons through Jesus Christ"

**Blessed with Every Spiritual Blessing:**
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 3|Ephesians 1:3]]**: "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places"
- **Justification**: "There is therefore now no condemnation" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]])
- **Sanctification**: "You were washed, you were sanctified, you were justified" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 11|1 Corinthians 6:11]])
- **Glorification**: "Those whom he justified he also glorified" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 30|Romans 8:30]])—already accomplished in God's eternal decree

**Seated in Heavenly Places:**
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]]**: "God... raised us up with him and seated us with him in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus"
- **Present Reality**: Not merely future hope but current position

### 2. BE FRUITFUL AND MULTIPLY - Spiritual Offspring

**Gospel Multiplication:**
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 6#Acts 6 . 7|Acts 6:7]]**: "The word of God continued to increase, and the number of the disciples multiplied greatly in Jerusalem"
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 9#Acts 9 . 31|Acts 9:31]]**: "The church... multiplied"
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 12#Acts 12 . 24|Acts 12:24]]**: "The word of God increased and multiplied"
- **[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 6|Colossians 1:6]]**: "The gospel... is bearing fruit and increasing"

**From Jerusalem to Rome:**
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 8|Acts 1:8]]**: "You will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth"
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 41|Acts 2:41]]**: 3,000 added at Pentecost
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 4|Acts 4:4]]**: Number of men reached 5,000
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 6#Acts 6 . 1|Acts 6:1]]**: "The disciples were increasing in number"
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 28#Acts 28 . 31|Acts 28:31]]**: Paul in Rome "proclaiming the kingdom of God"—mission advancing

**Discipleship Chain:**
- **[[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 2|2 Timothy 2:2]]**: "What you have heard from me in the presence of many witnesses entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also"
- **Four Generations**: Paul → Timothy → Faithful Men → Others Also

**Spiritual Children:**
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 15|1 Corinthians 4:15]]**: "I became your father in Christ Jesus through the gospel"
- **Philemon 10**: "My child, Onesimus, whose father I became in my imprisonment"
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 19|Galatians 4:19]]**: "My little children, for whom I am again in the anguish of childbirth until Christ is formed in you"

### 3. FILL THE EARTH - Extending God's Presence

**To All Nations:**
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 14|Matthew 24:14]]**: "This gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole world as a testimony to all nations, and then the end will come"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 18|Romans 10:18]]**: "Their voice has gone out to all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world"
- **[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 23|Colossians 1:23]]**: The gospel "has been proclaimed in all creation under heaven"

**Temple Expanding Globally** (Beale):
- **Local Churches = Outposts**: Each congregation is mini-temple where God's presence dwells
- **Acts 13-28**: Paul's missionary journeys plant churches across Roman Empire
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 21|Ephesians 2:21-22]]**: "The whole structure, being joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord. In him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God by the Spirit"

**From One City to the World:**
- **Jerusalem** (Acts 1-7): Birth of Church
- **Judea/Samaria** (Acts 8-12): Gospel spreads beyond Jerusalem
- **Asia Minor** (Acts 13-14): First missionary journey (Galatia)
- **Greece** (Acts 15-18): Second/third journeys (Philippi, Thessalonica, Corinth, Athens)
- **Rome** (Acts 28): Heart of empire—"to the end of the earth"

**Daniel's Stone Becomes Mountain:**
- **[[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 35|Daniel 2:35]]**: "The stone... became a great mountain and filled the whole earth"
- **[[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 44|Daniel 2:44]]**: "The God of heaven will set up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed"
- **Church Age = Progressive Filling**: Kingdom advances through gospel proclamation

### 4. SUBDUE - Spiritual Warfare

**Weapons of Warfare:**
- **[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 10#2 Corinthians 10 . 3|2 Corinthians 10:3-5]]**: "The weapons of our warfare are not of the flesh but have divine power to destroy strongholds. We destroy arguments and every lofty opinion raised against the knowledge of God, and take every thought captive to obey Christ"
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 10|Ephesians 6:10-18]]**: Armor of God (truth, righteousness, gospel, faith, salvation, Word, prayer)

**Binding the Strong Man:**
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 28|Matthew 12:28-29]]**: "If it is by the Spirit of God that I cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you. Or how can someone enter a strong man's house and plunder his goods, unless he first binds the strong man?"
- **Church's Authority**: "I have given you authority... over all the power of the enemy" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 19|Luke 10:19]])

**Gates of Hell Shall Not Prevail:**
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 16#Matthew 16 . 18|Matthew 16:18]]**: "I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it"
- **Offensive Posture**: Church advances, hell's gates cannot withstand
- **Victory Assured**: "Thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumphal procession" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 2#2 Corinthians 2 . 14|2 Corinthians 2:14]])

**Satan Defeated (but still active):**
- **[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]]**: "He disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open shame, by triumphing over them in him"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 10|Revelation 12:10-11]]**: "The accuser... has been thrown down. And they have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony"
- **[[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 8|1 John 3:8]]**: "The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil"

### 5. RULE - Delegated Authority

**Keys of the Kingdom:**
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 16#Matthew 16 . 19|Matthew 16:19]]**: "I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you bind on earth shall be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven"
- **Church Discipline**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 18#Matthew 18 . 15|Matthew 18:15-20]]—exercising authority in Christ's name
- **Word and Sacrament**: Proclaiming gospel (key of preaching), administering baptism/Lord's Supper

**Ambassadors of Christ:**
- **[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 20|2 Corinthians 5:20]]**: "We are ambassadors for Christ, God making his appeal through us"
- **Royal Representatives**: Speak with King's authority
- **Embassy Outposts**: Churches represent heavenly kingdom on earth

**Judging Angels:**
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 2|1 Corinthians 6:2-3]]**: "Do you not know that the saints will judge the world?... Do you not know that we are to judge angels?"
- **Future Role**: Participation in Christ's eschatological judgment
- **Present Implication**: If future judges, how much more should we exercise wisdom now

**Co-Heirs with Christ:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 17|Romans 8:17]]**: "If children, then heirs—heirs of God and fellow heirs with Christ, provided we suffer with him in order that we may also be glorified with him"
- **Inheritance**: Not merely heaven but new creation dominion
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 26|Revelation 2:26-27]]**: "The one who conquers... I will give him authority over the nations, and he will rule them with a rod of iron"

**Beale's Son of Man Theology Applied:**
- **[[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]]**: Son of Man receives dominion → **given to saints** ([[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 27|Daniel 7:27]])
- **"The saints of the Most High shall receive the kingdom"**: Corporate participation in Christ's rule
- **Church as Son of Man's Body**: We share His authority NOW (already) and FULLY (not yet)
