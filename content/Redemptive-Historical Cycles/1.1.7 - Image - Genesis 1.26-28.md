## Text

**"Then God said, 'Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth.' So God created man in his own image, in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them."** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-27]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Imago Dei**: Humanity bears God's image - uniquely representing Him on earth

**Reflecting God's Work**: We are to continue what God began - ordering, filling, subduing, ruling

**Royal Commission**: Image-bearing is tied to dominion - humans as God's vice-regents

**Male and Female**: Both together fully reflect God's image

**Relational**: Made for communion with God and one another

**Functional**: The image is not just ontological but functional - we image God by doing what He does

**Priestly Role**: Humanity serves as priests in the garden-temple, mediating God's presence to creation
