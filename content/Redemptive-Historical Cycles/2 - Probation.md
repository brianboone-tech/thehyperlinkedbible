# 2 - PROBATION

---

## Overview

Adam's probationary period in the Garden of Eden. God places Adam in the garden-temple with a commission and a test.

---

## Subsections

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/2.1 - Commission|2.1 - Commission - Genesis 2:15-17]]
The original commission given to Adam: work, keep, and obey in the garden sanctuary.

---

## Key Themes

**Covenant of Works**: Adam's probation under the command not to eat from the tree of knowledge of good and evil

**Garden-Temple**: Eden as the primordial sanctuary where God walks with Adam

**Priestly Commission**: Adam called to "work and keep" (Hebrew: עָבַד and שָׁמַר) - language used for priestly service

**Test of Obedience**: The tree of knowledge as the test of covenant faithfulness

**Life or Death**: Obedience leads to confirmed life; disobedience brings death
