# 1.1.6 - Subdue the chaos - Genesis 1:3-10

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1 - God's Creation Cycle|← Back to 1.1]]

---

## Text

**Days 1-3**: God subdues the formless void, establishing boundaries and order.

---

## Theological Significance

**Separation**: God separates light from darkness, waters above from waters below, sea from land

**Boundaries Established**: God sets limits - "thus far and no farther" ([[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 38#Job 38 . 11|Job 38:11]])

**Subduing Chaos**: The tohu wabohu (formless void) is brought under control

**Adamic Commission Anticipated**: Humans will be commanded to "subdue" the earth ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]])

**Cosmic Battle**: God demonstrates victory over chaotic forces - a theme throughout Scripture
