# 4.2.2.1 - Chaos - continuing physical exile

## Overview

**The Chaos Believers Inherit:**

Despite being spiritually seated in heavenly places ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]]), the Church still experiences the **chaos of exile** in this present evil age.

---

## Physical/Political Chaos

**Persecution from the Beginning:**
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 1|Acts 4:1-3]]**: Peter and John arrested for preaching resurrection
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 54|Acts 7:54-60]]**: Stephen stoned to death—first Christian martyr
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 12#Acts 12 . 1|Acts 12:1-2]]**: James killed by Herod's sword
- **[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 16#Acts 16 . 22|Acts 16:22-24]]**: Paul and Silas beaten, imprisoned
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 13|Revelation 2:13]]**: Antipas martyred in Pergamum

**Roman Empire:**
- **Nero's Persecution** (AD 64): Christians blamed for Rome fire, mass executions
- **Domitian's Persecution** (AD 81-96): Emperor worship enforced, refusal = death
- **"If anyone worships the beast... he will be tormented"** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 9|Revelation 14:9-10]])
- **No King but Christ**: Conflicts with Caesar's claims to divinity

**Worldwide Hostility:**
- **"In the world you will have tribulation"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 33|John 16:33]])
- **"All who desire to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted"** ([[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 3#2 Timothy 3 . 12|2 Timothy 3:12]])
- **"The world has hated them"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 14|John 17:14]])

## Spiritual Chaos

**Satan's Continued Opposition:**
- **"Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion"** ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 5#1 Peter 5 . 8|1 Peter 5:8]])
- **Temptation**: "Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 6#Matthew 6 . 13|Matthew 6:13]])
- **Deception**: "The god of this world has blinded the minds of unbelievers" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 4#2 Corinthians 4 . 4|2 Corinthians 4:4]])
- **Accusations**: "The accuser of our brothers... accuses them day and night" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 10|Revelation 12:10]])

**False Teachers:**
- **"Fierce wolves will come in among you"** ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 20#Acts 20 . 29|Acts 20:29]])
- **Galatians**: Judaizers pervert gospel of grace
- **[[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 1|2 Peter 2:1]]**: "False prophets... will secretly bring in destructive heresies"
- **Revelation 2-3**: Nicolaitans, Jezebel, false apostles infiltrate churches

**Internal Sin/Strife:**
- **Corinthian Divisions**: "I follow Paul," "I follow Apollos" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 12|1 Corinthians 1:12]])
- **Galatian Backsliding**: "You who would be justified by the law" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 4|Galatians 5:4]])
- **Ephesian Love Lost**: "You have abandoned the love you had at first" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 4|Revelation 2:4]])
- **Laodicean Lukewarmness**: "Neither cold nor hot" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 15|Revelation 3:15-16]])

## Cosmic Chaos

**Creation Still Groaning:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 22|Romans 8:22]]**: "The whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth until now"
- **Earthquakes, Famines, Pestilences**: Signs of fallen world ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 7|Matthew 24:7]])
- **Natural Disasters**: Tsunamis, volcanoes, floods—effects of curse

**Death Still Reigns (in one sense):**
- **"It is appointed for man to die once"** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 27|Hebrews 9:27]])
- **Mortality**: "This perishable body must put on the imperishable" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 53|1 Corinthians 15:53]])
- **Graveyards Fill**: Even believers' bodies return to dust ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 19|Genesis 3:19]])

**Exile Status:**
- **"Elect exiles of the Dispersion"** ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 1|1 Peter 1:1]])
- **"Our citizenship is in heaven"** ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 20|Philippians 3:20]])—we are foreigners here
- **"Seeking a homeland"** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 14|Hebrews 11:14]])—like Abraham, we are sojourners
- **No Earthly Rest**: "Here we have no lasting city" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 14|Hebrews 13:14]])

**Kline's Insight**: The Church experiences the **most paradoxical** form of exile—spiritually restored to Eden/temple presence, yet physically awaiting new creation. The tension is acute because the "already" is so real (Spirit indwells), yet the "not yet" persists (bodies decay).
