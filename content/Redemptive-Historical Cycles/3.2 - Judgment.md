# 3.2 - Judgment

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/3 - Fall|← Back to 3 - Fall]]

---

## Overview

God's pronouncement of curse upon the serpent, the woman, the man, and the ground; exile from Eden.

---

## Judgment on the Serpent (Genesis 3:14-15)

**Curse**: "Cursed are you above all livestock and above all beasts of the field; on your belly you shall go, and dust you shall eat all the days of your life."

**Proto-Evangelium** (First Gospel):
**"I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring; he shall bruise your head, and you shall bruise his heel."** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]])

- **Enmity**: Ongoing spiritual warfare
- **Seed of the Woman**: Messiah (singular seed - [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|Galatians 3:16]])
- **Head Crushed**: Fatal blow to Satan (Christus Victor)
- **Heel Bruised**: Christ's suffering on the cross

---

## Judgment on the Woman (Genesis 3:16)

**"I will surely multiply your pain in childbearing; in pain you shall bring forth children. Your desire shall be contrary to your husband, and he shall rule over you."**

**Pain in Childbearing**: Physical consequence of the curse

**Relational Strife**: Desire to control/usurp husband's leadership; husband's harsh rule - marriage becomes battleground

**Redemptive Note**: Through childbearing the Seed will come ([[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 15|1 Timothy 2:15]])

---

## Judgment on the Man (Genesis 3:17-19)

**"Cursed is the ground because of you; in pain you shall eat of it all the days of your life; thorns and thistles it shall bring forth for you... By the sweat of your face you shall eat bread, till you return to the ground, for out of it you were taken; for you are dust, and to dust you shall return."**

**Ground Cursed**: Creation itself subjected to futility ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 20|Romans 8:20-22]])

**Toilsome Labor**: Work becomes frustrating, exhausting

**Thorns and Thistles**: Nature resists human cultivation

**Death Pronounced**: "Dust to dust" - physical death inevitable

---

## Exile from Eden (Genesis 3:22-24)

**"Then the LORD God said, 'Behold, the man has become like one of us in knowing good and evil. Now, lest he reach out his hand and take also of the tree of life and eat, and live forever—' therefore the LORD God sent him out from the garden of Eden to work the ground from which he was taken. He drove out the man, and at the east of the garden of Eden he placed the cherubim and a flaming sword that turned every way to guard the way to the tree of life."**

**Expulsion**: Driven from God's presence - the ultimate judgment

**Cherubim Guard**: No return to Eden - the way back is barred

**Tree of Life Forbidden**: Cannot live forever in fallen state

**Decreation Begins**: Return to chaos outside the garden-temple

---

## Theological Significance

**Justice**: God must punish sin - His holiness demands it

**Mercy in Judgment**: Death is delayed; promise of Seed given; animal skins provided (foreshadowing atonement)

**Pattern Established**: Sin → Judgment → Exile - repeated throughout Scripture

**Need for Redemption**: Humanity cannot save itself - must await God's intervention

**Hope**: Proto-evangelium promises ultimate victory through the Seed
