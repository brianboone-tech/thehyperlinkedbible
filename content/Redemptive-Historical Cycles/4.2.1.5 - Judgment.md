## Overview

**Already/Not Yet Tension: The Intrusion's Paradox**

This is where Kline's framework becomes crucial: Christ's work inaugurates the Consummation BUT the delay continues. The Intrusion is **more intense** than ever, yet Common Grace remains operative.

---

## A. The "Already" - Spiritual Restoration Accomplished

**Exile Ended in Principle:**

**1. Reconciliation Achieved:**
- **"God was in Christ reconciling the world to himself"** ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 19|2 Corinthians 5:19]])
- **Enmity Abolished**: "He... has broken down... the dividing wall of hostility" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 14|Ephesians 2:14]])
- **Peace Made**: "Through him to reconcile to himself all things... making peace by the blood of his cross" ([[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 20|Colossians 1:20]])

**2. Access to Father Restored:**
- **Veil Torn**: "The curtain of the temple was torn in two, from top to bottom" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 51|Matthew 27:51]])
- **Boldness to Enter**: "We have confidence to enter the holy places by the blood of Jesus" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 19|Hebrews 10:19]])
- **No More Separation**: "Through him we... have access in one Spirit to the Father" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 18|Ephesians 2:18]])

**3. Presence of God Returns:**
- **Pentecost**: Spirit poured out ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 2#Acts 2 . 1|Acts 2:1-4]])—reversing Babel's scattering
- **Indwelling**: "Do you not know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in you?" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 16|1 Corinthians 3:16]])
- **Immanuel Fulfilled**: "I am with you always" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 28#Matthew 28 . 20|Matthew 28:20]])

**4. Covenant Curse Removed:**
- **"There is therefore now no condemnation"** ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]])
- **Deuteronomy 28 Reversed**: Curse falls on Christ ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]])
- **New Covenant Enacted**: "I will be their God, and they shall be my people" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 10|Hebrews 8:10]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]])

**5. Spiritual Death Conquered:**
- **"Passed from death to life"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 24|John 5:24]])
- **Born Again**: "You have been born again... through the living and abiding word of God" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 23|1 Peter 1:23]])
- **New Creation Now**: "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]])

**Beale**: "The 'already' of Christ's work means believers are **now** seated with Christ in heavenly places ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]]), **now** part of the new creation, **now** partaking of the age to come ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 4|Hebrews 6:4-5]])."

## B. The "Not Yet" - Physical Exile Continues

**Common Grace Delay Persists:**

**1. Physical Death Remains:**
- **"It is appointed for man to die once"** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 27|Hebrews 9:27]])
- **Saints Still Die**: Stephen stoned (Acts 7), James killed ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 12#Acts 12 . 2|Acts 12:2]])
- **"We ourselves groan"** ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 23|Romans 8:23]]): Awaiting redemption of bodies
- **Mortality Continues**: "This perishable body must put on the imperishable" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 53|1 Corinthians 15:53]])

**2. Suffering Continues:**
- **"In the world you will have tribulation"** ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 33|John 16:33]])

- **Creation Groans**: "The whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 22|Romans 8:22]])
- **Tears Not Yet Wiped**: Mourning, crying, pain still present (cf. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]]—future)

**3. Satan Still Active (Though Defeated):**

- **Awaiting Final Judgment**: Cast into lake of fire ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]])—future tense

**4. Sin's Effects Linger:**

- **Sanctification Ongoing**: "Being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]])
- **Not Yet Perfected**: "Not that I have already obtained this or am already perfect" ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 12|Philippians 3:12]])

**5. Political Exile/Oppression:**
- **Rome Still Rules**: Caesar demands taxes ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 21|Matthew 22:21]])
- **No Earthly Kingdom Yet**: "My kingdom is not of this world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 18#John 18 . 36|John 18:36]])

- **Awaiting King's Return**: "This same Jesus... will come in the same way" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 11|Acts 1:11]])

## C. Kline's Intrusion Framework Applied

**The Paradox Explained:**

The **Consummation has intruded** with unprecedented intensity in Christ, YET the **delay continues** because:

**1. Common Grace Still Operative:**
- **Wheat/Tares Grow Together**: "Let both grow together until the harvest" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13#Matthew 13 . 30|Matthew 13:30]])
- **Time for Repentance**: "The Lord... is patient toward you, not wishing that any should perish" ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 9|2 Peter 3:9]])
- **Gospel to All Nations**: "This gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed... to all nations" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 14|Matthew 24:14]])

**2. Two Ages Overlap:**
- **"This Present Evil Age"**: Still exists ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 1#Galatians 1 . 4|Galatians 1:4]])
- **"Age to Come"**: Has broken in ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 6#Hebrews 6 . 5|Hebrews 6:5]])
- **Overlapping**: We live in tension between two ages

**Beale's Diagram:**

```
This Age          |====CHRIST====|
                         ↓
Age to Come       [Inaugurated]============[Consummated]
                  (Already)                (Not Yet)
```

**3. Visible vs. Invisible Realities:**

**Core (Invisible/Spiritual) - ALREADY:**
- Seated in heavenly places ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]])
- New creation ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 17|2 Corinthians 5:17]])
- Justified, adopted, sanctified

**Periphery (Visible/Physical) - NOT YET:**
- Bodies still mortal
- World still fallen
- Satan still active (though defeated)

**Kline**: "In the New Testament age, the core of realized eschatology is more intense than ever, but the periphery of Common Grace delay remains until Christ's return."

## D. Ethical Implications (Kline's Contribution)

**Different Ethics for Different Ages:**

**1. During Common Grace (Now):**
- **Love Enemies**: "Pray for those who persecute you" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 44|Matthew 5:44]])
- **Good Samaritan Ethics**: Unbeliever is neighbor ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 25|Luke 10:25-37]])
- **Mercy/Patience**: "Be kind to one another" ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 32|Ephesians 4:32]])
- **Not Avenging**: "Vengeance is mine, I will repay, says the Lord" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 19|Romans 12:19]])

**2. At Consummation (Future):**
- **Judgment on Enemies**: "They will suffer the punishment of eternal destruction" ([[Readable Bible/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 1#2 Thessalonians 1 . 9|2 Thessalonians 1:9]])
- **No More Mercy**: "Depart from me, you cursed" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|Matthew 25:41]])
- **Great Gulf Fixed**: No passing from one side to other ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 16#Luke 16 . 26|Luke 16:26]])
- **Saints Approve**: "The righteous will rejoice when he sees the vengeance" ([[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 58#Psalms 58 . 10|Psalm 58:10]])

**Christ's Work Bridges:**
- He **now** exercises judgment authority ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 27|John 5:27]])
- Yet **delays** final execution for gospel advance
- When He returns, **no more delay** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 10#Revelation 10 . 6|Revelation 10:6]])

## F. Summary - The Intrusion's Zenith

**Christ's Work = Peak Intrusion:**
- **More "Already"**: Than any previous intrusion (true temple, true Israel, actual resurrection)
- **Still "Not Yet"**: Consummation delayed for gospel advance
- **Unique Position**: We live in the **most intense period of intrusion** in redemptive history

**The Pattern Broken:**
- All previous intrusions: Chaos → Creation → Commission → **Sin** → Judgment
- Christ's intrusion: Chaos → Creation → Commission → **NO SIN** → Delayed Judgment

**Next Intrusion (4.2.2):**
The church inherits this tension—living as new creation people in fallen world, continuing Christ's mission until the final intrusion (5.1) when all delay ends.

---

**Beale's Conclusion**: "Jesus' life, death, resurrection and ascension are the definitive moment in redemptive history when the end-time new creation invades the old creation and begins the irreversible process of transforming it. The Consummation has begun but not yet been completed."

**Kline's Conclusion**: "This intrusion is normative for Christian ethics today—we live by Consummation realities (spiritual resurrection, new creation) while still governed by Common Grace principles (love enemies, show mercy) until the Lord returns and the delay ends."
