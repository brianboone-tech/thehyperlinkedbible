## Text

**"Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them. And on the seventh day God finished his work that he had done, and he rested on the seventh day from all his work that he had done."** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 1|Genesis 2:1-2]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Sabbath Established**: The seventh day is set apart as holy rest

**Work Completed**: "Finished" - the work is fully accomplished (echoes Christ's "It is finished" - [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]])

**Goal of Creation**: Creation is not an end in itself but moves toward rest in God's presence

**Not Exhaustion**: God doesn't rest because He's tired - He rests because the work is done

**Pattern for Humanity**: We are called to enter God's rest (Hebrews 3-4)

**Eschatological**: Points forward to eternal Sabbath rest in new creation

**Temple Presence**: Rest is defined as dwelling in God's presence - Eden as sanctuary

---

## Redemptive-Historical Trajectory

- **Creation**: God's Sabbath rest in Eden
- **Canaan**: The land of rest ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 9|Deuteronomy 12:9-10]])
- **David**: Seeking rest from enemies ([[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 1|2 Samuel 7:1]])
- **Solomon**: Builds temple as place of rest ([[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 22#1 Chronicles 22 . 9|1 Chronicles 22:9]])
- **Christ**: "Come to me... and I will give you rest" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28]])
- **New Creation**: Eternal Sabbath ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-11]]; Revelation 21-22)
