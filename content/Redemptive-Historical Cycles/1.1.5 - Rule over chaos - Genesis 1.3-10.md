# 1.1.5 - Rule over chaos - Genesis 1:3-10

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/1.1 - God's Creation Cycle|← Back to 1.1]]

---

## Text

**Days 1-3**: God creates light, separates waters, establishes dry land.

---

## Theological Significance

**Divine Authority**: God exercises absolute rule over chaotic elements

**Word Power**: God speaks and it is so - His word has creative power

**Ordering**: God brings structure to formlessness

**Dominion Demonstrated**: This is the pattern humans are called to imitate

**Light Conquers Darkness**: The first act is bringing light - anticipating Christ ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1-5]])
