## Overview

God's progressive work of redemption through intrusions of grace, culminating in Christ. This section traces the repeated pattern of God raising up new "Adams" to accomplish the original commission, each one escalating toward the final fulfillment in Jesus Christ.

**Kline's Intrusion Framework**: Each redemptive cycle represents the Consummation breaking through into the age of Common Grace delay.

**Beale's Corporate Adam Pattern**: Each intrusion features a representative figure recapitulating Adam's calling.

---

## Two Main Divisions

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.1 - The Delay - The Age of Preparation|4.1 - The Delay - The Age of Preparation]]
Old Testament intrusions from Cain & Abel through the post-exilic prophets. Each cycle follows the pattern: Chaos → Creation → Commission → Sin → Judgment.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2 - The Delay - The Age of Fulfillment|4.2 - The Delay - The Age of Fulfillment]]
New Testament fulfillment in Jesus Christ and the Church. The pattern is broken in Christ who does not sin, inaugurating the age to come.

---

## The Fivefold Pattern

Each intrusion (except Christ's) follows this structure:

1. **Chaos**: The state of exile and disorder following previous judgment
2. **Creation**: God's new creative act - raising up a deliverer or new community
3. **Commission**: The Adamic mandate restated (blessed, be fruitful, multiply, fill, subdue, rule)
4. **Sin**: The failure of the corporate Adam figure
5. **Judgment**: Exile, removal of presence, curse - leading to next cycle's chaos

**CRITICAL**: In Christ (4.2.1), the pattern is **broken** at step 4 - He does not sin. This inaugurates the Already/Not Yet tension.

---

## Key Theological Concepts

**Escalation**: Each cycle intensifies, moving closer to final fulfillment

**Typology**: Earlier intrusions serve as types/shadows pointing to Christ

**Temple Presence**: God's glory progressively fills the earth through His people

**Already/Not Yet**: Inaugurated in Christ - Consummation has begun but not completed
