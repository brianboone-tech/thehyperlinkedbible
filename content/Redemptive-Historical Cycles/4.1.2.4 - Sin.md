## The Sin: Forbidden Union and Covenant Compromise

### "The Sons of God Saw... And Took" (Genesis 6:2)

**Identifying the Sons of God** (Sethite Interpretation):

The "sons of God" (בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים, *b'nei ha'elohim*) = descendants of Seth, the godly line who "call upon the name of the LORD" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 26|Genesis 4:26]]).

**Rationale** (see full discussion in main overview, [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.1.2 - Intrusion - Sons of God and Daughters of Men|4.1.2]]):
1. Context: Genesis 4-5 carefully distinguishes Seth's line (worshipers) from Cain's line (cultural achievers without God)
2. Covenant language: "Sons of God" used elsewhere for covenant people (cf. [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 14#Deuteronomy 14 . 1|Deuteronomy 14:1]]; [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 10|Hosea 1:10]])
3. Corporate Adam typology: Fits pattern of godly remnant called to holy separation
4. Later biblical pattern: Intermarriage with ungodly repeatedly condemned
5. Human moral responsibility: Makes best sense of human culpability for wickedness that follows

**The "Daughters of Man"** = Cain's ungodly line, possibly emphasizing their earthly/fleshly orientation.

### The Progressive Sin: Seeing, Desiring, Taking

**Stage 1: "Saw that the daughters of man were attractive"**
- External beauty, physical attraction
- Echoes Eve: "saw that the tree was... a delight to the eyes" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 6|Genesis 3:6]])
- Seeing leads to coveting when not governed by God's Word
- Problem: Making decisions based on external appearance rather than spiritual compatibility

**Stage 2: "Took as their wives"**
- Active seizure, not passive fall
- "Any they chose" — lust-driven selection, disregard for God's boundaries
- Polygamy implied ("wives" plural in context of [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 19|Genesis 4:19]], Lamech's two wives)
- Breaking of holy separation

**Theological Significance**:
- Covenant people compromising with the world
- Spiritual considerations subordinated to physical attraction
- Gradual drift from "calling on the name of the LORD" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 26|Genesis 4:26]]) to "took any they chose"
- Pattern: When worship declines, worldliness increases

### Biblical Pattern of Forbidden Intermarriage

This is the **first** but not the last time God's people compromise through intermarriage:

**Old Testament Examples**:
- Israel commanded not to intermarry with Canaanites: "You shall not intermarry with them" ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 3|Deuteronomy 7:3-4]])
- Reason: "For they would turn away your sons from following me, to serve other gods"
- Samson's downfall with Delilah ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 16#Judges 16 . 4|Judges 16:4-21]])
- Solomon's apostasy through foreign wives: "His wives turned away his heart" ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 1|1 Kings 11:1-8]])
- Ahab marrying Jezebel, bringing Baal worship to Israel ([[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 16#1 Kings 16 . 31|1 Kings 16:31]])
- Post-exilic crisis: Ezra's horror at intermarriage ([[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 9#Ezra 9 . 1|Ezra 9:1-4]])
- Nehemiah's confrontation: "Did not Solomon... sin on account of such women?" ([[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 23|Nehemiah 13:23-27]])

**New Testament Principle**:
- "Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 14|2 Corinthians 6:14-18]])
- "Only in the Lord" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 7#1 Corinthians 7 . 39|1 Corinthians 7:39]])

**The Principle**: When God's people marry unbelievers, spiritual compromise follows inevitably. The believing partner is pulled down, not the unbelieving partner pulled up.

## God's Response: Spirit Will Not Strive Forever (Genesis 6:3)

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 3|Genesis 6:3]]**: "My Spirit shall not abide in man forever, for he is flesh: his days shall be 120 years."

### Interpretation Options

**View 1: 120 Years Until the Flood** (most likely):
- God announces a period of grace before judgment
- 120 years to repent ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 5|2 Peter 2:5]]: "Noah, a herald of righteousness")
- Demonstrates divine patience despite provocation
- Pattern: God delays judgment, giving opportunity for repentance

**View 2: Reduced Human Lifespan**:
- Previously 700-900 years ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 1|Genesis 5]]), now maximum 120
- Actually, post-flood lifespans decline gradually (Abraham 175, Moses 120)
- Less likely given context focuses on impending judgment, not general lifespan reduction

### "My Spirit Shall Not Abide in Man Forever"

**"Abide" or "strive" (דּוּן, *dun*)**:
- God's patient restraint of judgment
- The Holy Spirit convicting, calling to repentance
- God's longSuffering will not continue indefinitely
- Echoes later: "How long shall this wicked congregation grumble?" ([[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 14#Numbers 14 . 27|Numbers 14:27]])

**"For he is flesh"**:
- Humanity's weakness, mortality, corruption
- Contrast to "walking with God" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 22|Genesis 5:22]]) — that's living by the Spirit
- "Sons of God" have become merely "flesh" — carnality has overcome spirituality
- Paul echoes: "Those who are in the flesh cannot please God" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 8|Romans 8:8]])

**Theological Significance**:
- God's patience has limits
- Common grace period will end
- Opportunity for repentance, but not indefinitely
- Application: "Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 3#Hebrews 3 . 7|Hebrews 3:7-8]])

## The Result: Nephilim and Violence (Genesis 6:4)

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 4|Genesis 6:4]]**: "The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God came in to the daughters of man and they bore children to them. These were the mighty men who were of old, the men of renown."

### Who Were the Nephilim?

**"Nephilim" (נְפִלִים)** = Possibly from "נָפַל" (*naphal*), "to fall"
- Traditionally: "fallen ones" or "those who cause others to fall"
- Could denote: Giants, mighty warriors, tyrants
- Mentioned again after the Flood in Canaan ([[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 13#Numbers 13 . 33|Numbers 13:33]])

**"Mighty men... men of renown"**:
- Heroes, warriors, famous individuals
- Not necessarily positive — cf. Nimrod "a mighty hunter before the LORD" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 10#Genesis 10 . 9|Genesis 10:9]])
- Cultural achievement, fame, power — but apart from God
- Echoes Lamech's boast ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 23|Genesis 4:23-24]])

**Sethite Interpretation**:
- Offspring of mixed marriages (godly + ungodly lines)
- Possibly physically impressive due to genetic vigor or social status
- More importantly: Spiritually corrupt, embodying worst of both lines
- Became oppressors, violent, dominating society

### Violence Fills the Earth

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 11|Genesis 6:11]]**: "Now the earth was corrupt in God's sight, and the earth was filled with violence."

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 13|Genesis 6:13]]**: "And God said to Noah, 'I have determined to make an end of all flesh, for the earth is filled with violence through them.'"

**"Violence" (חָמָס, *chamas*)**:
- Injustice, oppression, cruelty
- Escalation from Cain's murder → Lamech's boast → universal violence
- Social breakdown, might makes right
- No justice, no mercy, no righteousness

**Pattern of Escalating Sin**:
1. Cain murders Abel ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 8|Genesis 4:8]]) — individual violence
2. Lamech boasts of killing ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 23|Genesis 4:23-24]]) — family-level violence
3. Earth "filled with violence" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 11|Genesis 6:11]]) — societal violence

When covenant people compromise, wickedness multiplies exponentially.

## The Extent of Wickedness: Total Depravity (Genesis 6:5)

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Genesis 6:5]]**: "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."

### The Most Comprehensive Statement of Human Depravity in Scripture

**Four Qualifiers** that leave no room for human goodness:

**1. "Every intention"** (כָּל-יֵצֶר, *kol-yetser*)
- **Universal** scope — not some, but all
- Every plan, purpose, imagination of the heart
- No exceptions, no mixed motives

**2. "Of the thoughts of his heart"**
- **Internal** focus — not just external actions but internal thoughts
- The hidden motivations, secret desires
- Jesus later: Adultery and murder begin in the heart ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 21|Matthew 5:21-28]])

**3. "Only evil"** (רַק רַע, *raq ra*)
- **Comprehensive** — exclusively, solely evil
- No redeeming goodness
- Utterly corrupt, no glimmer of righteousness

**4. "Continually" (כָּל-הַיּוֹם, *kol-hayom*)
- **Constant** — all the day, always
- Not occasional lapses, but perpetual wickedness
- Uninterrupted, unrelenting evil

### Theological Implications

**Total Depravity Defined**:
- Not that humans are as evil as possible (restraining grace prevents this)
- But that sin affects every part of human nature (mind, will, affections, body)
- No part untainted, no faculty uncorrupted
- Inability to save oneself or choose God apart from grace

**Later Biblical Testimony**:
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 9|Jeremiah 17:9]]: "The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately sick; who can understand it?"
- [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 10|Romans 3:10-18]]: "None is righteous, no, not one... no one seeks for God"
- [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 1|Ephesians 2:1]]: "Dead in the trespasses and sins"

**The Question**: How did humanity reach this level of wickedness?
**The Answer**: Covenant compromise led to spiritual collapse. When "sons of God" stopped calling on the LORD and took ungodly wives, the entire society spiraled into total corruption.

## God's Heart: Grief and Regret (Genesis 6:6-7)

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 6|Genesis 6:6]]**: "And the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart."

### Anthropopathic Language: God's Emotions

**"Regretted" (נָחַם, *nacham*)**:
- Not divine mistake or change of mind (God is immutable, [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 6|Malachi 3:6]]; [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 23#Numbers 23 . 19|Numbers 23:19]])
- But divine sorrow over human sin
- Anthropopathism: Describing God's response in human emotional terms
- Real divine grief, expressed in language we can understand

**"It grieved him to his heart"**:
- Deep sorrow, not casual displeasure
- God's holy response to seeing His image-bearers utterly corrupted
- Love wounded by betrayal
- Justice demanding response to evil

**Theological Significance**:
- God is not impassive or unaffected by sin
- He deeply cares about human rebellion
- His judgments are not arbitrary but flow from grieved love
- Anticipates the cross: God's grief over sin leads to providing the Son

[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 30|Ephesians 4:30]]: "And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God."

## Theological Themes

### The Danger of Covenant Compromise

**Thesis**: When God's people compromise with the world, total corruption follows.

**Progression in Genesis 6**:
1. Holy separation maintained (Seth's line calls on the LORD)
2. Attraction to worldly beauty ("saw... attractive")
3. Mixed marriages ("took as wives")
4. Holy distinctiveness lost (Nephilim = mixed offspring)
5. Total corruption results ("only evil continually")
6. Judgment necessary (Flood announced)

**Application**:
- Small compromises lead to total collapse
- Gradual drift is still drift
- Maintaining boundaries is crucial for spiritual health
- What begins with "just" a friendship or dating relationship can end in apostasy

### Total Depravity Demonstrated

[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Genesis 6:5]] demolishes all human-centered theology:
- **Pelagianism** (humans basically good): Refuted — every intention is evil
- **Semi-Pelagianism** (humans can initiate toward God): Refuted — only evil, no seeking God
- **Arminianism** (prevenient grace makes all able to choose): Shows desperate need for irresistible grace
- **Calvinism** (total depravity, unconditional election): Confirmed — only Noah "found favor" by grace alone

**Implication**: Salvation must be entirely of God. Humans left to themselves will choose evil every time.

[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 44|John 6:44]]: "No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him."

### God's Patience Has Limits

**Common Grace Period** (120 years, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 3|Genesis 6:3]]):
- God delays judgment, allowing time for repentance
- Noah preaches righteousness for over a century ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 5|2 Peter 2:5]])
- Yet none repent (only 8 saved: Noah's family)

**Final Judgment Intrudes**:
- Patience exhausted
- Judgment announced: "I will blot out man" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 7|Genesis 6:7]])
- Pattern for final judgment ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 3|2 Peter 3:3-10]])

**Application**: "Now is the favorable time; behold, now is the day of salvation" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 2|2 Corinthians 6:2]]).

### The Seed of the Serpent Multiplies

[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] promised enmity between two seeds:
- **Seed of the woman**: Seth's line, calling on the LORD
- **Seed of the serpent**: Cain's line, away from God's presence

**What happens when they intermarry?**
- The seed of the serpent dominates
- Wickedness spreads, righteousness diminishes
- Only Noah's family remains righteous ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 8|Genesis 6:8-9]])

**Pattern**: The world always pulls down more than believers pull up. Holy separation is necessary for spiritual survival.

## Christological Fulfillment

### Christ: The Truly Holy One Who Does Not Compromise

Where sons of God failed to maintain separation, **Christ succeeds**:
- "Holy, innocent, unstained, separated from sinners" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 26|Hebrews 7:26]])
- Tempted in all ways, yet without sin ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 15|Hebrews 4:15]])
- "Friend of tax collectors and sinners" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 19|Matthew 11:19]]) yet "knew no sin" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]])

**Christ's Perfect Separation**:
- Enters the world but remains unstained
- Associates with sinners to save them, not to join them
- Models being "in the world but not of the world" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 14|John 17:14-16]])

### Christ Bears the Judgment for Human Wickedness

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Genesis 6:5]]** shows the depth of human sin; **the cross** shows the depth of God's love:
- Every evil intention of the heart → Laid on Christ ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 6|Isaiah 53:6]])
- God's grief over sin → Poured out on the Son ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 46|Matthew 27:46]])
- Judgment deserved by all → Borne by One ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 8|Romans 5:8-9]])

**The Flood** = Type of final judgment; **Christ** = Ark of salvation ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20-21]])

### Christ Calls His Bride to Holiness

Where sons of God chose wives based on physical beauty, **Christ chose His bride (the church) before the foundation of the world**:
- [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 25|Ephesians 5:25-27]]: "Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her, that he might sanctify her... present the church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle"
- He calls us to be holy, uncompromising, distinct from the world
- Empowers us by the Spirit to maintain separation where Adam's sons failed

## Connection to Intrusion Pattern

**Sin** (4.1.2.4) → Sons of God compromise through intermarriage, total corruption spreads, violence fills earth, God's patience runs out

This sin necessitates:

**Judgment** (4.1.2.5) → God announces He will send the Flood to blot out humanity, but Noah finds favor

The pattern: Sin escalates → Judgment becomes necessary → Grace preserves a remnant.

## Practical Application

### Guard Against "Small" Compromises

**The Warning**:
- Intermarriage seemed like a "small" compromise (love is love, right?)
- Result: Total societal collapse within generations
- Sin never stays small — it grows, spreads, dominates

**Application**:
- Guard your eyes: What you "see" affects what you "desire" and what you "take"
- Guard your relationships: "Bad company ruins good morals" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 33|1 Corinthians 15:33]])
- Guard your marriages: Marry believers only ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 7#1 Corinthians 7 . 39|1 Corinthians 7:39]])

### Recognize the Reality of Total Depravity

**The Truth**:
- Apart from grace, we are "only evil continually"
- This humbles pride, crushes self-righteousness
- Drives us to Christ in desperate dependence

**Questions**:
- Do I truly believe I'm a sinner, or just a "good person who makes mistakes"?
- Do I recognize my need for a Savior, or am I relying on my own goodness?

### Respond to God's Patience While There's Time

**God's patience** (120 years) **is** **not** **indifference**:
- He delays judgment to allow repentance
- But the day of grace will end
- "Now is the day of salvation" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 2|2 Corinthians 6:2]])

**Urgency**:
- For unbelievers: Repent while there's time
- For believers: Witness while there's opportunity
- For all: Take seriously the reality of coming judgment

### Grieve Over Sin as God Does

**God's response** to sin: **Grief** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 6|Genesis 6:6]]), not indifference:
- Do we grieve over our sin, or excuse it?
- Do we grieve over society's wickedness, or grow numb to it?
- Do we grieve the Holy Spirit ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 30|Ephesians 4:30]]), or harden our hearts?

**Call**: Holy sorrow over sin should drive us to repentance and sanctification.
