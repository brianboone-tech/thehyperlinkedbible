## Text

**Days 3-6**: God fills the earth with vegetation, sea creatures, birds, and land animals.

**Key Phrase**: "Be fruitful and multiply" - given to sea creatures and birds ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 22|Genesis 1:22]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Filling**: The second three days fill what the first three days prepared

**Life Multiplication**: God's creative power produces abundant life

**Pattern for Humanity**: This command will be given to humans ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]]) and repeated throughout redemptive history

**Blessing**: Fruitfulness is tied to divine blessing
