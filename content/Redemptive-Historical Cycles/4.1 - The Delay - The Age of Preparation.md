## Overview

Old Testament intrusions from Cain & Abel through the post-exilic prophets. Each cycle follows the pattern: Chaos → Creation → Commission → Sin → Judgment.

**Pattern**: All OT intrusions follow the fivefold structure, always ending in failure and judgment, pointing forward to the One who will not fail.

## Key Theme

**Escalating Failure**: Each intrusion escalates toward the final fulfillment but ends in sin and judgment, demonstrating humanity's inability to accomplish the Adamic commission apart from Christ.
