# 5.1.1 - Chaos - Chaos of last death of heaven and earth

## Overview

The final chaos before the eternal order - the present heavens and earth pass away. This is the ultimate de-creation, the cosmic undoing that precedes the final re-creation.

---

## The Final Cosmic De-Creation

**The Last Chaos Before Eternal Order:**

This is not merely another judgment in history—this is the **final and total de-creation** of the present fallen order. What Christ experienced in microcosm at the cross (darkness at noon, earth quaking, rocks splitting) now happens on a universal scale.

### **A. The Heavens and Earth Pass Away:**

**[[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 10|2 Peter 3:10-13]] - The Day of the Lord:**

"But the day of the Lord will come like a thief, and then **the heavens will pass away with a roar**, and the **heavenly bodies will be burned up and dissolved**, and **the earth and the works that are done on it will be exposed**. Since all these things are thus to be dissolved, what sort of people ought you to be in lives of holiness and godliness, waiting for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which **the heavens will be set on fire and dissolved, and the heavenly bodies will melt as they burn**! But according to his promise we are waiting for **new heavens and a new earth in which righteousness dwells**."

**Key Elements:**
- **Comes like a thief**: Sudden, unexpected for the unprepared ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 43|Matthew 24:43-44]])
- **Heavens pass with a roar**: στοιχεῖα (stoicheia) = elemental forces, cosmic order dissolved
- **Fire**: Purifying judgment consuming the old order
- **Works exposed**: All human achievements judged, tested by fire ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 12|1 Corinthians 3:12-15]])
- **New creation awaits**: Destruction is not the end but the transition to eternal order

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 11|Revelation 20:11]] - Earth and Sky Flee:**

"Then I saw a great white throne and him who was seated on it. **From his presence earth and sky fled away, and no place was found for them**."

- **Great White Throne**: Final judgment seat of Christ
- **Earth and sky flee**: Cannot endure the unveiled glory of God
- **No place found**: The old order has no place in the new creation
- **Cosmic reconfiguration**: Preparation for new heavens and new earth

**Revelation 21:1a - First Heaven and Earth Passed:**

"Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for **the first heaven and the first earth had passed away**."

- **"Passed away"**: παρῆλθεν (parēlthen) = gone, departed, no longer exists
- **"First" implies "Second"**: The original creation gives way to the final creation
- **Continuity with discontinuity**: Not annihilation but transformation ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21]])

### **B. The Sea Was No More:**

**Revelation 21:1b**:
"...and **the sea was no more**."

**Theological Significance of "No More Sea":**

**1. Symbol of Chaos Removed:**
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]]**: "The Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters"—primordial chaos
- **Ancient Near Eastern imagery**: Sea = realm of chaos, death, and evil (Leviathan, Rahab)
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 13#Revelation 13 . 1|Revelation 13:1]]**: The beast rises from the sea—chaos produces evil
- **Final victory**: God completely subdues all chaos forever

**2. No More Separation:**
- Sea separated nations and peoples ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 5#Revelation 5 . 9|Revelation 5:9]]—"from every tribe and language")
- New creation = unified people, no barriers ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24-26]])
- **[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 9|Isaiah 11:9]]**: "The earth will be full of the knowledge of the LORD as the waters cover the sea"—sea imagery redeemed

**3. No More Death:**
- Sea associated with death and the grave ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 13|Revelation 20:13]]—"the sea gave up the dead")
- **Death destroyed** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 14|Revelation 20:14]]), therefore its symbol (sea) also removed
- New creation = pure life, no threat of chaos/death

**4. No More Instability:**
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 46#Psalms 46 . 2|Psalm 46:2-3]]**: "Though the mountains be moved into the heart of the sea, though its waters roar and foam"—instability
- **New Jerusalem**: Stable, secure, unshakable ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 28|Hebrews 12:28]])
- **Crystal sea before throne** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 4#Revelation 4 . 6|Revelation 4:6]]): Chaos tamed, reflecting God's glory

**Beale**: "The absence of the sea symbolizes the complete eradication of chaos, evil, and death from God's new creation. What began in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] with the Spirit hovering over chaotic waters culminates in [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]] with chaos forever banished."

### **C. Echoes of Previous De-Creations:**

**Pattern of De-Creation Throughout Scripture:**

**1. The Flood (Genesis 6-9):**
- **Waters cover earth**: Return to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] chaos
- **Judgment on sin**: De-creation of fallen world
- **Followed by new creation**: Noah as new Adam, covenant established
- **Incomplete**: Sin and death remained

**2. The Exodus Plagues (Exodus 7-12):**
- **Darkness**: Reversal of Genesis 1:3 "Let there be light"
- **Waters to blood**: Creation order disrupted
- **Death of firstborn**: Judgment on Egypt
- **Red Sea**: Waters of chaos defeat Pharaoh's army
- **Followed by new creation**: Israel as God's son ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 22|Exodus 4:22-23]])
- **Incomplete**: Israel rebelled in wilderness

**3. Exile to Babylon ([[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 4#Jeremiah 4 . 23|Jeremiah 4:23-26]]):**
- **"I looked on the earth, and behold, it was without form and void"**: Echoes [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]]
- **"The heavens had no light"**: De-creation language
- **"The fruitful land was a desert"**: Reversal of Eden
- **Judgment on Judah's sin**
- **Followed by return**: But exile never fully ended (as seen in NT)
- **Incomplete**: No Davidic king, no glory cloud

**4. Christ's Crucifixion ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 45|Matthew 27:45]], 51):**
- **Darkness at noon**: De-creation
- **Temple veil torn**: Old order ending
- **Earth quakes, rocks split**: Cosmic upheaval
- **Followed by resurrection**: New creation inaugurated
- **Incomplete**: "Not yet" aspect—creation still groans ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 22|Romans 8:22]])

**5. Final Consummation (2 Peter 3; Revelation 20-21):**
- **Heavens dissolve, earth passes**: Ultimate de-creation
- **Fire purifies**: Final judgment
- **Followed by new creation**: Eternal, irreversible, complete
- **COMPLETE**: No more sin, death, chaos, or curse

### **D. Why De-Creation is Necessary:**

**1. Sin Has Corrupted the Entire Creation:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 20|Romans 8:20-22]]**: "The creation was subjected to futility... the whole creation has been groaning together in the pains of childbirth"
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-18]]**: Ground cursed because of Adam's sin—thorns and thistles
- **Cannot be merely fixed**: Requires total renewal

**2. Death Permeates the Present Order:**
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 26|1 Corinthians 15:26]]**: "The last enemy to be destroyed is death"
- **[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 27|Hebrews 9:27]]**: "It is appointed for man to die once"—universal mortality
- **Death must be swallowed up**: Requires new creation where death cannot exist

**3. Satan's Kingdom Must Be Completely Eradicated:**
- **[[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 19|1 John 5:19]]**: "The whole world lies in the power of the evil one"
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 12|Ephesians 6:12]]**: "Spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places"
- **Cannot coexist with new creation**: Old order must be destroyed

**4. God's Glory Requires a Suitable Dwelling:**
- **[[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]**: "The earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 23|Revelation 21:23]]**: "The city has no need of sun or moon... for the glory of God gives it light"
- **Present creation insufficient**: Cannot contain unmediated glory ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 20|Exodus 33:20]])

### **E. Kline's Intrusion Framework - Final Judgment Intrusion:**

**The Consummation Fully Breaks In:**

Throughout redemptive history, the Consummation has been **intruding** into the period of Common Grace:
- Flood, Exodus, Exile judgments = partial intrusions
- Christ's cross and resurrection = climactic intrusion
- Church Age = ongoing tension between Already and Not Yet

**At the Parousia:**
- **Common Grace ENDS**: No more delay ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 10#Revelation 10 . 6|Revelation 10:6]])
- **Consummation OVERTAKES**: Completely replaces the old order
- **No more periphery of delay**: Core of eschatological reality becomes the only reality

**This chaos is not random destruction but purposeful de-creation:**
- Clearing away the old to make room for the new
- Judgment on all that is tainted by sin
- Preparation for eternal dwelling of God with his people

**Beale's Conclusion**: "The final chaos is the necessary prelude to the final creation. Just as [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] chaos preceded God's ordering work, so the cosmic de-creation of 2 Peter 3 and [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]] precedes the eternal ordering of the new heavens and new earth. This is not pessimism but profound hope—the groaning creation will finally be liberated ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21]])."
