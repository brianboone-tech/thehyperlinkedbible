## Overview

The Church inherits Christ's Already/Not Yet tension. Believers participate in Christ's ongoing intrusion, continuing the Great Commission until the Parousia.

---

## The Fivefold Pattern

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.2.1 - Chaos|4.2.2.1 - Chaos - continuing physical exile]]
Persecution, Satan's opposition, false teachers, creation groaning, death still reigning, exile status.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.2.2 - Creation|4.2.2.2 - Creation - New Covenant Church Empowered by Spirit]]
Pentecost as new creation event, new covenant enacted, Church as eschatological temple community.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.2.3 - Commission|4.2.2.3 - Commission - Great Commission]]
Fivefold Adamic commission applied to Church: Blessed (adopted), Be Fruitful (gospel multiplication), Fill Earth (temple expanding), Subdue (spiritual warfare), Rule (delegated authority).

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.2.4 - Sin|4.2.2.4 - Sin - of Church not abiding in Christ]]
Revelation 2-3 churches, Corinthian/Galatian sins, indwelling sin persists, glorification not yet complete.

### [[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.2.5 - Judgment|4.2.2.5 - Judgment - Church Discipline and Apostasy]]
[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 18#Matthew 18 . 15|Matthew 18:15-20]], excommunication, Revelation warnings, Hebrews warning passages, Kline's intrusion framework applied.
