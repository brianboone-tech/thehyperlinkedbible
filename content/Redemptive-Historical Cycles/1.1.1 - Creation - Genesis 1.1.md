## Text

**"In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth."** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Creatio Ex Nihilo**: God creates from nothing - no pre-existing matter

**Sovereignty**: God is absolutely sovereign over all creation

**Foundation**: The opening statement establishes the foundation for all that follows

**Heavens and Earth**: Merism - God created everything (the entirety of reality)
