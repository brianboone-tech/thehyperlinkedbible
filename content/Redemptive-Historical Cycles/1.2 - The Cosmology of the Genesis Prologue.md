## Overview

The theological framework and worldview established in Genesis 1-2. This section explores the cosmological structure presented in the Genesis prologue and its implications for understanding all of Scripture.

---

## Key Themes

**Three-Tiered Universe**: Heavens, earth, and waters under the earth - the biblical worldview

**Temple Cosmology**: The cosmos structured as God's temple-palace

**Ordered Space vs. Chaos**: Creation as bringing order to the formless void

**God's Sovereignty**: YHWH alone is Creator - no pantheon, no divine conflict

**Good Creation**: Seven times "it was good" - creation is fundamentally good before the fall

**Humanity's Place**: Commissioned as God's image-bearers in the cosmic temple

**Sabbath Pattern**: The week culminates in rest - anticipating eschatological rest

---

## Theological Implications

This creation account establishes:
- God's absolute sovereignty
- Humanity's dependent yet exalted status
- The goodness of the material world
- The goal of creation (rest in God's presence)
- The pattern that will be recapitulated in redemption
