## The Final Intrusion: When the Already Becomes the Not Yet No More

**The Ultimate Consummation:**

This is the **final and ultimate intrusion**—when the core of Consummation reality that has been breaking through in all previous intrusions now fully overtakes and replaces the periphery of Common Grace. The Already/Not Yet tension is resolved. The "age to come" completely swallows up "this present evil age."

**Kline's Framework Completed**: "There will be no more delay" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 10#Revelation 10 . 6|Revelation 10:6]]). The period of Common Grace ends. The Consummation that has been intruding throughout redemptive history now arrives in fullness with no remaining delay.

**Beale's Framework Completed**: The temple expansion that began in Eden finally fills the entire new creation. "Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man. He will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]]).

### Key Differences from All Previous Intrusions:

**1. Permanence - No More Cycles:**
- All previous intrusions were temporary foretastes
- This intrusion is **eternal and irreversible**
- Pattern finally broken permanently: Chaos → Creation → Commission → NO SIN → NO JUDGMENT (only for the wicked)

**2. Visibility - Core and Periphery Merge:**
- Previous: Spiritual realities hidden, physical groaning continued
- Now: **"Every eye will see him"** ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 7|Revelation 1:7]])—complete manifestation
- Invisible becomes visible, hidden becomes revealed

**3. Completeness - No More Already/Not Yet:**
- Church Age: Already spiritually resurrected, not yet physically glorified
- Consummation: **Both spiritual AND physical reality fully present**
- Redemption of bodies ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 23|Romans 8:23]]), creation liberated ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 21|Romans 8:21]])

**4. Universality - All Creation Renewed:**
- Previous intrusions: Localized (Israel, Church)
- Final intrusion: **Cosmic scope**—new heavens and new earth ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]])
- Satan bound forever ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]]), death destroyed ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 14|Revelation 20:14]])

### The Two-Stage Consummation:

**Stage 1: Parousia (Second Coming)**
- Christ returns visibly and bodily ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 1#Acts 1 . 11|Acts 1:11]])
- Resurrection of the dead ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 16|1 Thessalonians 4:16-17]])
- Final judgment ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|Matthew 25:31-46]])
- Satan and death cast into lake of fire ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]], 14)

**Stage 2: New Creation (Eternal State)**
- New heavens and new earth ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]])
- New Jerusalem descends ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 2|Revelation 21:2]])
- God dwells with his people forever ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 3|Revelation 21:3]])
- No more tears, death, mourning, crying, pain ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]])
- Tree of life accessible ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]])
- Saints reign forever ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 5|Revelation 22:5]])

### Theological Significance:

**Amillennial/Inaugurated Eschatology** (Beale):
- The millennium = Church Age between Christ's two comings
- Satan bound NOW ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 1|Revelation 20:1-3]]; [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 29|Matthew 12:29]]), but will be released briefly before the end
- One general resurrection and judgment at Christ's return
- Kingdom already inaugurated ([[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 15|Mark 1:15]]), will be consummated at Parousia

**Common Grace Ends** (Kline):
- No more gospel proclamation—"the door is shut" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 10|Matthew 25:10]])
- No more mercy for the wicked—final separation ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 46|Matthew 25:46]])
- No more wheat and tares growing together ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13#Matthew 13 . 30|Matthew 13:30]])
- Judgment without restraint for those outside Christ

**Pattern Completed**:
- All previous "Adams" failed (except Christ)
- Church inherited Christ's mission but still battles sin
- **Saints glorified**: Finally confirmed in righteousness, unable to sin
- Adamic commission perfectly and eternally fulfilled
