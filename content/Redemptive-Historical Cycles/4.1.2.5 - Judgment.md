## The Judgment Announced: "I Will Blot Out Man"

### Divine Decree of Destruction

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 7|Genesis 6:7]]**: "I will blot out man whom I have created from the face of the land."

**"Blot out" (מָחָה, *machah*)**:
- To wipe away, erase, obliterate
- Used of erasing writing from a scroll
- Total removal, complete destruction
- Later used of God blotting out sin ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 25|Isaiah 43:25]]: "I, I am he who blots out your transgressions")
- Here: God blots out sinners themselves, not just their sins

**Scope of Judgment**:
- **Man** — primary target, as primary sinners
- **Animals** — domestic livestock
- **Creeping things** — reptiles, small creatures
- **Birds of the heavens** — flying creatures

**Why animals?**:
- Created for humanity's sake (dominion mandate, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]])
- Share in the consequences of human sin (cf. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 20|Romans 8:20-22]]: "creation was subjected to futility")
- De-creation reverses creation order (undoing [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1]])
- Demonstrates cosmic scope of judgment

**Exceptions**:
- Fish not mentioned (they survive in water)
- Noah, his family, and ark animals (grace preserved)

### "For I Am Sorry That I Have Made Them"

**Divine Grief** (see [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 6|Genesis 6:6]]):
- Not regret over mistake (God is sovereign, [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 46#Isaiah 46 . 10|Isaiah 46:10]])
- But sorrow over sin's devastation
- Anthropopathic language expressing God's holy response to evil
- Judgment flows from grieved love, not arbitrary wrath

**Theological Balance**:
- God's holiness demands judgment of sin
- God's love grieves over need to judge
- Both truths held in tension at the cross: Justice satisfied, love demonstrated

## The Flood: Cosmic De-Creation

### Reversal of Creation Order (Genesis 1)

**Creation Week** (Genesis 1):
- Day 2: Waters separated — firmament created ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 6|Genesis 1:6-8]])
- Day 3: Waters gathered — dry land appears ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 9|Genesis 1:9-10]])
- Days 5-6: Sea creatures, birds, land animals, humanity created

**Flood = Undoing Creation**:
- Firmament breached: "Windows of the heavens were opened" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 11|Genesis 7:11]])
- Deep unleashed: "All the fountains of the great deep burst forth" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 11|Genesis 7:11]])
- Waters recombined above and below — return to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] chaos
- Earth covered with water — dry land disappears
- Air-breathing life destroyed — reverses Days 5-6

**Pattern**: Sin leads to de-creation. Only God's grace prevents total return to chaos.

### Language of Universal Judgment

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 17|Genesis 6:17]]**: "Everything that is on the earth shall die."

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 21|Genesis 7:21-23]]**:
"And all flesh died that moved on the earth, birds, livestock, beasts, all swarming creatures that swarm on the earth, and all mankind. Everything on the dry land in whose nostrils was the breath of life died. He blotted out every living thing that was on the face of the ground, man and animals and creeping things and birds of the heavens. They were blotted out from the earth. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark."

**Universality Emphasized**:
- "All flesh died"
- "Everything on the dry land"
- "Every living thing"
- "Blotted out from the earth"
- "Only Noah was left"

**Global vs. Local Flood?**:
- Language suggests universal flood
- Purpose: Remove all wicked humanity (universal wickedness, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Genesis 6:5]])
- Covenant sign: Rainbow promise "never again... a flood to destroy the earth" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 11|Genesis 9:11]]) — if local, contradicted by later floods
- Geological/archaeological debates aside, theological point is clear: Total judgment on wicked world

### Duration of the Flood

**Timeline** (from [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 1|Genesis 7-8]]):
- **40 days/nights**: Rain falls ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 12|Genesis 7:12]])
- **150 days**: Waters prevail, covering mountains ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 24|Genesis 7:24]])
- **150+ days**: Waters recede gradually
- **Total**: About 1 year in ark (entered [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 11|Genesis 7:11]], exited [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 14|Genesis 8:14]])

**Theological Significance**:
- God is patient (120 years warning, [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 3|Genesis 6:3]]) but thorough in judgment
- Lengthy process demonstrates deliberateness, not hasty anger
- Year-long duration tests Noah's faith and obedience

## Grace in Judgment: "But Noah Found Favor"

### The Crucial "But"

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 8|Genesis 6:8]]**: "But Noah found favor in the eyes of the LORD."

This small word —"but"— turns the narrative from total despair to hope:
- All humanity wicked → **But** Noah found favor
- All flesh to be destroyed → **But** Noah and family preserved
- De-creation imminent → **But** re-creation will follow

**Pattern throughout Scripture**:
- [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 4|Ephesians 2:4]]: "But God, being rich in mercy..."
- The gospel is a "but" interrupting our deserved judgment

### "Found Favor" = Grace, Not Merit

**"Found favor" (מָצָא חֵן, *matsa chen*)**:
- **Grace** — unmerited favor
- Noah didn't earn it; he received it

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 9|Genesis 6:9]]**: "Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generation. Noah walked with God."

**Critical Question**: Was Noah saved by his righteousness or by grace?

**Answer**: Both/And (properly understood):
- **Grace is primary**: "Found favor" precedes "was righteous"
- **Righteousness is result**: Grace enabled Noah to walk with God
- **Faith is means**: "By faith Noah... became an heir of the righteousness that comes by faith" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 7|Hebrews 11:7]])

**Pattern**: Grace → Faith → Righteousness (not vice versa)

**Noah righteous, but not sinless**:
- Later: Drunkenness and nakedness ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 21|Genesis 9:21]])
- Demonstrates: Even the righteous need grace
- Points to need for Perfect Righteous One (Christ)

### The Ark: God's Provision of Salvation

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 14|Genesis 6:14-16]]**: God commands Noah to build the ark with specific dimensions.

**Ark = Means of Salvation**:
- God provides the plan (Noah doesn't design it)
- Noah builds in faith (no rain yet, no flood in sight)
- Ark alone provides safety from judgment waters
- Entry by God's invitation: "Go into the ark" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 1|Genesis 7:1]])
- God shuts the door ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 16|Genesis 7:16]]): "And the LORD shut him in"

**Typology** (see [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20-21]]):
- **Ark = Christ**: Refuge from judgment, sole means of salvation
- **Flood waters = Baptism**: Passing through judgment to new life
- **Noah and family = Church**: Preserved remnant, new humanity
- **Animals = All nations**: Representatives from every kind saved

### The Covenant Promise

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 18|Genesis 6:18]]**: "But I will establish my covenant with you, and you shall come into the ark, you, your sons, your wife, and your sons' wives with you."

**First Explicit "Covenant" (בְּרִית, *berit*) in Scripture**:
- Formalized after the Flood ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 8|Genesis 9:8-17]])
- Previewed here before judgment falls
- Promise: God will preserve Noah and re-establish creation order

**Elements of Noahic Covenant**:
- **Preservation**: Noah and family saved
- **Re-creation**: Post-flood commission to be fruitful and multiply ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 1|Genesis 9:1]])
- **Common Grace**: "Never again will I curse the ground" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 21|Genesis 8:21]])
- **Stability**: Seasons and day/night continue ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 8#Genesis 8 . 22|Genesis 8:22]])
- **Sign**: Rainbow as reminder ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 13|Genesis 9:13]])

**Theological Significance**:
- God's judgment always includes provision for grace
- Covenant precedes salvation (God initiates)
- Remnant theology: God preserves a people through whom redemption continues

## Theological Themes

### Justice and Mercy Held in Tension

**God's Justice**:
- Wickedness cannot go unpunished
- "Every intention... only evil continually" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Genesis 6:5]]) demands response
- Holy God must judge sin comprehensively

**God's Mercy**:
- 120 years of patience ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 3|Genesis 6:3]])
- Noah sent as "herald of righteousness" ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 5|2 Peter 2:5]]) to call to repentance
- Ark provided for all who would enter (yet only Noah's family believed)
- Remnant preserved to continue seed promise

**Both Truths Essential**:
- Deny justice → God becomes impotent, sin trivial
- Deny mercy → God becomes tyrant, humanity hopeless
- The cross holds both: Justice satisfied by Christ's death, mercy extended through His resurrection

### The Flood as Type of Final Judgment

**2 Peter 3 makes the connection explicit**:

**[[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 5|2 Peter 3:5-7]]**:
"For they deliberately overlook this fact, that the heavens existed long ago, and the earth was formed out of water and through water by the word of God, and that by means of these the world that then existed was deluged with water and perished. But by the same word the heavens and earth that now exist are stored up for fire, being kept until the day of judgment and destruction of the ungodly."

**Parallels**:
| Flood | Final Judgment |
|-------|----------------|
| Universal wickedness ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 5|Gen 6:5]]) | Widespread apostasy ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 18#Luke 18 . 8|Luke 18:8]]) |
| God's patience (120 years) | God's patience ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 9|2 Pet 3:9]]) |
| Noah preaches righteousness | Gospel preached to all nations ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 14|Matt 24:14]]) |
| Judgment by water | Judgment by fire ([[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 3#2 Peter 3 . 7|2 Pet 3:7]]) |
| Few saved (8 people) | Few find narrow way ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 7#Matthew 7 . 14|Matt 7:14]]) |
| Ark as refuge | Christ as refuge ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Pet 3:20-21]]) |
| New creation after Flood | New heavens/earth after judgment ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Rev 21:1]]) |

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 37|Matthew 24:37-39]]**:
"For as were the days of Noah, so will be the coming of the Son of Man. For as in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day when Noah entered the ark, and they were unaware until the flood came and swept them all away, so will be the coming of the Son of Man."

**Application**: The Flood is not myth but historical event and prophetic type. Final judgment is certain, sudden, comprehensive.

### Remnant Theology

**Only 8 Saved** ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20]]):
- Out of potentially millions of people, only Noah's family
- Demonstrates: Salvation is always of the few, not the many
- Majority is not right; popularity is not truth

**Pattern throughout Scripture**:
- Abraham called out from pagan Ur
- Israel: Remnant returns from exile ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 21|Isaiah 10:21-22]])
- NT: "Fear not, little flock" ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 12#Luke 12 . 32|Luke 12:32]])
- Revelation: 144,000 sealed, then great multitude ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 7#Revelation 7 . 1|Revelation 7:1-9]])

**Comfort**: God always preserves a remnant. The church will not fail.

**Warning**: Don't assume you're safe because of family, culture, or religious heritage. Each must personally enter the ark (be in Christ).

### De-Creation and Re-Creation Pattern

**Thesis**: Sin leads to de-creation; grace leads to re-creation.

**Genesis 1-11 Pattern**:
- Creation ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1-2]]) → Fall (Genesis 3) → De-creation (Flood) → Re-creation (Genesis 8-9) → Fall again (Babel)

**This Pattern Repeats**:
- Egypt: Israel enslaved (chaos) → Exodus (new creation)
- Exile: Babylon destroys Jerusalem → Return and rebuild
- Cross: Christ dies (de-creation, darkness covers earth) → Resurrection (new creation)
- End: This world destroyed by fire → New heaven and earth

**Central Truth**: Every de-creation by judgment prepares for re-creation by grace. The final re-creation ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21-22]]) will have no subsequent fall.

## Christological Fulfillment

### Christ as the True Ark

**Noah's Ark** saved through judgment waters; **Christ** saves through God's wrath:
- Only way of salvation (no other ark; no other name, [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 12|Acts 4:12]])
- God provides the plan (salvation is God's design, not human invention)
- Must enter by faith (building ark required faith; trusting Christ requires faith)
- God seals us in ([[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 13|Ephesians 1:13]]: "sealed with the promised Holy Spirit")
- Safety during judgment (Christ bears wrath in our place)

**[[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 3#1 Peter 3 . 20|1 Peter 3:20-21]]**:
"God's patience waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was being prepared, in which a few, that is, eight persons, were brought safely through water. Baptism, which corresponds to this, now saves you, not as a removal of dirt from the body but as an appeal to God for a good conscience, through the resurrection of Jesus Christ."

**Baptism** = Identification with Christ in His death (passing through judgment) and resurrection (emerging to new life).

### Christ as the Righteous Remnant

Where Noah was "righteous... blameless" ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 9|Genesis 6:9]]) yet later sinned, **Christ** is perfectly righteous:
- "He committed no sin, neither was deceit found in his mouth" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 22|1 Peter 2:22]])
- "In him there is no sin" ([[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 5|1 John 3:5]])
- The Righteous One who justifies the unrighteous ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 19|Romans 5:19]])

### Christ Bears the Flood of God's Wrath

**Flood imagery** used for divine judgment in Psalms:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 1|Psalm 69:1-2]]: "Save me, O God! For the waters have come up to my neck. I sink in deep mire, where there is no foothold; I have come into deep waters, and the flood sweeps over me."
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 88#Psalm 88 . 7|Psalm 88:7]]: "Your wrath lies heavy upon me, and you overwhelm me with all your waves."

**Christ on the cross**:
- Experiences the "flood" of God's wrath against sin
- Cries out: "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 46|Matthew 27:46]])
- Darkness covers the land (echoes Flood's cosmic scope)
- Emerges victorious: "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]])

**Those in Christ**:
- Have passed through judgment (represented in baptism)
- Are safe from final wrath ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]]: "no condemnation")
- Await new creation ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-5]])

### Christ Establishes the New Covenant

Where God established covenant with Noah ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 18|Genesis 6:18]]), **Christ** establishes the new and better covenant:
- **Noahic Covenant**: Never again destroy by flood ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 11|Genesis 9:11]])
- **New Covenant**: Eternal salvation, sin forgiven, Spirit given ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|Hebrews 8:6-13]])

**[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 20|Luke 22:20]]**: "This cup that is poured out for you is the new covenant in my blood."

## Connection to Intrusion Pattern

**Judgment** (4.1.2.5) → God announces Flood to blot out wicked humanity, but preserves Noah by grace; de-creation followed by re-creation

This judgment completes the second intrusion and creates the chaos for the next:

**Next Intrusion** (4.1.3) → Noah emerges from ark to re-created world, receives renewed commission, but eventually fails (drunkenness); pattern continues

The rhythm: Sin → Judgment → Remnant preserved → New creation → Commission → Failure → Need for greater Savior.

## Practical Application

### The Urgency of the Gospel

**Lessons from the Flood**:
- God's patience has limits (120 years ended)
- Judgment comes suddenly ("the flood came and swept them all away," [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 39|Matthew 24:39]])
- Once door is shut, it's too late ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 7#Genesis 7 . 16|Genesis 7:16]])

**Application**:
- Preach the gospel urgently while there's time
- "Behold, now is the favorable time; behold, now is the day of salvation" ([[Readable Bible/46 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 2|2 Corinthians 6:2]])
- Don't presume on God's patience

### Don't Be Deceived by Normalcy

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 38|Matthew 24:38-39]]**: "For as in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day when Noah entered the ark, and they were unaware until the flood came and swept them all away..."

**The Danger**:
- Life seemed normal until judgment came
- People mocked Noah's warnings
- Business as usual ≠ safety from judgment

**Application**:
- Don't be lulled by cultural Christianity or religious activity
- Don't assume "peace and safety" means no judgment coming ([[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 3|1 Thessalonians 5:3]])
- Be watchful, not complacent

### The Necessity of Being "In Christ"

**Being in the ark** = **Being in Christ**:
- Not enough to admire the ark; must be inside it
- Not enough to know about Christ; must be united to Him by faith
- No other refuge: "There is salvation in no one else" ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 4#Acts 4 . 12|Acts 4:12]])

**Questions for Self-Examination**:
- Am I in Christ by faith alone?
- Or am I trusting in family, church membership, morality?
- Have I personally entered the Ark, or am I standing outside admiring it?

### God's Grief Over Sin Should Move Us

**God's response** to wickedness: Grief ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 6|Genesis 6:6]]):
- Not indifference or casual judgment
- Deep sorrow over need to judge

**Our Response**:
- Grieve over our own sin (godly sorrow leads to repentance, [[Readable Bible/46 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 7#2 Corinthians 7 . 10|2 Corinthians 7:10]])
- Grieve over society's rebellion (like Lot, "tormented... by their lawless deeds," [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 8|2 Peter 2:8]])
- Never become numb or callous to evil

### Persevere in Faith Like Noah

**Noah's faith** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 7|Hebrews 11:7]]):
- Built ark for 120 years without seeing rain
- Preached righteousness to mocking world
- Obeyed when it seemed foolish

**Our calling**:
- Persevere in obedience even when results aren't visible
- Proclaim truth even when culture rejects it
- Trust God's Word over society's opinions

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 36|Hebrews 10:36]]**: "For you have need of endurance, so that when you have done the will of God you may receive what is promised."

### The Certainty of New Creation

**After the Flood**: New creation, rainbow promise, fresh start:
- God is not only God of judgment but God of new beginnings
- Every de-creation prepares for greater re-creation
- Ultimate promise: New heavens and new earth ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1-5]])

**Hope**:
- Our suffering is temporary
- Judgment will purify, not merely destroy
- "Behold, I am making all things new" ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 5|Revelation 21:5]])
