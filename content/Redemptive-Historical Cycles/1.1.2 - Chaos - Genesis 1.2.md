## Text

**"The earth was without form and void, and darkness was over the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters."** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]])

---

## Theological Significance

**Tohu Wabohu** (תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ): "Without form and void" - chaotic, unordered, uninhabitable

**Darkness**: Absence of God's ordering light

**The Deep** (תְּהוֹם, tehom): Primordial waters representing chaos

**Spirit Hovering**: The Ruach (רוּחַ) of God hovers like a bird over its nest - about to bring forth life and order

**Pattern Established**: This chaos will be echoed in every redemptive cycle - exile, disorder, awaiting God's new creative act
