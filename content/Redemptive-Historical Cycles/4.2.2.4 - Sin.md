## Overview

**The Church's Failure: Sin Despite New Creation Status**

Unlike Christ who did not sin, the Church—though new creation—**still sins**. This is the "not yet" aspect: glorification awaits, indwelling sin persists.

**Critical Distinction**:
- **Christ**: Perfect obedience, breaks the pattern (no sin)
- **Church**: Progressive sanctification, still battles sin ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 7#Romans 7 . 14|Romans 7:14-25]])

---

## A. Corporate Sins - Revelation 2-3 Churches

**Ephesus ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 1|Revelation 2:1-7]]):**
- **Sin**: "You have abandoned the love you had at first" (2:4)
- **Consequences**: Lampstand removed if no repentance (2:5)
- **Pattern**: Orthodoxy without love—religious formalism

**Pergamum ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 12|Revelation 2:12-17]]):**
- **Sin**: "You have some there who hold the teaching of Balaam... and the teaching of the Nicolaitans" (2:14-15)
- **Consequences**: Christ will war against them with sword of His mouth (2:16)
- **Pattern**: Tolerating false teaching, sexual immorality, idolatry

**Thyatira ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 18|Revelation 2:18-29]]):**
- **Sin**: "You tolerate that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess and is teaching and seducing my servants to practice sexual immorality and to eat food sacrificed to idols" (2:20)
- **Consequences**: Jezebel and her children struck with plague (2:22-23)
- **Pattern**: Compromise with worldliness

**Sardis ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 1|Revelation 3:1-6]]):**
- **Sin**: "You have the reputation of being alive, but you are dead" (3:1)
- **Consequences**: Christ will come like a thief if they don't wake up (3:3)
- **Pattern**: Dead orthodoxy, reputation without reality

**Laodicea ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 14|Revelation 3:14-22]]):**
- **Sin**: "You are neither cold nor hot... You say, I am rich, I have prospered, and I need nothing, not realizing that you are wretched, pitiable, poor, blind, and naked" (3:15-17)
- **Consequences**: "I will spit you out of my mouth" (3:16)
- **Pattern**: Self-sufficient lukewarmness, spiritual pride

## B. Individual Sins in Apostolic Churches

**Ananias and Sapphira ([[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 5#Acts 5 . 1|Acts 5:1-11]]):**
- **Sin**: Lying to the Holy Spirit about their offering
- **Judgment**: Immediate death—severe warning against hypocrisy
- **Lesson**: God's presence in new creation temple is holy

**Corinthian Sins:**
- **Divisions** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 10|1 Corinthians 1:10-13]]): "I follow Paul," "I follow Apollos"
- **Sexual Immorality** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 . 1|1 Corinthians 5:1]]): Man sleeping with father's wife—worse than pagans
- **Lawsuits** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 1|1 Corinthians 6:1-8]]): Suing fellow believers before unbelievers
- **Lord's Supper Abuse** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 11#1 Corinthians 11 . 17|1 Corinthians 11:17-34]]): Getting drunk, shaming poor, not discerning body
- **Denying Resurrection** ([[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 12|1 Corinthians 15:12]]): "How can some of you say that there is no resurrection?"

**Galatian Desertion:**
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 1#Galatians 1 . 6|Galatians 1:6]]**: "I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting him who called you"
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 1|Galatians 3:1]]**: "O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you?"
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 4|Galatians 5:4]]**: "You are severed from Christ, you who would be justified by the law"
- **Returning to Slavery**: Adding circumcision/law to gospel—denying sufficiency of Christ

**Colossian Heresy:**
- **[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 8|Colossians 2:8]]**: "See to it that no one takes you captive by philosophy and empty deceit"
- **[[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 16|Colossians 2:16-23]]**: Asceticism, angel worship, visionary experiences—"Let no one disqualify you"

- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 6|1 Corinthians 4:6]]**: "None of you may be puffed up in favor of one against another"
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 1|1 Corinthians 8:1]]**: "Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up"

**Hypocrisy:**
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 2#Galatians 2 . 11|Galatians 2:11-14]]**: Peter's compromise at Antioch—withdrawing from Gentiles
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 23#Matthew 23 . 27|Matthew 23:27-28]]**: "Whitewashed tombs... outwardly appear righteous, but within... full of hypocrisy"

**False Teaching:**
- **[[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 1|2 Peter 2:1]]**: "False prophets... will secretly bring in destructive heresies, even denying the Master"

**Love of Money:**
- **[[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 10|1 Timothy 6:10]]**: "The love of money is a root of all kinds of evils"
- **Demas**: "In love with this present world" ([[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 10|2 Timothy 4:10]])

**Immorality:**
- **[[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 4#1 Thessalonians 4 . 3|1 Thessalonians 4:3-5]]**: "This is the will of God, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 14|Revelation 2:14]]**: Balaam's teaching—sexual immorality and idolatry

## E. The "Not Yet" Reality

**Why the Church Still Sins (Unlike Christ):**

**1. Glorification Not Yet Complete:**
- **[[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 3#Philippians 3 . 12|Philippians 3:12]]**: "Not that I have already obtained this or am already perfect, but I press on"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 23|Romans 8:23]]**: "We ourselves... groan inwardly as we wait eagerly for... the redemption of our bodies"
- **Still "Being Saved"**: Sanctification is progressive ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]])

**2. Two Natures (Flesh vs. Spirit):**
- **[[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 16|Galatians 5:16-17]]**: "Walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh. For the desires of the flesh are against the Spirit, and the desires of the Spirit are against the flesh"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 12|Romans 8:12-13]]**: "If you live according to the flesh you will die, but if by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live"

**3. World, Flesh, Devil:**
- **[[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 2#1 John 2 . 15|1 John 2:15-17]]**: "Do not love the world or the things in the world"
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 1|Ephesians 2:1-3]]**: "Following... the prince of the power of the air"
- **[[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 4#James 4 . 1|James 4:1]]**: "What causes quarrels and fights among you? Is it not this, that your passions are at war within you?"

**Beale**: "The Church is simultaneously justified (positionally sinless in Christ) and sinful (experientially battling indwelling sin). This is the 'already/not yet' tension applied to sanctification."
