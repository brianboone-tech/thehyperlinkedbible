# 5.1.4 - Sin - Last resistance to sin by Saints

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/5.1 - Intrusion - Return of the King|← Back to 5.1]]

---

## Overview

The final and permanent victory over sin. Unlike every previous cycle in redemptive history (except Christ's perfect obedience), this stage ends in **complete and eternal triumph**. The pattern is not repeated but BROKEN forever—there will be no more sin, no more fall, no more exile.

---

## The Pattern Finally Broken

**Every Previous Cycle:**

1. **Adam/Eve in Eden**: Tested, fell, exiled
2. **Noah and sons**: New creation, sinned (Ham's sin, drunkenness), cursed
3. **Abraham and seed**: Promised blessing, repeatedly sinned, discipline followed
4. **Israel at Sinai**: Covenant made, golden calf, judgment
5. **Israel in Canaan**: Conquest, idolatry, exile
6. **Post-Exilic Israel**: Return, rebuilt temple, still under curse (no Davidic king, no glory)
7. **Church Age**: New creation in Christ, indwelling Spirit, BUT still battles sin ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 7#Romans 7 . 14|Romans 7:14-25]])

**ONLY Christ broke the pattern**: Tempted in all ways, yet without sin ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 15|Hebrews 4:15]]). He alone succeeded where every Adam-figure failed.

**Consummation - The Pattern Ends:**

**Chaos** → **Creation** → **Commission** → **NO SIN** → **NO JUDGMENT** (only deliverance)

The redemptive-historical cycle that has repeated from Genesis 3 onwards is now permanently concluded. Sin is not merely suppressed or restrained—it is **utterly eradicated**, and the saints are **confirmed in righteousness** such that sin is no longer possible.

## Glorification Completed - Romans 8:29-30

**[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 29|Romans 8:29-30]]**:
"For those whom he foreknew he also **predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son**, in order that he might be the firstborn among many brothers. And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified **he also glorified**."

**The Golden Chain of Salvation:**

1. **Foreknew**: God's eternal love and choice
2. **Predestined**: Appointed to be conformed to Christ's image
3. **Called**: Effectual summons through the gospel
4. **Justified**: Declared righteous through faith in Christ
5. **Glorified**: Perfected in holiness, confirmed in righteousness—**CONSUMMATION**

**Past Tense "Glorified":**
- **So certain** that Paul writes it as already accomplished
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 17|Romans 4:17]]**: God "calls into existence the things that do not exist"—future glorification is as good as done
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 6|Ephesians 2:6]]**: Already "seated... in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus"—positional reality becomes experiential

**Glorification Defined:**
- **Complete transformation**: Body and soul perfected
- **Sinlessness confirmed**: Not merely forgiven but unable to sin
- **Image of God fully restored**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26-27]] purpose achieved
- **Conformed to Christ**: Share His holiness, glory, and immortality

"Outside are the dogs and sorcerers and the sexually immoral and murderers and idolaters, and everyone who loves and practices falsehood."

**The Separation is Absolute:**

**Inside the City:**
- **Only the righteous**: Those whose names are in the Lamb's book of life
- **Perfected in holiness**: No remnant of sin remains

**Outside the City:**
- **The wicked excluded**: Final judgment executed ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 11|Revelation 20:11-15]])

- **No infiltration**: Gates are open ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 25|Revelation 21:25]]), yet no unclean thing enters—because none exist in new creation

**Why Sin Cannot Enter:**

**1. No Tempter:**
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]]**: "The devil... was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur... tormented day and night forever and ever"
- **No serpent in the garden**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 1|Genesis 3:1-5]] scenario impossible
- **No accuser**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 12#Revelation 12 . 10|Revelation 12:10]] accusations permanently silenced

**2. No Flesh:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 3|Romans 8:3]]**: "God has done what the law, weakened by the flesh, could not do"
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 50|1 Corinthians 15:50]]**: "Flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God"
- **Glorified bodies**: Not subject to decay, corruption, or sinful desires
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 7#Romans 7 . 18|Romans 7:18]] resolved**: "In my flesh dwells no good thing"—the flesh is GONE, replaced with glorified body

**3. No World System:**
- **[[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 2#1 John 2 . 15|1 John 2:15-17]]**: "The world is passing away along with its desires"—passed away
- **No temptations**: Lust of flesh, lust of eyes, pride of life—all absent
- **No cultural idolatry**: All nations bring purified glory ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 24|Revelation 21:24]])

## Confirmed in Righteousness - Philippians 1:6

**[[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 1#Philippians 1 . 6|Philippians 1:6]]**:
"And I am sure of this, that he who began a good work in you **will bring it to completion** at the day of Jesus Christ."

**The Process and the End:**

**Justification** (Already):
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 1|Romans 5:1]]**: "Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God"
- **Declared righteous**: Legal standing before God
- **Imputed righteousness**: Christ's righteousness credited to us ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]])

**Sanctification** (Already/Not Yet):
- **[[Readable Bible/52 - 1 Thessalonians/1 Thessalonians 5#1 Thessalonians 5 . 23|1 Thessalonians 5:23]]**: "May the God of peace himself sanctify you completely"
- **Progressive holiness**: Growing in conformity to Christ ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]])
- **Internal struggle**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 7#Romans 7 . 15|Romans 7:15]], "I do not understand my own actions. For I do not do what I want, but I do the very thing I hate"
- **Spirit vs. flesh**: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 5#Galatians 5 . 17|Galatians 5:17]], "The desires of the flesh are against the Spirit"

**Glorification** (Not Yet → Consummation):
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 51|1 Corinthians 15:51-54]]**: "We shall all be changed, in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye"
- **Instant perfection**: No gradual improvement but immediate transformation
- **Confirmed in righteousness**: Unable to sin, not by external constraint but by internal nature

**The Nature of Confirmation:**

**Not Mere Inability (External):**
- **NOT** like being chained so you cannot move
- **NOT** like being in prison where sin is prevented by force

**Perfect Desire (Internal):**
- **Desires perfectly aligned with God's will**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 37#Psalms 37 . 4|Psalm 37:4]], "Delight yourself in the LORD"—consummated
- **Love for God so complete**: Sin becomes unthinkable
- **Jonathan Edwards**: "The saints in heaven are not free to sin because sin is utterly contrary to their perfected nature"
- **No temptation appeals**: [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 2#1 John 2 . 16|1 John 2:16]] (lust of flesh, eyes, pride) cannot attract

**Augustine's Progression:**
- **Adam before fall**: *posse non peccare* (able not to sin)
- **Fallen humanity**: *non posse non peccare* (not able not to sin)
- **Redeemed in this life**: *posse non peccare* (able not to sin—but still struggle)
- **Glorified in eternity**: **non posse peccare** (not able to sin—perfected nature)

## No More Temptation - Satan Bound Forever

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 10|Revelation 20:10]]**:
"And the devil who had deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur where the beast and the false prophet were, and **they will be tormented day and night forever and ever**."

**The Tempter's Role in History:**

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 1|Genesis 3:1-5]]** - The First Temptation:
- **Serpent questions God's word**: "Did God actually say...?"
- **Promises autonomy**: "You will be like God"
- **Deceives Eve, Adam follows**: Fall of humanity

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]]** - Temptation of Christ:
- **Wilderness testing**: Food, kingdoms, presumption
- **Christ resists**: "It is written" (uses Scripture)
- **Satan departs temporarily**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 13|Luke 4:13]], "Until an opportune time"

**Church Age**:
- **[[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 5#1 Peter 5 . 8|1 Peter 5:8]]**: "Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour"
- **[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 14|2 Corinthians 11:14]]**: "Satan disguises himself as an angel of light"
- **[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 11|Ephesians 6:11]]**: "Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil"
- **[[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 4#James 4 . 7|James 4:7]]**: "Resist the devil, and he will flee from you"—temporary retreat

**Consummation - Permanent Destruction:**
- **Lake of fire**: Not annihilation but eternal conscious torment
- **No more access**: Cannot approach God's people
- **No more deception**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 3|Revelation 20:3]], "So that he might not deceive the nations any longer"—mission impossible forever
- **[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 15|Genesis 3:15]] fulfilled**: "He shall bruise your head"—final, fatal blow delivered

**No Tempter = No Temptation:**
- **[[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 1#James 1 . 13|James 1:13-14]]**: "God cannot be tempted with evil, and he himself tempts no one. But each person is tempted when he is lured and enticed by his own desire"
- **In the new creation**: No evil desires exist, no external tempter exists—temptation is IMPOSSIBLE

## Eternal Security - Hebrews 12:23

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 23|Hebrews 12:23]]**:
"And to the assembly of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven, and to God, the judge of all, and to **the spirits of the righteous made perfect**."

**"Made Perfect" (τετελειωμένων, teteleiōmenōn):**
- **Perfect tense**: Completed action with ongoing results
- **Not improving**: Already at the goal
- **Sinless forever**: Perfection is permanent, not probationary

**Contrast with Hebrews 11 - Faith's Pilgrims:**
- **[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 13|Hebrews 11:13]]**: "These all died in faith, not having received the things promised"
- **[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 39|Hebrews 11:39-40]]**: "All these, though commended through their faith, did not receive what was promised, since God had provided something better for us, that apart from us they should not be made perfect"
- **Now in consummation**: What was promised is received, what was hoped for is possessed

**Eternal State = Eternal Security:**
- **[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 28|John 10:28]]**: "I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 38|Romans 8:38-39]]**: "Nothing... will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord"
- **Jude 24**: "Now to him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you blameless before the presence of his glory"

**Probation Ended:**
- **Adam had probation**: Obey and live, disobey and die—he failed
- **Israel had probation**: Keep covenant and be blessed, break it and be cursed—they failed
- **Christ endured probation**: Perfectly obeyed, earned eternal life for His people—He succeeded
- **Saints confirmed**: No more testing, the verdict is final, the inheritance is sure

## Kline's Framework - The Ethics of Consummation

**Kline's Two-Register Ethics:**

**Common Grace Period:**
- **Mixed community**: Wheat and tares grow together ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13#Matthew 13 . 24|Matthew 13:24-30]])
- **Temporal blessings for wicked**: Sun and rain on just and unjust ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 45|Matthew 5:45]])
- **Restrained judgment**: [[Readable Bible/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 2#2 Thessalonians 2 . 6|2 Thessalonians 2:6-7]], "Restrainer" holds back full wrath
- **Turn the other cheek**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 39|Matthew 5:39]]—individual ethics under Common Grace

**Consummation:**
- **Pure community**: Only righteous remain ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]])
- **No blessings for wicked**: Lake of fire is their portion ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 15|Revelation 20:15]])
- **Unrestrained judgment executed**: Full wrath poured out on impenitent
- **Eternal separation**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 46|Matthew 25:46]], "These will go away into eternal punishment"

**In the New Creation:**
- **Consummation ethics ONLY**: No more Common Grace restraint
- **No compromise with sin**: Sin does not exist to compromise with
- **Perfect justice**: All evil judged, all righteousness rewarded
- **No moral ambiguity**: Every heart perfectly aligned with God's will

## The Pattern Broken - Theological Significance

**Why This is Different from All Previous Cycles:**

**1. Glorified Bodies:**
- **Not like Adam's body**: He could sin and die
- **Like Christ's resurrection body**: Cannot sin, cannot die ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 9|Romans 6:9]])
- **Imperishable**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 15#1 Corinthians 15 . 42|1 Corinthians 15:42]], immortal, incorruptible

**2. No Probation:**
- **Adam was on probation**: Could pass or fail
- **Israel was on probation**: Covenant blessings or curses
- **Church is on probation**: Persevere to the end ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 13|Matthew 24:13]])
- **Glorified saints NOT on probation**: Testing period over, verdict final

**3. Perfect Environment:**
- **No serpent**: Satan bound forever
- **No forbidden tree**: All trees accessible ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 2|Revelation 22:2]])
- **No curse**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17-19]] fully reversed
- **No death**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4]], "Death shall be no more"

**4. Beatific Vision:**
- **[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 12|1 Corinthians 13:12]]**: "Then I shall know fully, even as I have been fully known"
- **Seeing God face-to-face**: Produces and sustains perfect holiness
- **Transformative vision**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 18|2 Corinthians 3:18]] consummated—one eternal "degree of glory"

**Beale**: "The cycle of sin-exile-return that dominated Old Testament history is finally and forever broken. The pattern that repeated from Adam to Israel to the exile and return is concluded in Christ and consummated in the new creation. There is no 'return' from the new creation because there is no 'exile'—the saints are home forever."

**Kline**: "Probation belongs to the Common Grace period. In the Consummation, there is no more testing, no more delay, no more possibility of fall. The elect are confirmed in righteousness, and the reprobate are confirmed in their rebellion. The time for decision has passed; eternity has begun."

## Conclusion - Sin No More

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 4|Revelation 21:4-5]]**:
"He will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and death shall be no more, neither shall there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain anymore, **for the former things have passed away**. And he who was seated on the throne said, 'Behold, I am making all things new.'"

**The "All Things New" Include:**
- **Perfected nature**: Unable to sin, delighting in God

- **Eternal security**: No possibility of fall
- **Perfect joy**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 16#Psalms 16 . 11|Psalm 16:11]], "In your presence there is fullness of joy"

**The redemptive-historical pattern is BROKEN forever. Chaos led to Creation, Creation led to Commission, but NO MORE SIN leads to NO MORE JUDGMENT. Only eternal blessing, eternal life, eternal glory.**
