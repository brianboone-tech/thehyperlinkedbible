God's original creation and the establishment of the creation order. This section explores the seven-day creation account and its theological significance for understanding all subsequent redemptive history.

## Key Themes

**Temple Imagery**: Eden as the first temple where God's presence dwells with humanity

**Creation Order**: The pattern of chaos → order → filling → subduing → ruling → rest

**Image of God**: Humanity created to reflect God's glory and extend His presence throughout creation

**Commission Given**: The original Adamic mandate to be fruitful, multiply, fill, subdue, and rule
