# 4.2.1.4 - Sin - Christ resists sin

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/4.2.1 - Intrusion - Jesus and the Kingdom|← Back to 4.2.1]]

---

## Overview

**The Sinless One: Perfect Obedience Where All Others Failed**

This is the **crucial difference** between this intrusion and all previous ones. Unlike every other "Adam" in redemptive history, **Christ does not fall**. The five-part pattern (Chaos → Creation → Commission → Sin → Judgment) is **broken** at this point.

---

## A. Why Christ's Sinlessness Matters

**Theological Necessity:**
- **Qualified Sacrifice**: "A lamb without blemish or spot" ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 19|1 Peter 1:19]])
- **Representative Obedience**: "As by one man's disobedience the many were made sinners, so by one man's obedience the many will be made righteous" ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 19|Romans 5:19]])
- **Priestly Qualification**: "For we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who in every respect has been **tempted as we are, yet without sin**" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 15|Hebrews 4:15]])

**Covenantal Requirement:**
- **Covenant of Works Fulfilled**: What Adam failed to do (perfect obedience), Christ accomplishes
- **Active Obedience**: Positively fulfills all righteousness ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 15|Matthew 3:15]])
- **Passive Obedience**: Bears penalty of covenant-breaking ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]])

---

## B. Christ Recapitulates and Reverses Israel's Failures

**Beale's "True Israel" Motif:**

Christ lives out Israel's story **perfectly**, reversing every failure point:

**1. Egypt → Exodus:**
- **Israel**: "Out of Egypt I called my son" ([[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 1|Hosea 11:1]])—but they rebelled
- **Christ**: "Out of Egypt I called my son" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 15|Matthew 2:15]])—obedient Son

**2. Wilderness Temptation:**
- **Israel**: 40 years, grumbling, unbelief, golden calf (Exodus 32)
- **Christ**: 40 days, resists Satan, trusts Father ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]])
- **Quotes Deuteronomy**: Appeals to Israel's wilderness lessons ([[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 3|Deuteronomy 8:3]]; 6:16; 6:13)

**3. Jordan Crossing:**
- **Israel**: Spies bring bad report, refuse to enter (Numbers 13-14)
- **Christ**: Baptized in Jordan, inaugurates new exodus ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 13|Matthew 3:13-17]])

**4. Sinai/Law:**
- **Israel**: Breaks covenant immediately (golden calf)
- **Christ**: "I have kept my Father's commandments" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 10|John 15:10]])

**5. Conquest:**
- **Israel**: Incomplete obedience, leaves enemies (Judges 1)
- **Christ**: "It is finished" ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]])—complete victory

**6. Kingship:**
- **Israel's Kings**: All fail (even David/Solomon fall)
- **Christ**: Perfect King, never compromises ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 26|Hebrews 7:26]])

**7. Exile:**
- **Israel**: Sent to Babylon for sin
- **Christ**: Enters exile (cross) FOR our sin ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 5|Isaiah 53:5-6]])

## C. Specific Temptations Christ Overcame

**Wilderness ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 1|Matthew 4:1-11]]; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 1|Luke 4:1-13]]):**

**1. Bread from Stones:**
- **Temptation**: Turn stones to bread (physical provision)
- **Issue**: Trust God's word or physical sustenance?
- **Victory**: "Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 4|Matthew 4:4]]; [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 3|Deuteronomy 8:3]])
- **Contrast**: Adam/Eve ate forbidden fruit ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 6|Genesis 3:6]]); Israel grumbled for food ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16#Exodus 16 . 3|Exodus 16:3]])

**2. Temple Pinnacle:**
- **Temptation**: Throw yourself down, angels will catch you (presumption)
- **Issue**: Test God or trust God?
- **Victory**: "You shall not put the Lord your God to the test" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 7|Matthew 4:7]]; [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 16|Deuteronomy 6:16]])
- **Contrast**: Israel tested God at Massah ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 7|Exodus 17:7]])

**3. Kingdoms of the World:**
- **Temptation**: Worship Satan, receive all kingdoms (idolatry/shortcut)
- **Issue**: Path to glory—Satan's easy way or Father's cross?
- **Victory**: "You shall worship the Lord your God and him only shall you serve" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 10|Matthew 4:10]]; [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 13|Deuteronomy 6:13]])
- **Contrast**: Israel worshiped golden calf (Exodus 32), Baal ([[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 2#Judges 2 . 11|Judges 2:11-13]])

**Gethsemane ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 36|Matthew 26:36-46]]):**
- **Agony**: "My soul is very sorrowful, even to death"
- **Temptation**: Avoid the cup (escape suffering)
- **Victory**: "Not as I will, but as you will" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 39|Matthew 26:39]])
- **Contrast**: Adam chose his will over God's ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 6|Genesis 3:6]])

**Cross ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 39|Matthew 27:39-44]]):**
- **Mockery**: "If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross"
- **Temptation**: Prove deity by avoiding suffering
- **Victory**: Remains on cross, accomplishes atonement
- **Contrast**: Every OT figure failed under pressure

## D. Hebrews' Testimony

**"Without Sin" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 15|Hebrews 4:15]]):**
- **Greek**: χωρὶς ἁμαρτίας (chōris hamartias)—"apart from sin"
- **Tempted in All Points**: Real temptations, not play-acting
- **Yet Without Sin**: Never yielded, never compromised

**"Holy, Innocent, Unstained" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 26|Hebrews 7:26]]):**
- **Separated from Sinners**: Not in participation, but in character
- **Exalted Above Heavens**: Qualified High Priest

**"Learned Obedience" ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 5#Hebrews 5 . 8|Hebrews 5:8]]):**
- Not that He learned TO obey (He always obeyed)
- But learned obedience **through suffering**—experiential knowledge

## E. Two-Fold Obedience (Active and Passive)

**Active Obedience:**
- **Positively fulfills Law**: "I have not come to abolish but to fulfill" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 17|Matthew 5:17]])
- **Perfect righteousness**: "It is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness" ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 15|Matthew 3:15]])
- **Obedient Life**: "He humbled himself by becoming obedient" ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 8|Philippians 2:8]])

**Passive Obedience:**
- **Bears curse**: "Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us" ([[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|Galatians 3:13]])
- **Suffers penalty**: "He was wounded for our transgressions" ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 5|Isaiah 53:5]])
- **Dies in our place**: "For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin" ([[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5:21]])

**Both Necessary:**
- **Active**: Provides positive righteousness imputed to believers
- **Passive**: Removes penalty of sin, satisfies justice

## F. The Great Reversal (Beale's Recapitulation Theme)

| **Adam/Israel** | **Christ** |
|---|---|
| Fell at first temptation | Resisted all temptations |
| Disobeyed in paradise | Obeyed in wilderness |
| Ate forbidden fruit | Refused Satan's bread |
| Grasped equality with God | Emptied himself ([[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 2#Philippians 2 . 6|Philippians 2:6-7]]) |
| Brought death | Brought life ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 17|Romans 5:17]]) |
| Curse for disobedience | Blessing for obedience |

**Kline's Intrusion Note**: Christ's perfect obedience is the **intrusion of Consummation ethics**—He lives NOW with the moral perfection that will characterize the glorified saints in the age to come. His sinlessness is not merely exemplary but **eschatologically necessary** for inaugurating new creation.
