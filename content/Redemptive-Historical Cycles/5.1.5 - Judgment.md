# 5.1.5 - Judgment - Last deliverance of saints from Exile

[[Redemptive-Historical Cycles/5.1 - Intrusion - Return of the King|← Back to 5.1]]

---

## Overview

The final deliverance - full entrance into eternal Sabbath rest. For the saints, judgment means **salvation**, not condemnation. For the wicked, judgment means eternal separation. This is the conclusion of redemptive history: exile permanently ended for the righteous, fellowship with God permanently lost for the wicked.

---

## Final Judgment - Revelation 20:11-15

**The Great White Throne:**

**[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 11|Revelation 20:11-15]]**:
"Then I saw a great white throne and him who was seated on it. From his presence earth and sky fled away, and no place was found for them. And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and **books were opened**. Then another book was opened, which is **the book of life**. And **the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to what they had done**. And the sea gave up the dead who were in it, Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them, and they were judged, each one of them, according to what they had done. Then **Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire**. This is the second death, the lake of fire. And if anyone's name was not found written in the book of life, **he was thrown into the lake of fire**."

**Key Elements:**

**1. Great White Throne:**
- **White**: Absolute purity, perfect justice
- **Throne**: Divine sovereignty, ultimate authority
- **Him who was seated**: Christ as Judge ([[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 22|John 5:22]], 27; [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 17#Acts 17 . 31|Acts 17:31]])
- **Earth and sky fled**: Creation cannot endure unveiled glory (cf. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 1|Revelation 21:1]])

**2. Universal Resurrection:**
- **"The dead, great and small"**: All humanity—kings and peasants, rich and poor
- **Sea, Death, Hades give up dead**: No one escapes—Jonah's fish cannot hide you
- **[[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 2|Daniel 12:2]]**: "Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt"
- **[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 28|John 5:28-29]]**: "All who are in the tombs will hear his voice and come out, those who have done good to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil to the resurrection of judgment"

**3. Books Opened:**
- **Works recorded**: Every deed, word, thought documented
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 36|Matthew 12:36]]**: "I tell you, on the day of judgment people will give account for every careless word they speak"
- **[[Readable Bible/21 - Ecclesiastes/Ecclesiastes 12#Ecclesiastes 12 . 14|Ecclesiastes 12:14]]**: "For God will bring every deed into judgment, with every secret thing, whether good or evil"
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 16|Romans 2:16]]**: "On that day when, according to my gospel, God judges the secrets of men by Christ Jesus"

**4. Book of Life:**
- **Different from books of works**: Contains names of the elect
- **[[Readable Bible/50 - Philippians/Philippians 4#Philippians 4 . 3|Philippians 4:3]]**: "Whose names are in the book of life"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 13#Revelation 13 . 8|Revelation 13:8]]**: "Everyone whose name has not been written before the foundation of the world in the book of life of the Lamb who was slain"
- **Decisive criterion**: Not works as basis of salvation, but works as evidence of regeneration

**5. Judged According to Works:**
- **NOT justified by works**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 2#Ephesians 2 . 8|Ephesians 2:8-9]], "By grace you have been saved through faith... not a result of works"
- **BUT judged by works**: Works demonstrate reality of faith ([[Readable Bible/59 - James/James 2#James 2 . 17|James 2:17]], "Faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead")
- **For the elect**: Works show fruit of Spirit, confirm election ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 34|Matthew 25:34-40]])
- **For the reprobate**: Works prove guilt, demonstrate rebellion ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|Matthew 25:41-46]])

## Two Destinies - Eternal Life vs. Eternal Punishment

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|Matthew 25:31-46]] - The Sheep and the Goats:**

**[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|Matthew 25:31-34]], 41, 46**:
"When the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then he will sit on his glorious throne. Before him will be gathered all the nations... Then he will say to those on his right, '**Come, you who are blessed by my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world**'... Then he will say to those on his left, '**Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels**'... And these will go away into **eternal punishment**, but the righteous into **eternal life**."

**The Separation:**

**No Middle Ground:**
- **Right hand (sheep)**: Blessed, inherit kingdom, eternal life
- **Left hand (goats)**: Cursed, eternal fire, eternal punishment
- **No purgatory**: No second chances, no intermediate state for purification
- **No annihilation**: "Eternal punishment" not temporary or ceasing to exist

**No Second Chances:**
- **[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 27|Hebrews 9:27]]**: "It is appointed for man to die once, and after that comes judgment"
- **[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 16#Luke 16 . 26|Luke 16:26]]**: "Between us and you a great chasm has been fixed, in order that those who would pass from here to you may not be able, and none may cross from there to us"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 22#Revelation 22 . 11|Revelation 22:11]]**: "Let the evildoer still do evil, and the filthy still be filthy, and the righteous still do right, and the holy still be holy"—states are fixed

**Eternal Life:**
- **[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 3|John 17:3]]**: "And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent"
- **Quality, not just duration**: Knowing God, dwelling with Him forever
- **[[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 5#1 John 5 . 11|1 John 5:11-12]]**: "God gave us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. Whoever has the Son has life"

**Eternal Punishment:**
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|Matthew 25:41]]**: "Eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels"
- **[[Readable Bible/53 - 2 Thessalonians/2 Thessalonians 1#2 Thessalonians 1 . 9|2 Thessalonians 1:9]]**: "They will suffer the punishment of eternal destruction, away from the presence of the Lord and from the glory of his might"
- **[[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 11|Revelation 14:11]]**: "The smoke of their torment goes up forever and ever, and they have no rest, day or night"
- **Conscious, eternal**: Not annihilation but ongoing existence in torment

**Parallel Duration:**
- **Same Greek word (αἰώνιος, aionios)** for both "eternal life" and "eternal punishment"
- **If punishment is temporary, so is life**: Both are equally everlasting
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 46|Matthew 25:46]]**: "These will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life"—same duration for both

**Degrees of Reward:**

**[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 12|1 Corinthians 3:12-15]]**:

**[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 10|2 Corinthians 5:10]]**:

**Factors Determining Reward:**

- **Love**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 3|1 Corinthians 13:3]], works without love = nothing

**All Saints Perfectly Happy:**
- **Different capacities**: Like vessels of different sizes, all filled to capacity
- **No envy**: Perfect contentment in God's will
- **Greater service**: More responsibility in new creation ([[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 19#Luke 19 . 17|Luke 19:17-19]])
- **[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 16#Psalms 16 . 11|Psalm 16:11]]**: "In your presence there is fullness of joy"—all experience fullness

## Full Deliverance from Exile for the Righteous

**The Exile Narrative Throughout Scripture:**

1. **Adam/Eve expelled from Eden** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 23|Genesis 3:23-24]]): East of Eden, cherubim guard entry

4. **Israel exiled to Babylon** (2 Kings 25): Jerusalem destroyed, temple burned, deportation
5. **Post-Exilic return incomplete** (Ezra-Nehemiah): No Davidic king, no glory cloud, still under foreign domination
6. **Church as "elect exiles"** ([[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 1|1 Peter 1:1]]): In the world but not of it, awaiting homeland

- **No more mourning**: All grief, sorrow, loss removed
- **No more crying**: Tears wiped away ([[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]] fulfilled)
- **No more pain**: Curse of Genesis 3:16 undone

**[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 8|Isaiah 25:8]]**:
"He will swallow up death forever; and the Lord GOD will wipe away tears from all faces, and the reproach of his people he will take away from all the earth, for the LORD has spoken."

## Eternal Sabbath Rest - Hebrews 4:9-11

**The Sabbath Pattern:**

**[[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 1|Genesis 2:1-3]]**:
"Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them. And on the seventh day God finished his work that he had done, and he rested on the seventh day from all his work that he had done. So God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it God rested from all his work that he had done in creation."

**The Sabbath as Type:**
- **Weekly Sabbath**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]], rest from labor
- **Sabbath Year**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 4|Leviticus 25:4]], land rests every seventh year
- **Year of Jubilee**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 10|Leviticus 25:10-13]], every 50th year—liberty proclaimed
- **Canaan Land Rest**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 43|Joshua 21:43-45]], conquest complete, enemies subdued

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 1|Hebrews 4:1-11]] - The Greater Rest:**

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 1|Hebrews 4:1]], 3**:
"Therefore, while the promise of entering his rest still stands, let us fear lest any of you should seem to have failed to reach it... For we who have believed enter that rest."

**[[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 8|Hebrews 4:8-11]]**:
"For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken of another day later on. So then, there remains a **Sabbath rest** for the people of God, for whoever has entered God's rest has also rested from his works as God did from his. Let us therefore strive to enter that rest, so that no one may fall by the same sort of disobedience."

**Progressive Sabbaths:**

1. **Creation Sabbath** ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]]): God's rest after six days—prototype
2. **Weekly Sabbath** ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]]): Weekly remembrance—shadow
3. **Canaan Rest** ([[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 44|Joshua 21:44]]): Land possession, peace from enemies—partial fulfillment
4. **Christ's Rest** ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 28|Matthew 11:28-30]]): "Come to me... and I will give you rest"—inaugurated
5. **Eternal Sabbath** ([[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 4#Hebrews 4 . 9|Hebrews 4:9-11]]; [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 14#Revelation 14 . 13|Revelation 14:13]]): Final rest—consummated

- **Rest from groaning**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 23|Romans 8:23]] longing satisfied
- **Rest from persecution**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 10|Revelation 2:10]] tribulation ended

**Rest in What?:**
- **Rest in God's presence**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalms 16#Psalms 16 . 11|Psalm 16:11]], "In your presence there is fullness of joy"
- **Rest in completed redemption**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 30|John 19:30]], "It is finished"

## Beale's Conclusion - Ultimate Vindication

**G. K. Beale** (from *The Temple and the Church's Mission* and *A New Testament Biblical Theology*):

"The final judgment is the ultimate vindication of God's righteousness and the complete fulfillment of all redemptive promises. What began in Eden—God dwelling with humanity in His temple-garden—reaches its consummation in the New Jerusalem. The exile that began in Genesis 3 is finally, fully, and forever ended for God's people. They are not merely forgiven visitors but permanent residents, royal priests serving in God's cosmic temple.

The pattern of redemptive history—creation, commission, fall, exile, and return—culminates not in another cycle but in **eternal rest**. The 'already' of Christ's resurrection and the 'not yet' of our present groaning collapse into the single eternal 'now' of unbroken fellowship with God. There is no more intrusion because the Consummation has fully arrived. The periphery of delay has given way entirely to the core of eschatological reality.

For the unbeliever, judgment is the final and irrevocable loss of what they have spent their lives rejecting—God's presence, love, and glory. For the believer, judgment is the final and irrevocable reception of what they have spent their lives longing for—God's presence, love, and glory. Both destinies are eternal, both are just, and both display God's perfect righteousness."

## Kline's Framework - Common Grace Ends, Consummation Fully Arrives

**Meredith G. Kline's Intrusion Framework:**

Throughout redemptive history, there has been a **two-register reality**:
- **Core**: The eschatological reality of final judgment (Consummation)
- **Periphery of Delay**: The Common Grace period allowing coexistence of righteous and wicked

**Intrusions of the Consummation:**
- **Flood**: Judgment intrudes, but then Common Grace reestablished ([[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 8|Genesis 9:8-17]])
- **Exodus plagues**: Eschatological judgment on Egypt, then delay restored
- **Christ's cross**: Full Consummation judgment falls on Christ, resurrection inaugurates new age
- **Church Age**: "Already/not yet"—Kingdom present but not fully realized

**At the Parousia:**
- **Common Grace ENDS**: No more sun and rain on just and unjust ([[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 5#Matthew 5 . 45|Matthew 5:45]])
- **Delay REMOVED**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 10#Revelation 10 . 6|Revelation 10:6]], "There will be no more delay"
- **Consummation OVERTAKES**: Becomes the ONLY reality
- **Two-register collapses**: No more coexistence, only eternal separation

**In the New Creation:**
- **No probation**: Testing period over (Adam's probation, Israel's, Church's—all concluded in Christ)
- **No mixed community**: Only righteous remain ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 21#Revelation 21 . 27|Revelation 21:27]])
- **No temporal blessings for wicked**: They are in lake of fire ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 20#Revelation 20 . 15|Revelation 20:15]])
- **Only Consummation ethics**: Perfect righteousness, no compromise

**Kline**: "The ethics of the Sermon on the Mount—turn the other cheek, love your enemies—belong to the Common Grace period. In the Consummation, there are no enemies to love (they are judged), no cheeks to turn (no violence exists). The saints dwell in perfect righteousness because sin has been utterly eradicated. This is not triumphalism but the fulfillment of God's creational purpose: a people wholly devoted to Him, dwelling in His presence forever."

## The Pattern Completed - Chaos → Deliverance, Not Judgment

**The Unique Nature of Consummation Judgment:**

**Previous Cycles:**
- Chaos → Creation → Commission → Sin → **Judgment (Exile)**

**Final Cycle:**
- Chaos → Creation → Commission → No Sin → **Judgment (Deliverance)**

**For the Saints:**
- **[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 1|Romans 8:1]]**: "There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus"—CONSUMMATED
- **Judgment means salvation**: Acquittal, vindication, entrance into glory
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 34|Matthew 25:34]]**: "Come, you who are blessed by my Father, inherit the kingdom"
- **No wrath**: Christ bore all wrath at the cross ([[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 9|Romans 5:9]])

**For the Wicked:**
- **Judgment means condemnation**: Guilty verdict, eternal separation
- **[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|Matthew 25:41]]**: "Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire"
- **Full wrath**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 36|John 3:36]], "The wrath of God remains on him"

**The Difference:**
- **Book of Life**: Names written before foundation of world ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 13#Revelation 13 . 8|Revelation 13:8]])
- **Union with Christ**: In Him = no condemnation; not in Him = condemnation
- **Faith vs. Works**: Faith in Christ's work = justified; trust in own works = condemned

**The goal of all redemptive history is reached: God dwelling with His people, His people dwelling with God, forever and ever. Exile ended. Sabbath entered. Glory revealed. Home at last.**
