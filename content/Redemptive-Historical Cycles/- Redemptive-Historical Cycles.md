# Redemptive-Historical Cycles

*Following the Unfolding Eschatology Pattern with Beale's Redemptive-Historical Framework*

---

## Overview

This framework traces God's redemptive work from Creation to Consummation, integrating:
- **Beale's Biblical Theology**: Temple imagery, already/not yet eschatology, corporate Adam typology
- **Kline's Intrusion Framework**: The Consummation breaking into the age of Common Grace
- **Unfolding Eschatology Pattern**: Creation → Probation → Fall → Redemption → Consummation

## Key Theological Concepts

**Intrusion**: The breaking through of Consummation realities into the period of Common Grace delay (Kline)

**Already/Not Yet**: The overlap of "this present age" and "the age to come" inaugurated by Christ (Beale)

**Corporate Adam**: Each major redemptive cycle features a representative figure recapitulating Adam's commission

**Fivefold Pattern**: Chaos → Creation → Commission → Sin → Judgment (pattern broken only in Christ)

**Temple Theology**: Eden → Tabernacle → Temple → Christ → Church → New Jerusalem
