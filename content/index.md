The Bible is not 66 disconnected books—it is **one unified Story** with one divine Author, progressively revealing God's redemptive plan from Genesis to Revelation, with Jesus Christ as its climax and fulfillment.

The Hyperlinked Bible makes that unified Story **visible and navigable** through digital hypertext.

---

## The Core Principle

Every feature of The Hyperlinked Bible serves one purpose: **helping you read Scripture the way Jesus and the apostles read it**—seeing Christ in all the Scriptures, tracing how God's redemptive plan unfolds from Genesis to Revelation, and understanding how every part connects to the whole Story.
