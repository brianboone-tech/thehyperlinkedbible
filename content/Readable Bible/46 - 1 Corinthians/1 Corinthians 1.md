##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 1
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 1|1]] Paul, called to be an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, and our brother Sosthenes,

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 2
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 2|2]] To the church of God in Corinth, to those sanctified in Christ Jesus and called to be holy, together with all those everywhere who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, their Lord and ours:

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 3
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 3|3]] Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 4
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 4|4]] I always thank my God for you because of the grace He has given you in Christ Jesus.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 5
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 5|5]] For in Him you have been enriched in every way, in all speech and all knowledge,

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 6
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 6|6]] because our testimony about Christ was confirmed in you.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 7
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 7|7]] Therefore you do not lack any spiritual gift as you eagerly await the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 8
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 8|8]] He will sustain you to the end, so that you will be blameless on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 9
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 9|9]] God, who has called you into fellowship with His Son Jesus Christ our Lord, is faithful.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 10
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 10|10]] I appeal to you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that all of you agree together, so that there may be no divisions among you and that you may be united in mind and conviction.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 11
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 11|11]] My brothers, some from Chloe’s household have informed me that there are quarrels among you.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 12
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 12|12]] What I mean is this: Individuals among you are saying, “I follow Paul,” “I follow Apollos,” “I follow Cephas,” or “I follow Christ.”

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 13
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 13|13]] Is Christ divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Were you baptized into the name of Paul?

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 14
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 14|14]] I thank God that I did not baptize any of you except Crispus and Gaius,

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 15
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 15|15]] so no one can say that you were baptized into my name.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 16
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 16|16]] Yes, I also baptized the household of Stephanas; beyond that I do not remember if I baptized anyone else.

##### 1 Corinthians 1 . 17

[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 20|20]] Where is the wise man? Where is the scribe? Where is the philosopher of this age? Has not God made foolish the wisdom of the world?

[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 - 21|21]] For since in the wisdom of God the world through its wisdom did not know Him, God was pleased through the foolishness of what was preached to save those who believe.
