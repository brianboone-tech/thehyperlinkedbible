# 1 Corinthians-R 8

**[[Home/Indexes/Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians|1 Corinthians]]**

[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 7#1 Corinthians-R 7| 1 Corinthians 7]] - [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 9#1 Corinthians-R 9| 1 Corinthians 9]]

---

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 1
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 1|1]] Now about food sacrificed to idols: We know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 2
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 2|2]] The one who thinks he knows something does not yet know as he ought to know.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 3
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 3|3]] But the one who loves God is known by God.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 4
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 4|4]] So about eating food sacrificed to idols: We know that an idol is nothing at all in the world, and that there is no God but one.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 5
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 5|5]] For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth (as there are many so-called gods and lords),

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 6
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 6|6]] yet for us there is but one God, the Father, from whom all things came and for whom we exist. And there is but one Lord, Jesus Christ, through whom all things came and through whom we exist.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 7
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 7|7]] But not everyone has this knowledge. Some people are still so accustomed to idols that they eat such food as if it were sacrificed to an idol. And since their conscience is weak, it is defiled.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 8
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 8|8]] But food does not bring us closer to God: We are no worse if we do not eat, and no better if we do.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 9
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 9|9]] Be careful, however, that your freedom does not become a stumbling block to the weak.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 10
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 10|10]] For if someone with a weak conscience sees you who are well informed eating in an idol’s temple, will he not be encouraged to eat food sacrificed to idols?

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 11
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 11|11]] So this weak brother, for whom Christ died, is destroyed by your knowledge.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 12
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 12|12]] By sinning against your brothers in this way and wounding their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.

##### 1 Corinthians 8 . 13
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 - 13|13]] Therefore, if what I eat causes my brother to stumble, I will never eat meat again, so that I will not cause him to stumble.
