##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 1
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 1|1]] If any of you has a grievance against another, how dare he go to law before the unrighteous instead of before the saints!

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 2
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 2|2]] Do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if you are to judge the world, are you not competent to judge trivial cases?

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 3
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 3|3]] Do you not know that we will judge angels? How much more the things of this life!

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 4
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 4|4]] So if you need to settle everyday matters, do you appoint as judges those of no standing in the church?

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 5
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 5|5]] I say this to your shame. Is there really no one among you wise enough to arbitrate between his brothers?

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 6
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 6|6]] Instead, one brother goes to law against another, and this in front of unbelievers!

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 7
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 7|7]] The very fact that you have lawsuits among you means that you are thoroughly defeated already. Why not rather be wronged? Why not rather be cheated?

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 8
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 8|8]] Instead, you yourselves cheat and do wrong, even against your own brothers!

##### 1 Corinthians 6 . 9
[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 9|9]] Do you not know that the wicked will not inherit the kingdom of God? [[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 - 12|12]] “Everything is permissible for me,” but not everything is beneficial. “Everything is permissible for me,” but I will not be mastered by anything.
