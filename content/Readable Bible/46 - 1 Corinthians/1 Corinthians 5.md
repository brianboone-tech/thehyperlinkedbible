[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 - 9|9]] I wrote you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people.

[[Reference Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 - 10|10]] I was not including the sexually immoral of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters. In that case you would have to leave this world.
