- A. [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 15|15-16]] Which have forsaken the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">right way</span>, and are <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">gone astray</span>, following the way of Balaam the son of Bosor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness; But was rebuked for his iniquity: the dumb ass speaking with man's voice forbad the madness of the prophet.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 17|17-18]] These are wells without water, clouds that are carried with a tempest; to whom the mist of darkness is reserved for ever. For when they speak great swelling words of vanity, they allure through the lusts of the flesh, through much wantonness, those that were clean <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">escaped</span> from them who live in error.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 19|19]] While they promise them liberty, they themselves are the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">servants of corruption</span>:

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 19|19]] for of whom a man is overcome, of the same is he <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">brought in bondage</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 20|20]] For if after they have <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">escaped</span> the pollutions of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, they are again entangled therein, and overcome, the latter end is worse with them than the beginning.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/61 - 2 Peter/2 Peter 2#2 Peter 2 . 21|21-22]] For it had been better for them not to have known the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">way of righteousness</span>, than, after they have known it, to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">turn from</span> the holy commandment delivered unto them. But it is happened unto them according to the true proverb, The dog is turned to his own vomit again; and the sow that was washed to her wallowing in the mire.
