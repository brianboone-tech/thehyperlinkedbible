        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 24|24]] For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 25|25]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 27|27]] For <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">circumcision</span> verily profiteth, if thou keep the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">law</span>: but if thou be a breaker of the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">law</span>, thy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">circumcision</span> is made uncircumcision. Therefore if the uncircumcision keep the righteousness of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision? And shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law?

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 28|28]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 29|29]] For he is not a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Jew</span>, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh: But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter; whose praise is not of men, but of God.
