- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 140#Psalm 140 . 1|1-3]] Deliver me, O LORD, from <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the evil man</span>: preserve me from <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the violent man</span>; Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war. They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; adders' poison is under their <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">lips</span>. Selah.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 140#Psalm 140 . 4|4-5]] Keep me, O LORD, from the hands of <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">the wicked</span>; preserve me from <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the violent man</span>; who have purposed to overthrow my goings. The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the wayside; they have set gins for me. Selah.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 140#Psalm 140 . 6|6-8]] I said unto the LORD, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O LORD. O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle. Grant not, O LORD, the desires of <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">the wicked</span>: further not his wicked device; lest they exalt themselves. Selah.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 140#Psalm 140 . 9|9-13]] As for the head of those that compass me about, let the mischief of their own <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">lips</span> cover them. Let burning coals fall upon them: let them be cast into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not up again. Let not an evil speaker be established in the earth: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">evil</span> shall hunt <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the violent man</span> to overthrow him. I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and the right of the poor. Surely the righteous shall give thanks unto thy name: the upright shall dwell in thy presence.
