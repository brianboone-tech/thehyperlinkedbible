- A. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 8|8]] And the LORD spake unto Aaron, Behold, I also have given thee the charge of mine heave offerings of all the hallowed things of the children of Israel; unto thee have I given them by reason of the anointing, and to thy sons, by an ordinance for ever.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 9|9]] This shall be thine of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">most holy things</span>, reserved from the fire: every oblation of theirs, every <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">meat offering</span> of theirs, and every <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sin offering</span> of theirs, and every <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trespass offering</span> of theirs, which they shall render unto me, shall be most holy for thee and for thy sons.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 10|10]] In the most holy place shalt thou eat it; every male shall eat it: it shall be holy unto thee.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 11|11]] And this is thine; the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">heave offering</span> of their gift, with all the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wave offerings</span> of the children of Israel: I have given them unto thee, and to thy sons and to thy daughters with thee, by a statute for ever: <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">every one that is clean</span> in thy house shall eat of it.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 12|12]] All the best of the oil, and all the best of the wine, and of the wheat, the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">firstfruits</span> of them which they shall offer unto the LORD, them have I given thee.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 13|13]] And whatsoever is first ripe in the land, which they shall bring unto the LORD, shall be thine; <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">every one that is clean</span> in thine house shall eat of it.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 14|14]] Every thing <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">devoted</span> in Israel shall be thine.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 15|15]] Every thing that openeth the matrix in all flesh, which they bring unto the LORD, whether it be of men or beasts, shall be thine: nevertheless the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">firstborn</span> of man shalt thou surely redeem, and the firstling of unclean beasts shalt thou redeem.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 16|16]] And those that are to be redeemed from a month old shalt thou redeem, according to thine estimation, for the money of five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 17|17]] But the firstling of a cow, or the firstling of a sheep, or the firstling of a goat, thou shalt not redeem; they are holy: thou shalt sprinkle their blood upon the altar, and shalt burn their fat for an offering made by fire, for a sweet savour unto the LORD.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 18|18]] And the flesh of them shall be thine, as the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wave breast</span> and as the right shoulder are thine.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 19|19]] All the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heave offerings</span> of the holy things, which the children of Israel offer unto the LORD, have I given thee, and thy sons and thy daughters with thee, by a statute for ever: it is a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">covenant of salt</span> for ever before the LORD unto thee and to thy seed with thee.
