**Cycle 1: Signs Before the End (24:5-14)**

- A. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 5|5]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 6|6]] For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">deceive many</span>. And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the end is not yet</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 7|7]] For <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">nation shall rise against nation</span>, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 8|8]] All these are the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">beginning of sorrows</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 9|9]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 10|10]] Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted, and shall kill you: and ye shall be hated of all nations for my name's sake. And then shall many be offended, and shall <span style="color: #943126; font-weight: bold;">betray one another, and shall hate one another</span>.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 11|11]] And many <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many</span>.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 12|12]] And because iniquity shall abound, the <span style="color: #943126; font-weight: bold;">love of many shall wax cold</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 13|13]] But he that shall <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">endure unto the end</span>, the same shall be saved.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 14|14a]] And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">nations</span>;

- A'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 14|14b]] and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">then shall the end come</span>.

**Cycle 2: The Great Tribulation and Son of Man's Coming (24:15-31)**

- A. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 15|15]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 20|20]] When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, stand in the holy place, (whoso readeth, let him understand:) Then let them which be in Judaea flee into the mountains: Let him which is on the housetop not come down to take any thing out of his house: Neither let him which is in the field return back to take his clothes. And woe unto them that are with child, and to them that give suck in those days! But pray ye that your <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">flight</span> be not in the winter, neither on the sabbath day:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 21|21]] For then shall be <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">great tribulation</span>, such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 22|22]] And except those days should be shortened, there should no <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">flesh be saved</span>: but for the elect's sake those days shall be shortened.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 23|23]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 24|24]] Then if any man shall say unto you, Lo, here is Christ, or there; believe it not. For there shall arise <span style="color: #943126; font-weight: bold;">false Christs, and false prophets</span>, and shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 25|25]] Behold, <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">I have told you before</span>.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 26|26]] Wherefore if they shall say unto you, Behold, he is in the desert; go not forth: behold, he is in the secret chambers; believe it not.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 27|27]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 28|28]] For as the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be. For wheresoever the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">carcase</span> is, there will the eagles be gathered together.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 29|29]] Immediately after the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">tribulation</span> of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken:

- A'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 30|30]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 24#Matthew 24 . 31|31]] And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven</span> with power and great glory. And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.
