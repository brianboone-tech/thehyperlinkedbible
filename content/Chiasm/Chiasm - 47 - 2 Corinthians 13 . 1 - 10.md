[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 2|2]] This is the third time I am coming to you. In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established. I told you before, and foretell you, as if I were <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">present</span>, the second time; and being <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">absent</span> now I write to them which heretofore have sinned, and to all other, that, if I come again, I will not spare:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 3|3]]-[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 4|4]] Since ye seek a proof of Christ speaking in me, which to you-ward is not <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weak</span>, but is mighty in you. For though he was crucified through <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weakness</span>, yet he liveth by the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">power</span> of God. For we also are <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weak</span> in him, but we shall live with him by the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">power</span> of God toward you.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 5|5a]] Examine yourselves, whether ye be in <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the faith</span>; prove your own selves.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 5|5b]]-[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 6|6]] Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">reprobates</span>? But I trust that ye shall know that we are not <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">reprobates</span>.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 7|7a]] Now I pray to God that ye do no evil;

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 7|7b]] not that we should appear approved, but that ye should do that which is honest, though we be as <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">reprobates</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 8|8]] For we can do nothing against <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the truth</span>, but for <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the truth</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 9|9]] For we are glad, when we are <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weak</span>, and ye are <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">strong</span>: and this also we wish, even your perfection.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 10|10]] Therefore I write these things being <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">absent</span>, lest being <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">present</span> I should use sharpness, according to the power which the Lord hath given me to edification, and not to destruction.
