    - B'. [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 17|17-20]] And when Joshua heard the noise of the people as they shouted, he said unto Moses, There is a noise of war in the camp. For they said unto me, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Make us gods, which shall go before us: for as for this Moses, the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we wot not what is become of him.
