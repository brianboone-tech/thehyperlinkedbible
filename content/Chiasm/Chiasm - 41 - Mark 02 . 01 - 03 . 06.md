[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 2#Mark 2 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 2#Mark 2 . 12|12]] And again he entered into Capernaum after some days; and it was noised that he was in the house. And straightway many were gathered together, insomuch that there was no room to receive them, no, not so much as about the door: and he preached the word unto them. And they come unto him, bringing one <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sick of the palsy</span>, which was borne of four. And when they could not come nigh unto him for the press, they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed wherein the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sick of the palsy</span> lay. When Jesus saw their faith, he said unto the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sick of the palsy</span>, Son, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">thy sins be forgiven thee</span>. But there was certain of the scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts, Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only? And immediately when Jesus perceived in his spirit that they so reasoned within themselves, he said unto them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts? Whether is it easier to say to the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sick of the palsy</span>, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Thy sins be forgiven thee</span>; or to say, Arise, and take up thy bed, and walk? But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins, (he saith to the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sick of the palsy</span>,) I say unto thee, Arise, and take up thy bed, and go thy way into thine house. And immediately he arose, took up the bed, and went forth before them all; insomuch that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, We never saw it on this fashion.

[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 2#Mark 2 . 23|23]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 2#Mark 2 . 28|28]] And it came to pass, that he went through the corn fields on the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">sabbath</span> day; and his disciples began, as they went, to <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">pluck the ears of corn</span>. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 3#Mark 3 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 3#Mark 3 . 6|6]] And he entered again into the synagogue; and there was a man there which had a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">withered hand</span>. And they watched him, whether he would <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heal</span> him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse him. And he saith unto the man which had the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">withered hand</span>, Stand forth. And he saith unto them, Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? to save life, or to kill? But they held their peace. And when he had looked round about on them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their hearts, he saith unto the man, Stretch forth thine hand. And he stretched it out: and his hand was <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">restored</span> whole as the other. And the Pharisees went forth, and straightway took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.
