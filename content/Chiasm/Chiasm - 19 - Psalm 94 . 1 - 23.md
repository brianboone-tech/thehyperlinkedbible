He that <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">chastiseth</span> the heathen, shall not he correct? he that <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">teacheth</span> man knowledge, shall not he know?

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 94#Psalm 94 . 14|14-15]] For the LORD will not cast off his <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">people</span>, neither will he forsake his <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">inheritance</span>. But judgment shall return unto righteousness: and all the upright in heart shall follow it.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 94#Psalm 94 . 16|16-19]] Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the workers of iniquity</span>? Unless the LORD had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence. When I said, My foot slippeth; thy mercy, O LORD, held me up. In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 94#Psalm 94 . 20|20-23]] Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by a law? They gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood. But the LORD is my defence; and my God is the rock of my refuge. And <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">he shall bring upon them their own iniquity, and shall cut them off in their own wickedness</span>; yea, the LORD our God shall cut them off.
