    - B. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 1|1b-2]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Time</span> of trouble and deliverance; resurrection

        and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book. And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 3|3]] The <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wise</span> shine with brightness

            And they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament; and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars for ever and ever.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 4|4]] Daniel told to <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">seal the book</span> until the time of the end

                But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 5|5-6]] Daniel observes two figures; asks about <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the end of these wonders</span>

                    Then I Daniel looked, and, behold, there stood other two, the one on this side of the bank of the river, and the other on that side of the bank of the river. And one said to the man clothed in linen, which was upon the waters of the river, How long shall it be to the end of these wonders?

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 7|7]] Center: angel swears an oath about <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">time, times, and an half</span>

                        And I heard the man clothed in linen, which was upon the waters of the river, when he held up his right hand and his left hand unto heaven, and sware by him that liveth for ever that it shall be for a time, times, and an half; and when he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people, all these things shall be finished.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 8|8]] Daniel questions <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the end of these things</span>

                    And I heard, but I understood not: then said I, O my Lord, what shall be the end of these things?

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 9|9]] Words <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">sealed till the time of the end</span>

                And he said, Go thy way, Daniel: for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 10|10]] The <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wise</span> shall understand

            Many shall be purified, and made white, and tried; but the wicked shall do wickedly: and none of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 11|11-12]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Time</span> calculations for deliverance; blessed waiting

        And from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days. Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.
