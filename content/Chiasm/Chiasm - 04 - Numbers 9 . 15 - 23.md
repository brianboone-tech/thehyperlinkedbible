- A. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 15|15]] On the day that the tabernacle, the Tent of the Testimony, was set up, the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">cloud covered the tabernacle</span> and appeared like fire above the tabernacle from evening until morning.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 18|18]] At the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">commandment of the LORD</span> the Israelites set out, and at the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">commandment of the LORD</span> they camped. As long as the cloud remained over the tabernacle, they remained encamped.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 19|19]] Even when the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">cloud lingered over the tabernacle for many days</span>, the Israelites kept the LORD's charge and did not set out.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 20|20]] Sometimes the cloud remained over the tabernacle for only a <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">few days</span>,

                - E. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 20|20b]] and they would camp at the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">LORD's command</span> and set out at the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">LORD's command</span>.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 21|21]] Sometimes the cloud remained only from <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">evening until morning</span>, and when it lifted in the morning, they would set out. Whether it was by day or by night, when the cloud was taken up, they would set out.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 22|22]] Whether the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">cloud lingered for two days, a month, or longer</span>, the Israelites camped and did not set out as long as the cloud remained over the tabernacle; but when it was lifted, they would set out.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 23|23]] They camped at the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">LORD's command</span>, and they set out at the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">LORD's command</span>;

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 23|23b]] they carried out the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">LORD's charge</span> according to His command through Moses.
