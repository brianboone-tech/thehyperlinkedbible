- 1A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 1|1-2]] Then <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the word of the LORD came to me, saying</span>, "Son of man, prophesy against <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the shepherds of Israel</span>. Prophesy and tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says:

    - 1B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 2|2-3]] 'Woe to the shepherds of Israel, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">who only feed themselves! Should not the shepherds feed their flock?</span> You eat the fat, wear the wool, and butcher the fattened sheep, but you do not feed the flock.

        - 1C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 4|4]] You have not strengthened the weak, healed the sick, bound up the injured, brought back the strays, or <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">searched for the lost</span>. Instead, you have ruled them with violence and cruelty.

            - 1D. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 5|5]] They were scattered for <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">lack of a shepherd</span>,

                - 1E. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 5|5-6]] and when they were scattered they <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">became food for all the wild beasts</span>. My flock went astray on all the mountains and every high hill. They were <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">scattered over the face of all the earth</span>, with no one to search for them or seek them out.'

                - 1E'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 7|7-8]] Therefore, you shepherds, hear the word of the LORD: 'As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, because My flock lacks a shepherd and has <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">become prey and food for every wild beast</span>,

            - 1D'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 8|8]] and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">because there was no shepherd</span>

        - 1C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 8|8]] and because My shepherds <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">did not search for My flock</span>

    - 1B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 8|8]] but <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">fed themselves instead</span>,

- 1A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 9|9-10]] therefore, you shepherds, hear the word of the LORD!' This is what the Lord GOD says: 'Behold, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I am against the shepherds</span>, and I will demand from them My flock and remove them from tending the flock, so that they can no longer feed themselves. For I will deliver My flock from their mouths, and it will no longer be food for them.'

- 2A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 11|11-12]] For this is what the Lord GOD says: 'Behold, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I Myself will search for My flock and seek them out</span>. As a shepherd looks for his scattered sheep when he is among the flock, so I will look for My flock. I will rescue them from all the places to which they were scattered on a day of clouds and darkness.

    - 2B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 13|13]] I will bring them out from the peoples, gather them from the countries, and bring them into their own land. I will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">feed them on the mountains of Israel</span>, in the ravines, and in all the settlements of the land.

        - 2C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 14|14]] I will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">feed them in good pasture</span>, and the lofty mountains of Israel will be their grazing land.

            - 2D. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 14|14]] There they will <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">lie down in a good grazing land</span>;

            - 2D'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 14|14]] they will feed in <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">rich pasture on the mountains</span> of Israel.

        - 2C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 15|15]] I will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">tend My flock</span> and make them lie down, declares the Lord GOD.

    - 2B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 16|16]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">I will seek the lost, bring back the strays</span>, bind up the broken, and strengthen the weak; but the sleek and strong I will destroy. I will shepherd them with justice.'

- 3A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 16|16]] I will shepherd them with <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">justice</span>.'

    - 3B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 17|17]] This is what the Lord GOD says to you, My flock: '<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">I will judge between one sheep and another</span>, between the rams and the goats.

        - 3C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 18|18]] Is it not enough for you to feed on the good pasture? Must you also <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">trample the rest of the pasture</span> with your feet? Is it not enough for you to drink the clear waters? Must you also <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">muddy the rest</span> with your feet?

        - 3C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 19|19]] Why must My flock feed on what <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">your feet have trampled</span>, and drink what <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">your feet have muddied</span>?'

    - 3B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 20|20]] Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says to them: 'Behold, I Myself will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judge between the fat sheep and the lean sheep</span>.

- 3A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 22|22]] Since you shove with flank and shoulder, butting all the weak ones with your horns until you have scattered them abroad, I will save My flock, and they will no longer be prey. I will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">judge between one sheep and another</span>.

- 4A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|23-24]] I will appoint over them <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">one shepherd, My servant David</span>, and he will feed them. He will feed them and be their shepherd. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I, the LORD, will be their God</span>, and My servant David will be a prince among them. I, the LORD, have spoken.

    - 4B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 25|25-27]] I will make with them a <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">covenant of peace</span> and rid the land of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wild animals</span>, so that they may <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">dwell securely</span> in the wilderness and sleep in the forest. I will make them and the places around My hill a blessing. I will send down showers in season—showers of blessing. The <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">trees of the field will give their fruit</span>, and the land will yield its produce; My flock will be <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">secure in their land</span>. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I have broken the bars of their yoke and delivered them from the hands that enslaved them.

        - 4C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 27|27]] Then <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">they will know that I am the LORD</span>, when I have broken the bars of their yoke and delivered them from the hands that enslaved them.

    - 4B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 28|28-29]] They will no longer be prey for the nations, and the beasts of the earth will not consume them. They will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">dwell securely</span>, and no one will frighten them. And I will raise up for them a garden of renown, and they will no longer be victims of famine in the land or bear the scorn of the nations.

- 4A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 30|30-31]] Then they will know that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I, the LORD their God, am with them</span>, and that they, the house of Israel, are My people,' declares the Lord GOD. 'You are My flock, the sheep of My pasture, My people, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I am your God</span>,' declares the Lord GOD."
