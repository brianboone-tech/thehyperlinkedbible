- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 19#Psalm 19 . 1|19:1-4]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The heavens declare the glory of God</span>; and the firmament sheweth his handywork. Day unto day uttereth <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">speech</span>, and night unto night sheweth <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">knowledge</span>. There is no <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">speech</span> nor language, where their voice is not heard. Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">words</span> to the end of the world.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 19#Psalm 19 . 5|19:5-6]] In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun, Which is as a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, and rejoiceth as a strong man to run a race. His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and his circuit unto the ends of it: and there is <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">nothing hid from the heat thereof</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 19#Psalm 19 . 7|19:7-11]] The law of the LORD is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple. The statutes of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes. The fear of the LORD is clean, enduring for ever: the judgments of the LORD are true and righteous altogether. More to be desired are they than gold, yea, than much fine gold: sweeter also than honey and the honeycomb. Moreover by them is thy servant warned: and in keeping of them there is great reward.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 19#Psalm 19 . 12|19:12-13]] Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">secret faults</span>. Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be upright, and I shall be innocent from the great transgression.
