- A. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 1|1]] Hear the word that the LORD speaks to you, O house of Israel. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 2|2]] This is what the LORD says: "Do not learn the ways of the nations or be terrified by the signs in the heavens, though the nations themselves are terrified by them.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 3|3]] For the customs of the peoples are worthless; they <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">cut down a tree</span> from the forest; it is shaped with a chisel by the hands of a craftsman. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 4|4]] They adorn it with silver and gold and fasten it with hammer and nails, so that it will not totter.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 5|5]] Like scarecrows in a cucumber patch, their <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">idols cannot speak</span>. They must be carried because they cannot walk. <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Do not fear them</span>, for they can do no harm, and neither can they do any good."

            - D. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 6|6]] There is <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">none like You</span>, O LORD. <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">You are great</span>, and Your name is mighty in power. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 7|7]] Who would not <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">fear You</span>, O King of nations? This is Your due. For among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, there is <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">none like You</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 8|8]] But they are altogether <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">senseless and foolish</span>, instructed by <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">worthless idols</span> made of wood!

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 9|9]] Hammered silver is brought from Tarshish, and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">gold</span> from Uphaz—the work of a craftsman from the hands of a goldsmith. Their clothes are blue and purple, all fashioned by skilled workers.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 10|10]] But the LORD is the true God; He is the living God and eternal King. The earth quakes at His wrath, and the nations cannot endure His indignation.
