- A. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 2|2]] Elect according to the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">foreknowledge</span> of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Jesus Christ</span>: Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 3|3]] Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Jesus Christ</span>, which according to his abundant mercy hath <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">begotten us again</span> unto a lively hope by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

        - C. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 4|4]] To an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you,

            - D. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 5|5]] Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">salvation</span> ready to be revealed in the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">last time</span>.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 6|6]] Wherein ye greatly rejoice, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">though now</span> for a season, if need be, ye are in heaviness through <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">manifold temptations</span>:

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 7|7]] That the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">trial of your faith</span>, being much more precious than of gold that perisheth, though it be tried with fire, might be found unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ:

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 8|8]] Whom having not seen, ye love; in whom, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">though now</span> ye see him not, yet believing, ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory:

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 9|9]] Receiving the end of your faith, even the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">salvation</span> of your souls.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 10|10]] Of which salvation the prophets have enquired and searched diligently, who prophesied of the grace that should come unto you:

            - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 11|11]] Searching what, or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ which was in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">sufferings</span> of Christ, and the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">glory</span> that should follow.

            - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 12|12]] Unto whom it was revealed, that not unto themselves, but unto us they did minister the things, which are now reported unto you by them that have preached the gospel unto you with the Holy Ghost sent down from heaven; which things the angels desire to look into.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 13|13]] Wherefore <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">gird up the loins</span> of your mind, be sober, and hope to the end for the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ;

        - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 14|14]] As <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">obedient children</span>, not fashioning yourselves according to the former lusts in your ignorance:

        - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 15|15]] But as he which hath called you is <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">holy</span>, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation;

        - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 16|16]] Because it is written, Be ye <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">holy</span>; for I am holy.

        - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 17|17]] And if ye call on the Father, who without respect of persons judgeth according to every man's work, pass the time of your sojourning here in fear:

        - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 18|18]] Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers;

        - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 19|19]] But with the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb</span> without blemish and without spot:

- A'. [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 20|20]] Who verily was <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">foreordained</span> before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you,

    - [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 1#1 Peter 1 . 21|21]] Who by him do believe in God, that raised him up from the dead, and gave him glory; that your faith and hope might be in God.
