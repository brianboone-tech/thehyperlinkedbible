- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 2|2]] I say then, Hath <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">God cast away his people</span>? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">God hath not cast away his people</span> which he foreknew. Wot ye not what the scripture saith of Elias? how he maketh intercession to God against Israel, saying,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 3|3]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 4|4]] Lord, they have killed thy prophets, and digged down thine altars; and I am left alone, and they seek my life. But what saith the answer of God unto him? I have reserved to myself seven thousand men, who have not bowed the knee to the image of Baal.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 5|5]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 6|6]] Even so then at this present time also there is a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">remnant according to the election of grace</span>. And if by <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">grace</span>, then is it no more of works: otherwise <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">grace</span> is no more <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">grace</span>. But if it be of works, then is it no more grace: otherwise work is no more work.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 7|7]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 10|10]] What then? Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for; but the election hath obtained it, and the rest were <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">blinded</span> (According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day. And David saith, Let their table be made a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them: Let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see, and bow down their back alway.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 11|11]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 15|15]] I say then, Have they stumbled that they should fall? God forbid: but rather through their <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fall salvation is come unto the Gentiles</span>, for to provoke them to jealousy. Now if the fall of them be the riches of the world, and the diminishing of them the riches of the Gentiles; how much more their fulness? For I speak to you Gentiles, inasmuch as I am the apostle of the Gentiles, I magnify mine office: If by any means I may provoke to emulation them which are my flesh, and might save some of them. For if the casting away of them be the reconciling of the world, what shall the receiving of them be, but life from the dead?

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 16|16]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 21|21]] For if the firstfruit be holy, the lump is also holy: and if the root be holy, so are the branches. And if some of the branches be broken off, and thou, being a wild <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">olive tree</span>, wert <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">graffed in</span> among them, and with them partakest of the root and fatness of the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">olive tree</span>; Boast not against the branches. But if thou boast, thou bearest not the root, but the root thee. Thou wilt say then, The branches were broken off, that I might be graffed in. Well; because of unbelief they were broken off, and thou standest by faith. Be not highminded, but fear: For if God spared not the natural branches, take heed lest he also spare not thee.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 22|22]] Behold therefore the <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">goodness</span> and <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">severity</span> of God: on them which fell, <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">severity</span>; but toward thee, <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">goodness</span>, if thou continue in his goodness: otherwise thou also shalt be cut off.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 23|23]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 24|24]] And they also, if they abide not still in unbelief, shall be <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">graffed in</span>: for God is able to <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">graff them in</span> again. For if thou wert cut out of the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">olive tree</span> which is wild by nature, and wert graffed contrary to nature into a good <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">olive tree</span>: how much more shall these, which be the natural branches, be graffed into their own <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">olive tree</span>?

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 25|25]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 32|32]] For I would not, brethren, that ye should be ignorant of this <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mystery</span>, lest ye should be wise in your own conceits; that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Gentiles</span> be come in. And so all <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Israel shall be saved</span>: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob: For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins. As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes: but as touching the election, they are beloved for the fathers' sakes. For the gifts and calling of God are without repentance. For as ye in times past have not believed God, yet have now obtained <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span> through their unbelief: Even so have these also now not believed, that through your <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span> they also may obtain <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span>. For God hath concluded them all in unbelief, that he might have <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span> upon all.
