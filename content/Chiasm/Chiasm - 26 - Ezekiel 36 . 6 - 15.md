[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 6|6-7]] Therefore, prophesy concerning the land of Israel and tell the mountains and hills, the ravines and valleys, that this is what the Lord GOD says: Behold, I have spoken in My burning zeal because you have endured <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the reproach of the nations.</span> Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I have sworn with an uplifted hand that surely the nations around you will endure <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">reproach</span> of their own.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 8|8-9]] But you, O mountains of Israel, will produce branches and bear <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">fruit</span> for <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">My people Israel,</span> for they will soon come home.         - C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 10|10]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I will multiply the people upon you</span>—the house of Israel in its entirety. The cities will be inhabited and the ruins rebuilt.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 11|11]] I will fill you with people and animals, and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">they will multiply</span> and be fruitful. I will make you as inhabited as you once were, and I will make you prosper more than before. Then <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">you will know that I am the LORD.</span>

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 12|12-14]] Yes, I will cause <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">My people Israel</span> to walk upon you; they will possess you, and you will be their inheritance, and you will no longer deprive them of their children. For this is what the Lord GOD says: Because people say to you, 'You devour men and deprive your nation of its children,' therefore you will no longer devour men or deprive your nation of its children, declares the Lord GOD.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 15|15]] I will no longer allow the taunts of the nations to be heard against you, and you will no longer endure <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the reproach</span> of the peoples or cause your nation to stumble, declares the Lord GOD."
