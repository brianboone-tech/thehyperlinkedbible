[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 27#Psalm 27 . 1|1-3]] The LORD is my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">light</span> and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? When the wicked, even mine <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">enemies</span> and my foes, came upon me to eat up my flesh, they stumbled and fell. Though an host should encamp against me, my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heart</span> shall not fear: though war should rise against me, in this will I be confident.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 27#Psalm 27 . 8|8-10]] When thou saidst, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Seek</span> ye my <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">face</span>; my heart said unto thee, Thy face, LORD, will I seek. <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Hide</span> not thy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">face</span> far from me; put not thy servant away in anger: thou hast been my help; leave me not, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation. - A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 27#Psalm 27 . 11|11-14]] Teach me thy way, O LORD, and lead me in a plain path, because of mine <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">enemies</span>. Deliver me not over unto the will of mine enemies: for false witnesses are risen up against me, and such as breathe out cruelty. I had fainted, unless I had believed to see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living. Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heart</span>: wait, I say, on the LORD.
