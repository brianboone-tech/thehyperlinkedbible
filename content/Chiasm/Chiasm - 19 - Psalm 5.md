- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 1|1-3]] Give ear to my words, O LORD, consider my meditation. Hearken unto the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">voice of my cry</span>, my King, and my God: for unto thee will I pray. My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O LORD; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 4|4-5]] For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">wickedness</span>: neither shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">evil</span> dwell with thee. The foolish shall not stand in thy sight: thou hatest all workers of iniquity.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 6|6]] Thou shalt destroy them that <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">speak leasing</span>: the LORD will abhor the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">bloody</span> and deceitful man.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 7|7]] But as for me, I will come into thy house in the multitude of thy mercy: and in thy fear will I <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">worship</span> toward thy holy temple.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 8|8]] Lead me, O LORD, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 9|9]] For there is no faithfulness in their <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mouth</span>; their inward part is very wickedness; their throat is an <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">open sepulchre</span>; they <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">flatter with their tongue.</span>

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 10|10]] Destroy thou them, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions; for they have <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">rebelled</span> against thee.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 11|11-12]] But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice: let them ever <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">shout for joy</span>, because thou defendest them: let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee. For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield.
