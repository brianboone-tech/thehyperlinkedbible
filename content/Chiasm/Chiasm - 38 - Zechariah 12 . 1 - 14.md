[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 1|1]] The burden of the word of the LORD for Israel, saith the LORD, which stretcheth forth the heavens, and layeth the foundation of the earth, and formeth the spirit of man within him.

- A. a. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 2|2]] Behold, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I will make Jerusalem</span> a cup of trembling unto <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">all the people</span> round about,

	- b. when they shall be in the siege both against Judah and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">against Jerusalem</span>.

	- a'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 3|3]] And <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">in that day will I make Jerusalem</span> a burdensome stone for <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">all people</span>:

	- b'. all that burden themselves with it shall be cut in pieces, though <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">all the people (nations)</span> of the earth be gathered together <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">against it</span>.

	- B. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 4|4]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span>, saith the LORD, I will smite every horse with astonishment, and his rider with madness: and I will open mine eyes upon <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the house of Judah</span>, and will smite every horse of the people with blindness. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 5|5]] And <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the governors of Judah</span> shall say in their heart, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">The inhabitants of Jerusalem</span> shall be my strength <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">in the LORD of hosts their God</span>.

		- C. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 6|6]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> will I make <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">the governors of Judah</span> like an hearth of fire among the wood, and like a torch of fire in a sheaf; and they shall devour all the people round about, on the right hand and on the left: <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">and Jerusalem shall be inhabited</span> again in her own place, even in <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Jerusalem</span>.

	- B'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 7|7]] The <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">LORD</span> also shall save the tents of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Judah</span> first, that the glory of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the house of David</span> and the glory of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the inhabitants of Jerusalem</span> do not magnify themselves against <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Judah</span>. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 8|8]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> shall the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">LORD</span> defend <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the inhabitants of Jerusalem</span>; and he that is feeble among them at that day shall be as <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">David</span>; and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the house of David</span> shall be as God, as the angel of the LORD before them.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 9|9]] And it shall come to pass <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">in that day</span>, that I will seek to destroy <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">all the nations that come against Jerusalem</span>.

- A. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|10]] And I will pour upon <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the house of David, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem</span>, the spirit of grace and of supplications:

	- B. and they shall look upon me whom they have pierced, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">they shall mourn</span> for him, as one <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">mourneth</span> for his only son, and shall be in bitterness for him, as one that is in bitterness for his firstborn.

		- C. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 11|11]] In that day shall there be a great mourning in Jerusalem, as the mourning of Hadadrimmon in the valley of Megiddon.

	- B'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 12|12]] And the land <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">shall mourn</span>, every family apart; the family of the house of David apart, and their wives apart; the family of the house of Nathan apart, and their wives apart; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 13|13]] The family of the house of Levi apart, and their wives apart; the family of Shimei apart, and their wives apart; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 14|14]] All the families that remain, every family apart, and their wives apart.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 1|13:1]] In that day there shall be a fountain opened to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem</span> for sin and for uncleanness.
