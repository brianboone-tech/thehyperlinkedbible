- A. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 1|1-4]] Jude, the servant of Jesus Christ, and brother of James, to them that are sanctified by God the Father, and preserved in Jesus Christ, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">called</span>: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Mercy</span> unto you, and peace, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">love</span>, be multiplied. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Beloved</span>, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort you that ye should earnestly contend for the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">faith</span> which was once delivered unto the saints. For there are certain men crept in unawares, who were before of old ordained to this condemnation, ungodly men, turning <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the grace of our God</span> into lasciviousness, and denying <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the only Lord God</span>, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">our Lord Jesus Christ</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 5|5-7]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Three examples</span> - I will therefore put you in remembrance, though ye once knew this, how that the Lord, having <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">saved</span> the people out of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Egypt</span>, afterward <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">destroyed</span> them that believed not. And the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">angels</span> which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span> of the great day. Even as <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Sodom and Gomorrha</span>, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">fornication</span>, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 8|8]] Likewise also these <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">filthy dreamers</span> <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">defile the flesh</span>, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">despise dominion</span>, and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">speak evil of dignities</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 9|9]] Yet <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Michael the archangel</span>, when contending with the devil he disputed about the body of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Moses</span>, durst not bring against him a <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">railing accusation</span>, but said, The Lord rebuke thee.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 10|10]] But these <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">speak evil</span> of those things which they know not: but what they <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">know naturally</span>, as <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">brute beasts</span>, in those things they corrupt themselves.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 11|11-19]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Three examples</span> - Woe unto them! for they have gone in the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">way of Cain</span>, and ran greedily after the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">error of Balaam</span> for reward, and perished in the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">gainsaying of Core</span>. These are <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">spots</span> in your feasts of charity, when they feast with you, feeding themselves without fear: clouds they are without water, carried about of winds; trees whose fruit withereth, without fruit, twice dead, plucked up by the roots; Raging <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">waves of the sea</span>, foaming out their own shame; wandering stars, to whom is reserved the blackness of darkness for ever. And <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Enoch</span> also, the seventh from Adam, prophesied of these, saying, Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints, To execute <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span> upon all, and to convince all that are ungodly among them of all their ungodly deeds which they have ungodly committed, and of all their hard speeches which ungodly sinners have spoken against him. These are <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">murmurers</span>, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">complainers</span>, walking after their own lusts; and their mouth speaketh great <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">swelling words</span>, having men's persons in admiration because of advantage. But, beloved, remember ye the words which were spoken before of the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ; How that they told you there should be <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">mockers</span> in the last time, who should walk after their own ungodly lusts. These be they who <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">separate themselves</span>, sensual, having not the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Spirit</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/65 - Jude/Jude 1#Jude 1 . 20|20-25]] But ye, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">beloved</span>, building up yourselves on your most holy <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">faith</span>, praying in the Holy Ghost, Keep yourselves in the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">love</span> of God, looking for the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span> of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life. And of some have compassion, making a difference: And others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire; hating even the garment spotted by the flesh. Now unto him that is <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">able to keep you from falling</span>, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy, To <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the only wise God our Saviour</span>, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen.
