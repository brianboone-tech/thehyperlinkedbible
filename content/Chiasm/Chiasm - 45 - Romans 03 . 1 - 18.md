    - B.         - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 4|4b]] as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 5|5]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 8|8]] But if our unrighteousness commend the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (I speak as a man) God forbid: for then how shall God judge the world? For if the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">truth of God</span> hath more abounded through my <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">lie</span> unto his glory; why yet am I also judged as a sinner? And not rather, (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 9|9]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 18|18]] What then? are we better than they? No, in no wise: for we have before proved both <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Jews</span> and Gentiles, that they are all under sin; As it is written, There is <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">none</span> righteous, no, not one: There is <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">none</span> that understandeth, there is <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">none</span> that seeketh after God. They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">none</span> that doeth good, no, not one. Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips: Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness: Their feet are swift to shed blood: Destruction and misery are in their ways: And the way of peace have they not known: There is no fear of God before their eyes.
