- A. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 1|1-3]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Nebuchadnezzar</span> the king made <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">an image</span> of gold, whose height was threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof six cubits: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">he set it up</span> in the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon. Then Nebuchadnezzar the king sent to gather together the princes, the governors, and the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellers, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the image which Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up. Then the princes, the governors, and captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellers, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, were gathered together unto the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up; and they stood before <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 4|4-7]] Then an herald cried aloud, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">To you it is commanded</span>, O people, nations, and languages, That at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, ye fall down and worship the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king hath set up: And whoso falleth not down and worshippeth shall the same hour be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace. Therefore at that time, when <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">all the people heard the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and all kinds of musick</span>, all the people, the nations, and the languages, fell down and worshipped the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 8|8]] Wherefore at that time certain Chaldeans came near, and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">accused the Jews</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 9|9-12]] They spake and said to the king Nebuchadnezzar, O king, live for ever. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Thou, O king, hast made a decree</span>, that every man that shall hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, shall fall down and worship the golden image: And whoso falleth not down and worshippeth, that he should be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace. There are certain Jews whom thou hast set over the affairs of the province of Babylon, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>; these men, O king, have not regarded thee: <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">they serve not thy gods</span>, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.

        - D. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 13|13]] Then <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Nebuchadnezzar</span> in his <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">rage and fury</span> commanded to bring <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>. Then they brought these men before the king.

            - E. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 14|14]] Nebuchadnezzar spake and said unto them, Is it true, O <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>, <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">do not ye serve my gods, nor worship the golden image which I have set up</span>?

                - F. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 15|15]] Now if ye be ready that at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, ye fall down and worship the image which I have made; well: but if ye worship not, <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">ye shall be cast the same hour into the midst of a burning fiery furnace</span>; and <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">who is that God that shall deliver you out of my hands</span>?

                    - G. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 16|16]] <span style="color: #27ae60; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>, answered and said to the king, O Nebuchadnezzar, we are not careful to answer thee in this matter.

                - F'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 17|17]] If it be so, <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace</span>, and he will deliver us out of thine hand, O king.

            - E'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 18|18]] But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up</span>.

        - D'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 19|19]] Then was <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Nebuchadnezzar</span> <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">full of fury</span>, and the form of his visage was changed against <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>:

            - H. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 19|19-23]] therefore he spake, and commanded that they should heat <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">the furnace</span> one seven times more than it was wont to be heated. And he commanded the most mighty men that were in his army to bind <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>, and to <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">cast them into the burning fiery furnace</span>. Then these men were <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">bound</span> in their coats, their hosen, and their hats, and their other garments, and were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace. Therefore because the king's commandment was urgent, and the furnace exceeding hot, the flame of the fire slew those men that took up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">fell down bound into the midst of the burning fiery furnace</span>.

                - I. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 24|24]] Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was astonied, and rose up in haste, and spake, and said unto his counsellers, Did not we cast <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">three men bound</span> into the midst of the fire?

                    - J. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 24|24b]] They answered and said unto the king, True, O king.

                - I'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 25|25]] He answered and said, Lo, I see <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">four men loose</span>, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">walking</span> in the midst of the fire, and they have no hurt; and the form of the fourth is like the Son of God.

            - H'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 26|26-27]] Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the mouth of <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">the burning fiery furnace</span>, and spake, and said, <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>, ye servants of the most high God, <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">come forth, and come hither</span>. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">came forth of the midst of the fire</span>. And the princes, governors, and captains, and the king's counsellers, being gathered together, saw these men, <span style="color: #2c3e50; font-weight: bold;">upon whose bodies the fire had no power, nor was an hair of their head singed, neither were their coats changed, nor the smell of fire had passed on them</span>.

        - K. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 28|28]] Then Nebuchadnezzar spake, and said, Blessed be <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">the God</span> of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>, who hath sent his angel, and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">delivered</span> his servants that trusted in him, and have changed the king's word, and yielded their bodies, that they might not serve nor worship any god, except their own God.

        - K'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 29|29]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Therefore I make a decree</span>, That every people, nation, and language, which speak any thing amiss against <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">the God of</span> <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego</span>, shall be cut in pieces, and their houses shall be made a dunghill: because <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">there is no other God that can deliver</span> after this sort.
