- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 6|6-7]] Tell the rebellious house of Israel that this is what the Lord GOD says: 'I have had enough of all your abominations, O house of Israel. In addition to all your other abominations, you brought in foreigners uncircumcised in both heart and flesh to occupy My sanctuary; you defiled My temple when you offered My food—<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the fat and the blood;</span> you broke My covenant.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 8|8]] And you have not kept charge of My holy things, but have appointed others to <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">keep charge of My sanctuary</span> for you.'

        - C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 9|9]] This is what the Lord GOD says: <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">No foreigner</span> uncircumcised in heart and flesh may enter My sanctuary—not even a foreigner who lives among the Israelites.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 10|10]] Surely the Levites who wandered away from Me when Israel went astray, and who wandered away from Me after their idols, will <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">bear the consequences of their iniquity.</span>

                - E. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 11|11]] Yet they shall be <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">ministers in My sanctuary,</span> having charge of the gates of the temple and ministering there. They shall slaughter the burnt offerings and other sacrifices for the people and stand before them to minister to them.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 12|12]] Because they ministered before their idols and became a stumbling block of iniquity to the house of Israel, therefore I swore with an uplifted hand concerning them that <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">they would bear the consequences of their iniquity,</span> declares the Lord GOD.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 13|13]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">They must not approach Me</span> to serve Me as priests or come near any of My holy things or the most holy things. They will bear the shame of the abominations they have committed.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 14|14]] Yet I will appoint them to <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">keep charge</span> of all the work for the temple and everything to be done in it.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 15|15-16]] But the Levitical priests, who are descended from Zadok and who kept charge of My sanctuary when the Israelites went astray from Me, are to approach Me to minister before Me. They will stand before Me to offer Me <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fat and blood,</span> declares the Lord GOD. They alone shall enter My sanctuary and draw near to My table to minister before Me. They will keep My charge.
