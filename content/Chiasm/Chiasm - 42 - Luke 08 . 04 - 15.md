- A. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 4|4]] And when much people were gathered together, and were come to him out of every city, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">he spake by a parable:</span>

    - B. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 5|5a]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">A sower went out to sow his seed:</span>

        - C. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 5|5b]] and as he sowed, some fell <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">by the way side;</span> and it was trodden down, and the fowls of the air devoured it.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 6|6]] And some fell <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">upon a rock;</span> and as soon as it was sprung up, it withered away, because it lacked moisture.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 7|7]] And some fell <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">among thorns;</span> and the thorns sprang up with it, and choked it.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 8|8]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 10|10]] And other fell on <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">good ground,</span> and sprang up, and bare fruit an hundredfold. And when he had said these things, he cried, He that hath ears to hear, let him hear. And his disciples asked him, saying, What might this parable be? And he said, Unto you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God: but to others in parables; that seeing they might not see, and hearing they might not understand.

    - A'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 11|11]] Now <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the parable is this:</span>

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 11|11b]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The seed is the word of God.</span>

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 12|12]] Those <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">by the way side</span> are they that hear; then cometh the devil, and taketh away the word out of their hearts, lest they should believe and be saved.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 13|13]] They <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">on the rock</span> are they, which, when they hear, receive the word with joy; and these have no root, which for a while believe, and in time of temptation fall away.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 14|14]] And that which fell <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">among thorns</span> are they, which, when they have heard, go forth, and are choked with cares and riches and pleasures of this life, and bring no fruit to perfection.

                    - F'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 15|15]] But that on the <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">good ground</span> are they, which in an honest and good heart, having heard the word, keep it, and bring forth fruit with patience.
