                    - F. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 17#Psalm 17 . 8|17:8]] <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings</span>,

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 17#Psalm 17 . 9|17:9]] From the wicked that oppress me, from my deadly <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">enemies</span>, who compass me about.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 17#Psalm 17 . 10|17:10]] They are inclosed in their own fat: with their mouth they <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">speak proudly</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 17#Psalm 17 . 11|17:11-12]] They have now compassed us about: they have set their eyes bowing down to the earth; Like as a lion that is greedy of his prey, and as it were a young lion lurking in secret places.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 17#Psalm 17 . 13|17:13-14]] Arise, O LORD, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">disappoint him</span>, cast him down: deliver my soul from the wicked, which is thy sword: From men which are thy hand, O LORD, from men of the world, which have their portion in this life, and whose belly thou fillest with thy hid treasure: they are full of children, and leave the rest of their substance to their babes.
