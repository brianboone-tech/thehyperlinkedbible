**Overall Chiastic Structure of Romans**

Note: This represents the book-level structure showing how Paul's epistle is organized chiastically across all 16 chapters. For detailed verse-by-verse chiasms within specific sections, see the individual chiasm files for Romans chapters 9-16.

- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 1|1:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 17|17]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Introduction and Theme: The Gospel of God</span>

    - Paul's apostleship and the gospel revealed

    - "The righteous shall live by faith" (1:17)

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 18|1:18]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 20|3:20]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Universal Sinfulness</span>

        - Gentiles under sin (1:18-32)

        - Jews under sin (2:1-3:20)

        - "All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God"

        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 21|3:21]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 5#Romans 5 . 21|5:21]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Justification by Faith</span>

            - Righteousness through faith apart from works

            - Abraham's example of faith

            - Peace with God through Christ

            - Adam and Christ contrasted

            - D. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 6#Romans 6 . 1|6:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 39|8:39]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Sanctification and Life in the Spirit</span>

                - Dead to sin, alive to God (6:1-23)

                - Freed from the law (7:1-25)

                - Life in the Spirit (8:1-39)

                - "No condemnation for those in Christ Jesus"

                - E. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 1|9:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 36|11:36]] <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">Israel's Rejection and Future Restoration</span>

                    - God's sovereignty and Israel's unbelief

                    - Gentiles grafted in

                    - Israel's future salvation

                    - "God's gifts and call are irrevocable"

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 1|12:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 13#Romans 13 . 14|13:14]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Practical Sanctification and Christian Living</span>

                - Present bodies as living sacrifices

                - Transformed minds

                - Love in action

                - Submission to authorities

                - "Put on the Lord Jesus Christ"

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 14#Romans 14 . 1|14:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 13|15:13]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Unity in Faith</span>

            - Accepting the weak and strong

            - Living for the Lord

            - Christ received both Jews and Gentiles

            - Harmony among believers

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 14|15:14]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 33|33]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Paul's Ministry to the Gentiles</span>

        - Apostle to the Gentiles

        - Gospel preached from Jerusalem to Illyricum

        - Travel plans to visit Rome and Spain

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 16#Romans 16 . 1|16:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 16#Romans 16 . 27|27]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Conclusion and Doxology: The Gospel Mystery Revealed</span>

    - Personal greetings

    - Warning against divisive people

    - Final doxology: "To God be the glory through Jesus Christ forever"

**Parallel Themes:**
- A/A': <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Gospel</span> introduction and conclusion

- B/B': <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Universal scope</span> (all sinners / Paul's universal ministry)

- C/C': <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Faith and unity</span> (justification / practical unity)

- D/D': <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Sanctification</span> (theological / practical)

- E: <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">Central section</span> - God's plan for Israel and the Gentiles
