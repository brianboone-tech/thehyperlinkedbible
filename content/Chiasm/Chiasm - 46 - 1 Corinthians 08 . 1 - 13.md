- A. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 3|3]] Now as touching things <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">offered unto idols</span>, we know that we all have <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">knowledge</span>. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Knowledge</span> puffeth up, but <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">charity</span> edifieth. And if any man think that he <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">knoweth</span> any thing, he <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">knoweth</span> nothing yet as he ought to know. But if any man <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">love</span> God, the same is known of him.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 4|4]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 6|6]] As concerning therefore the eating of those things that are offered in sacrifice unto <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">idols, we know that an idol is nothing</span> in the world, and that there is none other God but one. For though there be that are called gods, whether in heaven or in earth, (as there be gods many, and lords many,) But to us there is <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">but one God</span>, the Father, of whom are all things, and we in him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we by him.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 7|7]] Howbeit there is not in every man <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">that knowledge</span>: for some with conscience of <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the idol</span> unto this hour eat it as a thing offered unto an <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">idol</span>; and their conscience being weak is defiled.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 8|8]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 8#1 Corinthians 8 . 13|13]] But <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">meat</span> commendeth us not to God: for neither, if we eat, are we the better; neither, if we eat not, are we the worse. But take heed lest by any means this liberty of yours become a stumblingblock to them that are weak. For if any man see thee which hast <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">knowledge</span> sit at meat in the idol's temple, shall not the conscience of him which is weak be emboldened to eat those things which are offered to idols; And through <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">thy knowledge</span> shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the weak brother</span> perish, for whom Christ died? But when ye sin so against the brethren, and wound their <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">weak</span> conscience, ye sin against Christ. Wherefore, if <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">meat</span> make my brother to offend, I will eat no flesh while the world standeth, lest I make my brother to offend.
