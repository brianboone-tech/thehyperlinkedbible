[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 23|23]]-[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 24|24]] For the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">husband is the head</span> of the wife, even as <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Christ is the head</span> of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.             - D. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 26|26]] That he might <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">sanctify and cleanse</span> it with the washing of water by the word,

                - E. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 27|27a]] That he might present it to himself a glorious church,

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 27|27b]] not having spot, or wrinkle, or any such thing; but that it should be <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">holy and without blemish</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 28|28]] So ought men to <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">love their wives</span> as their own bodies. He that <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">loveth his wife loveth himself</span>.

    - B'. For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh. This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Christ and the church</span>.
