- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 2|2]] What shall we say then that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Abraham our father</span>, as pertaining to the flesh, hath found? For if Abraham were justified by works, he hath whereof to glory; but not before God.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 3|3]] For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">counted</span> unto him for <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 4|4]] Now to him that worketh is the reward not <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">reckoned</span> of grace, but of debt.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 5|5]] But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">his faith is counted for righteousness</span>.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 6|6]] Even as David also describeth <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the blessedness of the man</span>, unto whom God <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">imputeth</span> righteousness without works,

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 7|7]] Saying, <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Blessed</span> are they whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 8|8]] <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Blessed is the man</span> to whom the Lord will not <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">impute</span> sin.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 9|9]] Cometh this blessedness then upon the circumcision only, or upon the uncircumcision also? for we say that <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">faith was reckoned to Abraham for righteousness</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 10|10]] How was it then <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">reckoned</span>? when he was in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 11|11]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 12|12]] And he received the sign of circumcision, a seal of the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span> of the faith which he had yet being uncircumcised: that he might be the father of all them that believe, though they be not circumcised; that <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span> might be <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">imputed</span> unto them also: And the father of circumcision to them who are not of the circumcision only, but who also walk in the steps of that faith of our father Abraham, which he had being yet uncircumcised.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 4#Romans 4 . 13|13]] For the promise, that he should be the heir of the world, was not to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Abraham</span>, or to his seed, through the law, but through the righteousness of faith.
