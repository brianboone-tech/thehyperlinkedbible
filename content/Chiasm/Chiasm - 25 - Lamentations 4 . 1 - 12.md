- A. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 1|1-2]] How the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">gold</span> has become tarnished, the pure gold has become dull! The gems of the temple lie scattered on every street corner. How the precious sons of Zion, once worth their weight in pure <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">gold</span>, are now esteemed as jars of clay, the work of a potter's hands!

    - B. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 3|3]] Even jackals offer their breasts to nurse their young, but the daughter of my people has become <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">cruel</span>, like an ostrich in the wilderness.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 4|4-6]] The nursing infant's tongue clings in thirst to the roof of his mouth. Little children beg for <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">bread</span>, but no one gives them any. Those who once ate delicacies are destitute in the streets; those brought up in crimson huddle in ash heaps. The punishment of the daughter of my people is greater than that of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Sodom</span>, which was overthrown in an instant without a hand turned to help her.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 7|7]] Her dignitaries were brighter than <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">snow</span>, whiter than milk; their bodies were more <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">ruddy</span> than rubies, their appearance like sapphires.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 8|8]] But now their appearance is <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">blacker</span> than soot; they are not recognized in the streets. Their skin has <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">shriveled</span> on their bones; it has become as dry as a stick.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 9|9]] Those slain by the sword are better off than those who die of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">hunger</span>, who waste away, pierced with pain because the fields lack produce.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 10|10]] The hands of compassionate women have cooked their own <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">children</span>, who became their food in the destruction of the daughter of my people.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 11|11-12]] The LORD has exhausted His wrath; He has poured out His fierce anger; He has kindled a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fire</span> in Zion, and it has consumed her foundations. The kings of the earth did not believe, nor any people of the world, that an enemy or a foe could enter the gates of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Jerusalem</span>.
