- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 28#Psalm 28 . 1|1-2]] Unto thee will I cry, O LORD my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Hear the voice</span> of my supplications, when I cry unto thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy oracle.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 28#Psalm 28 . 3|3-5]] Draw me not away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, which <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">speak peace</span> to their neighbours, but <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">mischief</span> is in their hearts. Give them according to their deeds, and according to the wickedness of their endeavours: give them after the work of their hands; render to them their desert. Because they regard not <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the works of the LORD</span>, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them, and not build them up.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 28#Psalm 28 . 6|6-9]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Blessed</span> be the LORD, because he hath <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heard the voice</span> of my supplications. The LORD is my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">strength</span> and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him. The LORD is their <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">strength</span>, and he is the saving strength of his anointed. Save thy people, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bless</span> thine inheritance: feed them also, and lift them up for ever.
