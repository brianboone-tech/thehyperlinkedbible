---

- A. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 1|1-2]] And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, Make thee two <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trumpets of silver</span>; of a whole piece shalt thou make them: that thou mayest use them for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying of the camps.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 3|3-4]] And when they shall blow with them, all the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">assembly</span> shall assemble themselves to thee at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation. And if they blow but with one trumpet, then the princes, which are heads of the thousands of Israel, shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">gather</span> themselves unto thee.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 5|5-7]] When ye blow an <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">alarm</span>, then the camps that lie on the east parts shall go forward. When ye blow an <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">alarm</span> the second time, then the camps that lie on the south side shall take their journey: they shall blow an <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">alarm</span> for their journeys. But when the congregation is to be gathered together, ye shall blow, but ye shall not sound an <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">alarm</span>.

    - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 9|9]] And if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">alarm</span> with the trumpets; and ye shall be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">remembered</span> before the LORD your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 10|10]] Also in the day of your gladness, and in your solemn days, and in the beginnings of your months, ye shall blow with the trumpets over your burnt offerings, and over the sacrifices of your peace offerings; that they may be to you for a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">memorial</span> before your God: I am the LORD your God.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 8|8]] And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow with the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trumpets</span>; and they shall be to you for an <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">ordinance for ever</span> throughout your generations.
