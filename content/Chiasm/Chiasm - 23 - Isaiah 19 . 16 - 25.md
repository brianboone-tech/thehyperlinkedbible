- A. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Egypt in fear and awe of YHWH</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 16|16]] <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> shall Egypt be like unto women: and it shall be afraid and fear because of the shaking of the hand of the LORD of hosts, which he shaketh over it. 17 And the land of Judah shall be a terror unto Egypt, every one that maketh mention thereof shall be afraid in himself, because of the counsel of the LORD of hosts, which he hath determined against it.

    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Egypt cities swear to YHWH</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 18|18]] <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> there will be five cities in the land of Egypt that speak the language of Canaan and swear allegiance to the LORD of hosts. One of these will be called the City of Destruction.

        - C. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">Egypt healed by YHWH</span>

            - a. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 19|19]] <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> shall there be an altar to the LORD in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a pillar at the border thereof to the LORD. 20 And it shall be for a sign and for a witness unto the LORD of hosts in the land of Egypt: for they shall cry unto the LORD because of the oppressors, and he shall send them a saviour, and a great one, and he shall deliver them.

                - b. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 21|21]] And the LORD shall be known to Egypt, and the Egyptians shall know the LORD in that day, and shall do sacrifice and oblation; yea, they shall vow a vow unto the LORD, and perform it.

            - a'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 22|22]] And the LORD shall smite Egypt: he shall smite and heal it: and they shall return even to the LORD, and he shall be intreated of them, and shall heal them.

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Egypt has highway to Assyria</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 23|23]] <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> shall there be a highway out of Egypt to Assyria, and the Assyrian shall come into Egypt, and the Egyptian into Assyria, and the Egyptians shall serve with the Assyrians.

- A'. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Egypt become the people of YHWH</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 24|24]] <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> shall Israel be the third with Egypt and with Assyria, even a blessing in the midst of the land: 25 Whom the LORD of hosts shall bless, saying, Blessed be Egypt my people, and Assyria the work of my hands, and Israel mine inheritance.
