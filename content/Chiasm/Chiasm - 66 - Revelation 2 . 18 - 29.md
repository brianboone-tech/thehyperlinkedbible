- A. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 18|18]] And unto the angel of the church in <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Thyatira</span> write; These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 19|19]] I know thy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">works</span>, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">works</span>; and the last to be more than the first.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 20|20-23]] Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Jezebel</span>, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols. And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not. Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds. And I will kill her children with death; and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts: and I will give unto every one of you according to your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">works</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 24|24-25]] But unto you I say, and unto the rest in Thyatira, as many as have not this doctrine, and which have not known the depths of Satan, as they speak; I will put upon you none other burden. But that which ye have already <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">hold fast</span> till I come.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 26|26-27]] And he that <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">overcometh</span>, and keepeth my <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">works</span> unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations: And he shall rule them with a rod of iron; as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers: even as I received of my Father.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 28|28-29]] And I will give him the morning star. He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.
