- A. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The secure city</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 1|1]] In that day shall this song be sung in the land of Judah; We have a strong city; salvation will God appoint for walls and bulwarks. 2 Open ye the gates, that the righteous nation which keepeth the truth may enter in. 3 Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace (<span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">shalom, shalom</span>), whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee. 4 Trust ye in the LORD for ever: for in the LORD Jehovah (YAH YAHWEH) is everlasting strength:

    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Down to the dust</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 5|5]] For he bringeth down them that dwell on high; the lofty city, he layeth it low; he layeth it low, even to the ground; he bringeth it even to the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">dust</span>. 6 The foot shall tread it down, even the feet of the poor, and the steps of the needy.

        - C. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The righteous waiting for the Kingdom of God</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 7|7]] The way of the just is uprightness: thou, most upright, dost weigh the path of the just. 8 Yea, in the way of thy judgments, O LORD, have we waited for thee; the desire of our soul is to thy name, and to the remembrance of thee. 9 With my soul have I desired thee in the night; yea, with my spirit within me will I seek thee early: for when thy judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world will learn righteousness.

            - D. <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">The wicked blind to God but they will eventually have to acknowledge Him</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 10|10]] Let favour be shewed to the wicked, yet will he not learn righteousness: in the land of uprightness will he deal unjustly, and will not behold the majesty of the LORD. 11 LORD, when thy hand is lifted up, they will not see: but they shall see, and be ashamed for their envy at the people; yea, the fire of thine enemies shall devour them.

        - C'. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The Kingdom comes</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 12|12]] LORD, thou wilt ordain peace for us: for thou also hast wrought all our works in us. 13 O LORD our God, other lords beside thee have had dominion over us: but by thee only will we make mention of thy name. 14 They are dead (<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mut</span>), they shall not live; they are deceased (<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">rapha</span>), they shall not rise: therefore hast thou visited and destroyed them, and made all their memory to perish. 15 Thou hast increased the nation, O LORD, thou hast increased the nation: thou art glorified: thou hadst removed it far unto all the ends of the earth.

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Out of the dust - deliverance and the resurrection</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 16|16]] LORD, in trouble have they visited thee, they poured out a prayer when thy chastening was upon them. 17 Like as a woman with child, that draweth near the time of her delivery, is in pain, and crieth out in her pangs; so have we been in thy sight, O LORD. 18 We have been with child, we have been in pain, we have as it were brought forth wind; we have not wrought any deliverance in the earth; neither have the inhabitants of the world fallen. 19 Thy dead men (<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mut</span>) shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">dust</span>: for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast out the dead (<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Rapha</span>).

- A'. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The safe house</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 20|20]] Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers, and shut thy doors about thee: hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast. 21 For, behold, the LORD cometh out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity: the earth also shall disclose her blood, and shall no more cover her slain.
