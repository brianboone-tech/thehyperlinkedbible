- A. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Moab's ruin foretold</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 15#Isaiah 15 . 1|15:1]] The burden of <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">Moab</span>. Because in the night Ar of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence; because in the night Kir of Moab is laid waste, and brought to silence;

    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Moab weeps and howls</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 15#Isaiah 15 . 2|2]] He is gone up to Bajith, and to Dibon, the high places, to <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weep</span>: Moab shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">howl</span> over Nebo, and over Medeba: on all their heads shall be baldness, and every beard cut off. 3 In their streets they shall gird themselves with sackcloth: on the tops of their houses, and in their streets, every one shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">howl</span>, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weeping</span> abundantly. 4 And Heshbon shall cry, and Elealeh: their voice shall be heard even unto Jahaz: therefore the armed soldiers of Moab shall cry out; his life shall be grievous unto him.

        - C. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The LORD grieves for Moab – food stocks gone</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 15#Isaiah 15 . 5|5]] My heart shall cry out for Moab; his fugitives shall flee unto Zoar, an heifer of three years old: for by the mounting up of Luhith with <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weeping</span> shall they go it up; for in the way of Horonaim they shall raise up a cry of destruction. 6 For the waters of Nimrim shall be desolate: for the hay is withered away, the grass faileth, there is no green thing. 7 Therefore the abundance they have gotten, and that which they have laid up, shall they carry away to the brook of the willows. 8 For the cry is gone round about the borders of Moab; the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">howling</span> thereof unto Eglaim, and the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">howling</span> thereof unto Beerelim. 9 For the waters of Dimon shall be full of blood: for I will bring more upon Dimon, lions upon him that escapeth of Moab, and upon the remnant of the land.

                    - b. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 3|3]] Take counsel, execute judgment; make thy shadow as the night in the midst of the noonday; <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">hide the outcasts</span>; bewray not him that wandereth.

                    - b'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 4|4]] <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Let mine outcasts dwell with thee</span>, Moab; be thou a covert to them from the face of the spoiler:

                - a'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 4|4b-5]] for the extortioner is at an end, the spoiler ceaseth, the oppressors are consumed out of the land. 5 And in mercy shall <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">the throne</span> be established: and he shall sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">David</span>, judging, and seeking judgment, and hasting righteousness.

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Moab's weeps and howls</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 6|6]] We have heard of the pride of Moab; he is very proud: even of his haughtiness, and his pride, and his wrath: but his lies shall not be so. 7 Therefore shall Moab <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">howl</span> for Moab, every one shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">howl</span>: for the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">foundations</span> of Kirhareseth shall ye mourn; surely they are stricken. 8 For the fields of Heshbon languish, and the vine of Sibmah: the lords of the heathen have broken down the principal plants thereof, they are come even unto Jazer, they wandered through the wilderness: her branches are stretched out, they are gone over the sea.

        - C'. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The LORD's grief over Moab's plight – no harvest</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 9|9]] Therefore I will bewail with the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">weeping</span> of Jazer the vine of Sibmah: I will water thee with my tears, O Heshbon, and Elealeh: for the shouting for thy summer fruits and for thy harvest is fallen. 10 And gladness is taken away, and joy out of the plentiful field; and in the vineyards there shall be no singing, neither shall there be shouting: the treaders shall tread out no wine in their presses; I have made their vintage shouting to cease. 11 Wherefore my bowels shall sound like an harp for Moab, and mine inward parts for Kirharesh. 12 And it shall come to pass, when it is seen that Moab is weary on the high place, that he shall come to his sanctuary to pray; but he shall not prevail.

- A'. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Moab's Ruin foretold</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 13|13]] This is the word that the LORD hath spoken concerning <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">Moab</span> since that time. 14 But now the LORD hath spoken, saying, Within three years, as the years of an hireling, and the glory of Moab shall be contemned, with all that great multitude; and the remnant shall be very small and feeble.
