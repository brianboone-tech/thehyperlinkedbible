- A. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 1|1]] In the six and thirtieth year of the reign of Asa <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah</span>, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">built Ramah</span>, to the intent that he might let none go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 2|2]] Then Asa brought out <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">silver and gold</span> out of the treasures of the house of the LORD and of the king's house, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sent to Benhadad king of Syria</span>, that dwelt at Damascus, saying, [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 3|3]] There is a league between me and thee, as there was between my father and thy father: behold, I have sent thee silver and gold; go, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">break thy league with Baasha king of Israel</span>, that he may depart from me.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 4|4]] And <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Benhadad hearkened unto king Asa</span>, and sent the captains of his armies against the cities of Israel; and they smote Ijon, and Dan, and Abelmaim, and all the store cities of Naphtali. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 5|5]] And it came to pass, when <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Baasha heard it</span>, that he left off building of Ramah, and let his work cease.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 6|6]] Then Asa the king took all Judah; and they <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">carried away the stones of Ramah, and the timber thereof, wherewith Baasha was building</span>; and he built therewith Geba and Mizpah.
