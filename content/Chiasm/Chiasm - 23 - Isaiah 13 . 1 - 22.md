- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 1|1]] The burden of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Babylon</span>, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see.

- A. <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">The command to attack Babylon</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 2|2]] Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles. 3 I have commanded my sanctified ones, I have also called my mighty ones for mine anger, even them that rejoice in my highness.

    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The armies mustered</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 4|4]] The noise of a multitude in the mountains, like as of a great people; a tumultuous noise of the kingdoms of nations gathered together: the LORD of hosts mustereth the host of the battle. 5 They come from a far country, from the end of heaven, even the LORD, and the weapons of his indignation, to destroy the whole land.

        - C. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">Fear at the approaching armies</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 6|6]] Howl ye; for <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the day of the LORD</span> is at hand; it shall come as a destruction from the Almighty. 7 Therefore shall all hands be faint, and every man's heart shall melt: 8 And they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces shall be as flames.

            - D. <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">The day of the LORD will bring an end to Babylon</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 9|9]] Behold, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the day of the LORD</span> cometh, cruel both with <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">wrath</span> and <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">fierce anger</span>, to lay <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">the land</span> desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it. 10 For the stars of <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">heaven</span> and the constellations thereof shall not give their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not cause her light to shine.

                - a. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 11|11]] And I will punish the world for their evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible. 12 I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 13|13]] Therefore I will shake <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the heavens</span>, and <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">the earth</span> shall remove out of her place, in the <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">wrath</span> of the LORD of hosts, and in the day of his <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">fierce anger</span>.

        - C'. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">Flight from Babylon</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 14|14]] And it shall be as the chased roe, and as a sheep that no man taketh up: they shall every man turn to his own people, and flee every one into his own land. 15 Every one that is found shall be thrust through; and every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword. 16 Their children also shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses shall be spoiled, and their wives ravished.

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The armies are led by the Medes</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 17|17]] Behold, I will stir up <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the Medes</span> against them, which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it. 18 Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces; and they shall have no pity on the fruit of the womb; their eye shall not spare children.

- A'. <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">The end of Babylon</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 19|19]] And <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Babylon</span>, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees' excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah. 20 It shall never be inhabited, neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation: neither shall the Arabian pitch tent there; neither shall the shepherds make their fold there. 21 But wild beasts of the desert shall lie there; and their houses shall be full of doleful creatures; and owls shall dwell there, and satyrs shall dance there. 22 And the wild beasts of the islands shall cry in their desolate houses, and dragons in their pleasant palaces: and her time is near to come, and her days shall not be prolonged.
