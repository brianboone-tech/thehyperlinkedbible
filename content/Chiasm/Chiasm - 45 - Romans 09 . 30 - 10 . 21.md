- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 30|30]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 33|33]] What shall we say then? That the Gentiles, which followed not after righteousness, have attained to righteousness, even the righteousness which is of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">faith</span>. But Israel, which followed after the law of righteousness, hath not attained to the law of righteousness. Wherefore? Because they sought it not by <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">faith</span>, but as it were by the works of the law. For they stumbled at that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">stumblingstone</span>; As it is written, Behold, I lay in Sion a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">stumblingstone</span> and rock of offence: and whosoever <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">believeth</span> on him shall not be ashamed.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 1|10:1]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 5|5]] Brethren, my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is, that they might be saved. For I bear them record that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge. For they being ignorant of <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">God's righteousness</span>, and going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteousness of God</span>. For Christ is the end of the law for <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span> to every one that believeth. For Moses describeth the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span> which is of the law, That the man which doeth those things shall live by them.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 8|8]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 13|13]] But what saith it? The word is nigh thee, even in thy mouth, and in thy heart: that is, the word of faith, which we preach; That if thou shalt <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">confess</span> with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">believe</span> in thine <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">heart</span> that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">saved</span>. For with the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">heart</span> man <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">believeth</span> unto righteousness; and with the mouth <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">confession</span> is made unto salvation. For the scripture saith, Whosoever <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">believeth</span> on him shall not be ashamed. For there is no difference between the Jew and the Greek: for the same Lord over all is rich unto all that call upon him. For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">saved</span>.

But Esaias is very bold, and saith, I was found of them that sought me not; I was made manifest unto them that asked not after me.
