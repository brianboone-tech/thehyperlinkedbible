- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 1|1-2]] And the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">this is what the Lord GOD says</span>:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 2|2]] Your heart is proud, and you have said, '<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">I am a god</span>; I sit in the seat of gods in the heart of the sea.' Yet you are <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">a man and not a god</span>, though you have regarded your heart as that of a god.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 3|3-5]] Behold, you are <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wiser than Daniel</span>; no secret is hidden from you! By your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span> and understanding you have gained your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wealth</span> and amassed gold and silver for your treasuries. By your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">great skill</span> in trading you have increased your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">riches</span>, and thine heart is lifted up because of thy riches.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 6|6]] Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">thou hast set thine heart as the heart of God</span>,

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 7|7-8]] behold, I will bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless of nations. They will draw their swords against the beauty of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">your wisdom</span> and will defile your splendor. They will bring you down to the Pit, and you will die a violent death in the heart of the seas.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 9|9]] Will you still say, '<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">I am a god</span>,' in the presence of those who slay you? You will be only <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">a man, not a god</span>, in the hands of those who wound you.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 10|10]] You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners. For I have spoken, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">declares the Lord GOD</span>."
