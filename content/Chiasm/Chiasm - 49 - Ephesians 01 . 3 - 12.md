- A. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 3|3]] Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">in Christ</span>:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 4|4]]-[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 5|5a]] According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love: Having <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">predestinated us</span> unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself,

        - C. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 5|5b]] according to the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">good pleasure of his will</span>,

            - D. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 6|6]] To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">us</span> accepted in the beloved.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 7|7]] In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace;

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 8|8]] Wherein he hath abounded toward <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">us</span> in all wisdom and prudence;

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 9|9]]-[[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 10|10]] Having made known unto us the mystery of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">his will</span>, according to his <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">good pleasure</span> which he hath purposed in himself: That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him:

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 11|11]] In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">predestinated</span> according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will:

- A'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 12|12]] That we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">in Christ</span>.
