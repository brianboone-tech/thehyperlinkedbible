[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 3|3-5]] And will make an offering by fire unto the LORD, a burnt offering, or a sacrifice in performing a vow, or in a freewill offering, or in your solemn feasts, to make a sweet savour unto the LORD, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">of the herd, or of the flock</span>: Then shall he that offereth his offering unto the LORD bring a meat offering of a tenth deal of flour mingled with the fourth part of an hin of oil.     - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 6|6-7]] Or for a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">ram</span>, thou shalt prepare for a meat offering two tenth deals of flour mingled with the third part of an hin of oil. And for a drink offering thou shalt offer the third part of an hin of wine, for a sweet savour unto the LORD.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 8|8-10]] And when thou preparest a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">bullock</span> for a burnt offering, or for a sacrifice in performing a vow, or peace offerings unto the LORD: Then shall he bring with a bullock a meat offering of three tenth deals of flour mingled with half an hin of oil. And thou shalt bring for a drink offering half an hin of wine, for an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 11|11]] Thus shall it be done for one <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">bullock</span>, or for one ram, or for a lamb, or a kid.
