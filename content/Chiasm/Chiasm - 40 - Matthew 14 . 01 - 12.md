[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 3|3]] For Herod <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">had laid hold on John, and bound him, and put him in prison</span> for Herodias' sake, his brother Philip's wife. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 5|5]] And when he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 6|6]] But when Herod's birthday was kept, the daughter of Herodias danced before them, and pleased Herod. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 7|7]] Whereupon <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">he promised with an oath</span> to give her whatsoever she would ask.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 8|8]] And she, being before instructed of her mother, said, Give me here John Baptist's head in a charger.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 9|9]] And the king was sorry: nevertheless <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">for the oath's sake</span>, and them which sat with him at meat, he commanded it to be given her.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 14#Matthew 14 . 10|10]] And <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">he sent, and beheaded John in the prison</span>.
