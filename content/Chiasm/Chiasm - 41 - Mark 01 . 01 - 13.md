- A. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 3|3]] This is the beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.     - B. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 4|4]] John the Baptist appeared in the wilderness, preaching a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">baptism</span> of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 5|5]] People went out to him from all of Jerusalem and the countryside of Judea. Confessing their sins, they were <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">baptized by him</span> in the Jordan River.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 6|6]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 7|7]] John was clothed in camel's hair, with a leather belt around his waist. His food was locusts and wild honey. And he began to proclaim: "After me will come <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">One more powerful than I</span>, the straps of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and untie.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 8|8]] I <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">baptize</span> you with water, but He will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">baptize</span> you with the Holy Spirit."

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 9|9]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 11|11]] In those days <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Jesus came</span> from Nazareth in Galilee and was <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">baptized</span> by John in the Jordan. And a voice came from heaven: "You are My beloved Son; in You I am well pleased."
