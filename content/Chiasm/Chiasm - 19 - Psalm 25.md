- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 25#Psalm 25 . 1|1-7]] Unto thee, O LORD, do I lift up my soul. O my God, I <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trust</span> in thee: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">let me not be ashamed</span>, let not <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mine enemies</span> triumph over me. Yea, let none that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wait on thee</span> be ashamed: let them be ashamed which transgress without cause. Shew me thy ways, O LORD; teach me thy paths. Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day. Remember, O LORD, thy tender <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercies</span> and thy lovingkindnesses; for they have been ever of old. Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: according to thy <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span> remember thou me for thy goodness' sake, O LORD.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 25#Psalm 25 . 8|8-10]] Good and upright is the LORD: therefore will he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">teach sinners in the way</span>. The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">teach his way</span>. All the paths of the LORD are mercy and truth unto such as keep his <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">covenant</span> and his testimonies.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 25#Psalm 25 . 11|11]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">For thy name's sake</span>, O LORD, pardon mine iniquity; for it is great.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 25#Psalm 25 . 12|12-15]] What man is he that feareth the LORD? him shall he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">teach in the way</span> that he shall choose. His soul shall dwell at ease; and his seed shall inherit the earth. The secret of the LORD is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">covenant</span>. Mine eyes are ever toward the LORD; for he shall pluck my feet out of the net.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 25#Psalm 25 . 16|16-22]] Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted. The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses. Look upon mine affliction and my pain; and forgive all my sins. Consider <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mine enemies</span>; for they are many; and they hate me with cruel hatred. O keep my soul, and deliver me: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">let me not be ashamed</span>; for I put my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trust</span> in thee. Let integrity and uprightness preserve me; for I <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wait on thee</span>. Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles.
