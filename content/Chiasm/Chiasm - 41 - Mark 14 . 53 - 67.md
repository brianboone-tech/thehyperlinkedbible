But <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">neither so did their witness agree together</span>.

        - C'.     - B'. And some began to spit on him, and to cover his face, and to buffet him, and to say unto him, Prophesy: and the servants did strike him with the palms of their hands.
