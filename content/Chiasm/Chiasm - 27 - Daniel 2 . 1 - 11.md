- A. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 1|1-2]] In the second year of his reign, Nebuchadnezzar had dreams that troubled his spirit, and sleep escaped him. So the king gave orders to summon the magicians, enchanters, sorcerers, and astrologers <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">to explain his dreams</span>. When they came and stood before the king,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 3|3]] he said to them, "I have had a dream, and my spirit is anxious <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">to understand it</span>."

        - C. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 4|4]] Then the astrologers answered the king in Aramaic, "O king, may you live forever! <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Tell your servants the dream, and we will give the interpretation</span>."

            - D. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 5|5]] The king replied to the astrologers, "<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">My word is final</span>:

                - E. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 5|5b]] If you do not tell me <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the dream and its interpretation</span>, you will be cut into pieces and your houses will be reduced to rubble.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 6|6]] But if you tell me <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the dream and its interpretation</span>, you will receive from me gifts and rewards and great honor.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 6|6b]] So tell me <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">the dream and its interpretation</span>."

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 7|7]] They answered a second time, "<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Let the king tell the dream to his servants, and we will give the interpretation</span>."

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 8|8-9]] The king replied, "I know for sure that you are stalling for time, because you see that my word is final. If you do not tell me the dream, there is only one decree for you. You have conspired to speak before me false and fraudulent words, hoping the situation will change. Therefore <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">tell me the dream</span>, and I will know that you can <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">give me its interpretation</span>."

- A'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 10|10-11]] The astrologers answered the king, "No one on earth can do what the king requests! No king, however great and powerful, has ever asked anything like this of any magician, enchanter, or astrologer. What <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the king requests</span> is so difficult that no one can tell it to him except the gods, whose dwelling is not with mortals."
