## 1 Timothy 6:11-12

- A. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 11|11]] But thou, O <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">man of God, flee</span> these things;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 11|11b]] and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">follow after</span> righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 12|12]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Fight the good fight</span> of faith,

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 12|12b]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">lay hold on</span> eternal life,

- A'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 12|12c]] whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good confession <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">before many witnesses</span>.

## 1 Timothy 6:13-16

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 6#1 Timothy 6 . 16|16]] Who only hath <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">immortality</span>, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto;
