**Ammon:**

- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 1|1-3]] The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying, Son of man, set thy face against the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Ammonites,</span> and prophesy against them; And say unto the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Ammonites,</span> Hear the word of the Lord GOD; Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thou saidst, Aha, against my sanctuary, when it was profaned; and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">against the land of Israel</span>, when it was desolate; and against the house of Judah, when they went into captivity;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 4|4-5]] Behold, therefore I will deliver thee to the men of the east for a possession, and they shall set their palaces in thee, and make their dwellings in thee: they shall eat thy fruit, and they shall drink thy milk. And I will make Rabbah a stable for camels, and the Ammonites a couchingplace for flocks: and ye shall know that I am the LORD.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 6|6-7]] For thus saith the Lord GOD; Because thou hast clapped thine hands, and stamped with the feet, and rejoiced in heart with all thy despite <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">against the land of Israel;</span> Behold, therefore I will stretch out mine hand upon thee, and will deliver thee for a spoil to the heathen; and I will cut thee off from the people, and I will cause thee to perish out of the countries: I will destroy thee; and thou shalt know that I am the LORD.

**Moab:**

- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 8|8]] Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Moab</span> and Seir do say, Behold, the house of Judah is like unto all the heathen;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 9|9-10]] Therefore, behold, I will open the side of Moab from the cities, from his cities which are on his frontiers, the glory of the country, Bethjeshimoth, Baalmeon, and Kiriathaim, Unto the men of the east with the Ammonites, and will give them in possession, that the Ammonites may not be remembered among the nations.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 11|11]] And I will execute judgments upon <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Moab</span>; and they shall know that I am the LORD.

**Edom:**

- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 12|12]] Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Edom</span> hath dealt against the house of Judah by taking <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vengeance</span>, and hath greatly offended, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">revenged</span> himself upon them;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 13|13]] Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; I will also stretch out mine hand upon Edom, and will cut off man and beast from it; and I will make it desolate from Teman; and they of Dedan shall fall by the sword.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 14|14]] And I will lay my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vengeance</span> upon <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Edom</span> by the hand of my people Israel: and they shall do in Edom according to mine anger and according to my fury; and they shall know my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vengeance</span>, saith the Lord GOD.

**Philistines:**

- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 15|15]] Thus saith the Lord GOD; Because the Philistines have dealt by <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">revenge</span>, and have taken <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vengeance</span> with a despiteful heart, to destroy it for the old hatred;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 16|16]] Therefore thus saith the Lord GOD; Behold, I will stretch out mine hand upon the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethims, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 25#Ezekiel 25 . 17|17]] And I will execute great <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vengeance</span> upon them with furious rebukes; and they shall know that I am the LORD, when I shall lay my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vengeance</span> upon them.
