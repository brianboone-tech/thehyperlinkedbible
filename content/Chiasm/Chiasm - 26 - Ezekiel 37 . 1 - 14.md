- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 1|1-3]] The hand of the LORD was upon me, and He brought me out by His Spirit and set me down in the middle of the valley, and it was full of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bones.</span> He led me all around among them, and I saw a great many <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bones</span> on the floor of the valley, and indeed, they were very dry. Then He asked me, "Son of man, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">can these bones come to life?</span>" "O Lord GOD," I replied, "only You know."

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 4|4-8]] And He said to me, "Prophesy concerning these bones and tell them, 'Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD! This is what the Lord GOD says to these bones: I will cause <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">breath</span> to enter you, and you will come to life. I will attach tendons to you and make flesh grow upon you and cover you with skin. I will put <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">breath</span> within you so that you will come to life. Then you will know that I am the LORD.'" So I prophesied as I had been commanded. And as I prophesied, there was suddenly a noise, a rattling, and the bones came together, bone to bone. As I looked on, tendons appeared on them, flesh grew, and skin covered them; but there was <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">no breath in them.</span>

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 9|9-10]] Then He said to me, "Prophesy to the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">breath;</span> prophesy, son of man, and tell the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">breath</span> that this is what the Lord GOD says: Come from the four winds, O <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">breath,</span> and breathe into these slain, so that they may live!" So I prophesied as He had commanded me, and the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">breath</span> entered them, and they came to life and stood on their feet—<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">a vast army.</span>

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 11|11-14]] Then He said to me, "Son of man, these <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bones</span> are the whole house of Israel. Look, they are saying, 'Our <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bones</span> are dried up, and our hope has perished; we are cut off.' Therefore prophesy and tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says: 'O My people, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I will open your graves and bring you up from them,</span> and I will bring you back to the land of Israel. Then you, My people, will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">know that I am the LORD,</span> when I open your graves and bring you up from them. I will put My Spirit in you and you will live, and I will settle you in your own land. Then you will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">know that I, the LORD, have spoken,</span> and I will do it, declares the LORD.'"
