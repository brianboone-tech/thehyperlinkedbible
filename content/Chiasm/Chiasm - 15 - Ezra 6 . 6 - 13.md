- A. [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 6|6]] Therefore Darius decreed: To <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Tattenai governor of the region west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and your associates</span> and officials in the region: You must stay away from that place!

    - B. [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 7|7]] Leave this work on <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the house of God</span> alone. Let the governor and elders of the Jews rebuild <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">this house of God</span> on its original site.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 8|8-10]] I hereby decree what you must do for these elders of the Jews who are rebuilding this house of God: The cost is to be paid in full to these men from the royal treasury out of the taxes of the provinces west of the Euphrates, so that the work will not be hindered. Whatever is needed—young <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">bulls, rams, and lambs</span> for burnt offerings to the God of heaven, as well as <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wheat, salt, wine, and oil</span>, as requested by the priests in Jerusalem—must be given to them daily without fail. Then they will be able to offer <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">sacrifices of a sweet aroma</span> to the God of heaven and to <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">pray for the lives of the king and his sons</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 11|11]] I also decree that if any man interferes with this directive, a beam is to be torn from his house and raised up, and he is to be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">impaled on it</span>. And his own house shall be made a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">pile of rubble</span> for this offense.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 12|12]] May God, who has caused His Name to dwell there, overthrow any king or people who lifts a hand to alter this decree or to destroy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">this house of God</span> in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have issued the decree. Let it be carried out with diligence.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 13|13]] In response, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Tattenai the governor of the region west of the Euphrates, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates</span> diligently carried out what King Darius had decreed.
