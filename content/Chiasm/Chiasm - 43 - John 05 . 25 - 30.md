- A1. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 25|25a]] Verily, verily, I say unto you, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The hour is coming, and now is</span>,

    - B1. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 25|25b]] when <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God</span>:

        - C1. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 25|25c]] and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">they that hear shall live</span>.

            - D1. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 26|26]] For as <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the Father hath life in himself; so hath he given to the Son to have life in himself</span>;

                - E1. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 27|27]] And hath given him <span style="color: #943126; font-weight: bold;">authority to execute judgment</span> also, because he is the Son of man.

- A2. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 28|28a]] Marvel not at this: for <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the hour is coming</span>,

    - B2. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 28|28b]] in the which <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">all that are in the graves shall hear his voice</span>,

        - C2. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 29|29]] And shall <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">come forth</span>; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation.

            - D2. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 30|30a]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I can of mine own self do nothing</span>: as I hear, I judge:

                - E2. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 30|30b]] and my <span style="color: #943126; font-weight: bold;">judgment is just</span>; because I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me.
