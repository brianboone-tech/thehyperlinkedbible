- A. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 1|1-3]] All the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sixteen years old</span>, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah. Uzziah was the one who rebuilt Eloth and restored it to Judah after King Amaziah rested with his fathers. Uzziah was sixteen years old when he became king, and he <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">reigned in Jerusalem fifty-two years</span>. His mother's name was Jecoliah; she was from Jerusalem.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 4|4-5]] And he did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, just as his father Amaziah had done. He <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sought God</span> throughout the days of Zechariah, who instructed him in the fear of God. And as long as he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sought the LORD, God gave him success</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 6|6-15a]] Uzziah went out to wage war against the Philistines, and he tore down the walls of Gath, Jabneh, and Ashdod. Then he built cities near Ashdod and among the Philistines. God helped him against the Philistines, against the Arabs living in Gur-baal, and against the Meunites. The Ammonites brought tribute to Uzziah, and his fame spread as far as the border of Egypt, for he had become exceedingly powerful. Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate, the Valley Gate, and the angle in the wall, and he fortified them. Since he had much livestock in the foothills and in the plain, he built towers in the desert and dug many cisterns. And since he was a lover of the soil, he had farmers and vinedressers in the hill country and in the fertile fields. Uzziah had an army ready for battle that went out to war by assigned divisions, as recorded by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer under the direction of Hananiah, one of the royal officers. The total number of family leaders of the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mighty men of valor</span> was 2,600. Under their authority was an army of 307,500 trained for war, a powerful force to support the king against his enemies. Uzziah supplied the entire army with shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and slingstones. And in Jerusalem he made skillfully designed devices to shoot arrows and catapult large stones from the towers and corners.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 15|15b]] So his <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">fame spread far and wide</span>, for he was helped tremendously so that he <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">became powerful</span>.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 16|16a]] But when Uzziah <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">grew powerful</span>, his arrogance led to his own <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">destruction</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 16|16b-20]] He was unfaithful to the LORD his God, for he entered the temple of the LORD to burn incense on the altar of incense. Then Azariah the priest, along with eighty <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">brave priests</span> of the LORD, went in after him. They took their stand against King Uzziah and said, "Uzziah, you have no right to offer incense to the LORD. Only the priests, the descendants of Aaron, are consecrated to burn incense. Leave the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully; you will not receive honor from the LORD God." Uzziah, with a censer in his hand to offer incense, was enraged. But while he raged against the priests in their presence in the house of the LORD before the altar of incense, leprosy broke out on his forehead. When Azariah the chief priest and all the priests turned to him and saw his leprous forehead, they rushed him out. Indeed, he himself hurried to get out, because the LORD had afflicted him.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 21|21]] So King Uzziah was a leper until the day of his death. He lived in isolation, leprous and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">cut off from the house of the LORD</span>, while his son Jotham had charge of the royal palace to govern the people of the land.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 22|22-23]] As for the rest of the acts of Uzziah, from beginning to end, they are recorded by the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz. And Uzziah rested with his fathers and was buried near them in a field of burial that belonged to the kings; for the people said, "He was a leper." And his son Jotham <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">reigned in his place</span>.
