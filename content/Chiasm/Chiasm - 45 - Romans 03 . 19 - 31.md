- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 27|27]]-[[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 31|31]] Where is boasting then? It is excluded. By what <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span>? of works? Nay: but by the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span> of faith. Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span>. Is he the God of the Jews only? is he not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also: Seeing it is one God, which shall justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith. Do we then make void the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span> through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span>.
