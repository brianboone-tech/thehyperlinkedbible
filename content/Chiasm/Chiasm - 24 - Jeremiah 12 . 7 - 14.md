            - D'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 12#Jeremiah 12 . 11|11]] They have made it a <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">desolation</span>; desolate before Me, it mourns. All the land is laid waste, but no man takes it to heart.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 12#Jeremiah 12 . 12|12]] Over all the barren heights in the wilderness the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">destroyers</span> have come, for the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">sword of the LORD</span> devours from one end of the earth to the other.     - B'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 12#Jeremiah 12 . 13|13]] They have <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">sown wheat but harvested thorns</span>. They have exhausted themselves to no avail. Bear the shame of your harvest because of the fierce anger of the LORD."
