[[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 4#Proverbs 4 . 1|1-5]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Hear</span>, ye children, the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">instruction</span> of a father, and attend to know understanding. For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law. For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother. He <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">taught</span> me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">retain</span> my words: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">keep</span> my commandments, and live. Get <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span>, get understanding: forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 4#Proverbs 4 . 8|8-9]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Exalt her</span>, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">she shall promote thee</span>: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">embrace her</span>. - A'. [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 4#Proverbs 4 . 10|10-13]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Hear</span>, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many. I have <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">taught</span> thee in the way of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span>; I have led thee in right paths. Take fast hold of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">instruction</span>; let her not go: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">keep</span> her; for she is thy life.
