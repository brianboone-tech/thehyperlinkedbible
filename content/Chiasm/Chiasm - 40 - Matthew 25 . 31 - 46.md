[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 31|31]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 33|33]] When the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Son of man shall come in his glory</span>, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory: And before him shall be gathered all nations: and he shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats</span>: And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.

    - a. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 34|34]] Then shall the King say unto them on his <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">right hand</span>, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:

        - b. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 35|35]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 36|36]] For <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in: Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me</span>.

    - a'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 37|37]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 39|39]] Then shall the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">righteous</span> answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink? When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee? Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?

        - c. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 40|40]] And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me</span>.

    - a. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 41|41]] Then shall he say also unto them on the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">left hand</span>, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:

        - b. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 42|42]]-[[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 43|43]] For <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not</span>.

    - a'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 44|44]] Then shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">they</span> also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?

        - c'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 45|45]] Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 25#Matthew 25 . 46|46]] And these shall go away into <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">everlasting punishment</span>: but the righteous into <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">life eternal</span>.
