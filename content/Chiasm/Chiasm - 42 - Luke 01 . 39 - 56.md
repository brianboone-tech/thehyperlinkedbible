He hath put down the mighty from their seats, and <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">exalted them of low degree</span>. He hath filled the hungry with good things; and the rich he hath sent empty away. He hath holpen his servant Israel, in remembrance of his <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span>; As he spake to our fathers, to Abraham, and to his seed for ever.
