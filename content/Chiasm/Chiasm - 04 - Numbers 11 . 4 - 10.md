We remember the fish, which we did eat in Egypt freely; the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlick:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 6|6]] But now our soul is dried away: there is nothing at all, beside <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">this manna</span>, before our eyes.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 7|7]] And <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the manna</span> was as coriander seed, and the colour thereof as the colour of bdellium.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 8|8]] And the people went about, and <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">gathered it, and ground it in mills, or beat it in a mortar, and baked it in pans, and made cakes of it</span>:

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 8|8]] and the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">taste of it</span> was as the taste of fresh oil.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 9|9]] And when the dew fell upon the camp in the night, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the manna</span> fell upon it.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 10|10]] Then Moses heard <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the people weep</span> throughout their families, every man in the door of his tent: and the anger of the LORD was kindled greatly; Moses also was displeased.
