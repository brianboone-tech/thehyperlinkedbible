- A. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 27#2 Chronicles 27 . 1|1]] Jotham was <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">twenty-five years old</span> when he became king, and he <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years</span>. His mother's name was Jerushah daughter of Zadok.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 27#2 Chronicles 27 . 2|2]] And he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">did what was right in the eyes of the LORD</span>, just as his father Uzziah had done. In addition, he did not enter the temple of the LORD. But the people still behaved corruptly.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 27#2 Chronicles 27 . 3|3-4]] Jotham <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">rebuilt the Upper Gate</span> of the house of the LORD, and he worked extensively on the wall at the hill of Ophel. He also <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">built cities</span> in the hill country of Judah and fortresses and towers in the forests.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 27#2 Chronicles 27 . 5|5]] Jotham <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">waged war</span> against the king of the Ammonites and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">defeated them</span>, and that year they gave him a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand cors of wheat, and ten thousand cors of barley. They paid him the same in the second and third years.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 27#2 Chronicles 27 . 6|6]] So Jotham grew powerful because he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">ordered his ways before the LORD his God</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 27#2 Chronicles 27 . 7|7-9]] As for the rest of the acts of Jotham, along with all his wars and his ways, they are indeed written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah. He was <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">twenty-five years old</span> when he became king, and he <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">reigned in Jerusalem sixteen years</span>. And Jotham rested with his fathers and was buried in the City of David. And his son Ahaz reigned in his place.
