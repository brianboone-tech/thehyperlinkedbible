- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 97#Psalm 97 . 1|1-5]] The LORD reigneth; let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of isles be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">glad</span> thereof. Clouds and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">darkness</span> are round about him: righteousness and judgment are the habitation of his throne. A fire goeth before him, and burneth up his enemies round about. His lightnings enlightened the world: the earth saw, and trembled. The hills melted like wax at the presence of the LORD, at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 97#Psalm 97 . 6|6-7]] The <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">heavens</span> declare his righteousness, and all the people see his glory. Confounded be all they that serve graven images, that boast themselves of idols: worship him, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">all ye gods</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 97#Psalm 97 . 8|8]] Zion heard, and was glad; and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of thy judgments, O LORD.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 97#Psalm 97 . 9|9-10]] For thou, LORD, art high <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">above all the earth</span>: thou art exalted far above <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">all gods</span>. Ye that love the LORD, hate evil: he preserveth the souls of his saints; he delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 97#Psalm 97 . 11|11-12]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Light</span> is sown for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Rejoice</span> in the LORD, ye righteous; and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness.
