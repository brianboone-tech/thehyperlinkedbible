[[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 3|3]] And he shall judge among many people, and rebuke strong nations afar off; <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruning hooks</span>: nation shall not lift up a sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more. [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 1|5:1]] Now gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops: he hath laid siege against us: they shall smite the judge of Israel with a rod upon the cheek.
