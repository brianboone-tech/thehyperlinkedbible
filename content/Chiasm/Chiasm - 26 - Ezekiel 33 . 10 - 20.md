- 2A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 12|12]] Therefore, son of man, say to your people: '<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The righteousness of the righteous man will not deliver him in the day of his transgression</span>;

    - 2B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 12|12]] neither will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">the wickedness of the wicked man</span> cause him to stumble on the day <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">he turns from his wickedness</span>.

    - 2A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 12|12-13]] Nor will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the righteous man be able to survive by his righteousness on the day he sins</span>. If I tell the righteous man that he will surely live, but he then trusts in his righteousness and commits iniquity, then none of his righteous works will be remembered; he will die because of the iniquity he has committed.

    - 2B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 14|14-16]] But if I tell <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">the wicked man</span>, 'You will surely die,' and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">he turns from his sin</span> and does what is just and right—if he restores a pledge, makes restitution for what he has stolen, and walks in the statutes of life without practicing iniquity—then he will surely live; he will not die. None of the sins he has committed will be held against him. He has done what is just and right; he will surely live.
