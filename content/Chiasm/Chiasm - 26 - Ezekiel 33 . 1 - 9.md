[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 1|1-2]] Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Son of man</span>, speak to your people and tell them: 'Suppose I bring the sword against a land, and the people of that land choose a man from among them, appointing him as their watchman,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 3|3-5]] and he sees the sword coming against that land and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">blows the ram's horn to warn the people</span>. Then if anyone hears the sound of the horn but fails to heed the warning, and the sword comes and takes him away, his blood will be on his own head. Since he heard the sound of the horn but failed to heed the warning, his blood will be on his own head. If he had heeded the warning, he would have saved his life.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 6|6]] But if the watchman sees the sword coming and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fails to blow the horn to warn the people</span>, and the sword comes and takes away a life, then that one will be taken away in his iniquity, but I will hold the watchman accountable for his blood.'

    - C'.     - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 33#Ezekiel 33 . 9|9]] But if you <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">warn the wicked man to turn</span> from his way, and he does not turn from it, he will die in his iniquity, but you will have saved your life.
