- A. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 1|1-2]] In the third year of the reign of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Jehoiakim king of Judah</span>, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it. And the Lord delivered into his hand Jehoiakim king of Judah, along with some of the articles from the house of God. He carried these off to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, where he put them in the treasury of his god.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 3|3-4]] Then the king ordered Ashpenaz, the chief of his court officials, to bring in some Israelites from the royal family and the nobility— young men without blemish, handsome, gifted in all <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span>, knowledgeable, quick to <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">understand</span>, and qualified to serve in the king's palace—and to teach them the language and literature of the Chaldeans.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 5|5]] The king assigned them daily provisions of the royal food and wine. They were to be trained for three years, after which they were to <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">enter the king's service</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 6|6]] Among these young men were some from Judah: <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah</span>.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 7|7]] The chief official <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">gave them new names</span>: To Daniel he gave the name Belteshazzar; to Hananiah, Shadrach; to Mishael, Meshach; and to Azariah, Abednego.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 8|8]] But Daniel made up his mind that he would not defile himself with <span style="color: #27ae60; font-weight: bold;">the king's food</span> or <span style="color: #27ae60; font-weight: bold;">wine</span>. So he asked the chief official for permission not to defile himself.

                        - G. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 9|9-10a]] Now God had granted Daniel favor and compassion from the chief official, but he said to Daniel, "I fear my lord the <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">king</span>, who has assigned your <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">food and drink</span>.

                            - H. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 10|10b]] For why should he see your <span style="color: #e74c3c; font-weight: bold;">faces looking thinner than those of the other young men</span> your age? You would endanger my head before the king!"

                                - I. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 11|11-12]] Then Daniel said to the steward whom the chief official had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah, "Please <span style="color: #9b59b6; font-weight: bold;">test your servants for ten days</span>. Let us be given only vegetables to eat and water to drink.

                                    - J. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 13|13]] Then <span style="color: #34495e; font-weight: bold;">compare our appearances</span> with those of the young men who are eating the royal food, and deal with your servants according to what you see."

                                - I'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 14|14]] So he consented to this and <span style="color: #9b59b6; font-weight: bold;">tested them for ten days</span>.

                            - H'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 15|15a]] And at the end of ten days, they looked <span style="color: #e74c3c; font-weight: bold;">healthier and better nourished than all the young men</span>

                        - G'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 15|15b]] who were eating the <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">king's food</span>.

                    - F'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 16|16]] So the steward continued to withhold <span style="color: #27ae60; font-weight: bold;">their choice food</span> and the <span style="color: #27ae60; font-weight: bold;">wine</span> they were to drink, and he gave them vegetables instead.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 17|17]] To these four young men <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">God gave</span> knowledge and understanding in every kind of literature and wisdom. And Daniel had insight into all kinds of visions and dreams.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 18|18-19a]] Now at the end of the time specified by the king, the chief official presented them to Nebuchadnezzar. And the king spoke with them, and among all the young men he found no one equal to <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 19|19b]] So they <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">entered the king's service</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 20|20]] In every matter of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span> and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">understanding</span> about which the king consulted them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters in his entire kingdom.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 21|21]] And Daniel remained there until <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the first year of King Cyrus</span>.
