[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 1|7:1-4]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Exhortation to Ahaz to "fear not"</span> - 1 And it came to pass in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up toward Jerusalem to war against it, but could not prevail against it. 2 And it was told the house of David, saying, Syria is confederate with Ephraim. And his heart was moved, and the heart of his people, as the trees of the wood are moved with the wind. 3 Then said the LORD unto Isaiah, Go forth now to meet Ahaz, thou, and Shearjashub thy son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller's field; 4 And say unto him, Take heed, and be quiet; <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">fear not, neither be fainthearted</span> for the two tails of these smoking firebrands, for the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria, and of the son of Remaliah.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 10|10-16]] The sign of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Immanuel (God with us)</span> - 10 Moreover the LORD spake again unto Ahaz, saying, 11 Ask thee a sign of the LORD thy God; ask it either in the depth, or in the height above. 12 But Ahaz said, I will not ask, neither will I tempt the LORD. 13 And he said, Hear ye now, O house of David; Is it a small thing for you to weary men, but will ye weary my God also? 14 Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Immanuel</span>. 15 Butter and honey shall he eat, that he may know to refuse the evil, and choose the good. 16 For before the child shall know to refuse the evil, and choose the good, the land that thou abhorrest shall be forsaken of both her kings.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 17|17-20]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The invasion of the King of Assyria</span> - 17 The LORD shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon thy father's house, days that have not come, from the day that Ephraim departed from Judah; <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">even the king of Assyria</span>. 18 And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD shall hiss for the fly that is in the uttermost part of the rivers of Egypt, and for the bee that is in the land of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Assyria</span>. 19 And they shall come, and shall rest all of them in the desolate valleys, and in the holes of the rocks, and upon all thorns, and upon all bushes. 20 In the same day shall the Lord shave with a razor that is hired, namely, by them beyond <span style="color: #138d75; font-weight: bold;">the river</span>, by <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the king of Assyria</span>, the head, and the hair of the feet.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 8#Isaiah 8 . 1|8:1-4]] The Sign of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Mahershalalhashbaz</span> - deliverance - 1 Moreover the LORD said unto me, Take thee a great roll, and write in it with a man's pen concerning <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">Mahershalalhashbaz</span>. 2 And I took unto me faithful witnesses to record, Uriah the priest, and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah. 3 And I went unto the prophetess; and she conceived, and bare a son. Then said the LORD to me, Call his name <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">Mahershalalhashbaz</span>. 4 For before the child shall have knowledge to cry, My father, and my mother, the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.

[[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 8#Isaiah 8 . 5|5-8]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">The invasion of Immanuel's land by the King of Assyria</span> - 5 The LORD spake also unto me again, saying, 6 Forasmuch as this people refuseth the waters of Shiloah that go softly, and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah's son; 7 Now therefore, behold, the LORD bringeth up upon them <span style="color: #138d75; font-weight: bold;">the waters of the river</span>, strong and many, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">even the king of Assyria</span>, and all his glory: <span style="color: #138d75; font-weight: bold;">and he shall come up over all his channels, and go over all his banks</span>: 8 And he shall pass through Judah; <span style="color: #138d75; font-weight: bold;">he shall overflow and go over</span>, he shall reach even to the neck; and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 8#Isaiah 8 . 11|11-15]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Exhortation to Isaiah not to fear</span> - 11 For the LORD spake thus to me with a strong hand, and instructed me that I should not walk in the way of this people, saying, 12 Say ye not, A confederacy, to all them to whom this people shall say, A confederacy; <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">neither fear ye their fear, nor be afraid</span>. 13 Sanctify the LORD of hosts himself; <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread</span>. 14 And he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem. 15 And many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken.
