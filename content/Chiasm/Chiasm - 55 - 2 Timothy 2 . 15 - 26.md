- A. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 15|15]] Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman who accurately handles the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">word of truth</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 16|16-18]] But avoid irreverent, empty <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">chatter</span>, which will only lead to more ungodliness, and the talk of such men will spread like gangrene. Among them are <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Hymenaeus and Philetus</span>, who have deviated from the truth. They say that the resurrection has already occurred, and they undermine the faith of some.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 19|19]] Nevertheless, God's firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: "<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">The Lord knows those who are His</span>," and, "Everyone who calls on the name of the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Lord</span> must turn away from <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">iniquity</span>."

            - D. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 20|20]] A large house contains not only <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">vessels</span> of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay. Some indeed are for <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">honorable use</span>, but others are for <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">common use</span>.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 21|21]] So if anyone cleanses himself of what is unfit, he will be a <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">vessel</span> for <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">honor</span>: sanctified, useful to the Master, and prepared for every good work.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 22|22]] Flee also youthful <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">lusts</span> but pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, together with those who call on the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Lord</span> out of pure hearts.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 23|23]] But <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">foolish and unlearned questions</span> avoid, knowing they generate <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">strifes</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 2#2 Timothy 2 . 24|24-26]] And a servant of the Lord must not be quarrelsome, but must be kind to everyone, able to teach, and forbearing. He must gently reprove those who oppose him, in the hope that God may grant them repentance leading to a knowledge of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">truth</span>. Then they will come to their senses and escape the snare of the devil, who has taken them captive to his will.
