    [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 11#Jeremiah 11 . 11|Jeremiah 11:11]] - B. Therefore thus saith the LORD, Behold, I will bring evil upon them, which they shall not be able to escape; and though they shall cry unto me, I will not hearken unto them.
