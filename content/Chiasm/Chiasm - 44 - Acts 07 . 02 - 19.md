- A. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 2|2]]-[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 3|3]] Men, brethren, and fathers, hearken; The God of glory appeared unto <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">our father Abraham</span>, when he was in Mesopotamia, before he dwelt in Charran, And said unto him, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and come into the land which I shall shew thee.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 4|4]] Then came he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">out of the land of the Chaldaeans, and dwelt in Charran</span>: and from thence, when his father was dead, he removed him into this land, wherein ye now dwell.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 5|5]] And <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">he gave him none inheritance</span> in it, no, not so much as to set his foot on: yet he promised that he would give it to him for a possession, and to his seed after him, when as yet he had no child.

        - B'. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 6|6]]-[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 7|7]] And God spake on this wise, That his seed should <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sojourn in a strange land</span>; and that they should bring them into bondage, and entreat them evil four hundred years. And the nation to whom they shall be in bondage will I judge, said God: and after that shall they come forth, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">serve me in this place</span>.

    - A'. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 8|8]] And he gave him the covenant of circumcision: and so <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Abraham begat Isaac</span>, and circumcised him the eighth day; and Isaac begat Jacob; and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Jacob begat the twelve patriarchs</span>.

- A. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 9|9]]-[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 10|10]] And the patriarchs, moved with envy, sold Joseph into Egypt: but God was with him, And delivered him out of all his afflictions, and <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">gave him favour and wisdom</span> in the sight of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and he made him governor over Egypt and all his house.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 11|11]]-[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 12|12]] Now there came a dearth over all the land of Egypt and Chanaan, and great affliction: and our fathers found no sustenance. But when Jacob heard that there was corn in Egypt, he sent out our fathers first.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 13|13]] And at the second time <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">Joseph was made known to his brethren</span>; and Joseph's kindred was made known unto Pharaoh.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 14|14]]-[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 16|16]] Then sent Joseph, and called his father Jacob to him, and all his kindred, threescore and fifteen souls. So Jacob went down into Egypt, and died, he, and our fathers, And were carried over into Sychem, and laid in the sepulchre that Abraham bought for a sum of money of the sons of Emmor the father of Sychem.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 17|17]]-[[Readable Bible/44 - Acts/Acts 7#Acts 7 . 19|19]] But when the time of the promise drew nigh, which God had sworn to Abraham, the people grew and multiplied in Egypt, Till <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">another king arose, which knew not Joseph</span>. The same dealt subtilly with our kindred, and evil entreated our fathers, so that they cast out their young children, to the end they might not live.
