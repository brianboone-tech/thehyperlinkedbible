    - 1B. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 1|1b]] and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">stewards</span> of the mysteries of God.

    - 1B'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 2|2a]] Moreover it is required in <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">stewards</span>,

- 1A'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 2|2b]] that a man be found <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">faithful</span>.

- 2A. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 3|3a]] But with me it is a very small thing that I should be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">judged</span> of you, or of man's <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span>:

    - 2B. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 3|3b]] yea, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">I judge not mine own self</span>.

        - 2C. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 4|4a]] For I know nothing by myself; yet am I not hereby justified:

    - 2B'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 4|4b]] but <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">he that judgeth me</span> is the Lord.

- 2A'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 5|5]] Therefore <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">judge</span> nothing before the time, until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts: and then shall every man have praise of God.

- 3A. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 6|6]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 8|8]] And these things, brethren, I have in a figure transferred to myself and to Apollos for your sakes; that ye might learn in us not to think of men above that which is written, that no one of you be puffed up for one against another. For who maketh thee to differ from another? and what hast thou that thou didst not receive? now if thou didst receive it, why dost thou glory, as if thou hadst not received it? Now ye are full, now ye are rich, ye have reigned as kings without us: and I would to God ye did reign, that we also might reign with you.

    - 3B. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 9|9]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 10|10]] For I think that God hath set forth us the apostles last, as it were appointed to death: for we are made a spectacle unto the world, and to angels, and to men. We are fools for Christ's sake, but ye are wise in Christ; we are weak, but ye are strong; ye are honourable, but we are despised.

        - 3C. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 11|11]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 13|13]] Even unto this present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no certain dwellingplace; And labour, working with our own hands: being <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">reviled</span>, we bless; being persecuted, we suffer it: being <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">defamed</span>, we intreat: we are made as the filth of the world, and are the offscouring of all things unto this day.

    - 3B'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 14|14]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 15|15]] I write not these things to shame you, but as my beloved sons I warn you. For though ye have ten thousand instructers in Christ, yet have ye not many fathers: for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the gospel.

- 3A'. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 16|16]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 4#1 Corinthians 4 . 17|17]] Wherefore I beseech you, be ye followers of me. For this cause have I sent unto you Timotheus, who is my beloved son, and faithful in the Lord, who shall bring you into remembrance of my ways which be in Christ, as I teach every where in every church.
