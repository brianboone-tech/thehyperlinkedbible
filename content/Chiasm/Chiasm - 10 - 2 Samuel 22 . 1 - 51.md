- A. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 2|2-3]] He said: "The LORD is my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">rock</span>, my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fortress</span>, and my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">deliverer</span>. My God is my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">rock</span>, in whom I take refuge, my shield, and the horn of my salvation. My stronghold, my refuge, and my Savior, You save me from violence."

    - B. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 4|4-7]] I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised; so shall I be saved from my enemies. For the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">waves of death</span> engulfed me; the torrents of chaos overwhelmed me. The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me. In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried out to my God. And from His temple He <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">heard</span> my voice, and my cry for help reached His ears.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 8|8-16]] Then the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">earth shook</span> and quaked; the foundations of the heavens trembled; they were shaken because He burned with anger. Smoke rose from His nostrils, and consuming <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fire</span> came from His mouth; glowing coals blazed forth. He parted the heavens and came down with dark clouds beneath His feet. He mounted a cherub and flew; He soared on the wings of the wind. He made darkness a canopy around Him, a gathering of water and thick clouds. From the brightness of His presence coals of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fire</span> blazed forth. The LORD thundered from heaven; the voice of the Most High resounded. He shot His <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">arrows</span> and scattered the foes; He hurled lightning and routed them. The channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were exposed at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of His nostrils.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 17|17-20]] He <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">reached down</span> from on high and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">took hold</span> of me; He <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">drew</span> me out of deep waters. He rescued me from my powerful enemy, from foes too mighty for me. They confronted me in my day of calamity, but the LORD was my support. He <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">brought me out</span> into the open; He rescued me because He delighted in me.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 21|21-25]] The LORD has <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">rewarded</span> me according to my <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span>; He has <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">repaid</span> me according to the cleanness of my hands. For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. For all His ordinances are before me; I have not disregarded His statutes. And I have been blameless before Him and kept myself from iniquity. So the LORD has <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">repaid</span> me according to my <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span>, according to my cleanness in His sight.

                    - X. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 26|26-28]] To the faithful You show Yourself <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">faithful</span>, to the blameless You show Yourself <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">blameless</span>; to the pure You show Yourself <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">pure</span>, but to the crooked You show Yourself shrewd. You save an afflicted people, but Your eyes are on the haughty to bring them down.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 29|29-33]] For You, O LORD, are my lamp; the LORD lights up my darkness. For in You I can charge an army; with my God I can scale a wall. As for God, His <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">way is perfect</span>; the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">word</span> of the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him. For who is God besides the LORD? And who is the Rock except our God? God is my strong fortress and He makes my way clear.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 34|34-37]] He makes my feet like those of a deer and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">stations me</span> upon the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">heights</span>. He trains my hands for battle; my arms can bend a bow of bronze. You have given me Your shield of salvation, and Your gentleness exalts me. You broaden the path beneath me so that my ankles do not give way. He <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">set me</span> on <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">high places</span> and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">enlarged</span> my steps.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 38|38-43]] I <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">pursued</span> my enemies and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">destroyed</span> them; I did not turn back until they were <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">consumed</span>. I devoured and crushed them so they could not rise; they have fallen under my feet. You have armed me with strength for battle; You have subdued my foes beneath me. You have made my enemies retreat before me; I put an end to those who hated me. They looked, but there was no one to save them—to the LORD, but He did not answer. I ground them as the dust of the earth; I crushed and trampled them like mud in the streets.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 44|44-46]] You have delivered me from the strife of my people; You have preserved me as the head of nations; a people I had not known shall serve me. Foreigners cower before me; when they <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">hear</span> me, they obey me. Foreigners lose heart and come trembling from their strongholds.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 47|47-51]] The LORD lives, and blessed be my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Rock</span>! And may God, the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Rock</span> of my salvation, be exalted—the God who avenges me and brings down nations beneath me, who frees me from my enemies. You exalt me above my foes; You rescue me from violent men. Therefore I will praise You, O LORD, among the nations; I will sing praises to Your name. Great <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">salvation</span> He brings to His king. He shows loving devotion to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever."
