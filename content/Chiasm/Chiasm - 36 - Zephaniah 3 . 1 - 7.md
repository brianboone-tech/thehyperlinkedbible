- A. [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 1|3:1-2]] Woe to her who is rebellious and defiled, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the oppressing city</span>! <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">She listens to no voice; she accepts no correction</span>. She does not trust in the Lord; she does not draw near to her God.

- B. [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 3|3:3-4]] Her officials within her <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">are roaring lions</span>; her judges <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">are evening wolves</span> that leave nothing till the morning. Her prophets are fickle, treacherous men; her priests profane what is holy; they do violence to the law.

- B'. [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 5|3:5]] The Lord within her <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">is righteous</span>; he does no injustice; every morning he shows forth his justice; each dawn he does not fail; but the unjust knows no shame.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 6|3:6-7]] "I have cut off nations; their battlements are in ruins; I have laid waste their streets so that no one walks in them; their <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">cities</span> have been made desolate, without a man, without an inhabitant. I said, 'Surely you will fear me; <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">you will accept correction</span>. Then your dwelling would not be cut off according to all that I have appointed against you.' But all the more they were eager to make all their deeds corrupt.
