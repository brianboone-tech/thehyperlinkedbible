- A. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 1|1-2]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The house of David threatened</span> - 1 And it came to pass in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up toward Jerusalem to war against it, but could not prevail against it. 2 And it was told the house of David, saying, Syria is confederate with Ephraim. And his heart was moved, and the heart of his people, as the trees of the wood are moved with the wind.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 3|3-6]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Isaiah's son; northern powers's plans</span> - 3 Then said the LORD unto Isaiah, Go forth now to meet Ahaz, thou, and Shearjashub <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">thy son</span>, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller's field; 4 And say unto him, Take heed, and be quiet; fear not, neither be fainthearted for the two tails of these smoking firebrands, for the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria, and of the son of Remaliah. 5 Because Syria, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah, have taken evil counsel against thee, saying, 6 <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">Let us go up against Judah, and vex it, and let us make a breach therein for us, and set a king in the midst of it, even the son of Tabeal</span>:

        - C. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 7|7-9]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">YHWH's assurance</span> - 7 Thus saith the Lord GOD, It shall not stand, neither shall it come to pass. 8 For the head of Syria is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin; and within threescore and five years shall Ephraim be broken, that it be not a people. 9 And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah's son. <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 10|10-12]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Response of unbelief</span> - 10 Moreover the LORD spake again unto <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">Ahaz</span>, saying, 11 <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">Ask thee a sign of the LORD</span> thy God; ask it either in the depth, or in the height above. 12 But <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">Ahaz</span> said, <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">I will not ask</span>, neither will I tempt the <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">LORD</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 13|13-15]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">YHWH's sign</span> - 13 And he said, Hear ye now, O house of David; Is it a small thing for you to weary men, but will ye weary my God also? 14 <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign</span>; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel. 15 Butter and honey shall he eat, that he may know to refuse the evil, and choose the good.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 16|16]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Virgin's son; destruction of the northern powers</span> - 16 For before <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the child</span> shall know to refuse the evil, and choose the good, <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">the land that thou abhorrest shall be forsaken of both her kings</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 17|17]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The house of David destroyed</span> - 17 The LORD shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon thy father's house, days that have not come, from the day that Ephraim departed from Judah; even the king of Assyria.
