- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 1|1-2]] Lord, thou hast been our <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">dwelling place</span> in all generations. Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 3|3-6]] Thou <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">turnest</span> man to destruction; and sayest, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Return</span>, ye children of men. For a thousand years in thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night. Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep: in the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">morning</span> they are like grass which groweth up. In the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">morning</span> it flourisheth, and groweth up; in the evening it is cut down, and withereth.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 7|7-9]] For we are consumed by thine anger, and by thy <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wrath</span> are we troubled. Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, our secret sins in the light of thy countenance. For all our <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">days</span> are passed away in thy <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wrath</span>: we spend our years as a tale that is told.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 10|10]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">The days of our years are threescore years and ten; and if by reason of strength they be fourscore years, yet is their strength labour and sorrow; for it is soon cut off, and we fly away.</span>

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 11|11-12]] Who knoweth the power of thine anger? even according to thy fear, so is thy <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wrath</span>. So teach us to number our <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">days</span>, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 13|13-15]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Return</span>, O LORD, how long? and let it repent thee concerning thy servants. O satisfy us <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">early</span> with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days. Make us glad according to the days wherein thou hast afflicted us, and the years wherein we have seen evil.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 90#Psalm 90 . 16|16-17]] Let thy work appear unto thy servants, and thy glory unto their children. And let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">establish thou the work of our hands</span> upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.
