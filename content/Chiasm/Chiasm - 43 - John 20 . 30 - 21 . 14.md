        - C. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 4|4]] But when the morning was now come, Jesus stood on the shore: but the disciples <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">knew not</span> that it was <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Jesus</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 5|5]] Then Jesus saith unto them, Children, have ye any <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">meat</span>? They answered him, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">No</span>.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 6|6]] And he said unto them, <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Cast the net</span> on the right side of the ship, and ye shall find. They cast therefore, and now they were not able to draw it for the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">multitude of fishes</span>.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 7|7]]-[[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 8|8]] Therefore that disciple whom Jesus loved saith unto Peter, <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">It is the Lord</span>. Now when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he girt his fisher's coat unto him, (for he was naked,) and did cast himself into the sea. And the other disciples came in a little ship; (for they were not far from land, but as it were two hundred cubits,) <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">dragging the net with fishes</span>.

                        - PIVOT. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 9|9]] As soon then as they were come to land, they saw a <span style="color: #943126; font-weight: bold;">fire of coals there, and fish laid thereon, and bread</span>.

                    - F'. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 21#John 21 . 10|10]] Jesus saith unto them, <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Bring of the fish</span> which ye have now caught.
