- A. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Babylon's crimes against the people</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 4|4]] That thou shalt take up this proverb against the king of <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">Babylon</span>, and say, How hath the oppressor ceased! the golden city ceased! 5 The LORD hath broken the staff of the wicked, and the sceptre of the rulers. 6 He who smote <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">the people</span> in wrath with a continual stroke, he that ruled the nations in anger, is persecuted, and none hindereth. 7 The whole <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">earth</span> is at rest, and is quiet: they break forth into singing. 8 Yea, the fir trees rejoice at thee, and the cedars of Lebanon, saying, Since thou art laid down, no feller is come up against us.

    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The kings in the graveyard makes room for Babylon</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 9|9]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Hell</span> from beneath is moved for thee to meet thee at thy coming: it stirreth up the dead for thee, even all the chief ones of the earth; it hath raised up from their thrones <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">all the kings of the nations</span>. 10 All they shall speak and say unto thee, Art thou also become weak as we? art thou become like unto us? 11 Thy pomp is brought down to the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">grave</span>, and the noise of thy viols: the worm is spread under thee, and the worms cover thee.

        - C. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">Babylon's ambition to become God</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 12|12]] How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer, son of the morning! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst weaken the nations! 13 For thou hast said in thine heart, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God: I will sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the sides of the north: 14 I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the most High.</span>

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">But no royal sepulchre in the graveyard for Babylon</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 15|15]] Yet thou shalt be brought down to <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">hell</span>, to the sides of the pit. 16 They that see thee shall narrowly look upon thee, and consider thee, saying, Is this the man that made the earth to tremble, that did shake kingdoms; 17 That made the world as a wilderness, and destroyed the cities thereof; that opened not the house of his prisoners? 18 <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">All the kings of the nations</span>, even all of them, lie in glory, every one in his own house. 19 But thou art cast out of thy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">grave</span> like an abominable branch, and as the raiment of those that are slain, thrust through with a sword, that go down to the stones of the pit; as a carcase trodden under feet.

- A'. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Babylon crimes against their own people</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 20|20]] Thou shalt not be joined with them in burial, because thou hast destroyed <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">thy land</span>, and slain <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">thy people</span>: the seed of evildoers shall never be renowned. 21 Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities. 22 For I will rise up against them, saith the LORD of hosts, and cut off from <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">Babylon</span> the name, and remnant, and son, and nephew, saith the LORD.
