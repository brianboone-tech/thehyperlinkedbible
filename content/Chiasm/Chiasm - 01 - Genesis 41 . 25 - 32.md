- A. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 25|25]] At this, Joseph said to Pharaoh, "The dreams of Pharaoh are one and the same. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">God has revealed to Pharaoh what He is about to do</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 26|26]] The <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven good cows are seven years</span>, and the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven ripe heads of grain are seven years</span>. The dreams have the same meaning.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 27|27]] Moreover, the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven thin, ugly cows</span> that came up after them <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">are seven years</span>, and so are the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven worthless heads of grain</span> scorched by the east wind—they are <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven years of famine</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 28|28]] It is just as I said to Pharaoh: <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">God has shown Pharaoh what He is about to do</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 29|29]] Behold, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven years of great abundance</span> are coming throughout the land of Egypt,

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 30|30]] but <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">seven years of famine</span> will follow them. Then all the abundance in the land of Egypt will be forgotten, and the famine will devastate the land.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 31|31]] The abundance in the land will not be remembered, since the famine that follows it will be so severe.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 32|32]] Moreover, because the dream was given to Pharaoh in two versions, the matter has been <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">decreed by God, and He will carry it out shortly</span>.
