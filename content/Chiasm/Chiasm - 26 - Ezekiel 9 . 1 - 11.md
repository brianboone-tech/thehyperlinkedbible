- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 1|1]] Then I heard Him call out in a loud voice, saying, "Draw near, O executioners of the city, each with a weapon of destruction in hand." [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 2|2]] And I saw six men coming from the direction of the Upper Gate, which faces north, each with a weapon of slaughter in his hand. With them was another <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">man clothed in linen who had a writing kit at his side.</span> And they came in and stood beside the bronze altar.

    - i. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 3|3]] Then the glory of the God of Israel rose from above the cherubim, where it had been, and moved to the threshold of the temple.

    - i'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 3|3]] And He called to the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">man clothed in linen who had the writing kit at his side.</span> [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 4|4]] "Go throughout the city of Jerusalem," said the LORD, "and put a mark on the foreheads of the men sighing and groaning over all the abominations committed there."

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 5|5]] And as I listened, He said to the others, "Follow him through the city and start killing; <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">do not show pity or spare anyone!</span>

        - C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 6|6]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Slaughter</span> the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">old men, the young men and maidens, the women and children;</span> but do not go near anyone who has the mark. Now begin at My sanctuary." So they began with <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the elders</span> who were before the temple.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 7|7]] Then He told them, "Defile the temple and fill the courts with the slain. Go forth!" So they went out and began killing throughout the city.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 8|8]] While they were <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">killing,</span> I was left alone. And I fell facedown and cried out, "Oh, Lord GOD, when You pour out Your wrath on Jerusalem, will You destroy the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">entire remnant of Israel?</span>"

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 9|9]] He replied, "The iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah is exceedingly great. The land is full of bloodshed, and the city is full of perversity. For they say, 'The LORD has forsaken the land; the LORD does not see.' [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 9#Ezekiel 9 . 10|10]] But as for Me, I will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">not look on them with pity, nor will I spare them.</span> I will bring their deeds down upon their own heads."
