---

- A. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 1|1]] This is a true saying, If a man desire the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">office</span> of a bishop, he desireth a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">good work</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 2|2]] A bishop then must be <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">blameless</span>, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 3|3]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Not given to wine</span>, no striker, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">not greedy of filthy lucre</span>; but patient, not a brawler, not covetous;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 4|4]] One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 5|5]] (For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?)

        - C. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 6|6]] Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fall</span> into the condemnation of the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">devil</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 7|7a]] Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without;

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 7|7b]] lest he <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fall</span> into reproach and the snare of the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">devil</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 8|8]] Likewise must the deacons be grave, not doubletongued, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">not given to much wine</span>, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">not greedy of filthy lucre</span>;

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 9|9]] Holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 10|10]] And let these also first be proved; then let them use the office of a deacon, being found <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">blameless</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 11|11]] Even so must their wives be grave, not slanderers, sober, faithful in all things.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 12|12]] Let the deacons be the husbands of one wife, ruling their children and their own houses well.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 3#1 Timothy 3 . 13|13]] For they that have used the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">office</span> of a deacon well purchase to themselves a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">good degree</span>, and great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus.
