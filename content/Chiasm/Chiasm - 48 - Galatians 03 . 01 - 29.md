---

- A. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 1|1]] O foolish Galatians, who hath bewitched you, that ye should not obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth, crucified among you? [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 2|2]] This only would I learn of you, Received ye <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the Spirit</span> by the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">works of the law</span>, or by the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">hearing of faith</span>?

    - B. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 3|3]] Are ye so foolish? <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">having begun in the Spirit, are ye now made perfect by the flesh?</span> [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 4|4]] Have ye suffered so many things in vain? if it be yet in vain.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 5|5]] He therefore that ministereth to you <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the Spirit</span>, and worketh miracles among you, doeth he it by the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">works of the law</span>, or by the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">hearing of faith</span>?

            - D. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 6|6]] Even as <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Abraham believed God</span>, and it was accounted to him for righteousness. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 7|7]] Know ye therefore that <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">they which are of faith</span>, the same are the children of Abraham. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 8|8]] And the scripture, foreseeing that God would <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">justify the heathen through faith</span>, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">In thee shall all nations be blessed</span>. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 9|9]] So then <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">they which be of faith</span> are blessed with faithful Abraham.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 10|10]] For as many as are of the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">works of the law</span> are under the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">curse</span>: for it is written, Cursed is every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 11|11]] But that no man is justified by the law in the sight of God, it is evident: for, <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">The just shall live by faith</span>.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 12|12]] And the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">law</span> is not of faith: but, The man that doeth them shall live in them. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 13|13]] Christ hath redeemed us from the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">curse of the law</span>, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree: [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 14|14]] That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the Spirit</span> through faith.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 15|15]] Brethren, I speak after the manner of men; Though it be but a man's covenant, yet if it be confirmed, no man disannulleth, or addeth thereto. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 16|16]] Now to <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Abraham</span> and his seed were the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">promises</span> made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 17|17]] And this I say, that the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, which was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul, that it should make the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">promise</span> of none effect. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 18|18]] For if the inheritance be of the law, it is no more of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">promise</span>: but God gave it to <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Abraham</span> by <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">promise</span>. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 19|19]] Wherefore then serveth the law? It was added because of transgressions, till the seed should come to whom the promise was made; and it was ordained by angels in the hand of a mediator. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 20|20]] Now a mediator is not a mediator of one, but God is one. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 21|21]] Is the law then against the promises of God? God forbid: for if there had been a law given which could have given life, verily righteousness should have been by the law. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 22|22]] But the scripture hath concluded all under sin, that the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">promise</span> by <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">faith</span> of Jesus Christ might be given to them that believe.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 23|23]] But before <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">faith</span> came, we were kept under the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">law</span>, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 24|24]] Wherefore the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">law</span> was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">faith</span>. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 25|25]] But after that <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">faith</span> is come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 26|26]] For <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus</span>. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 27|27]] For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 28|28]] There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus. [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 3#Galatians 3 . 29|29]] And if ye be Christ's, then are ye <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Abraham's seed</span>, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heirs according to the promise</span>.
