[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 1|Jeremiah 2:1]] Moreover the word of the LORD came to me, saying, [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 2|Jeremiah 2:2]] Go and cry in the ears of Jerusalem, saying,

Oracle 1
Thus saith the LORD;
I remember thee,
the kindness of thy youth,
the love of thine espousals,
when thou wentest after me in the wilderness,
in a land that was not sown.

[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 3|Jeremiah 2:3]] Israel was holiness unto the LORD,
and the firstfruits of his increase:
all that devour him shall offend;
evil shall come upon them,
— saith the LORD.

Oracle 2
[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 4|Jeremiah 2:4]] Hear ye the word of the LORD, O house of Jacob, and all the families of the house of Israel:

- A. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 5|Jeremiah 2:5]] Thus saith the LORD, What iniquity have your fathers found in me, that they are <span style="color: #c0392b;">gone far from me</span>, and have <span style="color: #c0392b;">walked after vanity</span>, and are become vain?

    - B. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 6|Jeremiah 2:6]] Neither said they, <span style="color: #1f618d;">Where is the LORD</span> that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, that led us through the wilderness, through a land of deserts and of pits, through a land of drought, and of the shadow of death, through a land that no man passed through, and where no man dwelt?

        - C. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 7|Jeremiah 2:7]] And I brought you into a plentiful country, to eat the <span style="color: #16a085;">fruit</span> thereof and the <span style="color: #16a085;">goodness</span> thereof;

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 7|Jeremiah 2:7]] but when ye entered, ye <span style="color: #16a085;">defiled</span> my land, and made mine heritage an <span style="color: #16a085;">abomination</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 8|Jeremiah 2:8]] The priests said not, <span style="color: #1f618d;">Where is the LORD?</span> and they that handle the law knew me not: the pastors also transgressed against me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal,

- A'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 8|Jeremiah 2:8]] and <span style="color: #c0392b;">walked after things that do not profit</span>. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 9|Jeremiah 2:9]] Wherefore I will yet plead with you, — saith the LORD, and with your children's children will I plead.

Oracle 3

[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 10|Jeremiah 2:10]] For pass over the isles of Chittim, and see; and send unto Kedar, and consider diligently, and see if there be such a thing.

[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 11|Jeremiah 2:11]] Hath a nation changed their gods, which are yet no gods? but my people have changed their glory for that which doth not profit.

[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 12|Jeremiah 2:12]] Be astonished, O ye heavens, at this, and be horribly afraid, be ye very desolate, — saith the LORD.
