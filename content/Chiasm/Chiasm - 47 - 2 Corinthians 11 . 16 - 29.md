- A. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 16|16]] I say again, Let no man think me a fool; if otherwise, yet as a fool receive me, that I may boast myself a little.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 17|17a]] That which I speak, I speak it not after the Lord, but as it were foolishly,

        - C. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 17|17b]] in this confidence of boasting.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 18|18]] Seeing that many glory after the flesh,

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 18|18b]] I will glory also.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 19|19]]-[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 20|20]] For ye suffer fools gladly, seeing ye yourselves are wise. For ye suffer, if a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour you, if a man take of you, if a man exalt himself, if a man smite you on the face.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 21|21a]] I speak as concerning reproach, as though we had been weak.

- A. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 21|21b]] Howbeit whereinsoever any is bold, (I speak foolishly,) I am bold also.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 22|22]] Are they Hebrews? so am I. Are they Israelites? so am I. Are they the seed of Abraham? so am I.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 23|23]] Are they ministers of Christ? (I speak as a fool) I am more; in labours more abundant, in stripes above measure, in prisons more frequent, in deaths oft.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 24|24]]-[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 25|25]] Of the Jews five times received I forty stripes save one. Thrice was I beaten with rods, once was I stoned, thrice I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day I have been in the deep;

                - E. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 26|26]] In journeyings often, in perils of waters, in perils of robbers, in perils by mine own countrymen, in perils by the heathen, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils in the sea, in perils among false brethren;

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 27|27]] In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 28|28]]-[[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 11#2 Corinthians 11 . 29|29]] Beside those things that are without, that which cometh upon me daily, the care of all the churches. Who is weak, and I am not weak? who is offended, and I burn not?
