- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 1|1]] The mighty God, even the LORD, hath spoken, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">called</span> the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">earth</span> from the rising of the sun unto the going down thereof.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 2|2-3]] Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined. Our God shall come, and shall not keep silence: a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fire</span> shall devour before him, and it shall be very tempestuous round about him.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 4|4]] He shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">call</span> to the heavens from above, and to the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">earth</span>, that he may judge his people.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 5|5-6]] Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">sacrifice</span>. And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge himself. Selah.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 7|7-13]] Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: I am God, even thy God. I will not reprove thee for thy <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">sacrifices</span> or thy <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">burnt offerings</span>, to have been continually before me. I will take no <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">bullock</span> out of thy house, nor <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">he goats</span> out of thy folds. For every beast of the forest is mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills. I know all the fowls of the mountains: and the wild beasts of the field are mine. If I were hungry, I would not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness thereof. Will I eat the flesh of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">bulls</span>, or drink the blood of <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">goats</span>?

            - D. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 14|14-15]] Offer unto God <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">thanksgiving</span>; and pay thy vows unto the most High: And call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 16|16-21]] But unto the wicked God saith, What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth? Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee. When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers. Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit. Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother's son. These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 22|22-23]] Now consider this, ye that forget God, lest I tear you in pieces, and there be none to deliver. Whoso offereth <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">praise</span> glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God.
