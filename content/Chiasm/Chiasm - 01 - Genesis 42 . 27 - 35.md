    - B. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 42#Genesis 42 . 29|29]] When they reached their father Jacob in the land of Canaan, they described to him all that had happened to them:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 42#Genesis 42 . 30|30]] "The man who is lord of the land spoke harshly to us and accused us of spying on the country.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 42#Genesis 42 . 31|31]] But we told him, 'We are <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">honest men</span>, not <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">spies</span>.
