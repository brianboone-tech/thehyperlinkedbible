- 2A. [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 7|7-10]] He also tore down the quarters of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">male shrine prostitutes</span> that were in the house of the LORD, where the women had woven tapestries for Asherah. Then Josiah brought all the priests from the cities of Judah and desecrated the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">high places</span>, from Geba to Beersheba, where the priests had burned incense. Although the priests of the high places did not come up to the altar of the LORD in Jerusalem, they ate unleavened bread with their fellow priests. He also desecrated <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Topheth</span> in the Valley of Ben-hinnom so that no one could sacrifice his son or daughter in the fire to Molech.

    - 2B. [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 11|11]] And he removed from the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">entrance to the house of the LORD</span> the horses that the kings of Judah had dedicated to the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sun</span>. They were in the court near the chamber of an official named Nathan-melech. And Josiah burned up the chariots of the sun.

    - 2B'. [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 12|12]] He pulled down the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">altars</span> that the kings of Judah had set up on the roof near the upper chamber of Ahaz, and the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">altars</span> that Manasseh had set up in the two <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">courtyards of the house of the LORD</span>. - 2A'. [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 13|13-14]] The king also desecrated the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">high places</span> east of Jerusalem, to the south of the Mount of Corruption, which King Solomon of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the abomination of the Sidonians, for Chemosh the abomination of the Moabites, and for Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites. He smashed the sacred pillars to pieces, cut down the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Asherah poles</span>, and covered the sites with human bones.
