- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 42#Psalm 42 . 1|42:1-4]] As the hart panteth after the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">water</span> brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God. My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God: when shall I come and appear before God? My tears have been my meat day and night, while they continually say unto me, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Where is thy God?</span> When I remember these things, I pour out my soul in me: for I had gone with the multitude, I went with them to the house of God, with the voice of joy and praise, with a multitude that kept holyday.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 42#Psalm 42 . 5|42:5]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted in me?</span> hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him for the help of his countenance.

        - 2A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 42#Psalm 42 . 6|42:6-10]] O my God, my soul is cast down within me: therefore will I remember thee from the land of Jordan, and of the Hermonites, from the hill Mizar. Deep calleth unto deep at the noise of thy waterspouts: all thy waves and thy billows are gone over me. Yet the LORD will command his lovingkindness in the daytime, and in the night his song shall be with me, and my prayer unto the God of my life. I will say unto God my rock, Why hast thou forgotten me? why go I <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">mourning</span> because of the oppression of the enemy? As with a sword in my bones, mine enemies reproach me; while they say daily unto me, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Where is thy God?</span>

        - 2B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 42#Psalm 42 . 11|42:11]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me?</span> hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God.

            - 3A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 43#Psalm 43 . 1|43:1-4]] Judge me, O God, and plead my cause against an ungodly nation: O deliver me from the deceitful and unjust man. For thou art the God of my strength: why dost thou cast me off? why go I <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">mourning</span> because of the oppression of the enemy? O send out thy light and thy truth: let them lead me; let them bring me unto thy holy hill, and to thy tabernacles. Then will I go unto the altar of God, unto God my exceeding joy: yea, upon the harp will I praise thee, O God my God.
