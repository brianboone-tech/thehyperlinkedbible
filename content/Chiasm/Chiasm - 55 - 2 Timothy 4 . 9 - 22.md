- A. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 9|9]] Make every effort to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">come to me quickly</span>,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 10|10-13]] because <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Demas</span>, in his love of this world, has deserted me and gone to Thessalonica. <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Crescens</span> has gone to Galatia, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Titus</span> to Dalmatia. Only <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Luke</span> is with me. Get <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Mark</span> and bring him with you, because he is useful to me in the ministry. <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Tychicus</span>, however, I have sent to Ephesus. When you come, bring the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">cloak</span> that I left with Carpus at Troas, and my <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">scrolls</span>, especially the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">parchments</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 14|14-15]] Alexander the coppersmith did great harm to me. The Lord will repay him according to his <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">works</span>. You too should beware of him, for he has vigorously opposed our message.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 16|16]] At my first defense, <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">no man stood with me</span>, but everyone deserted me. May it not be charged against them.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 17|17]] But the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Lord stood with me</span> and strengthened me, so that through me the message would be fully proclaimed, and all the Gentiles would hear it. So I was delivered from the mouth of the lion.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 18|18]] And the Lord will rescue me from every <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">evil work</span> and bring me safely into His heavenly kingdom. To Him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 19|19-20]] Greet <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Prisca and Aquila</span>, as well as the household of <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Onesiphorus</span>. <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Erastus</span> has remained at Corinth, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Trophimus</span> I left sick in Miletus.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/55 - 2 Timothy/2 Timothy 4#2 Timothy 4 . 21|21]] Make every effort to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">come to me before winter</span>. Eubulus sends you greetings, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers.
