- A. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 1|1]] And the LORD said to Moses, [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 2|2]] "Speak to the Israelites and tell them that if a man or woman makes a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">special vow</span>, the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vow of a Nazirite</span>, to separate himself to the LORD,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 3|3]] he is to abstain from <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wine and strong drink</span>. He must not drink vinegar made from wine or strong drink, and he must not drink any grape juice or eat fresh grapes or raisins. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 4|4]] All the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">days of his separation</span>, he is not to eat anything that comes from the grapevine, not even the seeds or skins.

        - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 5|5]] For the entire period of his vow of separation, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">no razor</span> shall pass over his head. He must be holy until the time of his <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">separation to the LORD</span> is complete; he must let the hair of his head grow long.

            - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 6|6]] Throughout the <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">days of his separation to the LORD</span>, he must not go near a <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">dead body</span>. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 7|7]] Even if his father or mother or brother or sister should die, he is not to defile himself, because the crown of consecration to his God is upon his head. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 8|8]] Throughout the time of his <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">separation, he is holy to the LORD</span>.

                - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 9|9]] If someone suddenly dies in his presence and defiles his consecrated <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">head of hair</span>, he must <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">shave his head</span> on the day of his cleansing—the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">seventh day</span>.

                    - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 10|10]] On the <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">eighth day</span> he must bring <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">two turtledoves or two young pigeons</span> to the priest at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting.

                        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 11|11]] And the priest is to offer one as a <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">sin offering</span> and the other as a <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">burnt offering</span> to make atonement for him, because he has sinned by being in the presence of the dead body. On that day he must consecrate his head again.

                    - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 11|11b]] On that day he must <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">consecrate his head</span> again.

                - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 12|12]] He must rededicate his time of separation to the LORD and bring a year-old male lamb as a guilt offering. But the preceding <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">days shall not be counted</span>, because his separation was <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">defiled</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 13|13]] Now this is the law of the Nazirite when his time of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">separation is complete</span>: He must be brought to the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 14|14]] and he is to present an offering to the LORD of an unblemished year-old male lamb as a burnt offering, an unblemished year-old female lamb as a sin offering, and an unblemished ram as a peace offering— [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 15|15]] together with their grain offerings and drink offerings—and a basket of unleavened cakes made from fine flour mixed with oil and unleavened wafers coated with oil. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 16|16]] The priest is to present all these before the LORD and make the sin offering and the burnt offering. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 17|17]] He shall also offer the ram as a peace offering to the LORD, along with the basket of unleavened bread. And the priest is to offer the accompanying grain offering and drink offering. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 18|18]] Then at the entrance to the Tent of Meeting, the Nazirite is to shave his consecrated head, take the hair, and put it on the fire under the peace offering. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 19|19]] And the priest is to take the boiled shoulder from the ram, one unleavened cake from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and put them into the hands of the Nazirite who has just shaved the hair of his consecration. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 20|20]] The priest shall then wave them as a wave offering before the LORD. This is a holy portion for the priest, in addition to the breast of the wave offering and the thigh that was presented. After that, the Nazirite may drink <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wine</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 21|21]] This is the law of the Nazirite who <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">vows his offering</span> to the LORD for his <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">separation</span>, in addition to whatever else he can afford; he must fulfill whatever vow he makes, according to the law of his separation."
