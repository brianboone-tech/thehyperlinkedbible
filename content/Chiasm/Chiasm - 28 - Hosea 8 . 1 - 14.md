- A. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 1|1-3]] Set the trumpet to thy mouth. He shall come as an eagle against the house of the LORD, because they have <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">transgressed</span> my covenant, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trespassed</span> against my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span>. Israel shall cry unto me, My God, we know thee. Israel hath cast off the thing that is good: the enemy shall pursue him.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 4|4-5]] They have set up <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">kings</span>, but not by me: they have made <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">princes</span>, and I knew it not: of their silver and their gold have they made them idols, that they may be cut off. Thy calf, O Samaria, hath cast thee off; mine anger is kindled against them: how long will it be ere they attain to innocency?

        - C. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 6|6]] For from <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Israel</span> was it also: the workman made it; therefore it is not God: but the calf of Samaria shall be broken in pieces.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 7|7]] For they have <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind</span>: it hath no stalk: the bud shall yield no meal: if so be it yield, the strangers shall swallow it up.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 8|8-9]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Israel</span> is swallowed up: now shall they be among the Gentiles as a vessel wherein is no pleasure. For they are gone up to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself: Ephraim hath hired lovers.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 10|10-11]] Yea, though they have hired among the nations, now will I gather them, and they shall sorrow a little for the burden of the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">king</span> of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">princes</span>. Because Ephraim hath made many altars to sin, altars shall be unto him to sin.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 12|12-14]] I have written to him the great things of my <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">law</span>, but they were counted as a strange thing. They sacrifice flesh for the sacrifices of mine offerings, and eat it; but the LORD accepteth them not; now will he remember their <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">iniquity</span>, and visit their <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sins</span>: they shall return to Egypt. For Israel hath forgotten his Maker, and buildeth temples; and Judah hath multiplied fenced cities: but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour the palaces thereof.
