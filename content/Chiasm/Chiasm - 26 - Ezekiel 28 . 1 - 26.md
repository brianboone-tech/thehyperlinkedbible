- A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 1|1-2]] And the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre that this is what the Lord GOD says: Your <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heart is proud</span>, and you have said, 'I am a god; I sit in the seat of gods in the heart of the sea.' Yet you are a man and not a god, though you have <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">regarded your heart as that of a god</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 3|3-4]] Behold, you are wiser than Daniel; no secret is hidden from you! By your <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span> and understanding you have gained your <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wealth</span> and amassed gold and silver for your treasuries.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 5|5]] By your great skill in trading you have increased your <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">wealth</span>, but your heart has grown proud because of it.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 6|6-7]] Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">regard your heart as the heart of a god</span>, behold, I will bring foreigners against you, the most ruthless of nations. They will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and will defile your splendor.

- 2A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 8|8]] They will bring you down to the Pit, and you will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">die a violent death</span> in the heart of the seas.

    - 2B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 9|9]] Will you still say, '<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">I am a god</span>,' in the presence of those who slay you? You will be only a man, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">not a god</span>, in the hands of those who wound you.

- 2A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 10|10]] You will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">die the death</span> of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners. For I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD."

- 3A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 11|11-13]] Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "Son of man, take up a lament for the king of Tyre and tell him that this is what the Lord GOD says: 'You were the seal of perfection, full of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span> and perfect in <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">beauty</span>. You were in <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Eden</span>, the garden of God. Every kind of precious stone adorned you: ruby, topaz, and diamond, beryl, onyx, and jasper, sapphire, turquoise, and emerald. Your mountings and settings were crafted in gold, prepared on the day of your creation.

    - 3B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 14|14]] You were <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">anointed as a guardian cherub</span>, for I had ordained you. You were on the holy mountain of God; you walked among the fiery stones.

        - 3C. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 15|15]] From the day you were created you were <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">blameless in your ways</span>—until <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">wickedness was found in you</span>.

    - 3B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 16|16]] By the vastness of your trade, you were filled with violence, and you sinned. So I drove you in disgrace from the mountain of God, and I banished you, O <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">guardian cherub</span>, from among the fiery stones.

- 3A'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 17|17-19]] Your heart grew proud of your <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">beauty</span>; you corrupted your <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wisdom</span> because of your splendor; so I cast you to the earth; I made you a spectacle before kings. By the multitude of your iniquities and the dishonesty of your trading you have profaned your sanctuaries. So I made fire come from within you, and it consumed you. I reduced you to ashes on the ground in the eyes of all who saw you. All the nations who know you are appalled over you. You have come to a horrible end and will be no more.'"

- 4A. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 20|20-22]] Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying, "Son of man, set your face against Sidon and prophesy against her. And you are to declare that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">this is what the Lord GOD says</span>: 'Behold, I am against you, O Sidon, and I will be glorified within you. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">They will know that I am the LORD</span> when I execute judgments against her and demonstrate My holiness through her.

    - 4B. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 23|23]] I will send a plague against her and shed blood in her streets; the slain will fall within her, while the sword is against her on every side. Then <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">they will know that I am the LORD</span>.

    - 4B'. [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 24|24-26]] For the people of Israel will no longer face a pricking brier or a painful thorn from all around them who treat them with contempt. Then <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">they will know that I am the Lord GOD</span>.' This is what the Lord GOD says: 'When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they have been scattered, I will show Myself holy among them in the sight of the nations. Then they will dwell in their own land, which I have given to My servant Jacob. And there they will dwell securely, build houses, and plant vineyards. They will dwell securely when I execute judgments against all those around them who treat them with contempt. Then <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">they will know that I am the LORD their God</span>.'"
