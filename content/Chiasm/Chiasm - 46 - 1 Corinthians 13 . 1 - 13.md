- A. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 1|1]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 3|3]] Though I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, and have not <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">charity</span>, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal. And though I have the gift of prophecy, and understand all mysteries, and all knowledge; and though I have all faith, so that I could remove mountains, and have not <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">charity</span>, I am nothing. And though I bestow all my goods to feed the poor, and though I give my body to be burned, and have not <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">charity</span>, it profiteth me nothing.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 4|4]]-[[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 13#1 Corinthians 13 . 7|7]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Charity</span> suffereth long, and is kind; <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">charity</span> envieth not; <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">charity</span> vaunteth not itself, is not puffed up, Doth not behave itself unseemly, seeketh not her own, is not easily provoked, thinketh no evil; Rejoiceth not in iniquity, but rejoiceth in the truth; Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things.

            - D.
