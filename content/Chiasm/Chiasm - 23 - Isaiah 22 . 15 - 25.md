    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Shebna's death predicted – he will be cast out of the country</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 22#Isaiah 22 . 17|17]] Behold, the LORD will carry thee away with a mighty captivity, and will surely cover thee. 18 He will surely violently turn and toss thee like a ball into a large country: there shalt thou die, and there the chariots of thy <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">glory</span> shall be the shame of thy lord's <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">house</span>. 19 And I will drive thee from thy station, and from thy state shall he pull thee down.

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Eliakim will take over from Shebna</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 22#Isaiah 22 . 20|20]] And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">Eliakim</span> the son of Hilkiah: 21 And I will clothe him with thy robe, and strengthen him with thy girdle, and I will commit thy government into his hand: and he shall be a father to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and to <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">the house</span> of Judah. 22 And the key of <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">the house</span> of David will I lay upon his shoulder; so he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open. 23 And <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">I will fasten him as a nail in a sure place</span>; and he shall be for a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">glorious</span> throne to his father's <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">house</span>. 24 And they shall hang upon him all the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">glory</span> of his father's <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">house</span>, the offspring and the issue, all vessels of small quantity, from the vessels of cups, even to all the vessels of flagons.

- A'. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Shebna's removal predicted?</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 22#Isaiah 22 . 25|25]] In that day, saith the LORD of hosts, shall <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">the nail that is fastened in the sure place</span> be removed, and be cut down, and fall; and the burden that was upon it shall be cut off: for the LORD hath spoken it.
