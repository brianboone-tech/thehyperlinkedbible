**First Section: Children and Parents (6:1-4):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 1|1]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Children, obey your parents</span> in the Lord: for this is right.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 2|2a]] Honour thy father and mother;

        - C. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 2|2b]] (which is the first commandment with promise;)

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 3|3]] That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 4|4]] And, ye <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fathers, provoke not your children</span> to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

**Second Section: Servants and Masters (6:5-9):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 5|5a]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Servants, be obedient</span> to them that are your masters according to the flesh,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 5|5b]] with <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fear and trembling</span>, in <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">singleness of your heart</span>, as unto Christ;

        - C. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 6|6a]] Not with <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">eyeservice</span>, as <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">menpleasers</span>;

            - D. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 6|6b]] but as the servants of Christ, <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">doing the will of God from the heart</span>;

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 7|7]] With <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">good will</span> doing service, as to the Lord, and not to men:

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 8|8]] Knowing that whatsoever good thing any man doeth, the same shall he receive of the Lord, whether he be bond or free.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 6#Ephesians 6 . 9|9]] And, ye <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">masters, do the same things</span> unto them, forbearing threatening: knowing that your Master also is in heaven; neither is there respect of persons with him.
