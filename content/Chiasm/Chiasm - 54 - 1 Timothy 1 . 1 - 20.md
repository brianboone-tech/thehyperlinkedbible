- A. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 2|2]] Unto <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Timothy, my own son</span> in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace, from God our Father and Jesus Christ our Lord.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 3|3]] As I besought thee to abide still at Ephesus, when I went into Macedonia, that thou mightest <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">charge</span> some that they teach no other doctrine,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 4|4]] Neither give heed to fables and endless genealogies, which minister questions, rather than godly edifying which is in faith: so do.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 5|5]] Now the end of the commandment is charity out of a pure heart, and of a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">good conscience</span>, and of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">faith</span> unfeigned:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 6|6]] From which some having swerved have turned aside unto vain jangling;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 7|7]] Desiring to be teachers of the law; understanding neither what they say, nor whereof they affirm.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 8|8]] But we know that the law is good, if a man use it lawfully;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 9|9]] Knowing this, that the law is not made for a righteous man, but for the lawless and disobedient, for the ungodly and for <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sinners</span>, for unholy and profane, for murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, for manslayers,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 10|10]] For whoremongers, for them that defile themselves with mankind, for menstealers, for liars, for perjured persons, and if there be any other thing that is contrary to sound doctrine;

        - C. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 11|11]] According to the glorious gospel of the blessed God, which was committed to <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">my</span> trust.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 12|12a]] And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord,

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 12|12b]] who hath enabled <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">me</span>, for that he counted <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">me</span> faithful, putting <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">me</span> into the ministry;

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 13|13]] Who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious: but I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 14|14]] And the grace of our Lord was exceeding abundant with faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 15|15]] This is a faithful saying, and worthy of all acceptation, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sinners</span>; of whom I am chief.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 16|16]] Howbeit for this cause I obtained mercy, that in me first Jesus Christ might shew forth all longsuffering, for a pattern to them which should hereafter believe on him to life everlasting.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 1#1 Timothy 1 . 17|17]] Now unto the King eternal, immortal, invisible, the only wise God, be honour and glory for ever and ever. Amen.
