- A. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 2|2-3]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the violence in Judah</span> — O Lord, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! even cry out unto thee of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">violence</span>, and thou wilt not save! Why dost thou shew me iniquity, and cause me to behold grievance? for spoiling and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">violence</span> are before me: and there are that raise up strife and contention.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 4|4]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">injustice in Judah</span> — Therefore the law is slacked, and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span> doth never go forth: for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore wrong <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span> proceedeth.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 5|5]] Behold ye among the heathen, and regard, and wonder marvellously: for I will work a work in your days, which ye will not believe, though it be told you.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 6|6-7]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">God's justice</span> — For, lo, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, which shall march through the breadth of the land, to possess the dwellingplaces that are not theirs. They are terrible and dreadful: their <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span> and their dignity shall <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">proceed</span> of themselves.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 8|8-11]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the violence of the Babylonians</span> — Their horses also are swifter than the leopards, and are more fierce than the evening wolves: and their horsemen shall spread themselves, and their horsemen shall come from far; they shall fly as the eagle that hasteth to eat. They shall come all for <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">violence</span>: their faces shall sup up as the east wind, and they shall gather the captivity as the sand. And they shall scoff at the kings, and the princes shall be a scorn unto them: they shall deride every strong hold; for they shall heap dust, and take it. Then shall his mind change, and he shall pass over, and offend, imputing this his power unto his god.
