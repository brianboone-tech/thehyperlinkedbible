- A. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 1|1]] This is what the LORD says: "Go and buy a clay jar from a potter. Take some of the elders of the people and leaders of the priests, [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 2|2]] and go out to the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Valley of Ben-hinnom</span> near the entrance of the Potsherd Gate. Proclaim there the words I speak to you,

    - B. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 3|3]] saying, 'Hear the word of the LORD, O <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">kings of Judah and residents of Jerusalem</span>. This is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: I am going to bring such disaster on this place that the ears of all who hear of it will ring,

        - C. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 4|4]] because they have <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">abandoned Me</span> and made this a foreign place. They have burned incense in this place to other gods that neither they nor their fathers nor the kings of Judah have ever known. They have filled this place with the blood of the innocent. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 5|5]] They have built high places to Baal on which to burn their children in the fire as offerings to Baal—something I never commanded or mentioned, nor did it even enter My mind.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 6|6]] So behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when this place will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom, but the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Valley of Slaughter</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 19#Jeremiah 19 . 7|7]] And in this place I will ruin the plans of <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Judah and Jerusalem</span>. I will make them fall by the sword before their enemies, by the hands of those who seek their lives, and I will give their carcasses as food to the birds of the air and the beasts of the earth.
