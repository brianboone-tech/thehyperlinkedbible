    - B. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 7|7]] And they asked him, saying, Master, but when shall these things be? and what <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sign</span> will there be when these things shall come to pass?

        - C. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 8|8]] And he said, Take heed that ye be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">not deceived</span>: for many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and the time draweth near: go ye not therefore after them.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 9|9]] But when ye shall hear of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">wars and commotions</span>, be not terrified: for these things must first come to pass; but the end is not by and by.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 10|10]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 11|11]] Then said he unto them, <span style="color: #7b241c; font-weight: bold;">Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom</span>: And great earthquakes shall be in divers places, and famines, and pestilences; and fearful sights and great <span style="color: #7b241c; font-weight: bold;">signs</span> shall there be from heaven.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 12|12]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 17|17]] But before all these, they shall lay their hands on you, and <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">persecute you</span>, delivering you up to the synagogues, and into prisons, being brought before kings and rulers <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">for my name's sake</span>. And it shall turn to you for a testimony. Settle it therefore in your hearts, not to meditate before what ye shall answer: For I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to gainsay nor resist. And ye shall be betrayed both by parents, and brethren, and kinsfolks, and friends; and some of you shall they cause to be put to death. And ye shall be hated of all men <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">for my name's sake</span>.

                        - G. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 18|18]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 19|19]] But there shall not an hair of your head perish. In your <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">patience</span> possess ye your souls.

                    - F'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 20|20]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 24|24]] And when ye shall see Jerusalem compassed with armies, then know that the <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">desolation thereof is nigh</span>. Then let them which are in Judaea <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">flee to the mountains</span>; and let them which are in the midst of it depart out; and let not them that are in the countries enter thereinto. For these be the days of vengeance, that all things which are written may be fulfilled. But woe unto them that are with child, and to them that give suck, in those days! for there shall be great distress in the land, and wrath upon this people. And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 25|25]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 27|27]] And there shall be <span style="color: #7b241c; font-weight: bold;">signs</span> in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars; and upon the earth distress of nations, with perplexity; the sea and the waves roaring; Men's hearts failing them for fear, and for looking after those things which are coming on the earth: for the powers of heaven shall be shaken. And then shall they see <span style="color: #7b241c; font-weight: bold;">the Son of man coming in a cloud</span> with power and great glory.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 28|28]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 33|33]] And when these things begin to come to pass, then look up, and lift up your heads; for your <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">redemption draweth nigh</span>. And he spake to them a parable; Behold the fig tree, and all the trees; When they now shoot forth, ye see and know of your own selves that summer is now nigh at hand. So likewise ye, when ye see these things come to pass, know ye that the kingdom of God is nigh at hand. Verily I say unto you, This generation shall not pass away, till all be fulfilled. Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 34|34]]-[[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 35|35]] And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares. For as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 36|36]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Watch</span> ye therefore, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">pray</span> always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.
