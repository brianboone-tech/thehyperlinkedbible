- A'. [[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 4#Jonah 4 . 6|6]] And the LORD God prepared a gourd, and made it to come up over Jonah, that it might be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his grief. So Jonah was <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">exceeding glad</span> of the gourd.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 4#Jonah 4 . 7|7-8]] But God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd that it withered. And it came to pass, when the sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and wished in himself to die, and said, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">It is better for me to die than to live</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 4#Jonah 4 . 9|9]] And God said to Jonah, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Doest thou well to be angry</span> for the gourd? And he said, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I do well to be angry</span>, even unto death.
