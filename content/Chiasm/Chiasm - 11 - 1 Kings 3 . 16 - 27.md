- 1A. [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 16|16]] Then came there <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">two women, that were harlots, unto the king, and stood before him</span>.

    - 1B. [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 17|17-21]] And the one woman said, O my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house; and I was delivered of a child with her in the house. And it came to pass the third day after that I was delivered, that this woman was delivered also: and we were together; there was no stranger with us in the house, save we two in the house. And this woman's child died in the night; because she overlaid it. And she arose at midnight, and took my son from beside me, while thine handmaid slept, and laid it in her bosom, and laid her dead child in my bosom. And when I rose in the morning to give my child suck, behold, it was dead: but when I had considered it in the morning, behold, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">it was not my son, which I did bear</span>.

    - 1B'. [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 22|22]] And the other woman said, Nay; but <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the living is my son, and the dead is thy son</span>. And this said, No; but the dead is thy son, and the living is my son.

- 1A'. [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 22|22]] (continued) <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Thus they spake before the king</span>.
