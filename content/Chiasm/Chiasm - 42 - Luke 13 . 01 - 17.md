    - 2B. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 13#Luke 13 . 7|7]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">Then said he unto the dresser of his vineyard,</span> Behold, <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">these three years</span> I come seeking <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">fruit</span> on this fig tree, and find none:

        - 2C. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 13#Luke 13 . 7|7b]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">cut it down; why cumbereth it the ground?</span>

    - 2B'. [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 13#Luke 13 . 8|8]] And <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">he answering said unto him,</span> Lord, <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">let it alone this year also,</span> till I shall dig about it, and dung it:
