- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 61#Psalm 61 . 1|1-4]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Hear my cry, O God</span>; attend unto my prayer. From the end of the earth will I cry unto thee, when my heart is overwhelmed: lead me to the rock that is higher than I. For thou hast been a shelter for me, and a strong tower from the enemy. I will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">abide</span> in thy tabernacle <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">for ever</span>: I will trust in the covert of thy wings. Selah.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 61#Psalm 61 . 5|5-8]] For thou, O God, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">hast heard my vows</span>: thou hast given me the heritage of those that fear thy name. Thou wilt prolong the king's life: and his years as many generations. He shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">abide</span> before God <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">for ever</span>: O prepare mercy and truth, which may preserve him. So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows.
