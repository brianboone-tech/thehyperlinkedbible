**First Chiasm (10:9-10):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 9|9]] That if thou shalt <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">confess with thy mouth</span> the Lord Jesus, and shalt <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">believe</span> in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 10|10]] For with <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the heart</span> man <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">believeth</span> unto righteousness; and with the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">mouth confession</span> is made unto salvation.

**Second Chiasm (10:11-13):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 11|11]] For the scripture saith, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Whosoever believeth</span> on him shall not be ashamed.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 12|12]] For there is <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">no difference</span> between <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the Jew and the Greeks</span>: for <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the same</span> Lord over <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">all</span> is rich unto <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">all</span> that call upon him.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 13|13]] For <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">whosoever</span> shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

**Third Chiasm (10:14-15):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 14|14a]] How then shall they <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">call</span> on him in whom they have not believed?

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 14|14b]] and how shall they <span style="color: #d35400; font-weight: bold;">believe</span> in him of whom they have not heard?

        - C. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 14|14c]] and how shall they <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">hear</span> without a preacher?

            - D. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 15|15a]] And how shall they <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">preach</span>, except they be sent?

                - E. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 15|15b]] as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!

**Fourth Chiasm (10:16-18):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 16|16]] But they have not all <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">obeyed</span> the gospel. For Esaias saith, Lord, who hath believed our report?

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 17|17]] So then <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">faith cometh by hearing</span>, and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">hearing by the word of God</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 18|18]] But I say, Have they not <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">heard</span>? Yes verily, their sound went into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world.

**Fifth Chiasm (10:19-21):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 19|19]] But I say, Did not Israel know? First Moses saith, I will provoke you to <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">jealousy</span> by them that are no people, and by a foolish nation I will anger you.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 20|20]] But Esaias is very bold, and saith, I was <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">found</span> of them that <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">sought</span> me not; I was made manifest unto them that asked not after me.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 21|21]] But to Israel he saith, All day long I have stretched forth my hands unto a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">disobedient</span> and gainsaying people.
