        - C. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 3|11:3]] I taught <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">EPHRAIM</span> also to go, taking them by their arms; but they knew not that I healed them.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 5|11:5a]] <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">HE SHALL NOT RETURN</span> into the land of Egypt,

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 6|11:6]] And the sword shall abide on his cities, and shall consume his branches, and <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">DEVOUR</span> them, because of their own counsels.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 8|11:8a]] How shall I give thee up, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">EPHRAIM</span>?
