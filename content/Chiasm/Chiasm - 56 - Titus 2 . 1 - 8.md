- A. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 1|1]] But speak thou the things which become <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sound doctrine</span>:

- A. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 2|2]] That the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">aged men</span> be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sober</span>, grave, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">temperate</span>, sound in faith, in charity, in patience.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 3|3]] The <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">aged women</span> likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 4|4-5]] That they may teach the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">young women</span> to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children, To be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 6|6]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Young men</span> likewise exhort to be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sober minded</span>.

- A. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 7|7]] In all things shewing thyself a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">pattern of good works</span>:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 7|7]] in <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">doctrine</span> shewing un-corruptness, gravity, sincerity,

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 8|8a]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Sound speech</span>, that cannot be condemned;

- A'. [[Readable Bible/56 - Titus/Titus 2#Titus 2 . 8|8b]] that he that is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">no evil thing to say of you</span>.
