- A. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 1|1-2]] Indignation of YHWH – 1 Come near, ye nations, to hear; and hearken, ye people: let the earth hear, and all that is therein; the world, and all things that come forth of it. 2 For the indignation of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">LORD</span> is upon all nations, and his fury upon all their armies: he hath utterly destroyed them, he hath delivered them to the slaughter.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 3|3-4]] Blood shed – 3 Their slain also shall be cast out, and their stink shall come up out of their carcases, and the mountains shall be melted with <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">their blood</span>. 4 And all the host of heaven shall be dissolved, and the heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll: and all their host shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine, and as a falling fig from the fig tree.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 5|5]] In Edom – For my sword shall be bathed in heaven: behold, it shall come down upon <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Idumea</span>, and upon the people of my curse, to judgment.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 6|6]] The sword of YHWH – The <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">sword of the LORD</span> is filled with blood, it is made fat with fatness, and with the blood of lambs and goats, with the fat of the kidneys of rams:

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 6|6]] In Edom – for the LORD hath a sacrifice in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Idumea</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 7|7]] Blood shed – And the unicorns shall come down with them, and the bullocks with the bulls; and their land shall be soaked <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">with blood</span>, and their dust made fat with fatness.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 8|8]] Day of YHWH's vengeance – For it is the day of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">LORD'S</span> vengeance, and the year of recompences for the controversy of Zion.

- A. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 9|9-10]] From generation to generation – 9 And the streams thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the dust thereof into brimstone, and the land thereof shall become burning pitch. 10 It shall not be quenched night nor day; the smoke thereof shall go up for ever: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">from generation to generation</span> it shall lie waste; none shall pass through it for ever and ever.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 11|11]] Habitation of wild beasts – But the cormorant and the bittern shall possess it; the owl also and the raven shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">dwell in it</span>: and he shall stretch out upon it the line of confusion, and the stones of emptiness.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 12|12-13]] The land empty of civilisation – 12 They shall call the nobles thereof to the kingdom, but none shall be there, and all her princes shall be nothing. 13 And thorns shall come up in her palaces, nettles and brambles in the fortresses thereof:

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 13|13-15]] Habitation of wild beasts – and it shall be an habitation of dragons, and a court for owls. 14 The wild beasts of the desert shall also meet with the wild beasts of the island, and the satyr shall cry to his fellow; the screech owl also shall rest there, and find for herself a place of rest. 15 There shall the great owl make her nest, and lay, and hatch, and gather under her shadow: there shall the vultures also be gathered, every one with <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">her mate</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 16|16-17]] From generation to generation – 16 Seek ye out of the book of the LORD, and read: no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate: for my mouth it hath commanded, and his spirit it hath gathered them. 17 And he hath cast the lot for them, and his hand hath divided it unto them by line: they shall possess it for ever, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">from generation to generation</span> shall they dwell therein.
