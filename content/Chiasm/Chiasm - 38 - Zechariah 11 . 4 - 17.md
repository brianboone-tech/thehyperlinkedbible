[[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 4|4]] Thus saith the LORD my God;

- A. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Feed the flock of the slaughter</span>;

	- B. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 5|5]] Whose possessors slay them, and hold themselves not guilty: and they that sell them say, Blessed be the LORD; for I am rich:

		- C. and their own shepherds <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">pity them not</span>.

		- C'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 6|6]] For <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I will no more pity</span> the inhabitants of the land, saith the LORD:

	- B'. but, lo, I will deliver the men every one into his neighbour's hand, and into the hand of his king: and they shall smite the land, and out of their hand I will not deliver them.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 7|7]] And <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I will feed the flock of slaughter</span>, even you, O poor of the flock.

- A. judgment against the evil shepherds — [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 8|8]] And I took unto me two staves; the one I called Beauty, and the other I called Bands; and I fed the flock. Three <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">shepherds</span> also I cut off in one month; and my soul lothed them, and their soul also abhorred me.

	- B. shepherd forsakes flock — [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 9|9]] Then said I, I will not <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">feed</span> you: that that dieth, let it die; and that that is to be cut off, let it be cut off; and let the rest <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">eat every one the flesh</span> of another.

		- C. Beauty broken — [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 10|10]] And <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I took my staff</span>, even Beauty, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">and cut it asunder</span>, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">that I might break</span> my covenant which I had made with all the people. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 11|11]] And it was broken in that day: and so the poor of the flock that waited upon me knew that it was the word of the LORD.

			- D. a. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 12|12]] And I said unto them, If ye think good, give me my <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">price</span> (saker); and if not, forbear.

				- b. So they weighed for my <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">price</span> (saker) <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">thirty pieces of silver</span>.

			- D'. a'. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 13|13]] And the LORD said unto me, Cast it unto the potter: a goodly <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">price</span> (yaqar) that I was prised (yaqar) at of them.

				- b'. And I took <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the thirty pieces of silver</span>, and cast them to the potter in the house of the LORD.

		- C'. Bands broken — [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 14|14]] Then <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I cut asunder mine other staff</span>, even Bands, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">that I might break</span> the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.

	- B'. foolish shepherd appointed over flock — [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 15|15]] And the LORD said unto me, Take unto thee yet the instruments of a foolish <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">shepherd</span>. [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 16|16]] For, lo, I will raise up a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">shepherd</span> in the land, which shall not visit those that be cut off, neither shall seek the young one, nor heal that that is broken, nor feed that that standeth still: but he shall <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">eat the flesh</span> of the fat, and tear their claws in pieces.

- A'. judgment against the evil shepherd — [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 17|17]] Woe to the idol <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">shepherd</span> that leaveth the flock! the sword shall be upon his arm, and upon his right eye: his arm shall be clean dried up, and his right eye shall be utterly darkened.
