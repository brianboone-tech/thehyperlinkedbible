- A. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|8:1-5]] Now of the things which we have spoken this is the sum: We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens; A minister of the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sanctuary</span>, and of the true <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">tabernacle</span>, which the Lord pitched, and not man. For every high priest is ordained to offer gifts and sacrifices: wherefore it is of necessity that this man have somewhat also to offer. For if he were on earth, he should not be a priest, seeing that there are priests that offer gifts according to the law: Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">to make the tabernacle</span>: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 6|8:6-7]] But now hath he obtained a more excellent ministry, by how much also he is the mediator of a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">better covenant</span>, which was established upon better promises. For if <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">that first covenant</span> had been faultless, then should no place have been sought for the second.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 8|8:8]] For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">a new covenant</span> with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah:

            - D. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 9|8:9]] Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt; because they continued not in my covenant, and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">I regarded them not</span>, saith the Lord.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 10|8:10a]] For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, saith the Lord; <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts</span>:

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 10|8:10b]] and <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">I will be to them a God, and they shall be to me a people</span>:

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 11|8:11]] And they shall not teach every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">all shall know me</span>, from the least to the greatest.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 12|8:12]] For <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">I will be merciful</span> to their unrighteousness, and their sins and their iniquities will I remember no more.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 13|8:13]] In that he saith, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">A new covenant</span>, he hath made the first old. Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 13|8:13]] he hath made <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">the first</span> old.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 1|9:1-5]] Then verily the first covenant had also ordinances of divine service, and a worldly <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">sanctuary</span>. For there was a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">tabernacle</span> made; the first, wherein was the candlestick, and the table, and the shewbread; which is called the sanctuary. And after the second veil, the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">tabernacle</span> which is called the Holiest of all; Which had the golden censer, and the ark of the covenant overlaid round about with gold, wherein was the golden pot that had manna, and Aaron's rod that budded, and the tables of the covenant; And over it the cherubims of glory shadowing the mercyseat; of which we cannot now speak particularly.
