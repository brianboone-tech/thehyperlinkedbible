And very early in the morning the first day of the week, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">they came unto the sepulchre</span> at the rising of the sun.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 4|4]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 6|6]] And when they looked, they saw that the stone was rolled away: for it was very great. And <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">entering into the sepulchre, they saw a young man</span> sitting on the right side, clothed in a long white garment; and they were <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">affrighted</span>. And he saith unto them, Be not affrighted: Ye seek Jesus of Nazareth, which was crucified: he is risen; he is not here: behold the place where they laid him.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 7|7]] But go your way, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">tell his disciples and Peter</span> that he goeth before you into Galilee: there shall ye see him, as he said unto you.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 8|8]] And they went out quickly, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fled from the sepulchre</span>; for they trembled and were amazed: neither said they any thing to any man; for they were afraid.

        - C'. And she went and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">told them that had been with him</span>, as they mourned and wept.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 12|12]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 14|14]] After that he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">appeared in another form</span> unto two of them, as they walked, and went into the country. Afterward he appeared unto the eleven as they sat at meat, and upbraided them with their <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">unbelief</span> and hardness of heart, because they believed not them which had seen him after he was risen.

    - 3B. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 16|16]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 18|18]] He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned. And these <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">signs shall follow them that believe</span>; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues; They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover.

- 3A'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 19|19]] So then after the Lord had spoken unto them, he was <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">received up into heaven</span>, and sat on the right hand of God.

    - 3B'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 20|20]] And they went forth, and preached every where, the Lord working with them, and <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">confirming the word with signs following</span>. Amen.
