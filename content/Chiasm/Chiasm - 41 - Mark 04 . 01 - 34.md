And some fell among thorns, and the thorns grew up, and choked it, and it yielded no fruit. And other fell on good ground, and did yield fruit that sprang up and increased; and brought forth, some thirty, and some sixty, and some an hundred. And he said unto them, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</span>

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 4#Mark 4 . 14|14]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 4#Mark 4 . 23|23]] The sower soweth the word. And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown; but when they have heard, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts. And these are they likewise which are sown on stony ground; who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with gladness; And have no root in themselves, and so endure but for a time: afterward, when affliction or persecution ariseth for the word's sake, immediately they are offended. And these are they which are sown among thorns; such as hear the word, And the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becometh unfruitful. And these are they which are sown on good ground; such as hear the word, and receive it, and bring forth fruit, some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some an hundred. And he said unto them, Is a candle brought to be put under a bushel, or under a bed? and not to be set on a candlestick? For there is nothing hid, which shall not be manifested; neither was any thing kept secret, but that it should come abroad. If any man <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">have ears to hear, let him hear</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 4#Mark 4 . 24|24]]-[[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 4#Mark 4 . 34|34]] And he said unto them, Take heed what ye <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">hear</span>: with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you: and unto you that hear shall more be given. For he that hath, to him shall be given: and he that hath not, from him shall be taken even that which he hath. And he said, So is the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">kingdom of God</span>, as if a man should cast seed into the ground; And should sleep, and rise night and day, and the seed should spring and grow up, he knoweth not how. For the earth bringeth forth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the ear. But when the fruit is brought forth, immediately he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come. And he said, Whereunto shall we liken the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">kingdom of God</span>? or with what comparison shall we compare it? It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown in the earth, is less than all the seeds that be in the earth: But when it is sown, it groweth up, and becometh greater than all herbs, and shooteth out great branches; so that the fowls of the air may lodge under the shadow of it. And with many such <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">parables</span> spake he the word unto them, as they were able to hear it. But without a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">parable</span> spake he not unto them: and when they were alone, he expounded all things to his disciples.
