- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 1|1-11]] My God, my God, why hast thou <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">forsaken me</span>? why art thou so <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">far</span> from helping me, and from the words of my roaring? O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent. But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel. Our fathers <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">trusted</span> in thee: they trusted, and thou didst <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">deliver</span> them. They cried unto thee, and were delivered: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded. But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people. All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying, He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him. But thou art he that took me out of the womb: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts. I was cast upon thee from the womb: thou art my God from my mother's belly. Be not <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">far from me</span>; for trouble is near; for there is none to help.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 12|12-18]] Many <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">bulls</span> have <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">compassed</span> me: strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round. They gaped upon me with their mouths, as a ravening and a roaring <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">lion</span>. I am poured out like water, and all my <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">bones</span> are out of joint: my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels. My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death. For <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">dogs</span> have <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">compassed</span> me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet. I may tell all my <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">bones</span>: they look and stare upon me. They part my <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">garments</span> among them, and cast lots upon my vesture.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 19|19-21]] But be not thou far from me, O LORD: O my strength, haste thee to help me. <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Deliver</span> my soul from the sword; my darling from the power of the dog. Save me from the lion's mouth: for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 22|22-26]] I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">praise</span> thee. Ye that fear the LORD, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">praise</span> him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel. For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard. My <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">praise</span> shall be of thee in the great congregation: I will pay my vows before them that fear him. The meek shall eat and be satisfied: they shall praise the LORD that seek him: your heart shall live for ever.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 27|27-31]] All the ends of the world shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">remember</span> and turn unto the LORD: and all the kindreds of the nations shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">worship</span> before thee. For the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">kingdom</span> is the LORD'S: and he is the governor among the nations. All they that be fat upon earth shall eat and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">worship</span>: all they that go down to the dust shall bow before him: and none can keep alive his own soul. A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation. They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this.
