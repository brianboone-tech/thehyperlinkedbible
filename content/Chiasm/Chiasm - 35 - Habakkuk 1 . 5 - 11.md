        - C. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 5|5]] Behold ye among the heathen, and regard, and wonder marvellously: for I will work a work in your days, which ye will not believe, though it be told you.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 6|6-7]] ’. For, lo, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, which shall march through the breadth of the land, to possess the dwellingplaces that are not theirs. They are terrible and dreadful: their <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">judgment</span> and their dignity shall proceed of themselves.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 9|9-11]] ’. Their horses also are swifter than the leopards, and are more fierce than the evening wolves: and their horsemen shall spread themselves, and their horsemen shall come from far; they shall fly as the eagle that hasteth to eat. They shall come all for <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">violence</span>: their faces shall sup up as the east wind, and they shall gather the captivity as the sand. And they shall scoff at the kings, and the princes shall be a scorn unto them: they shall deride every strong hold; for they shall heap dust, and take it. Then shall his mind change, and he shall pass over, and offend, imputing this his power unto his god.
