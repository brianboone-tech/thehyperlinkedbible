[[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 1|1]] A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet upon Shigionoth.

- A. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 2|2]] O LORD, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I have heard thy speech, and was afraid</span>: O LORD, revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; in wrath remember mercy.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 3|3-6]] God came from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran. Selah. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">His glory covered the heavens</span>, and the earth was full of his praise. And his brightness was as the light; he had horns coming out of his hand: and there was the hiding of his power. Before him went the pestilence, and burning coals went forth at his feet. He stood, and measured the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">earth</span>: he beheld, and drove asunder the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">nations</span>;

        - C. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 6|6b-7]] and the everlasting <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mountains</span> were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: his ways are everlasting. I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction: and the curtains of the land of Midian did tremble.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 8|8]] Was the LORD displeased against the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">rivers</span>? was thine anger against the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">rivers</span>? was thy wrath against the sea, that thou didst ride upon thine horses and thy chariots of salvation?

                - E. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 9|9a]] Thy <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">bow</span> was made quite naked, according to the oaths of the tribes, even thy word. Selah.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 9|9b]] Thou didst cleave the earth with <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">rivers</span>.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 10|10-11]] The <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mountains</span> saw thee, and they trembled: the overflowing of the water passed by: the deep uttered his voice, and lifted up his hands on high. The sun and moon stood still in their habitation: at the light of thine arrows they went, and at the shining of thy glittering spear.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 12|12-15]] Thou didst march through the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">land</span> in indignation, thou didst thresh the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">heathen</span> in anger. Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, even for salvation with thine anointed; thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, by discovering the foundation unto the neck. Selah. Thou didst strike through with his staves the head of his villages: they came out as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly. Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, through the heap of great waters.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 16|16]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">When I heard, my belly trembled</span>; my lips quivered at the voice: rottenness entered into my bones, and I trembled in myself, that I might rest in the day of trouble: when he cometh up unto the people, he will invade them with his troops.

- A. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 17|17]] Although the fig tree shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">not blossom</span>, neither shall fruit be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">in the vines</span>;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 17|17b]] the labour of the olive shall <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">fail</span>, and the fields shall <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">yield no meat</span>;

        - C. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 17|17c]] the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">flock shall be cut off</span> from the fold, and <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">there shall be no herd</span> in the stalls:

- A'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 18|18]] Yet <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I will rejoice</span> in the LORD, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">I will joy</span> in the God of my salvation.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 19|19a]] The LORD God is my <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">strength</span>,

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 19|19b]] and he will make my <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">feet like hinds' feet</span>, and he will make <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">me to walk</span> upon mine high places.
