- A. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 1|1-4]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Amnon is in love</span> with Absalom's sister, Tamar.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 5|5-9a]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Tamar comes to Amnon's house</span> and bakes bread for him.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 9|9b-10]] Amnon <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">orders his servants out</span>, that he might be alone with Tamar.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 11|11-14a]] Amnon <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">commands Tamar to come lie with him</span>; she pleads with him but to no avail.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 14|14b-15a]] Amnon <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">rapes Tamar</span>, and his love for her turns to hate.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 15|15b-16]] Amnon <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">commands Tamar to get out</span>; she pleads with him but to no avail.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 17|17]] Amnon <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">calls a servant back</span> and orders him to lock Tamar out.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 18|18-19]] <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Tamar leaves Amnon's house</span>, mourning her fate.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 13#2 Samuel 13 . 20|20-22]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Absalom hates Amnon</span> for having raped Tamar.
