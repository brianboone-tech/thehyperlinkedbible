- A. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Children that do not seek YHWH's guidance</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 1|1]] Woe to the rebellious <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">children</span>, saith the LORD, that take counsel, but not of me; and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, that they may add sin to sin: 2 That walk to go down into Egypt, and have not asked at my mouth; to strengthen themselves in the strength of Pharaoh, and to trust in the shadow of Egypt!

    - B. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Egypt no help – they are staying where they are!</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 3|3]] Therefore shall the strength of Pharaoh be your shame, and the trust in the shadow of <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Egypt</span> your confusion. 4 For his princes were at Zoan, and his ambassadors came to Hanes. 5 They were all ashamed of a people that could <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">not profit them</span>, nor be an <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">help</span> nor <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">profit</span>, but a shame, and also a reproach.

    - B'. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Egypt no help – Israel's ambassadors travel through dangerous territory to no avail</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 6|6]] The burden of the beasts of the south: into the land of trouble and anguish, from whence come the young and old lion, the viper and fiery flying serpent, they will carry their riches upon the shoulders of young asses, and their treasures upon the bunches of camels, to a people that shall <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">not profit them</span>. 7 For the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Egyptians</span> shall <span style="color: #1a5490; font-weight: bold;">help</span> in vain, and to no purpose: therefore have I cried concerning this, Their strength is to sit still.

- A'. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Children that reject YHWH's guidance through the prophets</span> - [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 8|8]] Now go, write it before them in a table, and note it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever: 9 That this is a rebellious people, lying <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">children</span>, <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">children</span> that will not hear the law of the LORD: 10 Which say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not unto us right things, speak unto us smooth things, prophesy deceits: 11 Get you out of the way, turn aside out of the path, cause the Holy One of Israel to cease from before us.
