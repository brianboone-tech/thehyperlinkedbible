- A. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 12|12-15]] And Moses sent to call <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Dathan and Abiram</span>, the sons of Eliab: which said, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">We will not come up</span>: Is it a small thing that thou hast brought us up out of a land that floweth with milk and honey, to kill us in the wilderness, except thou make thyself altogether a prince over us? Moreover thou hast not brought us into a land that floweth with milk and honey, or given us inheritance of fields and vineyards: wilt thou put out the eyes of these men? we will not come up. And Moses was very wroth, and said unto the LORD, Respect not thou their offering: I have not taken one ass from them, neither have I hurt one of them.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 16|16-18]] And Moses said unto Korah, Be thou and all thy company before the LORD, thou, and they, and Aaron, to morrow: And take every man his <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">censer</span>, and put <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">incense</span> in them, and bring ye before the LORD every man his censer, two hundred and fifty censers; thou also, and Aaron, each of you his censer. And they took every man his censer, and put fire in them, and laid incense thereon, and stood in the door of the tabernacle of the congregation with Moses and Aaron.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 19|19-21]] And Korah <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">gathered all the congregation</span> against them unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation: and the glory of the LORD appeared unto all the congregation. And the LORD spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying, Separate yourselves from among this <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">congregation</span>, that I may consume them in a moment.

            - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 22|22-27]] And they fell upon their faces, and said, O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh, shall <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">one man sin, and wilt thou be wroth with all the congregation</span>? And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, Speak unto the congregation, saying, Get you up from about the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. And Moses rose up and went unto Dathan and Abiram; and the elders of Israel followed him. And he spake unto the congregation, saying, Depart, I pray you, from the tents of these wicked men, and touch nothing of theirs, lest ye be consumed in all their sins. So they gat up from the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram, on every side: and Dathan and Abiram came out, and stood in the door of their tents, and their wives, and their sons, and their little children.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 35|35-39]] And there came out a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">fire</span> from the LORD, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">offered incense</span>. And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, Speak unto Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, that he take up the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">censers</span> out of the burning, and scatter thou the fire yonder; for they are hallowed. The censers of these sinners against their own souls, let them make them broad plates for a covering of the altar: for they offered them before the LORD, therefore they are hallowed: and they shall be a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">sign</span> unto the children of Israel. And Eleazar the priest took the brasen censers, wherewith they that were burnt had offered; and they were made broad plates for a covering of the altar:

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 16#Numbers 16 . 28|28-34]] And Moses said, Hereby ye shall know that the LORD hath sent me to do all these works; for I have not done them of mine own mind. If these men die the common death of all men, or if they be visited after the visitation of all men; then the LORD hath not sent me. But if the LORD make a new thing, and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the earth open her mouth, and swallow them up</span>, with all that appertain unto them, and they <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">go down quick into the pit</span>; then ye shall understand that these men have provoked the LORD. And it came to pass, as he had made an end of speaking all these words, that the ground clave asunder that was under them: And <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up</span>, and their houses, and all the men that appertained unto Korah, and all their goods. They, and all that appertained to them, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">went down alive into the pit</span>, and the earth closed upon them: and they <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">perished</span> from among the congregation. And all Israel that were round about them fled at the cry of them: for they said, Lest the earth swallow us up also.
