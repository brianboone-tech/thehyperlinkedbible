- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 1|1-3]] I will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">extol</span> thee, my God, O king; and I will <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bless</span> thy name for ever and ever. Every day will I bless thee; and I will praise thy name for ever and ever. Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; and his greatness is unsearchable.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 4|4-7]] One generation shall praise thy works to another, and shall <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">declare</span> thy mighty acts. I will speak of the glorious honour of thy majesty, and of thy wondrous works. And men shall <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">speak</span> of the might of thy terrible acts: and I will declare thy greatness. They shall abundantly utter the memory of thy great goodness, and shall sing of thy <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 8|8-9]] The LORD is <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">gracious</span>, and full of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">compassion</span>; slow to anger, and of great <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mercy</span>. The LORD is good to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 10|10-13]] All thy works shall praise thee, O LORD; and thy saints shall bless thee. They shall speak of the glory of thy <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">kingdom</span>, and talk of thy power; To make known to the sons of men his mighty acts, and the glorious majesty of his <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">kingdom</span>. Thy <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">kingdom</span> is an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 14|14-16]] The LORD <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">upholdeth</span> all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down. The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest them their meat in due season. Thou openest thine hand, and satisfiest the desire of every living thing.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 17|17-20]] The LORD is <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">righteous</span> in all his ways, and holy in all his works. The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth. He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him: he also will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">hear</span> their cry, and will save them. The LORD preserveth all them that love him: but all the wicked will he destroy.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 145#Psalm 145 . 21|21]] My mouth shall <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">speak</span> the praise of the LORD: and let all flesh <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">bless</span> his holy name for ever and ever.
