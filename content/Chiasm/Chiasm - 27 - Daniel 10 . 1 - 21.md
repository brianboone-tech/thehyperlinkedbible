- A. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 1|1]] Introduction: <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">king of Persia</span> and the word revealed

    In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia a thing was revealed unto Daniel, whose name was called Belteshazzar; and the thing was true, but the time appointed was long: and he understood the thing, and had understanding of the vision.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 2|2-6]] Daniel's mourning and fasting; vision of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">a certain man</span> in radiant appearance

        In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks. I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled. And in the four and twentieth day of the first month, as I was by the side of the great river, which is Hiddekel; Then I lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a certain man clothed in linen, whose loins were girded with fine gold of Uphaz: His body also was like the beryl, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his feet like in colour to polished brass, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 7|7-9]] Daniel alone witnesses vision, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">loses all strength</span>, falls into deep sleep

            And I Daniel alone saw the vision: for the men that were with me saw not the vision; but a great quaking fell upon them, so that they fled to hide themselves. Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no strength in me: for my comeliness was turned in me into corruption, and I retained no strength. Yet heard I the voice of his words: and when I heard the voice of his words, then was I in a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 9|9-10]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Face toward ground</span>; angelic hand touches Daniel

                And, behold, an hand touched me, which set me upon my knees and upon the palms of my hands.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 11|11]] Angel speaks, commanding Daniel to <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">stand upright</span>

                    And he said unto me, O Daniel, a man greatly beloved, understand the words that I speak unto thee, and stand upright: for unto thee am I now sent. And when he had spoken this word unto me, I stood trembling.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 12|12]] Angel reassures: <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">thy words were heard</span>

                        Then said he unto me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thine heart to understand, and to chasten thyself before thy God, thy words were heard, and I am come for thy words.

                        - G. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 13|13]] Center: <span style="color: #27ae60; font-weight: bold;">Prince of Persia</span> resisted angel 21 days; Michael came to help

                            But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me; and I remained there with the kings of Persia.

                    - F'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 14|14]] Angel explains purpose: to make Daniel <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">understand</span>

                        Now I am come to make thee understand what shall befall thy people in the latter days: for yet the vision is for many days.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 15|15]] Angel speaks again; Daniel responds with <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">face toward ground</span>

                    And when he had spoken such words unto me, I set my face toward the ground, and I became dumb.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 16|16]] Daniel's <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">face toward ground</span>; angelic figure touches his lips

                And, behold, one like the similitude of the sons of men touched my lips: then I opened my mouth, and spake, and said unto him that stood before me, O my lord, by the vision my sorrows are turned upon me, and I have retained no strength.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 17|17]] Daniel speaks but <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">retains no strength</span>

            For how can the servant of this my lord talk with this my lord? for as for me, straightway there remained no strength in me, neither is there breath left in me.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 18|18-19]] Figure appearing like <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">a man</span> touches and strengthens Daniel

        Then there came again and touched me one like the appearance of a man, and he strengthened me, And said, O man greatly beloved, fear not: peace be unto thee, be strong, yea, be strong. And when he had spoken unto me, I was strengthened, and said, Let my lord speak; for thou hast strengthened me.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 20|20-21]] References <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">prince of Persia</span> and scripture of truth; future conflicts

    Then said he, Knowest thou wherefore I come unto thee? and now will I return to fight with the prince of Persia: and when I am gone forth, lo, the prince of Grecia shall come. But I will shew thee that which is noted in the scripture of truth: and there is none that holdeth with me in these things, but Michael your prince.
