- A. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 11|11]] Then the LORD said to Moses, [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 12|12]] "Speak to the Israelites and tell them that if any man's <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wife goes astray</span> and is unfaithful to him [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 13|13]] by sleeping with another man, and it is concealed from her husband and her impurity is undetected (since there is no witness against her and she was not caught in the act), [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 14|14]] and if a feeling of jealousy comes over her husband and he suspects his wife who has <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">defiled herself</span>—or if a feeling of jealousy comes over him and he suspects her even though she has not defiled herself—

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 15|15]] then he is to <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">bring his wife to the priest</span>. He must also bring for her an offering of a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He is not to pour oil over it or put frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering for jealousy, an offering of memorial as a reminder of iniquity.

        - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 16|16]] The <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">priest</span> is to bring the wife forward and have her <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">stand before the LORD</span>. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 17|17]] Then he is to take some holy water in a clay jar and put some of the dust from the tabernacle floor into the water. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 18|18]] After the priest has the woman stand before the LORD, he is to let down her hair and place in her hands the grain offering of memorial, which is the grain offering for jealousy. The priest is to hold the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">bitter water</span> that brings a curse.

            - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 19|19]] And he is to put the woman under <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">oath</span> and say to her, [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 20|20]] 'If no other man has slept with you and you have not gone astray and become defiled while under your husband's authority, may you be immune to this bitter water that brings a curse. But if you have gone astray while under your husband's authority and have defiled yourself and lain carnally with a man other than your husband'—

                - a. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 21|21]] and the priest shall have the woman swear under the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">oath of the curse</span>—'then may the LORD make you an attested curse among your people by making your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">thigh shrivel</span> and your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">belly swell</span>.

                    - b. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 22|22]] May this water that brings a curse enter your stomach and cause your belly to swell and your thigh to shrivel.' Then the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">woman is to say, 'Amen, Amen.'</span>

                - a'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 23|23]] And the priest shall write <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">these curses</span> on a scroll and wash them off into the bitter water. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 24|24]] He is to have the woman drink the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">bitter water that brings a curse</span>, and it will enter her and cause her bitter suffering.

        - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 25|25]] The <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">priest</span> shall take from her hand the grain offering for jealousy, wave it <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">before the LORD</span>, and bring it to the altar. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 26|26]] Then the priest is to take a handful of the grain offering as a memorial portion and burn it on the altar; after that he is to have the woman drink the water. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 27|27]] When he has made her drink the water, if she has defiled herself and been unfaithful to her husband, then the water that brings a curse will enter her and cause bitter suffering; her <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">belly will swell</span>, her <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">thigh will shrivel</span>, and she will become accursed among her people. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 28|28]] But if the woman has not defiled herself and is clean, she will be unaffected and able to conceive children.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 29|29]] This is the law of jealousy when a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wife goes astray and defiles herself</span> while under her husband's authority, [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 30|30]] or when a feeling of jealousy comes over a husband and he suspects his wife. He is to have the woman stand before the LORD, and the priest is to apply to her this entire law. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 31|31]] The husband will be free from guilt, but the woman shall bear her <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">iniquity</span>."
