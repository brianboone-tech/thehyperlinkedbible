- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 1|1]] An oracle concerning Damascus.

- A. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 1|1b]] Behold, Damascus will cease to be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">a city</span> and will become a heap of ruins. 2 The <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">cities</span> of Aroer are <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">deserted</span>; they will be for flocks, which will lie down, and none will make them afraid. 3 The fortress will disappear from Ephraim, and the kingdom from Damascus; and the remnant of Syria will be like <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">the glory of the children of Israel</span>, declares the LORD of hosts.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 4|4]] <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">And in that day</span> the glory of Jacob will be brought low, and the fat of his flesh will grow lean. 5 And it shall be as when the reaper gathers standing grain and his arm <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">harvests</span> the ears, and as when one gleans the ears of grain in the Valley of Rephaim. 6 Gleanings will be left in it, as when an olive tree is beaten— two or three berries in the top of the highest bough, four or five on the branches of a fruit tree, declares the Lord GOD of Israel.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 7|7]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> man will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">look</span> to his Maker, and his eyes will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">look</span> on the Holy One of Israel.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 8|8]] He will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">not look</span> to <span style="color: #138d75; font-weight: bold;">the altars</span>, the work of his hands, he will <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">not look</span> on, either the Asherim or <span style="color: #138d75; font-weight: bold;">the altars</span> of incense.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 9|9]] <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">In that day</span> their strong <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">cities</span> will be like the <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">deserted</span> places of the wooded heights and the hilltops, which they <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">deserted</span> because of <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">the children of Israel</span>, and there will be desolation.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 17#Isaiah 17 . 10|10]] For you have forgotten the God of your salvation and have not remembered the Rock of your refuge; therefore, though you plant pleasant plants and sow the vine-branch of a stranger, 11 though you make them grow on the day that you plant them, and make them blossom in the morning that you sow, yet the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">harvest</span> will flee away <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">in a day</span> of grief and incurable pain.
