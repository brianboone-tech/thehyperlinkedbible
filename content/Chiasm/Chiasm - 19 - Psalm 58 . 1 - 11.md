- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 58#Psalm 58 . 1|1-2]] Do ye indeed speak <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">righteousness</span>, O congregation? do ye <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">judge</span> uprightly, O ye sons of men?     - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 58#Psalm 58 . 3|3-5]] The wicked are estranged from the womb: they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies. Their <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">poison</span> is like the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">poison</span> of a <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">serpent</span>: they are like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear; Which will not hearken to the voice of charmers, charming never so wisely.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 58#Psalm 58 . 6|6-9]] Break their <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">teeth</span>, O God, in their mouth: break out the great <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">teeth</span> of the <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">young lions</span>, O LORD. Let them melt away as waters which run continually: when he bendeth his bow to shoot his arrows, let them be as cut in pieces. As a snail which melteth, let every one of them pass away: like the untimely birth of a woman, that they may not see the sun. Before your pots can feel the thorns, he shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living, and in his wrath.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 58#Psalm 58 . 10|10-11]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The righteous</span> shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked. So that a man shall say, Verily there is a reward for the righteous: verily he is a God that <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">judgeth</span> in the earth.
