- A. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 11|11]] Then the LORD said to me, "<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Do not pray</span> for the well-being of this people.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 12|12]] Although they may fast, I will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">not listen</span> to their cry; although they may offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">not accept them</span>. Instead, I will finish them off by <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">sword and famine and plague</span>."

        - C. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 13|13]] "Ah, Lord GOD!" I replied, "Look, the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">prophets</span> are telling them, 'You will not see the sword or suffer famine, but I will give you lasting peace in this place.'"

            - D. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 14|14]] "The <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">prophets are prophesying lies</span> in My name," replied the LORD. "<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">I did not send them</span> or appoint them or speak to them. They are prophesying to you a false vision, a worthless divination, the futility and delusion of their own minds.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 15|15]] Therefore this is what the LORD says about the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">prophets</span> who prophesy in My name: <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">I did not send them</span>, yet they say, 'No sword or famine will touch this land.' By <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">sword and famine</span> these very prophets will meet their end!

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 16|16]] And the people to whom they prophesy will be thrown into the streets of Jerusalem because of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">famine and sword</span>. There will be <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">no one to bury them</span> or their wives, their sons or their daughters. I will pour out their own evil upon them.
