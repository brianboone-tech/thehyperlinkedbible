- A. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 28|28-29]] These are the tribes of Israel, twelve in all, and this was what their father said to them. He blessed them, and he blessed each one with a suitable blessing. Then Jacob instructed them, "I am about to be gathered to my people. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Bury me with my fathers in the cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 30|30-32]] The cave is in the field of <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">Machpelah</span> near Mamre, in the land of Canaan. This is the field Abraham purchased from Ephron the Hittite as a burial site. There Abraham and his wife Sarah are buried, there Isaac and his wife Rebekah are buried, and there I buried Leah. The field and the cave that is in it were purchased from the Hittites."

        - C. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 33|33]] - [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 3|50:3]] When Jacob had finished instructing his sons, he pulled his feet into the bed and breathed his last, and he was gathered to his people. Then Joseph fell upon his father's face, wept over him, and kissed him. And Joseph directed the physicians in his service to embalm his father Israel. So they embalmed him, taking the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">forty days</span> required to complete the embalming. And the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Egyptians mourned</span> for him <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">seventy days</span>.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 4|4-5]] When the days of mourning had passed, Joseph said to Pharaoh's court, "If I have found favor in your eyes, please tell Pharaoh that my father <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">made me swear an oath</span> when he said, 'I am about to die. You must bury me in the tomb that I dug for myself in the land of Canaan.' Now let me go and bury my father, and then return."

                - E. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 6|6]] Pharaoh replied, "<span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Go up and bury your father</span>, as he made you swear to do."

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 7|7-9]] Then Joseph went to bury his father, and all the servants of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Pharaoh</span> accompanied him—the elders of <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Pharaoh's household</span> and all the elders of the land of Egypt—along with all of Joseph's household, and his brothers, and his father's household. Only their children and flocks and herds were left in Goshen. Chariots and horsemen alike went up with him, and it was an exceedingly large procession.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 10|10-11]] When they reached the threshing floor of Atad, which is across the Jordan, they lamented and wailed loudly, and Joseph <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mourned</span> for his father <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">seven days</span>. When the Canaanites of the land saw the mourning at the threshing floor of Atad, they said, "This is a solemn ceremony of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">mourning by the Egyptians</span>." Thus the place across the Jordan is called Abel-mizraim.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 12|12-13]] So Jacob's sons did as he had charged them. They carried him to the land of Canaan and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">buried him in the cave at Machpelah</span> in the field near Mamre, which Abraham had purchased from Ephron the Hittite as a burial site.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 14|14]] After Joseph had <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">buried his father</span>, he returned to Egypt with his brothers and all who had gone with him to bury his father.
