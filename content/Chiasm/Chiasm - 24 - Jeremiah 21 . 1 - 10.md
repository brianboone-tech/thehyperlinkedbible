[[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 21#Jeremiah 21 . 5|5]] And I Myself will <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">fight against you</span> with an outstretched hand and a mighty arm, with anger, fury, and great wrath. [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 21#Jeremiah 21 . 6|6]] I will strike down the residents of this city, both man and beast. They will die in a terrible plague.'

    - B'. He will put them to the sword; he will not spare them or show pity or compassion.'
