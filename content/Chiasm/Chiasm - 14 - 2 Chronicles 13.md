- A. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 1|1]] Now in the eighteenth year of king Jeroboam began <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Abijah to reign over Judah</span>. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 2|2]] He reigned <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">three years in Jerusalem</span>. His mother's name also was Michaiah the daughter of Uriel of Gibeah. And there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 3|3]] And Abijah set the battle in array with an army of valiant men of war, even <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">four hundred thousand</span> chosen men: Jeroboam also set the battle in array against him with <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">eight hundred thousand</span> chosen men, being mighty men of valour.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 4|4]] And Abijah stood up upon mount Zemaraim, which is in mount Ephraim, and said, Hear me, thou Jeroboam, and all Israel; [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 5|5]] Ought ye not to know that the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">LORD God of Israel gave the kingdom over Israel to David</span> for ever, even to him and to his sons by a <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">covenant of salt</span>? [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 6|6]] Yet Jeroboam the son of Nebat, the servant of Solomon the son of David, is risen up, and hath rebelled against his lord. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 7|7]] And there are gathered unto him vain men, the children of Belial, and have strengthened themselves against Rehoboam the son of Solomon, when Rehoboam was young and tenderhearted, and could not withstand them.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 8|8]] And now ye think to withstand the kingdom of the LORD in the hand of the sons of David; and ye be a great multitude, and there are with you <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">golden calves</span>, which <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Jeroboam made you for gods</span>. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 9|9]] Have ye not <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">cast out the priests of the LORD, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites</span>, and have made you priests after the manner of the nations of other lands? so that whosoever cometh to consecrate himself with a young bullock and seven rams, the same may be a priest of them that are no gods.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 10|10]] But as for us, the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">LORD is our God</span>, and we have not forsaken him; and the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">priests, which minister unto the LORD, are the sons of Aaron, and the Levites</span> wait upon their business: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 11|11]] And they burn unto the LORD every morning and every evening burnt sacrifices and sweet incense: the shewbread also set they in order upon the pure table; and the candlestick of gold with the lamps thereof, to burn every evening: for we keep the charge of the LORD our God; but ye have forsaken him.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 12|12]] And, behold, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">God himself is with us for our captain</span>, and his priests with sounding trumpets to cry alarm against you. O children of Israel, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">fight ye not against the LORD God of your fathers; for ye shall not prosper</span>.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 13|13]] But Jeroboam caused an ambushment to come about behind them: so they were before Judah, and the ambushment was behind them. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 14|14]] And when Judah looked back, behold, the battle was before and behind: and they cried unto the LORD, and the priests sounded with the trumpets. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 15|15]] Then the men of Judah gave a shout: and as the men of Judah shouted, it came to pass, that God smote Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 16|16]] And the children of Israel fled before Judah: and God delivered them into their hand. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 17|17]] And Abijah and his people slew them with a great slaughter: so there fell down slain of Israel <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">five hundred thousand</span> chosen men. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 18|18]] Thus the children of Israel were brought under at that time, and the children of Judah prevailed, because they relied upon the LORD God of their fathers. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 19|19]] And Abijah pursued after Jeroboam, and took cities from him, Bethel with the towns thereof, and Jeshanah with the towns thereof, and Ephrain with the towns thereof. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 20|20]] Neither did Jeroboam recover strength again in the days of Abijah: and the LORD struck him, and he died.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 21|21]] But <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Abijah waxed mighty</span>, and married fourteen wives, and begat <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">twenty and two sons, and sixteen daughters</span>. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 22|22]] And the rest of the acts of Abijah, and his ways, and his sayings, are written in the story of the prophet Iddo.
