- A. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 1|1-3]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Daniel is preferred in the kingdom</span>. It pleased Darius to set over the kingdom an hundred and twenty princes, which should be over the whole kingdom; And over these three presidents; of whom <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Daniel was first</span>: that the princes might give accounts unto them, and the king should have no damage. Then this <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Daniel was preferred</span> above the presidents and princes, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king thought to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">set him over the whole realm</span>.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 4|4-9]] The jealous presidents persuade the King to sign <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">the decree</span> that everyone should pray to him. Then the presidents and princes sought to find occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom; but they could find none occasion nor fault; forasmuch as he was faithful, neither was there any error or fault found in him. Then said these men, We shall not find any occasion against this Daniel, except we find it against him concerning the law of his God. Then these presidents and princes assembled together to the king, and said thus unto him, King Darius, live for ever. All the presidents of the kingdom, the governors, and the princes, the counsellors, and the captains, have consulted together to establish a royal statute, and to make a firm decree, that whosoever shall ask a petition of any God or man for thirty days, save of thee, O king, he shall be cast into the den of lions. Now, O king, <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">establish the decree</span>, and sign the writing, that it be not changed, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which altereth not. Wherefore king Darius signed the writing and <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">the decree</span>.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 10|10-13]] Daniel prays to God and is <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">accused</span> for it. Now when Daniel knew that the writing was signed, he went into his house; and his windows being open in his chamber toward Jerusalem, he kneeled upon his knees three times a day, and prayed, and gave thanks before his God, as he did aforetime. Then these men assembled, and found Daniel praying and making supplication before his God. Then they came near, and spake before the king concerning the king's decree; Hast thou not signed a decree, that every man that shall ask a petition of any God or man within thirty days, save of thee, O king, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">shall be cast into the den of lions</span>? The king answered and said, The thing is true, according to the law of the Medes and Persians, which altereth not. Then answered they and said before the king, That Daniel, which is of the children of the captivity of Judah, regardeth not thee, O king, nor the decree that thou hast signed, but maketh his petition three times a day.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 14|14]] The King attempts to deliver Daniel but fails. Then the king, when he heard these words, was sore <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">displeased</span> with himself, and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">set his heart</span> on Daniel to deliver him: and he <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">laboured till the going down of the sun</span> to deliver him.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 15|15-16a]] Law enacted - Daniel is cast into <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the lion's den</span>. Then these men assembled unto the king, and said unto the king, Know, O king, that the law of the Medes and Persians is, That no decree nor statute which <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the king establisheth</span> may be changed. Then the king commanded, and they brought Daniel, <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">and cast him into the den of lions</span>.

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 16|16b]] King says God will deliver Daniel. Now the king spake and said unto Daniel, <span style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">Thy God whom thou servest continually, he will deliver thee</span>.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 17|17]] Stone sealed on <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the mouth of the den</span>. And a stone was brought, <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">and laid upon the mouth of the den</span>; and <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">the king sealed it</span> with his own signet, and with the signet of his lords; that the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 18|18-22]] Has God delivered Daniel - Yes. Then the king went to his palace, and passed the night fasting: neither were instruments of musick brought before him: and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">his sleep went from him</span>. Then the king <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">arose very early</span> in the morning, and <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">went in haste</span> unto the den of lions. And when he came to the den, he <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">cried with a lamentable voice</span> unto Daniel: and the king spake and said to Daniel, O Daniel, servant of the living God, is thy God, whom thou servest continually, able <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">to deliver thee</span> from the lions? Then said Daniel unto the king, O king, live for ever. My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lions' mouths, that they have not hurt me: forasmuch as before him innocency was found in me; and also before thee, O king, have I done no hurt.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 23|23-24]] Daniel restored and the <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">accusers executed</span>. Then was the king exceeding <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">glad</span> for him, and commanded that they should take Daniel up out of the den. So Daniel was taken up out of <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">the den</span>, and no manner of hurt was found upon him, because he believed in his God. And the king commanded, and they brought those men which had <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">accused Daniel</span>, <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">and they cast them into the den of lions</span>, them, their children, and their wives; and the lions had the mastery of them, and brake all their bones in pieces or ever they came at the bottom of the den.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 25|25-27]] The King's <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">decree</span> to praise honour Daniel's God. Then king Darius wrote unto all people, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth; Peace be multiplied unto you. <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">I make a decree</span>, That in every dominion of my kingdom men tremble and fear before the God of Daniel: for he is the living God, and stedfast for ever, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed, and his dominion shall be even unto the end. He delivereth and rescueth, and he worketh signs and wonders in heaven and in earth, who hath delivered Daniel from the power of the lions.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 6#Daniel 6 . 28|28]] <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Daniel prospers</span>. So this <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Daniel prospered</span> in the reign of Darius, and in the reign of Cyrus the Persian.
