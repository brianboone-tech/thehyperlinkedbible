For we that are in this tabernacle do groan, being burdened: not for that we would be unclothed, but clothed upon, <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">that mortality might be swallowed up of life</span>.

For whether we be beside ourselves, <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">it is to God</span>: or whether we be sober, it is for your cause.
