- A. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 1|1]] How the Lord has covered the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Daughter of Zion</span> with the cloud of His anger! He has cast the glory of Israel from heaven to earth. He has abandoned His footstool in the day of His anger.

    - B. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 1|1b]] Cast down <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">beauty</span>, forgot footstool.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 2|2]] Without pity the Lord has <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">swallowed up</span> all the dwellings of Jacob. In His wrath He has demolished the fortified cities of the Daughter of Judah. He brought to the ground and defiled her kingdom and its princes.

            - D. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 3|3]] In fierce anger He has cut off every horn of Israel and withdrawn His right hand at the approach of the enemy. He has burned in Jacob like a <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">flaming fire</span> that consumes everything around it.

                - E. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 4|4a]] He has bent His <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">bow</span> like an enemy; His right hand is positioned. Like a foe He has killed all who were pleasing to the eye.

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 4|4b]] He has poured out His wrath like <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">fire</span> on the tent of the Daughter of Zion.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 5|5]] The Lord is like an enemy; He has <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">swallowed up</span> Israel. He has swallowed up all her palaces and destroyed her strongholds. He has multiplied mourning and lamentation for the Daughter of Judah.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 6|6-7]] He has laid waste His tabernacle like a garden booth; He has <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">destroyed</span> His place of meeting. The LORD has made Zion forget her appointed feasts and Sabbaths. In His fierce anger He has despised both king and priest. The Lord has rejected His altar; He has abandoned His sanctuary; He has delivered the walls of her palaces into the hand of the enemy. They have raised a shout in the house of the LORD as on the day of an appointed feast.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 8|8-9a]] The LORD determined to destroy the wall of the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">Daughter of Zion</span>. He stretched out a measuring line and did not withdraw His hand from destroying. He made the ramparts and walls lament; together they waste away. Her gates have sunk into the ground; He has destroyed and shattered their bars.

        - F. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 9|9b]] Her king and her <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">princes</span> are exiled among the nations.

            - G. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 9|9c]] the <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">law</span> is no more.

        - F'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 9|9d]] and even her <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">prophets</span> find no vision from the LORD.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 10|10]] The elders of the Daughter of Zion sit on the ground in silence. They have thrown dust on their heads and put on sackcloth. The young women of Jerusalem have bowed their heads to the ground.

    - H. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 11|11-12]] My eyes fail from <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">weeping</span>; I am churning within. My heart is poured out in grief over the destruction of the daughter of my people, because children and infants faint in the streets of the city. They cry out to their mothers: "Where is the grain and wine?" as they faint like the wounded in the streets of the city, as their lives fade away in the arms of their mothers.

        - I. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 13|13]] What can I say for you? To what can I compare you, O <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Daughter of Jerusalem</span>? To what can I liken you, that I may console you, O Virgin Daughter of Zion? For your wound is as deep as the sea. Who can ever heal you?

            - J. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 14|14]] The visions of your <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">prophets</span> were empty and deceptive; they did not expose your guilt to ward off your captivity. The burdens they envisioned for you were empty and misleading.

                - K. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 15|15]] All who pass by clap their hands at you in scorn. They hiss and shake their heads at the Daughter of Jerusalem: "Is this the city that was called the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">perfection of beauty</span>, the joy of all the earth?"

                - K'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 16|16]] All your <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">enemies</span> open their mouths against you. They hiss and gnash their teeth, saying, "We have swallowed her up. This is the day for which we have waited. We have lived to see it!"

            - J'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 17|17]] The LORD has done what He planned; He has accomplished His <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">decree</span>, which He ordained in days of old; He has overthrown you without pity. He has let the enemy gloat over you and exalted the horn of your foes.

        - I'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 18|18]] The hearts of the people cry out to the Lord. O wall of the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Daughter of Zion</span>, let your tears run down like a river day and night. Give yourself no relief, and your eyes no rest.

    - H'. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 19|19-20]] Arise, cry out in the night from the first watch of the night. Pour out your heart like water in the presence of the Lord. Lift up your hands to Him for the lives of your <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">children</span> who are fainting from hunger on the corner of every street. Look, O LORD, and consider: Whom have You ever treated like this? Should women eat their offspring, the infants they have nurtured? Should priests and prophets be killed in the sanctuary of the Lord?

- A''. [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 2#Lamentations 2 . 21|21-22]] Both young and old lie together in the dust of the streets. My young men and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">maidens</span> have fallen by the sword. You have slain them in the day of Your anger; You have slaughtered them without compassion. You summoned my attackers on every side, as for the day of an appointed feast. In the day of the LORD's anger no one escaped or survived; my enemy has destroyed those I nurtured and reared.
