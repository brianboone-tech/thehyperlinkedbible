- A. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 1|1-3]] When Jehoshaphat king of Judah had <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">returned safely</span> to his home in Jerusalem, Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to confront him and said to King Jehoshaphat, "Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wrath of the LORD</span> is upon you. However, some good is found in you, for you have removed the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart on <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">seeking God</span>."

    - B. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 4|4]] Jehoshaphat <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">lived in Jerusalem</span>, and once again he <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">went out</span> among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim and <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">turned them back to the LORD</span>, the God of their fathers.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 5|5-6a]] He appointed <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">judges</span> in the land, in each of the fortified cities of Judah. Then he said to the judges, "<span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Consider carefully what you do</span>,

            - D. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 6|6b]] for you are not judging for man, but <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">for the LORD</span>,

                - E. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 6|6c]] <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">who is with you when you render judgment</span>.

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 7|7a]] And now, may the <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">fear of the LORD</span> be upon you.

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 7|7b]] <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Be careful what you do</span>, for with the LORD our God there is no injustice or partiality or bribery."

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 8|8]] Moreover, Jehoshaphat appointed <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">in Jerusalem</span> some of the Levites, priests, and heads of the Israelite families to judge on behalf of the LORD and to settle disputes. And they <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">lived in Jerusalem</span>.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 9|9-11]] He commanded them, saying, "You must serve faithfully and wholeheartedly in the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">fear of the LORD</span>. For every dispute that comes before you from your brothers who dwell in their cities—whether it regards bloodshed or some other violation of law, commandments, statutes, or ordinances—you are to warn them, so that they will not incur guilt before the LORD and <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wrath will not come</span> upon you and your brothers. Do this, and you will not incur guilt. Note that Amariah, the chief priest, will be over you in all that pertains to the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, in all that pertains to the king. And the Levites will serve as officers before you. Act resolutely; may the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">LORD be with the upright</span>!"
