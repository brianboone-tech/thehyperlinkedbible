[[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 6|6]] And among the cities which ye shall give unto the Levites there shall be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">six cities for refuge</span>, which ye shall appoint for the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">manslayer</span>, that he may flee thither:

    - B. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 6|6-7]] and to them ye shall add forty and two cities. So all the cities which ye shall give to the Levites shall be <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">forty and eight cities</span>: them shall ye give with their suburbs.

        - C. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 8|8]] And the cities which ye shall give shall be <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">of the possession of the children of Israel</span>:

            - D. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 8|8]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">from them that have many ye shall give many; but from them that have few ye shall give few</span>:

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 8|8]] every one shall give of his cities unto the Levites <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">according to his inheritance</span> which he inheriteth.

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 9|9-12]] And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye be come over Jordan into the land of Canaan; Then ye shall appoint you <span style="color: #2980b9; font-weight: bold;">cities to be cities of refuge</span> for you; that the slayer may flee thither, which killeth any person at unawares. And they shall be unto you cities for refuge from the avenger; that the manslayer die not, until he stand before the congregation in judgment.

- A'. [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 13|13-15]] And of these cities which ye shall give <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">six cities shall ye have for refuge</span>. Ye shall give three cities on this side Jordan, and three cities shall ye give in the land of Canaan, which shall be cities of refuge. These six cities shall be a refuge, both for the children of Israel, and for the stranger, and for the sojourner among them: that every one that killeth any person unawares may flee thither.
