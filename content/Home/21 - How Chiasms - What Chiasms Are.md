## What Chiasms Are and Why Authors Use Them

### Definition

A **chiasm** is an extension of parallelism to units of text of varying length. Mary Douglas describes it this way:

"Ring composition is found all over the world... It is a construction of parallelisms that must open a theme, develop it, and round it off by bringing the conclusion back to the beginning."

The structure takes its name from the Greek letter chi (X), reflecting how the pattern creates a crossing or mirroring effect:

![[Resources/Chiastic-Structure-Diagram.jpg]]

### Five Functions of Chiastic Structure

Hamilton identifies five key functions that chiasms serve in biblical literature, arranged (naturally) in chiastic order:

#### 1. **An Extension of Parallelism** (A)

Chiasms build on the well-known parallelism of Hebrew poetry, extending it from individual poetic lines to entire narratives. What works in a single verse can work across chapters or even entire books.

**Example from Psalm 2:10:**

- A. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 10|10]] Therefore be <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">wise</span>, O kings;

    - B. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 10|10]] be <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">admonished</span>,

- A'. [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 10|10]] O <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">judges</span> of the earth.

#### 2. **Providing Structure and Boundaries** (B)

Like an acrostic that goes through the alphabet to create an A-to-Z completeness, chiastic structures "cover a range of topics in a way that satisfies their audience as the presentation comes full circle." The form itself communicates comprehensiveness and closure.

Hamilton notes: "The form causes more meaning to be generated than could be accounted for by the sum of its parts."

#### 3. **An Aid to Memory** (C - CENTER)

At the heart of chiasm's purpose is **memorability**. In oral cultures, chiastic structures functioned like a "memory palace"—an architectural superstructure that enabled listeners to recall where topics stood in relation to one another.

As Victor Wilson explains:

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The chiasm is self-tutoring, prodding the memory to fill in the elements of the form with balanced pairs..."</span>

This is why chiasms appear universally across cultures and epochs: they respond to something fundamental in how the human brain processes and retains information.

#### 4. **Creating Synergy** (B')

The corresponding units in a chiastic structure generate meaning through their relationship. By placing topics across from each other, authors invite readers to compare, contrast, and synthesize.

**Key insight:** "Chiasms often place their most important points at the center of the chiastic structure." The reader is brought up the mountain to its peak, from which the discussion descends in corresponding steps on the other side.

**Example from [[Chiasm/Chiasm - 01 - Genesis 3 . 1 - 24|Genesis 3:1-24]] (The Fall narrative):**

- A. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 1|1]] Serpent questions woman about eating from <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">garden trees</span>

    - B. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 2|2-3]] Woman explains God's command; threat of <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">death</span> emphasized

        - C. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 4|4-5]] Serpent denies death; promises <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">knowledge of good and evil</span>

            - D. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 6|6-7]] Woman and husband eat; <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">they sewed fig leaves together</span>

                - E. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 8|8]] Man and wife <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">hide from God</span> in fear

                    - F. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 9|9-12]] God confronts <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">the man</span> about eating forbidden fruit

                        - G. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 13|13]] God confronts <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">the woman</span> about her actions

                            - H. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 14|14-15]] God sentences <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">serpent</span>; protevangelium announced

                        - G'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 16|16]] God sentences <span style="color: #7d3c98; font-weight: bold;">the woman</span>

                    - F'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|17-19]] God sentences <span style="color: #a93226; font-weight: bold;">the man</span> regarding the tree

                - E'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 20|20]] Man names wife "Eve," <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">trusting God's promise</span>

            - D'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 21|21]] <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">God made coats of skins, and clothed them</span>

        - C'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|22]] Man becomes "as one of us, to <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">know good and evil</span>"

    - B'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|22]] Threat of endless life; "lest he...<span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">live forever</span>"

- A'. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 23|23-24]] Expulsion from garden; access to <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">tree of life</span> denied

Understanding where an author has placed something in the chiastic structure tells you how important that element is to the overall argument.

#### 5. **A Vehicle for Artistic Beauty** (A')

Chiastic structures create "balance, proportion, harmony, symmetry, and in the hands of an artist, radiance."

Hamilton writes:

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Many times has it been my joy to engage in close study of the text of Scripture, feel that I am picking up the clues the biblical author has laid down... and when I have arrived at what he seems to have done, I have sat back in wonder at the beauty of the literary artistry."</span>

But it's more than aesthetics: <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The form functions to communicate meaning, and if we do not see the form we will not feel the force of the truth the author presents."</span>
