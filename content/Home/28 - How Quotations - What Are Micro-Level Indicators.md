## What Are Micro-Level Indicators?

**Micro-level indicators** are specific textual markers that biblical authors use to connect their writing to earlier Scripture. These include:

1. **Direct quotations** of earlier biblical texts
2. **Reuse of key terms** or rare vocabulary
3. **Repetition of event sequences** (typological patterns)
4. **Similarities in covenantal significance** (salvation-historical parallels)

These indicators reveal **authorial intent** because biblical writers deliberately employed them to signal connections, establish patterns, and demonstrate how God's promises shape history.
