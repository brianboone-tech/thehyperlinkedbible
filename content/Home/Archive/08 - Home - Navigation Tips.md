## Navigation Tips

### In Readable Bible

Each verse ends with **inline links** on the right:

![[Resources/Inline-Links-Example.png]]

- Click <span style="color: #3b82f6; font-weight: bold;">9</span> (verse number) → Hebrew/Greek interlinear in Reference Bible
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">IP</span> → Intertextuality Pair showing how this verse is quoted/echoed
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">C</span> → Chiasm containing this verse
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">TOSK</span> → Treasury of Scripture Knowledge cross-references
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">TT</span> → Trajectory Table tracing typological development
