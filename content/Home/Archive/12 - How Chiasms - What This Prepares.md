### <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">What This Prepares</span>

<span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">The fruitfulness claim at the center raises a question:</span>

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">WHO has the power to make the gospel bear fruit globally and fulfill the creation mandate?</span>

<span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">Paul's answer (vv15-20):</span>
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Christ is "the image of the invisible God"</span> (v15) - The true Image-Bearer
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">"The firstborn over all creation"</span> (v15) - Agent of original creation
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">"All things were created through Him and for Him"</span> (v16) - The Creator gave the original mandate
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">"The firstborn from the dead"</span> (v18) - Agent of new creation/resurrection
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">"In Him all things hold together"</span> (v17) - Sustainer of creation
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">"Through Him to reconcile to Himself all things"</span> (v20) - Completer of creation's purpose

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">The chiastic center prepares the reader to ask the right question, so Paul can reveal the answer: Jesus is the Creator-Redeemer who accomplishes what Adam failed to do.</span>
