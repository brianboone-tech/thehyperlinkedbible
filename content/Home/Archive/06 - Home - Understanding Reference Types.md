## Understanding Reference Types

### **IP - Intertextuality Pairs**

**What it is**: How OT or NT passages quote, allude to, or echo other passages

**Two categories**:
- **OT to OT**: How later OT writers interpreted earlier OT texts
- **NT to OT**: How NT writers understood OT passages to point to Christ

**Example**: [[Intertextuality Pairs/OT to OT/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3.14 to Isaiah 65.25|Genesis 3:14 → Isaiah 65:25]]
- Genesis 3:14 curses the serpent to eat dust
- Isaiah 65:25 in the new creation vision retains only this curse: "dust shall be the serpent's food"
- Shows prophetic understanding that the serpent-crushing promise would be fulfilled while creation's other curses are removed

**Why it matters**: Shows **progressive revelation**—how God unfolded His plan through later biblical authors interpreting earlier texts

### **C - Chiasms**

**What it is**: Literary structures (A-B-C-B'-A') revealing authorial emphasis

**Example from [[Chiasm/Chiasm - 01 - Genesis 3 . 1 - 24|Genesis 3:1-24]]:**

The Fall narrative has an 8-level chiastic structure (A through H and back). The center is Genesis 3:14-15:

- A. Serpent questions about garden trees → A'. Expulsion from garden
- B. Threat of death → B'. Lest they live forever
- C. Serpent's lie about knowing good/evil → C'. Man now knows good/evil
- D. Fig leaf coverings → D'. God's coats of skins
- E. Hiding from God → E'. Adam names Eve (faith in promise)
- F. God confronts Adam → F'. God sentences Adam
- G. God confronts woman → G'. God sentences woman
- **H. CENTER: God sentences serpent + protoevangelium**

**Why the center matters**:
- The **center is normally the main point** (God's judgment on the serpent and promise of redemption)
- **Corresponding pairs** create meaning:
  - D/D': Human solution (fig leaves) vs. Divine solution (animal skins—first death, first covering)
  - E/E': Fear and hiding vs. Faith (Adam names his wife "Eve"—mother of all living—trusting God's promise despite death sentence)
- Shows **authorial intent**—Moses structured this to emphasize redemption at the heart of judgment

**Color coding**: Each pair uses matching colors (A/A' = red, B/B' = blue, C/C' = teal, etc.) to visually connect parallel elements

### **TOSK - Treasury of Scripture Knowledge**

**What it is**: R.A. Torrey's comprehensive 1897 cross-reference database with 500,000+ connections

**How it's organized**: References grouped by keyword/phrase within each verse

**Example from Genesis 3:15:**
- **enmity**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 6|Numbers 21:6-7]], [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 9#Amos 9 . 3|Amos 9:3]], [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 16#Mark 16 . 18|Mark 16:18]], [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 10#Luke 10 . 19|Luke 10:19]]
- **thy seed**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 7|Matthew 3:7]], [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 34|Matthew 12:34]], [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 8#John 8 . 44|John 8:44]], [[Readable Bible/62 - 1 John/1 John 3#1 John 3 . 8|1 John 3:8]]
- **her seed**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132:11]], [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]], [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 3|Micah 5:3]], [[Readable Bible/48 - Galatians/Galatians 4#Galatians 4 . 4|Galatians 4:4]]
- **it shall bruise**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 16#Romans 16 . 20|Romans 16:20]], [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 2#Colossians 2 . 15|Colossians 2:15]], [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 2#Hebrews 2 . 14|Hebrews 2:14-15]]

**Why it matters**: Provides the most comprehensive cross-reference network available, showing thematic connections across all of Scripture

### **TT - Trajectory Tables**

**What it is**: Typological development tracing OT types (shadows) through progressive revelation to NT fulfillment (substance)

**Structure**: Each trajectory table includes:
- **Trajectory Summary**: Overview of the typological development
- **OT to OT sections**: How later OT texts developed the theme
- **NT to OT sections**: How NT identifies Christ as fulfillment
- **Foundation Texts**: Deep exegesis with Hebrew/Greek analysis

**Example**: [[Trajectory Tables/005 - Adam (The First and Last Adam)|Adam (The First and Last Adam)]]

This trajectory traces:
1. **Original creation**: Adam as image-bearer with dominion (Genesis 1:26-28)
2. **The Fall**: Adam's disobedience brings death to all (Genesis 3)
3. **OT development**: Psalm 8 meditates on Adam's dignity and calling
4. **Prophetic anticipation**: Isaiah's new creation vision (Isaiah 65:17)
5. **NT fulfillment**: Christ as "last Adam" (1 Corinthians 15:45)
6. **NT superiority**: Where Adam brought death, Christ brings life (Romans 5:12-21)
7. **Eschatological consummation**: New creation in Revelation

**Why it matters**: Shows how Scripture is one unified Story—types in the OT find their fulfillment in Christ, demonstrating God's progressive revelation of His redemptive plan
