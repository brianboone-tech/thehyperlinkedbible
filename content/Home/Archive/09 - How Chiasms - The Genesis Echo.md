### <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The Genesis 1:28 Echo at the Center</span>

<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Paul's words in Greek:</span>
- <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">καρποφορούμενον</span> (karpophoroumenon) = <span style="color: #16a085;">"bearing fruit"</span>
- <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">αὐξανόμενον</span> (auxanomenon) = <span style="color: #16a085;">"increasing"</span>

<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Genesis 1:28 in the LXX (Greek translation Paul's readers knew):</span>
- <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">αὐξάνεσθε</span> (auxanesthe) = <span style="color: #16a085;">"increase!"</span>
- <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">πληθύνεσθε</span> (plēthynesthe) = <span style="color: #16a085;">"multiply!"</span>

<span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">The verbal link</span>: Paul uses the exact word <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">αὐξάνω</span> (auxanō, <span style="color: #16a085;">"increase/grow"</span>) that appears in Genesis 1:28 LXX.

<span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Plus</span>: Paul pairs it with <span style="color: #16a085;">"bearing fruit,"</span> creating a <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">dual-mandate echo</span> of Genesis 1:28's <span style="color: #16a085;">"be fruitful and multiply."</span>

<span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Paul's claim</span>: The gospel is accomplishing the <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">creation mandate</span> that God gave to Adam!
