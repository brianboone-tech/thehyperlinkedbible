## How Biblical Authors Use Micro-Level Indicators

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **God makes promises** that shape expectations (e.g., Genesis 3:15)
2. **Earlier events unfold** according to God's sovereign plan (e.g., Cain murders Abel)
3. **Biblical authors discern patterns** shaped by those promises (e.g., Cain = seed of serpent)
4. **Authors signal the patterns** using quotations, key terms, repeated sequences, and covenantal parallels
5. **Later authors recognize the patterns** and continue them (e.g., Canaan cursed; exodus repeated; prophets promise "new exodus")
6. **Patterns escalate** toward ultimate fulfillment in Christ

### The Result

**Biblical authors intended to communicate these patterns.** They were not:
- Imposing meanings on texts
- Engaging in creative allegory
- Reading back later meanings into earlier texts

**They were recognizing God's deliberate design in history**, preserved and highlighted through the inspiration of the Holy Spirit.
