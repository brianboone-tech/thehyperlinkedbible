## The Impress of the Type

The Greek word **τύπος** (typos, "type") literally means **"a mark made as the result of a blow or pressure"**—like a stamp pressed into wax.

**How this applies**:
- God's **promises** are pressed into the consciousness of those who believe them
- That impress shapes how they **interpret reality**
- Biblical authors discern **promise-shaped patterns** in history
- They record these patterns intentionally, signaling them through quotations, key terms, repeated sequences, and covenantal parallels

This is not **allegory** (imposing meanings from outside). This is **typology** (recognizing God's design in actual history).

---

## Typology: God-Ordained, Author-Intended Patterns

### Definition of Typology

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">**Typology is God-ordained, author-intended historical correspondence and escalation in significance between people, events, and institutions across the Bible's redemptive-historical story (in covenantal context).**</span>

### Key Features of Typology

| **Historical Correspondence** | **Escalation in Significance** |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Established by: | When patterns repeat, expectations accumulate: |
| 1. Key terms (e.g., "ark") | • Each new installment **increases** significance |
| 2. Quotations (e.g., "I am Yahweh who brought you out...") | • Abraham's mini-exodus → Exodus from Egypt → Conquest of Canaan → Ultimate Exodus in Christ |
| 3. Repeated sequences (e.g., exodus pattern) | • The pattern points **forward** to greater fulfillment |
| 4. Covenantal significance (e.g., "cursed are you") | • Christ is the **climax** of all these patterns |
