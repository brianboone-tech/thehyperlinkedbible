### <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">The Flow of Paul's Argument</span>

<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">The Structure Reveals the Logic:</span>

1. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">A/A'</span> (Outer frame): The **saints** - Paul addresses believers' identity
   - Opens: "To the saints" (v2)
   - Closes: "Inheritance of the saints" (v12)

2. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">B/B'</span>: **Thanksgiving to the Father** - Paul's gratitude frames the section
   - Opens: "We thank God the Father" (v3)
   - Closes: "Giving thanks to the Father" (v12)

3. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">C/C'</span>: **Hearing and praying** - Reciprocal relationship
   - Opens: "We heard... we pray for you" (vv3-4)
   - Closes: "From the day we heard, we pray for you" (v9)

4. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">D/D'</span>: **Love** - Community reality
   - Opens: "Your love for all the saints" (v4)
   - Closes: "Your love in the Spirit" (v8)

5. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">E/E'</span>: **Hearing the truth of the gospel** - Initial reception
   - Opens: "You heard in the word of truth, the gospel" (v5)
   - Closes: "You heard it and understood the grace of God in truth" (v6d)

6. <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">F/F'</span>: **Scope shift**: World → You
   - Opens: "In all the world" (v6a) - Cosmic reality
   - Closes: "Among you" (v6c) - Local participation

7. <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">G (CENTER)</span>: **BEARING FRUIT AND GROWING** (v6b)
   - <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">THE MAIN POINT</span>: The gospel is accomplishing the creation mandate globally

<span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">The architecture shows</span>: Paul moves from universal reality (the gospel is bearing fruit in all the world) to personal participation (as it does among you), bracketed by thanksgiving, all centered on <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">gospel fruitfulness that fulfills Genesis 1:28</span>.
