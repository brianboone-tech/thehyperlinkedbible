# For Different Users

**[[Home/01 - Getting Started|← Getting Started]]**

---

The Hyperlinked Bible serves three audiences—each can go as deep as they want.

---

### The Serious Layperson

**Profile**: Loves Scripture, wants to go deeper, but lacks seminary training.

**Start here**:
- Read in **Readable Bible** — flowing, readable text
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">IP</span> to see how passages quote or echo each other
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">C</span> to explore chiasms and see what the author emphasized
- Click <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">TT</span> to trace how types point to Christ

**What you'll gain**: See how the Bible fits together without needing technical training.

---

### The Pastor or Teacher

**Profile**: Preparing sermons or Bible studies, needs quick access to cross-references and structure.

**Use these resources**:
- <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">TOSK</span> — 500,000+ cross-references organized by keyword for sermon research
- <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">C</span> — 1,700+ chiasms provide ready-made sermon outlines
- <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">TT</span> — Show congregations how OT types point to Christ
- **Foundation Texts** — Deep exegetical analysis for key passages

**What you'll gain**: Cut sermon prep time while going deeper into the text.

---

### The Scholar or Seminarian

**Profile**: Researching original languages, intertextuality, or biblical theology.

**Use these resources**:
- <span style="color: #3b82f6; font-weight: bold;">Verse numbers</span> → Hebrew/Greek interlinear in Reference Bible
- **Lexicon** — Strong's definitions with Hebrew/Greek terms
- **LXX Reference** — Septuagint for tracing NT quotations
- <span style="color: #7c3aed; font-weight: bold;">IP</span> — 12,000+ NT→OT connections with verbal/conceptual analysis
- **Foundation Texts** — Full ninefold analysis including Jewish backgrounds

**What you'll gain**: Original language access and scholarly tools integrated with the biblical text.
