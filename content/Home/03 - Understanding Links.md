## The Inline Link System

Every verse in the [[Home/08 - Scripture Index|Readable Bible]] ends with small inline links that connect you to deeper resources. Here's what each one means:

---

## Link Types

### Verse Numbers (Blue)

Click any **verse number** to see the Hebrew/Greek interlinear in the Reference Bible.

**Example**: Clicking "1" in Genesis 1:1 shows you the Hebrew words *bereshit bara elohim* with their definitions.

---

### IP - Intertextuality Pairs (Purple)

**IP** links show how passages **quote or echo** each other.

- **OT to OT**: How later OT texts reference earlier OT texts (911 pairs)
- **NT to OT**: How NT authors understood OT passages pointing to Christ (12,000+ pairs)

**Example**: Genesis 3:15 has IP links showing connections to Isaiah 65:25, Romans 16:20, and Revelation 12.

---

### C - Chiasms (Purple)

**C** links reveal **chiastic structures** - literary mirror patterns where the author places emphasis at the center.

![[Resources/Chiasm-Example.png]]

**Example**: The story of the Flood (Genesis 6-9) is structured as a chiasm with the center at "God remembered Noah."

---

### TOSK - Treasury of Scripture Knowledge (Purple)

**TOSK** links connect to the **Treasury of Scripture Knowledge**, a classic cross-reference resource with 500,000+ connections compiled by R.A. Torrey (1897).

**Example**: Each verse has comprehensive parallel passages organized by theme.

---

### TT - Trajectory Tables (Purple)

**TT** links connect to **Trajectory Tables** that trace typological themes from OT shadows to NT fulfillment.

**Pattern**: OT Type → Prophetic Development → NT Fulfillment → Eschatological Consummation

**Example**: Genesis 3:15 connects to the "Seed Promise" trajectory table showing how this theme develops through Abraham, David, and culminates in Christ.

---

## Multiple Links

When a verse has multiple connections of the same type, you'll see superscript numbers:

- **IP¹ IP² IP³** = Three different Intertextuality Pairs
- **TT¹ TT² TT³** = Three different Trajectory Tables
- **C¹ C²** = Two different chiastic structures overlap here

---

## Try It Yourself

Go to [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3|Genesis 3]] and explore the links on verse 14-15. You'll find:
- Multiple IP links showing how this verse echoes through Scripture
- A C link revealing the chiastic structure of the Fall narrative
- TT links connecting to trajectory tables on the Seed Promise and other themes
- TOSK showing comprehensive cross-references
