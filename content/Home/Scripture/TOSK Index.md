## Overview

The **Treasury of Scripture Knowledge** (TOSK) is a classic Bible reference work containing over 500,000 cross-references compiled by R.A. Torrey and others. The Hyperlinked Bible integrates these references with inline links from each verse.

| Statistic | Value |
|-----------|------:|
| **Books Covered** | 66 |
| **Chapter Files** | 1189 |

> **About TOSK**: Originally published in 1836, the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge represents centuries of careful study identifying thematic, verbal, and conceptual connections between Bible passages.

## How to Use TOSK

1. **From any verse**: Click the  link at the end of a verse
2. **Browse by book**: Use the tables above to explore a book's cross-references
3. **Study themes**: Follow chains of references to trace ideas across Scripture

### Reference Format

Each TOSK entry lists cross-references organized by verse, showing:
- **Parallel passages** - Similar events or teachings
- **Verbal connections** - Shared vocabulary or phrases  
- **Thematic links** - Related theological concepts
- **Prophetic connections** - Prophecy and fulfillment
