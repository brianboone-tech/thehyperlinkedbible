## <span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Dangers of Missing the Chiastic Structure</span>

<span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Hamilton offers a sobering warning:</span>

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Often those who do not perceive the beautiful chiastic structures in biblical texts will allege that the statements are arranged in a **haphazard, disjointed, unorganized, repetitive way**. These judgments say more about the **failure of such readers to understand** than they do about what the biblical authors communicated."</span>

<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">When modern readers complain about:</span>
- Repetitive genealogies
- Disconnected episodes
- Lack of clear organization
- Redundant stories

...they are often revealing their own failure to recognize the deliberate literary structure the author created.

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Miss the form, miss the force.</span> If we don't see the chiastic architecture, we won't feel the full weight of the truth being presented.
