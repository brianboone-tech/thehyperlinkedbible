## Examples Across Scripture

### Example 1: The Exodus Pattern

**Abraham's mini-exodus** (Gen 12-15) → **Israel's exodus** (Exod 1-20) → **Conquest as new exodus** (Exod 15:16; Josh 3) → **Prophetic promise of new exodus** (Isa 43:16-21; Jer 23:7-8) → **Christ's exodus** (Luke 9:31; accomplished at the cross)

**Micro-level indicators**:
- Quotation: "I am Yahweh who brought you out..." (Gen 15:7 → Exod 20:2)
- Key terms: "stone" (Exod 15:5 → 15:16)
- Repeated sequence: Descent → oppression → plagues → deliverance → covenant
- Covenantal significance: God as deliverer making covenant with His people

### Example 2: The Seed Promise

**Genesis 3:15** (seed will crush serpent) → **Genesis 4:1** (Eve hopes for the seed) → **Genesis 12:3** (blessing through Abraham's seed) → **Genesis 22:17-18** (Abraham's seed will possess gates of enemies) → **Galatians 3:16** ("The seed" = Christ)

**Micro-level indicators**:
- Key term: "seed" (זֶרַע)
- Quotation: "cursed are you" formula
- Repeated conflict: Seed of woman vs. seed of serpent (Cain/Abel; Isaac/Ishmael; Jacob/Esau; Israel/Canaan)
- Covenantal significance: The promised seed brings blessing by defeating the curse

### Example 3: The Ark Typology

**Noah's ark** (Gen 6-9) → **Moses's ark-basket** (Exod 2:3-5) → **Christ our ark** (1 Pet 3:20-21)

**Micro-level indicators**:
- Key term: תֵּבָה ("ark") appears **only** in Noah and Moses narratives
- Quotation: Gen 1:28 quoted in Gen 9:1 (covenant renewal)
- Repeated sequence: Judgment → salvation through water → covenant
- Covenantal significance: Mediator of covenant after judgment
