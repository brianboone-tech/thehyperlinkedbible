## How It Works

1. **Start in the Readable Bible** - read Scripture naturally
2. **Click inline links** when you want to explore deeper
3. **Follow connections** to chiasms, cross-references, or trajectory tables
4. **Return to reading** when you're ready

The inline links at the end of each verse are your doorways to the interconnected web of Scripture.

---

## Navigation Tips

- **Readable Bible** → Click any verse number to see Hebrew/Greek in Reference Bible
- **IP links** → Show how this verse is quoted or echoed elsewhere
- **C links** → Reveal the chiastic structure containing this verse
- **TOSK links** → Show Treasury of Scripture Knowledge cross-references
- **TT links** → Connect to Trajectory Tables tracing typological themes
