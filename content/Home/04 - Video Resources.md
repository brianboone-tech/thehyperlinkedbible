## Educational Videos Throughout the Site

The Trajectory Table pages in The Hyperlinked Bible include **embedded YouTube videos** that complement the written content. These videos provide additional teaching on biblical theology, typology, and the interconnections of Scripture.

---

## Where to Find Videos

### Trajectory Tables

**103 of 126 trajectory tables** include embedded educational videos. These videos explain:

- The typological connection between OT and NT
- How the type develops through Scripture
- The Christological fulfillment
- Practical application for understanding Scripture

**Example**: The [[Trajectory Tables/001 - Aaron (The Great High Priest)|Aaron (The Great High Priest)]] trajectory table includes a video explaining how Aaron's priesthood points to Christ's perfect priesthood in Hebrews.

## Video Content

The videos come from various biblical teaching resources and cover topics such as:

- **Biblical typology** - How OT patterns point to Christ
- **Literary structures** - Chiasms and narrative patterns
- **Redemptive history** - The unfolding story from creation to consummation
- **Word studies** - Hebrew and Greek insights
- **Theological themes** - Covenant, kingdom, temple, and more

---

## Using Videos Effectively

1. **Read the trajectory table first** - Get the overview of the type and its development
2. **Watch the video** - Hear the teaching explained in a different format
3. **Explore the links** - Click through to the Scripture passages mentioned
4. **Return to the Readable Bible** - See how this knowledge enriches your reading
