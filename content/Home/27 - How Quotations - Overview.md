## How Quotations Reveal Authorial Intent

Quotations, allusions, and echoes are **micro-level indicators** of authorial intent. Biblical authors quote earlier Scripture, reuse key terms, and repeat sequences of events to show how God's redemptive plan unfolds progressively.
