### <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">What's at the Center?</span>

**G (Center):** <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"[The gospel] is <span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">bearing fruit and growing</span>"</span> (v. 6)

<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Why does Paul place THIS at the structural center?</span>

Because chiastic structures function as <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">macro-level indicators of authorial intent</span>. The center typically reveals **the main point** the author wants to emphasize.
