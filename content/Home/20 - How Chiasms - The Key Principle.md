## <span style="color: #1f618d; font-weight: bold;">The Key Principle</span>

<span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Chiasms are macro-level indicators of authorial intent.</span>

Just as we rely on micro-level indicators (repeated words, quoted phrases, similar events) to understand what an author means, we must recognize the larger architectural structures authors build into their texts.

<span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">As James Hamilton Jr. writes:</span>

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The form functions to communicate meaning, and if we do not see the form we will not feel the force of the truth the author presents."</span>

<span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Miss the form, miss the force.</span>
