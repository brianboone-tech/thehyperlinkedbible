## How to Use This in Study

### Step 1: Look for Quotations

When reading a passage, ask:
- Does this quote or echo earlier Scripture?
- What is the original context?
- Why did the author connect these two passages?

### Step 2: Notice Key Terms

Ask:
- Are rare or distinctive words used?
- Where else does this term appear?
- Is the author creating a deliberate link?

### Step 3: Identify Repeated Sequences

Ask:
- Does this event mirror an earlier event?
- What are the similarities and differences?
- Is the pattern escalating toward greater fulfillment?

### Step 4: Trace Covenantal Connections

Ask:
- How does this relate to God's covenant promises?
- Who are the covenant partners?
- How does this advance God's redemptive plan?

---

## Macro + Micro = Full Picture

**Macro-Level Indicators** (chiasms) show **what the author emphasized** through structure
**Micro-Level Indicators** (quotations, terms, sequences, covenantal parallels) show **how the author connected their work to earlier Scripture**

**Together**, they reveal the **full authorial intent**: what the biblical writers meant to communicate and how they intended their texts to be read in light of God's unfolding redemptive plan.
