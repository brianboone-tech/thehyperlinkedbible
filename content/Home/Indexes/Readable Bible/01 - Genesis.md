## 📊 At a Glance

> [!abstract] בְּרֵאשִׁית (*Bereshit* — "In the beginning")
> Torah—the foundational books of Moses

**Theme**: Origins—of the cosmos, humanity, sin, redemption, and the covenant people through whom blessing comes to all nations.

**Key Theological Contributions**:
- Creation mandate and image of God (1:26-28)
- Protoevangelium—first gospel promise (3:15)
- Abrahamic covenant—blessing to all nations (12:1-3)
- Typological foundations: Adam, Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph
