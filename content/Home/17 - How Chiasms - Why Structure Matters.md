### <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Why the Structure Matters</span>

<span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Without seeing the chiastic structure, you might read Colossians 1:6 as:</span>
- Nice metaphor about gospel growth
- Encouraging but not structurally significant
- Random agricultural language

<span style="color: #117864; font-weight: bold;">With the chiastic structure, you see:</span>
- <span style="color: #8e44ad; font-weight: bold;">Structurally central</span> = Paul's main emphasis in this thanksgiving
- <span style="color: #2874a6; font-weight: bold;">Deliberate echo</span> of Genesis 1:28 creation mandate
- <span style="color: #16a085; font-weight: bold;">Theological argument</span>: Gospel fulfills what Adam failed to accomplish
- <span style="color: #d68910; font-weight: bold;">Prepares for vv15-20</span>: Raises the question "WHO has power to fulfill the creation mandate?"
- <span style="color: #c0392b; font-weight: bold;">Answer</span>: Christ the Creator-Redeemer (vv15-20)

**<span style="color: #922b21; font-weight: bold;">Miss the structure, miss the meaning.</span>**
