## DIVINE IDENTITY (DEITY OF CHRIST) TRAJECTORY TABLE

The Divine Identity trajectory traces the NT's systematic application of Yahweh-exclusive OT texts directly to Jesus Christ, demonstrating not merely that He possesses divine attributes but that He shares the unique divine identity. In the OT, Yahweh alone bears the self-existent name "I AM WHO I AM" (Exodus 3:14), alone receives worship as the one true God (Deuteronomy 6:4), alone sits enthroned in unapproachable glory (Isaiah 6:1-5), and alone receives the universal homage of every knee bowing (Isaiah 45:22-23). The NT authors, writing within strict Jewish monotheism, apply these Yahweh-exclusive prerogatives directly to Jesus: He is the "I AM" existing before Abraham (John 8:58), the enthroned LORD whom Isaiah saw in the temple (John 12:41), the one to whom every knee will bow in the name of Jesus (Philippians 2:10-11), and the God whose throne is forever (Hebrews 1:8). This is not divinization or exaltation of a creature but identification of Jesus with Yahweh Himself—the Son shares the Father's divine identity while remaining personally distinct, revealing the Trinitarian nature of the one God of Israel.

## Canonical Intertextuality Pairs

### OT to OT

**23 - Isaiah**

- [[Intertextuality Pairs/OT to OT/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6.3 to Habakkuk 2.14\|Isaiah 6.3 to Habakkuk 2.14]] - This pair connects the seraphim's Trisagion ("Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of his glory") with Habakkuk's eschatological vision ("the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD"). Both texts emphasize Yahweh's glory filling the earth—a core divine identity marker. Isaiah 6:3 establishes God's transcendent holiness with threefold repetition (later connected to Trinity), and John 12:41 identifies the enthroned one as Jesus' pre-incarnate glory. Habakkuk anticipates universal knowledge of Yahweh's glory, fulfilled when Christ is revealed. The glory theme directly supports divine identity—Jesus shares the divine glory (John 17:5). Strong conceptual connection to the trajectory's core theme.

**35 - Habakkuk**

- [[Intertextuality Pairs/OT to OT/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2.14 to Isaiah 6.3\|Habakkuk 2.14 to Isaiah 6.3]] - Reverse of Isa 6:3 → Hab 2:14 analyzed above. Glory filling the earth connects Isaiah's throne vision (which John 12:41 identifies as Jesus' glory) with eschatological fulfillment. Strong connection to divine identity through glory-sharing theme.

## Four-Step Application

### 1. What You Must Do

You must acknowledge that Jesus is not simply a teacher to be evaluated but Yahweh Incarnate to be worshiped. The claim is total: every knee must bow. This includes yours. You cannot remain neutral or casually interested. Divine identity demands divine response—worship, surrender, allegiance.

### 2. Why You Can't Do It

Your entire life is built on self-rule. You evaluate religious claims by your own standards. You accept teachings that seem reasonable and reject those that don't. You are the final authority in your universe. To truly bow before Christ's divine identity requires the death of your autonomous self—and everything in you resists that death. You keep wanting to make Jesus an advisor rather than a Lord, a consultant rather than a King.

### 3. How He Did It

The eternal I AM—before whom seraphim veil their faces, whom angels worship, who laid the foundations of the earth—"emptied himself, by taking the form of a servant, being born in the likeness of men" (Philippians 2:7). He who was rich became poor. He who was worshiped became despised. He who was immortal died. The divine Son became human not to leave us in our rebellion but to rescue us from it. The Judge took the judgment. The Creator bore the curse of creation.

### 4. How Through Him You Can

Because Jesus is fully divine, His salvation is complete. A mere human teacher could give you advice; the eternal Son can give you redemption. His divine identity guarantees that His sacrifice is infinitely sufficient, His resurrection is cosmically authoritative, and His intercession is eternally effective. You can bow before Him not as a defeated subject bowing to a tyrant but as a rescued rebel bowing to a gracious King. His divinity doesn't threaten you—it saves you. The I AM became Immanuel so that you could call the infinite God "Abba, Father."

## Lexicon Findings

The Divine Identity trajectory reveals a precise lexical network connecting OT Yahweh-texts to NT Jesus-texts, demonstrating the NT authors' deliberate inclusion of Christ in the unique divine identity. The lexical connections establish not merely that Jesus possesses divine attributes, but that He shares the self-existent name and exclusive worship rights of Yahweh.

**Hebrew Foundation**: [[Lexicon/H1901-2000#H1961|H1961]] (*hayah*, "to be/exist") forms the root of Yahweh's self-revelation in Exodus 3:14 as "I AM WHO I AM" (*ehyeh asher ehyeh*, אֶהְיֶה אֲשֶׁר אֶהְיֶה), emphasizing God's self-existent, eternal being. This covenant name [[Lexicon/H3001-3100#H3068|H3068]] (*Yahweh*, יְהוָה) derives from *hayah* and designates "the self-Existent One"—the one whose essence is absolute, underived existence. The Shema (Deuteronomy 6:4) confesses Yahweh as [[Lexicon/H201-300#H259|H259]] (*echad*, אֶחָד, "one")—the unique, undivided God of Israel. Psalm 45:6-7 addresses the messianic king as [[Lexicon/H401-500#H430|H430]] (*Elohim*, אֱלֹהִים, "God"), establishing precedent for royal-divine language later applied to Christ.

**LXX Translation Pattern**: The Septuagint translates Exodus 3:14's divine self-designation as *egō eimi ho ōn* (ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν, "I am the one being"), using [[Lexicon/G1501-1600#G1510|G1510]] (*eimi*, "to be/exist") to render the Hebrew *ehyeh*. This establishes *egō eimi* ("I am") as the Greek equivalent of Yahweh's covenant name. The LXX consistently translates *Yahweh* (H3068) with [[Lexicon/G2901-3000#G2962|G2962]] (*kyrios*, κύριος, "Lord"), and *Elohim* (H430) with [[Lexicon/G2301-2400#G2316|G2316]] (*theos*, θεός, "God"). This LXX pattern becomes foundational for NT Christology.

**NT Continuity**: John's Gospel applies the absolute *egō eimi* (G1510) directly to Jesus in contexts demanding divine identity—"Before Abraham was, I AM" (John 8:58), and the theophanic moment in Gethsemane when soldiers "drew back and fell to the ground" at Jesus' self-identification (John 18:5-6). Paul transfers Yahweh-exclusive worship to Jesus in Philippians 2:10-11 and Romans 10:13, using *kyrios* (G2962) for both Yahweh and Jesus interchangeably. Hebrews 1:6 commands angelic [[Lexicon/G4301-4400#G4352|G4352]] (*proskyneō*, προσκυνέω, "worship")—reserved exclusively for God—to be directed toward the Son. The Father's address to the Son as *ho theos* (G2316, "O God") in Hebrews 1:8 is the most explicit lexical identification of Jesus with deity. Revelation 22:13 applies the Alpha-and-Omega title—first used of the Father (1:8)—directly to Jesus, showing both share the unique divine identity designated by *ho theos*.

- **Hebrew**: *hayah* (H1961) / *ehyeh* (*I AM*) - foundation of Yahweh's name (Exodus 3:14)
- **LXX**: *egō eimi* (G1510) / *ho ōn* (*the one being*) - standard translation of divine name
- **NT**: *egō eimi* (G1510) - Jesus' self-designation (John 8:58, 18:5-6)
- **Hebrew**: *Yahweh* (H3068) - covenant name of God
- **LXX/NT**: *kyrios* (G2962) - translates Yahweh, applied to Jesus
- **Hebrew**: *Elohim* (H430) - God/gods (plural intensive)
