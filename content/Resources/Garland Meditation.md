





Luther  
  
A simple way to pray  
  
[https://wmpl.org/filed/resources/public/Literature/ASimpleWaytoPray.pdf](https://wmpl.org/filed/resources/public/Literature/ASimpleWaytoPray.pdf)  
  
1. Find a quite regular place (prep)  
2. Ask God to illuminate your mind, warm your heart and mold your will (prep)  
3. Start with slow reading (divine reading) - to read with the heart [note or mark whatever strikes you]  
4. From what you read choose a very short portion. Re-read that 3-4 times asking God to speak to you through this.  
5. Distill the main teaching / teachings  
◦ What does this tell me about Christ (person and work, who he is and what he has done)  
◦ What does this tell me about who we are and what we should be. (What we should think, feel and do, and what we actually think, feel, and do).  
6. Now we do garland meditation - intertwine various strings to get a rope around the T which is the teaching you wrap Adoration Confession and Supplication asking:  
◦ How can I love and adore God on the basis of this?  
◦ What can I confess to God on the basis of this?  
◦ What can I ask God to do on the basis of this?  
7. Maybe warm up first  
8. Write out you T’s and add the ACS as garland around the T  
9. Pray your ACTS - If as you are meditating or as you are praying your meditations  
◦ If suddenly clarity comes - write everything down, it’s the Holy Spirit preaching to you.
