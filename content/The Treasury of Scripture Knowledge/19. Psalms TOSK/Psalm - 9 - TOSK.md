[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 9#Psalm 9 . 1|Psalm 9 . 1]] , David praises God for executing judgment; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 9#Psalm 9 . 11|Psalm 9 . 11]] , He incites others to praise him; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 9#Psalm 9 . 13|Psalm 9 . 13]] , He prays that he may have cause to praise him. 

Probably , “the death of the champion . ” so the Chaldee has , “A Psalm of David , to be sung concerning the death of the man who went out between ( mibbeyney ) the camps;” evidently considering labben , of the same import as bainayim , “a middle-man or champion ,” as Goliath is termed , [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 17#1 Samuel 17 . 4|1 Samuel 17 . 4]] , concerning whose defeat this psalm is generally supposed to have been composed.
