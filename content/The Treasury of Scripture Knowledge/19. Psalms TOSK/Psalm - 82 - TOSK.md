[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 82#Psalm 82 . 1|Psalm 82 . 1]] , The psalmist , having exhorted the judges , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 82#Psalm 82 . 5|Psalm 82 . 5]] , and reproved their negligence , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 82#Psalm 82 . 8|Psalm 82 . 8]] , prays God to judge.
