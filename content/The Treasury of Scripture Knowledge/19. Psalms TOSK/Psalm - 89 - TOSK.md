Introduction 

Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 1|Psalm 89 . 1]] , The psalmist praises God for his covenant; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 5|Psalm 89 . 5]] , for his wonderful power; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 15|Psalm 89 . 15]] , for the care of his church; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 19|Psalm 89 . 19]] , for his favour to the kingdom of David; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 38|Psalm 89 . 38]] , Then complaining of contrary events , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 46|Psalm 89 . 46]] , he expostulates , prays , and blesses God. 

A Psalm for Ethan , the Ezrahite , to give instructions. This Psalm is generally supposed to have been written during the Babylonian captivity , when , the family of David being dethroned , and the royal family ruined , the Divine promises had apparently failed.
