Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 1|Psalm 132 . 1]] , David in his prayer commends unto God the religious care he had for the ark; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 8|Psalm 132 . 8]] , His prayer at the removing of the ark; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132 . 11]] , with a repetition of God’s promises. 

Some attribute this Psalm to Solomon; and others refer it to the building of the second temple; but it seems more probable that it was sung at the solemn induction of the ark into the tabernacle of Mount Zion , expressing the holy joy and triumph of that event.
