Introduction 

Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 139#Psalm 139 . 1|Psalm 139 . 1]] , David praises God for his all-seeing providence; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 139#Psalm 139 . 17|Psalm 139 . 17]] , and for his infinite mercies; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 139#Psalm 139 . 19|Psalm 139 . 19]] , He defies the wicked; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 139#Psalm 139 . 23|Psalm 139 . 23]] , He prays for sincerity. 

This Psalm is supposed to have been composed by David when made king of Israel; though some think it was written by him when accused of traitorous designs against Saul. It is a most sublime ode on the wisdom , knowledge , presence , and justice of God . t he sentiments are grand , the style highly elevated , and the images various , beautiful , and impressive.
