Introduction 

Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 70#Psalm 70 . 1|Psalm 70 . 1]] , David solicits God to the speedy destruction of the wicked , and preservation of the godly. 

This Psalm is almost word for word the same as the five [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 40#Psalm 40 . 13|Psalm 40 . 13]] of Psalm 40; and it is written as a part of the succeeding Psalm in about 27 manuscripts. Both Psalm evidently appear to have been written by David during the rebellion of Absalom , and probably at the crisis when he heard of the sanguinary counsel which Ahithophel had given respecting him; or , as some suppose , when beyond Jordan. 2Sam. 17 . 1-21
