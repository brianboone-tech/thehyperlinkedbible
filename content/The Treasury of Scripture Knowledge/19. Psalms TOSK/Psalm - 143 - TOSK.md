Introduction 

Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 1|Psalm 143 . 1]] , David prays for favour in judgment; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 3|Psalm 143 . 3]] , He complains of his griefs; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 5|Psalm 143 . 5]] , He strengthens his faith by meditation and prayer; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 7|Psalm 143 . 7]] , He prays for grace; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 9|Psalm 143 . 9]] , for deliverance; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 10|Psalm 143 . 10]] , for sanctification; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 12|Psalm 143 . 12]] , for destruction of his enemies; 

The LXX , Vulgate , Ethiopic , and Arabic state that this Psalm was composed by David on the rebellion of his son Absalom; and there are several passages in it which agree remarkably well with that period; for then he had most reason to fear lest God should deal with him according to his sins; which he deprecates with such a deep sense of his unworthiness , that it has hence been numbered among the penitential Psalm , of which it is the last.
