Introduction 

Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 144#Psalm 144 . 1|Psalm 144 . 1]] , David blesses God for his mercy both to him and to man; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 144#Psalm 144 . 5|Psalm 144 . 5]] , He prays that God would powerfully deliver him from his enemies; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 144#Psalm 144 . 9|Psalm 144 . 9]] , He promises to praise God; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 144#Psalm 144 . 11|Psalm 144 . 11]] , He prays for the happy state of the kingdom. 

Calmet and others think that this Psalm was composed by David after the death of Absalom; and from a collation of it with Psa. 18 , in which the same ideas and form of expression occur , there can be no doubt of both having proceeded from the same pen , and that David was the author.
