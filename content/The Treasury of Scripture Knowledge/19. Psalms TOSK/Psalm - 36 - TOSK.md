Overview 

[[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 36#Psalm 36 . 1|Psalm 36 . 1]] , The grievous estate of the wicked; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 36#Psalm 36 . 8|Psalm 36 . 8]] , The excellency of God’s mercy; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 36#Psalm 36 . 10|Psalm 36 . 10]] , David prays for favour to God’s children. 

This Psalm is supposed by some to have been composed by David at the beginning of Saul’s persecutions; but Calmet and others , on good grounds , are of opinion that it was written during the Babylonian captivity.
