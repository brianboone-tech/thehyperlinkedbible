the goldsmiths .  The word tzeraphim may denote smiths , or refiners , or persons that worked in metals of any kind; but it is generally understood of those who worked in gold. From the remotest period of the history of the Jews , they had artists in all the elegant and ornamental trades; and it appears that goldsmiths , apothecaries , and merchants were formed into companies in the time of Nehemiah.
