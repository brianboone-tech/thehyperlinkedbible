the month .  [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 2|Exodus 12 . 2-20]] , [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 18|Exodus 34 . 18]] , [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 5|Leviticus 23 . 5]] , [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 2|Numbers 9 . 2-5]] , [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28#Numbers 28 . 16|Numbers 28 . 16]] 

the passover .  This word comes from the Hebrew verb pasach , to pass , to leap or skip over. The destroying angel passed over the houses marked with the blood of the Paschal Lamb , so the wrath of God passes over those whose souls sprinkled with the blood of Christ. [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 5#1 Corinthians 5 . 7|1 Corinthians 5 . 7]]. As the paschal lamb was killed before Israel was delivered , so by the death of Christ , we have redemption through his blood. It was killed before the tables of the law were delivered to Moses , or Aaron's sacrifices were enjoined; thus deliverance comes to men , not by the works of the law , but by the only true passover , the Lamb of God. [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 25|Romans 3 . 25]]. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 14|Hebrews 9 . 14]]. It was killed the first month of the year , which prefigured that Christ should suffer death in that month. [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 18#John 18 . 28|John 18 . 28]]. it was killed in the evening. [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 6|Exodus 12 . 6]]. Christ suffered at that time of the day. [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 46|Matthew 27 . 46]]. [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 2|Hebrews 1 . 2]]. At even the sun sets; at Christ's passion , universal darkness was upon the whole earth. The passover was roasted with fire , denoting the sharp and dreadful pains that Christ should suffer , not only from men , but God also. It was to be eaten with bitter herbs , [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 8|Exodus 12 . 8]] ,not only to put them in remembrance of their bitter bondage in Egypt , but also to testify our mortification to sin , and readiness to undergo afflictions for Christ , [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 24|Colossians 1 . 24]] ,and likewise to teach us the absolute necessity of true repentance in all that would profitably feed by faith on Christ , the true paschal lamb.
