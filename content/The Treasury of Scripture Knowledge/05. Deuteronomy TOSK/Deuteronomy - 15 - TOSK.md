#### Verse 13 

This is a most humane and merciful addition to the law in[[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 2|Exodus 21 . 2-11]] , enforced upon the Israelites by the consideration of their Egyptian bondage. As a faithful servant has made no property for himself while honestly serving his master , so now , when he quits his service , he has nothing to begin the world with except what the kindness of his master may bestow upon him as a remuneration for his zeal and fidelity. #### Verse 22 

the unclean .  [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 15|Deuteronomy 12 . 15]] , [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 21|Deuteronomy 12 . 21]] , [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 22|Deuteronomy 12 . 22]] 

the roebuck .  Tzevee , in Arabic zaby , Chaldee and Syriac tavya , denotes the gazelle or antelope , so called from its stately beauty , as the word imports. In size it is smaller than the roe , of an elegant form , and it motions are light and graceful. It bounds seemingly without effort , and runs with such swiftness that few creatures can exceed it . [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 2#2 Samuel 2 . 18|2 Samuel 2 . 18]]. Its fine eyes are so much celebrated as even to become a proverb; and its flesh is much esteemed for food among eastern nations , having a sweet , musky taste , which is highly agreeable to their palates . [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 23|1 Kings 4 . 23]]. If to these circumstances we add , that they are gregarious , and common all over the East , whereas the roe is either not known at all , or else very rare in these countries , little doubt can remain that the gazelle and not the roe is intended by the original word.
