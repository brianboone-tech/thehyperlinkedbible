#### Verse 8 

am 3075 , bc 929 

In the twenty .  Baasha began to reign in the third year of Asa , and reigned 24 years; yet he died and was succeeded by Elah in the 26th year of Asa; and , in like manner , Elah , who began to reign in the 26th year of Asa , and was killed in the 27th , is said to have reigned two years. Thus it is evident that a part of a year is calculated as a whole year. In the Chinese annals , the whole year in which a king dies is ascribed to his reign , the years of the succeeding king being reckoned only from the beginning of the following year.
