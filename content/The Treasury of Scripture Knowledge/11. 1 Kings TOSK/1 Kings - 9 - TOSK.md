Cabul .  that is , Displeasing , or dirty. Josephus says that Cabul , in the Phoenician language , signifies ןץך בסוףךןם , displeasing; and that these cities were situated in the neighbourhood of Tyre. Most commentators are persuaded that the city Cabul in the tribe of Asher was one; and probably from this Hiram took occasion to give this name to all the other cities which Solomon had ceded to him.
