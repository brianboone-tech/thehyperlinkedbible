Shibboleth .  which signifieth a stream , or flood , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 2|Psalm 69 . 2]] , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 15|Psalm 69 . 15]] , [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 12|Isaiah 27 . 12]] , *Heb .  Shibboleth also means an ear of corn (Job 24 . 24) , and sibboleth signifies a burden . [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 6#Exodus 6 . 6|Exodus 6 . 6]] , and a heavy burden they were obliged to bear who could not pronounce this test letter. It is well known that several nations cannot pronounce certain letters. The sound of th cannot be pronounced by the Persians , no more than by some of our Continental neighbours; though it is a common sound among the Arabians. To this day , many of the German Jews cannot articulate ת th , for which they substitute ss; thus for baith , a house , they say baiss.

At the last census of the Israelites (Numbers 26 . 37) the whole tribe of Ephraim only amounted to 32 ,500 , compared with which this last number appears far too great.
