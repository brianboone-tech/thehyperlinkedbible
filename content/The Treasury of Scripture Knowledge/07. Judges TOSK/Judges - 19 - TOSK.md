the day groweth to an end .  Heb. it is the pitching time of the day , [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 6#Jeremiah 6 . 4|Jeremiah 6 . 4]]. That is , it was near the time in which travellers ordinarily pitched their tents , to take up their lodging for the night. In the latter part of the afternoon , eastern travellers begin to look out for a place for this purpose. So Dr. Shaw observes , "Our constant practice was to rise at break of day , set forward with the sun , and travel to the middle of the afternoon; at which time we began to look out for encampments of Arabs; who , to prevent such parties as ours from living at free charges upon them , take care to pitch in woods , valleys , or places the least conspicuous."
