The lifting up of the hand was a gesture used in speaking , and signifying any weighty thing , [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 22|Isaiah 49 . 22]] , and particularly in swearing , [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 22|Genesis 14 . 22]] , praying , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 28#Psalm 28 . 2|Psalm 28 . 2]] , and blessing , either of God , [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 134#Psalm 134 . 2|Psalm 134 . 2]] , or of men , as in this place. Paul , speaking of prayer , uses the phrase , "lifting up holy hands;" as also David .  "Let the lifting up of my hands be as the evening sacrifice."
