the ephod .  The ephod seems to have been a garment worn by persons of distinction of various characters ([[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 6#2 Samuel 6 . 4|2 Samuel 6 . 4]]); the description of which in the book of Exodus ([[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 6|Exodus 28 . 6]] , etc.) relates only to its materials. As to its shape , the LXX calls it ונשליע , which signifies that it was worn on the shoulders. So also Josephus , who says it was a cubit long. St. Jerome compares it with the Roman caracalla , which was a sort of short cloak , only that it had a head or hood , which the ephod had not.

coats .  The kethoneth , or coat , was made of linen; but the form of it is no where described in Scripture , except in the visionary appearance of Christ to St. John , in the form and habit of a priest ([[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 13|Revelation 1 . 13]]); where he is said to be וםהוהץלוםןע נןהחסח , "clothed with a garment down to the feet ," which perfectly agrees with the description the Jewish writers give of it; who also say , that it had sleeves which came down to the wrist , and was tied about the neck; so that it was not unlike a long shirt. It was common to all the priests; but the tunic of the high priest was made of finer linen , or wove in a more curious manner . [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 4|Exodus 28 . 4]].

[[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 2|Leviticus 8 . 2]] , [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 8#Leviticus 8 . 29|Leviticus 8 . 29]] , [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 7#Leviticus 7 . 37|Leviticus 7 . 37]] , [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 29#Exodus 29 . 19|Exodus 29 . 19-31]] , [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 19|John 17 . 19]] , [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 1#1 Corinthians 1 . 30|1 Corinthians 1 . 30]] , [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 5#2 Corinthians 5 . 21|2 Corinthians 5 . 21]] , [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 25|Ephesians 5 . 25]] , [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 5#Ephesians 5 . 27|Ephesians 5 . 27]] , [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1 . 5]] , [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 6|Revelation 1 . 6]] , "The ram of consecration" was evidently a sacrifice of peace offering though presented on a particular occasion. Then part of the blood reserved upon the altar , probably in a basin for that purpose , was mingled with the holy anointing oil , and sprinkled on the garments both of Aaron and his sons , to hallow them to the Lord. #### Verse 36 

Aaron .  This was necessary to be added , to show the exact fulfilment of the commandments delivered to Moses , and which are recorded in Exodus 29; and consequently the complete consecration and preparation of Aaron and his sons to fill the awfully important office of priests and mediators between God and the children of Israel , to offer sacrifices , and make atonement for the sins of the people.
