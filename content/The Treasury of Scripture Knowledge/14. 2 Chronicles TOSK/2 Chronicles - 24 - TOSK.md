The event soon verified this prediction; for , before the year was expired , the Syrians came up against Jerusalem , and destroyed all the princes of the people , and Joash himself was slain in his bed by his own servants. Many circumstances served to aggravate this barbarous act. Zechariah was a high-priest and a prophet , upright and unblameable in the discharge of his high offices; this murder was perpetrated within the very precincts of the courts of the Lord; and this truly good man was by blood the nearest relative of Joash , and the son of the man who had save him from being murdered , and raised him to the throne!
