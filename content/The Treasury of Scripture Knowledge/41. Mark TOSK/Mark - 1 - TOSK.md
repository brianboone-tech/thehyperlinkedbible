Galilee .  Galilee was a province of Palestine , being bounded , says Josephus , on the west by Ptolemais and mount Carmel; on the south by the country of Samaria and Scythopolis , on the river Jordan; on the east by the cantons of Hyppos , Gadara , and Gaulon; and on the north by the confines of the Tyrians. It was divided into Lower and Upper Galilee; . Upper Galilee , so called from its being mountainous , was termed Galilee of the Gentiles . [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 15|Matthew 4 . 15]] , because inhabited , says Strabo , by Egyptians , Arabians , and Phoenicians , and comprehended the tribes of Asher and Naphtali; the Lower Galilee contained the tribes of Zebulun and Issachar , and was sometimes termed the Great Field. It was , says Josephus , very populous and rich , containing 204 cities and towns.
