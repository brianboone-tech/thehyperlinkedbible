#### Verse 7 

Now Samuel .  Or , Thus did Samuel before he knew the Lord , and before the word of the Lord was revealed unto him. 

did not yet .  Samuel was not destitute of the knowledge of God , in that sense which implies the total absence of true piety , as Eli's sons were; for he knew and worshipped the God of Israel , but he did not know him as communicating special revelations of his will to him , in the manner in which he made it known to the prophets. #### Verse 10 

the Lord came .  This seems to imply a visible appearance , as well as an audible voice. 

as at other .  [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 3#1 Samuel 3 . 4|1 Samuel 3 . 4-6]] , [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 3#1 Samuel 3 . 8|1 Samuel 3 . 8]] , Samuel did not now rise and run as before , when thought he Eli called , but lay still and listened. All must be silent , when God speaks. Observe , however , Samuel in his reply left out one word .  he did not say , Speak , Lord , but only Speak , for thy servant heareth; perhaps , as Bp. Patrick suggests , out of uncertainty , whether it was God that spake to him or not. However , by this answer way was made for the message he was now to receive , and Samuel was brought acquainted with the words of God and visions of the Almighty.
