onyx stones .  Avney shoham , which was , probably , not the precious stone or gem called onyx , but a marble called in Greek onychites , which Pliny mentions as a stone Caramania; for one would hardly think that gems of any kind were used externally in such a building as the temple. Antiquity gave both stones this name , because of their resemblance to the nail of the finger. marble stones . Josephus says that the temple was built of large blocks of white marble , beautifully polished , so as to produce a most splendid appearance.
