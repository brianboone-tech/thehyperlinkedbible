[[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 8|Ezekiel 45 . 8]] , out of which he is to give an inheritance for his sons . Ezekiel 46 . 18. These appear plainly to be political rules for common princes , and for a succession of them; but as no such rules were observed under the second temple , the fulfilment of it must still be future.
