#### Verse 18 

Thy signet .  Chothem , or chothemeth , as in [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 38#Genesis 38 . 25|Genesis 38 . 25]] , is properly a ring-seal , with which impressions were made to ascertain property , etc. From [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 22#Jeremiah 22 . 24|Jeremiah 22 . 24]] , we find that it was worn on the hand; though it might also have been suspended from the neck by a ribband , as the Arabs still wear it. [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 38#Genesis 38 . 25|Genesis 38 . 25-26]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 22#Jeremiah 22 . 24|Jeremiah 22 . 24]]; [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 15#Luke 15 . 22|Luke 15 . 22]] 

bracelets .  Pathil , from pathal , to twist , wreathe , may denote either a wreath for the arm or neck , a twisted collar , or bracelet. In the former sense the LXX render it by ןסליףךןם , and Aquila and Symmachus by ףפסונפןם; and in the latter sense , the Vulgate renders it by armillam. It may have been a collar by which the signet was suspended; though its being used in the plural seems to favour the opinion of its being a bracelet.
