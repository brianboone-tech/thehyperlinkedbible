ten thousand .  ץסישם [Strong's G3461] , פבכבםפשם [Strong's G5007] , a myriad of talents , the highest number known in Greek arithmetical notation. According to Prideaux , the Roman talent was equal to 216£; ten thousand of which would amount to 2 ,160 ,000£. If the Jewish talent of silver be designed , which is estimated by the same learned writer at 450£ , this sum amounts to 4 ,500 ,000£; but if the gold talent is meant , which is equal to 7 ,200£ , then the amount is 72 ,000 ,000£. This immense sum represents our boundless obligations to God , and our utter incapacity , as sinners infinitely indebted to Divine justice , of paying one mite out of the talent.
