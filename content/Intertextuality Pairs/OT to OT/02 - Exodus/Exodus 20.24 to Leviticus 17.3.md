# Exodus 20:24 to Leviticus 17:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 24|Exodus 20:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 3|Leviticus 17:3]]

**Subject**: sacrifices exclusively at tent of meeting

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 20 establishes, Leviticus 17 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
