# Exodus 15:17 to Psalm 78:68

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 17|Exodus 15:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 68|Psalm 78:68]]

**Subject**: mountain and sanctuary in poetic retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Exodus 15 and Psalm 78, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
