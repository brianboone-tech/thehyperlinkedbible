# Exodus 21:2 to Leviticus 25:35

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 2|Exodus 21:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 35|Leviticus 25:35]]

**Subject**: debt, service, and Jubilee

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 21 and Leviticus 25 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
