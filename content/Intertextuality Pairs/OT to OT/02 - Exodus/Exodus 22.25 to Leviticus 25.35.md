# Exodus 22:25 to Leviticus 25:35

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 25|Exodus 22:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 35|Leviticus 25:35]]

**Subject**: debt and Jubilee

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 22 and Leviticus 25 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
