# Exodus 7 to Psalm 105:26

**Text**: Exodus 7

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 26|Psalm 105:26]]

**Subject**: eight cosmic terrors against Egyptians in poetic retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 7 and Psalm 105 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
