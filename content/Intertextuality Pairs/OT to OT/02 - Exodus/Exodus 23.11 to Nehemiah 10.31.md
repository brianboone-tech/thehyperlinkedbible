# Exodus 23:11 to Nehemiah 10:31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 11|Exodus 23:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 31|Nehemiah 10:31]]

**Subject**: restoration assembly oath

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 23's portrayal of restoration assembly oath finds development in Nehemiah 10, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
