# Exodus 22:25-27 to Leviticus 25:35-43

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 25|Exodus 22:25-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 35|Leviticus 25:35-43]]

**Subject**: Treatment of strangers and foreigners

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Exodus 22 and Leviticus 25, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
