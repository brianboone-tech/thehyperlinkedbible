# Exodus 13:2 to Numbers 3:11-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 11|Numbers 3:11-13]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 13 and Numbers 3, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
