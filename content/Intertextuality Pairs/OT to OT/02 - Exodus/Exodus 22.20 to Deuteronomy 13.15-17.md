# Exodus 22:20 to Deuteronomy 13:15-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 20|Exodus 22:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 13#Deuteronomy 13 . 15|Deuteronomy 13:15-17]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 22 to Deuteronomy 13, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
