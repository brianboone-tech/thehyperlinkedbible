# Exodus 20:5 to Psalm 78:58

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 5|Exodus 20:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 58|Psalm 78:58]]

**Subject**: false image in poetic retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Exodus 20 and Psalm 78, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
