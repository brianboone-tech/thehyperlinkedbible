# Exodus 33:11 to Deuteronomy 34:10-12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 11|Exodus 33:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 34#Deuteronomy 34 . 10|Deuteronomy 34:10-12]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 33 and Deuteronomy 34, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
