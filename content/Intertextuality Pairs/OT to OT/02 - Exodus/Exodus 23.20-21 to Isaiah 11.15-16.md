# Exodus 23:20-21 to Isaiah 11:15-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 15|Isaiah 11:15-16]]

**Subject**: Prophetic reference to foundational events

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 23 and Isaiah 11, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
