# Exodus 35:2-3 to Numbers 15:32-36

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 35#Exodus 35 . 2|Exodus 35:2-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 32|Numbers 15:32-36]]

**Subject**: Peace and prosperity

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Exodus 35 and Numbers 15, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
