# Exodus 16:30 to Genesis 2:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16#Exodus 16 . 30|Exodus 16:30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2]]

**Subject**: rest on Sabbath day

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 16's portrayal of rest on sabbath day finds development in Genesis 2, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
