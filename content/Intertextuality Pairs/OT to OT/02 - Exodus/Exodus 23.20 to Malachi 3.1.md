# Exodus 23:20 to Malachi 3:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1]]

**Subject**: preparing the way

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 23 and Malachi 3 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
