# Exodus 12:48 to Leviticus 19:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 48|Exodus 12:48]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 18|Leviticus 19:18]]

**Subject**: love the residing foreigner

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Exodus 12 and Leviticus 19, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
