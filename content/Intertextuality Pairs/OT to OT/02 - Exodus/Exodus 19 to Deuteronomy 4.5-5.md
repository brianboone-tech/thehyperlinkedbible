# Exodus 19 to Deuteronomy 4:5-5

**Text**: Exodus 19

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 5|Deuteronomy 4:5-5]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 19 and Deuteronomy 4 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
