# Exodus 20:16 to Deuteronomy 19:18-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 16|Exodus 20:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 18|Deuteronomy 19:18-21]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 20 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 19 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
