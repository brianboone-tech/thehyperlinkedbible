# Exodus 23:20 to Isaiah 63:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 9|Isaiah 63:9]]

**Subject**: divine presence

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 23 to Isaiah 63, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
