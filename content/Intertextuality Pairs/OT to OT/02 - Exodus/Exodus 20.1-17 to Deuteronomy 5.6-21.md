# Exodus 20:1-17 to Deuteronomy 5:6-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 1|Exodus 20:1-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 6|Deuteronomy 5:6-21]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 20 and Deuteronomy 5, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
