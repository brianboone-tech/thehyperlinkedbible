# Exodus 22:25-27 to Nehemiah 5:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 25|Exodus 22:25-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 5#Nehemiah 5 . 4|Nehemiah 5:4]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 22 and Nehemiah 5 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
