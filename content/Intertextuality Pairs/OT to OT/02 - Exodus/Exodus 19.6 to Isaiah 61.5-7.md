# Exodus 19:6 to Isaiah 61:5-7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 5|Isaiah 61:5-7]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Exodus 19 and Isaiah 61, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
