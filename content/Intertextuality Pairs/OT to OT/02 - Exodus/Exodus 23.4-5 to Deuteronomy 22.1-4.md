# Exodus 23:4-5 to Deuteronomy 22:1-4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 4|Exodus 23:4-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 1|Deuteronomy 22:1-4]]

**Subject**: Exile and restoration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 23 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 22 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
