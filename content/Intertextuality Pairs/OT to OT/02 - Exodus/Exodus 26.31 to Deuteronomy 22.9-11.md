# Exodus 26:31 to Deuteronomy 22:9-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 26#Exodus 26 . 31|Exodus 26:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 9|Deuteronomy 22:9-11]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 26 to Deuteronomy 22, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
