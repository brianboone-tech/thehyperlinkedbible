# Exodus 13:12 to Nehemiah 10:36

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 12|Exodus 13:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 36|Nehemiah 10:36]]

**Subject**: solemn oath of restoration assembly

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 13's portrayal of solemn oath of restoration assembly finds development in Nehemiah 10, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
