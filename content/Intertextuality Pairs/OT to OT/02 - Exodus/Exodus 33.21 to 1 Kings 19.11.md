# Exodus 33:21 to 1 Kings 19:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 21|Exodus 33:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 11|1 Kings 19:11]]

**Subject**: mountain theophany

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Exodus 33 to 1 Kings 19, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
