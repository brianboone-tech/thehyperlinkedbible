# Exodus 19 to Deuteronomy 4:5

**Text**: Exodus 19

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 5|Deuteronomy 4:5]]

**Subject**: revelation at the mountain

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Exodus 19 and Deuteronomy 4, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
