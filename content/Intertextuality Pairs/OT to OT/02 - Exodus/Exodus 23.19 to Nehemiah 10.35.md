# Exodus 23:19 to Nehemiah 10:35

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 19|Exodus 23:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 35|Nehemiah 10:35]]

**Subject**: firstfruits oath

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Exodus 23 and Nehemiah 10, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
