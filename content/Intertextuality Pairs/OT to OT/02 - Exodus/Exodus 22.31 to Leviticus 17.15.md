# Exodus 22:31 to Leviticus 17:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 31|Exodus 22:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 15|Leviticus 17:15]]

**Subject**: eating animals that die naturally

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Exodus 22 and Leviticus 17 (eating animals that die naturally) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
