# Exodus 34:6 to Psalm 103:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 103#Psalm 103 . 7|Psalm 103:7-8]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 34 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 103 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
