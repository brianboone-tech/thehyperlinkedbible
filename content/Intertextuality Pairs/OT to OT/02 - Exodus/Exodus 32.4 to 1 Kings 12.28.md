# Exodus 32:4 to 1 Kings 12:28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 4|Exodus 32:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 12#1 Kings 12 . 28|1 Kings 12:28]]

**Subject**: golden calves comparison

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Exodus 32 establishes, 1 Kings 12 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
