# Exodus 34:11 to Ezra 9:1-2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 11|Exodus 34:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 9#Ezra 9 . 1|Ezra 9:1-2]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 34 and Ezra 9 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
