# Exodus 21:15 to Deuteronomy 21:18-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 15|Exodus 21:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 18|Deuteronomy 21:18-21]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 21 and Deuteronomy 21 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
