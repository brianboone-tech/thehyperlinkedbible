# Exodus 22:28 to Leviticus 24:10-23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 28|Exodus 22:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 10|Leviticus 24:10-23]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Exodus 22 and Leviticus 24, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
