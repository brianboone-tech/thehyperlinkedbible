# Exodus 34:16 to Ezra 10:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 16|Exodus 34:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 10#Ezra 10 . 10|Ezra 10:10-11]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 34 and Ezra 10 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
