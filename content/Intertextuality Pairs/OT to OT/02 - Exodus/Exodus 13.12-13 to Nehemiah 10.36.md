# Exodus 13:12-13 to Nehemiah 10:36

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 12|Exodus 13:12-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 36|Nehemiah 10:36]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 13 and Nehemiah 10 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
