# Exodus 23:14-17 to Deuteronomy 16:16-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 14|Exodus 23:14-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 16|Deuteronomy 16:16-17]]

**Subject**: Passover observance and regulations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 23 and Deuteronomy 16 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
