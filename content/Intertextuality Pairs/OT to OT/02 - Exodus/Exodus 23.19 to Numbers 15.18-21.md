# Exodus 23:19 to Numbers 15:18-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 19|Exodus 23:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 18|Numbers 15:18-21]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Exodus 23 finds development in Numbers 15, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
