# Exodus 2:24 to Leviticus 26:40-42

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 2#Exodus 2 . 24|Exodus 2:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40-42]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Exodus 2 establishes, Leviticus 26 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
