# Exodus 22:7-8 to Deuteronomy 17:2-7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 7|Exodus 22:7-8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 17#Deuteronomy 17 . 2|Deuteronomy 17:2-7]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Exodus 22 establishes, Deuteronomy 17 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
