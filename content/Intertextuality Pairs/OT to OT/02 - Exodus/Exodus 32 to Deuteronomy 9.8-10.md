# Exodus 32 to Deuteronomy 9:8-10

**Text**: Exodus 32

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 9#Deuteronomy 9 . 8|Deuteronomy 9:8-10]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 32 and Deuteronomy 9 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
