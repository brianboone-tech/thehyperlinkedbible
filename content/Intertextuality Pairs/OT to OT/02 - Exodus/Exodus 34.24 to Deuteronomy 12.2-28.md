# Exodus 34:24 to Deuteronomy 12:2-28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 24|Exodus 34:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 2|Deuteronomy 12:2-28]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Exodus 34 finds development in Deuteronomy 12, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
