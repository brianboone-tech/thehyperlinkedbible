# Exodus 34:6 to Jonah 4:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 4#Jonah 4 . 2|Jonah 4:2]]

**Subject**: anger and mercy

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the theme of divine mercy across Scripture. What Exodus 34 reveals, Jonah 4 expands, showing mercy as essential to God's character. Christ is the ultimate expression of mercy (Titus 3:5), through whom believers receive 'mercy upon mercy.'
