# Exodus 34:18-20 to Exodus 23:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 18|Exodus 34:18-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 15|Exodus 23:15]]

**Subject**: Passover observance and regulations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 34 and Exodus 23 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
