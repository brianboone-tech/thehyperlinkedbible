# Exodus 25:9 to 1 Chronicles 28:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 9|Exodus 25:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 28#1 Chronicles 28 . 19|1 Chronicles 28:19]]

**Subject**: temple blueprint revelation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 25 to 1 Chronicles 28, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
