# Exodus 34:11-16 to Deuteronomy 7:1-5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 11|Exodus 34:11-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 1|Deuteronomy 7:1-5]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 34 and Deuteronomy 7 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
