# Exodus 21:12-14 to Deuteronomy 19:1-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 12|Exodus 21:12-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 1|Deuteronomy 19:1-13]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Exodus 21 finds development in Deuteronomy 19, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
