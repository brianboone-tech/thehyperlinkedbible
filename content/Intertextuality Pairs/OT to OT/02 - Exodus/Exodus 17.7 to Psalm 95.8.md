# Exodus 17:7 to Psalm 95:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 7|Exodus 17:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 8|Psalm 95:8]]

**Subject**: rebellion at Meribah and Massah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Exodus 17 and Psalm 95 (rebellion at meribah and massah) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
