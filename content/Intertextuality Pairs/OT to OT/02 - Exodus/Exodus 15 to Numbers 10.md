# Exodus 15 to Numbers 10

**Text**: Exodus 15

**OT Text Referred to**: Numbers 10

**Subject**: wilderness temptations all over again

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 15 and Numbers 10 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
