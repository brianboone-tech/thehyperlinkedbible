# Exodus 4:10 to Jeremiah 1:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 10|Exodus 4:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 6|Jeremiah 1:6]]

**Subject**: prophet deflects commissioning based on poor speech

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 4 and Jeremiah 1, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
