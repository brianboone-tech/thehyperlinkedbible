# Exodus 4:23 to Hosea 11:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 23|Exodus 4:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 1|Hosea 11:1]]

**Subject**: Israel my son

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Exodus 4 establishes, Hosea 11 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
