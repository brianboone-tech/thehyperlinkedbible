# Exodus 6:16-25 to 1 Chronicles 6:1-4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 6#Exodus 6 . 16|Exodus 6:16-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6#1 Chronicles 6 . 1|1 Chronicles 6:1-4]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 6 and 1 Chronicles 6 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
