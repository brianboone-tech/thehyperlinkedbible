# Exodus 23:6 to Psalm 82:1-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 6|Exodus 23:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 82#Psalm 82 . 1|Psalm 82:1-6]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 23 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 82 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
