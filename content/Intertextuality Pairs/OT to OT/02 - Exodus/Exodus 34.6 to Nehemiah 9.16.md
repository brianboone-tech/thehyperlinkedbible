# Exodus 34:6 to Nehemiah 9:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 16|Nehemiah 9:16]]

**Subject**: wilderness rebellion retrospective

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 34 and Nehemiah 9 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
