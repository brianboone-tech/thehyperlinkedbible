# Exodus 20:24-25 to Leviticus 17:3-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 24|Exodus 20:24-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 3|Leviticus 17:3-9]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 20's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Leviticus 17, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
