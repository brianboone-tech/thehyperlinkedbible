# Exodus 22:25 to Deuteronomy 23:19-20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 25|Exodus 22:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 19|Deuteronomy 23:19-20]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 22 and Deuteronomy 23 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
