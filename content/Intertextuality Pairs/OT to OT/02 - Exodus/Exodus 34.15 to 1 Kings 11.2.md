# Exodus 34:15 to 1 Kings 11:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 15|Exodus 34:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 2|1 Kings 11:2]]

**Subject**: Solomon's heart turned

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 34 reveals about divine speech, 1 Kings 11 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
