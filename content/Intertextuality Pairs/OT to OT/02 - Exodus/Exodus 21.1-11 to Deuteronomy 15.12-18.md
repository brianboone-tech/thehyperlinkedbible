# Exodus 21:1-11 to Deuteronomy 15:12-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 1|Exodus 21:1-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 12|Deuteronomy 15:12-18]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 21 and Deuteronomy 15 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
