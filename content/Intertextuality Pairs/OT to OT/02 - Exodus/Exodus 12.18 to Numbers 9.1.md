# Exodus 12:18 to Numbers 9:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 18|Exodus 12:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 1|Numbers 9:1]]

**Subject**: alternate time for Passover

**Source**: Scholarly Analysis

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 12 and Numbers 9 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
