# Exodus 7 to Psalm 105:26-36

**Text**: Exodus 7

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 26|Psalm 105:26-36]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 7 and Psalm 105 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
