# Exodus 33:21-22 to 1 Kings 19:11-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 21|Exodus 33:21-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 11|1 Kings 19:11-13]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 33 and 1 Kings 19 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
