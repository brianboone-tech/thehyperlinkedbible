# Exodus 7 to Psalm 78:44-51

**Text**: Exodus 7

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 44|Psalm 78:44-51]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 7 and Psalm 78 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
