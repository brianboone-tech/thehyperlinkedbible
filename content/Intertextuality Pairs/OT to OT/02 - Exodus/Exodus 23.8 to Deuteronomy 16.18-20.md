# Exodus 23:8 to Deuteronomy 16:18-20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 8|Exodus 23:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 18|Deuteronomy 16:18-20]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 23 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 16 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
