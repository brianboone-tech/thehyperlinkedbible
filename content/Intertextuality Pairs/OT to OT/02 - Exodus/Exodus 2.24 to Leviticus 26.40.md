# Exodus 2:24 to Leviticus 26:40

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 2#Exodus 2 . 24|Exodus 2:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40]]

**Subject**: humble uncircumcised hearts

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 2 reveals about divine speech, Leviticus 26 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
