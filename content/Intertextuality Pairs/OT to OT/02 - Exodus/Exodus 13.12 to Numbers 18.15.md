# Exodus 13:12 to Numbers 18:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 12|Exodus 13:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 15|Numbers 18:15]]

**Subject**: firstborn male of all ritually impure animals

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Exodus 13 and Numbers 18, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
