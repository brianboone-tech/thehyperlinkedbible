# Exodus 22:21 to Leviticus 19:33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 21|Exodus 22:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 33|Leviticus 19:33]]

**Subject**: love the foreign resident

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Exodus 22 and Leviticus 19, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
