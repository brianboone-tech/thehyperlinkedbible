# Exodus 19:4 to Deuteronomy 32:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 4|Exodus 19:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 10|Deuteronomy 32:10-11]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 19 and Deuteronomy 32 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
