# Exodus 21:22 to Deuteronomy 25:11-12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 22|Exodus 21:22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 25#Deuteronomy 25 . 11|Deuteronomy 25:11-12]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Exodus 21 informs Deuteronomy 25's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
