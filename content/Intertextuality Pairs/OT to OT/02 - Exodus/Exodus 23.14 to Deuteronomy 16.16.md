# Exodus 23:14 to Deuteronomy 16:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 14|Exodus 23:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 16|Deuteronomy 16:16]]

**Subject**: pilgrimage festivals

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Exodus 23 and Deuteronomy 16, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
