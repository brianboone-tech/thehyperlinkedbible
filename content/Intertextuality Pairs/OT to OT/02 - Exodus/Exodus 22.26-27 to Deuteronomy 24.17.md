# Exodus 22:26-27 to Deuteronomy 24:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 26|Exodus 22:26-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 17|Deuteronomy 24:17]]

**Subject**: Exile and restoration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 22 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 24 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
