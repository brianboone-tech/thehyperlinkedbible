# Exodus 20:4 to Psalm 78:58-59

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 4|Exodus 20:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 58|Psalm 78:58-59]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 20's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Psalm 78, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
