# Exodus 4:10 to Ezekiel 3:5-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 10|Exodus 4:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 3#Ezekiel 3 . 5|Ezekiel 3:5-6]]

**Subject**: Prophetic reference to foundational events

**Source**: Scholarly Analysis

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 4 and Ezekiel 3, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
