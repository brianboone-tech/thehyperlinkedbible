# Exodus 32:4 to 1 Kings 12:28-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 4|Exodus 32:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 12#1 Kings 12 . 28|1 Kings 12:28-29]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 32's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in 1 Kings 12, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
