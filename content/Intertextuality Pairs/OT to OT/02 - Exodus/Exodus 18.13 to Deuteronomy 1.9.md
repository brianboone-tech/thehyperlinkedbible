# Exodus 18:13 to Deuteronomy 1:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 18#Exodus 18 . 13|Exodus 18:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 9|Deuteronomy 1:9]]

**Subject**: appointing judges

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 18 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 1 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
