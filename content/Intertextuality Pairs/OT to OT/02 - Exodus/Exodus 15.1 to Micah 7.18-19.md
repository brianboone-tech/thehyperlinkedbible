# Exodus 15:1 to Micah 7:18-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 1|Exodus 15:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 18|Micah 7:18-19]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 15 establishes about divine justice, Micah 7 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
