# Exodus 25:2 to 1 Chronicles 29:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 2|Exodus 25:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 29#1 Chronicles 29 . 6|1 Chronicles 29:6]]

**Subject**: freewill offerings

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Exodus 25 establishes, 1 Chronicles 29 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
