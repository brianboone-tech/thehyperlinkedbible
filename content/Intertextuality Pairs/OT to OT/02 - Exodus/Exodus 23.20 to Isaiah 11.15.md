# Exodus 23:20 to Isaiah 11:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 15|Isaiah 11:15]]

**Subject**: wilderness highway

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Exodus 23 and Isaiah 11 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
