# Exodus 20:5 to Psalm 109:9-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 5|Exodus 20:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 109#Psalm 109 . 9|Psalm 109:9-15]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 20 to Psalm 109, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
