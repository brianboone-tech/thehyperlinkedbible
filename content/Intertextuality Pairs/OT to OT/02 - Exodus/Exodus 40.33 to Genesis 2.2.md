# Exodus 40:33 to Genesis 2:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 33|Exodus 40:33]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2]]

**Subject**: creation/tabernacle completion

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 40's portrayal of creation/tabernacle completion finds development in Genesis 2, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
