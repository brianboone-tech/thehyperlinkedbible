# Exodus 21:12 to Deuteronomy 19:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 12|Exodus 21:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 1|Deuteronomy 19:1]]

**Subject**: motivation for law of asylum

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 21 and Deuteronomy 19 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
