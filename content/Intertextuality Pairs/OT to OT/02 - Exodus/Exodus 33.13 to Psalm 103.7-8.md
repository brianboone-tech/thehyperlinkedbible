# Exodus 33:13 to Psalm 103:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 13|Exodus 33:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 103#Psalm 103 . 7|Psalm 103:7-8]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Exodus 33 and Psalm 103, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
