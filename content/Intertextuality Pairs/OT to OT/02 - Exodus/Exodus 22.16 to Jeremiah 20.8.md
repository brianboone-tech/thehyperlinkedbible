# Exodus 22:16 to Jeremiah 20:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 16|Exodus 22:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 20#Jeremiah 20 . 8|Jeremiah 20:8]]

**Subject**: seduced, seized, and crying out

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Exodus 22 and Jeremiah 20 (seduced, seized, and crying out) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
