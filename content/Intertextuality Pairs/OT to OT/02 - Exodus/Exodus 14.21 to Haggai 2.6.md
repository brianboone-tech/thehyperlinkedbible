# Exodus 14:21 to Haggai 2:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 21|Exodus 14:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 6|Haggai 2:6]]

**Subject**: shake the heavens and dry land

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Exodus 14 finds development in Haggai 2, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
