# Exodus 21:17 to Deuteronomy 21:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 17|Exodus 21:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 18|Deuteronomy 21:18]]

**Subject**: rebellious son

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Exodus 21 establishes, Deuteronomy 21 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
