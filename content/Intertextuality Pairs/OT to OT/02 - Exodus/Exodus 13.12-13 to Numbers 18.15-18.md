# Exodus 13:12-13 to Numbers 18:15-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 12|Exodus 13:12-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 15|Numbers 18:15-18]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Exodus 13 informs Numbers 18's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
