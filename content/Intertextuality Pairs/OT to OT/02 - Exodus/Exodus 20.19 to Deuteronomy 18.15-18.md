# Exodus 20:19 to Deuteronomy 18:15-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 19|Exodus 20:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 18#Deuteronomy 18 . 15|Deuteronomy 18:15-18]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Exodus 20 and Deuteronomy 18, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
