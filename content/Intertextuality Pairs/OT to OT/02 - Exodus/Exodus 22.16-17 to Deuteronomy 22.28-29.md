# Exodus 22:16-17 to Deuteronomy 22:28-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 16|Exodus 22:16-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 28|Deuteronomy 22:28-29]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 22 and Deuteronomy 22 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
