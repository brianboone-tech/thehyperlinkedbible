# Exodus 16:30 to Genesis 2:2-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16#Exodus 16 . 30|Exodus 16:30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2-3]]

**Subject**: Sabbath observance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 16's portrayal of sabbath observance finds development in Genesis 2, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
