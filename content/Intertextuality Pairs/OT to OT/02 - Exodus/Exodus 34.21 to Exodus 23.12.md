# Exodus 34:21 to Exodus 23:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 21|Exodus 34:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 12|Exodus 23:12]]

**Subject**: Sabbath rest clarification

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 34's portrayal of sabbath rest clarification finds development in Exodus 23, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
