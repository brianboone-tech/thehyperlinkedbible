# Exodus 22:21 to Leviticus 19:33-34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 21|Exodus 22:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 33|Leviticus 19:33-34]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Exodus 22 finds development in Leviticus 19, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
