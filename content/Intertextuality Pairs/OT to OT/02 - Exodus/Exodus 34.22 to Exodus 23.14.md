# Exodus 34:22 to Exodus 23:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 22|Exodus 34:22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 14|Exodus 23:14]]

**Subject**: worship travel anticipation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Exodus 34 and Exodus 23 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
