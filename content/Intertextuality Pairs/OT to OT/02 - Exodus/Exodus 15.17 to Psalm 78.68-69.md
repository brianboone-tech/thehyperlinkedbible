# Exodus 15:17 to Psalm 78:68-69

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 17|Exodus 15:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 68|Psalm 78:68-69]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 15 to Psalm 78, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
