# Exodus 20:4 to Psalm 135:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 4|Exodus 20:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 135#Psalm 135 . 6|Psalm 135:6]]

**Subject**: over all creation in poetic retrospective

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Exodus 20's portrayal of over all creation in poetic retrospective finds development in Psalm 135, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
