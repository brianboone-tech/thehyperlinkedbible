# Exodus 15:1 to Micah 7:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 1|Exodus 15:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 18|Micah 7:18]]

**Subject**: God who pardons

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the theme of divine mercy across Scripture. What Exodus 15 reveals, Micah 7 expands, showing mercy as essential to God's character. Christ is the ultimate expression of mercy (Titus 3:5), through whom believers receive 'mercy upon mercy.'
