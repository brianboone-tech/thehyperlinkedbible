# Exodus 40:34 to 2 Chronicles 7:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 34|Exodus 40:34]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1]]

**Subject**: temple dedication glory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Exodus 40 to 2 Chronicles 7, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
