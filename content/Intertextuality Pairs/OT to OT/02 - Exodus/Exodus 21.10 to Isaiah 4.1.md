# Exodus 21:10 to Isaiah 4:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 10|Exodus 21:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 1|Isaiah 4:1]]

**Subject**: passing up standard support of concubine

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Exodus 21 informs Isaiah 4's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
