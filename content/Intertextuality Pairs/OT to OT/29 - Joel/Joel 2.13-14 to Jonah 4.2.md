# Joel 2:13-14 to Jonah 4:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 13|Joel 2:13-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 4#Jonah 4 . 2|Jonah 4:2]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Joel 2 establishes about divine justice, Jonah 4 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
