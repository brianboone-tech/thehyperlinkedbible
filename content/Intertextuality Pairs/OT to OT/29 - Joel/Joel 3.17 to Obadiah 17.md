# Joel 3:17 to Obadiah 17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 17|Joel 3:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Obadiah 17

**Subject**: Yahweh roars from Zion

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Joel 3 and Obadiah 17, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
