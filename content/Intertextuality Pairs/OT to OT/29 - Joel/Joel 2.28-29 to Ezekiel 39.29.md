# Joel 2:28-29 to Ezekiel 39:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 39#Ezekiel 39 . 29|Ezekiel 39:29]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Joel 2 and Ezekiel 39, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
