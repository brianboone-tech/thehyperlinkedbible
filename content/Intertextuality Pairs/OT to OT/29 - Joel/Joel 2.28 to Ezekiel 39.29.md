# Joel 2:28 to Ezekiel 39:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 39#Ezekiel 39 . 29|Ezekiel 39:29]]

**Subject**: pour out my spirit

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Joel 2 reveals about God's Spirit, Ezekiel 39 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
