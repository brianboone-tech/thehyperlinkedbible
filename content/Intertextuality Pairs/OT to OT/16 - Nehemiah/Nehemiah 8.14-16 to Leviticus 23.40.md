# Nehemiah 8:14-16 to Leviticus 23:40

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8#Nehemiah 8 . 14|Nehemiah 8:14-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 40|Leviticus 23:40]]

**Subject**: Making temporary shelters for Tabernacles (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Nehemiah 8 establishes about God's dwelling, Leviticus 23 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
