# Nehemiah 1:8-9 to Leviticus 26:40

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 1#Nehemiah 1 . 8|Nehemiah 1:8-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40]]

**Subject**: Yahweh scatters his unfaithful people (C) (* see collective confession network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Nehemiah 1 establishes about divine justice, Leviticus 26 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
