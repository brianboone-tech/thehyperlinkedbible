# Nehemiah 13:1-3 to Deuteronomy 23:3-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 1|Nehemiah 13:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 3|Deuteronomy 23:3-6]]

**Subject**: Responding to the law of the assembly (B) (* see assembly network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Nehemiah 13 and Deuteronomy 23 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
