# Nehemiah 13:17-18 to Jeremiah 17:21-27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 17|Nehemiah 13:17-18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 21|Jeremiah 17:21-27]]

**Subject**: Sabbath breaking by merchandizing (B) (* see Sabbath network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Nehemiah 13 and Jeremiah 17, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
