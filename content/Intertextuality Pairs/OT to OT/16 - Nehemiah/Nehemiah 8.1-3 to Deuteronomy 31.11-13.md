# Nehemiah 8:1-3 to Deuteronomy 31:11-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8#Nehemiah 8 . 1|Nehemiah 8:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 11|Deuteronomy 31:11-13]]

**Subject**: Pre-Tabernacles Torah reading (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Nehemiah 8 establishes about God's dwelling, Deuteronomy 31 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
