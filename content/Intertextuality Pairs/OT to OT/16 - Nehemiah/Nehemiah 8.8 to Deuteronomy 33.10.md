# Nehemiah 8:8 to Deuteronomy 33:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8#Nehemiah 8 . 8|Nehemiah 8:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 10|Deuteronomy 33:10]]

**Subject**: Levitical instruction (C) (* see teachers network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Nehemiah 8 and Deuteronomy 33 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
