**Significance**: This link develops the theme of return/restoration in Israel's hope. The pattern in Nehemiah 7 informs Ezra 2's expectation. Christ accomplishes the ultimate return from exile—not merely geographical, but spiritual—bringing God's scattered people home (Eph 2:12-13).
