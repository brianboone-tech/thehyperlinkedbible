# Nehemiah 11:25-30 to Joshua 15:20-34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 11#Nehemiah 11 . 25|Nehemiah 11:25-30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 15#Joshua 15 . 20|Joshua 15:20-34]]

**Subject**: Restoration of villages of Judah (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Nehemiah 11's portrayal of restoration of villages of judah (b) finds development in Joshua 15, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
