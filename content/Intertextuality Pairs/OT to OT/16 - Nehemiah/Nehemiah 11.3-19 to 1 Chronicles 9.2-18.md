# Nehemiah 11:3-19 to 1 Chronicles 9:2-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 11#Nehemiah 11 . 3|Nehemiah 11:3-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 9#1 Chronicles 9 . 2|1 Chronicles 9:2-18]]

**Subject**: Inhabitants of Jerusalem (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From Nehemiah 11 to 1 Chronicles 9, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
