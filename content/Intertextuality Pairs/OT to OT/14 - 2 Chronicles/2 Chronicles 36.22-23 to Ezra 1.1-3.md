# 2 Chronicles 36:22-23 to Ezra 1:1-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 22|2 Chronicles 36:22-23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 1|Ezra 1:1-3]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 36 and Ezra 1, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
