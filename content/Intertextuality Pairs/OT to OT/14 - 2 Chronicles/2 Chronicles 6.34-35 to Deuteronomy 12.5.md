# 2 Chronicles 6:34-35 to Deuteronomy 12:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 6#2 Chronicles 6 . 34|2 Chronicles 6:34-35]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 5|Deuteronomy 12:5]]

**Subject**: Warfare and divine victory

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What 2 Chronicles 6 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 12 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
