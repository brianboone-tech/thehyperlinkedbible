# 2 Chronicles 13:5 to 1 Chronicles 17:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 5|2 Chronicles 13:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 17#1 Chronicles 17 . 14|1 Chronicles 17:14]]

**Subject**: Abijah affirms permanent Davidic rule over Israel

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Chronicles 13 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 17 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
