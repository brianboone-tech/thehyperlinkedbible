# 2 Chronicles 15:6 to Zechariah 11:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 15#2 Chronicles 15 . 6|2 Chronicles 15:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 6|Zechariah 11:6]]

**Subject**: Threat of crushing

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between 2 Chronicles 15 and Zechariah 11 (threat of crushing) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
