# 2 Chronicles 11:15 to 1 Kings 12:28-33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 11#2 Chronicles 11 . 15|2 Chronicles 11:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 12#1 Kings 12 . 28|1 Kings 12:28-33]]

**Subject**: Jeroboam's aberrant renegade worship (* see place network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between 2 Chronicles 11 and 1 Kings 12 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
