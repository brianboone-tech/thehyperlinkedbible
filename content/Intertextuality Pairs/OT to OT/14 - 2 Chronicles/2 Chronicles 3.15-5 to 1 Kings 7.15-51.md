# 2 Chronicles 3:15-5 to 1 Kings 7:15-51

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 3#2 Chronicles 3 . 15|2 Chronicles 3:15-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 7#1 Kings 7 . 15|1 Kings 7:15-51]]

**Subject**: Hiram-abi's work on temple accessories

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects 2 Chronicles 3 to 1 Kings 7, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
