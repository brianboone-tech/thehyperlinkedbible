# 2 Chronicles 15:2 to Jeremiah 29:13-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 15#2 Chronicles 15 . 2|2 Chronicles 15:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 29#Jeremiah 29 . 13|Jeremiah 29:13-14]]

**Subject**: Azariah speaks of seeking and finding Yahweh

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 15 and Jeremiah 29, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
