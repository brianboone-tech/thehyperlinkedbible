# 2 Chronicles 10:16 to 2 Samuel 20:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 10#2 Chronicles 10 . 16|2 Chronicles 10:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 20#2 Samuel 20 . 1|2 Samuel 20:1]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 10 and 2 Samuel 20, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
