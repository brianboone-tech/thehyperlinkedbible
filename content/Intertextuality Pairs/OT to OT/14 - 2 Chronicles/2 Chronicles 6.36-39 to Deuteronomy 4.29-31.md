# 2 Chronicles 6:36-39 to Deuteronomy 4:29-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 6#2 Chronicles 6 . 36|2 Chronicles 6:36-39]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 29|Deuteronomy 4:29-31]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in 2 Chronicles 6 informs Deuteronomy 4's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
