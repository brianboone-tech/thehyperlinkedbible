# 2 Chronicles 20:15 to 1 Samuel 17:47

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 15|2 Chronicles 20:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 17#1 Samuel 17 . 47|1 Samuel 17:47]]

**Subject**: Jehaziel declares divine victory over Moab, Ammon, and Edom

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces salvation theology through Israel's Scriptures. What 2 Chronicles 20 reveals about deliverance, 1 Samuel 17 expands. Christ is the ultimate savior (Matt 1:21; Acts 4:12), accomplishing the salvation that all previous deliverances merely foreshadowed.
