# 2 Chronicles 16:9 to Zechariah 4:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 16#2 Chronicles 16 . 9|2 Chronicles 16:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 10|Zechariah 4:10]]

**Subject**: Eyes of Yahweh range through the earth

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. 2 Chronicles 16's portrayal of eyes of yahweh range through the earth finds development in Zechariah 4, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
