# 2 Chronicles 2:1 to 1 Kings 5:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 2#2 Chronicles 2 . 1|2 Chronicles 2:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 5#1 Kings 5 . 15|1 Kings 5:15]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 2 and 1 Kings 5, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
