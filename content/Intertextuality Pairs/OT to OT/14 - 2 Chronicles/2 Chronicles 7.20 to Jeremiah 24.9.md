# 2 Chronicles 7:20 to Jeremiah 24:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 20|2 Chronicles 7:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 24#Jeremiah 24 . 9|Jeremiah 24:9]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects 2 Chronicles 7 to Jeremiah 24, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
