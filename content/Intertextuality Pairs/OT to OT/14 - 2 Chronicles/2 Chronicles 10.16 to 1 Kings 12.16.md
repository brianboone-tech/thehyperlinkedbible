# 2 Chronicles 10:16 to 1 Kings 12:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 10#2 Chronicles 10 . 16|2 Chronicles 10:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 12#1 Kings 12 . 16|1 Kings 12:16]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 10 and 1 Kings 12, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
