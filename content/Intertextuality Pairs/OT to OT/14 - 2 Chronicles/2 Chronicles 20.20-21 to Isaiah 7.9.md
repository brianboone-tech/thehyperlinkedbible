# 2 Chronicles 20:20-21 to Isaiah 7:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 20|2 Chronicles 20:20-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 9|Isaiah 7:9]]

**Subject**: Jehoshaphat calls for faith and appoints lay singers

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in 2 Chronicles 20 informs Isaiah 7's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
