# 2 Chronicles 6:1-42 to Isaiah 55:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 6#2 Chronicles 6 . 1|2 Chronicles 6:1-42]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 3|Isaiah 55:3]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 2 Chronicles 6 establishes, Isaiah 55 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
