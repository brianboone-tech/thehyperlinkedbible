# 2 Chronicles 9:25-28 to 1 Kings 10:26-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 9#2 Chronicles 9 . 25|2 Chronicles 9:25-28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 10#1 Kings 10 . 26|1 Kings 10:26-29]]

**Subject**: Solomon's international horse trade (* see Abrahamic covenant network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 2 Chronicles 9 establishes, 1 Kings 10 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
