# 2 Chronicles 6:32-33 to Deuteronomy 12:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 6#2 Chronicles 6 . 32|2 Chronicles 6:32-33]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 11|Deuteronomy 12:11]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between 2 Chronicles 6 and Deuteronomy 12 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
