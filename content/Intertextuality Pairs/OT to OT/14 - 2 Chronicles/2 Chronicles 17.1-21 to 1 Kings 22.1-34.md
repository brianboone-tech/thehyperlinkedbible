# 2 Chronicles 17:1-21 to 1 Kings 22:1-34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 17#2 Chronicles 17 . 1|2 Chronicles 17:1-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 22#1 Kings 22 . 1|1 Kings 22:1-34]]

**Subject**: Rule of Jehoshaphat

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 17 and 1 Kings 22, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
