# 2 Chronicles 6:1-42 to Psalm 132:8-10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 6#2 Chronicles 6 . 1|2 Chronicles 6:1-42]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 8|Psalm 132:8-10]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Chronicles 6 and Psalm 132, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
