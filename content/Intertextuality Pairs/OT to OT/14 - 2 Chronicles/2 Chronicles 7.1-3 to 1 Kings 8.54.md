# 2 Chronicles 7:1-3 to 1 Kings 8:54

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 1|2 Chronicles 7:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 54|1 Kings 8:54]]

**Subject**: Fire falls from heaven

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From 2 Chronicles 7 to 1 Kings 8, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
