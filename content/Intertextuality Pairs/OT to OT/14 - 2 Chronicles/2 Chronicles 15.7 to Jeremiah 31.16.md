# 2 Chronicles 15:7 to Jeremiah 31:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 15#2 Chronicles 15 . 7|2 Chronicles 15:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 16|Jeremiah 31:16]]

**Subject**: Your work shall be rewarded

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What 2 Chronicles 15 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 31 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
