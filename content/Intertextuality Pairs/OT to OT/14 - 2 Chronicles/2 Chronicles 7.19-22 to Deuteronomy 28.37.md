# 2 Chronicles 7:19-22 to Deuteronomy 28:37

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 19|2 Chronicles 7:19-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 28#Deuteronomy 28 . 37|Deuteronomy 28:37]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between 2 Chronicles 7 and Deuteronomy 28 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
