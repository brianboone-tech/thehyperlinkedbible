# Joshua 6:17 to Deuteronomy 7:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 6#Joshua 6 . 17|Joshua 6:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 2|Deuteronomy 7:2]]

**Subject**: holy war exception

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Joshua 6 and Deuteronomy 7, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
