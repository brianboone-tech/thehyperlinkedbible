# Joshua 7:1 to 1 Chronicles 2:3-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 7#Joshua 7 . 1|Joshua 7:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3-17]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Joshua 7 establishes about divine justice, 1 Chronicles 2 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
