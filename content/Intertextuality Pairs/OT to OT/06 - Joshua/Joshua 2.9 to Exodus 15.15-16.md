# Joshua 2:9 to Exodus 15:15-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 2#Joshua 2 . 9|Joshua 2:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 15|Exodus 15:15-16]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Joshua 2 finds development in Exodus 15, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
