# Joshua 1:8 to Psalm 1:2-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 8|Joshua 1:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 1#Psalm 1 . 2|Psalm 1:2-3]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Joshua 1 and Psalm 1 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
