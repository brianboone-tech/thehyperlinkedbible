# Joshua 15:14 to Judges 1:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 15#Joshua 15 . 14|Joshua 15:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 1#Judges 1 . 10|Judges 1:10]]

**Subject**: inheritance claims

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Joshua 15 finds development in Judges 1, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
