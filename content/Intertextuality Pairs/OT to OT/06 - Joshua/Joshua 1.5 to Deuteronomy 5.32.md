# Joshua 1:5 to Deuteronomy 5:32

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 5|Joshua 1:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 32|Deuteronomy 5:32]]

**Subject**: covenant obedience

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Joshua 1 establishes, Deuteronomy 5 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
