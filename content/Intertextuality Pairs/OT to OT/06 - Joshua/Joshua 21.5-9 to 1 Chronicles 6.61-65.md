# Joshua 21:5-9 to 1 Chronicles 6:61-65

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 5|Joshua 21:5-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6#1 Chronicles 6 . 61|1 Chronicles 6:61-65]]

**Subject**: Peace and prosperity

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Joshua 21 and 1 Chronicles 6, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
