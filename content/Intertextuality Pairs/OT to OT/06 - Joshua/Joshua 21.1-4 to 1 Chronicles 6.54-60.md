# Joshua 21:1-4 to 1 Chronicles 6:54-60

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 1|Joshua 21:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6#1 Chronicles 6 . 54|1 Chronicles 6:54-60]]

**Subject**: Priestly service and duties

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Joshua 21 and 1 Chronicles 6, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
