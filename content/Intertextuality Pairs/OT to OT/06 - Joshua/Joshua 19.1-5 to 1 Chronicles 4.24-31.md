# Joshua 19:1-5 to 1 Chronicles 4:24-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 19#Joshua 19 . 1|Joshua 19:1-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 4#1 Chronicles 4 . 24|1 Chronicles 4:24-31]]

**Subject**: Land inheritance and tribal boundaries

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Joshua 19 finds development in 1 Chronicles 4, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
