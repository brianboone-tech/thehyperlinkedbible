# Joshua 15:20-34 to Nehemiah 11:25-30

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 15#Joshua 15 . 20|Joshua 15:20-34]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 11#Nehemiah 11 . 25|Nehemiah 11:25-30]]

**Subject**: Land inheritance and tribal boundaries

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Joshua 15 finds development in Nehemiah 11, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
