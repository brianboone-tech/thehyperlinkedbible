# Joshua 1:8 to Psalm 1:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 8|Joshua 1:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 1#Psalm 1 . 2|Psalm 1:2]]

**Subject**: spiritual vitality

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Joshua 1 reveals about God's Spirit, Psalm 1 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
