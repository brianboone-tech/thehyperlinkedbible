# Joshua 20:4-6 to Numbers 35:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 20#Joshua 20 . 4|Joshua 20:4-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 12|Numbers 35:12]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Joshua 20 and Numbers 35 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
