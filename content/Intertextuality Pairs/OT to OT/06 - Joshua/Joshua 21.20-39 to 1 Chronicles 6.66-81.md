# Joshua 21:20-39 to 1 Chronicles 6:66-81

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 21#Joshua 21 . 20|Joshua 21:20-39]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6#1 Chronicles 6 . 66|1 Chronicles 6:66-81]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Joshua 21 finds development in 1 Chronicles 6, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
