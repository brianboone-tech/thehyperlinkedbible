# 2 Samuel 7:12-15 to Psalm 132:11-12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132:11-12]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 7 and Psalm 132, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
