# Zechariah 13:5 to Genesis 4:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 5|Zechariah 13:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 2|Genesis 4:2]]

**Subject**: Denying prophetic vocation

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Zechariah 13 and Genesis 4, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
