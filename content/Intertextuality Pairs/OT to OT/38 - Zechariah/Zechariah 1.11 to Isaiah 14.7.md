# Zechariah 1:11 to Isaiah 14:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 1#Zechariah 1 . 11|Zechariah 1:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 7|Isaiah 14:7]]

**Subject**: The whole earth at rest

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Zechariah 1's portrayal of the whole earth at rest finds development in Isaiah 14, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
