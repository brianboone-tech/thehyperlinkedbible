# Zechariah 6:11-14 to Jeremiah 33:14-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 6#Zechariah 6 . 11|Zechariah 6:11-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14-18]]

**Subject**: Civil and priestly rulers (* see branch and Davidic covenant networks)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Zechariah 6 establishes, Jeremiah 33 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
