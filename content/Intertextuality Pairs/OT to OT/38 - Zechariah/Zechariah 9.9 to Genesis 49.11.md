# Zechariah 9:9 to Genesis 49:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 9|Zechariah 9:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11]]

**Subject**: King riding into Jerusalem (* see Judah-king network)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Zechariah 9 and Genesis 49, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
