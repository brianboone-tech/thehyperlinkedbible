# Zechariah 9:10 to Psalm 72:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 10|Zechariah 9:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 8|Psalm 72:8]]

**Subject**: Rule to ends of the earth (* see Davidic covenant network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Zechariah 9 establishes, Psalm 72 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
