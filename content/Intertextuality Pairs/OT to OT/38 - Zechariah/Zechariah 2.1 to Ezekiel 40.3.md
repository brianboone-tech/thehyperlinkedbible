# Zechariah 2:1 to Ezekiel 40:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 1|Zechariah 2:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 40#Ezekiel 40 . 3|Ezekiel 40:3]]

**Subject**: A man to measure

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Zechariah 2 and Ezekiel 40, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
