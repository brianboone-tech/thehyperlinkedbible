# Zechariah 8:21-22 to Micah 4:2-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 21|Zechariah 8:21-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 2|Micah 4:2-3]]

**Subject**: International pilgrimage to Jerusalem

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Zechariah 8 and Micah 4, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
