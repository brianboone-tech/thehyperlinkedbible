# Zechariah 12:1 to Isaiah 42:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 1|Zechariah 12:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 5|Isaiah 42:5]]

**Subject**: Creator of heaven, earth, and the human spirit

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Zechariah 12 reveals about God's Spirit, Isaiah 42 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
