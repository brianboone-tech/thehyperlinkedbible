# Zechariah 12:1 to Genesis 2:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 1|Zechariah 12:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 7|Genesis 2:7]]

**Subject**: Creator of heaven, earth, and the human spirit

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Zechariah 12 reveals about God's Spirit, Genesis 2 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
