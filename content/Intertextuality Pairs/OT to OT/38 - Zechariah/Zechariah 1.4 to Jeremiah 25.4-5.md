# Zechariah 1:4 to Jeremiah 25:4-5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 1#Zechariah 1 . 4|Zechariah 1:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 4|Jeremiah 25:4-5]]

**Subject**: Message of the former prophets

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Zechariah 1 and Jeremiah 25, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
