# Zechariah 2:11 to Isaiah 56:1-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 11|Zechariah 2:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 56#Isaiah 56 . 1|Isaiah 56:1-8]]

**Subject**: Nations joined to Yahweh

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Zechariah 2 and Isaiah 56, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
