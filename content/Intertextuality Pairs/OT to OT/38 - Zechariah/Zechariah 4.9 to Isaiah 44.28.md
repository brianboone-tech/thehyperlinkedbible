# Zechariah 4:9 to Isaiah 44:28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 9|Zechariah 4:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 28|Isaiah 44:28]]

**Subject**: Zerubbabel lays temple foundation (* see branch, new exodus, and seventy years networks)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Zechariah 4 and Isaiah 44 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
