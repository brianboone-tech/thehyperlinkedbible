# Zechariah 11:4-16 to Ezekiel 37:15-28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 4|Zechariah 11:4-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 15|Ezekiel 37:15-28]]

**Subject**: Breaking the staff and appointing a bad shepherd (* see Davidic covenant network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Zechariah 11 establishes, Ezekiel 37 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
