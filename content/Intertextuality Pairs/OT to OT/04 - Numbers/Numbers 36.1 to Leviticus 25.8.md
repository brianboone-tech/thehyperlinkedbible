# Numbers 36:1 to Leviticus 25:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 36#Numbers 36 . 1|Numbers 36:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 8|Leviticus 25:8]]

**Subject**: inheritance law amendment

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 36 and Leviticus 25 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
