# Numbers 27:4 to Ruth 4:5-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 4|Numbers 27:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 5|Ruth 4:5-6]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Numbers 27 finds development in Ruth 4, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
