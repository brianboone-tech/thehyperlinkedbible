# Numbers 24:17 to Jeremiah 48:28-47

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 17|Numbers 24:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 48#Jeremiah 48 . 28|Jeremiah 48:28-47]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 24 and Jeremiah 48 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
