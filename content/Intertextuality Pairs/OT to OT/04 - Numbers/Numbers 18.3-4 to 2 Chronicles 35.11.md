# Numbers 18:3-4 to 2 Chronicles 35:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 3|Numbers 18:3-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 11|2 Chronicles 35:11]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Numbers 18 to 2 Chronicles 35, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
