# Numbers 9:1-14 to Exodus 12:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 1|Numbers 9:1-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 12#Exodus 12 . 18|Exodus 12:18]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Numbers 9 and Exodus 12, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
