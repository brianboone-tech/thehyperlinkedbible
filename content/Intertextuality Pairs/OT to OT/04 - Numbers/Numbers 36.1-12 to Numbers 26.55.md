# Numbers 36:1-12 to Numbers 26:55

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 36#Numbers 36 . 1|Numbers 36:1-12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 26#Numbers 26 . 55|Numbers 26:55]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Numbers 36 finds development in Numbers 26, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
