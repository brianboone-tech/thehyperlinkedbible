# Numbers 11:10-30 to Deuteronomy 1:9-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 10|Numbers 11:10-30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 9|Deuteronomy 1:9-18]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 11 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 1 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
