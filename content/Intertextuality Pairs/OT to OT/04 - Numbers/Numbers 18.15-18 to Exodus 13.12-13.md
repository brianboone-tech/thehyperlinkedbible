# Numbers 18:15-18 to Exodus 13:12-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 15|Numbers 18:15-18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 12|Exodus 13:12-13]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Numbers 18 informs Exodus 13's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
