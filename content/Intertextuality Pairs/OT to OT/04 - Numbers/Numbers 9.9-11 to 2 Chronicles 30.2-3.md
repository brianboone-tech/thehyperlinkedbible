# Numbers 9:9-11 to 2 Chronicles 30:2-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 9|Numbers 9:9-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 30#2 Chronicles 30 . 2|2 Chronicles 30:2-3]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Numbers 9 and 2 Chronicles 30, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
