# Numbers 15:18 to Leviticus 23:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 18|Numbers 15:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 10|Leviticus 23:10]]

**Subject**: firstfruits offerings

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 15 establishes, Leviticus 23 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
