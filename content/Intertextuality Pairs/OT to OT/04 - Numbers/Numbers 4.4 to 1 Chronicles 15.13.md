# Numbers 4:4 to 1 Chronicles 15:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 4#Numbers 4 . 4|Numbers 4:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 15#1 Chronicles 15 . 13|1 Chronicles 15:13]]

**Subject**: ark transportation protocol

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Numbers 4 to 1 Chronicles 15, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
