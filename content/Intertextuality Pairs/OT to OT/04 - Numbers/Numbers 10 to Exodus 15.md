# Numbers 10 to Exodus 15

**Text**: Numbers 10

**OT Text Referred to**: Exodus 15

**Subject**: wilderness testing patterns

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Numbers 10 and Exodus 15 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
