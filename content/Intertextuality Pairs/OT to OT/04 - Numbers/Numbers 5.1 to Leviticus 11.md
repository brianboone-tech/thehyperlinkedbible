# Numbers 5:1 to Leviticus 11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 1|Numbers 5:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Leviticus 11

**Subject**: relocating ritually impure persons

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Numbers 5 establishes, Leviticus 11 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
