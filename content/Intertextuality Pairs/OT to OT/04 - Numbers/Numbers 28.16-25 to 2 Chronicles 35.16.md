# Numbers 28:16-25 to 2 Chronicles 35:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28#Numbers 28 . 16|Numbers 28:16-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 16|2 Chronicles 35:16]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 28 and 2 Chronicles 35 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
