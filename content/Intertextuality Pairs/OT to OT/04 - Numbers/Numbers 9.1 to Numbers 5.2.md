# Numbers 9:1 to Numbers 5:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 1|Numbers 9:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 2|Numbers 5:2]]

**Subject**: alternate Passover timing

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Numbers 9 and Numbers 5 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
