# Numbers 5:1-4 to Numbers 2:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 1|Numbers 5:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 2#Numbers 2 . 3|Numbers 2:3]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 5 and Numbers 2 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
