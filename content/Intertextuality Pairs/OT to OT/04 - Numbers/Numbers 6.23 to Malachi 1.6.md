# Numbers 6:23 to Malachi 1:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 23|Numbers 6:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 6|Malachi 1:6]]

**Subject**: priestly blessing reversal

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Numbers 6 and Malachi 1, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
