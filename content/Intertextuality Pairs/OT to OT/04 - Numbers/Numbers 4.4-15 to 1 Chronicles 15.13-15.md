# Numbers 4:4-15 to 1 Chronicles 15:13-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 4#Numbers 4 . 4|Numbers 4:4-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 15#1 Chronicles 15 . 13|1 Chronicles 15:13-15]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 4 establishes about divine justice, 1 Chronicles 15 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
