# Numbers 24:17 to Isaiah 25:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 17|Numbers 24:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 10|Isaiah 25:10]]

**Subject**: Moab judgment

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 24 establishes about divine justice, Isaiah 25 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
