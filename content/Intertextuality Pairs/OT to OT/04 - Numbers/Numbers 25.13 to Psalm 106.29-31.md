# Numbers 25:13 to Psalm 106:29-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25#Numbers 25 . 13|Numbers 25:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 29|Psalm 106:29-31]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 25 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 106 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
