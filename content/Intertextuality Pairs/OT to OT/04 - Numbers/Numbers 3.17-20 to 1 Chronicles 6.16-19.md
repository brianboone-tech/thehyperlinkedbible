# Numbers 3:17-20 to 1 Chronicles 6:16-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 17|Numbers 3:17-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6#1 Chronicles 6 . 16|1 Chronicles 6:16-19]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 3 and 1 Chronicles 6 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
