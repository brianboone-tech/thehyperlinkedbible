# Numbers 10:35-36 to Psalm 132:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 35|Numbers 10:35-36]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 8|Psalm 132:8]]

**Subject**: Warfare and divine victory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 10 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 132 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
