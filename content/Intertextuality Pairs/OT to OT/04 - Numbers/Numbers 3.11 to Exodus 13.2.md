# Numbers 3:11 to Exodus 13:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 3#Numbers 3 . 11|Numbers 3:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 2|Exodus 13:2]]

**Subject**: substitution for firstborn of Israel

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Numbers 3 establishes, Exodus 13 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
