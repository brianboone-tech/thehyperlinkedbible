# Numbers 18:1 to 2 Chronicles 29:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 1|Numbers 18:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 29#2 Chronicles 29 . 11|2 Chronicles 29:11]]

**Subject**: priestly election

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Numbers 18 and 2 Chronicles 29, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
