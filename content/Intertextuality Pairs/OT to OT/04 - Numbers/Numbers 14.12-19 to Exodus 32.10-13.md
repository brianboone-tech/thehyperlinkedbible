# Numbers 14:12-19 to Exodus 32:10-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 14#Numbers 14 . 12|Numbers 14:12-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 32#Exodus 32 . 10|Exodus 32:10-13]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 14 establishes about divine justice, Exodus 32 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
