# Numbers 32:8 to Nehemiah 13:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 32#Numbers 32 . 8|Numbers 32:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 17|Nehemiah 13:17]]

**Subject**: Sabbath commerce

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Numbers 32's portrayal of sabbath commerce finds development in Nehemiah 13, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
