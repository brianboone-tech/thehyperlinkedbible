# Numbers 10:35 to Psalm 132:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 35|Numbers 10:35]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 8|Psalm 132:8]]

**Subject**: ark procession liturgy

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Numbers 10 to Psalm 132, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
