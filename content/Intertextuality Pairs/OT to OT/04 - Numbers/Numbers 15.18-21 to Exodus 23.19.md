# Numbers 15:18-21 to Exodus 23:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 18|Numbers 15:18-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 19|Exodus 23:19]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Numbers 15 finds development in Exodus 23, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
