# Numbers 35:9-34 to Exodus 21:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 35#Numbers 35 . 9|Numbers 35:9-34]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 13|Exodus 21:13]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 35 and Exodus 21 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
