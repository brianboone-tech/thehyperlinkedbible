# Numbers 18:20 to Lamentations 3:22-23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 20|Numbers 18:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 3#Lamentations 3 . 22|Lamentations 3:22-23]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Numbers 18 finds development in Lamentations 3, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
