# Numbers 21:28-29 to Jeremiah 48:45-46

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 28|Numbers 21:28-29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 48#Jeremiah 48 . 45|Jeremiah 48:45-46]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Numbers 21 to Jeremiah 48, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
