# Numbers 15:32 to Exodus 35:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 32|Numbers 15:32]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 35#Exodus 35 . 2|Exodus 35:2]]

**Subject**: Sabbath violation case

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Numbers 15's portrayal of sabbath violation case finds development in Exodus 35, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
