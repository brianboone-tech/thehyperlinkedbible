# Numbers 18:19 to 2 Chronicles 13:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 19|Numbers 18:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 13#2 Chronicles 13 . 5|2 Chronicles 13:5]]

**Subject**: covenant salt reference

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Numbers 18 establishes, 2 Chronicles 13 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
