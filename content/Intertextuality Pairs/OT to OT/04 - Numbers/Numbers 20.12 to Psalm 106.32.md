# Numbers 20:12 to Psalm 106:32

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 12|Numbers 20:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 32|Psalm 106:32]]

**Subject**: Moses' sin

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Numbers 20 and Psalm 106, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
