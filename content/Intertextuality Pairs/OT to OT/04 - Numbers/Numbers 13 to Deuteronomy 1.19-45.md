# Numbers 13 to Deuteronomy 1:19-45

**Text**: Numbers 13

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 19|Deuteronomy 1:19-45]]

**Subject**: Wilderness journey and law

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 13 and Deuteronomy 1 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
