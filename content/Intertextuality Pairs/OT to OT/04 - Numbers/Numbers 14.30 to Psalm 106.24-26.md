# Numbers 14:30 to Psalm 106:24-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 14#Numbers 14 . 30|Numbers 14:30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 24|Psalm 106:24-26]]

**Subject**: Worship and theological reflection

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 14 and Psalm 106 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
