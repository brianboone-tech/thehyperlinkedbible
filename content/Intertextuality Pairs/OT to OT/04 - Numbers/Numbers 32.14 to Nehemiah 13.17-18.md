# Numbers 32:14 to Nehemiah 13:17-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 32#Numbers 32 . 14|Numbers 32:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 17|Nehemiah 13:17-18]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Numbers 32 finds development in Nehemiah 13, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
