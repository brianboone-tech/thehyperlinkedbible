# Numbers 6:23-27 to Malachi 1:6-2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 6#Numbers 6 . 23|Numbers 6:23-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 6|Malachi 1:6-2]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Numbers 6 and Malachi 1, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
