# Numbers 18:1-7 to Ezekiel 44:9-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 1|Numbers 18:1-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 9|Ezekiel 44:9-16]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Numbers 18 to Ezekiel 44, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
