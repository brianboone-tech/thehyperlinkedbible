# Numbers 20:15-16 to Deuteronomy 26:5-10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 20#Numbers 20 . 15|Numbers 20:15-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 26#Deuteronomy 26 . 5|Deuteronomy 26:5-10]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Numbers 20 and Deuteronomy 26, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
