# Numbers 27:14 to Psalm 106:32-33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 14|Numbers 27:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 32|Psalm 106:32-33]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 27 and Psalm 106 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
