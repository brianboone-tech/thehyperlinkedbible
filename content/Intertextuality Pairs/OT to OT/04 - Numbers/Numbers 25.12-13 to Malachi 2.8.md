# Numbers 25:12-13 to Malachi 2:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25#Numbers 25 . 12|Numbers 25:12-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 8|Malachi 2:8]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Numbers 25 establishes, Malachi 2 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
