# Numbers 18:21-24 to Deuteronomy 14:22-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 21|Numbers 18:21-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 14#Deuteronomy 14 . 22|Deuteronomy 14:22-29]]

**Subject**: Exile and restoration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 18 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 14 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
