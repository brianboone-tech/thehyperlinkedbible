# Numbers 11:17 to Isaiah 63:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 17|Numbers 11:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 11|Isaiah 63:11]]

**Subject**: spirit empowerment

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 11 reveals about God's Spirit, Isaiah 63 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
