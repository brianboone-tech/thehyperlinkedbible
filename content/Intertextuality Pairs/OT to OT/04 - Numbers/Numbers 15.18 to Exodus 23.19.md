# Numbers 15:18 to Exodus 23:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 18|Numbers 15:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 19|Exodus 23:19]]

**Subject**: firstfruits offerings

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 15 establishes, Exodus 23 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
