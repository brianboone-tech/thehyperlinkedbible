# Numbers 13:32 to Ezekiel 36:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 13#Numbers 13 . 32|Numbers 13:32]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 12|Ezekiel 36:12]]

**Subject**: land defilement concept

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Numbers 13 finds development in Ezekiel 36, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
