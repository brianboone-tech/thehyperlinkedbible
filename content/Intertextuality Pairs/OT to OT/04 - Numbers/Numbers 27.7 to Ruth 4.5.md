# Numbers 27:7 to Ruth 4:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 7|Numbers 27:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 5|Ruth 4:5]]

**Subject**: inheritance law application

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Numbers 27 and Ruth 4 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
