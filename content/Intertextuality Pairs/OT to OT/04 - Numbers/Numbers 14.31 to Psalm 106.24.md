# Numbers 14:31 to Psalm 106:24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 14#Numbers 14 . 31|Numbers 14:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 24|Psalm 106:24]]

**Subject**: wilderness judgment

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Numbers 14 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 106 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
