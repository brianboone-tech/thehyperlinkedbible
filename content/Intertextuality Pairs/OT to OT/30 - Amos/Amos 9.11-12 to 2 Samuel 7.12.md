# Amos 9:11-12 to 2 Samuel 7:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 9#Amos 9 . 11|Amos 9:11-12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Amos 9 and 2 Samuel 7, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
