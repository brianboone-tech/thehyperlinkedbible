# Amos 1:3-2 to 2 Samuel 8:1-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 1#Amos 1 . 3|Amos 1:3-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 8#2 Samuel 8 . 1|2 Samuel 8:1-14]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Amos 1 and 2 Samuel 8, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
