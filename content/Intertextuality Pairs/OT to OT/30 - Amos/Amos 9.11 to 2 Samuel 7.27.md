# Amos 9:11 to 2 Samuel 7:27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 9#Amos 9 . 11|Amos 9:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 27|2 Samuel 7:27]]

**Subject**: Davidic shelter restored

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Amos 9 establishes regarding David, 2 Samuel 7 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
