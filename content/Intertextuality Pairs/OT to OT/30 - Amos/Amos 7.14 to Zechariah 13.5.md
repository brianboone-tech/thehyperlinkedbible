# Amos 7:14 to Zechariah 13:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 7#Amos 7 . 14|Amos 7:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 5|Zechariah 13:5]]

**Subject**: denying prophetic vocation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Amos 7 and Zechariah 13, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
