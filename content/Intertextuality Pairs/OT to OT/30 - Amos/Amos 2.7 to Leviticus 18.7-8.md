# Amos 2:7 to Leviticus 18:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 2#Amos 2 . 7|Amos 2:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 7|Leviticus 18:7-8]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Amos 2's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Leviticus 18, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
