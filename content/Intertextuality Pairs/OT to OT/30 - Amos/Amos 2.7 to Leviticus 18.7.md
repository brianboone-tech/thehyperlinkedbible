# Amos 2:7 to Leviticus 18:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 2#Amos 2 . 7|Amos 2:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 7|Leviticus 18:7]]

**Subject**: father and son with same female

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Amos 2 establishes, Leviticus 18 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
