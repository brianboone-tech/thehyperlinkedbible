# Amos 1:2 to Joel 3:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 1#Amos 1 . 2|Amos 1:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 17|Joel 3:17]]

**Subject**: Yahweh roars from Zion

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Amos 1 and Joel 3, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
