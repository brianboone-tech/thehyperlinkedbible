# Nehemiah 8:1-3 to Deuteronomy 31:11-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8#Nehemiah 8 . 1|Nehemiah 8:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 31#Deuteronomy 31 . 11|Deuteronomy 31:11-13]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Nehemiah 8 and Deuteronomy 31 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
