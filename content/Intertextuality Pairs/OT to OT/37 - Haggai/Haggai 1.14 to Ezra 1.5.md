# Haggai 1:14 to Ezra 1:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 1#Haggai 1 . 14|Haggai 1:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 5|Ezra 1:5]]

**Subject**: Stir up the spirit

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Haggai 1 reveals about God's Spirit, Ezra 1 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
