# Haggai 2:11 to Leviticus 10:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 11|Haggai 2:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10]]

**Subject**: Priestly ruling on holy and unclean (* see teachers network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Haggai 2 and Leviticus 10, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
