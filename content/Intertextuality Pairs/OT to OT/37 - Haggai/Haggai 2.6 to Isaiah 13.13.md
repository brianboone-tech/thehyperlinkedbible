# Haggai 2:6 to Isaiah 13:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 6|Haggai 2:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 13|Isaiah 13:13]]

**Subject**: Shake the heavens and dry land

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Haggai 2 finds development in Isaiah 13, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
