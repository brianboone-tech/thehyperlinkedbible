# Genesis 46:12 to 1 Chronicles 2:1-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 46#Genesis 46 . 12|Genesis 46:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 1|1 Chronicles 2:1-17]]

**Subject**: Genealogies of Judah Through Family of David

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Genesis 46 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 2 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
