# Genesis 13:17 to Deuteronomy 19:1-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 13#Genesis 13 . 17|Genesis 13:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 1|Deuteronomy 19:1-13]]

**Subject**: Motivation for Law of Asylum

**Source**: Scholarly Analysis

**Significance**: The connection between Genesis 13 and Deuteronomy 19 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
