**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 28#1 Chronicles 28 . 4|1 Chronicles 28:4-6]]

**Subject**: Election of Solomon Based on Choosing Judah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge
