# Genesis 22:2 to 2 Chronicles 3:1-2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 2|Genesis 22:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 3#2 Chronicles 3 . 1|2 Chronicles 3:1-2]]

**Subject**: Building the Temple in Solomon's Fourth Year

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Genesis 22 to 2 Chronicles 3, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
