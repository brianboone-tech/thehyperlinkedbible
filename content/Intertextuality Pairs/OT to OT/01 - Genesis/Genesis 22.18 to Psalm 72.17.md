# Genesis 22:18 to Psalm 72:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Genesis 22:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 17|Psalm 72:17]]

**Subject**: Nations Bless Him

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Genesis 22 and Psalm 72, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
