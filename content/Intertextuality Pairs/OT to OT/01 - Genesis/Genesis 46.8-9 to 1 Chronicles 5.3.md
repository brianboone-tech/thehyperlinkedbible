# Genesis 46:8-9 to 1 Chronicles 5:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 46#Genesis 46 . 8|Genesis 46:8-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 5#1 Chronicles 5 . 3|1 Chronicles 5:3]]

**Subject**: Sons of Reuben

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Genesis 46 establishes, 1 Chronicles 5 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
