# Genesis 12:3 to Numbers 24:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 9|Numbers 24:9]]

**Subject**: Expected King

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Genesis 12 and Numbers 24, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
