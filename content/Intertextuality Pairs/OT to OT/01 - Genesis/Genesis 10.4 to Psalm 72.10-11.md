# Genesis 10:4 to Psalm 72:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 10#Genesis 10 . 4|Genesis 10:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 10|Psalm 72:10-11]]

**Subject**: All Kings Bring Tribute and Serve

**Source**: Scholarly Analysis

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Genesis 10 and Psalm 72, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
