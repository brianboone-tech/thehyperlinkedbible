# Genesis 35:18-19 to Micah 5:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 18|Genesis 35:18-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]

**Subject**: Ruler from Bethlehem Ephrathah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Genesis 35 and Micah 5, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
