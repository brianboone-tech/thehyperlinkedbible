# Genesis 11:10-29 to 1 Chronicles 1:1-27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 10|Genesis 11:10-29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1#1 Chronicles 1 . 1|1 Chronicles 1:1-27]]

**Subject**: Genealogies Adam to Abraham

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Genesis 11 establishes, 1 Chronicles 1 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
