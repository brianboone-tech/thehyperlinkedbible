# Genesis 49:10 to Psalm 2:8-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 8|Psalm 2:8-9]]

**Subject**: Scepter Against the Nations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Genesis 49 and Psalm 2, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
