# Genesis 1 to Psalm 104

**Text**: Genesis 1

**OT Text Referred to**: Psalm 104

**Subject**: Hymn of Creation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Genesis 1's portrayal of hymn of creation finds development in Psalm 104, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
