# Genesis 35:15 to Hosea 12:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 15|Genesis 35:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 4|Hosea 12:4]]

**Subject**: Jacob Wrestles with a Man

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Genesis 35 and Hosea 12, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
