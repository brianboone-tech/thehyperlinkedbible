# Genesis 25:23 to Hosea 12:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 23|Genesis 25:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 3|Hosea 12:3]]

**Subject**: Jacob Wrestles in Womb

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Genesis 25 establishes, Hosea 12 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
