# Genesis 1:28 to Psalm 8:3-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 3|Psalm 8:3-8]]

**Subject**: What Are Mortals?

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Genesis 1 and Psalm 8, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
