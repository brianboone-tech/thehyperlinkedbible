# Genesis 2:2 to Exodus 40:33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 2|Genesis 2:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 40#Exodus 40 . 33|Exodus 40:33]]

**Subject**: Tabernacle Finished

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Genesis 2 establishes about God's dwelling, Exodus 40 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
