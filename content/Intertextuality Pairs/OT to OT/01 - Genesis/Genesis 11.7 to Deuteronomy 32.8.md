# Genesis 11:7 to Deuteronomy 32:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 11#Genesis 11 . 7|Genesis 11:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 8|Deuteronomy 32:8]]

**Subject**: Dividing the Nations

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Genesis 11 and Deuteronomy 32, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
