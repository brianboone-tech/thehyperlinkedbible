# Genesis 4:1-2 to Zechariah 13:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 4#Genesis 4 . 1|Genesis 4:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 5|Zechariah 13:5]]

**Subject**: Denying Prophetic Vocation

**Source**: Scholarly Analysis

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Genesis 4 and Zechariah 13, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
