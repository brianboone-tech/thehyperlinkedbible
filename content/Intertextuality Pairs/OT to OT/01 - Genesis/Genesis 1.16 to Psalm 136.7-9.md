# Genesis 1:16 to Psalm 136:7-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 16|Genesis 1:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 136#Psalm 136 . 7|Psalm 136:7-9]]

**Subject**: Maker of Great Lights in Covenantal Loyalty Retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Genesis 1 establishes, Psalm 136 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
