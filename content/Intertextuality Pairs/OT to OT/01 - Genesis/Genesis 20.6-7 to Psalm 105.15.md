# Genesis 20:6-7 to Psalm 105:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 20#Genesis 20 . 6|Genesis 20:6-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 15|Psalm 105:15]]

**Subject**: Protecting Messiahs and Prophets in Poetic Retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link develops messianic expectation through progressive revelation. What Genesis 20 anticipates, Psalm 105 clarifies, building Israel's hope for God's anointed deliverer. Jesus is the Christ (Messiah), anointed by the Spirit to fulfill all that Israel's prophets, priests, and kings foreshadowed.
