# Genesis 18:18 to Deuteronomy 26:5-10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 18|Genesis 18:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 26#Deuteronomy 26 . 5|Deuteronomy 26:5-10]]

**Subject**: Firstfruits Liturgy

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Genesis 18 and Deuteronomy 26, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
