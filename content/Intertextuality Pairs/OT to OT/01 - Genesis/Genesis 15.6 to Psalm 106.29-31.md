# Genesis 15:6 to Psalm 106:29-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 6|Genesis 15:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 29|Psalm 106:29-31]]

**Subject**: Credited It to Him as Righteousness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Genesis 15 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 106 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
