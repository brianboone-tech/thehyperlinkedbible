# Genesis 2:3 to Exodus 20:8-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 3|Genesis 2:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 8|Exodus 20:8-11]]

**Subject**: Sabbath

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Genesis 2's portrayal of sabbath finds development in Exodus 20, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
