# Genesis 2:7 to Ecclesiastes 12:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 7|Genesis 2:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/21 - Ecclesiastes/Ecclesiastes 12#Ecclesiastes 12 . 7|Ecclesiastes 12:7]]

**Subject**: Return Dust to the Earth and Spirit to God

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Genesis 2 reveals about God's Spirit, Ecclesiastes 12 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
