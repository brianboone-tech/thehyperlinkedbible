# Genesis 15:18 to Nehemiah 9:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 15#Genesis 15 . 18|Genesis 15:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 7|Nehemiah 9:7-8]]

**Subject**: Covenant with Abraham in Panoramic Retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Genesis 15 establishes, Nehemiah 9 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
