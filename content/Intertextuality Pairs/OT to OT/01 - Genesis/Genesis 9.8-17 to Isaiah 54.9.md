# Genesis 9:8-17 to Isaiah 54:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 8|Genesis 9:8-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 9|Isaiah 54:9]]

**Subject**: Like the Days of Noah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Genesis 9 establishes about divine justice, Isaiah 54 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
