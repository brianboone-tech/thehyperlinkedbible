# Genesis 17 to Leviticus 26:40-42

**Text**: Genesis 17

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40-42]]

**Subject**: Humble Uncircumcised Hearts

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Genesis 17 reveals about divine speech, Leviticus 26 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
