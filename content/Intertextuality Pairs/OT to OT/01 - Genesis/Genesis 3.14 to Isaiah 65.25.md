# Genesis 3:14 to Isaiah 65:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 14|Genesis 3:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 25|Isaiah 65:25]]

**Subject**: Curse Removed with the Exception of Upon the Serpent

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: This connection traces the curse motif through Israel's Scriptures. What Genesis 3 establishes about sin's consequences, Isaiah 65 develops. Christ became a curse for us (Gal 3:13), absorbing the curse to set us free and enabling the blessing of Abraham to flow to all peoples.
