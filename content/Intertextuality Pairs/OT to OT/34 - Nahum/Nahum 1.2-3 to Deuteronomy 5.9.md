# Nahum 1:2-3 to Deuteronomy 5:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/34 - Nahum/Nahum 1#Nahum 1 . 2|Nahum 1:2-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 9|Deuteronomy 5:9]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Nahum 1 to Deuteronomy 5, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
