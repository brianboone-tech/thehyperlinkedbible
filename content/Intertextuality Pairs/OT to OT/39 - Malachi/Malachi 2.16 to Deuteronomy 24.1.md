# Malachi 2:16 to Deuteronomy 24:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 16|Malachi 2:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 1|Deuteronomy 24:1]]

**Subject**: If he hates and sends away (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Malachi 2 and Deuteronomy 24 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
