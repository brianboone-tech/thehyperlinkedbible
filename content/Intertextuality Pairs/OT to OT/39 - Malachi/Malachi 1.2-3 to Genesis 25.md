# Malachi 1:2-3 to Genesis 25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Genesis 25

**Subject**: Love Israel, hate Esau (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Malachi 1 establishes, Genesis 25 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
