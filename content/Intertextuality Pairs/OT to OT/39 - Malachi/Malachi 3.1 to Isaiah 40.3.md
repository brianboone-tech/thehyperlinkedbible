# Malachi 3:1 to Isaiah 40:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 3|Isaiah 40:3]]

**Subject**: Sending delegate to prepare the way (C) (* see new exodus network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Malachi 3 and Isaiah 40 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
