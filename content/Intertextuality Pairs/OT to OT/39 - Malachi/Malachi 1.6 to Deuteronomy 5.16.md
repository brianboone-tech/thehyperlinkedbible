# Malachi 1:6 to Deuteronomy 5:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 6|Malachi 1:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 16|Deuteronomy 5:16]]

**Subject**: Son honors father (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Malachi 1 establishes, Deuteronomy 5 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
