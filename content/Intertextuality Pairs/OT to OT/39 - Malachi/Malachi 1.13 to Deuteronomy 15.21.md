# Malachi 1:13 to Deuteronomy 15:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 13|Malachi 1:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 21|Deuteronomy 15:21]]

**Subject**: Defective sacrifices (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Malachi 1 establishes, Deuteronomy 15 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
