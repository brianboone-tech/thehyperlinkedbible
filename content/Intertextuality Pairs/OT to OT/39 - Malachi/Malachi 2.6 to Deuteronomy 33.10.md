# Malachi 2:6 to Deuteronomy 33:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 6|Malachi 2:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 33#Deuteronomy 33 . 10|Deuteronomy 33:10]]

**Subject**: Instruction of Levi (B) (* see teachers network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Malachi 2 and Deuteronomy 33 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
