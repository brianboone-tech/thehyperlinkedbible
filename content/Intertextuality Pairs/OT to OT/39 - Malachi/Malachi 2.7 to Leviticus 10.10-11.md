# Malachi 2:7 to Leviticus 10:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 7|Malachi 2:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10-11]]

**Subject**: Instruction of priests (C) (* see teachers network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Malachi 2 and Leviticus 10, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
