# Judges 11:15-27 to Numbers 21:10-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 11#Judges 11 . 15|Judges 11:15-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 10|Numbers 21:10-26]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Judges 11 finds development in Numbers 21, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
