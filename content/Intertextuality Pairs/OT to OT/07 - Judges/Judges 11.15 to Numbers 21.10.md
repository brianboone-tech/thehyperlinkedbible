# Judges 11:15 to Numbers 21:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 11#Judges 11 . 15|Judges 11:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 10|Numbers 21:10]]

**Subject**: historical precedent

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Judges 11 and Numbers 21 (historical precedent) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
