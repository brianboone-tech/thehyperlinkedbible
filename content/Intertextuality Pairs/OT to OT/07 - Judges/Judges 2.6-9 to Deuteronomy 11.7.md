# Judges 2:6-9 to Deuteronomy 11:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 2#Judges 2 . 6|Judges 2:6-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 11#Deuteronomy 11 . 7|Deuteronomy 11:7]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Judges 2 finds development in Deuteronomy 11, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
