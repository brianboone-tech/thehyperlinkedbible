# Judges 9:53 to 2 Samuel 11:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 9#Judges 9 . 53|Judges 9:53]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 11#2 Samuel 11 . 21|2 Samuel 11:21]]

**Subject**: battlefield irony

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Judges 9 establishes about divine justice, 2 Samuel 11 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
