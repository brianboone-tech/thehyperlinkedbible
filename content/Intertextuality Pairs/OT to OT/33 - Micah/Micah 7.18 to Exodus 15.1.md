# Micah 7:18 to Exodus 15:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 18|Micah 7:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 15#Exodus 15 . 1|Exodus 15:1]]

**Subject**: Day of the Lord

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The 'Day of the Lord' theme connects Micah 7 and Exodus 15, developing eschatological expectation. This day finds its initial fulfillment in Christ's first coming (Acts 2:16-21) and awaits consummation at his return, when he will judge the living and the dead.
