# Micah 5:2 to 1 Samuel 16:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 11|1 Samuel 16:11]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Micah 5 and 1 Samuel 16, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
