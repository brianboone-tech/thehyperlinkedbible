# Micah 4:5 to Isaiah 2:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 5|Micah 4:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 5|Isaiah 2:5]]

**Subject**: Walk before Yahweh

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Micah 4 and Isaiah 2 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
