# Micah 4:2-3 to Zechariah 8:21-22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 2|Micah 4:2-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 21|Zechariah 8:21-22]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Micah 4 and Zechariah 8 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
