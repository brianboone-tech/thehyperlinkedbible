# Micah 4:4 to 2 Kings 18:31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 4|Micah 4:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 31|2 Kings 18:31]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Micah 4 and 2 Kings 18, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
