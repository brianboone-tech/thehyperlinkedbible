# Micah 4:1 to Isaiah 2:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 1|Micah 4:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 1|Isaiah 2:1]]

**Subject**: Mountain of Yahweh

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Micah 4 and Isaiah 2, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
