# Micah 5:8-9 to Genesis 49:8-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 8|Micah 5:8-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 8|Genesis 49:8-9]]

**Subject**: Like a lion (* see Judah-king network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Micah 5 and Genesis 49, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
