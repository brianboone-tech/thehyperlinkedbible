# Micah 7:18-19 to Exodus 34:6-7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 18|Micah 7:18-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6-7]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Micah 7 establishes about divine justice, Exodus 34 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
