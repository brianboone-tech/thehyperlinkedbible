# Micah 3:12 to Jeremiah 26:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 3#Micah 3 . 12|Micah 3:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 26#Jeremiah 26 . 18|Jeremiah 26:18]]

**Subject**: Zion plowed

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Micah 3 and Jeremiah 26, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
