# Micah 5:2 to Genesis 35:18-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 18|Genesis 35:18-19]]

**Subject**: Ruler from Bethlehem Ephrathah (* see Davidic covenant network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Micah 5 establishes, Genesis 35 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
