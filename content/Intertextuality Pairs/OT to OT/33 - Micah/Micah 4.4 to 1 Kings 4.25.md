# Micah 4:4 to 1 Kings 4:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 4|Micah 4:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 25|1 Kings 4:25]]

**Subject**: Under own vine and fig tree

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The life theme links Micah 4 and 1 Kings 4, revealing God as the source of all life. Christ is 'the resurrection and the life' (John 11:25), bringing life to the dead and abundant life to all who believe (John 10:10).
