# Micah 4:3 to Joel 3:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 3|Micah 4:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 10|Joel 3:10]]

**Subject**: Swords and plowshares (ironically)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Micah 4 reveals about divine speech, Joel 3 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
