# Micah 5:2 to 1 Samuel 9:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 9#1 Samuel 9 . 21|1 Samuel 9:21]]

**Subject**: Ruler from Bethlehem Ephrathah (* see Davidic covenant network)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Micah 5 establishes, 1 Samuel 9 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
