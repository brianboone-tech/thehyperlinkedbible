# Isaiah 30:2 to 2 Kings 18:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 2|Isaiah 30:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 21|2 Kings 18:21]]

**Subject**: depending on Egypt

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Isaiah 30 and 2 Kings 18 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
