# Isaiah 43:1-2 to Exodus 14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 1|Isaiah 43:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Exodus 14

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Isaiah 43's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Exodus 14, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
