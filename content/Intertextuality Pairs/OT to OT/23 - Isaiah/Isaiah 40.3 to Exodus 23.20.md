# Isaiah 40:3 to Exodus 23:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 3|Isaiah 40:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20]]

**Subject**: highway in the wilderness

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Isaiah 40 and Exodus 23 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
