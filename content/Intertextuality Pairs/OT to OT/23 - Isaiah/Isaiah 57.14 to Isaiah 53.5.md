# Isaiah 57:14 to Isaiah 53:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 57#Isaiah 57 . 14|Isaiah 57:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 5|Isaiah 53:5]]

**Subject**: restoration of the contrite

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Isaiah 57's portrayal of restoration of the contrite finds development in Isaiah 53, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
