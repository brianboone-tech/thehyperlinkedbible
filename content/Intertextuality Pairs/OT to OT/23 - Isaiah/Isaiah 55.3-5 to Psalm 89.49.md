# Isaiah 55:3-5 to Psalm 89:49

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 3|Isaiah 55:3-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 49|Psalm 89:49]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Isaiah 55 establishes, Psalm 89 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
