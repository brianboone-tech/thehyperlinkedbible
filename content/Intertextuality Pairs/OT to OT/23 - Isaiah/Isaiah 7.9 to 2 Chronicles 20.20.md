# Isaiah 7:9 to 2 Chronicles 20:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 9|Isaiah 7:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 20|2 Chronicles 20:20]]

**Subject**: faith

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Isaiah 7 and 2 Chronicles 20 (faith) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
