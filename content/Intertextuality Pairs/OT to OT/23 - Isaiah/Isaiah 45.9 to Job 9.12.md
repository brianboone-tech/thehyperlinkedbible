# Isaiah 45:9 to Job 9:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 45#Isaiah 45 . 9|Isaiah 45:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 9#Job 9 . 12|Job 9:12]]

**Subject**: What are you doing?

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Isaiah 45 and Job 9 (what are you doing?) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
