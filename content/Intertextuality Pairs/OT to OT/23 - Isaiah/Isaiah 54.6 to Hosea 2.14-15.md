# Isaiah 54:6 to Hosea 2:14-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 6|Isaiah 54:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 14|Hosea 2:14-15]]

**Subject**: Holy Spirit promise

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Isaiah 54 finds development in Hosea 2, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
