# Isaiah 2:1-5 to Micah 4:1-5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 1|Isaiah 2:1-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 1|Micah 4:1-5]]

**Subject**: Day of the Lord

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The 'Day of the Lord' theme connects Isaiah 2 and Micah 4, developing eschatological expectation. This day finds its initial fulfillment in Christ's first coming (Acts 2:16-21) and awaits consummation at his return, when he will judge the living and the dead.
