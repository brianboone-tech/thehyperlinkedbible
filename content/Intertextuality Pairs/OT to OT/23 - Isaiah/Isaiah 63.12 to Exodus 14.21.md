# Isaiah 63:12 to Exodus 14:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 12|Isaiah 63:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 14#Exodus 14 . 21|Exodus 14:21]]

**Subject**: divided the sea

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Isaiah 63 and Exodus 14 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
