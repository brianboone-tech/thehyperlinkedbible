# Isaiah 44:28 to 2 Chronicles 36:22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 28|Isaiah 44:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 22|2 Chronicles 36:22]]

**Subject**: his anointed one Cyrus to commission rebuilding of temple

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Isaiah 44 to 2 Chronicles 36, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
