# Isaiah 6:13 to Exodus 19:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 13|Isaiah 6:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]

**Subject**: holy seed

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Isaiah 6 and Exodus 19, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
