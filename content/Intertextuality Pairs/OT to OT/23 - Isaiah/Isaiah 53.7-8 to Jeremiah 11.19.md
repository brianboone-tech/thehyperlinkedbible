# Isaiah 53:7-8 to Jeremiah 11:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 7|Isaiah 53:7-8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 11#Jeremiah 11 . 19|Jeremiah 11:19]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Isaiah 53 finds development in Jeremiah 11, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
