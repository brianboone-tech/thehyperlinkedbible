# Isaiah 1:2 to Deuteronomy 32:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 1#Isaiah 1 . 2|Isaiah 1:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 1|Deuteronomy 32:1]]

**Subject**: hear heavens, listen earth

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Isaiah 1 to Deuteronomy 32, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
