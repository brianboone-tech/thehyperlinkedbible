# Isaiah 25:6 to Deuteronomy 14:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 6|Isaiah 25:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 14#Deuteronomy 14 . 26|Deuteronomy 14:26]]

**Subject**: eating before Yahweh on the mountain

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Isaiah 25 and Deuteronomy 14, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
