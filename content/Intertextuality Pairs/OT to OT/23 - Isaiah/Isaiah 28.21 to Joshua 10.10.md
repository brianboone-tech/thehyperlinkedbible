# Isaiah 28:21 to Joshua 10:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 28#Isaiah 28 . 21|Isaiah 28:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 10#Joshua 10 . 10|Joshua 10:10]]

**Subject**: reversal of divine intervention at Mount Perazim and Gibeon

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Isaiah 28 and Joshua 10 (reversal of divine intervention at mount perazim and gibeon) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
