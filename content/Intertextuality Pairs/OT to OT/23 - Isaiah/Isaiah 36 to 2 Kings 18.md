# Isaiah 36 to 2 Kings 18

**Text**: Isaiah 36

**OT Text Referred to**: 2 Kings 18

**Subject**: siege of Jerusalem and Hezekiah's pride

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From Isaiah 36 to 2 Kings 18, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
