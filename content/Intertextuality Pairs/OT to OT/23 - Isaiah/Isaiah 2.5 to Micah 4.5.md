# Isaiah 2:5 to Micah 4:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 5|Isaiah 2:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 5|Micah 4:5]]

**Subject**: Nations and God's plan

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Isaiah 2 and Micah 4, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
