# Isaiah 6:9-10 to Deuteronomy 29:2-4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 9|Isaiah 6:9-10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 2|Deuteronomy 29:2-4]]

**Subject**: Wisdom and understanding

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The wisdom theme connects Isaiah 6 and Deuteronomy 29, revealing God's order for creation and life. Wisdom personified in the OT finds ultimate expression in Christ, who is 'the wisdom of God' (1 Cor 1:24, 30), in whom 'are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge' (Col 2:3).
