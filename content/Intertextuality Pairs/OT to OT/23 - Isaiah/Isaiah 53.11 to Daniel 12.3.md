# Isaiah 53:11 to Daniel 12:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 11|Isaiah 53:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 3|Daniel 12:3]]

**Subject**: lead many to righteousness

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Isaiah 53 and Daniel 12, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
