# Isaiah 51:9-11 to Isaiah 27:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 51#Isaiah 51 . 9|Isaiah 51:9-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 1|Isaiah 27:1]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Isaiah 51 establishes about divine justice, Isaiah 27 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
