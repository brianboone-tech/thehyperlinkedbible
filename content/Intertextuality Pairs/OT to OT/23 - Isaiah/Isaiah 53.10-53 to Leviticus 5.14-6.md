# Isaiah 53:10-53 to Leviticus 5:14-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 10|Isaiah 53:10-53]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 14|Leviticus 5:14-6]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Isaiah 53's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Leviticus 5, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
