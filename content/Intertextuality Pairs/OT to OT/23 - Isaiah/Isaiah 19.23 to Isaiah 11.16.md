# Isaiah 19:23 to Isaiah 11:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 19#Isaiah 19 . 23|Isaiah 19:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 16|Isaiah 11:16]]

**Subject**: highway between Assyria and Egypt

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Isaiah 19 and Isaiah 11 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
