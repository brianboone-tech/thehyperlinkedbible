# Isaiah 41:15-16 to Daniel 2:45

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 15|Isaiah 41:15-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 45|Daniel 2:45]]

**Subject**: Major prophets connection

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Isaiah 41 and Daniel 2, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
