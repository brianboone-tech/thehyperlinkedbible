# Isaiah 40:4 to Zechariah 4:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 4|Isaiah 40:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 7|Zechariah 4:7]]

**Subject**: mountain becomes level

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Isaiah 40 and Zechariah 4, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
