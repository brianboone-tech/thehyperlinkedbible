# Isaiah 31:3 to 2 Kings 18:23-24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 31#Isaiah 31 . 3|Isaiah 31:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 23|2 Kings 18:23-24]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Isaiah 31 and 2 Kings 18, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
