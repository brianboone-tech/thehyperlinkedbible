# Isaiah 11:6-9 to Genesis 3:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 6|Isaiah 11:6-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 14|Genesis 3:14]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Isaiah 11 and Genesis 3, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
