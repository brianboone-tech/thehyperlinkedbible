# Isaiah 42:5 to Zechariah 12:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 5|Isaiah 42:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 1|Zechariah 12:1]]

**Subject**: creator of heaven, earth, and the human spirit

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Isaiah 42 reveals about God's Spirit, Zechariah 12 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
