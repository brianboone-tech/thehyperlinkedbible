# Isaiah 16:13-14 to Isaiah 15:1-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 13|Isaiah 16:13-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 15#Isaiah 15 . 1|Isaiah 15:1-16]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Isaiah 16 and Isaiah 15, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
