# Isaiah 28:13 to Isaiah 28:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 28#Isaiah 28 . 13|Isaiah 28:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 28#Isaiah 28 . 10|Isaiah 28:10]]

**Subject**: strange speech and judgment

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Isaiah 28 establishes about divine justice, Isaiah 28 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
