# Isaiah 66:22 to Genesis 1:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 22|Isaiah 66:22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]]

**Subject**: new heaven and new earth endure

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Isaiah 66 and Genesis 1 traces the eschatological hope of cosmic renewal. What began in Genesis finds prophetic elaboration, pointing to the day when God will make all things new. This hope is inaugurated in Christ's resurrection (the firstfruits of new creation) and consummated in the new heavens and new earth (Rev 21:1-5).
