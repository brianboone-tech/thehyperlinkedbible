# Isaiah 56:1 to Deuteronomy 23:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 56#Isaiah 56 . 1|Isaiah 56:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 1|Deuteronomy 23:1]]

**Subject**: eunuchs and foreigners in Yahweh's house

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Isaiah 56 and Deuteronomy 23, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
