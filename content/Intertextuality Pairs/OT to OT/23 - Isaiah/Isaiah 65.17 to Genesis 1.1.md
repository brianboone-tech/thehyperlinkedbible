# Isaiah 65:17 to Genesis 1:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 17|Isaiah 65:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]]

**Subject**: new heavens and new earth

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Isaiah 65 and Genesis 1 traces the eschatological hope of cosmic renewal. What began in Genesis finds prophetic elaboration, pointing to the day when God will make all things new. This hope is inaugurated in Christ's resurrection (the firstfruits of new creation) and consummated in the new heavens and new earth (Rev 21:1-5).
