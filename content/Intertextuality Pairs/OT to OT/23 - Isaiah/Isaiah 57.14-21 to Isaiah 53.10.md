# Isaiah 57:14-21 to Isaiah 53:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 57#Isaiah 57 . 14|Isaiah 57:14-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 10|Isaiah 53:10]]

**Subject**: Peace and prosperity

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Isaiah 57 and Isaiah 53, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
