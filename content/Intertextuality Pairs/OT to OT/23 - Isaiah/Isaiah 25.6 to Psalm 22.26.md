# Isaiah 25:6 to Psalm 22:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 6|Isaiah 25:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 26|Psalm 22:26]]

**Subject**: eating before Yahweh on the mountain

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Isaiah 25 and Psalm 22, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
