# Isaiah 13:13 to Haggai 2:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 13#Isaiah 13 . 13|Isaiah 13:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 6|Haggai 2:6]]

**Subject**: shake the heavens and dry land

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Isaiah 13 finds development in Haggai 2, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
