# Isaiah 54:6 to Hosea 2:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 6|Isaiah 54:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 14|Hosea 2:14]]

**Subject**: restoration like the wife of youth

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Isaiah 54's portrayal of restoration like the wife of youth finds development in Hosea 2, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
