# Isaiah 11:9 to Habakkuk 2:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 9|Isaiah 11:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 14|Habakkuk 2:14]]

**Subject**: earth filled with glory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Isaiah 11 to Habakkuk 2, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
