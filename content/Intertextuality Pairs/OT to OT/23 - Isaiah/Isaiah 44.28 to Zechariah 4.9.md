# Isaiah 44:28 to Zechariah 4:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 28|Isaiah 44:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 9|Zechariah 4:9]]

**Subject**: Zerubbabel lays temple foundation

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Isaiah 44 to Zechariah 4, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
