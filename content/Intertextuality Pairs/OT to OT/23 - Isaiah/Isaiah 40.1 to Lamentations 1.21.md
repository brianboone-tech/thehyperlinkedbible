# Isaiah 40:1 to Lamentations 1:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 1|Isaiah 40:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 1#Lamentations 1 . 21|Lamentations 1:21]]

**Subject**: comfort Jerusalem

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From Isaiah 40 to Lamentations 1, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
