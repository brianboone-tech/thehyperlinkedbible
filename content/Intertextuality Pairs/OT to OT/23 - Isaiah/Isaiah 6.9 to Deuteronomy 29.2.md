# Isaiah 6:9 to Deuteronomy 29:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 9|Isaiah 6:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 2|Deuteronomy 29:2]]

**Subject**: obstructing eyes, ears, hearts

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Isaiah 6 reveals about divine speech, Deuteronomy 29 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
