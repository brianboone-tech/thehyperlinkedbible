# Isaiah 61:5 to Exodus 19:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 5|Isaiah 61:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]]

**Subject**: priests as rulers over people

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Isaiah 61 and Exodus 19, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
