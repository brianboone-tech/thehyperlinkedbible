# Isaiah 49:23 to Psalm 72:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 23|Isaiah 49:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 9|Psalm 72:9]]

**Subject**: lick the dust

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Isaiah 49 and Psalm 72 (lick the dust) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
