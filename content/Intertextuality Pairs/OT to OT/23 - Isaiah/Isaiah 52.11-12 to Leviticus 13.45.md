# Isaiah 52:11-12 to Leviticus 13:45

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 11|Isaiah 52:11-12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 13#Leviticus 13 . 45|Leviticus 13:45]]

**Subject**: Ceremonial cleanness and purification

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Isaiah 52 informs Leviticus 13's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
