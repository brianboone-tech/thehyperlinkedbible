# Isaiah 52:1 to Deuteronomy 23:3-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 1|Isaiah 52:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 3|Deuteronomy 23:3-6]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Isaiah 52 to Deuteronomy 23, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
