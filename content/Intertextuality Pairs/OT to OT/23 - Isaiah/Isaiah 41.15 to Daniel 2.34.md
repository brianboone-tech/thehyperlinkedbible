# Isaiah 41:15 to Daniel 2:34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 15|Isaiah 41:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 34|Daniel 2:34]]

**Subject**: hewn rock destroys empires and becomes mighty kingdom

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Isaiah 41 and Daniel 2, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
