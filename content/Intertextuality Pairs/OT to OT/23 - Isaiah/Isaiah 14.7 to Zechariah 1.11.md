# Isaiah 14:7 to Zechariah 1:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 14#Isaiah 14 . 7|Isaiah 14:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 1#Zechariah 1 . 11|Zechariah 1:11]]

**Subject**: the whole earth at rest

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Isaiah 14's portrayal of the whole earth at rest finds development in Zechariah 1, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
