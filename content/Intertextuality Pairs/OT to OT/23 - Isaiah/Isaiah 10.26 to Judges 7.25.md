# Isaiah 10:26 to Judges 7:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 26|Isaiah 10:26]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 7#Judges 7 . 25|Judges 7:25]]

**Subject**: judgment like Midian at rock of Oreb and staff over the sea

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Isaiah 10 establishes about divine justice, Judges 7 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
