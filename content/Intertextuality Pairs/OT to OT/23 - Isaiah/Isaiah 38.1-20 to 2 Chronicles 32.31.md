# Isaiah 38:1-20 to 2 Chronicles 32:31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 38#Isaiah 38 . 1|Isaiah 38:1-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 32#2 Chronicles 32 . 31|2 Chronicles 32:31]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Isaiah 38 and 2 Chronicles 32, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
