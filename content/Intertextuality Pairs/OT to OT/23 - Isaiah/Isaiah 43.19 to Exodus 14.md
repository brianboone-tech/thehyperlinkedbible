# Isaiah 43:19 to Exodus 14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 19|Isaiah 43:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Exodus 14

**Subject**: Israel's creator, redeemer, and provider

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The intertextual connection develops Israel's understanding of divine redemption. What Isaiah 43 portrays, Exodus 14 expands, showing redemption as central to God's character and purpose. Christ is the ultimate Redeemer, purchasing his people with his own blood (1 Pet 1:18-19; Eph 1:7).
