# Isaiah 51:10 to Isaiah 63:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 51#Isaiah 51 . 10|Isaiah 51:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 13|Isaiah 63:13]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Isaiah 51's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Isaiah 63, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
