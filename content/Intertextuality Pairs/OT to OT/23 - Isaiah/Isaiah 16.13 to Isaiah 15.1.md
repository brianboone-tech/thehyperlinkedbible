# Isaiah 16:13 to Isaiah 15:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 16#Isaiah 16 . 13|Isaiah 16:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 15#Isaiah 15 . 1|Isaiah 15:1]]

**Subject**: downsizing hopes of Moab

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces restoration hope through Israel's Scriptures. From Isaiah 16 to Isaiah 15, the expectation builds for God to restore what sin has broken. Christ inaugurates restoration through his death and resurrection, and will complete it when he returns to make all things new.
