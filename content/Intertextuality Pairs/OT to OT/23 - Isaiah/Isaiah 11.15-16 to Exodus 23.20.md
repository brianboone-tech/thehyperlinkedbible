# Isaiah 11:15-16 to Exodus 23:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 15|Isaiah 11:15-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Isaiah 11 and Exodus 23 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
