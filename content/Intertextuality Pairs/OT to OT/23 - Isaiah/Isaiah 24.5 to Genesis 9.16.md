# Isaiah 24:5 to Genesis 9:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 24#Isaiah 24 . 5|Isaiah 24:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 9#Genesis 9 . 16|Genesis 9:16]]

**Subject**: everlasting covenant

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Isaiah 24 establishes, Genesis 9 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
