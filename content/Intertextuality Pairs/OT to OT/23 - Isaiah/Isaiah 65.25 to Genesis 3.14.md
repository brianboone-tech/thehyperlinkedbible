# Isaiah 65:25 to Genesis 3:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 25|Isaiah 65:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 14|Genesis 3:14]]

**Subject**: curse removed with the exception of the serpent

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the curse motif through Israel's Scriptures. What Isaiah 65 establishes about sin's consequences, Genesis 3 develops. Christ became a curse for us (Gal 3:13), absorbing the curse to set us free and enabling the blessing of Abraham to flow to all peoples.
