# Isaiah 7:1 to 2 Kings 16:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 1|Isaiah 7:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 16#2 Kings 16 . 5|2 Kings 16:5]]

**Subject**: collaborative opposition of Rezin of Damascus and Pekah of Israel

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Isaiah 7 establishes, 2 Kings 16 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
