# Isaiah 23:17 to Deuteronomy 23:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 23#Isaiah 23 . 17|Isaiah 23:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 18|Deuteronomy 23:18]]

**Subject**: wages of Tyre's prostitution sanctified to Yahweh

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Isaiah 23 and Deuteronomy 23, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
