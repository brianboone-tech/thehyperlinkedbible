# 1 Samuel 16:14 to Psalm 51:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 14|1 Samuel 16:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 51#Psalm 51 . 11|Psalm 51:11]]

**Subject**: spirit departs from Saul

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What 1 Samuel 16 reveals about God's Spirit, Psalm 51 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
