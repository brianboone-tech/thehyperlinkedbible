# 1 Samuel 21:4 to Leviticus 10:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 21#1 Samuel 21 . 4|1 Samuel 21:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10]]

**Subject**: bread of the presence for David

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Samuel 21 establishes regarding David, Leviticus 10 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
