# 1 Samuel 1:1 to 1 Chronicles 6:27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 1#1 Samuel 1 . 1|1 Samuel 1:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 6#1 Chronicles 6 . 27|1 Chronicles 6:27]]

**Subject**: Elkanah, Samuel, and his sons in the line of Kohath

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Samuel 1 establishes, 1 Chronicles 6 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
