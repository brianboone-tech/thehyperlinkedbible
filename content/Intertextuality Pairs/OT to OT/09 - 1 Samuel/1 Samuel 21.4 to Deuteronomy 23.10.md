# 1 Samuel 21:4 to Deuteronomy 23:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 21#1 Samuel 21 . 4|1 Samuel 21:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 10|Deuteronomy 23:10]]

**Subject**: bread of the presence for David

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Samuel 21 establishes regarding David, Deuteronomy 23 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
