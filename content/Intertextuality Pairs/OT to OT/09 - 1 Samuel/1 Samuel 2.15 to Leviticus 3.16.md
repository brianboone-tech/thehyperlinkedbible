# 1 Samuel 2:15 to Leviticus 3:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 2#1 Samuel 2 . 15|1 Samuel 2:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 3#Leviticus 3 . 16|Leviticus 3:16]]

**Subject**: taking the fat of offerings

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Samuel 2 and Leviticus 3, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
