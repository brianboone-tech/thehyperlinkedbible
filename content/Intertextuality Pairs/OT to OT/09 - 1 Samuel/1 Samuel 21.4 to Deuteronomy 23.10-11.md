# 1 Samuel 21:4 to Deuteronomy 23:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 21#1 Samuel 21 . 4|1 Samuel 21:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 10|Deuteronomy 23:10-11]]

**Subject**: Ceremonial cleanness and purification

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in 1 Samuel 21 informs Deuteronomy 23's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
