# 1 Samuel 28:3-25 to 1 Chronicles 10:1-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 28#1 Samuel 28 . 3|1 Samuel 28:3-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 10#1 Chronicles 10 . 1|1 Chronicles 10:1-14]]

**Subject**: Warfare and divine victory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What 1 Samuel 28 establishes about divine justice, 1 Chronicles 10 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
