# 1 Samuel 9:21 to Micah 5:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 9#1 Samuel 9 . 21|1 Samuel 9:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]]

**Subject**: ruler from Bethlehem Ephrathah

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Samuel 9 and Micah 5, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
