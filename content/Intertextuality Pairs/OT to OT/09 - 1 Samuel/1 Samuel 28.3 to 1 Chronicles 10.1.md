# 1 Samuel 28:3 to 1 Chronicles 10:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 28#1 Samuel 28 . 3|1 Samuel 28:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 10#1 Chronicles 10 . 1|1 Chronicles 10:1]]

**Subject**: death of Saul

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the Bible's theology of death as the consequence of sin. From 1 Samuel 28 to 1 Chronicles 10, death's reality is unflinching. Christ conquers death through his own death and resurrection, swallowing up death in victory (1 Cor 15:54-57).
