# 1 Samuel 8:5 to Deuteronomy 17:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 8#1 Samuel 8 . 5|1 Samuel 8:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 17#Deuteronomy 17 . 17|Deuteronomy 17:17]]

**Subject**: a king like the nations

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Samuel 8 and Deuteronomy 17, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
