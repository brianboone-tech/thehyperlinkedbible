# Psalm 78:13 to Isaiah 63:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 13|Psalm 78:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 12|Isaiah 63:12]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Psalm 78 to Isaiah 63, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
