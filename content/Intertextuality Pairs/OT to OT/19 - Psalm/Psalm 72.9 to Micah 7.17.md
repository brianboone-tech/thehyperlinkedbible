# Psalm 72:9 to Micah 7:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 9|Psalm 72:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 17|Micah 7:17]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Psalm 72 to Micah 7, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
