# Psalm 72:9 to Isaiah 49:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 9|Psalm 72:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 23|Isaiah 49:23]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Psalm 72 and Isaiah 49, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
