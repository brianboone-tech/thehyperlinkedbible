# 1 Chronicles 16:7-36 to Psalms 105:11-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 16#1 Chronicles 16 . 7|1 Chronicles 16:7-36]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 105#Psalm 105 . 11|Psalms 105:11-13]]

**Subject**: Psalm of worship (* see Abrahamic covenant and place networks)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Chronicles 16 establishes, Psalms 105 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
