# 1 Chronicles 2:3-17 to Genesis 38:29-30

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 38#Genesis 38 . 29|Genesis 38:29-30]]

**Subject**: Genealogies of Judah through family of David (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Chronicles 2 establishes regarding David, Genesis 38 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
