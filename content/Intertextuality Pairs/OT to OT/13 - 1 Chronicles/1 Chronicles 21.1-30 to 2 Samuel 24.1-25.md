# 1 Chronicles 21:1-30 to 2 Samuel 24:1-25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 21#1 Chronicles 21 . 1|1 Chronicles 21:1-30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 1|2 Samuel 24:1-25]]

**Subject**: David's sin of the census

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Chronicles 21 establishes regarding David, 2 Samuel 24 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
