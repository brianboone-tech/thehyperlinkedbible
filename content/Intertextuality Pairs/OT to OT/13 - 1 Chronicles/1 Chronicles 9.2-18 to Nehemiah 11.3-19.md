# 1 Chronicles 9:2-18 to Nehemiah 11:3-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 9#1 Chronicles 9 . 2|1 Chronicles 9:2-18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 11#Nehemiah 11 . 3|Nehemiah 11:3-19]]

**Subject**: First returned exiles (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link develops the theme of return/restoration in Israel's hope. The pattern in 1 Chronicles 9 informs Nehemiah 11's expectation. Christ accomplishes the ultimate return from exile—not merely geographical, but spiritual—bringing God's scattered people home (Eph 2:12-13).
