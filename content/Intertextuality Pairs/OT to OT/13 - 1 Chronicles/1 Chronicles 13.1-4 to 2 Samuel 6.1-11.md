# 1 Chronicles 13:1-4 to 2 Samuel 6:1-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 13#1 Chronicles 13 . 1|1 Chronicles 13:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 6#2 Samuel 6 . 1|2 Samuel 6:1-11]]

**Subject**: All Israel to get the ark

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects 1 Chronicles 13 to 2 Samuel 6, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
