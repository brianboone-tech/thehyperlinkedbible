# 1 Chronicles 2:3-17 to Ruth 4:18-22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 18|Ruth 4:18-22]]

**Subject**: Historical narrative and kingship

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Chronicles 2 and Ruth 4, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
