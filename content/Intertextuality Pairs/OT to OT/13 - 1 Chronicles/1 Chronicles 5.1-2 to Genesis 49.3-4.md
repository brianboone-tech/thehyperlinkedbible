# 1 Chronicles 5:1-2 to Genesis 49:3-4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 5#1 Chronicles 5 . 1|1 Chronicles 5:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 3|Genesis 49:3-4]]

**Subject**: Reuben's incest (* see Judah-king network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Chronicles 5 and Genesis 49, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
