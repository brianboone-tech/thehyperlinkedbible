# 1 Chronicles 1:1-27 to Genesis 5:13-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1#1 Chronicles 1 . 1|1 Chronicles 1:1-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 5#Genesis 5 . 13|Genesis 5:13-18]]

**Subject**: Genealogies Adam to Abraham (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Chronicles 1 establishes, Genesis 5 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
