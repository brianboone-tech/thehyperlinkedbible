# 1 Chronicles 3:1-9 to 2 Samuel 3:13-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 3#1 Chronicles 3 . 1|1 Chronicles 3:1-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 3#2 Samuel 3 . 13|2 Samuel 3:13-16]]

**Subject**: Sons born to David in Jerusalem

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Chronicles 3 establishes regarding David, 2 Samuel 3 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
