# 1 Chronicles 2:1-2 to Genesis 35:22-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 1|1 Chronicles 2:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 35#Genesis 35 . 22|Genesis 35:22-26]]

**Subject**: Sons of Israel (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Chronicles 2 establishes, Genesis 35 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
