# 1 Chronicles 11:18-19 to Deuteronomy 12:23-25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 11#1 Chronicles 11 . 18|1 Chronicles 11:18-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 23|Deuteronomy 12:23-25]]

**Subject**: Water represents the blood of life (C)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The intertextual connection develops the theology of blood atonement. From 1 Chronicles 11 to Deuteronomy 12, Scripture teaches that without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness (Heb 9:22). Christ's blood is the reality to which all previous blood offerings pointed, securing eternal redemption (Heb 9:12).
