# 1 Chronicles 17:16-27 to 2 Samuel 7:18-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 17#1 Chronicles 17 . 16|1 Chronicles 17:16-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 18|2 Samuel 7:18-29]]

**Subject**: Davidic covenant and David's prayer (* see Davidic covenant, Judah-king, and place networks)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Chronicles 17 establishes, 2 Samuel 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
