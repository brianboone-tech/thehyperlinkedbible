# 1 Chronicles 15:13-15 to Numbers 4:4-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 15#1 Chronicles 15 . 13|1 Chronicles 15:13-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 4#Numbers 4 . 4|Numbers 4:4-15]]

**Subject**: Levites carry the ark on their shoulders (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection develops the Levitical priesthood's significance across Scripture. The pattern in 1 Chronicles 15 informs Numbers 4, showing the need for consecrated mediators. Christ transcends the Levitical order as a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek (Heb 7:11-28).
