# 1 Chronicles 1:34-54 to Genesis 36:1-43

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 1#1 Chronicles 1 . 34|1 Chronicles 1:34-54]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 36#Genesis 36 . 1|Genesis 36:1-43]]

**Subject**: Genealogies and kings of ancient Edom (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Chronicles 1 and Genesis 36, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
