# Psalms 136:5 to Proverbs 3:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 136#Psalm 136 . 5|Psalms 136:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 19|Proverbs 3:19]]

**Subject**: Made the heavens by understanding in covenantal loyalty retrospective (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Psalms 136 establishes, Proverbs 3 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
