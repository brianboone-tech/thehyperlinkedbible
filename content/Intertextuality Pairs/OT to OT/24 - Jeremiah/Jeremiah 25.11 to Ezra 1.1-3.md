# Jeremiah 25:11 to Ezra 1:1-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 11|Jeremiah 25:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 1|Ezra 1:1-3]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Jeremiah 25 and Ezra 1, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
