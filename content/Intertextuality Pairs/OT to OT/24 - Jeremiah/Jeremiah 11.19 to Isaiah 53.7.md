# Jeremiah 11:19 to Isaiah 53:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 11#Jeremiah 11 . 19|Jeremiah 11:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 7|Isaiah 53:7]]

**Subject**: lamb to slaughter

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Jeremiah 11 establishes, Isaiah 53 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
