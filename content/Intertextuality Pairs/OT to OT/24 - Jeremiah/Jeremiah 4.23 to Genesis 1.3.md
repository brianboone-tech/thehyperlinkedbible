# Jeremiah 4:23 to Genesis 1:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 4#Jeremiah 4 . 23|Jeremiah 4:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 3|Genesis 1:3]]

**Subject**: anti-creation

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link reveals how Israel's prophets used creation-reversal imagery to depict divine judgment. The uncreation motif in Genesis 1 echoes Jeremiah 4, showing that sin brings cosmic consequences. Yet Christ's work reverses the curse: where Adam's sin brought death to creation, Christ's obedience brings life (Rom 5:12-21; 8:19-22).
