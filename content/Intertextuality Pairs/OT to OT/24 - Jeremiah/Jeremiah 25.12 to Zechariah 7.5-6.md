# Jeremiah 25:12 to Zechariah 7:5-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 12|Jeremiah 25:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 7#Zechariah 7 . 5|Zechariah 7:5-6]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Jeremiah 25 and Zechariah 7, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
