# Jeremiah 13:11 to Deuteronomy 26:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 13#Jeremiah 13 . 11|Jeremiah 13:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 26#Deuteronomy 26 . 19|Deuteronomy 26:19]]

**Subject**: people as Yahweh's renown, praise, and honor

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Jeremiah 13 and Deuteronomy 26 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
