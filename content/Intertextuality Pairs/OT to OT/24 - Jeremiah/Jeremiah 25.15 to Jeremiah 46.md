# Jeremiah 25:15 to Jeremiah 46

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 15|Jeremiah 25:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Jeremiah 46

**Subject**: oracles against the nations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Jeremiah 25 and Jeremiah 46, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
