# Jeremiah 33:14 to 1 Kings 8:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 25|1 Kings 8:25]]

**Subject**: righteous branch and Levitical priest

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Jeremiah 33 and 1 Kings 8, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
