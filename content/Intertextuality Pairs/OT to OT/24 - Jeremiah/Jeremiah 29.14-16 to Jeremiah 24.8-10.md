# Jeremiah 29:14-16 to Jeremiah 24:8-10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 29#Jeremiah 29 . 14|Jeremiah 29:14-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 24#Jeremiah 24 . 8|Jeremiah 24:8-10]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Jeremiah 29 and Jeremiah 24, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
