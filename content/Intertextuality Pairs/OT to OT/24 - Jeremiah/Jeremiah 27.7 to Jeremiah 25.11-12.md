# Jeremiah 27:7 to Jeremiah 25:11-12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 27#Jeremiah 27 . 7|Jeremiah 27:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 11|Jeremiah 25:11-12]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Jeremiah 27 and Jeremiah 25, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
