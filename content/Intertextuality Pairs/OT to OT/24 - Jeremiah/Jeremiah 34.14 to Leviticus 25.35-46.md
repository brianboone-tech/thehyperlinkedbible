# Jeremiah 34:14 to Leviticus 25:35-46

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 34#Jeremiah 34 . 14|Jeremiah 34:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 35|Leviticus 25:35-46]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Jeremiah 34 and Leviticus 25 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
