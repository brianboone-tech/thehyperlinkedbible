# Jeremiah 3:1 to Deuteronomy 24:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 3#Jeremiah 3 . 1|Jeremiah 3:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 1|Deuteronomy 24:1]]

**Subject**: divorce and remarriage eliminate reconciliation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Jeremiah 3 establishes, Deuteronomy 24 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
