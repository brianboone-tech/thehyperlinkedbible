# Jeremiah 15:4 to 2 Kings 23:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 15#Jeremiah 15 . 4|Jeremiah 15:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 26|2 Kings 23:26]]

**Subject**: because of Manasseh

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Jeremiah 15 and 2 Kings 23, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
