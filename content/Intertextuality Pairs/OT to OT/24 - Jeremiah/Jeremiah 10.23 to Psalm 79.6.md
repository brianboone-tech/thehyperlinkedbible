# Jeremiah 10:23 to Psalm 79:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 23|Jeremiah 10:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 79#Psalm 79 . 6|Psalm 79:6]]

**Subject**: request for sovereign Yahweh to pour wrath on nations

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the theology of divine wrath against sin. From Jeremiah 10 to Psalm 79, Scripture reveals God's holy anger at evil. Christ absorbs God's wrath on behalf of his people (Rom 3:25; 1 Thess 1:10), demonstrating both God's justice and his love.
