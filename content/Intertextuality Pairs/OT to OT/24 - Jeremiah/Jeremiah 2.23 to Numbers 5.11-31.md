# Jeremiah 2:23 to Numbers 5:11-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 23|Jeremiah 2:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 11|Numbers 5:11-31]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Jeremiah 2 and Numbers 5 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
