# Jeremiah 33:14 to Jeremiah 31:35

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 35|Jeremiah 31:35]]

**Subject**: righteous branch and Levitical priest

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Jeremiah 33 and Jeremiah 31, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
