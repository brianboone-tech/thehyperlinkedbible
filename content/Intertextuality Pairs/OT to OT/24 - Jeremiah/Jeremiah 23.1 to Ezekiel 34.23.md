# Jeremiah 23:1 to Ezekiel 34:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 1|Jeremiah 23:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23]]

**Subject**: Davidic shepherd

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Jeremiah 23 establishes regarding David, Ezekiel 34 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
