# Jeremiah 17:7 to Psalm 1:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 7|Jeremiah 17:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 1#Psalm 1 . 3|Psalm 1:3]]

**Subject**: like a tree by water

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Jeremiah 17's portrayal of like a tree by water finds development in Psalm 1, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
