# Jeremiah 48:5 to Numbers 21:28-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 48#Jeremiah 48 . 5|Jeremiah 48:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 28|Numbers 21:28-29]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Jeremiah 48 to Numbers 21, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
