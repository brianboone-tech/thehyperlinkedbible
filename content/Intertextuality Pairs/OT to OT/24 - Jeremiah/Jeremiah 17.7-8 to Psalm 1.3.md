# Jeremiah 17:7-8 to Psalm 1:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 7|Jeremiah 17:7-8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 1#Psalm 1 . 3|Psalm 1:3]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Jeremiah 17 and Psalm 1, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
