# Jeremiah 17:21-27 to Nehemiah 13:17-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 21|Jeremiah 17:21-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 17|Nehemiah 13:17-18]]

**Subject**: Sabbath observance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Jeremiah 17's portrayal of sabbath observance finds development in Nehemiah 13, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
