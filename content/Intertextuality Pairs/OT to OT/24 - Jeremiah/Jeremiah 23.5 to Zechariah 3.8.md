# Jeremiah 23:5 to Zechariah 3:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 8|Zechariah 3:8]]

**Subject**: branch

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link develops messianic expectation through progressive revelation. What Jeremiah 23 anticipates, Zechariah 3 clarifies, building Israel's hope for God's anointed deliverer. Jesus is the Christ (Messiah), anointed by the Spirit to fulfill all that Israel's prophets, priests, and kings foreshadowed.
