# Jeremiah 27:16-22 to Ezra 1:7-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 27#Jeremiah 27 . 16|Jeremiah 27:16-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 7|Ezra 1:7-11]]

**Subject**: Exile and captivity

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Jeremiah 27 establishes about divine justice, Ezra 1 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
