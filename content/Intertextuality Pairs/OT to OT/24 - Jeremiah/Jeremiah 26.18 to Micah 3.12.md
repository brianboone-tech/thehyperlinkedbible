# Jeremiah 26:18 to Micah 3:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 26#Jeremiah 26 . 18|Jeremiah 26:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 3#Micah 3 . 12|Micah 3:12]]

**Subject**: Micah's prophecy against Jerusalem

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From Jeremiah 26 to Micah 3, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
