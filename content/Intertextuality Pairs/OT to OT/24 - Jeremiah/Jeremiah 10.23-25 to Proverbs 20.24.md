# Jeremiah 10:23-25 to Proverbs 20:24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 23|Jeremiah 10:23-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20#Proverbs 20 . 24|Proverbs 20:24]]

**Subject**: Wisdom and understanding

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The wisdom theme connects Jeremiah 10 and Proverbs 20, revealing God's order for creation and life. Wisdom personified in the OT finds ultimate expression in Christ, who is 'the wisdom of God' (1 Cor 1:24, 30), in whom 'are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge' (Col 2:3).
