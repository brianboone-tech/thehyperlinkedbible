# Jeremiah 34:14 to Leviticus 25:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 34#Jeremiah 34 . 14|Jeremiah 34:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 10|Leviticus 25:10]]

**Subject**: releasing slaves

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Jeremiah 34 informs Leviticus 25's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
