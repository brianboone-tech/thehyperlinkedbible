# Jeremiah 27:16-22 to 2 Chronicles 36:7-10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 27#Jeremiah 27 . 16|Jeremiah 27:16-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 7|2 Chronicles 36:7-10]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Jeremiah 27 to 2 Chronicles 36, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
