# Jeremiah 31:31 to Deuteronomy 29:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 1|Deuteronomy 29:1]]

**Subject**: new covenant

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Jeremiah 31 establishes, Deuteronomy 29 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
