# Jeremiah 33:14-22 to Deuteronomy 18:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 18#Deuteronomy 18 . 5|Deuteronomy 18:5]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Jeremiah 33 establishes, Deuteronomy 18 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
