# Jeremiah 23:5-6 to Zechariah 3:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 3#Zechariah 3 . 8|Zechariah 3:8]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Jeremiah 23 and Zechariah 3, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
