# Jeremiah 23:5 to Isaiah 4:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 5|Jeremiah 23:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 4#Isaiah 4 . 2|Isaiah 4:2]]

**Subject**: righteous branch

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link develops messianic expectation through progressive revelation. What Jeremiah 23 anticipates, Isaiah 4 clarifies, building Israel's hope for God's anointed deliverer. Jesus is the Christ (Messiah), anointed by the Spirit to fulfill all that Israel's prophets, priests, and kings foreshadowed.
