# Jeremiah 33:14-22 to 1 Kings 8:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 14|Jeremiah 33:14-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 25|1 Kings 8:25]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Jeremiah 33 establishes, 1 Kings 8 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
