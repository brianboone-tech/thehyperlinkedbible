# Jeremiah 1:6-9 to Exodus 4:10-12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 6|Jeremiah 1:6-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 10|Exodus 4:10-12]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Jeremiah 1 and Exodus 4 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
