# Jeremiah 49:12-13 to Obadiah 7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 49#Jeremiah 49 . 12|Jeremiah 49:12-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Obadiah 7

**Subject**: Wisdom and understanding

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The wisdom theme connects Jeremiah 49 and Obadiah 7, revealing God's order for creation and life. Wisdom personified in the OT finds ultimate expression in Christ, who is 'the wisdom of God' (1 Cor 1:24, 30), in whom 'are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge' (Col 2:3).
