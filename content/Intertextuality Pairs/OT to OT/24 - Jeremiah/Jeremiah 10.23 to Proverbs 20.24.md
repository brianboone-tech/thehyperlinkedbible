# Jeremiah 10:23 to Proverbs 20:24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 23|Jeremiah 10:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 20#Proverbs 20 . 24|Proverbs 20:24]]

**Subject**: request for sovereign Yahweh to pour wrath on nations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the theology of divine wrath against sin. From Jeremiah 10 to Proverbs 20, Scripture reveals God's holy anger at evil. Christ absorbs God's wrath on behalf of his people (Rom 3:25; 1 Thess 1:10), demonstrating both God's justice and his love.
