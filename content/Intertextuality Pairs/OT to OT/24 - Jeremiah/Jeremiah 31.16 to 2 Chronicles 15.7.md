# Jeremiah 31:16 to 2 Chronicles 15:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 16|Jeremiah 31:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 15#2 Chronicles 15 . 7|2 Chronicles 15:7]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Jeremiah 31 and 2 Chronicles 15, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
