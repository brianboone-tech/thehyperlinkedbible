# Jeremiah 2:23 to Numbers 5:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 23|Jeremiah 2:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 11|Numbers 5:11]]

**Subject**: suspected adulteress denying defilement

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Jeremiah 2 and Numbers 5 (suspected adulteress denying defilement) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
