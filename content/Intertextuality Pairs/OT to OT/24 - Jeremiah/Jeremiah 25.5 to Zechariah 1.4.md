# Jeremiah 25:5 to Zechariah 1:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 5|Jeremiah 25:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 1#Zechariah 1 . 4|Zechariah 1:4]]

**Subject**: message of the former prophets

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Jeremiah 25 and Zechariah 1, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
