# Jeremiah 33:21 to Malachi 2:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 33#Jeremiah 33 . 21|Jeremiah 33:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 8|Malachi 2:8]]

**Subject**: covenant with Levi

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Jeremiah 33 establishes, Malachi 2 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
