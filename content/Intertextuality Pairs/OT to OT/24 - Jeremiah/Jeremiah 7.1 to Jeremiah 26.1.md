# Jeremiah 7:1 to Jeremiah 26:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 7#Jeremiah 7 . 1|Jeremiah 7:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 26#Jeremiah 26 . 1|Jeremiah 26:1]]

**Subject**: temple sermon heading

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Jeremiah 7 to Jeremiah 26, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
