# Jeremiah 17:21 to Nehemiah 13:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 21|Jeremiah 17:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 13#Nehemiah 13 . 15|Nehemiah 13:15]]

**Subject**: Sabbath breaking by merchandizing

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Jeremiah 17 and Nehemiah 13, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
