# Jeremiah 25:15-26 to Jeremiah 46

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 15|Jeremiah 25:15-26]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Jeremiah 46

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Jeremiah 25 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 46 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
