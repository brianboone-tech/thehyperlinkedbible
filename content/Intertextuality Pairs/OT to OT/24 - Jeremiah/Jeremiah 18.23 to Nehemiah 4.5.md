# Jeremiah 18:23 to Nehemiah 4:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 18#Jeremiah 18 . 23|Jeremiah 18:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 4#Nehemiah 4 . 5|Nehemiah 4:5]]

**Subject**: do not forgive them

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the theme of divine mercy across Scripture. What Jeremiah 18 reveals, Nehemiah 4 expands, showing mercy as essential to God's character. Christ is the ultimate expression of mercy (Titus 3:5), through whom believers receive 'mercy upon mercy.'
