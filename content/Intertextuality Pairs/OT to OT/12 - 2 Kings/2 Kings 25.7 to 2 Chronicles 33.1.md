# 2 Kings 25:7 to 2 Chronicles 33:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 25#2 Kings 25 . 7|2 Kings 25:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 33#2 Chronicles 33 . 1|2 Chronicles 33:1]]

**Subject**: rule of Manasseh

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects 2 Kings 25 and 2 Chronicles 33, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
