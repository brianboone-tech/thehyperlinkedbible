# 2 Kings 15:1-7 to 2 Chronicles 26:3-23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 15#2 Kings 15 . 1|2 Kings 15:1-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 3|2 Chronicles 26:3-23]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 15 and 2 Chronicles 26, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
