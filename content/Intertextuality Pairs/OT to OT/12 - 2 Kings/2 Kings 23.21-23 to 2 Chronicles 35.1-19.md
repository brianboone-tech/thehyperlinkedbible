# 2 Kings 23:21-23 to 2 Chronicles 35:1-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 21|2 Kings 23:21-23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 1|2 Chronicles 35:1-19]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 2 Kings 23 establishes, 2 Chronicles 35 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
