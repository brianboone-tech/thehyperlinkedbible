# 2 Kings 11:1 to 2 Chronicles 22:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 11#2 Kings 11 . 1|2 Kings 11:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 22#2 Chronicles 22 . 10|2 Chronicles 22:10]]

**Subject**: Athaliah usurps rule

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 11 and 2 Chronicles 22, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
