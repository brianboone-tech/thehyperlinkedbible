# 2 Kings 18:32 to Deuteronomy 8:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 32|2 Kings 18:32]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 7|Deuteronomy 8:7-8]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in 2 Kings 18 informs Deuteronomy 8's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
