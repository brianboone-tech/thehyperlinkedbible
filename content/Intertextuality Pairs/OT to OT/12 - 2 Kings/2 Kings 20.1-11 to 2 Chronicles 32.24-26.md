# 2 Kings 20:1-11 to 2 Chronicles 32:24-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 20#2 Kings 20 . 1|2 Kings 20:1-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 32#2 Chronicles 32 . 24|2 Chronicles 32:24-26]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects 2 Kings 20 and 2 Chronicles 32, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
