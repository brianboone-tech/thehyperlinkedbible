# 2 Kings 24:13 to Jeremiah 27:16-22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 24#2 Kings 24 . 13|2 Kings 24:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 27#Jeremiah 27 . 16|Jeremiah 27:16-22]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. 2 Kings 24's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Jeremiah 27, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
