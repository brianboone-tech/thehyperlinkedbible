# 2 Kings 20:7-8 to Isaiah 38:21-22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 20#2 Kings 20 . 7|2 Kings 20:7-8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 38#Isaiah 38 . 21|Isaiah 38:21-22]]

**Subject**: Historical narrative and kingship

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 20 and Isaiah 38, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
