**Subject**: nearly unprecedented Passover

**Significance**: This link connects 2 Kings 23 and 2 Chronicles 30 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
