# 2 Kings 18:17-19 to Isaiah 36:2-37

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 17|2 Kings 18:17-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 36#Isaiah 36 . 2|Isaiah 36:2-37]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 18 and Isaiah 36, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
