# 2 Kings 18:5 to Isaiah 30:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 5|2 Kings 18:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 2|Isaiah 30:2]]

**Subject**: Hezekiah's faith; depending on Egypt/horses; Yahweh shall deliver Jerusalem

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in 2 Kings 18 informs Isaiah 30's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
