# 2 Kings 8:16 to 2 Chronicles 21:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 8#2 Kings 8 . 16|2 Kings 8:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 21#2 Chronicles 21 . 2|2 Chronicles 21:2]]

**Subject**: rule of Jehoram of Judah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 8 and 2 Chronicles 21, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
