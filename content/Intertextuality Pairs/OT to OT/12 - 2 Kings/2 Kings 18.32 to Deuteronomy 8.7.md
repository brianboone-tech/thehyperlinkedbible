# 2 Kings 18:32 to Deuteronomy 8:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 32|2 Kings 18:32]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 7|Deuteronomy 8:7]]

**Subject**: good land

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in 2 Kings 18 finds development in Deuteronomy 8, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
