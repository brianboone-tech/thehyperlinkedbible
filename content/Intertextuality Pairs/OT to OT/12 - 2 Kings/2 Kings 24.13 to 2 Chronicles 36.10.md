# 2 Kings 24:13 to 2 Chronicles 36:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 24#2 Kings 24 . 13|2 Kings 24:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 10|2 Chronicles 36:10]]

**Subject**: removal of temple vessels

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects 2 Kings 24 to 2 Chronicles 36, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
