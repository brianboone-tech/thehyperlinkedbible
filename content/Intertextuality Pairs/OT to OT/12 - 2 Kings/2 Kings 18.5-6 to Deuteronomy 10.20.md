# 2 Kings 18:5-6 to Deuteronomy 10:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 5|2 Kings 18:5-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 20|Deuteronomy 10:20]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 18 and Deuteronomy 10, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
