# 2 Kings 9:27-29 to 2 Chronicles 22:7-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 9#2 Kings 9 . 27|2 Kings 9:27-29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 22#2 Chronicles 22 . 7|2 Chronicles 22:7-9]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 9 and 2 Chronicles 22, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
