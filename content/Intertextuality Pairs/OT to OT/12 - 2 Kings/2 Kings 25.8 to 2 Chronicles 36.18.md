# 2 Kings 25:8 to 2 Chronicles 36:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 25#2 Kings 25 . 8|2 Kings 25:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 18|2 Chronicles 36:18]]

**Subject**: exile of Judah

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What 2 Kings 25 establishes about divine justice, 2 Chronicles 36 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
