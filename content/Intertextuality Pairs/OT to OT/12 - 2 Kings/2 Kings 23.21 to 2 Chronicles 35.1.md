# 2 Kings 23:21 to 2 Chronicles 35:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 21|2 Kings 23:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 1|2 Chronicles 35:1]]

**Subject**: Josiah keeps the Passover

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects 2 Kings 23 and 2 Chronicles 35 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
