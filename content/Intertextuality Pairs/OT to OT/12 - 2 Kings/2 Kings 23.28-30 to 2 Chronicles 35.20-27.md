# 2 Kings 23:28-30 to 2 Chronicles 35:20-27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 28|2 Kings 23:28-30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 20|2 Chronicles 35:20-27]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 23 and 2 Chronicles 35, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
