# 2 Kings 23:28 to 2 Chronicles 35:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 28|2 Kings 23:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 20|2 Chronicles 35:20]]

**Subject**: death of Josiah

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the Bible's theology of death as the consequence of sin. From 2 Kings 23 to 2 Chronicles 35, death's reality is unflinching. Christ conquers death through his own death and resurrection, swallowing up death in victory (1 Cor 15:54-57).
