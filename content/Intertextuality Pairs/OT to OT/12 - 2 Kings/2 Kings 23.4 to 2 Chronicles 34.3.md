# 2 Kings 23:4 to 2 Chronicles 34:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 34#2 Chronicles 34 . 3|2 Chronicles 34:3]]

**Subject**: cultic reforms of Jerusalem

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From 2 Kings 23 to 2 Chronicles 34, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
