# 2 Kings 21:3-5 to 2 Chronicles 33:21-25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 21#2 Kings 21 . 3|2 Kings 21:3-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 33#2 Chronicles 33 . 21|2 Chronicles 33:21-25]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. 2 Kings 21's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in 2 Chronicles 33, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
