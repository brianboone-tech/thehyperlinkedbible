# 2 Kings 14:6 to Deuteronomy 24:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 14#2 Kings 14 . 6|2 Kings 14:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 16|Deuteronomy 24:16]]

**Subject**: each dies for own sins

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in 2 Kings 14 informs Deuteronomy 24's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
