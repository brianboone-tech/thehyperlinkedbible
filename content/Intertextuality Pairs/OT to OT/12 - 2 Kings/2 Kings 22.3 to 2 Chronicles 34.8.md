# 2 Kings 22:3 to 2 Chronicles 34:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 22#2 Kings 22 . 3|2 Kings 22:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 34#2 Chronicles 34 . 8|2 Chronicles 34:8]]

**Subject**: Torah discovered in temple

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects 2 Kings 22 to 2 Chronicles 34, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
