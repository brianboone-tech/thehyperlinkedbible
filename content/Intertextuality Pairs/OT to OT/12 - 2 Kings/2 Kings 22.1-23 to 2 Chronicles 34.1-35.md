# 2 Kings 22:1-23 to 2 Chronicles 34:1-35

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 22#2 Kings 22 . 1|2 Kings 22:1-23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 34#2 Chronicles 34 . 1|2 Chronicles 34:1-35]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 22 and 2 Chronicles 34, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
