# 2 Kings 18:31 to Micah 4:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 31|2 Kings 18:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 4|Micah 4:4]]

**Subject**: under one's own vine and fig tree

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The life theme links 2 Kings 18 and Micah 4, revealing God as the source of all life. Christ is 'the resurrection and the life' (John 11:25), bringing life to the dead and abundant life to all who believe (John 10:10).
