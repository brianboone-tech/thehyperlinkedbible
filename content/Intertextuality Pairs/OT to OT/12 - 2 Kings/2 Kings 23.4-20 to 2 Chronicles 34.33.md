# 2 Kings 23:4-20 to 2 Chronicles 34:33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 23#2 Kings 23 . 4|2 Kings 23:4-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 34#2 Chronicles 34 . 33|2 Chronicles 34:33]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. 2 Kings 23's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in 2 Chronicles 34, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
