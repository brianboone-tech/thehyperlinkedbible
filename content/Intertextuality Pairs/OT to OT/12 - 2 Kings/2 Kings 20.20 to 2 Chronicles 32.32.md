# 2 Kings 20:20 to 2 Chronicles 32:32

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 20#2 Kings 20 . 20|2 Kings 20:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 32#2 Chronicles 32 . 32|2 Chronicles 32:32]]

**Subject**: death of Hezekiah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the Bible's theology of death as the consequence of sin. From 2 Kings 20 to 2 Chronicles 32, death's reality is unflinching. Christ conquers death through his own death and resurrection, swallowing up death in victory (1 Cor 15:54-57).
