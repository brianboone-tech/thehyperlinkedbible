# 2 Kings 24:18-25 to Jeremiah 52:1-34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 24#2 Kings 24 . 18|2 Kings 24:18-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 52#Jeremiah 52 . 1|Jeremiah 52:1-34]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 24 and Jeremiah 52, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
