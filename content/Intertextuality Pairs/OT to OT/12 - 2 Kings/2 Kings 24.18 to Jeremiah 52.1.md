# 2 Kings 24:18 to Jeremiah 52:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 24#2 Kings 24 . 18|2 Kings 24:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 52#Jeremiah 52 . 1|Jeremiah 52:1]]

**Subject**: fall of Jerusalem

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From 2 Kings 24 to Jeremiah 52, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
