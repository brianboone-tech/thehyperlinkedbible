# 2 Kings 18:7 to 1 Samuel 18:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 18#2 Kings 18 . 7|2 Kings 18:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 18#1 Samuel 18 . 14|1 Samuel 18:14]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Kings 18 and 1 Samuel 18, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
