# Ezekiel 47:13-48 to Joshua 13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 13|Ezekiel 47:13-48]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Joshua 13

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Ezekiel 47 finds development in Joshua 13, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
