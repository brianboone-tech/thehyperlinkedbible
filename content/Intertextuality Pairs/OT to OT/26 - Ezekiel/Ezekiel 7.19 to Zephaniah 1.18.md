# Ezekiel 7:19 to Zephaniah 1:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 7#Ezekiel 7 . 19|Ezekiel 7:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 1#Zephaniah 1 . 18|Zephaniah 1:18]]

**Subject**: silver and gold cannot deliver

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Ezekiel 7 informs Zephaniah 1's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
