# Ezekiel 16:53 to Deuteronomy 29:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 16#Ezekiel 16 . 53|Ezekiel 16:53]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 23|Deuteronomy 29:23]]

**Subject**: restoration of Sodom

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Ezekiel 16's portrayal of restoration of sodom finds development in Deuteronomy 29, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
