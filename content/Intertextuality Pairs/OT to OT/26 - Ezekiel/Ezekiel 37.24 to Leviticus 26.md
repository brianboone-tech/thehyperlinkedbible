# Ezekiel 37:24 to Leviticus 26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 24|Ezekiel 37:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Leviticus 26

**Subject**: restoration of Davidic king/prince

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezekiel 37 and Leviticus 26, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
