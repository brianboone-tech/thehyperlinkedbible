# Ezekiel 44:17-19 to Deuteronomy 22:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 17|Ezekiel 44:17-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 11|Deuteronomy 22:11]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Ezekiel 44's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Deuteronomy 22, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
