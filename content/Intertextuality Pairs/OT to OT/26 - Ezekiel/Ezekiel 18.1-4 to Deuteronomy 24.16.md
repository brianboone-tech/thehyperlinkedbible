# Ezekiel 18:1-4 to Deuteronomy 24:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 18#Ezekiel 18 . 1|Ezekiel 18:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 16|Deuteronomy 24:16]]

**Subject**: Sin and transgression

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Ezekiel 18 informs Deuteronomy 24's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
