# Ezekiel 45:25 to Exodus 23:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 25|Ezekiel 45:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 15|Exodus 23:15]]

**Subject**: Passover and Tabernacles

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Ezekiel 45 establishes about God's dwelling, Exodus 23 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
