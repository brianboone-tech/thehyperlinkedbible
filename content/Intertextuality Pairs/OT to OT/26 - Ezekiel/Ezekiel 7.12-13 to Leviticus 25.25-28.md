# Ezekiel 7:12-13 to Leviticus 25:25-28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 7#Ezekiel 7 . 12|Ezekiel 7:12-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 25|Leviticus 25:25-28]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 7 establishes about divine justice, Leviticus 25 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
