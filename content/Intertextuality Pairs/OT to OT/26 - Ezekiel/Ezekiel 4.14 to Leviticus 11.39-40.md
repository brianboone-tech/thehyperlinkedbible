# Ezekiel 4:14 to Leviticus 11:39-40

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 4#Ezekiel 4 . 14|Ezekiel 4:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 11#Leviticus 11 . 39|Leviticus 11:39-40]]

**Subject**: Ceremonial cleanness and purification

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Ezekiel 4 informs Leviticus 11's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
