# Ezekiel 44:17-19 to Exodus 28:39-43

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 17|Ezekiel 44:17-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 39|Exodus 28:39-43]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Ezekiel 44's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Exodus 28, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
