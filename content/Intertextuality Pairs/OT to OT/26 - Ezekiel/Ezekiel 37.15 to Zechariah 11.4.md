# Ezekiel 37:15 to Zechariah 11:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 15|Ezekiel 37:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 4|Zechariah 11:4]]

**Subject**: breaking the staff and appointing a bad shepherd

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezekiel 37 and Zechariah 11, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
