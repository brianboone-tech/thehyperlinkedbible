# Ezekiel 14:1-11 to Leviticus 17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 14#Ezekiel 14 . 1|Ezekiel 14:1-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Leviticus 17

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Ezekiel 14 and Leviticus 17 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
