# Ezekiel 39:28-29 to Joel 2:27-28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 39#Ezekiel 39 . 28|Ezekiel 39:28-29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 27|Joel 2:27-28]]

**Subject**: Exile and restoration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 39 establishes about divine justice, Joel 2 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
