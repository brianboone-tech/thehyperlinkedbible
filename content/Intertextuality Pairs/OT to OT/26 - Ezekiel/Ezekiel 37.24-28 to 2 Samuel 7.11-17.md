# Ezekiel 37:24-28 to 2 Samuel 7:11-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 24|Ezekiel 37:24-28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 11|2 Samuel 7:11-17]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Ezekiel 37 and 2 Samuel 7 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
