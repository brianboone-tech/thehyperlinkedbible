# Ezekiel 45:21 to Numbers 29:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 21|Ezekiel 45:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 29#Numbers 29 . 12|Numbers 29:12]]

**Subject**: Passover and Tabernacles

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Ezekiel 45 establishes about God's dwelling, Numbers 29 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
