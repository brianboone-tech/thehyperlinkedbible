# Ezekiel 44:11-12 to 2 Chronicles 35:5-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 11|Ezekiel 44:11-12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 5|2 Chronicles 35:5-6]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Ezekiel 44 to 2 Chronicles 35, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
