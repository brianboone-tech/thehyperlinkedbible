# Ezekiel 47:22 to Leviticus 19:34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 22|Ezekiel 47:22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 34|Leviticus 19:34]]

**Subject**: remapping the social identity of residing foreigners

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezekiel 47 and Leviticus 19, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
