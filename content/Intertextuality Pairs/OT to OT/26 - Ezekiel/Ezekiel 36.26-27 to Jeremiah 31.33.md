# Ezekiel 36:26-27 to Jeremiah 31:33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]]

**Subject**: Major prophets connection

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Ezekiel 36 and Jeremiah 31, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
