# Ezekiel 14:1 to Leviticus 17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 14#Ezekiel 14 . 1|Ezekiel 14:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Leviticus 17

**Subject**: heart idolatry

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 14 reveals about divine speech, Leviticus 17 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
