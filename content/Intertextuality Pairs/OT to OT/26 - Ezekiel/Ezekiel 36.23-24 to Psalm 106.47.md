# Ezekiel 36:23-24 to Psalm 106:47

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 23|Ezekiel 36:23-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 47|Psalm 106:47]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Ezekiel 36 and Psalm 106, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
