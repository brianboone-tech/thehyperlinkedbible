# Ezekiel 45:21-24 to Leviticus 23:4-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 21|Ezekiel 45:21-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 4|Leviticus 23:4-8]]

**Subject**: Passover observance and regulations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Ezekiel 45 and Leviticus 23 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
