# Ezekiel 16:53-57 to Deuteronomy 29:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 16#Ezekiel 16 . 53|Ezekiel 16:53-57]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 23|Deuteronomy 29:23]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 16 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 29 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
