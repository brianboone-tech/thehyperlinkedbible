# Ezekiel 20:9 to Psalm 106:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 20#Ezekiel 20 . 9|Ezekiel 20:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 8|Psalm 106:8]]

**Subject**: redemption for name's sake in poetic retrospective

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Ezekiel 20 to Psalm 106, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
