# Ezekiel 45:25 to Leviticus 23:33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 25|Ezekiel 45:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 33|Leviticus 23:33]]

**Subject**: Passover and Tabernacles

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Ezekiel 45 establishes about God's dwelling, Leviticus 23 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
