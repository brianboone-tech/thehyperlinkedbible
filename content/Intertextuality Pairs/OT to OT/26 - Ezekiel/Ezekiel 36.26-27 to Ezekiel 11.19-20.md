# Ezekiel 36:26-27 to Ezekiel 11:19-20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 19|Ezekiel 11:19-20]]

**Subject**: Major prophets connection

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Ezekiel 36 and Ezekiel 11, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
