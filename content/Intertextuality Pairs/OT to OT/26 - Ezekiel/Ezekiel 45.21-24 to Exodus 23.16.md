# Ezekiel 45:21-24 to Exodus 23:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 21|Ezekiel 45:21-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 16|Exodus 23:16]]

**Subject**: Passover observance and regulations

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Ezekiel 45 and Exodus 23 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
