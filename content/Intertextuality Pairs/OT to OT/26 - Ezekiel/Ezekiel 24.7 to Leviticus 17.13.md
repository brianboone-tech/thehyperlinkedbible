# Ezekiel 24:7 to Leviticus 17:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 24#Ezekiel 24 . 7|Ezekiel 24:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 13|Leviticus 17:13]]

**Subject**: pouring out blood

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The intertextual connection develops the theology of blood atonement. From Ezekiel 24 to Leviticus 17, Scripture teaches that without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness (Heb 9:22). Christ's blood is the reality to which all previous blood offerings pointed, securing eternal redemption (Heb 9:12).
