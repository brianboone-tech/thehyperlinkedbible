# Ezekiel 44:17 to Exodus 28:39

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 17|Ezekiel 44:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 39|Exodus 28:39]]

**Subject**: standards for garments of priests

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Ezekiel 44 and Exodus 28, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
