# Ezekiel 3:5-6 to Exodus 4:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 3#Ezekiel 3 . 5|Ezekiel 3:5-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 10|Exodus 4:10]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Ezekiel 3 and Exodus 4 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
