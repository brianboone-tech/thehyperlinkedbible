# Ezekiel 22:26 to Leviticus 10:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 22#Ezekiel 22 . 26|Ezekiel 22:26]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10]]

**Subject**: priests do violence against Torah

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Ezekiel 22 and Leviticus 10, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
