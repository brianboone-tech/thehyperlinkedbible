# Ezekiel 38:6 to Numbers 24:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 38#Ezekiel 38 . 6|Ezekiel 38:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 7|Numbers 24:7]]

**Subject**: identity of Gog and his militia

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Ezekiel 38 and Numbers 24 (identity of gog and his militia) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
