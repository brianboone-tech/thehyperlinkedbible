# Ezekiel 44:20 to Leviticus 21:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 20|Ezekiel 44:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 21#Leviticus 21 . 5|Leviticus 21:5]]

**Subject**: priests not shave head or grow hair long

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Ezekiel 44 and Leviticus 21, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
