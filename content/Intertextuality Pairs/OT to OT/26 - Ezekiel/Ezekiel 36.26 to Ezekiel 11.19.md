# Ezekiel 36:26 to Ezekiel 11:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 19|Ezekiel 11:19]]

**Subject**: new heart

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 36 reveals about divine speech, Ezekiel 11 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
