# Ezekiel 34:23-31 to Jeremiah 23:1-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23-31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 1|Jeremiah 23:1-6]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezekiel 34 and Jeremiah 23, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
