# Ezekiel 44:17 to Deuteronomy 22:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 17|Ezekiel 44:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 11|Deuteronomy 22:11]]

**Subject**: standards for garments of priests

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Ezekiel 44 and Deuteronomy 22, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
