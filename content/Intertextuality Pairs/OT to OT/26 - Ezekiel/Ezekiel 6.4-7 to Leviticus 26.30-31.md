# Ezekiel 6:4-7 to Leviticus 26:30-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 6#Ezekiel 6 . 4|Ezekiel 6:4-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 30|Leviticus 26:30-31]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Ezekiel 6 and Leviticus 26 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
