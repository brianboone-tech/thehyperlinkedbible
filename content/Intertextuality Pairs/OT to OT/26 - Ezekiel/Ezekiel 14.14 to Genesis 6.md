# Ezekiel 14:14 to Genesis 6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 14#Ezekiel 14 . 14|Ezekiel 14:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Genesis 6

**Subject**: even if Noah, Danel, and Job were in the land

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 14 establishes about divine justice, Genesis 6 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
