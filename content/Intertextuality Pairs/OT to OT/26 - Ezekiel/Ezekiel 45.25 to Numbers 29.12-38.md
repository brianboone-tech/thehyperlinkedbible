# Ezekiel 45:25 to Numbers 29:12-38

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 45#Ezekiel 45 . 25|Ezekiel 45:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 29#Numbers 29 . 12|Numbers 29:12-38]]

**Subject**: Passover observance and regulations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Ezekiel 45 and Numbers 29 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
