# Ezekiel 44:21 to Leviticus 10:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 21|Ezekiel 44:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 9|Leviticus 10:9]]

**Subject**: priests sober when in inner court

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Ezekiel 44 and Leviticus 10, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
