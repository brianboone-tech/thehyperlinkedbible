# Ezekiel 34:23 to Ezekiel 37:24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 24|Ezekiel 37:24]]

**Subject**: Davidic shepherd

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Ezekiel 34 establishes regarding David, Ezekiel 37 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
