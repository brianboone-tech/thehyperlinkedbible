# Ezekiel 29:17-20 to Ezekiel 26:7-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 29#Ezekiel 29 . 17|Ezekiel 29:17-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 26#Ezekiel 26 . 7|Ezekiel 26:7-14]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezekiel 29 and Ezekiel 26, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
