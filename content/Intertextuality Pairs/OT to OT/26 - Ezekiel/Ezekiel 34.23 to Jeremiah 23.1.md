# Ezekiel 34:23 to Jeremiah 23:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 1|Jeremiah 23:1]]

**Subject**: Davidic shepherd

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Ezekiel 34 establishes regarding David, Jeremiah 23 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
