# Ezekiel 6:4 to Leviticus 26:30

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 6#Ezekiel 6 . 4|Ezekiel 6:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 30|Leviticus 26:30]]

**Subject**: judgment upon cities and high places

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezekiel 6 establishes about divine justice, Leviticus 26 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
