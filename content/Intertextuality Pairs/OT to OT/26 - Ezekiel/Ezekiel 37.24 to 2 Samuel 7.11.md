# Ezekiel 37:24 to 2 Samuel 7:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 24|Ezekiel 37:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 11|2 Samuel 7:11]]

**Subject**: restoration of Davidic king/prince

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezekiel 37 and 2 Samuel 7, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
