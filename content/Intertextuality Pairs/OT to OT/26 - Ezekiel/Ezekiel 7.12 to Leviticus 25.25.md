# Ezekiel 7:12 to Leviticus 25:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 7#Ezekiel 7 . 12|Ezekiel 7:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 25|Leviticus 25:25]]

**Subject**: sellers shall not return to what is theirs

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link develops the theme of return/restoration in Israel's hope. The pattern in Ezekiel 7 informs Leviticus 25's expectation. Christ accomplishes the ultimate return from exile—not merely geographical, but spiritual—bringing God's scattered people home (Eph 2:12-13).
