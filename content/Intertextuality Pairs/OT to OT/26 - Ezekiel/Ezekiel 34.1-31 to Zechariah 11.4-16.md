# Ezekiel 34:1-31 to Zechariah 11:4-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 1|Ezekiel 34:1-31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 11#Zechariah 11 . 4|Zechariah 11:4-16]]

**Subject**: Major prophets connection

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Ezekiel 34 and Zechariah 11, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
