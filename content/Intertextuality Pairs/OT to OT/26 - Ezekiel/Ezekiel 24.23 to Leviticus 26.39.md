# Ezekiel 24:23 to Leviticus 26:39

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 24#Ezekiel 24 . 23|Ezekiel 24:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 39|Leviticus 26:39]]

**Subject**: rot in iniquity

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Ezekiel 24 informs Leviticus 26's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
