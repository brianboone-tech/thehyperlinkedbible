# Zephaniah 2:11 to Genesis 10:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 2#Zephaniah 2 . 11|Zephaniah 2:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 10#Genesis 10 . 5|Genesis 10:5]]

**Subject**: Islands of the nations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Zephaniah 2 finds development in Genesis 10, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
