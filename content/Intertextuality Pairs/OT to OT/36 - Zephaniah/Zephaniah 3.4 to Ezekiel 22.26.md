# Zephaniah 3:4 to Ezekiel 22:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 4|Zephaniah 3:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 22#Ezekiel 22 . 26|Ezekiel 22:26]]

**Subject**: Priests do violence against Torah (* see teachers network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Zephaniah 3 and Ezekiel 22, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
