# Zephaniah 1:2-3 to Genesis 1:20-28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 1#Zephaniah 1 . 2|Zephaniah 1:2-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 20|Genesis 1:20-28]]

**Subject**: Anti-creation

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link reveals how Israel's prophets used creation-reversal imagery to depict divine judgment. The uncreation motif in Genesis 1 echoes Zephaniah 1, showing that sin brings cosmic consequences. Yet Christ's work reverses the curse: where Adam's sin brought death to creation, Christ's obedience brings life (Rom 5:12-21; 8:19-22).
