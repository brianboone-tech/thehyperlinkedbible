# Hosea 5:10 to Deuteronomy 19:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 5#Hosea 5 . 10|Hosea 5:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 14|Deuteronomy 19:14]]

**Subject**: displace boundary marker

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Hosea 5 to Deuteronomy 19, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
