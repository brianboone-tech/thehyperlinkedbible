# Hosea 2:14 to Isaiah 54:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 14|Hosea 2:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 6|Isaiah 54:6]]

**Subject**: restoration like the wife of youth

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Hosea 2's portrayal of restoration like the wife of youth finds development in Isaiah 54, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
