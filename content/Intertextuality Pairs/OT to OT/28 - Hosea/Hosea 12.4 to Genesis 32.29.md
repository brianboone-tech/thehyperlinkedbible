# Hosea 12:4 to Genesis 32:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 4|Hosea 12:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 32#Genesis 32 . 29|Genesis 32:29]]

**Subject**: Jacob wrestles with a man

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Hosea 12 and Genesis 32, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
