# Hosea 8:13 to Jeremiah 14:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 8#Hosea 8 . 13|Hosea 8:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 10|Jeremiah 14:10]]

**Subject**: Yahweh remembers their wickedness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Hosea 8 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 14 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
