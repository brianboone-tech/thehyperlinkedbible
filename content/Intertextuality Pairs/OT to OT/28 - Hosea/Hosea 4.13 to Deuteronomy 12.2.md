# Hosea 4:13 to Deuteronomy 12:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 4#Hosea 4 . 13|Hosea 4:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 2|Deuteronomy 12:2]]

**Subject**: upon hills and under trees

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The life theme links Hosea 4 and Deuteronomy 12, revealing God as the source of all life. Christ is 'the resurrection and the life' (John 11:25), bringing life to the dead and abundant life to all who believe (John 10:10).
