# Hosea 3:5 to Jeremiah 30:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 3#Hosea 3 . 5|Hosea 3:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 30#Jeremiah 30 . 9|Jeremiah 30:9]]

**Subject**: renewal of Davidic rule

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Hosea 3 establishes regarding David, Jeremiah 30 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
