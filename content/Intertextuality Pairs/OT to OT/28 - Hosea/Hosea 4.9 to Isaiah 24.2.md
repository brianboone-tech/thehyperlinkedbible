# Hosea 4:9 to Isaiah 24:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 4#Hosea 4 . 9|Hosea 4:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 24#Isaiah 24 . 2|Isaiah 24:2]]

**Subject**: as people so priest

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Hosea 4 and Isaiah 24, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
