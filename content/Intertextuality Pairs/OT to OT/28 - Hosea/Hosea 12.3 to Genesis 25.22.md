# Hosea 12:3 to Genesis 25:22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 12#Hosea 12 . 3|Hosea 12:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 22|Genesis 25:22]]

**Subject**: Jacob wrestles in womb

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Hosea 12 establishes, Genesis 25 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
