# Hosea 2:14 to Jeremiah 2:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 14|Hosea 2:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 2|Jeremiah 2:2]]

**Subject**: Israel as youthful bride in the wilderness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Hosea 2 and Jeremiah 2 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
