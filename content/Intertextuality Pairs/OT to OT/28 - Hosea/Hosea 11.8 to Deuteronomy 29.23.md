# Hosea 11:8 to Deuteronomy 29:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 8|Hosea 11:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 23|Deuteronomy 29:23]]

**Subject**: like Admah and Zeboyim

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Hosea 11 and Deuteronomy 29 (like admah and zeboyim) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
