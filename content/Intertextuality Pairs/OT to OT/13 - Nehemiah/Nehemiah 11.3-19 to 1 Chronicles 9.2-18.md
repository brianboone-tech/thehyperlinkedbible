# Nehemiah 11:3-19 to 1 Chronicles 9:2-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 11#Nehemiah 11 . 3|Nehemiah 11:3-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 9#1 Chronicles 9 . 2|1 Chronicles 9:2-18]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Nehemiah 11 and 1 Chronicles 9, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
