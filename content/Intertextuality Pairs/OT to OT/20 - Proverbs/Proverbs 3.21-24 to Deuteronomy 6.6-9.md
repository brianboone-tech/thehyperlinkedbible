# Proverbs 3:21-24 to Deuteronomy 6:6-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 21|Proverbs 3:21-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 6|Deuteronomy 6:6-9]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Proverbs 3 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 6 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
