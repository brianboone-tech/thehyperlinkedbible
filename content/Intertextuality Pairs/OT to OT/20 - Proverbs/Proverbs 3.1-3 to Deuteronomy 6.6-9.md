# Proverbs 3:1-3 to Deuteronomy 6:6-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 1|Proverbs 3:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 6|Deuteronomy 6:6-9]]

**Subject**: Wisdom literature parallels

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The wisdom theme connects Proverbs 3 and Deuteronomy 6, revealing God's order for creation and life. Wisdom personified in the OT finds ultimate expression in Christ, who is 'the wisdom of God' (1 Cor 1:24, 30), in whom 'are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge' (Col 2:3).
