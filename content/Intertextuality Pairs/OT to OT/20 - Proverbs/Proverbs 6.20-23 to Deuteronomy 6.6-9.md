# Proverbs 6:20-23 to Deuteronomy 6:6-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 6#Proverbs 6 . 20|Proverbs 6:20-23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 6|Deuteronomy 6:6-9]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Proverbs 6 and Deuteronomy 6 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
