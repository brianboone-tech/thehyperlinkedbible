# Proverbs 7:1 to Deuteronomy 6:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 7#Proverbs 7 . 1|Proverbs 7:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 6|Deuteronomy 6:6]]

**Subject**: Instructing the next generation (B)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Proverbs 7 and Deuteronomy 6 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
