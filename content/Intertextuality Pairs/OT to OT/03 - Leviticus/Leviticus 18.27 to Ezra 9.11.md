# Leviticus 18:27 to Ezra 9:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 27|Leviticus 18:27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 9#Ezra 9 . 11|Ezra 9:11]]

**Subject**: intermarriage pollution

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Leviticus 18 establishes, Ezra 9 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
