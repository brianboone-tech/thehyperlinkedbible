# Leviticus 21:1-3 to Ezekiel 44:24-27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 21#Leviticus 21 . 1|Leviticus 21:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 24|Ezekiel 44:24-27]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 21 and Ezekiel 44 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
