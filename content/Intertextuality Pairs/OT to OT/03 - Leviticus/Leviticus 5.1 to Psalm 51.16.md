# Leviticus 5:1 to Psalm 51:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 1|Leviticus 5:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 51#Psalm 51 . 16|Psalm 51:16]]

**Subject**: sacrifice insufficiency

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 5 establishes, Psalm 51 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
