# Leviticus 26:40 to Psalm 106:44

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 44|Psalm 106:44]]

**Subject**: covenant remembrance

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Leviticus 26 establishes, Psalm 106 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
