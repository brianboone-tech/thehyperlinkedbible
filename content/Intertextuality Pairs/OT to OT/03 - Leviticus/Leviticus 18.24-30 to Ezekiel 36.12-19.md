# Leviticus 18:24-30 to Ezekiel 36:12-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 24|Leviticus 18:24-30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 12|Ezekiel 36:12-19]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Leviticus 18 finds development in Ezekiel 36, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
