# Leviticus 17:13 to Ezekiel 24:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 13|Leviticus 17:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 24#Ezekiel 24 . 7|Ezekiel 24:7-8]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 17 and Ezekiel 24 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
