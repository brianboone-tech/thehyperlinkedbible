# Leviticus 25:35-46 to Jeremiah 34:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 35|Leviticus 25:35-46]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 34#Jeremiah 34 . 14|Jeremiah 34:14]]

**Subject**: Exile and restoration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 25 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 34 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
