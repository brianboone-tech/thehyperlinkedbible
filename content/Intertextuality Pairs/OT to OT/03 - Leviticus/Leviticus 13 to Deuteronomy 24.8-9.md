# Leviticus 13 to Deuteronomy 24:8-9

**Text**: Leviticus 13

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 8|Deuteronomy 24:8-9]]

**Subject**: Law and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 13 and Deuteronomy 24 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
