# Leviticus 27:30-33 to Numbers 18:21-24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 27#Leviticus 27 . 30|Leviticus 27:30-33]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 21|Numbers 18:21-24]]

**Subject**: Exile and restoration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 27 establishes about divine justice, Numbers 18 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
