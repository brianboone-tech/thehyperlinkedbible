# Leviticus 10:10-11 to Ezekiel 44:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 23|Ezekiel 44:23]]

**Subject**: Ceremonial cleanness and purification

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Leviticus 10 informs Ezekiel 44's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
