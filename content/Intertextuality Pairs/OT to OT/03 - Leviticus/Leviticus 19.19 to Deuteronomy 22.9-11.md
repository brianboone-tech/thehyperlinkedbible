# Leviticus 19:19 to Deuteronomy 22:9-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 19|Leviticus 19:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 9|Deuteronomy 22:9-11]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Leviticus 19's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Deuteronomy 22, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
