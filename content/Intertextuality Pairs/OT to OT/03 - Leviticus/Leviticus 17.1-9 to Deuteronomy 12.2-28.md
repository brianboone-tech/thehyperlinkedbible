# Leviticus 17:1-9 to Deuteronomy 12:2-28

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 17#Leviticus 17 . 1|Leviticus 17:1-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 2|Deuteronomy 12:2-28]]

**Subject**: Nations and God's plan

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Leviticus 17 and Deuteronomy 12, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
