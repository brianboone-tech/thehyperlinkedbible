# Leviticus 25:25 to Ezekiel 7:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 25|Leviticus 25:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 7#Ezekiel 7 . 12|Ezekiel 7:12]]

**Subject**: irreversible sales

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Leviticus 25 and Ezekiel 7 (irreversible sales) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
