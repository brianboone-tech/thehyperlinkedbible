# Leviticus 20:9 to Deuteronomy 21:18-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 20#Leviticus 20 . 9|Leviticus 20:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 18|Deuteronomy 21:18-21]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Leviticus 20 and Deuteronomy 21, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
