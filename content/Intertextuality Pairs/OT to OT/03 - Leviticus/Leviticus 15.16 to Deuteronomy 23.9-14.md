# Leviticus 15:16 to Deuteronomy 23:9-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 15#Leviticus 15 . 16|Leviticus 15:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 9|Deuteronomy 23:9-14]]

**Subject**: Warfare and divine victory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 15 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 23 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
