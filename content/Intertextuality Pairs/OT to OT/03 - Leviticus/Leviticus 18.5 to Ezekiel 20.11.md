# Leviticus 18:5 to Ezekiel 20:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 5|Leviticus 18:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 20#Ezekiel 20 . 11|Ezekiel 20:11]]

**Subject**: life through ordinances

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The life theme links Leviticus 18 and Ezekiel 20, revealing God as the source of all life. Christ is 'the resurrection and the life' (John 11:25), bringing life to the dead and abundant life to all who believe (John 10:10).
