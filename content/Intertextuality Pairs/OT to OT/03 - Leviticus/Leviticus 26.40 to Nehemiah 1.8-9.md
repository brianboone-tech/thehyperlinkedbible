# Leviticus 26:40 to Nehemiah 1:8-9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 1#Nehemiah 1 . 8|Nehemiah 1:8-9]]

**Subject**: Sin and transgression

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Leviticus 26 informs Nehemiah 1's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
