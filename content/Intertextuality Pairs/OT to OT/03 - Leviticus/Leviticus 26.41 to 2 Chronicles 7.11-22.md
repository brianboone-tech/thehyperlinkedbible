# Leviticus 26:41 to 2 Chronicles 7:11-22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 41|Leviticus 26:41]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 11|2 Chronicles 7:11-22]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Leviticus 26 and 2 Chronicles 7, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
