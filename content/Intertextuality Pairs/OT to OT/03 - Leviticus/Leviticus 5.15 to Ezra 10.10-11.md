# Leviticus 5:15 to Ezra 10:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 15|Leviticus 5:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 10#Ezra 10 . 10|Ezra 10:10-11]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Leviticus 5 to Ezra 10, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
