# Leviticus 19:15 to Psalm 82:1-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 15|Leviticus 19:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 82#Psalm 82 . 1|Psalm 82:1-6]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 19 establishes about divine justice, Psalm 82 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
