# Leviticus 17 to Ezekiel 14:1

**Text**: Leviticus 17

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 14#Ezekiel 14 . 1|Ezekiel 14:1]]

**Subject**: heart idolatry

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 17 reveals about divine speech, Ezekiel 14 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
