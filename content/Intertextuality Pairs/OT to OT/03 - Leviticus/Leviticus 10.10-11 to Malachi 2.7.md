# Leviticus 10:10-11 to Malachi 2:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 2#Malachi 2 . 7|Malachi 2:7]]

**Subject**: Wisdom and understanding

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The wisdom theme connects Leviticus 10 and Malachi 2, revealing God's order for creation and life. Wisdom personified in the OT finds ultimate expression in Christ, who is 'the wisdom of God' (1 Cor 1:24, 30), in whom 'are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge' (Col 2:3).
