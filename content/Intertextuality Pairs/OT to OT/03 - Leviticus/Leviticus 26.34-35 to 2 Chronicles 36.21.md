# Leviticus 26:34-35 to 2 Chronicles 36:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 34|Leviticus 26:34-35]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 36#2 Chronicles 36 . 21|2 Chronicles 36:21]]

**Subject**: Warfare and divine victory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 26 establishes about divine justice, 2 Chronicles 36 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
