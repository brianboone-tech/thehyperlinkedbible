# Leviticus 1:5 to 2 Chronicles 30:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 1#Leviticus 1 . 5|Leviticus 1:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 30#2 Chronicles 30 . 16|2 Chronicles 30:16]]

**Subject**: blood ritual procedures

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The intertextual connection develops the theology of blood atonement. From Leviticus 1 to 2 Chronicles 30, Scripture teaches that without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness (Heb 9:22). Christ's blood is the reality to which all previous blood offerings pointed, securing eternal redemption (Heb 9:12).
