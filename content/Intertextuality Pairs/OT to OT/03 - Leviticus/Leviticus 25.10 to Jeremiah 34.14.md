# Leviticus 25:10 to Jeremiah 34:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 10|Leviticus 25:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 34#Jeremiah 34 . 14|Jeremiah 34:14]]

**Subject**: slave release mandate

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Leviticus 25 and Jeremiah 34, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
