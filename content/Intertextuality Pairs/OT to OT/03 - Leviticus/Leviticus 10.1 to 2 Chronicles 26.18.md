# Leviticus 10:1 to 2 Chronicles 26:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 1|Leviticus 10:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 18|2 Chronicles 26:18]]

**Subject**: holy/common distinction

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Leviticus 10 and 2 Chronicles 26, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
