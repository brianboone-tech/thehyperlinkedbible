# Leviticus 26:13 to Ezekiel 34:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 13|Leviticus 26:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 23|Ezekiel 34:23]]

**Subject**: Davidic shepherding

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What Leviticus 26 establishes regarding David, Ezekiel 34 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
