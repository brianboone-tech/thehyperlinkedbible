# Leviticus 26:41 to 2 Chronicles 7:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 41|Leviticus 26:41]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 11|2 Chronicles 7:11]]

**Subject**: conditional blessings

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Leviticus 26 and 2 Chronicles 7, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
