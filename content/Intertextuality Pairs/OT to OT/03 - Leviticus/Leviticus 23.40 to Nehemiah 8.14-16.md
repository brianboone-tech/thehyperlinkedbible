# Leviticus 23:40 to Nehemiah 8:14-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 40|Leviticus 23:40]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 8#Nehemiah 8 . 14|Nehemiah 8:14-16]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 23 and Nehemiah 8 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
