# Leviticus 10:10-11 to Haggai 2:11-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 11|Haggai 2:11-14]]

**Subject**: Ceremonial cleanness and purification

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Leviticus 10 informs Haggai 2's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
