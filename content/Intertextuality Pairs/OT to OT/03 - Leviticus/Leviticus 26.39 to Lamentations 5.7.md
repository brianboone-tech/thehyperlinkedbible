# Leviticus 26:39 to Lamentations 5:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 39|Leviticus 26:39]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 5#Lamentations 5 . 7|Lamentations 5:7]]

**Subject**: ancestral guilt bearing

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Leviticus 26 informs Lamentations 5's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
