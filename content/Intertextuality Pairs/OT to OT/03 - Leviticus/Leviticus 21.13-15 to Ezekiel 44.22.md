# Leviticus 21:13-15 to Ezekiel 44:22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 21#Leviticus 21 . 13|Leviticus 21:13-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 44#Ezekiel 44 . 22|Ezekiel 44:22]]

**Subject**: Priestly service and duties

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Leviticus 21 and Ezekiel 44, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
