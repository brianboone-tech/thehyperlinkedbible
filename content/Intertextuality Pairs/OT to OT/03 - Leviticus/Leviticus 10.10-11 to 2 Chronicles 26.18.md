# Leviticus 10:10-11 to 2 Chronicles 26:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 26#2 Chronicles 26 . 18|2 Chronicles 26:18]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 10 and 2 Chronicles 26 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
