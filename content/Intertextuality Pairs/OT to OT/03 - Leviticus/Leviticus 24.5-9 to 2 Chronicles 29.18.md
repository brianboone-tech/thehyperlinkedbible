# Leviticus 24:5-9 to 2 Chronicles 29:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 5|Leviticus 24:5-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 29#2 Chronicles 29 . 18|2 Chronicles 29:18]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Leviticus 24 and 2 Chronicles 29, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
