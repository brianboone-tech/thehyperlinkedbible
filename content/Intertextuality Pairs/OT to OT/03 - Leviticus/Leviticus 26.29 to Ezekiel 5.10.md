# Leviticus 26:29 to Ezekiel 5:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 29|Leviticus 26:29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 5#Ezekiel 5 . 10|Ezekiel 5:10]]

**Subject**: cannibalism judgment

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 26 establishes about divine justice, Ezekiel 5 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
