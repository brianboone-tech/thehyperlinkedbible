# Leviticus 11:1-28 to Deuteronomy 14:3-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 11#Leviticus 11 . 1|Leviticus 11:1-28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 14#Deuteronomy 14 . 3|Deuteronomy 14:3-21]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Leviticus 11 and Deuteronomy 14, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
