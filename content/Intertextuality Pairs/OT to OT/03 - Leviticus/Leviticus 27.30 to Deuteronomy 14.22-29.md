# Leviticus 27:30 to Deuteronomy 14:22-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 27#Leviticus 27 . 30|Leviticus 27:30]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 14#Deuteronomy 14 . 22|Deuteronomy 14:22-29]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Leviticus 27 finds development in Deuteronomy 14, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
