# Leviticus 5:15 to Ezra 10:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 15|Leviticus 5:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 10#Ezra 10 . 10|Ezra 10:10]]

**Subject**: reparation offering context

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 5 establishes, Ezra 10 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
