# Leviticus 10:10 to Ezekiel 22:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 22#Ezekiel 22 . 26|Ezekiel 22:26]]

**Subject**: Torah violation consequences

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Leviticus 10 and Ezekiel 22 (torah violation consequences) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
