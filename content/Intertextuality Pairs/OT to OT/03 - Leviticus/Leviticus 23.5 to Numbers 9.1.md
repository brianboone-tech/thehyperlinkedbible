# Leviticus 23:5 to Numbers 9:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 5|Leviticus 23:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 9#Numbers 9 . 1|Numbers 9:1]]

**Subject**: Passover timing

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects Leviticus 23 and Numbers 9 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
