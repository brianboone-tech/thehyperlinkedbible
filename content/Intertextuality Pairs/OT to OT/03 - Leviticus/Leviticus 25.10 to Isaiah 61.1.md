# Leviticus 25:10 to Isaiah 61:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 10|Leviticus 25:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 61#Isaiah 61 . 1|Isaiah 61:1]]

**Subject**: Jubilee proclamation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Leviticus 25 and Isaiah 61 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
