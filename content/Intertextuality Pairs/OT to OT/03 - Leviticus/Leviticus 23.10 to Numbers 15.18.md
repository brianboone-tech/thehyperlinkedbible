# Leviticus 23:10 to Numbers 15:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 10|Leviticus 23:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 18|Numbers 15:18]]

**Subject**: firstfruits offering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Leviticus 23 establishes, Numbers 15 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
