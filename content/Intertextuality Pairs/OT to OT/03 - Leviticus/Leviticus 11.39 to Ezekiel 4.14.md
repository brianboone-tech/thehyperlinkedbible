# Leviticus 11:39 to Ezekiel 4:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 11#Leviticus 11 . 39|Leviticus 11:39]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 4#Ezekiel 4 . 14|Ezekiel 4:14]]

**Subject**: avoiding impure diet

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Leviticus 11 and Ezekiel 4, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
