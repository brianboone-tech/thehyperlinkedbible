# Leviticus 19:13 to Deuteronomy 24:14-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 13|Leviticus 19:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 14|Deuteronomy 24:14-15]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 19 and Deuteronomy 24 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
