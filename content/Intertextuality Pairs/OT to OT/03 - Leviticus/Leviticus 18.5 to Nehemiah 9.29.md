# Leviticus 18:5 to Nehemiah 9:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 5|Leviticus 18:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 29|Nehemiah 9:29]]

**Subject**: covenant rebellion cycles

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Leviticus 18 establishes, Nehemiah 9 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
