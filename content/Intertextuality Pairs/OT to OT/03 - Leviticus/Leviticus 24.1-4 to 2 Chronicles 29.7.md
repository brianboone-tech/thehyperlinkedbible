# Leviticus 24:1-4 to 2 Chronicles 29:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 1|Leviticus 24:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 29#2 Chronicles 29 . 7|2 Chronicles 29:7]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 24 and 2 Chronicles 29 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
