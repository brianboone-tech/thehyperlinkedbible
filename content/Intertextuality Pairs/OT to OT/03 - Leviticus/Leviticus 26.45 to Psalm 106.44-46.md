# Leviticus 26:45 to Psalm 106:44-46

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 45|Leviticus 26:45]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 44|Psalm 106:44-46]]

**Subject**: Sin and transgression

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Leviticus 26 informs Psalm 106's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
