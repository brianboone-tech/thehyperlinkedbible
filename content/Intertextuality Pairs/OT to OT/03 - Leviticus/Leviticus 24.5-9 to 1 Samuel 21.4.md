# Leviticus 24:5-9 to 1 Samuel 21:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 5|Leviticus 24:5-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 21#1 Samuel 21 . 4|1 Samuel 21:4]]

**Subject**: Priestly service and duties

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Leviticus 24 and 1 Samuel 21, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
