# Leviticus 10:10 to Haggai 2:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 10#Leviticus 10 . 10|Leviticus 10:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 11|Haggai 2:11]]

**Subject**: holy/unclean rulings

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Leviticus 10 and Haggai 2, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
