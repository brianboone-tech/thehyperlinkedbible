# Leviticus 6:12-13 to Numbers 28:3-8

**Source Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 6#Leviticus 6 . 12|Leviticus 6:12-13]]

**Target Text**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 28#Numbers 28 . 3|Numbers 28:3-8]]

**Confidence**: A - Certain

**Type**: // Direct verbal parallel

**Subject**: Perpetual altar fire enables continual pleasing aroma through daily burnt offerings

**Explanation**: Leviticus 6:12-13 commands that the fire on the altar must never go out, burning perpetually. Numbers 28:3-8 prescribes the daily burnt offering (tamid) that maintains this perpetual fire, producing a continual pleasing aroma to YHWH. The connection establishes that Israel's worship was to be unceasing—morning and evening sacrifices ensured constant propitiation and divine favor. This perpetual fire and aroma typologically anticipate Christ's once-for-all sacrifice that eternally satisfies God and the believer's continuous access to divine presence.
