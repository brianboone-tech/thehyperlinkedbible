# Leviticus 27:1-25 to Numbers 30:1-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 27#Leviticus 27 . 1|Leviticus 27:1-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 30#Numbers 30 . 1|Numbers 30:1-16]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 27 and Numbers 30 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
