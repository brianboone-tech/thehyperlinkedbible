# Leviticus 11 to Numbers 5:1-4

**Text**: Leviticus 11

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 5#Numbers 5 . 1|Numbers 5:1-4]]

**Subject**: Law and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 11 and Numbers 5 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
