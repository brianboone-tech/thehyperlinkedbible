# Leviticus 1:4 to Psalm 51:16-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 1#Leviticus 1 . 4|Leviticus 1:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 51#Psalm 51 . 16|Psalm 51:16-17]]

**Subject**: Worship and theological reflection

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 1 and Psalm 51 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
