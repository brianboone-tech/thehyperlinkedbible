# Leviticus 5:1-6 to Psalm 51:16-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 1|Leviticus 5:1-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 51#Psalm 51 . 16|Psalm 51:16-17]]

**Subject**: Sin and transgression

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Leviticus 5 informs Psalm 51's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
