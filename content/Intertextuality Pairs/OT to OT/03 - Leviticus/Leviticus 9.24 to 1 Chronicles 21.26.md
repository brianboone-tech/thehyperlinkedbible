# Leviticus 9:24 to 1 Chronicles 21:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 9#Leviticus 9 . 24|Leviticus 9:24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 21#1 Chronicles 21 . 26|1 Chronicles 21:26]]

**Subject**: divine fire manifestation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Leviticus 9 and 1 Chronicles 21, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
