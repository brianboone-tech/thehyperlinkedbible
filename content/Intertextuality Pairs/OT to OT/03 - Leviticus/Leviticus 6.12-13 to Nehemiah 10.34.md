# Leviticus 6:12-13 to Nehemiah 10:34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 6#Leviticus 6 . 12|Leviticus 6:12-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 34|Nehemiah 10:34]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Leviticus 6 and Nehemiah 10 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
