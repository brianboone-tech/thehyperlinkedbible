# Obadiah 1 to Jeremiah 49:14-16

**Text**: Obadiah 1

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 49#Jeremiah 49 . 14|Jeremiah 49:14-16]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment on nations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Obadiah 1 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 49 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
