# Obadiah 17 to Joel 3:17

**Text**: Obadiah 17

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 3#Joel 3 . 17|Joel 3:17]]

**Subject**: Zion shall be holy

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Obadiah 17 and Joel 3, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
