# Obadiah 5 to Jeremiah 49:9-10

**Text**: Obadiah 5

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 49#Jeremiah 49 . 9|Jeremiah 49:9-10]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment on nations

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Obadiah 5 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 49 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
