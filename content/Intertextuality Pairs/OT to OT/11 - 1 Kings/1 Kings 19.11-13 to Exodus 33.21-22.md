# 1 Kings 19:11-13 to Exodus 33:21-22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 11|1 Kings 19:11-13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 21|Exodus 33:21-22]]

**Subject**: Historical narrative and kingship

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 19 and Exodus 33, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
