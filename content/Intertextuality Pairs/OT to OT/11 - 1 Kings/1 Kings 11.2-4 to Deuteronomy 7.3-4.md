# 1 Kings 11:2-4 to Deuteronomy 7:3-4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 2|1 Kings 11:2-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 3|Deuteronomy 7:3-4]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Kings 11 establishes, Deuteronomy 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
