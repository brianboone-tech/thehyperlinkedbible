# 1 Kings 8:15-21 to 2 Samuel 7:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 15|1 Kings 8:15-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 8|2 Samuel 7:8]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 8 and 2 Samuel 7, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
