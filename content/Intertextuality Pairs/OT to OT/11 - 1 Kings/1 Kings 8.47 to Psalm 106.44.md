# 1 Kings 8:47 to Psalm 106:44

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 47|1 Kings 8:47]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 44|Psalm 106:44]]

**Subject**: remembering the covenant in poetic retrospective

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Kings 8 establishes, Psalm 106 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
