# 1 Kings 1:45 to 2 Samuel 6:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 1#1 Kings 1 . 45|1 Kings 1:45]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 6#2 Samuel 6 . 17|2 Samuel 6:17]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 1 and 2 Samuel 6, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
