# 1 Kings 2:10-11 to 1 Chronicles 29:27-30

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 2#1 Kings 2 . 10|1 Kings 2:10-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 29#1 Chronicles 29 . 27|1 Chronicles 29:27-30]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 2 and 1 Chronicles 29, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
