# 1 Kings 16:5 to Isaiah 7:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 16#1 Kings 16 . 5|1 Kings 16:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 1|Isaiah 7:1]]

**Subject**: collaborative opposition of Rezin of Damascus and Pekah of Israel

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Kings 16 establishes, Isaiah 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
