# 1 Kings 1:5 to 2 Samuel 3:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 1#1 Kings 1 . 5|1 Kings 1:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 3#2 Samuel 3 . 4|2 Samuel 3:4]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 1 and 2 Samuel 3, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
