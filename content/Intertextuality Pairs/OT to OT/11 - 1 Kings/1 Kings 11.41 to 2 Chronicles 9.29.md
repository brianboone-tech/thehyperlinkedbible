# 1 Kings 11:41 to 2 Chronicles 9:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 41|1 Kings 11:41]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 9#2 Chronicles 9 . 29|2 Chronicles 9:29]]

**Subject**: death of Solomon

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the Bible's theology of death as the consequence of sin. From 1 Kings 11 to 2 Chronicles 9, death's reality is unflinching. Christ conquers death through his own death and resurrection, swallowing up death in victory (1 Cor 15:54-57).
