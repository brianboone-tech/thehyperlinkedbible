# 1 Kings 14:21-31 to 2 Chronicles 12:1-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 14#1 Kings 14 . 21|1 Kings 14:21-31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 12#2 Chronicles 12 . 1|2 Chronicles 12:1-16]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between 1 Kings 14 and 2 Chronicles 12 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
