# 1 Kings 11:41-43 to 2 Chronicles 9:29-31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 41|1 Kings 11:41-43]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 9#2 Chronicles 9 . 29|2 Chronicles 9:29-31]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. 1 Kings 11's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in 2 Chronicles 9, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
