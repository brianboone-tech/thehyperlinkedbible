# 1 Kings 11:1 to Deuteronomy 23:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 1|1 Kings 11:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 7|Deuteronomy 23:7]]

**Subject**: women of Egypt and Edom as well as Ammon and Moab

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link connects 1 Kings 11 and Deuteronomy 23 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
