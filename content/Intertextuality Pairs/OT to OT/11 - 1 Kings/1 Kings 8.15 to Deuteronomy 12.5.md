# 1 Kings 8:15 to Deuteronomy 12:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 15|1 Kings 8:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 5|Deuteronomy 12:5]]

**Subject**: choosing a place and choosing David

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Kings 8 establishes regarding David, Deuteronomy 12 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
