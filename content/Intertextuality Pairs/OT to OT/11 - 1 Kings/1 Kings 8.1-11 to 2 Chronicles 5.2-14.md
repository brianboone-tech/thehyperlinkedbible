# 1 Kings 8:1-11 to 2 Chronicles 5:2-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 1|1 Kings 8:1-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 5#2 Chronicles 5 . 2|2 Chronicles 5:2-14]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 1 Kings 8 establishes, 2 Chronicles 5 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
