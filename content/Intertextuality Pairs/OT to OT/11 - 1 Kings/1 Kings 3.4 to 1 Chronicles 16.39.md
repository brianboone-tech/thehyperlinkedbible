# 1 Kings 3:4 to 1 Chronicles 16:39

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 3#1 Kings 3 . 4|1 Kings 3:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 16#1 Chronicles 16 . 39|1 Chronicles 16:39]]

**Subject**: sacrifices at tabernacle at Gibeon

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What 1 Kings 3 establishes about God's dwelling, 1 Chronicles 16 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
