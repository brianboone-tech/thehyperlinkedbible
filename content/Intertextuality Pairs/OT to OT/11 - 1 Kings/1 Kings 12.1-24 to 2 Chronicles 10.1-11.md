# 1 Kings 12:1-24 to 2 Chronicles 10:1-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 12#1 Kings 12 . 1|1 Kings 12:1-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 10#2 Chronicles 10 . 1|2 Chronicles 10:1-11]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 12 and 2 Chronicles 10, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
