# 1 Kings 15:9-24 to 2 Chronicles 14:1-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 15#1 Kings 15 . 9|1 Kings 15:9-24]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 14#2 Chronicles 14 . 1|2 Chronicles 14:1-16]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 15 and 2 Chronicles 14, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
