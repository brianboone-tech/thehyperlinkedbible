# 1 Kings 2:10 to 1 Chronicles 29:27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 2#1 Kings 2 . 10|1 Kings 2:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 29#1 Chronicles 29 . 27|1 Chronicles 29:27]]

**Subject**: death of David

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Kings 2 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 29 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
