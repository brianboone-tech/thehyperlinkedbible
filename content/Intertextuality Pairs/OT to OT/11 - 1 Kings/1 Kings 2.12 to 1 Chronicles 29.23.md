# 1 Kings 2:12 to 1 Chronicles 29:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 2#1 Kings 2 . 12|1 Kings 2:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 29#1 Chronicles 29 . 23|1 Chronicles 29:23]]

**Subject**: Solomon sits upon throne

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces the throne motif through Israel's Scriptures. From 1 Kings 2 to 1 Chronicles 29, the expectation builds of a righteous King on David's throne. Christ now reigns at the Father's right hand (Eph 1:20-21), and will visibly reign when he returns to establish his kingdom fully.
