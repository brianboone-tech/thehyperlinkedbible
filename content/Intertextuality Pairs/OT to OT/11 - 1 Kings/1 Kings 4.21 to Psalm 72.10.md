# 1 Kings 4:21 to Psalm 72:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 21|1 Kings 4:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 10|Psalm 72:10]]

**Subject**: all kings bring tribute and serve

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 4 and Psalm 72, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
