# 1 Kings 8:47 to Psalm 106:44-46

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 47|1 Kings 8:47]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 44|Psalm 106:44-46]]

**Subject**: Worship and theological reflection

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between 1 Kings 8 and Psalm 106 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
