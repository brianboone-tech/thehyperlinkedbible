# 1 Kings 4:31 to 1 Chronicles 2:3-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 31|1 Kings 4:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3-17]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 4 and 1 Chronicles 2, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
