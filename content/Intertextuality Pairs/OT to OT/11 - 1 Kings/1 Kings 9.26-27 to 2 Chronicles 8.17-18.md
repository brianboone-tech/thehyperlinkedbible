# 1 Kings 9:26-27 to 2 Chronicles 8:17-18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 9#1 Kings 9 . 26|1 Kings 9:26-27]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 8#2 Chronicles 8 . 17|2 Chronicles 8:17-18]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 9 and 2 Chronicles 8, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
