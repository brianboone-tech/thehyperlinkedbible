# 1 Kings 14:21 to 2 Chronicles 12:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 14#1 Kings 14 . 21|1 Kings 14:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 12#2 Chronicles 12 . 1|2 Chronicles 12:1]]

**Subject**: Shishak attacks Jerusalem

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From 1 Kings 14 to 2 Chronicles 12, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
