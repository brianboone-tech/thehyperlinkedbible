# 1 Kings 6:2-38 to 2 Chronicles 3:3-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 6#1 Kings 6 . 2|1 Kings 6:2-38]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 3#2 Chronicles 3 . 3|2 Chronicles 3:3-14]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 6 and 2 Chronicles 3, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
