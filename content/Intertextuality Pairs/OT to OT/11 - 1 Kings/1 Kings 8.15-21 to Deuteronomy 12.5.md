# 1 Kings 8:15-21 to Deuteronomy 12:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 15|1 Kings 8:15-21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 5|Deuteronomy 12:5]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 8 and Deuteronomy 12, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
