# 1 Kings 22:41-51 to 2 Chronicles 20:31-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 22#1 Kings 22 . 41|1 Kings 22:41-51]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 20#2 Chronicles 20 . 31|2 Chronicles 20:31-21]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 22 and 2 Chronicles 20, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
