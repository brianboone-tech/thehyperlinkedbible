# 1 Kings 5:1-5 to 2 Samuel 5:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 5#1 Kings 5 . 1|1 Kings 5:1-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 11|2 Samuel 5:11]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 5 and 2 Samuel 5, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
