# 1 Kings 8:62 to 2 Chronicles 7:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 62|1 Kings 8:62]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 7#2 Chronicles 7 . 4|2 Chronicles 7:4]]

**Subject**: dedicate altar and celebrate Tabernacles

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What 1 Kings 8 establishes about God's dwelling, 2 Chronicles 7 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
