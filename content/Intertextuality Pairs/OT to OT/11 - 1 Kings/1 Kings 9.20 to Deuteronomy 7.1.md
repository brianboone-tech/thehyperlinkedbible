# 1 Kings 9:20 to Deuteronomy 7:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 9#1 Kings 9 . 20|1 Kings 9:20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 1|Deuteronomy 7:1]]

**Subject**: enslaving remaining peoples of Canaan

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects 1 Kings 9 and Deuteronomy 7, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
