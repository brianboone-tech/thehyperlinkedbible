# 1 Kings 11:15-16 to Psalm 60

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 15|1 Kings 11:15-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Psalm 60

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 11 and Psalm 60, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
