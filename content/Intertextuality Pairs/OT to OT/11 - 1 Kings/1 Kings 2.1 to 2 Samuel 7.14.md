# 1 Kings 2:1 to 2 Samuel 7:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 2#1 Kings 2 . 1|1 Kings 2:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14]]

**Subject**: David commissions Solomon

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Kings 2 establishes regarding David, 2 Samuel 7 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
