# 1 Kings 5:3 to Psalm 110:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 5#1 Kings 5 . 3|1 Kings 5:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]]

**Subject**: enemies under feet of Davidic son

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Kings 5 establishes regarding David, Psalm 110 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
