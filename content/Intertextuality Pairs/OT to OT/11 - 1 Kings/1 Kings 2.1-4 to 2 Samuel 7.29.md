# 1 Kings 2:1-4 to 2 Samuel 7:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 2#1 Kings 2 . 1|1 Kings 2:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 29|2 Samuel 7:29]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 1 Kings 2 and 2 Samuel 7, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
