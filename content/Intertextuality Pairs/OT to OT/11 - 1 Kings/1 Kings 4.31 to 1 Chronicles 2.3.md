# 1 Kings 4:31 to 1 Chronicles 2:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 31|1 Kings 4:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3]]

**Subject**: genealogies of Judah through family of David

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 1 Kings 4 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 2 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
