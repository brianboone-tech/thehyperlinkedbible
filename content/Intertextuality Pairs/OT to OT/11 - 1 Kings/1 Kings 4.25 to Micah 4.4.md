# 1 Kings 4:25 to Micah 4:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 4#1 Kings 4 . 25|1 Kings 4:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 4#Micah 4 . 4|Micah 4:4]]

**Subject**: sit under vine and fig tree

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The life theme links 1 Kings 4 and Micah 4, revealing God as the source of all life. Christ is 'the resurrection and the life' (John 11:25), bringing life to the dead and abundant life to all who believe (John 10:10).
