# 1 Kings 11:2 to Deuteronomy 7:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 2|1 Kings 11:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 3|Deuteronomy 7:3]]

**Subject**: turning Solomon's heart away

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What 1 Kings 11 reveals about divine speech, Deuteronomy 7 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
