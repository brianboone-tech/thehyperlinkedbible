# 1 Kings 8:12 to 2 Chronicles 6:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 12|1 Kings 8:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 6#2 Chronicles 6 . 1|2 Chronicles 6:1]]

**Subject**: dedication of temple

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects 1 Kings 8 to 2 Chronicles 6, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
