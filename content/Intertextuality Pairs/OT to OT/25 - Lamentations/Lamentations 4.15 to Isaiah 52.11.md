# Lamentations 4:15 to Isaiah 52:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 4#Lamentations 4 . 15|Lamentations 4:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 11|Isaiah 52:11]]

**Subject**: Depart! Depart! Do not touch (B) (* see temple vessels network)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Lamentations 4 to Isaiah 52, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
