# Lamentations 5:7 to Jeremiah 14:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 5#Lamentations 5 . 7|Lamentations 5:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 20|Jeremiah 14:20]]

**Subject**: Bearing the ancestors' iniquities (B) (* see personal responsibility network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Lamentations 5 establishes, Jeremiah 14 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
