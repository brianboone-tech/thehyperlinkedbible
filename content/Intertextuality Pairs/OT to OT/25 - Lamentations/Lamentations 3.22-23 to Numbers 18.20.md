# Lamentations 3:22-23 to Numbers 18:20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/25 - Lamentations/Lamentations 3#Lamentations 3 . 22|Lamentations 3:22-23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 18#Numbers 18 . 20|Numbers 18:20]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Lamentations 3 establishes about divine justice, Numbers 18 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
