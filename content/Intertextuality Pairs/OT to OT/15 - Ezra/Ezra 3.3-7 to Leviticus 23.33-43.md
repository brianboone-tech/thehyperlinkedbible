# Ezra 3:3-7 to Leviticus 23:33-43

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 3#Ezra 3 . 3|Ezra 3:3-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 33|Leviticus 23:33-43]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Ezra 3 to Leviticus 23, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
