# Ezra 1:1-3 to Jeremiah 25:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 1|Ezra 1:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 25#Jeremiah 25 . 11|Jeremiah 25:11]]

**Subject**: Prophetic fulfillment of exile end

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Ezra 1 and Jeremiah 25, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
