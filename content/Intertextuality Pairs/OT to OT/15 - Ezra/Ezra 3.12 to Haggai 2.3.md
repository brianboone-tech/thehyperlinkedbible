# Ezra 3:12 to Haggai 2:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 3#Ezra 3 . 12|Ezra 3:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 2#Haggai 2 . 3|Haggai 2:3]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Ezra 3 to Haggai 2, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
