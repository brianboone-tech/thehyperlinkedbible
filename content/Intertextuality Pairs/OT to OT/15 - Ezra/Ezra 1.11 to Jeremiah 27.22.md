# Ezra 1:11 to Jeremiah 27:22

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 11|Ezra 1:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 27#Jeremiah 27 . 22|Jeremiah 27:22]]

**Subject**: Vessels of temple returned (B) (* see temple vessels network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Ezra 1 to Jeremiah 27, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
