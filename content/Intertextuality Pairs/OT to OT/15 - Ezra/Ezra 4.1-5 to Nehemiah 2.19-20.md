# Ezra 4:1-5 to Nehemiah 2:19-20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 4#Ezra 4 . 1|Ezra 4:1-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 2#Nehemiah 2 . 19|Nehemiah 2:19-20]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ezra 4 and Nehemiah 2, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
