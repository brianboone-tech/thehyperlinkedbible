# Ezra 2:62-63 to Nehemiah 7:64-65

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 2#Ezra 2 . 62|Ezra 2:62-63]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 7#Nehemiah 7 . 64|Nehemiah 7:64-65]]

**Subject**: Ceremonial cleanness and purification

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Ezra 2 informs Nehemiah 7's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
