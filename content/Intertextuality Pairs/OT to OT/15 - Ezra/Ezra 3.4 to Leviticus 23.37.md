# Ezra 3:4 to Leviticus 23:37

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 3#Ezra 3 . 4|Ezra 3:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 23#Leviticus 23 . 37|Leviticus 23:37]]

**Subject**: Observing Tabernacles as it is written (B)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops the tabernacle theology of divine presence. What Ezra 3 establishes about God's dwelling, Leviticus 23 expands. The tabernacle points to Christ, who 'tabernacled' among us (John 1:14), making God's presence accessible and ultimately bringing the dwelling of God with humanity (Rev 21:3).
