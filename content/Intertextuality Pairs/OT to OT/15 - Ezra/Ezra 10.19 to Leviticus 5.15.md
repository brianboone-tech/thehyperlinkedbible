# Ezra 10:19 to Leviticus 5:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 10#Ezra 10 . 19|Ezra 10:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 5#Leviticus 5 . 15|Leviticus 5:15]]

**Subject**: Reparation offering and mass divorce (B+C) (* see assembly and devoting networks)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezra 10 establishes, Leviticus 5 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
