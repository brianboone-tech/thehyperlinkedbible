# Ezra 1:4 to Exodus 35:29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 1#Ezra 1 . 4|Ezra 1:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 35#Exodus 35 . 29|Exodus 35:29]]

**Subject**: Tithes and offerings

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This link traces the sacrificial system's development through Israel's Scriptures. What Ezra 1 establishes, Exodus 35 interprets, showing sacrifice as central to approaching God. Christ is the ultimate sacrifice, offering himself once for all (Heb 10:10-14), fulfilling and transcending all that the sacrificial system anticipated.
