# Ezra 9:1-2 to Exodus 34:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 9#Ezra 9 . 1|Ezra 9:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 11|Exodus 34:11]]

**Subject**: Mingling the holy seed (B) (* see assembly and devoting networks)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Ezra 9 and Exodus 34, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
