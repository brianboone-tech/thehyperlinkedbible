# Ezra 6:18 to Deuteronomy 12:13-14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 6#Ezra 6 . 18|Ezra 6:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 13|Deuteronomy 12:13-14]]

**Subject**: Worship personnel serve at Jerusalem (C)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Ezra 6 and Deuteronomy 12 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
