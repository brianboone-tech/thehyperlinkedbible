# Ezra 2:1-3 to Nehemiah 7:6-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 2#Ezra 2 . 1|Ezra 2:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 7#Nehemiah 7 . 6|Nehemiah 7:6-8]]

**Subject**: List of returning exiles

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link develops the theme of return/restoration in Israel's hope. The pattern in Ezra 2 informs Nehemiah 7's expectation. Christ accomplishes the ultimate return from exile—not merely geographical, but spiritual—bringing God's scattered people home (Eph 2:12-13).
