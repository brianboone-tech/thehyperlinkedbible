# Habakkuk 3:19 to 2 Samuel 22:33-34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 3#Habakkuk 3 . 19|Habakkuk 3:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 22#2 Samuel 22 . 33|2 Samuel 22:33-34]]

**Subject**: Prophetic vision and response

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Habakkuk 3 and 2 Samuel 22, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
