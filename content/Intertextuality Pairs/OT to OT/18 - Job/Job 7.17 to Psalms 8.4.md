# Job 7:17 to Psalms 8:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 7#Job 7 . 17|Job 7:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 4|Psalms 8:4]]

**Subject**: What are humans? (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Job 7 and Psalms 8, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
