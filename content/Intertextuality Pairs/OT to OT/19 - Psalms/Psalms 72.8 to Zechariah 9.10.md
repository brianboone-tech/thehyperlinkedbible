# Psalms 72:8 to Zechariah 9:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 8|Psalms 72:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 10|Zechariah 9:10]]

**Subject**: Universal reign (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Psalms 72 and Zechariah 9, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
