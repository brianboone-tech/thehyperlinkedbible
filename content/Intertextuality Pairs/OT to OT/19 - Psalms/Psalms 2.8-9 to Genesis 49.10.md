# Psalms 2:8-9 to Genesis 49:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 8|Psalms 2:8-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]]

**Subject**: Scepter against nations (C) (* see Judah-king networks)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Psalms 2 and Genesis 49, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
