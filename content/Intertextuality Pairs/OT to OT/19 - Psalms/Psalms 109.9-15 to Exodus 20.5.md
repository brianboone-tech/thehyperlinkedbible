# Psalms 109:9-15 to Exodus 20:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 109#Psalm 109 . 9|Psalms 109:9-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 5|Exodus 20:5]]

**Subject**: Generational judgment (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Psalms 109 establishes about divine justice, Exodus 20 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
