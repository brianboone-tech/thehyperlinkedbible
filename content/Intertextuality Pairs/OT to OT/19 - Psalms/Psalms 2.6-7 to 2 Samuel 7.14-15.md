# Psalms 2:6-7 to 2 Samuel 7:14-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 6|Psalms 2:6-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14-15]]

**Subject**: Divine begetting of the king (B) (* see Davidic covenant)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Psalms 2 establishes, 2 Samuel 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
