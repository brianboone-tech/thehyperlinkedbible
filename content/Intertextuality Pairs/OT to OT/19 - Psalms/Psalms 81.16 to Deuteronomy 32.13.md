# Psalms 81:16 to Deuteronomy 32:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 81#Psalm 81 . 16|Psalms 81:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 13|Deuteronomy 32:13]]

**Subject**: Wilderness provision (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Psalms 81 and Deuteronomy 32 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
