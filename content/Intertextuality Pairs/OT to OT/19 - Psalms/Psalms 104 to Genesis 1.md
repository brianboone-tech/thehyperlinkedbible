# Psalms 104 to Genesis 1

**Text**: Psalms 104

**OT Text Referred to**: Genesis 1

**Subject**: Creation hymn (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Psalms 104's portrayal of creation hymn (b) finds development in Genesis 1, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
