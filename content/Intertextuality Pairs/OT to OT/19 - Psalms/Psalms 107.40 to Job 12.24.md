# Psalms 107:40 to Job 12:24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 107#Psalm 107 . 40|Psalms 107:40]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 12#Job 12 . 24|Job 12:24]]

**Subject**: Leadership reversal

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Psalms 107 and Job 12 (leadership reversal) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
