# Psalms 132:8 to Numbers 10:35-36

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 8|Psalms 132:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 10#Numbers 10 . 35|Numbers 10:35-36]]

**Subject**: Ark journey (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Psalms 132 to Numbers 10, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
