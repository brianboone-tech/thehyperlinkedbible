# Psalms 110:1 to 1 Kings 5:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalms 110:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 5#1 Kings 5 . 3|1 Kings 5:3]]

**Subject**: Messianic rule (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Psalms 110 and 1 Kings 5, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
