# Psalms 103:14 to Genesis 2:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 103#Psalm 103 . 14|Psalms 103:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 7|Genesis 2:7]]

**Subject**: Human frailty (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Psalms 103 and Genesis 2, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
