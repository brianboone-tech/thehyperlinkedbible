# Psalms 109:9-15 to Deuteronomy 5:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 109#Psalm 109 . 9|Psalms 109:9-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 9|Deuteronomy 5:9]]

**Subject**: Generational judgment (C)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Psalms 109 establishes about divine justice, Deuteronomy 5 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
