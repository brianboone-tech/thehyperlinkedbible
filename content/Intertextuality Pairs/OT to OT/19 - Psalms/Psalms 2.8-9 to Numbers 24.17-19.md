# Psalms 2:8-9 to Numbers 24:17-19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 8|Psalms 2:8-9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 24#Numbers 24 . 17|Numbers 24:17-19]]

**Subject**: Scepter against nations (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Psalms 2 and Numbers 24, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
