# Psalms 144:3 to Job 7:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 144#Psalm 144 . 3|Psalms 144:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 7#Job 7 . 17|Job 7:17]]

**Subject**: Human insignificance (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Psalms 144 and Job 7, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
