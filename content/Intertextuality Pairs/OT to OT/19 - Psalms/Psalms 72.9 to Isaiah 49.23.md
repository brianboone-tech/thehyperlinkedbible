# Psalms 72:9 to Isaiah 49:23

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 9|Psalms 72:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 23|Isaiah 49:23]]

**Subject**: Subjugation imagery

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Psalms 72 and Isaiah 49, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
