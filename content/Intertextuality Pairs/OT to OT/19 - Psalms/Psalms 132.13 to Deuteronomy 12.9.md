# Psalms 132:13 to Deuteronomy 12:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 13|Psalms 132:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 9|Deuteronomy 12:9]]

**Subject**: Zion election (C)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Psalms 132 and Deuteronomy 12, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
