# Psalms 1:2-3 to Joshua 1:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 1#Psalm 1 . 2|Psalms 1:2-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 1#Joshua 1 . 8|Joshua 1:8]]

**Subject**: Like a tree by water (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Psalms 1's portrayal of like a tree by water (b) finds development in Joshua 1, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
