# Psalms 89:3-4 to 2 Samuel 7:11-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 3|Psalms 89:3-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 11|2 Samuel 7:11-16]]

**Subject**: Davidic covenant (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Psalms 89 establishes, 2 Samuel 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
