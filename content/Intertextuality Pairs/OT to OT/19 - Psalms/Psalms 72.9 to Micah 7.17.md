# Psalms 72:9 to Micah 7:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 9|Psalms 72:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 17|Micah 7:17]]

**Subject**: Subjugation imagery

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Psalms 72 and Micah 7, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
