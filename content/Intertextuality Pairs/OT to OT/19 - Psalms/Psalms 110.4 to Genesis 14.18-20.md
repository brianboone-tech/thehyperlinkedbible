# Psalms 110:4 to Genesis 14:18-20

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalms 110:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 18|Genesis 14:18-20]]

**Subject**: Eternal priesthood (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The priestly theme links Psalms 110 and Genesis 14, developing Israel's understanding of mediation between God and humanity. All priestly ministry points to Christ, our great High Priest (Heb 4:14-16) who perfectly represents God to us and us to God, securing eternal access to the Father.
