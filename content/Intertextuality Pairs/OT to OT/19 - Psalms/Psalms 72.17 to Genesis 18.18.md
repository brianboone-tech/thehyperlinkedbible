# Psalms 72:17 to Genesis 18:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 17|Psalms 72:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 18|Genesis 18:18]]

**Subject**: Abrahamic blessing (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Psalms 72 and Genesis 18, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
