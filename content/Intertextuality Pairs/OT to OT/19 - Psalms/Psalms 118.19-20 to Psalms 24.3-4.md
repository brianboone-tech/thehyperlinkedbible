# Psalms 118:19-20 to Psalms 24:3-4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 19|Psalms 118:19-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 24#Psalm 24 . 3|Psalms 24:3-4]]

**Subject**: Righteous access (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Psalms 118 establishes about divine justice, Psalms 24 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
