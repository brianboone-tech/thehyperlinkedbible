# Psalms 114:3 to Psalms 77:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 114#Psalm 114 . 3|Psalms 114:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 77#Psalm 77 . 16|Psalms 77:16]]

**Subject**: Exodus remembrance (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Psalms 114 and Psalms 77 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
