# Psalms 95:8-11 to Exodus 17:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 8|Psalms 95:8-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 7|Exodus 17:7]]

**Subject**: Wilderness testing (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Psalms 95 and Exodus 17 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
