# Psalms 79:6-7 to Jeremiah 10:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 79#Psalm 79 . 6|Psalms 79:6-7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 10#Jeremiah 10 . 25|Jeremiah 10:25]]

**Subject**: National judgment

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Psalms 79 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 10 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
