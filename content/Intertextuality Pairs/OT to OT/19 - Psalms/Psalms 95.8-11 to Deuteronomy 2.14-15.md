# Psalms 95:8-11 to Deuteronomy 2:14-15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 8|Psalms 95:8-11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 2#Deuteronomy 2 . 14|Deuteronomy 2:14-15]]

**Subject**: Wilderness testing (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Psalms 95 and Deuteronomy 2 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
