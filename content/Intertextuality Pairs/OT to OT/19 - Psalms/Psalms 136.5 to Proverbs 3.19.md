# Psalms 136:5 to Proverbs 3:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 136#Psalm 136 . 5|Psalms 136:5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 19|Proverbs 3:19]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Psalms 136's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Proverbs 3, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
