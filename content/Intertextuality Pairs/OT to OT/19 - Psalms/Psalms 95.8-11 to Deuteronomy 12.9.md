**Subject**: Wilderness testing (B)

**Significance**: This link connects Psalms 95 and Deuteronomy 12 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
