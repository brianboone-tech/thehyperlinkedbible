# Psalms 51:11 to 1 Samuel 16:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 51#Psalm 51 . 11|Psalms 51:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 16#1 Samuel 16 . 14|1 Samuel 16:14]]

**Subject**: Spirit departure (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Psalms 51 reveals about God's Spirit, 1 Samuel 16 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
