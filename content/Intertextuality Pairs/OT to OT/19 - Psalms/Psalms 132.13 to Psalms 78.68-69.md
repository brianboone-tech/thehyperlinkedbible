# Psalms 132:13 to Psalms 78:68-69

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 13|Psalms 132:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 68|Psalms 78:68-69]]

**Subject**: Zion election (C)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The Zion tradition links Psalms 132 and Psalms 78, developing the theology of God's chosen dwelling. All Zion hope points to the heavenly Jerusalem (Heb 12:22), where Christ reigns and gathers his people from every nation.
