# Psalms 15:1-5 to Deuteronomy 16:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 15#Psalm 15 . 1|Psalms 15:1-5]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 6|Deuteronomy 16:6]]

**Subject**: Temple access requirements (C)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Psalms 15 to Deuteronomy 16, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
