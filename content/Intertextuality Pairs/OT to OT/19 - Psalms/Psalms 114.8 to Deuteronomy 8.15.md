# Psalms 114:8 to Deuteronomy 8:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 114#Psalm 114 . 8|Psalms 114:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 15|Deuteronomy 8:15]]

**Subject**: Wilderness provision (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Psalms 114 and Deuteronomy 8 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
