# Psalms 1:1 to Deuteronomy 6:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 1#Psalm 1 . 1|Psalms 1:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 7|Deuteronomy 6:7]]

**Subject**: Walk, stand, sit (C) (* see concealing revelation network)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Psalms 1 and Deuteronomy 6 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
