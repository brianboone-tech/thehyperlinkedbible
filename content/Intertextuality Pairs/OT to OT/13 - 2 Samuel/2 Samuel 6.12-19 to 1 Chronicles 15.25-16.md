# 2 Samuel 6:12-19 to 1 Chronicles 15:25-16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 6#2 Samuel 6 . 12|2 Samuel 6:12-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 15#1 Chronicles 15 . 25|1 Chronicles 15:25-16]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 2 Samuel 6 establishes, 1 Chronicles 15 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
