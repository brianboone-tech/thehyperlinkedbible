# Deuteronomy 5:17 to Jeremiah 7:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 17|Deuteronomy 5:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 7#Jeremiah 7 . 9|Jeremiah 7:9]]

**Subject**: covenant violations

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Deuteronomy 5 establishes, Jeremiah 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
