# Deuteronomy 8:2 to 2 Chronicles 32:24-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 2|Deuteronomy 8:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 32#2 Chronicles 32 . 24|2 Chronicles 32:24-26]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Deuteronomy 8 and 2 Chronicles 32, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
