# Deuteronomy 32:4 to Jeremiah 2:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 4|Deuteronomy 32:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 5|Jeremiah 2:5]]

**Subject**: covenant fidelity

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Deuteronomy 32 establishes, Jeremiah 2 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
