# Deuteronomy 16:7 to 2 Chronicles 35:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 7|Deuteronomy 16:7]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 35#2 Chronicles 35 . 13|2 Chronicles 35:13]]

**Subject**: Passover preparation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Deuteronomy 16 and 2 Chronicles 35 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
