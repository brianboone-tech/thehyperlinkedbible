# Deuteronomy 7:6 to Isaiah 6:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 6|Deuteronomy 7:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 13|Isaiah 6:13]]

**Subject**: holy remnant

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The holiness theme links Deuteronomy 7 and Isaiah 6, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
