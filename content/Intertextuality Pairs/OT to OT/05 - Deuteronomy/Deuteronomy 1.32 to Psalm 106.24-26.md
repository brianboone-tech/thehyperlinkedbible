# Deuteronomy 1:32 to Psalm 106:24-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 32|Deuteronomy 1:32]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 24|Psalm 106:24-26]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Deuteronomy 1 informs Psalm 106's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
