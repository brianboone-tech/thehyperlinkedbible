# Deuteronomy 4:21 to Psalm 106:32

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 21|Deuteronomy 4:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 32|Psalm 106:32]]

**Subject**: prophetic limitation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Deuteronomy 4 and Psalm 106, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
