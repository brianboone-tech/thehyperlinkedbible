# Deuteronomy 5:9 to Psalm 81:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 9|Deuteronomy 5:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 81#Psalm 81 . 9|Psalm 81:9]]

**Subject**: exclusive worship

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Deuteronomy 5 and Psalm 81 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
