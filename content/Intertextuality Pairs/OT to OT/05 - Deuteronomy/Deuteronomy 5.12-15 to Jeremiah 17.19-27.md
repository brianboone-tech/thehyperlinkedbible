# Deuteronomy 5:12-15 to Jeremiah 17:19-27

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 12|Deuteronomy 5:12-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 17#Jeremiah 17 . 19|Jeremiah 17:19-27]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Deuteronomy 5 and Jeremiah 17, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
