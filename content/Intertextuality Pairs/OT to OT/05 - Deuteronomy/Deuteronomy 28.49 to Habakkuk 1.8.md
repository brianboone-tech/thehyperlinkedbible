# Deuteronomy 28:49 to Habakkuk 1:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 28#Deuteronomy 28 . 49|Deuteronomy 28:49]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 1#Habakkuk 1 . 8|Habakkuk 1:8]]

**Subject**: invasion imagery

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Deuteronomy 28 and Habakkuk 1, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
