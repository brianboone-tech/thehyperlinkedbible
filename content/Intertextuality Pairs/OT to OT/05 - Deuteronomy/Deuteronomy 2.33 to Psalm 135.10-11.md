# Deuteronomy 2:33 to Psalm 135:10-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 2#Deuteronomy 2 . 33|Deuteronomy 2:33]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 135#Psalm 135 . 10|Psalm 135:10-11]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Deuteronomy 2 informs Psalm 135's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
