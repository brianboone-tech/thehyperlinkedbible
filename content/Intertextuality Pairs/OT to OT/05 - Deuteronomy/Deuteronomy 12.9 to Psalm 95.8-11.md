# Deuteronomy 12:9 to Psalm 95:8-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 9|Deuteronomy 12:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 8|Psalm 95:8-11]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Deuteronomy 12 finds development in Psalm 95, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
