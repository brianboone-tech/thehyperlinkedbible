# Deuteronomy 23:3-8 to Ruth 4:17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 3|Deuteronomy 23:3-8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 17|Ruth 4:17]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Deuteronomy 23 and Ruth 4, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
