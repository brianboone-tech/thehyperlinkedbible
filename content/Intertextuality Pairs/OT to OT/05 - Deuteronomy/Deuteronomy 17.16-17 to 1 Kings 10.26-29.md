# Deuteronomy 17:16-17 to 1 Kings 10:26-29

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 17#Deuteronomy 17 . 16|Deuteronomy 17:16-17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 10#1 Kings 10 . 26|1 Kings 10:26-29]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Deuteronomy 17 and 1 Kings 10, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
