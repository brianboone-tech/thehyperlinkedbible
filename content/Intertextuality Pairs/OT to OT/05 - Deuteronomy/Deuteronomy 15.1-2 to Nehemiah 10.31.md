# Deuteronomy 15:1-2 to Nehemiah 10:31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 1|Deuteronomy 15:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 10#Nehemiah 10 . 31|Nehemiah 10:31]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Deuteronomy 15 finds development in Nehemiah 10, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
