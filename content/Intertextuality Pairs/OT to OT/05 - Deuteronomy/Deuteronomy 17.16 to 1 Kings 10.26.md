# Deuteronomy 17:16 to 1 Kings 10:26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 17#Deuteronomy 17 . 16|Deuteronomy 17:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 10#1 Kings 10 . 26|1 Kings 10:26]]

**Subject**: royal excess

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection between Deuteronomy 17 and 1 Kings 10 (royal excess) reveals the organic unity of Scripture. Later biblical writers interpreted earlier texts under divine inspiration, showing how themes develop progressively toward their fulfillment in Christ. What the OT anticipates in shadow, Christ fulfills in substance, demonstrating that all Scripture testifies to him (John 5:39; Luke 24:27).
