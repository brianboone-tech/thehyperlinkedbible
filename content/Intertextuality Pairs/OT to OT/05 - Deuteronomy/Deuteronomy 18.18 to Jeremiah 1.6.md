# Deuteronomy 18:18 to Jeremiah 1:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 18#Deuteronomy 18 . 18|Deuteronomy 18:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1#Jeremiah 1 . 6|Jeremiah 1:6]]

**Subject**: prophetic calling

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Deuteronomy 18 and Jeremiah 1, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
