# Deuteronomy 5:9 to Job 21:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 9|Deuteronomy 5:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 21#Job 21 . 19|Job 21:19]]

**Subject**: Temple worship and sacrificial system

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Deuteronomy 5 to Job 21, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
