# Deuteronomy 7:10 to Job 21:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 10|Deuteronomy 7:10]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 21#Job 21 . 19|Job 21:19]]

**Subject**: Wisdom literature parallels

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The wisdom theme connects Deuteronomy 7 and Job 21, revealing God's order for creation and life. Wisdom personified in the OT finds ultimate expression in Christ, who is 'the wisdom of God' (1 Cor 1:24, 30), in whom 'are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge' (Col 2:3).
