# Deuteronomy 29:1-4 to Jeremiah 31:31-34

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 1|Deuteronomy 29:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Deuteronomy 29's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Jeremiah 31, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
