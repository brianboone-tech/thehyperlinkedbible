# Deuteronomy 7:1-3 to Ezra 9:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 1|Deuteronomy 7:1-3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 9#Ezra 9 . 1|Ezra 9:1]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Deuteronomy 7 finds development in Ezra 9, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
