# Deuteronomy 22:25 to Jeremiah 20:7-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 25|Deuteronomy 22:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 20#Jeremiah 20 . 7|Jeremiah 20:7-8]]

**Subject**: Torah law and history

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Deuteronomy 22 and Jeremiah 20 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
