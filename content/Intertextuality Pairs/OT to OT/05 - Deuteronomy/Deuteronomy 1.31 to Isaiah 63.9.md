# Deuteronomy 1:31 to Isaiah 63:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 31|Deuteronomy 1:31]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 63#Isaiah 63 . 9|Isaiah 63:9]]

**Subject**: Deliverance and salvation

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the theme of divine deliverance through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern established in Deuteronomy 1 informs Isaiah 63's expectation of salvation. Christ is the ultimate Deliverer, rescuing his people from the domain of darkness (Col 1:13) and guaranteeing final deliverance at his return.
