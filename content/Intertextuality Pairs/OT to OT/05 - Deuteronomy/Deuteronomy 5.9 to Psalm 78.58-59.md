# Deuteronomy 5:9 to Psalm 78:58-59

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 9|Deuteronomy 5:9]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 58|Psalm 78:58-59]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Deuteronomy 5's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Psalm 78, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
