# Deuteronomy 9:3 to Nehemiah 9:24-25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 9#Deuteronomy 9 . 3|Deuteronomy 9:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 24|Nehemiah 9:24-25]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Deuteronomy 9 establishes, Nehemiah 9 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
