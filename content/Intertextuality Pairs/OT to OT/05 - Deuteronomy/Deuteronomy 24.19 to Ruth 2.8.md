# Deuteronomy 24:19 to Ruth 2:8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 19|Deuteronomy 24:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 2#Ruth 2 . 8|Ruth 2:8]]

**Subject**: Blessing and divine favor

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects Deuteronomy 24 and Ruth 2, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
