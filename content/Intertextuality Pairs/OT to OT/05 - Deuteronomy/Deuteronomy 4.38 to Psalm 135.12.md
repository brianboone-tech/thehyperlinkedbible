# Deuteronomy 4:38 to Psalm 135:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 38|Deuteronomy 4:38]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 135#Psalm 135 . 12|Psalm 135:12]]

**Subject**: inheritance fulfillment

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Deuteronomy 4 finds development in Psalm 135, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
