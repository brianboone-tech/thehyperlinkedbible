# Deuteronomy 22:25 to Jeremiah 20:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 22#Deuteronomy 22 . 25|Deuteronomy 22:25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 20#Jeremiah 20 . 7|Jeremiah 20:7]]

**Subject**: prophetic lament

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Deuteronomy 22 and Jeremiah 20, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
