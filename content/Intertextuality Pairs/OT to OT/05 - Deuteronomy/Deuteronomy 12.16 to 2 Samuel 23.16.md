# Deuteronomy 12:16 to 2 Samuel 23:16

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 16|Deuteronomy 12:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23#2 Samuel 23 . 16|2 Samuel 23:16]]

**Subject**: blood symbolism

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The intertextual connection develops the theology of blood atonement. From Deuteronomy 12 to 2 Samuel 23, Scripture teaches that without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness (Heb 9:22). Christ's blood is the reality to which all previous blood offerings pointed, securing eternal redemption (Heb 9:12).
