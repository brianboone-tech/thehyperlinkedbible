**Significance**: This link connects Deuteronomy 12 and Psalm 95 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
