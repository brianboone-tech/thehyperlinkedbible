# Deuteronomy 16:18-19 to Amos 5:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 16#Deuteronomy 16 . 18|Deuteronomy 16:18-19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 5#Amos 5 . 12|Amos 5:12]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Deuteronomy 16 establishes about divine justice, Amos 5 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
