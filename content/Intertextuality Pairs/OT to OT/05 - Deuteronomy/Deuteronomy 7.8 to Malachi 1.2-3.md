# Deuteronomy 7:8 to Malachi 1:2-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 8|Deuteronomy 7:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2-3]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Deuteronomy 7 establishes, Malachi 1 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
