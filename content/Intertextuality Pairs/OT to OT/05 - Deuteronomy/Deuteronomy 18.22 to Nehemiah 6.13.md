# Deuteronomy 18:22 to Nehemiah 6:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 18#Deuteronomy 18 . 22|Deuteronomy 18:22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 6#Nehemiah 6 . 13|Nehemiah 6:13]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Deuteronomy 18 and Nehemiah 6, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
