# Deuteronomy 8:12-14 to Hosea 13:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 12|Deuteronomy 8:12-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 13#Hosea 13 . 6|Hosea 13:6]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Deuteronomy 8 to Hosea 13, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
