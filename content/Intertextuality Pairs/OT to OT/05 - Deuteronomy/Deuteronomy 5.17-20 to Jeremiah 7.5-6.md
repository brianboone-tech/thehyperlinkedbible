# Deuteronomy 5:17-20 to Jeremiah 7:5-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 17|Deuteronomy 5:17-20]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 7#Jeremiah 7 . 5|Jeremiah 7:5-6]]

**Subject**: Righteousness and justice

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Deuteronomy 5 establishes about divine justice, Jeremiah 7 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
