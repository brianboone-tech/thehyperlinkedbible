# Deuteronomy 12:13 to Ezra 3:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 13|Deuteronomy 12:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/15 - Ezra/Ezra 3#Ezra 3 . 2|Ezra 3:2]]

**Subject**: Divine glory and presence

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces the glory theme through Scripture. From Deuteronomy 12 to Ezra 3, God's glory is revealed progressively. Christ is 'the radiance of God's glory' (Heb 1:3), and believers behold his glory, being transformed from glory to glory (2 Cor 3:18).
