# Deuteronomy 24:1-4 to Jeremiah 3:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 1|Deuteronomy 24:1-4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 3#Jeremiah 3 . 1|Jeremiah 3:1]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects Deuteronomy 24 and Jeremiah 3, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
