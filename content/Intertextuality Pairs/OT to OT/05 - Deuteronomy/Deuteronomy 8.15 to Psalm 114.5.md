# Deuteronomy 8:15 to Psalm 114:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 15|Deuteronomy 8:15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 114#Psalm 114 . 5|Psalm 114:5]]

**Subject**: redemption imagery

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Deuteronomy 8 and Psalm 114, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
