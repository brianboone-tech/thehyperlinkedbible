# Deuteronomy 8:12 to Hosea 13:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8#Deuteronomy 8 . 12|Deuteronomy 8:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 13#Hosea 13 . 6|Hosea 13:6]]

**Subject**: spiritual complacency

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops pneumatology through Israel's Scriptures. What Deuteronomy 8 reveals about God's Spirit, Hosea 13 expands. The Spirit who hovered over creation, empowered judges, and inspired prophets is poured out through Christ (Acts 2:33) to indwell all believers.
