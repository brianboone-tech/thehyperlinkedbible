# Deuteronomy 3:36 to Psalm 106:32-33

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 3#Deuteronomy 3 . 36|Deuteronomy 3:36]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 32|Psalm 106:32-33]]

**Subject**: Worship and theological reflection

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Deuteronomy 3 and Psalm 106 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
