# Deuteronomy 6:11 to Nehemiah 9:24-25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 11|Deuteronomy 6:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/16 - Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9#Nehemiah 9 . 24|Nehemiah 9:24-25]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Deuteronomy 6 and Nehemiah 9, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
