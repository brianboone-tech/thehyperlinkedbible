# Deuteronomy 4:39 to Joshua 2:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 39|Deuteronomy 4:39]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 2#Joshua 2 . 11|Joshua 2:11]]

**Subject**: universal sovereignty

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Deuteronomy 4 and Joshua 2, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
