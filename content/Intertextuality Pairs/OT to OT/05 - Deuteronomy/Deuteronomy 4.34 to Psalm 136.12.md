# Deuteronomy 4:34 to Psalm 136:12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 34|Deuteronomy 4:34]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 136#Psalm 136 . 12|Psalm 136:12]]

**Subject**: Exodus power

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Deuteronomy 4 and Psalm 136 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
