# Deuteronomy 29:1 to Jeremiah 31:31

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 1|Deuteronomy 29:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31]]

**Subject**: new covenant

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Deuteronomy 29 establishes, Jeremiah 31 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
