# Deuteronomy 4:2 to Proverbs 30:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 2|Deuteronomy 4:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 30#Proverbs 30 . 5|Proverbs 30:5]]

**Subject**: divine word integrity

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the theme of God's word through Israel's Scriptures. What Deuteronomy 4 reveals about divine speech, Proverbs 30 develops. Christ is the Word made flesh (John 1:14), the ultimate revelation of God who speaks and acts with divine authority.
