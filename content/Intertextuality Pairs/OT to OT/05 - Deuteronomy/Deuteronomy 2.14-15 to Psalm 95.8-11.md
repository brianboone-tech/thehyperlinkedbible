# Deuteronomy 2:14-15 to Psalm 95:8-11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 2#Deuteronomy 2 . 14|Deuteronomy 2:14-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 95#Psalm 95 . 8|Psalm 95:8-11]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Deuteronomy 2 establishes, Psalm 95 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
