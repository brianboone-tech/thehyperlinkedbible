# Deuteronomy 1:32 to Psalm 106:24

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 32|Deuteronomy 1:32]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 106#Psalm 106 . 24|Psalm 106:24]]

**Subject**: wilderness rebellion

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This link connects Deuteronomy 1 and Psalm 106 through the exodus paradigm that shapes Israel's hope. The deliverance from Egypt becomes the pattern for understanding all God's saving acts. Christ accomplishes the true exodus (Luke 9:31), delivering his people not from physical bondage but from sin, death, and Satan.
