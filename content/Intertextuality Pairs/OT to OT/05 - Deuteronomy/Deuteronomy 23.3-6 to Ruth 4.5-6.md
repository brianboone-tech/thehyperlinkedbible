# Deuteronomy 23:3-6 to Ruth 4:5-6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 3|Deuteronomy 23:3-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 5|Ruth 4:5-6]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Deuteronomy 23 finds development in Ruth 4, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
