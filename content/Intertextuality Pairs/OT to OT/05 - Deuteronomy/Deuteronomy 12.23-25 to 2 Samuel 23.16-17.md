# Deuteronomy 12:23-25 to 2 Samuel 23:16-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 12#Deuteronomy 12 . 23|Deuteronomy 12:23-25]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23#2 Samuel 23 . 16|2 Samuel 23:16-17]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Deuteronomy 12 and 2 Samuel 23, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
