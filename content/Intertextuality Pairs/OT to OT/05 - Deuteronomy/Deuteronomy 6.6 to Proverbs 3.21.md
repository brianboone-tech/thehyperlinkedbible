# Deuteronomy 6:6 to Proverbs 3:21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 6|Deuteronomy 6:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 3#Proverbs 3 . 21|Proverbs 3:21]]

**Subject**: generational instruction

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Deuteronomy 6 and Proverbs 3 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
