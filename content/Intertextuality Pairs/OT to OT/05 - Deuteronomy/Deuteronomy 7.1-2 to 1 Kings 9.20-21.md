# Deuteronomy 7:1-2 to 1 Kings 9:20-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 1|Deuteronomy 7:1-2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 9#1 Kings 9 . 20|1 Kings 9:20-21]]

**Subject**: Land promises and inheritance

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces God's covenant faithfulness across Israel's history. The promise in Deuteronomy 7 finds development in 1 Kings 9, demonstrating the organic unity of God's redemptive plan. Christ is the ultimate seed of promise, the one in whom all God's promises find their fulfillment (Gal 3:16; 2 Cor 1:20).
