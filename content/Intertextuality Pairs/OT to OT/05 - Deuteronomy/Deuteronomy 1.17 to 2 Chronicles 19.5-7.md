# Deuteronomy 1:17 to 2 Chronicles 19:5-7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 1#Deuteronomy 1 . 17|Deuteronomy 1:17]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/14 - 2 Chronicles/2 Chronicles 19#2 Chronicles 19 . 5|2 Chronicles 19:5-7]]

**Subject**: Divine judgment and justice

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What Deuteronomy 1 establishes about divine justice, 2 Chronicles 19 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
