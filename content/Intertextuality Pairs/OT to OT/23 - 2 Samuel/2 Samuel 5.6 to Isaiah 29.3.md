# 2 Samuel 5:6 to Isaiah 29:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 6|2 Samuel 5:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 29#Isaiah 29 . 3|Isaiah 29:3]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 5 and Isaiah 29, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
