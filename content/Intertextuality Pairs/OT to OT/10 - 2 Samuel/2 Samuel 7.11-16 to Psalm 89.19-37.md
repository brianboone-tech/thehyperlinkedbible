# 2 Samuel 7:11-16 to Psalm 89:19-37

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 11|2 Samuel 7:11-16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 19|Psalm 89:19-37]]

**Subject**: Prophetic declaration

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The prophetic theme connects 2 Samuel 7 and Psalm 89, showing how God speaks through his messengers. The prophetic pattern points to Christ, the Prophet like Moses (Deut 18:15; Acts 3:22) who speaks God's final word (Heb 1:1-2) and embodies the message he proclaims.
