# 2 Samuel 8:1-14 to 1 Chronicles 18:1-13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 8#2 Samuel 8 . 1|2 Samuel 8:1-14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 18#1 Chronicles 18 . 1|1 Chronicles 18:1-13]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 8 and 1 Chronicles 18, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
