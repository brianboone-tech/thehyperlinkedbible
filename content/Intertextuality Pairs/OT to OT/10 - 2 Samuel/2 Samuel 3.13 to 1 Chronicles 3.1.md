# 2 Samuel 3:13 to 1 Chronicles 3:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 3#2 Samuel 3 . 13|2 Samuel 3:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 3#1 Chronicles 3 . 1|1 Chronicles 3:1]]

**Subject**: sons born to David in Jerusalem

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 3 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 3 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
