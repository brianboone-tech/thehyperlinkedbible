# 2 Samuel 20:23 to 1 Chronicles 18:14

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 20#2 Samuel 20 . 23|2 Samuel 20:23]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 18#1 Chronicles 18 . 14|1 Chronicles 18:14]]

**Subject**: David's leading officers

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 20 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 18 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
