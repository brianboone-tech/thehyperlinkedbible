# 2 Samuel 24:1 to Exodus 30:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 1|2 Samuel 24:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 30#Exodus 30 . 11|Exodus 30:11]]

**Subject**: sin of the census

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in 2 Samuel 24 informs Exodus 30's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
