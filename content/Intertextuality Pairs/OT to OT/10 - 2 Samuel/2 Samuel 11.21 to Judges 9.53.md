# 2 Samuel 11:21 to Judges 9:53

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 11#2 Samuel 11 . 21|2 Samuel 11:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 9#Judges 9 . 53|Judges 9:53]]

**Subject**: a woman kills Abimelek

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects 2 Samuel 11 and Judges 9, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
