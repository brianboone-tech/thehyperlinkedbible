# 2 Samuel 7:14 to Psalm 2:6

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 6|Psalm 2:6]]

**Subject**: divine begetting of the king

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 7 and Psalm 2, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
