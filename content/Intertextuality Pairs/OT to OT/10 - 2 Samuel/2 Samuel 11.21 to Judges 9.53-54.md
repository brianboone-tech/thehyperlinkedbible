# 2 Samuel 11:21 to Judges 9:53-54

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 11#2 Samuel 11 . 21|2 Samuel 11:21]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 9#Judges 9 . 53|Judges 9:53-54]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal history

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 11 and Judges 9, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
