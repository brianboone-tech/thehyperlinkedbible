# 2 Samuel 7:28 to Psalm 132:11-12

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 28|2 Samuel 7:28]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132:11-12]]

**Subject**: Covenant promises and faithfulness

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 2 Samuel 7 establishes, Psalm 132 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
