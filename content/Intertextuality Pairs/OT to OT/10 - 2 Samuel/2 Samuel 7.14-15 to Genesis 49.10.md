# 2 Samuel 7:14-15 to Genesis 49:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]]

**Subject**: Nations and God's plan

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The blessing theme connects 2 Samuel 7 and Genesis 49, tracing God's intent to bless humanity. The blessing pattern points to Christ, through whom all nations are blessed (Gal 3:8-9, 14), reversing the curse and restoring all that Adam lost.
