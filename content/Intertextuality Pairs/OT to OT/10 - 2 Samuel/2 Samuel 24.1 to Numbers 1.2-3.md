# 2 Samuel 24:1 to Numbers 1:2-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 1|2 Samuel 24:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 1#Numbers 1 . 2|Numbers 1:2-3]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 24 and Numbers 1, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
