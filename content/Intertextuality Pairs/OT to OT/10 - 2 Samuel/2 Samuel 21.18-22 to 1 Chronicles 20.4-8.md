# 2 Samuel 21:18-22 to 1 Chronicles 20:4-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 21#2 Samuel 21 . 18|2 Samuel 21:18-22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 20#1 Chronicles 20 . 4|1 Chronicles 20:4-8]]

**Subject**: Warfare and divine victory

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection traces judgment theology through Israel's Scriptures. What 2 Samuel 21 establishes about divine justice, 1 Chronicles 20 expands, showing God's righteous response to sin. Christ bears the judgment we deserve (Isa 53:5-6; 2 Cor 5:21), enabling believers to escape condemnation (Rom 8:1).
