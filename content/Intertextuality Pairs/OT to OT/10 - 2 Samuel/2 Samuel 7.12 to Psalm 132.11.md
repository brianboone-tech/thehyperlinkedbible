# 2 Samuel 7:12 to Psalm 132:11

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 132#Psalm 132 . 11|Psalm 132:11]]

**Subject**: oath to David

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 7 establishes regarding David, Psalm 132 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
