# 2 Samuel 20:23-26 to 1 Chronicles 18:14-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 20#2 Samuel 20 . 23|2 Samuel 20:23-26]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 18#1 Chronicles 18 . 14|1 Chronicles 18:14-17]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 20 and 1 Chronicles 18, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
