# 2 Samuel 23:16 to 1 Chronicles 11:18

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 23#2 Samuel 23 . 16|2 Samuel 23:16]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 11#1 Chronicles 11 . 18|1 Chronicles 11:18]]

**Subject**: water represents the blood of life

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The intertextual connection develops the theology of blood atonement. From 2 Samuel 23 to 1 Chronicles 11, Scripture teaches that without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness (Heb 9:22). Christ's blood is the reality to which all previous blood offerings pointed, securing eternal redemption (Heb 9:12).
