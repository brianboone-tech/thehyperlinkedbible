# 2 Samuel 6:19 to 1 Chronicles 16:43

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 6#2 Samuel 6 . 19|2 Samuel 6:19]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 16#1 Chronicles 16 . 43|1 Chronicles 16:43]]

**Subject**: returning home and David's blessing

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 6 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 16 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
