# 2 Samuel 11:1 to 1 Chronicles 20:1-3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 11#2 Samuel 11 . 1|2 Samuel 11:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 20#1 Chronicles 20 . 1|1 Chronicles 20:1-3]]

**Subject**: Kingship and royal lineage

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 11 and 1 Chronicles 20, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
