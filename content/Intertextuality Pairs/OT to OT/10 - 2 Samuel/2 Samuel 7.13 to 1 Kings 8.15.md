# 2 Samuel 7:13 to 1 Kings 8:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 13|2 Samuel 7:13]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 15|1 Kings 8:15]]

**Subject**: choosing a place and choosing David

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 7 establishes regarding David, 1 Kings 8 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
