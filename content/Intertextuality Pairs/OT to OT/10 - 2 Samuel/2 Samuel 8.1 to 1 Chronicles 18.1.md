# 2 Samuel 8:1 to 1 Chronicles 18:1

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 8#2 Samuel 8 . 1|2 Samuel 8:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 18#1 Chronicles 18 . 1|1 Chronicles 18:1]]

**Subject**: David establishes a regional empire

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 8 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 18 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
