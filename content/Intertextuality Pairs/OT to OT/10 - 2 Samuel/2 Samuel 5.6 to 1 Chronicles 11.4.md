# 2 Samuel 5:6 to 1 Chronicles 11:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 6|2 Samuel 5:6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 11#1 Chronicles 11 . 4|1 Chronicles 11:4]]

**Subject**: David captures Jerusalem

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 5 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 11 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
