# 2 Samuel 2:18 to 1 Chronicles 2:3-17

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 2#2 Samuel 2 . 18|2 Samuel 2:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3-17]]

**Subject**: Consecration and redemption of firstborn

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The holiness theme links 2 Samuel 2 and 1 Chronicles 2, revealing God's transcendent purity and Israel's call to reflect it. Christ makes his people holy (Heb 10:10), enabling them to share in divine holiness and stand before the thrice-holy God.
