# 2 Samuel 7:14-15 to 1 Chronicles 28:7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 28#1 Chronicles 28 . 7|1 Chronicles 28:7]]

**Subject**: Law and divine commandments

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between 2 Samuel 7 and 1 Chronicles 28 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
