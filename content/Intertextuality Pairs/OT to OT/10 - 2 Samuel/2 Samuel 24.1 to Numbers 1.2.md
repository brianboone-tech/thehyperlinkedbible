# 2 Samuel 24:1 to Numbers 1:2

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 24#2 Samuel 24 . 1|2 Samuel 24:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 1#Numbers 1 . 2|Numbers 1:2]]

**Subject**: sin of the census

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in 2 Samuel 24 informs Numbers 1's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
