# 2 Samuel 7:14 to Genesis 49:10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 10|Genesis 49:10]]

**Subject**: covenant loyalty shall not depart

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What 2 Samuel 7 establishes, Genesis 49 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
