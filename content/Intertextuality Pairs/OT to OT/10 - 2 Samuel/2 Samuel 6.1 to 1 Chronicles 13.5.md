# 2 Samuel 6:1 to 1 Chronicles 13:5

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 6#2 Samuel 6 . 1|2 Samuel 6:1]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 13#1 Chronicles 13 . 5|1 Chronicles 13:5]]

**Subject**: failed attempt to bring ark to Jerusalem

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops Jerusalem's theological significance. From 2 Samuel 6 to 1 Chronicles 13, the city represents God's presence and purposes. The new Jerusalem descends from heaven (Rev 21:2), the eternal city where God dwells with his redeemed people through Christ.
