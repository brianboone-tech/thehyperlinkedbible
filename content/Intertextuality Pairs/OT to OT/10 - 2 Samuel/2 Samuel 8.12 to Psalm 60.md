# 2 Samuel 8:12 to Psalm 60

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 8#2 Samuel 8 . 12|2 Samuel 8:12]]

**OT Text Referred to**: Psalm 60

**Subject**: striking down an enemy in the valley of salt

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The royal theme connects 2 Samuel 8 and Psalm 60, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
