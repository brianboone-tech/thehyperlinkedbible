# 2 Samuel 2:18 to 1 Chronicles 2:3

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 2#2 Samuel 2 . 18|2 Samuel 2:18]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/13 - 1 Chronicles/1 Chronicles 2#1 Chronicles 2 . 3|1 Chronicles 2:3]]

**Subject**: genealogies of Judah through family of David

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: This intertextual link develops Davidic covenant theology. What 2 Samuel 2 establishes regarding David, 1 Chronicles 2 expands, pointing to the greater David to come. Christ fulfills the Davidic promises as the eternal King whose throne is established forever (2 Sam 7:12-16; Acts 2:29-36).
