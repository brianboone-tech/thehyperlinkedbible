# 2 Samuel 7:14-15 to Psalm 2:6-7

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14-15]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 6|Psalm 2:6-7]]

**Subject**: Worship and theological reflection

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between 2 Samuel 7 and Psalm 2 illuminates Israel's worship theology. Patterns of true worship develop through Scripture, finding their fulfillment in Christ, who enables worship 'in spirit and truth' (John 4:23-24) as both our sacrifice and our High Priest.
