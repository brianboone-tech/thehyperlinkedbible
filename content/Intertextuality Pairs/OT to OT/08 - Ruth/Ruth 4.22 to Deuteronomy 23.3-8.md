# Ruth 4:22 to Deuteronomy 23:3-8

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 22|Ruth 4:22]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 23#Deuteronomy 23 . 3|Deuteronomy 23:3-8]]

**Subject**: Moabitess matriarch of David (B) (* see assembly and Davidic covenant networks)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Ruth 4 establishes, Deuteronomy 23 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
