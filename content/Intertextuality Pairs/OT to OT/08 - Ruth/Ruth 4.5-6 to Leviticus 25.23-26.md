# Ruth 4:5-6 to Leviticus 25:23-26

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 5|Ruth 4:5-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 25#Leviticus 25 . 23|Leviticus 25:23-26]]

**Subject**: Legal blend of laws of inheritance, responsibility of near relative, levirate marriage, and the assembly (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: The connection between Ruth 4 and Leviticus 25 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
