# Ruth 2:8 to Leviticus 19:9-10

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 2#Ruth 2 . 8|Ruth 2:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 9|Leviticus 19:9-10]]

**Subject**: Moabitess granted residing foreigner gleaning privileges (B) (* see assembly network)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ruth 2 and Leviticus 19, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
