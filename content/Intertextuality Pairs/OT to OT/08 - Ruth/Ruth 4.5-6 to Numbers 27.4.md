# Ruth 4:5-6 to Numbers 27:4

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 4#Ruth 4 . 5|Ruth 4:5-6]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 27#Numbers 27 . 4|Numbers 27:4]]

**Subject**: Legal blend of laws of inheritance, responsibility of near relative, levirate marriage, and the assembly (B)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The connection between Ruth 4 and Numbers 27 develops Torah theology within Israel. The law reveals God's character and human inability to achieve righteousness. Christ fulfills the law (Matt 5:17), becoming our righteousness (1 Cor 1:30) and writing the law on believers' hearts through the Spirit.
