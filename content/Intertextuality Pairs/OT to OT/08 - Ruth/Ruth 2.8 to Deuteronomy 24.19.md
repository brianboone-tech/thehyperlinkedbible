# Ruth 2:8 to Deuteronomy 24:19

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/08 - Ruth/Ruth 2#Ruth 2 . 8|Ruth 2:8]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 24#Deuteronomy 24 . 19|Deuteronomy 24:19]]

**Subject**: Moabitess granted residing foreigner gleaning privileges (B)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Ruth 2 and Deuteronomy 24, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
