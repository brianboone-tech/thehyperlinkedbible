# Daniel 9:11 to Deuteronomy 29:20-21

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 9#Daniel 9 . 11|Daniel 9:11]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 29#Deuteronomy 29 . 20|Deuteronomy 29:20-21]]

**Subject**: Curses written in Torah (B)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces the curse motif through Israel's Scriptures. What Daniel 9 establishes about sin's consequences, Deuteronomy 29 develops. Christ became a curse for us (Gal 3:13), absorbing the curse to set us free and enabling the blessing of Abraham to flow to all peoples.
