# Daniel 9:4 to Deuteronomy 7:9

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 9#Daniel 9 . 4|Daniel 9:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 9|Deuteronomy 7:9]]

**Subject**: Covenantal fidelity (B) (* see attribute formula network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection develops the covenant theme central to redemptive history. What Daniel 9 establishes, Deuteronomy 7 expands and clarifies, showing the progressive unfolding of God's covenant purposes. All covenants find their 'yes' in Christ (2 Cor 1:20), who is both the mediator of the new covenant and the one in whom all covenant promises are fulfilled.
