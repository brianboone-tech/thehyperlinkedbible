# Daniel 12:3 to Isaiah 52:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 3|Daniel 12:3]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 13|Isaiah 52:13]]

**Subject**: Lead many to righteousness (C+B)

**Source**: Intertextual Bible

**Significance**: The imago Dei theme connects Daniel 12 and Isaiah 52, developing humanity's identity and calling. Christ is the perfect image of God (Col 1:15; 2 Cor 4:4), and believers are being conformed to his image (Rom 8:29), fulfilling humanity's original design and destiny.
