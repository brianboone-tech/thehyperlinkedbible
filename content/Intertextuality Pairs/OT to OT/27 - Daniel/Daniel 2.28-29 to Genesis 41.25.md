# Daniel 2:28-29 to Genesis 41:25

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 28|Daniel 2:28-29]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 41#Genesis 41 . 25|Genesis 41:25]]

**Subject**: Creation account and divine ordering

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This intertextual connection traces the creation theme through Israel's Scriptures. Daniel 2's portrayal of creation account and divine ordering finds development in Genesis 41, revealing how Israel's poets and prophets understood creation as pointing forward to God's ultimate creative work. In Christ, the Creator enters creation to restore what was marred by the Fall and inaugurate new creation (2 Cor 5:17; Col 1:15-20).
