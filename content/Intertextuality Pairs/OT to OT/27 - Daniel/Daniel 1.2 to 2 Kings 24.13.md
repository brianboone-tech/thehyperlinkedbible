# Daniel 1:2 to 2 Kings 24:13

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 1#Daniel 1 . 2|Daniel 1:2]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 24#2 Kings 24 . 13|2 Kings 24:13]]

**Subject**: Plundering temple vessels during siege of Jerusalem in Jehoiakim's rule (C) (* see temple vessels network)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The temple theme connects Daniel 1 to 2 Kings 24, revealing Israel's understanding of sacred space where God dwells with his people. This anticipates Christ, who is the true temple (John 2:19-21) where God and humanity meet, and whose body, the church, becomes the dwelling place of God by the Spirit (Eph 2:19-22).
