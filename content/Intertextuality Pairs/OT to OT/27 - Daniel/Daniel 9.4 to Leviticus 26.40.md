# Daniel 9:4 to Leviticus 26:40

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 9#Daniel 9 . 4|Daniel 9:4]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 40|Leviticus 26:40]]

**Subject**: Collective confession of sin (B) (* see collective confession network)

**Source**: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge

**Significance**: This connection traces atonement theology through Israel's Scriptures. The pattern in Daniel 9 informs Leviticus 26's understanding of how God deals with sin. Christ is our propitiation (Rom 3:25; 1 John 2:2), the true Day of Atonement sacrifice who removes sin definitively.
