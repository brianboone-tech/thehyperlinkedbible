# Daniel 2:45 to Isaiah 41:15

**Text**: [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 2#Daniel 2 . 45|Daniel 2:45]]

**OT Text Referred to**: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 15|Isaiah 41:15]]

**Subject**: Hewn rock destroys empires and becomes mighty kingdom (C)

**Source**: Unknown

**Significance**: The royal theme connects Daniel 2 and Isaiah 41, developing Israel's messianic hope. The kingship pattern points to Christ, the Son of David who reigns forever (Luke 1:32-33). Where human kings failed, Christ succeeds as the righteous King who rules with justice and brings shalom to his people.
