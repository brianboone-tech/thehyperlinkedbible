# Luke 17:11-19 to 2 Kings 5:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 17#Luke 17 . 11|Luke 17:11-19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 5#2 Kings 5 . 1|2 Kings 5:1]]
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 5#2 Kings 5 . 1|2 Kings 5:1]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: point of the allusion is both christological and theological: the story underscores again the connection between Jesus and God’s prophets in the Scriptures, and it emphasizes that not only do Israelites receive the benefits of salvation, but foreigners do as well (Green 1997: 620).
