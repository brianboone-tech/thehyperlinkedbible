# Luke 23:32-43 to Psalms 22:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 32|Luke 23:32-43]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 1|Psalms 22:1]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: point to the second part of the psalm, which emphasizes Yahweh’s universal salvation, implying that the allusion shows the scriptural and thus divine necessity of the pattern of messianic suffering and glory (Larkin [1974: 526] does not find such a connection).
