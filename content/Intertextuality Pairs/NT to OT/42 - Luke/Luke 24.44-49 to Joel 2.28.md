# Luke 24:44-49 to Joel 2:28

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 44|Luke 24:44-49]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: This passage demonstrates Luke's use of Old Testament Scripture to establish Jesus' identity and mission. The reference to Joel 2:28 shows how Jesus fulfills Israel's prophetic expectations and inaugurates the age of the Spirit. Luke's allusion highlights how Jesus himself is the key to understanding Scripture, as he opens the disciples' minds to comprehend how the Law, Prophets, and Psalms all point to his suffering, resurrection, and the proclamation of repentance to all nations. This interpretive move shapes our understanding of Jesus as the focal point of all Scripture, revealing that the entire Old Testament finds its ultimate fulfillment in Christ's redemptive work and the global mission of the church empowered by the promised Holy Spirit.
