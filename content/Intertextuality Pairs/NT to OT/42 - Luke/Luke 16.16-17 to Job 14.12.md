# Luke 16:16-17 to Job 14:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 16#Luke 16 . 16|Luke 16:16-17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 14#Job 14 . 12|Job 14:12]]
- [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 9#Amos 9 . 8|Amos 9:8]]

**Type**: Echo

**Significance**: This passage demonstrates Luke's use of Old Testament Scripture to establish Jesus' identity and mission. The reference to Job 14:12;, Amos 9:8 shows how Jesus fulfills Israel's prophetic expectations. Luke's echo highlights the continuity between God's work in the OT and its culmination in Christ. This interpretive move shapes our understanding of Jesus as the focal point of all Scripture, revealing God's redemptive plan unfolding through history. The passage demonstrates that properly understanding Jesus requires reading the Old Testament through the lens of his person and work.
