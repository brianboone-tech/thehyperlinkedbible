# Luke 16:15 to 1 Kings 8:39

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 16#Luke 16 . 15|Luke 16:15]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 8#1 Kings 8 . 39|1 Kings 8:39]]
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 11#1 Kings 11 . 5|1 Kings 11:5]]

**Type**: Echo

**Significance**: Verse 15 states that money may help a person to secure an image of uprightness in the eyes of fellow citizens, but that this means nothing to God, who knows the depths and desires of the human heart, and who alone establishes the value of human beings (see Fitzmyer 1981–1985: 1112).
