# Luke 17:26-37 to Isaiah 54:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 17#Luke 17 . 26|Luke 17:26-37]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 54#Isaiah 54 . 9|Isaiah 54:9]]
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 18#Job 18 . 15|Job 18:15]]
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 19#Genesis 19 . 26|Genesis 19:26]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: In 17:26–37 Jesus describes the “days of the Son of Man” (17:22–25) and compares them with “the days of Noah” (17:26–27) and with “the days of Lot” (17:28–29), warning against the indifference and nonchalance of “this generation,” which will face judgment on the day of the Son of Man (17:30–35).
