# Luke 9:49-50 to Numbers 11:26-29

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 9#Luke 9 . 49|Luke 9:49-50]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 11#Numbers 11 . 26|Numbers 11:26-29]] (Eldad and Medad prophesying)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: The significance of this dialogue between John and Jesus connects to the story of Eldad and Medad in Numbers 11:26-29, where Moses responds to unauthorized prophesying with openness rather than restriction, wishing all God's people would prophesy.
