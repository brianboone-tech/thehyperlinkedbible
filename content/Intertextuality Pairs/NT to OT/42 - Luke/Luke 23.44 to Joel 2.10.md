# Luke 23:44 to Joel 2:10

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 23#Luke 23 . 44|Luke 23:44]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 10|Joel 2:10]]
- [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 8#Amos 8 . 9|Amos 8:9]]

**Type**: Echo

**Significance**: significance of Jesus’ death, both in terms of the cosmic stage on which the final hours of Jesus’ life are played out and in terms of the arrival of the “last days” for which God’s presence and judgment were prophesied (see Bock 1994–1996: 1858; Green 1997: 824–25).
