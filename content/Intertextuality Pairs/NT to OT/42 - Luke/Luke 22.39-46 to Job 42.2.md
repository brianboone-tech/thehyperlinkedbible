# Luke 22:39-46 to Job 42:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 39|Luke 22:39-46]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 42#Job 42 . 2|Job 42:2]]
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 5|1 Kings 19:5]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: This passage demonstrates Luke's use of Old Testament Scripture to establish Jesus' identity and mission. The reference to Job 42:2;, 1 Kings 19:5–6 shows how Jesus fulfills Israel's prophetic expectations. Luke's allusion highlights the continuity between God's work in the OT and its culmination in Christ. This interpretive move shapes our understanding of Jesus as the focal point of all Scripture, revealing God's redemptive plan unfolding through history. The passage demonstrates that properly understanding Jesus requires reading the Old Testament through the lens of his person and work.
