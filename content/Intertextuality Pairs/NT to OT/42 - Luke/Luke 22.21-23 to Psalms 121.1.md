# Luke 22:21-23 to Psalms 121:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 21|Luke 22:21-23]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 121#Psalm 121 . 1|Psalms 121:1]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: This passage demonstrates Luke's use of Old Testament Scripture to establish Jesus' identity and mission. The reference to Psalm 121 shows how Jesus fulfills Israel's prophetic expectations. Luke's allusion highlights the continuity between God's work in the OT and its culmination in Christ. This interpretive move shapes our understanding of Jesus as the focal point of all Scripture, revealing God's redemptive plan unfolding through history. The passage demonstrates that properly understanding Jesus requires reading the Old Testament through the lens of his person and work.
