# Luke 21:8-11 to Daniel 7:21-22

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 21#Luke 21 . 8|Luke 21:8-11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 21|Daniel 7:21-22]] (wars and tribulation before kingdom)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 14#Jeremiah 14 . 15|Jeremiah 14:15]] (false prophets)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 29#Jeremiah 29 . 8|Jeremiah 29:8]] (do not be deceived by prophets)

**Type**: Apocalyptic allusion

**Significance**: In his Olivet Discourse (Luke 21:8-11), Jesus uses apocalyptic language echoing OT prophecy. He warns against false messiahs (cf. Jeremiah's warnings about false prophets in 14:15; 29:8), describes wars and cosmic upheavals typical of judgment passages. These signs precede the end but aren't the end itself—disciples must endure through tribulation, as Daniel 7:21-22 depicts persecution before kingdom arrival.
