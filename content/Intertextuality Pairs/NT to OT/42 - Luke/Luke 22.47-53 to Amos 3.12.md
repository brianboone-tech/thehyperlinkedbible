# Luke 22:47-53 to Amos 3:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 22#Luke 22 . 47|Luke 22:47-53]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 3#Amos 3 . 12|Amos 3:12]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: significance of Judas’s kiss as a sign of identification and thus of betrayal (contrast this with Mark 14:44), and so the kiss may be nothing more than the traditional mode of greeting.
