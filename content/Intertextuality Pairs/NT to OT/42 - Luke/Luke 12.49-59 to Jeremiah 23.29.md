# Luke 12:49-59 to Jeremiah 23:29

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 12#Luke 12 . 49|Luke 12:49-59]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23#Jeremiah 23 . 29|Jeremiah 23:29]] (God's word like fire)
- [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 6|Micah 7:6]] (son against father, daughter against mother in days of judgment)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment/Allusion

**Significance**: Jesus' saying about bringing fire and division (Luke 12:49-53) echoes prophetic themes. The fire imagery connects to Jeremiah 23:29 where God's word is like fire, refining and dividing. The familial division (12:53) directly quotes Micah 7:6, describing covenant unfaithfulness and coming judgment. Jesus applies this to the crisis his mission creates—response to him divides even families. This isn't contradiction of peace promises but recognition that gospel demands create conflict before eschatological peace arrives.
