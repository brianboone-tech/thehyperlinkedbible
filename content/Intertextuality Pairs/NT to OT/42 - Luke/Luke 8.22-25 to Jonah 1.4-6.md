# Luke 8:22-25 to Jonah 1:4-6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 8#Luke 8 . 22|Luke 8:22-25]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/32 - Jonah/Jonah 1#Jonah 1 . 4|Jonah 1:4-6]] (storm threatens boat, sleeper awoken by fearful sailors)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 107#Psalm 107 . 23|Psalm 107:23-30]] (those who go down to sea in ships cry to Lord in storm)

**Type**: Typological parallel

**Significance**: Jesus calming the storm in Luke 8:22-25 echoes Jonah's storm narrative (Jonah 1:4-6), but with Jesus as one greater than Jonah. Where Jonah slept during the storm and was awoken by fearful sailors, Jesus sleeps and is awoken by fearful disciples. Where Jonah's presence caused the storm (judgment), Jesus' presence calms it (salvation). The disciples' question "Who is this?" parallels the sailors' fear and wonder. This typological connection reveals Jesus' divine authority over creation, as only YHWH calms the seas (Psalm 107:23-30).
