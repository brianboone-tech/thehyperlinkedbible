# Luke 24:50-51 to 2 Kings 2:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 24#Luke 24 . 50|Luke 24:50-51]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 2#2 Kings 2 . 1|2 Kings 2:1]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: This passage demonstrates Luke's use of Old Testament Scripture to establish Jesus' identity and mission. The reference to 2 Kings 2 (Elijah's ascension) shows how Jesus fulfills and transcends the prophetic pattern established in Israel's history. Luke's allusion highlights the continuity between God's work in the OT and its culmination in Christ. By echoing Elijah's departure, Luke presents Jesus' ascension as the fitting climax to his earthly ministry, demonstrating his exaltation and return to the Father. The passage demonstrates that Jesus' ascension completes the redemptive pattern foretold in Scripture, positioning him to pour out the promised Spirit and rule as the exalted Lord who will return in glory.
