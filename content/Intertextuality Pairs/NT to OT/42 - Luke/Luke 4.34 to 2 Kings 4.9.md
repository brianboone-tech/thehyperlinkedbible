# Luke 4:34 to 2 Kings 4:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/42 - Luke/Luke 4#Luke 4 . 34|Luke 4:34]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 4#2 Kings 4 . 9|2 Kings 4:9]]

**Type**: Allusion/Echo

**Significance**: points to Jesus’ special status as one who is able to overpower the unclean spirits, and thus it is used as a way to denote “the sphere of the divine” (Twelftree 1993: 67).
