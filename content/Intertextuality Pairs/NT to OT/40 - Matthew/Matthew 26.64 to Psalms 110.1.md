# Matthew 26:64 to Psalms 110:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 64|Matthew 26:64]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalms 110:1]] (seated at God's right hand)
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (Son of Man coming on clouds)

**Type**: Composite quotation/Messianic claim

**Significance**: Before the Sanhedrin, Jesus makes his clearest claim to divine messianic status by combining Psalm 110:1 (Messiah seated at God's right hand) with Daniel 7:13 (Son of Man coming on clouds). These represent the two highest Christological texts in Jewish Scripture. Jesus asserts he is both the exalted heavenly Lord currently enthroned with God and the eschatological judge who will return in glory. The high priest recognizes this as a blasphemous claim to divine status, tearing his robes. Jesus' statement precipitates his execution but also prophesies his ultimate vindication.
