# Matthew 11:10 to Exodus 23:20

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 11#Matthew 11 . 10|Matthew 11:10]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 23#Exodus 23 . 20|Exodus 23:20]] (angel/messenger preparing way)
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1]] (messenger preparing way for the Lord)

**Type**: Composite quotation/Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Jesus combines Exodus (angel leading Israel through wilderness) with Malachi (forerunner preparing for Yahweh's coming) to identify John as the promised messenger. This composite quotation demonstrates sophisticated hermeneutics, linking exodus deliverance with eschatological arrival. Malachi 4:5 identifies the forerunner as Elijah, which Jesus explicitly applies to John (11:14), showing John fulfills the Elijah-like prophetic ministry. By identifying John as Yahweh's messenger, Jesus implicitly claims to be Yahweh himself coming to his people.
