# Matthew 4:10 to Deuteronomy 6:13

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 10|Matthew 4:10]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 13|Deuteronomy 6:13]] (exclusive worship of Yahweh)

**Type**: Direct citation/Typological application

**Significance**: The third wilderness quotation establishes exclusive worship of Yahweh as fundamental. Jesus embodies perfect covenant faithfulness that Israel failed to maintain, repeatedly falling into idolatry. All three temptation responses come from Deuteronomy 6-8, Israel's wilderness testing period. Jesus successfully passes the test Israel failed, qualifying him as the faithful Son who can lead a new people in covenant faithfulness.
