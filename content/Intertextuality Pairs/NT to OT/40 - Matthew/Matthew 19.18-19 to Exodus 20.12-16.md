# Matthew 19:18-19 to Exodus 20:12-16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 19#Matthew 19 . 18|Matthew 19:18-19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 12|Exodus 20:12-16]]; [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 16|Deuteronomy 5:16-20]] (Decalogue - commandments 5-9)
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 18|Leviticus 19:18]] (love your neighbor)

**Type**: Summary of moral law

**Significance**: Jesus cites the second table of the Decalogue (duties toward humans) plus Leviticus 19:18 to establish continuing validity of moral law. These commandments remain permanently binding in the new covenant era. However, Jesus' subsequent challenge to sell possessions reveals the law's deeper purpose - exposing the heart's true condition. The young man's confidence in his own obedience demonstrates misunderstanding of the law's comprehensive demands. This illustrates moral law's continuity while showing its inadequacy to produce righteousness apart from heart transformation, pointing to need for grace.
