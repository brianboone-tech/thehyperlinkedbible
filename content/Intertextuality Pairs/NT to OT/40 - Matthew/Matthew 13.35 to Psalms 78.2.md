# Matthew 13:35 to Psalms 78:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 13#Matthew 13 . 35|Matthew 13:35]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 2|Psalms 78:2]] (Asaph opening mouth in parable/riddle)

**Type**: Typological

**Significance**: Psalm 78 uses parable and riddle to recount Israel's history for instructing the next generation. Jesus' parables similarly reveal previously hidden truths about God's kingdom through figurative speech. Both Asaph and Jesus use this method to disclose and explain God's ways to those with ears to hear. The inauguration of the new age brings new revelation about kingdom realities that were concealed in previous epochs. Jesus unveils mysteries hidden since creation's foundation, demonstrating his authority as the one who brings eschatological disclosure.
