# Matthew 4:7 to Deuteronomy 6:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 4#Matthew 4 . 7|Matthew 4:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 16|Deuteronomy 6:16]] (command not to test God as at Massah)

**Type**: Direct citation/Typological application

**Significance**: Jesus again succeeds where Israel failed. At Massah (Exodus 17), Israel tested God by demanding proof of his presence and care; Jesus refuses to test his Father. This demonstrates proper sonship characterized by trust rather than demands for signs. Where Israel's unbelief led to testing God, Jesus' faith leads to confident obedience without needing miraculous verification.
