# Matthew 1:22-23 to Isaiah 7:14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 22|Matthew 1:22-23]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 7#Isaiah 7 . 14|Isaiah 7:14]] (the sign to Ahaz)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment (double fulfillment)

**Significance**: Matthew sees double fulfillment - near fulfillment in Isaiah's time (young woman's conception as sign to Ahaz) and ultimate fulfillment in Mary's virginal conception. The name "Immanuel" (God with us) is programmatic for Matthew's Christology, forming an inclusio with 28:20 ("I am with you always"). This establishes Jesus as the embodiment of divine presence among his people, surpassing temple and tabernacle.
