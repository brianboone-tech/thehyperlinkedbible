# Matthew 21:16 to Psalms 8:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 16|Matthew 21:16]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 2|Psalms 8:2]] (children's praise silencing enemies)

**Type**: Typological application

**Significance**: Jesus applies Psalm 8's description of God receiving praise from children and infants, accepting the children's worship as properly directed to himself. This carries profound Christological implications - since Psalm 8 speaks of Yahweh, Jesus' application to himself claims divine status. The contrast is striking: children discern Jesus' true identity while religious leaders remain blind. God reveals truth to the humble while concealing it from the supposedly wise. The children's spontaneous praise fulfills Scripture's pattern of God establishing strength through the weak to silence his enemies.
