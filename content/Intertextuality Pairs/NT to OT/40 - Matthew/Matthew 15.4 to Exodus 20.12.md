# Matthew 15:4 to Exodus 20:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 15#Matthew 15 . 4|Matthew 15:4]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 12|Exodus 20:12]]; [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 16|Deuteronomy 5:16]] (Fifth Commandment)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 21#Exodus 21 . 17|Exodus 21:17]] (capital punishment for cursing parents)

**Type**: Direct citation/Moral law

**Significance**: Jesus quotes both the Decalogue and case law to demonstrate that written Torah takes absolute precedence over oral tradition. The Pharisees' qorban practice allowed people to declare resources "devoted to temple" to avoid helping aging parents, directly violating the fifth commandment. This exposes how human tradition can undermine God's explicit commands. Jesus establishes that moral obligations from Torah remain permanently binding and cannot be circumvented by religious innovations. Scripture trumps tradition when they conflict.
