# Matthew 21:5 to Isaiah 62:11

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 5|Matthew 21:5]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 62#Isaiah 62 . 11|Isaiah 62:11]] (introduction: "Say to daughter of Zion")
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 9|Zechariah 9:9]] (king coming on donkey)

**Type**: Composite quotation/Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Jesus deliberately enacts Zechariah's prophecy of the humble, peaceful messianic king entering Jerusalem mounted on a donkey rather than a warhorse. Matthew combines Isaiah's prophetic formula with Zechariah's specific prophecy. Zechariah 9 juxtaposes this peaceful entry with ultimate military victory over enemies, suggesting a two-stage fulfillment - Jesus' first advent emphasizes peace and salvation, while his second coming brings militant judgment. This corrects misguided expectations of immediate political liberation while affirming Messiah's ultimate triumph.
