# Matthew 22:32 to Exodus 3:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 32|Matthew 22:32]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 6|Exodus 3:6, 15-16]] (God's self-identification to Moses at burning bush)

**Type**: Theological inference from present tense

**Significance**: Jesus argues from the present tense "I am" (not "I was") the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob to prove resurrection. If God currently identifies as their God centuries after their deaths, they must still be alive to him. God does not maintain covenant relationship with non-existent persons. This demonstrates Jesus' sophisticated hermeneutical reasoning, extracting resurrection doctrine from Torah's grammatical details. He answers the Sadducees on their own authoritative ground, proving they understand neither Scripture nor God's power to grant resurrection life.
