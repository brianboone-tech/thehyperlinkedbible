# Matthew 2:23 to Isaiah 11:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 23|Matthew 2:23]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1]] (נֵצֶר = "branch" - wordplay with Nazareth/Nazarene)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 3|Isaiah 53:3]] (despised and rejected - thematic connection to Nazareth's low reputation)
- Possibly [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 13#Judges 13 . 5|Judges 13:5-7]] (Nazirite vow - phonetic similarity)

**Type**: Composite/Wordplay/Thematic

**Significance**: No single OT text says "He shall be called a Nazarene," explaining why Matthew uses "prophets" (plural). He likely indicates either wordplay between נֵצֶר ("branch" - messianic title) and Nazareth, or thematic connection to Nazareth's despised status reflecting Isaiah 53's suffering servant. This demonstrates Matthew's sophisticated hermeneutics, recognizing that fulfillment includes verbal, thematic, and typological connections.
