# Matthew 18:16 to Deuteronomy 19:15

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 18#Matthew 18 . 16|Matthew 18:16]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 15|Deuteronomy 19:15]] (requirement of multiple witnesses)

**Type**: Legal principle

**Significance**: Jesus applies Torah's witness requirement to church discipline, demonstrating continuity of God's judicial principles from old to new covenant communities. The requirement of two or three witnesses protects the accused from false charges while ensuring proper accountability for genuine sin. This shows that moral and judicial principles from Torah remain binding for the church. Jesus adapts Mosaic legal procedures to the new covenant context, establishing the church as a covenant community governed by God's righteous standards adapted for the new era.
