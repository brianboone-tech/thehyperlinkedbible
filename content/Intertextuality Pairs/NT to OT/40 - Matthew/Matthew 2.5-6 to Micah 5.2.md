# Matthew 2:5-6 to Micah 5:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 5|Matthew 2:5-6]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 5#Micah 5 . 2|Micah 5:2]] (Messiah's birthplace)
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 5#2 Samuel 5 . 2|2 Samuel 5:2]] (shepherd imagery for David)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Micah's prophecy explicitly identifies Bethlehem as Messiah's birthplace, connecting the coming ruler to David's origins. Matthew's paraphrase emphasizes Jesus' role as shepherd-king who will govern God's people. The reference establishes Jesus' Davidic credentials while ironically showing religious leaders possessed correct knowledge but failed to recognize fulfillment.
