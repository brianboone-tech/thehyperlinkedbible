# Matthew 3:3 to Isaiah 40:3

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 3#Matthew 3 . 3|Matthew 3:3]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 3|Isaiah 40:3]] (voice in wilderness preparing way for God's coming)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Isaiah announced the end of Babylonian exile and God's return to Zion, introducing the "Book of Consolation" (Isaiah 40-55). John fulfills this as forerunner preparing for Yahweh's arrival in Jesus, implicitly identifying Jesus with Yahweh himself. The wilderness setting recalls Israel's formation as God's people during the Exodus, suggesting a new exodus and new beginning for God's people.
