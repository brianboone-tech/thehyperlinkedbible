# Matthew 1:1 to 2 Samuel 7:12-16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 1#Matthew 1 . 1|Matthew 1:1]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12-16]] (Davidic covenant - eternal dynasty)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 1|Genesis 12:1-3]]; [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 18|Genesis 22:18]] (Abrahamic covenant - blessing to nations)

**Type**: Typological/Covenantal

**Significance**: Opening verse establishes Jesus' dual messianic identity as Davidic king and Abrahamic seed through whom all nations are blessed. The genealogy traces redemptive history through key covenantal figures, demonstrating God's sovereign plan culminating in Christ. Matthew presents Jesus as the fulfillment of both the royal promise to David and the universal blessing promise to Abraham.
