# Matthew 22:24 to Deuteronomy 25:5-6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 22#Matthew 22 . 24|Matthew 22:24]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 25#Deuteronomy 25 . 5|Deuteronomy 25:5-6]] (levirate marriage law)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 38#Genesis 38 . 8|Genesis 38:8]] (Onan and Tamar)

**Type**: Legal citation

**Significance**: Sadducees cite levirate marriage law to construct what they consider a reductio ad absurdum argument against resurrection. Jesus responds on two levels: first, by distinguishing resurrection life from current existence (no marriage in resurrection), and second, by proving resurrection from the Torah itself (Exodus 3:6), which Sadducees accepted as most authoritative. This demonstrates Jesus' superior hermeneutical skill and exposes the Sadducees' failure to understand either Scripture or God's power.
