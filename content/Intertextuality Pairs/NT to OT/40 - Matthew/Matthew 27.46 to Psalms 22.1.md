# Matthew 27:46 to Psalms 22:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 27#Matthew 27 . 46|Matthew 27:46]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 1|Psalms 22:1]] (opening cry of David's lament)

**Type**: Direct quotation/Typological

**Significance**: Jesus' cry represents the climactic moment of divine abandonment as he bears sin's penalty. Debate exists whether Jesus quotes the full psalm (which ends in triumph and vindication) or solely expresses dereliction. The cry captures the most profound mystery of atonement - the Son forsaken by the Father so that sinners might be accepted. Jesus fulfills the righteous sufferer pattern of Psalm 22 but at infinite cost. This moment of God-forsakenness accomplishes reconciliation, with vindication following in resurrection.
