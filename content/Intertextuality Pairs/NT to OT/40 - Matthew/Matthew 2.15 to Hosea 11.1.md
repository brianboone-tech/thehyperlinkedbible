# Matthew 2:15 to Hosea 11:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 2#Matthew 2 . 15|Matthew 2:15]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 11#Hosea 11 . 1|Hosea 11:1]] (God calling Israel out of Egypt)

**Type**: Typological (corporate-to-individual recapitulation)

**Significance**: Classic example of Matthew's typology where Hosea's original reference to the Exodus is applied to Jesus. Jesus recapitulates Israel's history but succeeds where corporate Israel failed. This establishes Jesus as the true Israel, the faithful Son who perfectly obeys the Father. Matthew sees Jesus not merely predic ted by OT but reliving Israel's story in his own life.
