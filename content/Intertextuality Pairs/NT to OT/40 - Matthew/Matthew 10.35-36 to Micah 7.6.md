# Matthew 10:35-36 to Micah 7:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 10#Matthew 10 . 35|Matthew 10:35-36]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 7#Micah 7 . 6|Micah 7:6]] (household strife as sign of judgment)

**Type**: Typological/Eschatological fulfillment

**Significance**: Micah described family conflict during apostasy as a sign of societal breakdown and divine judgment. Jesus applies this pattern to the gospel age, warning that his coming exposes hearts and creates division as people respond differently. The gospel doesn't cause conflict but reveals it, forcing individuals to choose for or against Christ. What Micah described as judgment imagery becomes the reality of kingdom inauguration, where allegiance to Christ supersedes even family bonds.
