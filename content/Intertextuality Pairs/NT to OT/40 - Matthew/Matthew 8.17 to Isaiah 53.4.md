# Matthew 8:17 to Isaiah 53:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 8#Matthew 8 . 17|Matthew 8:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 4|Isaiah 53:4]] (Suffering Servant bearing infirmities)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment (double fulfillment)

**Significance**: Matthew applies the Suffering Servant passage to Jesus' healing ministry, not only to his atoning death. Isaiah 53:4 speaks of both physical and spiritual healing - bearing infirmities and diseases. This demonstrates that messianic ministry includes physical restoration as a sign and foretaste of broader redemption. Jesus' healings manifest the kingdom's in-breaking power over all effects of the fall.
