# Matthew 12:7 to Hosea 6:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 7|Matthew 12:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 6#Hosea 6 . 6|Hosea 6:6]] (repeated from 9:13)

**Type**: Prophetic principle/Hermeneutical correction

**Significance**: Jesus' second use of Hosea 6:6 applies the prophet's priority of mercy over sacrifice to Sabbath interpretation. The moral law (mercy, compassion for human need) takes precedence over ritual/ceremonial regulations when they conflict. Jesus demonstrates that proper Sabbath observance serves human flourishing rather than burdening people with restrictive traditions. As Lord of the Sabbath, he has divine authority to interpret Torah correctly, revealing that God desires covenant faithfulness expressed through mercy rather than mere external compliance with ritual.
