# Matthew 26:31 to Zechariah 13:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 26#Matthew 26 . 31|Matthew 26:31]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 7|Zechariah 13:7]] (shepherd struck, sheep scattered)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Zechariah's prophecy appears in an eschatological context describing God striking his shepherd-representative, resulting in the flock's scattering. Jesus applies this to his impending death and the disciples' desertion. The striking comes from God himself, indicating divine purpose in Messiah's suffering. This reveals that God's sovereign plan encompasses even the disciples' temporary failure and flight. Yet the prophecy's larger context includes restoration, hinting that scattering is not final. Jesus fulfills the shepherd role who must be struck so that redemption can proceed.
