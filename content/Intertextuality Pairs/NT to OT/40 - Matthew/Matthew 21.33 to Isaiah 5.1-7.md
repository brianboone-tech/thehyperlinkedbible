# Matthew 21:33 to Isaiah 5:1-7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 33|Matthew 21:33]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] (Song of the Vineyard)

**Type**: Parabolic re-enactment/Typological

**Significance**: Jesus deliberately retells Isaiah's vineyard parable with a crucial shift in focus. Isaiah's vineyard (Israel) produced bad fruit instead of justice; Jesus' version focuses on wicked tenants (religious leaders) who reject the owner's servants (prophets) and finally kill his son (Jesus himself). This announces judgment specifically on Israel's current leadership rather than the nation as a whole. The parable predicts Jesus' death and warns of kingdom stewardship being transferred from unfaithful leaders to a new people who will produce proper fruit.
