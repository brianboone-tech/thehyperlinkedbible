# Matthew 21:9 to Psalms 118:25-26

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 21#Matthew 21 . 9|Matthew 21:9]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 25|Psalms 118:25-26]] (Hallel psalm - "Save now! Blessed is he who comes")

**Type**: Liturgical acclamation/Messianic application

**Significance**: The crowds acclaim Jesus using Psalm 118 from the Hallel collection traditionally sung at Passover and Tabernacles. "Hosanna" means "save now," invoking divine deliverance. "He who comes in the Lord's name" carried messianic connotations in Second Temple Judaism. By adding "Son of David," the crowds make explicit messianic interpretation unmistakable. The scene is deeply ironic - crowds acclaim without full understanding of what kind of Messiah Jesus is, and many will later demand his crucifixion. Yet their words speak prophetic truth about Jesus' identity.
