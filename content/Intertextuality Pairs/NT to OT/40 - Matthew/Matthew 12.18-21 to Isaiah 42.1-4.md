# Matthew 12:18-21 to Isaiah 42:1-4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/40 - Matthew/Matthew 12#Matthew 12 . 18|Matthew 12:18-21]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1-4]] (First Servant Song)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment (double fulfillment)

**Significance**: The longest OT quotation in Matthew identifies Jesus as Isaiah's Servant - gentle, humble, non-coercive, yet ultimately triumphant. This balances popular expectations of a conquering military Messiah with the suffering servant who accomplishes redemption through gentle ministry. The emphasis on bringing justice to the Gentiles foreshadows the Great Commission and universal scope of Jesus' mission. Jesus' refusal to break the bruised reed or quench the smoldering wick demonstrates his compassionate approach to weak and failing people, revealing the Messiah's tender care.
