# 1 Timothy 2:13-14 to Genesis 2:7-8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/54 - 1 Timothy/1 Timothy 2#1 Timothy 2 . 13|1 Timothy 2:13-14]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 7|Genesis 2:7-8]], [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 15|15]] (creation of Adam)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 18|Genesis 2:18-23]] (creation of Eve)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 1|Genesis 3:1-13]] (temptation and fall)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 16|Genesis 3:16]] (judgment on woman - possibly extending to 2:15a)

**Type**: Intensive adaptation / Allusive retelling

**Significance**: Paul draws on both creation (Gen. 2) and fall (Gen. 3) narratives through specific verbal links: the verb "form" (plassō) connecting to Genesis 2:7-8, 15 where God formed Adam, the sequence "first...then" emphasizing creation order, and the verb "deceived" (apataō/exapataō) recalling Eve's statement in Genesis 3:13. The function of this OT material likely addresses heretical speculation on Genesis in Ephesus—if false teaching promoted an overrealized eschatology leading women to think they were free from fall constraints, Paul's allusion reminds them that creation order remains in effect and the present age is still marked by fall consequences. This interpretation coheres with women's vital ministry roles elsewhere in Paul's churches while explaining his specific instructions to the Ephesian context. The allusion aptly illustrates how women in Ephesus were being deceived by false teachers just as Eve was deceived by the serpent (cf. 2 Cor. 11:3).
