# 2 Corinthians 3:6 to Jeremiah 31:31

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 6|2 Corinthians 3:6]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31]] (new covenant promise - first explicit "new covenant" language)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 14|Ezekiel 37:14]] (Spirit gives life to dead bones)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]] (Spirit within, new heart)

**Type**: Allusion / New Covenant Terminology / Fulfillment Declaration

**Significance**: Paul explicitly claims to be a "minister of the new covenant"—the very covenant Jeremiah predicted. "Letter kills" = the law written externally on stone condemns (cf. 3:7 "ministry of death"); "Spirit gives life" = Ezekiel's dry bones vision where Spirit animates the dead. Paul distinguishes covenants not by content but by mode of administration: old covenant written externally → condemnation; new covenant written internally by Spirit → life.
