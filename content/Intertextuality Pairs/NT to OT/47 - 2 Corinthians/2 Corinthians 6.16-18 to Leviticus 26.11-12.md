# 2 Corinthians 6:16-18 to Leviticus 26:11-12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 6#2 Corinthians 6 . 16|2 Corinthians 6:16-18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 11|Leviticus 26:11-12]] (temple-dwelling, covenant formula)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27]] (dwelling place promise)
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14]] (father-son language)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 11|Isaiah 52:11]] (call to separate from unclean)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 6|Isaiah 43:6]] (sons and daughters)

**Type**: Composite quotation / Catena / Temple theology / Covenant formula

**Significance**: Paul creates a testimonia-style composite weaving together five OT texts into unified temple ecclesiology. The church has become God's dwelling place (replacing physical temple), requiring holiness. Leviticus 26 and Ezekiel 37's promises of God dwelling among his people find fulfillment in the Spirit-indwelt church. Isaiah 52's call for exiles to leave Babylon is reapplied to separation from idolatry. 2 Samuel 7's Davidic covenant (father-son relationship) is universalized to all believers as God's sons and daughters. This composite technique demonstrates early Christian interpretive method - grouping related OT passages to establish theological points about the church's identity as new covenant temple-community.
