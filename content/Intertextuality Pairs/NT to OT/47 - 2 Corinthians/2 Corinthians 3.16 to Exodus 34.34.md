# 2 Corinthians 3:16 to Exodus 34:34

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 16|2 Corinthians 3:16]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 34|Exodus 34:34]] (whenever Moses went in before the LORD to speak with him, he would remove the veil)

**Type**: Typological Adaptation

**Significance**: Paul adapts Moses's action of removing his veil when entering God's presence to describe conversion. Key interpretive move: Moses removed veil → anyone who turns to Christ has veil removed. The "Lord" in v.16 is Christ (cf. v.17 "the Lord is the Spirit"). Physical veil → spiritual veil over hearts; Moses entering presence → believer turning to Christ; Moses unveiled before glory → believer transformed by beholding glory.
