# 2 Corinthians 3:3 to Exodus 31:18

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 3#2 Corinthians 3 . 3|2 Corinthians 3:3]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 31#Exodus 31 . 18|Exodus 31:18]] (tablets of stone written by finger of God)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 12|Exodus 24:12]] (stone tablets promised)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 1|Exodus 34:1]] (replacement stone tablets)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 19|Ezekiel 11:19]] (heart of stone → heart of flesh)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36#Ezekiel 36 . 26|Ezekiel 36:26-27]] (new heart, Spirit within)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 33|Jeremiah 31:33]] (law written on hearts)

**Type**: Allusion / New Covenant Fulfillment

**Significance**: Paul contrasts old covenant's external stone tablets with new covenant's internal transformation. The "finger of God" that wrote on stone (Exod 31:18) now writes on hearts via the Spirit. Ezekiel's promise of heart transplant (stone → flesh) and Spirit indwelling finds fulfillment in new covenant ministry. The Corinthians themselves are Paul's credentials—living letters demonstrating Spirit's transformative work.
