# 2 Corinthians 13:1 to Deuteronomy 19:15

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 13#2 Corinthians 13 . 1|2 Corinthians 13:1]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 19#Deuteronomy 19 . 15|Deuteronomy 19:15]] (primary - witness law)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 17#Deuteronomy 17 . 6|Deuteronomy 17:6]] (capital cases require witnesses)

**Type**: Legal principle / Procedural application

**Significance**: Paul applies Deuteronomic legal procedure to church discipline - charges require two or three witnesses, not one. This protects the accused from false accusation while ensuring truth is established. The most likely interpretation: Paul will conduct formal discipline hearings following OT legal standards when he arrives. This demonstrates the church as covenant community continuing Israel's role in maintaining justice and holiness through proper procedures. Though believers are not "under law" for justification, the law's procedural wisdom guides Christian community life. Jesus used the same text for church discipline (Matthew 18:16), likely informing Paul's application. The warning is serious - Deuteronomic context included severe penalties, which Paul applies to church discipline through excommunication rather than physical death.
