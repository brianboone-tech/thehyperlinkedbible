# 2 Corinthians 9:9 to Psalms 112:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/47 - 2 Corinthians/2 Corinthians 9#2 Corinthians 9 . 9|2 Corinthians 9:9]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 112#Psalm 112 . 9|Psalms 112:9]] (direct quotation - righteous generosity)
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 22#Proverbs 22 . 8|Proverbs 22:8]] (allusion - "God loves cheerful giver")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 10|Isaiah 55:10]] (allusion - seed to sower, bread for food)

**Type**: Multiple - Direct Quotation (Psalm 112:9); Unmarked Allusions (Proverbs 22:8 LXX, Isaiah 55:10)

**Significance**: Paul weaves three OT texts to establish generous giving as covenant continuity. Psalm 112's righteous person who "scatters gifts to the poor" with "righteousness enduring forever" becomes the model believer. Proverbs 22:8 LXX (not in MT) provides the "cheerful giver" principle. Isaiah 55's divine provision metaphor (seed to sower, bread for food) assures that God multiplies givers' resources. The theological logic: God's provision creates abundance not for hoarding but for sharing, maintaining church equality and unity. The collection symbolizes Gentile-Jewish unity while demonstrating covenant ethics - what Israel was exhorted to do, the new covenant community now fulfills, recognizing all provision as God's gift enabling generosity.
