# Romans 11:2-4 to 1 Kings 19:10

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 2|Romans 11:2]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 10|1 Kings 19:10]] (Elijah at Horeb)
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 19#1 Kings 19 . 18|1 Kings 19:18]] ("I will leave seven thousand in Israel")

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul applies Elijah narrative typologically: Just as God preserved remnant in Elijah's day (7,000), so now God has preserved a remnant. Elijah's despair was unfounded then; Jewish Christian despair unfounded now.
