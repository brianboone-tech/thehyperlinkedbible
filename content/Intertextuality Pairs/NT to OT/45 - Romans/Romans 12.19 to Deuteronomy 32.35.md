# Romans 12:19 to Deuteronomy 32:35

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 19|Romans 12:19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35]] (Song of Moses)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul applies God's vengeance on Israel's enemies (in Deut 32 context) to believers leaving judgment to God. Believers must not usurp God's role as judge. Paul adds "says the Lord" as pronouncement formula for emphasis.
