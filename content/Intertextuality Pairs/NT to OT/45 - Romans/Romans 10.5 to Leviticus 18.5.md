# Romans 10:5 to Leviticus 18:5

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 5|Romans 10:5]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 18#Leviticus 18 . 5|Leviticus 18:5]] ("You shall keep my statutes and rules; if a person does them, he shall live by them")

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul cites Leviticus to describe the "righteousness based on law" requiring perfect obedience. Contrasted with righteousness by faith in following verses.
