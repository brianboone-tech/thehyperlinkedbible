# Romans 10:18 to Psalm 19:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 18|Romans 10:18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 19#Psalm 19 . 4|Psalm 19:4]] (MT 19:5; LXX 18:5) (The heavens declare God's glory)

**Type**: Direct quotation / Applied to gospel

**Significance**: Paul applies the universal testimony of creation to the universal proclamation of the gospel. If creation's witness reaches everywhere, how much more the gospel message.
