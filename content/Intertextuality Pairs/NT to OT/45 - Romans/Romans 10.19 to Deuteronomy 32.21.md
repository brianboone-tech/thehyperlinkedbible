# Romans 10:19 to Deuteronomy 32:21

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 19|Romans 10:19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 21|Deuteronomy 32:21]] (Song of Moses)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: God's threat to provoke Israel to jealousy through a "non-nation" is fulfilled in Gentile acceptance of Messiah while Israel rejects. Critical text for understanding Israel's jealousy leading to salvation (developed in ch. 11).
