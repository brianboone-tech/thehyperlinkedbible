# Romans 8:32 to Genesis 22:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 32|Romans 8:32]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 16|Genesis 22:16]] (Abraham did not withhold his son)

**Type**: Allusion / Typological

**Significance**: Paul echoes the Akedah (binding of Isaac). God did what he prevented Abraham from doing - actually sacrificed his own beloved Son. The typological connection is profound: Isaac spared, Christ sacrificed.
