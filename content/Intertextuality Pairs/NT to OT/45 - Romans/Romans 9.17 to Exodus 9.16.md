# Romans 9:17 to Exodus 9:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 17|Romans 9:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 9#Exodus 9 . 16|Exodus 9:16]] (God's word to Pharaoh)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul uses Pharaoh as example of God's sovereign hardening to accomplish his purposes. God raised Pharaoh up to demonstrate divine power and proclaim his name universally.
