# Romans 3:20 to Psalm 143:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 20|Romans 3:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 143#Psalm 143 . 2|Psalm 143:2]] (LXX 142:2) - "No living person will be justified before you"

**Type**: Allusion / adapted quotation

**Significance**: Paul adapts the Psalmist's plea for mercy to demonstrate that justification cannot come through law observance. He adds "by works of the law" to make explicit what is implicit in the psalm.
