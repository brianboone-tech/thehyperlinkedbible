# Romans 11:35 to Job 41:11

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 35|Romans 11:35]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 41#Job 41 . 11|Job 41:11]] (MT 41:3) / Targum Job 41:3 ("Who has preceded me in works of creation that I must repay?")

**Type**: Allusion / Targumic form

**Significance**: No one can put God in debt. Applied to salvation: God's mercy cannot be earned or obligated by human works.
