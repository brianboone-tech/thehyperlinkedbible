# Romans 9:6-9 to Genesis 21:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 6|Romans 9:6]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 12|Genesis 21:12]] ("Through Isaac shall your offspring be named")
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 10|Genesis 18:10]] ("I will return... and Sarah shall have a son")

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul establishes the principle of election within Israel itself. God's promise to Abraham was specifically through Isaac, not Ishmael, demonstrating that physical descent alone does not guarantee promise fulfillment.
