# Romans 1:17 to Habakkuk 2:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 17|Romans 1:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/35 - Habakkuk/Habakkuk 2#Habakkuk 2 . 4|Habakkuk 2:4]]

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Foundation verse for Paul's doctrine of justification by faith. The Habakkuk context speaks of living through faithfulness during judgment; Paul applies this to justification through faith in Christ.
