# Romans 15:11 to Psalm 117:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 11|Romans 15:11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 117#Psalm 117 . 1|Psalm 117:1]] (LXX 116:1) (Shortest psalm)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Universal call to praise YHWH. Paul transposes "Lord" to end of first clause, possibly for emphasis. All peoples called to praise, fulfilling prophetic vision of nations worshiping true God. "Lord" here may include Christ in divine identity.
