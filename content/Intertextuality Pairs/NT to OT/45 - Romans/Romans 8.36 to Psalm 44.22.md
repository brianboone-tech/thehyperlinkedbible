# Romans 8:36 to Psalm 44:22

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 36|Romans 8:36]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 44#Psalm 44 . 22|Psalm 44:22]] (MT 44:23; LXX 43:23)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul applies Israel's suffering for God to the church's experience. The psalm context is communal lament over undeserved suffering; Paul sees this pattern fulfilled in Christian suffering.
