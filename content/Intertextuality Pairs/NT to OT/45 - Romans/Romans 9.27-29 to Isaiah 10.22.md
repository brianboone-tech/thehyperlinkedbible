# Romans 9:27-29 to Isaiah 10:22

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 27|Romans 9:27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 22|Isaiah 10:22]] (Remnant theology)
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 10|Hosea 1:10]] (MT 2:1) ("Number of Israel like sand of sea" - inserted into Isaiah quote)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 1#Isaiah 1 . 9|Isaiah 1:9]] ("If the Lord of hosts had not left us survivors...")

**Type**: Direct quotation / Merged citations

**Significance**: Paul merges Isaiah and Hosea to show both judgment (only remnant saved) and hope (remnant will be saved). The insertion of Hosea 1:10 into Isaiah 10:22 creates theological tension: vast number yet only remnant, pointing to future salvation.
