# Romans 9:33 to Isaiah 28:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 33|Romans 9:33]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 28#Isaiah 28 . 16|Isaiah 28:16]] ("Behold, I am laying in Zion... a cornerstone... whoever believes will not be in haste")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 8#Isaiah 8 . 14|Isaiah 8:14]] ("He will become... a stone of stumbling and rock of offense")

**Type**: Merged quotation

**Significance**: Paul combines two Isaiah texts about a stone in Zion - one positive (cornerstone of faith), one negative (stumbling stone). Christ is both: cornerstone for believers, stumbling stone for unbelievers. Brilliant conflation showing dual response to Christ.
