# Romans 10:6-8 to Deuteronomy 30:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 6|Romans 10:6]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 12|Deuteronomy 30:12]] (The word is not in heaven... not beyond the sea... but very near you)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 9#Deuteronomy 9 . 4|Deuteronomy 9:4]] (Context: righteousness not by Israel's works)

**Type**: Highly interpretive quotation / Christological midrash

**Significance**: Paul radically reinterprets Moses' words about the accessibility of the law to speak of the accessibility of Christ through faith. One of Paul's most creative uses of OT - the "near word" is now the gospel word about Christ.
