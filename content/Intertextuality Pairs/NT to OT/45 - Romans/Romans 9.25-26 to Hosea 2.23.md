# Romans 9:25-26 to Hosea 2:23

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 25|Romans 9:25]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 2#Hosea 2 . 23|Hosea 2:23]] (MT 2:25) ("I will say to Not My People, 'You are my people'")
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 1#Hosea 1 . 10|Hosea 1:10]] (MT 2:1) ("In the place where it was said to them, 'You are not my people,' it shall be said to them, 'Children of the living God'")

**Type**: Direct quotation (inverted order)

**Significance**: Paul applies Hosea's prophecy about Israel's restoration after judgment to the calling of Gentiles. The pattern is: rejection → calling → acceptance. Controversial application of Israel-specific prophecy to Gentiles.
