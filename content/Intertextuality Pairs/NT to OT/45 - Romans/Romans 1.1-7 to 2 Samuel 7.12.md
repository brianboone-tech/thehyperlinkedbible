# Romans 1:1-7 to 2 Samuel 7:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 1#Romans 1 . 1|Romans 1:1]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 12|2 Samuel 7:12]] (Davidic covenant - "seed of David")
- General: The entire prophetic corpus (promise of gospel in Scripture)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment / Typological

**Significance**: Paul establishes that the gospel announced is not new but promised throughout the OT Scriptures. Christ's identity as Davidic seed fulfills the covenant promise to David.
