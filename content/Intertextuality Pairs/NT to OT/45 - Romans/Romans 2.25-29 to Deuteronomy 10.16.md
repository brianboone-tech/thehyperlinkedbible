# Romans 2:25-29 to Deuteronomy 10:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 2#Romans 2 . 25|Romans 2:25]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10#Deuteronomy 10 . 16|Deuteronomy 10:16]] (circumcise your heart)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 4#Jeremiah 4 . 4|Jeremiah 4:4]] (circumcision of heart vs. flesh)
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 26#Leviticus 26 . 41|Leviticus 26:41]] (uncircumcised heart)

**Type**: Allusion / Typological fulfillment

**Significance**: Paul's "circumcision of the heart by the Spirit" fulfills the OT promise of God's transformative work in Deuteronomy 30:6. The eschatological inward reality surpasses the outward sign.
