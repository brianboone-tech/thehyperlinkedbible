# Romans 10:15 to Isaiah 52:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 15|Romans 10:15]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 7|Isaiah 52:7]] (Herald announcing good news of salvation to Zion)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul applies Isaiah's herald bringing news of Israel's salvation from exile to apostolic gospel preaching. The eschatological salvation announced to Zion is now proclaimed universally.
