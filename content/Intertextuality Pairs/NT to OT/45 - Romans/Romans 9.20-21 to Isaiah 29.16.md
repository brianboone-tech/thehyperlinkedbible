# Romans 9:20-21 to Isaiah 29:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 20|Romans 9:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 29#Isaiah 29 . 16|Isaiah 29:16]] ("Shall the potter be regarded as the clay?")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 45#Isaiah 45 . 9|Isaiah 45:9]] ("Woe to him who strives with his Maker! Shall the clay say to him who forms it, 'What are you making?'")
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 18#Jeremiah 18 . 6|Jeremiah 18:6]] ("Like clay in the potter's hand, so are you in my hand")

**Type**: Allusion / Combined echo

**Significance**: Potter-clay imagery is foundational OT metaphor for Creator-creature relationship. Paul uses it to assert God's sovereign right as Creator to form vessels for different purposes.
