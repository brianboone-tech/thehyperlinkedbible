# Romans 9:15 to Exodus 33:19

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 15|Romans 9:15]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 19|Exodus 33:19]] (God's self-revelation to Moses)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: God's declaration of sovereign freedom in showing mercy, given in the context of post-golden calf intercession. Establishes that mercy is entirely God's prerogative, not earned by human effort.
