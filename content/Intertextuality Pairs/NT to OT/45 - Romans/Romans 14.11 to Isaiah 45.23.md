# Romans 14:11 to Isaiah 45:23

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 14#Romans 14 . 11|Romans 14:11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 45#Isaiah 45 . 23|Isaiah 45:23]] (Every knee shall bow, every tongue shall swear)
- Multiple texts with oath formula "As I live" (Num 14:28; Isa 49:18; Jer 22:24; Ezek 5:11; etc.)

**Type**: Direct quotation with modified oath formula

**Significance**: Paul substitutes Ezekiel's oath formula for Isaiah's lengthy asseveration. Isaiah context is salvific - God calls all nations to turn and be saved. Paul applies this to final judgment where all will confess God/Christ. Identifies Christ with YHWH (see Phil 2:10-11).
