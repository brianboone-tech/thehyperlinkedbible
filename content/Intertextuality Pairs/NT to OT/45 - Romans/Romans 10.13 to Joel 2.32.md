# Romans 10:13 to Joel 2:32

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 10#Romans 10 . 13|Romans 10:13]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 32|Joel 2:32]] (MT 3:5)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul applies Joel's prophecy about calling on YHWH to calling on Christ as Lord, identifying Jesus with YHWH. Universal scope ("everyone") includes Gentiles in Joel's eschatological promise.
