# Romans 15:21 to Isaiah 52:15

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 15#Romans 15 . 21|Romans 15:21]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 15|Isaiah 52:15]] (Suffering Servant song)

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Mirrors Paul's earlier use of Isaiah 53:1 (in 10:16). There: Isaiah's lament over Israel's unbelief. Here: Nations who never heard will see and understand the exalted Servant. This drives Paul's mission to unreached peoples. The Servant brings light to nations (Isa 42:6; 49:6).
