# Romans 11:1 to 1 Samuel 12:22

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 1|Romans 11:1]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 12#1 Samuel 12 . 22|1 Samuel 12:22]] ("The Lord will not abandon his people")
- Multiple judgment texts (Judges 6:13; 2 Kings 17:20; Psalm 60:1; Jeremiah 7:29; etc.)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Paul alludes to Samuel's assurance and to judgment texts where God "rejected" Israel, establishing tension: Has rejection happened again? His answer: No, because remnant exists.
