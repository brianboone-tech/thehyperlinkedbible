# Romans 12:20 to Proverbs 25:21

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 12#Romans 12 . 20|Romans 12:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 25#Proverbs 25 . 21|Proverbs 25:21]] -22

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Love for enemies may bring shame leading to repentance (or further condemnation). Paul omits Proverbs' "the Lord will reward you" because he's already addressed recompense in v.19. Combines hope for enemy's conversion with allowance for divine judgment.
