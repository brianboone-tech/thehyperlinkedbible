# Romans 11:26-27 to Isaiah 59:20

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 11#Romans 11 . 26|Romans 11:26]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 20|Isaiah 59:20]] (The Redeemer will come to/for Zion)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 27#Isaiah 27 . 9|Isaiah 27:9]] ("This is my blessing when I take away his sin")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 14#Psalm 14 . 7|Psalm 14:7]] (Salvation from Zion, turning captivity)

**Type**: Merged quotation / Highly interpretive

**Significance**: Paul changes Isaiah's "to Zion" to "from Zion" - the Redeemer comes FROM heavenly Zion FOR exiled Jacob. Brilliant move: Israel is in exile from sin; Christ comes from above to redeem. Combines Isaiah's exile-return theology with christological fulfillment. Central text for Israel's future salvation.
