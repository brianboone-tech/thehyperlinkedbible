# Romans 8:20 to Genesis 3:17

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 8#Romans 8 . 20|Romans 8:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 17|Genesis 3:17]] (Curse on creation)
- Ecclesiastes (hebel/vanity/futility)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: The creation's subjection to futility refers to the Genesis curse. Paul sees creation's liberation tied to the revelation of the sons of God.
