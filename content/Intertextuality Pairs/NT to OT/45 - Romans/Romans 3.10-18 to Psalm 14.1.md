# Romans 3:10-18 to Psalm 14:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 3#Romans 3 . 10|Romans 3:10]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 14#Psalm 14 . 1|Psalm 14:1]] (= Psalm 53:1-3) - "None is righteous"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 5#Psalm 5 . 9|Psalm 5:9]] (MT 5:10) - "Their throat is an open grave"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 140#Psalm 140 . 3|Psalm 140:3]] (MT 140:4; LXX 139:4) - "Viper's venom under their lips"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 10#Psalm 10 . 7|Psalm 10:7]] - "Mouth full of curses and bitterness"
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 59#Isaiah 59 . 7|Isaiah 59:7]] -8 - "Feet swift to shed blood, ruin and misery in their paths"
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 36#Psalm 36 . 1|Psalm 36:1]] (MT 36:2; LXX 35:2) - "No fear of God before their eyes"

**Type**: Catena (chain quotation)

**Significance**: Paul assembles a "catena" of OT texts to demonstrate universal sinfulness. This is one of the most extensive chains in Romans, establishing the comprehensive scope of human guilt before God.
