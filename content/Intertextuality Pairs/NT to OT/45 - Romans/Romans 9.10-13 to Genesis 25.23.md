# Romans 9:10-13 to Genesis 25:23

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/45 - Romans/Romans 9#Romans 9 . 10|Romans 9:10]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 25#Genesis 25 . 23|Genesis 25:23]] (Oracle to Rebekah: "The older shall serve the younger")
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 2|Malachi 1:2]] ("Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated")

**Type**: Direct quotation

**Significance**: Paul uses these texts to demonstrate God's sovereign election before birth, independent of works. The Malachi quotation applies to nations (Israel and Edom), showing corporate election patterns.
