# 1 Peter 2:9 to Exodus 19:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 9|1 Peter 2:9]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 6|Exodus 19:6]] (LXX: βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα - "royal priesthood")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 20|Isaiah 43:20-21]] (chosen people who declare praise)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 6|Deuteronomy 7:6]] (treasured possession)

**Type**: Direct quotation (LXX verbatim) with allusive blend

**Significance**: Peter quotes Exodus 19:6 (LXX) verbatim—βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα ("royal priesthood")—applying Israel's covenant identity directly to the church. The fourfold description combines Exodus 19:6 with Isaiah 43:20-21 and Deuteronomy 7:6, transferring Israel's unique status to believers in Christ. Critical shift: from conditional promise ("if you obey") to accomplished reality ("you ARE")—based not on law-keeping but on Christ's blood. The church inherits Israel's priestly-royal calling, mediating God's presence to the nations by proclaiming His excellencies. What was ethnically restricted becomes universally available through redemption; what was conditionally promised becomes unconditionally given through union with the true High Priest-King.
