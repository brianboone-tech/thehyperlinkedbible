# 1 Peter 2:3 to Psalms 34:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/60 - 1 Peter/1 Peter 2#1 Peter 2 . 3|1 Peter 2:3]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 34#Psalm 34 . 8|Psalms 34:8]] (taste and see the Lord is good)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 34#Psalm 34 . 1|Psalms 34]] (righteous sufferer's testimony)

**Type**: Echo/thematic parallel

**Significance**: Peter employs Psalm 34's experiential language to describe Christian maturation through encountering God's goodness. The psalmist's testimony of God's deliverance from trouble resonates with 1 Peter's context of suffering believers. Tasting God's goodness is not merely intellectual assent but lived experience that produces spiritual growth. The milk metaphor connects to the psalm's invitation, showing that genuine spiritual development flows from encountering Christ's goodness. Peter thus bridges OT worship language with NT sanctification, demonstrating how God's goodness tasted by Israel finds fuller expression in Christ, whose followers grow through experiencing his saving grace.
