# Hebrews 1:3 to Psalms 110:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 3|Hebrews 1:3]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalms 110:1]] (sitting at God's right hand)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: First of five allusions to Psalm 110:1 in Hebrews (1:3; 8:1; 10:12-13; 12:2). Establishes the exaltation theme and Christ's session at God's right hand after completing his atoning work. The seated posture contrasts with Levitical priests who stood daily offering repeated sacrifices, signaling the finality and sufficiency of Christ's once-for-all sacrifice. This royal psalm becomes foundational to Hebrews' entire argument about Christ's superiority and present heavenly ministry.
