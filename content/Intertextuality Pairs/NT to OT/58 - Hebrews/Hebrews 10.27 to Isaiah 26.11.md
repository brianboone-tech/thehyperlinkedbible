# Hebrews 10:27 to Isaiah 26:11

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 27|Hebrews 10:27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 26#Isaiah 26 . 11|Isaiah 26:11]] ("fire will consume the enemies")
- [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 1#Zephaniah 1 . 18|Zephaniah 1:18]] (fire of judgment)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Harshest warning in Hebrews (10:26-31). Those who reject new covenant truth face eschatological judgment by fire. Context oriented to "the day" (10:25) and second coming. Fire as judgment motif common in OT prophets. The prophetic fire imagery intensifies the warning—apostasy brings not mere earthly death but eschatological destruction. "Adversaries" (hypentantioi) indicates those who oppose God become enemies deserving judgment.
