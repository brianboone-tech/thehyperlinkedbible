# Hebrews 1:7 to Psalms 104:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 7|Hebrews 1:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 104#Psalm 104 . 4|Psalms 104:4]]

**Type**: Direct Quotation

**Significance**: Contrasts mutable, servant nature of angels (who are "made") with immutable, ruling nature of the Son. Angels are changeable servants functioning at God's command; the Son is eternal ruler. The use of "makes" (poieō) emphasizes angels are creatures subject to change, setting up the contrast with the Son's unchanging divine nature in verses 8-12.
