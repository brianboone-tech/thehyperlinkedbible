# Hebrews 11:17-19 to Genesis 22:1-10

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 17|Hebrews 11:17-19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 1|Genesis 22:1-10]] (Binding of Isaac/Aqedah)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Supreme example of Abraham's faith. Trusted God could raise the dead (received Isaac back "figuratively"). Shows faith perseveres even when God's commands seem contradictory to his promises. The author's interpretation is sophisticated—Abraham reasoned God could raise Isaac from the dead, making resurrection hope foundational to obedience. Receiving Isaac back becomes a "type" (parabolē) of resurrection, prefiguring Christ's actual resurrection.
