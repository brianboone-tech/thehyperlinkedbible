# Hebrews 11:18 to Genesis 21:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 18|Hebrews 11:18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 21#Genesis 21 . 12|Genesis 21:12]]

**Type**: Quotation

**Significance**: Promise that descendants would come through Isaac, yet Abraham willing to sacrifice him. Faith trusts God's faithfulness to promises. The logic: if God promised descendants through Isaac, and Abraham must sacrifice Isaac, then God must raise him from the dead. Faith holds together divine promise and divine command by trusting God's power and faithfulness.
