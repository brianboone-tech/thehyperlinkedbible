# Hebrews 11:22 to Genesis 50:22-26

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 22|Hebrews 11:22]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 50#Genesis 50 . 22|Genesis 50:22-26]] (Joseph's death, instructions about bones)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 13#Exodus 13 . 19|Exodus 13:19]] (Moses takes Joseph's bones)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Faith in God's promises about future deliverance. Looked ahead to exodus. Joseph's concern for his bones shows faith in God's promise to bring Israel out of Egypt back to the promised land. Faith outlasts one's own lifetime, trusting God's faithfulness to future generations.
