# Hebrews 12:12-13 to Isaiah 35:3

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 12|Hebrews 12:12-13]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 35#Isaiah 35 . 3|Isaiah 35:3]] ("Strengthen the weak hands... the feeble knees")
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 4#Proverbs 4 . 26|Proverbs 4:26]] ("make straight paths for your feet")

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Encouragement to hope in God's salvation. Isaiah 35 context: God's way of holiness, redemption coming. Combined with Proverbs on choosing right path. Addresses spiritual discouragement. The Isaiah context promises eschatological salvation—strengthening the weak anticipates God's coming deliverance when the lame will leap and the mute sing. The call to "make straight paths" (Prov 4:26) has corporate dimension—so the lame may not be put out of joint but healed. The strong must persevere for the sake of the weak.
