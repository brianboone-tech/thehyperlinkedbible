# Hebrews 7:1-4 to Genesis 14:17-20

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 7#Hebrews 7 . 1|Hebrews 7:1-4]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 14#Genesis 14 . 17|Genesis 14:17-20]] (Melchizedek meets Abraham, receives tithe, blesses Abraham)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 4|Psalms 110:4]] (priesthood according to order of Melchizedek)

**Type**: Typological

**Significance**: Melchizedek's superiority to Levitical priesthood demonstrated: (1) Abraham gave him tithes, (2) blessed Abraham, (3) name meanings ("king of righteousness" and "king of peace"), (4) scriptural silence about genealogy taken as typological—"made like the Son of God." Establishes basis for Christ's superior, eternal priesthood. The hermeneutical move is sophisticated—what Scripture doesn't say about Melchizedek (no genealogy, no beginning/end) is interpreted typologically to prefigure Christ's eternal priesthood. Since Levi was "in the loins of Abraham" when Melchizedek blessed him, even the Levitical priesthood acknowledged Melchizedek's superiority through Abraham's tithe-giving.
