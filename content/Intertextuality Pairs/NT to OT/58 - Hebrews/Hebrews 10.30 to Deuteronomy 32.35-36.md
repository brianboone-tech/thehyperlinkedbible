# Hebrews 10:30 to Deuteronomy 32:35-36

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 10#Hebrews 10 . 30|Hebrews 10:30]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 35|Deuteronomy 32:35-36]] (Song of Moses)

**Type**: Direct Quotation (two-part)

**Significance**: Reinforces warning with two parts of Deut. 32 joined by "and again." God's vengeance is certain. Though 32:36 could mean vindication, in this context means judgment (followed by "fearful to fall into hands of living God" 10:31). Eschatological judgment awaits apostates. The Song of Moses context (covenant rebellion bringing judgment) fits the warning's logic. God who judges his people will not overlook apostasy—falling into the living God's hands for judgment is terrifying.
