# Hebrews 9:23 to Leviticus 16:16-19

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 23|Hebrews 9:23]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 16#Leviticus 16 . 16|Leviticus 16:16-19]] (Day of Atonement cleansing of holy place)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Atonement made for holy place/tent of meeting because of people's impurity (Lev. 16:16-19). Heavenly tabernacle cleansed in conjunction with cleansing God's people through Christ's new covenant sacrifice. Not that heaven is impure, but Christ's blood cleanses both people and worship context. The reasoning moves from lesser to greater—if even the earthly sanctuary required blood purification, how much more must Christ's blood be necessary to cleanse believers' consciences to serve the living God in the true heavenly sanctuary.
