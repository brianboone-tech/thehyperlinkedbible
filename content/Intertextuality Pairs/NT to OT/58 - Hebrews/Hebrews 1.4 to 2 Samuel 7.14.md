# Hebrews 1:4 to 2 Samuel 7:14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 4|Hebrews 1:4]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14]] (sonship and name inheritance)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalms 2:7]] (divine sonship declaration)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Introduces the superiority-to-angels theme that dominates chapters 1-2, grounded in the superior "name" (Son) inherited by Christ. The background in 2 Samuel 7 and Psalm 2 establishes that "Son" is not merely a title but represents unique relationship, authority, and inheritance. This prepares for the seven-quotation chain that follows, demonstrating from Scripture why the Son's name exceeds angelic dignities.
