# Hebrews 11:20 to Genesis 27:27-29

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 20|Hebrews 11:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 27#Genesis 27 . 27|Genesis 27:27-29]] (Isaac blesses Jacob and Esau)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Faith looks to future, invoking blessings on descendants. The blessings concern future realities Isaac would not see, demonstrating faith's confidence in God's promises extending beyond one's lifetime.
