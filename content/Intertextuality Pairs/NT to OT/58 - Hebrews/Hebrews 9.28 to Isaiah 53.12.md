# Hebrews 9:28 to Isaiah 53:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 28|Hebrews 9:28]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] (Suffering Servant "bore the sins of many")

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Alludes to fourth Suffering Servant song. "Bore sins of many" connects Christ's sacrifice to Day of Atonement imagery. Context of Isaiah 53: carried sickness, wounded for transgressions, guilt offering, bore iniquities (53:4-5, 10-12). Reinforces substitutionary atonement. The Suffering Servant motif interprets Christ's death as vicarious—he bore "the sins of many" (not his own), fulfilling Isaiah's prophecy. The once-for-all emphasis contrasts with repeated Levitical sacrifices, while the eschatological note ("to save those waiting") orients hope toward Christ's return.
