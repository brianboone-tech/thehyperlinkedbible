# Hebrews 8:1 to Psalms 110:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 8#Hebrews 8 . 1|Hebrews 8:1]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalms 110:1]]

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Third allusion to Psalm 110:1. Shifts focus to heavenly realm where Christ now ministers. "In the heavens" (not just "on high" as in 1:3). Introduces contrast between earthly and heavenly tabernacles. The session at God's right hand now specifies location ("in the heavens") and ministry context ("minister in the sanctuary"), preparing for the argument that Christ serves in the true tabernacle not made with hands. This spatial move is crucial—Christ's superior priesthood requires a superior sanctuary.
