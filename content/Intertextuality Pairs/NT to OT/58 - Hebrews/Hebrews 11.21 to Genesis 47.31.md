# Hebrews 11:21 to Genesis 47:31

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 21|Hebrews 11:21]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 47#Genesis 47 . 31|Genesis 47:31]] (Jacob worships, leans on staff)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Faith expressed in worship and blessing future generations even at death. Jacob's leaning on his staff while worshiping shows faith perseveres through physical weakness, maintaining eschatological hope and transmitting blessing to the next generation.
