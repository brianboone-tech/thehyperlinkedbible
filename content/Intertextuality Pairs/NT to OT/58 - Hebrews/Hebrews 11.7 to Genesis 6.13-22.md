# Hebrews 11:7 to Genesis 6:13-22

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 7|Hebrews 11:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 6#Genesis 6 . 13|Genesis 6:13-22]] (Noah and ark)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Faith leads to obedient preparation. Condemned the world, became heir of righteousness by faith. Noah's construction of the ark based on God's warning "about events as yet unseen" exemplifies faith's forward-looking trust. His obedient action both saved his household and judged the world—a pattern for Hebrews' readers facing their own judgment context.
