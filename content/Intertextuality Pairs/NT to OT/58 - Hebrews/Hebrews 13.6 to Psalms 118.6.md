# Hebrews 13:6 to Psalms 118:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 13#Hebrews 13 . 6|Hebrews 13:6]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 6|Psalms 118:6]] (Passover/Hallel psalm)

**Type**: Direct Quotation

**Significance**: Confession of confidence in face of persecution. Psalm 118 context: deliverance from affliction, God as helper superior to human help, victory over enemies. Perfect response to God's promise in 13:5. God's help removes fear of what humans can do. The Hallel context (Passover liturgy) connects to Israel's paradigmatic deliverance, applied now to believers' confidence amidst persecution. If the Lord is helper, human opposition becomes impotent—"What can man do to me?" This rhetorical question expects the answer: nothing that ultimately matters.
