# Hebrews 1:2 to Psalms 2:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 1#Hebrews 1 . 2|Hebrews 1:2]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 8|Psalms 2:8]] (inheritance motif)
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8#Proverbs 8 . 22|Proverbs 8:22-31]] (wisdom in creation)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1]] (creation account)

**Type**: Allusion/Echo

**Significance**: Establishes Christ's role as both creator and heir, fundamental to Hebrews' high Christology showing the Son's supremacy. The allusion to Psalms 2 connects messianic inheritance with the Son's appointment, while Proverbs 8's personified Wisdom prefigures Christ's role in creation. This opening salvo demonstrates that Christ is not merely another prophet but the divine agent of creation and consummation.
