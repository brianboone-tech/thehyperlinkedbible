# Hebrews 11:12 to Genesis 22:17

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 11#Hebrews 11 . 12|Hebrews 11:12]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 17|Genesis 22:17]] (God's promise to Abraham)

**Type**: Quotation/Allusion

**Significance**: God's promise of innumerable descendants fulfilled. Connected to Aqedah in 11:17-19. The fulfillment from "one man, and him as good as dead" demonstrates God's power to bring life from death—foreshadowing resurrection hope that grounds Abraham's willingness to offer Isaac.
