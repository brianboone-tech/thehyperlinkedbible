# Hebrews 12:18-21 to Exodus 19:16-22

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 12#Hebrews 12 . 18|Hebrews 12:18-21]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 16|Exodus 19:16-22]]; [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 18|20:18-21]] (Sinai theophany)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 4#Deuteronomy 4 . 11|Deuteronomy 4:11-12]]; [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 23|5:23-27]] (fear at mountain)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 9#Deuteronomy 9 . 19|Deuteronomy 9:19]] (Moses feared)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: Description of Sinai covenant establishment—terrifying, impersonal, inaccessible. Fire, darkness, gloom, trumpet, voice, touch forbidden. Even Moses terrified. Sets up contrast with Mount Zion (12:22-24). Old covenant characterized by distance and fear; new covenant by access and celebration. The fearsome theophany underscores old covenant's character—mediated through Moses, maintained by distance, enforced by threat of death for touching the mountain. This inaccessible holiness contrasts dramatically with new covenant access "to Mount Zion... the heavenly Jerusalem" (12:22ff), where believers have already come through Christ's mediation.
