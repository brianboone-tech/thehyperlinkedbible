# Hebrews 9:20 to Exodus 24:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/58 - Hebrews/Hebrews 9#Hebrews 9 . 20|Hebrews 9:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]] (ratification of Sinai covenant with blood)

**Type**: Direct Quotation

**Significance**: Moses sprinkled blood to inaugurate old covenant. Demonstrates covenants established by blood sacrifice. Author adds elements from Exodus 12:22; Leviticus 14:4-7; Numbers 19:6, 18 (water, scarlet wool, hyssop) to show ceremonial sprinkling pattern. Parallels Christ's words at Last Supper establishing new covenant in his blood. The typological pattern: blood ratified the first covenant, therefore blood (Christ's superior blood) ratifies the new. "Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness of sins" (9:22) becomes the theological principle undergirding the necessity of Christ's sacrifice.
