# Colossians 1:6 → Genesis 1:28

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/51 - Colossians/Colossians 1#Colossians 1 . 6|Colossians 1:6]] — "bearing fruit and increasing in all the world"

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] (LXX: αὐξάνεσθε - "increase!")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1]] (fruitfulness - פָּרָה *parah*)

**Type**: Allusion (verbal echo)

**Significance**: Creation mandate fulfilled through gospel expansion. Paul uses Genesis 1:28's fruitfulness language (αὐξάνω) to describe the gospel's spread "in all the world," showing Christ accomplishes what Adam failed to do.
