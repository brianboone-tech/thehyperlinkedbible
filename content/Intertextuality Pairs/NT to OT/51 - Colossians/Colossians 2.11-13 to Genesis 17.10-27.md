# Colossians 2:11-13 to Genesis 17:10-27

**NT Text**: "circumcision made without hands... removal of the body of flesh... circumcision of Christ"

**OT Source(s)**:
- Genesis 17:10-27 (4x "flesh of your uncircumcision" - σάρξ + ἀκροβυστία)
- Deuteronomy 10:16; Jeremiah 4:4 (circumcise your heart)
- Deuteronomy 30:6 (God will circumcise your heart in latter days - prophetic)
- Leviticus 26:41 (uncircumcised heart)

**Type**: Typological/Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Physical circumcision pointed to eschatological spiritual circumcision accomplished by Christ
