# Colossians 3:9-10 to Genesis 1:26-27

**NT Text**: "put off the old man... put on the new man... being renewed... according to the image of the one who created him"

**OT Source(s)**:
- Genesis 1:26-27 (κατ' εἰκόνα τοῦ κτίσαντος αὐτόν - "according to image... created him")
- Genesis 3:7, 21 (clothing metaphor - fig leaves removed, God clothes Adam/Eve)
- Genesis 2:17; 3:2-3 (knowledge/epignosis theme)

**Type**: Allusion (Adamic typology)

**Significance**: Believers strip off old Adam's clothing (fallen nature), don new Adam's clothing (resurrection life); restored to God like Adam/Eve after fall
