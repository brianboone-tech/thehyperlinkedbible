# Colossians 1:12-14 to Exodus 6:6

**NT Text**: "delivered from... darkness... redemption... inheritance of the saints in light"

**OT Source(s)**:
- Exodus 6:6; Deuteronomy 7:8; 13:5; 24:18; 2 Samuel 7:23 (redemption from Egypt - λυτρόω)
- Isaiah 42:7, 16; 49:9; 51:11; 52:3 (second exodus - darkness to light)
- Exodus 22:31; Leviticus 11:44-45; 19:2; 20:7, 26 (becoming "saints")
- Numbers/Deuteronomy/Joshua (15x "share of inheritance" - μερὶς + κλῆρος)

**Type**: Typological (exodus pattern applied to church)

**Significance**: Church undergoes greater exodus - spiritual deliverance from satanic darkness
