# Colossians 1:15 to Genesis 1:26-27

**NT Text**: "the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation"

**OT Source(s)**:
- Genesis 1:26-27 (image of God - εἰκών)
- Genesis 5:1-4 (Adam's sonship linked to image)
- Psalm 89:27-28 (88:27-28 LXX) (firstborn = messianic king)
- Proverbs 8:22-27 (wisdom as "beginning" before creation - possible)
- Wisdom of Solomon 7:26 (wisdom as "image of his goodness" - possible)

**Type**: Allusion (Christ as true/last Adam)

**Significance**: Christ embodies what Adam should have been - divine image bearer and ruler
