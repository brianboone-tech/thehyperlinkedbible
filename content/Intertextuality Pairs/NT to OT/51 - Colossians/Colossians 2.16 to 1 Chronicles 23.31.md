# Colossians 2:16 to 1 Chronicles 23:31

**NT Text**: "food or drink... festival or new moon or Sabbath days"

**OT Source(s)**:
- 1 Chronicles 23:31; 2 Chronicles 2:4; 31:3; Nehemiah 10:33; Isaiah 1:13-14; Ezekiel 45:17; Hosea 2:11 (tripartite formula - feasts + new moons + Sabbaths)
- Letter of Aristeas 142 (Jewish purity rules - "eat, drink, touch, hear, see")

**Type**: Direct reference to OT law

**Significance**: OT festivals were shadows pointing to Christ
