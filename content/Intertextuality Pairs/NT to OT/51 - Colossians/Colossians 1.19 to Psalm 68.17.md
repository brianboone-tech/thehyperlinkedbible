# Colossians 1:19 to Psalm 68:17

**NT Text**: "in him all the fullness was pleased to dwell"

**OT Source(s)**:
- Psalm 68:17 (67:17 LXX) (εὐδοκέω + κατοικέω - "well-pleased to dwell" in Zion/temple)
- Haggai 1:8; 2:7 (God pleased with house, will fill with glory)
- 2 Maccabees 14:35; 3 Maccabees 2:15-16 (pleased to dwell in temple)
- Targum 1 Kings 8 (God's good pleasure to dwell/Shekinah in temple)

**Type**: Allusion (temple imagery)

**Significance**: Christ is the new temple - locus of God's presence on earth
