# Colossians 2:2-3 to Daniel 2:19-23

**NT Text**: "the mystery of God, which is Christ, in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge"

**OT Source(s)**:
- Daniel 2:19-23 (mystery + wisdom + understanding + knowledge + hidden things)
- Proverbs 2:3-6 (wisdom + understanding + treasures + knowledge)

**Type**: Allusion (Daniel merged with Proverbs)

**Significance**: Christ is true wisdom (Torah's typological fulfillment)
