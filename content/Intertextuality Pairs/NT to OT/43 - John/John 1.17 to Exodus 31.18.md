# John 1:17 to Exodus 31:18

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 17|John 1:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 31#Exodus 31 . 18|Exodus 31:18]] (giving of the law)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]] (God's covenant faithfulness)

**Type**: Contrast and climax

**Significance**: Not sharp contrast but climactic eschatological revelation. Jesus fulfills Torah by perfect obedience, completion, and revealing true meaning. Transforms rather than abolishes. Jesus superior to Moses as mediator.
