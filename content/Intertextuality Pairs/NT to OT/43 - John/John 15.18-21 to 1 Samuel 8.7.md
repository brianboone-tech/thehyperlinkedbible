# John 15:18-21 to 1 Samuel 8:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 18|John 15:18-21]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 8#1 Samuel 8 . 7|1 Samuel 8:7]] (reception of prophet's message linked to stance toward God)
- [[Readable Bible/09 - 1 Samuel/1 Samuel 12#1 Samuel 12 . 22|1 Samuel 12:22]] (God's great name)

**Type**: Prophetic pattern

**Significance**: Second first-class condition (15:20) has OT parallels linking reception of prophet's message with people's stance toward God himself. "On account of my name" (15:21) reflects OT terminology for God and his great name, though here essentially synonymous with "me."
