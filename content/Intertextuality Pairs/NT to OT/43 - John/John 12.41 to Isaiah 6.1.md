# John 12:41 to Isaiah 6:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 41|John 12:41]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 1|Isaiah 6:1]] (Isaiah's vision: "I saw the Lord seated on a throne")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 6#Isaiah 6 . 5|Isaiah 6:5]] (glory of the King)

**Type**: Christological interpretation

**Significance**: Background = Isaiah 6 call narrative. Targum Jonathan changes "I saw the Lord" to "I saw the glory of the Lord" and "King" to "glory of the shekinah." Evangelist says Isaiah saw Jesus' glory (not Jesus himself directly). Isaiah foresaw God's pleasure in Suffering Servant who would be "lifted up" (52:13) yet "pierced" (53:5, 12). Like Abraham, Isaiah saw Jesus' "day" (cf. 8:58). Demonstrates Suffering Servant = Jesus.
