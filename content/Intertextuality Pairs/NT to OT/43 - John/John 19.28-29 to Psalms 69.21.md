# John 19:28-29 to Psalms 69:21

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 28|John 19:28-29]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 21|Psalms 69:21]] ("They... gave me vinegar for my thirst")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 15|Psalms 22:15]] (thirst)
- Matthew 27:34, 48 (Synoptic parallels)
- 1QH XII, 11 (Qumran citation of Psalm 69:21)

**Type**: Allusion/Fulfillment

**Significance**: Reference in 19:28 to Scripture being fulfilled builds on 19:24, continues evangelist's emphasis on Jesus actively bringing about respective passion events. John 19:28-29 most likely represents allusion to Psalm 69:21: "They... gave me vinegar for my thirst" (cf. Matthew 27:34, 48; Psalm 69:21 also cited in 1QH XII, 11; Carson 1988). Allusion may involve entire psalm in portrayal of righteous sufferer (Ridderbos; Carson). Choice of teleiōthē rather than plērōthē seems function of presence of tetelestai immediately preceding (19:28) and following again (19:30).
