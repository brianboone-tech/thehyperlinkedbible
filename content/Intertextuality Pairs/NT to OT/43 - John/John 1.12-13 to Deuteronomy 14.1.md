# John 1:12-13 to Deuteronomy 14:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 12|John 1:12-13]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 14#Deuteronomy 14 . 1|Deuteronomy 14:1]] (Israel as God's children)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 22|Exodus 4:22]] (Israel as God's son and firstborn)

**Type**: Typological transformation

**Significance**: Contrasts illegitimate claim of divine sonship based on physical descent from Abraham with true divine sonship through faith in Messiah. Not ethnic but spiritual birth determines membership in God's family.
