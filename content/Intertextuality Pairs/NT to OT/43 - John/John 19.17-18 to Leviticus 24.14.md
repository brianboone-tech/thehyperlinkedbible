# John 19:17-18 to Leviticus 24:14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 17|John 19:17-18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 14|Leviticus 24:14]] (executions outside camp/city)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 16|Psalms 22:16]] ("band of evil men has encircled me")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] ("numbered with the transgressors")

**Type**: Fulfillment/Typological

**Significance**: "He went out" (19:17) in keeping with Jewish custom prescribing executions outside camp/city. Evangelist may find crucifixion between two criminals (19:18) reminiscent of Psalm 22:16: "band of evil men has encircled me." May also echo Isaiah 53:12: "numbered with the transgressors" (Beasley-Murray; Bultmann; Brown).
