# John 12:13 to Psalms 118:25-26

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 13|John 12:13]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 25|Psalms 118:25-26]] (Hallel psalm sung at Passover/Tabernacles)

**Type**: Messianic acclamation

**Significance**: Royal processional psalm celebrating king's vice-regency under Yahweh. "Coming one" = messianic designation. "Hosanna" = "save now!" Psalm 118 well-known from Feast of Tabernacles/Passover liturgy. Crowds acclaim without full understanding (will later call for crucifixion). John adds "king of Israel" making messianic interpretation explicit (epexegetical definition or second acclamation).
