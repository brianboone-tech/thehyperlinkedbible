# John 1:51 to Genesis 28:12

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 51|John 1:51]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 12|Genesis 28:12]] (Jacob's ladder at Bethel)
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (Son of Man coming on clouds)

**Type**: Typological transformation

**Significance**: Jesus is "new Israel" and "new Bethel"—the place where God is revealed, where heaven and earth meet. Replaces Jacob's vision with greater revelation. Jesus surpasses Jacob/Israel (4:12). Son of Man designation links to Daniel's apocalyptic figure with eschatological authority. Fulfilled in Jesus' signs, ministry, and supremely in crucifixion.
