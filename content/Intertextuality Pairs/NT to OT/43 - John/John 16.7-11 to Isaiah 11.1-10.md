# John 16:7-11 to Isaiah 11:1-10

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 7|John 16:7-11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 1|Isaiah 11:1-10]] (pouring out of Spirit)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]] (new covenant)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 17|Ezekiel 11:17-20]] (Spirit, new heart)
- [[Readable Bible/29 - Joel/Joel 2#Joel 2 . 28|Joel 2:28-32]] (Spirit poured out)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 64#Isaiah 64 . 6|Isaiah 64:6]] (unrighteousness of world)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Anticipation of Spirit-pouring and kingdom age in OT prophetic literature. Convicting work: world's sin (expressed in hostility toward Jesus, 15:18-25), Jesus' righteousness as basis for world's judgment, resulting judgment of world itself (cf. 16:33). Carson: world's lack of righteousness may be in view (Isaiah 64:6).
