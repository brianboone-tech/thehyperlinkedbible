# John 17:18 to Exodus 28:41

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 18|John 17:18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 28#Exodus 28 . 41|Exodus 28:41]] (Moses consecrates priests)
- Sirach 45:4 (Moses consecrated by God)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 15#Deuteronomy 15 . 19|Deuteronomy 15:19]] (setting apart sacrificial animals)

**Type**: Commissioning pattern

**Significance**: Anticipates commissioning in 20:21. Just as Jesus "set apart" and sent, so disciples set apart to be sent. Jesus' relationship with Father = pattern for disciples' relationship to Jesus as sender (Köstenberger 1998b). Partial OT parallel: Moses consecrated by God (Sirach 45:4) in order to consecrate others as priests (Exodus 28:41). Jesus' self-sacrifice also reminiscent of OT "setting apart" sacrificial animals (Deuteronomy 15:19; Michaels).
