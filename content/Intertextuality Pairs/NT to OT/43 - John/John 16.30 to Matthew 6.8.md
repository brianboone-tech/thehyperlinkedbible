# John 16:30 to Matthew 6:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 30|John 16:30]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- Matthew 6:8 (God knows before asking)
- Josephus, Antiquities 6.230 (ability to anticipate questions = divinity)
- Also John 2:23-25 (Jesus' knowledge of people)

**Type**: Divine attribute

**Significance**: Jewish notion: ability to anticipate questions = mark of divinity. Jesus displays supernatural knowledge of hearts/needs.
