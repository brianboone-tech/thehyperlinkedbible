# John 5:37 to Exodus 19:3-6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 37|John 5:37]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 3|Exodus 19:3-6]] (Moses as mediator at Sinai)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 1|Genesis 18:1-2]] (theophanies)

**Type**: Contrast with direct revelation

**Significance**: Wilderness Israel received law through mediator without seeing God's form. Many OT leaders heard God's voice or "saw" God in limited ways, but direct unmediated vision impossible (cf. 1:18).
