# John 20:9 to Psalms 16:10

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 20#John 20 . 9|John 20:9]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 16#Psalm 16 . 10|Psalms 16:10]] ("You will not abandon me to the realm of the dead")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 10|Isaiah 53:10-12]] (Servant sees his offspring, prolongs his days after suffering)
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 6#Hosea 6 . 2|Hosea 6:2]] ("After two days he will revive us; on the third day he will restore us")
- Luke 24:25-27, 32, 44-47 (Scripture in its entirety pointing to Messiah's resurrection)

**Type**: General scriptural pattern/Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Evangelist's aside in 20:9 that Peter and "other disciple" did not yet understand Scripture—"that he [Jesus] must rise from dead"—may point to specific OT passage (suggestions include Psalm 16:10; Isaiah 53:10-12; and Hosea 6:2), although possible that reference is to Scripture in its entirety (cf. Luke 24:25-27, 32, 44-47).
