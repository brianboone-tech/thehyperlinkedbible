# John 12:38 to Isaiah 53:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 38|John 12:38]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 1|Isaiah 53:1]] (opening of fourth Servant Song)

**Type**: Fulfillment quotation

**Significance**: First of series of "in order that Scripture might be fulfilled" (hina plērōthē) citations dominating passion narrative. Identical to LXX = MT. Provides theodicy for Jewish rejection—it fulfills scriptural pattern. "Arm of the Lord" = God's power (frequent exodus metaphor: Exodus 6:1, 6; 15:16; Deuteronomy 4:34). Suffering Servant rejected but exalted. Connects Jesus with Isaianic Servant.
