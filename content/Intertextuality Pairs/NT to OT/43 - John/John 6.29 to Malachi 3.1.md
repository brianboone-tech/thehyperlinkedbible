# John 6:29 to Malachi 3:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 29|John 6:29]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1]] ("the one he has sent")
- General Jewish emphasis on "works of the law" (Romans 3:20, 27-28; Galatians 2:16)

**Type**: Radical redefinition

**Significance**: Stunning answer: God's requirement = believing in the sent Messiah. Contrasts with Jewish confidence in ability to meet God's demands through works.
