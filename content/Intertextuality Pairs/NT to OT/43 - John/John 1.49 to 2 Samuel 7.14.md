# John 1:49 to 2 Samuel 7:14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 49|John 1:49]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 14|2 Samuel 7:14]] (Davidic covenant: "I will be his father, he will be my son")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalms 2:7]] (messianic enthronement: "You are my Son")
- [[Readable Bible/36 - Zephaniah/Zephaniah 3#Zephaniah 3 . 15|Zephaniah 3:15]] ("The King of Israel, the Lord, is with you")

**Type**: Messianic titles

**Significance**: "Son of God" and "King of Israel" are roughly equivalent messianic titles. Reflects Jewish expectation of Davidic king-messiah. Current in Jesus' day (1Q28a II, 11-12; 4 Ezra 7:28-29).
