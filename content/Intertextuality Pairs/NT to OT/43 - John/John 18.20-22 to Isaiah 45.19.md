# John 18:20-22 to Isaiah 45:19

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 18#John 18 . 20|John 18:20-22]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 45#Isaiah 45 . 19|Isaiah 45:19]] (Yahweh's words)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 22#Exodus 22 . 28|Exodus 22:28]] ("Do not blaspheme God or curse the ruler of your people")
- Acts 23:5 (Paul's similar situation)

**Type**: Echo/Legal appeal

**Significance**: "I spoke nothing in secret" echoes Yahweh's words in Isaiah (45:19; 48:16; Brown; Schlatter). Jesus' point: not sinister conspiracy; message same in private as public (Morris; Carson; Borchert). When challenged (18:21-22), Jesus alludes to Exodus 22:28 law, denies violating it.
