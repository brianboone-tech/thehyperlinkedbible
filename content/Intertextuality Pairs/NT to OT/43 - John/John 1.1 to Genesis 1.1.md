# John 1:1 to Genesis 1:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 1|John 1:1]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 1|Genesis 1:1]] (creation account opening)

**Type**: Echo/Allusion

**Significance**: Establishes canonical link between Genesis and John. Jesus' existence precedes creation ("was the Word" vs. "God created"). The Word is agent of creation, present from eternity past, setting stage for John's lofty Christology. Echoes Proverbs 8 (personified Wisdom present at creation).
