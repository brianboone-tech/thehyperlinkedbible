# John 19:33-37 to Leviticus Rabbah 15:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 33|John 19:33-37]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- Leviticus Rabbah 15:2 on Leviticus 13:2 (blood and water = fully human)
- 1 John 5:6-8 (testimony of water and blood)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 6|Exodus 17:6]] ("Strike the rock, and water will come out")
- m. Pesah. 5:5-8; cf. m. Ohal. 3:5 (Passover allusion: hyssop, unbroken bones, mingled blood)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 34#Psalm 34 . 20|Psalms 34:20]] (bones not broken—see 19:36 below)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]] (piercing—see 19:37 below)

**Type**: Typological/Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: When soldiers came to Jesus and saw already dead, didn't break legs but one pierced side with spear; at once blood and water came out (19:34). Flow of blood and water indicates Jesus died as fully human being (Leviticus Rabbah 15:2 on Leviticus 13:2; cf. 1 John 5:6-8). John may also be alluding to Exodus 17, especially v. 6: "Strike the rock, and water will come out for the people to drink" (cf. Numbers 20:11; Carson).
