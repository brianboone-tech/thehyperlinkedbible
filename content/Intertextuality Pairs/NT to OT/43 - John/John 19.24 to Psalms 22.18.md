# John 19:24 to Psalms 22:18

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 24|John 19:24]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 18|Psalms 22:18]] (Davidic lament of righteous sufferer)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 15|Psalms 22:15]] (thirst)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 16|Psalms 22:16]] (pierced hands and feet)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 22#Psalm 22 . 17|Psalms 22:17]] (bones preserved)
- Matthew 27:46/Mark 15:34 (Jesus recites Psalm 22:1 on cross)

**Type**: Davidic typology/Fulfillment

**Significance**: Four Roman soldiers took Jesus' clothes (belt, sandals, head-covering, outer garment), divided into four shares. Outer garment seamless, woven from top to bottom—decide not to tear but cast lots. Evangelist asserts: "happened in order that the Scripture might be fulfilled" (19:24). OT references become more frequent as passion approaches (presumably because Jewish audience particularly skeptical about crucified Messiah; Carson; Witherington). OT reference clearly Psalm 22:18, Davidic passage regarding righteous sufferer. Synoptics quote Jesus reciting at least opening line (perhaps entire psalm) on cross (Matthew 27:46/Mark 15:34).
