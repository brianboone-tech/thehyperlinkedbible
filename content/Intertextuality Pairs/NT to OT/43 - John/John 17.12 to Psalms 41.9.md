# John 17:12 to Psalms 41:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 12|John 17:12]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 41#Psalm 41 . 9|Psalms 41:9]] (applied to Judas in John 13:18)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 25|Psalms 69:25]] (cited in Acts 1:20)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 57#Isaiah 57 . 4|Isaiah 57:4]] (character sense)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 34#Isaiah 34 . 5|Isaiah 34:5]] (destiny sense)
- Matthew 23:15; 1QS IX, 16; X, 19; CD-A VI, 15; XIII, 14 (Semitism parallels)

**Type**: Fulfillment/Theodicy

**Significance**: "Son of destruction" = Semitism (cf. Matthew 23:15; Dead Sea texts). Could refer to Judas's character (Isaiah 57:4 LXX; Jubilees 10:3) or destiny (Isaiah 34:5 LXX). In keeping with Johannine theodicy (12:38-40), even Judas's betrayal said to occur "in order for Scripture to be fulfilled." Doesn't alter Judas making decision as responsible agent (Mark 14:21/Matthew 26:24). God sovereignly overrode Judas's evil to bring about good purposes (cf. Genesis 50:20).
