# John 20:28 to Psalms 35:23-24

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 20#John 20 . 28|John 20:28]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 35#Psalm 35 . 23|Psalms 35:23-24]] (both expressions juxtaposed with reference to Yahweh)
- John 1:1, 18 (inclusio—Word was God; only God has made Father known)
- Numerous OT passages using "Lord" and "God" together for Yahweh

**Type**: Christological identification with Yahweh

**Significance**: Climactic christological confession, in inclusio with 1:1, 18, is Thomas's confession of Jesus as his "Lord" and "God" in 20:28 (Harris; Ridderbos). In OT these two expressions frequently juxtaposed with reference to Yahweh (e.g., Psalm 35:23-24).
