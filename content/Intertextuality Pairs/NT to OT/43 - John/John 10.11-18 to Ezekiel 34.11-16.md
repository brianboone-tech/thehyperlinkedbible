# John 10:11-18 to Ezekiel 34:11-16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 11|John 10:11-18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34#Ezekiel 34 . 11|Ezekiel 34:11-16]] (God himself will shepherd; David shepherd under him)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 24|Ezekiel 37:24]] (servant David as shepherd)
- Psalm 23 (Lord as shepherd)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 11|Isaiah 40:11]] (shepherd gathering lambs)

**Type**: Messianic fulfillment

**Significance**: Jesus fulfills divine shepherd role promised in Ezekiel. Davidic shepherd typology. Voluntary sacrificial death unprecedented—shepherd who dies FOR sheep (not just risking life). Resurrection power implicit (10:17-18).
