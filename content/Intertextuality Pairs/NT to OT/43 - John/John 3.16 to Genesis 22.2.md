# John 3:16 to Genesis 22:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 16|John 3:16]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 22#Genesis 22 . 2|Genesis 22:2]] (Abraham's "only" son Isaac)

**Type**: Typological/Soteriological

**Significance**: Monogenēs = "one-of-a-kind," not "only begotten." Isaac as Abraham's "only son" (though Ishmael existed) = son of promise. What Abraham was spared at last minute, God actually did—gave his unique Son. Isaac as typos of Christ (Hebrews 11:17). Contains soteriological dimension: God's love expressed through sending unique Son for world's salvation.
