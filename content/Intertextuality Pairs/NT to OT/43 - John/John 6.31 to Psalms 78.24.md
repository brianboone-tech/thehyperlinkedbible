# John 6:31 to Psalms 78:24

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 31|John 6:31]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 78#Psalm 78 . 24|Psalms 78:24]] (Asaph's historical psalm)
- Exodus 16 (manna narrative)

**Type**: Scriptural citation by crowd

**Significance**: People invoke Scripture to test Jesus. Irony: they just witnessed the sign but demand another. Jesus will correct: not Moses but God gave bread; Jesus himself is true bread from heaven (6:32-58).
