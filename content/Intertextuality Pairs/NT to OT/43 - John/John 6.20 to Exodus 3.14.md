# John 6:20 to Exodus 3:14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 6#John 6 . 20|John 6:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 3#Exodus 3 . 14|Exodus 3:14]] ("I AM")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 77#Psalm 77 . 16|Psalms 77:16]] (God's manifestation at exodus)
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 9#Job 9 . 8|Job 9:8]] (God walks on waters)

**Type**: Divine self-identification

**Significance**: Plain meaning = self-identification; deeper level = epiphany overtones. "Do not be afraid" is frequent God-message to his people (Genesis 26:24; Deuteronomy 1:21; Joshua 1:9). Jesus walking on water echoes Job 9:8.
