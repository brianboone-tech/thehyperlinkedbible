# John 2:1-11 to Isaiah 25:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 1|John 2:1-11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 25#Isaiah 25 . 6|Isaiah 25:6]] (messianic banquet, wedding imagery)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 12|Jeremiah 31:12-14]] (wine flowing freely)
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 14#Hosea 14 . 7|Hosea 14:7]] (abundance of wine in messianic age)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11]] (messianic ruler, wine abundance)

**Type**: Symbolic/Typological

**Significance**: Running out of wine symbolizes barrenness of first-century Judaism. Jesus' abundant provision (six large jars filled "to the brim") fulfills prophetic vision of messianic joy and abundance. "To the brim" emphasizes completeness. Reveals Jesus' glory as at Sinai/tabernacle/temple (cf. Exodus 33:22; Ezekiel 10:4).
