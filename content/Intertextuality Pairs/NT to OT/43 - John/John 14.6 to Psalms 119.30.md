# John 14:6 to Psalms 119:30

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 6|John 14:6]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 119#Psalm 119 . 30|Psalms 119:30]] ("way[s] of truth" = life in Torah)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 16#Psalm 16 . 11|Psalms 16:11]] ("way" + "life" combined)
- 1QS VIII, 12-16; IX, 17-20 (Qumran as "the way," based on Isaiah 40:3)

**Type**: Replacement/Fulfillment

**Significance**: OT/Second Temple: "ways of truth" = life conforming to law. Dead Sea Scrolls: contrast ways of truth vs. darkness/deceit; community = "the way" (Isaiah 40:3 basis). Now Jesus IS the way. Exclusive claim (14:6b). Where OT people expressed faith by keeping law, now faith expressed through Jesus.
