# John 16:28 to Isaiah 55:10-11

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 28|John 16:28]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 10|Isaiah 55:10-11]] (Word of God going out and returning)

**Type**: Sent-and-returning pattern

**Significance**: Sums up Jesus' entire mission from sending (3:16) through incarnation (1:10-11, 14) to departure (14:19) and return to Father (dominant theme of chs. 14-17). Develops prophetic portrayal of sent and returning Word of God in Isaiah 55:10-11 (see commentary at 1:1; Brown).
