# John 1:14 to Exodus 25:8-9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 14|John 1:14]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 25#Exodus 25 . 8|Exodus 25:8-9]] (tabernacle dwelling)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 34#Exodus 34 . 6|Exodus 34:6]] ("loving-kindness [ḥesed] and truth [ĕmet]")
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 6|2 Samuel 7:6]] (God dwelling with Israel)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37#Ezekiel 37 . 27|Ezekiel 37:27-28]] (future dwelling promise)

**Type**: Typological fulfillment

**Significance**: Jesus replaces tabernacle/temple as place of God's dwelling. The phrase "full of grace and truth" alludes to Exodus 34:6's covenant faithfulness language, now finding ultimate expression in incarnate Son. God's glory, once in tabernacle/temple, now revealed in Jesus.
