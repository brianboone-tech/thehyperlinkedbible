# John 4:36-37 to Amos 9:13

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 36|John 4:36-37]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/30 - Amos/Amos 9#Amos 9 . 13|Amos 9:13]] (eschatological abundance: reaper overtaken by plowman)
- [[Readable Bible/33 - Micah/Micah 6#Micah 6 . 15|Micah 6:15]] (judgment: "You shall sow but not reap")

**Type**: Eschatological fulfillment

**Significance**: Messianic age has dawned where sowing and reaping coincide—image of abundance and prosperity. Immediate application: approaching Samaritans. Jesus and predecessors (John the Baptist) did hard work; disciples bring in harvest.
