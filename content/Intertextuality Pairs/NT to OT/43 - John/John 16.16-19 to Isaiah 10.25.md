# John 16:16-19 to Isaiah 10:25

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 16|John 16:16-19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 10#Isaiah 10 . 25|Isaiah 10:25]] (God's judgment/salvation announced)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 51#Jeremiah 51 . 33|Jeremiah 51:33]] (prophetic announcements)

**Type**: Prophetic formula

**Significance**: Harks back to previous instances (13:33; 7:33; 12:35; 14:19). OT prophets use similar terms for God's judgment/salvation. "Then after a little while" reminiscent of Isaiah 26:20 (also reference to Isaiah 26:17 in John 16:21). Context plainly: brief period between crucifixion and resurrection (first "little while"), then resurrection appearances (second "little while"). Rightly Carson; cf. Morris; Burge.
