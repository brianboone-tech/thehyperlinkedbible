# John 14:26 to Psalms 51:11

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 26|John 14:26]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 51#Psalm 51 . 11|Psalms 51:11]] (rare OT "Holy Spirit" references)
- Wisdom 9:17; Psalms of Solomon 17:37 (Second Temple refs)

**Type**: New covenant promise

**Significance**: Only "Holy Spirit" reference in Farewell Discourse (cf. 1:33; 20:22). Rare in OT. Spirit's future ministry: teaching (authentic exposition; cf. Qumran's "Teacher of Righteousness") + reminding. Teaching vital in Judaism.
