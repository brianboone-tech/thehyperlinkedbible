# John 11:48 to Jeremiah 7:14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 11#John 11 . 48|John 11:48]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 7#Jeremiah 7 . 14|Jeremiah 7:14]] (God's judgment on temple)
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 9#1 Kings 9 . 7|1 Kings 9:7]] (Solomon warned about temple destruction)
- 2 Maccabees 5:19 (temple and nation)

**Type**: Historical-theological concern

**Significance**: "Place" (māqôm) = temple (most concrete referent). Fear of repeated exile/temple destruction if disobedient to God. Ironic: their rejection of Messiah brings very judgment they fear.
