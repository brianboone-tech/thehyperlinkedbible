# John 16:13 to Psalms 25:4-5

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 13|John 16:13]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 25#Psalm 25 . 4|Psalms 25:4-5]] (longing for divine guidance)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 19|Isaiah 43:19]] (God led Israel in wilderness by Spirit)
- Wisdom 9:11; 10:10, 17 (divine Wisdom guides)
- 1QS IV, 2; Philo, Moses 2.265 (guidance terminology)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 21|Isaiah 41:21-29]] (esp. vv. 22-23); 42:9; 44:7; 45:19; 46:10; 48:14 (declaring things to come = Yahweh's exclusive domain)

**Type**: Divine prerogative/Wisdom tradition

**Significance**: Divine guidance already psalmist's longing. Isaiah recounts God leading Israel in wilderness by Spirit, predicts renewed guidance in future. Second Temple literature applies "guidance" to figure of divine Wisdom. Jesus' reference may contain echo of OT terminology. Verb anangellō ("proclaim") occurs 40+ times in Isaiah where declaring things to come = Yahweh's exclusive domain (Isaiah 48:14).
