# John 14:20 to Isaiah 2:11

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 14#John 14 . 20|John 14:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 11|Isaiah 2:11]] (prophetic "on that day" = end-time)
- Matthew 24:36 (eschatological day)

**Type**: Eschatological language

**Significance**: "That day" frequent in OT prophetic literature with end-time connotations. Most take as Jesus' resurrection; more plausible = Pentecost (Spirit's coming). Mutual indwelling fulfilled.
