# John 3:14 to Numbers 21:8-9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 14|John 3:14]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 21#Numbers 21 . 8|Numbers 21:8-9]] (bronze serpent)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 52#Isaiah 52 . 13|Isaiah 52:13]] (Servant "lifted up and highly exalted")

**Type**: Typological

**Significance**: Primary analogy: looking at bronze serpent brought physical life; looking in faith at crucified Son of Man brings eternal life. Secondary parallel: Israel's sin/judgment requiring divine remedy mirrors humanity's predicament. "Lifted up" has dual meaning (crucifixion + exaltation), drawing on Isaiah's Suffering Servant language. First of three "lifted up" sayings in John (cf. 8:28; 12:32).
