# John 4:20-22 to Deuteronomy 11:29

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 4#John 4 . 20|John 4:20-22]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 11#Deuteronomy 11 . 29|Deuteronomy 11:29]] (Gerizim and proper worship place)
- [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 8#Joshua 8 . 33|Joshua 8:33]] (altar on Gerizim)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 122#Psalm 122 . 1|Psalms 122:1-5]] (Jerusalem temple)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 76#Psalm 76 . 1|Psalms 76:1]] ("In Judah God is known")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 2#Isaiah 2 . 3|Isaiah 2:3]] ("Out of Zion shall go the law")

**Type**: Salvation-historical statement

**Significance**: Jesus affirms Jewish salvation-historical preeminence as God's chosen people and instrument of redemption. Yet neither Gerizim nor Jerusalem remains ultimate—Jesus replaces both as locus of worship (cf. 2:19-22).
