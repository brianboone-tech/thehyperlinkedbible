# John 15:1-8 to Isaiah 5:1-7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 1|John 15:1-8]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 5#Isaiah 5 . 1|Isaiah 5:1-7]] (vineyard songs: Israel as vine/vineyard)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 80#Psalm 80 . 8|Psalms 80:8-16]] (Israel as vine brought from Egypt)
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 2#Jeremiah 2 . 21|Jeremiah 2:21]] (vineyard/vine imagery)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 15#Ezekiel 15 . 1|Ezekiel 15:1-8]] (vine imagery)
- [[Readable Bible/28 - Hosea/Hosea 10#Hosea 10 . 1|Hosea 10:1-2]] (Israel as vine)
- Leviticus Rabbah 36:2 (allegorical Israel as vine)

**Type**: Replacement/Typological fulfillment

**Significance**: OT frequently uses vineyard/vine as symbol for Israel (God's covenant people). Regularly stresses Israel's FAILURE to produce good fruit, issuing in judgment. Jesus claims to be "true vine"—fulfills Israel's destiny as true vine of God. Brings forth fruit Israel failed to produce. Jesus as Messiah/Son of God embodies God's true intentions. Displaces Israel as focus of God's salvation plan.
