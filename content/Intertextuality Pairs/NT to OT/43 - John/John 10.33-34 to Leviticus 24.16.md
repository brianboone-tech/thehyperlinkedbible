# John 10:33-34 to Leviticus 24:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 10#John 10 . 33|John 10:33-34]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 24#Leviticus 24 . 16|Leviticus 24:16]] (blasphemy = death)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 82#Psalm 82 . 6|Psalms 82:6]] (Asaph: "I said, 'You are "gods"'")

**Type**: Rabbinic argument (lesser to greater)

**Significance**: Jesus argues: if Scripture calls human judges/leaders "gods" (ĕlōhîm—divine representatives administering justice), how much more appropriate for him whom Father consecrated and sent. Not abolishing monotheism but revealing union of divine and human. Affirms inviolability of Scripture (10:35: "Scripture cannot be broken"). "Your law" = ironic polemic (cf. 8:17).
