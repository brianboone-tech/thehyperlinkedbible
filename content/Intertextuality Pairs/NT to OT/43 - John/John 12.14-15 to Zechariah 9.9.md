# John 12:14-15 to Zechariah 9:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 12#John 12 . 14|John 12:14-15]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 9|Zechariah 9:9]] (king coming on donkey)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 9|Isaiah 40:9]] ("Do not be afraid" added from this text)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 49#Genesis 49 . 11|Genesis 49:11]] ("colt of a donkey" from this text)

**Type**: Composite prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Jesus deliberately enacts Zechariah's prophecy of peaceful messianic king. Independent adaptation of LXX/MT: "do not fear" replaces "rejoice greatly" (from Isaiah 40:9); "sitting" not "mounting" (emphasizes regal dignity); "colt of donkey" from Genesis 49:11. Omits "righteous and having salvation, humble"—but still implied. Donkey = peace, humility (contrast war horse). Juxtaposes peace with ultimate victory. Disciples didn't understand until after resurrection (12:16).
