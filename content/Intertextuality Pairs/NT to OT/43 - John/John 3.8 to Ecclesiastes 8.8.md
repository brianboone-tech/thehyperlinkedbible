# John 3:8 to Ecclesiastes 8:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 8|John 3:8]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/21 - Ecclesiastes/Ecclesiastes 8#Ecclesiastes 8 . 8|Ecclesiastes 8:8]] (mystery of wind's origin)

**Type**: Wisdom allusion

**Significance**: Wind (pneuma) and Spirit (pneuma) share same Greek/Hebrew word (rûaḥ). Both mysterious in origin yet real in effects. Spirit's operation not subject to human control.
