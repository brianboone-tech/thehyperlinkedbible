# John 5:22-23 to Genesis 18:25

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 5#John 5 . 22|John 5:22-23]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 18#Genesis 18 . 25|Genesis 18:25]] (God as Judge)
- [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 11#Judges 11 . 27|Judges 11:27]] (Lord as Judge)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 2|Psalms 2:2]] (Messiah's role subordinate in judgment)

**Type**: Divine prerogative claimed

**Significance**: Judgment is God's exclusive domain. Messiah in Second Temple literature remains in background regarding judgment. Jesus' claim to equal honor amounts to claim to worship—God's glory (Isaiah 42:8; 48:11) not compromised by honor given to Son.
