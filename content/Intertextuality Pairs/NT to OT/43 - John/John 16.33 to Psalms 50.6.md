# John 16:33 to Psalms 50:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 33|John 16:33]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 50#Psalm 50 . 6|Psalms 50:6]] (God as conqueror)
- Wisdom 4:2; 16:10; 18:22; 1 Esdras 3:12 (nikaō = "triumph," more frequent in apocrypha/pseudepigrapha than canonical OT)

**Type**: Victory announcement

**Significance**: Only instance of nikaō ("triumph") in John's Gospel. Occurs several times in 1 John (2:13, 14; 4:4; 5:4-5) and dozen+ times in Revelation (especially letters to seven churches, chs. 2-3). Occurs rarely in canonical OT Greek; more often in apocryphal/pseudepigraphical literature (Wisdom; 1 Esdras). Psalm 50:6 LXX shows God as conqueror. Schlatter; cf.
