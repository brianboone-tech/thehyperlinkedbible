# John 13:18 to Psalms 41:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 13#John 13 . 18|John 13:18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 41#Psalm 41 . 9|Psalms 41:9]]

**Type**: Davidic typology/Fulfillment

**Significance**: One of only two fulfillment citations attributed to Jesus in John (cf. 15:25; also 17:12; 19:24, 36). Close to MT, not LXX. Uses trōgō (not LXX's esthiō), linking to 6:54-58 "bread of life" discourse. Omits "in whom I trusted" to avoid implying Jesus surprised. Davidic psalm portraying righteous sufferer facing betrayal by close friend.
