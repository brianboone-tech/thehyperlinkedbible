# John 20:30-31 to Sirach 43:27

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 20#John 20 . 30|John 20:30-31]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- Sirach 43:27 ("We could say more but could never say enough")
- 1 Maccabees 9:22 ("Now the rest of the acts of Judas...")
- Jewish concluding formulas in intertestamental writings

**Type**: Literary convention

**Significance**: Concluding statement in 20:30-31 finds literary parallels in several intertestamental Jewish writings (e.g., Sirach 43:27; 1 Maccabees 9:22, cited in Köstenberger).
