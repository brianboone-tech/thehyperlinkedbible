# John 16:32 to Zechariah 13:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 32|John 16:32]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 7|Zechariah 13:7]] ("Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered")
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 22#1 Kings 22 . 17|1 Kings 22:17]] (shepherdless sheep)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 6|Isaiah 53:6]] (scattering of God's flock)
- [[Readable Bible/17 - Esther/Esther 5#Esther 5 . 10|Esther 5:10]] (eis ta idia = going to own home)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Probable allusion to Zechariah 13:7 (quoted by Jesus in Matthew 26:31/Mark 14:27; cf. Matthew 26:56b). Image of shepherdless sheep and scattering also in other OT passages. John's context: not disciples left without shepherd, but Jesus deserted by his sheep. As in 1 Kings 22:17, scattering linked with everyone "going to his own home" (eis ta idia; cf. John 19:27).
