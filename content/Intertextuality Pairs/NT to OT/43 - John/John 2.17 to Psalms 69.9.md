# John 2:17 to Psalms 69:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 2#John 2 . 17|John 2:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 9|Psalms 69:9]] (Davidic lament of righteous sufferer)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 119#Psalm 119 . 139|Psalms 119:139]] ("Zeal for your house consumed me")

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment/Davidic typology

**Significance**: Change from LXX past tense to future tense prophetically spotlights Jesus' cross-death. Jesus fulfills pattern of righteous sufferer zealous for God but persecuted. Disciples recognize Jesus in terms of Psalm 69 (quoted again in 15:25; alluded to in 19:28). Jesus shares psalmist's alienation even from own brothers (7:1-9; cf. Ps 69:8). Jesus' zeal extends beyond temple to comprehensive concern for Jewish nation—renewing, replacing, fulfilling festival/institution symbolism.
