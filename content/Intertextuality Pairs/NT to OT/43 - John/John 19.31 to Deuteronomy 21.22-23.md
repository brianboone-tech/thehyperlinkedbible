# John 19:31 to Deuteronomy 21:22-23

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 19#John 19 . 31|John 19:31]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 21#Deuteronomy 21 . 22|Deuteronomy 21:22-23]] (bodies of hanged criminals must not remain on tree overnight)
- [[Readable Bible/06 - Joshua/Joshua 8#Joshua 8 . 29|Joshua 8:29]] (taking down bodies)
- Philo, Flaccus 83-85 (bodies taken down during feast days)
- Josephus, Jewish War 4.317 (application to crucified)
- Galatians 3:13 (Paul's use)

**Type**: Legal requirement

**Significance**: Jews' attitude based on Deuteronomy 21:22-23: bodies of hanged criminals must not defile land by remaining on tree overnight. Evidence that at times, especially during feast days, bodies taken down and given to relatives (Philo, Flaccus 83-85). Josephus indicates application extended to cover crucified (Jewish War 4.317; cf. Galatians 3:13).
