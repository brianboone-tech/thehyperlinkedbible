# John 1:18 to Exodus 33:20

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 18|John 1:18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 33#Exodus 33 . 20|Exodus 33:20]] (Moses cannot see God's face)

**Type**: Typological advance

**Significance**: Denies possibility of direct, unmediated vision of God (cf. John 5:37; 6:46). Jesus as unique revealer transcends Moses' limited vision. The "one-of-a-kind Son" (monogenēs) uniquely explains Father.
