# John 17:3 to Deuteronomy 6:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 3|John 17:3]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 4|Deuteronomy 6:4]] (Shema: "The Lord our God, the Lord is one")
- 1 Thessalonians 1:9; 1 John 5:20 (only true God)
- Wisdom 15:3 (knowledge of God contingent upon salvation)
- CD-A III, 20; 1QS II, 3; IV, 22; XI, 3-4 ("life"/"eternal life" and "eternal knowledge" in parallelism)

**Type**: Covenant knowledge/Exclusive claim

**Significance**: "Knowing" God not merely cognitive—means living in fellowship (cf. Hebrew "know" encompassing sexual intimacy, Genesis 4:1 LXX). Though God can be known through creation (Romans 1:18-25), ultimately knowledge contingent upon salvation (Barrett; Wisdom 15:3). Dead Sea Scrolls: "life"/"eternal life" and "eternal knowledge" in close parallelism. God = "only true God" affirmed supremely in Shema (Deuteronomy 6:4). Jesus = exclusive agent, sole authorized representative; God-sent Messiah.
