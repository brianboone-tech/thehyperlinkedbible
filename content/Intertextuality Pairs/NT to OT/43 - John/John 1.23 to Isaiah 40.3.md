# John 1:23 to Isaiah 40:3

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 1#John 1 . 23|John 1:23]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 3|Isaiah 40:3]] (voice preparing way)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Baptist identifies role as herald of new exodus, announcing God's return to Zion. Calls people to repentance to prepare for Messiah. Invokes larger exodus motif with themes of salvation and God's glory. Qumran community also applied this passage to themselves (1QS VIII, 14; 4Q259 III, 4-5).
