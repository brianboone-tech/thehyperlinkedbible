# John 17:5 to Exodus 19:5

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 5|John 17:5]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 5|Exodus 19:5]] (Yahweh's instructions to Moses)
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 25|2 Samuel 7:25-26]] (conclusion of David's prayer)
- Wisdom 7:25; 9:10-11 (wisdom's preexistence)
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8#Proverbs 8 . 28|Proverbs 8:28]] (wisdom at creation)

**Type**: Preexistence claim

**Significance**: "And now" found also in OT in Yahweh's instructions to Moses, conclusion of David's prayer, with reference to result following from truth of certain facts (Beasley-Murray). Preexistence ascribed also to wisdom in Second Temple literature (Wisdom 7:25; 9:10-11) based on Proverbs portrayal (esp. 8:28, 30). In John's Gospel preexistence ascribed to Jesus by: designation "the Word" (1:1, 14); title "Son of Man" (3:13; 6:62); "I am" preceding Abraham (8:58); coming from Father and returning (16:28).
