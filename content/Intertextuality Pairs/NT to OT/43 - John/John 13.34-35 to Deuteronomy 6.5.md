# John 13:34-35 to Deuteronomy 6:5

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 13#John 13 . 34|John 13:34-35]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6#Deuteronomy 6 . 5|Deuteronomy 6:5]] ("Love the Lord your God with all your heart")
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 19#Leviticus 19 . 18|Leviticus 19:18]] ("Love your neighbor as yourself")
- [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31#Jeremiah 31 . 29|Jeremiah 31:29-34]] (new covenant, inner transformation)

**Type**: New covenant ethic

**Significance**: Takes departure from Mosaic love commands but transcends. Resembles yet exceeds Dead Sea community (which stressed love within but "everlasting hatred for men of pit," 1QS IX, 21-22). Jesus preaches love for enemies (Matthew 5:43-48). New standard: "as I have loved you" (sacrificial, humble service). Possibly alludes to new covenant involving inner transformation (Jeremiah 31; Ezekiel 36).
