# John 3:28 to Malachi 3:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 28|John 3:28]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 3#Malachi 3 . 1|Malachi 3:1]] ("I send my messenger to prepare the way before me")

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment

**Significance**: Baptist as messenger/forerunner preparing for Messiah. Malachi 4:5 identifies forerunner as Elijah, which Jesus applies to Baptist in Synoptics. Baptist stands ready to do bridegroom's bidding, with Jesus as Israel's awaited king (cf. Isaiah 62:4-5; Jeremiah 2:2; Hosea 2:16-20 for Israel as bride of Yahweh).
