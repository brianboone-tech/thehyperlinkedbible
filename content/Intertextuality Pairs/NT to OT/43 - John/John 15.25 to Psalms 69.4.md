# John 15:25 to Psalms 69:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 15#John 15 . 25|John 15:25]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 69#Psalm 69 . 4|Psalms 69:4]] (Davidic lament: "Those who hate me without reason")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 35#Psalm 35 . 19|Psalms 35:19]] (also Davidic: "who hate me without cause")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 109#Psalm 109 . 3|Psalms 109:3]] (groundless hatred motif, different wording)

**Type**: Davidic typology/Fulfillment

**Significance**: Longest introductory formula in John (if not entire NT). Elliptical: "But [this happened] in order that the word might be fulfilled..." "Their law" ironically highlights Jews convicted by "their own law"; "law" = broadly "Scripture." Psalm 69 more likely source (widely regarded as messianic; evangelist previously cited 69:9 in 2:17; see also 19:28 possible allusion to 69:21). Parallelism between hate and persecution found in Psalm 68:5a-b LXX. Alternative: Psalm 35:19 (also Davidic prayer for deliverance from enemies "without cause"). Both psalms feature righteous sufferer zealous for God but persecuted by enemies. False charges highlighted (35:11, 21, 20; 69:4).
