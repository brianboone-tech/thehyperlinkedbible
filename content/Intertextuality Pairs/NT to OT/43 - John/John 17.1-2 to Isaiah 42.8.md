# John 17:1-2 to Isaiah 42:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 17#John 17 . 1|John 17:1-2]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 8|Isaiah 42:8]] (God will not give his glory to another)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 9#Isaiah 9 . 6|Isaiah 9:6-7]] (authority granted to Messiah)
- Matthew 11:27; 28:18; Wisdom 10:2 (granted authority)

**Type**: Divine claim/Messianic authority

**Significance**: Jesus' petition implies deity claim—OT affirms God won't give glory to another (Isaiah 42:8; 48:11). God's granting authority to Jesus marks inbreaking of new era (cf. Isaiah 9:6-7; Daniel 7:13-14; also Matthew 11:27; 28:18; Wisdom 10:2).
