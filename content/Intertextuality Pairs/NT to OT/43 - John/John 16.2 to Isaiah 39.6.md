# John 16:2 to Isaiah 39:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 16#John 16 . 2|John 16:2]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 39#Isaiah 39 . 6|Isaiah 39:6]] ("days are coming")
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 66#Isaiah 66 . 5|Isaiah 66:5]] (persecutors claim divine sanction)
- Numbers Rabbah 21:3; cf. Numbers 25:13 (slaying heretics = divine worship)

**Type**: Prophetic/Apocalyptic formula

**Significance**: "Time is coming" reminiscent of prophetic/apocalyptic expressions. Most likely refers to Jewish (not Roman) persecution. Some rabbinic authorities held slaying heretics = act of divine worship, though rarely public policy. Jewish persecution generally spontaneous; seasoned voices counseled moderation (Acts 5:33-40; cf. m. Sanh.
