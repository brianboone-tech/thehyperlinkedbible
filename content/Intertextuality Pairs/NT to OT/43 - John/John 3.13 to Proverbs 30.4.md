# John 3:13 to Proverbs 30:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/43 - John/John 3#John 3 . 13|John 3:13]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 30#Proverbs 30 . 4|Proverbs 30:4]] ("Who has ascended to heaven and come down?")
- Contrast with: Enoch (Genesis 5:24), Elijah (2 Kings 2:1-12), Moses (Exodus 24:9-11)

**Type**: Exclusive claim

**Significance**: Jesus contrasts himself with alleged heavenly travelers in apocalyptic literature. Only Son of Man has both descended and will ascend. Defeats Second Temple speculation about figures entering heaven.
