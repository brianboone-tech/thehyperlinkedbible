# Mark 3:29 to Numbers 15:30-31

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 3#Mark 3 . 29|Mark 3:29]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 15#Numbers 15 . 30|Numbers 15:30-31]] (sin with high hand, cut off from people)

**Type**: Unforgivable sin / Deliberate rebellion

**Significance**: Numbers 15:30-31 distinguishes between unintentional sin (forgivable through sacrifice) and high-handed rebellion (unforgivable, resulting in being cut off). Calling the Holy Spirit's work demonic constitutes willful, persistent rejection of God's evident grace—high-handed sin. This isn't about a single slip but sustained, hardened opposition to obvious divine work. The unforgivability stems from permanent rejection of the only means of forgiveness.
