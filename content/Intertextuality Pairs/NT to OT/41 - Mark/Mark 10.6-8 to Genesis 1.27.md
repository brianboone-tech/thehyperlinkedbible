# Mark 10:6-8 to Genesis 1:27

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 6|Mark 10:6-8]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 27|Genesis 1:27]] (So God created man in his own image... male and female he created them)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 24|Genesis 2:24]] (Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh)

**Type**: Creational hermeneutic / Torah interpretation

**Significance**: Jesus employs striking hermeneutical move—using earlier Scripture (creation ordinances) to interpret later Scripture (Mosaic legislation), prioritizing God's intent "from the beginning" over Deuteronomy 24:1-4's divorce provision. Genesis 1:27 establishes male-female complementarity and equality in God's image, while Genesis 2:24's "one flesh" describes covenantal unity beyond sexual union. Jesus declares any divorce contradicts God's will, transcending rabbinic debates and demonstrating his interpretive authority. This shows that new creation/new exodus involves restoration to creation's original design, and elevates women's status in a culture allowing casual male-initiated divorce.
