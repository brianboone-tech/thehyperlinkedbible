# Mark 3:4 to Deuteronomy 30:15-20

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 3#Mark 3 . 4|Mark 3:4]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 15|Deuteronomy 30:15-20]] (life and death, blessing and curse)

**Type**: Covenantal framework / Life/death choice

**Significance**: Jesus' question echoes Deuteronomy's covenant choice between life and death. The irony is sharp: religious leaders plot Jesus' death (3:6) on the Sabbath while condemning him for bringing life. Jesus reframes the Sabbath issue—it's not about ritual minutiae but covenant faithfulness. Will they choose life (healing) or death (plotting murder)? Their hardness of heart (3:5) mirrors Israel's covenant unfaithfulness.
