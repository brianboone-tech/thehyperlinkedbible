# Mark 12:10-11 to Psalms 118:22-23

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 12#Mark 12 . 10|Mark 12:10-11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 118#Psalm 118 . 22|Psalms 118:22-23]] (The stone that the builders rejected has become the cornerstone)

**Type**: Messianic vindication / Rejection-exaltation pattern

**Significance**: Jesus quotes Psalm 118:22-23, applying the rejected-stone-become-cornerstone pattern to himself. The builders (Jewish leaders) reject the stone (Jesus), but God makes him the cornerstone of the new temple-community. This continues Psalm 118's theme from the triumphal entry (11:9-10) and establishes the rejection-vindication pattern central to Mark's gospel. The religious leaders recognize Jesus' self-application (12:12) and seek to arrest him. This stone saying becomes foundational for early Christian ecclesiology and christology, showing how rejection leads to exaltation.
