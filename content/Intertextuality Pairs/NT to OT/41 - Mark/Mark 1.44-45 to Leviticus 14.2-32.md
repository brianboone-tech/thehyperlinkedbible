# Mark 1:44-45 to Leviticus 14:2-32

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 44|Mark 1:44-45]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/03 - Leviticus/Leviticus 14#Leviticus 14 . 2|Leviticus 14:2-32]] (law for cleansing lepers)

**Type**: Legal fulfillment / Testimony to priests

**Significance**: Jesus honors Torah's requirements for cleansing, providing testimony to the priests that God's kingdom power is breaking in. The commanded offering serves as witness that messianic restoration has begun, as Isaiah prophesied the messianic age would include cleansing of lepers. Yet the healed man's disobedience in spreading the news illustrates the messianic secret theme—Jesus' identity must not be misunderstood as merely a wonder-worker before the cross reveals his true mission.
