# Mark 13:26-27 to Daniel 7:13-14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 13#Mark 13 . 26|Mark 13:26-27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13-14]] (one like a son of man comes with clouds to the Ancient of Days, receiving authority, glory, and kingdom)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 2#Zechariah 2 . 6|Zechariah 2:6]] (I have spread you abroad as the four winds)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 30#Deuteronomy 30 . 4|Deuteronomy 30:4]] (from there the LORD will gather you)

**Type**: Son of Man vindication / New exodus gathering

**Significance**: Jesus' vindication and gathering of the elect follow Jerusalem's judgment. Daniel 7:13-14 provides Son of Man imagery—crucially, this is ascent to God's throne (not descent to earth), receiving authority and kingdom. "Coming in clouds" echoes Yahweh's presence. Jesus' opponents will witness validation of his claims in their lifetimes (cf. 9:1; 14:62; "this generation" in 13:30) through the temple's destruction (AD 70). This fulfills new exodus promises—Zechariah 2:6 and Deuteronomy 30:4 promised gathering scattered exiles. Mark's Jesus expands gathering to include Gentiles, not to destroyed Jerusalem but to the new temple with Jesus as cornerstone (12:10-11), establishing the new spiritual temple-people where God's presence dwells.
