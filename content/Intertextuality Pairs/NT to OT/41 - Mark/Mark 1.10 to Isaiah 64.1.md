# Mark 1:10 to Isaiah 64:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 10|Mark 1:10]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 64#Isaiah 64 . 1|Isaiah 64:1]] (Oh that you would rend the heavens and come down)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 1#Ezekiel 1 . 1|Ezekiel 1:1]] (the heavens were opened)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 2|Genesis 1:2]] (Spirit of God hovering over waters)

**Type**: Typological fulfillment / New creation imagery

**Significance**: The tearing open of the heavens answers Isaiah 64:1's prayer for God to rend the heavens and come down to deliver his people. The Spirit descending like a dove evokes Genesis 1:2, marking this as a new creation moment. Ezekiel 1:1's opened heavens accompanied divine revelation. Mark uniquely uses "torn" (schizō), the same verb used for the temple veil at Jesus' death (15:38), creating a frame around the Gospel.
