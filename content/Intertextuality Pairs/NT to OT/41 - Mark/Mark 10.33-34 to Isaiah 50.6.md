# Mark 10:33-34 to Isaiah 50:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 33|Mark 10:33-34]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 50#Isaiah 50 . 6|Isaiah 50:6]] (I gave my back to those who strike, and my cheeks to those who pull out the beard; I hid not my face from disgrace and spitting)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 3|Isaiah 53:3-5]] (despised and rejected... oppressed and afflicted)

**Type**: Suffering Servant fulfillment / Detailed prophecy

**Significance**: This third prediction provides the most detailed account with close verbal parallels to Isaiah's Servant Songs. Isaiah 50:6 specifically mentions back-striking and spitting, precisely fulfilled in Mark 14:65. The specific details—mocking, spitting, flogging—demonstrate Jesus' sovereign awareness, portraying his suffering not as unfortunate martyrdom but as willing fulfillment of Scripture's prophetic pattern. The prediction immediately precedes James and John's glory request (10:35-40), highlighting the disciples' continued failure to grasp that suffering precedes exaltation and that discipleship involves sharing the servant's suffering path.
