# Mark 14:24 to Exodus 24:8

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 24|Mark 14:24]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- Primary: [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 24#Exodus 24 . 8|Exodus 24:8]] (And Moses took the blood and threw it on the people and said, "Behold the blood of the covenant that the LORD has made with you")
- Secondary: [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 53#Isaiah 53 . 12|Isaiah 53:12]] (he poured out his soul to death... he bore the sin of many)
- Tertiary: [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 9#Zechariah 9 . 11|Zechariah 9:11]] (because of the blood of my covenant with you)

**Type**: New covenant inauguration / Substitutionary sacrifice

**Significance**: Jesus innovatively combines Passover with Exodus 24's covenant meal and Isaiah 53's atoning servant death. "Blood of the covenant" echoes Exodus 24:8 where Moses bound Israel to Yahweh at Sinai, while "poured out for many" derives from Isaiah 53:12. Jesus compresses Israel's founding moment (exodus and Sinai covenant) around himself, claiming his death alone effects Passover deliverance and covenant inauguration. This supplants both Passover and temple, establishes Jesus as covenant initiator (Yahweh's role), and includes Gentiles ("the many") in Israel's "kingdom of priests" vocation.
