# Mark 14:27 to Zechariah 13:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 14#Mark 14 . 27|Mark 14:27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 13#Zechariah 13 . 7|Zechariah 13:7]] (Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered)

**Type**: Prophetic fulfillment / Shepherd-sheep imagery

**Significance**: Jesus quotes Zechariah 13:7, where God commands his sword to strike his shepherd, resulting in the flock's scattering. Jesus applies this to his impending arrest and death—God's shepherd will be struck, causing the disciples to scatter. Yet Jesus adds resurrection promise ("after I am raised, I will go before you to Galilee," 14:28), indicating the scattering is temporary. This continues Mark's shepherd christology (6:34) and shows Jesus' death as divinely ordained.
