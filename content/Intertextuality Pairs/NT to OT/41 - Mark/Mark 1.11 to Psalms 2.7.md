# Mark 1:11 to Psalms 2:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 1#Mark 1 . 11|Mark 1:11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalms 2:7]] (You are my Son)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1]] (my chosen one in whom my soul delights)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 4#Exodus 4 . 22|Exodus 4:22]] (Israel as God's son)

**Type**: Composite quotation / Typological synthesis

**Significance**: This divine declaration integrates multiple OT trajectories. Psalm 2:7 brings messianic Davidic sonship with royal authority. Isaiah 42:1 contributes the Spirit-anointed servant identity, with expectations of gentle justice but also suffering. The synthesis resolves Isaiah's servant ambiguity: the servant is Israel's Davidic deliverer. The Exodus 4:22 echo identifies Jesus as true Israel, recapitulating Israel's exodus (water, wilderness testing). This declaration establishes Jesus as both triumphant king and suffering servant—a paradox resolved through the cross.
