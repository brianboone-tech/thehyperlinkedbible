# Mark 3:27 to Isaiah 49:24-25

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 3#Mark 3 . 27|Mark 3:27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 24|Isaiah 49:24-25]] (Can the prey be taken from the mighty? God will contend with your contenders)

**Type**: Messianic warfare / Satan-binding prophecy

**Significance**: Isaiah 49:24-25 promises Yahweh will rescue captives from the mighty one, contending with Israel's oppressors. Jesus presents his exorcisms as fulfillment—he's the stronger one who binds Satan (the strong man) and plunders his household (delivering the demon-possessed). This echoes the Isaianic new exodus theme: liberation from spiritual Babylon. Jesus' ministry demonstrates the inbreaking kingdom overpowering Satan's reign.
