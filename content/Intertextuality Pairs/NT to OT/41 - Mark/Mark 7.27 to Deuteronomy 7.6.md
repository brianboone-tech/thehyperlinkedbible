# Mark 7:27 to Deuteronomy 7:6

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 7#Mark 7 . 27|Mark 7:27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7#Deuteronomy 7 . 6|Deuteronomy 7:6]] (you are a people holy to the LORD your God, chosen as his treasured possession)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 6|Isaiah 49:6]] (I will make you as a light for the nations, that my salvation may reach to the end of the earth)

**Type**: Salvation-historical priority / Gentile inclusion

**Significance**: Jesus' statement reflects Israel's covenant priority—the children (Israel) must first be fed before the bread goes to dogs (Gentiles). Yet his willingness to heal the woman's daughter demonstrates that the "first" implies a "then," anticipating Gentile inclusion. Isaiah 49:6 promised Israel would be a light to nations, and Mark shows this beginning to unfold. The woman's clever response (even dogs eat crumbs from the table) receives Jesus' approval, illustrating that Gentile faith can participate in Israel's messianic blessings even now, foreshadowing the full Gentile mission after the resurrection.
