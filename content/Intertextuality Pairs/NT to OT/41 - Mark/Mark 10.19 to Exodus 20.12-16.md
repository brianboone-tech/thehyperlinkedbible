# Mark 10:19 to Exodus 20:12-16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 10#Mark 10 . 19|Mark 10:19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 20#Exodus 20 . 12|Exodus 20:12-16]] (Fifth through ninth commandments)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5#Deuteronomy 5 . 16|Deuteronomy 5:16-20]] (parallel Decalogue)

**Type**: Pedagogical use of Law / Wealth idolatry exposure

**Significance**: Jesus strategically omits the first table (duties toward God) and focuses on social obligations to test the man's functional idolatry—his wealth. The addition "do not defraud" reflects Leviticus 19:13 and Malachi 3:5, particularly relevant for wealthy individuals. The man's claim to have kept these "from my youth" sets up Jesus' penetrating diagnosis: he lacks "one thing"—total commitment to God through renouncing wealth (10:21). The commandments serve pedagogically to expose the man's true allegiance and his failure to love God wholeheartedly, illustrating that external law-keeping doesn't indicate heart transformation.
