# Mark 9:7 to Psalms 2:7

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/41 - Mark/Mark 9#Mark 9 . 7|Mark 9:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalms 2:7]] (You are my Son)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 42#Isaiah 42 . 1|Isaiah 42:1]] (my chosen, in whom my soul delights)
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 18#Deuteronomy 18 . 15|Deuteronomy 18:15]] (The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me... to him you shall listen)

**Type**: Divine confirmation / Composite quotation

**Significance**: This second divine voice adds "listen to him" (Deuteronomy 18:15), identifying Jesus as the prophet like Moses whom Israel must heed on pain of judgment. This validates Jesus' shocking teaching about necessary suffering that Peter just rebuked (8:32-33). The transfiguration setting evokes Sinai (mountain, cloud, divine voice, glory), yet Jesus surpasses Moses by radiating glory from within. The presence of Moses and Elijah establishes continuity with Law and Prophets while demonstrating Jesus transcends both as the definitive interpreter of Torah and Prophets, embodying the messianic servant-prophet whose words carry ultimate divine authority.
