# Ephesians 1:19-21 to Isaiah 40:26

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 19|Ephesians 1:19-21]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 40#Isaiah 40 . 26|Isaiah 40:26]] (allusion - "the might of his strength" in creation)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 147#Psalm 147 . 4|Psalm 147:4]] (allusion - God's power in naming and ordering stars)

**Type**: Allusion / Thematic

**Significance**: Paul alludes to Isaiah's language of divine creative power ("the might of his strength") to establish that the same power that ordered the cosmos now operates in Christ's resurrection. This cosmic perspective prepares readers for the subsequent use of Psalm 8:6, demonstrating that God's creative authority over stars extends to Christ's exaltation over all cosmic powers. The theological move connects creation theology with Christology, showing believers that the Creator who names stars has definitively defeated spiritual forces through Christ. This frames Ephesians' spiritual warfare theme within God's sovereign ordering of the universe.
