# Ephesians 1:20-22 to Psalm 110:1

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 1#Ephesians 1 . 20|Ephesians 1:20-22]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 110#Psalm 110 . 1|Psalm 110:1]] (direct allusion - "sit at my right hand")
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 8#Psalm 8 . 6|Psalm 8:6]] (direct quotation - "subjected all things under his feet")
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 26|Genesis 1:26]], [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 1#Genesis 1 . 28|Genesis 1:28]] (background - dominion over creation)

**Type**: Direct quotation (Ps 8:6) / Clear allusion (Ps 110:1) / Typological (Adam theme)

**Significance**: Paul combines Psalm 110:1 (messianic enthronement) and Psalm 8:6 (Adamic dominion) to present Christ as the Last Adam who restores humanity's intended rule over creation. Through resurrection and exaltation, Christ fulfills both the messianic expectation of sharing Yahweh's throne and the creation mandate of human dominion. Paul's hermeneutical move connects Jewish hopes that "the glory of Adam" would be restored to Israel with the reality that Christ has already begun reclaiming universal sovereignty. The church participates in this restored dominion as Christ's body, experiencing what Second Temple Judaism anticipated but could not achieve - the subjection of all cosmic powers under humanity's feet through the Messiah's victory.
