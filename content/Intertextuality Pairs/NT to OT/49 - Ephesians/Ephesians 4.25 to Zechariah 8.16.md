# Ephesians 4:25 to Zechariah 8:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/49 - Ephesians/Ephesians 4#Ephesians 4 . 25|Ephesians 4:25]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 16|Zechariah 8:16]] (direct quotation)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 2|Zechariah 8:2-8]], [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 12|Zechariah 8:12-15]], [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 8#Zechariah 8 . 18|Zechariah 8:18-23]] (broader context - eschatological restoration)

**Type**: Direct quotation (unannounced)

**Significance**: Paul quotes Zechariah 8:16 to ground ethical instruction in eschatological fulfillment - the church as God's restored community where God dwells by the Spirit. The hermeneutical move applies Zechariah's command to the future restored remnant (gathered from exile, with God dwelling on Zion, nations streaming to worship) directly to the church as that realized eschatological community. Paul's contextual awareness connects to his prior use of Psalm 68 (divine triumph, universal dominion) and his temple imagery (2:21-22). The theological significance lies in the indicative-imperative pattern: because believers are the "new man" created in God's likeness and constitute God's dwelling place, truthfulness flows necessarily from their eschatological identity as the restored people Zechariah envisioned.
