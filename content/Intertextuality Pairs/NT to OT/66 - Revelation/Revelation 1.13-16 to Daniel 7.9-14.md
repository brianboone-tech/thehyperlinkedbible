# Revelation 1:13-16 to Daniel 7:9-14

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 13|Revelation 1:13-16]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 9|Daniel 7:9-14]] (son of man; Ancient of Days attributes transferred to Christ)
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 5|Daniel 10:5-6]] (heavenly man's description)
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 3#Daniel 3 . 26|Daniel 3:26]] (furnace)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 1#Ezekiel 1 . 24|Ezekiel 1:24, 27]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 2|43:2]] (voice like many waters)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 11#Isaiah 11 . 4|Isaiah 11:4]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 49#Isaiah 49 . 2|49:2]] (sharp sword from mouth)
- [[Readable Bible/07 - Judges/Judges 5#Judges 5 . 31|Judges 5:31]] (face shining like sun)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 4#Zechariah 4 . 2|Zechariah 4:2-3, 11-14]] (seven lamps; kingly and priestly figures)

**Type**: Allusion (composite portrait)

**Significance**: Christ appears as kingly, priestly, and judicial figure, with divine attributes of the Ancient of Days transferred to the Son of Man. This unprecedented fusion identifies Jesus with YHWH as eternal judge. The imagery evokes Daniel 10's heavenly messenger who revealed judgment decrees (Dan 10:21-12:13), positioning Christ as revealer and executor of divine judgment. The composite portrait establishes Christ's authority over the churches and His role as coming judge.
