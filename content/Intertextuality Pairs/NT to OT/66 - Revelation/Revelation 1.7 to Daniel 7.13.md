# Revelation 1:7 to Daniel 7:13

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 7|Revelation 1:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 7#Daniel 7 . 13|Daniel 7:13]] (son of man coming with clouds → enthronement over nations after judgment)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 12#Zechariah 12 . 10|Zechariah 12:10]] (pierced one mourned; end-time repentance of Israel)
- [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 17|Zechariah 14:17]] ("of the earth" added)
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 12#Genesis 12 . 3|Genesis 12:3]]; [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 28#Genesis 28 . 14|28:14]]; [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 72#Psalm 72 . 17|Psalm 72:17]]; [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 14#Zechariah 14 . 17|Zechariah 14:17]] (πᾶσαι αἱ φυλαὶ τῆς γῆς = universal, not just Israel)

**Type**: Combined quotation (modified)

**Significance**: John modifies Zechariah's "tribes of Israel" to "all tribes of the earth," universalizing the promise to include Gentiles. The mourning represents repentance rather than judgment, as Gentiles assume the role of repentant Israel. Christ's coming with clouds signifies His inaugurated kingship and enthronement over the nations, though accomplished through suffering. This verse announces the central theme of Revelation: Christ's sovereign rule over all nations through His death and resurrection.
