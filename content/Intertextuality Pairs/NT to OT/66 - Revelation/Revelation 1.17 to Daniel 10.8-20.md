# Revelation 1:17 to Daniel 10:8-20

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 17|Revelation 1:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 10#Daniel 10 . 8|Daniel 10:8-20]] (fourfold pattern: vision → fall in fear → strengthened → revelation introduced by λαλέω)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 4|Isaiah 41:4, 10]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 6|44:6]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 12|48:12]] ("first and last" = YHWH self-predication)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 2|Isaiah 41:2]] (sword)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 10|Isaiah 41:10]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 2|44:2]] ("do not fear" + comfort + "strengthen" with right hand)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 13|Isaiah 48:13]] ("my right hand spread out the heavens")

**Type**: Allusion (prophetic comfort pattern + divine identity)

**Significance**: John follows the prophetic pattern from Daniel 10 (vision, fear, divine touch, strengthening, revelation), identifying with OT prophetic tradition. Christ's use of "first and last" directly claims YHWH's identity from Isaiah's monotheistic declarations. The divine comfort formula from Isaiah reinforces Christ's deity, as the same God who comforted Israel now comforts John through Christ. This establishes Jesus' full divine authority to reveal and execute God's purposes.
