# Revelation 2:14 to Numbers 25:1-2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 14|Revelation 2:14]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 25#Numbers 25 . 1|Numbers 25:1-2]]; [[Readable Bible/04 - Numbers/Numbers 31#Numbers 31 . 16|31:16]] (Balaam's counsel → idolatry and immorality)

**Type**: Allusion (negative example)

**Significance**: Balaam functions as paradigmatic false teacher who led Israel into idolatry and sexual immorality at Baal-Peor through his counsel to Balak. Christ applies this negative example to warn against syncretistic compromise with Roman imperial cult practices. Just as Balaam's teaching seduced Israel to eat food sacrificed to idols and practice sexual immorality, false teachers in Pergamum were leading Christians into similar compromise with paganism. The allusion emphasizes the severity and consequences of doctrinal and moral compromise.
