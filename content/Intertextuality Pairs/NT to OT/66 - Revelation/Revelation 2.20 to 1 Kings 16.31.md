# Revelation 2:20 to 1 Kings 16:31

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 20|Revelation 2:20]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 16#1 Kings 16 . 31|1 Kings 16:31]]; [[Readable Bible/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 21#1 Kings 21 . 25|21:25-26]]; [[Readable Bible/12 - 2 Kings/2 Kings 9#2 Kings 9 . 22|2 Kings 9:22]] (Jezebel → idolatry, immorality)

**Type**: Allusion (negative typology)

**Significance**: The original Jezebel epitomized syncretistic compromise by introducing Baal worship into Israel and persecuting faithful prophets. Christ applies her name typologically to a false prophetess in Thyatira who similarly seduces God's people into idolatry and immorality. The parallel warns that tolerating such false teaching will result in divine judgment comparable to Jezebel's violent death. This typology emphasizes the ongoing pattern of apostasy through syncretistic compromise and the necessity of church discipline against false teachers.
