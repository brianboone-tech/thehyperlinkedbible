# Revelation 1:18 to Deuteronomy 32:40

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 18|Revelation 1:18]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/05 - Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 32#Deuteronomy 32 . 40|Deuteronomy 32:40]]; [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 4#Daniel 4 . 34|Daniel 4:34]]; [[Readable Bible/27 - Daniel/Daniel 12#Daniel 12 . 7|12:7]] (ὁ ζῶν εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 22#Isaiah 22 . 22|Isaiah 22:22]] (keys = authority)

**Type**: Allusion (divine life + authority)

**Significance**: The tripartite formula (living one, died, alive forevermore) parallels the threefold divine name in 1:4 (ho ōn, ho ēn, ho erchomenos), affirming Christ's divine status. Through death and resurrection, Christ conquered death and secured eternal life, gaining sovereign authority over Death and Hades. The key imagery from Isaiah 22:22 signifies absolute authority to open and shut, here applied to control over the realm of death. Christ's victory assures believers of their own resurrection hope.
