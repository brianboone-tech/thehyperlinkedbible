# Revelation 2:27 to Psalm 2:8-9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 27|Revelation 2:27]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 8|Psalm 2:8-9]] (messianic king given nations as inheritance)

**Type**: Quotation

**Significance**: Psalm 2 promises the messianic king will receive the nations as inheritance and rule them with an iron scepter, shattering them like pottery. Christ applies this messianic promise to believers, indicating they will share in His judicial authority over rebellious nations. This corporate solidarity with Christ's rule demonstrates that the church participates in executing divine judgment. The promise reverses the apparent powerlessness of persecuted Christians, assuring them of ultimate victory and authority alongside the conquering Messiah.
