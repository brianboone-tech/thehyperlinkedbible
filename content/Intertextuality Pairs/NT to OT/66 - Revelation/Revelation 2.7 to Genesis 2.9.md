# Revelation 2:7 to Genesis 2:9

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 7|Revelation 2:7]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 9|Genesis 2:9]]; [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 3#Genesis 3 . 22|3:22-24]] (tree of life in Eden)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 28#Ezekiel 28 . 13|Ezekiel 28:13]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 31#Ezekiel 31 . 8|31:8-9]] (Eden = garden of God)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 47#Ezekiel 47 . 12|Ezekiel 47:12]] (trees bearing fruit)

**Type**: Allusion (Eden restoration)

**Significance**: The promise reverses the curse of Genesis 3, where humanity lost access to the tree of life after the fall. Through Christ's victory, believers gain restored access to eternal life symbolized by Eden's tree. The "paradise of God" language from Ezekiel identifies the eschatological new creation as restored Eden. This reward appears again in Revelation 22:2, framing the entire book with restoration themes.
