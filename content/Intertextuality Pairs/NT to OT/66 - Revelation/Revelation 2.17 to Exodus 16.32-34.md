# Revelation 2:17 to Exodus 16:32-34

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 2#Revelation 2 . 17|Revelation 2:17]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 16#Exodus 16 . 32|Exodus 16:32-34]] (manna preserved in ark)
- 2 Maccabees 2:4-8 (Jeremiah hides ark with manna until restoration)

**Type**: Allusion (eschatological provision)

**Significance**: The "hidden manna" draws on Jewish tradition that Jeremiah hid the ark containing preserved manna, to be restored in the messianic age. This eschatological feeding contrasts with compromise through eating food sacrificed to idols. Just as God provided manna during Israel's wilderness exodus, Christ promises superior new exodus provision to faithful believers. The hidden manna symbolizes intimate spiritual nourishment and participation in the messianic banquet of the age to come.
