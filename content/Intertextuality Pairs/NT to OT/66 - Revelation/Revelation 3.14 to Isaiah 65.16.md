# Revelation 3:14 to Isaiah 65:16

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 14|Revelation 3:14]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 16|Isaiah 65:16]] ("God of truth" = literally "God of Amen")
- [[Readable Bible/20 - Proverbs/Proverbs 8#Proverbs 8 . 22|Proverbs 8:22]] (Wisdom as "beginning" of creation - possible)

**Type**: Allusion

**Significance**: "The Amen" identifies Christ with the "God of Amen" from Isaiah 65:16, attributing divine reliability and truthfulness to Him. As "faithful and true witness" (echoing 1:5), Christ embodies complete trustworthiness in contrast to Laodicea's lukewarm unreliability. "Beginning of God's creation" designates Christ as source and originator of creation (cf. Colossians 1:15, 18), not as a created being but as the one through whom all creation came into existence. Together, these titles establish Christ's divine authority to judge the Laodicean church's compromised condition.
