# Revelation 1:5 to Psalm 89:27-28

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 5|Revelation 1:5]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 89#Psalm 89 . 27|Psalm 89:27-28, 37-38]] (all three phrases uniquely occur together)
- [[Readable Bible/10 - 2 Samuel/2 Samuel 7#2 Samuel 7 . 13|2 Samuel 7:13-16]] (Davidic covenant - eternal throne)
- [[Readable Bible/19 - Psalms/Psalm 2#Psalm 2 . 7|Psalm 2:7-8]] (messianic king)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 55#Isaiah 55 . 4|Isaiah 55:4]] (witness to peoples)
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 43#Isaiah 43 . 10|Isaiah 43:10-13]] (faithful witness)

**Type**: Allusion (messianic fulfillment)

**Significance**: These three titles uniquely appear together in Psalm 89's Davidic covenant context, which John applies to Jesus as the ideal Davidic king. Through His death and resurrection, Jesus secures eternal kingship promised to David's line. "Firstborn" indicates privileged position and supremacy, while "ruler of kings" affirms His present sovereignty over earthly powers. Jesus fulfills Israel's messianic hope as faithful witness who accomplishes what David's line failed to achieve.
