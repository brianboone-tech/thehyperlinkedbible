# Revelation 1:10-11 to Ezekiel 2:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 10|Revelation 1:10-11]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 2#Ezekiel 2 . 2|Ezekiel 2:2]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 3#Ezekiel 3 . 12|3:12, 14, 24]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 11#Ezekiel 11 . 1|11:1]]; [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 43#Ezekiel 43 . 5|43:5]] (rapture in Spirit)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 19#Exodus 19 . 16|Exodus 19:16, 19-20]] (voice like trumpet at Sinai)
- [[Readable Bible/02 - Exodus/Exodus 17#Exodus 17 . 14|Exodus 17:14]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 30#Isaiah 30 . 8|Isaiah 30:8]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 36#Jeremiah 36 . 2|Jeremiah 36:2]]; [[Readable Bible/24 - Jeremiah/Jeremiah 45#Jeremiah 45 . 1|45:1]] (command to write in book)

**Type**: Allusion (prophetic commission)

**Significance**: John identifies with OT prophets, particularly Ezekiel, through Spirit-induced rapture into the heavenly realm. The trumpet-like voice evokes Moses' Sinai theophany, establishing John's divine authority comparable to Moses. The command to write in a book parallels prophetic testaments of judgment, signaling that Revelation functions as covenant lawsuit. John stands in the prophetic tradition, mediating God's authoritative word to His people.
