# Revelation 1:8 to Isaiah 41:4

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 1#Revelation 1 . 8|Revelation 1:8]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 41#Isaiah 41 . 4|Isaiah 41:4]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 44#Isaiah 44 . 6|44:6]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 48#Isaiah 48 . 12|48:12]] ("first and last")
- [[Readable Bible/37 - Haggai/Haggai 1#Haggai 1 . 1|Haggai]], [[Readable Bible/38 - Zechariah/Zechariah 1#Zechariah 1 . 1|Zechariah]], [[Readable Bible/39 - Malachi/Malachi 1#Malachi 1 . 1|Malachi]] (παντοκράτωρ "Almighty")

**Type**: Allusion (merism)

**Significance**: The "Alpha and Omega" merism expresses God's sovereignty over the beginning and end of history, transcending all time. This echoes Isaiah's "first and last" declarations of YHWH's exclusive deity. The title "Almighty" (παντοκράτωρ), prominent in the post-exilic prophets, emphasizes God's omnipotent control over cosmic and historical events. Together, these titles assure believers that the God who initiated creation will bring history to its appointed consummation.
