# Revelation 3:12 to Isaiah 62:2

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/66 - Revelation/Revelation 3#Revelation 3 . 12|Revelation 3:12]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 62#Isaiah 62 . 2|Isaiah 62:2]]; [[Readable Bible/23 - Isaiah/Isaiah 65#Isaiah 65 . 15|65:15]] (new name for restored Israel)
- [[Readable Bible/26 - Ezekiel/Ezekiel 40#Ezekiel 40 . 1|Ezekiel 40-48]] (new temple)

**Type**: Allusion (eschatological identity)

**Significance**: The pillar imagery from temple architecture symbolizes permanent, structural belonging in God's dwelling place, contrasting with Philadelphia's vulnerability to earthquakes. Isaiah's promises of a new name for restored Israel now apply to believers, signifying transformed identity in the new creation. Ezekiel's vision of the eschatological temple finds spiritual fulfillment in believers' secure place in God's presence. The threefold naming emphasizes complete identification with God, the new Jerusalem, and Christ, establishing believers' eternal identity and unshakeable security in the new creation community.
