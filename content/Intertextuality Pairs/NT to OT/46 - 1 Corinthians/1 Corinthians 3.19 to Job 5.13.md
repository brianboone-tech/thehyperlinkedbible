# 1 Corinthians 3:19 to Job 5:13

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 3#1 Corinthians 3 . 19|1 Corinthians 3:19]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/18 - Job/Job 5#Job 5 . 13|Job 5:13]] (God catches the wise in their craftiness)

**Type**: Direct Quotation / Judgment Theme

**Significance**: Job 5:13 comes from Eliphaz's speech describing God's sovereign reversal of human plans. Paul applies this to show that God actively frustrates worldly wisdom's schemes. The original context in Job addresses the fate of the wicked whose clever plots backfire. Paul uses this to warn the Corinthians: their factional "wisdom" (dividing into parties, boasting in human leaders) is precisely the kind of craftiness God overthrows. This quotation reinforces chapter 1's theme from Isaiah 29:14 - God doesn't merely ignore human wisdom, He actively destroys it. Applied to Corinth, this warns that their party divisions, though appearing "wise" by worldly standards, are doomed to divine judgment.
