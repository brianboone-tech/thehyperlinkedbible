# 1 Corinthians 6:16 to Genesis 2:24

**NT Text**: [[Readable Bible/46 - 1 Corinthians/1 Corinthians 6#1 Corinthians 6 . 16|1 Corinthians 6:16]]

**OT Source(s)**:
- [[Readable Bible/01 - Genesis/Genesis 2#Genesis 2 . 24|Genesis 2:24]] (A man shall leave father and mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh)

**Type**: Direct Quotation / Creation Ordinance Applied to Ethics

**Significance**: Genesis 2:24 defines marriage as a creation ordinance involving "one flesh" union. Paul applies this to sexual immorality, showing that sexual intercourse always creates "one body" union, whether in marriage or not. This is a profound hermeneutical move: Paul treats the creation account not as prescriptive for marriage only, but as descriptive of sexual union's inherent nature. By joining with a prostitute, believers violate their prior union with Christ (6:17: "joined to the Lord, one spirit"). The Genesis text establishes that sexuality carries covenant significance - it creates union. For Christians, this means sexual immorality is not merely personal sin but corporate betrayal, since believers' bodies belong to Christ and the church community.
