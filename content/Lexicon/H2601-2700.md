## חֶצְרוֹנִי / חצרוני (Chetsrôwnîy)

_(khets-ro-nee' | hets-roh-NEE | hets-roh-NEE)_

### Definition

masculine noun; enclosed, division of song; patronymically from [[H2601-2700#H2696|H2696]]; a Chetsronite or (collectively) descendants of Chetsron; Hezronites.

- a descendant of Hezron

# H2700

## חֲצַרְמָוֶת / חצרמות (Chătsarmâveth)

_(khats-ar-maw'-veth | huh-tsahr-maw-WET | huh-tsahr-ma-VET)_

### Definition

proper masculine noun; village of death; from [[H2601-2700#H2691|H2691]] and [[H4101-4200#H4194|H4194]]; village of death; Chatsarmaveth, a place in Arabia; Hazarmaveth.

- the 3rd in order of the sons of Joktan (noun proper masculine)


### Note

Hazarmaveth may have founded the region of Hadhramaut (حضرموت‎) in the southern part of the Arabian peninsula near the Gulf of Aden, currently belonging to Yemen.
