# G3201

## μέμφομαι (mémphomai)

_(mem'-fom-ahee | MEM-foh-meh | MAME-foh-may)_

### Definition

middle voice of an apparently primary verb; to blame; find fault.

- to blame, find fault

# G3202

## μεμψίμοιρος (mempsímoiros)

_(mem-psim'-oy-ros | mem-PSEE-moo-rose | mame-PSEE-moo-rose)_

### Definition

from a presumed derivative of [[G3201-3300#G3201|G3201]] and μοῖρα (fate; akin to the base of [[G3301-3400#G3313|G3313]]); blaming fate, i.e., querulous (discontented); complainer.

- complaining of one's lot, querulous, discontented
