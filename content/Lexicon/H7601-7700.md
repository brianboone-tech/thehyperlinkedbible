## שֵׁגָל / שגל (shêgâl)

_(shay-gawl' | shay-ɡAWL | shay-ɡAHL)_

### Definition

feminine noun; corresponding to [[H7601-7700#H7694|H7694]]; a (legitimate) queen; wife.

- king's wife, king's concubine, (royal) consort

# H7696

## שָׁגַע / שגע (shâgaʻ)

_(shaw-gah' | shaw-ɡA | sha-ɡA)_

### Definition

verb; a primitive root; to rave through insanity; (be, play the) mad (man).

- to be mad
    - (Pual)
        - to be mad
        - to be maddened (participle)
    - (Hithpael) to show madness
