_(kes-aw-lone' | keh-saw-LONE | keh-sa-LONE)_

### Definition

proper locative noun; hopes; from [[H3601-3700#H3688|H3688]]; fertile; Kesalon, a place in Palestine; Chesalon.

- a town of the northern border of Judah approx 10 miles west of Jerusalem

# H3694

_(kes-ool-loth' | keh-sool-LOTE | keh-soo-LOTE)_

# H3695

## כַּסְלֻחִים / כסלחים (Kaçluchîym)

_(kas-loo'-kheem | kahs-loo-HEEM | kahs-loo-HEEM)_

### Definition

noun; fortified; a plural probably of foreign derivation; Casluchim, a people cognate to the Egyptians; Casluhim.

- a people or tribe descended from Mizraim (Egypt) progenitors of the Philistines and Caphtorim

_(kaw-saf' | kaw-SAHP | ka-SAHF)_
