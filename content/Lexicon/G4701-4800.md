# G4800

## συζάω (syzáō)

_(sood-zah'-o | soo-ZA-oh | syoo-ZA-oh)_

### Definition

from [[G4801-4900#G4862|G4862]] and [[G2101-2200#G2198|G2198]]; to continue to live in common with, i.e., co-survive (literally or figuratively); live with.

- to live together with one
  - of physical life on earth
  - to live a new life in union with Christ, i.e., dedicated to God
