# Greek Lexicon

**[[Home/08 - Scripture Index|← Scripture Index]]**

---

## Overview

The **Lexicon** contains Greek word studies organized by Strong's numbering system. Each entry includes the Greek word, transliteration, definition, and usage notes.

---

## How to Access

Lexicon entries are accessed through inline links in the [[Readable Bible/index|Readable Bible]] chapters. When you see a word with a lexicon link, click it to view the full word study.
