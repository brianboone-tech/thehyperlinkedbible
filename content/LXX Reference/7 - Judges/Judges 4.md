# Judges 4

[[LXX Reference/07 - Judges/Judges 3|←]] • [[LXX Reference/07 - Judges/Judges 5|→]]

---

#### Judges 4 - 1
And proceeded to act wickedly before and Ehud died.

#### Judges 4 - 2
And delivered into of Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; and the ruler of his force and he dwelt in Harosheth of the nations.

#### Judges 4 - 3
And cried out to because nine hundred chariots of iron his. And he afflicted Israel by might for twenty years.

#### Judges 4 - 4
And Deborah, a woman prophetess, wife of Lapidoth she judged Israel in that time.

#### Judges 4 - 5
And she sat down under of Deborah, between Rama and between Beth-el, in mount Ephraim; and ascended there to be judged.

#### Judges 4 - 6
And Deborah sent and called Barak son of Abinoam from out of Kedesh Naphtali. And she said to him, Did not give charge to you, that, You shall go forth unto mount Tabor, and you shall take with yourself ten thousand men of the sons of Naphtali, and of the sons of Zebulun?

#### Judges 4 - 7
And I shall bring to you at the rushing stream Kishon Sisera ruler of the force of Jabin, and his chariots, and his multitude; and I will deliver him into your hand.

#### Judges 4 - 8
And said If you should go with me, I will go; and if you should not go, will I not go.

#### Judges 4 - 9
And said In going I will go with you. Except know that will not be yours in the way which you go; for by of a woman Sisera. And Deborah rose up and went with Barak from out of Kedesh.

#### Judges 4 - 10
And Barak summoned Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh. And ascended by his feet ten thousand men; and Deborah ascended with him.

#### Judges 4 - 11
And the near ones of the Kenite were separated from Kena, from the sons of Hobab father-in-law of Moses. And he pitched his tent by Oak of the ones Resting, which is next to Kedesh.

#### Judges 4 - 12
And it was announced Sisera that ascended unto mount Tabor.

#### Judges 4 - 13
And Sisera called all his chariots  nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the people with him, from Harosheth of the nations unto the rushing stream Kishon.

#### Judges 4 - 14
And Deborah said to Barak, Rise up! for this day in which Sisera into your hand. Behold is not gone in front of you? And Barak went down from mount Tabor, and ten thousand men behind him.

#### Judges 4 - 15
And Sisera, and all his chariots, and all his camp, by of the broadsword before Barak. And Sisera came down from on top of his chariot, and he fled his feet.

#### Judges 4 - 16
And Barak pursued after the chariots, and after the camp unto Oak of the Nations. And fell by of the broadsword; left behind even one.

#### Judges 4 - 17
And Sisera withdrew his feet to of Jael wife of Heber the Kenite; for there was peace between Jabin king of Hazor and between the house of Heber the Kenite.

#### Judges 4 - 18
And Jael came forth for a meeting with Sisera, and she said to him, Turn aside, O my master, turn aside to me, do not fear! And he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with her hide covering.

#### Judges 4 - 19
And Sisera said to her, Give a drink please to me, a little water! for I am thirsty. And she opened the leather bag of the milk, and she gave him to drink, and she covered his person.

#### Judges 4 - 20
And he said to her, Stand in the door of the tent! And it will be if a man should come to you, and should ask you, and should say to you, Is here That you shall say, There is not.

#### Judges 4 - 21
And took the peg of the tent, and put the hammer in her hand, and she entered to him tranquilly, and she hammered in the peg into his cheek, and thrust it through into the ground. And he being startled was enveloped in darkness and died.

#### Judges 4 - 22
And, behold, Barak Sisera. And Jael came forth for meeting him, and she said to him, Come, and I will show to you the man whom you seek. And he entered with her. And behold, having fallen dead, and the peg in his temple.

#### Judges 4 - 23
And abased in that day Jabin king of Canaan in front of the sons of Israel.

#### Judges 4 - 24
And went forth going, and hardening against Jabin king of Canaan, until of which time they utterly destroyed him.
