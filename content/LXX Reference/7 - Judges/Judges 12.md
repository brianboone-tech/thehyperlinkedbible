# Judges 12

[[LXX Reference/07 - Judges/Judges 11|←]] • [[LXX Reference/07 - Judges/Judges 13|→]]

---

#### Judges 12 - 1
And gathered together and they came to Zaphon, and said to Jephthah, Why you went to wage war against the sons of Ammon, and us to go with you? your house over you with fire.

#### Judges 12 - 2
And Jephthah said to them, a man and my people, and the sons of Ammon humbled me exceedingly, and I yelled out to you, and you did not deliver me from out of their hand.

#### Judges 12 - 3
And I saw that one delivering, and I put my life in my hand, and I passed over to of Ammon, and delivered them over into my hand. And why did you ascend against me in this day to wage war against me?

#### Judges 12 - 4
And Jephthah gathered together all the men of Gilead, and waged war Ephraim. And struck Ephraim; for said You in of Ephraim, and in of Manasseh.

#### Judges 12 - 5
And Gilead was first to take the ford of the Jordan of Ephraim. And it came to pass when said We should pass over indeed! That said not And they said, We are not.

#### Judges 12 - 6
Then they said to them, Say indeed, Stachys! And straightly able to speak thus, then they would take hold of them, and slay them at the ford of the Jordan. And they that fell from Ephraim in that time and two thousand.

#### Judges 12 - 7
And Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then died and he was entombed in his city Gilead.

#### Judges 12 - 9
And there were to him thirty sons, and thirty daughters being sent out, and thirty for his sons from outside. And he judged Israel seven years.

#### Judges 12 - 10
And Ibzan died, and he was entombed in Beth-lehem.

#### Judges 12 - 11
And judged and he judged Israel ten years.

#### Judges 12 - 12
And died and was entombed in Aijalon in of Zebulun.

#### Judges 12 - 14
And there were to him forty sons, and thirty sons of his sons being mounted upon seventy foals. And he judged Israel eight years.

#### Judges 12 - 15
And died and he was entombed in Pirathon in of Ephraim, in Amalek.
