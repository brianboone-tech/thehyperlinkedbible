## Overview

The **LXX Reference** contains the Septuagint (Greek Old Testament) text. The Septuagint was the Greek translation of the Hebrew Scriptures used by the early church and frequently quoted in the New Testament.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Books** | 39 (Old Testament) |
| **Language** | Greek |

---

## Why the Septuagint Matters

- Many NT quotations of the OT follow the LXX rather than the Hebrew Masoretic Text
- Understanding the LXX helps interpret how NT authors understood OT passages
- The LXX was the Bible of the early church
