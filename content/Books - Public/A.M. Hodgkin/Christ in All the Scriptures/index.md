# Christ in All the Scriptures

**Author:** A.M. Hodgkin (1909)

A classic work tracing the person and work of Christ through every book of the Bible. Originally published in 1909, this public domain work surveys how the Old Testament points forward to Christ and how the New Testament reveals Him.
