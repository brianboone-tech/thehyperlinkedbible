# Looking Unto Jesus

**Author:** Isaac Ambrose (1604-1664)
**First Published:** 1658

A comprehensive Puritan meditation on Christ's work in salvation, tracing "the soul's eyeing of Jesus as carrying on the great work of man's salvation, from first to last." This work examines Christ across the entire scope of redemptive history - from eternity past through His second coming.

---

## About the Author

Isaac Ambrose was a Puritan divine and minister at Garstang in Lancashire, England. Known for his profound devotional writing and practice of extended meditation, he would spend one month each year in solitary retreat for prayer and contemplation. His works are characterized by deep Christological focus and practical application.

## Pattern of Each Book

Each book follows a consistent devotional pattern:
1. **Doctrinal exposition** of Christ's work in that phase
2. **Nine practical responses** to that truth:
   - Considering Jesus
   - Desiring Jesus
   - Hoping in Jesus
   - Believing in Jesus
   - Loving Jesus
   - Joying in Jesus
   - Calling on Jesus
   - Conforming to Jesus

---

## Typological Value

This work is rich in typological content, particularly:
- **Book II**: Christ in the Old Testament promises and types (creation to incarnation)
- **Book V**: Parallels between Christ's death and Levitical sacrifices
- **Book VII**: Christ's session and the Levitical high priestly ministry
- **Book VIII**: Christ's intercession compared to the high priests of old
