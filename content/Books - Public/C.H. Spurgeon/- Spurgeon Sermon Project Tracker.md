## Placeholder File Template

Each sermon placeholder file follows this format:

```markdown
**Sermon #{number}**
**Delivered in {year}**
**By The Rev. C. H. Spurgeon**
**At {location}**

---

##### Text
*{scripture reference}*

---

*[Paste sermon content here for formatting]*

---

*Taken from The {series}, Volume {volume}.*
```

**Location:**
- Volumes 1-6: "At New Park Street Chapel, Southwark"
- Volumes 7-63: "At the Metropolitan Tabernacle, Newington"

**Series:**
- Volumes 1-6: "New Park Street Pulpit"
- Volumes 7-63: "Metropolitan Tabernacle Pulpit"

---

## File Naming Convention

Files are named: `{number:04d} - {title}.md`

Examples:
- `0001 - The Immutability of God.md`
- `0054 - The Call of Abraham.md`
- `3561 - The Drawings of Love.md`

**Windows Restrictions:**
- No colons in filenames (use periods instead for scripture references)
- No question marks in filenames (removed from titles)
- Em-dashes replaced with hyphens
