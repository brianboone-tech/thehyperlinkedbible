# C.H. Spurgeon Sermon Collection

**Charles Haddon Spurgeon (1834-1892)** was known as the "Prince of Preachers." This collection contains his complete published sermons from **The New Park Street Pulpit** and **The Metropolitan Tabernacle Pulpit**.
