> *Hebrews 3:16 . Τινὲς γὰρ ἀκούσαντες παρεπίκραναν . The Syriac begins here the interrogatory part of this discourse: “For who are they that when they have heard provoked him?” But τινὲς is indefinite, and not interrogative, as the following words manifest, for the process is not by a redditive pronoun, but an exceptive adverb.*

Διὰ Μωυσέως , בְּיַר מרּשֵׁח “By the hands of Moses;” a frequent Hebraism for guidance or conduct.
