# John Owen's Exposition of Hebrews

**Author**: John Owen (1616-1683)

**Source**: StudyLight.org (public domain)

---

## About This Work

John Owen's *Exposition of the Epistle to the Hebrews* is considered one of the greatest commentaries ever written on a single book of the Bible. Owen (1616-1683) was the leading Puritan theologian of his era, serving as Vice-Chancellor of Oxford University and chaplain to Oliver Cromwell.

This massive work, originally published in four volumes, represents the apex of Owen's exegetical scholarship. It combines rigorous attention to the Greek text, extensive engagement with Hebrew sources and rabbinic literature, and profound theological reflection.

## Key Themes

- The superiority of Christ over angels, Moses, and the Levitical priesthood
- Christ as the eternal High Priest after the order of Melchizedek
- The new covenant's superiority over the old covenant
- Faith as the substance of things hoped for
- Perseverance and the danger of apostasy
