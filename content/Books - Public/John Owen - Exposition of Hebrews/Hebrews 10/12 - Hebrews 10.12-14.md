# Hebrews 10:12-14 - John Owen

[[- Index|← Back to Hebrews 10]]

---

> *Hebrews 10:12-14 . In these verses the apostle opposeth that one sacrifice of Christ unto the legal offerings that the priests attended unto; and that in three things:*

1. In the nature of it, and its perfection, Hebrews 10:12 .
2. The consequence on the part of Christ, by whom it was offered, Hebrews 10:12-13 .
3. In the effect of it towards the church, Hebrews 10:14 .
