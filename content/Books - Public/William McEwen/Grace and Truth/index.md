# Grace and Truth: The Glory and Fullness of the Redeemer Displayed

**Author:** William McEwen (1735-1762)
**Full Title:** Grace and Truth: Or, The Glory and Fulness of the Redeemer Displayed, in an Attempt to Explain, Illustrate, and Enforce the Most Remarkable Types, Figures, and Allegories of the Old Testament
**First Published:** 1761

---

## About the Author

William McEwen served as minister of the gospel at the Associate Congregation in Dundee, Scotland. He studied divinity under Ebenezer Erskine and James Fisher, leaders in the Scottish secession from the national church. Though he died at only 28 years of age, he left a remarkable work on biblical typology.

As John McLeod wrote in *Scottish Theology*: "He was one of the brightest ornaments of the Secession movement. He died at the early age of twenty-eight. But he left a book that embalms his memory."

## Typological Value

This work is one of the most systematic catalogs of biblical typology from the 18th century Scottish tradition. McEwen methodically traces Christ through:

- **13 Personal Types** (patriarchs, judges, kings, prophets)
- **17 Typical Things** (ceremonial objects, events, laws)
- **Multiple Typical Places** (sanctuary, land, city, feasts)

His approach combines careful exegesis with warm devotional application, making this work valuable for both study and meditation.
