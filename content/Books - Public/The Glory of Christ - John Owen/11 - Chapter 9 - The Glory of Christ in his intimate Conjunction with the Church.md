## <span style="color: #008080;">The Mystical Union</span>

The eighth thing wherein we may behold the glory of Christ is in **his intimate conjunction and union with his church**. This union is so close and real that Scripture describes it as one body, one spirit, and even as a marriage relationship.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones,"</span> Ephesians 5:30. This mystical union between Christ and his people is one of the most glorious truths revealed in Scripture.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of This Union</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. It is a Spiritual Union</span>

This union is not physical or merely legal, but spiritual—a real joining of Christ and believers by the Holy Spirit.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Agent of Union</span>

The Holy Spirit is the agent who creates and maintains this union:
- He **unites** believers to Christ
- He **dwells** in both Christ and his people
- He **communicates** the life of Christ to believers
- He **maintains** the living connection
- He **testifies** of this relationship

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit,"</span> 1 Corinthians 6:17.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Reality of the Connection</span>

This union is not metaphorical but real:
- It involves **actual participation** in Christ's nature
- It creates **genuine fellowship** between Christ and believers
- It establishes **mutual indwelling**—Christ in us and we in him
- It produces **shared experiences** of joy, sorrow, and glory

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"At that day ye shall know that I am in my Father, and ye in me, and I in you,"</span> John 14:20.

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. It is an Intimate Union</span>

The closeness of this relationship surpasses all human analogies, though Scripture uses several to help us understand it.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Marriage Union</span>

The most intimate earthly relationship is used to illustrate this spiritual reality:

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh. This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church,"</span> Ephesians 5:31-32.

In this spiritual marriage:
- Christ is the **bridegroom**, the church the **bride**
- There is **mutual love** and affection
- There is **faithful commitment** and covenant
- There is **shared inheritance** and resources
- There is **fruitfulness** in spiritual offspring

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Body and Head Union</span>

Another analogy emphasizes the organic nature of this relationship:

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church, Which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all,"</span> Ephesians 1:22-23.

As head of the body, Christ:
- **Directs** all the movements of his people
- **Supplies** life and nourishment to every member
- **Coordinates** the various gifts and functions
- **Protects** the body from harm
- **Represents** the whole body before God

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Vine and Branches Union</span>

Christ himself used this analogy to teach the dependence involved in this union:

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing,"</span> John 15:5.

This illustrates:
- Our **complete dependence** on Christ for spiritual life
- The **flow of grace** from Christ to believers
- The **necessity of abiding** in him for fruitfulness
- The **organic unity** of all believers in Christ

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Foundation of This Union</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. The Eternal Purpose</span>

This union was not an afterthought but was planned in eternity past.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">Election in Christ</span>

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love,"</span> Ephesians 1:4.

From eternity, God:
- **Chose** his people in Christ
- **Predestined** them to adoption as sons
- **Appointed** Christ to be their head and representative
- **Planned** their ultimate union with him

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Covenant of Grace</span>

In the eternal covenant between the Father and the Son:
- The Father **gave** a people to the Son
- The Son **accepted** them as his bride and body
- The Spirit **agreed** to apply the union
- All three persons **committed** to its accomplishment

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out,"</span> John 6:37.

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. The Incarnation Basis</span>

The incarnation made this union possible by uniting divine and human natures in Christ's person.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">Taking Our Nature</span>

By assuming human nature, Christ:
- **Qualified** himself to be our representative
- **Enabled** the union of humanity with divinity
- **Prepared** the way for our exaltation
- **Demonstrated** God's desire for fellowship with mankind

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Forasmuch then as the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same; that through death he might destroy him that had the power of death,"</span> Hebrews 2:14.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Means of This Union</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. Faith as the Instrumental Cause</span>

Faith is the means by which individual believers enter into this union with Christ.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of Union Faith</span>

This faith:
- **Receives** Christ as offered in the gospel
- **Rests** upon him alone for salvation
- **Trusts** in his person and work
- **Submits** to his lordship
- **Cleaves** to him with the whole heart

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name,"</span> John 1:12.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Work of the Spirit</span>

The Holy Spirit:
- **Creates** faith in the heart
- **Draws** the soul to Christ
- **Applies** the union effectually
- **Witnesses** to its reality
- **Maintains** its continuance

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit,"</span> 1 Corinthians 12:13.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Benefits of This Union</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. What This Union Secures</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Justification</span>

Because of our union with Christ:
- His **righteousness** becomes ours
- His **obedience** is credited to our account
- His **satisfaction** covers all our sins
- His **acceptance** with the Father includes us

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him,"</span> 2 Corinthians 5:21.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Sanctification</span>

Union with Christ ensures our progressive holiness:
- His **life** flows into us
- His **Spirit** works within us
- His **nature** is gradually formed in us
- His **image** is progressively restored

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord,"</span> 2 Corinthians 3:18.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Adoption</span>

Through union with the Son, we become sons and daughters:
- We **share** in his sonship
- We **inherit** with him
- We have **access** to the Father
- We receive the **Spirit of adoption**

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together,"</span> Romans 8:17.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Resurrection and Eternal Life</span>

Our union with Christ guarantees our resurrection:
- His **resurrection** secures ours
- His **life** becomes our life
- His **victory** over death includes us
- His **glorification** ensures ours

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Because I live, ye shall live also,"</span> John 14:19.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Privileges of This Union</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. The Present Blessings</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Security</span>

This union provides unshakeable security:
- We are **hidden** with Christ in God
- We are **kept** by his power
- We **cannot** be separated from his love
- Our **life is secured** in him

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?"</span> Romans 8:35.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Fellowship</span>

Union brings intimate fellowship:
- **Communion** with Christ in prayer
- **Participation** in his sufferings and joys
- **Sharing** of his thoughts through his Word
- **Experience** of his presence

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Sympathy</span>

Christ shares our experiences:
- He **feels** our sorrows
- He **bears** our burdens
- He **understands** our temptations
- He **intercedes** for our needs

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin,"</span> Hebrews 4:15.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Strength</span>

From this union flows spiritual strength:
- **Grace** for every trial
- **Power** for every duty
- **Wisdom** for every decision
- **Comfort** for every sorrow

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me,"</span> Philippians 4:13.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Responsibilities of This Union</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VIII. What This Union Demands</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Holiness</span>

Union with the Holy One requires holiness in us:
- **Separation** from sin
- **Consecration** to God
- **Transformation** of character
- **Conformity** to Christ's image

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation,"</span> 1 Peter 1:15.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Love</span>

Union with Love himself produces love:
- **Love to Christ** above all
- **Love to fellow members** of his body
- **Love to the lost** for whom he died
- **Love to enemies** as he taught

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Obedience</span>

Union with Christ involves submission to his will:
- **Keeping** his commandments
- **Following** his example
- **Seeking** his glory
- **Serving** his kingdom

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"If ye love me, keep my commandments,"</span> John 14:15.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Fruitfulness</span>

This union must produce spiritual fruit:
- **Good works** that glorify God
- **Spiritual graces** that adorn the gospel
- **Service** that builds the kingdom
- **Witness** that wins the lost

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit; so shall ye be my disciples,"</span> John 15:8.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Unbelievers</span>

This glorious union is offered to all who will believe. Come to Christ and be united to him forever. Outside of union with Christ, there is no salvation.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Doubting Believers</span>

If you have truly believed in Christ, this union is yours regardless of your feelings. Rest in the objective reality of what God has accomplished, not in subjective experiences.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For All Christians</span>

Live in the reality of this union:
- **Draw** upon Christ's strength
- **Claim** your privileges in him
- **Fulfill** your responsibilities to him
- **Rejoice** in this blessed relationship

You are not your own; you are Christ's, and Christ is yours.

---

*What a mystery is this union between Christ and his church! It surpasses the closest earthly relationships in its intimacy, security, and blessedness. To be united to Christ is to be united to the source of all life, light, love, and glory. In him we live and move and have our being. We are complete in him who is the head of all principality and power.*
