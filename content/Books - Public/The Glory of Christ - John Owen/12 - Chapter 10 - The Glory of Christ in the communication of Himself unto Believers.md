## <span style="color: #008080;">The Divine Self-Communication</span>

The ninth thing wherein we may behold the glory of Christ is in **his gracious communication of himself unto believers**. This is the practical working out of the union described in the previous chapter—how Christ actually imparts his life, grace, and glory to his people.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace,"</span> John 1:16. This communication is not merely external but internal, real, and transforming.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of This Communication</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. It is a Personal Communication</span>

Christ does not merely give gifts to his people; he gives himself. The communication is not just of benefits but of his very person.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">Christ Himself is the Gift</span>

- He **dwells** in believers by his Spirit
- He **manifests** himself to their souls
- He **reveals** his love personally
- He **shares** his very life with them
- He **communes** with them intimately

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"At that day ye shall know that I am in my Father, and ye in me, and I in you,"</span> John 14:20.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Reality of His Presence</span>

This is not metaphorical but actual:
- Christ **truly** dwells in believers
- He **actually** lives within them
- He **really** shares their experiences
- He **genuinely** affects their lives
- He **personally** guides and comforts them

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me,"</span> Galatians 2:20.

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. It is a Spiritual Communication</span>

This communication is through the Holy Spirit, who is the agent of all Christ's operations in believers.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Spirit as the Communicator</span>

The Holy Spirit:
- **Applies** Christ to the soul
- **Reveals** Christ within
- **Conformes** believers to Christ
- **Enables** fellowship with Christ
- **Witnesses** of Christ's presence

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me,"</span> John 15:26.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Spiritual Nature</span>

This communication is:
- **Inward** rather than external
- **Supernatural** rather than natural
- **Transforming** rather than superficial
- **Experiential** rather than merely intellectual
- **Life-giving** rather than merely informational

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Means of This Communication</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. Through the Word of God</span>

Christ communicates himself primarily through his Word, which is the appointed means of grace.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Word as Vehicle</span>

Through Scripture, Christ:
- **Reveals** himself to faith
- **Speaks** to the heart
- **Instructs** the mind
- **Comforts** the soul
- **Guides** the life

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me,"</span> John 5:39.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Living Word</span>

The written Word becomes a living voice when:
- The **Spirit** illuminates it
- **Faith** receives it
- The **heart** responds to it
- **Obedience** follows it
- **Experience** confirms it

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart,"</span> Hebrews 4:12.

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. Through Prayer and Communion</span>

In prayer, believers enjoy direct fellowship with Christ, receiving fresh communications of his grace.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Privilege of Access</span>

Through prayer, believers:
- **Approach** Christ directly
- **Pour out** their hearts to him
- **Receive** his responses
- **Experience** his presence
- **Enjoy** his fellowship

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need,"</span> Hebrews 4:16.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Two-Way Communication</span>

In prayer:
- Believers **speak** to Christ
- Christ **speaks** to believers
- Hearts are **opened** to receive
- Grace is **poured** out abundantly
- Fellowship is **sweetly** enjoyed

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. Through the Ordinances</span>

Christ has appointed special ordinances where he particularly communicates himself to his people.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Lord's Supper</span>

In communion, Christ:
- **Presents** himself as the bread of life
- **Nourishes** souls with his body and blood
- **Renews** the covenant relationship
- **Strengthens** faith and love
- **Anticipates** the heavenly feast

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"This is my body which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me,"</span> 1 Corinthians 11:24.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">Baptism</span>

In baptism, Christ:
- **Represents** union with himself
- **Signifies** death and resurrection with him
- **Confirms** the washing of regeneration
- **Declares** public allegiance to him
- **Initiates** into his visible church

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Content of This Communication</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. What Christ Communicates</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. His Life</span>

Christ imparts his own divine life to believers:
- **Spiritual** life that was dead
- **Eternal** life that never ends
- **Abundant** life that satisfies
- **Victorious** life that overcomes
- **Growing** life that develops

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly,"</span> John 10:10.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. His Righteousness</span>

Christ clothes believers with his perfect righteousness:
- **Justifying** righteousness that acquits
- **Sanctifying** righteousness that purifies
- **Practical** righteousness that guides
- **Progressive** righteousness that grows
- **Perfect** righteousness that satisfies divine justice

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him,"</span> 2 Corinthians 5:21.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. His Peace</span>

Christ gives his own peace to troubled hearts:
- **Peace with God** through reconciliation
- **Peace of God** that passes understanding
- **Peace in conscience** through forgiveness
- **Peace in trials** through his presence
- **Peace in death** through his victory

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid,"</span> John 14:27.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. His Joy</span>

Christ shares his joy with his people:
- **Joy in salvation** freely given
- **Joy in fellowship** with God
- **Joy in service** for the kingdom
- **Joy in trials** through grace
- **Joy in hope** of glory

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full,"</span> John 15:11.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">5. His Love</span>

Christ fills believers with his divine love:
- **Love for God** above all
- **Love for Christ** himself
- **Love for fellow believers** as brethren
- **Love for the lost** as objects of mercy
- **Love for enemies** as Christ commanded

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And we have known and believed the love that God hath to us. God is love; and he that dwelleth in love dwelleth in God, and God in him,"</span> 1 John 4:16.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Manner of This Communication</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. How Christ Communicates Himself</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Gradually</span>

This communication is progressive, not instantaneous:
- **Beginning** with the new birth
- **Continuing** through sanctification
- **Increasing** as faith grows
- **Developing** through experience
- **Perfecting** in glorification

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord,"</span> 2 Corinthians 3:18.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Seasonally</span>

There are special seasons of fuller communication:
- **Times of revival** in the soul
- **Moments of crisis** requiring grace
- **Periods of worship** and communion
- **Seasons of service** and sacrifice
- **Hours of suffering** and trial

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Conditionally</span>

While based on grace, this communication responds to certain conditions:
- **Faith** that receives
- **Prayer** that seeks
- **Obedience** that submits
- **Humility** that acknowledges need
- **Love** that desires fellowship

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him,"</span> John 14:23.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Effects of This Communication</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VIII. What This Communication Produces</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Transformation</span>

Christ's communication transforms believers:
- **Inwardly** by changing the heart
- **Morally** by producing holiness
- **Intellectually** by renewing the mind
- **Emotionally** by sanctifying affections
- **Practically** by directing behavior

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new,"</span> 2 Corinthians 5:17.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Conformity</span>

The goal is conformity to Christ's image:
- **His character** reproduced in believers
- **His mind** formed within them
- **His heart** beating in their breasts
- **His will** governing their choices
- **His life** lived through them

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren,"</span> Romans 8:29.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Fruitfulness</span>

This communication produces spiritual fruit:
- **Fruit of righteousness** in conduct
- **Fruit of the Spirit** in character
- **Fruit of service** in ministry
- **Fruit of witness** in evangelism
- **Fruit of worship** in praise

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit; so shall ye be my disciples,"</span> John 15:8.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Obstacles to This Communication</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">IX. What Hinders the Flow</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Unbelief</span>

Lack of faith blocks Christ's communication:
- **Doubt** concerning his promises
- **Distrust** of his goodness
- **Disbelief** in his presence
- **Denial** of his power
- **Dependence** on other sources

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Sin</span>

Unconfessed and cherished sin grieves the Spirit:
- **Known sins** maintained
- **Secret sins** hidden
- **Besetting sins** indulged
- **Willful sins** committed
- **Habitual sins** practiced

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me,"</span> Psalm 66:18.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Worldliness</span>

Preoccupation with earthly things crowds out heavenly communion:
- **Excessive** care for temporal things
- **Inordinate** love of pleasure
- **Consuming** pursuit of success
- **Overwhelming** busyness
- **Misplaced** priorities

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Seeking This Communication</span>

- **Seek** Christ earnestly in his Word
- **Ask** for the Spirit's illumination
- **Remove** known obstacles
- **Wait** patiently for his timing
- **Expect** his gracious response

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Experiencing Dryness</span>

- **Examine** your life for hindrances
- **Confess** any discovered sins
- **Renew** your commitment to Christ
- **Continue** in the means of grace
- **Trust** his faithful love

### <span style="color: #008080;">For All Believers</span>

Prize this privilege above all earthly treasures. To have Christ communicate himself to you is the highest honor and greatest blessing possible in this life.

Live in constant expectation of fresh communications from your risen Lord. He delights to reveal himself to those who seek him with their whole heart.

---

*What condescension is this, that the eternal Son of God should communicate himself to sinful creatures! What love is this, that he should share his very life with those who deserved only death! What grace is this, that he should dwell within the hearts of his redeemed people! This communication of Christ to believers is the spring of all spiritual life, the source of all true happiness, and the foretaste of eternal glory.*
