## <span style="color: #008080;">The Transition from Humiliation to Exaltation</span>

The sixth thing wherein we may behold the glory of Christ is in **his exaltation after he had finished the work of mediation in this world**. Having humbled himself unto death, even the death of the cross, God has highly exalted him and given him a name which is above every name.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth,"</span> Philippians 2:9-10.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Steps of His Exaltation</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. His Resurrection from the Dead</span>

The first step in Christ's exaltation was his resurrection from the dead on the third day. This was the Father's seal upon his finished work and his vindication as the Son of God.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of His Resurrection</span>

Christ's resurrection was:
- **Actual and bodily**, not merely spiritual
- **Victorious** over death and the grave
- **Voluntary**, for he had power to lay down his life and take it again
- **Evidential** of his divine nature and finished work
- **Representative** of all his people

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And declared to be the Son of God with power, according to the spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead,"</span> Romans 1:4.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Glory of This Victory</span>

In his resurrection, Christ displayed his glory by:
- **Conquering** the last enemy, death
- **Fulfilling** all Old Testament prophecies
- **Proving** the efficacy of his sacrifice
- **Opening** the way to eternal life
- **Assuring** the resurrection of all believers

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept,"</span> 1 Corinthians 15:20.

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. His Ascension into Heaven</span>

Forty days after his resurrection, Christ ascended bodily into heaven in the sight of his disciples, returning to the Father who sent him.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Manner of His Ascension</span>

Christ's ascension was:
- **Visible** to his apostles
- **Bodily** in his human nature
- **Triumphant** as a conqueror
- **Public** before heavenly hosts
- **Final** completion of his earthly work

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them, and carried up into heaven,"</span> Luke 24:51.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Purpose of His Ascension</span>

Christ ascended to:
- **Return** to his pre-incarnate glory
- **Prepare** a place for his people
- **Send** the Holy Spirit
- **Begin** his heavenly intercession
- **Take** possession of his kingdom

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also,"</span> John 14:3.

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. His Session at the Right Hand of God</span>

The climax of Christ's exaltation is his sitting at the right hand of the Majesty on high, sharing the throne of universal dominion.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Significance of the Right Hand</span>

To sit at God's right hand signifies:
- **Equality** with the Father in glory
- **Authority** over all creation
- **Honor** above all creatures
- **Power** to execute divine will
- **Rest** from the work of redemption

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"So then after the Lord had spoken unto them, he was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God,"</span> Mark 16:19.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Activities of His Session</span>

At the Father's right hand, Christ:
- **Rules** over all things for his church
- **Intercedes** for his people
- **Dispenses** gifts and graces
- **Subdues** his enemies
- **Awaits** his final triumph

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us,"</span> Romans 8:34.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of His Exalted Glory</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. The Character of This Exaltation</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. It is Personal</span>

This exaltation belongs to the person of Christ as God-man. It is not merely his divine nature (which was always glorious) but his whole person, including his human nature, that is exalted.

Our nature in him is raised to the highest pinnacle of created glory.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. It is Earned</span>

This exaltation was earned by his perfect obedience and sacrificial death. It is the reward of his finished work.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and to enter into his glory?"</span> Luke 24:26.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. It is Universal</span>

Christ is exalted far above all principality and power and might and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world but also in that which is to come.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church,"</span> Ephesians 1:22.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. It is Eternal</span>

This exaltation is not temporary but everlasting. Christ sits forever at the Father's right hand.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But this man, after he had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down on the right hand of God,"</span> Hebrews 10:12.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Benefits of His Exaltation</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. What His Exaltation Accomplishes for Us</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Our Justification</span>

Christ's exaltation is the proof and completion of our justification. He was raised for our justification and exalted as evidence that our sins are forgiven.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Who was delivered for our offences, and was raised again for our justification,"</span> Romans 4:25.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Our Acceptance</span>

Because Christ is accepted and exalted, we are accepted in him. His exaltation secures our standing before God.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved,"</span> Ephesians 1:6.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Our Spiritual Blessings</span>

From his exalted position, Christ pours out spiritual gifts and graces upon his church.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Wherefore he saith, When he ascended up on high, he led captivity captive, and gave gifts unto men,"</span> Ephesians 4:8.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Our Future Glory</span>

Christ's exaltation is the guarantee and pattern of our own future exaltation with him.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together,"</span> Romans 8:17.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Present Work of the Exalted Christ</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. What He Does in His Exalted State</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. He Governs All Things</span>

From his throne, Christ rules over all creation, directing all things according to the counsel of his will for the good of his church.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The LORD hath prepared his throne in the heavens; and his kingdom ruleth over all,"</span> Psalm 103:19.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. He Intercedes Continually</span>

Christ's intercession is not occasional but constant. He ever lives to make intercession for his people.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them,"</span> Hebrews 7:25.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. He Sends the Holy Spirit</span>

The exalted Christ is the giver of the Holy Spirit to his church, fulfilling his promise to be with us always.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Therefore being by the right hand of God exalted, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Ghost, he hath shed forth this, which ye now see and hear,"</span> Acts 2:33.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. He Prepares Our Dwelling Place</span>

In his Father's house, Christ is preparing mansions for all his people, ensuring their eternal home.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you,"</span> John 14:2.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Future Manifestation of His Glory</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. His Coming Again in Glory</span>

The full manifestation of Christ's exalted glory awaits his second coming, when every eye shall see him and every knee shall bow.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. He Will Come Visibly</span>

Christ's return will be personal, visible, and glorious, unlike his first coming in humiliation.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him,"</span> Revelation 1:7.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. He Will Judge All Men</span>

As the exalted Lord, Christ will judge the living and the dead according to their works.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Because he hath appointed a day, in the which he will judge the world in righteousness by that man whom he hath ordained; whereof he hath given assurance unto all men, in that he hath raised him from the dead,"</span> Acts 17:31.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. He Will Deliver Up the Kingdom</span>

After subduing all enemies, Christ will deliver up the kingdom to the Father, that God may be all in all.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Then cometh the end, when he shall have delivered up the kingdom to God, even the Father; when he shall have put down all rule and all authority and power,"</span> 1 Corinthians 15:24.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Our Response to His Exaltation</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VIII. How We Should Respond</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. With Worship and Adoration</span>

The exalted Christ deserves our highest worship and praise. We should bow before him as our Lord and God.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth,"</span> Philippians 2:10.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. With Confidence and Hope</span>

His exaltation gives us confidence in prayer and hope for the future. Our Savior is on the throne!

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need,"</span> Hebrews 4:16.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. With Submission and Service</span>

We should submit to his authority and serve in his kingdom, acknowledging him as our rightful King.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father,"</span> Philippians 2:11.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. With Expectation of His Return</span>

We should live in constant expectation of his glorious appearing, when we shall see him as he is.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ,"</span> Titus 2:13.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Facing Trials</span>

Remember that your Savior is exalted far above all your troubles and enemies. He rules for your good and will make all things work together for your benefit.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Struggling with Sin</span>

Look to the exalted Christ who ever lives to make intercession for you. His advocacy ensures your ultimate victory over sin.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For All Believers</span>

Live as those whose citizenship is in heaven, where Christ sits at the right hand of God. Set your affections on things above, not on things on the earth.

---

*Behold the glory of our exalted Savior! From the cross to the crown, from the grave to the throne, from humiliation to exaltation—what a journey of love and triumph! Our nature in him is exalted to the highest heavens. We have a man upon the throne of the universe, our elder brother, our kinsman-redeemer, our Lord and our God!*
