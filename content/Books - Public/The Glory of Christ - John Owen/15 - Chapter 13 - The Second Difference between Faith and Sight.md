## <span style="color: #008080;">The Contrast in Clarity</span>

The second great difference between our present sight of Christ's glory by faith and our future vision in heaven is that **our present sight is obscure and imperfect, while our future sight will be clear and perfect**.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face: now I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known,"</span> 1 Corinthians 13:12.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Obscurity of Our Present Vision</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. The Nature of Present Darkness</span>

Our present sight of Christ's glory, though real and precious, is characterized by significant obscurity and limitation.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Metaphor of the Dark Glass</span>

The apostle uses the figure of seeing "through a glass, darkly" (Greek: en ainigmati):
- Ancient **mirrors** were made of polished metal, not clear glass
- They gave **dim** and **distorted** reflections
- The image was **unclear** and **uncertain**
- Details were **lost** in the darkness
- The **reality** was greater than the reflection

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image from glory to glory, even as by the Spirit of the Lord,"</span> 2 Corinthians 3:18.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Causes of Present Obscurity</span>

Several factors contribute to the darkness of our present vision:

**1. The Weakness of Faith**
- Faith is **imperfect** in this life
- It **fluctuates** in strength and clarity
- It is **hindered** by remaining corruption
- It **struggles** against doubt and unbelief
- It **varies** according to circumstances

**2. The Limitations of the Understanding**
- Our **minds** are finite and fallen
- Our **comprehension** is partial and slow
- Our **knowledge** is fragmentary
- Our **wisdom** is limited
- Our **perception** is clouded by sin

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the LORD. For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts,"</span> Isaiah 55:8-9.

**3. The Influence of Remaining Sin**
- **Pride** dims spiritual sight
- **Worldliness** clouds the vision
- **Unbelief** creates darkness
- **Carnality** hinders perception
- **Self-will** distorts understanding

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. The Specific Areas of Obscurity</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. The Person of Christ</span>

Even believers struggle to comprehend:
- The **mystery** of the incarnation
- The **union** of divine and human natures
- The **communication** of properties
- The **glory** of his person
- The **wisdom** of his constitution

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory,"</span> 1 Timothy 3:16.

We know these truths by faith, but our understanding is **limited**, our apprehension **partial**, and our sight **obscure**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. The Work of Christ</span>

The obscurity extends to his redemptive work:
- The **depths** of his love are unfathomable
- The **extent** of his sacrifice is immeasurable
- The **efficacy** of his atonement surpasses understanding
- The **wisdom** of his mediation exceeds our grasp
- The **glory** of his accomplishment is too bright for present sight

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. The Benefits of Christ</span>

Even the blessings we receive are imperfectly understood:
- **Justification** - we know we are righteous but dimly see the glory
- **Adoption** - we are sons but barely comprehend the dignity
- **Sanctification** - we experience growth but see it obscurely
- **Glorification** - we have the promise but cannot imagine the reality

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is,"</span> 1 John 3:2.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Effects of This Obscurity</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. How Darkness Affects Our Experience</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Limited Satisfaction</span>

The obscurity of our present vision limits our spiritual satisfaction:
- Our **joy** is mixed with sorrow
- Our **peace** is interrupted by trouble
- Our **comfort** is partial and temporary
- Our **delight** in Christ is constrained
- Our **contentment** is incomplete

We taste that the Lord is good, but we do not yet feast at his table.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Weak Assurance</span>

The darkness affects the strength of our assurance:
- **Doubts** arise about our salvation
- **Fears** trouble our hearts
- **Questions** disturb our peace
- **Uncertainties** cloud our confidence
- **Hesitations** weaken our boldness

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God,"</span> Romans 8:16.

Yet even this witness sometimes seems **dim** and **unclear** to our darkened understanding.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Imperfect Transformation</span>

Our conformity to Christ's image is hindered by limited sight:
- We see his **beauty** but dimly reflect it
- We know his **holiness** but imperfectly imitate it
- We understand his **love** but weakly express it
- We perceive his **grace** but inadequately display it
- We behold his **glory** but faintly mirror it

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. The Struggles of Present Vision</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. The Battle with Unbelief</span>

In our present state, faith constantly struggles against unbelief:
- **Skeptical thoughts** arise unbidden
- **Questioning voices** whisper doubts
- **Contradictory evidence** challenges faith
- **Difficult circumstances** test belief
- **Spiritual darkness** clouds the mind

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief,"</span> Mark 9:24.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. The Conflict with Worldliness</span>

Present things often overshadow eternal realities:
- **Visible** things seem more real than invisible
- **Temporal** concerns crowd out eternal perspectives
- **Material** interests compete with spiritual priorities
- **Earthly** pleasures diminish heavenly desires
- **Present** troubles obscure future glory

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. The Weakness of Spiritual Appetite</span>

Our desire for Christ is often **weak** and **inconsistent**:
- We **hunger** for him, but not constantly
- We **thirst** for him, but not intensely
- We **seek** him, but not always earnestly
- We **love** him, but not perfectly
- We **delight** in him, but not exclusively

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Perfect Clarity of Future Vision</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. The Nature of Heavenly Sight</span>

In contrast to our present obscure vision, our future sight of Christ will be perfectly clear and undimmed.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">Face to Face Vision</span>

The apostle promises we shall see "face to face":
- **Direct** sight without any medium
- **Clear** vision without any obscurity
- **Perfect** perception without any limitation
- **Immediate** knowledge without any delay
- **Complete** understanding without any confusion

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him: And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads,"</span> Revelation 22:3-4.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Removal of All Hindrances</span>

In heaven, everything that now obscures our sight will be removed:
- **Sin** will be completely gone
- **Corruption** will be entirely abolished
- **Weakness** will be fully remedied
- **Ignorance** will be perfectly enlightened
- **Mortality** will be swallowed up by life

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. The Perfection of Heavenly Knowledge</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Complete Understanding</span>

Our knowledge of Christ will be:
- **Full** rather than partial
- **Clear** rather than obscure
- **Certain** rather than doubtful
- **Immediate** rather than mediated
- **Perfect** rather than imperfect

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face: now I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known,"</span> 1 Corinthians 13:12.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Unveiled Glory</span>

We shall see Christ's glory as it truly is:
- His **divine nature** in all its majesty
- His **human nature** in all its perfection
- His **person** in all its mystery and beauty
- His **work** in all its wisdom and efficacy
- His **love** in all its depths and heights

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Satisfied Vision</span>

This perfect sight will bring perfect satisfaction:
- **All questions** will be answered
- **All doubts** will be resolved
- **All darkness** will be dispelled
- **All desires** will be fulfilled
- **All longings** will be satisfied

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"As for me, I will behold thy face in righteousness: I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness,"</span> Psalm 17:15.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Transformation This Will Bring</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. The Effects of Perfect Vision</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Perfect Conformity</span>

Seeing Christ clearly will transform us completely:
- We shall be **like him** in holiness
- We shall **reflect** his glory perfectly
- We shall **display** his character fully
- We shall **express** his nature completely
- We shall **manifest** his image clearly

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is,"</span> 1 John 3:2.

The **sight** and the **transformation** are inseparably connected.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Perfect Joy</span>

This clear vision will produce unmingled joy:
- **No sorrow** will interrupt it
- **No pain** will diminish it
- **No fear** will threaten it
- **No doubt** will cloud it
- **No sin** will corrupt it

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Perfect Peace</span>

With clear sight comes perfect rest:
- **All conflicts** will cease
- **All struggles** will end
- **All warfare** will be over
- **All agitation** will be stilled
- **All disturbance** will be gone

### <span style="color: #008080;">VIII. The Eternal Duration</span>

This perfect vision will be:
- **Constant** without any interruption
- **Increasing** without any decrease
- **Fresh** without any staleness
- **Satisfying** without any diminishment
- **Eternal** without any end

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And so shall we ever be with the Lord,"</span> 1 Thessalonians 4:17.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Practical Implications</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">IX. For Our Present Experience</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Patience with Present Limitations</span>

Understanding this difference should produce:
- **Patience** with our current limitations
- **Acceptance** of our present weaknesses
- **Contentment** despite imperfect sight
- **Hope** for future perfection
- **Perseverance** through present darkness

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us,"</span> Romans 8:18.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Diligent Use of Present Means</span>

While our sight is dim, we must:
- **Study** Scripture diligently
- **Pray** for spiritual illumination
- **Seek** Christ earnestly
- **Use** all means of grace faithfully
- **Pursue** closer communion with him

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Humble Acknowledgment of Ignorance</span>

We should:
- **Confess** our limited understanding
- **Acknowledge** our spiritual darkness
- **Admit** our need for greater light
- **Depend** on divine teaching
- **Submit** to scriptural revelation

### <span style="color: #008080;">X. For Our Future Hope</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Eager Anticipation</span>

This truth should create:
- **Longing** for perfect sight
- **Desire** for heavenly vision
- **Anticipation** of clear understanding
- **Hope** for complete knowledge
- **Expectation** of full satisfaction

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Present Comfort</span>

In times of spiritual darkness, remember:
- Your **present struggles** are temporary
- Your **future sight** will be perfect
- Your **current confusion** will give way to clarity
- Your **present questions** will all be answered
- Your **dim vision** will become brilliant sight

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory; While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal,"</span> 2 Corinthians 4:17-18.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Discouraged by Darkness</span>

Do not despair if your spiritual sight seems dim. The darkness is temporary; the light is eternal.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Proud of Their Knowledge</span>

Remember that your present understanding, however good, is but a glimpse compared to the perfect sight you will have in glory. Humility becomes those who see through a glass darkly.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For All Believers</span>

Live in the tension between present darkness and future light. Use your dim sight well while longing for perfect vision. What you see imperfectly now by faith will be seen perfectly then by sight.

---

*How great is the difference between our dark sight and bright vision! Now we strain to see through clouds and shadows; then we shall behold in perfect light. Now we know in part and see obscurely; then we shall know fully and see clearly. Let us be patient with our present limitations while eagerly anticipating our future perfection. The dawn is breaking, and perfect day is coming when all shadows will flee away.*
