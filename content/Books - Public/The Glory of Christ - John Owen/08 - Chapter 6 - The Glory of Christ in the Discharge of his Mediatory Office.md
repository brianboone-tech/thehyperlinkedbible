## <span style="color: #008080;">The Threefold Office</span>

The fifth thing wherein we may behold the glory of Christ is in **the discharge of his mediatory office**, wherein he appears as our Prophet, Priest, and King. In these offices, all the glory of his person and work is concentrated and displayed.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken,"</span> Deuteronomy 18:15. This Prophet is Christ, who is also our great High Priest and eternal King.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Christ as Our Prophet</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. The Prophetical Office</span>

Christ as Prophet is the great teacher and revealer of God's will to his people. In this office, he displays the glory of divine wisdom and knowledge.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of His Prophetical Work</span>

As our Prophet, Christ:
- **Reveals** the Father to us
- **Declares** the divine will
- **Interprets** the Scriptures
- **Instructs** in righteousness
- **Illuminates** our understanding

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him,"</span> John 1:18.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Excellence of His Teaching</span>

Christ's teaching surpassed all other prophets:

- He taught with **authority**, not as the scribes
- His words were **spirit and life**
- He spoke from **personal knowledge** of heavenly things
- His doctrine was **pure and perfect**
- He confirmed his teaching with **mighty works**

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And they were astonished at his doctrine: for he taught them as one that had authority, and not as the scribes,"</span> Mark 1:22.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Manner of His Revelation</span>

Christ revealed divine truth:

- By his **preaching** during his earthly ministry
- By his **life and example**
- By his **death and resurrection**
- By his **Spirit** sent to guide into all truth
- By the **Scriptures** which testify of him

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me,"</span> John 5:39.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Christ as Our Priest</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. The Priestly Office</span>

In his priestly office, Christ appears as our mediator and intercessor, offering himself as a sacrifice for sin and making reconciliation between God and man.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Sacrifice He Offered</span>

The sacrifice Christ offered was unlike all others:

- It was **himself** that he offered
- It was offered **once for all**
- It was of **infinite value**
- It **perfectly satisfied** divine justice
- It **eternally redeemed** his people

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"So Christ was once offered to bear the sins of many; and unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation,"</span> Hebrews 9:28.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Perfection of His Sacrifice</span>

What made Christ's sacrifice perfect?

- The **dignity of his person** as God-man
- The **voluntariness** of his offering
- The **completeness** of his atonement
- The **acceptability** to the Father
- The **efficacy** for all his people

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"By the which will we are sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all,"</span> Hebrews 10:10.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">His Continuing Intercession</span>

Christ's priestly work continues in heaven:

- He **appears** in the presence of God for us
- He **pleads** his own sacrifice
- He **intercedes** for his people
- He **presents** our prayers and services
- He **blesses** us with spiritual gifts

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them,"</span> Hebrews 7:25.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Christ as Our King</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. The Kingly Office</span>

In his kingly office, Christ exercises dominion over his church and all his enemies, ruling with power and glory.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of His Kingdom</span>

Christ's kingdom is:

- **Spiritual** in nature
- **Universal** in extent
- **Eternal** in duration
- **Absolute** in authority
- **Righteous** in character

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Jesus answered, My kingdom is not of this world: if my kingdom were of this world, then would my servants fight, that I should not be delivered to the Jews: but now is my kingdom not from hence,"</span> John 18:36.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">His Rule Over His Church</span>

As King of his church, Christ:

- **Governs** by his Word and Spirit
- **Protects** from all enemies
- **Provides** all necessary grace
- **Disciplines** in love
- **Leads** to eternal glory

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church,"</span> Ephesians 1:22.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">His Victory Over Enemies</span>

Christ's kingly power is seen in his triumph over:

- **Sin** and its dominion
- **Satan** and his kingdom
- **Death** and the grave
- **The world** and its opposition
- **All spiritual enemies**

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death,"</span> 1 Corinthians 15:26.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Unity of the Three Offices</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. How the Offices Work Together</span>

These three offices are not separate but united in the one person of Christ:

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Prophet Prepares for Priest</span>

As Prophet, Christ reveals our need of salvation and points to himself as the sacrifice for sin. His teaching prepares us to receive his priestly work.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Priest Enables the King</span>

As Priest, Christ removes the obstacles to his reign by making atonement for sin. His sacrificial work establishes his right to rule over us.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. King Applies Prophet and Priest</span>

As King, Christ applies the benefits of his prophetical and priestly work to his people by his Spirit and grace.

All three offices are necessary for our complete salvation.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Glory Displayed in These Offices</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. The Divine Attributes Manifested</span>

In the discharge of his mediatory offices, Christ displays:

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Infinite Wisdom</span>

- In **devising** the plan of salvation
- In **revealing** divine truth
- In **accomplishing** redemption
- In **governing** his church
- In **overruling** all things for good

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"In whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge,"</span> Colossians 2:3.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Perfect Holiness</span>

- In his **teaching** of righteousness
- In his **sinless** sacrifice
- In his **just** government
- In his **pure** laws
- In his **holy** requirements

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Almighty Power</span>

- In **confirming** his doctrine with miracles
- In **offering** himself without spot to God
- In **rising** from the dead
- In **subduing** all enemies
- In **preserving** his people

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Unfailing Love</span>

- In **compassionately** teaching the ignorant
- In **willingly** dying for sinners
- In **gently** ruling his people
- In **patiently** bearing with their weakness
- In **faithfully** keeping his promises

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Benefits to Believers</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. What These Offices Secure for Us</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">From His Prophetical Office</span>

- **Knowledge** of God and divine things
- **Understanding** of our duty
- **Guidance** in all truth
- **Comfort** from his promises
- **Direction** for life and godliness

#### <span style="color: #008080;">From His Priestly Office</span>

- **Forgiveness** of all sin
- **Justification** before God
- **Access** to the throne of grace
- **Acceptance** of our persons and services
- **Eternal redemption**

#### <span style="color: #008080;">From His Kingly Office</span>

- **Protection** from all enemies
- **Provision** of all necessities
- **Government** according to wisdom
- **Discipline** for our good
- **Final victory** and glory

In Christ we have all that we need for time and eternity.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Our Response to These Offices</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. How We Should Respond</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">To Christ as Prophet</span>

- **Hear** his voice with attention
- **Receive** his word with faith
- **Study** his teachings diligently
- **Submit** to his instruction humbly
- **Follow** his guidance implicitly

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him,"</span> Matthew 17:5.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">To Christ as Priest</span>

- **Trust** in his sacrifice alone
- **Plead** his blood for forgiveness
- **Approach** God through him
- **Depend** on his intercession
- **Rest** in his finished work

#### <span style="color: #008080;">To Christ as King</span>

- **Submit** to his authority
- **Obey** his commandments
- **Serve** in his kingdom
- **Fight** under his banner
- **Look** for his appearing

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth,"</span> Philippians 2:10.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Seeking Salvation</span>

Come to Christ in all his offices. You need him as:
- **Prophet** to teach you the way of salvation
- **Priest** to atone for your sins
- **King** to rule and guide your life

Receive him completely, not partially.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Believers</span>

Prize these offices of Christ:
- Value his **teaching** above all human wisdom
- Trust his **sacrifice** above all your works
- Submit to his **rule** above all earthly authority

Let Christ be your all in all—your Prophet, Priest, and King forever.

---

*What glory shines forth in the discharge of these offices! Christ is the perfect Prophet, revealing God; the perfect Priest, reconciling us to God; the perfect King, ruling for God. In him we see divine wisdom, holiness, power, and love perfectly displayed and eternally exercised for our salvation and God's glory.*
