## <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of Divine Love</span>

The fourth thing wherein we may behold the glory of Christ is in **his love—that love wherewith he hath loved his church and given himself for it**. This love is not only an act of his will, but a glorious perfection of his nature as God-man, our mediator.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"As the Father hath loved me, so have I loved you: continue ye in my love,"</span> John 15:9. Here Christ compares his love to us with the Father's love to him—a love infinite, eternal, and unchangeable.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Eternal Foundation of This Love</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. Love Before the Foundation of the World</span>

The love of Christ to his people is not a new thing, begun in time when he came into the world. It is eternal, flowing from the eternal counsel and covenant of peace between the Father and the Son.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love,"</span> Ephesians 1:4.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Origin in Election</span>

This love has its foundation in eternal election. Before we had any being, before we had done good or evil, Christ set his love upon his elect people. This love is the cause of our election, not the effect of it.

The apostle declares that <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated,"</span> Romans 9:13, and this before they were born or had done any good or evil.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Covenant of Grace</span>

In the eternal covenant of grace, Christ undertook to be the head and husband of his church, engaging to love, cherish, and provide for her as his own body.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it,"</span> Ephesians 5:25.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Manifestation of This Love</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. In His Incarnation and Life</span>

The incarnation of Christ was the first great manifestation of his love to us. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Herein is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son to be the propitiation for our sins,"</span> 1 John 4:10.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Manner of His Coming</span>

How did this love manifest itself in his incarnation?

- He came in the **likeness of sinful flesh**, though he knew no sin
- He took our **nature**, with all its innocent infirmities
- He was born in **poverty** and laid in a manger
- He lived a life of **labor** and suffering

All this was love—pure, free, sovereign love!

#### <span style="color: #008080;">His Life of Love</span>

Throughout his earthly life, every action demonstrated his love:

- His **compassion** for the multitudes
- His **healing** of the sick
- His **comfort** to the distressed
- His **patience** with his disciples
- His **tears** over Jerusalem

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people,"</span> Matthew 4:23.

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. In His Death</span>

The greatest manifestation of Christ's love is seen in his death upon the cross. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends,"</span> John 15:13.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Nature of His Death</span>

Consider what his death involved:

- **Separation** from the Father he loved
- **Bearing our sins** in his own body
- **Enduring the curse** of the law
- **Suffering the wrath** of God
- **Experiencing abandonment** and desolation

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?"</span> Matthew 27:46.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Voluntariness of His Sacrifice</span>

This death was not forced upon him, but was his own voluntary act of love:

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Therefore doth my Father love me, because I lay down my life, that I might take it again. No man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of myself,"</span> John 10:17-18.

He could have called twelve legions of angels to his defense, but love constrained him to endure the cross and despise the shame.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Characteristics of This Love</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. The Attributes of Christ's Love</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. It is Free and Unmerited</span>

Christ's love to us is absolutely free. There was nothing in us to attract his love or merit his favor. We were enemies, rebels, dead in trespasses and sins.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us,"</span> Romans 5:8.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. It is Unchangeable</span>

The love of Christ never varies. It is <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"the same yesterday, and today, and forever,"</span> Hebrews 13:8. Our sins cannot diminish it, our failures cannot exhaust it, our coldness cannot quench it.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Having loved his own which were in the world, he loved them unto the end,"</span> John 13:1.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. It is Infinite</span>

The love of Christ passes knowledge. It is broader than our thoughts, deeper than our sins, higher than our fears, and longer than our lives.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"That ye, being rooted and grounded in love, may be able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height; and to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge,"</span> Ephesians 3:17-19.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. It is Effectual</span>

Christ's love is not a mere feeling or affection, but an active, working principle that accomplishes its purpose. What his love designs, it achieves.

It justifies, sanctifies, preserves, and glorifies all its objects.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Fruits of This Love</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. What This Love Accomplishes</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Our Redemption</span>

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace,"</span> Ephesians 1:7.

Love moved Christ to pay our ransom price and set us free from the bondage of sin and Satan.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Our Justification</span>

Love moved Christ to fulfill all righteousness in our stead and to bear the punishment due to our sins, that we might be justified freely by God's grace.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Who was delivered for our offences, and was raised again for our justification,"</span> Romans 4:25.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Our Sanctification</span>

Christ's love does not leave us as it finds us, but works to conform us to his image and make us holy as he is holy.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word, that he might present it to himself a glorious church,"</span> Ephesians 5:26-27.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Our Glorification</span>

This love will not rest until it has brought us to eternal glory. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am; that they may behold my glory,"</span> John 17:24.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Evidence of This Love</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. How We May Know This Love</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. In His Word</span>

The Scriptures are full of declarations and promises of Christ's love. Every promise is a love token, every command is love's direction, every warning is love's concern.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"All the promises of God in him are yea, and in him Amen, unto the glory of God by us,"</span> 2 Corinthians 1:20.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. In His Providence</span>

All the dealings of Christ with his people, whether prosperous or adverse, are expressions of his love. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"All things work together for good to them that love God,"</span> Romans 8:28.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. In His Spirit</span>

The Holy Spirit is given to believers as the earnest and seal of Christ's love. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"The love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us,"</span> Romans 5:5.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Our Response to This Love</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. What This Love Requires</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Faith</span>

We must believe this love. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And we have known and believed the love that God hath to us,"</span> 1 John 4:16.

Many are ready to believe God's power, his justice, his holiness, but slow to believe his love. Yet his love is as real and certain as any other attribute.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Love in Return</span>

Such love demands love in return. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"We love him, because he first loved us,"</span> 1 John 4:19.

If Christ has loved us unto death, should we not love him unto life? If he gave himself for us, should we not give ourselves to him?

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Obedience</span>

Love expresses itself in obedience. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"If ye love me, keep my commandments,"</span> John 14:15.

True love to Christ will show itself in keeping his words, following his example, and walking in his ways.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">4. Gratitude and Praise</span>

Such love should fill our hearts with gratitude and our mouths with praise. <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"What shall I render unto the LORD for all his benefits toward me?"</span> Psalm 116:12.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Those Who Doubt God's Love</span>

Are you questioning whether God loves you? Look to the cross! There you see love demonstrated beyond all doubt. If God spared not his own Son but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Cold and Declining Christians</span>

Has your first love grown cold? Consider afresh the love of Christ. Remember from whence you are fallen, and repent, and do the first works.

Meditate on his love until your heart is melted and inflamed again with love to him.

### <span style="color: #008080;">For All Believers</span>

Let this love be:
- The **foundation** of your confidence
- The **spring** of your obedience
- The **comfort** in your trials
- The **joy** of your hearts
- The **theme** of your praise

---

*O the depths of the love of Christ! It is an ocean without bottom, a mystery without solution, a wonder without end. Angels desire to look into it, but cannot fathom it. Saints experience it, but cannot express it. Let us lose ourselves in adoring contemplation of this love that passeth knowledge.*
