## <span style="color: #008080;">The Remaining Distinctions</span>

Having considered the two great differences between our present sight of Christ's glory by faith and our future vision in heaven—namely, that our present sight is **mediate** while our future sight will be **immediate**, and that our present sight is **obscure** while our future sight will be **clear**—we now turn to examine **other significant differences that distinguish our temporal from our eternal vision**.

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face: now I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known,"</span> 1 Corinthians 13:12.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Third Difference: Partial vs. Complete</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">I. The Fragmentary Nature of Present Knowledge</span>

Our present knowledge of Christ's glory is necessarily **partial** and **incomplete**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Apostolic Declaration</span>

Paul explicitly states this limitation: <span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Now I know in part"</span> (1 Corinthians 13:12).

This partial knowledge affects every aspect of our understanding:
- We know **some** things about Christ, but not **all**
- We understand **portions** of his glory, but not the **whole**
- We comprehend **fragments** of his work, but not the **complete** design
- We see **aspects** of his person, but not the **full** reality
- We grasp **elements** of his love, but not its **infinite** depths

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Reasons for Present Limitations</span>

**1. The Finite Capacity of Human Understanding**
- Our **minds** are created and therefore limited
- Our **comprehension** has boundaries
- Our **faculties** are restricted
- Our **knowledge** is progressive
- Our **wisdom** is developmental

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the LORD. For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts,"</span> Isaiah 55:8-9.

**2. The Infinite Nature of Christ's Glory**
- His glory is **boundless** and immeasurable
- His excellencies are **numberless**
- His perfections are **fathomless**
- His works are **unsearchable**
- His ways are **past finding out**

**3. The Graduated Revelation of Truth**
- God reveals truth **progressively**
- Knowledge comes **line upon line**
- Understanding develops **precept upon precept**
- Wisdom grows **here a little, there a little**
- Maturity advances **from glory to glory**

### <span style="color: #008080;">II. The Completeness of Future Knowledge</span>

In contrast to our present partial knowledge, our future understanding will be **complete** and **perfect**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Promise of Full Knowledge</span>

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Then shall I know even as also I am known,"</span> 1 Corinthians 13:12.

This doesn't mean we will know **everything** that God knows (for we remain creatures), but rather:
- We will know **completely** what God intends us to know
- We will understand **perfectly** what is revealed to us
- We will comprehend **fully** our relationship to Christ
- We will see **clearly** all aspects of his glory meant for our enjoyment
- We will grasp **entirely** the purposes of his redemptive work

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Scope of Perfect Understanding</span>

In heaven we shall fully understand:
- **The Person of Christ** - the mystery of the incarnation will be clear
- **The Work of Christ** - every aspect of redemption will be comprehended
- **The Love of Christ** - its heights, depths, length, and breadth
- **The Ways of Christ** - all his dealings with us will be vindicated
- **The Glory of Christ** - every ray of his excellence will be perceived

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And we have known and believed the love that God hath to us. God is love; and he that dwelleth in love dwelleth in God, and God in him,"</span> 1 John 4:16.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Fourth Difference: Interrupted vs. Constant</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">III. The Intermittent Nature of Present Vision</span>

Our present sight of Christ's glory is **interrupted** and **inconsistent**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Causes of Interruption</span>

**1. External Hindrances**
- **Worldly** cares and distractions
- **Temporal** troubles and afflictions
- **Physical** weakness and illness
- **Social** pressures and conflicts
- **Satanic** opposition and temptation

**2. Internal Obstacles**
- **Spiritual** lethargy and coldness
- **Mental** preoccupation with earthly things
- **Emotional** disturbances and anxieties
- **Moral** failures and compromises
- **Intellectual** doubts and questionings

**3. The Weakness of Faith**
- Faith **fluctuates** in strength
- It **wavers** under pressure
- It **dims** in times of trial
- It **weakens** through neglect
- It **struggles** against unbelief

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief,"</span> Mark 9:24.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Seasonal Character of Spiritual Experience</span>

Even the most mature believers experience:
- **Seasons** of clear vision alternating with times of dimness
- **Periods** of sweet communion followed by spiritual dryness
- **Moments** of bright assurance mixed with hours of doubt
- **Times** of fervent love interrupted by periods of coldness
- **Days** of spiritual vigor broken by seasons of weakness

### <span style="color: #008080;">IV. The Constancy of Heavenly Vision</span>

In heaven, our sight of Christ's glory will be **uninterrupted** and **constant**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Eternal Continuity</span>

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And so shall we ever be with the Lord,"</span> 1 Thessalonians 4:17.

This vision will be:
- **Perpetual** without any breaks
- **Unceasing** without any pauses
- **Continuous** without any interruptions
- **Steady** without any fluctuations
- **Permanent** without any variations

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Removal of All Hindrances</span>

In glory, every obstacle to clear vision will be removed:
- **No sin** to cloud our sight
- **No corruption** to distort our perception
- **No weakness** to interrupt our vision
- **No distraction** to turn our attention
- **No enemy** to oppose our fellowship

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him: And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads,"</span> Revelation 22:3-4.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Fifth Difference: Struggling vs. Effortless</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">V. The Effort Required in Present Vision</span>

Our present sight of Christ's glory requires **constant effort** and **diligent seeking**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Necessity of Spiritual Discipline</span>

To maintain clear sight of Christ, we must:
- **Study** Scripture diligently
- **Pray** fervently and consistently
- **Meditate** on his person and work
- **Resist** the distractions of the world
- **Fight** against spiritual lethargy

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night,"</span> Psalm 1:2.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Battle for Spiritual Focus</span>

Believers must constantly struggle:
- **Against** the flesh that wars against the spirit
- **With** the world that seeks to conform us to its pattern
- **Versus** the devil who would blind our eyes
- **Through** circumstances that obscure our vision
- **Beyond** feelings that contradict our faith

### <span style="color: #008080;">VI. The Ease of Heavenly Vision</span>

In contrast, our heavenly sight of Christ will be **natural** and **effortless**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Perfect Environment</span>

In heaven:
- **No effort** will be required to see clearly
- **No struggle** will be needed to maintain focus
- **No discipline** will be necessary to sustain vision
- **No resistance** will be encountered from opposing forces
- **No weariness** will result from spiritual exercise

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Transformed Nature</span>

Our glorified nature will:
- **Naturally** incline toward Christ
- **Spontaneously** delight in his glory
- **Instinctively** focus on his beauty
- **Automatically** respond to his love
- **Effortlessly** commune with his person

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality,"</span> 1 Corinthians 15:53.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Sixth Difference: Mixed vs. Pure</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">VII. The Mixture in Present Experience</span>

Our present sight of Christ's glory is **mixed** with many imperfections and hindrances.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Contaminating Elements</span>

Even our best spiritual experiences are tainted by:
- **Selfish** motives that corrupt our worship
- **Pride** that seeks glory for ourselves
- **Distraction** that divides our attention
- **Doubt** that questions what we see
- **Carnality** that drags us downward

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Bittersweet Nature of Faith</span>

Our present vision brings:
- **Joy** mixed with sorrow
- **Peace** interrupted by conflict
- **Love** hindered by selfishness
- **Hope** clouded by fear
- **Assurance** troubled by doubt

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For we that are in this tabernacle do groan, being burdened: not for that we would be unclothed, but clothed upon, that mortality might be swallowed up of life,"</span> 2 Corinthians 5:4.

### <span style="color: #008080;">VIII. The Purity of Heavenly Vision</span>

Our future sight of Christ will be **pure** and **unmixed**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Perfect Purity</span>

In heaven, our vision will be:
- **Uncontaminated** by sin
- **Undiluted** by earthly concerns
- **Undistracted** by competing interests
- **Unhindered** by physical limitations
- **Unmixed** with any imperfection

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Single Focus</span>

Our entire being will be:
- **Concentrated** on Christ alone
- **Absorbed** in his glory
- **Satisfied** with his beauty
- **Delighted** in his presence
- **Fulfilled** in his love

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And there shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie: but they which are written in the Lamb's book of life,"</span> Revelation 21:27.

## <span style="color: #008080;">The Seventh Difference: Temporal vs. Eternal</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">IX. The Temporary Nature of Present Vision</span>

Our present sight of Christ, precious as it is, remains **temporal** and **transient**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Limitations of Time</span>

In this life:
- Our **days** are numbered
- Our **opportunities** are limited
- Our **time** for growth is restricted
- Our **seasons** of clarity are brief
- Our **moments** of communion are fleeting

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Approaching End</span>

This present vision will cease when:
- **Death** separates us from earthly means of grace
- **Faith** gives way to sight
- **Hope** is fulfilled in possession
- **Time** yields to eternity
- **Mortality** is swallowed up by life

### <span style="color: #008080;">X. The Eternal Duration of Heavenly Vision</span>

Our future sight of Christ will be **eternal** and **endless**.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Infinite Duration</span>

This vision will:
- **Never** cease or fade
- **Always** remain bright and clear
- **Continuously** increase in sweetness
- **Perpetually** satisfy and delight
- **Eternally** fulfill and complete us

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"And so shall we ever be with the Lord,"</span> 1 Thessalonians 4:17.

#### <span style="color: #008080;">The Ever-Fresh Experience</span>

Though eternal, this vision will never become:
- **Stale** or boring
- **Familiar** to the point of indifference
- **Repetitive** in a wearying way
- **Exhausted** of its wonder
- **Diminished** in its appeal

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, the things which God hath prepared for them that love him,"</span> 1 Corinthians 2:9.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Practical Applications</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Our Present Journey</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Patience with Limitations</span>

Understanding these differences should produce:
- **Patience** with our current partial knowledge
- **Acceptance** of our intermittent vision
- **Contentment** despite present struggles
- **Hope** for future perfection
- **Perseverance** through temporary difficulties

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Diligent Use of Present Means</span>

While our vision is limited, we must:
- **Value** every opportunity for clear sight
- **Use** all means of grace faithfully
- **Seek** Christ earnestly in his Word
- **Maintain** spiritual disciplines consistently
- **Fight** for clarity of vision

#### <span style="color: #008080;">3. Proper Perspective on Struggles</span>

Remember that present difficulties are:
- **Temporary** not eternal
- **Partial** not complete
- **Interrupted** not constant
- **Mixed** not pure
- **Limited** not infinite

### <span style="color: #008080;">For Our Future Hope</span>

#### <span style="color: #008080;">1. Eager Anticipation</span>

These truths should create:
- **Longing** for perfect vision
- **Desire** for complete knowledge
- **Anticipation** of constant communion
- **Hope** for effortless sight
- **Expectation** of pure enjoyment

#### <span style="color: #008080;">2. Present Comfort</span>

In times of spiritual difficulty, remember:
- Your **partial** knowledge will become complete
- Your **interrupted** vision will become constant
- Your **struggling** effort will become effortless
- Your **mixed** experience will become pure
- Your **temporal** sight will become eternal

<span style="color: #1e90ff;">"For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us,"</span> Romans 8:18.

## <span style="color: #008080;">Final Application</span>

### <span style="color: #008080;">For All Believers</span>

Live in the tension between present limitations and future perfection:
- **Prize** your current glimpses of Christ's glory
- **Use well** your present means of grace
- **Be patient** with your limitations
- **Long earnestly** for perfect sight
- **Press forward** toward the goal

The differences are great, but the glory that awaits makes all present struggles worthwhile. What we see now in part, we shall see completely. What we know now partially, we shall understand fully. What we experience now intermittently, we shall enjoy constantly.

---

*How numerous and significant are the differences between our present and future vision of Christ! Now partial, then complete; now interrupted, then constant; now struggling, then effortless; now mixed, then pure; now temporal, then eternal. Yet even our present sight is glorious, for it is a true sight of the true Christ. Let us value what we have while longing for what we shall receive. The best is yet to come, and it will be worth all our present waiting and watching.*
