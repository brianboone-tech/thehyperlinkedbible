## About This Work

**The Typology of Scripture** is Patrick Fairbairn's comprehensive two-volume study of biblical typology, first published in 1864. This work examines how Old Testament persons, events, and institutions prefigure and point to their fulfillment in Jesus Christ and the New Testament.

**Key Themes:**
- Principles of typical interpretation
- Primeval and patriarchal dispensations
- The Mosaic dispensation and ceremonial law
- Christological fulfillment of Old Testament types

**Total Content:** 32 chapters across 2 volumes, plus 9 appendices
