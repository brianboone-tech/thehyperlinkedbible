# Christ in All the Scriptures

**Author**: A.M. Hodgkin
**Published**: 1909 (Third Edition)
**Source**: [Archive.org](https://archive.org/details/christinallscrip00hodguoft)

## About This Book

"And beginning at Moses and all the prophets he expounded unto them in all the Scriptures the things concerning Himself" (Luke 24:27)

A.M. Hodgkin takes readers through every book of the Bible, demonstrating how each points to Christ. This is a classic work of Christocentric biblical interpretation.
