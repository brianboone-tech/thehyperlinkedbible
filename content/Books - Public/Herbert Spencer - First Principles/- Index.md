# First Principles

**Herbert Spencer** (1863)

---

## About This Work

*First Principles* is the foundational volume of Herbert Spencer's ambitious "System of Synthetic Philosophy." Published in 1862, it establishes Spencer's framework for understanding the universe through the lens of evolution—a concept he applied not just to biology, but to all phenomena: physical, psychological, and social.

The work is divided into two parts:
- **Part I: The Unknowable** — Examines the limits of human knowledge and argues for a reconciliation between religion and science
- **Part II: Laws of the Knowable** — Develops Spencer's universal law of evolution and its applications
