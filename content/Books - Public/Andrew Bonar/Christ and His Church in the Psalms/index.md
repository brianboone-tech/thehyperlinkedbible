# Christ and His Church in the Book of Psalms

**Author:** Andrew A. Bonar (1810-1892)
**First Published:** 1859
**Publisher:** James Nisbet & Co., London

A Christocentric commentary on all 150 Psalms, showing how Christ and His Church are present throughout the Psalter. Bonar writes: "The voice of Christ and His Church was well-nigh the only voice to be heard in the Psalms."

---

## About the Author

Andrew Alexander Bonar was a minister of the Free Church of Scotland and close friend of Robert Murray M'Cheyne. He is best known for his *Memoir of M'Cheyne* and his commentaries on Leviticus and the Psalms. As a member of the Scottish "brotherhood" that included Horatius Bonar and others, he represented the warm evangelical piety and typological interpretation characteristic of 19th century Scottish Reformed theology.

## Hermeneutical Approach

Bonar follows Augustine's principle: *"Vix est ut in Psalmis inveniamus vocem nisi Christi et Ecclesiae"* — "The voice of Christ and His Church was well-nigh the only voice to be heard in the Psalms."

His interpretive method:
1. First establish the **literal and historical sense**
2. Then ask what the **Holy Spirit intended to teach in all ages**
3. Keep "our left eye on David, while we have our right eye full on Christ"
