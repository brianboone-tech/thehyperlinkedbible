## About the Author

Charles Henry Mackintosh (1820-1896), generally known as C.H.M., was an Irish Bible teacher associated with the Plymouth Brethren movement. His *Notes on the Pentateuch* is his most famous work, beloved for its warm, devotional exposition and typological insight. D.L. Moody reportedly said of these notes: "They have been more help to me than anything else I have ever read."

## Hermeneutical Approach

Mackintosh writes with a warm, pastoral heart while maintaining careful attention to:
- **Typological interpretation** - seeing Christ prefigured in OT narratives
- **Practical application** - connecting Scripture to Christian living
- **Grace-centered theology** - emphasizing God's redemptive work
